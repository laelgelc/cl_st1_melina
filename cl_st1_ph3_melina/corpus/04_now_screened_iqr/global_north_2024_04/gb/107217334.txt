<p> Palestinian Redwan Abu Alkas lost his daughter in an Israeli airstrike . His home was destroyed and the family 's dream of opening its own business , a pizzeria in the once bustling Gaza Strip , was shattered . <p> The family had saved up the money and bought all the equipment . That was before the Israeli bombardment of one of the most densely populated areas in the world began in the conflict between Israel and the Palestinian Hamas militants who rule Gaza . <p> " In this war , I lost my daughter , first of all . I used to have money to start a good project .
