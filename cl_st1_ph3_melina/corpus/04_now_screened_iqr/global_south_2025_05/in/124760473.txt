<p> Indian academic and Georgetown University visiting scholar Badar Khan Suri has been released from US immigration detention after nearly two months of incarceration that he described as a " Kafkaesque " ordeal . <p> Indian academic and Georgetown University visiting scholar Badar Khan Suri has been released from US immigration detention after nearly two months of incarceration that he described as a " Kafkaesque " ordeal . Arrested outside his Virginia home on March 17 by plainclothes federal agents , Suri was held without formal charges at multiple detention facilities , including the Prairieland Detention Center in Texas . <p> " There was no charge , there was nothing . They made a sub-human out of me , " said Suri , recounting how he was shackled from head to toe . " My ankles , my wrist , my body everything was chained . For the first seven or eight days , I even missed my shadow .
