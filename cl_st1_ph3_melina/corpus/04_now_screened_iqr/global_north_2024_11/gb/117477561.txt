<h> Artists unite by donating works to help raise money for families in Gaza <h> Artists unite by donating work to raise money for families in Gaza <p> MORE than 60 artists have donated a range of artworks for a silent auction in aid of stricken families in Gaza . <p> The auction is billed as a rare opportunity to buy a number of " stunning " works of art including paintings , prints , ceramics and sculptures . <p> Also on offer are a selection of artists ' books and zines and a host of online workshops , including poetry , project development and short film-making . <p> Organiser Crystal Bennes , an artist , writer and researcher , said every penny raised would provide much-needed support to families in Gaza and local solidarity organisation The Sameer Project , which is currently active on the ground there . <p> She said : " After more than a year of the current genocide , it is so crucial that financial support is sent directly to families and smaller organisations in Gaza . <p> " Artists For Gaza is helping to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Project , a grassroots organisation led by Palestinians in the diaspora .
