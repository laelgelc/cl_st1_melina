<h> Sderot band Shufuni goes abroad to explain what life is like in Israel 's South <p> To understand the evolution of the Sderot-based band Shufuni , now touring the North American diaspora , you need to look at the larger picture . <p> In the 1980s , before the southern city became the target of rockets , it was a town of music . Israeli bands like Sfatayim , Teapacks , and Knesiyat Hasechel burst from the local music scene to nationwide success . <p> " In those years , music insiders referred to Sderot as the Liverpool of Israel , and in the nineties , as Israel 's Seattle , " says Ohad Peretz , an indie musician and producer , and Shufuni 's founder and manager . <p> A burgeoning arts scene was growing in the city . By the 2010s , a cultural phenomenon was taking place , known as Ha-Tarbush . <p> A group of singers , musicians , and other artists , Peretz included , formed a cooperative that gathered to make art , music , and theatre , reflecting life along the Gaza @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ manager . " But everything that happened afterwards -- how they could turn it into a performance -- was difficult for them .
