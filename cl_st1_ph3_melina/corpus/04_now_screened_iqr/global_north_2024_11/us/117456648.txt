<p> " How are you ? " It is a simple question I get in daily messages from my friends -- Kenyan , Nigerian , Turkish , British , Jordanian , Iranian and Moroccan . While it may seem like an ordinary question for friends to ask each other , I feel they use it as a way to reassure themselves that I am still alive . <p> I understand why they are doing it but every time I see a message with this question , I find it hard to answer . <p> How am I , when I keep thinking of my home which I left in panic on the second day of the genocide ? How am I , knowing that the place I spent years building with my husband has been obliterated by an Israeli air strike ? How am I , remembering the images I saw in media reports showing just a pile of rubble where my home used to stand , where so many sweet memories were made ? <p> How am I , as my dreams of completing my PhD lies under the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ I hear my little girl crying and asking me what happened to her toys ?
