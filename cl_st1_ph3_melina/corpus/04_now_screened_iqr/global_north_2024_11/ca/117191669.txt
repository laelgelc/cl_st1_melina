<h> Opinion : Academic boycotts of Israel are an ethical imperative <p> Content warning : This article discusses death and the ongoing violence in Palestine . <p> A few years back , I celebrated the completion of my undergraduate studies with Professor Sofyan Taya . Taya is a renowned physicist and president of the Islamic University of Gaza , and was a visiting scholar at the University of Waterloo when we met . I shared my interest in pursuing a similar career : one day being a part of the esteemed enterprise of higher education . <p> However , it is necessary to understand how North American universities partnering with Israeli academic institutions contributed to Israel 's scholasticide -- the systemic obliteration of education -- during this genocide of Palestinians . <p> Since the 1980s , the University of Toronto has consistently opposed academic boycotts on the grounds of " promoting academic freedom " and " freedom of expression .
