<p> The US informed Lebanese officials on Monday night that a ceasefire between Israel and Hizbullah could be announced " within hours " . Lebanon 's deputy speaker of parliament Elias Bou Saab told Reuters that there were " no serious obstacles " remaining between the sides . <p> The Israeli security cabinet will convene on Tuesday and is expected to approve the ceasefire agreement , with diplomatic sources in Israel indicating that a deal is very close after more than 13 months of fighting . <p> The powerful Shia militia , backed by Iran , began firing rockets into northern Israel from Lebanon the day after the Hamas attack on Israel on October 7th , 2023 .
