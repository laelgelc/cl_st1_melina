<h> Donegal County Council gives unanimous support to the Occupied Territories Bill <h> The Occupied Territories Bill was first tabled in 2018 by Senator Frances Black and aims to make it an offence in Ireland to import , attempt to import or assist another person in importing goods made in Israeli settlements <p> Donegal County Council has given its unanimous backing to the Occupied Territories Bill , which would ban trade with Israel 's illegal colonial settlements in the Occupied Territories . <p> The Occupied Territories Bill was first tabled in 2018 by Senator Frances Black and aims to make it an offence in Ireland to import , attempt to import or assist another person in importing goods made in Israeli settlements , which are considered illegal under international law by the UN and most states . <p> It is hoped the Occupied Territories Bill will be passed into law by the new government following the general election . <p> The Donegal branch of the Ireland Palestine Solidarity Committee said : " Today is a great day for Donegal when our elected representatives showed that the people of Donegal stand @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ against oppression under the Israeli apartheid state and its ongoing campaign of genocide .
