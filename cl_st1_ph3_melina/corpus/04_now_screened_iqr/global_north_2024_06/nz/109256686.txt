The departure of Benny Gantz from the Israeli war cabinet , which had served as a checking forum against the conventional security cabinet , presented a perfect opportunity for those who felt his presence stifling . In these febrile times , Gantz , the leader of the opposition National Unity party , passes as a moderate centrist and had been one of its three voting members , alongside Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Defence Minister Yoav Gallant . <p> The resignation was prompted by Netanyahu 's tardy attitude towards formulating a plan to end the war in Gaza .
