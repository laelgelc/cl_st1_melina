<p> DUBAI - Palestinian swimmer Yazan Al Bawwab , who is training for the 2024 Olympic Games , has a mission . He wants to represent Palestinians suffering from Israel 's bombardment of Gaza by making a splash on the international stage . <p> These Olympics , which kick off in Paris on July 26 , are perhaps more important than the last games in 2021 when he competed in Tokyo , he said . <p> " We , as Palestinian players , are here to raise the flag and show people that we are here , and even if we face difficulties , we will be there and represent the Palestinian people , " he said . <p> The 2.3 million Palestinians living in Gaza need all the help they can get as they endure dire conditions .
