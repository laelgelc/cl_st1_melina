<h> At least 27 dead , 90 injured in Gaza shooting <p> At least 27 people were killed and over 90 injured by Israeli forces near a humanitarian aid distribution center in the southern Gaza Strip on Tuesday morning , according to the Hamas-run Ministry of Health in Gaza . <p> This marks the second fatal incident in three days near an aid site . The ministry said victims were waiting to collect food when the shooting began . <p> Of those killed , 24 bodies and 37 injured individuals were brought to the Nasser Medical Complex in Khan Younis .
