<p> Before 1979 , Iran was a linchpin of Western strategy in the Middle East . Under Shah Mohammad Reza Pahlavi ( 1925 -- 79 ) , Tehran was one of the United States ' closest regional partners and an unofficial Israeli ally . The 1953 CIA-backed coup that restored the Shah entrenched this alliance . Iran received extensive American military aid , acquired nuclear assistance , and became a vital oil supplier to Israel . The Shah 's regime even hosted an Israeli embassy and built an Iran -- Israel oil pipeline as part of Israel 's " periphery doctrine " to break its regional isolation . <p> In 1979 , the Islamic Revolution dismantled the pro-Western monarchy . Ayatollah Khomeini seized power , branding the U.S. the " Great Satan " and Israel the " Little Satan . " Diplomatic ties were abruptly severed : Iranian students stormed the U.S. embassy , taking hostages for 444 days , and Jewish and Israeli staff were expelled . Iran 's new constitution denounced Israel 's legitimacy and cast itself as the champion of the Palestinian cause .
