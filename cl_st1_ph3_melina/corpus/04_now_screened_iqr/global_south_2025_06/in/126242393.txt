Why Is It Being Banned Now ? <p> Palestine Action is a UK-based protest group founded in 2020 that describes itself as a direct action movement focused on ending British complicity in what it sees as Israel 's war crimes . The group targets arms manufacturers , government offices , financial institutions , and other entities it accuses of supporting or enabling Israel 's military operations , particularly in Gaza . Its ultimate aim is to disrupt and shut down the supply chains that it believes contribute to Israeli military violence . <p> The group 's central target has been Elbit Systems UK , the British arm of Israel 's largest private arms company . Palestine Action accuses Elbit of manufacturing drones and weapon components used in attacks on Palestinians . Other frequent targets include Thales Group , a French defense contractor with UK operations , as well as universities and banks allegedly tied to defense industries or Israeli government contracts . <p> Palestine Action is known for its disruptive and high-profile protest tactics .
