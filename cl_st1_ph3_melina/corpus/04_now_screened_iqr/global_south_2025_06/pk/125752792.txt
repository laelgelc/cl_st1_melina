<h> May Cowards Never Sleep <p> I still remember the words of the late Allama Khadim Hussain Rizvi -- may Allah grant him a high rank in Jannah -- who used strong language throughout his life against Western oppressors of Muslims . Whenever any crime was committed against Muslims , he condemned Muslim rulers as cowards , wishing they would never sleep for their inaction . His words ring true today for those rulers paralysed by the fear of death , watching passively as Gaza suffers before our eyes . <p> O cowardly rulers , you have forgotten Gaza . Since March 2 , the brutal state of Israel has blocked aid to Gazans , leaving them to starve . The world watches the Muslim world 's silence . One can scarcely imagine life where there is no humanitarian aid , where roads for relief are blocked , and where people await their hour of martyrdom . As a writer , I can not restrain my sorrow nor my pride in speaking for the Palestinians . I can not fathom the stone-hearted indifference of those who , despite having power and weapons , remain silent . <p> May those Muslim rulers who fear death never sleep . Death is inevitable , but resistance is eternal . Why fear death when it is decreed by Allah ? We belong to Allah and to Him we will return . O cowardly Muslims , may you never sleep , for your inaction has betrayed your Palestinian brothers , sisters , and children . Have you ever imagined living where death sirens wail daily ? <p> O Arab world , may you never sleep . This cowardice has dulled Muslim awareness . May cowards never sleep . All will die , but those who fall defending their Muslim brothers will earn high ranks in Jannah . <p> It is a source of deep sorrow that no Muslim country dares attack Israel to defend Gaza .
