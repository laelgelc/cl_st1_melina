<p> A video recovered from the cellphone of an aid worker killed in Gaza alongside other rescuers shows their final moments , according to the Palestine Red Crescent , with clearly marked ambulances and emergency lights flashing as heavy gunfire erupts . <p> The aid worker was among 15 humanitarian personnel who were killed on March 23 in an attack by Israeli forces , according to the United Nations and the Palestine Red Crescent Society ( PRCS ) . <p> The Israeli military has said its soldiers " did not randomly attack " any ambulances , insisting they fired on " terrorists " approaching them in " suspicious vehicles " . <p> Military spokesman Lieutenant Colonel Nadav Shoshani said that troops opened fire on vehicles that had no prior clearance from Israeli authorities and had their lights off . <p> But the video released by PRCS on Saturday appears to contradict the Israeli military 's claims , showing ambulances travelling with their headlights and emergency lights clearly flashing . <p> The six minute and 42 second video , apparently filmed from inside a moving vehicle , captures a red firetruck @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stop beside another on the roadside , and two uniformed men exit .
