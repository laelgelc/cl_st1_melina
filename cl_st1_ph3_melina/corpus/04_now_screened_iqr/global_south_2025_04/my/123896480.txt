<p> By AFP , JerusalemThe Latin patriarch of Jerusalem , Archbishop Pierbattista Pizzaballa , on April 22 hailed Pope Francis 's support for Gazans and engagement with the small Catholic community in the war-battered Palestinian territory . <p> The Catholic church 's highest authority in the region , who is considered a potential successor to the late pontiff , Pizzaballa told journalists in Jerusalem that " Gaza represents , a little bit , all what was the heart of his pontificate . " <p> Pope Francis , who died on Monday aged 88 , advocated peace and " closeness to the poor ... and to the neglected one , " said the patriarch . <p> These positions became particularly evident in Francis 's response to the Israel-Hamas war , which broke out in October 2023 , Pizzaballa said . <p> " He was very close to the community of Gaza , the parish of Gaza , he kept calling them many times -- for a certain period , also every day , every evening at 7 pm , " said the patriarch . <p> He added that by doing so @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and also comforting for them , and he knew this . " <p> Out of the Gaza Strip 's 2.4 million people , about 1,000 are Christians . Most of them are Orthodox , but according to the Latin Patriarchate , there are about 135 Catholics in the territory . <p> Since the early days of the war , members of the Catholic community have been sheltering at Holy Family Church compound in Gaza City , and some Orthodox Christians have also found refuge there . <p> Pope Francis repeatedly called for an end to the war . The day before his death , in a final Easter message delivered on Sunday , he condemned the " deplorable humanitarian situation " in the besieged territory . <p> " Work for justice ... but without becoming part of the conflict , " said Pizzaballa of the late pontiff 's actions . <p> " For us , for the Church , it leaves an important legacy . " <p> The patriarch thanked the numerous Palestinian and Israeli public figures who have offered their condolences , preferring not to comment on the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Benjamin Netanyahu . <p> Even as " the local authorities ... were not always happy " with the pope 's positions or statements , they were " always very respectful , " he said . <p> Pizzaballa said he will travel to Rome on April 23 , after leading a requiem mass for the pope at the Church of the Holy Sepulchre in Jerusalem in the morning . <p> As one of the 135 cardinal electors , the Latin patriarch will participate in the conclave to elect a new pope . <p> Pizzaballa , a 60-year-old Italian Franciscan who also speaks English and Hebrew , arrived in Jerusalem in 1990 and was made a cardinal in September 2023 , just before the Gaza war began . <p> His visits to Gaza and appeals for peace since then have attracted international attention. --ucanews.com
