<p> Israel 's top military commander Lieutenant General Eyal Zamir has strongly criticized attempts to politicize military personnel during the ongoing Gaza conflict . During a field tour in Shuja'iyya , Zamir emphasized the IDF 's commitment to its core mission while respecting soldiers ' democratic rights . He rejected a protest letter by reservists demanding an immediate end to the war , stating that such group statements are unacceptable . Zamir underscored the military 's professional approach , focusing on defending the state , returning hostages , and defeating Hamas . <p> " The IDF will continue to operate in a state-like manner and will not allow disputes to penetrate its ranks " - Lt.
