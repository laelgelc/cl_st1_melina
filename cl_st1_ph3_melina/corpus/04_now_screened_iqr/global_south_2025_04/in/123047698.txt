<h> Israel orders mass evacuation in Rafah amid renewed Gaza offensive <p> Displaced Palestinians , who flee from Rafah , arrive in Khan Younis , Gaza , on Monday , March 31 , 2025 , after the Israeli military issued sweeping evacuation orders covering most of Rafah AP <p> Israel issued sweeping evacuation orders in Rafah , Gaza 's southernmost city , on March 31 , prompting tens of thousands of Palestinians to flee at a time when Muslims were celebrating Eid ul-Fitr , marking the end of the holy month of Ramadan . The orders , affecting at least 1.4 lakh people according to Philippe Lazzarini of the UN agency for Palestinian refugees ( UNRWA ) , hinted at an impending major ground operation . Lazzarini described the situation as treating people " like pinballs " , with constant military directives upending their lives . <p> The evacuation covered Rafah and the areas between Rafah and Khan Younis . The IDF 's Arabic-language spokesman , Avichay Adraee , asked people to shift out of Al-Shawka , Al-Nasr and the neighbourhoods of Al-Salam , Al-Manara and Qizan an-Najjar . In northern Gaza , the IDF claimed to have dismantled a Hamas tunnel and a rocket workshop , killing 50 " terrorists " . The last major escalation in Rafah , located close to the Egyptian border , took place last May and left the city in ruins . Israel seized the Rafah crossing , Gaza 's only non-Israeli-controlled external link , and a border corridor to curb weapons smuggling . <p> A ceasefire signed in January this year under American pressure required Israel to leave the corridor . These are conditions that were not part of the original ceasefire deal and which Hamas rejects .
