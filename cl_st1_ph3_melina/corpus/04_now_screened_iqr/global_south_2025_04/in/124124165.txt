The hearings began Monday in The Hague and will continue throughout the week . <p> The United Nations General Assembly requested the ICJ 's intervention last year . It asked the court to assess whether Israel is fulfilling its legal duty to provide essential aid to Gaza . <p> Over the next five days , 38 countries will present their arguments to the 15-judge panel .
