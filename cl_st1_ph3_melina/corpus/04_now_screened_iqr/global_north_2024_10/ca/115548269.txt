<p> Jon Allen is a Senior Fellow at the Munk School of Global Affairs and Public Policy and a former Canadian Ambassador to Israel and Spain . <p> It has been a year since the Hamas-led massacre of Oct. 7 in southern Israel . In light of the Israeli responses that followed in Gaza , in the West Bank and more recently in Lebanon , what concerns me perhaps more than anything else is the extreme polarization these events have spurred both in the region and in Canada . <p> In Israel , the brutal attack , hostage taking , and failure of the Israeli military to respond for hours , provoked a widespread sense of fear , shame and a strong desire for revenge . Israelis had fought and died in various wars since 1948 in which the outcomes and the country 's very existence seemed at risk . <p> But in none of those wars had they felt so humiliated , or suffered a similar blow to their sense of safety and security .
