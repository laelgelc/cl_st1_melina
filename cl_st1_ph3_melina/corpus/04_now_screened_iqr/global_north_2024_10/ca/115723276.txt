<p> Quebec Superior Court granted a temporary injunction against a pro-Palestine organization from blocking buildings or protesting within five metres of campus buildings at McGill University , while Jewish students are continuing to commemorate the one-year anniversary of Hamas 's Oct. 7 attacks . <p> The provisional injunction , issued on Oct. 9 -- which will remain valid for 10 days -- is intended to mitigate further threats by Students for Palestine 's Honour and Resistance ( SPHR ) , a club not officially registered with the school , which announced a ' Week of Rage ' from Oct. 7 to 11 . <p> A video shows protesters barging through a metal fence , which was erected by Montreal police , after McGill University announced that access to campus would be restricted to students , faculty and essential visitors from Oct. 5 to Oct. 11 . The posts also show windows being smashed . <p> According to the SPHR caption , protesters targeted the construction site of the Sylvan Adams ' Sports Science Institute , " which hopes to establish a permanent partnership with Tel Aviv University , " on Monday . <p> " The site was met with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ no peace so long as McGill continues to partner with institutions complicit in genocide , " the SPHR Instagram caption says . <p> The Students ' Society of McGill University ( SSMU ) Board of Directors removed the club status of SPHR McGill , after receiving a notice from the university 's president office that informed them of hateful behaviour and demanded that the club 's status be revoked . <p> McGill sought the injunction to " protect its academic mission and defend students ' and instructors ' right to learn and work in an environment that is safe , stable , and suitable for teaching , learning , and research , " according to a statement from McGill 's deputy provost Angela Campbell and vice-president Fabrice Labeau . <p> The injunction will be in place while students write midterm exams , the court decision noted , with more than 13,600 midterms scheduled for this week . <p> The decision , written by Justice Babak Barin , orders protesters affiliated with SPHR to " cease , desist and refrain from blocking or otherwise obstructing " the entrance to any buildings @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to protest within five metres of any buildings on campus and to refrain from " intimidating , harassing and/or threatening " anyone entering a building on campus . <p> Barin wrote that he could not disagree with McGill 's submission that SPHR 's announcement of a Week of Rage , " poses an imminent and major threat to the stability of academic life and the rights of students , staff and faculty at the university .
