<p> NEW YORK : Aid access to the Gaza Strip is extremely limited with less than 1,000 truckloads of humanitarian assistance entering the enclave since May 7 , after Israel began a military operation in southern Gaza 's Rafah area , the United Nations said on Friday . <p> The U.N . Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs ( OCHA ) said that between May 7 and May 23 , only 906 truckloads entered the enclave of 2.3 million people , where a famine looms amid the war between Israel and Palestinian Hamas . <p> U.N . spokesperson Stephane Dujarric said about 800 of those truckloads were food supplies . <p> OCHA said 143 truckloads passed through the Israel-controlled Kerem Shalom crossing in Gaza 's south , while in Gaza 's north 62 passed through the Erez crossing and 604 via Erez West . It said 97 truckloads have come through a U.S.-built floating pier in central Gaza that began operating a week ago . <p> The Rafah crossing from Egypt into Gaza has been closed since Israel began stepping up its military operation in the area , creating a backlog of aid in Egypt where some of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the United States had called on Egypt , which is also concerned about the risk of Palestinians being displaced from Gaza , to reopen the border . Egypt had said it was closed due to the threat posed to aid work by Israel 's military operation . <p> On Friday , Egypt 's President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi agreed with U.S. President Joe Biden by phone to temporarily send humanitarian aid and fuel to the U.N . via the Kerem Shalom crossing , the Egyptian presidency said . <p> " There are a lot of doorways into Gaza . ... Whether by land or by sea , we do n't control those doorways , but we want them all to be open , " Dujarric said on Thursday . <p> OCHA said on Friday its figures do not include commercial trucks because the U.N . has been unable to observe private-sector deliveries through Kerem Shalom crossing due to insecurity . <p> " Additionally , just over 1 million litres of fuel have entered the Gaza Strip since the beginning of the military operation in Rafah , " OCHA said in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ average of 29% of fuel allocations that would have been received under arrangements in place prior to 6 May , further affecting the functioning of bakeries , hospitals , water wells , and other critical infrastructure , " it said . <p> The U.N . says at least 500 trucks a day of aid and commercial goods need to enter Gaza . In April , an average of 189 trucks entered a day - the highest since the war started in October . <p> Israel is retaliating against Hamas , which rules Gaza , over an Oct. 7 attack by the Palestinian in which more than 1,200 people were killed and over 250 taken hostage , according to Israeli tallies . Nearly 130 hostages are believed to remain captive in Gaza . <p> Israel launched an air , ground and sea assault on the blockaded Palestinian territory , killing more than 35,000 Palestinians , according to Gaza health authorities .
