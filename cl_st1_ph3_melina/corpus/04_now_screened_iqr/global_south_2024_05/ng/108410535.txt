The post which was published on Monday , claimed that the UN published different figures in its breakdown of fatalities in Gaza where the Israeli Armed forces killed some people in a war against Hamas . <p> It was stated in the post that the UN OCHA " revised its child fatality figure from Gaza war sharply downward , reporting more than 14,500 deaths on May 6 , but then 7,797 on May 8 . OCHA also revised downward its figure for women fatalities from more than 9,500 deaths to 4,959 deaths . Here is the true story : <p> The UN OCHA , on May 8 , in its infographic visualization of fatalities in Gaza referred to a figure of 34,844 as total Palestinian deaths . However , it explained that " 24,686 identified as of April 30 included : 10,006 men , 4,959 women , 7,797 children , 1,924 elderly " . The so-called " new number " of 24,686 refers only to bodies that have been identified -- of those , 7,797 were children , 4,959 women and 1,924 were elderly people . More than 10,000 recovered bodies were yet to be identified . <p> CONCLUSION <p> The United Nations did not reverse its figures of casualties in Gaza . It only did a breakdown of identified bodies .
