In Gaza , the 2.3 million population -- nearly all of whom are displaced from their homes -- will be allowed to move freely around the territory , and there is expected to be a huge increase in aid supplies . <p> Israel 's stated objectives in the 15-month war in Gaza , which was triggered by the Hamas attack of 7 October 2023 , are to completely destroy the Palestinian group , and to bring the remaining 100 or so hostages home .
