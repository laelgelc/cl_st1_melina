Israelis , including two women and an 80-year-old man , as well as five Thai nationals during the next hostage release , set for Thursday , officials from Israel and Hamas said as a tenuous ceasefire between the sides moves ahead . <p> The officials named the Israeli women as Arbel Yehoud , 29 , Agam Berger , 20 , and the man as Gadi Moses , 80 . <p> The officials , who spoke on Wednesday on condition of anonymity because they were not authorised to speak to the media , said the hostages ' families had approved the publication of their names . <p> The identities of the Thai nationals were not immediately known . <p> A number of foreign workers were taken captive along with dozens of Israeli civilians and soldiers during Hamas 's October 7 2023 attack that set off the war in Gaza . <p>
