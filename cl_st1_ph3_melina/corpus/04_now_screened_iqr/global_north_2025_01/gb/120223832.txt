<p> Relatives of Palestinian prisoner Khalida Jarrar were bracing on Saturday for her release in the first wave of exchanges agreed upon as part of the Gaza ceasefire deal . <p> The Palestinian political leader and human rights and feminist advocate has been held in administrative detention since December 2023 .
