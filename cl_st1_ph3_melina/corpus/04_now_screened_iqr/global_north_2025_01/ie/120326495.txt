The announcement triggered an immediate cascade of changes in Israel 's military leadership . <p> " On the morning of October 7 , under my command , the IDF failed in its mission to protect the citizens of Israel , " Halevi wrote in his resignation letter addressed to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Defence Minister Israel Katz . " The State of Israel paid a heavy and painful price in lives lost , in those taken hostage , in the wounded -- both physically and mentally . The accelerated timeline suggests Zamir may secure the appointment , given his previous consideration for the role . <p> The rapid leadership transition comes as the military reevaluates its war strategy amid the ceasefire agreement with Gaza .
