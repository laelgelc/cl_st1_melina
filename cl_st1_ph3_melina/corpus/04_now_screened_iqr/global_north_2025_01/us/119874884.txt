Throughout history , Jews and Christians have intertwined paths , and today , we need to continue to stand together , advocating for the rights of the Jewish people to live and prosper in their ancestral homeland . <p> Our trip underscored this commitment , revealing that their support is not just ideological but also deeply personal and practical .
