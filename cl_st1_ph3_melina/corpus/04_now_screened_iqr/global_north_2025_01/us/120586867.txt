" <p> " The euphoria fades , and there 's the euphoria of the great relief of the release itself . " <h> Psychological scars <p> Dr. Yehene has worked with released hostages from previous exchanges , and her conclusions are not at all euphoric : " What @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is that none of them have ever found themselves again in this world . " <p> " The integration is difficult , and people carry complex post-traumatic symptoms . Some of the captives walk around with therapy dogs , some ca n't go anywhere without family members . They also have nutritional deficiencies and a sense of alienation . " <p> " Until all the hostages return , they wo n't be able to fully rehabilitate , " she added . <p> According to her research , full rehabilitation can not occur without the return of all the hostages . She views this as a deep wound for both the returned hostages and their families : " One thing that stands out as a central element is that as long as there are hostages in Gaza , this struggle will not end , and until all of them return , there will be no full rehabilitation for either them or the public . The psychological scars will persist . "
