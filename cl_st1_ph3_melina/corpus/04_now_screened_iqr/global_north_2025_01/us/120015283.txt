<p> There is a look I have come to recognise -- the way a child 's eyes widen when they see me , wearing a press vest and holding the microphone . It is not curiosity . It is hope . A fragile , desperate hope that maybe I carry answers I do not have . <p> " When will this end ? " a boy once asked me , tugging at my sleeve as I filmed near his shelter . He could not have been older than five , his feet bare and caked with dust . <p> His friends gathered around him , watching me as if I held some secret key to the future . " When can we go home ? " <p> I did not know what to say . I never do . Because , like them , I am displaced . Like them , I do not know when or if this war will ever end . But in their eyes , I am someone who might know .
