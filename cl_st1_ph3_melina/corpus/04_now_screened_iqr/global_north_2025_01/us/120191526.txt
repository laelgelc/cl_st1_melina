<h> Eliya Cohen was shot in the leg by invading terrorists on October 7 then dragged to Gaza . He is expected to be released in the first phase of the ceasefire deal . <p> Eliya Cohen was 26 when Hamas terrorists abducted him from the Nova Music Festival on October 7 2023 - now , over a year later , his family await his release as part of phase one of an incoming hostage-ceasefire deal . <p> Eliya was enjoying the festival with his partner Ziv , her nephew , and her nephew 's girlfriend when Hamas invaded .
