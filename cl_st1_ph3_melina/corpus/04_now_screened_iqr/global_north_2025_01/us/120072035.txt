Under the first stage , which will take place over the course of six weeks , Israeli forces are to withdraw from populated areas in Gaza and be stationed along the border , allowing for a hostage swap and the return of displaced Palestinians to their homes . Hamas will release 33 hostages , including all women , children and elderly people in exchange for the release of a number of Palestinians detained in Israeli prisons , Al Thani said .
