<p> Thousands of Palestinians gathered in the West Bank city of Ramallah on Saturday to celebrate the arrival of dozens of prisoners who were released in exchange for four Israeli hostages . <p> Supporters cheered and followed the buses carrying the prisoners down a winding hill to a recreational complex , where they were hoisted on people 's shoulders and paraded through the wild crowd . <p> Two hundred Palestinian prisoners were released on Saturday , including 121 people who were serving life sentences . Israel has said that those who were serving time for killing Israelis would not be going home . A Palestinian prisoner advocacy group said 70 released prisoners had been deported first to Egypt , and then onward to other countries willing to take them in . <p> Israel and Hamas agreed to a ceasefire last week , bringing the 15-month war to an end . Under the first part of the three-phase agreement , which is expected to last 42 days , 33 hostages are to be freed in exchange for Palestinian prisoners .
