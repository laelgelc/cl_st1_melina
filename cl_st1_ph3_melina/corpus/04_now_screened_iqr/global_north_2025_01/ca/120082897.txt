<h> Lasting peace will require new Israeli and Palestinian leadership <p> Jon Allen is a senior fellow at the Munk School of Global Affairs and Public Policy and Canada 's former ambassador to Israel and Spain . <p> The announcement Wednesday of a ceasefire -- which still needs to be approved by the Israeli cabinet -- comes after 15 months of death and destruction in Gaza , Israel and Lebanon . What began as a horrific massacre of Israelis in kibbutzim and at a music festival in southern Israel morphed into a two-front war between Israel and Hamas in Gaza and eventually between Israel and Hezbollah in Lebanon . It also provoked the first ever missile attacks by Iran on Israel and other missile attacks from Houthi rebels in Yemen . <p> The ceasefire , which had largely been negotiated months ago with the mediation of U.S. , Qatari and Egyptian officials , and should have ended the suffering of all then , was advanced by the incoming Trump administration 's Middle East envoy , Steve Witkoff . Mr. Witkoff broke Israeli protocol by demanding a meeting with Israeli Prime @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and made clear that Donald Trump was not Joe Biden and that " yes and then no " or " maybe " were not the right answers to his demands to finalize the deal . <p> If last-minute disputes can be resolved and the ceasefire agreement is implemented , it will bring great joy and relief to the families of the 33 Israeli hostages ( women , children , the elderly and some wounded ) who will be released , to the IDF soldiers and their families , to the families of the approximately 1,300 Palestinian prisoners who will be released , and to the mostly innocent 2.3 million Gazans who may soon be able to sleep without fear of bombings and starvation . <p> This was a war , like many others before it between Hamas and Israel , that did n't have to happen . Hamas must have known how Israel would respond to an attack of the nature it perpetrated on Oct. 7 . I doubt , however , that anyone would have predicted that the Israeli response would have lasted 15 months , killed approximately 46,000 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of Gaza . Nor did Hezbollah have any business joining Hamas ' fight or understand how much sophisticated intelligence Israel had in respect to its leadership and fighting force . After all , Hezbollah 's primary role was not to defend Hamas or the Palestinian people , but rather as a proxy to protect Iran from Israel with its 150,000 missiles and drones . Hezbollah , Iran and the Assad family of Syria all now regret Hezbollah leader Hassan Nasrallah 's strategic error in attacking Israel on Oct. 8 , 2023 , and forcing the evacuation of some 60,000 Israelis from their homes . <p> So , what next ? If the deal moves forward , it will be key that both sides -- Israel , on the one hand , and Hamas and the Palestinian Islamic Jihad , on the other -- strictly comply with their obligations under the ceasefire agreement . If not , the release of the remaining hostages and the ceasefire itself could be in jeopardy . <p> If the ceasefire becomes permanent , a realistic post-conflict plan to deliver massive amounts of humanitarian assistance , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and rebuild Gaza infrastructure , must kick in as soon as possible . None of this can fully be realized until the IDF leaves Gaza and a multinational force , likely led by Gulf states with significant contributions from Western militaries , is able to provide security and stability in Gaza . <p> Finally , following free and fair elections and significant support on governance from countries like Canada , the U.K. and others , a new Palestinian-led government will hopefully take control of and unite both Gaza and the West Bank , thereby beginning the process of creating a viable partner for peace with Israel going forward . <p> The above will take time and wo n't be able to be fully executed until both the Palestinian President , Mahmoud Abbas , and Mr. Netanyahu , pass from the scene and more moderate leaders who have a vision of two sovereign states occupying the land between " the river and the sea " gain power in Israel and Palestine .
