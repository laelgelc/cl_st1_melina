<p> The theme of the biblical exodus from Egypt recorded in Hebrew scripture reflects the belief in God 's intervention in history . <p> So significant is this fundamental , theological posture , Jews are reminded twice daily in their worship to remember that God " brought out His people Israel from their midst into everlasting freedom ... and from Egypt you redeemed us , Lord our God and from the slave-house you delivered us ... freeing captives and redeeming those in need , helping the poor and answering His people when they cry out to Him . " <p> Moreover , on Passover at the Seder , these sentiments are linked to four verbs of redemption accompanying the four cups of wine : " I will free you ... and deliver you ... I will redeem you ... and I will take you to be My people .
