<p> On Friday night 's Late Late Show , Irish-language rap group Kneecap took a moment to draw attention to the " genocide " in Gaza . <p> The trio from Belfast - Mo Chara , Moglai Bap and DJ Provai - chatted with host Patrick Kielty about their music , their origins , and their new film , which is the first-ever Irish language movie to premiere at the famous Sundance Film Festival . Patrick Kielty asked them how they got into rap music , joking : " When you grow up in West Belfast , the idea of being attracted to rap music , I mean , you know , when I was growing up , Belfast was less Run-DMC , more run from the RUC " . <p> This was a playful nod to the 1980s hip-hop group Run-DMC from New York City . As Mo Chara started to answer , DJ Provai - known for his tricolour balaclava - took off his jacket to reveal a jersey with an Arabic slogan and a Palestinian flag on the crest , showing his support for the Palestinian people . <p> Israel 's assault in the Gaza Strip @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has resulted in the death of nearly 30,000 Palestinians so far . Some horrific stuff is happening there . I 'm also obliged to say that in politics , there is another side and some people might not agree with what you 've done . But that 's why we 're here and we use our platform to highlight the genocide that 's happening in Palestine at the moment .
