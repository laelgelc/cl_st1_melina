<h> UN chief appoints group for independent external review of UNRWA <p> United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres announced an independent review of the agency 's office that focuses on the Palestinian territories following allegations that a handful of employees participated in terrorism . <p> Catherine Colonna , France 's former minister of foreign affairs , will lead the review , which Guterres announced on Monday . She will work with the Raoul Wallenberg Institute in Sweden , the Chr . Michelsen Institute in Norway , and the Danish Institute for Human Rights . <p> They will begin their review on Feb. 14 and will submit a preliminary report to Guterres , who said cooperation by Israeli authorities will be critical to their work , by late March . <p> The review group will " assess whether the Agency is doing everything within its power to ensure neutrality and to respond to allegations of serious breaches when they are made , " the secretary-general said in a statement . <p> This review will take place in parallel with an investigation by the U.N .
