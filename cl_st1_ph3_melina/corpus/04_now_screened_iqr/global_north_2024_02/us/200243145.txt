<h> Girl Scouts threaten to sue troop over Gaza fundraiser <p> A Girl Scouts troop in St Louis has been threatened with a lawsuit over a fundraiser they planned to raise money for children in Gaza . <p> Nawal Abuhamdeh , the troop leader and mother of a 10-year-old member , told the St Louis Post-Dispatch that many of the girls in the troop have been heartbroken by the death and destruction in Gaza . <p> Instead of selling Girl Scout cookies this year , they decided to do a community service project . The children came up with the idea to sell homemade bracelets to benefit the Palestine Children 's Relief Fund , Ms Abuhamdeh said . <p> More than 29,000 Palestinians have been killed -- 13,000 of them children -- in Israel 's brutal siege on Gaza since 7 October , when 1,200 people in Israel were killed in attacks by Hamas . <p> After spreading the word about the fundraiser on social media , the Girl Scouts of Eastern Missouri emailed Ms Abuhamdeh , saying the troop was required to " stay neutral " and could not engage in " partisan politics .
