<p> Calls for Coventry to twin with a city in Gaza as war rages in the Middle East have gone before the council . A petition asking for the council to formally link the city with Khan Younis was presented to councillors this week . <p> The appeal , signed by more than 400 people , referred to the " brutal bombardment " of Coventry in the Second World War and said the situation is being " mirrored " in the Gazan city . " We believe it is only fitting due to our exceptional history that we twin Coventry City with Khan Younis and extend the hope for both peace and reconciliation , " it added . <p> Labour councillor Shakila Nazir , who represents Foleshill , sponsored the petition , which was set up by a local resident .
