<p> More than 70 MPs have now signed a motion expressing no confidence in Commons Speaker Sir Lindsay Hoyle over the handling of the Gaza vote in Parliament . <p> Members from the Conservative Party and the Scottish National Party are supporting this " early day motion " started by senior Tory William Wragg in a move intended to pressure the Speaker to step down . <p> By Friday afternoon , 71 MPs had put their names to the motion , including Sir Graham Brady , who leads the 1922 Committee of Conservative MPs , and former Tory deputy chairmen Lee Anderson and Brendan Clarke-Smith . Stephen Flynn and Mhairi Black from the SNP at Westminster , also signed . <p> More than half of the SNP 's Westminster contingent have now signed the motion . It comes after Wednesday 's SNP Opposition Day vote descended into chaos after Sir Lindsay approved Labour 's bid to amend an SNP motion calling for an immediate ceasefire in the Israel-Hamas war .
