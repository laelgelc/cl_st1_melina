<p> The Rafah crossing between the Gaza Strip and Egypt is the sole route for aid to enter Gaza directly from outside Israel and the only exit that does not lead to Israeli territory . <p> It has become a focus in the intensifying conflict between Israel and the Palestinian group Hamas , as hundreds of thousands of Palestinians have headed towards south Gaza after Israel warned them to leave Gaza City and the enclave 's north . <h> Where is the Rafah crossing and who controls it ? <p> The crossing is at the south of the Gaza Strip , a narrow slither of land that is home to 2.3 million people and wedged between Israel , Egypt and the Mediterranean Sea . <p> That means the only likely route for humanitarian aid to enter Gaza is via Rafah from Egypt 's Sinai region .
