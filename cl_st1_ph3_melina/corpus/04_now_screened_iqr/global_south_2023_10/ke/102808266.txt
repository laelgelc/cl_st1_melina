<h> UN chief visits Rafah crossing as aid convoy waits to enter Gaza <p> RAFAH , Egypt , Oct. 21 ( Xinhua ) -- UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres on Friday visited the Egyptian side of the Rafah border crossing with Gaza , ahead of the anticipated opening of the crossing to bring needed aid into the besieged Palestinian enclave . <p> At the Rafah crossing , the only crossing point between Egypt and the Gaza Strip , Guterres said the large convoy loaded with food and medicine was " the difference between life and death for the ( Palestinian ) people . " <p> " We absolutely need to have these trucks moving as quickly as possible and as many as necessary , " the UN chief said , adding there needed to be trucks entering " every day into Gaza to provide enough support for the Gazan people .
