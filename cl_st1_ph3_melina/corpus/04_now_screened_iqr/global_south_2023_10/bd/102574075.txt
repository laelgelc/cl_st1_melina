Gaza 's infrastructure has been devastated by indiscriminate bombardments from air , land and sea , and those currently displaced have nowhere to go , " the expert said . <p> " Tripling the displaced population overnight will decimate and permanently alter the civilian population of Gaza , " she warned . <p> The Special Rapporteur said that humanitarian actors in Gaza were facing an overwhelming rise in needs against a backdrop of attacks on healthcare facilities and health workers , threats against humanitarian workers , and a draconian siege that has cut off access to water , fuel , medicine , electricity , communications , and essential humanitarian supplies . <p> " More than two-thirds of internally displaced persons are sheltering in UNRWA schools which are not fit for this purpose , and have themselves been bombarded . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have been injured since 7 October , leaving hospitals overwhelmed , " she said . <p> " Because there is no conceivable way for hospitals to evacuate the most serious cases , the WHO has called the evacuation order an effective ' death sentence ' for the sick , " added Gaviria Betancur added . <p> Recalling the earlier statement from a group of UN experts , she urged Israel to adhere to international law . <p> " I would like to remind Israel that observance of international law is compulsory , not optional , during any conflict , " the expert said . <p> " To that end , I call for the strict respect of international humanitarian law and its provisions , including unrestricted humanitarian access to those in need , the cessation of indiscriminate attacks against civilians , and an end to forced displacement of populations and blockade , " she said .
