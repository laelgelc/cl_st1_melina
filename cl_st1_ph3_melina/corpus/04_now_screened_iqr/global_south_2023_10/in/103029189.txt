This sheds light on why Israel and the US were caught off guard by the Hamas attack . Hamas concealed the planning of the operation through traditional counter-intelligence methods , the intelligence shared by Israel with US officials reveals . This involved holding in-person planning meetings and avoiding digital communications , which could be tracked by the Israelis , in favour of using hardwired phones in the tunnels . In the course of these two years of planning , they refrained from using computers or cell phones throughout the two-year planning period to avoid detection by the Israeli or US intelligence , the sources told a media house .
