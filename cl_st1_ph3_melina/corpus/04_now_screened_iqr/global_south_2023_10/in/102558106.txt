<h> As Gaza war rages , India does a balancing act between Israel and Palestine <p> A Palestinian girl reacts in the aftermath of an Israeli strike in the southern Gaza Strip Reuters <p> The balancing act has begun . India stands firmly behind Israel as Prime Minister Narendra Modi conveyed to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu . But New Delhi 's traditional stand on Palestine remains . The ministry of external affairs said that India supported the " sovereign , independent and viable " state of Palestine living side by side with Israel . <p> This is the first time India has made its position clear on Palestine since the Hamas attack on Israel on October 9 and the subsequent siege by Israel on Gaza . <p> " Our policy has been longstanding and consistent . India always advocated the resumption of direct negotiations towards establishing a sovereign , independent and viable state of Palestine , living within secure and recognised borders , side by side at peace with Israel . I think that position remains the same , " said Arindam Bagchi , spokesperson of the MEA , at the weekly press briefing on Thursday . <p> Interestingly @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for India , Bagchi said : " The designation of terrorist organization is a legal matter . We see it as a terrorist attack . " <p> Unlike in the situation between Ukraine and Russia , where India had appealed for de-escalation , so far no such statement of restraint has been made . Instead , India has crafted a statement , which is very much a middle ground . <p> When asked about the humanitarian situation in Gaza -- which is dire as the shelling continues killing 303 people and injuring 1,140 in the last 24 hours according to figures by the Gaza Information office -- Bagchi said : " There was a universal obligation to observe humanitarian law . There is also the responsibility to fight the menace of terrorism in all its forms and manifestations . " <p> In short , India has chosen to keep the balance in the middle -- and has chosen to take no sides .
