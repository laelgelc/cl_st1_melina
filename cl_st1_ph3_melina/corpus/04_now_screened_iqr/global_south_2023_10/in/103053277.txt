<h> Israel-Hamas war : Former Minister Liberman revealed that he had presented the secret document to PM Benjamin Netanyahu in December 2016 <p> Nearly seven years before the deadly October 7 attack on Israel , then-Defence Minister Avigdor Liberman drafted an 11-page document in 2016 , warning of an incursion by Palestinian outfit Hamas . Newly released media reports suggest that the government at the time did not take these warnings seriously . <p> The document foreshadowed key elements of the unprecedented assault and indicated that Israeli officials had been aware for several years of the potential for such a Hamas offensive , The Times of Israel reported citing excerpts published by Yedioth Ahronoth newspaper . <p> A day after the 50th anniversary of the start of the 1973 Yom Kippur war , when Israeli forces were caught off guard by Syrian and Egyptian forces , the Israeli military appeared once again to have been surprised by a sudden attack this month . Some 2,500 terrorists infiltrated Israel via land , sea , and air , resulting in the death of 1,400 people . Hamas and their allied factions also took over 200 hostages back to the Gaza Strip @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ top secret <p> The 2016 document , classified as top secret , explicitly outlined Hamas 's intentions to breach Israeli territory by deploying a well-trained force , such as the Nukhba ( commandos ) , with the objective of seizing an Israeli community on the Gaza border , potentially multiple communities , and taking hostages . During that period , Liberman stressed the broader implications of such an attack , stating , " Beyond the physical harm to the people , this will also lead to significant harm to the morale and feelings of the citizens of Israel . " <p> In an interview on Saturday night , Liberman revealed that he had presented the document to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu in December 2016 , underscoring the urgency to neutralise Hamas 's capabilities to prevent an attack akin to the October 7 assault . However , he noted that the matter was met with reluctance when raised at a cabinet meeting , with the document being summarily dismissed , even by security chiefs , while he was made to feel " arrogant " for bringing it to their attention . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ at the center of a political storm after he blamed the country 's security services for failure to anticipate the Hamas attack . Netanyahu was slammed by members of the war cabinet , including National Unity leader Benny Gantz , for a post on X , in which he was critical of security agencies . The Israeli PM later deleted that post and apologised .
