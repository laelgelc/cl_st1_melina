The region , however , did not evolve according to India 's preferences . <p> The division between secular republics and conservative monarchies was too simplistic to capture the regional complexities . Several developments in the Middle East since the late 1970s led to the rise of violent religious extremism and Islamic republicanism that today threaten both secular and conservative regimes . <p> Over the last few years , India has drawn closer to Arab moderates and Israel .
