<h> Repatriation of Filipinos from Gaza voluntary <p> THE Philippines has put Gaza under Alert Level 3 amid the prevailing conflict between the Hamas Group and Israel government , the Department of Foreign Affairs ( DFA ) said Thursday , October 12 , 2023 . <p> Alert Level 3 means repatriation of Filipinos in Gaza is now voluntary . <p> The DFA earlier said there were 137 Filipinos in Gaza . <p> DFA Undersecretary Eduardo de Vera earlier said 70 Filipinos in Gaza were already requesting for repatriations but the problem is that Gaza is under blockade . <p> " There is no way in or out ( of Gaza ) , " he said . <p> In an interview with reporters in the Senate , DFA Secretary Enrique Manalo said they are discussing plans on how to get Filipinos out of Gaza . <p> The DFA , on the other hand , placed Israel , which houses around 30,000 Filipinos , under Alert Level 2 , which means restricted deployment and non-essential movements . <p> It urged Filipinos in Israel to avoid places in
