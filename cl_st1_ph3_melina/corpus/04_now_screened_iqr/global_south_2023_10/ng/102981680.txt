<h> Before the Israeli-Hamas war escalates <p> AS diplomatic efforts to halt the ongoing Israeli-Hamas War falter , Israeli tank units staged an incursion into Gaza on Wednesday , while its jets pounded the besieged territory , and its artillery fended off barrages of rockets . Casualties are mounting ; the humanitarian cost is also rising , and the conflict looks likely to widen and draw in more belligerents . Increasingly , the capability of the international community to maintain global peace appears shaky . Statesmen should rise and halt the bloodbath . <p> Gaza , blockaded and bombarded , is going down in ruins . By Wednesday evening , the territory 's health ministry was reporting 6,500 persons killed , many of them civilians . Since October 7 when Hamas fighters crossed into Israel in coordinated military and terrorist attacks that killed 1,400 persons and about 250 others kidnapped , the Middle-East has been boiling . <p> Deployed in tanks and armoured vehicles , the Israeli Defence Force confirmed that troops carried out a ' targeted raid , ' in northern Gaza on Wednesday in " preparation for the next stages of combat .
