<p> Gaza : How to Survive a Warzone follows the lives of four youngsters in the Gaza Strip , and was broadcast last month on BBC Two . It focuses on 13-year-old Abdullah Al-Yazouri whose father was revealed to be the deputy minister of agriculture in the Hamas-run Gaza government .
