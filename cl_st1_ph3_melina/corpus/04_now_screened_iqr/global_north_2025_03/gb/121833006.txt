<h> Pro Palestine rally hold weekly without fail in Blackburn <p> Drivers may have spotted a group of protestors at a busy junction in Blackburn . We caught up with them to find out more about what continues to inspire them to keep turning out week in , week out through wind , rain and icy temperatures . <p> The pro-Palestine protestors have been meeting on the corner of Victoria Street and Barbara Castle Way every Friday since the summer . <p> They unfurl a large banner which is then removed after the protest finishes . <p> The barrage of bombing on Gaza has displaced almost the entire population and killed tens of thousands of civilians , many women and children . <p> In recent weeks , the demonstrators have been joined by activists from across Lancashire who have brought drums . <p> During the month of Ramadan the protestors said they will be meeting on Saturdays instead of their regular Friday nights . <p> Yasin Chopdat , who helps to organise the protests , said the small group have received great support from passers-by and drivers . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ area and there is no disruption to traffic . <p> Yasin said : " We have a few people who travel from Accrington , from Burnley , Clitheroe and Nelson . <p> " A group also came from Manchester .
