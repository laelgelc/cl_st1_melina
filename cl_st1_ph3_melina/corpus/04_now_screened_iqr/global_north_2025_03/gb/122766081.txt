Both had been vocal in support of the Palestinians . Lawyers for both men are disputing the legality of the Trump administration 's efforts to deport them . <p> Another student green card holder , Yunseo Chung , who had also attended protests at Columbia , sued the administration on Monday after immigration officials tried to arrest her .
