He must be proud . The Israeli prime minister can now go down in the Guinness Book of Records as the man who single-handedly displaced the most people within the smallest territory . <p> I , myself , am one of these 1.9 million . I was displaced twice : the first time at the beginning of the genocidal war and then again a year later . <p> Many Palestinian families were displaced repeatedly , some 10 times or more . <p> It was a clear strategy by Netanyahu to divide us . The north was cut off from the south . " Northerners " were forcibly expelled to the south . Then " southerners " and the other displaced were forced to move to the centre . <p> But this was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a large-scale campaign to wipe out housing across the Gaza Strip , especially in the north and south . According to Al Mezan Center for Human Rights , the Israeli army has not stopped demolishing homes in Rafah throughout the ceasefire . <p> Advertisement <p> According to the World Food Programme , as of January , more than 2 million people were fully dependent on food assistance , and hundreds of thousands were facing " catastrophic levels of food insecurity " . <p> Netanyahu has now ordered all humanitarian aid to be cut off again and is planning to forcibly expel Palestinians from the north to the south once again . <p> His aim is clear : to tear apart communities , to separate and weaken us , to turn us against each other through extreme deprivation . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and it will fail again . <p> In the face of a genocidal war , the people of Gaza showed immense solidarity with each other . Whoever had a home standing would open it to shelter the displaced , including their families , friends , neighbours and even strangers . Whoever had some food would also share . <p> When we were under siege in our neighbourhood , Sheikh Radwan , in December 2023 , we used to throw water bottles through the windows to our neighbour and his daughter to make sure they had something to drink . We also provided food to other people in need by throwing it over the wall separating our home from other homes . <p> During our second displacement , a friend of my father 's opened his home for us in the south , and we remained there for four months . <p> On January 15 when the ceasefire was announced , the people of Gaza won against Netanyahu and his strategy of " divide and rule " . Four days later , some of the displaced from Rafah were able to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " big return " . Hundreds of thousands of Palestinians headed back to the north . <p> Advertisement <p> For the majority of the displaced , " return " meant discovering homelessness . People walked long distances on foot just to find their houses damaged or destroyed . The word we use to describe wrecked homes in Gaza right now is " biscuit " -- a home smashed flat like a biscuit . <p> The homeless returnees had few options : to go to schools turned into shelters , to pitch a tent in open spaces or next to the rubble of their homes , or to try to repair any standing walls into a living space . <p> Families are suffering in the heavy rain , strong wind and cold . Many , while cleaning , repairing or searching in the rubble to find their belongings , have found the bodies of loved ones and dug them out to bury them . <p> But even in the harsh reality of homelessness , Palestinians still find solidarity . <p> People share what little they have of food , water and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ repair broken walls and roofs . Some with half-damaged houses offer shelters to those in need . Volunteers initiate campaigns for distributing food and clothes to schools , shelters and tent camps . <p> Some youth gather daily to cook in communal kitchens , ensuring no one is left hungry . People provide emotional support through WhatsApp groups and mental health meetings . At night , families gather to share stories and comfort each other to reduce the loneliness . <p> The men of our neighbourhood made a schedule to help each other in making shelters in damaged houses . They helped us put up tarps and secure them with poles to the ground and mend walls in our damaged home .
