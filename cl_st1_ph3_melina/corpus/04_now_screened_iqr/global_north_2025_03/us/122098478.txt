The decision was carried out , the statement said , " due to the school 's continued inaction in the face of persistent harassment of Jewish students . As the protests unfolded , Columbia came under heavy criticism from Republicans in Congress -- as well as some Democrats -- who said the university was failing to protect Jewish students from antisemitism .
