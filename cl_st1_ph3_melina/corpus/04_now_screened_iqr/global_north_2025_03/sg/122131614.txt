<h> Police question 6 people over memorial staged in NUS for Gaza war victims <h> March 08 , 2025 , 10:45 AM <p> Six individuals have been questioned by the police after a memorial event was held without a valid permit outside a building at the National University of Singapore ( NUS ) on Jan. 13 over the Israel-Hamas war . <p> In response to media queries , the Singapore Police Force ( SPF ) confirmed that a police report was lodged against the public assembly and that police investigations are ongoing . <h> Interviewed some of those involved <p> On Feb. 27 , the police visited the residences of the six people , aged 21 to 28 , to investigate their alleged involvement , the police said . <p> Three of the six were present in their residences when the police visited and preferred to have their statements recorded at their residences . <p> Another individual was at his residence but requested to be interviewed the next day . <p> He was subsequently interviewed at Clementi Police Division on Feb. 28 . <p> The remaining two were not available @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to inform them to attend an in-person interview at Clementi Police Division on Feb. 28 to assist in investigations . <p> It is understood that both individuals attended the interview on Feb. 28 . <p> Communication devices believed to contain evidence related to the public assembly and clothing believed to be used in the incident were seized for investigation . <p> " The police take no issue with such calls . People are free to make such ( or other ) calls , as long as it is not against the law . But if persons want to have a public assembly , then they have to get a police permit , " the police said . <p> However , no police permit was obtained for the event and the police investigation into potential offences are ongoing under Section 16 of the Public Order Act 2009 . <p> The police statement came in the wake of posts on Feb. 27 by four Instagram accounts - sgacadboycott , sgpforpalestine48 , camira.asrori and loveaidsg - which jointly alleged that " police officers showed up en masse and unannounced at the homes of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " . <p> " This reeks of an attempt to intimidate students into silence , and we condemn the police 's disproportionate response to students ' act of grief , " the posts added . <h> No police permit <p> Students had shared on social media that they held a staged memorial in support for the Palestinian cause and called for universities in Singapore to reconsider their ties with Israeli institutions . <p> According to the social media post , 124 students and alumni in Singapore staged a memorial to " mourn and honour the countless Palestinian student martyrs who have been unjustly murdered by Israel " . <p> They placed 124 pairs of shoes and a white burial shroud in front of a NUS building that allegedly houses a research alliance between Singapore and Israel .
