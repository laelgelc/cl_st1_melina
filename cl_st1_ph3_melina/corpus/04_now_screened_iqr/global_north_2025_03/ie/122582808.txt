<h> It is claimed that over 1,000 died in fresh bomb attacks and nearly half are kids <p> An Irish medic in Gaza has revealed the horrors of surviving Israeli bombing as he saves the lives of dozens of children under constant bombardment . Dr Morgan McMonagle , whose job is to choose which young bomb victims ' lives can be saved by surgery , operates with bombs landing nearby . A fragile truce began in January but Israeli bombs this week have caused 1,000 deaths in Gaza , according to its health ministry . <p> Trauma surgeon Morgan said : " It 's been very difficult this week . I have been here five weeks now and no-one expected the ceasefire to fall apart this soon . It came as a shock . We were woken around 2am on Tuesday by very loud explosions . They were very close . I was due to leave that morning via Jordan . We went to the emergency department to respond and went into mass casualty mode to receive the injured and dead .
