<h> Donald Trump signs order withdrawing US from several UN organizations including UNHRC <p> President Trump has issued an executive order to withdraw U.S. participation from several UN agencies , including the UN Human Rights Council and the UN relief agency for Palestinians . This move follows concerns about anti-American bias and aims to reassess U.S. financial contributions to the UN . <p> US President Donald Trump on Tuesday signed an executive order to withdraw US from several UN organisations , including the Human Rights Council ( UNHRC ) , while initiating a comprehensive assessment of US financial contributions to the multinational organisation.The White House also withdrew from the primary UN relief organisation for Palestinians ( UNRWA ) , alongside a planned evaluation of involvement with the United Nations Educational , Scientific and Cultural Organization ( UNESCO ) .
