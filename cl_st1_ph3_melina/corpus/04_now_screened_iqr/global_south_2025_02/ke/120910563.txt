<h> Gaza : five things to know about the war-torn Palestinian territory <p> The Gaza Strip , devastated by 15 months of war between Hamas and Israel , is a Palestinian territory under Israeli blockade since 2007 . <p> A truce that took effect on January 19 is fragile and gives no guarantee of a lasting peace . <p> And if there is uncertainty over the latter stages of the ceasefire , due to be hashed out in negotiations , there is even less clarity over the territory 's future governance . <p> Israel has said there can be no post-war role for Hamas , the Palestinian militant group that has ruled Gaza since 2007 , and sparked the war with its unprecedented October 7 , 2023 attack on Israel . <p> But while Hamas has been greatly degraded in the fighting , it remains the dominant force in Gaza despite Israeli vows to eradicate it . <p> Here is what you need to know about Gaza : <p> - Small patch by the Mediterranean - <p> A small strip of land of 365 square kilometres ( 140 square miles @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> It has a border with Israel to the east and north and Egypt to the south with the Mediterranean Sea on its west coast . <p> After the 1948-1949 Arab-Israeli war that led to the creation of Israel , Gaza was placed under Egyptian administration . <p> Most of Gaza 's residents are descendants of Palestinians who fled from their homes during the Arab-Israeli war , according to the UN 's agency for Palestinian refugees , UNRWA . <p> Israel occupied Gaza during the 1967 Six-Day War and it was only fully returned to Palestinians in 2005 , when Israel withdrew soldiers and settlers . <p> - Young , urban population - <p> Gaza has a young population , with 47 per cent under 18 and only 4.8 per cent over 60 , according to the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics . <p> The population grows by 2.7 percent per year , due to a strong birth rate ( 3.4 children per woman ) . <p> Before the war , the population was three-quarters urban , with Gaza City home to 778,000 inhabitants , according to the CIA . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Palestinians were displaced at least once during the war , and many who returned home after the ceasefire have had to camp near the ruins of their destroyed homes . <p> - Blockade - <p> In 2007 , Israel imposed an air , land and sea blockade after Hamas -- which Israel , the United States and the European Union classify as a terrorist group -- took control of Gaza . <p> Gaza 's only entrance point not controlled by Israel , the Rafah crossing on the border with Egypt , was closed after Israel seized it in May 2023 . It reopened , with Israeli coordination , on February 1 as part of the ongoing ceasefire deal . <p> The United Nations has said the humanitarian situation in Gaza is " catastrophic " . <p> Just over four-fifths of Gaza 's population lived in poverty before the war , according to UNRWA . <p> Almost half of the population was unemployed ( 46.6 per cent ) , with a higher proportion of Gaza 's young people -- 62.3 per cent of 15-29-year-olds -- unemployed . <p> - Fifth war @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Palestinian militants in Gaza and Israel since 2008 . <p> The wars in 2008 , 2012 , 2014 and 2021 were marked by devastating Israeli air strikes on Gaza and barrages of rockets raining down on Israel from the other direction .
