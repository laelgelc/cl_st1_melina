<h> Shared humanity <p> Hassim provided an overview of the risks of genocidal acts and the perpetual vulnerability to acts of genocide committed by Israel . <p> " This is a case that underscores the very essence of our shared humanity as expressed in the preamble to the Genocide Convention ... <p> " South Africa contends that Israel has transgressed Article 2 of the Convention by committing actions that fall within the definition of genocide . The actions show systematic patterns of conduct from which genocide can be inferred . " <h> Genocide <p> Hassim said the " first genocidal act is the mass killing of Palestinians in Gaza " while showing photos of mass graves where bodies were buried , " often unidentified " . <p> " Israel deployed 6,000 bombs per week . At least 200 times , it has deployed 2,000-pound bombs in southern Gaza which it designated safe . No one is spared . Not even newborns . UN chiefs have described it as a graveyard for children . " <p> Hassim then argued " Israel 's second genocidal act " . <p> " Israel 's infliction of serious bodily or mental harm to Palestinians @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Israel 's attacks have left close to 60,000 Palestinians wounded and maimed again , the majority of them women and children . This , in circumstances where the healthcare system has all but collapsed . <p> " Large numbers of Palestinian civilians , including children , are arrested , blindfolded , and forced to undress , loaded onto trucks and taken to unknown locations . The suffering of the Palestinian people , physical and mental , is undeniable , " Hassim said . <h> Restrictions <p> Presenting a map of the Gaza strip showing the restrictions imposed by Israel , Hassim said , the ICJ must hold Israel accountable for acts in Gaza . <p> " There is no indication that Israel accepts responsibility for rebuilding what it has destroyed . Instead , the destruction is celebrated by the Israeli army . Soldiers film themselves joyfully detonating entire apartment blocks and town squares , erecting the Israeli flag over the wreckage . " <h> Order <p> Hassim said the court does n't need to come to a final view on genocide , but must find there has been breaches to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stop the suffering except an order from this court , " Hassim concluded . <p> Minister of Justice and Correctional Services Ronald Lamola is leading South Africa 's delegation to The Hague . <p> In his opening address , Lamola said South Africa unequivocally condemned the taking of hostages by Hamas . <p> Lamola also said no armed attack on a state 's territory " even an attack involving atrocity crimes " can justify the violation of the Genocide Convention .
