And every day those requests for coordinated movement were denied , " he told reporters in New York . <p> Only around 16 out of 36 hospitals in Gaza are " minimally or partially functioning " after more than three months of conflict . <p> Hospitals are crammed with thousands of patients and people escaping fighting which has displaced nearly 85 per cent of the population , 1.9 million people .
