In contrast , the mostly young , educated Russians who have emigrated since the invasion of Ukraine in 2022 tend to identify as left-wing or centrist , according to polling on Russian wartime migration by Outrush , a research project on Russian emigration . The polling found that 75 per cent of the post-2022 immigrants viewed themselves as political and civil activists . <p> Andrey X is an independent Russian Israeli journalist and activist working in the occupied West Bank .
