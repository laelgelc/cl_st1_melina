<p> On one of Gaza 's heavy mornings , journalist Yahya Sobeih ( 33 ) took the hand of his four-year-old son Baraa and walked him to his kindergarten in the Shuja'iyya neighbourhood , east of the city . Though exhausted , he smiled at his child and whispered : " Take care of your mother and your siblings while I 'm away .
