<p> I met Alan and Hazel Kerr in 2006 and became inspired by their selflessness and dedication . I wanted to learn more about them and shine a light on their achievements . <p> I 've been trying to highlight social issues through documentary film making for 25 years . I have always struggled to obtain funding and this project was no different . We provided most of the funding but it would n't have been possible to complete it without the generosity of a small number of donors . <p> Our initial intention was to follow Dr Alan in his work in the West Bank and Gaza but we also developed a very special relationship with Hazel . <p> While Dr Alan was operating , Hazel took herself all over the West Bank and Gaza , volunteering to help in refugee camps , schools and community centres . We tagged along and realised that Dr Alan and his work was the heart of the film but Hazel was the soul . Hence , the title became The Doctor 's Wife . <p> I was due to return to Palestine @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was struck down by a rare auto immune condition which left me paralysed . It was n't until 2012 that I was able to return to Palestine . <p> Wheelchair made things hard However , being in a wheelchair made everything near on impossible , not to mention my mental state which was not conducive to being creative . In 2013 , tragedy struck again when my 22-year-old son died , and I shut down for a year . <p> Again , the project seemed so far away , destined for the shelf . Which is where it sat for the next few years while I tried to figure out how to live in a wheelchair and support myself and my daughter . <p> The project was re-energised when I made two arts documentaries in Palestine , making sure we filmed Alan while we were there and connecting with a NZ trauma nurse who was also filming . <p> By 2022 , we knew we needed to complete the doco . We started sorting through many years of footage in different formats , getting the interviews transcribed and edited . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ funds and got a few people to help with the logistics . <p> I spent six months with three editors and then we used the rough cut to do one last fundraiser that helped us over the line , finally finishing it in March of 2025 . <p> Our documentary shows the humanity of everyday Palestinians , pre-2022 , as told through the eyes of a retired NZ heart surgeon , his wife and two committed female film makers who were told in 2006 that no one cares about old people , sick Palestinian children or Palestine . <p> They were wrong . We cared and maybe you do , too . <p> What is happening in 2025 means it 's even more important now for people to see the ordinary people of Palestine <p> Dr Alan and his wife , Hazel are now 90 and 85 years old respectively . They are the most wonderfully humble humans . Their work over 20 years is nothing short of inspiring . <p> This article is republished under a community partnership agreement with RNZ.
