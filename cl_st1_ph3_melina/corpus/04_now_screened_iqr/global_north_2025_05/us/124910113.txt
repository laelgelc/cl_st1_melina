<h> Where is Newsom as refineries teeter ? <p> The Bay Area News Group recently ran an article that discussed the possible closing of Valero 's Benicia refinery . In fact , three California refineries are now slated to close by the end of 2026 : Phillips ' Wilmington refinery , Valero 's Wilmington refinery and Valero 's Benicia refinery . This is a loss of 369,000 barrels per day of crude oil production , or nearly 23% of California 's current refinery capacity . <p> Fundamental economics tells us that the result will be a dramatic increase in gasoline prices . In a recent paper , professor Michael Miche of USC predicted that the impact could be gasoline prices of over $8 a gallon . This was before Valero confirmed the closing of its Benicia refinery by the end of 2026 . <p> The economic impact will be devastating , and Gov . Newsom has been AWOL . <p> R.P.
