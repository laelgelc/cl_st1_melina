<p> For Palestinians living in the West Bank , stepping onto the roads of the territory occupied by Israel is a daily journey of resilience . <p> With movement shackled by a complex web of Israeli checkpoints and barriers , homegrown mapping solutions have become their lifelines . These digital networks help Palestinians reach work , schools , and hospitals as blockades have continued to increase since the latest Israel-Gaza war started in October 2023 . <p> Apps such as Doroob Navigator and Azmeh , which crowdsource traffic data and information about road closures , guide them through the numerous obstacles they face in their commutes . The situation has worsened dramatically in recent months . Simple trips that once took 45 minutes now stretch beyond three hours , with some routes completely sealed , commuters say . <p> Before the war , the UN counted about 565 obstacles across the West Bank .
