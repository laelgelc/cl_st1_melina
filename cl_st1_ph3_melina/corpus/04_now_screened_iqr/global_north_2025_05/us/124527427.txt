<p> Being pro-Palestinian is not the same as being antisemitic and anti-Zionism is not the same as antisemitism either . These are only labels that have come to define contemporary discourse about Israel , Gaza and the West Bank . The conflation of accusative labelling led to the forced resignation of a tenured associate professor at Muhlenberg College last year . According to The Morning Call ( " Report says Muhlenberg firing was in violation , " April 30 , 2025 ) , Professor Maura Finkelstein appears to have been coerced to resign from her tenured appointment under what can only be described as duress . Shame on Muhlenberg College . <p> Professor Finkelstein is critical of Israeli settlement policies in the West Bank and critical of the conduct of the war in Gaza . She appears to subscribe to an academic theory called settler-colonialism . For example , the ideas that states ' rights mainly caused the American Civil War , Hitler did not know about the Holocaust , structural racism has held back Black Americans in our society , the strategic bombing of German cities by our air force in World War II was justified , the Trail of Tears was a genocide and modern Israel is a settler-colonial state . <p> These are theories of history and they are argued in academia using bodies of evidence and fact . Uncomfortable as many theories of history may be , valid or not , they have a legitimate place in our universities . Because , during the Cold War , American universities collectively understood that it was important to know something about ideology as a driving force in the Soviet Union . <p> During the Vietnam War , I understood the nuances of communism , when many Americans did not . I understood that Marxism , Leninism , Trotskyism , Maoism , and Castroism were different ideologically from the nationalist-based communist ideology of Ho Chi Minh . My professors at Colgate University , who were much like Finkelstein , believed that Americans needed to understand what was on the other side of the Iron Curtain . I was not being indoctrinated ; I was being educated . <p> What about Professor Finkelstein 's take on history ? Zionism was a late 19th century ideology based on returning the Jewish diaspora to the Promised Land of Abraham , which was then part of the Ottoman Empire . Zionism achieved its aim with the establishment of Israel in 1948 . Is Zionism a form of settler colonialism and is Israel a settler-colonialist state ? Unfortunately , for many on the right wing of American politics , anti-Zionism has come to mean antisemitism . Likewise , for many Americans , being pro-Palestinian has been conflated to mean pro-Hamas . <p> These conflations are patently non sequiturs . These terms are not interchangeable and to use them interchangeably is dangerously inflammatory and harmful to those thusly labelled . Do we really believe that Finklestein 's positions make her a pro-Palestinian antisemite ? Further , do her positions in the public sphere , somehow make her pro-Hamas and dangerous to Americans ?
