<p> ( NEW YORK ) -- A federal judge in Vermont has ordered that a Tufts University doctoral student be released on bail from ICE custody after her visa was revoked by the Trump administration . <p> Rumeysa Ozturk testified remotely at her bail hearing Friday from the detention facility in Louisiana where she has been held since ICE agents detained her near her home in Massachusetts on March 25 . <p> Her lawyers argued that the Turkish national and former Fulbright scholar is being targeted by the Trump administration because of a column she co-wrote in her student newspaper criticizing the university 's response to resolutions approved by the Tufts Community Union Senate . <p> Those resolutions called on the university to " acknowledge the Palestinian genocide , apologize for University President Sunil Kumar 's statements , disclose its investments and divest from companies with direct or indirect ties to Israel , " she wrote in the op-ed . <p> The judge ordered her released on bail while the government 's case against her proceeds . <p> In sworn declarations and court hearings , Ozturk and her lawyers stressed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ she has had at least 12 asthma attacks since she was detained .
