The bodies of two others were buried beneath the rubble . <p> A few miles away , 11-year-old Yaqeen Hammad , known as Gaza 's youngest social media influencer , was killed after a series of heavy Israeli airstrikes hit the house where she lived with her family . She was watering flowers in a tiny patch of greenery eked out of a displacement camp when she died . Her cousin , 16-year-old Eyad , was gravely wounded . <p> Even by the terrible standards of the Gaza conflict , the deaths had the power to shock .
