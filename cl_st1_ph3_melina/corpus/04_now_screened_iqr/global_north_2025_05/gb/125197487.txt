" <p> The letter accused Israel of committing " genocide " in Gaza and " war crimes , crimes against humanity and serious violations of international humanitarian law " more widely . <p> In support of these claims , it cited statements from Israeli ministers , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ IDF would " conquer , cleanse and stay in Gaza -- until Hamas is destroyed .
