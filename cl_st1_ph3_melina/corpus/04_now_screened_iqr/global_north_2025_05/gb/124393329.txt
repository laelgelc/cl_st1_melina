<h> Pope Francis 's popemobile transformed into mobile clinic for Gaza children <p> One of Pope Francis ' popemobiles is to be transformed into a mobile clinic for children in Gaza . <p> Not long before his death , Pope Francis donated one of his popemobiles to be converted into a mobile clinic for children in the war-torn region . <p> The popemobile in question was reportedly the one Pope Francis used when visiting Bethlehem in May 2014 , during his historic visit to the Holy Land . The vehicle has since remained on display in a public square in Bethlehem . <p> " The popemobile has been refurbished and upgraded to fulfil a new and hopeful mission : to provide medical assistance to injured and malnourished children who currently have no access to any type of health care , " Peter Brune , secretary-general of Caritas Sweden explained . <p> The former popemobile , which has been named " Vehicle of Hope " , will contain basic medical equipment including rapid diagnostic kits , vaccines , syringes , oxygen , suture materials , medication and other vital supplies . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ asked Caritas Jerusalem to enact the initiative in response to the humanitarian crisis in Gaza , where hundreds of thousands of children are living without access for food , clean water or medical care . The vehicle will be operated by medics and drivers from Caritas . <p> About a million Palestinian children have been displaced as a result of fighting between Hamas and Israeli forces . <p> Pope Francis 's concern for the plight of the Palestinians antagonised the Israeli government during his papacy . <p> In November , the Pope said the international community should examine if the Israeli military actions in Gaza amounted to genocide . He described the humanitarian situation inside the enclave as " shameful " . <p> Francis had a particular concern for Gaza and made regular phone calls to a priest who runs the only Catholic church in the enclave . <p> " The closeness he showed to the most vulnerable during his earthly mission is radiating even after his death , and this most recent surprise is no exception : his popemobile , the very vehicle from which he waved and was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is being transformed into a mobile health unit for the children of Gaza . " <p> The mobile clinic will be deployed in the Palestinian territory as soon as humanitarian access is restored , with the mission of " providing basic care in the most isolated areas and reminding the world that children 's rights and dignity must always be protected " , Brune explained . <p> " It is not just a medical tool but a symbol that the world has not forgotten the children of Gaza , " Brune added . <p> Photo : A man holds up a phone for Father Gabriel Romanelli , Parish Priest of the Roman Catholic Church of the Holy Family , to have a video conference call with Pope Francis as the latter blesses the congregation during Christmas Eve mass at the church in the Zaytoun neighbourhood of Gaza City on December 24 .
