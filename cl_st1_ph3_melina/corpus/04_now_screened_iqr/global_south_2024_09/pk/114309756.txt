<p> BEIRUT : An Israeli drone strike on a car crossing through a Syrian checkpoint near the border with Lebanon on Wednesday killed three Palestinian fighters and one member of Hezbollah , two security sources said . <p> The Israeli military said the strike killed Faris Qasim , who it said was a significant figure in the operations division of the Islamic Jihad , responsible for operational plans in Syria and Lebanon . <p> It said he had a central role in recruiting Palestinians to Hezbollah to attack Israel from Lebanon . Other Islamic Jihad fighters who were on their way from Syria to Lebanon were also killed in the strike , it said , without providing details . <p> The security sources said the car was not transporting weapons .
