<h> Jordanians to vote amid woes over economy and Gaza war <p> Jordanians will head to the polls on Tuesday to vote for a new parliament amid widespread frustrations over a lagging economy and the ongoing war in Gaza . <p> Just two days ahead of the vote , in a rare surge of violence , a Jordanian killed three Israeli guards at the border crossing between Jordan and the occupied West Bank . <p> The elections , which take place every four years , are the first to be held under a law passed in January 2022 that increased the overall number of seats in the house , reserved a higher number for women and lowered the minimum age for candidates .
