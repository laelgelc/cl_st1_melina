<h> Israel-Hamas war : Kenya supports proposal for immediate ceasefire <p> Prime Cabinet Secretary Musalia Mudavadi has amplified Kenya 's support for a mediated proposal announced to end the Israel-Hamas war . <p> Mudavadi said Kenya has noted the joint statement released by Egypt , the State of Qatar and the United States on the fighting between the State of Israel and Hamas . <p> The statement , he said , broadly echoes the principles outlined on 31st May 2024 by the United States President Joe Biden and was in line with efforts by key stakeholders who are involved in a protracted mediation process for a ceasefire . <p> " Kenya views the proposals contained in this statement as representing a critical way out of this complicated and intransigent conflict , " he said . <p> The proposal is based on the following roadmap : <p> That in the first phase of the proposal there would be a 6-week ceasefire ; withdrawal of Israeli forces from populated areas of Gaza ; release of a number of hostages and remains of Israel nationals in exchange for the release of hundreds of Palestinian prisoners ; the return of Palestinians to North @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ aid ; and the start of restoration of essential services . <p> That in the second phase there would be a permanent cessation of hostilities together with the return of all hostages and the withdrawal of Israeli forces from Gaza . The parties would continue to engage in negotiations to finalize the arrangements for this second phase . <p> That the third phase would see the return of any remains of Israel nationals still in Gaza and the start of an internationally-backed multi-year reconstruction program . <p> Implementation of the proposal will also help Israel families to re-unite with their kin who are currently held by Hamas as hostages and prisoners of war . <p> Mudavadi said Kenya believes this proposal should only be the initial step in creating the conditions for self-determination for the Palestinian people and advancing a long-term two-state solution . <p> In the short term , implementation of this proposal will bring immediate relief to the Palestinians who are currently facing a humanitarian catastrophe , hunger and widespread death . <p> " Kenya re-affirms and reiterates its position that the only and most realistic solution to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and embrace a two-state solution , " he said . <p> Mudavadi urged the two sides to accept the proposal in the interests of not only providing immediate relief but also creating the necessary conditions for long-term peace .
