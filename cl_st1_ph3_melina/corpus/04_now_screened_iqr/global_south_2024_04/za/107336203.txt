<p> The bill says : " In contrast to its stated stance of nonalignment , the South African Government has a history of siding with malign actors , including Hamas , a U.S. designated Foreign Terrorist Organization and a proxy of the Iranian regime , and continues to pursue closer ties with the People 's Republic of China ( ' PRC ' ) and the Russian Federation " . <p> The bill alleges that the African National Congress ( ANC ) has had ties with Hamas since 1994 , when the ANC party first came to power .
