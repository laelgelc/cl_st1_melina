" These words of Cardinal Pierbattista Pizzaballa , the Latin Patriarch of Jerusalem , clearly illustrate the present situation experienced in the Holy Land with the conflict between Hamas and Israel still ongoing after over 10 months . <p> Speaking to Vatican Media before the opening of the Rimini Meeting - which he inaugurated with a conference entitled : " A presence for Peace " - the Patriarch underlines the need to " work for a ceasefire and suspend the military operations to begin a healing process , to build trust with each other .
