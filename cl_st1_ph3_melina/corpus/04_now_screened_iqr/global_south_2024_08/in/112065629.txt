Israeli evacuation orders triggered yet another exodus of Palestinians from the heavily destroyed eastern districts of Khan Younis , where many had just returned less than two weeks ago -- after the Israeli military 's last incursion into the city in July . Thousands fled Thursday , carrying essentials like small gas cylinders , mattresses , tents , backpacks and blankets . Officials from Israel and the United States have said they believe Yahya Sinwar , Hamas ' newly named top leader and one of the architects of the October 7 attack on Israel , could be hiding in tunnels under Khan Younis .
