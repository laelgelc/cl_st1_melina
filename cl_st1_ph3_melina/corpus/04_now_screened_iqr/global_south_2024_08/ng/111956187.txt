<h> Who is Yahya Sinwar , Hamas new leader <p> On Tuesday the Hamas announced Yahya Sinwar as the political chief at-large to succeed the late Ismail Haniyeh who was assassinated in a suspected Israeli attack in Tehran last week . <p> Born in 1962 in Khan Younis , Sinwar is often portrayed as one of the most uncompromising top Hamas officials . He was arrested by Israel repeatedly in the early 1980s for his involvement in anti-occupation activism at the Islamic University in Gaza . <p> After his graduation , he helped establish a network of fighters to take up armed resistance against Israel . The group would later become the Qassam Brigades , the military wing of Hamas . <p> Sinwar joined Hamas as one of its leaders almost as soon as the group was founded by Sheikh Ahmed Yassin in 1987 . The following year , he was arrested by the Israeli forces and handed four life sentences -- the equivalent of 426 years in jail -- for alleged involvement in the capture and killing of two Israeli soldiers and four suspected Palestinian spies . <p> He spent 23 years in Israeli jail where he learned @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ politics . He was freed in 2011 as part of the prisoner exchange deal that saw the release of Israeli soldier Gilad Shait who had been captured by Hamas . <p> After his release , Sinwar quickly rose through Hamas 's ranks again . In 2012 , he was elected to the group 's political bureau and was tasked with coordinating with the Qassam Brigades . <p> In 2017 , Sinwar became Hamas 's chief in Gaza , succeeding Haniyeh , who was elected as the chair of the group 's political bureau . <p> Unlike Haniyeh , who had travelled regionally and delivered speeches throughout the continuing war on Gaza , until his assassination , Sinwar has been tight-lipped since October 7 . <p> But in a 2021 interview with Vice News , Sinwar said that while Palestinians do not seek war due to its high cost , they will not " wave the white flag " . <p> " For long periods , we tried peaceful and popular resistance . The next year , the United States labelled Sinwar as a " specially designated global terrorist " . <p> Asked about Hamas 's tactics , including firing indiscriminate rockets that could harm civilians , Sinwar said Palestinians are fighting with the means at their disposal . He accused Israel of deliberately killing Palestinian civilians en masse , despite having advanced , precise weaponry . <p> " Does the world expect us to be well-behaved victims while we are being killed , for us to be slaughtered without making a noise ?
