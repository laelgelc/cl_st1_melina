She wanted to see the Prime Minister answer a question from Green Party co-leader Chl ? e Swarbrick about a recent ruling from the International Court of Justice with regards to the occupation of Gaza . But security guards told her she could not enter the gallery while wearing the keffiyeh . <p> Zaiton told Newsroom it was the first she had heard of the policy .
