<p> Keir Starmer has told the Iranian President not to attack Israel during a crunch phone call as tensions escalate . <p> The PM warned Masoud Pezeshkian that there is a " serious risk of miscalculation " and said war was in no one 's interest . Iran is feared to be on the verge of launching an assault against Israel in retaliation for a bombing which killed a Hamas leader in Tehran last month . <p> He also told the Iranian leader that the targeting of dissidents on British soil must stop , and called on him not to continue aiding Russia 's invasion of Ukraine . World leaders have called for calm and warned that an attack in Israel could scupper hopes of a ceasefire in Gaza . <p> Following the call between Mr Starmer and Mr Pezeshkian a Downing Street spokesman said : " The Prime Minister said that he was deeply concerned by the situation in the region and called on all parties to de-escalate and avoid further regional confrontation . <p> " There was a serious risk of miscalculation and now was the time for calm and careful consideration , the Prime Minister said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , adding that war was not in anyone 's interests . " <p> The PM told the Iranian leader that the focus should be on " diplomatic negotiations " to reach a ceasefire agreement , secure the release of hostages and increase aid to Gaza . <p> The spokesman continued : " The Prime Minister also raised the cases of foreign detainees in Iran , adding that it was vital that they received the necessary medical care . The leaders agreed that a constructive dialogue between the UK and Iran was in both countries ' interests . The Prime Minister added that could only be furthered if Iran ceased its destabilising actions including threats against individuals in the UK and did not further aid Russia 's invasion of Ukraine . " <p> In a joint statement on Monday evening with the leaders of the US , France , Germany and Italy , Mr Starmer said there is " no time to lose " in a ceasefire and hostage deal . They said Iran must " stand down " any threats to attack Israel . <p> The five said : " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ addition , unfettered delivery and distribution of aid is needed . " In a further warming to Iran they said : " We expressed our support for the defense of Israel against Iranian aggression and against attacks by Iran-backed terrorist groups . <p> " We called on Iran to stand down its ongoing threats of a military attack against Israel and discussed the serious consequences for regional security should such an attack take place . " <p> Iran is widely expected to retaliate after the killing of Hamas political leader Ismail Haniyeh in Tehran on July 31 . At the weekend the Iranian foreign ministry accused Israel of " genocide and crimes against humanity " following an attack on a school that killed around 100 civilians . <p> Israel said it was targeting a Hamas command centre . The US has been beefing up its presence in the region amid speculation of strikes by Iran and Hezbollah .
