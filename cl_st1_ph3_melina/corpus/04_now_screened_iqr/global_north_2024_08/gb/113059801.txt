<h> Israeli forces rescue hostage from underground tunnel in Gaza <h> Israeli forces rescue hostage from underground tunnel in Gaza <p> Israel has rescued a hostage from an underground tunnel in Gaza , freeing one of the scores of people abducted during the October 7 attack by Hamas that ignited the war in Gaza . <p> The rescue brought a rare moment of joy to Israelis after 10 months of war but also served as a painful reminder that dozens of hostages are still in captivity as international mediators try to broker a ceasefire in which they would be released . <p> The military said Qaid Farhan Alkadi was rescued from a tunnel in a " complex operation " in the southern Gaza Strip but provided few other details . <p> Mr Alkadi was one of eight members of Israel 's Arab Bedouin minority who were abducted . He was working as a guard at a packing factory in Kibbutz Magen , one of several farming communities that came under attack .
