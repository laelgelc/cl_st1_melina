" But Netanyahu and his government rejected ( the July 2nd proposal ) , put new conditions , they assassinated the head of our movement , " referring to the assassination of Ismail Haniyeh , the head of Hamas ' political wing , in the Iranian capital of Tehran late last month . Haniyeh had been Hamas ' lead negotiator in the cease-fire talks . <p> " Therefore we wo n't participate " in the Aug. 15 talks , Abdul Hadi added , " and we will go back to square one .
