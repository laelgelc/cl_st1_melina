<p> Hamas has posted a video of three desperate hostages it is holding as they begged " please bring us home . " <p> The haunting clip features 26-year-old Noa Argamani , who was among around 240 people originally abducted 100 days ago on October 7 . Tai Tversky , 38 , and Yossi Sharabi , 53 , were also included in the recording . They have also been held hostage since Hamas first launched its bloodied incursion into Israel . <p> The video is the first time Ms Argamani , from Beersheba , southern Israel , has been seen since her chilling disappearance .
