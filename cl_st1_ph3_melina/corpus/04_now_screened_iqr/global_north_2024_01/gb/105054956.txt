<h> Thousands demand immediate ceasefire in Gaza at London pro-Palestinian march <h> Thousands demand immediate ceasefire in Gaza at London pro-Palestinian march <p> Thousands of pro-Palestine protesters took to the streets of London on Saturday , calling for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza . <p> Around 1,700 police officers patrolled the capital as the march made its way to Parliament Square , arresting a total of nine people . <p> Three people were arrested on suspicion of showing support for a proscribed organisation , which is an offence under the Terrorism Act , by distributing leaflets . <p> There were three arrests for inciting racial hatred -- one related to a placard and two for chanting -- while there were a further two arrests for racially aggravated public order offences . <p> A ninth arrest was made for possession of stickers to be used for criminal damage . <p> Thousands of people took part in the march ( Lucy North/PA ) <p> The protest , part of a global
