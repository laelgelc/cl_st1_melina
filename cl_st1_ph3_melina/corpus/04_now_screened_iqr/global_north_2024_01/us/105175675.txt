<p> At its first meeting under new leadership , the Minneapolis City Council on Monday dove headlong into one of the most polarizing topics of the day : the bloody Israel-Hamas war . <p> In their first meeting after being sworn in for two-year terms , council members voted to discuss a symbolic resolution on the conflict -- amid chants from a crowd of people critical of Israel 's continued bombardment of the Gaza Strip following Hamas ' Oct. 7 attack on Israel . <p> The council did not vote to take a formal position .
