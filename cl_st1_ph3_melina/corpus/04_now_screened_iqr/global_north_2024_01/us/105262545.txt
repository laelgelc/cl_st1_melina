<p> As the Gaza Strip enters its second week of a near-total phone and internet blackout , some people in the war-torn enclave are using a lesser-known technology -- eSIM cards -- as a last grasp at contact with the outside world . <p> Most newer smartphones let users load an electronic SIM card rather than a physical one , both of which grant users access to wireless networks . Some Palestinians have resorted to using donated phone plans registered in other countries to catch a roaming signal from Israeli or Egyptian cell towers to reach people outside the region . <p> " Some of the Israeli networks reach inside Gaza , " Suhail Nassar , a photographer in Gaza who uses Instagram to document the war , told NBC via WhatsApp . " We do n't have any local network available right now .
