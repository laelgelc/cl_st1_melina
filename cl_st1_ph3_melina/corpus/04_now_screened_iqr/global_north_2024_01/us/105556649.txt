Ilhan Omar splits with colleagues on Israel resolution <p> The Minnesota Democrat was among a handful of House members in opposition to a resolution of support for Israel in its fight with Hamas . She said it should have recognized innocent victims of Israel 's counterattack . <p> Rep. Ilhan Omar broke from her Minnesota colleagues and most other members of the U.S. House on Wednesday by voting against a resolution in support of Israel in the wake of attacks by Hamas . <p> Omar , the Democrat who represents Minneapolis and several of its suburbs , was one of just 10 House members to oppose the measure , and the only one from Minnesota .
