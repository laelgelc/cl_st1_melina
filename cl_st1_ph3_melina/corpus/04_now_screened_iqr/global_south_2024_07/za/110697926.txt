<h> Pandor says Israel is ' ignoring ' the ICJ order to stop killings in Gaza <p> Thursday , February 1 , 2024 <p> International Relations and Cooperation Minister , Dr Naledi Pandor , said she was of the view that Israel was flouting Friday 's International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) ruling to stop the killings in Gaza . <p> " I do believe the rulings of the court have been ignored by Israel . Hundreds of people have been killed in the past three or four days . And clearly , Israel believes it has the licence to do as it wishes . So , the world has to reflect because we have to come to a point to say what do we do to stop such acts occurring , not just with Israel but any party in the world , " Pandor said on Wednesday . <p> The Minister was briefing the media on a range of issues including the ongoing Israeli " offensive " on Gaza in the wake of the recent ICJ ruling . <p> The ICJ ordered Israel to , among others , take all measures within its power " to prevent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " and to immediately ensure that Palestinians have access to basic services and humanitarian assistance . <p> Pandor said she believes that the findings made it clear that it was " plausible " that genocide was taking place against the Palestinian people in Gaza . <p> While South Africa had called for the suspension of Israeli military operations in Gaza , the court did not grant this provisional measure . <p> However , Pandor said the ICJ 's decision marks a decisive victory for international law and a significant milestone in the search for justice for the Palestinian people . <p> " It also affirms the importance of global governance institutions , including organs of the United Nations . It remains vital for all Member States to respect and implement the decisions of the court . " <p> South Africa had approached the ICJ to garner not only a ceasefire of Israel 's military barrage of Palestine but also to ask the court to find the Israeli government guilty of committing acts of genocide . <p> " And I suppose what confronts us now is what do we do if @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ all nations must answer today because it is a body of the United Nations that has set out these provisional measures . It 's not the South African government ... And if its orders are not respected , what does this mean for every other government that commits atrocities against a people . " <p> She is of the view that this is the big question that faces the global community . <p> Citing Oxfam 's report , the Minister said figures indicate that the average daily killing of Palestinians by the Israeli military since 7 October 2023 surpasses the daily death toll of any major conflict in recent years . <p> " The deaths of children are almost incomparable both in terms of actual numbers killed and the rate at which children have been killed . More journalists have been killed in Gaza in the last 100 days than were killed during World War II and the Vietnam War . " <p> Pandor said South Africa will continue to do everything within its power to preserve the existence of the Palestinian people as a group , and to end @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " We will walk with them towards the realisation of their collective right to self-determination , for , as Nelson Mandela momentously declared , ' Our freedom is incomplete without the freedom of the Palestinians ' . " <p> " The UN peace and security architecture is clearly not able to give effect to the right to self-determination of the Palestinian people or to protect them from grave war crimes and the threat of genocide , necessitating concerned States to turn to the judicial institutions of the UN . " <p> Pandor also took the time to welcome the support expressed by several countries . <p> " We encourage States that are so inclined to approach the Court to intervene in the proceedings to send a strong message to the international community that the situation in the Gaza Strip is indefensible .
