<h> Time running out for Canadians seeking visas for family in Gaza <p> ST . JOHN 'S , N.L. -- Time is running out for Canadians hoping a federal visa program will provide an escape route for Palestinian family members trapped in Gaza , leaving some still grappling with the complex application process and worried their loved ones wo n't get out . <p> Damey Lee is part of a group of immigration lawyers who launched a Federal Court lawsuit representing 53 family members of Palestinian Canadians still stuck at the first stage of the application process . The suit filed earlier this month asks the court to order the Canadian immigration minister to process their initial paperwork and allow them to file their visa applications before the limited program ends in April . <p> " There has been no justification for the delay , " Lee said in a recent interview . " There is no admissibility stage , there is nothing that would take time . The program closes on April 22 , and a maximum of 5,000 visas will be handed out . <p> To apply , Palestinian Canadians first file a statutory declaration form identifying family members in Gaza who might be eligible for a visa . They submit the form , as well as identification information for their family , through a Department of Immigration , Refugees and Citizenship Canada website . <p> If the paperwork is in order , the department sends each of those family members a unique code they must use to begin their visa applications .
