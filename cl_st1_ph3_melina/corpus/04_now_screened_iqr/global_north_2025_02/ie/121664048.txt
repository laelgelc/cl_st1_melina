However , by Tuesday they were back in Tel Aviv after they were advised not to go out in public -- and their flights home were paid for by an Israeli insurance company . <p> According to The Times of Israel , the insurer , PassportCard , has allocated $1 million to cover the cost of repatriating Israelis to avoid possible prosecution for alleged crimes during the war in Gaza which followed the Hamas attack on Israel on October 7th , 2023 . <p> Similar incidents are reported to have happened in 12 countries .
