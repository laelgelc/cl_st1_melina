<p> Palestinians refer to their 1948 expulsion from what is now Israel as the Nakba , Arabic for catastrophe . <p> Some 700,000 Palestinians -- a majority of the prewar population -- fled or were driven from their homes before and during the 1948 Arab-Israeli war that followed Israel 's establishment . <p> After the war , Israel refused to allow them to return because it would have resulted in a Palestinian majority within its borders . <p> Instead , they became a seemingly permanent refugee community that now numbers some six million , with most living in urban refugee camps in Lebanon , Syria , Jordan and the Israeli-occupied West Bank . <p> In Gaza , the refugees and their descendants make up around three-quarters of the population . <p> Israel 's rejection of what Palestinians say is their right to return to their 1948 homes has been a core grievance in the conflict and was one of the thorniest issues in peace talks that last collapsed 15 years ago . <p> Now , many Palestinians fear a repeat of their painful history on an even more cataclysmic scale @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have been loading up cars and donkey carts or setting out on foot to visit their destroyed homes after a ceasefire in the Israel-Hamas war took hold on January 19 .
