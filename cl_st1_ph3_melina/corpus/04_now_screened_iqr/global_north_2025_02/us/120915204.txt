<h> KKL-JNF to Dedicate the Swords of Iron Forest in the Western Negev . <p> On Tuesday , February 11 , Keren Kayemeth LeIsrael - Jewish National Fund ( KKL-JNF ) will dedicate the Swords of Iron Forest together with bereaved families who have lost their loved ones during the war , since October 7 , 2023 . This forest will serve as a living tribute to fallen civilians and IDF personnel . The dedication will take place two days before Tu Bishvat , the Jewish " New Year " for Trees . <h> A Forest of Memory and Renewal <p> " We are planting the future , renewing the forest , and breathing fresh air into the region , " says Daniel Ben-David , KKL-JNF 's Western Negev Director . " In a place where life was cut short , we will plant new roots and create a forest of memory and renewal .
