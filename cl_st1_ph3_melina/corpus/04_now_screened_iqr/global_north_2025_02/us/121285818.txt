<p> Tricia Tuttle , the new director of the Berlinale , the Berlin International Film Festival , which opened for its 75th edition on Thursday , took part in a demonstration on the red carpet that showed solidarity with the hostages held by Hamas . <p> As a snowstorm blanketed Berlin and Germany reeled from a terrorist attack in Munich hours before , Tuttle joined a group of German actors holding up a photo of David Cunio , who was kidnapped from Kibbutz Nir Oz on October 7 , 2023 , and is still being held . Cunio has a special connection to the Berlinale , because in 2013 , he starred in Youth , a movie by Tom Shoval , which was shown at the festival . At this year 's festival , a new documentary by Shoval will be shown , Letter to David , about Cunio 's kidnapping and his work on Youth , will be shown . <p> Before the red-carpet moment , a vigil took place at Potsdamer Platz , just steps from the main festival venue .
