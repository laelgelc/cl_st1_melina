<p> Gaza City , Palestine -- Aya Hassouna is thin with a pale face . Her eyes are red , and her voice is full of sadness . <p> She had a husband , Abdullah , and two children , four-year-old Hamza , and two-year-old Raghad . But as she returned with hundreds of thousands of other Palestinians to northern Gaza after months of displacement in the south of the enclave , she was travelling alone . <p> Abdullah , Hamza and Raghad were killed in an Israeli attack on August 9 , as they prepared for a day trip to the beach , an attempt to escape the daily horrors of the war . <p> Aya described a strong explosion , smoke , and then her children lying dead on the ground with blood pouring from their heads . <p> Abdullah , who had earlier gone to buy ingredients for a cake and some snacks for the beach , was dead too . <p> " Since that time , I have been trying to be strong .
