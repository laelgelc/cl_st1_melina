<p> IDF Chief of Staff Lt.-Gen . Herzi Halevi met with four out of the five female observers who were released from captivity in Gaza and apologized for their time in captivity on Friday . <p> Halevi met with Agam Berger , Liri Albag , Naama Levy and Karina Ariev . Daniella Gilboa was the only one not present at the meeting . The observers told Halevi about what they went through in Nahal Oz and in captivity and the struggles they faced . <p> Halevi noted that the IDF will investigate everything that happened at the outpost on October 7 from where they were kidnapped and told them that it 's important to him that they participate in the investigation . <p> Shortly before meeting with the observers , Halevi met with the family of IDF Sergeant Gur Kehati , a Golani soldier who fell in battle three months ago in Lebanon in the same incident as Israeli researcher Ze'ev ' Jabo ' Hanoch Erlich was killed .
