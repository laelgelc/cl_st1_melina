<h> Editorial : Double standards on refugees : the Trump-Starmer conundrum <p> DOUBLE standards . The Prime Minister vows to close a " loophole " that allowed a Gaza family , whose home was destroyed by Israeli bombing , to claim asylum under a scheme designed for Ukrainian refugees . <p> There are deserving and undeserving refugees . Ukrainians are white , and fleeing from an army we do n't like , Russia 's . Palestinians are n't and they are fleeing from an army we advise , fund and equip . <p> Labour 's anti-immigrant braggadocio wo n't shorten NHS waiting lists or lower housing costs . Its zeal for action contrasts with its foot-dragging over employment rights and its indifference to rising energy and water bills . Keir Starmer only punches down : he cowers before the corporate crooks bleeding this country dry but talks tough when it comes to the powerless and penniless . <p> Most of all he cowers before Donald Trump . White people make up just 8 per cent of South Africa 's population but own 75 per cent of the land . <p> The US-South Africa feud has global significance .
