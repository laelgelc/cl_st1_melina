<p> The oldest Israeli abducted by Hamas , an 86-year-old who survived Jewish pogroms in the Second World War , was dead before he entered Gaza , according to the Israeli Defence Forces ( IDF ) . <p> Shlomo Mansour , 86 , was beaten , handcuffed and kidnapped by Hamas from his home in Kibbutz Kissufim on October 7 . He is one of the 33 hostages who were expected to be released in the first stage of the ceasefire deal . <p> It had been believed that Mansour was kidnapped alive and would be reunited with his family . But on Tuesday the IDF said Mansour had been killed on October 7 and his body taken to Gaza . <p> The decision to confirm his death was " based on intelligence gathered in recent months " , the IDF said in a statement . <p> In all , 76 people abducted from Israel remain in Gaza , four of whom were taken before October 7 . They include 36 dead and 40 hostages whose condition is unknown .
