<p> Australia star Usman Khawaja has been charged by the ICC after wearing a black armband during the first Test against Pakistan . <p> Khawaja has been keen to use his position to raise awareness of the humanitarian crisis unfolding in Gaza since Israel invaded to fight Hamas . Palestinian officials claim that 20,000 people have died in the conflict , which started when Hamas launched an assault on Israel on October 7 . <p> Before the opening Test against Pakistan in Perth on December 14 , Khawaja released a video on social media in which he spoke passionately about his beliefs . He had worn in Australia training which stated " all lives are equal " and " freedom is a human right " , before opting for a black armband during the match . <p> Khawaja has been backed by his captain Pat Cummins , but had not been given permission by the ICC to wear the black armband and has therefore fallen foul of the rules .
