<p> Pope Francis said in his Christmas message on Monday that children dying in wars , including in Gaza , are the " little Jesuses of today " and that Israeli strikes there were reaping an " appalling harvest " of innocent civilians . <p> In his Christmas Day " Urbi et Orbi " ( to the city and world ) address , Francis also called the Oct. 7 attack on Israel by Hamas militants " abominable " and again appealed for the release of around 100 hostages still being held in Gaza . <p> Speaking from the central balcony of St. Peter 's Basilica to thousands of people in the square below , he took another swipe at the armaments industry , saying it ultimately controlled the " puppet-strings of war " . <p> The 87-year-old Francis , celebrating the 11th Christmas of his pontificate , called for an end to conflicts , political , social or military , in places including Ukraine , Syria , Yemen , Lebanon , Armenia and Azerbaijan , and he defended the rights of migrants around the world . <p> " How many innocents are being slaughtered in our world !
