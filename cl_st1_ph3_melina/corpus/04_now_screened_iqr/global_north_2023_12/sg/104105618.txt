<h> Cao Desheng <p> BEIJING -- Foreign Minister Wang Yi and US Secretary of State Antony Blinken exchanged views on the Palestine-Israel conflict during a telephone conversation on Wednesday , and they agreed to maintain communication on the Middle East situation . <p> Wang , who is also a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee , said that the most urgent task is to achieve an immediate cease-fire in Gaza . <p> At the crossroads between war and peace , major countries need to uphold fairness and justice , maintain objectivity and rationality , and spare no effort to de-escalate the tension and prevent a larger-scale humanitarian disaster , he said . <p> Wang emphasized that any solution to the current Gaza crisis must adhere to the two-state solution and reflect the will of the Palestinian people for the future of Palestine . <p> China maintains that the core of the solution lies in respecting Palestine 's right to statehood and self-determination while pursuing a " Palestinian-owned , Palestinian-led " approach , with Palestinians governing Palestine , he said , adding that China is willing to work with all parties to achieve @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ visits to the Middle East as well as US views about the development of the Palestinian situation , saying the US is in favor of implementing the two-state solution . <p> The telephone conversation came following the summit meeting between President Xi Jinping and US President Joe Biden in San Francisco in mid-November .
