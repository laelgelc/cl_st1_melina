<p> The sooner President Joe Biden acknowledges that Americans will likely be drawn into a fight to protect shipping traffic through the Suez Canal , the more time the U.S. military has to plan , and the less severe the harm will be to the global economy . For months , ever since a deadly Hamas incursion into Israel triggered a massive Israeli military campaign in Gaza , the United States has sought to deter Israel 's enemies , most notably Iran and its proxy Hezbollah , from spreading the conflict to other fronts in the Middle East . <p> The administration 's fears are warranted but also moot . The war is already expanding in a way that endangers the global economy -- specifically , through attacks by Iranian-backed forces on the crucial shipping lane from the Indian Ocean through the Red Sea and the Suez Canal to the Mediterranean Sea .
