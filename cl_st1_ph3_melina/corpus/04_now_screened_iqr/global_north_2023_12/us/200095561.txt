<h> Palestinian children find some moments of joy at Gaza shelter school <p> Rawan , a 10-year-old Palestinian girl , said she is fearful living in Gaza amid the Israel-Hamas war . But she can find moments to be a kid again and cheer on others at her shelter school . <p> " We play to forget the pain we experienced , " Rawan , who lost a cousin in the war , told ABC News on Wednesday during a visit to a shelter school run by the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees in the Near East . <p> Volunteers at the school organize things like soccer games , and children can play with toys like hula hoops and balls . Volunteer Filastin Zoroub said the children are thrilled to just hold a toy . <p> " They forgot the joy , they forgot the smile , they forgot that they could play with each other , " Zoroub told ABC News .
