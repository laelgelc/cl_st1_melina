He said Hamas is " now mounting a deliberate defense " against the IDF , following the end of the temporary cease-fire . <p> Scher and Van Den Hoek used radar waves and satellite imagery from the European Space Agency 's Copernicus Sentinel-1 to analyze and map damage in the Gaza Strip . <p> Data collected from Nov. 29 through Dec. 4 showed that likely damage was mostly concentrated in Gaza City and northern Gaza .
