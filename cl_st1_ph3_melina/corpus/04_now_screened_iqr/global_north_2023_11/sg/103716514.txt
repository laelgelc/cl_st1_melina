PHOTO : BERITA HARIAN/THE STRAITS TIMES <p> November 23 , 2023 <p> SINGAPORE -- The Islamic Religious Council of Singapore ( Muis ) and its partner religious organisations have developed a guidebook with advice for the local Muslim community on how to understand and approach the Middle East conflict . <p> The forum was convened by local Muslim leadership to discuss how best to provide guidance for the different segments of the Singapore Muslim community on the Middle East conflict , Muis said in a statement on Nov 22 . <p> The guidebook , which is available only in Malay for now , contains religious advice for processing the conflict that rests on Islamic values . An English version is in the works . <p> The advice emphasises Islam 's rejection of all forms of oppression , no matter the perpetrator , said Muis , adding that Islam prioritises the pursuit of world peace . <p> At the same time , Islam acknowledges human emotions like sorrow and grief , and encourages Muslims to express these feelings @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ like the one unfolding in Gaza , the reaction of the Muslim community should be guided by religious principles and the " immutable values " of Islam , added Muis . <p> It cautioned that the war in the Middle East had led to the proliferation of apocalyptic religious texts and hadiths on social media . <p> Muslims should refer to the interpretations of " authoritative scholars " to understand the context of these texts , Muis said , so as to avoid confusion and panic that would prevent them from making contributions to the well-being and prosperity of their community . <p> The Singapore Muslim community is in a unique position and Muslims here should continue to preserve the atmosphere of peace , protecting it from any threat of division or strife caused by geopolitical conflicts , Muis said . <p> The advice also recognises and supports the contributions of the youth , calling on Muslims to avoid dismissing young people as naive or uninformed , even though they may seem idealistic about making positive change in the world . <p> Muis underlined the importance of providing a " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ family , friends , teachers or asatizah ( religious teachers ) . The support network should give them someone to rely on and the safety to share their feelings and concerns with others , Muis added . <p> Those supporting Muslim youth can help them renew their intentions , put their thoughts into perspective and allow young people to take a more balanced view on issues close to the community 's hearts . <p> The guidebook also offers tips on how to recognise signs of burnout in those engaged in advocacy work . <p> " It is our hope that this guidebook will be of benefit to the community in navigating the myriad and complex challenges that we face today , " said Muis . <p> The advice will be shared with the community at Muis ' weekly Friday sermon and disseminated on social media and other online platforms .
