<h> The Nation <p> A team of Thai negotiators returned from Iran and said that Hamas negotiators would release Thai hostages once its call for a 72-hour ceasefire is accepted by Israel . PHOTO : THE NATION <p> November 17 , 2023 <p> BANGKOK -- Muk Sulaiman , secretary to House speaker Wan Muhammad Noor Matha , held a press conference with team members at Parliament to offer a briefing on the outcome of the talks . <p> Muk said the negotiation team went to Iran for talks with Hamas representatives and returned to Thailand on Thursday . <p> The Thai negotiators were assured that the 25 Thai hostages were still safe although Israel continued to bombard the Gaza Strip with rockets . <p> " Hamas representatives assured the Thai negotiators that they would release the hostages when the situation was safe or when there was a ceasefire for 72 hours as called by the international community , " Muk said . <p> He said the ball was now in Israel 's court whether to accept the 72-hour ceasefire condition . <p> " I believe the chances of Israel agreeing to the ceasefire is high because all the countries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ believe the Thai hostages will be released soon , " Muk said . <p> Muk also called on Thai workers who have decided to stay on in Israel to change their mind and return to Thailand as soon as possible , saying the situation in that country was still volatile . <p> Lerpong Syed , president of the Thai-Iranian student alumni and one of negotiators , told the same press conference that the Thai negotiators had held direct talks with Hamas leaders . <p> He said Hamas could not release the hostages now for fear of safety , as Israel has continued to bombard the Gaza Strip . <p> Lerpong said that if Israel announced a 72-hour ceasefire , Hamas would release about 50 hostages . <p> However , Lerpong said he could not be certain if all the 25 Thai hostages would be among the first group of 50 hostages to be released . <p> Meanwhile , bodies of five Thai
