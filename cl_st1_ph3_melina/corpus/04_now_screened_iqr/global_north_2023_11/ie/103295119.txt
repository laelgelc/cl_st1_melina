Photo : Reuters <p> Editorial <p> Yesterday at 03:30 <p> A s the world recoils from the catastrophic death toll in Gaza , UN chiefs have declared : " Enough is enough . " <p> On average , a child is killed and two are injured every 10 minutes during the war , the UN relief agency for Palestinians said . <p> Meanwhile , the Hamas-run health ministry in Gaza claims Israeli attacks have now killed more than 10,000 Palestinians . The BBC recently provided sobering context to the extent of the carnage . It compared figures with those in Ukraine , where the UN says around 9,700 civilians have been killed since the full-scale Russian invasion 21 months ago . <p> Some of the Palestinian dead would have been part of Hamas . Even if that proportion is as high as 10pc -- which is said to be unlikely -- it would still mean that Israel has killed as many Palestinian civilians in just over a month as Russia has killed in Ukraine since February 2022 . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , denied access to the essentials for survival , bombed in their homes , shelters , hospitals and places of worship . This is unacceptable , " the UN chiefs said in a joint statement . " We need an immediate humanitarian ceasefire . " <p> It 's all the more regrettable , then , that the diplomatic whirlwind undertaken by US Secretary of State Antony Blinken did not yield better results . <p> Hamas 's culpability from the outset must be kept in mind . But Israel can not be absolved from its responsibility for the indefensible number of Palestinian civilian deaths . <p> Global criticism for the severity of the indiscriminate bombardment , and the fact that the air , land and sea attacks lack an endgame , is growing . Mr Blinken has said the efforts to secure humanitarian pauses are a work in progress . <p> Unfortunately the pace of that progress is costing lives . As the world 's greatest superpower , the US needs to use maximum influence to persuade its ally to allow a pause for sufficient aid to get through . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Arab world before it explodes is becoming more problematic as days pass , and deaths mount alarmingly . <p> The Biden administration has said it is deeply distressed , but against such utter devastation , more might be expected than expressions of discomfort . <p> " Life 's most persistent and urgent question is , ' What are you doing for others ? ' " said Martin Luther King . <p> It 's a pertinent question for those with influence and agency , and who might use it to ease the terrible suffering in the besieged territory . To date , Israel 's sole objective has been to wipe out Hamas . <p> But there is no guarantee the offensive will achieve this . Violent escalation and regional destabilisation of an already volatile zone has to be avoided . A solution will not be found solely through a hammer-and-anvil approach . Some respite is needed right now . <p> Looking ahead : it may be hard to visualise an end to the conflict , yet one must be envisaged . The purely eye-for-an-eye approach just perpetuates the darkness .
