<p> Thousands of pro-Palestine demonstrators took to the streets in Manchester for the fourth weekend in a row , before taking over Piccadilly Station for a ' sit-in ' with hundreds piling in . <p> ' Free Palestine ' protesters met at St Peter 's Square at 12pm before marching through the city centre . Surrounded by swirling green and red smoke , waving flags , and hundreds of signs , they wound their way through town . <p> Chants of ' free , free Palestine ' was among the slogans that rang out in Manchester today ( November 4 ) . Hours of speeches were followed by a move to Piccadilly Station , where organisers were ready to protest for even longer amid reports that the station had to be ' closed ' because of the chaos . <p> Banners were draped over balconies besides passengers sipping Saturday afternoon pints , before demonstrators dispersed more than five hours after the start of the protest .
