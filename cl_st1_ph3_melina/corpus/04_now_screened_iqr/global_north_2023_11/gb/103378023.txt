<h> ' To me it feels right ' : Dorset campaigners to join march in London today <h> Dorset campaigners to join pro-Palestinian protest in London <p> Campaigners from Dorset will be joining a controversial protest in London today . <p> More than half a million people could attend the pro-Palestinian protest calling for a ceasefire in Gaza - including 53 members of the Dorset Palestine Solidarity Campaign - in the capital today , Armistice Day . <p> The planned demonstration , which will be heavily policed , has come under fire as it coincides with Armistice Day .
