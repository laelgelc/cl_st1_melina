<h> Thousands join pro-Palestinian rally in Dublin city <h> Thousands join pro-Palestinian rally in Dublin city <p> Thousands have marched through the streets of Dublin in solidarity with the Palestinian people and to call for a ceasefire in Gaza . <p> The protest began at the Garden of Remembrance and marched across the River Liffey , with demonstrators chanting : " In our thousands in our millions , we are all Palestinians " ; " From the river to the sea , Palestine will be free " , and : " Ceasefire now " . <p> At the Department of Foreign Affairs building , Iveagh House , the protesters took part in a sit-in . <p> The demonstration was organised by the Ireland-Palestine Solidarity Campaign , and saw members of People Before Profit , the Labour Party , the Social Democrats , and Sinn Fein take part . <p> Protesters during a pro-Palestine rally in Dublin ( PA ) <p> The march comes as 24 Irish citizens and dependants left Gaza through the Rafah crossing on Friday night , while other families with Irish passports landed in Dublin after fleeing the enclave in the days before . <p> There @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ who had spent more than a month in the war-torn territory feared for their loved ones they had left behind . <p> In a statement to the PA news agency , the Department of Foreign Affairs confirmed that a total of 50 Irish citizens and dependants had left Gaza in recent days . <p> " Only small numbers of citizens or accompanying dependants who have expressed a wish to leave remain in Gaza , " it said . <p> " The department remains in contact in each case and will continue to work consistently with the relevant authorities to ensure they are able to leave Gaza as soon as possible .
