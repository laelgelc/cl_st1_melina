<p> One-and-a-half-year-old Palestinian boy , Fati Ala , who suffered burn injuries during the Gaza war , now peacefully sleeps snuggled next to a teddy bear doll at a private hospital in Abu Dhabi . No longer waking up to the deafening sounds of Israeli bombardment , Fati Ala is on the path to recovery , and a semblance of normalcy is gradually returning to his life . <p> The toddler , his mother , and a few Palestinians , caught in the crossfire of ongoing war , were part of the first evacuation flight to Abu Dhabi from Egypt 's El Arish .
