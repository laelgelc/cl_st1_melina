<p> Alserkal Art Week opened in Dubai on November 20 with a moving exhibition of artworks and archival photographs featuring Palestine , particularly Gaza , and titled On This Land , after a Mahmoud Darwish poem . <p> On This Land had originally been planned by the Birzeit-based Palestinian Museum to showcase the cultural legacy of Gaza , but was put on hold given the recent crisis . Instead , it resulted in a collaborative effort between three cultural institutions : Alserkal Art Foundation , Barjeel Art Foundation and Palestinian Museum . It was put together in record time , just two weeks , as a response to the devastating humanitarian crisis unfolding in Gaza . <p> There are more than 100 artworks on display , including paintings , drawings , sculpture , embroidery and film . The artworks have been provided by the Barjeel Art Foundation founded by one of the most well-respected art collectors and educators in the country , Sultan Sooud Al Qassemi . These include works by Palestinian artists such as Sliman Mansour , Laila Shawa , Ismail Shammout and Jawad Al Malhi . It features the martyrdom of 16-year-old Lina Al Nabulsi who was shot by an Israeli soldier on her way home from school , a few days after participating in a protest with other students . <p> Another poignant painting is Layan Shawabkeh 's ' Ladies of Gaza ' ( 2009 ) . Born in 1986 in Jerusalem , Shawabkeh died at the age of 23 in December 2009 , following a battle with cancer .
