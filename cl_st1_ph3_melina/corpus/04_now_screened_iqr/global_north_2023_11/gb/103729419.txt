<p> The first Israeli hostages to be released from Gaza happened on Friday afternoon Tel Aviv and Hamas began a four-day truce after coming to a landmark agreement . The deal , brokered by the state of Qatar , came into force today at 5am GMT - a day later than originally scheduled . Under the agreement , 13 Israeli hostages have now been released by the militant group and transferred to Egypt by the Red Cross . <p> In exchange , 39 Palestinian prisoners were freed this evening in exchange for the Israeli hostages . It is the first prolonged break in fighting since the devastating war began on October 7 . A further 12 Thai nationals taken hostage by Hamas have also been released in a deal mediated by Egypt .
