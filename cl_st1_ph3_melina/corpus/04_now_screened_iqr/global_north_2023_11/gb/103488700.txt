<p> President Joe Biden speaks during a news conference after his meeting with China 's President President Xi Jinping ( Doug Mills/The New York Times/AP ) <p> US President Joe Biden has said he believes Israel 's war against Hamas in Gaza will only stop once the militant group 's ability to kill and injure Israelis is degraded . <p> He said he was urging Israel to exercise caution as it carried out military operations in Gaza 's largest hospital . <p> Mr Biden was speaking at a news conference on Wednesday after meeting China 's leader Xi Jinping on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperative ( Apec ) conference in California . <p> " I think it 's going to stop when Hamas no longer maintains the capacity to murder , " Mr Biden said of the war , which was sparked by Hamas 's October 7 assault on Israel . <p> An Israeli @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Gaza Strip , as seen from southern Israel ( Leo Correa , AP ) <p> Mr Biden said he had discussed with the Israelis their need to " be incredibly careful " as they worked to clear Shifa Hospital , where the president said he was absolutely confident sharing Israel 's assessment that Hamas maintains a significant military presence intermixed with civilian infrastructure . <p> He maintained that " the only ultimate answer here is a two-state solution " with Israel and Palestinians living side by side . <p> Israeli forces searched a hospital on Wednesday where they claim Hamas militants operate . <p> Mohammed Zaqout , the director of hospitals in Gaza , said Israeli tanks were inside the medical compound and soldiers had entered buildings , including the emergency and surgery departments , which house intensive care units . <p> Shifa Hospital has become a symbol of the suffering of Palestinian civilians
