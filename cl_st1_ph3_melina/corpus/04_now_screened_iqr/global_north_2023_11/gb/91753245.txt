<p> Hundreds of demonstrators in Los Angeles shut down a stretch of Hollywood Boulevard on Wednesday afternoon in a protest demanding Joe Biden and US lawmakers call for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza . <p> In the heart of Hollywood , along the palm tree-lined streets near Ripley 's Believe It or Not ! and the Walk of Fame , protesters waved Palestinian flags and held signs -- " not in our name " and " let Gaza live " . More than 1,000 people gathered in the rain , including a group that staged a sit-in at the intersection with flowers in hand and shirts reading " Jews say ceasefire now " as tourists watched . <p> " We demand an end to the killing and the return of the hostages , " one speaker said . " Ceasefire now . " <p> The demonstration , organized by the groups Jewish Voice for Peace Los Angeles and IfNotNow LA , followed large gatherings around the US in Washington DC , New York and Seattle earlier in the month .
