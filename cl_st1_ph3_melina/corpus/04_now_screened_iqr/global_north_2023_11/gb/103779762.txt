<p> Three Palestinian men have been shot while on their way to a Thanksgiving dinner in the US . <p> Police were called to reports of gunfire at around 6.30pm last night in Burlington , Vermont . Two injured men were discovered at the scene , with a third found a short distance away . <p> They have been named locally as Hisham Awartani , Tahseen Ali and Kenen Abdulhamid , though police have not yet confirmed their identities . The three are all believed to be students at prestigious US universities . <p> The three men were said to have been wearing the Palestinian keffiyeh , which some are speculating made them a target as tensions rise globally due to the conflict in Israel and Gaza . One victim was reportedly shot in the back while another is said to have been shot in the chest and a third sustained minor injuries . <p> Basil Awartani , the cousin of Hisham , took to Twitter to say : " My cousin Hisham has been shot in the back while walking with his friends in Burlington for simply wearing kuffiyehs and speaking Arabic .
