<h> World Health Organization praises staff at Gaza 's Al Shifa hospital <p> GENEVA - The World Health Organization ( WHO ) praised staff at Gaza 's Al Shifa hospital on Tuesday for doing " everything they can " to care for patients despite being surrounded by Israeli forces trying to wipe out Hamas . <p> The hospital has ceased functioning normally since Israel began its offensive in Gaza following the deadly Hamas attack on southern Israel in Oct. 7 , and has insufficient electricity , water and basic equipment . <p> Israel says the hospital sits on top of an underground headquarters for Hamas fighters . The militant group denies this . <p> " We know there 's not enough food , that the staff are struggling to get any clean water because their water tanks were destroyed , but they are still doing everything they can to keep providing medical care for the desperately ill patients they have , " WHO spokesperson Margaret Harris said in Geneva . <p> " We actually still describe Al Shifa as a functioning hospital because of the heroic efforts the staff are making .
