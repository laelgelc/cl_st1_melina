<h> In pictures : Thousands march in Scotland 's streets for Gaza <p> THOUSANDS took to the streets across Scotland for pro-Palestine protests . <p> Marches took place in major cities including Edinburgh , Glasgow , Dundee and Aberdeen on Saturday , calling for a ceasefire in Gaza as the UK commemorates an end to fighting in World War One . <p> In Glasgow , the sizeable crowd on Buchanan Street was addressed by eight-year-old Jeewan Wadi ( Below ) , who was born in Gaza in 2014 . <p> Through tears and being held up by her father , Jeewan recounted how , while pregnant with her , her mother was forced to hide from bombs . <p> " Listen everybody , I only care about the children , babies , women and men that are getting bombed in their own homes , " she said . <p> Ending her remarks , Jeewan led the crowd in a chant of " ceasefire now " . <p> Scotland 's former justice secretary was also booed by activists in Glasgow after saying Israel has a right to " defend itself against terrorism " . <p> Keith Brown , who served @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ event on Saturday in front of a large crowd at the city 's Buchanan Street steps . <p> The first speaker during the rally , Brown said he was speaking in a personal capacity , as opposed to as the SNP 's depute leader and a former minister . <p> " We need to be clear , collective punishment is a war crime , and it 's wrong that the Palestinian people are currently being collectively punished for the actions of a terrorist group , " he said . <p> " The only way that we 're going to end the perpetual cycle of violence in Israel and Palestine is by addressing the root cause . <p> " That 's why we stand for a two state solution , which recognises the sovereignty , the independence , the territorial integrity and the right to self determination of both Palestine and Israel .
