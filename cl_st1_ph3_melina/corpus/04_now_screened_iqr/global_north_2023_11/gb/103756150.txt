<p> Crowds gathered at Cardiff city centre on Saturday to show their support for Palestine . Hundreds , if not thousands , of people met outside Central Square near Cardiff Central train station , before marching through the city . <p> It is thought that the large turn out contributed to slow traffic in Cardiff , along with Christmas shoppers . The rally saw several Palestinian speakers including Sumayya Ahmed from the Palestine Social Club , Palestine Solidarity Campaign honorary president Betty Hunter and Swansea cardiologist Dr Ahmed Sabra .
