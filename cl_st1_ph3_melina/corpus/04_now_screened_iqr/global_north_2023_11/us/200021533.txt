<h> Walmart Foundation commits $1 million to support humanitarian aid in Gaza <p> Statement from Donna Morris , Chief People Officer , Walmart <p> November 25 , 2023 08:08 PM Eastern Standard Time <p> BENTONVILLE , Ark. -- ( BUSINESS WIRE ) --Walmart , Inc. and the Walmart Foundation have continued to look for ways to support people impacted by the devastating violence in Israel and Gaza that has unfolded over the past month following the terrorist attacks by Hamas .
