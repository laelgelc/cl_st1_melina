" You proceeded stating , " This terrorist attack and how many people it has taken -- thousands of Israeli and other families impacted . We hurt for and we pray for each and every one of those families . " <p> By acknowledging the violence committed in Israel and not addressing the atrocities committed by the Israeli occupation forces in Gaza , you negligently imply the ethnic cleansing and suffering of the Palestinians under Israeli occupation is nonexistent .
