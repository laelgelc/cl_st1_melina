<p> GAZA STRIP - A ship towing a barge loaded with food arrived off Gaza on Friday , witnesses said , in a test run for a new aid route by sea from Cyprus into the devastated Palestinian enclave where famine looms after five months of Israel 's military campaign . <p> The ship , arranged by the World Central Kitchen ( WCK ) charity , is carrying nearly 200 tonnes of aid to be delivered via a jetty being prepared in Gaza , with a second ship expected to sail soon . <p> Floating on a barge attached by rope to a salvage ship , rough seas appeared to slow down the cargo reaching land , footage posted by a WCK official on social media showed . <p> WCK have been constructing a makeshift jetty which would allow the flat-bottomed barge to approach Gaza 's shallow waters for lack of proper port infrastructure . <p> " So far 2 crates already delivered from the barge , " WCK founder Jose Andres , a Michelin-starred chef , said in a post on X.
