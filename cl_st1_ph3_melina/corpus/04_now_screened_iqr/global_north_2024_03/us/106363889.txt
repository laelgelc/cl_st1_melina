<p> Voting probably was n't the first thing on our parents ' or grandparents ' minds when they arrived in the U.S. from countries and provinces where democracy was a pipe dream . Working ( perhaps several jobs ) , learning the language and setting up home in the community took precedence over politics , and it was all in service of their top priority : creating a better future for their kids . <p> This week , that seemingly bygone promise of the American dream played out in real time inside Michigan polling centers .
