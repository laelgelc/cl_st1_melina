<h> More than 10% of buildings in Gaza have been destroyed in war : UN <p> Satellite imagery analyzed by the United Nations shows that roughly 35% of all buildings in the Gaza Strip have been damaged during the Israel -- Hamas war , while more than 10% have been destroyed . <p> The United Nations Satellite Center released an update on Wednesday regarding the destruction caused by nearly six months of war in the enclave . It found that 88,868 buildings have either been destroyed , severely damaged , or moderately damaged , and this represents a nearly 20,000 increase since the previous assessment in January . <p> Its statistics indicate there are nearly 254,000 buildings within the entire strip . <p> It found that nearly 31,200 buildings were destroyed , or approximately 12% ; nearly 17,000 were severely damaged , or approximately 7% ; and more than 40,700 buildings were moderately damaged , about 16% . <p> Much of the new damage has come in Khan Yunis , one of the primary locations where Israel has carried out large-scale operations against Hamas this year . <p> Palestinians look at the destruction after an Israeli strike on residential @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on Thursday , Feb. 22 , 2024 .
