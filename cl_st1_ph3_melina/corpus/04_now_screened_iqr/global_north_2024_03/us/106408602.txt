<h> Harris calls for ' immediate ceasefire ' in Gaza to end ' humanitarian catastrophe ' <p> Vice President Kamala Harris echoed an increasing number of Democrats calling for a ceasefire in the Israel -- Hamas war , putting pressure on Hamas to accept a deal amid what she described as a " humanitarian catastrophe . " <p> " People in Gaza are starving , conditions inhumane , and our common humanity compels us to act , " Harris told a crowd Sunday in Selma , Alabama . " Given the immense scale of suffering in Gaza , there must be an immediate ceasefire for at least the next six weeks , which is what is currently on the table . " <p> She continued , " This will get the hostages out and get a significant amount of aid in ; this would allow us to build something more enduring to ensure Israel is secure and to respect the rights of Palestinian people .
