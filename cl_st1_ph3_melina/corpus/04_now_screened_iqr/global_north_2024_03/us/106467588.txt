" That movement was meant to signal to President Joe Biden that large factions of his own party want him to establish a permanent cease-fire . <p> The proposed language of the resolution , authored by Council Member Cheniqua Johnson , urges the Biden administration to discontinue " unconditional U.S. military aid to Israel without clear guarantees for civilian safety .
