<p> Labour unions have called on the Canadian government to reverse its decision to suspend United Nations Relief and Works Agency ( UNRWA ) funding . Six labour unions sent a joint letter to prime minister Justin Trudeau on February 9 , stating that the pause in funding is cruel considering the ongoing humanitarian crisis that the Palestinian people are currently facing . <p> Canada joined several countries in the suspension of UNRWA funding after allegations arose that some of their workers took part in the Hamas attack on October 7 . The US , Germany , and Australia are among ome of the other countries to withhold funding to UNRWA . <p> " Now is not the time to withdraw funding from the United Nations Relief Work Agency , " said Bert Blundon , president of the National Union of Public and General Employees ( NUPGE ) . <p> Along with NUPGE , national labour unions joined together to sign the statement to reverse their decision and reinstate UNRWA funding .
