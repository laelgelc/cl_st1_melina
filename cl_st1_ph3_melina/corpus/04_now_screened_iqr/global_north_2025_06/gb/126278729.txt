<h> Gaza film by York schoolgirl with Filmlab Palestine <p> This article is brought to you by our exclusive subscriber partnership with our sister title USA Today , and has been written by our American colleagues . It does not necessarily reflect the view of The Herald . <p> Now her Gaza children 's story We Are Here , Still is set to premiere ahead of festival submissions . <p> At just 19 years old , Ava Bounds has turned a deep sense of justice into impactful storytelling . In response to the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Gaza , Ava has partnered with a Palestinian production company in Ramallah to create We Are Here , Still -- a powerful two-minute short film told through the eyes of children . <p> " I 'm not a politician or a soldier -- but I 'm a storyteller . And sometimes , that 's enough to start change . " <p> The film was conceptualised and written during Ava 's flight to Morocco , where she single-handedly cast local child actors , sourced costumes , found locations , created the visual effects , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " It was just me , my camera , and a story I felt needed to be told . <p> " Children still play , still dream -- even in the hardest places . That 's what I wanted to capture . " <p> Back in the UK , Ava edited the footage herself and collaborated with Filmlab Palestine for colour grading , with support from professional filmmaker Nour Abukamal in Bethlehem . <p> A Filmlab Palestine spokesperson said : " We are truly encouraged by Ava 's initiative to create an impact campaign around this film . We wholeheartedly believe in using art as a tool for advocacy and justice . " <p> We Are Here , Still is set for international film festival submissions and can be viewed from 24 June online to reach a wider audience . <p> Ava hopes it will inspire both awareness and support for ongoing humanitarian efforts in Gaza . <p> As The Press reported back in 2021 , aged just 15 , Ava was invited to New York as her film , Beth , was nominated for best film at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ film competition in the USA for school students . <p> Out of 2,500 films , Beth won Best International Film . Ava was also nominated for Best Director , Best Drama and Best Overall Film . <p> Ava made her first experimental Sci-Fi film Players with the help of her Huby neighbours who came along as cast members , just after the first Covid lockdown .
