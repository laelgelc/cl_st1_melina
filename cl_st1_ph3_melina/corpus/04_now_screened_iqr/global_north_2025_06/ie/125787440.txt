<h> Inside Politics <p> It is something else that can be blamed on Brexit . When Britain decided to leave the EU in 2016 it no longer belonged to the European bond market . The market allows sovereign states to raise finance by issuing bonds that they pay back over time . <p> The European Prospectus Regulation for bonds deemed that one European state would be the " home state " that would determine if a non-EU state selling bonds had the financial wherewithal to trade the bonds in EU markets . The state would be chosen not by the EU but by the country selling the bonds . <p> Until 2016 Britain was the country which decided if a bond prospectus offered by Israel could be approved for sale in the EU .
