<h> Pope meets with Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas <p> Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas visits the Vatican , and discusses the need for a ceasefire in Gaza . Speaking to Vatican Media , he says that he asked the Pope to continue to promote recognition of the State of Palestine in the international community . <p> By Joseph TullochPope Francis held a 30-minute long meeting this morning with Mahmoud Abbas , President of the State of Palestine . <p> Speaking to Vatican Media 's Roberto Cetera immediately afterwards , President Abbas described the meeting as " fruitful " , adding that " every time I meet the Pope , it 's like meeting an old friend " . <p> It was the pair 's first face-to-face meeting in three years . <p> " I thanked the Holy Father for his constant words about peace in the Middle East " , President Abbas said , " and for the solidarity he always expresses with the Palestinian civilians who are victims of the war in Gaza . " <p> " I asked him to continue to promote the recognition of the State of Palestine in the international community , " Abbas added . " If peace is to be achieved , there is no alternative to the two-state solution . " <p> Statement from Holy See Press Office <p> A statement from the Holy See Press Office noted that , after his meeting with the Pope , President Abbas met with the Holy See 's Secretary of State , Cardinal Pietro Parolin , and the Holy See 's Secretary for Relations with States , Archbishop Paul Gallagher . <p> The statement said that the " very serious humanitarian situation in Gaza " was discussed at the meetings , as was the hope for " a ceasefire and the release of all hostages as soon as possible " . <p> Another topic was " the Catholic Church 's important contribution to Palestinian society " , including its current humanitarian efforts in Gaza . <p> The Holy See Press Office statement also noted that the discussions had covered the necessity of implementing a two-state solution , not with violence but via " diplomacy and dialogue " , and the importance of a special status for Jerusalem , in order that it might be a " place of friendship for the three great monotheistic religions " . <p> Finally , the Catholic Church 's upcoming 2025 Holy Year was discussed , and hope was expressed that the Jubilee Year might lead to " the return of pilgrims to the Holy Land " . <p> Exchange of giftsAt the end of the Pope 's meeting with President Abbas , the pair exchanged gifts . <p> Pope Francis gave the Palestinian President several presents , including his message for the World Day of Peace and a bas-relief with the words " Peace is a fragile flower " . <p> President Abbas gave the Pope several gifts in return , including a painting depicting him with the Pope and one depicting the Pope during his visit to Bethlehem in 2014 . <p> He also presented the Pope with an icon of Saint Porphyrius , the patron saint of Gaza -- a gift from Patriarch Theophilus , the Greek Orthodox Patriarch of Jerusalem. --Vatican News
