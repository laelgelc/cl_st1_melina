" <p> In its report , Amnesty International combined hundreds of interviews with satellite images , video footage , and other material , including publicly available material relating to South Africa 's ICJ case against Israel . <h> SA ICJ case <p> In accordance with ICJ rules , the filing remains confidential until it is published by the court . <p> Presidency spokesperson Vincent Magwenya said that South Africa 's filing at the ICJ includes 750 pages of primary text , along with more than 4,000 pages of exhibits and annexes , to demonstrate that Israel is intentionally seeking to destroy Palestinians in Gaza . <p> Israel must convince the ICJ that South Africa 's assertions of intent are without merit . It has until July 2025 to file its counter-memorial . <p> If accepted , South Africa 's case would mark a historic first , as no state has successfully prosecuted another for genocide under the Genocide Convention of 1948.
