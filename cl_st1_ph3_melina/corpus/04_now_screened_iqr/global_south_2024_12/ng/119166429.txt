<h> Ceasefire hopes dim as Israeli strikes kill 28 in Gaza <p> Gaza 's civil defence agency said on Sunday that Israeli strikes killed at least 28 Palestinians across the territory , more than 14 months into the Israel-Hamas war . <p> The violence came even as Palestinian groups involved in the fighting said a ceasefire deal was " closer than ever " . <p> Israel has faced growing criticism of its actions during the war , triggered by Hamas 's October 7 , 2023 attack , including from rights groups accusing it of " acts of genocide " which the Israeli government strongly denies . <p> Pope Francis denounced on Sunday the " cruelty " of Israel 's bombardment , highlighting the deaths of children and attacks on schools and hospitals in Gaza . <p> It was his second such comment in as many days , despite Israel 's accusing the pontiff of " double standards " . <p> On the ground in Gaza , Civil Defence Agency spokesman Mahmud Bassal said at least 13 people were killed in an air strike on a house in central Gaza 's Deir el-Balah belonging to the Abu Samra family . <p> An AFP photographer saw residents searching through the debris for survivors , while others looked for belongings they could salvage . <p> In a nearby compound , bodies covered in blankets lay on the sandy ground . <p> The military said it targeted an Islamic Jihad militant who was operating in Deir el-Balah . <p> " According to an initial examination , the reported number of casualties resulting from the strike does not align with the information held by the IDF ( military ) , " it said to AFP in a statement , which did not give its toll . <p> Bassal said in a statement that a separate strike , overnight into Sunday , killed three people in Rafah , in the south . <p> And a drone strike on Sunday morning hit a car in Gaza City , killing four people , the spokesman added . <p> Israel in early October began a major military operation in Gaza 's north , which it said aimed to prevent Hamas from regrouping there . <p> A United Nations official who visited Gaza City said late last month that people were living in " inhumane conditions with severe food shortages and terrible sanitary conditions " . <p> On Sunday a hospital director in northern Gaza said Israeli forces were bombing buildings near the facility . <p> Hossam Abu Safia , director of Kamal Adwan Hospital , said in a statement that the facility 's generators were hit and that " the army is attempting to target the fuel tank , which is full of fuel and poses a significant fire risk . " <p> Contacted by AFP , the military said it was unaware of any strikes on the hospital , one of only two still operating in northern Gaza . <p> The unprecedented Hamas attack last year that sparked the war resulted in the deaths of 1,208 people , most of them civilians , according to an AFP tally based on official Israeli figures . <p> Militants also took 251 hostages , of whom 96 remain in Gaza , including 34 the Israeli military says are dead . <p> Israel 's retaliatory offensive in Gaza has killed at least 45,259 people , a majority of them civilians , according to figures from the Hamas-run territory 's health ministry that the United Nations considers reliable . <p> Hamas and two other Palestinian armed groups said in a rare joint statement on Saturday that an agreement to end the bloodshed was " closer than ever " , after Qatari-hosted talks that followed months of stalled negotiations . <p> Negotiations have faced multiple challenges since a one-week truce in November 2023 , with the primary point of contention being the establishment of a lasting ceasefire .
