<p> Europe seems to be suffering paroxysms of withdrawal , notably when it comes to international conventions . Many states on the continent seem to have decided that international law is a burden onerous and in need of lightening . Poland , Finland and the three Baltic states , for instance , have concluded that using landmines , despite their indiscriminately murderous quality , somehow fits their mould of self-defence against the Russian Bear . That spells the end of their obligations under the Anti-Personnel Landmines Convention . Lithuania 's government has thought it beneath it to continue abiding by the Convention on Cluster Munitions , withdrawing last month . <p> The International Criminal Court now promises to be one member short .
