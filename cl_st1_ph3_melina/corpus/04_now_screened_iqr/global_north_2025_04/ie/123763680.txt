<h> Commanding officer to be reprimanded and deputy commander dismissed <p> The Israeli army has dismissed a deputy commander and censured a brigade chief over the killing of 14 Gaza medics . The military also admitted that its initial investigation on the incident , based on reports from soldiers on the ground , was false . <p> Fourteen emergency workers and a United Nations employee were killed on March 23rd after a convoy of Palestinian Red Crescent Society ambulances , a UN car and a fire truck came under fire by soldiers , in a roadside ambush close to the southern Gaza city of Rafah . <p> The Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) investigation , released on Sunday , admitted for the first time that 15 people were killed in the incident , and that soldiers shot at a convoy of ambulances .
