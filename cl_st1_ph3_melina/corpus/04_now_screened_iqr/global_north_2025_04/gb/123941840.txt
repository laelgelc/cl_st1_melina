<p> Israeli authorities are denying a Palestinian paramedic , one of two aid workers who survived an attack on emergency responders in Gaza last month , from having legal representation or visitation rights during his detention , his family has said . <p> In a petition to the High Court of Justice on Wednesday , the family of medic Assad al-Nassasra sought details about the unnamed facility he is being held in . <p> On behalf of the paramedic 's wife , HaMoked , an NGO dedicated to helping Palestinians amid increasing Israeli restrictions and violations , filed an urgent petition " against the military 's refusal to disclose the whereabouts of the Gazan paramedic detained 23 March . " <p> In their latest update , HaMoked noted that the military had barred Nassasra from meeting with a lawyer until 7 May , in accordance with the amended Unlawful Combatants Law . <p> The legislation grants the military the power to detain individuals indefinitely if they find them to pose a threat to Israel 's security without having to provide proof for these claims . <h> New MEE newsletter : @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and analysis on Israel-Palestine , alongside Turkey Unpacked and other MEE newsletters <p> Several amendments have been made since 7 October 2023 , including the ability to refuse detainees access to legal representation for the first 45 days of their imprisonment . The video showed emergency workers in uniform , operating clearly marked ambulances and fire trucks with lights on , being fired upon by soldiers . <p> The International Committee of the Red Cross said in a statement that it had " received information that the PRCS medic Assad al-Nassasra has been detained in an Israeli place of detention " , but it did not provide information about where he was being held . <p> Another survivor from the attack , Munther Abed , reported that he had seen Nassasra being led away alive and blindfolded by Israeli officers following the killings . <p> On Sunday , the Israeli army confirmed Nassasra 's detention . <p> Aminah Komber , an attorney who represents the paramedics family on behalf of HaMoked , spoke to Haaretz about @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ right of a detainee -- that his arrest and whereabouts be confirmed . <p> " It is also the right of the detainee 's family to know the fate of their loved one , " Komber added . <p> " Israeli authorities must immediately inform the family of al-Nassasra 's whereabouts .
