<p> Israel says the killings of 15 medics in Gaza was due to ' professional failures ' <p> Mourners gather around the bodies of 8 Red Crescent emergency responders , recovered in Rafah a week after an Israeli attack , as they are transported for burial from a hospital in Deir al-Balah , Gaza Strip , March 31 , 2025 <p> AN ISRAELI investigation into the killings of 15 Palestinian medics in Gaza last month has blamed a chain of " professional failures , " leading to the sacking of a deputy commander . <p> With medical workers enjoying special protection under international humanitarian law , the shootings caused widespread outrage in the international community , with some calling them a war crime . <p> The International Red Crescent called the attack the deadliest on its personnel in eight years . <p> Israel at first claimed that the medics ' vehicles did not have emergency signals on when troops opened fire , but it later backtracked.Mobile phone footage recovered from one medic contradicted Israel 's initial account . It said the deputy commander , operating with " poor night visibility , " had believed his troops to be under threat when the ambulances sped toward their position and medics rushed out to check the victims . The military said the flashing lights were less visible on night-vision drones and goggles.The ambulances immediately came under a barrage of gunfire that went on for more than five minutes , with brief pauses . Troops bulldozed over the bodies along with their mangled vehicles , burying them in a mass grave .
