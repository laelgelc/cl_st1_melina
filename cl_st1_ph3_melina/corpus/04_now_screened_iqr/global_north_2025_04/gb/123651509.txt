It comes as the UN has raised the alarm over Israel 's six-week-old blockade stopping food and supplies entering the territory . <p> Last month , Israel ended the ceasefire with Hamas -- renewing its bombardment , killing hundreds and seizing large parts of Gaza to pressure the militants to accept changes to the agreement .
