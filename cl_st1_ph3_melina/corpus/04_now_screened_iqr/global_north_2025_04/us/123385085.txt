On May 7 , 2024 , members of Islami Jamiat-e-Talaba -- the student wing of Jamaat-e-Islami -- held a pro-Palestinian rally outside a KFC restaurant near Islamabad , calling for a nationwide boycott of Israeli-linked products . <p> Earlier @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , in Pakistan-administered Kashmir , set fire to a KFC branch during an anti-Israel and anti-American demonstration . In response , Israel launched a large-scale offensive that has drawn global condemnation for its toll on Palestinian civilians . In Pakistan , where anti-American sentiment runs deep , the conflict has reignited anger not just at Israeli policies but also at Washington 's enduring role as Israel 's key ally .
