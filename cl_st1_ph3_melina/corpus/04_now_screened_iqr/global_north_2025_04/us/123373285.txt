<p> After 18 months of slaughter , it is still possible to be shocked by events in Gaza . More than 50,000 people have been killed , according to Palestinian health authorities . More are starving because Israel has cut off aid . The offensive is intensifying again -- with 100 children killed or maimed each day since Israel resumed heavy strikes last month , the U.N . reports . <p> Even so , Israel 's killing of 15 Palestinian paramedics and rescue workers is particularly chilling . Though they died March 23 , it took days for Israel to grant access to the site , the U.N . said . Another man was last seen in Israeli custody . <p> Two grounds for seeing this not only as tragic but as a war crime stand out . The first is that the U.N . says the men were shot " one by one , " and a forensic expert said that preliminary evidence " suggests they were executed , not from a distant range , " given the " specific and intentional " locations of bullet wounds .
