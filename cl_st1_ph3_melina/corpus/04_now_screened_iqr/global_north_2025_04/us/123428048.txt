<p> The Israel delegation at the Inter-Parliamentary Union assembly in Tashkent , Uzbekistan , proposed and then retracted a request for the inclusion of an emergency item in the assembly agenda in favor of enabling emigration from Gaza . <p> The proposal marked the first time such a proposal was made on a multi-national diplomatic stage . <p> The resolution 's title was " Ending the Double Standard : Calling on the International Community to Accept Refugees from Gaza Instead of Exploiting Them to Attack Israel , " and it was presented by Likud MK Dan Illouz . <p> According to the resolution , Israel 's military operation in Gaza is a necessary and justified war to eliminate Hamas , and Israel is making extensive efforts to minimize civilian casualties . However , the resolution says , " Civilian casualties occur in Gaza due to the extreme density of the urban battlefield and Hamas 's deliberate strategy of embedding itself within civilian areas .
