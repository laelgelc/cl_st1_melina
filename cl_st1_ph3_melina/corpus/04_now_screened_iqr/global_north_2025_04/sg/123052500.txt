The people of Gaza have little to celebrate as Israel shattered the fragile ceasefire on March 18 , and resumed its genocidal war against this forsaken territory . Over 900 people have been massacred since the truce fell apart , while overall more than 50,000 Palestinians have been slaughtered by Israel in Gaza since Oct 7 , 2023 .
