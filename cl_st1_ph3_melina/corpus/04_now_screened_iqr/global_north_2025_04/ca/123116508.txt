<p> Israel launched an offensive late Tuesday to seize " broad areas " of the Gaza Strip , signaling a prolonged military presence there . <p> The announcement by Defense Minister Israel Katz came as international outrage is mounting over the war . This week , United Nations officials said they discovered a " mass grave " with the bodies of 15 aid workers killed by Israel . Israel said the deaths occurred in a strike on terrorists using emergency vehicles . <p> The operation in southern Gaza began about two weeks after Israel ended a ceasefire with Hamas and resumed its war to defeat the terror group .
