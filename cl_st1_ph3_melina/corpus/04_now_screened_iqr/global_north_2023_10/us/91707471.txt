<p> Commodity prices , especially oil and gas , could rise sharply if the war in Israel and Gaza escalates , according to a new report from the World Bank . <p> Energy prices have risen by 9% since the start of the current conflict , and the World Bank warned Monday that prices could see further shocks based on historical precedents . <p> " History suggests that an escalation of the conflict in the region could trigger substantial oil supply disruptions , " the World Bank said . " A major escalation could cause an initial surge in oil prices , with disruptive knock on effects on other commodity markets . " <p> Energy prices make up the majority of the World Bank 's commodity price index , so any price shocks would lead the whole index higher , it said . The commodity price index rose 5% during the third quarter , largely pushed by supply-side issues in oil markets , it said . <p> So far , the effect on markets have been muted , with oil prices pulling back after an initial shock , though they remain higher .
