We all snuggled together in bed , bleary-eyed . The kids started a game that soon turned into low-grade squabble , when , suddenly , an air-raid siren blared . We ducked into our building 's stairwell , our " safe space " for lack of any other in this old area of the city . Some neighbors were already there , in their pajamas , everyone smiling at one another awkwardly .
