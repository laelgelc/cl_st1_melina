<p> The Jewish Federation of Greater Nashville held a community solidarity rally on Monday amid war in Israel <p> It was attended by hundreds , including city and state leaders . <p> The crowd of hundreds harmonized and sang the Hatikvah 's verses in total synchronization on Monday night , even when the slow and sorrowful tempo quickened to a cantor with upbeat chords on the guitar . <p> Many began clapping and others were crying , but the voices singing Israel 's national anthem resounded as one through the Gordon Jewish Community Center auditorium . <p> Concluding a community solidarity rally for Israel , the Hatikvah encapsulated the larger message the rally sought to promote . That despite Jewish Nashvillians ' differences , they all are hurting for Israel right now and can find comfort in each other . <p> " In this moment , despite their differences , the Israeli people are united as one , " said Rabbi Daniel Horwitz , CEO of the Jewish Federation of Greater Nashville , in an address at the rally .
