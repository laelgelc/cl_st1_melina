<p> Roughly 400 people gathered near Birmingham 's Railroad Park on Sunday to support Palestine and protest Israel 's recent bombings in Gaza . <p> The protestors met at the intersection of 16th Street and First Avenue South and marched for several blocks . Palestinian flags flew in the air , and many protesters wore keffiyehs , black and white patterned scarves , or held handmade and printed signs with messages like " Resistance against occupation is a human right , " " End all U.S. aid to Israel , " and " Jews for a free Palestine . " <p> A man wearing a Palestinian keffiyeh leads a group of protestors in a chant .
