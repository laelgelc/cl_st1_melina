It is not a defense force . Its overarching goal and its very reason for existence , has always been the annihilation of Israel . Like other Middle East terror groups -- Hezbollah in Lebanon and Houthi militants in Yemen -- it is funded , trained , armed , and supported by Iran . <p> That is important to note . This weekend 's violence was not some rogue attack .
