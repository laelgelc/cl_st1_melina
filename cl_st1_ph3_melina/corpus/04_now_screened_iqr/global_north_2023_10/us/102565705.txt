" <p> He added that people seeing the events unfold in the conflict may not meet the full criteria for PTSD but still have some of the symptoms , which can linger and have an effect on a person 's ability to function . <h> Health risks of displacement <p> Many Israelis and Palestinians have been forced to flee to other cities , and even neighboring countries , which can affect mental health . <p> Smoke rises following Israeli airstrikes in Gaza City , Oct. 11 , 2023 . <p> Adel Hana/AP <p> " It can be very disruptive to their mental health in everyday activities , " Tsai said .
