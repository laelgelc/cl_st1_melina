<h> North Korea denies its weapons used by Hamas against Israel <p> Jack Kim and Josh Smith <p> October 12 , 2023 at 9:03 PM <p> By Jack Kim and Josh Smith <p> SEOUL ( Reuters ) -North Korea denied on Friday its weapons were used by Hamas in the attack against Israel , saying the claim made in some media reports was a bid by Washington to divert the blame for the conflict from itself to a third country . <p> Military experts said this week that photos from the conflict showed Hamas militants may be using North Korean weapons , including possible F-7 rocket-propelled grenades . <p> There have been reports of Hamas using the F-7 for several years , said Bruce Bechtol , a professor at Angelo State University in Texas , who has done research on North Korea 's arms sales . <p> " This could be new supplies or from previous shipments going back as far as 2009 , " he said , adding that in any case the weapons likely took an indirect route to Hamas from North Korea through Iran or Syria . <p> The North 's official KCNA news agency called @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " a groundless and false rumour " . <p> " It is nothing but a bid to shift the blame for the Middle East crisis caused by ( the United States ' ) wrong hegemonic policy onto a third country and thus evade the international criticism focused on the empire of evil , " it said . <p> According to European researchers Joost Oliemans and Stijn Mitzer , the F-7 is easily discernible from similar RPGs by the red band around the warhead .
