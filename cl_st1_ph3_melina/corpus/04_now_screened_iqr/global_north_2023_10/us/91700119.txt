<p> The Israel-Hamas war , which began after Hamas militants on Oct. 7 launched a surprise , unprecedented attack by storming into Israeli towns from the blockaded Gaza Strip , has so far claimed at least 3,200 lives on both sides . <p> Israeli airstrikes have razed whole districts and put Gaza , home to more than 2 million people , under siege . On Saturday , Gaza 's health ministry said Israeli airstrikes had killed at least 2,000 people . According to the United Nations , more than one million people have been displaced in the Gaza Strip so far , with many taking refuge in shelters and schools or huddling in the streets . <p> Israel has cut power and water to the coastal enclave until hostages are released . Clean water was running out on Saturday , leading to a " life and death " situation , the U.N . said . Fuel for hospital generators is also dwindling and internet and phone service cutting out , aid groups have reported .
