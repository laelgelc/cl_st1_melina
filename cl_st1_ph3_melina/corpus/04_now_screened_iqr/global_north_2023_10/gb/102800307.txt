<p> Thousands of residents kickstarted their Saturday morning on a positive note by gathering for a noble cause of compassion and unity . <p> Gathering as early as 8am at Qalaat Alremaal hall on Dubai-Al Ain Road , volunteers joined hands to contribute to making a difference in the lives of those affected by war in Gaza . <p> Residents came together to pack relief packages launched by Dubai Cares and the Emirates Red Crescent Society aimed at supporting and bringing relief to the victims . This is a campaign in line with the UAE 's approach to humanitarianism , ' Tarahum - for Gaza ' , which was launched in the country last week to provide relief to Palestinians affected by the war in the Gaza Strip . <p> The campaign is supervised by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in cooperation with the World Food Programme , and in coordination with the Ministry of Community Development . <h> For our brothers and sisters <p> It was not only adults who participated , but children and seniors who rolled up their sleeves and got to work .
