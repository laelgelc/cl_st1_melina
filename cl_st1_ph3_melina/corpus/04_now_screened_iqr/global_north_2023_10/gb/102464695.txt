<p> Austria and Germany said on Monday they were suspending aid worth tens of millions of euro to Palestinians in response to Islamist group Hamas ' deadly attack on Israel to ensure funds were not flowing into the wrong hands . <p> Both countries said they wanted to review their engagement with the Palestinian territories , and discuss that with Israel and international partners . Berlin said it wanted to ensure it was fostering peace in the region and send a sign of solidarity . <p> European Union foreign ministers will hold an emergency meeting on Tuesday to discuss the situation , including reviewing aspects of development aid , the EU 's top diplomat said on Monday .
