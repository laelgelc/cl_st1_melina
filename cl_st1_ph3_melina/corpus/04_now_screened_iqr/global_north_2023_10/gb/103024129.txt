<h> Thousands came together during peaceful protest for Palestine in Bolton town centre <h> Bolton : Thousands came together during peaceful protest <p> Thousands came together in Bolton town centre during a peaceful protest for Palestine . <p> Families and friends marched from Queens Park to Victoria Square , waving placards and flags , where they chanted ' free Palestine ' . <p> It comes as the violence has continued to escalate today after Israel expanded its ground operation in Gaza , sending in tanks and infantry backed by massive strikes from the air and sea . <p> The Palestinian death toll in Gaza on Saturday rose to just over 7,700 people since October 7 , with 377 deaths reported since late Friday , according to the territory 's health ministry . <p> One protester said : " If we stand by and do nothing whilst this is going on , I feel like we are almost complicit in what 's going on in that genocide . <p> " We are here to show our solidarity for the people of Palestine and to also call for a ceasefire , and that must happen immediately .
