<p> Keir Starmer has continued to resist calls to sack rebel frontbenchers who have demanded a ceasefire in Gaza . <p> The Labour leader tried to reclaim the initiative amid a growing revolt among activists , MPs and shadow ministers over his stance on Israel 's offensive in the Strip .
