<p> As part of its ongoing " Tarahum - for Gaza " campaign , the UAE today sent an aircraft carrying 68 tonnes of food supplies and relief aid to Al Areesh , Egypt , in preparation for transport into Gaza Strip through the Rafah Border Crossing , in cooperation with the UN World Food Programme ( WFP ) .
