<p> A university professor watched in horror as his family home in Gaza was destroyed in a raid on TV . <p> Dr Mohammed Alshurafa saw the home reduced to rubble in the Israeli strike while watching the news , terrified his parents and younger brother would be inside . The 33-year-old academic , who is originally Palestinian but teaches at Heriot Watt University in Edinburgh , frantically rushed to call his loved ones , believing they may be dead . <p> The residential building was hit by a rocket shortly after 3am local time on Sunday in retaliation over violence launched by Hamas at the weekend .
