<p> Scotland 's first minister has revealed he took a panicked call from his in-laws saying their goodbyes after a false bombing evacuation alert . <p> In a moving interview the SNP leader Humza Yousaf said he received a call at 1am on Sunday from his in-laws Elizabeth and Maged El-Nakla . The couple had travelled to Gaza last week to visit relatives but became trapped after Hamas fighters massacred and kidnapped hundreds of Israelis . <p> The attack has led to a major retaliation from Benjamin Netanyahu 's government and a bombardment of Gaza . Concerns are also growing for the fate of civilians ahead of an expected ground invasion from Israel . Speaking on the BBC 's Sunday with Laura Kuenssberg , the SNP leader Mr Yousaf detailed the " difficult " call with his in-laws . <p> He said : " We got a call at one in the morning from my mother in law in a panic . She Elizabeth was out on the street with all of her family and there had been a false alarm as it turned out . " But somebody next door in the neighbourhood they believe was told to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ So everybody in that neighbourhood in what would have been three in the morning in Gaza was running to goodness and god knows where because nowhere seems safe in Gaza . " <p> He added : " It ended up being a false alarm but you can imagine the panic and my mother-in-law was even saying her goodbyes which was pretty hard to hear . " Now it turned out to be a false alarm which I am pleased about because they are safe for now , but still trapped in Gaza , so only as safe as you can be in a war-zone . " <p> Elizabeth El-Nakla sent a tearful video to her son-in-law last week , which was posted on Twitter , where she questioned : " Where is humanity ? Where 's people 's hearts in the world , to let this happen in this day and age ? " <p> The SNP leader also used the interview on Sunday to reiterate his call for an immediate humanitarian corridor in Gaza and ceasefire to prevent further loss of life . He added : " This is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this is a man-made humanitarian catastrophe that is taking place . " <p> Mr Yousaf said : " We all accept ... that after the barbaric terrorism we saw a week past Saturday , of course Israel has a right to protect itself . " But that can not come at the price of 2.2million innocent men , women and children . It can not justify cutting off water , food , fuel supplies . <p> " I spoke to my mother-in-law yesterday - the whole day she had one egg I think she told me . She had virtually just a couple of sips of water because they have dozens of people now in their house and they have little drinking water . " And if that is true of my in-laws , who by Gazan standards have money , then what on earth is the plight of those who are suffering the greatest ? "
