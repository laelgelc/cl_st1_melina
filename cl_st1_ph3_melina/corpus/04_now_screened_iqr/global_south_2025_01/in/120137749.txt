The agreement , signed in Qatar , was confirmed by a statement from Benjamin Netanyahu 's office , which mentioned that the Israeli Prime Minister had scheduled a security cabinet meeting for Friday to vote on the deal . Though the deal has been done , voting may be delayed to Saturday , according to a Jerusalem Post report , which will further delay the release of the first hostages until Monday instead of Sunday as originally planned . <p> The full Israeli cabinet will later convene to formally approve the agreement .
