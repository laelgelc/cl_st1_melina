<h> Letters From Gaza <h> ' We are not okay ' <p> Over the past 14 months , I have received hundreds of messages from family members throughout the Gaza Strip . The nature of the messages often conveyed a sense of urgency and panic but , at times , contentment in God 's will . <p> Some of those who wrote these notes have been killed in Israeli strikes , like my sister , Dr Soma Baroud ; others lost children , siblings , cousins , neighbours and friends . It may seem strange that none of those who communicated with me throughout the war has ever questioned their faith and have often , if not always , begun their messages by checking on me and my children . <p> The samples of the messages below have been edited for length and clarity . <p> Ibrahim : <p> " How are you ?
