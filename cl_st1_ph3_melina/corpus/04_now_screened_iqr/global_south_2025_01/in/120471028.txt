<h> Hamas releases four Israeli female soldiers as part of ceasefire deal <h> The four soldiers were handed over to the Israeli Defense Forces and Shin Bet . <p> Tel Aviv : Palestinian group Hamas on Saturday , 25 January 2025 released four Israeli female soldiers , Karina Ariev , Daniella Gilboa , Naama Levy , and Liri Albag after 477 days of captivity . The release is a part of the ongoing ceasefire between Israel and Gaza . This was confirmed by Israel . <p> The four soldiers were handed over to the Israeli Defense Forces ( IDF ) and Shin Bet in Gaza by the Red Cross . They were later taken to Israel for medical evaluations and reunions with their families . <p> Sharing a photo of the female soldiers on social media , the Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs ( MFA ) said , " After 477 days in hell .
