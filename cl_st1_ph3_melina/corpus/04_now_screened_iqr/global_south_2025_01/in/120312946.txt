<p> ISRAEL Prime Minister Mr. Benjamin Netanyahu has made his intentions pretty clear as the ceasefire-for-hostage deal with the Hamas militant group kicked-off in Gaza on Sunday . Mr. Netanyahu has also talked about the backing of the United States to him to restart military action in such circumstances . Mr. Netanyahu can not be faulted for his apprehensions nor he can be accused of being a war-mongerer given the fragility surrounding the ceasefire deal . <p> The world must understand the fear of Mr. Netanyahu over falling apart of the agreement between Israel and Hamas . It is a long-drawn process where the militants would release Israeli hostages in phases which will be complimented by Israel by releasing Palestinian prisoners . There is still some ambiguity over the release of hostages as there is no clear guideline over status of the people held .
