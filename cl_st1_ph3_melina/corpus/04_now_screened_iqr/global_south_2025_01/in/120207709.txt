<h> A Gaza ceasefire deal went into effect , involving a phased Israeli withdrawal and the release of hostages by Hamas . The agreement includes significant humanitarian aid deliveries and involves mediator guarantees from Qatar , Egypt , and the United States . The deal outlines a six-week timeline with specific releases and negotiations for a permanent ceasefire and Gaza 's reconstruction . <p> AP <p> After 15 months of conflict that has killed tens of thousands of Palestinians and inflamed the Middle East , the ceasefire finally went into effect . <p> The start of the ceasefire was delayed for almost three hours after Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu asked Hamas to provide a list of the hostages who were due to be released during the day . An Israeli official said later that Israel had received the list . <p> Celebrations erupted across the war-ravaged territory and some Palestinians began returning to their homes despite the delay , which underscored the fragility of the agreement .
