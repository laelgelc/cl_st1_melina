<h> Thousands of displaced Palestinians return to Gaza <p> After 15 months of forced displacement , thousands of Palestinians are returning to northern Gaza following a complex hostage release negotiation . The agreement , mediated by Qatar , allows residents to go back to areas that have been largely destroyed during the conflict . Families are experiencing mixed emotions - relief at returning home combined with anxiety about the extent of damage .
