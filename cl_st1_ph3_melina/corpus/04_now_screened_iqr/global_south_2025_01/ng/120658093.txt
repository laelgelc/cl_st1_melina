She emerged from the rubble at the Jabalia refugee camp in Gaza , which has been repeatedly targeted by Israeli forces since October 2023 . <p> Later on Thursday , another Israeli civilian , Arbel Yehoud , was released near the former residence of Hamas ' ex-political leader Yahya Sinwar in the southern city of Khan Younis . <p> As part of the ceasefire arrangement , Israel is expected to release 110 Palestinian prisoners from its jails .
