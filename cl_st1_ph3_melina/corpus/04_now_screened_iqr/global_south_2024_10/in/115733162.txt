It followed that hiding misdeeds , like war crimes and other forms of mass violence , would now be almost impossible . This global awareness was expected to lead to mass mobilisation against such atrocities . <p> On February 15 , 2003 , all these expectations were realised when over 15 million people in 600 cities across the world held coordinated protests against the impending invasion of Iraq by the US and UK . In response to this , there have been passionate protests in many countries , against Israel 's war on the Palestinians . But in most societies , the protesters appear to be marginal . There has been no mass global upsurge to match the scale of the atrocities being inflicted in Gaza and Lebanon . <p> It is time to sift false hopes from the actual unfolding of reality . In this context , we first need to understand , empathically , the evolutionary limitations of human beings .
