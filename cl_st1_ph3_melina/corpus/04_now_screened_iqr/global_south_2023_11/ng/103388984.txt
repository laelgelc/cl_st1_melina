<p> More than 300,000 people took part in a pro-Palestinian march in London on Saturday , police said . It 's the latest and biggest so far in a series of weekend protests held in the British capital since the outbreak of the war between Israel and Hamas last month . <p> The protesters called for a cease-fire in the Gaza Strip . The march coincided with Remembrance Day , when the United Kingdom commemorates the end of World War I , prompting some UK lawmakers to call for the march to be stopped . <p> Police said the demonstration was peaceful , but far-right counterprotesters were involved in violent clashes with the police , leading to almost 100 individuals being arrested . <p> Skirmishes between police and counterprotesters Counterprotesters attacked police near the Cenotaph war memorial , close to the Houses of Parliament and in Westminster . <p> Police said later that they had arrested 82 counterprotesters in an effort to keep the peace , as far-right groups had tried to get close to the pro-Palestinian rally .
