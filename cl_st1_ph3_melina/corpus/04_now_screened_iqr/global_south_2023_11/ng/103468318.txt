<p> How has the Hamas October 7 terrorist attack and the subsequent military conflict impacted Israeli football ? The conflict has led to the suspension of Israeli domestic football until later this month , if all goes to plan from now on . International matches involving Israel 's national teams and clubs were postponed following the Hamas attack , but these have since resumed . Last Sunday , the men 's national team lost 1-0 to Kosovo in a EURO 2024 qualifier played in Pristina . Last Thursday , Maccabi Haifa lost 2-1 to Spanish club Villarreal in Larnaca , Cyprus in the Europa League .
