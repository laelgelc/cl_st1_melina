solely religious in nature . -- Bernama photo <p> KUALA LUMPUR ( Nov 21 ) : Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim today clarified that his participation in international conferences where he raised issues related to Palestine does not mean that domestic problems , especially those affecting minorities , are ignored . <p> In addition to accepting invitations from world leaders , Anwar said he also saw these conferences as an opportunity for him to address humanitarian issues , emphasising that the concerns are not solely religious in nature . <p> " No minorities in our country are mass-murdered , no churches and temples are destroyed en masse , so while there are those who disagree with our support for Palestine , I do n't think it 's reasonable , sensible , and rational to bring up this issue of hatred . <p> " ... this is not just about Islam ( and Muslims ) because Christian populations are also involved , even though most mosques are destroyed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ( so ) I hereby stress the need for us to look at this issue , " he said during the Minister 's Question Time at the Dewan Rakyat sitting today . <p> Anwar said this in reply to Aminolhuda Hassan ( PH-Sri Gading ) who wanted to know the outcome of the recently concluded 8th Extraordinary Islamic Summit of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) in Saudi Arabia regarding crimes and genocide committed by Israel against the Palestinian people . <p> At the summit hosted by Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman , Anwar also urged OIC member states to press for an immediate ceasefire , while humanitarian aid to the people of Gaza to be expedited . <p> At the same time , Anwar , who is also Finance Minister , said that he had also raised the issues of Palestine at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation ( Apec ) Economic Leaders ' Meeting ( AELM ) in San Francisco and called for the immediate cessation of genocide and atrocities in Gaza . <p> The Prime Minister said that during the Apec Economic Leaders ' Informal Dialogue @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ President Joe Biden , he also reiterated Malaysia 's stand not to allow any party or world power to dictate the country 's stance on the issue . <p> " Do n't let
