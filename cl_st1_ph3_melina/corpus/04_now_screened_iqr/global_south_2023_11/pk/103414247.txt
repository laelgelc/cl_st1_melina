<p> Hundreds of thousands of protesters have poured onto the streets across the world in support of Palestinians , condemning Israel 's ongoing aggression in the Gaza Strip and calling for an immediate ceasefire . <p> More than 11,000 Palestinians have lost their lives since Israel started its ruthless bombardment campaign on the besieged enclave over a month ago in response to Hamas ' surprise attack . <p> Israel has put Gaza -- home to 2.3 million people -- under siege amid ongoing ground battles with Hamas . In the last few days , more than 20,000 Palestinians have been forced to relocate to the south of the strip as Israeli forces have laid siege on major hospitals in the enclave . <p> Israeli attacks on
