<h> DFA : 26 Pinoys opt to remain in Gaza <p> TWENTY-six Filipinos have decided to stay in the Gaza Strip despite continued bombardment and ground operations by the Israeli forces in the enclave . <p> Of the 137 Filipinos in Gaza , the DFA said it has already extricated 111 , including their children . <p> DFA Undersecretary Eduardo de Vega said the 26 remaining Filipinos in Gaza have already been cleared by the Israel and Egyptian governments to leave the enclave , but they chose to stay despite the threat to their lives . <p> One of them is a nun who does not want to leave Gaza City where intense fighting between Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) and the Hamas militants continues . <p> " She said it is her mission in life . She asked us to pray for her and the safety of 800 people taking refuge in the church in Gaza City , " Philippine Ambassador to Amman Wilfredo Santos told the Business Mirror . <p> Other Filipinos refused to leave their Palestinian spouses who were not cleared to leave Gaza by either the Israel or Egyptian security agencies . <p> But @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Bojer Capati , is ready to " receive and assist more Filipinos " in case they change their mind and decide to flee Gaza . <p> Also , there are two Filipino workers who are already being " assumed " to be among the 200 hostages taken to Gaza by Hamas operatives during the October 7 surprise attacks . <p> Filipinos in Gaza were among the 2.5 million Palestinians and foreigners trapped in the enclave after Israel closed the borders and launched a massive military assault against Hamas . <p> The DFA called for mandatory evacuation as the security situation deteriorated but it took more than a month before Israel and Egypt agreed to allow foreigners , including Filipinos and their Palestinian spouses to leave Gaza . <p> The evacuation of Filipinos were conducted in five batches : <p> 1st batch : 2 Filipino medical workers from Doctors Without Borders <p> 2nd batch : 34 Filipinos who arrived in Manila Friday last week <p> 3rd batch : 41 Filipino who arrived in Manila Sunday night <p> The fourth batch of evacuees is composed of 14 Filipino nationals , including three @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ joined them . <p> They flew from Cairo Monday and are expected to arrive in Manila on Tuesday . <p> The fifth batch is composed of 13 Filipinos and 6 Palestinian spouses who crossed the Rafah border Sunday . <p> Two of the evacuees were a father and son based in Oman who were taking a vacation in Gaza with the Palestinian wife when the war started .
