The first part of this reflection is inspired by its evocative title Eyeless in Gaza , which seems so telling in our times . The second part of this essay , which is to follow , will consider another of Huxley 's works , his later novel , Devils in Loudun ( 1952 ) . <p> Eyeless in Gaza is a ponderous tome that few remember , and no one seems to read these days . Published in 1936 , it is Huxley 's longest , over 600 pages long , and , some would say , his most ambitious novel .
