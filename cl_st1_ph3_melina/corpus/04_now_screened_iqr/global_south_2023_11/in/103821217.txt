<p> Tammy Ben Haim , Consul General of Israel to South India , says Hamas does n't follow any norms or guidelines . <p> CHENNAI <p> On 21 November , the Israeli government approved a four-day truce in the ongoing conflict with Hamas . The humanitarian truce will see the release of 50 hostages ( women and children ) held captive by Hamas . Around 240 people were taken hostage by Hamas in their brutal attack on Israel on 7 October and only four have been released by them so far . In this exclusive conversation with The Sunday Guardian , Tammy Ben Haim , Consul General of Israel to South India , talks about the ceasefire , its significance and steps being taken to secure the release of the remaining hostages from Hamas .
