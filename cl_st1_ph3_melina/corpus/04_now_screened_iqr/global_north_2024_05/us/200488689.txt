Up the coast in Santa Cruz , unionized academic workers walked off the job in the first of what could become a slate of strikes across the UC campuses , aimed at officials over their handling of Gaza war protests . And the campus strife has the attention of federal lawmakers in Washington , D.C. <p> Here 's a quick primer on the various controversies swirling across the UC system . <p> The UCLA encampment was deemed an unlawful assembly by school officials on April 30 .
