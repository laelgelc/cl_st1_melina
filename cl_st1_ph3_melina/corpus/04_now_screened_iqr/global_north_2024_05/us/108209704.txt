We stand on the throats of terrorists , but we also stand shoulder to shoulder with our allies . <p> That 's why I 'm introducing legislation to restore former President Donald Trump 's sanctions on the International Criminal Court . <p> In 2020 , Trump issued an executive order sanctioning nearly 14,000 ICC employees , citing the agency 's corruption and bias against the U.S. government .
