<p> OSLO - Norway will recognise an independent Palestinian state in the hope that this will help to bring peace with Israel , Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Stoere said on Wednesday . <p> Ireland and Spain will also announce the recognition of a Palestinian state , sources said on Wednesday . <p> European Union members Slovenia and Malta have also indicated in recent weeks that they plan to make the recognition , arguing a two-state solution is essential for lasting peace in the region . <p> " In the middle of a war , with tens of thousands of dead and injured , we must keep alive the only thing that can provide a safe home for both Israelis and Palestinians : two states that can live in peace with each other , " Stoere told a press conference . <p> Before the announcement , some 143 out of 193 member-states of the United Nations recognised a Palestinian state . <p> European countries have approached the issue differently .
