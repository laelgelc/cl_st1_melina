<h> Desmond Cole on resilience and solidarity with Palestine <p> On April 17 , 2024 a pro-Palestine protest encampment was built at Columbia University where students called on their school to disclose and divest their investments in companies linked to Israel and its war on Gaza . This inspired a movement in universities across North America -- and the globe -- for students to create their own on-campus encampments . <p> Today on rabble radio , freelance reporter Stephen Wentzell sits down with journalist and activist Desmond Cole to outline the misconceptions some had about the student encampments and what responsible reporting for Palestine looks like . <p> Desmond Cole is a journalist , radio host , and activist . His debut book , The Skin We 're In , won the Toronto Book Award and was a finalist for the Forest of Reading Evergreen Award and the Rakuten Kobo Emerging Writer Prize . It was also named a best book of 2020 by The Globe and Mail , NOW Magazine , CBC , Quill &amp; Quire , and Indigo .
