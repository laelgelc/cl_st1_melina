<p> EXCLUSIVE -- The Anti-Defamation League is requesting the IRS launch investigations into a pair of anti-Israel nonprofit groups that it argued in a letter to the agency appear to be " abusing their tax-exempt status . " <p> In a Monday letter obtained by the Washington Examiner , the ADL made the case to the IRS why WESPAC Foundation and Alliance for Global Justice may be violating federal law . The two organizations are connected to anti-Israel protests after the Hamas-led Oct. 7 attack on Israel last year and , through an arrangement known as fiscal sponsorship , lend their tax-exempt status as charities to projects that the ADL warned are " engaging in activities that are contrary to public policy and raise serious concerns that we believe warrant further investigation by the IRS .
