<p> National Park Service staffers began power-washing the vandalized Christopher Columbus statue and fountain near Union Station on Thursday , launching what they expect to be a multiday effort to clear the graffiti left by demonstrators protesting Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's speech to Congress . <p> The area outside Union Station was the site of some of the most tense confrontations Wednesday between police and demonstrators , thousands of whom marched and chanted in various areas of D.C. The vandalism drew condemnations from top U.S. officials , including Vice President Harris . <p> In Columbus Circle , demonstrators took down American flags and set one ablaze , along with an effigy of Netanyahu . On the Christopher Columbus fountain , " Hamas is coming " had been spray-painted with an inverted triangle above it .
