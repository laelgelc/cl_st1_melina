<h> Israel kills five medical staff as another baby freezes to death in Gaza <p> Doctor , lab technician and two maintenance workers among those killed as a fourth baby in three days dies of cold during Israel 's genocide . <p> Palestinians walk among debris after an Israeli attack on the courtyard of Kamal Adwan Hospital and its surrounding buildings in Beit Lahiya , Gaza , on December 25 , 2024 Khalil Ramzi Alkahlut/Anadolu <p> Published On 26 Dec 202426 Dec 2024 <p> Israel 's genocide has killed five staff members at one of northern Gaza 's last functioning hospitals , the facility 's director says , as yet another baby freezes to death in the besieged and bombed Palestinian enclave . <p> Hussam Abu Safia , head of Kamal Adwan Hospital in Beit Lahiya , on Thursday said the " martyrs included a doctor . " <p> Al Jazeera 's Hind Khoudary , reporting from central Gaza , said a laboratory technician and two maintenance workers were among those killed in the attack . <p> " We know that the hospital is one of the only medical facilities still operating , and it is working with the bare minimum human resources and a lack of medical supplies , " she said . <p> " Israeli forces have been attacking the surroundings of Kamal Adwan Hospital , sending quadcopters inside the hospital and shooting Palestinians . " <p> The hospital has been rendered nonoperational after weeks of near-daily attacks . Israeli forces previously killed the director of the hospital 's ICU , Dr Ahmed al-Kahlout , and wounded dozens of medical staff in attacks on and near the facility . <p> Khoudary said Palestinian rescue workers have been unable to reach the bodies of those killed at the hospital . " People are unable to bury those Palestinians who are being killed every single day by the Israeli forces in northern Gaza , " she added . <p> Advertisement <p> Israel launched a large-scale ground offensive in northern Gaza on October 5 , saying it aimed to prevent the Palestinian group Hamas from regrouping . <p> Since then , no sufficient humanitarian aid , including food , medicine and fuel , has been allowed into the area , leaving the remaining population on the verge of famine . <p> The World Health Organization has described conditions at Kamal Adwan Hospital as " appalling " and said it was operating at a " minimum " level . <h> Another baby freezes to death <p> Meanwhile , a fourth infant has died due to extreme cold within 72 hours in Gaza , the Wafa news agency reported on Thursday . <p> Medical sources said the baby died due to a drop in temperature as the humanitarian conditions across the enclave are dire . <p> " The tents do not protect from the cold , and it gets very cold at night with no way to keep warm , " said Dr Ahmed al-Farra , the chief paediatric doctor at Nasser Hospital in southern Gaza 's Khan Younis area . <p> In more than a year of Israeli attacks and lack of aid deliveries , many families in Gaza have been left without adequate shelter and resources to cope with the changing weather . <p> Local health officials told Wafa a lack of food among mothers was contributing to a rise in health issues among children , further straining medical facilities and emergency services . <p> Israel 's bombardment and ground invasion of Gaza has killed more than 45,300 Palestinians , more than half of them women and children . The offensive has also caused widespread destruction and displaced about 90 percent of Gaza 's 2.3 million residents , often multiple times . <p> Advertisement <p> Hundreds of thousands of people are packed into tent camps along the coast as the cold , wet winter sets in . Aid groups have struggled to deliver food and supplies and said there are shortages of blankets , warm clothing and firewood .
