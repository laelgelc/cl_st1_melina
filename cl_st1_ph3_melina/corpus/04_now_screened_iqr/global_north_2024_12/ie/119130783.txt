<h> Israel reportedly struggles to counter Houthi attacks <p> Israeli emergency services work at the scene of a missile strike that , according to Israel 's military , was launched from Yemen and landed in Jaffa , south of capital Tel Aviv , Israel , December 21 , 2024. / Photo : Reuters <p> Israeli military forces have faced significant challenges in countering Houthi missile and drone attacks since the beginning of the war on Gaza in October 2023 , according to Israeli newspaper Maariv . <p> The latest incident occurred earlier Saturday when a ballistic missile launched by the Houthis struck the Israeli capital Tel Aviv , injuring 20 people , according to local sources . The Houthi group claimed responsibility for the strike . <p> Maariv 's report said that the Houthis have launched over 200 ballistic missiles and 170 drones at Israeli targets , severely testing Israel 's defence capabilities . <p> It added that Israel 's defence mechanisms , particularly the Iron Dome and other air defence systems , have struggled to cope with the evolving threat posed by the Houthis . <p> Although many missiles and drones have been intercepted by Israeli and American forces , the sheer volume and sophistication of the attacks have continued to strain Israel 's defences , it noted . <p> The report emphasised that the Israeli military is facing significant difficulties in both defending against and responding to the attacks . <p> It acknowledged that since the war 's beginning , the Houthis have inflicted significant damage on the regional economy , particularly Israel 's . <p> The report criticised Israel 's military response and intelligence preparation , claiming that the country was unprepared for the threat posed by the Houthis . <p> " Israel was not ready - either politically or in terms of intelligence - to deal with the threat coming from Yemen , " the report said . <p> The Israeli military and intelligence agencies only began reacting after the Houthis ' missile and drone strikes intensified , according to the report . <p> Maariv stressed that efforts by the Israeli military , including air strikes on Houthi positions in Yemen , were seen as little more than public relations gestures . <p> Loading ... <p> Air defence systems failure <p> The report also highlighted the increasing involvement of Iran in supporting the Houthi forces . <p> " Iran has invested more in the Houthis in recent weeks following the collapse of the Shia axis , making the Houthi movement a leader of this axis , " the report noted . <p> The failures of Israel 's air defence systems were underscored , with Maariv reporting that the " Arrow " missile defence system , Israel 's main line of defence against ballistic missiles , had failed four times in a row to intercept missiles , including three launched from Yemen and one from Lebanon . <p> The Houthis , in solidarity with Gaza , which has been facing an Israeli genocidal war since October 7 , 2023 , have targeted Israeli cargo ships or those associated with Tel Aviv in the Red Sea with missiles and drones , expressing a determination to continue operations until the end of the onslaught on the enclave .
