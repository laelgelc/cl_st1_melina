<p> Israeli troops stormed one of the last hospitals operating in the northernmost part of Gaza on Friday , forcing many of the staff and patients out of the facility , the territory 's health ministry said . <p> The Kamal Adwan Hospital has been hit multiple times over the past three months by Israeli troops waging an offensive against Hamas fighters in surrounding neighbourhoods , according to staff . <p> Advertisement <p> The ministry said a strike on the hospital a day earlier killed five medical staff . <p> Israel 's military said it was conducting operations against Hamas infrastructure and fighters in the area of the hospital , without providing details . <p> It repeated claims that Hamas fighters were operating inside Kamal Adwan , though it provided no evidence . <p> Advertisement <p> Hospital officials have denied the accusations . <p> The Health Ministry said troops forced medical personnel and patients to assemble in the hospital yard and remove their clothes amid the winter temperatures . <p> Advertisement <p> They were led out of the hospital , some to an unknown location , while some patients were sent to the nearby Indonesian hospital , which was knocked out of operation after an Israel raid earlier this week . <p> The ministry said troops set fires in several parts of Kamal Adwan , including the hospital 's lab and surgery department . It said 25 patients and 60 health workers remained in the hospital out of 75 patients and 180 staff who had been there . <p> The ministry 's account could not be independently confirmed , and attempts to reach hospital staff were unsuccessful . <p> Israel 's military said there was only a small fire in an empty hospital building , and was unaware that the blaze was caused by Israeli fire . <p> Advertisement <p> " Fire is ablaze everywhere in the hospital , " an unidentified member of the staff said in an audio message from the hospital posted on the social media accounts of its director Hossam Abu Safiya . <p> The worker said some evacuated patients had been unhooked from oxygen . " There are currently patients who could die at any moment , " she said . <p> In raids , Israeli troops frequently carry out mass detentions , stripping men down to their underwear for questioning in what the military says is a security measure as they search for Hamas fighters . <p> Since October , Israel 's offensive has virtually sealed off the north Gaza areas of Jabaliya , Beit Hanoun and Beit Lahiya and levelled large parts of the districts . <p> The area has been cut off from food and other aid for months , raising fears of famine . The UN says Israeli troops had only allowed four humanitarian deliveries to the area from December 1-23 . <p> The Israeli rights groups Physicians for Human Rights-Israel earlier this week petitioned Israel 's High Court of Justice seeking a halt to military attacks on Kamal Adwan . <p> It warned that forcibly evacuating the hospital would " abandon thousands of residents in northern Gaza . " <p> Before the latest deaths on Thursday , the group documented five other staff members killed by Israeli fire since October . <p> Israel 's nearly 15-month-old campaign of bombardment and offensives in Gaza have devastated the territory 's health sector . A year ago , it carried out a wave of raids on hospitals in northern Gaza , including Kamal Adwan , Indonesian and nearby al-Awda Hospital , saying they served bases for Hamas , though it presented little evidence . <p> Israel 's campaign has killed more than 45,400 Palestinians , more than half of them women and children , and wounded more than 108,000 others , according to the Health Ministry . <p> Its count does not distinguish between civilians and combatants . More than 90% of Gaza 's 2.3 million Palestinians have been driven from their homes , most of them now sheltering in sprawling , squalid tent camps in south and central Gaza . <p> Israel launched its campaign vowing to destroy Hamas after the group 's October 7 , 2023 , attack on southern Israel in which militants killed around 1,200 people and abducted some 250 others . <p> Around 100 Israelis remain captive in Gaza , around a third of whom are believed to be dead . <p> A Palestinian from the West Bank stabbed to death an 83-year-old woman in Israel on Friday , police said . <p> The attack took place in the town of Herzliya on the Mediterranean coast outside Tel Aviv .
