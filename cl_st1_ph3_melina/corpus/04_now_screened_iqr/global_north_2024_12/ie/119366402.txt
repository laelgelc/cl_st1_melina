<p> The World Health Organization said on Saturday that the Kamal Adwan hospital was " now empty " following an Israeli military raid which put north Gaza 's last major health facility out of service . <p> The WHO said it was " appalled " by Friday 's raid , saying " hospitals have once again become battlegrounds " . <p> " The systematic dismantling of the health system and a siege for over 80 days on north Gaza puts the lives of the 75,000 Palestinians remaining in the area at risk , " the UN health agency said in a statement . <p> Israel 's military said on Saturday it had ended its raid on " a Hamas command centre " in the hospital and had detained its director , accusing him of being an operative for the Palestinian resistance group of Hamas . <p> Israeli army began broader assaults in northern Gaza in October . <p> " Kamal Adwan is now empty , " the WHO said . <p> The remaining 15 critical patients , 50 caregivers and 20 health workers were transferred Friday to the Indonesian Hospital , which it described as " destroyed and non-functional " . <p> " The movement and treatment of these critical patients under such conditions pose grave risks to their survival . <p> " WHO is deeply concerned for their wellbeing , as well as for the Kamal Adwan Hospital director . " <p> The WHO said initial reports indicated that some areas of the hospital were burnt and severely damaged during the raid . <p> Those included the laboratory , surgical unit , engineering and maintenance department , operations theatre and the medical store . <p> It said that earlier Friday , 12 patients had reportedly been forced to evacuate to the Indonesian Hospital . <p> " Additionally , some people were reportedly stripped and forced to walk toward southern Gaza . <p> " An urgent WHO mission to Indonesian Hospital is being planned for tomorrow to assess the situation at the facility , provide basic medical supplies , food and water , and safely move critical patients to Gaza City for continued care . " <p> The WHO said the raid on Kamal Adwan had come following a period of escalating restrictions and repeated attacks . <p> It said only 10 out of 21 WHO missions to the hospital had been partially facilitated between early October and December , but the deployment of international emergency medical teams had been " repeatedly denied . " <p> The UN health agency has verified at least 50 attacks on health at or near the hospital since early October this year . <p> " With Kamal Adwan and Indonesian hospitals entirely out of service , and Al-Awda Hospital barely able to function , and severely damaged due to recent air strikes , the healthcare lifeline for those in north Gaza is reaching a breaking point , " it said . <p> " WHO and partners ' efforts to sustain the hospitals ' operations have been undone . <p> " WHO calls for urgently ensuring that hospitals in north Gaza can be supported to become functional again . " <p> The organisation said health facilities , workers and patients " must be actively protected and never be attacked , nor used for military purposes " , but such calls " remain unheard " .
