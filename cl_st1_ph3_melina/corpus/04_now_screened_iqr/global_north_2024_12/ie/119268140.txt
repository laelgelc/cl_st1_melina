<p> Dr Fadel Naim , an orthopaedic surgeon , says the healthcare system in the enclave is on the verge of collapse , with hospitals unable to cope with the mounting numbers of Palestinian casualties in Israel 's war on Gaza . <p> AA Archive <p> Relatives of the Palestinians who lost their lives in Israeli attacks arrive at the al-Ahli Arab Hospital for funeral preparations in Gaza City , Gaza on December 13 , 2024. / Photo : AA Archive <p> Israel is systematically destroying the healthcare system in Gaza amid its genocidal war on the enclave , a Palestinian surgeon said . <p> Dr Fadel Naim , an orthopaedic surgeon at al-Ahli Arab Hospital in Gaza City , said on Tuesday that the healthcare system in the enclave is on the verge of collapse with hospitals unable to cope with the mounting numbers of casualties . <p> " Since the beginning of the ( Israeli ) aggression , the occupation has systematically destroyed every aspect of life in Gaza , with healthcare being among the most affected sectors , " Naim said in a video published on his X account . <p> " Among the most affected sectors has been the healthcare system , which has been intentionally targeted at all levels from infrastructure , systems , equipment to medical personnel . " <p> He said that a deadly Israeli strike targeted al-Ahli Arab Hospital in October 2023 , leaving over 500 people dead and causing partial damage to the hospital . <p> " The occupation systematically targeted all hospitals and medical facilities in Gaza , including major hospitals such as al-Shifa , Nasser , al-Quds , Kamal Adwan , al-Rantisi , al-Awda and smaller healthcare centres , " he continued . <p> URGENT APPEAL : Israel is systematically destroying Gaza 's healthcare system , This is a humanitarian catastrophe unfolding before the world 's eyes . <p> We call on the international community to act immediately &amp; stop this massacre . The price of inaction is paid by innocent lives . **26;210;TOOLONG <p> The Israeli attacks have forced almost all hospitals across Gaza out of service , leaving the al-Ahli Arab Hospital the only operational medical facility in Gaza City . <p> " After the destruction of all hospitals in Gaza City and the northern governorate , the hospital has become the only facility offering surgical , internal medicine , and other health services , " Naim explained . <p> " The normal capacity of the hospital is 30 to 50 beds , but today we have over 150 patients , and at times this number has reached 300 , " he said . <p> " We have been forced to utilise every space in the hospital , whether the church or the library , converting them into wards for the injured . " <p> The surgeon highlighted the dire shortage of essential resources at the hospital amid a crippling Israeli blockade on Gaza . <p> " Daily , we face the risk of diesel running out which compels us to halt essential services such as washing surgical linens , sterilizing surgical equipment or even operating the only CT scanner in northern Gaza at the al-Ahli Arab Hospital . " <p> The hospital is also struggling to care for vulnerable patients . <p> " A particularly pressing issue is the large number of patients with chronic illnesses , including the elderly , children , those with diabetes , hypertension , cancer , kidney failure and complex pediatric conditions , " Naim said . <p> " These patients are often overlooked due to the hospital overcrowding and the lack of capabilities and specialised medical personnel . " <p> Call for urgent action <p> The Palestinian doctor appealed for urgent action to halt Israeli attacks on hospitals in Gaza . <p> " There is an urgent need to exert maximum pressure on the occupation to ensure the protection of medical facilities and personnel , " he said . <p> " We also call for establishing specialised field hospitals and providing the necessary medical teams to operate these hospitals . " <p> The situation at al-Ahli Arab Hospital is emblematic of the broader healthcare crisis in Gaza , where medical facilities are struggling to cope with the massive destruction and lack of resources amidst Israeli attacks . <p> The Israeli army has systematically targeted public facilities , residential buildings , and infrastructure over the course of its deadly war on Gaza , exacerbating the already bad living conditions for over two million Palestinians in the enclave . <p> Israel has continued a genocidal war on Gaza that has killed more than 45,300 people , most of them women and children , since an attack by the Palestinian resistance group Hamas on October 7 , 2023 . <p> Last month , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza . <p> Israel is also facing a genocide case at the International Court of Justice for its actions in Gaza .
