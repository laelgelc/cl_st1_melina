<h> On Christmas , a moment of brief cheer for children caught in Gaza war <p> Team of amputee cyclists in the besieged enclave plays Santa to children facing a genocidal war that has killed and maimed thousands of minors . <p> Others <p> Children in Gaza pose for a photo at a pizza party on Christmas eve . Photo : Gaza Sunbirds <p> A pizza-making party on Christmas Eve brought joy -- and a brief respite from war -- to children in southern Gaza , thanks to a cycling team of amputee athletes who played the collective Santa Claus in the festive season . <p> The celebration featured a Christmas tree , festive decorations and a newly-constructed food truck , taking the children 's minds off Israel 's year-long war that has caused a widespread food shortage across Palestine 's war-torn Gaza . <p> " People in Gaza are sick of eating tinned foods and plain bread for those lucky enough to find it . It 's been nearly a year of genocide and common comforts are rare to come by , " says Karim Ali , a cofounder of Gaza Sunbirds , the non-governmental organisation that prepared and distributed pizzas among the youth of Al-Zawayde in the southern part of Gaza . <p> Being the birthplace of Jesus and home to generations of Christians , Palestine has a strong connection to Christmas . <p> The town of Bethlehem in the occupied West Bank , the birthplace of Jesus , has cancelled Christmas celebrations for the second year in a row over the war on Gaza , which has killed more than 45,000 people , mostly women and children , since October 2023 . <p> According to UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres , malnutrition is rampant in Gaza while the war-torn territory faces imminent famine amid a collapse of the health system . <p> Israel , which early in the conflict imposed a complete siege for a period on the territory , has blamed aid issues on what it says is the inability of relief organisations to handle and distribute large quantities of aid . <p> This is despite the fact that international aid organisations have repeatedly raised alarm over the deteriorating conditions in Gaza , warning that civilians are on the brink of famine . <p> " Like sports , the feeling of getting a pizza is universal . By teaching kids how to twist the dough and getting the community involved , we make it an opportunity for some much-deserved distraction , " Ali of the amputee athletes ' NGO noted . <p> Forced to give up training for this year 's Paralympics due to the war , the team of amputee athletes has been delivering food parcels , hot meals , nappies and sanitary products across Gaza since October 2023 . Its members have risked their lives to distribute $330,000 of emergency supplies and even built a 25-tent displacement camp . <p> There have now been 10 events where over 2,500 children have been fed and entertained whilst learning to make 1,100 pizzas since the start of September . The parties have previously taken place in southern Gaza in collaboration with Mohammed al Amareen , Gaza 's renowned pizza chef . <p> Australian human rights lawyer Chris Sidoti says the number of children killed in Gaza by Israel is the highest in any conflict in this century . <p> More than 12,000 students in Gaza have been killed and over 20,000 wounded as a result of Israeli attacks , according to the Palestinian Education Ministry . Israel has also killed at least 616 teachers and damaged 90 percent of school buildings in Gaza . <p> Moreover , about 788,000 students have been deprived of access to schools and universities since October 2023.
