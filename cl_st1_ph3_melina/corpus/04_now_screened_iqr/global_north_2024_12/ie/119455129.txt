<p> Emily 's release in November came during a temporary cessation of hostilities as part of an agreement between Israel and Hamas . More than 100 Israeli and foreign national hostages were released at that time . <p> She was released after spending 50 days , including her ninth birthday , as a hostage in Gaza . <p> Mr Hand later told Reuters that Ireland has been instrumental in Emily 's release but strongly criticised recognition of Palestine . <p> " I 've been a very proud Irishman all my life . I advertise the fact , I tell people yes I 'm Irish , " he said in June of this year . <p> " I 'm not so proud anymore . I 'm actually embarrassed , ashamed to call myself Irish now . After what 's happened to Emily , and they Irish authorities were integral in the whole process of it ... Okay , she 's only an individual but she is an Irish citizen . <p> " Knowing everything Hamas has done , I 'm sure they 've seen the videos of their GoPros , they 've seen all the evidence . " <p> He said recognising a Palestinian state served as a reward for Hamas . <p> " Okay , here is your reward then for absolute violence . Here 's your reward , we recognise you as a state . Nice one , " he told Reuters . <p> Ireland recognised the state of Palestine alongside Spain and Norway from May 28 . Taoiseach Simon Harris said it was an act " of powerful , political and symbolic value " . <p> " Ireland 's dream is that the Israeli and Palestinian children of May 28th 2024 will grow up to be neighbours at peace , " Mr Harris said at the time of the announcement . <p> " Both the Palestinian and Israeli people are inherently kind and decent . " <p> " We 're praying for it a peaceful resolution to happen and for all of this to end . All they Hamas have to do is hand over the hostages and the war is over . " <p> It was revealed at the time that since they had been reunited , Mr Hand and Emily have been moving from one place of temporary accommodation to another with just a few belongings and their family dog Johnsie . <p> He said he is trying to provide a sense of normality for his daughter and she is making her way towards a full recovery despite one or two triggers . <p> Earlier this month , Israel announced it is going to close its embassy in Dublin as they claim that the Irish government has " extreme anti-Israel policies " and crossed " all red lines " towards Israel . <p> However , describing the closure as a " setback " for both Ireland and Israel , Archbishop Eamon Martin said he disagreed with the " anti-semitic " labelling .
