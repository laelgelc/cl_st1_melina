<h> Israeli minister sees chance for Gaza hostage release deal <h> Share this article <p> Relatives of victims of the October 7 attacks visit an installation bearing the photos of their loved ones who were killed or kidnapped during the attacks , at the site of the Nova festival , in Re'im , southern Israel . Photo / AFP <p> Relatives of victims of the October 7 attacks visit an installation bearing the photos of their loved ones who were killed or kidnapped during the attacks , at the site of the Nova festival , in Re'im , southern Israel . Photo / AFP <p> Israeli Defence Minister Israel Katz has told his US counterpart Lloyd Austin there is " currently a chance " for a deal to release the remaining hostages held in Gaza for more than 14 months . <p> " There is currently a chance for a new deal , " Katz told Austin in a phone call , according to a statement from the office of the US Secretary of Defence . <p> " We are hoping for the release of all the hostages , including US citizens , " he said . <p> The US , with Egypt and Qatar , has been unsuccessfully attempting to mediate a ceasefire and hostage release deal between Israel and the Palestinian group Hamas for more than a year . <p> Palestinian militants took 251 people hostage during Hamas ' surprise attack on October 7 , 2023 . A total of 96 remain in Gaza , including 34 the Israeli military has confirmed are dead . <p> " If Israel agrees to the Egyptian proposal , I think the exchange deal will be ready for implementation , " the source said . <p> Another upbeat assessment came from Qatar , which said the election of Donald Trump as the next US President had created new " momentum " for negotiations . <p> At the same time , a source close to the Hamas delegation said that Turkey , as well as Egypt and Qatar , had been " making commendable efforts to stop the war " , and a new round of talks could begin soon . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu also hinted at potential progress , telling the families of hostages that Israel 's military successes against Hezbollah and Hamas would facilitate negotiations for their release . <p> Protesters , including relatives of the hostages , have repeatedly called for a deal to free the captives and accuse Netanyahu of prolonging the war for political purposes . <p> The unprecedented October 7 attack resulted in the deaths of 1208 people , mostly civilians , according to an AFP tally based on official figures . <p> Israel 's retaliatory military campaign in Gaza has killed at least 44,805 people , a majority of civilians , according to figures from the Hamas-run territory 's health ministry which the UN considers reliable . <p> Seven individuals with US citizenship remain in Gaza , with four confirmed dead . Last week , the Israeli military notified the family of US-Israeli soldier Omer Neutra that he was killed on October 7 and his body held in Gaza .
