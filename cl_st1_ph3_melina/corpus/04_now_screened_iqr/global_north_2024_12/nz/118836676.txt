Image : Al Jazeera screenshot APR <p> The global media freedom watchdog Reporters Without Borders ( RSF ) has revealed an " alarming intensification of attacks on journalists " in its 2024 annual roundup -- especially in conflict zones such as Gaza . <p> Gaza stands out as the " most dangerous " region in the world , with the highest number of journalists murdered in connection with their work in the past five years . <p> Since October 2023 , the Israeli military have killed more than 145 journalists , including at least 35 whose deaths were linked to their journalism , reports RSF . <p> Also 550 journalists are currently imprisoned worldwide , a 7 percent increase from last year . <p> " This violence -- often perpetrated by governments and armed groups with total impunity -- needs an immediate response , " says the report . <p> " RSF calls for urgent action to protect journalists and journalism . " <p> Asia second most dangerous Asia is the second most dangerous region for journalists due to the large number of journalists killed in Pakistan ( seven ) and the protests that rocked Bangladesh ( five ) , says the report . <p> " Journalists do not die , they are killed ; they are not in prison , regimes lock them up ; they do not disappear , they are kidnapped , " said RSF director-general Thibaut Bruttin . <p> " These crimes -- often orchestrated by governments and armed groups with total impunity -- violate international law and too often go unpunished . <p> " We need to get things moving , to remind ourselves as citizens that journalists are dying for us , to keep us informed . We must continue to count , name , condemn , investigate , and ensure that justice is served . <p> " Fatalism should never win .
