<p> Human Rights Watch ( HRW ) has accused Israel of " acts of genocide " against Palestinians in Gaza by depriving them of adequate water supplies . <p> The group , in an extensive report , found that between October 2023 and September 2024 , Israeli authorities deprived Palestinians of what the World Health Organization ( WHO ) says is the minimum quantity of water required for survival in prolonged emergency situations . <p> This has contributed to thousands of deaths and the spread of numerous diseases , the report found . <p> The Biden administration said today that it disagreed with the report 's findings but is " continuing to press " Israel on the issue , according to a State Department spokesperson . <p> " Specifically when it comes to a determination of something like genocide , the legal standard is just incredibly high , and so the finding in this scenario we just disagree with , " State Department Deputy Spokesperson Vedant Patel said at a press briefing . <p> Patel added that the US has brought up water access as part of conversations with Israel . <p> " We need to be doing everything we can to ensure that water access is strong , and that everybody who needs water in Gaza is able to get it , " he said . <p> According to WHO , a person needs between 50 and 100 litres of water per day to ensure their " basic needs are met " . <p> In protracted emergency situations , the minimum amount of water can drop to 15 to 20 litres per day for drinking and washing . <p> For the over two million Palestinians living in the Gaza Strip , even this is out of reach , HRW found . <p> Most or all of the water Palestinians in Gaza have access to is not safe to drink . <p> HRW says Israel 's actions amount to acts of genocide under the Genocide Convention and the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) . <p> A young girl carries jerry cans in Khan Yunis on December 4 . We do n't have any information that says that there 's a water problem in Gaza . " <p> Pressed on that claim , he reiterated : " There 's no water problems in Gaza " . <h> ' Brazen breach of international law ' <p> Israel has previously faced accusations of using water as a weapon of war in Gaza . <p> Pedro Arrojo-Agudo , the UN special rapporteur on the human rights to safe drinking water and sanitation , said in November 2023 that " every hour that passes with Israel preventing the provision of safe drinking water in the Gaza strip , in brazen breach of international law , puts Gazans at risk of dying of thirst and diseases related to the lack of safe drinking water " . <p> The crisis has fueled diseases in Gaza and caused deaths estimated in the thousands , HRW said . <p> Palestinians collect clean water after international organisations restored uninterrupted electricity in Deir al-Balah , Gaza , for the first time since the beginning of the war . ( Ashraf Amra/Anadolu/Getty Images via CNN Newsource ) <p> The scale of the devastation caused by a lack of water may likely never be fully understood , the organisation warned , due to the decimation of Gaza 's health care system including disease tracking . <p> Polio , a highly contagious viral disease often caused by insufficient access to safe water and sanitation , is one example of this . <p> WHO testing first discovered the polio virus in samples of sewage taken from overcrowded tents of people displaced by Israeli airstrikes in Gaza in July . <p> On August 16 , the Palestinian Ministry of Health confirmed a case of polio in a 10-month-old child - the first case in Gaza in 25 years . <p> Bacterial infections like diarrhea have become commonplace in the enclave due to the consumption of contaminated water . <p> Describing the impact of being forced to drink from an unclean well , one man told HRW : " I was getting sick , my kids were vomiting and had diarrhea , and I had diarrhea ... This was from the moment we started drinking the ( dirty ) water . " <p> The HRW report comes as an Israel-Hamas ceasefire deal remains elusive , although negotiations appear to have taken a more positive turn in recent days . <p> In an official statement on Tuesday , Hamas said reaching an agreement was " possible " . <p> A Hamas source also added that talks were " positive and optimistic " . <p> However , the militant group cautioned that hurdles remain . <p> Both Israeli and Hamas teams are in the Qatari capital for indirect negotiations .
