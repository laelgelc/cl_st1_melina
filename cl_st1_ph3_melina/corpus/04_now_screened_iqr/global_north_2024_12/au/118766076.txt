Source : AAP / Dean Lewis <h> Key Points <p> Australia 's antisemitism envoy wants restrictions placed on when and where pro-Paletsinian protests can take place . <p> One Palestinian advocate said Jillian Segal was trying to " silence critics of the Israeli government " . <p> Prime Minister Anthony Albanese has said he would support a ban on protests being held outside places of worship . <p> Australia 's antisemitism envoy has called for pro-Palestinian protests to be banned from city centres , saying they have become " intimidatory " to the Jewish community . <p> Rallies in solidarity with people in Gaza have been held weekly in Melbourne and Sydney since the Hamas-Israel war began on 7 October , 2023 . <p> The government 's special envoy to combat antisemitism , Jillian Segal , said while she understood protesters ' concerns over the war and their right to demonstrate , restrictions should be placed on when and where such rallies are allowed to take place . <p> " The cities should not be utilised for that , " Segal told SBS News . <p> " There should be places designated away from where the Jewish community might venture , where people can demonstrate . <p> " That is not to in any way attack the right of people to express their views , but at some point , holding protest every single week becomes intimidatory in the city . " <p> Segal claimed the regularity of the protests had resulted in them moving from " an expression of one 's democratic right to express one 's view " to " something more sinister " . <p> " It 's not only a view about attacking the government or better conditions for Palestinians , but it 's morphed into attacking the Jewish community , " she said . <p> " The flags that are there represent flags from a terrorist organisation , from time to time , or what is cried out has morphed into anti-Jewish sentiments , and that is attacking part of our own community . " <p> NSW Council for Civil Liberties president Timothy Roberts cautioned against " kneejerk reactions " , telling The Guardian that those remarks were ill-considered . <p> Albanese also said he supported " people 's right to demonstrate peacefully and go to a park " .
