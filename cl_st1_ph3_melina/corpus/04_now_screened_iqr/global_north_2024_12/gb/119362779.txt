<p> Northern Gaza has no functioning hospitals after Israel 's military raided the region 's last major health facility and detained its director as a suspected Hamas operative , leaving Palestinians needing urgent medical care with few options . <p> Patients and staff at the last remaining hospital , Kamal Adwan in the city of Beit Lahia , northern Gaza , were evacuated before a raid on Friday . <p> The Israel Defence Forces ( IDF ) ordered 350 people , including 75 patients and 185 medical staff , to leave . The patients were taken to the nearby non-functional Indonesian Hospital . <p> " The IDF and ISA the security agency completed a targeted operation against a Hamas command centre in the Kamal Adwan Hospital , " the military said in a statement . " The forces apprehended over 240 terrorists in the area . They face dwindling options to get treatment . <p> In 2019 , the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics reported there were about 2,500 beds spread across 32 hospitals in the Gaza Strip . As of December 4 , the WHO said that only 17 hospitals remain functional , with a cumulative capacity of 1,500 beds . <p> There were also 11 field hospitals either fully or partially operational across Gaza as of December 4 , managed by aid organisations including M ? decins Sans Fronti ? res ( Doctors Without Borders ) or the Palestinian Red Crescent Society , providing an additional capacity of about 600 beds . However , field hospitals tend to be rudimentary care centres and are unable to offer specialised or intensive care . <p> Since December 4 , the IDF has continued to target hospitals and medical centres .
