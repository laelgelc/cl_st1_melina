<h> Israel forcibly evacuates Gaza hospital and detains medical staff <p> Israeli forces have detained and interrogated medical staff after forcibly evacuating the last major hospital in northern Gaza , the Hamas-run health ministry says . <p> The director of Kamal Adwan Hospital , Dr Hussam Abu Safiya , was among those taken for questioning by the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) , which accused him of " being a Hamas terrorist operative " , without providing evidence . <p> On Friday an estimated 50 people , including medical staff were killed in Israeli air strikes targeting the vicinity of the hospital , the health ministry said . <p> The IDF said it had carried out an operation there , alleging the hospital was a " Hamas terrorist stronghold " . <p> The WHO said it was " appalled by yesterday 's raid " on the hospital , which it said now meant the area 's last major health facility was out of service . <p> Eid Sabbah , head of the nursing department at Kamal Adwan , told the BBC the military had ordered the evacuation around 07:00 on Friday , giving the hospital about 15 minutes to move patients and staff into the courtyard . <p> Israeli troops then entered the hospital and removed the remaining patients , he said . <p> The IDF said it had " facilitated the secure evacuation of civilians , patients and medical personnel " before beginning the operation . <p> The city of Beit Lahia , where the hospital is located , has been under a tightening Israeli blockade imposed on parts of northern Gaza since October . <p> The UN has said the area has been under " near-total siege " as the Israeli military heavily restricts access of aid deliveries to an area where an estimated 10,000 to 15,000 people remain . <p> In recent days , the hospital 's administrators have issued desperate pleas appealing to be protected , as they say the facility has become a regular target for Israeli shelling and explosives . <p> But the IDF and Israeli Security Agency ( ISA ) alleged that intelligence had shown " terrorists " were using the Kamal Adwan Hospital as a command centre for its military operations in Jabalia . <p> In a statement on Saturday , the IDF said it apprehended 240 combatants . <p> It said IDF officers were attacked during the operation and weapons were found and confiscated in the area of the hospital . <p> It added Dr Abu Safiya was among medical staff taken for questioning and is " suspected of being a Hamas terrorist operative , as well as Hamas engineering and anti-tank missile operatives . " <p> Hamas has called on the United Nations to intervene to protect the remaining medical facilities in Gaza . <p> " We also demand that UN observers be sent to these facilities in order to determine the truth of what is happening , " it said in statement on Saturday . <p> Getty Images <p> Photo dated 25 December : Palestinians search for victims after an Israeli strike on the courtyard of Kamal Adwan Hospital <p> Gaza 's deputy minister of health , Dr Yousef Abu-Al Rish , told the BBC that around 100 medical staff were being questioned by the IDF and alleged that some were exposed to psychological torture . <p> The IDF did not comment on the latter allegation when approached by the BBC . <p> Concerns remain about the state of the Indonesian Hospital , which doctors have warned is not equipped for patients . <p> " You ca n't call it a hospital , it 's more of a shelter , " Dr Abu-Al Rish said on Friday . <p> The WHO added that an urgent mission was being planned to the Indonesian Hospital to move patients to southern Gaza for medical care .
