<h> Five Gaza journalists killed in Israeli strike targeting armed group <p> The vehicle , which had " press " markings , was parked at a hospital when it was hit <p> A Palestinian TV channel says five of its journalists have been killed in an Israeli strike in the central Gaza Strip . <p> They were in a Quds Today van parked outside al-Awda hospital , where the wife of one of the journalists was about to give birth , in the central Nuseirat refugee camp . <p> The channel posted a video of what it said was the burning vehicle with " press " signage on the back doors . <p> The Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) said it had targeted " Islamic Jihad operatives posing as journalists " and that steps were taken to avoid harming civilians . <p> The Committee to Protect Journalists ( CPJ ) said it was " devastated by the reports " . <p> " Journalists are civilians and must always be protected , " it said . <p> The BBC has not been able to verify claims made by either side , with international media being prevented by Israel from entering and freely working on the ground in Gaza . <p> Quds Today is affiliated with the Palestinian Islamic Jihad ( PIJ ) , an armed group that took part in the 7 October 2023 Hamas-led attack on Israel . The unprecedented attack triggered the war in Gaza . The TV channel is believed to receive funding from the group . <p> It said " intelligence from multiple sources confirmed " that all were PIJ operatives , and that a list found during an operation in Gaza " explicitly identified four " of them as such . <p> In a statement , Quds Today said the men " were killed as they carried out their media and humanitarian duty " . <p> As of 20 December , at least 133 Palestinian journalists have been killed during the course of the war , making it the deadliest conflict for journalists , according to the CPJ . <p> The press freedom organisation has called for accountability for Palestinian journalists who have been directly targeted by the Israeli military . <p> In a separate development , the director of the Kamal Adwan hospital on the northern edge of Gaza said that five of its staff had been killed in an Israeli strike . A paediatrician and two paramedics were among them . <p> At least another five people were also reported killed in Israeli strikes on Gaza City on Wednesday . <p> The Palestinian Wafa news agency , and the Hamas-run Gaza health ministry , also said a further 20 people were injured in the city 's al-Zeitoun neighbourhood . <p> The Israeli military has not commented on the reported bombing . <p> Meanwhile the father of a two-week-old Palestinian girl has told the BBC how his baby daughter froze to death in a tent in Gaza - the third child in a week to die in similar conditions . <p> Mahmoud Ismail Al-Faseeh said he woke up in the severe cold to find his daughter , Sila , suffering convulsions . She was rushed to hospital but died from hypothermia , the head of paediatrics at Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis told the Associated Press news agency . <p> The family was sheltering in al-Mawasi area on Gaza 's coast , a strip of land designated by the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) as a humanitarian zone but which has been hit by air strikes . <p> Ahmed al-Farra , the head of paediatrics , said two other babies - one three days old and the other a month old - had been brought in over the past 48 hours after dying from hypothermia . <p> Hopes of progress towards a ceasefire in recent days have begun to recede , with Hamas and Israel blaming each other . <p> Hamas accused the Israeli government of imposing " new conditions " that it said were delaying the agreement . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said the group was reneging on understandings that had already been reached about a possible ceasefire . <p> The latest statements mark a notable change of tone on both sides following optimistic signals . <p> The Israeli military launched air strikes and a ground offensive in the Gaza Strip in response to last year 's Hamas attack . About 1,200 people were killed in the attack and another 251 taken back to Gaza as hostages . <p> More than 45,000 Palestinians have been killed in Israel 's offensive , Gaza 's health ministry says . Almost two million people - 90% of the population - have been displaced , according to the UN .
