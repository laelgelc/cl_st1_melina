<p> The UN agency for Palestinian refugee ( Unrwa ) has warned that 945,000 Palestinians in Gaza are at risk of exposure to harsh winter conditions , as the humanitarian crisis in the enclave deepens . <p> Displaced families need protection from rain and cold , Unrwa said on X , adding that only 23 percent of this need has been met . <p> " Unrwa continues to provide support , distributing plastic tarpaulins and installing tents to offer temporary shelter and protection from the harsh conditions . Aid is urgently required to address the overwhelming needs as the crisis deepens , " the agency added . <p> According to the latest report published by Unrwa on Thursday , the Israeli war on Gaza has displaced at least 1.9 million people in the war-battered enclave , which makes up about 90 percent of the population . Some have been displaced 10 or more times . <p> The agency indicated that there are several problems hindering humanitarian supplies from entering into Gaza , including " deteriorating law and order , war and insecurity , damaged infrastructure , fuel shortages and access restrictions " . <h> New MEE newsletter : Jerusalem Dispatch <h> Sign up to get the latest insights and analysis on Israel-Palestine , alongside Turkey Unpacked and other MEE newsletters <p> Moreover , food security across Gaza is collapsing , leaving people in a " state of sheer desperation , " according to the report . <p> In one case last month , two girls and a woman were tragically killed in Deir al-Balah in central Gaza due to overcrowding outisde one of the few bakeries still operational . <p> Philippe Lazzarini , the general commissioner of Unrwa , raised concern over the worsening " humanitarian disaster " in the enclave . <p> " We are running out of words . Hunger and disease are rampant , " he said in a post on X. <p> " Humanitarians must be enabled to do their job . Hurdles to aid must be removed without further delay , otherwise more lives will be lost . This keeps testing our shared humanity " . <h> ' A crime against humanity ' <p> Aid access to northern Gaza has been especially challenging , leaving between 65,000 and 75,000 people without access to necessities such as food , water , electricity or reliable healthcare , according to Unrwa 's report . <p> " Intense military operations are ongoing amid a near total lack of humanitarian aid entering the area , in addition to severe communications and internet disruptions . Some parts of the North Gaza governorate have been under a tightened siege for 60 days , " the report says . <p> Balakrishnan Rajagopal , a law professor at MIT and UN special rapporteur on the right to housing , told Middle East Eye that Israel bears responsibility as a state for " all of these grave breaches of international law " . <p> " Imposing a siege , preventing all access to food , water , medicine and shelter , all with an intention to force a population to leave , is textbook ethnic cleansing and is a grave crime under international law . It is both a war crime and a crime against humanity , " Rajagopal said . <p> What is Israel 's ' Generals ' Plan ' and what does it mean for the war on Gaza ? <p> " Forcible displacement in northern Gaza under conditions of siege with an intent to eliminate their presence is genocidal , as it aims at the destruction of a people , " he added . <p> Israel has imposed a blackout on northern Gaza for over two months as part of a military plan that has severely hindered residents from contacting the outside world and sharing information about what they are enduring . Despite this , horrific reports of starvation , bombardment and displacement are still emerging . <p> Israeli media , analysts and military officials - as well as Palestinians in Gaza - say that the current campaign corresponds to the so-called " Generals ' Plan " , also known as the Eiland Plan . <p> The plan aims to effectively ethnically cleanse north Gaza of Palestinians before decalring the area a closed military zone . <h> ' Decimation of the health system ' <p> The UN report has also made mention of ongoing Israeli strikes that have resulted in civilian deaths and the destruction of residential buildings and public infrastructure . <p> With continuous Israeli aerial , land and maritime bombardments across the Gaza strip , the death toll has risen to 44,758 , with more than 106,134 wounded , the Palestinian Health Ministry latest figures show . <p> According to the World Health Organisation ( WHO ) , at least one quarter of those wounded by 23 July , around 22,500 people , are estimated to have " life-changing injuries that require rehabilitation services now and for years to come " , with severe limb wounds accounting for the majority of cases requiring rehabilitation <p> As a result of ongoing Israeli attacks , Gaza now has the highest number of child amputees per capita anywhere in the world , Unrwa indicates in its report . <p> " The huge surge in rehabilitation needs occurs in parallel with the ongoing decimation of the health system , " Richard Peeperkorn , a WHO Representative in the occupied Palestinian territory , said . <p> " Patients ca n't get the care they need . Acute rehabilitation services are severely disrupted and specialized care for complex injuries is not available , placing patients ' lives at risk . Immediate and long-term support is urgently needed to address the enormous rehabilitation needs . " <p> Attacks on health facilities have been reported across Gaza , which according to the United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs ( Ocha ) , has further hindered medical assistance and services in the enclave . <p> A severe shortage of medical supplies and consumables is also impeding the provision of essential healthcare in Gaza , with Unrwa warning that at least 60 medications will be depleted at its health facilities by the end of the year . <p> Middle East Eye delivers independent and unrivalled coverage and analysis of the Middle East , North Africa and beyond .
