<h> Palestine Solidarity holds vigil for peace in Weymouth <p> More than 50 protesters gathered in Weymouth calling for an end to the conflict in Gaza between Israel and Hamas . <p> The Dorset Palestine Solidarity Campaign staged a candlelit vigil as part of a national day of action organised by the Palestine Solidarity Campaign ( PSC ) under the banner ' End the Genocide . ' <p> Vigils for Gaza took place across the UK , including a 1,000 strong rally outside Downing Street where flowers and children 's toys were placed . <p> In Weymouth , 55 protestors gathered in New Bond Street on Saturday evening , to demand an arms embargo on Israel and an end to the bombing of Lebanon and Gaza . A two minute silence was held to commemorate the lives lost and candles lit in memory . <p> The vigil saw 55 protesters gather on New Bond Street ( Image : Dorset PSC ) The rally featured subversive carols , poetry from a local student and a few speakers . <p> Naira , a local student and youth activist joined the protest . <p> She said : " We can not shelter ourselves from the suffering of Palestinians , and stand gazing at festivities while Gaza 's nights are filled with fright . <p> " We must keep demanding an end to the violence and advocate for an immediate arms embargo . UK tax payers ' money should be used to improve people 's lives not destroy others ' futures . " <p> A protester at the vigil in Weymouth with a message of support ( Image : Dorset PSC ) Youth activist and organiser of the vigil , Poppy , also spoke about the the lives of children living and dying in the conflict area . <p> She said : " I am here to ask why Palestinian life does not have the same worth and value as ours . This vigil is for Hind Rajab and the 14 long and telling pages of children martyred by Israel . " <p> The latest death toll over a year of conflict in Gaza between Israel and Hamas stands at 45,646 including 17,492 children . <p> The protest also called for an immediate ceasefire in Lebanon where the fighting has spread to and for an arms embargo on Israel . <p> More protesters with messages of support written on cardboard signs at the vigil in Weymouth ( Image : Dorset PSC ) Rob Ferguson , secretary of Dorset Palestine Solidarity Campaign said : " We stand in memory and grief at lives lost . <p> " But we also stand in testament to the determination of the Palestinian people to refuse to submit to oppression and apartheid . <p> " We stand in solidarity ; we stand with resolution and determination . We will not be silenced . We will not cease protesting . <p> " We demand an end to complicity . And I also stand in hope . I believe walls can fall .
