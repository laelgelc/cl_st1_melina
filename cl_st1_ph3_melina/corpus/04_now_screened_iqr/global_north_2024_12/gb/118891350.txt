<p> The British Medical Association is investigating claims that its president " repeatedly amplified " allegedly antisemitic posts on her social media account . <p> Mary McCarthy , a leading GP , has been accused of creating a " hostile environment " for Jewish doctors by using her account on X to share provocative posts focusing on the conflict in Gaza . <p> The campaign group Labour Against Antisemitism wrote to the BMA last week describing McCarthy 's online activity as " deeply concerning " . The doctors ' union said it has brought in external investigators . <p> On Sunday Wes Streeting , the health secretary , urged regulators to take a " zero-tolerance approach " to medical professionals who share racist or extremist views . <p> He said doctors and nurses should be disciplined and potentially struck off if they use " the conflict in the Middle East as a pretext to attack communities " . <h> Advertisement <p> Streeting told The Sunday Telegraph : " I expect regulators to investigate any concerns suggesting patient safety is at risk due to discrimination or misconduct by a healthcare professional . <p> " Any worker espousing racist or extremist views should know they could end up in front of a disciplinary panel . " <p> Streeting added : " Any patient racially or violently abusing NHS staff , who are simply trying to do their jobs and care for us , can and should be turned away . <p> " We will ensure everyone working in or using the health service feels safe and respected , and stamp out this wickedness . " <h> Advertisement <p> The General Medical Council states that " any form of antisemitism or Islamophobia is completely unacceptable and the standards state that doctors must not discriminate against , bully or harass anyone they work with " . <p> The medical regulator said that this standard " applies in the workplace and when doctors are engaging on social media " . <p> The BMA said it had begun an investigation after a complaint was sent by Alex Hearn , a director of Labour Against Antisemitism ( LAAS ) . <p> A letter to the union on December 10 included several screenshots of messages that had been shared by McCarthy , a Shropshire GP , on her X account . One that she reposted described the conflict in Gaza as " a holocaust " . <h> Advertisement <p> The campaign group said she had shared " incendiary " posts by Sarah Wilkinson , a prominent pro-Palestinian activist . This allegedly included one post with a meme from the Quds News Network , a media group that critics say is linked to Hamas . <p> The letter further accused McCarthy of sharing disinformation by repeating claims that Israel had bombed an Orthodox church in Gaza which were later shown to be untrue . <p> Hearn wrote in the letter : " In her leadership role , Mary McCarthy has a duty to all the BMA membership to act responsibly instead of pouring fuel on the fire and contributing towards a hostile environment for her members who are Jewish . " <p> The BMA said : " We have received a complaint from LAAS and in accordance with BMA processes the concerns raised in their letter are being investigated by our external and independent investigators . <p> " We take complaints of this nature extremely seriously . The BMA stands firmly against all forms of discrimination and prejudice . "
