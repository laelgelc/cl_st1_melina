<p> CAIRO : Israeli military strikes across the Gaza Strip killed at least 28 Palestinians , including a journalist and rescue workers , medics said on Sunday ( Dec 15 ) , and the Israeli military said troops carried out air and ground attacks in the northern Gaza Strip , killing dozens of militants while capturing others . <p> An Israeli airstrike hit the civil emergency centre in the Nuseirat market area in the central Gaza Strip , killing Ahmed Al-Louh , a video journalist for Al Jazeera TV , and five other people , medics and fellow journalists said . <p> The TV network said he was working when he was killed . The Israeli military said they were looking into the report . Hamas media said the head of the civil emergency service in Nuseirat , Nedal Abu Hjayyer , was also killed . <p> " The civil emergency headquarters in Nuseirat camp was hit during the crews ' presence , they work around the clock to serve the people , " said Zaki Emadeldeen from the civil emergency service to reporters at the hospital . <p> " The civil emergency service is a humanitarian service and not political , they work in war and peace times for the service of the people , " he said , adding that the place was hit directly by Israeli airstrike . <p> Another air strike hit a group of Hamas-linked men tasked with protecting aid trucks west of Gaza City , and medics said several were killed or wounded but exact figures were unavailable as yet . <h> Related : <p> Residents said at least 11 people were killed in three separate Israeli airstrikes on Gaza City houses , nine were killed in the towns of Beit Lahiya , Beit Hanoun and Jabalia camp when clusters of houses were bombed or set ablaze , and two were killed by drone fire in Rafah . <p> The Israeli military said the three Gaza City houses belonged to militants planning imminent attacks . It said steps were taken to mitigate the risk of harming civilians beforehand , including the use of precise munitions and aerial surveillance . <p> The military issued a photo showing the weapons it said were seized in Beit Lahiya that including explosives and dozens of grenades . <p> In Beit Hanoun , Israeli forces besieged families sheltering in Khalil Aweida school before storming it and ordering them to head towards Gaza City , the medics and residents said . <p> Medics said several people were killed and wounded during the raid on the school while the army detained many men . The number killed was not immediately clear . <p> The military said it struck down dozens of militants from the air and on the ground and captured others in Beit Hanoun . <p> Reuters was unable to confirm whether any of the people killed were fighters . Hamas does not disclose its casualties , and the Palestinian health ministry does not distinguish in its daily death toll between combatants and non-combatants . <p> Separately , Israel said its air force struck a command and control centre in a compound in the Abu Shabak clinic in northern Gaza used by Hamas to store weapons and plan attacks . The Gaza health ministry said the medical centre , which also included a mental health clinic , was destroyed . <p> A man sits with two children , as Palestinians inspect the damage at a tent camp sheltering displaced people , following an Israeli strike , amid the Israel-Hamas conflict , in Deir Al-Balah in the central Gaza Strip , Dec 15 , 2024 .
