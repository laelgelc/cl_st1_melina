But the area where most of the Irish operate is right on the so-called Blue Line -- a de facto border between the two states that has seen a huge amount of attacks from both sides for almost a year . <p> Fighting erupted between Islamic group Hezbollah and Israel on 8 October last year -- a day after Gaza-based Hamas attacked the Jewish state further south , killing more than 1,200 people .
