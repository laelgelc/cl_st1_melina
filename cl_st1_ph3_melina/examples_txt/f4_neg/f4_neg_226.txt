Exclusively for Malay Mail readers : Use code VERSAMM10 <p> Wednesday , 04 Dec 2024 6:17 PM MYT <p> OSLO , Dec 4 -- Norway 's sovereign wealth fund , the world 's largest , has sold all its shares in Israel 's Bezeq as it provides telecoms services to the Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank , it said late on Tuesday . <p> The decision to divest comes after the fund 's ethics watchdog , the Council on Ethics , adopted a new , tougher interpretation of ethics standards for businesses that aid Israel 's operations in the occupied Palestinian territories . <p> The $1.8 trillion ( RM8 trillion ) fund has been an international leader in the environmental , social and governance ( ESG ) investment field . It owns 1.5 per cent of the world 's listed shares across 8,700 companies , and its size carries influence . <p> It is the latest decision by a European financial entity to cut back links to Israeli companies or those with ties to the country , as pressure mounts from foreign governments to end the war in Gaza . <p> Bezeq is Israel 's largest telecoms group . It did not reply immediately to a request for comment . <p> " The company , through its physical presence and provision of telecom services to Israeli settlements in the West Bank , is helping to facilitate the maintenance and expansion of these settlements , which are illegal under international law , " the fund 's watchdog said in its recommendation to divest . <p> " By doing so the company is itself contributing to the violation of international law , " it added . <p> The watchdog said it noted that the company had said it was also providing telecoms services to Palestinian areas in the West Bank , but that did not outweigh the fact that it was also providing services to Israeli settlements . <p> The watchdog makes recommendations to the board of the Norwegian central bank , which has the final say on divestments . <p> The advice on Bezeq was the first recommendation to divest since the watchdog toughened its policy in August . More decisions are expected . <p> Sharpened policy <p> The fund has now sold all its stock in the company . <p> Before that , it had cut its stake during the first half of 2024 , owning 0.76 per cent of the company 's shares valued at $23.7 million at the end of June , down from a holding of 2.2 per cent at the start of the year , fund data showed . <p> The watchdog 's new definition of ethical breaches was partly based on the International Court of Justice finding in July that " the occupation itself , Israel 's settlement policy and the way Israel uses the natural resources in the areas are in conflict with international law " , according to an Aug. 30 letter it addressed to the finance ministry . <p> Since the start of the war in Gaza in October 2023 , the council had been investigating whether more companies fall outside its permitted investment guidelines . <p> Before the announcement to divest , the fund had divested from nine companies operating in the occupied West Bank . <p> Their operations include building roads and homes in Israeli settlements in East Jerusalem and the West Bank and providing surveillance systems for an Israeli wall around the West Bank . -- Reuters
