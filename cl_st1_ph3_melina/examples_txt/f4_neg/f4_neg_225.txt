2022 as global weapons makers struggled to meet the increase in demand , but many of them managed to increase production last year , the authors of the report noted . <p> In a sign of this surge in reflect the scale of demand , and many companies have launched recruitment drives , suggesting they are optimistic about future sales , " Scarazzato added . <p> Smaller producers have been more effective in meeting the demand linked to the wars in Gaza and Ukraine , growing tensions in East Asia and rearmament programmes in other regions , the institute said . <p> " A lot of them specialise Production Programme , told AFP . <p> Among the leading producers , US companies recorded a 2.5 per cent increase in their sales last year and still account for half the world 's arms revenues , with 41 US weapons producers in the world 's top 100 . <p> Lockheed Martin and RTX ( formerly Raytheon Technologies ) , the world 's two largest arms makers , on the other hand , reported a fall in revenue
