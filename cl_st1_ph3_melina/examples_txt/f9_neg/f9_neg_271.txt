<p> The 60th anniversary training of the Union of African Journalists ( UAJ ) opened in Cairo with a forceful appeal for a ceasefire in Gaza , setting a bold tone for a gathering of young journalists from across the African continent . <p> Delivering the keynote address , Ambassador Ahmed Haggag , a long-time UAJ member and former African Union official , condemned the ongoing killing of journalists in Palestine and urged African journalists to take a united stand . <p> " To date , more than 100 journalists in Palestine have been killed in cold blood . These journalists were not only killed in the line of duty , but some were killed alongside their children , some even in front of their families . What is happening in Gaza must be condemned . We must take a stand , " Ambassador Haggag said . <p> Cross-section of young African Journalists and officials of the Union of African Journalists <p> He highlighted that Israel now ranks second globally in the number of journalists killed , according to the International Federation of Journalists in Brussels , most of them @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ journalists , no international media is reporting from inside Gaza . <p> Africa 's Longstanding Solidarity with Palestine <p> The opening session reaffirmed the African Union 's historic support for Palestine , with Ambassador Haggag noting that the issue has always been first on the agenda of AU summits .
