<p> A United Nations official expressed grave concern regarding the ongoing Israeli military actions in Gaza , highlighting civilian casualties and the destruction of medical facilities . <p> Stephanie Tremblay , a UN spokeswoman , condemned the continuing attacks across Gaza , which have resulted in significant Palestinian casualties . " We are alarmed by ongoing attacks across the Strip that have left scores of Palestinians dead , " Tremblay said in a statement to the press . <p> In a report from Kamal Adwan Hospital in Beit Lahia , northern Gaza , a medical source confirmed to Anadolu that Israeli forces had set fire to several sections of the hospital . Tremblay added that an Israeli airstrike near the hospital on Thursday night reportedly killed dozens , including healthcare workers . <p> " In Kamal Adwan hospital today , staff , patients , and their companions were forced out of the facility . Arrests and significant damage to the hospital have been reported , " she said . <p> The UN official further emphasized that the UN teams are facing " systematic denials " of access to the besieged northern Gaza region , with the latest attempt to reach these areas blocked by Israeli authorities . <p> " Just today , another attempt by the UN to reach besieged parts of North Gaza governorate was denied by the Israeli authorities , " Tremblay stated . <p> Turning to the West Bank , Tremblay raised concerns over the ongoing Israeli military operations , particularly in the Tulkarm refugee camp . <p> As of December 26 , at least 20 Palestinians had been killed by Israeli forces in a span of 10 days , including 12 deaths resulting from airstrikes . Tremblay noted , " Our humanitarian colleagues say Israel 's military operation in Tulkarm 's refugee camp caused extensive damage to infrastructure . " <p> She also pointed out the excessive use of force , noting , " Lethal , war-like tactics are being applied repeatedly during these operations in the West Bank , raising concerns over the use of force that exceeds law enforcement standards . " <p> According to the UN 's Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs ( OCHA ) , more than 4,700 Palestinians have been displaced across the West Bank this year , including nearly 2,000 children . <p> In response to the Israeli raid on Kamal Adwan Hospital , the Palestinian resistance group Hamas denied Israeli claims that it was targeting Hamas fighters inside the facility . <p> Hamas labeled the Israeli raid a " heinous crime , " accusing Israel of using the false claim of Hamas presence to justify the destruction of the hospital . <p> The group called for an independent investigation by the UN into Israeli actions in northern Gaza , which Hamas described as part of a broader " plan of genocide and forced displacement . " <p> Since October 5 , Israel has conducted a large-scale ground offensive in northern Gaza , claiming the operation targets Hamas , but Palestinians argue that the true aim is the occupation of the region and the forced displacement of its population . <p> Humanitarian aid has been severely restricted , exacerbating the dire situation for those remaining in the enclave . <p> As of December , the death toll in Gaza has surpassed 45,400 , with much of the area reduced to rubble following the escalation of violence after a Hamas attack on Israel on October 7 , 2023 . <p> The International Criminal Court recently issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes in Gaza . <p> Israel is also facing a case at the International Court of Justice for alleged genocide.
