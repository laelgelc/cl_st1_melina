<p> After 16 months , the current bombardment of Gaza and warfare between Hamas and Israel could soon be over , with Palestinian sources telling Andalou Agency a ceasefire deal could be signed by Friday . The draft has been presented at talks in Doha involving Israel , Hamas , the US ( including representatives for both President Joe Biden and President-elect Donald Trump ) , Qatar and Egypt . <p> By our count , this will have been at least the eighth ceasefire proposal since October 2023 . Here is a timeline of the previous proposals , and some of the reasons why they failed . <h> Nov 2023 : Proposed extension of temporary ceasefire <p> The initial Qatar-mediated temporary ceasefire agreement secured a four-day pause in fighting , from November 24-28 , to allow for the release of 50 Israeli hostages , 120 Palestinian political prisoners , and for humanitarian aid to enter Gaza . A two-day extension was agreed to in exchange for the release of a further 20 Israelis and 60 Palestinians . <p> After a final one-day extension securing the release of eight Israeli hostages @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ December 1 , after negotiations to further extend failed due to violations by both Israel and Hamas . <h> Dec 2023 : US vetoes UN ceasefire resolution <p> The United Arab Emirates proposed a resolution to the UN Security Council , calling for an immediate humanitarian ceasefire , unconditional release of all hostages , and the resumption of humanitarian access to Gaza . The resolution stated both " Palestinian and Israeli civilian populations must be protected in accordance with international humanitarian law " . <p> At a December 8 vote the resolution was supported by 13 council members but vetoed by the US ( using its power as one of five permanent members of the Security Council ) . The UK abstained from voting . Both cited the failure to condemn Hamas as their reason for not supporting the resolution . <h> Feb 2024 : Further vetoes at the UN <p> On February 20 , the US vetoed a ceasefire resolution presented by Algeria . It contained similar terms to the previous UAE-drafted resolution , with additional statements on preventing further escalation in the Middle East . It instead put forward an alternative resolution , which made a ceasefire conditional on the release of hostages . This was vetoed by permanent Security Council members Russia and China , who argued any ceasefire should be unconditional . <h> Mar 2024 : Israel ignores successful UN resolution <p> The fourth attempt by the Security Council to pass a ceasefire resolution succeeded , with the US abstaining from the vote . This proposal was put forward by the non-permanent members of the council ( known as the E-10 or " elected 10 " ) and called for a ceasefire for the duration of Ramadan as the first step towards a " lasting sustainable ceasefire " . <p> Israel 's then foreign minister Israel Katz immediately confirmed that Israel would not abide by the ceasefire , insisting his country would " destroy Hamas and continue to fight until the last of the hostages returns home " . <h> May 2024 : Hamas accepts Egypt-Qatar proposal , Israel invades Rafah <p> Stage one included a temporary ceasefire and retreat of Israeli forces , to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of displaced Palestinians , release of Israeli hostages and Palestinian political prisoners , and the beginning of reconstruction in Gaza . <p> Stage two included the permanent end of military operations , Israel 's complete withdrawal from Gaza , and the further exchange of captive Israelis and Palestinians . <p> Stage three included the final exchange of remaining captive Israelis and Palestinians , the beginning of a long-term reconstruction plan for Gaza , and the end of the siege on the Gaza Strip . <p> A few weeks later , the US put forward a very similar proposal -- with the key difference being it removed the reference to ending the siege on the Gaza Strip .
