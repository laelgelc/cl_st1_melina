<p> Israel has said that it will take " full security control " of the Gaza Strip once fighting has ended , as negotiators edged closer towards a deal to release the hostages still being held in the enclave . <p> Hopes of a ceasefire have been rising for several days , with sources on both sides briefing that an agreement , which would halt the conflict in exchange for the release of hostages , was closer than ever before . <p> This week , Israeli negotiators arrived in Qatar to work through the remaining impediments to a deal .
