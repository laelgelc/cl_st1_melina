<h> Building peace between Israel , Hezbollah <p> AFTER the ceasefire between Hezbollah and Israel took effect on November 27 , the guns have continued to boom albeit occasionally between the Israeli Army and Hezbollah . It signifies how difficult it is to make peace between Israel and Hezbollah . They have been at it for decades.Most peace agreements are like a ploy to buy time for the adversaries to rearm and fight another day . <p> Before the latest war which lasted over a year , there had been an uneasy calm between Israel and Hezbollah for about 24 years . The newly signed deal calls for an initial two-month halt to fighting and requires Hezbollah to end its armed presence in southern Lebanon , while Israeli troops are to return to their side of the border . <p> It offers both sides suspension of the war that has driven more than 1.2 million Lebanese and 50,000 Israelis from their homes . <p> With the truce in place , both sides are counting the cost of the hostilities . Heavy bombings by Israel have left more than 3,700 Lebanese dead , while over 130 people have been killed on the Israeli side . <p> Israel succeeded in killing Hezbollah 's top leader , Hassan Nasrallah , most of his senior commanders and destroying extensive militant infrastructure . <p> A sophisticated attack in September involving the explosion of hundreds of walkie-talkies and pagers used by Hezbollah was widely attributed to Israel . <p> The ceasefire offers relief to both sides , giving Israel 's army a break and allowing Hezbollah and the Lebanese people to have a respite from Israel 's relentless bombings . <p> Hezbollah got involved in the war with Israel after Hamas operatives attacked Israel and killed 1,200 citizens . So far , Israel has been attacking Hamas in Gaza relentlessly since October 8 , 2023 . Over 44,000 people have been killed . <p> In retaliation , Israel attacked Gaza ferociously and Hezbollah started firing rockets into northern Israel as a form of support for Hamas . <p> At some point , Iran joined the fray . First , in April , Israel struck an Iranian consulate in Syria , killing 13 people including a top Iran Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps Commander and his deputy.In reprisal , Iran attacked Israel with 300 missiles and drones on April 13 . Israel fought back by firing some missiles into Iran on April 19 . <p> In July 2024 , Israel allegedly killed Hamas leader , Ismail Haniyeh , in Tehran . Subsequently , in October , Iran carried out another major attack against Israel . On October 26 , Israel retaliated against Iran by firing missiles at the country . <p> The reality is that Iran is the main backer of Hamas , Hezbollah , and the Houthi rebels in Yemen and all the Iranian proxies were working together , until they paused recently , to attack Israel , especially after the Hamas 7 October attack and Israel 's retaliation . <p> The attacks against Hamas , Hezbollah , Houthis , and Iran by Israel seemed to have contributed to the rapid collapse of the Bashar al-Assad regime in Syria as all of Assad 's backers ; Russia , Iran , Hezbollah , and Hamas , are already weakened . <p> So , militarily , Israel has an upper hand in the Middle East now , but the crisis can not be permanently solved through force of arms . The crux of the conflict is the fight by Hamas and others for a Palestinian homeland . So , permanently solving the Hezbollah conflict with Israel is directly linked to finding a permanent solution to the Israeli-Palestinian dispute that has lasted decades . <p> Thus , creating a state for the Palestinians side by side Israel seems to be the only option to bringpeaceto the Middle East . <p> A truce like the one Hezbollah and Lebanon just had with Israel could only last a while before the next fight , so ultimately the need for a Palestinian State must be addressed . <p> All rights reserved .
