<p> Just two days after the shocking and devestating October 7 , 2023 , attack by Hamas , Israeli prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu uttered prophetic words : " We will change the face of the Middle East " . The proclamation set the tone for a series of tactical military operations aimed at dismantling Hamas 's capabilities in Gaza , crippling Hezbollah in Lebanon and taking back Golan Heights from Syria . The campaign against Hamas and Hezbollah was framed as not just a battle for Israel 's security but also a broader confrontation with Iranian influence that has historically extended from the Persian Gulf through Iraq and Syria to Lebanon . By actively engaging in these military confrontations , Netanyahu has sought to position Israel as the dominant power in the region . <h> Military actions in Gaza <p> Speaking about his decision that he took just two days after the Hamas attack that killed over 1,100 people : Netanyahu said in a recent media post : " My first decision , and that of the Security Cabinet , was to focus , first of all , on the south , and afterwards on the north : To eliminate Hamas 's military and governing capabilities in Gaza , and block the danger of a Hezbollah invasion in the north . " " Now we are working to demolish Hamas 's remaining military capabilities and all of its governing capabilities , and bring back the hostages , and I emphasise , down to the last of them . " <p> Since the outbreak of hostilities on October 7 , 2023 , Israel has engaged in extensive military operations in Gaza . The conflict has resulted in significant casualties , with reports indicating that over 41,400 people have died , predominantly women and children . Netanyahu 's government has justified these operations as necessary to dismantle Hamas 's military capabilities , asserting that they have destroyed key infrastructures both above and below ground.In his speeches , Netanyahu has emphasized a proactive approach to security , stating : " We do not wait for a threat ; we anticipate it " . <h> Escalation in Lebanon <p> Following the conflict in Gaza , tensions escalated significantly between Israel and Hezbollah in Lebanon . Netanyahu has made it clear that he intends to change the " balance of power " with Hezbollah through military action . " In Lebanon , we used a daring stratagem that struck a severe blow to Hezbollah : We eliminated the organization 's leadership , destroyed its terrorist strongholds close to the border -- and I say terrorist strongholds which were built over decades -- and destroyed a considerable part of its arsenal of Hezbollah 's missiles and rockets , " said Netanyahu . <h> Iran 's Axis of Resistance <p> Netanyahu has said that even before the elimination of Hezbollah leader Hassan Nasrallah , Israel had reached a clear conclusion : " Nasrallah himself was the axis of the axis " . " Strike him , and the axis will have been struck a mortal blow . Nasrallah not only fanned the flames of aggression against us , he was the connecting link between Hezbollah , Syria and Iran . Therefore , the elimination of Nasrallah was a turning point in the crumbling of the axis , " the PM said , adding : " Nasrallah is no longer with us , nor is the axis what it used to be . We are dismantling it stage by stage . " " Iran cleared a path of terrorism from the Persian Gulf to the Mediterranean Sea : From Iran to Iraq , from Iraq to Syria and from Syria to Lebanon . In the south , it armed Hamas and in the more distant south -- the Houthis , whom we have also struck hard . The axis has not yet disappeared , but as I promised , we are changing the face of the Middle East . The State of Israel is establishing itself as a focus of strength in our region , the likes of which have not been seen for decades . Whoever cooperates with us , gains greatly . Whoever attacks us , loses greatly . " <h> Syria crisis <p> The situation in Syria presented another front in Netanyahu 's strategy . With the recent collapse of Bashar al-Assad 's regime , Netanyahu grabbed the opportunity to further solidify Israel 's security posture and ordered Israeli forces to take control of positions in Golan Heights , previously held by Syrian troops , to prevent hostile entities from gaining a foothold near Israel 's borders.Netanyahu has warned the new Syrian regime against allowing Iran to establish a presence or transfer weapons to Hezbollah . He stated that while Israel does not intend to interfere in Syria 's internal affairs , it will take necessary measures if its security is threatened . <h> Regional reactions <p> Netanyahu 's vow to change the face of the Middle East through military action is unfolding against a backdrop of significant violence and upheaval . His government 's strategies in Gaza , Lebanon , and Syria reflect a commitment to altering regional power dynamics through forceful means . While this approach may temporarily bolster Israel 's security posture , it raises critical questions about long-term stability and peace in the region.The reactions from neighboring countries and international observers have been mixed . Many view Netanyahu 's aggressive posture as an attempt to capitalise on regional instability for territorial gain . Critics argue that such actions could lead to further escalation and conflict within an already volatile region . Furthermore , there are concerns about the humanitarian impact of these military campaigns . The ongoing conflict has led to widespread displacement and suffering among civilian populations in Gaza and Lebanon .
