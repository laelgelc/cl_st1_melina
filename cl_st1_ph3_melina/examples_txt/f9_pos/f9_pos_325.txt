<h> At least 24 Palestinians killed in Israeli raids on Gaza : Sources <p> Gaza , December 19 ( IANS ) At least 24 Palestinians were killed in Israeli raids across the Gaza Strip , Palestinian sources said . <p> Israeli warplanes bombed a house in Beit Hanoun town in northern Gaza on Wednesday , according to Palestinian security sources . <p> Mahmoud Basal , spokesman for Civil Defence in Gaza , told Xinhua that four people were killed in the strike , including two women , and several others are still missing under the rubble . <p> In a separate incident in northern Gaza , two more were killed and another seriously injured when an Israeli drone targeted a gathering of Palestinians in the Jabalia area , according to Basal . <p> In addition , a brief statement issued by Al-Awda Hospital in northern Gaza said that a paramedic was killed in an Israeli airstrike on a building in the town of Beit Lahia at dawn , Xinhua news agency reported . <p> The statement added that the Israeli army detonated a " robot " near the hospital , causing injuries among medical staff and patients , without disclosing their exact number and condition . <p> Meanwhile , Kamal Adwan Hospital said in a statement in the morning that at least 10 people were killed and several others injured in an Israeli shelling on a residential house in Beit Lahia . <p> The hospital 's intensive care unit also caught fire as a result of gunfire from Israeli vehicles , the statement said . <p> In central Gaza , medical and civil defence workers recovered the bodies of four people who were killed in Israeli airstrikes at dawn , one on a tent sheltering displaced people in Deir al-Balah city and the other around " Ard al-Mufti " park north of al-Nuseirat refugee camp , according to Basal . <p> In Khan Younis in southern Gaza , three Palestinians , including two women , were killed as a result of an Israeli shelling on a house east of the city , reported the Nasser medical compound after receiving the victims ' bodies . <p> The Israeli army has not commented on these incidents yet . <p> Israel has been launching a large-scale offensive against Hamas in Gaza to retaliate against a Hamas rampage through the southern Israeli border on October 7 , 2023 , during which about 1,200 people were killed and about 250 taken hostage . <p> The Palestinian death toll from ongoing Israeli
