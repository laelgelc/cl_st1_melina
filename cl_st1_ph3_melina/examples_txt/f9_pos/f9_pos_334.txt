<h> Four Palestinians killed in Israeli attack near Jenin in occupied West Bank <p> Attack on village of Sir brings Palestinian death toll in the territory to at least 790 since beginning of Gaza war . <p> Palestinians inspect a bullet-riddled car after an Israeli operation near the southern West Bank village of Sir , south of Jenin Jaafar Ashtiyeh/AFP <p> Published On 1 Dec 20241 Dec 2024 <p> At least four Palestinians have been killed in an Israeli operation in the occupied West Bank , according to authorities . <p> The attack occurred in the village of Sir near Jenin on Sunday , according to the Palestinian Ministry of Health . It said the killings were a result of " the occupation 's aggression " . Israel 's military also confirmed that four Palestinians were killed , describing them as " terrorists " . <p> The Palestine Red Crescent Society , meanwhile , reported that Israeli forces had been " preventing our teams from reaching the bombing site " . <p> Fares Irshaid , a resident of Sir , described the attack to the AFP news agency . <p> " In the morning , the planes came and started bombing this area , " Irshaid said . " Shortly after that , the army stormed the entire area . They declared it a closed military zone . " <p> " There was a group of young men , including my nephew and the son of our neighbours , and we do not know their fate , " he told the news agency . <p> Israel has not released the bodies of those killed although the military identified one of the dead as 31-year-old Wael Lahluh . <p> There has been a surge in Israeli military operations and violence by Israeli settlers in the occupied West Bank since the war in Gaza began on October 7 , 2023 . <p> At least 780 Palestinians have been killed in the West Bank , adding to the 44,429 Palestinians killed in Gaza since the outbreak of the war . <p> Israel said 24 Israelis have been killed in the West Bank during that period while Hamas 's attack on southern Israel on October 7 , 2023 , killed at least 1,139 people . <h> ' Drone attack ' <p> Speaking to Al Jazeera , Willem Marx , a journalist based in the West Bank , said Sunday 's operation was initially " a drone attack , using bullets against cars " . <p> " It seems the men inside the cars then fled on foot to a nearby agricultural storage space in quite a remote region just southeast of the city of Jenin , " he said . <p> He added the Israeli military has said those killed were reportedly responsible for several attacks on Israeli settlers . The military said Lahluh had killed a 23-year-old settler in August . <p> While Israeli settlements are illegal under international law and are seen as a major obstacle to a future Palestinian state , the government of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has continued their expansion . The military regularly intervenes to protect Israeli settlers . <p> The latest Israeli operation was carried out after Israel last month launched several raids in Jenin , killing nine people . <p> Two Palestinians , including a teenage boy , were also killed during an Israeli raid in the West Bank village of Yabad on November 24 , according to the Palestinian Authority .
