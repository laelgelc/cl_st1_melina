The caption to the logo reads " the protest of arts against the invasion @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the University Youth Festival every year . This year the youth festival has been named ' Intifada ' . " Intifada is an Arabic word , which means " shake-off . " <p> Palestinians use the term to mean ' civil uprising . ' The word was used by the Palestinians in the wake of violence that erupted in Gaza between Palestinians and the Israelis . The term ' intifada ' is historically linked with the militant/terror groups like the Hamas of Palestine , " Ashish says in his petition . <p> The youth festival can not be related to a riot or a war . But Ext.P1 ( name and logo of Festival ) sets a different theme to the youth of the state . There is no place for politics or geopolitics in a youth festival . Israel-Palestine conflict can not be the point of discussion or the theme of creative expression , " the petition says . <p> " The popular association of the term ' intifada ' with violent terror uprisings of Israel- Palestine conflict , is largely understood by the student body as a call @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and discussions which have opened up in the college in the backdrop of the youth festival , varies from terrorism to politics- global , national and state . " <p> " The student body which is a mix of diverse students from various religious and political backgrounds- is being rift into various groups , at this early stage in the youth festival announcement itself .
