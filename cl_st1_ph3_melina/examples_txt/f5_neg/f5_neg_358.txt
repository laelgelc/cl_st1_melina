<p> Israeli forces from the 162nd Division operating in the Gaza Strip . ( Credit : IDF ) <p> More than 100 Hamas operatives were killed by Israeli soldiers as they tried to escape Jabalia in northern Gaza on Sunday night , according to the Israel Defence Forces . <p> The IDF released footage showing armed terrorists trying to escape before being neutralized by units from the 162nd Division , including the 401st Brigade , Givati Brigade and the Multidimensional Unit , supported by strikes from the Air Force . <p> According to a report by Israeli journalist Doron Kadosh on Galei Tzahal , the Israeli Army Radio , some 106 Hamas operatives were killed in one night . <p> " This was the most significant night we can remember in the operation in the northern Gaza Strip , " the IDF said . <p> At roughly 9pm on Sunday , the IDF received information about terrorists ' intentions to attack Israeli forces and escape from the Jabaliya refugee camp . <p> Intelligence had indicated plans to exploit the stormy weather for an escape attempt , prompting the operation . <p> Shortly after , several battalions were prepared for ambushes and attack readiness , and after a few hours , reports began flowing to the division commander . <p> The first 15 Hamas operatives approached the forces of the Givati Brigade . Meanwhile , another six approached the soldiers of the multi-dimensional unit . Israeli forces began to engage them in battle , using the ' Zik ' drones . The multi-dimensional unit reportedly managed to capture a terrorist , who during the battle surrendered and was arrested . <p> Terrorists continued to arrive in groups , with some wearing military vests and others armed with Kalashnikovs as they approached the forces . <p> The largest attack attempt was reportedly late at night on the Shaked Battalion of Givati . After 30 terrorists tried to flank the battalion , the Israeli forces opened fire and went on the attack , killing 16 Hamas operatives . <p> Concurrently , in the 52nd Armored Brigade of Division 401 , another 10 terrorists were identified . Using tank fire and drones , the forces encircled the operatives , and in the process , identified another 10 . In the morning , a total of 24 bodies were counted which intelligence identified as Hamas fighters . <p> Also , in the Givati Patrol and the Sabar Battalion - squads of 10 terrorists each were identified and killed by Israeli forces . <p> By the end of the night , the Southern Command counted 106 dead Hamas operatives , while two surrendered and were taken captive . One testified today in the field investigation and said he was part of a squad of more than 10 other terrorists . <p> According to the reports , it is unclear whether each terrorist squad acted alone sporadically , or whether this was a coordinated attack by Hamas.
