He highlights research conducted by Nuphar Ishay-Krien during the First Intifada , which identified several groups of soldiers based on their responses to violence and brutality . Some soldiers felt empowered by the power they held , seeing themselves as untouchable in Gaza , which encouraged their sadism . One soldier 's chilling account stated , " It 's like a drug ... you feel like you are the law , you make the rules . As if from the moment you leave the place called Israel and enter the Gaza Strip , you are God . " <p> Elizur presents accounts from soldiers who engaged in pure sadism without remorse .
