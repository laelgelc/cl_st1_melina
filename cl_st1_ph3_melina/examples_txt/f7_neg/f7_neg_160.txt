<h> American-Israeli soldier held hostage in Gaza was killed on October 7 , says Israel <p> The Israeli military said that Omer Maxim Neutra , an American-Israeli citizen who was previously believed to be alive and held hostage in Gaza , was killed during the Hamas-led attack on October 7 , 2023 . <p> Neutra , 21 , served as a tank platoon commander in the Israel Defence Forces ( IDF ) during the time of the attack , the IDF said , adding that Neutra 's body is still being held hostage in Gaza . <p> It is unclear what caused the IDF to make the announcement . In previous cases when a hostage was declared dead , the finding was based on new intelligence and evidence . <p> American-Israeli Omer Neutra , who was previously believed to be alive and held hostage in Gaza , was killed during the Hamas-led attack on October 7 , 2023 . ' " <p> Israel 's Foreign Minister Israel Katz said in a statement that Neutra " fell in a heroic battle near Nir Oz on October 7 .
