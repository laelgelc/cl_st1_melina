Jerusalem, December 4 ( IANS ) Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has thanked US President-elect Donald Trump after the latter called for the hostages held in Gaza to be released by Hamas the day he takes charge as the President.

Thank you, President Trump."Trump ' s statement followed the release of a video by Hamas showing American-Israeli hostage Edan Alexander pleading for his release.
