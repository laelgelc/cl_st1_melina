But with the Egyptian-mediated ceasefire largely holding , Israel said some exports would be allowed out through its territory as of Monday morning . <p> " Following a security evaluation , a decision has been made for the first time since the end of ( the fighting ) to enable ... ( the ) limited export of agricultural produce from the Gaza Strip , " COGAT , a branch of Israel 's Defense Ministry , said . <p> COGAT said the measure was approved by Prime Minister Naftali Bennett 's government and was " conditional upon the preservation of security stability .
