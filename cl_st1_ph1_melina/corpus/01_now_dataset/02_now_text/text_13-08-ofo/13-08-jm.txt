They were an instant crowd favourite . <p> " Everybody danced well . It was a dream of ours to be number one in the world , " said Ryosuke Zushi , leader of the six-member outfit . <p> " We have big plans . We are taking this title as a symbol of cultural exchange ; to learn reggae dance and explore good food in Jamaica , " he continued . <p> Their choreography was sharp and effortless .
