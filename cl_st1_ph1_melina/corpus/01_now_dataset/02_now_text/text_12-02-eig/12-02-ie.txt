@@755517 <h> AdWorld News <h> Tania Banotti Appointed CEO of IAPI <p> IAPI has appointed Tania Banotti as its new chief executive . Banotti joins IAPI from Theatre Forum , where she was chief executive . <p> She replaces Sean McCrave as CEO who is stepping down to pursue other interests . <p> Theatre Forum is the representative association for the professional performing arts in Ireland and she has been its chief executive since 2003 . Before that she was Chief Executive of Screen Producers Ireland , the national trade association for film and TV independent production companies . For a number of years she worked in public affairs in Brussels representing companies including ITV , the Association of European Commercial Television and the Motion Picture Association of America . She has also worked for the United Nations in the Gaza Strip , Palestine . <p> It is understood that the position attracted considerable interest from within the industry with as many as 25 people applying for the job . Some of these candidates were senior people from within the wider advertising and media industry .
