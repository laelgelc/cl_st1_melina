@@31846685 <h> Mozambique elite police on trial for murder of poll observer <p> Six members of Mozambique 's elite police unit have gone on trial for the murder of a prominent election observer in a case that has gripped the southern African nation . <p> A spokesperson for bereaved relatives holds a portrait of Anastacio Matavele , an election monitor who was gunned down ahead of a key vote last October <p> AFP <p> Anastacio Matavele , 58 , was shot dead at the wheel of his car in the city of Xai-Xai shortly before nationwide elections last October . <p> Over the past week , half a dozen elite police officers have testified in court in the southern province of Gaza , four of whom have confessed to taking part in a plot to murder him . <p> The trial is unprecedented in a country where murders and abductions of members of civil society , opposition politicians and journalists have become routine since 2015 yet are typically sidelined for lack of evidence . <p> The remarkable case only came to light because the attackers had a car accident as they fled the scene . <p> One of the alleged trigger men , policeman Martins Wiliamo , died in the smash along with a second suspect , and a third was arrested . <p> Two other policemen were arrested a few weeks later , as well as a teacher who owned the vehicle used for the crime . <h> Point-blank range <p> Two of the officers have confessed their involvement but have fingered fellow policeman Agapito Matavele as the ringleader . <p> Matavele , who has the same family name as the victim but was not related to him , is on the run . <p> Extraordinary details have emerged of a Mob-style hit that left the victim 's car riddled with 13 bullet holes . <p> " I drove the car alongside the victim 's car and the order to fire came from the back seat , " Assistant Inspector Edson Silica , 34 , the driver on the day of the murder , told the court . <p> " Agapito and Martins Wiliamo lowered the windows and started shooting at point-blank range . " <p> Silica , a police officer for 13 years , said Agapito Matavele knew the details of the mission . <p> As for himself , he maintained that he was only told minutes before the crime that they were going to " assault an old man with a lot of money " . <p> Officer Euclidio Mapulasse , who was later arrested , said he had been on the back seat , sitting between the alleged shooters . <p> " I was in the middle seat . There was no way I could open fire , " Mapulasse said . <p> " The ones who opened fire were Agapito and Martins Wiliamo . " <p> The remaining defendants deny any involvement . <h> Political violenc <p> The assassination sparked an outcry , for it occurred in the runup to crucial elections troubled by violence and accusations of fraud . <p> Just months earlier , President Filipe Nyusi and the opposition Renamo party signed a truce aimed at halting fighting that had flared 27 years after a civil war that killed an estimated million people . <p> The Election Commission awarded victory to Nyusi and his Frelimo party , which has been in power since independence from Portugal in 1975 . <p> In the coming days , relatives of the deceased election observer and the two agents who died in the accident will testify . <p> But why was the murder carried out ? And did the order go higher up the chain of command ? <p> The prosecutors say they have no evidence for suggesting other than that the accused " acted on their own initiative . " <p> One of those arrested , Tudelo Guirugo , 46 , initially told prosecutors that he selected members of the hit squad on the orders of the elite unit 's boss , Alfred Macuacua . <p> But he changed this account in court , saying Macuacua was not involved . " I was desperate -- I thought I could get out of it by implicating him . " <p> Adriano Nuvunga , head of an NGO called the Centre for Democracy and Development , said the killing was " in actual fact a state crime . " <p> " The ones who are implicated are police officers in positions of responsibility , " Nuvunga said . <p> Relatives of the deceased election observer and of the two agents who died in the accident are scheduled testify in the coming days .
