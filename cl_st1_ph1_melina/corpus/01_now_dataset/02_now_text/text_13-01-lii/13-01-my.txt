@@4577872 <h> Govt meets 18 Cuepacs claims since 2007 <p> KUALA LUMPUR , Jan 29 ( Bernama ) -- Eighteen claims made by Cuepacs had been met by the government since 2007 , said its president Datuk Omar Osman . <p> Omar said it was a big achievement due to the government being sensitive to the needs of civil servants during the tenure of Tun Abdullah Ahmad Badawi and Datuk Seri Najib Tun Razak as prime minister . <p> " Among the important matters resolved were the salary adjustment which was done twice , extension of the retirement age , minimum pension payment , and increase in the cost of living allowance , service grades and maternity leave to 90 days , apart from various incentives . <p> " This proves that the government is always sensitive to the needs of the 1.4 million public servants in the country , " he said after the ' Cuepacs Dinner with the Media ' , here , today . <p> Omar said the success of Cuepacs ' claims could motivate civil servants to improve work productivity and the quality of services @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ other outstanding claims to be met by the government . <p> " We are waiting for the good news on salary increase for civil servants whose salary has reached the maximum and streamlining of the fixed monthly housing allowance to RM450 . <p> " We are also awaiting the announcement on the new scheme by the Special Commission for the Transformation of the Civil Service and Public Service Department to replace the Malaysia Remuneration Scheme ( SSM ) , " Omar said . <p> At the function , Cuepacs also gave a contribution of RM10,000 for the Palestinians in Gaza . <p> The mock cheque for the amount was handed over by Omar to a media representative , Bernama 's Photo Unit head Mustaza Mohd Yusoff to be channelled to Media Prima Berhad 's Palestine Humanitarian Fund .
