The poll , held between 26 and 28 May 2014 , resulted in a landslide victory for el-Sisi . Sisi was sworn into office as President of Egypt on 8 June 2014 . The Muslim Brotherhood and some liberal and secular activist groups boycotted the vote . Even though the military-backed authorities extended voting to a third day , the 46 per cent turnout was lower than the 52 per cent turnout in the 2012 election .
