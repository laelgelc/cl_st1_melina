@@32154234 <h> Nigeria Barred From 2022 US Visa Lottery <p> Nigerians have been barred from participating in the United States ' visa lottery in 2022 <p> This was disclosed in a document titled ' Instructions for the 2022 diversity immigrant visa program ( dv-2022 ) ' obtained from the US ' website on Thursday , October 15 . <p> " In Africa , natives of Nigeria are not eligible for this year 's Diversity Visa program , " the document stated . <p> It stated that Nigeria was barred because more than 50,000 of its citizens immigrated to the United States in the previous five years . <p> Citizens of other African countries are eligible to apply , but " persons born in the areas administered prior to June 1967 by Israel , Jordan , Syria , and Egypt are chargeable , respectively , to Israel , Jordan , Syria , and Egypt . Persons born in the Gaza Strip are chargeable to Egypt ; persons born in the West Bank are chargeable to Jordan ; persons born in the Golan Heights are chargeable to Syria .
