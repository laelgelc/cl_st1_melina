" <p> El Saadi said agency 's mission was to show solidarity with the people of Palestine . <p> " As citizens of a democratic and free South Africa that fought and defeated apartheid , we believe that it is up to us to ensure that their plight is brought to the world 's attention , " he said . <p> The agency was formed in 2006 as a response to pleas from Palestinians who required international help . <p> Last year , the members of the NGO drove 10 000km across Botswana , Zambia , Tanzania , Kenya , Ethiopia , Sudan and Egypt , to raise awareness throughout Africa before reaching their final destination in Gaza . <p> While there , they donated 122 generators to 53 hospitals and clinics , which have served to help save the lives of up to 600 people a day . <p> " This year 's ambitious trip requires a great deal of planning , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we urge South Africans to get involved and support us in delivering this aid , " eh said . <p> For more information , go to www.sarelief.net or call 031 306 7738 .
