@@897809 <h> World War One centenary plans take shape <p> FOCAL POINT : The Auckland War Memorial Museum is home to annual Anzac Day commemorations . <h> Relevant offers <p> Auckland 's plans to commemorate the centenary of the First World War could include a significant public artwork and a heritage trail . <p> Tomorrow 's parks , recreation and heritage council agenda reveals possible events and memorials to mark the four-year war , as well the 100th anniversary of Anzac troops landing at Gallipoli in 1915 . <p> The artwork would be displayed in the Auckland Domain - home to the Auckland War Memorial Museum , and a focal point for annual Anzac Day celebrations . <p> It would commemorate either Anzac Day in 2015 - the centenary of Gallipoli - or the centenary of the end of the war on November 11 , 2018 . <p> Another project involves identifying sites around the city significant to our involvement in the war . Information on each site would be presented on a plaque and included on a World War One heritage trail . <p> Events are also proposed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Passchendaele , Messines , Ypres , Bapume and Gaza . <p> An initiative called ' Our Boys ' is proposed to encourage local communities and schools to research and document local men who fought in the war . <p> Councillors will tomorrow discuss a team to organise and coordinate commemorations . <p> Parks , recreation and heritage officers and the Mayor 's office have begun preliminary work but no coordinated approach has been adopted . <p> New Zealand joined the war effort on August 4 , 1914 , alongside Britain . More servicemen and women were sent per head of capita than any other country and almost a fifth did not return - of the 18,166 who lost their lives 7,297 were from the Auckland region . <p> Work is already underway ensuring memorials and cenotaphs are well presented in the lead up to the war 's centenary . <p> In addition to regional activities Auckland is expected to host a number of national events which the Ministry of Culture and Heritage is working on , including the 2015 Anzac Day service at Auckland Museum .
