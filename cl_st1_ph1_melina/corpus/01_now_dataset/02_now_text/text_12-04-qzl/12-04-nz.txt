More than 40 were injured , and over a hundred left homeless . <p> Many schools were also destroyed , creating such a shortage that there are two sessions a day , and children attend either in the morning , or in the afternoon . <p> Such death and destruction is not merely a matter of the past -- it is ongoing , and constant , with drones and F-16s a perpetual presence in the skies above them , rendering the children -- and many adults -- fearful that at any moment they could be next , wondering will their home , their school , their loved ones , still be there when they return . <p> Against such a backdrop , the Association holds sessions twice a day for severely traumatised children , morning sessions for the **25;427;TOOLONG , and afternoon @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , I was amazed by the enthusiasm of the kids , and their excitement at having a New Zealander visit them .
