Whatever the merits of both sides , the United Nations membership has taken a decision which acknowledges that the Palestinians are a sovereign state . <p> Thursday , November 29 , 2012 was a day of joy and vindication for the Palestinians , a moment to savour amid the devastation and misery which is their reality . Israel and the United States have consistently opposed such a decision and voted against it on Thursday . <p> This is an implicit recognition of Palestine as a sovereign state .
