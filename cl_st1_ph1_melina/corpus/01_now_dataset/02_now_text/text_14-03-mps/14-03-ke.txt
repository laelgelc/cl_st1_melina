@@1866051 <h> Nairobi County Assembly passes a motion to deal with street families <p> Nairobi , Kenya : Members of Nairobi County Assembly have passed a motion to compel the County government to address the issue of street children and families within the city centre . <p> The motion moved by Kayole South Ward representative Elizabeth Manyala and passed on Tuesday seeks to have the urchins removed from the streets and further rehabilitated . <p> Members of the assembly pointed out that the street families have increased , becoming an economic problem and social nightmare . <p> " These street children are now extorting money from innocent pedestrians and motorists through intimidation and threats of use of human waste . This assembly urges the county government to move with speed and address this matter " stated Manyala . <p> The assembly wants a quick fix as a permanent solution is sought , saying some organisations have turned the matter into a cash cow . <p> Some who were rehabilitated in Social halls in Bahati and Kayole have gone back to the streets due to lack of some basic necessities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ especially when they relieve themselves along the streets and businesses ' doorsteps . <p> In the last budget the county government set aside Sh180 million for construction of a rehabilitation centre at Ruai , in Embakasi . <p> In Kayole the street urchins have joined criminal gangs that have been terrorizing residents and are spreading to other parts of the city . <p> " The criminal gang , Gaza boys and girls in Kayole South is composed of young kids previously in the streets . They have guns and knives and very dangerous . They are also spilling to the city centre " said Manyala . <p> Ziwani representative Millicent Mugadi called upon the county government to collaborate with the neighbouring counties to stop the urchins ' exodus from there to Nairobi .
