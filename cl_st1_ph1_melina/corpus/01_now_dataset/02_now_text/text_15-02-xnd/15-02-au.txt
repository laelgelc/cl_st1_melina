" <p> One of the graffiti works is of Greek goddess Niobe weeping , while another depicts an Israeli watchtower turned into a children 's ride . <p> A video uploaded on YouTube by Banksy is laced with ironic and provocative comments and purports to show Banksy climbing through a network of tunnels to an area of Gaza filled with concrete rubble and children sitting amongst it . <p> The end of the two-minute clip shows graffiti on a wall which reads : " If we wash our hands of the conflict between the powerful and the powerless , we side with the powerful and do n't remain neutral " .
