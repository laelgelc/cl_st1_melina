@@1349214 <h> Noam Chomsky joins GAA to set up team in Gaza <p> Noam Chomsky is a man of many different hats . He 's a Professor of Linguistics at MIT but he 's probably better known worldwide for his incisive criticism of big media and US foreign policy ( although not necessarily in that order ) . <p> Now he 's wearing yet another hat as co-president ( with former Tyrone player Peter Canavan ) of the new GAA club in Gaza . <p> Yes , that Gaza . <p> A story in today 's Irish Examiner tells how Chomsky and Canavan are the honorary co-presidents of the team set up by Irish activist John Hurson . <p> Apparently Chomsky got involved after visiting Ireland last year and being introduced to Hurson . <p> Chomsky said he was honored to be asked . " It is indeed a great honour to be asked to become involved with the Gaza GAA club , and with its deep community spirit , the GAA will bring a rich reward to the people in Gaza , " Chomsky said . " I @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ organisation . " <p> Hurson tells the Examiner : " The 10 year-olds here are the same as the 10 year-olds in Ireland . Every child loves to play football , to play outdoors with their mates , but in Gaza , because of the siege , people ca n't get provisions in and out . <p> " That includes vital stuff and simple things like footballs . Football may as well be banned in Gaza -- so children ca n't do that most basic of things , play ball together . " <p> Hurson joined the recent " Viva Palestina " convoy to Gaza which saw GAA footballs , goalposts and jerseys take their place in the convoy which was primarily made up of vital medical equipment . <p> Hurson says he knows the football gear is not as important as the medical supplies . But still . <p> " By bringing Gaelic football to people in Gaza , they know they are part of and connected to a 1 million strong organisation that cares about them . "
