" <p> About this Series <p> Because the persecution of Christians in the Islamic world is on its way to reaching epidemic proportions , " Muslim Persecution of Christians " was developed to collate some -- by no means all -- of the instances of persecution that surface each month . It serves two purposes : <p> To document that which the mainstream media does not : the habitual , if not chronic , Muslim persecution of Christians . <p> To show that such persecution is not " random , " but systematic and interrelated -- that it is rooted in a worldview inspired by Sharia . <p> Accordingly , whatever the anecdote of persecution , it typically fits under a specific theme , including hatred for churches and other Christian symbols ; sexual abuse of Christian women ; forced conversions to Islam ; apostasy and blasphemy laws that criminalize and punish with death those who " offend " Islam ; theft and plunder in lieu of jizya ( financial tribute expected from non-Muslims ) ; overall expectations for Christians to behave @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ; and simple violence and murder .
