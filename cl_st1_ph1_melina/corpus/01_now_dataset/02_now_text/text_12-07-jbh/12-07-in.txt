These protest meetings express the rage and frustrations of the millions of people at the exclusionary nature of globalisation , which has brought immense dividends to the corporate houses and a few individuals , but has left the vast majority no better off than they were , or , even worse off than they were earlier . The disparity in incomes between the rich and the poor has widened among and within nations .
