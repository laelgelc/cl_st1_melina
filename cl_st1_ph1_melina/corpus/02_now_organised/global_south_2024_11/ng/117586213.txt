<h> Tinubu Returns To Nigeria After Arab-Islamic Summit In Riyadh <h> President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has arrived Abuja after attending the Riyadh Joint Arab-Islamic Summit on the middle-east . <p> Naija News recalls that dduring Tinubu 's participation in the summit , President Tinubu called for an end to Israeli aggression in Gaza , warning that the conflict in Palestine has persisted for far too long , inflicting immeasurable suffering . <p> Addressing the extraordinary Arab-Islamic Summit , convened to address the current situation in the Middle East , President Tinubu expressed deep concern on the humanitarian conditions in Gaza . <p> The one-day summit was a follow-up to the Riyadh summit last year , and was attended by Heads of State and Government of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) and the League of Arab States .
