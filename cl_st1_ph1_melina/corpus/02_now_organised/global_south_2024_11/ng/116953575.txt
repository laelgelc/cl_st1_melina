<h> Israeli army demolishes UNRWA office in Nur Shams camp in West Bank <p> The Israeli army demolished the office of the UN agency for Palestinian refugees ( UNRWA ) in the Nur Shams refugee camp in the northern West Bank city of Tulkarem on Thursday , a local official said . <p> " Israeli bulldozers brought down the building , through which the UN agency runs the daily needs of residents , " Nehad al-Shaweeh , who heads a local service committee in the camp , told Anadolu . <p> " The building was partially damaged during previous Israeli raids and today it was completely demolished by Israeli forces , " he added . <p> The demolition came three days after the Knesset ( Israel 's parliament ) passed a law on Monday banning UNRWA from operating , which would eventually affect its work in Gaza , the occupied West Bank , and East Jerusalem . The legislation will take effect in 90 days . <p> UNRWA Commissioner-General Philippe Lazzarini called the Israeli ban " unprecedented and dangerous , " and in violation of the UN Charter . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are two schools and a health center run by UNRWA in Nur Shams camp . <p> Early on Thursday , two Palestinians , including a child , were killed in an Israeli drone strike in the camp , according to the Health Ministry . <p> Tension has been running high across the West Bank due to Israel 's brutal offensive on the Gaza Strip , which has killed over 43,100 people , mostly women and children , following a Hamas attack last year . <p> At least 766 Palestinians have since been killed and around 6,300 injured by Israeli army fire in the West Bank , according to the Palestinian Health Ministry .
