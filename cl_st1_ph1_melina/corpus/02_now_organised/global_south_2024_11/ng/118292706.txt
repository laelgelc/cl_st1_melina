It has called for an investigation by WCK and international organizations into the hiring of workers allegedly connected to terrorist activities . <p> World Central Kitchen expressed profound sorrow , confirming its staff 's deaths and announcing a pause in its Gaza operations . <p> " At this time , we are working with incomplete information and urgently seeking details , " the organization said , adding it had " no knowledge " of any staff 's ties to the October 7 attacks . <p> This marks the second fatal incident involving WCK workers this year . <p> In April , seven WCK staff were killed in an Israeli strike on an aid convoy , leading to widespread criticism and subsequent IDF accountability measures .
