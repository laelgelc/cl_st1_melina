<h> Gaza : Israeli PM , Netanyahu did worse than Hitler -- Fani-Kayode <p> Fani-Kayode claimed that what Netanyahu did to the Palestinians is worse than what Hitler did to the Jews . <p> The statement comes days after judges at the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) issued arrest warrants for Netanyahu . <p> A warrant was also issued for Mohammed Deif of Hamas , although Israel has said he was killed in an air strike in Gaza in July . <p> In a post on his X handle on Saturday , Fani-Kayode said Hitler never buried over 100,000 children alive under the rubble of destruction . <p> He said : " What Netanyahu did to the Palestinians is worse than what Hitler did to the Jews . As evil as Hitler was at least he never buried over 100,000 children alive under the rubble of destruction in the space of one year . "
