What did the people of Lebanon do ? What did the people of Valencia do ? We are hearing stories of earthquakes and incredible things happening that are sweeping the lives of hundreds of thousands of people on a daily basis . <p> " But in Nigeria , it is us who are collectively turning the knife on ourselves .
