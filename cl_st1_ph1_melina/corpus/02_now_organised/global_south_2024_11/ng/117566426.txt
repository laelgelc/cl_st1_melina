<h> President Tinubu returns to Abuja after attending Arab summit on Gaza war <h> President Tinubu returns to Abuja after attending Arab summit on Gaza war <p> President Bola Tinubu has returned to Abuja , Nigeria , after attending the extraordinary joint Arab-Islamic summit in Riyadh , Saudi Arabia . <p> The summit focused on the current situation in the Middle East . <p> The presidential aeroplane touched ground at the Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport at about 8 pm on Tuesday . <p> He was received on arrival by a delegation of top government functionaries led by the chief of staff , Femi Gbajabiamila . <p> The president joined other heads of state and government of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation , OIC , and the League of Arab States who attended the one-day summit . <p> At the summit on Monday , President Tinubu called for an end to Israeli aggression in Gaza , acknowledging that " the conflict has persisted for far too long , inflicting immeasurable suffering , " and expressed deep concern about the humanitarian conditions in Gaza .
