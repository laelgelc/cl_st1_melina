<h> Tinubu back to Abuja from Saudi Arabia summit <p> President Bola Tinubu is back in Abuja after his trip to Riyadh in Saudi Arabia , where he participated in the just concluded extraordinary Arab-Islamic summit . <p> The president arrived at the Presidential Wing of the Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport at about 8 pm and was received on arrival by senior members of the administration , including the Chief of Staff , Femi Gbajabiamila . <p> Also on hand to receive him were ministers of Federal Capital Territory , Nyesom Wike , Finance and Coordinating Minister of the Economy , Wale Edun , and National Chairman of the ruling All Progressives Congress ( APC ) , Abdullahi Ganduje . <p> While at the Summit , Tinubu called for decisive global action to end the ongoing violence in Gaza , urging world leaders to move beyond symbolic condemnations . <p> He called on parties in the conflict in the Middle East to respect the principles of proportionality and the basic rights of civilians , consistent with global legal and diplomatic frameworks . <p> " In a rules-based international order , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ take proportionality into account , in line with global legal , diplomatic -- and moral -- frameworks . <p> " An entire civilian population , their dreams and futures , can not be dismissed as collateral , " he said . <p> Meanwhile , on the sidelines of the Summit , President Tinubu held talks with Saudi Arabia 's Crown , Prince Mohammed bin Salman . <p> The President was accompanied on the trip by key officials , including the Minister of Foreign Affairs , Yusuf Tuggar ; the National Security Adviser ( NSA ) , Nuhu Ribadu ; Minister of Information and National Orientation , Mohammed Idris ; and the Director General of the National Intelligence Agency ( NIA ) , Mohammed Mohammed .
