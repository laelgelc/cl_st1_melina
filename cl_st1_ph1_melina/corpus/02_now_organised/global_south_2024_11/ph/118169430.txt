<h> Diplomacy will resolve Gaza crisis -- Marcos <p> President Ferdinand Marcos Jr . emphasized on Wednesday the significance of diplomacy in addressing the worsening Israeli attacks in Gaza . <p> In observance of International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People , Marcos said there is an urgent need to de-escalate violence and prioritize humanitarian access . <p> " The Philippines stands united with the Palestinian people in their collective aspiration for enduring peace and prosperity , " the president said . <p> " We call for rapid , safe , unimpeded , and sustained humanitarian access to all those in need . Diplomacy remains the cornerstone of achieving lasting peace in the Middle East , " he added . <p> Marcos also condemned attacks on civilians and civilian infrastructure , which have led to staggering casualties , especially among women and children . <p> " We are gravely concerned over the catastrophic humanitarian situation in Gaza and the increasing tensions in the Middle East .
