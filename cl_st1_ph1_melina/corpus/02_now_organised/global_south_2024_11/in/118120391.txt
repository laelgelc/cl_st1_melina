( AP ) <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced his readiness to implement a ceasefire agreement with Lebanon on Tuesday . The PM also pledged to respond decisively to any breach by Hezbollah . Together , we will continue until victory , " he affirmed , emphasizing Israel 's maintained military flexibility in collaboration with the United States .
