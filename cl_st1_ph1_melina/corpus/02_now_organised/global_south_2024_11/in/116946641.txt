Citi analysts have warned investors to sell any rally in the equity market if the Republicans sweep the elections . But that will not matter if high tariffs and enormous fiscal deficits lead to trade disruption , higher inflation , higher interest rates and a stronger dollar , which is what analysts are saying will happen if there 's a Republican sweep . As a matter of fact , the so-called Trump trades ahead of the election have already pushed up US bond yields and the dollar .
