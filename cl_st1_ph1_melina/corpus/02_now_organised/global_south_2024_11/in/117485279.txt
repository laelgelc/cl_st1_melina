<p> Qatar has announced that it will suspend its mediation efforts to broker a ceasefire between Hamas and Israel in Gaza . This decision comes after months of unsuccessful negotiations . Qatar , which has previously facilitated several rounds of peace talks , expressed that it could not continue its role unless both parties demonstrate a genuine commitment to finding a resolution . The failure of recent discussions , including one held in mid-October , prompted Qatar to halt its mediation efforts . The country has communicated this move to both Hamas and the Israeli government , as well as to international stakeholders , including the U.S. administration . Qatar has also emphasized that it will only re-engage in negotiations when there is real progress toward peace , and it condemned the continued failure to honor past agreements . The suspension of Qatar 's role adds another layer of complexity to the already tense situation in Gaza
