<p> This week there will be thousands of Diwali celebrations happening across the world , but there 's no denying that as a diaspora Desi things may look a little different when celebrating the Festival Of Lights . <p> The festival symbolises the victory of good over evil and is traditionally a time for family gatherings , mega feasting , and festivities . However , being away from the motherland during Diwali means that we have to get creative during this special time and come up with some new traditions . <p> From watching The Office Diwali episode to bringing mitthai ( Indian sweets ) to your actual office , everybody has their own thing to connect to their culture .
