The G7 , G20 , Quad . But the most effective is the BRICS which is a group initiated by Brazil , Russia , India , China , and South Africa . It is an important grouping bringing together the major emerging economies of the world . It represents 41% of the world population , 24% of the world GDP and over 16% share in the world trade . Formed in the year 2011 it deals with the important issues under the three pillars of political security , just economic cooperation and respect to cultural values of the people of the world .
