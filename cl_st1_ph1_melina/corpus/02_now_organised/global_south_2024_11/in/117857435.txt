<p> Brasilia : United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres urged the G20 nations to take lead in addressing global challenges , including achieving peace in war-ridden regions such as Gaza , Lebanon , Ukraine and Sudan . <p> He emphasised that peace requires actions grounded in the values of the UN Charter , the rule of law , and the principles of sovereignty and territorial integrity . <p> ' ' We must step up for peace in Gaza , Lebanon , Ukraine and Sudan . Everywhere , peace requires actions grounded in the values of the UN Charter , the rule of law , and the principles of sovereignty , political independence and the territorial integrity of States , " Guterres said . <p> On finance , the Secretary-General further highlighted the situation of vulnerable countries which " face tremendous headwinds and obstacles that are not of their making .
