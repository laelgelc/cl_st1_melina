<h> In Short <p> More than 3,000 people have been killed in Lebanon during 13 months of fighting between Israel and Hezbollah , Lebanon 's Health Ministry said Monday . At least 13,492 have been injured . <p> Hezbollah began firing rockets into northern Israel the day after Hamas ' surprise attack on Israel on October 7 , 2023 ignited the war in Gaza . Hezbollah and Hamas are both allied with Iran . <p> advertisement <p> The conflict dramatically escalated on September 23 with intense Israeli airstrikes on south and east Lebanon as well as Beirut 's southern suburbs , leaving hundreds dead and leading to the displacement of nearly 1.2 million people . <p> Israel began a ground invasion of south Lebanon on October 1 , causing wide destruction in border villages but making little advances on the ground inside Lebanon . <p> In Israel , 72 people have been killed by Hezbollah attacks , including 30 soldiers .
