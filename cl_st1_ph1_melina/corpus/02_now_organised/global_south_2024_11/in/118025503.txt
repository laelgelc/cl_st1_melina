The expectation is that Trump will push various parties to cease hostilities while simultaneously taking a harder stance against Iran . <p> It was a different Middle East during Trump 's first term . While the Accords still stand in light of the crisis in Gaza , as civilian casualties rise with little to no end of hostilities in sight , the Arab-Israeli friction is palpable , and the region Trump left behind has drastically changed .
