The buffer zone is to be patrolled by Lebanese forces and UN peacekeepers . However , ongoing skirmishes have raised concerns about the agreement 's durability . <p> The ceasefire follows 14 months of intense conflict , beginning after Hezbollah 's solidarity strikes following Hamas ' October 2023 attack from Gaza . Israeli airstrikes escalated in response , culminating in an all-out war in September .
