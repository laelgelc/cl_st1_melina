<h> Just In <h> Israel signs 5.2-billion USD deal to purchase 25 F-15 fighter jets <h> The Israeli Defence Ministry announced Thursday that it has signed a $5.2 billion deal with US-based Boeing to acquire 25 advanced F-15 fighter jets . <p> Jerusalem : The Israeli Defence Ministry announced Thursday that it has signed a $5.2 billion deal with US-based Boeing to acquire 25 advanced F-15 fighter jets . <p> The ministry said in a statement that the deal is part of a broader aid package approved by the US administration and Congress earlier this year . The agreement includes an option for 25 additional aircraft . <p> The deal , finalised on Wednesday with the US administration and Boeing , involves the delivery of F-15IA fighter jets equipped with weapon systems integrated with Israeli weapons , enhanced range , and greater payload capacity . <p> " These advantages will enable the Israeli Air Force to maintain its strategic superiority in addressing current and future challenges in the Middle East , " the ministry said . <p> Delivery is set to begin in 2031 , with four to six aircraft @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reported . <p> Eyal Zamir , director-general of the Israeli Defence Ministry , emphasised that the acquisition of the new F-15 squadron , in addition to the third F-35 squadron procured earlier this year , represents " a historic enhancement of our air power and strategic reach -- capabilities that proved crucial during the current war . " <p> Zamir noted that since the onset of the war in the Gaza Strip on October 7 , 2023 , Israel has secured procurement agreements worth nearly $40 billion .
