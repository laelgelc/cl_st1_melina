<h> Middle East crisis weighs on McDonald 's sales in West , South India &amp; it 's not ' lovin it ' <p> McDonald 's sales in West and South India have been severely impacted by geopolitical unrest , with some locations experiencing a 50% drop in sales . The decline is attributed to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East and its impact on consumer sentiment towards American brands . <p> NEW DELHI : Global geopolitical unrest , particularly the ongoing conflict in the Middle East , has significantly impacted McDonald 's sales in West and South India , with some locations seeing sales fall to less than half of pre-crisis levels . " I do n't think a lot of stores which got impacted last year have come up to the level of what they were pre-crisis , " Saurabh Kalra , managing director of Westlife Foodworld , McDonald 's exclusive operator in the region said , as quoted by Economic Times.Kalra noted that some stores in Mumbai , particularly in areas like Mumbai Central and Mazgaon , have been especially hard-hit . And I can quote a few examples of Mumbai Central , Mazgaon where what we used to do , we are not even doing half of what we used to do . So , it remains tough in some of those belts , " Kalra said <p> Westlife Foodworld reported a drastic 98.4% drop in profits for the September quarter , down to just Rs 35.7 lakh , while same-store sales growth declined 6.5% . The company attributed these declines to " subdued in-store business , " reflecting a broader trend of stress in the quick-service restaurant ( QSR ) industry in India . Other major QSR chains , including Sapphire Foods ( Pizza Hut and KFC ) and Devyani International , have similarly cited rising competition and food inflation as factors squeezing profits . <p> The impact of the Israel-Gaza conflict is not limited to India .
