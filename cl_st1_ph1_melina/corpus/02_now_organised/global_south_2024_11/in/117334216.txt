What is more , in the context of at least two of these wars , the possibility of these leading to World War 3 has continued to exist to various extents during the greater part of the year . The number of people displaced and affected by conflict related humanitarian crisis are also extremely high , even as the shortage of funds for meeting their essential needs is extremely acute . There has been a tendency , as in Sudan , Gaza and Lebanon , for conflict related devastation and displacement to spread extremely fast .
