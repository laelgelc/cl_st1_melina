<p> Residents stand next to destroyed buildings after they returned to Qana village , southern Lebanon , Thursday , Nov. 28 , 2024 following a ceasefire between Israel and Hezbollah that went into effect on Wednesday <p> Photo : AP <p> Beirut : Lebanon authorities on Thursday claimed that two people were wounded by Israeli fire in the southern part of the country . The alleged incident took place on the second day of the ceasefire between Israel and the Hezbollah militant group . <p> However , the Israeli military said it had fired at people trying to return to certain areas , reported The Associated Press . As per Lebanon 's state-run National News Agency two people were wounded by Israeli fire in Markaba , close to the border . <p> National News Agency further claimed Israel fired artillery in three other locations near the border . There are no reports of any casualties in the ceasefire violation . <p> The agreement was brokered by the United States and France . It includes an initial two-month cease-fire in which Hezbollah militants are to withdraw north of the Litani River @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the border . The buffer zone will also be patrolled by Lebanese troops and UN peacekeepers . <p> It ended 14 14 months of conflict between Israel and Hezbollah that began a day after the attack by Hamas on Israel on October 7 , 2023 . Israel retaliated with airstrikes , and the conflict intensified for nearly a year before turning into an all-out war in mid-September this year . Israel 's war against Hamas in Gaza is still raging with no end in sight . <p> More than 3,760 people were killed by Israeli fire in Lebanon during the conflict , many of them civilians , according to Lebanese health officials . Meanwhile , 70 people were also reportedly killed in Israel .
