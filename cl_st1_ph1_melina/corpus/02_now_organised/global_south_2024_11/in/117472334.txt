" " Famine thresholds may have already been crossed or else will be in the near future , " said the alert.On October 17 , the body projected that the number of people in Gaza facing " catastrophic " food insecurity between November and April 2025 would reach 345,000 , or 16 percent of the population . <p> The Integrated Food Security Phase Classification @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ -- a situation when " starvation , death , destitution and extremely critical acute malnutrition levels are evident .
