<p> As Donald Trump prepares to begin his second term on 20 January 2025 , there is growing speculation about his likely ' Africa policy ' . During his first term , Trump largely neglected Africa and even made some derogatory comments about the continent . He has never visited Africa and has shown little interest in expanding United States ( US ) military operations there . While he has refrained from intervening in the internal affairs of African nations , it is clear that Africa has not been a key priority for his administration . Trump 's second term seeks to strengthen his " America First " foreign policy stance , which focuses on pragmatic , investment-driven strategies centred on American interests .
