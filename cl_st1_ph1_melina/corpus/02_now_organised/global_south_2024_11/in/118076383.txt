<p> Hezbollah on Sunday claimed 50 attacks on Israeli troops , military positions and towns across the border and in south Lebanon , while the Israeli army reported 250 projectiles launched into Israeli territory . <p> It marks one of the highest number of attacks Hezbollah has claimed in a single day since it initiated cross-border clashes with the Israeli army in October last year over the Gaza war . <p> On September 23 , Israel intensified its air campaign in Lebanon , mainly targeting Hezbollah bastions in the south and east and in south Beirut , later sending ground troops across the border . <p> Israeli ground forces have entered several south Lebanon villages and towns near the border , including Khiam , where the NNA on Monday reported fierce clashes with Hezbollah fighters . <p> The conflict has killed at least 3,754 people in Lebanon since October 2023 , according to the health ministry , most of them since September . <p> On the Israeli side , authorities say at least 82 soldiers and 47 civilians have been killed .
