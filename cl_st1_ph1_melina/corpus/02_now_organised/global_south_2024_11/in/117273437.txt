<p> With only hours left before the November 5 U.S. presidential election , candidates Donald Trump and Kamala Harris are rallying their supporters with confident and high-energy messages . In Georgia , Trump urged his supporters to vote , promising to fix what he claims Harris has broken and to make America " party time " again . His final promises included tax cuts , reducing inflation , slashing prices , and bringing back factories to America , particularly to swing states like Georgia . Meanwhile , Harris campaigned in Michigan , where she spoke with optimism about uniting the nation and working together to achieve shared dreams . She also addressed the humanitarian crisis in Gaza , pledging to end the suffering and secure peace if elected . Polls reflect a tight race , with AtlasIntel 's latest numbers giving Trump a slight edge in swing states , at 49% versus Harris 's 47% . Reuters/Ipsos and ABC 's 538 polls show Harris leading narrowly , though her advantage has dwindled since September .
