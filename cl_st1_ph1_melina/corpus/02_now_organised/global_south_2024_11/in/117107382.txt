<h> Egypt hosts Fatah-Hamas ceasefire talks on Gaza <h> Cairo hosts discussions between Fatah and Hamas on forming a committee to manage Gaza , as part of post-war plans . The talks are part of Egypt 's mediation efforts to reach a ceasefire and facilitate humanitarian aid . Hamas aims for a comprehensive agreement , including a hostages-for-prisoners swap , while Israeli PM Netanyahu insists the war can only end with Hamas ' eradication . <p> IANS <p> Cairo is hosting meetings between Fatah and Hamas to discuss forming a committee to run Gaza as part of post-war plans , a senior Egyptian security source has told Egypt 's state affiliated Al Qahera News TV . <p> The move is part of Egypt 's mediation efforts involving Palestinian and Israeli parties to reach a ceasefire and expand humanitarian aid access . <p> The source also said that Hamas insists that talks should lead to a comprehensive agreement that secures an end to the war , and also achieves a hostages-for-prisoners swap deal . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu , however , has said war can only end once Hamas is eradicated .
