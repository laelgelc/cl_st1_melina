<h> West Asia Tensions : Ceasefire In Place Between Israel And Lebanon <p> The ceasefire announced Tuesday is considered a major step toward ending nearly 14 months of fighting sparked by the ongoing war in Gaza between Israel and Hamas . The ceasefire calls for an initial two-month halt to fighting .
