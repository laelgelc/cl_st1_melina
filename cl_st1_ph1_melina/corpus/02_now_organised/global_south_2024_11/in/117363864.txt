<h> Israel signs 5.2-bln-USD deal to purchase 25 F-15 fighter jets <p> JERUSALEM , Nov 7 : The Israeli Defense Ministry announced Thursday that it has signed a 5.2-billion-U.S. dollar deal with U.S.-based Boeing to acquire 25 advanced F-15 fighter jets . The ministry said in a statement that the deal is part of a broader aid package approved by the U.S. administration and Congress earlier this year . The agreement includes an option for 25 additional aircraft . The deal , finalised on Wednesday with the U.S. administration and Boeing , involves the delivery of F-15IA fighter jets equipped with weapon systems integrated with Israeli weapons , enhanced range , and greater payload capacity . " These advantages will enable the Israeli Air Force to maintain its strategic superiority in addressing current and future challenges in the Middle East , " the ministry said . Delivery is set to begin in 2031 , with four to six aircraft expected to be supplied each year .
