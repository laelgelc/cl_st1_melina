An extension to the established DHL Global Connectedness Report , it tracks how flows of trade , capital , information , and people move around the world . <p> The DHL Global Connectedness Tracker measures international relative to domestic activity on a spectrum from 0% to 100% . Currently at 25% , it shows that globalization is holding steady at a record high , highlighting the resilience of international flows in the face of geopolitical tensions and uncertainty .
