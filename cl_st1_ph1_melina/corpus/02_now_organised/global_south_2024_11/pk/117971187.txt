<h> Ireland ready to detain Netanyahu if he visits , says PM Simon Harris <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu would be detained if he arrives in Ireland , Irish Prime Minister Simon Harris said on Friday after the International Criminal Court issued an arrest warrant . <p> Asked by state broadcaster RTE if Ireland would arrest the Israeli prime minister if he came to Ireland , Harris said : " Yes , absolutely . " " We support international courts and we apply their warrants , " he added . <p> The ICC issued warrants for Netanyahu and his former defence minister Yoav Gallant on Thursday " for crimes against humanity and war crimes " committed between October 8 , 2023 , and May 20 this year . <p> It said there were " reasonable grounds " to believe the pair bore " criminal responsibility " for using starvation as a method of warfare and intentionally attacking civilians . <p> Netanyahu denounced the move as anti-Semitic and the court 's accusations as " absurd and false " . <p> The court also issued an arrest warrant for Mohammed Deif @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Israel said Deif was killed in an air strike in July . Hamas has not confirmed his death . <p> Harris told RTE that Ireland would also execute the warrant against Deif . The ICC has not been able to determine whether he was dead or alive . <p> Relations between Ireland and Israel have deteriorated since Dublin recognised the Palestinian state last May , a move that prompted Israel to recall its ambassador . <p> Ireland 's foreign minister Micheal Martin said Friday he disagreed with US President Joe Biden 's depiction of the warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant as " outrageous " . <p> Martin told Newstalk radio that war crimes have been committed in Gaza . " It 's a collective punishment of the people ... it 's genocidal , " he said .
