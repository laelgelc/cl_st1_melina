<h> 14 Palestinians killed in Israeli attacks across Gaza <p> At least 14 Palestinians were killed and several others injured in Israeli airstrikes across the Gaza Strip , local media said on Tuesday . <p> The official Palestinian news agency Wafa said six Palestinians , including women and children , were killed in a strike targeting a house late Monday in the Al-Tuffah neighborhood in northeastern Gaza City . <p> Two more Palestinians were killed in Israeli artillery shelling in the Zeitoun neighborhood in eastern Gaza City , Wafa added . <p> Three others were killed in an Israeli airstrike on a group of people in the Shati refugee camp in western Gaza City . <p> Another strike hit a house in the northern town of Jabalia early Tuesday , killing two people and injuring several others , a medical source said . <p> In the central Gaza Strip , at least one Palestinian was killed in an Israeli drone strike on the Bureij refugee camp . <p> Israeli military vehicles also opened fire towards northern and western parts of Rafah city in southern Gaza , witnesses said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ following a Hamas attack last year , killing more than 44,230 people , most of them women and children , and injuring over 104,600 . <p> The second year of genocide in Gaza has drawn growing international condemnation , with figures and institutions labeling the attacks and blocking of aid deliveries as a deliberate attempt to destroy a population . <p> On Thursday , the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza . <p> Israel also faces a genocide case at the International Court of Justice over its brutal war on Gaza .
