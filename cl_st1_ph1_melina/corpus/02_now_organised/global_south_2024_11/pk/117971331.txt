" <p> In doing so , it also unanimously rejected Israel 's challenges to jurisdiction under Articles 18 and 19 of the Rome Statute , the ICC 's governing treaty . <p> On their alleged crimes , the court said it " found reasonable grounds " to believe that Netanyahu and Gallant " each bear criminal responsibility @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ jointly with others : the war crime of starvation as a method of warfare ; and the crimes against humanity of murder , persecution , and other inhumane acts .
