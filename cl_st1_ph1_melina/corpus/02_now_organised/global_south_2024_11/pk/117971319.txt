<p> Hague - The International Criminal Court on Thursday issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former defence minister , as well as Hamas 's military chief Mohammed Deif . <p> The ICC 's move now theoretically limits the movement of Netanyahu as any of the court 's 124 national members would be obliged to arrest him on their territory . <p> " The Chamber issued warrants of arrest for two individuals , Mr Benjamin Netanyahu and Mr Yoav Gallant , for crimes against humanity and war crimes committed from at least 8 October 2023 until at least 20 May 2024 , the day the Prosecution filed the applications for warrants of arrest , " the ICC said in a statement . <p> A warrant had also been issued for Deif , it added . Israel said in early August it had killed Deif in an air strike in southern Gaza in July , but Hamas has not confirmed his death .
