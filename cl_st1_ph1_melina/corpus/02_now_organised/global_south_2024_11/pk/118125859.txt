<p> A ceasefire between Israel and Hezbollah in Lebanon , brokered by the United States and France , is set to take effect on Wednesday , US President Joe Biden announced on Tuesday . <p> The agreement aims to end a deadly conflict , linked to the previous year 's Gaza war , which claimed thousands of lives . Biden described it as a " permanent cessation of hostilities , " emphasizing that Hezbollah and other groups would no longer pose a security threat to Israel . <p> The ceasefire will begin at 4 am local time ( 0200 GMT ) .
