<h> PM Shehbaz calls for stronger ties with Turkiye in key areas <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif emphasised the importance of enhancing cooperation between Pakistan and Turkiye in trade , investment , and defence during a meeting with Turkiye 's Ambassador to Pakistan , Irfan Neziroglu , on Wednesday . <p> The Prime Minister expressed satisfaction with the growing bilateral ties and thanked Turkiye for its steadfast support regarding Kashmir . " Pakistan will also continue to lend its strong support to Turkiye on its core interests , " he affirmed . <p> Shehbaz Sharif welcomed the newly appointed ambassador and expressed his eagerness to collaborate closely to further strengthen the relationship between the two nations . He extended his best wishes to President Recep Tayyip Erdogan and reiterated an invitation for him to visit Pakistan at his earliest convenience . <p> Ambassador Neziroglu assured the Prime Minister of his commitment to enhancing the friendship between the two countries . The two also discussed the regional situation , particularly concerning Gaza and the broader Middle East .
