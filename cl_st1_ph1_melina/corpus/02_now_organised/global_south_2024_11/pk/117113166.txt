<h> Acting President and PM vow to protect journalists ' rights <p> In a message commemorating the International Day to End Impunity for Crimes against Journalists , Acting President Syed Yousuf Raza Gillani and Prime Minister Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif reiterated Pakistan 's strong commitment to press freedom and freedom of expression . <p> Acting President Gilani emphasised that press freedom is essential for democratic stability and transparency in Pakistan . He asserted that violence against journalists undermines democracy . <p> Gillani noted that the Constitution of Pakistan guarantees the rights to freedom of expression , press freedom , and access to information . He highlighted that measures are in place to protect journalists and media workers within the country . <p> The Acting President pointed to the passage of the Journalists and Media Workers Protection Act 2021 and the introduction of health insurance for journalists as significant steps toward their protection . <p> He called for an integrated strategy and comprehensive system to enhance journalist safety . He acknowledged the relentless efforts of journalists worldwide , including in Pakistan , to keep the public informed . <p> The Prime Minister lamented the numerous challenges journalists face in their pursuit of truth , including violence , threats , kidnapping , and even murder . <p> He noted that journalists continue to risk their lives in conflict zones to fulfill their reporting responsibilities , highlighting that despite international conventions concerning Gaza , many journalists have been deliberately targeted to suppress the truth . <p> Shehbaz Sharif recognised the significant sacrifices made by journalists in Pakistan for democracy , constitutional supremacy , and the rule of law . <p> He affirmed the government 's commitment to prioritise the rights and protection of journalists , emphasising that measures will be taken to ensure their safety and to hold accountable those who perpetrate crimes against them . <p> 2024 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ acting-President and Prime Minister comes a day after the Freedom Network 's Impunity Report 2024 marked 2024 as one of the deadliest for journalists in Pakistan , with the documenting multiple targeted killings and a shocking 57 violations against media professionals . <p> The report reveals that six journalists and one YouTuber were killed , while at least 57 incidents -- including threats , assaults , and legal harassment -- were reported .
