Yet should Pakistan really be looking up to American democracy ? <p> On the face of it , elections in the US have historically been conducted freely and fairly . The three branches of government moreover--the executive , the legislature and the judiciary--form a system of checks and balances such that no branch becomes too powerful or encroaches upon the other .
