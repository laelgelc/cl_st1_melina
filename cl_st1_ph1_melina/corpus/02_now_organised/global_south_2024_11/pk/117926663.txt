<p> Pakistan has once again called upon Afghanistan to take action against terror groups operating from Afghan soil . <p> At her weekly news briefing in Islamabad today , Foreign Office Spokesperson Mumtaz Zahra Baloch said Pakistan on several occasions have shared with Afghan authorities concrete evidence regarding the terror groups . <p> She hoped the Afghan authorities will consider terrorism as a serious threat not just to the region but to their own security and fulfill their obligations under various international agreements including Doha agreement . <p> The spokesperson regretted the result of last night 's vote at the UN Security Council and its inability to reach consensus on the resolution calling for ceasefire in Gaza .
