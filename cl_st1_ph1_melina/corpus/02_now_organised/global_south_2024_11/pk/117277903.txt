<h> Iran slams US deployment of B-52 bombers as ' destabilising ' <p> TEHRAN - Iran 's foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei on Monday criticised what he called the United States ' " destabilising presence " after the deployment of B-52 bombers in the region . <p> " We have always believed that the presence of America in the region is a destabilising presence , " said Baghaei told a news conference in response to a question about the deployment , adding that it " will not deter ( Iran 's ) resolve to defend itself " . <p> The US military announced on Saturday the deployment of B-52 bombers to the Middle East as a warning to Iran which has vowed to respond to Israeli strikes on its military sites on October 26 . Israel 's attack was in retaliation for an October 1 Iranian missile barrage , itself retaliation for the killing of Iran-backed militant leaders and a Revolutionary Guards commander . Baghaei said Iran 's response would be " definite and decisive " . He added that Iran supported " all initiatives and efforts " to push for a ceasefire in Gaza and Lebanon , where Israel is at war with the Iran-backed Hamas and Hezbollah armed groups , respectively . <p> Israel said its October 26 attack targeted Iran 's defence capabilities and missile production , but Tehran said its missile production remained intact . On Monday , President Masoud Pezeshkian said Iran had missiles so Israel " would n't dare attack us " . During the news conference , Baghaei said Iran 's official position against changing the nuclear doctrine and pursuing atomic weapons remained the same . <p> Citing a recent speech by Iran 's supreme leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei , Baghaei said the Islamic republic would be " equipped with everything necessary to defend " itself .
