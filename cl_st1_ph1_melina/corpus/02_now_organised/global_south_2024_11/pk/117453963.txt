Conflict management ( CM ) and conflict resolutions ( CR ) thus become the logical processes needed to deal with , control and resolve such enduring issues in interstate relations . CM denotes that the conflict has been addressed , mediated , handled and coped with pending eventual resolution whereas CR signifies that a conflict has been resolved , settled and brought to a fruitful closure . A rational progression of a conflict therefore moves from its inception to its peaceful management and on to its eventual mutually agreed to resolution . <p> CM is generally premised on the belief that wars and conflicts are inevitable and that it ( CM ) is the only viable way to deal with them .
