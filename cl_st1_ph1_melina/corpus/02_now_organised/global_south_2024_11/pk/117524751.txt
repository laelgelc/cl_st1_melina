<p> BEIRUT : Lebanese state-run media said Israel struck a house in the northern Akkar region on Monday , one of the farthest attacks from the border in its war against Hezbollah . <p> Since September 23 , Israel has intensified its air campaign mainly targeting Hezbollah strongholds in Lebanon 's east , south and south Beirut , rarely targeting the country 's north . <p> " An enemy strike targeted a house in the village of Ain Yaacoub , " some 150 kilometres ( 93 miles ) from Israel , said Lebanon 's official National News Agency . <p> Local official Rony al-Hage told AFP that " displaced people lived in the two-storey house " and that it was the northernmost Israeli attack since the full-blown war erupted . After it escalated its air raids , Israel sent ground troops in against Hezbollah in south Lebanon on September 30 . <p> Residents of a nearby village heard a loud explosion and ambulance sirens . <p> A local Facebook page broadcast a live video feed it said was from the scene of the strike that showed a completely destroyed house @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ using their phones as flashlights . <p> Lebanon 's health ministry earlier said an Israeli strike on the southern town of Saksakiyeh killed at least seven people on Monday . <p> The Lebanon war erupted after nearly a year of cross-border exchanges of fire , launched by Hezbollah in support of its Palestinian ally Hamas following their October 7 , 2023 attack on Israel . That attack triggered the ongoing Gaza war . <p> More than 3,240 people have been killed in Lebanon since the cross-border fire began last year , according to Lebanon 's health ministry , but most of the deaths have come since late September .
