Palestine during his participation at the summit . <p> He is expected to call for an immediate and unconditional ceasefire to halt the ongoing violence in Gaza , which has sparked international condemnation . <p> " Pakistan 's stance on Palestine remains firm , and we will continue to demand a halt to the genocide taking place in Gaza , " a government spokesperson said . <p> Shehbaz 's visit
