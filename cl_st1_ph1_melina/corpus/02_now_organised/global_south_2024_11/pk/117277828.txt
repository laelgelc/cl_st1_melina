<p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Tuesday said Pakistan desires to further strengthen its brotherly relationship with Iran through maintaining regular high-level exchanges and enhancing mutually advantageous cooperation across all spheres of shared interest . <p> The prime minister , in a meeting with Foreign Minister of Iran Seyyed Abbas Araghchi , reiterated Pakistan 's support to the Palestinian people in their just struggle for self-determination and statehood . <p> He strongly condemned and expressed grave concern over Israel 's ongoing genocidal campaign against the Palestinian people , according to a PM Office press release . <p> Welcoming Iranian FM Araghchi on his first official visit , , PM Shehbaz conveyed his regards and wishes to Iran 's Supreme Leader Ayatollah Khamenei and President Massoud Pezeshkian . <p> The prime minister also emphasised the need for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza , provision of unhindered humanitarian assistance and , above all , grant of the inalienable right to self-determination to the Palestinian people , as guaranteed to them by the relevant resolutions of the UN , as well as OIC.
