<p> ISLAMABAD - At a pivotal meeting of the Standing Committee on Budget and Planning of Asian Parliamentary Assembly ( APA ) , hosted by Qatar 's Shura Council , Senator Irfan-ul-Haq Siddiqui as representative from Pakistan issued an urgent call for international accountability and immediate intervention as violence escalates in Gaza and across the Middle East . <p> In his address , Pakistan 's representative condemned what he described as the " non-stop and indiscriminate use of force " by Israel against innocent civilians in Gaza , particularly impacting women and children . He labelled the crisis as " genocide " and warned that it poses a grave threat to peace across the region . <p> He called on the international community to hold Israel accountable for its actions , urging adherence to human rights and international law . <p> The representative also criticised Israel 's recent invasion of Lebanon and strikes against Iran , warning that these actions further destabilize the region and jeopardize the lives of innocent people . " These attacks threaten the very foundations of regional peace and security , " he stated .
