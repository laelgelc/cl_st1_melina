<p> ANKARA : Qatar 's Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad al Thani arrived in Ankara on Thursday to chair a Turkiye-Qatar High Strategic Committee meeting with President Tayyip Erdogan , after which the sides are expected to sign off on investment-related accords . <p> The visit , focused on investment and trade , comes after Doha said at the weekend it had told Hamas and Israel it will stall efforts to mediate a Gaza ceasefire and hostage release deal until they show willingness and seriousness . <p> Turkiye , which fiercely criticised Israel over its offensives in Gaza and in Lebanon , does not deem Hamas a terrorist group , and some of its political officials regularly visit Turkiye . <p> Erdogan and al Thani will hold a bilateral meeting and then will chair the tenth meeting of the Turkiye-Qatar High Strategic Committee . They will then attend a ceremony for the signing of various agreements .
