<p> Pakistan always supported the cause of Palestinians and urged them to give the people of Palestine their fundamental rights . The Prime Minister in his address at the UNGA session urged the international community to play its role in defusing tensions in the Middle East . Pakistan always condemned the cruel acts of the Israeli forces on innocent Palestinians . Unfortunately , some countries have tilt towards Israeli aggression despite the fact that many children , women and elders are being targeted by the Israeli forces . The houses have been destroyed and even the hospitals are being bombarded . Pakistan has a clear narrative on the Palestine issue and will never step back an inch from its stance . The world must ensure an immediate ceasefire in Gaza on humanitarian basis and look out for a solution to this issue .
