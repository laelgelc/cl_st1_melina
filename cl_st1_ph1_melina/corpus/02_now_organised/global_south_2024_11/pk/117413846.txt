<h> Eighth batch of Palestinian students departs for Pakistan <p> CAIRO - The 8th batch of eight Palestinian students from Gaza departed on Thursday for Pakistan to complete their medical studies . Pakistan embassy in Egypt facilitated their departure under directions of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and significant efforts of Global Relief Trust , Doctors of Rahman and Alkhidmat Foundation Pakistan , Pakistan embassy in Cairo wrote on its official X account . In July , the Government of Pakistan had announced to allow medical students from Gaza to complete their medical education in Pakistan on humanitarian grounds . The decision was made to enable Gaza students to continue the final years of their medical education in the fields of cardiology , orthopedics , oncology , pediatrics and surgery to address critical requirements in Gaza healthcare system .
