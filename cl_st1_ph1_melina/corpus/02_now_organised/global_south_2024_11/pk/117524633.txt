the United States , tension rises with rivals China and North Korea , and domestic pressure mounts to rein in the cost of living . <p> His Liberal Democratic Party and coalition partner Komeito won the biggest bloc of seats in the election but lost the majority held since 2012 , leaving him beholden to small opposition parties to pass his policy agenda . <p> " In light of the very challenging
