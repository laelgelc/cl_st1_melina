<h> DPM Dar calls for accountability of Israel 's war crimes in Gaza <p> Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar has urged for an immediate and unconditional ceasefire in Gaza , condemning Israel 's actions in the region during his address at the Council of Foreign Ministers ' Preparatory meeting in Riyadh . <p> The session was held ahead of the Second Arab-Islamic Summit on the Middle East situation . <p> Dar strongly condemned what he described as Israeli war crimes and crimes against humanity committed against Palestinians , demanding accountability for the attacks . <p> The death toll of innocent Palestinians has now surpassed 44,000 , with over 100,000 injured and millions displaced , he said . <p> In his remarks , Dar highlighted that civilian infrastructure , including homes , schools , hospitals , and humanitarian shelters , had been deliberately targeted and destroyed . <p> He also noted that international humanitarian access to Gaza had been severely restricted , with the UN Relief and Works Agency declaring Gaza the most dangerous place in the world for aid workers . <p> The Deputy Prime Minister expressed alarm over Israel @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is jeopardising peace and security , with the conflict now expanding to Lebanon , Syria , and Iran . Dar condemned Israel 's military strikes against these countries as flagrant violations of international law and the UN Charter . <p> Dar proposed the nomination of a Joint Arab-Islamic Special Envoy to help coordinate progress on implementing resolutions adopted at previous summits . <p> Reaffirming Pakistan 's support for Palestine , Dar reiterated the country 's commitment to advocating for the creation of a sovereign Palestinian state , based on pre-1967 borders , with Al-Quds Al-Sharif as its capital . <p> He commended the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) and the Arab League for their continued dedication to the Palestinian cause . <p> In a separate meeting on the sidelines of the Council , Dar discussed Pakistan-Turkey relations with Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan , focusing on the ongoing situation in Gaza . <p> Both leaders agreed on the need for the international community to unite in efforts to halt Israel 's attacks on Palestinians .
