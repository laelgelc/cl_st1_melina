<p> The Guardian - The international criminal court has issued arrest warrants for the Israeli prime minister , Benjamin Netanyahu , the country 's former defence minister Yoav Gallant and the Hamas leader Mohammed Deif for alleged war crimes relating to the Gaza war . <p> The warrants put Netanyahu and Gallant at risk of arrest if they travel abroad . There have been unconfirmed reports that Deif may have been killed by Israel . <p> The court 's chief prosecutor , Karim Khan , had requested the arrest warrants in May , saying there were reasonable grounds to believe Netanyahu and Gallant bore " criminal responsibility " for causing mass starvation in Gaza that constituted war crimes and crimes against humanity . <p> On Thursday the court said it had found reasonable grounds to believe that Deif was responsible for crimes against humanity and war crimes including murder , torture , rape and hostage taking relating to the 7 October Hamas attack on Israel in which fighters killed more than 1,200 Israelis , mostly civilians , and kidnapped 250 . <p> The US has previously welcomed ICC war crimes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ committed in Ukraine , while denouncing the court 's pursuit of Netanyahu and Gallant , a mixed stance which has exposed the Biden administration to accusations of double standards from many UN members , particularly from the global south . <p> The three-judge panel wrote in its unanimous decision to issue warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant : " The chamber considered that there are reasonable grounds to believe that both individuals intentionally and knowingly deprived the civilian population in Gaza of objects indispensable to their survival , including food , water , and medicine and medical supplies , as well as fuel and electricity . "
