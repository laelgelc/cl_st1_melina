<h> POLITICS <h> ICC arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant long overdue - EFF <p> Fighters say warrants represent an essential step toward justice for the people of Palestine and accountability for crimes against humanity <p> EFF welcomes ICC arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant <p> 21 November 2024 <p> The Economic Freedom Fighters ( EFF ) welcomes the long-overdue issuance of arrest warrants by the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) for Prime Minister of Israel Benjamin Netanyahu and Minister of Defense Yoav Gallant . These warrants represent an essential step toward justice for the people of Palestine and accountability for crimes against humanity and war crimes committed under their leadership . <p> The ICC 's Chamber has found reasonable grounds to believe that both Netanyahu and Gallant bear direct criminal responsibility for crimes committed from 8 October 2023 to 20 May 2024 . These include the war crime of starvation as a method of warfare and the crimes against humanity of murder , persecution , and other inhumane acts , perpetrated during Israel 's actions against the civilian population in Gaza . <p> The EFF has consistently condemned @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of Palestinians , unlawful occupation of Palestinian territories , murder , and the calculated deprivation of essential goods and services such as food , water , electricity , and medical supplies . According to the ICC 's report , civilians , including children , have died from malnutrition , dehydration , and preventable illnesses caused by the calculated withholding of humanitarian aid . <p> By obstructing the entry of medical supplies , including anaesthetics and anaesthesia machines , they inflicted severe suffering on wounded patients , forcing doctors to perform surgeries and amputations without adequate sedation . These actions , aimed at inflicting suffering and death on innocent civilians , especially children , are not only morally reprehensible but also clear violations of international humanitarian law . <p> --&gt; <p> The ICC 's findings confirm what the world has known for decades : that Israel , under leaders like Netanyahu and Gallant , has engaged in deliberate , widespread , and systematic genocide of Palestinian civilians . It is also a vindication of the Palestinian people 's struggle for justice and freedom . The EFF reiterates its solidarity with the people of Palestine and supports their right to self-determination and liberation from Israeli apartheid . <p> We call upon the international community to unequivocally support the ICC 's efforts and to ensure the enforcement of these arrest warrants . Justice must not be delayed any longer . The actions of Netanyahu and Gallant are not isolated incidents but part of a broader system of Israeli apartheid and colonial violence that demands global condemnation and tangible action . <p> Issued by Leigh-Ann Mathys , National Spokesperson , EFF , 21 November 2024
