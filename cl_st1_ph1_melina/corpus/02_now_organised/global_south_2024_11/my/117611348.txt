<p> JAKARTA : Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto met with United States ( US ) President Joe Biden at the White House on Tuesday afternoon local time as part of a state visit to the US , reported ANTARA news agency . <p> During the meeting , the two leaders discussed the strong diplomatic relations between Indonesia and the US . <p> " Welcome to the White House . It is great to have you here . We are celebrating an important anniversary : the 75th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Indonesia and the United States , " Biden told Prabowo , as quoted from a statement received on Wednesday . <p> During their meeting , Prabowo thanked Biden for reaching out to him following his victory in the 2024 Presidential Election . <p> " Thank you for receiving me . I also want to say thank you . You called me yourself during the ( Presidential ) Election . Finally , I could visit , and you received me today , " Prabowo remarked . <p> Furthermore , Biden expressed his pride in the strong US-Indonesia partnership and was keen @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ touched on geopolitical challenges , including the conflicts in Gaza and the South China Sea , with Prabowo . <p> " We also discussed global challenges in Gaza and the South China Sea . We look forward to our discussion and welcome , " he remarked . <p> Moreover , Biden identified several focus areas for cooperation , including strengthening a free and open Indo-Pacific region , supporting a clean energy transition where Indonesia plays a critical role , and building secure and resilient supply chains . <p> He then drew attention to the importance of a comprehensive strategic partnership that encompasses deepening security cooperation between both nations . <p> In his remarks , the Indonesian President-elect acknowledged the significant contributions of the US to Indonesia 's journey toward independence and its ongoing support through various challenges . <p> Prabowo also expressed his willingness to strengthen relations between Indonesia and the US . <p> Prabowo was accompanied by Minister of Foreign Affairs Sugiono and Cabinet Secretary Teddy Indra Wijaya in the meeting . -- Bernama-Antara
