<h> Indonesia , Malaysia lead global effort for Palestinian rights , advocating for stronger international action <p> JAKARTA : Indonesia and Malaysia , two key Southeast Asian nations , are playing a critical role in garnering global support for Palestine , according to Professor Sudarnoto Abdul Hakim of Syarif Hidayatullah State Islamic University . <p> As respected members of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) , he said both countries have consistently demonstrated their support for Palestine through diplomatic and humanitarian efforts . <p> Sudarnoto , who also serves as the Head of the Indonesian Ulema Council ( MUI ) for Foreign Relations and International Cooperation , recently visited a Palestinian refugee shelter in Amman , Jordan . <p> He shared that refugees expressed profound gratitude towards both Indonesia and Malaysia for their unwavering support . <p> " The appreciation for these nations ' commitment is deep . Their role has been essential in sustaining hope and advocating for justice , " Sudarnoto said . <p> In Malaysia , Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim has emerged as a vocal advocate for Palestinian rights on the global stage @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as a sovereign state and for Israel 's removal from the United Nations ( UN ) . <p> At high-level international meetings , such as the G20 Summit in Brazil and the APEC Economic Leaders ' Meeting in Peru , Malaysia , under Anwar 's leadership , has advocated for UN reform and highlighted Israel 's ongoing actions against Palestinians . <p> Anwar also raised the issue at the Extraordinary Arab and Islamic Summit in Riyadh , urging stronger international action against Israeli military actions in Palestine . <p> Similarly , Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto has used his platform at the G20 Summit to call for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza , as well as in Ukraine . <p> Indonesia 's Deputy Foreign Minister Anis Matta further emphasised the country 's steadfast support for Palestine at the OIC-Arab League Summit in Saudi Arabia . <p> " Indonesia and Malaysia are both advocating for global action , " Sudarnoto said , while also outlining several key initiatives to strengthen the two countries ' stance on the Palestinian issue and hold Israel accountable for its actions .
