<h> Ask about this document <h> West Bank Under Lockdown : A Growing Humanitarian Emergency <p> Since October 7 , 2023 , the West Bank has been drawn deeper into the crisis unfolding across Palestine . Israel has imposed an increasingly severe military lockdown , effectively cutting off Palestinian towns and villages behind more than 800 metal gates and concrete barriers . Movement is strangled . Families are trapped . Lives are in limbo . <p> This tightening of control comes as part of a broader escalation . The West Bank now endures near-daily Israeli military raids , with particular intensity in refugee camps in Jenin and Tulkarem . Since the beginning of 2025 , 137 Palestinians -- including 27 children -- have been killed in the West Bank . Since the war on Gaza began , the death toll has reached at least 943 , according to the United Nations .
