Picture : Hugo Mathy/AFP <p> Activist and campaigner Greta Thunberg gets right up the noses of conservatives around the world and now that she has set her sights on agitating for Palestinian rights , she has angered many more . <p> It is also true that Thunberg has a knack for gaining global publicity for her causes ... which is why many of her critics will have welcomed the Israeli action to prevent her and her Gaza-bound aid boat from reaching Palestinian territory . <p> However , what those Thunberg haters -- and supporters of Israel -- are ignoring is the fact that the boat was intercepted in international waters . <p> Israel already will not recognise Palestinian territorial waters and has blockaded the territory to prevent arms getting in.
