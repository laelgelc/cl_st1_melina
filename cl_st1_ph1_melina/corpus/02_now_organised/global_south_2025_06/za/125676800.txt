<p> Cairo -- The organisers of a Gaza-bound aid boat carrying Greta Thunberg and other activists said Israeli forces intercepted the vessel on Monday , after Israel vowed to prevent it from reaching the Palestinian territory . <p> AFP lost contact with the activists onboard early Monday morning after the organisers said alarms sounded and life jackets were being prepared for a possible interception . <p> " Connection has been lost on the ' Madleen ' . Israeli army have boarded the vessel , " the Freedom Flotilla Coalition , the activist group operating the vessel , posted on Telegram . It added that the passengers had been " kidnapped " by Israeli forces . <p> The activist group posted a series of pre-recorded videos from those onboard , including one from Thunberg . <p> " If you see this video we have been intercepted and kidnapped in international waters , " she said . <p> Mahmud Abu-Odeh , a Germany-based press officer with the coalition , told AFP that " the activists seemed to be arrested " . <p> Defence Minister Israel Katz said he had ordered Israel 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a blockade he described as needed to prevent Palestinian militants from importing weapons . <p> The Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs said the activists ' boat was instructed to change course as it approached " a restricted area " early Monday . About an hour later , it said the boat was being towed to Israeli shores . <p> " The passengers are expected to return to their home countries , " the ministry wrote on social media . <p> " The tiny amount of aid that was on the yacht and not consumed by the ' celebrities ' will be transferred to Gaza through real humanitarian channels , " it added . <p> Swedish climate campaigner Thunberg is among a multi-national group of activists aboard the Madleen , which departed from Italy on June 1 to raise awareness about the humanitarian plight in Gaza . <p> The United Nations has repeatedly warned of famine , malnutrition and disease throughout the 21 months of the Israel-Hamas war .
