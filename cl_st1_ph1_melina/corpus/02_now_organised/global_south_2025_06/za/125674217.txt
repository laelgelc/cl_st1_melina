<h> EFF protests Israeli participation in Comrades Marathon <p> The EFF in KwaZulu-Natal protested against the participation of Israelis in the Comrades Marathon on Sunday . <p> Members of the red berets carrying placards reading ' stop the genocide ' staged a protest along the route from Pietermaritzburg to Durban . The protest was joined by a group of Muslim community members . <p> KZN EFF chairperson Mongezi Twala said his party rejects and condemns the participation of Israelis in the annual Comrades Marathon . " As a revolutionary left movement committed to justice , human rights and international solidarity , we can not in good conscience allow individuals who represent a genocidal apartheid state to participate freely in events on our soil , especially during a time when the Israeli regime is actively committing crimes against humanity in Gaza , " said Twala . <p> The government of South Africa , through the department of international relations &amp; cooperation , took Israel to the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) for its ongoing genocide against the Palestinian people . <p> " The ICJ has ruled in favour of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ plausible case of genocide against Israel .
