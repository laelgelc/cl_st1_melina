<h> Huthis say fired missiles at Israel in attacks ' coordinated ' <p> Yemen 's Iran-backed Huthi rebels said Sunday they had launched several missiles at Israel as it came under Iranian fire , following a massive Israeli bombardment campaign on the Islamic republic . <p> AFP <p> The Huthis said in a statement they had " carried out a military operation targeting sensitive targets of the Israeli enemy " in central Israel . <p> The group , which holds vast swathes of territory in Yemen including the capital Sanaa , said it had targeted the area " at different times over the past 24 hours " with " a number of Palestine 2 hypersonic ballistic missiles " . <p> The Huthis said their attacks were " coordinated with the operations carried out by the Iranian military " . <p> The Yemeni rebels began launching missiles and drones at Israel and Israeli-linked targets after the start of the Gaza war in October 2023 . <p> Israel has carried out numerous attacks on Huthi-controlled areas of Yemen , including on ports and the airport in Sanaa.
