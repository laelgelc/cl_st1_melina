<h> DOCUMENTS <h> SAJBD condemns anti-Israeli harassment at Comrades Marathon <p> Wendy Kahn <p> 09 June 2025 <p> Board equally dismayed by ANCYL in KZN 's statement exposure of the identities of athletes <p> SAJBD statement on the Comrades Marathon <p> 9 June 2025 <p> The South African Jewish Board of Deputies condemns the scenes of anti-Israel protestors tracking and harassing Israeli runners during the Comrades Marathon . We are equally dismayed by the ANCYL in KwaZulu-Natal 's statement calling for the exclusion of Israeli athletes , identified by name and race number to facilitate targeted harassment . This is a form of doxing which is reprehensible . <p> The Comrades Marathon , named to honour the camaraderie and unity among diverse participants , embodies an ethos of perseverance , community , ubuntu , and collective spirit . It has always been a beacon of inclusivity and was a multi racial event even during the darkest days of Apartheid , having opened to all participants in 1975 . With this is mind , it becomes clear that the deliberate tracking , harassment and intimidation of Israeli athletes is the antithesis @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2025 Comrades Marathon attracted runners from over 80 countries , reflecting the global reach of the world 's oldest ultra-marathon . It is disturbing that only Israeli athletes were singled out for harassment , despite the presence of participants from nations with ongoing conflicts and serious human rights concerns , such as Russia . <p> The harassment of these athletes exemplifies the anti-Israeli movement 's unwanted and unconstitutional exploitation of public spaces to antagonize and intimidate those who challenge its narrow political bent . These theatrical displays make no contribution toward improving or saving a single life in Gaza . <p> --&gt; <p> Let us rather adhere to the Comrade Marathon 's spirit of inclusive participation and respect for diversity .
