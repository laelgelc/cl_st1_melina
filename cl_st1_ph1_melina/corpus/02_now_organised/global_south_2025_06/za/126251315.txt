Humanity is in the deepest crisis it has ever seen . <p> German critic Walter Benjamin , who died in 1940 , once said : " There is no document of civilisation which is not at the same time a document of barbarism . " These words predicted the scale of atrocity during the Nazi invasion of Europe . <p> Benjamin believed that barbarism was being transmitted from one hand to another .
