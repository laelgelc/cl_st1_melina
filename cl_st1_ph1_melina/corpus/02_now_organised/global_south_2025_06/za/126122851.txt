Now it is Iran 's turn . <p> Over the course of just a week , Israel 's Operation Rising Lion has reduced Iran 's feared military , terror and repressive apparatus to ruins . While much of the democratic world -- including the US , the United Kingdom , the EU and Canada -- designates Hamas as a terrorist organisation , SA joins only a handful of countries like Russia , China , and Turkey in refusing to do so .
