Thousands of protesters took to Kenya 's streets on Wednesday to mark a year since people stormed parliament at the peak of anti-government demonstrations , despite fears that they would be met by state-backed gangs and police violence . At least 60 people were killed last year by security forces in weeks of protests over tax rises and the dire economic situation for young Kenyans . Activists and families of victims have called for peaceful demonstrations to mark a year since the deadliest day of the unrest when parliament was invaded .
