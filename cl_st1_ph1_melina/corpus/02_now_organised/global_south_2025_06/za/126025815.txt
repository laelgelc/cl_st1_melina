A film that is more than just a movie . It is memory , pain , resistance , and healing . It was honest . In a world where violence is often glorified , and revenge is misrepresented as justice , it made sense that a child might wonder why retaliation was n't the obvious next step . It was a door cracked open to explain something extraordinary about SA 's history . <p> In that moment , I wished someone had said aloud what so many of us know . In this way , Sarafina shows us that peace is not passive ; it is practised , deliberate , and often an uncomfortable response to injustice . <p> We are not a perfect country , but we are a country that consistently chooses peace . It is why , even in moments of deep injustice like now , as we watch the horror unfold in Gaza and across the Middle East , SA stands firmly on the side of humanity . The genocide case brought before the International Court of Justice was not political theatre . It is a reflection of who we believe ourselves to be .
