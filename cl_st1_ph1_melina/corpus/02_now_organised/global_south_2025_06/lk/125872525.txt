<h> Heightened security for Chabad Houses in Sri Lanka <p> Sri Lanka Police said that it has received credible intelligence reports regarding an impending threat to Jewish Chabad Houses in Sri Lanka . Acting Police Spokesperson ASP F.U. Wootler told the Sunday Observer yesterday(14) that following the intelligence reports , Police have taken measures to increase security provided to the Chabad Houses and in the surrounding areas . <p> Accordingly , security has been tightened in and around the four Chabad Houses in Arugam Bay , Colombo , Weligama and Ella . He said that in addition the Chabad Houses in Sri Lanka have also requested the Sri Lanka Police to provide extra security to the establishments . <p> Earlier in January this year , Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya raised concerns on the construction of alleged illegal religious places of worship by tourists in Sri Lanka . <p> " The Government has not given any permission for Israeli citizens to build religious places of worship or related buildings in Sri Lanka .
