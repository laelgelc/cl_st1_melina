<h> Sri Lanka supports right of Palestinian people to statehood <p> Sri Lanka noted its unwavering support for the right of the Palestinian people to statehood in accordance with the provisions of the UN Charter and the relevant UN resolutions . <p> The Ambassador of Palestine to Sri Lanka Ihab Khalil called on the Minster of Foreign Affairs , Foreign Employment , and Tourism Vijita Herath at the Ministry and briefed him on the situation in Gaza and discussed bilateral issues . <p> The Ambassador of Palestine appreciated Sri Lanka for its stance on the Palestine issue and for the continuous solidarity and support extended to Palestine at regional and international fora . <p> He appreciated Sri Lanka for the donation of USD 1 Million made to the Gaza Children 's Fund through the United Nations Relief and Works Agency ( UNRWA ) . <p> He reaffirmed Sri Lanka 's unwavering support for the right of the Palestinian people to statehood in accordance with the provisions of the UN Charter and the relevant UN resolutions . <p> The Government said that Sri Lanka remains committed to supporting a negotiated settlement @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ side by side on the basis of the 1967 borders , which is key to achieving long term security , peace and prosperity .
