<p> African countries Sudan , South Sudan and Mali are among five global hunger hotspots of highest concern , warned a new joint United Nations report . <p> The UN 's Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) and World Food Programme ( WFP ) issued the Hunger Hotspots : FAO-WFP early warning on acute food insecurity -- June to October 2025 outlook report on June 16 , 2025 . The report identified conflict , economic shocks and climate-related hazards as the primary causes of starvation risks in Sudan , Palestine , South Sudan , Haiti and Mali . <p> The analysis of current data in the report highlighted that people in these critical areas will encounter extreme food shortages and a heightened risk of hunger and mortality unless urgent humanitarian aid is provided . <p> In Sudan , famine was declared in 2024 , and the situation is expected to continue because of conflict and ongoing displacement .
