<p> Greta Thunberg set sail from Italy to Gaza with 11 activists aboard the Madleen , aiming to deliver aid and protest Israel 's blockade . Joining her are Game of Thrones actor Liam Cunningham and French MEP Rima Hassan . Thunberg called the world 's silence on Gaza " more dangerous " than the mission itself . <p> Climate activist Greta Thunberg has joined a humanitarian mission to the Gaza Strip , setting sail on Sunday afternoon from Catania , a southern Italian port . The 23-year-old boarded the Madleen , a ship operated by the Freedom Flotilla Coalition , and was joined by 11 other activists in a bid to challenge Israel 's ongoing blockade of the war-torn Palestinian enclave . <p> Speaking emotionally at a press conference before departure , Thunberg broke down in tears as she explained her reasons for joining the dangerous voyage . " We are doing this because , no matter what odds we are against , we have to keep trying , " she said . " Because the moment we stop trying is when we lose our humanity . And no @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ even near as dangerous as the silence of the entire world in the face of the live-streamed genocide . " <p> The Madleen is headed for Gaza in an effort to deliver humanitarian aid and draw global attention to the severe humanitarian crisis unfolding there . The mission comes amid growing international concerns about famine and the limited flow of aid reaching the region . <p> The activists on board hope to complete their journey within seven days , provided they are not intercepted . Among the participants are notable figures such as ' Game of Thrones ' actor Liam Cunningham and French-Palestinian European Parliament member Rima Hassan . Hassan has previously been barred from entering Israel due to her criticism of the ongoing military campaign in Gaza . <p> Thunberg was originally scheduled to take part in a similar mission last month , but plans were disrupted after a different vessel in the Freedom Flotilla fleet , the Conscience , was reportedly damaged by drone strikes in international waters near Malta . The group has accused Israel of carrying out the attack .
