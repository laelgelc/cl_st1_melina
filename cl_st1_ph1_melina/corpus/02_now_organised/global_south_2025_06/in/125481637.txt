<p> Today , almost 19 per cent of the world 's children -- that 's over 473 million -- are living in conflict zones . Of them , 47.2 million have already been displaced by war and violence . <p> June 4 marks the International Day of Innocent Children Victims of Aggression -- a day to remind the world that children have become frontline victims in wars they did not start and can not escape . Nowhere is this more visible than in Gaza . <p> Since the end of the ceasefire on March 18 , 1,309 children have reportedly been killed and 3,738 injured . <p> Since October 2023 , over 50,000 children have been reported killed or injured . <p> How many more dead girls and boys will it take ? What level of horror must be livestreamed before the world acts ?
