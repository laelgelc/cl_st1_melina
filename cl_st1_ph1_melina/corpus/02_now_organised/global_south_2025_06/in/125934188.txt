<p> GENEVA , June 16 : The UN human rights chief said Israel 's warfare in Gaza is inflicting " horrifying , unconscionable suffering " on Palestinians and urged government leaders on Monday to exert pressure on Israel 's government and the militant group Hamas to end it . <p> Volker Turk made the comments at the opening of the latest Human Rights Council session on Monday , in a broad address that also raised concerns about escalating conflict between Iran and Israel , the fallout from US tariffs , and China 's human rights record -- alongside wars and conflict in places like Sudan and Ukraine . <p> The United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights , who has regularly spoken out about bloodshed in Gaza and called for the release of Israeli hostages held by armed Palestinian militants , used some of his most forceful words yet to highlight the Mideast violence . <p> " Israel 's means and methods of warfare are inflicting horrifying , unconscionable suffering on Palestinians in Gaza , " Turk told the 47-member-country body , which Israeli authorities have regularly accused of anti-Israel @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ States , Israel 's top ally , out of the council proceedings . <p> Israel 's military campaign has killed over 55,300 Palestinians , according to Gaza 's Health Ministry .
