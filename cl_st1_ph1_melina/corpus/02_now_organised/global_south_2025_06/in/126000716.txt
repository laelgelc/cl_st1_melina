<p> IRAN fired a new wave of missile attacks on Israel early Monday , killing at least eight people , while Israel warned residents of part of Tehran to evacuate ahead of new strikes . The warning came on the fourth day of the conflict , when the Israeli military claimed it had achieved air superiority over the Iranian capital and could fly over the city without facing major threats . The military has issued similar evacuation warnings for civilians in parts of Gaza and Lebanon ahead of strikes . " At this time , we can say that we have achieved full aerial superiority over Tehran 's skies , " said military spokesperson Brigadier General Effie Defrin .
