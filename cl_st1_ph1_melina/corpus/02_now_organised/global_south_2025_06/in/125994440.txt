In a statement on Tuesday , the MEA said that while the Embassy remains in constant touch with the Indian community in Iran , further advisories may be issued given the fluid and fast-changing scenario . <p> Indian nationals with independent means of transport have also been advised to vacate Tehran as a precaution .
