The issue had such a profound impact on her psyche that it spurred a series of radical changes in her lifestyle including going vegan and not travelling by airplanes . Born to two artists , with her mother being an opera singer and her father an actor , Greta expanded her support for the cause beyond her personal sphere in 2018 . <p> Her first bid to aid the cause was to push lawmakers into addressing climate change . This led to a school climate strike movement called " Fridays for Future , " for which she amassed global recognition .
