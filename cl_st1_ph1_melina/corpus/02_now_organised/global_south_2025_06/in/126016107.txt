Israel , emboldened by its air superiority , by its technological efficiency and with its disregard for international opinion , may target Iran 's energy infrastructure , with devastating consequences . The Strait of Hormuz and adjacent facilities are lifelines not just for Iran 's economy but for global energy supply . An Israeli strike on oil terminals , refineries , or tankers could effectively paralyse the movement of energy through the Persian Gulf . <p> The ripple effects of such an act would be global . Iran , too , is retaliating through missile strikes on urban areas , cyber disruptions and maritime harassment . In wars of this nature , one side often needs to blink first for a ceasefire to emerge . <p> There is a historical precedent . In earlier confrontations , Iran has resorted to intense rhetoric but has often stopped short of maximal retaliation , preserving room for a tactical drawdown .
