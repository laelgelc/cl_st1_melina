<h> G7 Summit 2025 : Crisis of values in a fragmenting global order <h> To restore its moral and strategic credibility , the G7 must prioritize unity over division , engaging the Global South as partners , not peripherals , and reforming its structure perhaps by expanding membership to reflect global realities . <p> The 51st G7 Summit , convened in Kananaskis , Alberta , from June 15-17 , 2025 , under Canada 's stewardship , was envisioned as a reaffirmation of shared democratic values and collective leadership . Hosted by Prime Minister Mark Carney , it sought to address global stability , energy transitions , and equitable partnerships . Instead , the summit exposed the G7 's existential fragility , as the Israel-Iran conflict , internal discord , and the absence of a joint communiqu ? underscored a deepening crisis of purpose . With the United States ' abrupt pivot under President Donald Trump and mounting geopolitical complexities , the G7 struggles to uphold its foundational principles , democracy , multilateralism , and global stewardship , in an increasingly multipolar world .
