<p> SRINAGAR : Delivering the Friday sermon at Srinagar 's Jamia Masjid , Mirwaiz Umar Farooq raised strong objections over the repeated bans on Eid prayers at the Eidgah and Jamia Masjid , calling on Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha to publicly explain the rationale behind what he termed as the denial of a basic religious right . <p> Addressing worshippers for the first time since Eid-ul-Adha , the Mirwaiz stated , " Both Eidgah and Jamia Masjid were locked down and I was placed under house arrest . For the past seven years , such measures have prevented Muslims in Kashmir from offering Eid prayers . " He criticised the administration for extending Eid greetings while simultaneously barring congregational worship , urging the LG to clarify the government 's position and motivations . <p> He condemned the restrictions as " inhuman diktats " and alleged that despite claims of normalcy following the abrogation of Article 370 , the ground reality remains repressive .
