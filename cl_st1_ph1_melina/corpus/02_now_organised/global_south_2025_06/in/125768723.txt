<h> ' Over 55,000 Palestinians killed in Israel-Hamas war ' <p> Deir al-Balah : The Palestinian death toll from the 20-month Israel-Hamas war has climbed past 55,000 , the Gaza Health Ministry said Wednesday . <p> The ministry does n't distinguish between civilians and combatants , but has said that women and children make up more than half the dead . <p> It 's a grim milestone in the war that began with Hamas ' attack into southern Israel on Oct 7 , 2023 , and shows no sign of ending . Israel says it only targets militants and blames civilian deaths on Hamas , accusing the militants of hiding among civilians , because they operate in populated areas . The ministry says 55,104 people have been killed since the start of the war and 127,394 wounded . <p> Many more are believed to be buried under the rubble or in areas that are inaccessible to local medics . The Health Ministry is part of Gaza 's Hamas-run government , but staffed by medical professionals who maintain and publish <p> detailed records .
