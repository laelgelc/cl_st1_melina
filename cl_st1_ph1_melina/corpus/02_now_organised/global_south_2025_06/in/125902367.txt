This attack on Iran 's sovereignty and encroachment of its rights , whether through aerial strikes or covert killings , only deepens instability and sows the seeds of further conflict , " the Congress said in a statement . <p> " The Congress party firmly believes that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ violence , are the only legitimate and sustainable path forward .
