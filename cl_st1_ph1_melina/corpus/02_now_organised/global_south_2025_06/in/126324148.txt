" Iran before the revolution , " the caption sighs -- a digital epitaph for a modernity America imagines it birthed . <p> This meme , shared with performative grief , erases the Shah 's SAVAK death squads , the feudal poverty , the US-backed dictatorship that birthed the revolution . Is n't history . It 's colonial fan fiction .
