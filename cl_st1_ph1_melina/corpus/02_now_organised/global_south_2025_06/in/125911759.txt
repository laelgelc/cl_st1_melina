Its vote -- or abstention -- on key resolutions concerning Israel 's military operations in Gaza is more than a diplomatic maneuver ; it is a signal of shifting values . <p> In October 2023 , the UN General Assembly overwhelmingly passed a resolution calling for an immediate humanitarian ceasefire in Gaza , a region under siege after the Hamas attacks on Israel .
