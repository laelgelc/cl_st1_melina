<h> Dozens Killed as Israeli Forces Open Fire Near Gaza Aid Sites <p> SRINAGAR : Israeli forces and drones reportedly killed dozens of Palestinians on Tuesday as they gathered near aid distribution points across Gaza . Palestinian health officials and witnesses said up to 50 people may have been killed , though the figure remains unverified . <p> The latest killings occurred as people assembled for aid near sites run by the Gaza Humanitarian Foundation ( GHF ) , backed by Israel and the United States . The head of UNRWA has earlier described the aid points as a " death trap . " <p> Medical sources told Al Jazeera that at least 25 people were killed on Salah al-Din Street , south of Wadi Gaza , with over 140 injured , including 62 in critical condition . Additional casualties were reported across Gaza , including 21 deaths elsewhere in the territory . <p> In Gaza City and Rafah , more casualties were reported at aid sites . Al Jazeera 's correspondent said emergency wards , including at al-Shifa Hospital , were overwhelmed , with several people dying while awaiting treatment .
