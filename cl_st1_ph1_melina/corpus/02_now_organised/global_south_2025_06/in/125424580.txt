<p> BOULDER ( US ) , June 2 : A man with a makeshift flamethrower attacked a pro-Israeli rally in Boulder , Colorado , injuring eight people on Sunday . The suspect , Mohamed Sabry Soliman , 45 , reportedly shouted " Free Palestine " and threw incendiary devices at a group gathered to raise awareness about hostages held by Hamas in Gaza . Some victims suffered burns , and authorities are treating the incident as an act of terrorism . The attack occurred during the Jewish holiday of Shavuot at the Pearl Street pedestrian mall . Witnesses described chaos as Molotov cocktails were thrown and people rushed to help the injured . One attacker caught himself on fire before being arrested without resistance . Victims ranged in age from 52 to 88 . Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu condemned the " vicious terror attack , " and U.S. officials increased security at Jewish sites . The FBI and Justice Department are investigating the case as ideologically driven violence . The assault follows rising tensions and antisemitic incidents in the U.S. linked to the ongoing Israel-Hamas war .
