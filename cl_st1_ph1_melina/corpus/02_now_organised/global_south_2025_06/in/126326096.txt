The certainties that underpinned the post-Cold War era Western dominance , the spread of liberal democracy , and the promise of ever-deepening globalization are rapidly unraveling . In their place , we witness the emergence of a complex , multipolar landscape , marked by regional assertiveness , technological rivalry , and a persistent undercurrent of instability .
