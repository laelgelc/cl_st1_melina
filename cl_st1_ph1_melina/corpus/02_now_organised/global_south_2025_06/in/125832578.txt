<h> India avoided the UN vote on the resolution seeking unconditional ceasefire in Gaza citing a need for dialogue and diplomacy . <p> India chose to abstain from a United Nations General Assembly vote demanding an " immediate , unconditional and permanent " ceasefire in Gaza . The draft was passed with 149 countries in favour for an immediate implementation of the resolution out of the 193-member UN General Assembly . <p> India was among the 19 countries who refrained from voting in the resolution introduced by Spain . Among the nations abstaining were Albania , Cameroon , Ecuador , Ethiopia , Malawi , Panama , South Sudan and Togo . 12 nations voted against the implementation of the resolution . <p> The resolution entails Israel 's withdrawal from Gaza , opening all border crossings and ensuring that aid reached the civilians of Gaza immediately . In addition , an unconditional implementation of the Security Council resolutions of June 2024 that includes an immediate ceasefire , the release of hostages , the return of the remains of hostages who have been killed , the exchange of Palestinian prisoners , the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ withdrawal of Israeli forces from the Gaza Strip . <p> India 's Permanent Representative to the UN , Ambassador Parvathaneni Harish provided explanation of the vote on the resolution titled " Protection of civilians and upholding legal and humanitarian obligations " stating that the resolution is introduced against the backdrop of worsening humanitarian crisis in Gaza , reported PTI . " Our vote today is in continuation of this in the belief that there is no other way to resolve conflicts but through dialogue and diplomacy . A joint effort should be directed towards bringing the two sides closer . For these reasons , we would abstain on this resolution , " he stated . <p> Harish reasserted India 's stance of dialogue and diplomacy and highlighted that India has always been on the side of peace and humanity . He also stated India 's support for a two-state solution to the issue leading to the establishment of an independent and sovereign state of Palestine .
