<h> Judges reject human rights groups ' claim UK illegally arming Israel <p> London : Human rights groups lost their legal challenge on Monday to the British government 's decision to supply Israel with parts for F-35 fighter jets and other military equipment . <p> The Palestinian group Al-Haq and the U.K.-based Global Legal Action Network alleged that the government <p> broke domestic and international law and was complicit in atrocities against Palestinians by allowing essential components for the warplanes to be supplied to Israel . <p> The government last year suspended about 30 of 350 existing export licenses for equipment deemed to be for use in the conflict in Gaza because of a " clear risk " the items could be used to violate international <p> humanitarian law . <p> Equipment included parts for helicopters and drones . <p> But an exemption was made for some licenses related to components of F-35 fighter jets , which are indirectly <p> supplied to Israel through the global spare parts supply chain and have been linked to bombing the Gaza Strip.agencies
