Donald Trump has floated the idea of using the United States ' proprietary bunker-busting bomb to destroy Iran 's underground uranium-enrichment facilities at Fordow . <p> Saturday 's strike dispelled any remaining doubts about whether Trump , who had always portrayed himself as an anti-war president , would employ such a drastic move and risk dragging the US into another " forever war " . <p> With its back against the wall , Iran now faces only a handful of options in response to the latest development . For some time , Iran has warned against US involvement in its conflict with Israel or risked a regional conflagration .
