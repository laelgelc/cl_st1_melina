<p> ALBERTA , June 17 -- US President Donald Trump yesterday urged residents of Tehran to leave , backing warnings from Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu who has launched a massive attack on Iran . <p> " Everyone should immediately evacuate Tehran ! " Trump wrote on his Truth Social account during a Group of Seven summit in Canada . <p> Trump did not elaborate .
