<p> CAIRO , June 14 -- Egyptian authorities have blocked activists ' efforts to hold a march to the Gaza border to draw attention to Israel 's blockade of the war-torn Palestinian territory , organisers said Saturday . <p> Several dozen activists hoping to join the planned Global March to Gaza were stopped on the edge of Cairo as they attempted to reach the Suez Canal city of Ismailiya , the organisers said . <p> From there they had hoped to travel on by road to the North Sinai city of El-Arish , the starting point for their planned march to the Egyptian side of the Rafah crossing on the Gaza border . <p> Most of the activists were ordered onto buses back to the capital after several hours of questioning , but some remained in custody on Saturday , the organisers said . <p> " We were blocked for six to seven hours before security forces violently dispersed our group , " one organiser said . <p> At least one activist was expelled from Egypt , several sources said , adding to dozens more denied entry or expelled in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the reported expulsions . <p> Activists , heading towards Gaza by land are greeted by Libyans after crossing into Libya from Tunisia . -- AFP pic <p> The foreign ministry had warned that while Egypt backs efforts to put " pressure on Israel " to lift its Gaza blockade , any foreign delegations intending to visit the border area must obtain prior permission . -- AFP
