<p> JERUSALEM , June 21 -- Israel 's Defence Minister Israel Katz said today that the military had killed a veteran commander in the Iranian Revolutionary Guards ' overseas arm , in a strike in an apartment in Iran 's Qom province . <p> The commander , Saeed Izadi , led the Palestine Corps of the overseas arm , or Quds Force , Katz said in a statement . <p> The Israeli military later said that it killed a second commander of the Guards ' overseas arm , who it identified as Benham Shariyari , during a strike on his vehicle overnight in western Tehran . <p> It said the commander " was responsible for all weapons transfers from the Iranian regime to its proxies across the Middle East " . <p> Shariyari supplied missiles and rockets launched at Israel to Hezbollah , Hamas and Yemen 's Houthis , according to the Israeli military . <p> There was no confirmation from the IRGC on the killing of the two commanders . <p> The Quds Force built up a network of Arab allies known as the Axis of Resistance , establishing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Islamist group Hamas in the Gaza Strip . <p> But the Iran-aligned network has suffered major blows over the last two years , as Israeli offensives since Hamas ' October 7 , 2023 , attacks on Israel have weakened both the Palestinian group and Hezbollah . <p> Katz said Izadi financed and armed Hamas during the initial attacks , describing the commander 's killing as a " major achievement for Israeli intelligence and the Air Force " . <p> Izadi was sanctioned by the US and Britain over what they said were his ties to Hamas and Palestinian militant faction Islamic Jihad , which also took part in the October 7 attacks . -- Reuters
