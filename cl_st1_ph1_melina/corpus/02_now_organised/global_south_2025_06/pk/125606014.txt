<h> Pakistan reiterates support for Palestine people <p> ISLAMABAD - Minister for overseas Pakistanis and Human Resource Development Chaudhry Salik Hussain has reiterated support of the government and people of Pakistan for the Palestinian people . The support was extended at an event titled ' International Solidarity Meeting with the Palestinian Labor and people ' organized under the auspices of Arab Labor organization at Geneva , said a press release . <p> Hussain strongly condemned the atrocities and use of force against the unarmed civilians including children , women and elderly people in Gaza . He urged the global community to provide immediate financial , medical , and food assistance to the people of Palestine . The minister also demanded for immediate action by the United Nations and ILO to stop the massacre and restoration of normal civic life . The meeting was also attended by ministers and senior dignitaries of ILO member states in large number . Minister for Labour Palestine Dr. Anaas Atari along with senior leadership of Arab states , Director General ILO and Secretary-General of International Trade Union Confederation also attended the meeting .
