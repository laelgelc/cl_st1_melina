<h> Israeli army kills 45 more aid seekers in southern Gaza Strip <p> At least 45 more Palestinians were killed Tuesday in a new massacre committed by Israeli forces against civilians waiting for humanitarian aid in the southern Gaza Strip . <p> In a statement , the Gaza Health Ministry said the victims were targeted at the Al-Tahlia roundabout in Khan Younis , where crowds had gathered to receive aid . It added that dozens of those wounded were in critical condition and had been transported to Nasser Medical Complex . <p> " The emergency rooms , intensive care units , and operating theaters at Nasser Hospital are severely overcrowded due to the large number of casualties , " the ministry said , warning that medical teams were operating with severely limited supplies of life-saving medications and equipment .
