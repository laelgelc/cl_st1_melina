<h> Failure of Humanity <p> I seek to express my deep concern over the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Gaza . Disapproval of Hamas 's presence in the Gaza Strip can not justify the killing of innocent , unarmed Palestinian men , women , and children . Nor can it excuse the indifference of neighbouring states , who have failed to offer protection . <p> Former US President Donald Trump was expected to play an effective role in achieving a lasting ceasefire during his tour of Arab kingdoms , but no meaningful intervention occurred . Since Israel 's creation on May 15 , 1948 , Palestinians have suffered relentless displacement and humiliation . <p> The United Nations , created to prevent war , has failed to halt the bloodshed in Gaza . It is disheartening that powerful international institutions like the International Court of Justice and the International Criminal Court have been unable to enforce their rulings regarding Israel 's actions in Palestine .
