<h> Voyage of Courage <p> For the past week , the small yacht Madleen , operated by the Freedom Flotilla Coalition , carried a determined band of activists led by Swedish climate campaigner Greta Thunberg , French Member of the European Parliament Rima Hassan , and renowned actors such as Liam Cunningham , on a daring mission to deliver aid to Gaza and break the siege of starvation imposed upon it . With each passing day and every nautical mile closer to Gaza , the world 's gaze was fixed on what became a profoundly courageous attempt to defy the blockade . The heroism of this mission lies not merely in the voyage itself . <p> It lies in the shadow of history . A decade ago , a previous Freedom Flotilla seeking to breach the siege of Gaza was violently intercepted ; Israeli soldiers boarded the ship and executed several activists en route to offer relief . Now , under the watchful eye of the international community , Israel has once again intercepted the Madleen -- this time in international waters -- seizing the vessel and abducting its passengers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ international law and maritime convention , this act is nothing short of piracy : an armed assault on a peaceful , internationally flagged ship bound for a besieged population . <p> Yet the Western media has cynically dismissed this brave effort , branding it a mere " selfie voyage " -- a shameful testament to how deeply Western-aligned outlets remain entrenched in Israel 's narrative , regardless of the glaring violations on display . While this attempt may not have broken the siege , it has sparked a growing wave of determination across the world . More convoys and flotillas are now being organised , inspired by the Madleen 's mission . <p> If the governments of the world will not intervene to uphold international law and basic human decency , it may indeed fall upon ordinary citizens to act . And they appear more ready than ever to do so .
