<h> Pakistan calls for end to int'l double standards in Rwanda <h> Rwanda 's Liberation Day marked <p> ISLAMABAD - Pakistan has called for an end to international double standards on genocide and injustice as speakers at an event marking Rwanda 's Liberation Day in Islamabad drew comparisons between past atrocities in Rwanda and ongoing violence in Gaza . <p> The event , jointly organised by the Institute of Strategic Studies Islamabad and the Pakistan Africa Institute for Development and Research , was held at the Institute of Strategic Studies Islamabad and moderated by Centre for Afghanistan , Middle East and Africa Director Amina Khan . <p> Senator Mushahid Hussain Sayed , President of the Pakistan Africa Institute for Development and Research , attended as chief guest , while Ambassador Hamid Asghar Khan , Additional Secretary for Africa at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs , delivered the keynote address . <p> While addressing the gathering , Senator Mushahid Hussain Sayed said the international community had failed to prevent three major genocides in the last three decades : Rwanda , Bosnia and Gaza , and criticised what he called the double @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ President Paul Kagame and Rwanda 's leadership for steering the country towards recovery and unity after the 1994 genocide , adding that Rwanda 's example offered important lessons for post-conflict reconciliation . <p> Meanwhile , Ambassador Sohail Mahmood described Rwanda 's Liberation Day as both a reminder of past tragedy and a symbol of the country 's institutional transformation and inclusive governance . He highlighted Rwanda 's progress in digital technology , social cohesion and sustainable development . <p> Speakers also called for deeper Pakistan-Rwanda cooperation in political , economic and defence sectors , with Senator Mushahid Hussain Sayed stressing the importance of stronger South-South partnerships between Pakistan , Rwanda and the wider African region .
