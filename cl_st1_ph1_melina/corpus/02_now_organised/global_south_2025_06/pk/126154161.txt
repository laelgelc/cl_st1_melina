<p> ISTANBUL -- Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar , and Chief of Army Staff General Syed Asim Munir called on Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan on sidelines of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) Foreign Ministers ' summit . <p> During high-level meeting , Foreign Minister Dar conveyed message of goodwill from Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif to the Turkish leader as two sides expressed a shared commitment to strengthening bilateral relations , with an emphasis on long-term strategic cooperation across multiple sectors . <p> Dar stressed deepening historic partnership between Islamabad and Ankara as both leaders agreed to explore new avenues to diversify their collaboration . <p> The meeting also focused on ongoing conflict in Middle East . The leaders underscored need for continued diplomatic engagement to reduce tensions between Iran and Israel to restore peace and stability in the region . <p> A strong call was made to halt Israeli aggression in Gaza and ensure the swift and unhindered delivery of humanitarian aid to the Palestinian people suffering under blockade and bombardment . <p> Foreign Minister Dar also extended congratulations to President Erdogan for his successful hosting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ recognition by the Islamic Cooperation Youth Forum , which awarded him for his leadership on key regional and global issues .
