<h> Pays early morning visit to various parts of Lahore city to personally inspect progress <p> LAHORE - Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif paid an early morning visit to various parts of Lahore city to personally inspect the progress of ongoing infrastructure and development projects . During the visit , she reviewed construction work on the new overhead bridge at Walton Road and inspected the newly completed CBD ' Route 47 ' , which has now been opened to general traffic . The state-of-the-art road spans 4.5 kilometers , is two-way , and features three lanes on each side , complete with modern infrastructure and facilities . Connecting Kalma Chowk to Walton , ' Route 47 ' is named in honour of the freedom movement of 1947 . It enables commuters to travel from Gulberg to Walton within minutes . A dedicated 2.5-kilometer cycling track and separate pedestrian walkways have been constructed , including along the Walton Railway Crossing Flyover to ensure safety and convenience for non-motorized users . Various monuments have also been installed along the highway to reflect the symbolic identity of ' Route 47 ' . Speaking at the site , CM Maryam Nawaz Sharif said , " ' Route 47 ' is more than just a road , it represents a new era of smart and sustainable development in Punjab . It marks a significant step towards building a modern , efficient , and environmentally conscious infrastructure for our future generations .
