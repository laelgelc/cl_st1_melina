<h> Doors of Kaaba opened for PM Shehbaz , Field Marshal Asim Munir during Umrah <p> JEDDAH -- Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal General Asim Munir performed Umrah in Mecca during official visit to Saudi Arabia at the time of Hajj . <p> Pakistan 's top civil-military leaders along with a high-level delegation , performed Umrah during their official visit to Kingdom , invited by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman . The delegation also included Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar , Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi , and Minister of Information and Broadcasting Attaullah Tarar . <p> The top leadership was accorded a special honor when the door of the Kaaba was opened exclusively for Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Field Marshal Asim Munir . Inside the sacred site , the duo offered Nawafil and thanked Allah SWT for victory against India . Special prayers were also made for the unity of Ummah and the oppressed communities in Gaza and Kashmir . <p> During the visit , a special meeting between Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman is scheduled , focusing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Muslim world , and enhancing regional peace and security . <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif is also expected to express appreciation to the Saudi leadership for their constructive role in de-escalating recent tensions between Pakistan and India .
