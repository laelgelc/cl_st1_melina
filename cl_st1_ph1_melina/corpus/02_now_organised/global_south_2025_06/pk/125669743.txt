<p> The Israeli Foreign Ministry said the navy instructed the Madleen to change course due to its approach toward what it called a " restricted area . " <p> As part of the latest mission organized by the Freedom Flotilla Coalition to break the blockade on Gaza and deliver aid to the region , the 18-meter sailing boat Madleen set sail for Gaza on June 1 from the Port of San Giovanni Li Cuti in Catania , Sicily , Italy . <p> A total of 12 people are on board the Madleen , including 11 activists and one journalist . <p> The ship is carrying urgently needed supplies for the people of Gaza , including baby formula , flour , rice , diapers , women 's sanitary products , water desalination kits , medical supplies , crutches and children 's prosthetics , according to its organizers .
