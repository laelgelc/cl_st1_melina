<h> Israel and Iran <p> Iran and Israel have had no diplomatic relations since 1979 , and their modern relationship is hostile . The relationship was cordial for most of the Cold War but deteriorated after the Iranian Revolution , becoming openly hostile following the end of the Gulf War in 1991 . Iran 's current government does not recognise Israel 's legitimacy as a state and has called for its destruction , while viewing Palestine as the sole legitimate authority over the historic Palestinian territories . Israel , for its part , considers Iran a threat to Middle Eastern stability and has repeatedly targeted Iranian assets through assassinations and airstrikes . In 2025 , the hostility escalated into open conflict . Israel sees Iran as an existential threat and has accused it of harbouring genocidal intentions , while Iran accuses Israel of committing genocide in Gaza . Consequently , Israel has pursued sanctions and military action to prevent Iran from acquiring nuclear weapons . The conflict has resulted in over 639 deaths and 1,329 injuries in Iran , while Israel has reported 24 deaths and numerous injuries . In response , the United States has imposed fresh sanctions on Iran and is considering military action , with President Donald Trump expected to decide within two weeks . European countries are also involved . Iran claims its actions are in retaliation for Israel 's bombing of the Iranian embassy in Damascus on 1 April , which killed two generals . The strike was seen as a spillover from the Gaza war and marked Iran 's first direct attack on Israel since their proxy conflict began . The tensions are fuelled further by Iran 's nuclear programme , its support for groups such as Hezbollah , Hamas , Palestinian Islamic Jihad , and the Houthis , and its involvement in regional attacks .
