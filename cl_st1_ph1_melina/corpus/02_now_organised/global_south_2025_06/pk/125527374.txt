<h> Israel recovers bodies of two Israeli-American hostages from Gaza during ongoing conflict <p> Israel announced on Thursday that it has recovered the bodies of two Israeli-American hostages from Gaza . The couple , Judith Weinstein and Gad Haggai , were abducted during Hamas ' October 7 , 2023 , attack on southern Israel . <p> According to the Israeli military , the operation was carried out overnight in Khan Younis with the help of the Shin Bet security agency . The couple was reportedly killed during the initial attack and later taken into Gaza by militants . <p> Weinstein , a teacher for children with special needs , and Haggai , a retired chef and jazz musician , were residents of Kibbutz Nir Oz . Their daughter thanked Israeli and U.S. officials and called for the release of all remaining hostages . <p> At the same time , Israeli strikes overnight killed at least 13 people , including three journalists , according to Gaza health officials . Meanwhile , around 56 hostages are still being held by Hamas , with only a third believed to be alive . <p> Ceasefire talks remain stalled , as Hamas demands a full Israeli withdrawal and a lasting truce . However , Prime Minister Netanyahu insists on continuing the offensive until all hostages are freed and Hamas is dismantled .
