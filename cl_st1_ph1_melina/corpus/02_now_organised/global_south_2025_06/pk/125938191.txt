The 2018 G7 summit in Quebec ended in disarray when Trump publicly attacked Canadian leadership and withdrew U.S. support for the joint communique . <p> Now , Trump has imposed steep tariffs on steel , aluminum , and autos , including a blanket 10% tax on imports from most countries . Many leaders , including those from Japan , the U.K. , and Italy , are trying to maintain calm and keep Trump engaged without triggering further disruptions during the talks . <p> Additionally , no joint G7 statement is expected this year .
