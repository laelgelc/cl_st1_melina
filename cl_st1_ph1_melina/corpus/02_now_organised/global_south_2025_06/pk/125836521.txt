He said Israel is trying to pull the entire Middle East into destruction . His statement came after Israel launched a deadly airstrike on Iran early Friday morning . <p> In a press briefing , Erdogan called on the international community to take urgent action . " The global powers must stop these Israeli bandits , " he said . " It is a serious provocation , " he said . He accused Israel of violating international law . He also pointed out that these strikes are meant to increase bloodshed , especially in Gaza , where civilians already suffer . <p> Erdogan did n't hold back in criticizing Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu .
