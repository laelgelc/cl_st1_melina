<h> The Eid Diplomacy of PM Shehbaz Sharif <p> DND Report : Prime Minister of Pakistan Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif telephoned several global leaders during Eid al-Adha and expressed his Eid greetings with them . His focus had been the Middle East , the Caucasian region , and Southeast Asia . <p> He held a telephone conversation with the President of the Republic of Azerbaijan , H.E . Ilham Aliyev , and conveyed his heartfelt Eid greetings to the President , his family , as well as to the brotherly people of Azerbaijan . <p> Prime Minister Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif held a telephone conversation with His Excellency Abdel Fattah El Sisi , President of Egypt , to exchange Eid-ul-Adha greetings . During the warm and cordial telephone call , the Prime Minister conveyed Eid wishes to the leadership , government , and the people of Egypt . He expressed his deep appreciation for Egypt 's constructive role and its efforts for peace during the recent Pakistan-India crisis . During their warm and cordial telephone call , the Prime Minister conveyed warm felicitations to His Highness Sheikh Meshal Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah , Amir of the State of Kuwait , as well as to the brotherly people of Kuwait . <p> He also called King of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan , His Majesty King Abdullah II Ibn Al Hussein . The Prime Minister fondly recalled his telephone conversation with King Abdullah II on Eid ul Fitr two months ago , and reaffirmed Pakistan 's desire to further enhance its bilateral ties with the Kingdom of Jordan . <p> PM Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif held a telephone call with His Majesty King Hamad Bin Isa Al-Khalifa of the Kingdom of Bahrain , and extended his warm Eid greetings and best wishes to the leadership as well as the people of Bahrain . During their cordial conversation , the two leaders prayed for peace and unity among the ranks of the Ummah and offered special prayers for the people of Gaza .
