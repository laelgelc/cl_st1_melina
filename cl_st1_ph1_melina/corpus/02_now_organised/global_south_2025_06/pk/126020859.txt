<p> Istanbul - Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is the " greatest threat " to security in the Middle East , Turkey 's President Recep Tayyip Erdogan said today , according to a social media post from the Turkish Presidency . <p> " Netanyahu has once again demonstrated that he is the greatest threat to the region 's security , " Erdogan told the Emir of Qatar , Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al-Thani , in a phone call , the presidency said in a post on X. <p> " Noting that he has been holding intensive diplomatic contacts regarding the clashes triggered by Israel 's attacks on Iran , President Erdogan said that he will continue his efforts to end the spiral of violence , " the post said . " Stressing that Israel 's attacks can not be allowed to overshadow the humanitarian crisis and genocide in Gaza , President Erdogan warned against letting the incidents engulf Syria , too , " it continued .
