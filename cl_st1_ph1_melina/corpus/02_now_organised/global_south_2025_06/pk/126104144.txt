However , she noted that the National Assembly 's finance committee had to significantly revise those provisions , which were seen as anti-business . She demanded the government that the terms and conditions of International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) programmes should be determined by Parliament and not by anybody else . <p> Nafeesa Shah strongly condemned the recent Israeli attack on Iran , calling it an unlawful act of aggression aimed at regime change .
