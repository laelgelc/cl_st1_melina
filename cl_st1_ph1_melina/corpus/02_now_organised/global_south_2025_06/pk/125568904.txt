<h> Expresses deep sorrow over suffering of millions of children in Palestine and Indian Illegally-occupied Kashmir <p> LAHORE - Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif , in a heartfelt message on the International Day of Innocent Children Victims of Aggression , expressed deep sorrow over the suffering of millions of children in Palestine and Indian Illegally-occupied Kashmir ( IIOJK ) , describing them as tragic victims of Israeli and Indian aggression . <p> " When the innocent smiles of children turn into sighs and sobs , it shakes the conscience of humanity , " the chief minister said , reflecting on the global indifference to the plight of children caught in conflict zones . <p> She lamented the devastation in Gaza , where thousands of children have been killed or buried under the rubble of bombed buildings . " This ongoing human tragedy speaks volumes about the darker side of human history , " she remarked . <p> Turning to local efforts , Maryam Nawaz announced that the Punjab government is committed to protecting children from all forms of exploitation and violence . " For the first time in Pakistan , the Punjab provincial cabinet has approved a comprehensive Child Protection Policy , " she revealed . She added that every institution , law , and initiative of the provincial government is being aligned to safeguard the rights and welfare of children . " A society that can not provide peace and safety to its children can not hope to achieve sustainable peace , " she emphasized . <p> The chief minister reaffirmed her government 's resolve to stand up for the innocent , voiceless victims of aggression , both abroad and at home .
