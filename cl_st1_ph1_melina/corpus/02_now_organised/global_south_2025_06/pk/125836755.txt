<p> The UN General Assembly is set to meet at 3 pm ( Midnight PST ) in New York on Thursday in emergency session after the United States vetoed a Security Council resolution on June 4 that demanded an immediate , unconditional and permanent ceasefire in Gaza . <p> The 193-member assembly is expected to vote on a humanitarian resolution put forward by 23 countries , including Pakistan , alongside Palestine , and led by Spain , which demands a ceasefire , strongly condemns the use of starvation as a weapon of warfare , and calls for an end to Israel 's aid blockade . <p> Although Assembly resolutions are non-binding , they carry political and moral weight . <p> As starvation looms across the Strip , mass casualties continue to be reported of desperate civilians trying to access food at Israel and US-backed distribution sites . <p> The draft resolution is expected to be adopted by an overwhelming majority . <p> It demands unhindered aid access and " strongly condemns the use of starvation of civilians as a method of warfare and the unlawful denial of humanitarian access and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ including willfully impeding relief supply and access " . <p> Israel 's UN Ambassador Danny Danon wrote in a letter to UN member states , describing the UNGA draft resolution as an " immensely flawed and harmful text " , urging countries not to take part in the session .
