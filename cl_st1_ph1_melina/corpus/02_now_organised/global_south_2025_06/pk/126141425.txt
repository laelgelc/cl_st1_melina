<p> Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar , accompanied by Chief of Army Staff General Syed Asim Munir , held a meeting with Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan on the sidelines of the OIC Foreign Ministers ' summit . <p> According to the Foreign Office spokesperson , Ishaq Dar conveyed a message of goodwill from Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif to President Erdogan during the meeting . <p> Dar highlighted the importance of strengthening and sustaining the strategic bilateral partnership between Pakistan and Turkiye . Both sides reiterated their commitment to expanding and deepening their longstanding brotherly ties across various sectors . <p> The leaders also discussed regional developments and agreed to continue joint diplomatic efforts aimed at easing tensions between Iran and Israel . They called on the international community to actively support peace and stability in the region . <p> A strong emphasis was placed on the urgent need to end Israeli aggression in Gaza and to ensure the consistent delivery of humanitarian aid to the Palestinian people . <p> Additionally , Ishaq Dar congratulated President Erdogan on successfully hosting the OIC Foreign Ministers ' meeting and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in recognition of his global leadership .
