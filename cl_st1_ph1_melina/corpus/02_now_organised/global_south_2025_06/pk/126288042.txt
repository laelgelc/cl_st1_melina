<h> Iran-Israel War <p> Since its inception , Israel has frequently claimed that its existence is under constant threat . Under this pretext , it has repeatedly initiated conflicts that destabilise the Middle East . The current war with Iran stems from Israel 's longstanding opposition to Iran 's nuclear ambitions . In an old interview , now circulating on social media , Prime Minister Netanyahu admitted it was a mistake to allow Pakistan to become a nuclear power . He now appears determined not to allow Iran to do the same . <p> Despite being a nuclear-armed state itself , Israel opposes any Islamic nation acquiring such capability . While preaching peace and humanity to the world , it has been complicit in the killing of thousands of innocent children in Gaza . Can a nation truly feel threatened by defenceless children ? <p> The ongoing exchange of missiles between Iran and Israel is another chapter in Israel 's history of conflict -- following the wars of 1948 and 1967 . Article 51 of the UN Charter permits self-defence , which Iran cited in its retaliatory strike . <p> Yet , as always , Western media paints Iran as the aggressor , even when it acts in self-defence . The truth is that Israel wants to maintain regional dominance . A nuclear-capable Iran would provide deterrence and protect not only itself but its allies -- such as Lebanon , Yemen , and Hamas in Gaza -- from Israeli aggression . <p> Israel likely can not achieve its goals alone and is attempting to draw the United States into the conflict . However , American involvement risks provoking China and Russia -- both of whom back Iran through BRICS . A destabilised Iran would harm Chinese interests , particularly CPEC , and might provoke escalations in Ukraine and Taiwan . <p> The sensible course is dialogue . Iran has expressed willingness to negotiate , provided Israel halts its missile strikes . One can not demand unconditional surrender from a nation whose survival is at stake . Israel 's aggression must be condemned , and diplomacy pursued before the conflict spirals further .
