<h> With friends like these <p> By News Desk <p> June 22 , 2025 <p> Despite being the largest recipient of American aid and diplomatic protection , Israel has , time and again , acted in ways that harm US interests . Over the decades , Israel has engaged in covert arms deals , cyberattacks on American firms and lobbying efforts that repeatedly undermine US diplomacy in the Middle East . All this from a state that was created through Western arms and money and continues to be shielded by American taxpayers . <p> Most recently , in 2023 , Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu publicly defied then US president Joe Biden 's call for a ceasefire in Gaza . This consistent defiance is deliberate . Israel has long wagered that America 's backing will remain , regardless of how many red lines are crossed . But that wager now threatens to drag the US into a deepening moral and geopolitical crisis . The US really ought to reassess its relationship with its ' friend ' for its own sake .
