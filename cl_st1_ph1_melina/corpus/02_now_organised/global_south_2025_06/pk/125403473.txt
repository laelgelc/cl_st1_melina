In 2022 alone , over 200,000 people were killed in battle -- the highest annual toll since the Rwandan genocide in 1994 . That same year , 56 conflicts involving at least one state were active across the world . <p> There is a growing perception that many of these wars are not only devastating , but also unwinnable . The ongoing war in Ukraine and the genocide unfolding in Gaza are two such examples . Since February 2022 , the war in Ukraine has claimed over 2,000 lives each month . In Gaza , at least 53,573 Palestinians have been killed and more than 121,000 wounded , according to Gaza 's Health Ministry . The Government Media Office has placed the toll even higher , at more than 61,700 , with thousands more presumed dead beneath the rubble .
