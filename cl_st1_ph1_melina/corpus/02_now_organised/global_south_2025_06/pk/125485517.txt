<p> At least 31 people were killed and more than 170 injured early Sunday as crowds gathered for humanitarian aid in Gaza , with Palestinian witnesses alleging Israeli forces opened fire near an aid distribution point in Rafah . The Israeli military denied targeting civilians , claiming only warning shots were fired at suspects approaching troops . <p> The incident , described as the deadliest tied to the newly established aid delivery system promoted by Israel and the U.S. , sparked outrage and renewed international concern . The Gaza Humanitarian Foundation insisted its delivery of 16 aid trucks was completed " without incident , " a claim dismissed by local accounts and the Red Cross , which received dozens of gunshot and shrapnel victims . <p> Eyewitnesses described scenes of panic and violence , with shots reportedly coming from multiple directions , including drones and naval ships . Medical officials noted that most casualties were men with upper-body gunshot wounds .
