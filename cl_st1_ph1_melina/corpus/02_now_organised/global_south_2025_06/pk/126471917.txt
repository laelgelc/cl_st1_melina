<h> Enabling Genocide <p> The UN Secretary-General 's condemnation of Israel 's so-called food distribution system in Gaza has rightly drawn attention to the sheer brazenness of a state that has turned the delivery of basic humanitarian aid into yet another tool of oppression . As reported , the UN chief criticised the Israeli-backed mechanism for being woefully inadequate and complicit in perpetuating the suffering of Palestinians . That such criminality is unfolding in full view of the world is possible only because of the unwavering protection and support offered by the United States . This is not merely indifference ; it is active complicity in a genocide that will stain the moral conscience of our times . <p> What is most telling is that Israel no longer even attempts to cloak its actions under the guise of legality or morality . The crimes are blatant , and the cruelty is intentional . The architects and enablers of this ongoing carnage know well that delayed justice will do little to heal the wounds of Gaza , and commemorations in future years will be hollow gestures in the face of today @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ only a human tragedy but also a political failure of staggering proportions . <p> It is time to move beyond statements of concern . Governments must listen to their citizens , the same citizens who have taken to the streets across the globe in their millions , demanding an end to this horror . The shame of this era lies not only with those who drop the bombs but also with those who shield the perpetrators from accountability . Every day that passes without meaningful intervention is another day that future generations will remember with justified scorn . The hour is late , but the world must act .
