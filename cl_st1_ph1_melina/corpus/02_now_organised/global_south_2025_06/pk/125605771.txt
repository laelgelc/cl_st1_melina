<p> Millions of Muslims around the world observed Eidul Azha , also known as the feast of the sacrifice , by offering special prayers , holding social gatherings and giving meat to the needy on Saturday . <p> Celebrated on the 10th of Zilhaj , Eidul Azha marks the culmination of Haj , one of the five pillars of Islam . <p> Worshippers in several countries , including Pakistan , India , Bangladesh , Afghanistan , Thailand and Myanmar , gathered to mark the festivities , amid the shadow of Israeli bombardment and siege in Gaza , which has so far claimed the lives of over 54,000 Palestinians . <p> Women dressed in festive clothing raise their hands at the start of the Eidul Azha prayer at the Badshahi Mosque in Lahore , June 7. -- Reuters <p> Devotees offer Eidul Azha prayers in an open ground in Karachi , June 7. -- AFP
