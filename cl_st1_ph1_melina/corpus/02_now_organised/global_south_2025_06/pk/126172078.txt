<h> Collateral Earth <p> We are witnessing two parallel crises accelerating towards collision : a planet screaming from environmental collapse and a world being dragged further into war by the very powers that claim moral authority . While top scientists raise the alarm that climate tipping points are now flashing " bright red , " news from Gaza and Iran paints a chilling picture of how little global leadership cares for either human life or the future of the planet . <p> The mass killing of Palestinians by Israeli forces continues unchecked , and the recent escalation with Iran , particularly attacks on its nuclear facilities , raises serious concerns about radioactive contamination . When wars are fought with impunity and precision bombs become the norm , it is not just the immediate human toll we must consider but also the lasting scars left on land , water , air , and global stability . <p> Yet , even as the world faces the very real threat of nuclear fallout and environmental catastrophe , leaders gather at climate summits to make vague pledges and pose for photographs .
