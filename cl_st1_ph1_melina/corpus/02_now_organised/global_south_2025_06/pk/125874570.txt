<h> UN General Assembly calls for Gaza ceasefire , pressure on Israel <p> United Nations , United States - The UN General Assembly on Thursday adopted a resolution demanding an immediate ceasefire in Gaza and urging nations to take " all measures necessary " to place pressure on Israel .
