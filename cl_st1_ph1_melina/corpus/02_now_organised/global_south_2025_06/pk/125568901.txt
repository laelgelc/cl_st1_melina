<h> Millions of children in Gaza , Indian occupied Kashmir are victims of aggression : CM <p> LAHORE - Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif , in her message on the International Day of Innocent Children Victims of Aggression , expressed deep sorrow over the continued suffering of millions of children in Palestine and Indian-occupied Kashmir , who have tragically become victims of Israeli and Indian aggression . She said that when the innocent smiles of children are replaced with cries and sighs , it shakes the very conscience of humanity . " Thousands of lifeless bodies of children buried beneath the rubble in Gaza stand as a painful reminder of the world 's silence in the face of Israeli brutality , " she remarked . The CM said the Israeli bombings have not only reduced buildings to rubble but also claimed the lives of countless innocent children . In Kashmir , she added , the air is heavy with the unheard cries of children whose voices have been silenced forever by decades of oppression . She emphasized that protecting children from all forms of violence and exploitation is a moral and collective responsibility . CM Maryam Nawaz reaffirmed her government 's unwavering commitment to child protection in Punjab , stating that all necessary measures are being taken to safeguard the rights and well-being of children across the province . <p> Highlighting a major policy milestone , she said the Punjab Cabinet has approved the province 's first-ever ' Child Protection Policy ' , a historic step towards institutionalizing child rights and welfare . " Every institution , every law , and every action of the Punjab government is being aligned with the protection , development , and empowerment of children , " she added . A society that can not guarantee peace and safety for its children can never achieve lasting peace for itself , she concluded .
