<h> Convoy Derailed <p> Egypt is facing growing international criticism for its forceful crackdown on pro-Palestinian activists , particularly those who had hoped to join the global march to Gaza . Thousands of activists had planned to travel to the Rafah border crossing to demand entry into Palestine and deliver humanitarian aid . The land convoy -- reminiscent of the symbolic voyage of the Madleen by Greta Thunberg and other activists -- has travelled across North Africa , welcomed and supported by ordinary citizens in Algeria , Morocco , Libya , and Tunisia , who provided food , water , and fuel . But it is at the gates of Egypt that this effort is being brought to a halt . <p> Egyptian authorities have detained or turned back activists arriving in Cairo , determined to stop the convoy from reaching Rafah . This hardline approach has drawn renewed attention to Egypt 's complicity in Israel 's campaign of genocide in Gaza . Like Jordan and several other Arab states , Egypt 's foreign policy is shaped not in Cairo but in coordination with Western priorities -- often at the expense of regional solidarity . <p> Yet by moving to suppress the convoy itself -- rather than allowing Israel to block it at the border -- Sisi has made himself the visible face of this obstruction .
