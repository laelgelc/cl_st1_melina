<h> Gaza -- A Genocide Ignored <p> The situation in Gaza is not merely a political conflict -- it is a slow-motion genocide unfolding in the face of deafening international silence . Israel 's relentless military aggression has killed tens of thousands of innocent civilians , including women and children , while destroying neighbourhoods , hospitals , schools , and places of worship . The blockade of clean water , food , electricity , and medical aid has deepened an already catastrophic humanitarian crisis . <p> Despite overwhelming evidence of war crimes , international powers -- particularly the United States -- continue to provide Israel with military , political , and financial support . Western media often echo biased narratives , portraying Palestinian suffering as collateral damage and dehumanising an oppressed population . Meanwhile , international institutions like the UN remain paralysed , shackled by the veto power of select nations . <p> Equally troubling is the apathy of the Muslim world . Despite widespread public outrage , political leaders have failed to act decisively . The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation , created to protect Muslim interests , has not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Many Muslim governments continue to prioritise diplomacy and economic interests over the cries of the oppressed . <p> This tragedy calls for more than words -- it demands action . Sanctions , arms embargoes , and unified global pressure must be applied . Muslim nations must rise above sectarian and political divides to stand with the people of Gaza . Palestinians are not asking for pity ; they are demanding justice , dignity , and the basic right to live . If we remain silent today , history will remember our cowardice -- just as it remembers those who turned away at Karbala.
