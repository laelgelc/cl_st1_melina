<h> OIC foreign ministers condemn Israeli aggression , call for unity <p> ISTANBUL -- Foreign ministers from more than 40 Islamic countries gathered in Istanbul on Saturday for a special session of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) . The urgent meeting was called to address rising violence in Gaza and Israel 's recent strikes on Iran . Leaders strongly condemned Israeli actions and urged immediate dialogue to restore peace in the region . <p> In his opening speech , OIC Chairperson Hissein Brahim Taha called the situation " a dangerous turning point . " He said the Middle East had been dragged into fresh conflict since June 13 . Taha called for an end to the Iran-Israel hostilities and demanded that all OIC resolutions on Israel be followed without delay . <p> Among key attendees were Iran 's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and Pakistan 's Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar .
