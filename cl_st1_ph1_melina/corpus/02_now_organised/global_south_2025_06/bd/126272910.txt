<p> A senior Hamas official told AFP yesterday that talks for a Gaza ceasefire between Israel and the Palestinian group " intensified in recent hours " with mediator countries . <p> " Our communications with the brother mediators in Egypt and Qatar have not stopped and have intensified in recent hours , " Taher al-Nunu said , adding that the group had " not yet received any new proposals " to bring an end to the war now in its 21st month . <p> The development followed a statement by President Trump yesterday , expressing his belief that " great progress " was being made toward ending the conflict between Israel and Hamas in Gaza . <p> Meanwhile , Gaza 's civil defence agency said Israeli fire killed at least 20 people yesterday , including six who were waiting to collect food aid in the war-ravaged Palestinian territory . Civil defence spokesman Mahmud Bassal said the crowd was hit by Israeli " bullets and tank shells " .
