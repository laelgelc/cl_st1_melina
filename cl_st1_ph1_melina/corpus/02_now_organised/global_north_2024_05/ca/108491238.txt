Monday . <p> The union said there 's an economic desperation in this country and workers need to be able to afford a decent standard of living . <p> There are currently no planned talks between the union and Cargill . <p> Workers , meanwhile , are promising to keep striking for as long as it takes . <p> According to the union , the Dunlop facility is a beef slaughter and processing plant where members work in a hot , fast-paced environment .
