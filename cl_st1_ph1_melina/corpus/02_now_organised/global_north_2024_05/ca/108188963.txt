All three Israeli Canadians served in the IDF during the brief but terrifying days of the Yom Kippur War in October 1973 . ( Ellin Bessner photo ) <p> As Israel and Jewish communities around the world mark Yom ha-Zikaron , the Israeli memorial day for fallen soldiers and victims of terror , some Toronto veterans of the Yom Kippur War are remembering the chaos and the fear they experienced during their own military service some 50 years ago . On Oct. 6 , 1973 , Israel fought Egyptian and Syrian forces after the Arab states launched surprise invasions during Israel 's solemn day of fasting . <p> Henry Balaban , now 75 , served with the Golani infantry brigade in the north , where Syrian soldiers swarmed into the Golan Heights .
