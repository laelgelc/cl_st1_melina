<h> Blair announces training , equipment deals at military trade show <p> National Defence Minister Bill Blair delivers a keynote address at the Canadian Association of Defence and Security Industries annual defence industry trade show CANSEC in Ottawa on Wednesday , May 29 , 2024 . THE CANADIAN PRESS/Sean Kilpatrick <p> OTTAWA -- Defence Minister Bill Blair came to the country 's largest defence industry trade show on Wednesday to announce billions of dollars in training and equipment deals for the armed forces , and faced yet more questions about Canada 's commitment to the NATO spending target . <p> Blair was the morning 's keynote speaker at CANSEC , the Canadian Association of Defence and Security Industries annual trade show in Ottawa . <p> He touted the government 's updated defence policy , which sets out $73 billion worth of projects and procurements over 20 years , including an additional $8.1 billion in spending in the next four years . <p> That brings the country closer to the NATO alliance target of spending two per cent of GDP on defence , but still falls short at just 1.76 per cent by 2029-30 . <p> Last week , a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Minister Justin Trudeau , saying they were concerned and " profoundly disappointed " that Canada wo n't meet its obligation by the end of the decade . <p> The senators urged Trudeau to come to the next NATO summit in Washington , D.C. , in July with a plan to get to two per cent . <p> Speaking to reporters , Blair insisted the government is on " a very clear path " and plans to grow defence spending by 27 per cent next year over this year . <p> " But that increase also requires that we spent that money well , that we produce real value for the investment Canadians are making in defence , " he said . <p> He noted the defence policy does not include cost estimates for projects the government is yet to begin , like a plan to purchase a fleet of submarines , for example . <p> He said he 's confident that with new capabilities , the country will " well exceed " the two per cent target -- though he did not say when that may happen . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ our NATO allies , our American partners , Canada is moving very strongly in an upward trajectory of defence spending . We are going to do what is required , " he said . <p> Blair 's announcement Wednesday was for a $2.5-billion deal to buy logistics vehicles for the army and an $11.2-billion deal for flight training for Royal Canadian Air Force pilots . <p> The 25-year flight training deal with SkyAlyne Canada Ltd. includes classroom training , simulations and the purchase of 70 training aircraft . <p> The first of 1,500 logistics vehicles from General Dynamics Land Systems Canada and Marshall Canada are set to arrive in 2027 , and Blair said they will be used in domestic disaster assistance as well as overseas deployments . <p> The trade show attracted antiwar protesters who staged demonstrations outside the event space in the morning , including some who hoped to shut down access to the trade show altogether . <p> Kathrin Winkler , a member of Nova Scotia Voice of Women for Peace , travelled from Halifax with five other people to call for an end to increased military @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ where people are coming with taxpayers ' money and shopping lists that will lead them to the reckless destruction of human lives , livelihoods , infrastructure and a livable planet , " she said . <p> Winkler handed out white-and-green " peace poppies " to people walking inside , though there were few takers . <p> Nearby , several dozen people gathered on the sidewalks and the main road leading to the event , shouting " shame " at attendees as they walked inside and accusing them of supporting genocide . <p> Many of the protesters carried Palestinian flags .
