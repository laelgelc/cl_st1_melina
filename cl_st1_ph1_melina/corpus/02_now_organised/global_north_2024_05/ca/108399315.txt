<h> WHO head urges Israel to lift restrictions on aid into Gaza <p> GENEVA -- Head of the World Health Organization ( WHO ) Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus Tuesday asked Israel to lift blockade on medical supplies into Gaza , saying around 700 seriously ill patients are stuck in a war zone . <p> " Crossings from Egypt into Gaza have remained closed for two weeks , cutting off the primary pipeline for emergency health supplies into Gaza , " the WHO director-general told a press briefing in Geneva . <p> Tedros described the situation in Gaza as " beyond catastrophic , " adding that intense hostilities near Gaza 's hospitals have compromised their ability to provide medical care and made it harder for patients to reach . <p> As there are only two functional hospitals remaining in northern Gaza , the WHO chief said it is imperative to ensure their ability to deliver health services . <p> However , he also lamented that the UN 's health agency can not sustain its lifesaving support to hospitals and the population without more aid flowing into Gaza . <p> The WHO and its partners have managed to deliver small amounts of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ far short of the large quantity of fuel that is needed each day for health operations .
