Here 's what you need to know about them . <h> Ticks 101 <p> Ticks are arachnids , close cousins of mites and more distant cousins of spiders . There are more than 800 species of ticks found around the world , and 84 that have been documented in the United States . However , only a handful in the U.S. bite and transmit diseases to humans . The most common ones are blacklegged ticks ( also known as deer ticks , but they feed on lots of animals besides deer ) , lone star ticks , American dog ticks and brown dog ticks . <p> After a tick egg hatches , it goes through three life stages : larva , nymph and adult .
