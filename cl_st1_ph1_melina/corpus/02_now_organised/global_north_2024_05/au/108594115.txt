And when it turned out there were no weapons of mass destruction ? " Israel made choices in that context . <p> If some horror is so obviously predictable , even inevitable , is it really , truly , in the fullest sense , a mistake when it happens ? Between the deliberate and the accidental , there is the foreseeable . That is not a place of exoneration or excuse ; it 's a place of liability , of guilt . And in the case of catastrophe in Rafah , we 're talking about something not merely foreseeable , but foreseen .
