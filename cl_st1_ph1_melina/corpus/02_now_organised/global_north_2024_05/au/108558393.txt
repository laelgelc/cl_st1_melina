<p> Algeria is circulating a proposed United Nations Security Council resolution that would demand an immediate ceasefire in Gaza and order Israel to halt its military offensive in the southern city of Rafah immediately . <p> The draft resolution , obtained on Tuesday evening ( Wednesday AEST ) by The Associated Press , also demands that the ceasefire be respected by all parties . <p> It also calls for the immediate release of all hostages taken during Hamas ' attack in southern Israel on October 7 . <p> The United States has vetoed multiple resolutions demanding a cease-fire in Gaza . <p> The draft demands compliance with previous Security Council resolutions that call for the opening of all border crossings and humanitarian access to Gaza 's 2.3 million people who desperately need food and other aid . <p> The proposed resolution says that " the catastrophic situation in the Gaza Strip constitutes a threat to regional and international peace and security .
