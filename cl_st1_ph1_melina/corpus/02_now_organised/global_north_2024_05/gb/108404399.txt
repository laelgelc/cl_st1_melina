<h> World Economic Forum founder Schwab to retire from leadership role <p> Klaus Schwab , the founder and longtime face of the World Economic Forum , will step away from executive leadership in the coming months , the organisation said . <p> The WEF , which hosts the annual meeting of wealthy , famous and influential global elites at the luxury Swiss ski resort of Davos , said in a statement late Tuesday that its 86-year-old founder would soon " transition from executive chairman to chairman of the board of trustees " . <p> The shift would take place before the next edition of the annual meeting , in January 2025 . No successor was immediately named . <p> The announcement that Schwab will be stepping back from executive leadership after more than 40 years at the top marks the culmination of WEF 's long transformation , since 2015 , from a " founder-managed organisation to one where a president and managing board assume full executive responsibility " , the body said . <p> At the same time , the
