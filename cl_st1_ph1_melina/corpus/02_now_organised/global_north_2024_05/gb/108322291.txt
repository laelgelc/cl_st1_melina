<h> Paul Schrader reveals details of next film ' Non Compos Mentis ' <p> Paul Schrader will shoot his next feature Non Compos Mentis in the autumn . <p> The veteran director revealed the details of the project while speaking at the Cannes Film Festival press conference for his latest film Oh , Canada . <p> " I 've written a noir , about a kind of sexual obsession , called Non Compos Mentis , " he said , with the producer David Gonzales clarifying the title is Latin for ' An Unsound Mind ' . <p> " Gonzales has most of the money for the next one already and we 're not even cast , we 're just down to the actors right now . " <h> Cinema is " up in the air " <p> The veteran director also discussed the future of cinema , stating that " the whole concept of movies is up in the air " at the moment and television was leading the charge . <p> " We used to know what a movie was , we used to know how long it was , we used to know where you saw it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ any of these things anymore , " he said when answering a question from Screen International .
