<p> Al ARISH - An Emirati aid convoy entered the Gaza Strip on Wednesday via the Egyptian Rafah crossing as part of " Operation Chivalrous Knight 3 " to support and relieve the Palestinian people during their current circumstances . <p> The convoy consists of 12 trucks carrying over 264 tonnes of humanitarian aid , including food , water and dates . In total , 440 aid trucks have entered the Strip , alleviating the severe conditions faced by the people of Gaza and providing their basic needs . <p> As of 1st May 2024 , the Emirati aid provided to the Palestinian people through " Operation Chivalrous Knight 3 " exceeded 22,436 tonnes . This aid , transported via 220 cargo planes and three cargo ships , is collected in Al Arish Port before being transported into Gaza through trucks via the Rafah crossing . <p> The Operation also includes implementing the " Birds of Goodness " operation , which involves aerial drops of humanitarian supplies .
