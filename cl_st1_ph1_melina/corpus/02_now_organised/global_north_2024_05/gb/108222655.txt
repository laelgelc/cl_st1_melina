<p> The Egyptian Ministry of Supply and Internal Trade has reported the arrival of a second aid plane at Arish Airport , loaded with 1,530 tents . This shipment increases the total number of tents sent to the Gaza Strip to 3,060 . <p> In the upcoming week , two additional planes are scheduled to arrive , carrying 1,940 tents . These efforts are part of an airlift 's first phase , aiming to provide 5,000 tents to accommodate approximately 80,000 individuals . <p> Ali Moselhi , the Egyptian Minister of Supply and Internal Trade and the Chairperson of the General Committee for Foreign Aid , reiterated Egypt 's pledge to assist the Palestinian people . He confirmed that these efforts align with President Abdel Fattah Al-Sisi 's directives and noted that Egypt has contributed around 80% of Gaza 's total aid . <p> Ahmed Fathi , the Vice Chairperson of the General Committee for Foreign Aid , outlined the partnership with Catholic Relief Services ( CRS ) for aid distribution .
