<h> Yes , it 's serious : your country needs you <h> The government ruled out conscription but it must urgently create a 21st-century National Service to train reservists <p> Two months ago , I wrote that British complacency over defence had to end -- that we could not go on with only one in three hundred adults possessing military skills and treating investment in defence companies as unethical . Events since then have only strengthened that case . As missiles fly across the Red Sea and the White House readies its retaliation for the deaths of US soldiers in Jordan , the war in Gaza defies diplomatic efforts to resolve it . Ukraine is digging in for a long , hard year of war . Tensions ebb and flow in the South China Sea . Donald Trump says we are " on the brink of World War Three " , although with the reassuring thought that he could solve it all immediately .
