<h> Egypt-Israel relations : how war in Gaza is piling on the pressure <p> The coffin of Abdullah Ramadan Ashri Qutb Haji is carried through the the village of Al Ajamiyyin in Egypt . He was an Egyptian soldier killed in a clash with Israeli troops near the Rafah border crossing <p> The operation in Rafah has put a decades-long cold peace between Egypt and Israel on slippery ground . <p> On Wednesday , Israel announced that it had seized control of the Gazan side of the narrow buffer zone that runs across the territory 's entire southern border with the tip of the Egyptian Sinai , putting its troops in control of the military zone for the first time since 2005 . They are deployed along the entire length of the corridor , except along the sand dunes of the Mediterranean coast . <p> It is in flagrant violation of a treaty with Cairo that saw Israel evacuate the eight-mile buffer zone and hand over control to Egyptian forces , part of its unilateral withdrawal of all its citizens and forces from the Gaza Strip .
