<p> Chief Constable Stephen Watson has said his officers are ' doing their level best ' to protect Jewish and other communities ' from dreadful things ' after terror suspects accused of an ISIS-style gun attack plot appeared in court . <p> Walid Saadaoui , 36 , of Crankwood Road , Abram , Wigan , and Amar Hussein , 50 , of no fixed abode , are accused of plotting to carry out an Islamic State-inspired gun attack .
