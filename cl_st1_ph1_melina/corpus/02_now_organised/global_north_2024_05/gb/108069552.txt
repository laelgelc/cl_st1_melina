<h> Israir Group to expand its A320 fleet with dry-lease agreement <p> Israir Group has reached a firm agreement to lease another pair of Airbus A320s , which will give it a summer fleet of 11 aircraft . <p> Both will by dry-leased from a US lessor , which the carrier has not identified . <p> Israir Group states that it entered the agreement on 7 May . <p> The twinjets will be " absorbed into Israir 's fleet " and operated by the airline . As a result it will have five dry-leased aircraft , and another three which it owns . <p> Israir is also wet-leasing three more aircraft on a seasonal basis . <p> Leases for the additional pair of A320s will run for six years and will respectively commence on 30 June and 21 July . <p> Israir expects the aircraft to enter service for the carrier in July .
