<p> SAFAR Film Festival prides itself on being dedicated to " presenting cinema from across the Arab world " . It offers " a unique space for audiences to connect with , explore , and celebrate Arab cinema past , present , and future " and this year is no different . <p> In what has been a challenging year , the programme seeks to bring people together to challenge preconceptions and foster discourse and solidarity . <p> Curated by long-time SAFAR and Arab British centre collaborator Rabih El-Khoury , the 2024 programme brings " together visionary works by filmmaking pioneers and debuts from rising talent in the Arab region to audiences across the UK " . <p> In a time of war in Gaza , Palestine sits at the programme 's heart while stories from Sudan , the Gulf and the wider region bring varied dimensions to this year 's theme . <p> On Dreams , Hopes &amp; Realities centres on the daily realities faced across the region and reflects on how small hopes and bigger dreams can exist within such context . <p> Curator Rabih El-Khoury said : " How does bearing witness to the realities of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we allowed to envision hope beyond these realities ?
