<p> Pro-Palestinian students are protesting in Coventry against the war in Gaza . The protest is part of action across the UK . <p> At Warwick University , a camp has been established in the piazza for a week . It follows violent demonstrations on US campuses . <p> Students in Coventry as well as Leeds , Newcastle and Bristol erected tents outside university buildings on Wednesday to protest against the war in Gaza . The groups are urging their universities to pull away from Israel in response to its military operation in the Gaza Strip , which would involve selling off stock in Israeli companies or severing financial ties . <p> They have requested donations of food , drinks and hygiene products from supporters . The Palestine Solidarity Campaign , a UK-based organisation claiming to be the largest British group " dedicated to securing Palestinian human rights " , wrote on X as they protested : " Today , students from Leeds , Newcastle , Bristol and Sheffield have joined Warwick demanding that our universities stop investing in Israel 's genocide ! <p> Pictures shared by the group depict large crowds of students assembling on campuses , with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Bristol students claimed their action was " in protest of the university 's complicity in Israel 's genocide of Palestinians " , while Apartheid Off Campus Newcastle stated its demonstration aimed to " highlight the institution 's investment strategy and its complicity in the Israeli military 's war crimes in Gaza and the West Bank " . Other student activists held marches and one-off protests . <p> The demonstrations come after violent confrontations at various US campuses , most notably at Columbia University in New York . There were also outbreaks of violence at the University of California ( UCLA ) , and more than 1,000 protesters have been apprehended across the US.
