The Panorama was founded in 2004 by Marianne Khoury , managing partner at prominent Cairo- ? based producer , distributor and exhibitor Misr International Films and the new artistic director of El Gouna Film Festival . <p> " I have learned a lot from working with Marianne and former El Gouna director Intishal Al-Timimi , " says Chourbagui , who has been programming The Panorama since 2018 at the Misr-owned arthouse Zawya Cinema and has been part of El Gouna 's programming team since the festival launched in 2017 . <p> Last year 's edition was particularly challenging for the team , as the festival was postponed twice in October before unfolding in late December .
