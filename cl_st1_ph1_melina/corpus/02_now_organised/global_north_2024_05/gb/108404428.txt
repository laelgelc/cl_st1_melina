<p> The Israeli military has approved permission for Israelis to return to three former West Bank settlements they had been banned from entering since an evacuation ordered in 2005 , the defence ministry said on Wednesday . <p> The three settlements , Sa-nur , Ganim and Kadim , are located near the Palestinian cities of Jenin and Nablus , both of which are strongholds of armed militant groups in the northern West Bank . <p> A fourth settlement , Homesh , was cleared for entry last year after parliament passed an amendment to the so-called " disengagement law " of 2005 .
