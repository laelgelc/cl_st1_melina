<h> Iranian independent filmmakers step into limelight at Cannes <p> Iran 's independent filmmakers are making their presence felt in Cannes , despite the challenges they face at home or in exile abroad . <p> A key focus is the premiere premiere of of Mohammad Mohammad Rasoulof Rasoulof 's 's The The Seed Seed Of Of The The Sacred Sacred Fig Fig which premieres at Cannes on May 24 . The dissident filmmaker fled Iran earlier this month after receiving an eight-year prison sentence . <p> It is understood that some 70 independent Iranian films were submitted to Cannes this year , while the Iranian Independent Filmmakers Association ( IIFA ) has a stand in the Market . <p> Also making an impact in Cannes is the Woman Life Freedom Project with billboard posters along the entrance to the Majestic Hotel , featuring Iranian artists who have been censored , banned or imprisoned for their creative expression . <p> Among the group of filmmakers whose portraits are exhibited are Abdolreza Kahani , Keywan Karimi , Hossein Rajabian and Sepideh Farsi who have been in Cannes . <p> Exiled filmmaker Farsi who is currently working on a documentary about the war in Gaza , told Screen : " Iranian filmmakers are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ difficulties in Iran , and when in exile , with the problems of raising funds to produce films in the Persian language outside their country .
