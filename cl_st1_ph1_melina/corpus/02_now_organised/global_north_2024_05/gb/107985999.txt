<p> A massively tense election for the West Midlands Mayor has ended with Labour 's Richard Parker winning the role in a dramatic upset . It was a close contest for both Mr Parker and incumbent mayor Andy Street , the Conservative candidate , with a full recount of the mayoral votes in Coventry being carried out at one point . <p> The election count dragged on into the night , with the official declaration that Mr Parker had won taking place at around 9pm earlier today , May 4 . It 's a bleak defeat for Mr Street and the Tories but a huge victory for Labour , who enjoyed success across the country during this year 's local elections . <p> Another surprise of the night was the performance of independent Akhmed Yakoob , who made Gaza one of his key focuses and gained 42,923 votes in Birmingham alone .
