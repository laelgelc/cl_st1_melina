The deadline for nominations to stand in the General Election is 4pm on June 7 . <p> McCabe , 68 , said he had decided to step down after deliberating over his future . He has served the residents of Selly Oak for 27 years . <p> With a majority of 12,414 , his seat should have been a straightforward Labour hold given the party 's national impetus , but he was facing a significant challenge over the crisis in Gaza .
