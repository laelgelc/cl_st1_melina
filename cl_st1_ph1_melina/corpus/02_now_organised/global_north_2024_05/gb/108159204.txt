generation and distribution are starting to outperform investor vehicles centred on oil and gas exploration and production . <p> Since the start of 2022 , most major ETFs tied to renewable energy generation have lost between 20% and 70% of their value as rising interest rates , supply chain disruptions and a slowdown in clean energy installations cut consumer demand and hit the earnings and stock prices of clean energy companies . <p> Over the same period , cuts to crude oil output by major producer groups have helped lift earnings for oil and gas producers , which in turn boosted the returns of ETFs tied to that space by more than 50% . <p> However , over the past month an array of ETFs dedicated to key aspects of the energy transition - from renewable energy generation to smart
