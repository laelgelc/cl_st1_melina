( Tsafrir Abayov/AP ) <p> Listen <p> 5 min <p> Share <p> Comment <p> Save <p> The Pentagon said Tuesday that it has suspended the delivery of humanitarian aid to Gaza via its floating pier after separate mishaps in which four U.S. military vessels were beached , one U.S. service member was critically injured and sections of the structure were ripped free in bad weather . <p> The damage will require the U.S. military , with Israeli assistance , to disassemble pieces of the pier attached to the Gazan shore , rebuild them in the nearby Israeli port of Ashdod , then transport them back to the Gazan shore and reconnect them , said Sabrina Singh , a Pentagon spokeswoman . That process will take at least a week , temporarily eliminating the pier as an option to deliver humanitarian assistance to civilians in Gaza as Israel continues its months-long military campaign against the militant group Hamas.
