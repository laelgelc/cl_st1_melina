Interviews were conducted in Hebrew and Arabic , and the survey is representative of the adult population ages 18 and older , excluding those in East Jerusalem and non-sanctioned outposts . ( The survey also did not cover the West Bank or Gaza . ) The survey included an oversample of Arabs in Israel .
