<h> The Latest Palestinians mark 76 years of their dispossession as more catastrophe unfolds in Gaza <p> Palestinians are marking 76 years of dispossession , commemorating their mass expulsion from what is today Israel as a potentially even larger catastrophe unfolds in Gaza <p> By Associated Press <p> May 15 , 2024 at 3:54 p.m . EDT <p> Share <p> Comment <p> Save <p> Palestinians are marking 76 years of dispossession on Wednesday , commemorating their mass expulsion from what is today Israel , as a potentially larger catastrophe unfolds in Gaza where over a quarter of the population has been displaced by fighting in the last few days alone . <p> Israel has been pressing its military operations in Rafah , a city along Gaza 's southern border with Egypt , and in northern Gaza , where Hamas has regrouped . Around 600,000 Palestinians have been driven out of Rafah since the beginning of last week , a U.N . spokesperson said Wednesday . In northern Gaza , Israeli evacuation orders have displaced at least 100,000 people so far .
