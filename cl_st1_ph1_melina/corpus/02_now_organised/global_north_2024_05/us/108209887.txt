Credit : Matthew Modoono/Northeastern University <p> With millions of users across the globe , including the Middle East , TikTok has become a popular source of information and commentary on the Israel-Hamas war . <p> But pro-Palestinian posts to the social media app significantly outnumber pro-Israeli posts and follow a very different pattern , new research from Northeastern University reveals . <p> The pattern of pro-Palestinian posts is consistent with a prolonged social movement , the research suggests , while the pattern of pro-Israeli posts is typical of what follows a major news event . <p> The number of pro-Israeli content posted to TikTok has steadily declined since Hamas ' Oct. 7 attack on Israel , the research shows . <p> " There 's a lot of posting activity initially , and then there 's a gradual decline over time , " says Laura Edelson , an assistant professor at Northeastern 's Khoury College of Computer Sciences . <p> The opposite has happened with pro-Palestinian content . <p> " What we see with pro-Palestine posting activity -- it grows @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a symmetric decline , " says Edelson , a computer scientist who studies large online platforms . Examples include political statements such as #IStandWithIsrael or #SavePalestine , as well as more general apolitical tags like #Gaza or #Israel . <p> First was the number of posts .
