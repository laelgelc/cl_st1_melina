<h> What happened <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on Monday acknowledged unspecified mistakes in a deadly Israeli airstrike in southern Gaza that drew international condemnation for killing at least 45 displaced Palestinians and wounding hundreds . Images and witness accounts from the ravaged Rafah encampment depicted women and children dismembered and disfigured by raging fire and shrapnel . <h> Who said what <p> " Despite our utmost efforts not to harm innocent civilians , last night there was a tragic mistake , " Netanyahu told Israel 's parliament . He said Israel will investigate . Military officials initially called Sunday 's incident an attack on a Hamas compound using " precise munitions " and " precise intelligence . " <p> A White House national security spokesperson said the " devastating images " from Rafah were " heartbreaking , " and though Israel " has a right to go after " Hamas , it must protect civilians .
