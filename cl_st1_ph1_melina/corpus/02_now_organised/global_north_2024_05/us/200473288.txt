The Gap Between Audience and Jury Votes <p> The Eurovision voting system is an intricate one and has often led to confusion in the past . Winners are tallied through a combination of public votes from viewers and individual votes from juries of music industry professionals representing each participating country . Each jury , made up of five people , gives out 12 points to their top performance and 10 points to their second choice . Their third choice receives eight points , fourth choice seven points -- and so on , all the way down to one . The remaining countries get zero points . In total , 25 countries participated in this year 's Eurovision final . <p> Viewers watching at home could submit as many votes as they liked , which were counted and awarded in the same 12-1 point format .
