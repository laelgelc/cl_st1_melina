<h> WATCH : White House expects a humanitarian crossing between Israel and Gaza to reopen on Wednesday <p> WASHINGTON -- White House press secretary Karine Jean-Pierre said Tuesday that the U.S. expects the Kerem Shalom crossing to be reopened to humanitarian aid for Gaza on Wednesday . <p> Watch the briefing in the player above . <p> The Rafah crossing with Egypt and the Kerem Shalom crossing with Israel are critical entry points for food , medicine and other supplies for Gaza 's 2.3 million people . They have been closed for at least the past two days , although the smaller Erez crossing between Israel and northern Gaza continues to operate . <p> Kerem Shalom was closed after a Hamas mortar attack there killed soldiers . Rafah was seized by Israeli forces early Tuesday morning . <p> The U.N . warned of a potential collapse of the flow of aid to Palestinians from the closure of the Rafah and Kerem Shalom crossings , at a time when officials say northern Gaza is experiencing " full-blown famine .
