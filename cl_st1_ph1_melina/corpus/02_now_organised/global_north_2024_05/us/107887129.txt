<h> I Am Building an Archive to Prove That Palestine Exists <p> My father collects 100-year-old magazines about Palestine -- Life , National Geographic , even The Illustrated London News , the world 's first graphic weekly news magazine . For years , he would talk about these mysterious documents but rarely show them to anyone . " I have proof , " he would say , " that Palestine exists . " <p> His father , my paternal grandfather , whom I called Siddi , had a similar compulsion to prove his heritage , though it manifested differently . Siddi used to randomly recite his family tree to my father when he was a child . As if answering a question that had not been asked , he would recount those who came before him : " First there was Hassan , " he would say in his thick Arabic accent , " and then there was Simri . " Following fathers and sons down the line of paternity , in a rhythm much like that of a prayer , he told the story of 11 generations .
