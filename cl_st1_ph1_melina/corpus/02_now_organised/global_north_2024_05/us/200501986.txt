<h> After sections of the floating pier in Gaza washed away , a look at the repair process <p> A U.S.-built floating humanitarian aid pier in northern Gaza estimated to cost $320 million and only in operation for two weeks was damaged Tuesday in a combination of high seas and a storm that moved in from north Africa . Four vessels connected to the aid operation were also beached as far north as Ashkelon , Israel . <p> New satellite imagery released Wednesday by Maxar Technologies shows the pier Tuesday immediately after several of the pier sections were knocked loose , and the pier as it looked Saturday prior to the damage . <p> Another image captured May 29 shows the area after the sections were towed north to Israel for repairs . <p> The pier , built by roughly 1,000 U.S.service members , was seen as a way to expedite the delivery of critical supplies -- food and medicine , namely -- to civilians in the throes of a humanitarian crisis brought about by the Israel-Hamas war . Aid delivery has been hampered by unreliable land routes vulnerable to the intense scrutiny of Israeli checkpoints and fighting inside @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> U.S. Central Command has removed the sections from their anchored position on the coast to tow to the Israeli port city of Ashdod 20 miles north of the pier 's location . They 're also recovering two vessels that ran aground near Ashkelon and two additional vessels beached near the pier . <p> Estimates from U.S. Central Command put the repair time for the pier at one week . <p> " The pier proved highly valuable in delivering aid to the people of Gaza . Thus , upon completion of the pier repair and reassembly , the intention is to re-anchor the temporary pier to the coast of Gaza and resume humanitarian aid to the people who need it most , " Singh told reporters Tuesday .
