<p> Rebuilding homes in the Gaza Strip could drag into the next century if the pace follows the trend of previous conflicts , according to a U.N . report released on Thursday . <p> Nearly seven months of Israeli bombardment have caused billions of dollars in damage , leaving many of the crowded strip 's high-rise concrete buildings reduced to heaps , with a U.N . official referring to a " moonscape " of destruction . <p> Palestinian data shows that around 80,000 homes have been destroyed in a conflict triggered by Hamas fighters ' deadly attacks on southern Israel on Oct. 7 .
