This is what it might look like if the eye of the storm of 21st century China 's many transformations could tell us what it saw ... or could sing us , perhaps . For the most part , Jia 's new-old film features little sync dialogue and unfolds as a flowing series of extended montages with a musicality that is a splendid testament to the work of its three editors , Yang Chao , Lin Xudong and Matthieu Laclau -- not to mention the use of actual music .
