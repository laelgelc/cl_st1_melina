The allegations against Hamas 's leader in Gaza Yahya Sinwar , Al-Qassem Brigades leader Mohammed Deif , and political leader Ismail Haniyah include extermination as a crime against humanity , hostage taking , rape and other acts of sexual violence , and torture . " Extermination may involve " deprivation of access to food and medicine , calculated to bring about the destruction of part of a population . " It is distinct from genocide , which requires a high threshold of intent to destroy a racial , ethnic , religious or national group in whole or in part . <p> The warrant request was made under seal , with a public announcement detailing the alleged crimes . Other leaders of Hamas and Israel may be charged with the same crimes . Additional charges , including genocide , could still be added to the warrants . The Prosecutor 's announcement did not mention Hamas 's responsibility for war crimes against Gaza 's civilian population , such as use of human shields , but such charges could be added later . <h> 2 . It operates on the principle of complementarity , undertaking investigations and trials only when a state 's domestic legal system is unable to do so . The Prosecutor 's announcement blasted Israel for not investigating its officials for the alleged crimes . Israel has claimed that has launched some investigations of its officials for crimes related to the Gaza war . In theory , Israel or the relevant suspect could challenge the admissibility of the case before the Pre-Trial Chamber . The case would not be admissible if the suspect were being investigated or tried domestically for substantially the same conduct for which he was brought before the ICC . The ICC case may continue if the domestic proceedings are not consistent with a genuine attempt to bring the individual to justice . <h> 4 . The International Criminal Court is not the International Court of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ separate from the Genocide Convention case against Israel in the International Court of Justice . The ICC hears cases against individuals accused of the gravest crimes of concern to the international community . It has jurisdiction over only four crimes : genocide , war crimes , crimes against humanity , and crimes of aggression . <p> The ICJ , by contrast , hears cases between states , such as South Africa 's claim that Israel violated the Genocide Convention through actions related to its military operation in Gaza . The ICJ also hears Advisory Opinions brought by UN organs . It held hearings this February in an Advisory Opinion referred by the UN General Assembly on " Legal Consequences arising from the Policies ad Practices of Israel in the Occupied Palestinian Territory , including East Jerusalem . " <p> The two courts are often confused by non-specialists -- not least because both are located in the Hague and have jurisdiction over the crime of genocide .
