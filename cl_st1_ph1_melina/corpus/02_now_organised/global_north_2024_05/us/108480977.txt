<h> The Union Leader Who Changed How We Think About Work <p> By the time Mary Kay Henry stepped down from the presidency of the Service Employees International Union , she and the union had helped change how we think about work . In 2012 , SEIU backed striking fast-food workers in the city who were advocating for what was then a bold demand : a $15-per-hour minimum wage and a union . A decade later , the efforts of Fight for $15 and a Union led to an estimated 26 million workers winning $150 billion in higher pay . ( The campaign is now known as the Fight for a Union . ) Three years ago , Starbucks baristas in Buffalo , New York , launched what would become a high-profile unionization campaign with Workers United , a SEIU affiliate ; now , over 400 other stores have voted to unionize . Workers led the charge . Henry lent them critical support .
