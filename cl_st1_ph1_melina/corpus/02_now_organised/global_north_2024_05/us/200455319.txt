<p> Despite all the coverage of the protests over Israel 's war in Gaza , it can be remarkably difficult to understand what the players are actually saying . On social media , partisans on both sides cherry-pick extreme comments or incidents as a way to suggest that their opponents are comprehensively rotten . Others invoke broadly held values -- free speech , peaceful protest , human rights -- without explaining how they apply in specific circumstances . And many of the media stories have only worsened the confusion , by employing imprecise and euphemistic language that obscures more than it illuminates . <p> As a result , the American public remains badly informed about both the war itself and the movement against it , a dynamic that has steadily grown worse as campus protests -- and the rate of ( sometimes violent ) arrests -- has intensified .
