<p> The death of President Ebrahim Raisi was greeted with grief and relief in Iran after he was confirmed to have been among those killed in a helicopter crash Sunday . <p> Video and photos showed a large crowd of people gathered in central Tehran 's Vali-e-Asr square starting Sunday night , with many appearing to pray and some visibly distressed on the news that Raisi was one of eight people on board who were killed . <p> " Our honorable Raisi worked tirelessly , " Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei , the country 's head of state , said Monday in posts on X. " He was totally dedicated to serving the people and Islam nonstop . " <p> Raisi and other officials , including Iranian Foreign Minister Hossein Amir-Abdollahian , had been returning from the border of Azerbaijan , where the president had inaugurated a dam with Azerbaijani President Ilham Aliyev , when the aircraft went down . <p> Video from Iran 's official news agency Monday appeared to show Red Crescent workers carrying a covered body on a stretcher through a forested area .
