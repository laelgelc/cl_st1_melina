<p> Harvard University said Tuesday it will no longer take public positions on matters that do not relate to its core function of academia , after a report by a faculty committee found that speaking officially on matters outside its area of expertise carries risks including compromising " the integrity and credibility of the institution . " <p> University leaders said in a statement that they had accepted the recommendations of the committee , which was established in April , and will avoid statements on public issues , including those of social and political significance .
