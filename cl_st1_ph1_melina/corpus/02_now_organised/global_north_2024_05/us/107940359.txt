The Australian Broadcasting Corp . reported a scuffle between the groups . <p> Supporters of both sides later backed down because of a heavy security presence . <p> University of Sydney Vice Chancellor Mark Scott said there was space for both groups of protesters . <p> " They may strongly disagree with the matters that have been discussed . ...
