<h> It could soon be illegal to publicly wear a mask for health reasons in NC <h> Senators skeptical of legal trouble for harmless masking after moving to make it illegal . <p> The North Carolina State Senate on Wednesday voted 30 -- 15 , along party lines , in favor of a Republican bill that would make it illegal for people in the state to wear a mask in public for health reasons . The bill is now moving to the House , where it could potentially see changes . <p> The proposed ban on health-based masking is part of a larger bill otherwise aimed at increasing penalties for people wearing masks to conceal their identity while committing a crime or impeding traffic . The bill was largely spurred by recent protests on university and college campuses across the country , including North Carolina-based schools , against the war in Gaza . In recent months , there have been demonstrations in Raleigh and Durham that have blocked roadways , as well as clashes on the nearby campus of the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill . Some demonstrators were seen wearing masks in those events . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ step further by making it illegal to wear a mask in public for health and safety reasons , either to protect the wearer , those around them , or both . Specifically , the bill repeals a 2020 legal exemption enacted amid the COVID-19 pandemic , which allowed for public health-based masking for the first time in decades . <p> Advertisement <p> Prior to 2020 , laws dating back to 1953 largely prohibited public masking . The prohibition was part of a crackdown on " secret societies " at the time , or more specifically , an attempt to curtail the activities of the Ku Klux Clan in the state .
