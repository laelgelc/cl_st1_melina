<p> The Carter Center said Thursday it is " monitoring " protests at universities across the United States , and at Emory University in particular . <p> " We stand in support of freedom of opinion , expression , and assembly as central tenets of democracy . We urge university leaders and law enforcement to uphold human rights principles and avoid unnecessary escalation ; and we urge protesters to remain peaceful as they use their voices to work for change , " the Atlanta-based nonprofit said in a written statement . <p> Student protests have spread across college campuses in response to Israel 's war in Gaza , where more than 34,000 Palestinians have been killed according to local health officials . <p> The Carter Center said in its statement that it was monitoring protests at Emory " in particular . " A forceful police response to a protest last week at the private , Atlanta-based university has drawn widespread scrutiny . <p> The center , founded in 1982 by former President Jimmy Carter and former first lady Rosalynn Carter , has a longstanding partnership with Emory.
