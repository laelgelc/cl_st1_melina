<h> Letters to the Editor : It 's not antisemitic to protest Israel 's brutal war or its far-right government <p> Israeli soldiers work on tanks at a staging ground near the border with the Gaza Strip on April 11 . ( Tsafrir Abayov / Associated Press ) <p> To the editor : In questioning the motives of protesters , one letter writer lists several instances of other governments ' misconduct in the form of persecution of Indigenous populations and , in Russia 's case , launching a war against Ukraine . <p> Although these are all causes worthy of protest , they are irrelevant to the Israeli-Hamas conflict . This is an example of the " whataboutism " pseudo-argument that tries to dilute or distract from the legitimate examination of a particular issue . <p> In expressing a concern that Israel has been " singled out " for criticism , the implication is that this criticism is driven by antisemitism .
