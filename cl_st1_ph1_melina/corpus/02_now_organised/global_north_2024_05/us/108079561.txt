<p> The job of international spokesperson for Israel , in a state of war , is fit for a patriot , a masochist , or a diva , or better yet all three . For most of the past six months , it was occupied by Eylon Levy , a 32-year-old British Israeli with an affinity for television cameras and seemingly infinite ability to absorb the abuse that comes from publicly defending Israel , at its least defensible and at its most .
