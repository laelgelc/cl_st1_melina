<h> Pro-Palestinian protesters set up encampments at universities in Australia <p> Protesting students occupy an area of the quadrangle at the University of Sydney on May 3 . Rick Rycroft/Associated Press <p> SYDNEY ( AP ) -- Pro-Palestinian protesters were camped on university campuses across Australia on Friday , with some scuffling with pro-Israel protesters in Sydney , mirroring similar events in the United States . <p> Students have set up encampments at universities in major Australian cities over the last two weeks to protest Israel 's offensive in Gaza . The students are demanding that universities sever all academic ties with Israel and cut off research partnerships with arms manufacturers . <p> No arrests were made , as the violence seen on some American campuses has not occurred in Australia . <p> Hundreds of pro-Palestinian protesters on Friday met a counterprotest supporting Israel at the University of Sydney , Australia 's oldest university . The Australian Broadcasting Corp . reported a scuffle between the groups . <p> Advertisement <p> Supporters of both sides later backed down because of a heavy security presence . <p> University of Sydney Vice Chancellor Mark Scott said there was space for both groups @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the matters that have been discussed . ... We can host that conversation and we should be able to do that in a non-threatening way , " he told ABC . <p> Scott said not all of the protesters were students , and that some might not be committed to peaceful and productive engagement . " We are working with security and police , " he said .
