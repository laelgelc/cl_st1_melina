<p> The campus police chief at the University of California , Los Angeles , has been removed from his post after drawing intense criticism for how he responded to pro-Palestinian student protests last month -- including a violent attack on an encampment that raged for hours before law enforcement officers intervened . <p> " John Thomas has been reassigned temporarily , pending an examination of our security processes , " Mary Osako , UCLA 's vice chancellor for strategic communications , said in a statement Wednesday . <p> Thomas faced criticism over an apparently slow response to the chaos that erupted at UCLA late April 30 after a mob attacked a pro-Palestinian encampment . In videos captured at the scene , counter-protesters could be seen beating people , hitting people with poles , spraying chemical irritants and setting off fireworks . <p> The clashes went on for hours before law enforcement officers arrived . The encampment was eventually taken down . <p> The news of Thomas ' reassignment was first reported by the Daily Bruin , UCLA 's student-run newspaper .
