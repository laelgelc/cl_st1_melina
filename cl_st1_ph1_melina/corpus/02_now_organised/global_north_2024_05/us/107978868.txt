<p> I went to the Rutgers encampment in New Brunswick Thursday morning , expecting to sympathize with the protesting students who had spent three nights in tents on Voorhees Mall to protest the slaughter and starvation in Gaza . <p> Because I agree with most of what they say : Israel has gone way overboard in its response to the horror of Oct. 7 . America is complicit and should cut offensive arms . The Netanyahu government 's inexcusable theft of Palestinian land in the West Bank makes peace impossible . Plus , I felt some kinship because when I was their age , I slept in the president 's office at my university for three days in a protest against investments in apartheid South Africa . It 's a truism that young people sometimes see with a moral clarity that fades with age . <p> By the end of the day , though , I wondered if these students were doing more harm than good to their cause .
