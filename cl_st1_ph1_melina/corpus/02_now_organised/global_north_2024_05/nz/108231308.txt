US weapons , the wonks in Washington find , are being used by the Israeli Defense Forces to kill their opponents , many of them Palestinians , and most of them civilians . The NSM-20 report , in addition to Israel , considers Colombia , Iraq , Kenya , Nigeria , Somalia and Ukraine .
