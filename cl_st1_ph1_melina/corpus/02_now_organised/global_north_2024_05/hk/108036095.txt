<h> Rafah hit soon after evacuation order <p> Israel 's military carried out airstrikes in Rafah yesterday , residents said , hours after Israel told Palestinians to evacuate parts of the southern Gaza city where more than a million people uprooted by the war have been sheltering . <p> Fears are growing of a full-blown assault in Rafah , long threatened by Israel , against holdouts of the Palestinian militant group Hamas as ceasefire talks in Cairo stall . <p> Hamas-affiliated Al-Aqsa TV said the airstrikes targeted areas in eastern Rafah near neighborhoods given evacuation orders . <p> Instructed by Arabic messages , phone calls , and flyers to move to what the Israeli military called an " expanded humanitarian zone " 20 kilometers away , some families began trundling away under chilly rain , piling children and possessions onto donkey carts and pick-ups or on foot through muddy streets .
