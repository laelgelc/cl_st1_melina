<h> Strike on Rafah tent city sparks European fury <p> An Israeli airstrike killed at least 35 people in a tent camp in the Gaza city of Rafah , medics said , drawing condemnation from European leaders yesterday who urged implementation of a World Court ruling to halt Israel 's offensive . <p> In scenes grimly familiar from a war in its eighth month , Palestinian families rushed to hospitals to prepare their dead for burial after the late-Sunday strike set tents and rickety shelters ablaze . <p> " The whole world is witnessing Rafah getting burned up by Israel and no one is doing anything to stop it , " Bassam , a Rafah resident , said . <p> Despite a global outcry at the toll on civilians , Israeli tanks continued to bombard eastern and central areas of the city yesterday , killing eight .
