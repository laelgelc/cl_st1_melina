<p> CMAT has pulled out of UK festival Latitude in solidarity with Palestine - and in protest of the event being sponsored by Barclays Bank . <p> The bank has been accused by campaigners of increasing its investment in arms companies that trade with Israel , amid the ongoing war in Palestine . The festival takes place in Henham Park in Suffolk from 25 to 28 July . <p> Irish band Pillow Queens have also pulled out of the festival in protest of the Barclays sponsorship . Taking to Instagram , the Meath singer said that Barclays have " increased their financing of various companies who are supplying weapons and military technology to Israel . " <p> She added : " I made Latitude aware of what my decision would be weeks ago , but was hoping that with all of the press surrounding the brave artists who pulled out of The Great Escape festival for the same reasons , that Latitude would divest from Barclays or find another sponsor . <p> " This has not happened and it has been made clear to me that it will not happen . As such I am now officially pulling out @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ music , which I love so much , to get into bed with violence . " <p> CMAT had been scheduled to perform on main stage at the festival on July 27 . In a statement on its website , Barclays said that it recognises the " profound human suffering " caused by the " complex and long-running " conflict in Gaza . <p> The bank said it does not make its own investments but instead provides financial services to businesses " including those in the defence sector " and this includes companies " that supply defence products to Nato and other allies including Ukraine " .
