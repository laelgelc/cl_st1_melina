Follow her on Bluesky : @gillian2.bsky.social <p> Just what we wanted to hear as we go into the holiday season : Canadians are no longer very proud to be Canadians . <p> So says a recent poll by Angus Reid . It details how pride in our country has dropped from a high of 78 per cent in 1985 to only 34 per cent at the end of 2024 . That 's a slide of 43 per cent in 40 years with Manitoba , Saskatchewan and Alberta recording the largest decline . What happened ? Why did we become so cynical and despairing ? <p> For people over 55 their patriotism is as strong as ever . It 's the younger people who are losing their pride in Canada . And what could be causing that ? <p> ARTICLE CONTINUES BELOW <p> Is it because inflation has got us down ? Or maybe the division that came out of the pandemic ? Or too much immigration and not enough housing ? Are immigrants too tied to their home country ? Or maybe it 's all Justin Trudeau 's fault . He 's stayed in office too long and we are just sick and tired of him . <p> That 's what we are hearing from the naysayers about our lack of patriotism . They would point to all those factors , add some more and then call for more flag raising or boasting of our achievements or talking more about our heritage moments . <p> But is n't this a malaise that affects almost everyone in the world -- except the multi-millionaires and billionaires ? We 're grumpy , so cranky that some countries have given up on democracy in favour of one-man rule ( it 's always a man ) . The rest are restless , except for the Scandinavian countries of course . Just look at Britain , or France , or India , or South Korea . They are all experiencing serious internal conflicts that are threatening the status quo . <p> Maybe what 's changing us is the rise of global connections to the detriment of national ones . We see it in our approach to climate change : it 's all countries working together not individual nations . We see it in our trade system -- which is why Donald Trump 's idea of the U.S going it alone is so ridiculous . We see it in our money system : the U.S dollar is the universal standard . The use of the Internet to contact people around the world or around the corner has become commonplace . <p> In another poll , Ipsos found that we are very much a multi-cultural nation . 67 per cent of first generation Canadians are not white . Also , 83 per cent of Canadians feel comfortable expressing their cultural identity while feeling part of Canadian society . " They see themselves as Canadian , and then some , " wrote the authors . If the world is becoming more global , so is Canadian society . <p> In his book " Sapiens : A Brief History of Humankind , " Yuval Harari says that the move to a more global form of governance is inevitable . " States are increasingly open to the machinations of global markets . To the interference of global companies and NGOs , and to the supervision of global public opinion and the international judicial system , " he writes . <p> ARTICLE CONTINUES BELOW <p> ARTICLE CONTINUES BELOW <p> According to Harari the global empire is being forged before our eyes and is held together by a common culture and common interests . Why is it that American pop culture has spread all over the world ? Why is it that safeguarding human rights has become so important to so many countries ? Why is voting seen as a sign of democracy , even in Russia ? Why are Palestinians all over the world able to rouse so many protests against the war in Gaza ? These are all signs of the ways in which the world is increasingly intertwined . <p> To be sure there is resistance to the idea of a global form of governance and that may be why we see various groups raise the flag of conspiracy theories about who exactly is running this global empire and to what end . <p> I know one thing for sure : I am glad I am not American given their re-election of Trump as president . And according to the Angus Reid poll 94 per cent of Canadians agree that we should not join the United States . <p> At least we are united on that question . <p> Opinion articles are based on the author 's interpretations and judgments of facts , data and events .
