It 's an act that displays elements of aggression and forcefulness that are unnecessary in the context of no development planned , " she said . <p> Wintrup previously told CBC his team has been surveying the site of the cemetery with the intent to preserve it . <p> He says Lemay Forest is a great location for housing in the city , but Loewen disagrees . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> " There are ways to balance housing and the environment , and we have to think more creatively and not be in the back pocket of the developers because that compromises the ability of councillors and citizens to think clearly and make good decisions for now and the future , " she said . <p> A deer in Lemay Forest on Dec. 24 , 2024 . <p> The Coalition to Save Lemay Forest , which Ann Loewen is a part of , says wildlife such as deer will lose habitat as the trees are cut down .
