<p> Twin sisters set to attend University of Waterloo to complete their PhDs have died in an Israeli airstrike in Gaza . <p> Dalia Ghazi Ibaid and Sally Ghazi Ibaid were planning to come to Waterloo to do their PhDs in System Design Engineering . They were both recipients of UWaterloo 's Student Relief Fellowship . <p> " Dalia and Sally were selected based on their outstanding academic achievement and demonstrated research potential , " read UWaterloo 's statement . <p> They were both killed in an airstrike on Dec. 5 . <p> Their parents sent a statement , saying the twins had been " vibrant , bubbly , excited and full of dreams and ambitions , " in the days leading up to their deaths . <p> " They constantly talked about their PhD study , jokingly calling each other Dr. Sally and Dr. Dalia , ? and talking about the things they want to do in Canada , " read their parents ' statement . <p> " Through your help , they saw an end to their and our suffering . You gave them and us hope . Unfortunately , they did not get their opportunity to realize their dreams . Thank you for caring about and helping ? our daughters . " <p> UWaterloo is reminding students if they need help to reach out to the community on campus and the supports available . <p> No details about where the twin sisters were when they were killed has been released . <p> The war in Gaza began after Hamas and other militants from Gaza stormed southern Israel on Oct. 7 , 2023 , killing some 1,200 people and taking well over 200 hostage . <p> Israel 's retaliatory offensive has killed almost 45,000 Palestinians , according to Gaza 's Health Ministry . The ministry 's count does not distinguish between combatants and civilians , but it says over half of the dead have been women and children .
