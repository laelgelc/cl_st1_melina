( Source : Facebook ) <p> Share <p> HALIFAX - <p> A Nova Scotia lawyer who had to be restrained by deputy sheriffs during a court appearance earlier this year has lost his appeal of a suspension following the bizarre incident . <p> In a written decision dated Nov. 26 , Pictou County lawyer Donn Fraser 's appeal of his suspension by the Nova Scotia Barristers ' Society was dismissed by a three-member panel of the Nova Scotia Court of Appeal . <p> Following the court 's decision , the society 's complaints investigation committee announced that Fraser , who has practised law as a member of the society for 20 years , had been suspended until further notice . <p> In its decision , the appeal court highlighted an incident in Nova Scotia Supreme Court in Pictou , N.S. , on Feb. 1 , describing an audio recording of what happened in the courtroom as " bedlam . " The court appearance was part of an ongoing legal dispute between Fraser and his former colleagues . <p> " In the courtroom , Mr. Fraser tossed water on his opponents , physically resisted the sheriff 's direction to move away and threatened then fought the sheriffs , " the panel wrote in its decision . " The audio playback is bedlam . Concerted force by four sheriffs should not be required for a lawyer to respect a court 's decorum . " <p> The decision also cites an email sent to the barristers ' society by lawyer Joel Sellers , who said Fraser approached him and lawyer Mary Jane Saunders in the courtroom and poured water on them , soaking their clothes . Sellers said it then took four deputy sheriffs to take Fraser to the ground before bringing him under control using handcuffs and shackles . <p> The judges noted that Fraser characterized the water-throwing incident as an isolated " lapse of judgment , " adding that he said little about the confrontation in his appeal . <p> They ruled that the society 's committee " made no error in law " when it concluded that " Fraser 's invective and actions went beyond uncivil speech and have nothing to do with resolute advocacy . " <p> " A reasonable and informed person ... would not conclude the CIC ( committee ) was biased or behaved in a manner that reflects a reasonable apprehension of bias , " the panel said . " We dismiss the appeal . " <p> On Oct. 22 , Fraser pleaded guilty to assault and wilfully obstructing a public officer during the court scuffle on Feb. 1 . <p> He also pleaded guilty to a mischief charge following a July 31 incident at Pictou provincial court , during which sheriff 's deputies removed him from the building . And he entered another guilty plea for wilfully obstructing a public officer following an Aug. 7 incident involving an off-duty RCMP officer . <p> Fraser was given a conditional discharge that included an 18-month probation order . <p> President-elect Donald Trump is demanding the immediate release of the Israeli hostages still being held in Gaza , saying that if they are not freed before he is sworn into office there will be " HELL TO PAY .
