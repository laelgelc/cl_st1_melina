<h> Chain of Hope mends 15 little hearts in the Middle East <p> In its ongoing effort to help children in need , international charity organisation Chain of Hope led a recent surgical mission to the Middle East . <p> There , the medical volunteer team , led by volunteer surgeon Dr Mark Turrentine , helped 15 children from Gaza , Syria and other countries in the region in partnership with Riley Hospital for Children in Indianapolis , USA , and Gift of Life Amman at Al Khalidi Hospital , Amman . <p> Among those whose hearts were mended is six-month-old Sohail from Gaza . <p> Suffering from tricuspid atresia and malposed great arteries , baby Sohail was born in Gaza amidst the ongoing conflict , with no access to the vital medical care he desperately needed . With the help of Gaza Kinder Relief , Sohail and his mother were safely evacuated out of Gaza for urgent medical attention . <p> Chain of Hope covered the costs of Sohail 's surgery at Al Khalidi Hospital in Jordan which , due to the severity of his case , was carried out by a local surgeon , as he could not afford to wait even a week longer for the volunteer medical team 's arrival . <h> Headlines Delivered to Your Inbox <p> " Sohail is now recovering well in Jordan with his mother by his side . We are deeply grateful to our donors , whose generosity has made it possible for Chain of Hope to fund their accommodation and living expenses throughout his recovery , " the charity organisation stated . <p> " A heartfelt thank you to our partners : Gift of Life Amman , Riley Hospital for Children , Gift of Life Indianapolis , Rotary Club Greenfield , the Josh Lindblom Foundation and Gaza Kinder Relief , as well as to all of Chain of Hope 's supporters and donors who enable us to continue carrying out our life-saving work , " the organisation said . <p> " Thank you to everyone for your continued support this year , and we take this opportunity to wish you a very Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year ! " <p> Persons and organisations can assist the Chain of Hope initiative by donating to @ChainofHope and @ChainofhopeJa.org.
