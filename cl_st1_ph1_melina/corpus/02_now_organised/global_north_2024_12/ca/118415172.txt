<h> 14 issued trespass notices after protest in Parliament <p> Dozens of demonstrators took over the lobby of the Confederation Building in Ottawa Tuesday morning to protest the Canadian government 's response to the Israel-Gaza war . <p> By Natasha O'Neill <p> Posted Dec 3 , 2024 7:43 am . <p> Last Updated Dec 3 , 2024 12:26 pm . <p> Dozens of demonstrators took over the lobby of the Confederation Building on Tuesday morning to protest the Canadian government 's response to the Israel-Gaza war . <p> According to a press release from the group , Jews Say No To Genocide Coalition , the occupation is in response to the " catastrophic levels of hunger " and ongoing " business as usual " with Israel by the federal government . <p> The policing service for the Hill cleared out the demonstrators within an hour . A spokesperson from the force told CityNews in an email that 14 people were " intercepted " and released with trespass notices on Parliament Hill . <p> " The Parliamentary Protective Service ( PPS ) is aware of the situation which took place earlier this morning at the Confederation Building , " the email reads . " As such , PPS has significantly adjusted its security posture on Parliament Hill and within the Parliamentary Precinct , and is ready to intervene as required . " <p> Ottawa police also responded to the area to assist the PPS . <p> " OPS continued the arrest of multiple individuals from PPS . All of the individuals were released unconditionally , " an OPS spokesperson said . <p> Group blocks the lobby of the Confederation Building on Dec. 3 . ( Contributed ) <p> Both Jewish Canadians and allied supporters of Palestinian liberation are participating in the sit-in , the group says . <p> " Canada can not continue business as usual with Israel when business as usual means being complicit in a genocide , " the release reads . " Canada must end its active participation in Israel 's genocide of the Palestinian people by halting all arms exports to Israel -- including components funnelled through U.S. loopholes -- and stop all arms imports from Israel . " <p> The organization is asking for a two-way arms embargo , which according to the group means Canada should cancel active military export permits to Israel , close loopholes allowing weapons to reach Israel from the U.S. without permits and stop ongoing purchases of Israeli military goods . <p> The same concerns have led Canada to abstain from voting on United Nations resolutions calling out Israel , after decades of voting down motions that Canada said were not balanced . <p> " Today 's action is grounded in the Torah 's command : ' Do not stand idly by the blood of your neighbour ' ( Leviticus 19:16 ) , " Rabbi David Mivasair , said in the release . " A small , well-funded pro-Israel lobby has hijacked Jewish identity to justify unspeakable atrocities . As Jews , we reject genocide in our name . Our liberation is bound with the liberation of all oppressed peoples -- because to be truly free , we must act against oppression everywhere . "
