phane Dujarric , speaking during his daily media briefing from New York . <p> He noted that Hama had previously been a destination for people fleeing hostilities in and around Idlib and Aleppo , " so one can only imagine the scale of the crisis in that city " . <p> Separately , the UN Children 's Fund ( UNICEF ) called for the protection of children amid the escalating crisis . <p> Advertisement - scroll to continue reading <p> The agency is seeking just over $488 million to support seven million people in the region , including 4.3 million children , with life-saving services such as water , sanitation and hygiene , healthcare , nutrition , education , and protection . <p> Mr. Dujarric said the UN and partners continue to provide support wherever and whenever they can , to people displaced by the ongoing hostilities . <h> Cross-border mission from T ? rkiye <p> On Wednesday , UN Deputy Regional Humanitarian Coordinator for the Syria Crisis , David Carden , led a cross-border mission to Idlib from T ? rkiye to assess the situation . <p> He was joined by several UN agencies including aid coordination office OCHA , the UN Children 's Fund ( UNICEF ) , the UN refugee agency UNHCR , the World Health Organization ( WHO ) , and staff from the UN security department . <p> The team also visited a reception center in Dana hosting dozens of newly displaced households and spoke with families who fled their homes in western Aleppo . <p> " During the visit , our refugee agency and local partners provided mattresses , blankets , cooking materials and other items . People at the reception centre urgently need water and sanitation support , as well as heating material , " said Mr. Dujarric . <h> Local hospitals overstretched <p> The mission also visited the Sham Surgical Hospital , which is treating patients wounded by the recent attacks . During the period from 27 November to 2 December , the hospital alone provided life-saving care to more than 200 people , while its ambulance system supported more than 130 people . <p> WHO is providing medical supplies to the hospital , including trauma kits , but added that health workers there are not being paid due to underfunding . <p> Since the start of the escalation of hostilities , more than 30 health facilities in northwest Syria have ceased operations , which is putting immense strain on the remaining functional hospitals . <h> Hot meals for hungry people <p> The World Food Programme ( WFP ) is scaling up assistance to affected people . Staff and partners on the ground are providing ready-to-eat rations and hot meals and have so far served more than 10,000 people . <p> One WFP-supported kitchen began operations in Aleppo on Tuesday , and another is now up and running in Homs . <p> " The agency is providing food to displaced people wherever they are , on both sides of the front lines and in all areas of control . WFP is working to negotiate safe supply corridors to allow a rapid and substantial response to all those in need , " said Mr. Dujarric . <h> Aid in southern Lebanon <p> Turning to Lebanon , he said UN aid coordination office OCHA reported that a humanitarian convoy reached the southern city of Nabatieh that day and delivered food for more than 4,000 people in the area . <p> Since 23 September , the UN and partners have distributed more than nine million packages of food to people impacted by the crisis . <p> OCHA further reported that in addition to the destruction of their homes and essential services in their communities , families in southern Lebanon continue to face insecurity and restricted access . <p> " These include the risk posed by unexploded ordnances and the daily warnings from the Israeli Army , urging civilians to avoid returning to many villages in the south . This is obviously complicating our humanitarian efforts , " said Mr. Dujarric . <h> Ongoing hostilities in Gaza <p> He then moved on to the Gaza Strip , where hostilities continue to kill and injure civilians , including health workers . <p> Some 130 people in central Gaza are now without shelter after an Israeli airstrike on Wednesday hit a school in Deir al-Balah operated by UN Palestine refugee agency UNRWA . <p> Another airstrike in the south that night hit a group of tents in Khan Younis near the road along the coast , reportedly causing nearly 50 casualties . <p> " OCHA carried out an assessment today and says the airstrike burned about 40 tents . We and our partners are mobilizing response efforts to support those who lost their shelters , " he said . <h> Not a target <p> Furthermore , a paramedic from the Palestine Red Crescent Society was fatally shot in Khan Younis on Wednesday after transporting patients for treatment . <p> " Over the past 14 months , hundreds of health and humanitarian workers in Gaza have been killed .
