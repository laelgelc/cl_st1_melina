<p> New Zealand 's international aid programme appears set for cutbacks , with a review concluding the Government should streamline the range of activities it funds . <p> Foreign affairs officials have told MPs that finding cost savings was not the motivation for the work , but exactly what the recommendations mean for the country 's aid budget remains to be seen . <p> The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade appeared before MPs on Thursday as part of Parliament 's scrutiny week , fielding questions about the country 's diplomatic relationships and foreign policy priorities . <h> read more <p> In July , Newsroom revealed Foreign Affairs Minister Winston Peters had commissioned a review of the country 's aid programme , following disappointment from NGOs about a lack of new funding in May 's Budget . <p> Bernadette Cavanagh , deputy secretary for the Pacific and development group , told MPs the review had found the ministry was " probably doing too many things " when it came to international aid . <p> " We have about 800 individual activities underway , and that 's actually quite hard to manage ... we 're probably maybe focused in too many different sectors , too many different places , and we probably are working through too many partners . " <p> Cavanagh said officials were now looking at how to consolidate the aid programme to ensure the Government was getting the greatest impact from its spending . <p> It would take some time to make changes given the need to complete work that was already underway . <p> " If we 're halfway through a programme , it would be really disruptive for our partner if we pull the plug now , but we are starting to need to do some signalling to say , ' Well actually going forward , this might not be a space that we 're going to invest in in the same way ' . " <p> Cavanagh said a more streamlined approach could help Pacific governments with whom New Zealand worked on aid projects , with an ' umbrella arrangement ' allowing them to shift resources into other priorities ( such as policing ) . <p> Asked whether the review had made any recommendations about the amount of money going towards the aid programme , and whether there was any intention to cut budgets , she said that had not been the purpose of the work . <p> " We were n't asked to look at the quantum at all : it was more , are we delivering against the Government 's priorities and are we delivering as effectively as we can ? " <p> One large unknown is whether the coalition will renew the $1.3 billion climate aid programme announced under the last Labour government , set to expire in 2025 . <p> The ministry 's chief executive Bede Corry said officials were in the middle of Budget discussions about " the future climate finance trajectory " , and were aware of the importance Pacific nations placed on such aid . <p> " There is a vanishingly small number of countries -- not even the Arab countries -- that are sanctioning Israel in the same way .
