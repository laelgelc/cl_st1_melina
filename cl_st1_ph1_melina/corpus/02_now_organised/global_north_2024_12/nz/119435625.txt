<p> " Repeated hostilities in and around hospitals have obliterated the healthcare system in northern Gaza , putting civilians at an unacceptably grave risk of going without lifesaving care , " the ICRC said in a statement . <p> It called for the respect and protection of medical facilities in line with international humanitarian law . <p> " This protection is a legal obligation and a moral imperative to preserve human life , " it added , saying hospitals were a lifeline for those sick or wounded in conflict . <p> The Al-Awda Hospital , previously supported by the ICRC with supplies , was now absorbing more pressure as one of the few functioning medical facilities in northern Gaza , the Geneva-based organisation said . <p> " The Kamal Adwan and Indonesian hospitals are now completely inoperable . For months , these medical facilities have struggled to provide care for patients as ongoing hostilities have damaged hospitals and endangered or harmed staff , patients and civilians . " <p> Israel 's military said on Sunday that its forces had killed about 20 Palestinian militants and apprehended " 240 terrorists " in the raid , calling it one of its " largest operations " conducted in the territory . <p> " The increasingly dangerous situation comes in addition to more than a year of insufficient provision of medical equipment and supplies , fuel , food and specialised healthcare capacities . " <p> The ICRC said it remained committed to supporting healthcare services wherever possible , including doing what it could to ensure the protection of medical responders and civilian access to healthcare , as well as facilitating movements of medical personnel and equipment .
