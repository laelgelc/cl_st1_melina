I hope it can progress . We are committed to the return of the hostages -- it 's a responsibility we must uphold . " <p> Saar asserted , however , that Hamas " can not be allowed " to continue to rule Gaza . <p> Speaking to CBS , Sullivan said US President Joe Biden was in close co-ordination with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's Government . <p> " He also spoke with Prime Minister Netanyahu that day of the Israel-Hezbollah ceasefire , and Prime Minister Netanyahu told him he agreed , the time is right .
