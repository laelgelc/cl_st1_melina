The agreement could be finalised before the end of this year , provided it is not disrupted by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's new conditions . " <p> He said if an agreement is reached , it will be implemented in phases , ending with " a serious prisoner exchange deal , a permanent ceasefire and a complete withdrawal of Israeli forces from Gaza " . <p> On Wednesday , US Secretary of State Antony Blinken said he was " hopeful " for a deal , but avoided making any predictions as to when it would actually materialise .
