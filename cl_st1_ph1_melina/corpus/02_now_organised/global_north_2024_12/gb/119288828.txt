<h> Walk through Worcester highlights children 's suffering in war <h> Walk through Worcester highlights children suffering in Gaza <p> An activist group has led a walk through Worcester city centre to highlight the impact of conflicts , such as the one in Gaza , on young people . <p> The Red Rebels , dressed in red , organised the Walk for Peace on Sunday , December 22 . <p> Members of local Palestinian support groups joined the walk , dressed in black , and carried banners with the names of young people killed in Gaza in recent months . <p> The Red Rebels walk through Worcester city centre ( Image : Dave Provis ) The procession moved silently through the city centre , with only the sound of a drum . <p> The Red Rebels , formed as a creative response to the climate crisis , say they stand for " peace , equality and freedom for all living things " . <p> A spokesperson for the group said : " One of the most sad and lamentable aspects of the human condition in modern times is the staggering loss of human life in recent conflicts . <p> The Red Rebels walk through Worcester city centre ( Image : Dave Provis ) " The actions and inactions of governments , and the weapons manufacturers they protect , prevent peaceful solutions , and this is something we feel equally moved to draw attention to .
