<h> Reform UK Scotland organiser sacked amid family links to terror group <p> Craig Campbell has been removed as Scotland organiser of Reform UK ( Image : BBC ) <p> REFORM UK 's party organiser in Scotland has been sacked following an investigation into his family 's links to a Loyalist terror plot . <p> Craig Campbell , 52 , has been removed from his position in Nigel Farage 's party after the Daily Record revealed his late dad was a UVF commander who was jailed after the bombing of Catholic pubs in Glasgow . <p> Jason Campbell , his cousin , was also jailed after he murdered Celtic fan Mark Scott but cutting his throat . <p> Craig Campbell -- who lives in Lanarkshire -- also has images on his own Facebook page which compared the SNP to the Nazis . <p> A Reform UK Scotland spokesman said : " Craig Campbell is no longer a Reform UK Scotland organiser . Reform UK Scotland will not tolerate discrimination of anyone in any form . " <p> On Tuesday , there were calls for Reform to root out extremism within its ranks . <p> Speaking before the sacking , Labour MSP Paul O'Kane said : " Mr Campbell must take this opportunity to publicly break with and condemn his family connection with terrorism . If Mr Campbell fails to do so , Reform UK must expel him . " <p> It is not known if Craig Campbell 's close family ties to Loyalist terror group the UVF were known to Farage ( above ) or party bosses . <p> His dad , William " Big Bill " Campbell , was the supreme commander of the UVF in Glasgow during the Troubles in Northern Ireland . <p> Bill and his brother Colin were two of nine UVF members jailed in 1979 after the bombing of the Clelland Bar and Old Barns pub in Glasgow . <p> All nine were found guilty of plotting to further the aims of the paramilitary group by criminal purposes , while four of the men in Bill Campbell 's cell were found guilty of the bombings . <p> He died in 1997 and a Loyalist plaque celebrates him in Northern Ireland , with the slogan : " In proud memory of a true soldier . Lest we forget . No5 Platoon . " <p> In 1996 , Craig Campbell 's cousin Jason was handed a life sentence after he brutally murdered 16-year-old Mark Scott in broad daylight after a Celtic match . <p> The murder led to the creation of anti-sectarianism charity Nil By Mouth . <p> Craig Campbell has spoken about politics on TV , commenting on the atrocities in Gaza on BBC 's Debate Night as an audience member . <p> He said : " Put pressure on Egypt . Tell them to open their borders , take all the refugees and everybody that wants to leave Gaza . And let Israel do what they have got to do , which is wipe Hamas from the face of the earth . " <p> Green MSP Maggie Chapman said : " Reform should ask itself why so many people with such extreme and repulsive views are so attracted to that party . All that it is offering is scapegoating , prejudice and bigotry .
