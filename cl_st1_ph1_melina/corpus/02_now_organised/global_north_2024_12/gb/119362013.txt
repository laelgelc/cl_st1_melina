It vowed to continue launching strikes on Israel until the conflict in the Gaza Strip ceased . <p> Houthi rebels have been attacking Israel since the first months of the war , which began in October 2023 when Palestinian militants launched a surprise attack on Israel , killing around 1,200 . <p> Israel has retaliated against Houthi attacks with intermittent strikes . <p> On Saturday , the Houthis said they had launched a strike on the Nevatim airbase in central Israel . The IDF said a missile from Yemen was intercepted by the air force before crossing into Israeli airspace . <p> The Houthis are an armed political and religious group backed by Iran .
