<h> Headline warnings about ' UK ' tap water may mislead Scottish readers <p> THE Guardian has reported that UK tap water safety is at risk after testing labs shut down , implying Scotland 's water is also at risk , which it 's not . <p> Publicly owned Scottish Water supplies Scotland 's domestic , not commercial , market , because English Labour privatised the latter before being booted from office in 2007 . <p> All nine English water companies are privately owned . 71% of shares are held by overseas hedge funds and foreign governments . Water quality in England is terrible because these private companies spew raw sewage into waterways , under-invest and then charge households the highest bills in the UK . <p> Scottish Water regularly tests at its treatment works and customers ' taps to ensure it meets required standards . It has a water hygiene microbiology lab in Livingston , and state-of-the-art process support laboratories in Edinburgh and Inverness . <p> The Public Water Supplies ( Scotland ) Amendment Regulations 2022 aligns Scotland 's water regulations with those of the EU . <p> Scottish Water is the only water company in the UK and Europe to be awarded accreditation for a new method of detecting the somatic coliphages virus in water at its Edinburgh and Inverness labs . Somatic coliphages are n't harmful to humans but they can indicate the presence of other viruses that are . <p> If Scottish Water 's labs can test for viruses , they can surely test and certify the chemicals needed to clean our drinking water .
