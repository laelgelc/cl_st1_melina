<h> John Griffiths MS Christmas Column for South Wales Argus <p> Happy Christmas and no doubt Christmas Day would have been a busy day for many spending times with family and friends as part of the celebrations . <p> At this time of year , I believe it is important to reflect on the last twelve months , but also look forward to the opportunities that 2025 might bring . <p> But before I do , especially in the wake of the recent floods which affected Wales , I want to thank our local authorities and wider public services for keeping us as safe as possible . <p> Earlier this year , I had the privilege of hosting the first Active Newport conference , bringing some of the big and small players from sport , fitness , health and wellbeing together . <p> Encouraging healthier lifestyles has always been one of my top priorities - and I 'm excited to take this into 2025 by working with organisations such as Newport Live , the health board and grassroots organisations on this important agenda . <p> I also continue to serve as the Chair of the Senedd 's Local Government &amp; Housing committee . The Welsh Government have made progress in building more homes , but we continue to scrutinise them on their policies and targets to ensure everyone in Wales has access to a stable , warm and safe home . <p> No one should be without a roof over their heads , and I will continue to work with the government and other groups so everyone has a place to call home . <p> It 's also incredibly important at this time of year to recognise some of the devastating injustices that people are facing in other parts of the world - particularly the ongoing conflicts in the Middle East . We 've seen this conflict play out on the news and on social media in excruciating detail , and the devastation which has been enacted on Gaza in particular is incomprehensible . <p> This is something which has deeply affected a number of our communities here in Newport , and I will continue to stand in solidarity with those affected . This Christmas , it 's important we spread the message of peace and unity , and we must carry that into the new year . <p> Looking ahead , I will continue to work with the local community and my colleagues in the Senedd to strive towards a healthier and more equitable future for everyone . <p> Working together is the best way to achieve a safe , healthy and supportive society for all .
