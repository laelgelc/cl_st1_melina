<h> Teenager grills Shabaz Ali and Adnan Hussain for talk show <h> Shabaz Ali and Adnan Hussain Blackburn library interview <p> Aleeza Isa Interviews is a talk-show style event which is filmed in front of a live audience . ( Image : One Voice ) <p> A 16-year-old quizzed new MP for Blackburn Adnan Hussain and a local TikTok star as part of her new talk show . <p> Aleeza Isa hosted her second episode in the form of Aleeza Isa Interviews at Blackburn Library . <p> In the latest episode , which has just been broadcast on YouTube , she also interviewed TikTik sensation Shabaz Ali aka Shabaz Says and One Voice alumni Aisha Seedat . <p> Aleeza Isa Interviews is a talk-show-style event which is filmed in front of a live audience and is filmed in the Hornby Lecture Theatre , part of Blackburn Library , and was launched in September this year . <p> Aleeza also hosts the Blackburn Christmas Lights Switch On , as well as being a part of the charity One Voice Blackburn which produces Aleeza Isa Interviews and runs the 1V Awards Dinner . <p> The former Queen Elizabeth Grammar School student said : " You can never get used to having your own show , but I think I am getting the hang of it now . <p> " The guests make the show , and I was so privileged that all three guests agreed to take part . <p> " I take encouragement that they do n't see me as a 16-year-old and do n't give me an easy ride . <p> " In return , I ensure that I do my research on every guest -- which takes weeks . " <p> Shabaz Ali has around two million followers on both Instagram and TikTok and is famous for his innovative and ingenious put-downs of the rich and entitled . <p> Blackburn born Shabaz talked about growing up in the town and the secret to his success . <p> He has recently published a book ' I 'm Rich , You 're Poor ' based on his TikTok videos , and will be going on tour in the new year . <p> Adnan Hussain MP , who pulled off a shock win over Labour in July 's General Eelction , spoke of his desire to represent all the constituents in Blackburn regardless of who they voted for . <p> He was also challenged on his position on the conflict in Gaza and how he will balance the issues in the town with international affairs . <p> The hour-long show also featured a question-and-answer session , before a networking opportunity for guests . <p> The next edition Aleeza Isa Interviews will take place in May 2025 , with the guests yet to be announced . <h> Comments : Our rules <p> We want our comments to be a lively and valuable part of our community - a place where readers can debate and engage with the most important local issues .
