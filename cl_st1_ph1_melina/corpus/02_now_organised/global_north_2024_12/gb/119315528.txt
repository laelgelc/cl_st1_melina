One of our plane 's crew members was injured , " Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus said on X. " At least two people were reported killed at the airport . " <p> " The air traffic control tower , the departure lounge - just a few metres from where we were - and the runway were damaged , " he said , adding that he was now waiting for the damaged airport to be repaired before he could depart with WHO staff . <p> Tedros was in Yemen as part of a mission to seek the release of detained UN staff and assess the health and humanitarian situations in the war-torn country . <p> He said the mission concluded today , and " we continue to call for the detainees ' immediate release " . <h> New MEE newsletter : Jerusalem Dispatch <h> Sign up to get the latest insights and analysis on Israel-Palestine , alongside Turkey Unpacked and other MEE newsletters <p> The WHO was founded after World War II as part of the United Nations .
