<h> Donald Trump will shape Europe and the UK . Are they ready ? <p> The President-elect 's in-tray is filling up fast . Wars in Gaza , Ukraine and the fallen regime in Syria are among the many geopolitical shifts facing Donald Trump 's new administration , alongside a spate of challenges at home . Is the United States -- and the world -- ready for Trump 2.0 ? <p> This week , Power Play brings you a trio of POLITICO insiders to reflect on Trump 's historic election win and what it will mean for those across the pond . Host Anne McElvoy is joined by Kate Day , POLITICO Europe 's deputy editor-in-chief , and Alex Burns , head of news , who flew in from Washington . Their conversation was recorded in front of a live audience of Westminster 's movers and shakers at iNHouse in central London .
