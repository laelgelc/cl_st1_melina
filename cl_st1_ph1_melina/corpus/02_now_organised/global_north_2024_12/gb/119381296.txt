<p> The Kamal Adwan Hospital has been hit multiple times over the past three months by Israeli troops , according to staff . <p> Israel says it is waging an offensive against Hamas fighters in surrounding neighborhoods . <p> Israel 's military said it was conducting operations against Hamas infrastructure and fighters in the area of the hospital , without providing details . <p> It repeated claims that Hamas fighters were operating inside Kamal Adwan Hospital , though it provided no evidence . Hospital officials have denied the accusations . <p> The Health Ministry said troops forced medical personnel and patients to assemble in the hospital yard and remove their clothes amid the winter temperatures . <p> They were led out of the hospital , some to an unknown location , while some patients were sent to the nearby Indonesian hospital , which was knocked out of operation after an Israeli raid earlier this week . <p> The ministry said troops set fires in several parts of Kamal Adwan , including the hospital 's lab and surgery department . <p> It said 25 patients and 60 health workers remained in the hospital out of 75 patients and 180 staff who had been there . <p> The ministry 's account could not be independently confirmed , and attempts to contact hospital staff were unsuccessful . <p> Israel 's military said there was only a small fire in an empty hospital building , and was unaware of any Israeli gunfire causing the blaze . <p> Since October , Israel 's offensive has virtually sealed off the north Gaza areas of Jabaliya , Beit Hanoun and Beit Lahiya and levelled large parts of the districts . <p> As Israeli and Palestinian negotiators in Doha try to reach a ceasefire agreement , the UN has reported that infants and newborns are dying of exposure in Gaza . <p> Edouard Beigbeder , UNICEF Regional Director for the Middle East and North Africa , reported in a statement on Friday that , according to the Palestinian Ministry of Health , four newborns and infants died in recent days from hypothermia . <p> " These preventable deaths lay bare the desperate and deteriorating conditions facing families and children across Gaza , " said Mr. Beigbeder . <p> " With temperatures expected to drop further in the coming days , it is tragically
