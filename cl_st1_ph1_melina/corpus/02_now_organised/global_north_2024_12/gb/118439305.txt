<p> Nazanin Zaghari-Ratcliffe , who was held in Iran for six years , has said she has forgiven Boris Johnson but " will not forget " his handling of her case . <p> The British-Iranian mother , 45 , who was wrongly imprisoned for spying , told Times Radio that although she had sat down with Johnson , then prime minister , at Downing Street , she did not receive an apology for his words while he was foreign secretary in 2017 . <p> The Iranian-British dual citizen was visiting family in Tehran in 2016 when she was arrested and falsely accused of spying , which she denied . <p> In 2017 , Johnson told MPs in the foreign affairs select committee that she had been in Iran to train journalists . Four days later , Zaghari-Ratcliffe was summoned to court and his comments were cited as evidence that she had engaged in " propaganda against the regime " . Her employer , Thomson Reuters Foundation , confirmed that she had been visiting her family . <h> Advertisement <p> She told John Pienaar on Times Radio Drive : " I was doing something in the kitchen in prison and the girls called me and said , ' Come , come , this is Boris Johnson in parliament . He 's talking about you . ' I could not believe that he just looked in the eye of the other MPs and he said that , ' Oh , in my opinion , she was simply training journalists . ' <p> " This is something I was trying to defend myself against over the previous 18 months . And he ? ruined it completely . To live under the shadow of that inaccurate comment was hell . " <p> Zaghari-Ratcliffe was released in March 2022 and met Johnson that May . " I did n't want his apology , " she insisted , " because what was his apology going to do to me ? It has already wasted my life . But what I wanted to have was some time to tell him , face to face what his inaccurate comment did to me . I think I have forgiven him . But I will not forget . " <p> She added that it " rips her heart " to see pictures and videos of her daughter , Gabriella , taken during the six years of her childhood that she missed . <p> Zaghari-Ratcliffe also called attention to hostages missing as a result of the Israel-Gaza conflict . <h> Advertisement <p> " For me , as someone who has been in their shoes , it does n't matter where you come from , Iran , Palestine , Israel , Cuba . You have the right to live and governments should find ways to take these people home , " she said .
