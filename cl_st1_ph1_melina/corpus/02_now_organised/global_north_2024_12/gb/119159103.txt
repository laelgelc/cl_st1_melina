<p> A group of doctors and lawyers are hoping to expand a mission to bring sick Palestinian children into Ireland for medical treatment after the first eight arrived in the country last Thursday . <p> The children , who were accompanied by eight carers and 11 siblings , arrived into Dublin airport on a plane from Egypt that was provided by the Slovakian government for medical evacuation , following government approval last September . <p> The group , who are aged between four and 17 , suffer from a range of illnesses such as haemophilia and Crohn 's disease , while one girl is recovering from a blast injury that required skin grafting and has damaged her muscles and nerves . <p> It is estimated that about 85 per cent of medical evacuation requests in Gaza are denied , with young men in particular being refused access to care . Egypt has accepted more than 2,300 medevac patients since the start of the war . <h> Advertisement <p> The Irish government has committed to medically evacuating a total of 30 Palestinian children , which is being done in co-ordination with the World Health Organisation , as well as heath services in Egypt and leaders in Palestine . <p> However , Trish Scanlan , a paediatric oncologist from Dublin and co-medical director of Children Not Numbers ( CNN ) , an organisation that offers rehabilitation services for children in Gaza , is hoping this is the beginning of a larger-scale operation . <p> Unicef claims more than 14,500 children have been killed in Gaza since the start of the war <p> ALAMY <p> CNN was founded by Sarah Ben Tarifite , a practising lawyer , and Somaya Ouazzani , a non-practising lawyer . <p> " We have about 400 children in our books and we triage them in terms of the intensity of care that they need or if they could be managed in Gaza or need rehab , and then we try as best we can to get them out , " Scanlan said . <p> " The process of getting a child out of Gaza , no matter how sick they are , is extraordinarily complex . The only way out is through one of the crossings into Israel , so you have to get approval from Israel to get any child out . We had evacuated a number of children into Egypt before May , and those children have been cared for in overwhelmed hospitals around Egypt . <h> Advertisement <p> " We took eight as a start and wanted to prove the Irish side of things would work really well .
