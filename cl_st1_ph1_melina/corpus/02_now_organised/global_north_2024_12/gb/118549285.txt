<p> Israeli President Isaac Herzog called Elon Musk " in the last few days " to discuss a revival of talks to secure the release of hostages currently held in Gaza , according to a source close to President Herzog . <p> The source told CNN Herzog had made the call at the request of hostage family members who hope Musk will be able to apply pressure on all parties to secure a deal . <p> The communication comes after President-elect Donald Trump , to whom Musk has closely allied himself , said Monday that there would be " ALL HELL TO PAY in the Middle East " if the hostages being held in Gaza are n't released before he is sworn in as president on January 20 . <p> A source close to the hostage families , who spoke with President Herzog and who is familiar with the conversation , told CNN : " There was a chat between the President and a few hostage families , where one of the subjects discussed was influence on Trump , and Elon Musk 's name came up as someone of influence on Trump . Therefore , keeping an open channel with him is important . " <p> It is believed there are 100 hostages , both dead and alive , currently being held in Gaza , 96 of whom were taken during Hamas ' October 7 attack on Israel last year . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is under intense pressure from the families of those captured to secure their release , but repeated efforts to strike a ceasefire-for-hostages agreement have fallen short . <p> Last November , Musk travelled to Israel to meet leaders and the relatives of hostages , as part of his efforts to walk back an antisemitic tweet in which he agreed with a user that Jewish communities push " hatred against Whites . "
