<h> Children among dozens killed in Israeli strikes , Gaza officials say <p> An attack on a house in central Gaza killed at least 13 people , according to Gaza 's civil defence agency <p> Jacqueline Howard <p> BBC News <p> Published <p> 4 hours ago <p> At least 28 people , including children , have died in a wave of Israeli military strikes throughout the Gaza Strip , according to Gaza 's civil defence agency . <p> A school sheltering displaced families was among the facilities struck , killing eight people including four children over the weekend , the agency said . <p> It comes as the UN issues a plea for Israel to cease its attacks in the vicinity of a hospital in Gaza 's north . <p> The Israeli military claimed a Hamas command centre was inside the compound of the Musa bin Nusair school in Gaza City , and has not commented on reports of attacks by the hospital . <p> " Hamas systematically violates international law , " the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) said on social media , adding that Israel 's response would be to " act with force and determination against the terrorist organizations " . <p> Gaza 's civil defence agency spokesperson Mahmoud Bassal told the AFP news agency that the school had been repurposed as a shelter for Palestinians displaced by the war . <p> One displaced man who had been staying at the school , Abu , told BBC Arabic that the attack came while he was asleep . <p> " We were sleeping peacefully , then suddenly we woke up to the sound of a very powerful explosion , " he said . <p> Another man Mahmoud said he was asleep in a tent in the schoolyard when the attack took place . <p> " Stones and shrapnel were flying , the school 's walls fell on our heads , " he told BBC Arabic . <p> On Sunday , Pope Francis condemned the Israeli attacks on Gaza for a second day in a row . <p> He expressed pain thinking " of such cruelty , to the machine-gunning of children , to the bombing of schools and hospitals " . <p> The director of the Kamal Adwan hospital , Dr Hussam Abu Safiya , said its generators had been hit and claimed the Israeli army was targeting the fuel tank . <p> The head of the World Health Organization ( WHO ) , Dr Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus , issued a plea to the IDF to cease attacks in the vicinity of the hospital . <p> Saturday night 's reports of " bombardment near Kamal Adwan Hospital and order to evacuate the hospital are deeply worrisome , " he said in a statement on social media . <p> " We call for an immediate ceasefire in the vicinity of the hospital and to protect the patients and health workers . " <p> The hospital 's director also released a statement that said Israeli forces were treating the hospital " as if we were a military installation " . <p> " Anyone who steps outside the hospital is at risk of being targeted , " Dr Hussam Abu Safiya said . <p> He added that relocating the operations of the hospital would jeopardise the patients , and called for health staff " be allowed to operate without the threat of evacuation " .
