<h> Figures show spike in Islamophobic and anti-Semitic hate crimes across county <h> Rise in Islamophobic and anti-Semitic hate crimes in county <p> Figures have revealed a spike in religious hate crime across West Yorkshire ( Image : PA ) <p> POLICE have commented after figures showed a spike in religious hate crimes across West Yorkshire . <p> Figures obtained under the Freedom of Information Act revealed 561 Islamophobic offences were recorded by West Yorkshire Police between October 2023 and September 2024 - up from 362 in the previous 12 months . <p> Meanwhile , the statistics showed 215 anti-Semitic offences were recorded by the force in the year to September 2024 - up from 77 in the previous 12 months . <p> A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said : " Since the conflict involving Israel and Gaza began last year , West Yorkshire Police has engaged with our communities and key individuals across the county to understand the impact of these international events locally . <p> " Our neighbourhood teams have also carried out increased visible patrols at key times to provide reassurance to our communities . <p> " This engagement has , of course , included continuing dialogue with members of our Muslim and Jewish communities , in order to listen to any concerns and provide extra reassurance policing when it has been necessary . <p> " Everyone has a right to live their life , and practice their religion , without the fear of targeted abuse for who they are . <p> " Anyone who has been subjected to a hate crime should not suffer in silence . <p> " We would urge anyone with concerns , or who has witnessed any offences , to please report it . " <p> Most UK forces saw a clear year-on-year increase in the total number of these hate crimes . <p> Iman Atta , director of Tell Mama , a national project which monitors anti-Muslim hate , said : " Anti-Muslim hate or Islamophobia spikes repeatedly when there are international issues . <p> " We have assisted over 5,000 British Muslims this year and the number keeps rising . " <p> " We will continue to work closely with police and the Crown Prosecution Service up and down the country , alongside local Jewish communities , to reduce the impact of this hatred . " <p> Dame Diana Johnson , Home Office minister for policing , fire and crime prevention , said : " We are determined to stamp out the toxic vitriol which is spread by a minority of people - and perpetrators of hate crime should be in no doubt that they will face the full force of the law . <p> " The significant increase in anti-Semitic and Islamophobic hate crime is very serious , which is why we have committed up to ? 47.4 million per year in protective security funding for Jewish and Muslim communities to 2027-28 .
