( Image : Supplied ) <p> FOR the umpteenth time , I watched " A Christmas Carol " . Clearly , in my view , it was the only version worth watching . <p> Aye , you 've guessed it , the one with the excellent and incomparable Alistair Sim , an exceptional actor from God 's ain country ! Naebody on the planet could ever play Scrooge as guid as oor Alistair ! <p> It got me thinking afterwards though . How many folk throughout Scotland , the UK and throughout the world would ever admit to being like the pre-repentant Scrooge ? Not many methinks . <p> Then how come the inexorable rise , year , after year of inequality ? It 's relentless ! If the world was jam-packed full of the generosity of the repentant Scrooge , we definitely would not be in this dire situation . <p> Naw , the world must contain an abundance of Scrooge-like folk that clearly have n't yet encountered the spirits of Christmas Past , Present and Yet to Come ! <p> Mind you , in 2024 , maybe greed , selfishness , heartlessness and callousness have become so embedded in so many , I reckon the vast majority of these folk experiencing exactly what Scrooge did with the spirits , would gie their face a guid splash wi cauld water the next morning , pit it doon to a bad dream , and jist cairy on regardless in their self-serving ways . <p> Far too many folk now are , like Scrooge , totally inured to the plight of those in abject poverty . They are involved in what some folk call rampant capitalism . As far as I 'm concerned , they can call it what they damn well like ! For me , it 's just a game that always turns up trumps ( pardon the pun ! ) for them and those of their ilk . <p> This game is rigged , for them to win big , again , again and again . The losers ? As Scrooge stated , " Are there no prisons ? ... And the Union workhouses ? " Nowadays the cry might be , " Are there no food banks , charities and an over-generous welfare system ? " <p> For me , it 's such a sad state of affairs that an excellent story by Charles Dickens from 1843 resonates so much today , almost 200 years later , in a world of such riches . <p> Some might say what a depressing letter to write at such a joyous time of year .
