<h> Tribute to Second World War veteran Cyril O'Connor , who lived on Sheppey all his life , and taught ballroom dancing at Sheppey Comprehensive School <p> A family has paid tribute to their war veteran granddad who was a ballroom dancing champion and teacher . <p> " Fun loving " Cyril O'Connor , who lived on Sheppey all his life , died just a few weeks before his 98th birthday . <p> Nick , Cyril and Arthur O'Connor outside the Holy Trinity Church in Queenborough on Remembrance Sunday in 2023 <p> Born in 1926 , he grew up in Queenborough with his mum Hilda , Irish-born dad John , who was a physical trainer for the Army , and five sisters . <p> When the Second World War broke out in 1939 , he was too young to serve and was evacuated to Mountain Ash in South Wales in 1940 . There he stayed with a welcoming family but had trouble with some of the kids in his new hometown . <p> Nick O'Connor , his son , says his dad remembered those who took him in fondly but was glad to return to the Island after the Battle of Britain . <p> As a child , he was in the air cadets and was a keen war plane spotter -- being able to identify the different types of aircraft flying over Kent . During this time as a cadet , he flew in a Lancaster bomber as a rear gunner . <p> After turning 18 in December 1943 he was called up to serve with an infantry unit , the Royal Hampshire Regiment , a month later , in January 1944 , which was devastating news for Cyril who wanted to put his flying training into practice in the RAF . <p> Cyril O'Connor as a child grew up in Queenborough - he was one of six children . Here he is pictured when he was evacuated to Mountain Ash in WalesCyril next a Lancaster bomber at the Imperial War Museum in London - a plane he flew in when he was an air cadet <p> As an infantryman , he did not see front-line combat but served Italy , Austria , Egypt and Palestine , as part of the occupying forces . <p> The granddad-of-two was particularly proud of guarding the Sch ? nbrunn Palace in Vienna , which had been the main residence of the Habsburg dynasty . <p> During his time in Palestine , he was bitten by a scorpion and was treated in a hospital in Gaza . <p> But after returning home in 1946 he ended up back in a hospital after being paralysed , which doctors believed was the cause of this bite . <p> Nick said : " Doctors told him he might not walk again . <p> Cyril riding a donkey in the Middle East during his service in the Second World War <p> " But he was determined he would and he walked out of the hospital . All the nurses and doctors lined up and clapped him out . " <p> Cyril met his wife of 65 years , then Joan Nickson , through a mutual friend in 1957 and they were married two years later . Their first home was in Queenborough Road before building a house in Scocles Road , Minster . <p> Joan , who grew up on the Island but whose parents were from Lancashire , had been working for Barkers of Kensington department store and Nick jokes that if she had not met his dad then she might have had a " more exciting life " . <p> However , Cyril was far from boring . <p> Outside of working hours , he was a keen ballroom dancer who won Kent titles and even competed nationally with dance partner Helen Francis . <p> Cyril with his dance partner Helen Francis and numerous competition titlesCyril and Joan O'Connor on their wedding day <p> He went on to teach ballroom dancing night classes with Joan , now 87 , at Sheppey Comprehensive School and Sheppey General Hospital . <p> Away from dancing he worked for Queenborough council for a period and in the 1970s started a job at Sheerness Dockyard as a buying manager -- a role which entailed purchasing equipment for dock workers . <p> He retired from the docks aged 65 but still had a part-time job there and was also a volunteer at HMP Standford Hill . <p> Joan gave birth to their only son Nick in 1970 at Sheppey General Hospital . <p> Nick remembers his dad as very " funny " with one episode involving ducks summing him up . <p> Cyril and Joan were married for 65 years <p> When he and Cyril , both Tottenham Hotspur fans , were heading home on an FA Cup final day when he saw a ' ducks for sale ' sign near a pullover of the A249 . <p> Nick said : " He bought two ducks and brought them home with no consent from mum and not having prepared to keep or house them . <p> " He was almost a dad to them as when they heard his car pulling up after coming from work they would come down to the house and peck at the back door . <p> " He was a really well-liked man so we joked that even the ducks liked him . " <p> Another well-told family story was of Cyril ploughing into tables and chairs at a coffee shop while skiing with Joan in Bulgaria . <p> Cyril and Joan O'Connor with Karen Hardy during a dancing lesson at her Chelsea studio after she had recently won Strictly Come DancingCyril during a tap dance rehearsal with Burgoyne Dancing Academy <p> He was unfazed by the crash and simply walked to the counter and ordered two teas . <p> Cyril was part of the Isle of Sheppey Singers and Meyrick Minstrels who often perform at Sheppey Little Theatre in Sheerness . <p> In his 70s he also took up tap dancing with the Burgoyne Dancing Academy and performed at the London Palladium in 2002 . <p> All of this was partly why he stayed on Sheppey for his whole life . Nick explained : " He loved the community of the Island and he knew so many people . <p> " They also liked living by the sea and they regularly visited Minster Leas . " <p> Cyril and Nick O'Connor <p> Cyril died on Wednesday , November 6 at Medway Maritime Hospital after falling ill with Covid and pneumonia . <p> Nick , who lives in Surrey but saw his parents in Minster around once a month , said : " I know how lucky I was to have him as my dad . <p> " He would find the fun in any situation and made us laugh until the very end . He just loved having fun . <p> " But what is more amazing is that he was a very supportive and demonstrative dad who would tell me how much he loved me and how proud he was of me which I know is rare for someone of his generation . <p> " The family miss him terribly but is comforted by having so many lovely memories from such a long , healthy and happy life . " <p> Cyril entertaining his grandchildren Arthur and Stanley <p> He leaves behind two grandchildren , Arthur , 11 , and Stanley , 9 , to whom he was ' grandpappy ' -- a moniker Cyril came up with based on old Wild West cartoons . <p> Nick said : " Both Arthur and Stanley were big fans of grandpappy . Stanley is like a mini-Cyril . He 's a performer , a clown and cheeky . So he lives on through Stanley . " <p> Cyril 's funeral is tomorrow ( Thursday , December 12 ) at the Garden of England Crematorium in Bobbing at midday . <p> Any donations will go to Sheppey Little Theatre , which is raising funds to buy solar panels .
