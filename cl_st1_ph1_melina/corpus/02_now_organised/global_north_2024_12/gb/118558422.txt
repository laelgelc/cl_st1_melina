" <p> She said Israel 's claims to its allies and others that its attack on Gaza was a legitimate response to the " horrific crimes " carried out by Hamas were not legitimate . <p> " That assertion simply does not stand up to scrutiny , " she said . <h> ' A wake up call ' <p> Israeli forces have killed nearly 45,000 Palestinians in 14 months of war on Gaza , with many more more missing and presumed dead under rubble . <p> Nearly 70 percent of victims are children and women , according to the UN . <p> The conflict , which followed a Hamas-led attack in southern Israel that killed around 1,200 people , has caused the displacement of 90 percent of Gaza 's 2.2 million residents , with homes , mosques , historical sites , hospitals , UN buildings , agricultural land and other facilities obliterated by attacks . <p> In addition , Israel 's war has killed more journalists over the past year than any other conflict over the past three decades . <p> ' States have a clear choice before them : they can decide to continue granting Israel impunity for its atrocity crimes against Palestinians or they can act now to stop it ' <p> - Grazia Careccia , Amnesty <p> Amnesty said it looked into 15 Israeli air strikes that took place in northern , central and southern Gaza between 7 October 2023 and 20 April 2024 . <p> They said " deliberately indiscriminate " strikes hit 12 homes and other residential buildings , a church and a street , killing 334 civilians , including at least 141 children , and wounding hundreds of others . <p> The report said investigations did not find " any evidence that any of the strikes were directed at a military objective " while a review of all available evidence showed that all those killed were " civilians not taking a direct part in hostilities " . <p> " Every single one of these attacks hit civilian objects , including homes . In all but one case , Israel gave no warning before carrying out the attack , " said Callamard . <p> " And in the last case , although Israel did give a warning , it was not effective . In none of these attacks did Amnesty , despite really looking , find evidence there was any legitimate military objective in or near the location struck . " <p> With regards to " specific intent " , Amnesty International pointed to comments made by a range of Israeli officials as proof that they were " deliberately inflicting on Palestinians in Gaza conditions of life calculated to bring about their physical destruction in whole or in part " and contradicted Israeli claims to be acting in self-defence . <p> " One of the reasons we are publishing this report is to issue a wake-up call to the international community and ensure states recognise this is genocide and it has to stop now , " Amnesty 's senior legal expert , Grazia Careccia , told Middle East Eye . <p> " States have a clear choice before them : they can decide to continue granting Israel impunity for its atrocity crimes against Palestinians or they can act now to stop it . States that continue to transfer arms to Israel need to know they face the risk of complicity in genocide if they continue to do so . " <h> ' Western states must act ' <p> Several Palestinian advocacy groups have characterised Israel 's actions in Gaza as genocide , while a case is also ongoing at the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) . <p> The ICJ in May issued a preliminary ruling that it was plausible that Israel had breached the Genocide Convention . <p> Biden and Starmer are destroying international law to protect Israel 's genocide <p> As an emergency measure , it ordered Israel to ensure that its army refrained from genocidal acts against Palestinians and halt its offensive on the south Gaza city of Rafah . <p> Israel has not abided by ICJ orders , however , branding the world 's top court antisemitic . <p> A spokesperson for the International Centre of Justice for Palestinians ( ICJP ) said it welcomed Amnesty 's conclusions and hoped they would be taken seriously . <p> " Western states must act on this evidence , and the evidence put forward by innumerable other NGOs and UN bodies , " the ICJP 's Jonathan Purcell told MEE . <p> " Continued inaction by any state simply shows a flagrant disregard for the Genocide Convention .
