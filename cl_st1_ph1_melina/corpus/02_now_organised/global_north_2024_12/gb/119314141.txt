<p> The Israel Defence Forces claimed they struck multiple " Houthi terrorist " targets in Yemen on Thursday , including the airport in the capital Sanaa and three ports along the western coast . <p> Director-General of the World Health Organisation , Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus , was in Sanaa airport at the time of the attack . <p> He said : " As we were about to board our flight from Sanaa , about two hours ago , the airport came under aerial bombardment . <p> " One of our plane 's crew members was injured . At least two people were reported killed at the airport . <p> " The air traffic control tower , the departure lounge -- just a few metres from where we were -- and the runway were damaged . " He added that he and his colleagues are safe . <p> The attacks hit Yemen 's Hezyaz and Ras Kanatib power stations as well as military infrastructure in the ports of Hodeidah , Salif and Ras Kanatib , Israel 's military added . <p> Israel said it struck " military targets " because the Houthis had " repeatedly attacked " Israel , and that these targets had been used to " smuggle Iranian weapons " into the region . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said following the attacks that " we will persist in severing the terror arm of the Iranian axis " . <p> The Houthi movement is a Shia Islamist political and military organisation that emerged in Yemen in the 1990s and is backed by Iran . <p> The group has been firing drones and missiles towards Israel in what it describes as acts of solidarity with Palestinians in Gaza since the Israel-Hamas conflict began . <p> The Houthis ' Al Masirah TV news outlet reported attacks on the airport , Hodeidah and on one power station . <p> Al Masirah also reported that three people were killed in the strikes -- two in Sanaa International Airport and another in Ras Issa -- and a further 11 were wounded . These figures have not been independently verified and it is not clear whether they relate to civilians or Houthi rebels . <p> Civilians at the site of an Israeli strike in a residential area of the Tuffah neighbourhood in the east of Gaza City today ( Photo : Omar Al-Qattaa/AFP ) <p> Iran described Israel 's strikes as a " violation " of peace and security . <p> Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam described the strikes as " a Zionist crime against all the Yemeni people " , AFP reported . <p> More than a year of Houthi attacks have disrupted international shipping routes , forcing firms to re-route to longer and more expensive journeys that have in turn stoked fears over global inflation . <p> Israel has instructed its diplomatic missions in Europe to try to get the Houthis designated as a terrorist organisation . <p> The UN Security Council is due to meet on Monday over Houthi attacks against Israel , Israel 's UN Ambassador Danny Danon said on Wednesday . <p> On Saturday , Israel 's military failed to intercept a missile from Yemen that fell in the Tel Aviv-Jaffa area , injuring 14 people . <p> The attacks follow the news that the Israeli military loosened its rules of engagement on the day of the 7 October attack , enabling mid-ranking officers to target Hamas despite the huge civilian cost , according to a report . <p> At 1pm on 7 October 2023 , hours after Hamas launched its deadly raids , Israel 's military leadership issued an order granting mid-ranking Israeli officers the authority to strike a wide range of Hamas militants and military sites , the New York Times reported . <h> Read Next <p> This was a change from previous Gaza wars , when senior Hamas commanders , arms depots and rocket launchers were the main focus . <p> In each strike , officers had the authority to risk killing up to 20 civilians . In practice , it meant the Israel Defence Forces could target rank-and-file militants while at home with family , instead of only when they were alone outside . <p> Yesterday in Gaza , authorities said an Israeli air strike killed five Palestinian journalists outside a hospital , though , the IDF said it had attacked a vehicle carrying Islamic Jihad militants . <p> Medics said the five were among at least 21 people killed in Israeli air assaults across the enclave before dawn . <p> The Palestinian Journalists Union said one strike killed five journalists from the Al-Quds Today channel who were in a broadcast vehicle in front of Al-Awda Hospital in the Al-Nuseirat refugee camp in central Gaza .
