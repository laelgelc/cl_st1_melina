<h> Looking back at the biggest stories of the second half of 2024 <h> Lancashire Telegraph year in review : July to December <p> It has been a very busy year across East Lancashire , with all sorts of huge breaking news stories and events taking place . <p> The second half of the year saw political earthquakes , stories that went global with a local person at the heart of them , big business news and tragedy in equal measure . <p> Throughout it all , the Lancashire Telegraph has been keeping a close eye on all that has taken place to provide our readers with accurate , up to date information about the biggest issues affecting our area . <p> Here is a roundup , from July to December , of the biggest stories that have hit East Lancashire over the past six months . <h> July <h> Election shockwaves <p> " Britain decides " was the message throughout July as the public flocked to the polls to elect their next Prime Minister and local MP in a shock General Election . <p> A Tory wipeout was predicted by pollsters with Labour expected to sweep to power , and in East Lancashire most seats followed the national trend . <p> Five Conservative MPs were dumped by the electorate and replaced with new Labour representatives in Hyndburn , Burnley , Rossendale and Darwen , and the altered seats of Pendle and Clitheroe , and the Ribble Valley . <p> However , the biggest shock of the night was independent candidate Adnan Hussain whose campaign focused on the ongoing war in Gaza saw Labour lose the seat for the first time since its creation in 1955 -- beating incumbent Kate Hollern by just 132 votes . <p> His victory had followed big gains for the newly-created 4BwD Gaza-centric independent group , made up of mainly former Labour councillors , in the Blackburn with Darwen local elections a month earlier <h> Jay Slater <p> A TV detective looking into the disappearance of Jay Slater made the headlines after claiming the teenager took a ? 12,000 watch shortly before going missing in Tenerife . <p> Mark Williams-Thomas , a former police officer turned investigative journalist , pointed to evidence from a post on Jay 's Snapchat saying he had taken a Rolex from a person but ruled out a hostage and kidnapping situation as no credible ransom demand had been made . <p> It was the second time he had waded into the disappearance of a Lancashire person in recent years , after getting heavily involved in the missing persons case of Nicola Bulley , who drowned after falling in a river in St Michaels-on-Wyre , and being heavily critical of the police . <p> Jay , 19 , from Oswaldtwistle , had gone missing in June while on holiday in Tenerife , and after a major search effort in a remote region of the island , his body was found on July 15 . <p> It was confirmed he died from injuries related to a fall , and his body was eventually repatriated to the UK and he was laid to rest in August . <h> August <h> Southport riots spill over <p> Following the tragic killing of three children in Southport , ' protesters ' took to the streets throughout the UK and dispersal orders were issued throughout Lancashire . <p> After the ugly scenes from rioters in Southport there were fears this would be mirrored in the county 's towns . <p> More than 20 people were arrested county-wide for offences including possession of an offensive weapon , police assault , possession of a bladed article , obstructing police and conspiracy to commit violent disorder . <p> Anti-racism counter-protests were also held and moments of solidarity were witnessed across the county with a moving video of pub-goers shaking hands with the Muslim communities in Accrington . <p> In East Lancashire , as well as Accrington coming together to stand against hate , a protest in Blackburn promoted by far-right agitator Tommy Robinson failed to launch , with a counter-march attracting many more people . <p> Pubgoers embrace and shake hands with demonstrators on march through town ( Image : X/ Taj Ali ) <h> Derelict mansion rebirth plans <p> Plans to restore a derelict former hotel , which dated back to 1851 , were also submitted to the council . <p> Under the proposals the Horncliffe Mansion , in Bury Road , Rawtenstall , would become a four-bed home , plus the construction of two four-bedroom ' luxury ' houses on the former car park . <p> Other works planned for the building include reinstating chimneys , cleaning and restoring stonework and pointing , replacing windows and door and installing CCTV to protect the site . <h> September <p> The Issa brothers sold stepped back from their positions in the EG group and Asda ( Image : NQ ) <h> All change for the Issa brothers <p> September was a big month for Blackburn 's billionaire brothers , with Mohsin Issa taking a step back from running Asda whilst Zuber Issa stepped down as co-chief executive at EG Group to start a new venture . <p> He took on the lead on the new UK petrol forecourt and convenience retail business branded ' EG on the Move ' . <p> On the same day , his brother Mohsin Issa confirmed he was stepping down from running the Asda supermarket chain and handed over the reins to chair Lord Stuart Rose and the management team . <p> Janine Parrington lost five stone in five months thanks to weight loss group ( Image : Slimming world ) <h> Woman 's weight loss journey <p> Janine Parrington , who had struggled with perimenopausal symptoms , hoped to inspire others after losing five stone in five months after joining a weight-loss club . <p> After her success , she began running a weekly class at Padiham Football Club , with the aim of helping other people struggling to live at a healthy weight achieve their goals . <h> October <p> Demi-Leigh Davies tragically died in a car accident on the M65 ( Image : Gemma Walsh ) <h> Teenage girl dies in horror crash <p> Tributes were paid to a 17-year-old girl from Darwen who was killed in a horror crash on the M65 , in an incident that left three other teenagers seriously injured . <p> Demi-Leigh Davies was a passenger in the car and suffered multiple injuries in the early hours smash , and died in hospital . <p> In a tribute , Demi 's family said : " Our beautiful angel has been taken from us far too soon . <p> " She had a smile that lit up any room and left a lasting impression . Words can not truly express the pain we are all going through knowing we will never see her beautiful face or hear her infectious laugh again . " <p> Helen Thompson was left infuriated with her car parking space ( Image : NQ ) <h> New-build home headache <p> A Longridge homeowner was infuriated when she said that the driveway at the side of her new-build home was too narrow and not fit for purpose . <p> While Helen Thompson 's car could fit on the driveway , she did n't have enough room to open the doors closest to the house wall . <p> She slammed the rules around driveway dimensions and said had she known prior about the issue she would not have bought the house . <h> November <p> A flooded Acre Park in Bacup ( Image : Daniel Higson ) <h> Flooding chaos <p> Streets throughout East Lancashire became flooded as rivers burst their banks as Storm Bert caused chaos . <p> Rossendale was heavily affected by the flooding with dangerous driving conditions and fields throughout the town submerged . <p> Rossendale councillor Scott Smith said it was " heartbreaking " to see towns in the borough flooded . <p> In Foulridge , a man , 80 , died after trying to drive his vehicle through a ford , triggering calls for drivers to think twice about using the route in extreme weather . <p> Robert Plant went for a tour of the stadium and left with a bag of goodies ( Image : Accrington Stanley ) <h> Zeppelin drops in <p> It was a big day for Led Zepplin 's ex-frontman Robert Plant as he was treated to a visit to Accrington Stanley . <p> The lead vocalist in the legendary 70s rock band was spotted in the club shop by supporter liaison officer Robert Houseman , and the musician admitted that he had always had a soft spot for the club , despite being a Wolverhampton Wanderers fan . <p> Mr Houseman took Mr Plant around the stadium and explained a short history of the club . <p> Mr Plant posed for some pictures and left with a bag of Stanley souvenirs . <h> December <p> Acrelands Farm was taken over but Nicholas White ( Image : Colin Lawton ) <h> Squatting scandal exclusively revealed <p> An East Lancashire businessman had to be forcibly removed from a million-pound farmhouse after a year-long squatting scandal . <p> Nicholas White , who owns the Freemasons pub in Wiswell , had paid off the previous tenants before moving in , making substantial changes to both the property , including starting a registered livery business , building horse 's stables and cutting down trees . <p> After a long legal battle , a possession order was granted , and police were called to remove him from the site after his 28-day notice was completed . <h> Shy chef claims top gong <p> One of the most humble and talented chefs in the country graced our screens in December as Dan Merriman , who is a senior sous chef at Old Langho 's Black Bull Inn , claimed the MasterChef : The Professionals crown . <p> His refined style with heaps of technique and high levels of consistency throughout the competition was enough to win , beating fellow East Lancastrian George Birtwell in the final . <p> Dan , who grew up in Worcestershire and now lives with his family in Accrington , said after his win : " No matter where life takes me , I 'm forever grateful .
