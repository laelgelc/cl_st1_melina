<h> The Palestinian pop-up shop hoping to become a permanent caf ? in Glasgow <p> Volunteers are asking Scots to help set up a Palestinian caf ? in Glasgow and build on the city 's twinning with Bethlehem ( Image : Laura Pollock ) <p> A PALESTINIAN pop-up shop in Glasgow is hoping to open a permanent cafe to support artisans and businesses in Gaza and the West Bank . <p> Activist Donald Reid , a former church minister , was seeking to give practical help to those in Palestine and suggested to The Alchemy Experiment on Glasgow 's Byres Road about opening a pop-up shop with products from those in the war-torn country . <p> " People were falling over themselves to help staff here to help make it happen , and people running off the streets trying to just grab stuff , and put money in as donations as well . <p> " It just proved that people want to do something really , really practical so they buy something . " <p> This was the the group 's first stint as a pop-up shop in April , and now , they are open and " tapping into the Christmas market hype " until Friday , December 6 . <p> The shop stocks ceramics , embroidered clothing , bags , books , olive oil , chocolate , and jewellery . A lot of the produce is supplied by Hadeel -- a non-profit trading arm of the Scottish charity Palcrafts based in Edinburgh , which supports Palestinians artisans . <p> A customer earlier in the day had told Reid he felt there was n't much he could do , but at least he could buy something that 's been made by somebody in Palestine . <p> EXC : A Palestinian pop-up shop in Glasgow is hoping to open a permanent cafe to support artisans and businesses in Gaza and the West Bank <h> Why are you making commenting on The National only available to subscribers ? <p> We know there are thousands of National readers who want to debate , argue and go back and forth in the comments section of our stories .
