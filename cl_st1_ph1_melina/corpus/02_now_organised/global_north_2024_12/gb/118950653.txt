<h> Humza Yousaf 's downfall : Where did it all go wrong ? <h> Humza Yousaf : What was the former First Minister 's downfall ? <p> Mr Yousaf officially stepped down as First Minister in May and now , in his own words , he is stepping back from ' frontline politics altogether ' come 2026 . So what has led to his downfall and where did it all go wrong ? <p> ' Regrets , achievements , mistakes I 've got all three ' , Humza Yousaf told Zeteo news as he recently announced he would not be running in the 2026 Holyrood elections and he would step away from frontline politics altogether . <p> So what has led to Mr Yousaf , aged only 39 , bowing out of politics after being the First Minister of Scotland up until earlier this year ? <p> Let 's rewind to a year ago when Mr Yousaf made history in the UK after he was elected as the first first minister from an ethnic minority background and the first Muslim to lead a Western country . <p> During the SNP leadership contest in 2023 , Mr Yousaf was styled as the continuity candidate to Nicola Sturgeon and although the former first minister Ms Sturgeon did not endorse any candidate , he was widely thought of as her preferred choice . <p> Mr Yousaf , however , only survived in the top job for 13 months . <p> He narrowly avoided being the shortest incumbent of the post , a title held by Labour 's Henry McLeish after he was in office for one year and 12 days between 2000 and 2001 . <p> His legacy as First Minister was defined by one critical decision he made : ending the power sharing agreement Ms Sturgeon had established with the Scottish Greens . After he shattered this earlier this year saying at the time it had reached its ' natural conclusion ' , his resignation shortly followed after . <h> The Bute House Agreement <p> In April this year , the government dropped its flagship target of reducing greenhouse gas emissions by 75% by 2030 . This created anger amongst their power-sharing partners the Scottish Greens . Causing further uproar amongst their Green colleagues at the time , there was also a pause on the prescription of new puberty blockers to young trans people . <p> This led to the Scottish Greens calling for a vote on whether or not the Bute House Agreement - the agreement which created a majority governance between the two parties - should continue . <p> Following this , Mr Yousaf called in the Green co-leaders to an early morning meeting at Bute House and ended their agreement . <p> I asked him when he made the announcement of ditching the Greens if he had jumped before he was pushed . He refused , saying : " I 've not been pushed . I 'm the First Minister of Scotland . " <p> Less than a week later , he announced his resignation . <p> After he terminated the Bute House Agreement with immediate effect , despite having previously described it as being " worth its weight in gold " , the Greens accused Mr Yousaf of " political cowardice " and declared they would vote against him in a vote of no confidence . <p> That vote took place little over 13 months after Mr Yousaf had become Scotland 's sixth first minister and Mr Yousaf resigned saying he " paid the price " for the way he ended the power-sharing deal with the Scottish Greens . <p> In his resignation speech , he said he had " clearly underestimated " the hurt he had caused the Greens . <h> Recent comments on the Bute House Agreement <p> Speaking in a recent interview , Mr Yousaf said the sacrifice of his own leadership in terminating the agreement was , in his opinion , " the right thing to have done . " He added : " My successor is no longer encumbered by that deal . " <p> Mr Yousaf said the Greens would have most likely rejected the Bute House Agreement and he was facing mounting pressure in his own party ranks to ditch them as power-sharing partners . <p> The former first minister said : " Things became really difficult in my party too , to be frank , after a pretty challenging interview for the Green co-leader on an issue on trans healthcare , there were people in my own party who were very supportive of the Bute House Agreement now demanding that it was terminated . " <p> However , a Green spokesperson told The Herald they do not know how Scottish Green members would have voted if they had been given the chance to decide . <p> The spokesperson added : " The agreement had originally been voted on and overwhelmingly supported by both parties , and there were members of both parties who wanted their concerns to be addressed , rather than ending the agreement . <p> " The agreement was clear in its support for trans people 's human rights , and it would be very peculiar to suggest it was worth Humza Yousaf bringing down a progressive pro-independence government over a longstanding position that he and his colleagues must have known that we held . It also does not match up with what he said at the time of his decision . <p> " As well as his own resignation it has led to a rightwards shift from the SNP on a range of issues , from cuts to vital climate budgets to the weakening of rent controls to dropping the commitment to end conversion practices . We still stand by these principles and have continued to argue for them and progress them from opposition . " <h> Other issues leading up to Mr Yousaf 's swift exit <p> Although the collapse of the Bute House Agreement led to Mr Yousaf 's downfall other issues leading up to this fallout did not help matters for the former first minister . <p> Almost as soon as he took on the role of First Minister , Mr Yousaf found out the SNP had been without auditors for months and days after he was sworn in , police searched his predecessor 's home and SNP headquarters . <p> Also within days of him taking charge , Ms Sturgeon 's husband , former SNP chief executive Peter Murrell , was arrested by police investigating the whereabouts of ? 600,000 of donations to the party . <p> Murrell was subsequently rearrested and charged in April 2024 in connection with the alleged embezzlement of funds . <p> His first year in office also saw the continued postponement of the deposit return scheme and the ditching of highly protected marine areas . It also saw the Scottish Government losing a Supreme Court case challenging the UK Government 's block on the gender recognition reforms . <p> In the same month he resigned as First Minister , Mr Yousaf also faced criticism when the hate crime laws which he had instigated as justice secretary came into effect . <p> Critics claimed the legislation could harm free speech , although the government insisted that it provided protection without harming individual expression . <p> One area which garnered support from some , including the Scottish Greens , however , was Mr Yousaf 's early call for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza when other political leaders had not committed to this .
