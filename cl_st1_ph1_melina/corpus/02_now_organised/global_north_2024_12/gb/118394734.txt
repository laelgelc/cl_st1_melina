<h> We are enforcing ceasefire with an iron fist , says Netanyahu <p> Israeli PM promises harsh response to any breach by Hezbollah , as defence minister warns that a return to hostilities would mean more far-reaching strikes in Lebanon <p> Edmund Bower , Beirut <p> Tuesday December 03 2024 , 8.35am , The Times <p> There have been reports of Israeli bombing in southern Lebanon every day since the agreement last Wednesday morning <p> MARWAN NAAMANI/ZUMA/ALAMY <p> Edmund Bower , Beirut <p> Tuesday December 03 2024 , 8.35am , The Times <p> Israeli prime minister Benyamin Netanyahu said on Tuesday that Israel was enforcing its ceasefire with Hezbollah with " an iron fist " amidst warnings from his administration that a return to hostilities would trigger more far-reaching Israeli strikes within Lebanon . <p> Netanyahu warned that the 60-day truce between Israel and Hezbollah , which came into effect last Wednesday morning , did not equal " the end of the war " . <p> His comments followed the deadliest day since the ceasefire came into effect last Wednesday morning . At least 11 people were killed in Israeli strikes on villages in southern Lebanon on Monday , after Israel said it was taking aim at dozens of Hezbollah targets in retaliation for an attack claimed by the militant group earlier that day . <p> " If we return to war we will act strongly , we will go deeper , and the most important thing they need to know : there will no longer be an exemption for the state of Lebanon , " defence minister Israel Katz said on Tuesday . <p> He called on the Lebanese state to keep Hezbollah out of southern Lebanon and to " dismantle their infrastructure " . Failure to do so , he warned , could lead Israeli forces to hit the Lebanese state directly . <h> Advertisement <p> " If until now we separated the state of Lebanon from Hezbollah , " said Katz , " it will no longer be like this . " <p> Israel has struck targets in southern Lebanon every day since the ceasefire was announced . <p> Both Israel and Hezbollah have accused each other of breaching the truce . Nearly 4,000 people have been killed by Israeli attacks in Lebanon since October last year , when Hezbollah stepped up its rocket attacks in support of Hamas in Gaza . The war has also sparked mass displacements on both sides of the border . <p> Lebanon 's health ministry said the strikes near the border with Israel killed six people and wounded two in the village of Haris , while in the village of Tallous another four people were killed and one wounded . There was at least one further victim in the village of Marjayoun . <p> Israel said two missiles were fired on the disputed area of Shebaa Farms , also known as Mount Dov <p> KARAMALLAH DAHER/REUTERS <p> Beirut has so far not been targeted since the ceasefire came into effect but an Israeli surveillance drone could be heard flying over the capital on Monday night . <h> Advertisement <p> The Israeli Defence Forces ( IDF ) said in a statement that it " struck Hezbollah terrorists , dozens of launchers and terrorist infrastructure throughout Lebanon " . <p> " Israel demands that the relevant parties in Lebanon fulfil their responsibilities and prevent Hezbollah 's hostile activity , " the statement said . <p> The IDF said that the strikes were carried out in response to the firing of two missiles on the disputed area known as Shebaa Farms or Mount Dov , which is under Israeli control but which Lebanon considers part of its territory . The missiles landed in open fields , causing no casualties . <p> In a statement , Hezbollah said that it fired on an Israeli military position as a " defensive and warning response " in response to " repeated violations " of the ceasefire deal . The statement said that its complaints to mediators had proved " futile in stopping these violations " . <p> Netanyahu said : " We are determined to uphold the ceasefire and respond to any violation by Hezbollah , no matter how minor or serious . " <h> Advertisement <p> Binyamin Netanyahu <p> AP <p> The speaker of the Lebanese parliament , Nabih Berri , who has at times negotiated on behalf of Hezbollah , accused Israel of a " flagrant violation " of the ceasefire on Monday , claiming to have identified more than 54 separate violations in recent days . There have been reports of Israeli bombing in southern Lebanon every day since the agreement last Wednesday morning . <p> The renewed violence increases uncertainty around the 60-day truce , which was hoped would provide the basis for a more permanent peace . Many of the over one million Lebanese who fled more than a month of Israeli attacks have since returned to their homes in southern and eastern Lebanon and Beirut 's southern suburbs . Under the agreement , IDF troops are meant to withdraw from southern Lebanon within 60 days , to be replaced by the Lebanese army . <p> " We 've gone from dozens of strikes down to one a day , maybe two a day , " the White House national security spokesman , John Kirby , told reporters on Monday . " We 're going to keep trying and see what we can do to get it down to zero . " <p> In Saudi Arabia , where the French president , Emmanuel Macron , is on a state visit , he and the Saudi crown prince , Mohammed bin Salman , pledged to " make every effort to contribute to de-escalation in the region " , including helping to consolidate the ceasefire between Israel and Lebanon . <h> Advertisement <p> The two leaders also called for presidential elections in Lebanon " with the aim of bringing the Lebanese people together and carrying out the reforms necessary for the stability and security of the country " , Macron 's office said .
