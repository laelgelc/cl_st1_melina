<p> Israel and Hezbollah exchanged air strikes and rocket fire on Monday as their 60-day ceasefire appeared to be unravelling within its first week . <p> Both sides faced accusations of breaching the truce , which came into force on Wednesday to end a war that has killed thousands in Lebanon and sparked mass displacements on both sides of the border . <p> The tit-for-tat exchange began on Monday afternoon , with the Israeli military saying two " projectiles " were launched towards the area of Mount Dov . No injuries were reported . <p> Hezbollah media , Al Mayadeen , said the group had carried out " a warning operation " following what it called Israeli violations of the fragile US/France-brokered ceasefire . <p> Benjamin Netanyahu , Israel 's prime minister , accused Hezbollah of a " serious violation " after the attack , and vowed to " respond forcefully " , while Israel Katz , his defence minister promised a " harsh response " . <p> This came overnight as the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) attacked targets in Lebanon in what it said were operations against terror infrastructure . <p> Lebanon 's official National News Agency reported air strikes on areas of south Lebanon , Hezbollah 's stronghold , located around 20 kilometres ( 12 miles ) from the border with Israel . <p> Sand bags are seen in Manara , in Israel , near the Lebanese border for the first time since Oct 7 2023 Credit : Amir Levy/Getty Images <p> Despite the retaliatory attacks , John Kirby , White House spokesman , told reporters on Monday that the " sporadic strikes " in recent days were to be expected . <p> " There has been a dramatic reduction in the violence . The monitoring mechanism is in full force and is working ... largely speaking the ceasefire is holding , " he said . <p> This did not appear to be the case on Monday , however . <p> At least two people were killed in the retaliatory strikes on southern Lebanon according to Lebanese authorities . <p> Al Mayadeen said the Hezbollah " warning operation " followed Israeli airstrikes which led to the death of civilians , in addition to the continued violation of Lebanese airspace by Israeli aircraft , all the way to the capital of Beirut . <p> Nabih Berri , Lebanese parliament speaker , closely affiliated with Iran-backed Hezbollah , said in a statement that over 54 violations had been committed by Israel . <p> " The actions of Israeli forces are a blatant violation of the ceasefire agreement , " he said . <p> " We call on the monitoring committee established to oversee the implementation of the ceasefire where it is in addressing these continuous breaches , now exceeding 54 . Meanwhile , Lebanon and the resistance remained fully committed to their obligations under the agreement . " <p> A statement on Hezbollah 's Telegram account said that " since the reviews of the relevant authorities to stop these violations did not succeed , the Islamic Resistance carried out an initial warning defensive response this evening " . <p> Israel 's public broadcaster Kan News reported on Monday that Amos Hochstein , the US envoy who was among the team which brokered the ceasefire , had warned Israel against alleged violations . <p> Jean-Noel Barrot , the French foreign minister , told Gideon Saar , the Israeli foreign minister , in a call of " the need for all sides to respect the ceasefire in Lebanon " , the foreign ministry said . <p> Saar rejected accusations that Israel had violated the truce . <p> " We hear claims that Israel is violating the ceasefire understandings in Lebanon . On the contrary ! Israel is enforcing them in response to Hezbollah 's violations , which demand immediate action , " he said in a statement . <p> He also said Hezbollah had attempted to move weapons in southern Lebanon and their presence " south of the Litani river is the most basic violation of the understandings " . <p> As part of the agreement , the Lebanese army and United Nations peacekeepers will deploy in southern Lebanon as the Israeli army withdraws over a period of 60 days . <p> Hezbollah began firing on Israel on Oct 8 last year , a day after the Oct 7 atrocities of Iran-backed Hamas in Gaza , in allegiance with the group . <p> Since then , over 17,000 projectiles have been fired into Israel . Tens of thousands have been displaced in Israel and hundreds of thousands displaced from southern Lebanon . <p> The UN 's peacekeepers , UNIFIL , are in place to monitor the ceasefire with thousands of Lebanese army troops deployed to try and enforce its terms . <p> On Monday , after a visit to Maroun El Ras , in southern Lebanon , Daniel Hagari , the Israeli military spokesman , said : " We are committed to the ceasefire agreement . <p> " The 60-day period is a gradual phase to ensure that Hezbollah does not harm us or violate the agreement , as happened in 2006 . <p> " The Lebanese government and UNIFIL troops must fulfil their roles , it is also in their interest . We need this period to ensure that there are no terrorist bases there . "
