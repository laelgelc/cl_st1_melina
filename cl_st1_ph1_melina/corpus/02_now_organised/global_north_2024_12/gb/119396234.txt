<h> A new year 's message from Oxfordshire political leaders <p> Oxfordshire MPs and local government leaders have sent messages looking ahead to 2025 , and wishing readers a happy new year . <h> Susan Brown <p> " This has been a big year for Oxford . The Cowley Road Carnival made its comeback , Oxford became the electric bus capital of the UK , and Oxford United got promoted . <p> " It 's also been a big year for Oxford City Council . <p> ( Image : Jon Lewis ) " We started construction on two new community centres , welcomed a new operator for our five leisure centres , started building hundreds of new homes in Blackbird Leys , got 27,000 people onto our Oxford Living Wage , and created the Oxford Climate Awards with the Oxford Mail . <p> " But I suspect , with more new homes coming forward and hopefully securing funding for the Cowley Branch Line , 2025 will be an even bigger year for our city . We can not wait . <p> " Happy New Year , everyone . " <h> Liz Leffman <p> " It is the end of December and the time has arrived for new year messages . <p> " I feel very strongly that I do so not on my own behalf but in representing the many county council staff who are your neighbours , family members and fellow professionals as well as the people who serve your community every day . <p> " They work diligently and out of a sense of community spirit and loyalty to the area as much as anything else . <p> ( Image : Contributed ) " I look forward to the county council continuing to provide high quality services for you in 2025 all focused on our main aim of making Oxfordshire greener , fairer and healthier . <p> " Council matters can sometimes be controversial and political , but the bottom line is that there are people in social care , highways , education , libraries , trading standards , fire and rescue , registration ( births , deaths and marriages ) , public health and others working for you day in and day out . <p> " A very happy new year to you and them . " <h> Anneliese Dodds <p> " Happy New Year to everyone in Oxford East and around the world ! <p> ( Image : Anneliese Dodds ) " Here 's to a year of hope , growth , and opportunity . <p> " Wishing you and your families health , happiness , and success in the year to come ! " <h> Layla Moran <p> " I wish everyone in Oxfordshire a very Happy New Year ! <p> " I am really looking forward to 2025 and all the opportunities and challenges it will bring . <p> ( Image : Layla Moran ) " I ca n't wait to continue my work on the Health &amp; Social Care Select Committee , pushing the government to make the changes needed to transform the quality of health and care services . <p> " And locally , let 's hope this is the year we finally see the completion of Lodge Hill Junction and the re-opening of Botley Road . <p> " I can assure you I will be doing everything I can to make them happen . " <h> Sean Woodcock MP <p> " How has 2024 been for you ? It 's been an eventful one for me to be sure . Getting married in February and elected in July , it is certainly hard to imagine one quite as eventful as this . <p> " Elsewhere it has been eventful for all the wrong reasons . <p> ( Image : Other . ) " For more than a year , we 've had conflict in the Middle East with hundreds still in captivity by Hamas , thousands killed in Israel , Gaza and Lebanon and millions displaced from their home . <p> " The devastation from this conflict is appalling and the human suffering must be addressed . <p> " The announcement of a ceasefire agreement to end hostilities between Israel and Lebanese Hizballah offers hope for the New Year . <p> " It must be a turning point that builds momentum towards a lasting peace across the Middle East . <p> " In Gaza , we need an immediate ceasefire , the release of all the hostages and an end to Israeli restrictions on aid . The intolerable suffering must end . <p> " In Sudan , we have the world 's largest humanitarian with women and girls disproportionately affected by the conflict . <p> " Sudan 's neighbours are also affected by the ongoing violence . That 's why the UK has doubled aid to the country . <p> " Meanwhile Ukrainians are spending a third Christmas at war . <p> " Russia 's assault on Ukraine is an unprovoked , premeditated and barbaric attack against a sovereign democratic state . <p> " We must support Ukraine 's fight for freedom , liberty and victory . <p> " The defence of the UK starts in Ukraine which is why the UK and our international partners stand united . <p> " Peace on Earth and goodwill to all would be welcome gifts this Christmas . And I wish you love , joy and happiness too . " <h> Calum Miller <p> " Since July , it has been an immense honour to serve as your MP . I 've enjoyed the privilege of attending dozens of events and meeting hundreds of residents . <p> " I have spoken with many of you about the challenges you face . My team and I have tried to help directly . <p> ( Image : Roger Harris ) " I 've also raised with Ministers your many concerns including flooding , provision for children with SEND , access to health and social care and funding rural transport . <p> " Next year , I 'm committed to ensuring the needs of our area are heard in government . For now , I wish you peace and joy this festive season .
