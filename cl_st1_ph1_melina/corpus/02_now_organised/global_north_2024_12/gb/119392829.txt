That we would suddenly lose beloved stars like Michael Mosley and Liam Payne ? <p> Or that we would end up talking about mystery drones , exploding pagers , a daring cheese heist , the return of ' milkshaking ' or the misfortune of a well-endowed Olympic pole vaulter ? <p> And of all the people we thought could lose their jobs this year , they probably did n't include a trusted BBC news presenter , a popular cooking show judge , the Archbishop of Canterbury and a dictator who seemed unshakable . <p> The shocks came no sooner than New Year celebrations were over last year , when it took an ITV drama to expose a miscarriage of justice few had heard about , and which caused a huge public outcry . <p> The ITV show helped bring the injustice to light after years of dedicated campaigning <p> Postmasters ' campaigner Alan Bates became a national hero , and former Post Office chief , a national villain , even before panto season was over . <p> But the year also began with national concern as King Charles announced he was battling cancer , just weeks after the Duchess of York , Sarah Ferguson , told how she had also been diagnosed with the disease . <p> Then , following abdominal surgery and weeks of speculation -
