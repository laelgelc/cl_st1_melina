<h> Starmer defends seeking investment from ' key partner ' Saudi Arabia <h> Starmer defends seeking investment from ' key partner ' Saudi Arabia <p> Sir Keir Starmer said economic growth was his " number one mission " as he held talks with Saudi Arabia 's crown prince Mohammad bin Salman . <p> Downing Street insisted Sir Keir would still be able to raise concerns about the Saudi government 's human rights record , despite it being a lower priority than the economy . <p> Campaign group Reprieve called on Sir Keir to raise the issue of children facing the death penalty in Saudi Arabia , while the widow of murdered journalist Jamal Khashoggi urged the Prime Minister to question the crown prince about the case . <p> Prime Minister Sir Keir Starmer at the King Khalid International Airport as he arrived in Riyadh ( Kirsty Wigglesworth/PA ) <p> Sir Keir accused predecessor Boris Johnson of " going cap in hand from dictator to dictator " when the former prime minister met the Saudi crown prince in 2022 . <p> But the Prime Minister defended his decision to visit Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates on Monday . <p> Following his talks with the crown prince , he told broadcasters in Riyadh : " Last week I launched my plan for change and made it clear that economic growth in the UK is my number one mission , and I want that to be people feeling better off , living standards driven up across the United Kingdom , in all parts of the United Kingdom . <p> " For that to happen we have to win contracts and investment around the world , and UAE and Saudi Arabia are key partners of ours . <p> " So I 've been making the case that now 's the time for further investment into our country . " <p> Adding that recent deals with Saudi Arabia have " yielded " 4,000 jobs to the UK , he said : " So I 'm here driving growth with the sole intention of making sure that when I say that living standards will go up in the UK , people will feel better off , that we 've got the plan to follow through and deliver on that . " <p> Sir Keir also offered to host the crown prince on a visit to the UK and suggested they watch a football match together . <p> In a readout of the talks , a Downing Street spokesperson said : " The Prime Minister invited the Crown Prince to the UK , and said he hoped the leaders would be able to watch a game of football in between meetings if he took up the offer . " <p> The two men also discussed steps Saudi Arabia was taking to improve human rights , the spokesperson added . <p> The spokesperson added : " Turning to the situation in Israel and Gaza , the Prime Minister updated on his view of the situation and welcomed the Crown Prince 's reflection on how the suffering on all sides could be ended . <p> " It was also vital there was an acceleration of aid into Gaza , the Prime Minister added . " <p> The UK and Saudi Arabia have agreed a new defence partnership , including on combat air capabilities , which is set to last for generations . <p> The Royal Saudi Air Force has operated UK-manufactured Typhoon combat aircraft since 2008 . <p> Before the talks with the Saudi leadership , No 10 said Sir Keir would not shy away from raising human rights concerns . <p> Prime Minister Sir Keir Starmer said economic growth was his number one priority ( Kirsty Wigglesworth/PA ) <p> Reprieve deputy executive director Dan Dolan said : " When Boris Johnson visited Mohammed bin Salman in 2022 , three days after the mass execution of 81 people , Sir Keir Starmer was rightly scathing of Johnson 's unconditional embrace of one of the world 's most prolific executioners of protesters . <p> " Now he is the Prime Minister , he has the opportunity to address the escalating execution crisis in Saudi Arabia . <p> " If he publicly raises the cases of child defendants Abdullah al-Howaiti and Abdullah al-Derazi when he meets with the Crown Prince , he could save their lives . " <p> Mr Khashoggi 's widow Hanan Elatr Khashoggi told the Guardian that Sir Keir should demand answers about what happened to her husband , who was killed inside the Saudi consulate in Istanbul in 2018 . <p> " I hope my husband 's case has not been forgotten , and I will always maintain to make sure it is not forgotten , " she said . " There is unfinished business here . There has to be justice for Jamal Khashoggi . " <p> The long-planned trip to the Middle East came amid regional uncertainty following the collapse of Bashar Assad 's regime in Syria . <p> The UK announced an additional ? 11 million of humanitarian aid for the most vulnerable people in Syria . <p> Share <h> Comments : Our rules <p> We want our comments to be a lively and valuable part of our community - a place where readers can debate and engage with the most important local issues .
