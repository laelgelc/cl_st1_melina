Meanwhile , the death toll rose to 13 following Israel 's bombing of a house in the Nuseirat refugee camp , in central Gaza . <p> Food security across Gaza is collapsing , leaving people in a " state of sheer desperation " , according to a new report by the UN agency for Palestinian refugees ( Unrwa ) . <p> The agency indicated that there are several problems hindering humanitarian supplies from entering into Gaza , including " deteriorating law and order , war and insecurity , damaged infrastructure , fuel shortages and access restrictions " . <p> Additionally , the entire population of Gaza remains at risk of famine and is currently experiencing an " emergency " level of acute food insecurity , the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification ( IPC ) said in late October . <p> The new UN-backed assessment added that the obstruction of humanitarian access and the intensity of the bombing campaign has significantly increased the risk of famine for residents in northern Gaza as food , water , fuel and medical supplies dwindle . <p> " Between November 2024 and April 2025 , almost two million people , more than 90 percent of the population , are classified in IPC Phase 3 ( Crisis ) or above , of which 345,000 people ( 16 percent ) are in Catastrophe ( IPC Phase 5 ) , and 876,000 people ( 41 percent ) in Emergency ( IPC Phase 4 ) , " the IPC said . <p> The IPC classifies the most extreme warning as Phase 5 , which has two levels , catastrophe and famine . <p> Um Mohammed al-Sonbaree , who lost her cousin in the attack , told MEE : " Of course , they Israelis targeted them .
