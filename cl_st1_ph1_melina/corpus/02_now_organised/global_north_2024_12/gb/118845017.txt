<p> Turkey 's President Recep Tayyip Erdogan is set to meet Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi in Cairo with talks focused on Syria , as Egypt turns to Ankara to allay concerns about Syria 's new government . <p> Erdogan will attend a summit of developing nations , called the D-8 , in the Egyptian capital on 19 December , one Egyptian official told Middle East Eye on Friday . <p> Egypt initially extended the invitation to Erdogan before the government of Bashar al-Assad was toppled in an offensive led by Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham ( HTS ) , a Turkish official confirmed to Middle East Eye on Friday . <p> Since then , Egyptian officials have looked to secure Erdogan 's visit as Sisi seeks direct talks with the Turkish leader on Syria , the Egyptian official said . <p> The visit underscores how Erdogan 's regional influence has expanded with the Assad government gone and Syrian rebels establishing a transition government in Damascus . <h> New MEE newsletter : Jerusalem Dispatch <h> Sign up to get the latest insights and analysis on Israel-Palestine , alongside Turkey Unpacked and other MEE newsletters <p> Erdogan was a longtime backer of Syrian rebels , and Turkey 's spy chief , Ibrahim Kalin , was in Damascus on Thursday , giving them unrivalled influence over the group , western and regional officials say . <p> During his visit , Turkey 's National Intelligence Organisation ( MIT ) chief , Ibrahim Kalin , was filmed in the Syrian capital . He was surrounded by armed bodyguards as he visited the Umayyad Mosque . <p> Kalin was later filmed being driven around Damascus by HTS military leader Abu Mohammad al-Jolani in a black sedan . <p> The US , EU and Turkey have labelled HTS , which is a former al-Qaeda affiliate , a terror group . <p> MEE reported previously that US officials have discussed the merits of removing layers of the terror designation . <p> Turkey 's ties to Syrian rebels go back to the 2011 Arab Spring when Turkey backed popular protests against Arab leaders . Turkey has traditionally held the greatest influence over what is now known as the Syrian National Army , formerly known as the Free Syrian Army . <p> The toppling of the Assad government , which had previously reconciled with Arab leaders despite hosting Iranian troops , has unnerved Arab states , including Egypt , Jordan , Saudi Arabia , the UAE and even Qatar , with concerns about the former al-Qaeda affiliate taking power in Syria . <p> However , that has n't stopped the meetings between HTS 's political affairs office and the ambassadors of Egypt , the UAE , Jordan , and Saudi Arabia earlier this week . HTS reassured them of plans to create an inclusive government that would not harbour terrorists , the Egyptian official told MEE . <p> A Qatari official told Reuters on Friday that it would meet with the Syrian interim government on Sunday to discuss reopening an embassy and facilitating humanitarian aid deliveries . <p> The collapse of the Assad government dealt a strategic blow to Iran 's so-called " axis of Resistance " , severing its main supply line to Lebanese Hezbollah . <h> Egypt as a bridge between Turkey and Iran <p> Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian is expected to attend the Cairo summit , and Sisi hopes to broker talks between Erdogan and his Iranian counterpart . <p> The Cairo summit comes amid a flurry of regional diplomacy . Jordan , which supports a constellation of southern Syrian rebel groups , is set to host a Syria summit with Arab , Turkish and US diplomats on Saturday . <p> However , the Erdogan-Sisi meeting will likely be the first between an Arab leader and Erdogan since HTS toppled the Assad government . <p> Egypt was ground zero for a regional battle for influence that played out between Ankara and Qatar on one side and Saudi Arabia and the UAE on the other during the Arab Spring . <p> Sisi ousted Egypt 's democratically elected Muslim Brotherhood president in a coup . Erdogan supported Morsi and , until a few years ago , refused to meet with Sisi , famously declaring , " I will never talk to someone like him " . <p> The two countries backed opposing sides in Libya 's civil war , with Egypt working with Russia and the UAE to support Libyan military chief Khalifa Haftar against a government in Tripoli backed by Turkish forces and mercenaries . <p> Erdogan and Sisi later reconciled and paid reciprocal visits to each other 's countries in 2024 . <p> Syria is shaping up to be a test of whether Arab states can put aside their old grudges as they look to navigate what analysts say is likely to be an unstable transition . <p> HTS includes an array of foreign fighters in its ranks . <p> Meanwhile , separate Turkish-backed Syrian rebels are fighting with the Syrian Democratic Forces , a US-backed offshoot of the Kurdistan Workers ' Party ( PKK ) , a group designated as a terrorist organisation by the US and Turkey . <p> Turkey hopes that stability in Syria will allow the roughly three million Syrian refugees there to return to their country . <p> Along with exercising political influence over a future Syrian government , Turkey could also play a significant part in Syria 's reconstruction , which the UN estimates at $400bn . <p> Egypt has long played a dominant role in the postwar reconstruction efforts of the Gaza Strip .
