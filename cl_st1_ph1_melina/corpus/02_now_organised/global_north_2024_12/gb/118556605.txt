<p> Rebel groups opposing the Syrian government of president Bashar al-Assad are within striking distance of Homs , having captured two key cities in a lightning sweep as they advance towards the capital Damascus . <p> After seizing Aleppo and Hama , Islamist-led opposition forces are closing in on Homs in central Syria , where thousands of civilians have fled overnight towards the western coast where Assad forces still maintain control . <p> Rebel forces were about 5km ( three miles ) from the city of Homs on Friday after taking two towns on the outskirts , according to the Syrian Observatory for Human Rights , a UK-based monitoring group . <p> The battle of Homs , dubbed the capital of the revolution will be crucial , said Fawaz Gerges , Professor of International Relations at the London School of Economics . <p> " Once you take Homs , you have many options to really go for the kill , the beating heart of Assad , " he said . <p> " It stands at a very strategic juncture . From Homs , you can go to Damascus , to the north , the coastal areas , the heartland of support for Assad ... once the opposition seizes Homs , it really means Damascus 's fate is sealed . <p> " I think the unthinkable in Syria could happen -- the possibility , or probability , of Assad 's fall . " <p> As they push further south , the stunning advances by opposition fighters over the past week have so far met little resistance from government forces . Analysts say US sanctions , along with the destructive effects of civil war and economic crisis in Lebanon , have exacted a heavy toll on both the Syrian state and society . <p> And Assad is nowhere to be seen , with rumours spreading that he has fled to Russia , said Professor Gerges . <p> " The army is not only demoralised , it 's starved , " he added . <p> The offensive is led by Hayat Tahrir al-Sham ( HTS ) , the former al-Qaeda affiliate in Syria that has become the most powerful rebel faction in the country . <p> Abu Mohammad al-Julani , leader of HTS , said the rebel fighters wanted to bring an end to the Assad regime . <p> A peaceful uprising against the president of Syria in 2011 turned into a full-blown civil war between government forces backed by Russia and Iran and various armed groups . <p> " When we talk about objectives , the goal of the revolution remains the overthrow of this regime , " Julani told CNN . <p> " The seeds of the regime 's defeat have always been within it ... the Iranians attempted to revive the regime , buying it time , and later the Russians also tried to prop it up . But the truth remains : this regime is dead . " <p> Israeli attacks on Syria , particularly in the last 14 months , have destroyed the military infrastructure , and the killing and the targeting of Iranian assets in the country , particularly leadership and command and control , have forced Tehran to pull out most of its forces that was providing support to the regime . <p> " Iran is deeply preoccupied with Gaza and Israel 's war in Lebanon , " Professor Gerges said . " Russia , too . It has pulled out most of its assets from Syria after its invasion of Ukraine . It still has a small air force but it is not really effective . <p> " They rebel forces realised that the regime is now at its most vulnerable moment . " <p> A Syrian army officer said reinforcements were arriving to help government forces consolidate positions around the city , and that Russian bombing overnight also destroyed the Rustan bridge along the key M5 highway , the main route to Homs , to prevent rebels using it . <p> " There were at least eight strikes on the bridge , " he told Reuters . <p> The M5 highway cuts through Syria 's four major cities : Damascus , Homs , Hama and Aleppo . The 280-mile road begins in southern Syria , near the border with Jordan , and runs to Aleppo near the Turkish border . The destruction along its path traces the arc of the Syrian war , which has killed an estimated half a million people and uprooted half the country 's pre-war population . <p> The Syrian Observatory for Human Rights reported on Friday that government forces have begun withdrawing soldiers from the eastern city of Deir ez-Zor and redeploying them to Damascus . <p> Syria 's defence minister , Gen Ali Mahmoud Abbas , said in a televised statement on Thursday night that government forces withdrew from Hama as " a temporary tactical measure " . <h> Read Next <p> He said the insurgents , whom he described as " takfiri " , or Muslim extremists , are backed by foreign countries .
