<h> Khalid Baqa legal action against Saracens Head , Hereford <p> A CONVICTED terrorist has demanded more than ? 2,000 in compensation from a Hereford pub whose sign he says offends him . <p> Khalid Baqa , who was previously jailed for spreading terrorist content through leaflets and CDs , is making a civil claim against the Saracens Head by the city 's old bridge in St Martin 's Street . <p> Mr Baqa 's claim centres on the pub 's sign , which depicts what he says is an " Arab/Turk " , and the name itself , which he claims " glorifies and encourages victories over Muslims and beheading a Saracen , Turk or Muslim . " <p> Khalid Baqa says he is offended by the pub 's sign ( Image : Julia Baker ) Mr Baqa is asking for ? 2,250 to compensate for the " distress and anxiety " he says he has experienced , claiming he " saw images of the Saracens Head in Hereford in late October 2024 which caused deep offence " . <p> Mr Baqa , 60 , from Essex , told the Hereford Times : " The main reason why the sign offends me is the caption and the association with the Crusades against the Muslims . <p> " If I am successful in any of my lawsuits then all money awarded to me will be donated towards orphans in Gaza and Syria . <p> " If the pub landlord in Hereford is facing financial difficulties , then I am willing to give him a hefty discount to alleviate any worries he may have . " <p> Landlord Simon Belsey said he has received several letters from Mr Baqa and told the Hereford Times : " The Saracens Head has been in Hereford and has had that name since 1738 . We should n't have to change our history .
