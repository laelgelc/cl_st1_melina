<p> The company which owns the Rosebank oil field off the coast of Shetland is facing a legal challenge over its links to illegal Israeli settlements . <p> Equinor , which is the Norwegian state energy company , has been granted permission to drill in the untapped field but is facing a judicial review into the decision to grant a licence . <p> Now the company is also facing a challenge by Greenpeace , which has filed a legal challenge at the Norwegian Consumer Authority . over its ties to Israeli firm Delek Group . <p> Equinor 's partner in the Rosebank project , Ithaca Energy , is majority owned by the company and owns 20% of the Rosebank field . <p> Headquartered in Aberdeen and listed on the London Stock Exchange , Ithaca Energy is 75% or more owned by Delek , an Israeli conglomerate which largely operates in the fossil fuel industry and owns one of the country 's largest chains of petrol stations . <p> Delek operates fuel and service stations in the West Bank and East Jerusalem , part of Palestine , as well as the Golan Heights which has been annexed from Syria . <p> All three territories are considered occupied under international law , with Israeli settlements in those territories also considered illegal . <p> The company was named in a United Nations database of companies helping to further settlement activity by the " provision of services and utilities supporting the maintenance and existence of settlements " and by " the use of natural resources , in particular water and land , for business purposes " . <p> The group also sells fuel to the Israeli military , which occupies the West Bank , East Jerusalem and the Golan Heights and has been accused of genocide by Amnesty International for its ongoing military campaign in the occupied Gaza Strip . <p> Norway 's largest pension fund , KLP , divested from Delek in 2021 due to its links to the settlements , as did the country 's sovereign wealth fund in 2023 . <p> Greenpeace Norway argues that Equinor has breached Norwegian law by failing to conduct proper due diligence or limit damage from its relationship with Delek Group under the 2022 Norwegian Transparency Act . The act requires Norwegian companies to ensure that their business operations and relationships respect basic human rights and are in line with international guidelines . <p> Frode Pleym , head of Greenpeace Norway commented : " While the Norwegian government is asking companies to end partnerships that contribute to the illegal settlements in Palestine , Equinor is prepared to direct millions to a company enabling and profiting from the war in Gaza . This has to be stopped . <p> " It 's the Norwegian government 's job to hold Equinor accountable and in line with Norwegian values . We are filing this complaint because they have failed to do this . " <p> Approached for comment , Equinor said it had no influence over the ownership structure of Ithaca Energy and had no business relationship with Delek Group . <p> It said the two partnerships it holds with Ithaca , Mariner and Rosebank are operated by Equinor based on its own management system , policies and procedures . <p> It said Equinor became a license partner with Ithaca as a result of Ithaca 's acquisition of Equinor 's former license partner Siccar Point Energy and that both the acquisition of Siccar Point Energy and the stock exchange listing the same year were vetted and approved by UK authorities . <p> A spokesperson said : " We have not seen any complaint from Greenpeace yet , so it is hard to comment in detail . Having said so , we do not believe we have breached the Norwegian Transparency Act . <p> " Should Equinor be contacted by the Norwegian Consumer Authority as suggested , we stand ready to respond to any questions as appropriate .
