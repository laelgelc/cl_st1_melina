<h> " When I meet other ambassadors , they ask me " why does Ireland hate Israel ? " <p> Israel 's move to close its embassy in Ireland means the diplomatic rift between the two countries is wider than ever , with potentially big implications for Ireland . <p> Israel has called Taoiseach Simon Harris , as well as the entire country , anti-Semitic . He , however is holding firm , and insists Ireland will not end its criticism of Israel 's actions in Gaza . <p> President Michael D Higgins has also responded , saying it is " deep slander " to accuse the Irish people of being anti-Semitic because of criticisms of the Israeli government . <p> Israel 's Ambassador to Ireland Dana Erlich has been at the forefront of her country 's criticism of Ireland and she spoke to Irish Times political editor Pat Leahy , who tells In the News what 's behind Israel 's move , why now -- and how damaging it is -- potentially -- for Ireland .
