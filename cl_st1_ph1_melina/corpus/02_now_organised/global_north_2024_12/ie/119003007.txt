<h> Editorial : Israel 's outrage at Ireland over Gaza war simply shows how insular its leaders have become <p> Israeli ambassador to Ireland , Dana Erlich <p> Editorial <p> Yesterday at 03:30 <p> For many , it may seem morally repugnant to be wasting time on diplomatic squabbles , when lives are being taken on a daily basis , for want of agreement . Such niceties of protocol and nuance will certainly be lost on people searching the rubble in Gaza for signs of life of loved ones , after more unrelenting shelling . <p> Against such an unchanging backdrop of apocalyptic scenes , it 's hard not to agree with Taoiseach Simon Harris 's assessment that Israel 's closure of its Dublin embassy is anything other than a " distraction " . <p> Far easier to spend time arguing over semantics , than to justify a cycle of civilian carnage that even the Israeli army has questioned , on the grounds that its military goals have been achieved in Gaza . <p> Ireland , like every other state , roundly condemns ? attacks on Israel . The taking of hostages , and the attacks by Hamas , were appalling acts . <p> But we have a voice , and therefore we have a responsibility not to be silenced when people are being killed on such an unprecedented scale , and all appeals for a ceasefire are dismissed . Figures show that 45,028 Palestinians have been killed in Gaza , and 106,900 injured , since October 7 last year . <p> A growing incitement and hatred towards anything Israeli <p> So taking all of the above into consideration , it was not surprising to hear Mr Harris say : " It is deeply regrettable that it ( Israel ) decided to shut its embassy . I do n't in any way minimise that decision , but it 's ? nothing in comparison to what 's happening in the Middle East , absolutely nothing . " <p> And her statement that " this is not criticism , but is just pure hatred and obsession that is now also directed to Israelis and the Jewish community " was both inflammatory and reckless . <p> Israel is at the centre of unprecedented international censure for its indiscriminate attacks . <p> Its complete resistance to a ceasefire while people are in dire need of emergency supplies to stave off famine is also a cause of global concern . <p> Instead of addressing these genuine humanitarian worries , Israel is deflecting attention from grave allegations of war crimes . It has decided to single out Ireland for daring to question the spiralling death toll . <p> Ms Erlich also attacked Ireland 's decision to intervene in South Africa 's case against Israel at the International Court of Justice . This , she said , is " an abuse of the international multilateral system by South Africa " . <p> But as T ? naiste Miche ? l Martin rightly said , this country 's position towards Israel has not been motivated by anything other than respect for international humanitarian law and human rights . <p> To claim that bringing allegations of crimes to the highest bar of international justice was " a hostile act " shows how insular Israel has become . <p> Instead of pointing fingers , it would far better serve the Israeli government -- and its ambassadors -- to concentrate all their energies on ending the war . ?
