<h> David Quinn : Why do anti-Israeli feelings run so deep in Ireland ? <p> A woman reacts as protesters rally in Tel Aviv , Israel , earlier this year - calling for the release of Israeli hostages held in Gaza since the deadly October 7 attack . Photo : Reuters/Hannah McKay <p> David Quinn <p> Today at 03:30 <p> Ireland is one of the most anti-Israel countries in Europe . Certainly , this is true of our political establishment , although I am not entirely sure how true it is of the general public . Yes , opinion polls show widespread opposition to Israeli actions in Gaza , and there is no doubt that a big majority of the public lean towards the Palestinians , not the Israelis , but how deep is that feeling really ? The war in Gaza barely registered with the public in the recent general election . <p> However , there is no question that anti-Israeli feeling runs very deep indeed in large parts of our political establishment , and not just in the left-wing parties . You can see it is in Fianna Fail as well , and to a lesser extent even in Fine Gael.
