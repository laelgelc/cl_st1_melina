<h> New Social Democrats TD Eoin Hayes refuses to confirm when he sold shares in firm supplying IDF <h> It emerged last week that Mr Hayes , the party 's newly elected TD in Dublin Bay South , worked for US firm Palantir for two years nearly eight years ago . He only sold his shares in the company this year . <p> Newly elected Social Democrats TD Eoin Hayes refused to confirm over two dozen times when he sold shares in a software company that supplies the Israeli Defence Forces . <p> It emerged last week that Mr Hayes , the party 's newly elected TD in Dublin Bay South , worked for US firm Palantir for two years nearly eight years ago . <p> However , he only sold his shares in the company this year . While refusing to confirm what month , he stated that it was done before he entered politics or when he was elected to Dublin City Council on June 7 2024 . <p> Palantir supplies technology to Israel and the IDF to assist in the ongoing war in Gaza . <p> On October 15 2023 , the company published a full-page advertisement in the New York Times reading , " Palantir stands with Israel . " <p> On October 6 2023 , the day before the Hamas strike on Israel , share prices in Palantir were $16.61 . On June 7 2024 , the day of the local elections when Mr Hayes won a Dublin City Council seat , they had risen to $23.31 . <p> As the Social Democrats gathered in Leinster House on Tuesday , Mr Hayes was asked over 30 questions about when he divested his shares . These included questions about whether or not he understood why the timing of the share sales was important , given that they had risen , and the price he sold them for . <p> He was also asked how much he earned from the divestment but refused to answer . <p> Mr Hayes said : " This is a company I worked for briefly eight years ago . There were shares granted as part of my compensation package . <p> " I have divested completely from the shares . I completely divested from them in the last 12 months . As soon as I became aware that Israel had very close relationships with that company , I divested from the shares . <p> " I divested from the shares entirely as soon as Palantir made very vocal support for the IDF . " <p> Mr Hayes later stated that the Occupied Territories Bill was the first bill he wanted passed in the new D ? il . <p> " The only reason you know about the shares is because I was transparent about them and I made them in those ethics declarations , " he continued . <p> " I 've been fully transparent . On top of that , I 've been fully supportive of the party 's position on Gaza and on Israel . " <p> It was pointed out to him that filling out ethics forms , including forms on share holdings , is a legal requirement for politicians . <p> Mr Hayes continuously said he had divested the shares " this year " and " before he entered politics " but refused to confirm what month . <p> The party 's deputy leader , Cian O'Callaghan , said on Tuesday , " Eoin 's been very clear " . <p> When Mr O'Callaghan was asked if he believed Mr Hayes should answer questions about when he divested the shares , he repeatedly said , " Eoin has answered your questions " . <p> Gary Gannon said Mr Hayes had done " exactly what we 're asking him to do , divest from Israeli companies " . <p> The back-and-forth between the media and Mr Hayes , Mr O'Callaghan and Mr Gannon lasted nearly 20 minutes before a press officer intervened to say it was " going around in circles , if you have any other topics " . <p> Join the Irish Mirror 's breaking news service on WhatsApp .
