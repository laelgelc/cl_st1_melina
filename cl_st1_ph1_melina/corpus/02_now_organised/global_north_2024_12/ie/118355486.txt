<h> Letters : Nostalgia for the past ignores dreadful treatment of women <p> A still from Ciaran Cassidy 's film ' Housewife of the Year ' <p> Carer Charlotte Fallon challenged Simon Harris in Kanturk , Co Cork <p> Letters to the editor <p> Sun 1 Dec 2024 at 03:30 <p> Sir -- Sometimes I look back with nostalgia on growing up in 1970s Ireland . It had two major benefits . First , there were no mobile phones , and , secondly , there were no concerns about global warming and the end of humanity . Yes , that was nice . <p> What frightened me most was the Troubles coming south , especially after the Dublin bombings which resulted in a wave of bomb scares that sent me running with my mother , one younger twin sibling in my arms , the other in hers . <p> Most of all I was afraid that the rumour spread by boys on our road that the IRA had hidden guns in our back gardens would turn out to be true . That kept me awake night after night as I waited for the banging on the front door by men in balaclavas returning to collect their stash . Every suburban road of a childhood has its own myth , and that was ours . <p> The recent discussions around Ciaran Cassidy 's poignant film , Housewife of the Year , have made me reflect once more on that era . <p> Setting aside my perception that we always got deep snow and warm summers , I remember the awful side . The misogyny that was deep-rooted , brazen and systemic . Just how far men 's legal power extended over women and girls was chilling . My relative was committed to a mental hospital for months by her husband with the co-operation of the local GP who drove her away in his own car . <p> She had been committed under the law known as the Dangerous Lunatic Law , that dates to 1838 and operated well into the 20th century . Among others who were regarded as problematic , it permitted a man to have his wife committed without any proper assessment by a medical team . <p> My relative was simply one of many housewives deemed to be unable to cope , an inability evident in her failure to run her husband 's household efficiently . <p> Cassidy 's film reminded me , so very starkly , of so much . I think I 'm losing my nostalgia -- and it feels good . <p> Anna Fitzgerald , author of Girl in the Making , Donnybrook , Dublin 4 <h> Carer Charlotte Fallon spoke truth to power <p> Sir -- Charlotte Fallon went shopping .
