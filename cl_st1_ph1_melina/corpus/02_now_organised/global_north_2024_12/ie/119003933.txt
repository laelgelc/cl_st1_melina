<h> Hundreds of Israeli students protest in Tel Aviv to demand prisoner swap <p> Protest was part of nationwide initiative that includes 200 schools across Israel . <p> Reuters <p> Protest against Israeli government 's management of the ongoing conflict in Gaza and to show support for the hostages , in Tel Aviv / Photo : Reuters <p> Hundreds of Israeli school students demonstrated in Tel Aviv on Wednesday to demand a prisoner swap deal with Palestinians . <p> According to the Israeli daily Haaretz , hundreds of students participated in a rally in Tel Aviv to call for a deal to guarantee the release of Israeli captives in Gaza . <p> The rally , which families of the hostages joined , was part of a nationwide initiative that includes 200 schools that plan to hold protests in several areas across Israel . <p> Israeli officials have reported progress in indirect negotiations to reach a Gaza cease-fire and prisoner exchange deal with Hamas . <p> The Palestinian resistance group said Tuesday that talks held in Qatar to reach a deal were " positive and serious . " <p> " Reaching a ceasefire and prisoner exchange agreement is possible if the ( Israeli ) occupation stops imposing new conditions , " it added in a brief statement . <p> Israel , which according to prisoners ' groups has more than 10,000 Palestinians in its prisons , estimates that there are around 100 Israeli captives in Gaza . Hamas has said that 33 captives had been killed in indiscriminate Israeli airstrikes in Gaza . <p> Mediation efforts led by the US , Egypt and Qatar to reach a cease-fire have so far failed due to Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's refusal to halt the war on Gaza . <p> Israel has continued a genocidal war on Gaza that has killed more than 45,000 people , most of them women and children , since an attack by Hamas on Oct. 7 , 2023 . <p> Last month , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defence Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza . <p> Israel is also facing a genocide case at the International Court of Justice for its actions in Gaza .
