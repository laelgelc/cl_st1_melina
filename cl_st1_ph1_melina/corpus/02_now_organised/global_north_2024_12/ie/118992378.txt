<h> Coosan ladies take the dip for UNICEF <p> A group of Athlone ladies is set to complete 12 dips in Coosan Point this December in aid of UNICEF . <p> The challenge comes as part of a fundraiser which has already raised a total of ? 1,545 from some 49 donations as of December 16 . <p> " We started three years ago - it 's a nice thing to do to get out during the winter when maybe you do n't want to ! " said group member Louise Galligan . " It 's good for the mind . " <p> Louise said the group consists of nine women , including both experienced dippers , newbies , and a 72-year-old woman who is joining the group for her second year . <p> " We have people who just joined us this year - it 's brilliant to have those new faces . " <p> The group chooses a charity that is close to its hearts every year , and this year members have chosen to donate funds to UNICEF . <p> " With everything going on in Gaza , we wanted to pick something that would make a difference on the ground . There are children living alone in tents with no food - and the next issue in Gaza is famine .
