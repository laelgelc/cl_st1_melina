<h> ' Dear Gary , this is senior hurlin ' , so do n't play the man ' -- an open letter to Social Democrats <p> It was the morning after the night before for the Social Democrats , who had just suspended their newly elected TD Eoin Hayes on foot of reporting by the Irish Daily Mail . I looked at my phone yesterday morning to see two missed calls from Gary Gannon , the party 's TD for Dublin Central . <p> It 's usually the other way around . Politicians are regularly left with a heap of notifications from journalists in a hurry to meet deadlines . Not this time . <p> I did n't need to ring him back to know what it was about . When I did , my suspicions were confirmed . <p> Gary Gannon was calling to apologise after he had directly called into question my journalistic integrity over my reporting Pic : Gareth Chaney/Collins Photos <p> Mr Hayes was dramatically suspended by the party on Tuesday after he lied to the media following the revelations in the Mail . <p> On Thursday last week ( December 5 ) , the Mail revealed that Mr Hayes had worked for Palantir -- a company that supplies artificial intelligence software to the IDF that has been used in the conflict in Gaza -- between 2015 and 2017 . <p> He continued to hold shares in the company until recently , but refused to say their value or precisely when they were sold , aside from repeatedly asserting that it was before his election to Dublin City Council in June . <p> In a quirky pre-election campaign video , Mr Hayes noted that as a small business owner , he knew ' what it means to make sure your investments pay off ' . <p> The Social Democrats have been to the fore of criticising the Government on their actions over Gaza . Most notably , party leader Holly Cairns said in November 2023 that ' words alone are not enough to convey our horror and outrage ' as she sought for Ireland to divest all economic ties with Israeli companies . Ms Cairns likened inaction to complicity in genocide . <p> The timeline of when exactly Mr Hayes disposed of these shares was important , for the reason that any time after the November 2023 comments would be politically hypocritical for him , and for his party . <p> This did n't seem to register with the Social Democrats , however . <p> On Thursday , the same day as our report , Mr Gannon appeared on the Echo Chamber podcast and was asked directly about my story on Mr Hayes 's links with Palantir . <p> That 's when Deputy Gannon decided to play the man , not the ball , lowering the blade to suggest that I was merely auditioning for a political adviser role in the government being currently formed . <p> Deputy Gannon said : ' I think there 's one particular journalist asking about Mr Hayes , his previous record ... I think what 's happening at the moment is that there 's a couple of journalists who realise there 's special adviser jobs coming up , and in other jobs you put in CVs , in these jobs you put in front pages . ' <p> Deputy Gannon went on to eulogise his Social Democrats colleague , who would soon be the cause of the biggest controversy the party has experienced in its short political life , saying that ' Eoin 's a great guy , he 's a really good guy ' . <p> Indeed , Mr Hayes 's biography on his own Twitter/X page describes him as ' that nice fella in the Soc Dems ' , if he does say so himself . <p> Any questioning about when this ' great guy ' disposed of his shares in a company that has helped the IDF in its campaign in Gaza would clearly not be tolerated , despite the Social Democrats making the Occupied Territories Bill an election issue when competing for votes on the left . <p> Just to make absolutely sure , Deputy Gannon returned to his theme : ' As I say , there 's special adviser jobs coming up and somehow this is how you flutter your eyelashes at them in this job . ' <p> Deputy Gannon decided against slandering all political journalists , however , saying : ' Not all them , just one or two . ' <p> The following day , last Friday , Social Democrats deputy leader Cian O'Callaghan said , in response to a question from me , that Mr Hayes disposed of his shares ' this year ' . Which is after November 2023 . <p> Which is after November 2023 . But why would they not say when exactly ? <p> There 's an axiom in politics -- and political journalism -- that you 're always more likely to be done for the cover-up than the actual original sin . <p> By Tuesday morning , the story had not gone away . At a Social Democrats press event to celebrate their electoral success and introduce the 11 TDs they had returned , Deputy Hayes refused on 23 occasions to say when he disposed of the shares or their monetary value . <p> What he did say , repeatedly , was that it was before he entered politics . This transpired to be completely untrue . <p> Around four hours passed between the farcical , agonising press conference at which Mr Hayes insisted , for 16 minutes , that he was being fully transparent while repeatedly dodging questions , and a party press release confirming this was not the case . Mr Hayes , we learned , had disposed of the shares only in July of this year and they were worth ? 199,000 . An embarrassment of riches , some might say . <p> Vindication is what I felt . On Morning Ireland yesterday , Irish Independent journalist Fionn ? n Sheahan raised Mr Gannon 's Echo Chamber comments and said that my integrity had been wrongly called into question , and that I was owed an apology . <p> That is why , when I saw the missed calls , I knew what Deputy Gannon wanted to talk about . <p> ' I 'm sorry , it was a poor comment and I want to retract it . I did n't realise what was there , ' he said . <p> A tough pill for him to swallow , no doubt , their election success well and truly deflated . <p> But it does beg a question beyond the actions of the now Independent Deputy Hayes . <p> The Social Democrats might not be used to handling controversies , but they are inevitable in politics . <p> This is senior hurlin ' now , folks . When legitimate questions are asked , they would be better served finding out the answers to them first , rather than insinuating there is some great conspiracy at play .
