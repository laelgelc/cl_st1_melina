Mr Hayes worked for the firm -- which lists the IDF and US intelligence agencies as its clients -- between 2015 and 2017 . <p> In November , while Mr Hayes was still a shareholder in the company , his party leader Holly Cairns was calling for economic sanctions against Israel . <p> Israeli businesswoman Orli Degani told the Mail that it was ' quite clear ' the same standards were not being applied across the party . <p> The Social Democrats have so far defended Mr Hayes 's actions and insisted he will be remaining in the party .
