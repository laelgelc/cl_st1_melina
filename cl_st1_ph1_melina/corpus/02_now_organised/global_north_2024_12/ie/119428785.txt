<h> Fourth baby dies of cold as thousands huddle in Gaza tents <p> Boy less than a month old found with his head as ' cold as ice ' as twin brother taken to intensive care <p> Imam Islam Abu Suaied prays over the bodies of two babies before their burial at the Al-Aqsa Martyrs Hospital in Gaza yesterday . Photo : AP <p> Wafaa Shurafa and Majdi Mohammed <p> Yesterday at 03:30 <p> A fourth infant has died of hypothermia in the Gaza Strip , where hundreds of thousands of Palestinians displaced by nearly 15 months of war huddle in tents along the rainy , windswept coast as winter arrives . <p> Jomaa al-Batran , 20 days old , was found with his head as " cold as ice " early yesterday , his father , Yehia , said . The baby 's twin brother , Ali , was moved to intensive care at Al-Aqsa Martyrs Hospital .
