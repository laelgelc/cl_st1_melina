<h> Humanitarian aid to north Gaza blocked for the past 66 days : UN <p> The UN Office said Israel has continued its siege on Beit Lahiya , Beit Hanoon and Jabaliya with Palestinians living there denied aid . <p> AA <p> 5,500 people were forcibly displaced from three schools in Beit Lahiya to Gaza City . / Photo : AA <p> Humanitarian aid to north Gaza , where Israel launched a ground offensive on October 6 , has largely been blocked for the past 66 days , the United Nations has said . <p> The offensive has left between 65,000 and 75,000 Palestinians without access to food , water , electricity or health care , according to the world body . <p> In the north , Israel has continued its siege on Beit Lahiya , Beit Hanoon and Jabaliya with Palestinians living there have been denied aid , the UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs , known as OCHA , said . <p> Recently , it said , about 5,500 people were forcibly displaced from three schools in Beit Lahiya to Gaza City . <p> Sigrid Kaag , the senior UN humanitarian and reconstruction coordinator for Gaza , told reporters after briefing the UN Security Council that civilians trying to survive in Gaza face an " utterly devastating situation . " <p> Kaag said she and other UN officials keep repeatedly asking Israel for access for convoys to north Gaza and elsewhere , to allow in commercial goods , to reopen the Rafah crossing from Egypt in the south , and to approve dual-use items . <p> Israel 's UN Mission said it had no comment on Kaag 's remarks . <p> The UN has established the logistics for an operation across Gaza , she said , but there is no substitute for political will that humanitarians do n't possess . <p> " Member states possess it , " Kaag said . And this is what she urged Security Council members and keeps urging the broader international community to press for -- the political will to address Gaza 's worsening humanitarian crisis .
