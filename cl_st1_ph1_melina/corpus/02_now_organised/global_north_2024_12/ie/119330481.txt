<h> Letters : That 4,645 of our children are in emergency shelter this Christmas is damning <p> Latest figures show 14,966 people are in emergency accommodation . Photo : Stock image <p> Letters to the editor <p> Thu 26 Dec 2024 at 03:30 <p> At least 4,645 children will be in emergency accommodation this Christmas in Ireland . That is witness to the failure by the State , which is among the wealthiest in the world . <p> Its persistence is causing long-term irreparable damage to these young lives , who can rightly claim that their human right to be treated in an equitable manner has been denied by an uncaring state . <p> We do n't need to bemoan that 2,000 years ago a pregnant woman , Mary of Nazareth , was denied appropriate accommodation in another land -- it is occurring before our very eyes on an unprecedented daily basis in 21st-century Ireland . <p> While political parties negotiate as to who will be in the next government , the focus over Christmas should be on reducing our out-of-control homelessness figures , which stands at 14,966 and which includes 4,645 children , according to recent figures . <p> The budget allocation for homeless accommodation for 2025 has risen to ? 303m , a 25pc increase from 2024 . <p> On top of this , we are accommodating 32,000 people in IPAS centres , costing the taxpayers ? 2.63m a day , with estimates for this year alone rising to ? 960m . <p> There are about 50,000 people in state-serviced accommodation at the moment with costs for private accommodation double that of state accommodation . <p> The numbers seeking asylum have risen to over 14,000 this year alone , putting an inevitable strain on the State -- that is , the taxpayers -- to accommodate them . <p> Then there is the issue of accommodating 100,000 Ukrainian refugees since 2022 . <p> The total cost this year alone for this could reach ? 1.49bn . <p> While more than 6,000 people were granted Irish citizenship at seven ceremonies held at the INEC in Killarney , Co Kerry , recently involving applicants from 140 countries , with over 30,000 citizenship decisions made in 2024 , up from 20,000 in 2023 , how , given the figures for homelessness , can we accommodate more refugees , asylum-seekers or international protection ( IP ) applicants when there is n't sufficient capacity to house those already in emergency accommodation ? <p> A small but noteworthy issue is the number of student deferrals , which has increased by 32pc on last year because of accommodation issues . <p> Then there is the renters , about 400,000 , unable to get on the housing ladder being charged exorbitant rents . <p> Who will grasp this thorny issue and which party in government will say we have over-extended ourselves and we will not be taking in any more refugees , asylum-seekers or IP applicants until we can properly house and accommodate our own citizens ? <p> Our open-door policy and our obligations have led us and other countries to this precipice . <p> Mahatma Ghandi said : " The greatest nations are defined by how they treat their weakest inhabitants . " In this case , it 's our homeless . <p> Christy Galligan , Letterkenny , Co Donegal <h> How can Verona Murphy speak for her people in her new ' neutral ' role as chair ? <p> When Verona Murphy ran in the recent general election , it was n't on the cards that she was going to be the Ceann Comhairle . <p> She very much ran on the premise of holding the Government to account , allied to delivering for her Wexford constituents . <p> It seems that some local Wexford media outlets have now been commenting along the lines that Ms Murphy as Ceann Comhairle has to be politically neutral and that she 's removed herself from fighting for the people of Wexford or holding the Government to account . <p> I found it intriguing to hear her assuring local radio in Wexford that being the Ceann Comhairle gives her great access to ministers . <p> The whole basis of being the Ceann Comhairle is that one is automatically re-elected after the next general election . <p> This is because one is taken off the pitch when one is in this role . <p> I do n't understand how these two things are going to work together where she serves as Ceann Comhairle while believing that she can serve her constituents and still be politically neutral . <p> I wish her the best of luck in her new role , however . <p> John O'Brien , Clonmel , Co Tipperary <h> With wars , Netanyahu just does n't have the time to attend Auschwitz event <p> Has Ms Toal forgotten that Benjamin Netanyahu , having defeated Hezbollah in Lebanon , is currently running a war on three fronts against Hamas , the Houthis and Iran ? <p> He is also in the thick of negotiations to release the 96 Israeli , Thai , English and American hostages still held by Hamas in Gaza .
