<h> Kildare art group hold raffle for Palestine <p> Kildare group , Irish Visual Artists for Gaza , are putting together a Christmas Bonanza draw to raise money for those experiencing the genocide in Palestine . <p> The draw features several leading Kildare artists on 18-19 December . The group has already raised more than ? 85,000 for life-saving aid . This Christmas , they aim to surpass ? 100,000 with the draw , featuring a range of prizes donated by artists from across Ireland . Among the artwork up for grabs are work by painter Ciara O'Neill , fashion designers Helen Steele and Claire Garvey , Laura Chambers Cashmere , The Costume Room Dublin , Peelo , Lillian Ecock , Mary Nagle , Rasher , Eoin O'Connor and Naas artist Niamh Moran with a stunning illustration entitled Moonlight at Sea . From Connemara to Lake Erie . <p> ' Little Chef ' Renad Ataullah from Gaza by Anne-Marie Farrell . <p> The group began earlier this year when Celbridge trio , art teacher Dee Conneely , HR professional Aoife Neasy , and human rights worker Leigh Brady , came together to organise art draws for families displaced in Palestine . <p> Speaking about the initiative , Dee said : " I 've always believed in the power of art to connect and inspire .
