<h> Iranian ambassador denies coup rumours in Damascus <p> Iranian ambassador denies coup rumours in Damascus / bne IntelliNews <p> By bne Tehran and Baghdad bureausDecember 1 , 2024 <p> Iran 's Ambassador to Syria , Hossein Akbari , has categorically dismissed rumours of a military coup in Damascus and an attempt to detain Syrian President Bashar al-Assad , Shafaq news reported on December 1 . <p> Recent reports on social media suggest that Syrian State Security Director General Hosam Louqa , who leads a group of Syrian Army soldiers , is planning a coup against Assad and an attempt to capture the president and his family . <p> The reports come amid the rise of armed rebel groups supposedly backed by Turkey that have seized control of large parts of Aleppo . <p> Akbari said reports about a coup published by some Iranian online platforms had surprised them and stressed that " the Syrian army knows what needs to be done . " <p> " This sedition will be crushed , God willing , " he said . <p> The Syrian Army General Command had previously rejected rumours of a coup in a statement , describing them as part of a wider media campaign orchestrated by " armed terrorist organisations . " The Command accused opposition forces of disseminating " false news " aimed at undermining the morale of Syrian civilians and soldiers . <p> " The counterterrorism operation is progressing successfully , " the Command declared , revealing plans for a counteroffensive to reclaim lost territories . <p> The Iranian ambassador also suggested a connection between the movements of opposition groups in Syria and what he called Israel 's " recent losses in Lebanon " . <p> Israel has recently reached a ceasefire agreement with the Hezbollah in Lebanon , which halted over a year of cross-border clashes . <p> Iran described the agreement as a victory for Hezbollah and a defeat for Israel . <p> The ceasefire , brokered by the United States and France , took effect on November 27 , right before the rebels ' rise in Syria . Iranian officials have linked the attack to Israel , claiming it has been backing the takfiri terrorist groups in Syria . <p> Akbari also claimed the militants are enjoying international support , including the provision of advanced military equipment linked to European countries . <p> This is while Hayat Tahrir al-Sham ( HTS ) , which leads the offensive in western Aleppo , has been designated as a terrorist organisation by both the United States and European countries . <p> Iran has been a crucial ally of the Syrian government since the civil war began in 2011 and has supported the government of Assad by both deploying members of the Islamic Revolution Guard Corps ( IRGC ) and backing allied militias such as Hezbollah . <p> Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Aragchi called the revival of fighting in northern Syria the result of a " US-Israeli project " , noting that developments followed the Israeli regime 's defeats in Lebanon and Palestinian territories . <p> Iran 's chief diplomat made this statement during a phone conversation with his Syrian counterpart Bassam al-Sabbagh on November 30 , reports Iranian channel Press TV . <p> Major General Salami , Commander-in-Chief of Iran 's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps ( IRGC ) , declared in a message that the recent attacks in Syria are being directed by forces recently defeated in Gaza and Lebanon . <h> Reports <h> Cookies on the bne IntelliNews website <p> This site uses cookies - small text files that are placed on your machine to help the site provide a better user experience .
