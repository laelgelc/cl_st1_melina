<p> A new trailer for The Bibi Files , the upcoming Alexis Bloom documentary focusing on Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's corruption trial , has been released , with the film set to hit Irish cinemas this Friday , December 13 . <p> Described as a " searing documentary focused on high-level familial corruption , and the disastrous lengths a political leader will go to escape accountability , " and " a film about a man singularly focused on his own political survival in a region beset by war , " The Bibi Files is produced by Oscar winner Alex Gibney ( Taxi to the Dark Side ) . <p> It features never-before-seen footage police interrogation videos of Netanyahu , his family , his closest associates , and benefactors as part of a corruption investigation . <p> Watch the trailer below : <p> " The leaked footage indicates a steady and continuous pattern of lavish gift-giving and quid pro quo favours in the media and business worlds , " the film 's official description says . " The interrogation videos are interwoven with archival footage that show Netanyahu 's rise to power , and how his nearly 30-year rule as Prime Minister has divided the state of Israel . <h> Advertisement <p> " Interspersed throughout are interviews with prominent journalists , " it continues , " Israeli statesmen , Netanyahu associates , and longtime friends , whose observations and anecdotes paint a chilling portrait of a leader who , in his effort to avoid justice has allied himself with radical right-wing extremists . <p> " Netanyahu 's fixation on his entitlement to remain Prime Minister has , in this way , led to political decisions that have endangered Israel 's safety , and destroyed her standing as a democracy in the Middle East . " <p> The announcement comes as Netanyahu today began testifying in his long-running corruption trial in Tel Aviv , as Israel continues its attacks on Gaza and contending with the fall of Bashar Assad in Syria . <p> Premiering at the Toronto Film Festival earlier this year , the film will be made available in UK and Irish cinemas and on demand from December 13.
