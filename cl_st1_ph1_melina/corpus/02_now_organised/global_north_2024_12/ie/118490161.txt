Pic : Bohemian FC <p> After the previous collaboration highlighted and raised funds to combat homelessness in Ireland with Focus Ireland , the new team-up will support Medical Aid for Palestinians . <p> The jersey , which features a striking neon blue/purple look , was designed by Fontaines guitarist Carlos O'Connell in conjunction with the League of Ireland club 's designers . <p> Fans will have the unique opportunity to be the first to buy the shirts alongside some exclusive items ahead of their pair of sold-out 3Arena shows this weekend . <p> The new jersey will serve as Bohemians ' third/alternative jerseys next season . Pic : Bohemian FC <p> A merch stall will be in operation at the halfway line of Dalymount Park and manned by Bohs member Pierce Callaghan , drummer of fast-rising post-punk Dublin band Gurriers . <p> Jerseys will be available both days alongside very limited edition Fontaines D.C. scarfs ( only available at Dalymount and one per person ) , while each shirt purchased comes with a free limited edition Fontaines D.C. badge . <p> In a press released , the club added : ' The collaboration brings together two of people 's greatest loves -- music and sport -- to support those affected by the crisis in Gaza . <p> Funds from the jersey will go help support Medical Aid for Palestinians .
