<h> 2024 in pictures : Politics frames the debate as the country went to the polls <p> Voters in Ireland were busy in 2024 , casting their ballot in five different polls -- for two referendums in March , local and European ballots in June and a general election in November . <p> The Assembly returned in the North after a long hiatus and a Westminster election also took place . <p> The country 's political comings and goings were further punctuated by an emotional announcement in March from Leo Varadkar that he was resigning as taoiseach and Fine Gael leader .
