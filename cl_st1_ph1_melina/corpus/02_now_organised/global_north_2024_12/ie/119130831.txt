<p> Tr ? caire has issued a stark warning over the humanitarian crisis in war-torn Sudan as over 25 million people -- or half of the population -- are currently facing the real risk of starvation . <p> The warning comes as the agency urged people in Galway to support its Christmas Appeal for ' Children in Conflict ' . <p> Tr ? caire 's CEO Caoimhe de Barra described it as ' a crisis of historic proportions ' . <p> " The world has neglected the people of Sudan over the past 20 months , " she said . <p> " More than 14 million people have had to flee their homes because of the conflict with little to no access to food , shelter and basic necessities . Approximately one in seven of the world 's internally displaced people are Sudanese . " <p> David O'Hare who works for Tr ? caire has recently returned from Sudan . <p> " I have worked in the humanitarian sector for nearly twenty years and yet I am still finding it difficult to comprehend the scale of this catastrophe , " he said . <p> " This has been recognised as the greatest humanitarian crisis in the world today . Millions of people are on the move with nothing -- no food , no water and no possessions . Millions of women and children are at particular risk in this situation from malnutrition and from violence , " he added . <p> Mr O'Hare said that the humanitarian crisis was particularly severe in the Nuba Mountains in South Kordofan . <p> " Firstly , the conflict has resulted in so many people fleeing to the region because of the fighting elsewhere in the country , " he said . <p> " Secondly , the last harvest was very poor because of a lack of rain and a plague of locusts . This has meant there has been no surplus or safety net for people here who have shared what they have with the newcomers and that has exacerbated the situation . " <p> Many of those who have been forced to flee have ended up in camps where conditions are dire with a lack of food and healthcare . <p> " I visited several camps and heard first-hand of the awful journeys people had to make to get there , " he revealed . <p> " One mother , Madina , was forced to walk for six days with her seven children to reach Thobo IDP camp . They had no food and had to eat leaves and grasses to stay alive . <p> " Her son Muriambi was extremely malnourished . He was treated in the nearby Tr ? caire clinic . When I met the little boy I would have guessed he was three years of age . He is nine . Severe malnutrition over a prolonged period of time had stunted his growth . <p> " However , the Tr ? caire staff told me his health has vastly improved since he first arrived . Unfortunately , there are so many women with experiences similar to Madina 's in the Nuba Mountains , " David said . <p> In South Kordofan and Blue Nile State , Tr ? caire is providing support for 15 rural health centres and eleven nutrition outreach sites focusing on : primary health care services ; maternal and child health ; immunisation ; malaria prevention , control and treatment ; nutrition treatment for children under five ; and capacity strengthening to partner staff , communities , and local authorities . Tr ? caire is also providing emergency food aid and medical care in the camps . <p> " We have witnessed this crisis unfold from an opportunity for democratic reform , to a battle for power with little regard for those in its path , " said Caoimhe de Barra . <p> " This is a man-made crisis , today , in real-time , forgotten , ignored even . Nothing will echo louder now for the people of Sudan than the silencing of the guns and the world 's haste in providing them support . Help delayed is help denied , " she added . <p> " It 's not just Sudan where children are suffering the effects of conflict . From Gaza to Ukraine , from Ethiopia to South Sudan , children are living in conflict zones . In many cases they have no food , no safe water to drink , no shelter , " she continued . <p> " We are asking people in Galway , if they can , to donate to our Christmas Appeal which will provide support for children in these terrible conditions as well as supporting critical work across Tr ? caire 's global programmes . <p> Donations can be made at www.trocaire.org or by calling 1800 408 408 . <p> Pictured : Muriambi was treated for severe malnutrition in the Tr ? caire clinic in Thobo .
