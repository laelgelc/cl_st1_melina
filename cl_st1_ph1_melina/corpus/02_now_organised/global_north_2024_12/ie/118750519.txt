<h> Hunger for Justice candlelit vigils in Mayo tomorrow <h> Image 1 of 2 <h> Image 2 of 2 <p> On Thursday , December 12 , more than 1,200 people across Ireland will be engaging in a 24-hour fast in solidarity with the people of Palestine and to raise funds for UNRWA - to provide essential life-saving provisions for the people of Gaza . <p> At least 17,000 of the more than 45,000 Palestinians who have been killed by Israeli forces are children ( many believe this is a very huge underestimation ) . The Lancet British medical journal places the number of people killed in Gaza at almost 200,000 , to include the thousands who remain unclaimed and unreachable under the rubble of Gaza . People in Northern Gaza are being systematically starved and ethnically cleansed . The government of Ireland , along with many more states and agencies across the globe , have named this onslaught on the Palestinian population as genocide . <p> Mayo IPSC is asking people in Mayo to show solidarity and empathy with the people of Palestine and to join them in two candlelit vigils taking place this Thursday , December 12 , at 5.30 p.m. , in Castlebar at Market Square and Westport , at the Octagon . <p> Published:Wed 11 Dec 2024 , 9:14 PM <p> Our Connaught Telegraph compact edition is out every Tuesday with the latest news , sport and entertainment in Castlebar and Mayo .
