<h> Singer Avraham Tal performs in honor of ZAKA at Jpost Gala <h> " You are so brave for the things that you do " . <p> Jerusalem PostConferences Avraham Tal invites ZAKA CEO Dubi Weissenstern and volunteer Simcha Greinman , Deputy Commander of ZAKA for the city of Modi'in to the stage at the Jpost Gala in Miami ( photo credit : Elliot La-Mer - DEMAGIC ) <p> Avraham Tal , the noted Israeli singer , musician , and musical producer , performed at the Gala event on Monday evening , launching the 2024 Jerusalem Post Summit . During the performance , Tal invited ZAKA CEO Dubi Weissenstern and volunteer Simcha Greinman , Deputy Commander of ZAKA for the city of Modi'in , to the stage to honor the organization for their work and sang the song " Shema Yisrael , " which he composed in the first days of the war with David Broza and Mika Ben Shaul . Said Weissenstern , " Singing helps us and gives us great strength . " <p> Greinman recounted ZAKA 's work in the Gaza communities , during which volunteers spent months searching house by house for materials that needed to be buried according to Jewish law . <p> " One of the families called me and said , ' We survived , and the mezuzot certainly survived as well . ' " He then displayed a mezuzah that he had saved from the destruction in Gaza and said , <p> " This is a mezuzah that I was able to take with me to show in front of the world , to speak about the atrocities , and tell about what happened to the Jewish people . "
