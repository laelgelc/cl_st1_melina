" <p> After four years in his post , Casey resigned in July over what he described as the US government 's unwavering support for Israel despite its devastating military campaign in the Gaza Strip . <p> His resignation -- first reported by The Guardian newspaper this week -- is the latest by a US official angered over President Joe Biden 's staunch military and diplomatic backing for Israel since the Gaza war began in October 2023 . <p> The conflict has plunged Gaza into a dire humanitarian crisis , and United Nations experts and leading human rights groups have accused the Israeli military of committing war crimes , including genocide . <p> The US has said it is working to secure a ceasefire and the release of captives held in Gaza . It also said it has pushed Israel to minimise civilian casualties in its military operations . <p> But Biden has refused to use American assistance to Israel as leverage to end the war , rebuffing calls to suspend US weapons transfers to the top ally . <p> The US provides Israel with at least $3.8bn in military assistance annually , and researchers at Brown University recently estimated that the Biden administration provided an additional $17.9bn since the start of the Gaza war . <p> In an interview with Al Jazeera on Saturday , Casey said his job in Jerusalem was primarily focused on writing about the situation in Gaza , from humanitarian concerns to economic and political issues . <p> He said the US government is aware of the dire conditions in the territory , including the widespread casualties and lack of humanitarian assistance . " And yet we continue to pursue these policies and support what the Israeli military is doing there , " he said . <p> " They receive all of our reporting , they have everything we write , and they just disregard it . " <p> Asked why US government policy is what it is , Casey said he believes part of the reason is that " there 's no concern for Palestinian suffering " . <p> Advertisement <p> " We ignore Palestinian suffering .
