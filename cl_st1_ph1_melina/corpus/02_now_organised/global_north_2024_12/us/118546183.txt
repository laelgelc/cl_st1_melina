<h> Are we your perfect victims now ? <p> Nahed Elrayes is a Palestinian writer , composer , and Development Manager at UNRWA USA <p> Published On 6 Dec 20246 Dec 2024 <p> Chef Mahmoud Almadhoun , 33 , is survived by his wife , Alaa , and their seven children - the youngest of whom is a baby girl named Aline , who was born just a few weeks ago GazaSoupKitchen via Instagram <p> It feels strange to grieve Chef Mahmoud . <p> There is something about mourning a stranger that condemns their real self to obscurity . I hesitate to grieve a brother I never had , a laugh I never heard , secrets I never learned , arguments and breakfasts we never shared . I hesitate to grieve the handshake I can never give him , for feeding thousands who may not survive , in a place I may never see again . <p> I hesitate , even as the deceased faced a cruel death , a death only possible in Gaza . I hesitate , even as I know his loved ones . Even knowing he honoured my family by name , even as I remember the times his brother 's eyes glimmered when he spoke of their work in Gaza 's North . <p> But when all is said and done , we know how this ends . He dies unjustly , and like a genetic imprint , we feel some burn of an old scalpel in our chest , the burn of a question , The Palestinian Question . <p> " How can I make this victim a hero ? No no ... how can I make this hero a victim ? <p> Can we do both ? " <p> As if it 's the English language that decides . <p> Advertisement <p> * <p> To a child , a " hero " wears a mask . But Chef Mahmoud had no mask . His face was shown from the start . His family was exposed from the start . They still are . <p> To an adult , a " hero " wears an army uniform and takes lives . Chef Mahmoud did nothing but save lives . He had no defence of camouflage , not even a rifle . His only weapon was the ladle in his hand -- and that weapon saved thousands . <p> How can anyone but a hero outdo the courage of all these characters -- fictional or not -- better able to defend themselves ? Will the world ever understand the sheer humanity of such a person ? Is it lost in translation ? <p> It feels like the world has lost fluency in the language that Palestine is most fluent in : the language of deeds . That language which Mahmoud Almadhoun left his mark on with the richest poetry . <p> There is the burn again . I feel the weight of my youth wasted outside Palestine , away from men no nation but Palestine could produce . Away from families who , under the crushing weight of an inevitable famine , say , " No thank you . We are too creative to starve , too upright to uproot . " <p> Or " you can hold a rifle to my head and strip me to my underwear , but I promise , you will never find what you are looking for . You will never incapacitate the heart that beats for Gaza . You can not kidnap it into cowardice , impoverish it into dependency , or parch it into silence . I will stay here , forever . " <p> And there he stayed . Forever . <p> * <p> It feels strange to grieve Chef Mahmoud . <p> At first , I wanted these words to exalt him . I thought that was why my chest burned . But it still burns , and now I realise : none of this exaltation is for me , for the Almadhoun family , or even for us Palestinians . No . I am actually assembling these English words to appeal to the humanity of our colonisers by proxy , like a space capsule sent out in the hopes of finding extraterrestrial life . <p> Advertisement <p> I suddenly realise the importance of refining my tone , and keeping any emotional asides within neat parentheses , ten words or less . ( I hate the fact that we have to die . I hate the fact that we know exactly who will die and how , that we predict this based on the boundless moral depravity of a genocidal colonising force that kills food workers , that slaughters our families one by one like it is nothing . I hate that I have to be articulate and proofread this , in case some in inconsistency in my writing flow fails to convince the reader that genocide is worth stopping . ) I look at the time . <p> It is 3am , and after four hours pretending this kind man 's death is not devastating me , pretending through words , I finally understand what the burn is . We Palestinians know who we are . We know what Israel is . But what remains is for the world to see it . <p> As a world , you told us to endure the worst terrors and humiliations of the occupation , without turning to violence . <p> You told us to teach our children love and science , even if Israel has bombed every school . <p> You told us to sing and smile and cook through our suffering . <p> You told us not to be beggars , nor to starve in silence . <p> You told us to resist , but without any weapons . <p> To count on your " eyes " to defend us . <p> Chef Mahmoud did all of those things . And was assassinated by drone strike . <p> Are we your perfect victims now ? <p> The views expressed in this article are the author 's own and do not necessarily reflect Al Jazeera 's editorial stance .
