<p> His sons , ages 19 and 15 , made it onto the inlet 's rocks , one on Avon 's L jetty and one across the inlet on the Belmar side , police reports say , and Selmi described . <p> Salem Salah , who many called by the Arabic nickname Fadel , lived with his family for many years in Newark , where he also worked as a mechanic . His son said they moved to Pottstown about five years ago for better schools and healthcare , and less violence . <p> Now , Selmi , 21 , is running the shop , Amana Auto Repair , along with his 19-year-old brother -- who almost drowned -- and doing his best to support his family , which is a household of 10 . <p> His father 's loss has not only devastated him and their family , but gone are his father 's decades in the auto repair business , which Selmi was learning . It 's been a bit tough filling his father 's shoes , like dealing with bills and the non-auto side of the business , but he 's proud to have stepped up . <p> " I have to , I have to provide for my family , " he said during a recent phone interview from the shop , as he worked from the shop and clangs rang out in the background . <p> His father loved to fish , and usually went to lakes and fished from boats , Selmi said . In September , his younger brothers had been begging him to go to the ocean to fish . <p> Selmi did not make the trip . From his family 's accounts , and the police report , which NJ Advance Media obtained through a public records request , this is what occurred : <p> The family was at the L jetty , which is a popular beach for gathering and fishing . The ocean was rough that day , due to offshore weather . <p> Another family was present too , his father 's best friend , which Selmi described as so close they call him his uncle , and his children their cousins . <p> His uncle went to pray and was gone for five minutes when Selmi 's brothers started struggling in the water , at about 6:30 p.m . Selmi said his brothers were cooling off in a calm area near the L jetty , with little waves . <p> The L jetty is unique , as part of it runs perpendicular to the coast , acting as a breakwater and apparent protection for swimmers . However , Selmi said his brothers were eventually swept out and around the jetty . <p> Salah entered the water after them . <p> Moments later , his uncle returned to chaos , and " people screaming and yelling , " Selmi said . <p> A 911 call had reported three swimmers in distress , and a police officer arrived and found one was climbing back onto the L jetty . Police dispatched the ANSWER team , a joint , volunteer water rescue organization . <p> The other two -- Salah and a son -- were being carried south by the current , toward the opening of the inlet . <p> Three rescue swimmers entered the water as police officers signaled them to the victims , and they reached Salah as he was submerged , police said . The other son was swept into the rocks , and to eventual safety . <p> Officers and rescuers pulled Salah onto the rocks on the Avon side and performed CPR . <p> Selmi said his mother watched in horror as everything unfolded . <p> Rescuers put Salah into an ambulance , and all the family members in police cars and headed to Jersey Shore University Medical Center in Neptune , where Salah was pronounced dead . <p> Family friends helped Selmi and his mother lay Salah to rest in New Jersey , his son said . He hopes to one day raise some extra funds and get him a nice headstone . <p> But for now , he 's focusing on keeping the repair shop going and helping to care for his siblings . <p> Selmi said 2024 has been " heavy . " <p> The family is Palestinian and originally from Gaza . <p> Selmi said he was in Gaza last October with his newborn daughter when the war broke out with Israel . He literally had rockets landing around him , saw death often , and suddenly found himself trying to flee a war zone . <p> His father saved him in a way , Selmi said . <p> Salah contacted U.S. State Department officials , " over and over and over , " and worked to get Selmi 's name on a list that allowed him to cross the border into Israel , and to back to Pennsylvania . <p> " He did it , " Selmi said . " My dad got me out . " <p> " This year has been like the craziest year in my life , " he said . <p> Salem Salah , shown in a family photo , died while trying to save his sons in the ocean at the Jersey Shore in September 2024 .
