<p> The head of IDF Military Intelligence , Major-General Shlomi Binder , and the head of the Military Intelligence Directorate , Maj.-Gen . Oded Basiuk presented the IDF General Staff 's investigation into the October 7 massacre to Defense Minister Israel Katz , Israeli media reported on Friday . <p> The IDF presented two investigations on Friday . The investigations deal with key events , gaps that were discovered , and initial conclusions for the purpose of drawing lessons and implementing them immediately . <p> One of them is an intelligence investigation into how Hamas prepared for the war , and it is expected to serve as evidence for understanding the failures of Military Intelligence . <p> The military operation that led to the discovery of the bodies of the six in a tunnel under Tel Sultan , Gaza , is called " The Nutcracker , " and it also included the rescue of Farhan Kadi , a hostage who survived captivity . Herzi Halevi . <h> Return of six dead hostages <p> The IDF referred to the incident as difficult and painful due to not being able to return the hostages . It also emphasized that there was no prior knowledge that the six were being held there . <p> According to the IDF , its military actions in Tel Sultan were sufficient . However , the military concluded that it was nevertheless likely that Israel 's operational activity did lead the Hamas terrorists guarding the hostages to execute them . <p> IDF sources said that although the military had believed there might be hostages in the general area , at the time in late August when they were maneuvering in the area , the chances of hostages being there had dropped significantly . <p> Due to this , the IDF was being more careful with its operations , including by using special forces , so as not to endanger hostages .
