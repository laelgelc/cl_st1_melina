<h> A tiny fintech stock is up 4,700% in 2 days after taking a stake in a company involved with Israel 's Iron Dome missile system <p> Angle down iconAn icon in the shape of an angle pointing down.Israel 's Iron Dome missile defense system fires interceptors at rockets launched from the Gaza Strip . Tsafrir Abayov/AP <p> Nukkleus stock surged over 4,700% in two days after it said it bought a stake in a defense firm . <p> The fintech acquired 51% of Star 26 Capital , which owns a controlling stake in a supplier to Israel 's Iron Dome missile system . <p> Nukkleus 's market value rose from $3 million to $125 million after the deal . <p> A little-known fintech company 's stock has soared more than 4,700% over the past two days after it announced a deal to acquire a controlling stake in a firm with ties to Israel 's Iron Dome missile defense system . <p> Advertisement <p> Before Tuesday , Nukkleus was a fintech company focused on the digital asset space . According to its website , its mission was to " aggregate an industry-leading ecosystem of disruptive blockchain and digital finance brands . " <p> But late Tuesday , the company announced that it acquired a 51% stake in Star 26 Capital , a defense acquisition company that held a 95% ownership stake in a firm called RIMON . <p> Advertisement <p> " RIMON is a leading Israeli supplier of components for the Iron Dome missile defense system , as well as other defense and tactical solutions , " Nukkleus said in its new release . <p> Israel 's Iron Dome system has long been viewed as an engineering marvel and has seen heavy use in the last year amid missile attacks by Hamas and Iran . <p> According to Nukkleus , the transaction was finalized on December 15 . The deal included cash , a promissory note , and common stocks and warrants valued at about $26 million , according to a filing made with the SEC . <p> Advertisement <p> The agreement also included an option agreement allowing Nukkleus to acquire the remaining equity stake in Star 26 at a later date . <p> Manny Shalom , Nukkleus 's newly appointed CEO , said that its new ownership stake in RIMON " strengthens Nukkleus 's capabilities in delivering innovative defense solutions . " <p> RIMON has two business lines : it sells generators , masts , and lighting solutions , and develops tactical vehicles for special defense forces . <p> Advertisement <p> Before the deal , Nukkleus had generated $0 in revenue in the trailing twelve months and had a market valuation of about $3 million . As of Wednesday afternoon , the company had a market valuation of about $125 million . <p> Nukkleus stock price soared from $1.39 before the deal was announced to as high as $67.77 on Wednesday , representing a jump of about 4,775% . <p> Representatives for Nukkleus did not respond to a request for comment on the deal .
