<h> Ambassador Gilad Cohens 's Hanukkah ceremony in Tokyo unites diplomacy and hope as Japan reaffirms support for Israel during wartime . <p> Ambassador to Japan Gilad Cohen hosted a ceremony on Thursday to light the second candle of Hanukkah . <p> Cohen hosted senior members of the Japanese Parliament , community leaders , prominent business figures , and cultural icons . <p> Israel and Japan have marked 72 years of diplomatic relations , with ties only deepening during the Israel-Hamas war as Japan has consistently expressed its commitment to fostering peace and stability in the region . <p> " This year , with 100 hostages still held in Gaza , we fervently pray for their swift release , " Cohen said . <p> " We are grateful to the Shalva organization for their unwavering efforts to advocate for the rights of people with disabilities , amplifying the voices of children with disabilities in Japan and sharing their painful experiences following the events of October 7 . " Israel 's Ambassador to Japan hosts a candle lighting ceremony in honor of Hanukkah. ( credit : ISRAELI EMBASSY IN JAPAN ) <h> Last year , the embassy hosted hostage families <p> This year 's event featured an art exhibition by the children of the Shalva Center -- an organization that provides services and support for people with disabilities -- many of whom have been displaced due to the war . <p> " The art of Shalva 's children is a vivid reminder that even in the darkest moments , beauty , creativity , and light can emerge , " Ambassador Cohen said .
