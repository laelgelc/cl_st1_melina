<h> In an additional incident , a soldier from the 931st Battalion , Nahal Brigade , was critically wounded during combat . <p> St.-Sgt . Yuval Shoham was killed in battle while fighting in the Gaza Strip , the IDF announced on Sunday evening . <p> St.-Sgt . Shoham , 22 , from Jerusalem , served in the 9th Battalion in the 401st Brigade . <p> He fell in the northern Gaza Strip . <p> Furthermore , a soldier from the 931st Battalion , Nahal Brigade , was critically wounded during combat in an additional incident within the northern Gaza Strip , the IDF added . <p> Israel 's military said that the soldier had been evacuated to a hospital to receive medical treatment . IDF soldiers in northern Gaza Strip conducting operation in the area of the Indonesian Hospital , where a Hamas launch site was located . ( credit : IDF SPOKESMAN 'S UNIT ) <p> According to the IDF 's tally , the death of Shoham raises the total of soldiers killed on or since October 7 of last year to 824 . <p> Some 392 of this number were killed since the start of the military 's ground operations in the Strip on October 27.
