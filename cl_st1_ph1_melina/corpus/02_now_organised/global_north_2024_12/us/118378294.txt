<p> It is unclear if or when Israel will respond to the Yemen Houthi ballistic missile attack on Israel on Sunday , sources have told or indicated to The Jerusalem Post . <p> The ballistic missile was shot down outside of Israeli territory and no Israelis were killed or wounded directly from the missile , but some shrapnel did land in certain central areas and a small number of persons got injured rushing for shelter . <p> The missile set off warning sirens for almost all of the Center since it was unclear exactly where it might hit if it had penetrated the country 's air defense . <p> Because no one was killed or wounded ; because Israel is trying to wrap up a ceasefire with Hezbollah and is then hoping to wrap up a deal with Hamas ; because Yemen is more than 1,800 km. away ; and because some of Israel 's grand strategy for the region is waiting for US President-elect Donald Trump to enter the White House on January 20 , there are no signs whatsoever to date of an Israeli response . <p> If Yemen does not attack again , Israel might choose not to respond at all.Sirens across central Israel following missile launch from Yemen , 1 December 2024 ( credit : Tzofar ) <h> Compare and contrast <p> Unlike the other two instances when the Houthis attacked in July and September and top Israeli officials quickly vowed a response , here officials were largely silent . <p> In contrast , almost every significant official commented on the need to respond harshly to two mortars Hezbollah fired into the Golan Heights on Monday , which objectively speaking were far less threatening . <p> Moreover , the cost of shooting down ballistic missiles can be as high as $2 million to $3m. per interceptor when the Arrow 2 or 3 is used , given that the Iron Dome can not shoot down ballistic missiles . <p> On September 29 , the IDF undertook a massive strike against the Houthis , one that greatly exceeded the massive strike on Hodeidah in July , sources told the Post ahead of an IDF announcement about the attack . <p> Dozens of Israeli aircraft , including F-15I fighter planes , participated in the operation , striking 1,800 km. from Israeli territory after the Houthis fired three ballistic missiles on the Tel Aviv and central Israel areas earlier in September . <p> The IDF noted that the Houthis have cooperated with Iraqi militias , who are Iranian proxies , to attack . <p> Pressed , multiple military sources implied in September that Israel was still trying to avoid directly striking these militias . This is done to prevent unnecessarily complicating the situation in that country for the US , which Jerusalem hopes can maintain its influence there . <p> The military said it was impressive that the air force had managed such a large and complex operation while also attacking Israel 's adversaries in Lebanon , Gaza , the West Bank , and elsewhere -- all in the last 16 hours . <p> At the time , IAF Commander Maj.-Gen . Tomer Bar , IDF Chief of Staff Lt.-Gen . Herzi Halevi , and defense minister Yoav Gallant all congratulated the pilots and vowed that Israel 's long arm would strike any enemies who continued to harass the Jewish state . <p> The terror group has launched many ballistic missiles and drones at Eilat . In July , a drone from the Houthis killed a civilian in Tel Aviv . <p> This led to the first Israeli airstrikes in July targeting oil-refining facilities in Hodeidah , as well as Yemeni air force assets , to disrupt the transport of Iranian weapons to Yemen . Reports indicate that those strikes resulted in the deaths or injuries of dozens of people . <p> Until July , the IDF had outsourced responses to the Houthis to the US , which was fighting the group over various maritime aggression issues . However , after the Houthis killed a civilian in Tel Aviv , the Jewish state struck back directly for the first time .
