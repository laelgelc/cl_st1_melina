If a traveler wants to remain on longer , they must request an extension of stay at Israel 's Population and Immigration Authority.Ben Gurion Airport ( credit : YONATAN SINDEL/FLASH90 ) <h> The application process <p> The application for an ETA-IL permit is done online . Applicants must first provide personal details , including a full name , date of birth , and nationality . <p> Next , travel information , including the reason for the visits and the dates of stay , must be given . <p> This will be followed by a request for the applicant 's passport information and a clear photo . <p> Visitors apply for the ETA-IL several weeks before their planned trip to Israel and must have had a valid passport for at least six months . <p> The permit does not automatically guarantee entry . It allows a passenger to reach the border crossing .
