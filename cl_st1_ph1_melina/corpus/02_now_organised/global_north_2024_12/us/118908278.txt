<p> Israel 's Foreign Minister Gideon Sa'ar announced the closure of the country 's embassy in Ireland on Sunday , accusing Ireland of " extreme anti-Israel policies . " <p> " The actions and antisemitic rhetoric used by Ireland against Israel are rooted in the delegitimization and demonization of the Jewish state , along with double standards . Ireland has crossed every red line in its relations with Israel , " Sa'ar said , according to a foreign ministry statement . <p> " Israel will invest its resources in advancing bilateral relations with countries worldwide according to priorities that also take into account the attitudes and actions of these states toward Israel , " he added . <p> The statement pointed to action taken by the Irish government , including Ireland 's decision to recognize a Palestinian state , and its support for South Africa 's legal action against Israel in the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) . <p> Yair Lapid , Israel 's opposition leader , criticized the move in a post on X , calling it a " victory for anti-Semitism and anti-Israel organizations .
