<h> US says it conducted strikes against Houthi targets in Yemen 's capital <p> US strikes on Sanaa come amid recent series of attacks between the Yemeni rebel group and the Israeli military . <p> Smoke rises from the site of strikes in Sanaa , Yemen , on October 4 , 2024 Khaled Abdullah/Reuters <p> Published On 21 Dec 202421 Dec 2024 <p> The United States military says it has conducted air strikes against targets linked to the Houthi rebels in Yemen 's capital Sanaa , including a missile storage facility and a " command-and-control " site . <p> US Central Command ( CENTCOM ) , which oversees US Army operations in the Middle East , said on Saturday that the strikes aimed " to disrupt and degrade Houthi operations " . <p> The Iran-allied group has previously launched attacks on US Navy and merchant vessels in the Red Sea , Bab al-Mandeb and Gulf of Aden , CENTCOM said in a social media post . <p> The US strikes come amid an uptick in attacks between the Houthis and the Israeli military this week . <p> Israel bombed several targets in Yemen on Thursday , including power stations near Sanaa . <p> The Israeli bombardment , which killed at least nine people , followed a missile launch by the Houthis , formally known as Ansar Allah , towards Tel Aviv . <p> Local emergency services said 16 people were " mildly injured " in the incident . <p> The Houthis have been targeting Israel with drones and missiles to pressure the US ally to end its war in Gaza , where the US-backed Israeli military has killed more than 45,000 people . <p> The Yemeni rebels also have been carrying out attacks on shipping lanes in and around the Red Sea as part of the same campaign , which they say is in support of Palestinians . <p> For months , the US and the United Kingdom have been bombing Houthi targets in Yemen in response to the Red Sea assaults . <p> The administration of US President Joe Biden has also imposed sanctions against the Houthis . <p> On Thursday , Washington sanctioned the governor of the central bank in Houthi-controlled Sanaa and several Houthi officials and associated companies , accusing them of helping the group acquire " dual-use and weapons components " .
