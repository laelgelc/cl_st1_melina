<h> Bringing tequila to the Negev : Leitner 's agave dream in Israel <p> DO N'T BE fooled ! This is not Mexico but Kibbutz Alumim in the Western Negev. ( photo credit : Avi Leitner ) <p> I met Avi Leitner at Cafeneto in Ra'anana . I was expecting some slickly dressed entrepreneurial whiz kid , bristling with overconfidence and well-rehearsed marketing platitudes . <p> He walked right past me when he came in , and I did n't give him a second look . However , when we finally hooked up , he was the opposite of what I had been expecting . <p> He was like any unassuming New York lawyer and I thought , " What a nice guy , " but I did not detect passion , drive , or the vision of a dreamer . <p> However , although there is no lack of Jewish New York lawyers who have made aliyah , only one has planted blue agave in the Negev in order to make an Israeli expression of a tequila-like alcoholic beverage.Yet again I learned you should not judge a book by its cover . As Leitner told me his story in a slightly self-deprecating , laid-back way , I was gradually struck by everything I missed . The three D 's -- determination , drive , and a dream -- were there all right but slightly camouflaged . AVI LEITNER is the kid with a crazy dream . ( credit : Avi Leitner ) <p> When I came to Israel , the wineries made the main spirits . Carmel Mizrahi made a product from imported molasses called Tehila , which was a poor imitation of tequila . <p> They also had products like Captain Rum and Lord Gin . You get the idea and can imagine the quality . All these disappeared when the import gates opened and the big global brands arrived . <p> When that happened , the feinschmeckers chose Scotch whisky , and the prize for volume went to vodka . <p> It was only in the last 15 years that a local artisan distillery trend began but on a very small scale . Julius Distillery led the way , followed by the likes of Golani , Pelter , M&amp;H , and Yerushalmi . <p> The one that reached the furthest in international circles is the M&amp;H Whisky Distillery , which has brought Israeli whisky to the world . <p> The spirit of choice in the Jewish world is whisky , especially Scotch malt whisky ( without an " e " ) as opposed to American , Canadian , or Irish whiskey ( with an " e " ) . <p> The rise of Kiddush clubs brought the new passion for whisky into the synagogue , and the growing number of whisky mavens would love talking about it , comparing notes almost as much as drinking . Part of the reason for its prominence was , in my opinion , because whisky was regarded as " ' safe treif . " THE PINA , or heart of the blue agave , resembles a pineapple. ( credit : Avi Leitner ) <p> So when Avi Leitner was invited for a tipple on a visit to Mexico , he asked for a whisky . His hosts sniggered among themselves , and he was told in no uncertain terms : " Well , here , we drink tequila . " <p> Thus this New Yorker was introduced to a whole new world . He remembers tequila as something you drank at a high school party to get drunk . <p> After his introduction to the quality and variety of tequila , he described it as every bit as complex and interesting as Scotch . <h> What is tequila ? <p> TEQUILA IS a spirit made in Mexico from the blue agave plant . It is made from the pina , the heart of the plant that looks a little like a pineapple . <p> It is cooked to break it down , crushed to make a mash , fermented , and then distilled twice . Pot stills give a more characterful expression ; continuous stills produce a more commercial , parve result . <p> Tequila is part of the mezcal family . In the same way as cognac is a brandy but not all brandy is cognac , tequila is a mezcal but not all mezcal is tequila . Emily Price , writing in Forbes , described the differences this way : " Tequila is the refined cousin who always follows the rules ; the mezcal is the artsy sibling who is always experimenting . " The product once did not have a good image . I asked someone what he knew about tequila , and he immediately responded : " Yuk , is that the one with a worm in it ? " In fact , the dreaded worm does not appear in tequila . If one appears at all , it will be in a cheap bottle of mezcal . ERAN BRAVERMAN is the person responsible for planting and caring for the blue agave. ( credit : Avi Leitner ) <p> In popular culture , the way to drink tequila in a bar was immortalized in films . It was served in a shot glass with salt and a slice of lime , and required a " lick , sip , and suck " in swift succession . I first became aware of tequila in the cocktail Tequila Sunrise . When I took my first steps in the drinks industry in Britain , every cocktail list seemed to have it . <p> The mixture of tequila , orange juice , and grenadine was striking to look at , particularly in a tall highball glass , hence its popularity . <p> These days , tequila has a more regal image . Perhaps it is most famous internationally as the integral part of the Margarita cocktail . <p> Along with a Negroni and Bloody Mary , it is my favorite cocktail . It is made from tequila , the orange-flavored Triple Sec , and freshly squeezed lime juice , and served with salt around the rim of the glass . <p> It has its own glass , which is a variation of the old champagne coupe ( a broad , flat , shallow glass ) . Though invented in the 1930s , the Margarita has stood the test of time and is usually part of even the shortest cocktail menu . It is zingy and refreshing . <p> A more recent trend asserts that quality tequila is far more rewarding and may be enjoyed differently . The dictionary of tequila includes words such as " joven ; " " bianco , " or " silver " ( young ) ; " reposado " ( aged in oak casks ) ; " anejo " ( barrel aged for a minimum of one year ) ; and " extra anejo " ( aged in casks for at least three years ) . The connoisseur will most likely nurse it in a brandy balloon , neat or on the rocks , and sip it slowly with occasional gentle swirling to release aromas . <p> WHEN LEITNER began to appreciate the nuances of tequila , he could not help noticing the conditions in which blue agave is grown . <p> He was not an entrepreneur , agriculturist , or even in the drinks business , yet in the spark of an idea , he became all three . <p> Of course , he had to have exhaustive patience and reserves of determination he did not know he possessed . <p> The Israeli bureaucracy stood in his way , but if Leitner could not get in the door , he would come down the chimney . <p> The Agriculture Ministry prohibited him from bringing in the plants , so he brought in cell tissue , grew them in a greenhouse for a year , and only then had the plants in hand . He also had to find someone who wanted to grow it . <p> He found willing partners in Kibbutz Alumim in the Western Negev . In Eran Braverman , he discovered a farmer who would have the same passion and eagerness to learn . <h> Dedicated to doing it right <p> They wanted to do it right . Taking advice from Mexican consultants at every step , he utilized Braverman 's skills as a farmer and the supportive commitment of the partners at the kibbutz . <p> They combined Israeli agri-tech and Zionist ingenuity with the natural attributes of the Negev Desert to plant 35 hectares of blue agave in the Negev . The next objective is to build a distillery . <p> Leitner does not just want to grow something unique in Israel , he wants to produce the first authentic tequila-style mezcal -- homegrown , distilled , and matured in the Holy Land . <p> However , he ca n't call it tequila ! That has been a challenge . Imagine the M&amp;H Distillery having to market its world-class product without using the word " whisky . " <p> In the meantime , Leitner has gotten closer to the spirits industry , speaking to all the Israeli players . NEWLY PLANTED young blue agave plants taking root in the Negev. ( credit : Avi Leitner ) <p> He became an investor in the Yerushalmi Distillery , which produces whisky . A good example for him is the very successful Golani Distillery at Katzrin . Leitner describes owner Davi Zibell as a " mentor , consultant , and model " . <p> The principle of investing in agriculture in the Negev began with David Ben-Gurion , who planted agave plants ( though not the blue agave ) at Moshav Gilat in the Negev in the 1950s . <p> Their purpose was to make rope , but when synthetic fibers came to be used , the plants were just left as a memorial to what seemed a good idea at the time . <p> Nineteen Thai and Nepalese workers were murdered in cold blood by the brutal terrorists . Two were kidnapped to Gaza ; one was released , but the other is still there , captive in Sinwar 's tunnels . <p> The plight of the foreign workers is sometimes forgotten because of the sheer mass of the horrors , but they came to work hard , with a smile , showing great loyalty , and were not part of any local dispute . <p> They just got caught up in the nightmare . It showed the brutality of Hamas that they were not spared . <p> The memory of each one of them is a blessing . May their families know no more sorrow . <p> Leitner 's initiative offers hope and support , as well as future tourism and employment in a region so scarred by the most unbelievable and unfathomable expression of evil in our times . <p> Leitner , president of the Blue Agave Israel Group , and his partner , Avi Rosenfeld , have done minimal public relations , preferring to wait until there is a product to show . <p> Leitner is not into gimmicks and does not have to resort to them because he will be the first . Recently , he took a group of journalists and industry influencers to see the Alumim fields growing row upon row of blue agave -- an amazing and impressive sight . <p> In Israel , good ideas are copied , and success can have many fathers . Imitation is the sincerest form of flattery . <p> Do you remember how many frozen yogurt stores and sushi restaurants instantly opened when they first came to Israel ? Well , another company has been formed called Negave Estates . Note the play on words between " Negev " and " agave . " <p> It has done the opposite to Leitner , immediately marketing at the outset and outlining its vision . <p> It has a slick website , full of promises . It will be making its first product distilled in tequila country in Mexico but will mature it in Israel ( like Thinkers Distillery ) . <p> But as of yet , the company is still at the very earliest stage , so even if the vision is exciting , the marketing noise may be premature . However , we will be watching it closely , too . <p> There is a place for everyone ( as is the case with the plethora of new Israeli whiskies ) , and one start-up will help the other . <p> Leitner made aliyah in 1991 and is now 60 . Talking to him , I realized that gloss and marketing slogans do not make authenticity . <p> He is totally authentic and is on a journey that he has forged on his own . <p> When Leonard Cohen was in his mid-70s , the legendary singer/songwriter looked back and described himself at 60 as " just a kid with a crazy dream . " That fits .
