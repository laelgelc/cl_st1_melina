" <h> What is polio ? <h> Polio outbreak in Gaza : new vaccination campaign <p> A polio case was reported in Gaza for the first time in 25 years . A 10-month-old boy contracted the disease , and is now paralyzed in the leg . The U.N . health agency is launching a vaccination campaign on Sunday . The goal is to vaccinate 640,000 Palestinian children against polio . Dr. Peter Chin-Hong , infectious disease expert , joins LiveNOW from FOX to explain the dangers of the disease , and the importance of the vaccinations . <p> Polio is a highly infectious disease , mostly affecting young children . It attacks the nervous system and can lead to spinal and respiratory paralysis , and in some cases death , according to the World Health Organization . <p> A major outbreak in New York City in 1916 killed more than 2,000 people . The nation 's worst outbreak in 1952 claimed more than 3,000 lives . <p> Many who survived the disease were left with lifelong impacts and needed leg braces , crutches or wheelchairs . Some had to use the iron lung , an artificial respirator designed to treat polio patients . <p> By 1950 , polio was killing or paralyzing more than 500,000 people a year . <p> Dr. Jonas Salk created the first successful polio vaccine in 1953 , eventually leading to mass vaccination campaigns around the world . Although polio has not been completely eradicated , cases have decreased by more than 99% , according to the U.S. Center for Disease Control and Prevention . <h> RFK 's anti-vaccine views <p> Kennedy has long advanced the debunked idea that vaccines cause autism . He has also pushed other conspiracy theories , such as that COVID-19 could have been " ethnically targeted " to spare Ashkenazi Jews and Chinese people , comments he later said were taken out of context . He has repeatedly brought up the Holocaust when discussing vaccines and public health mandates . <p> Kennedy has insisted he is not anti-vaccine , saying he only wants vaccines to be rigorously tested , but he also has shown opposition to a wide range of immunizations . Kennedy said in a 2023 podcast interview that " There 's no vaccine that is safe and effective . " In a 2021 podcast he urged people to " resist " CDC guidelines on when kids should get vaccines . <p> No medical intervention is risk-free . But doctors and researchers have proven that risks from disease are generally far greater than the risks from vaccines . <p> If confirmed by the Senate , Kennedy will control the world 's largest public health agency , and its $1.7 trillion budget . <h> The Source <p> This report includes information from the office of Sen.
