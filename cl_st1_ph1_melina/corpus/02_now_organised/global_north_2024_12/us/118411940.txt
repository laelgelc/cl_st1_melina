<h> 8 children wounded in Gaza 's ongoing war arrive in Chicago to cheers <h> The children , between 2 and 14 years old , were evacuated from Gaza to Jordan after suffering extensive injuries caused by airstrikes in the ongoing war . Two will remain in Chicago for medical care , and six will go elsewhere in the country . <p> Ibrahim , 14 , arrived Monday at O'Hare Airport . He lost an arm in a bombing in Gaza and will undergo medical treatment at Shriners Children 's Hospital Chicago . He was one of eight children who arrived in Chicago on a flight from Jordan . Six will go on to other U.S. cities for medical care . <p> After a more than 12-hour journey , eight children who were wounded in the war in Gaza were welcomed to Chicago on Monday afternoon . <p> " It 's a bittersweet feeling , " said Sarah Alrayyes , director of chapter operations for the Palestinian Children 's Relief Fund . " As excited and happy as we are to welcome them here , I know they are coming here with a lot of trauma . They are carrying a heavy load on their shoulders . " <p> The children , who were between 2 and 14 years old , were evacuated from Gaza to Jordan after suffering extensive injuries caused by airstrikes in the ongoing war . With help from the relief fund , the children were approved for medical evacuation that would allow them to receive treatment in the U.S. <p> A large crowd of doctors , volunteers and families welcomed the children at the arrival gate at O'Hare Airport . <p> Six of the children will be stopping over in Chicago on their way to hospitals elsewhere . For two teenagers , Chicago will be home for the next few months . <p> Eight children from Gaza , ranging in age from 2 to 14 , were evacuated from Gaza to Jordan after suffering extensive injuries caused by airstrikes in the ongoing war . <p> Tyler Pasciak LaRiviere/Sun-Times <p> Ibrahim and Jana , both 14 , each lost an arm in explosions from Israeli airstrikes . They , along with their guardians , will be staying with a host family while they receive ongoing treatment at Shriners Children 's Hospital . The last names of the patients and their parents and guardians were not disclosed for their safety . <p> " We 're excited to meet them , " said Badel Almoshelli , who will be hosting Ibrahim and his mother , Amani . " We are glad to be able to help in any way we can given all the tragic events that are going on . " <p> In November 2023 , Ibrahim and his family were sheltering at a school that was bombed . Ibrahim lost his hand in the explosion , but after making it to a nearby hospital , doctors had to amputate most of his arm . The blast also left him with internal injuries that caused the loss of his left kidney . <p> Since then , Ibrahim and his mother have not been able to reunite with the rest of their family . Ibrahim 's father and six siblings remain in Gaza . <p> " I left my other kids behind , but I 'm glad to be here , and I hope that my son gets the treatment that he needs , " Amani said through a translator <p> Jana had gone with her mother to visit her grandparents when an airstrike leveled a nearby home . She suffered injuries to both legs and had to have an arm amputated . <p> A crowd greets a group of young Palestinian children and teens from Gaza as they arrived Monday at O'Hare Airport . <p> Tyler Pasciak LaRiviere/Sun-Times <p> Jana was accompanied to Chicago by her aunt , Islam , after her mother 's request to travel was denied by the Israeli military . <p> Both Ibrahim and Jana hope to receive prosthetic limbs to help them function independently . <p> All the patients will remain in the U.S. until their treatments are fully completed and hope to be reunited with their families . <p> " These children do n't want to leave Gaza , but they have to leave to receive this medical treatment , " said Tareq Hailat , director of global patient affairs with the Palestinian Children 's Relief Fund . " They 're glad to be getting help , but their minds are constantly back home with their families . "
