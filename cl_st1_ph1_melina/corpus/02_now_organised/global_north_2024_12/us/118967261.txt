<h> Feds charge a Natick man , and an Iranian man , in connection to fatal drone strike abroad <p> U.S. Attorney for Massachusetts Joshua Levy speaks during a December press conference . ( Jesse Costa/WBUR ) <p> Federal officials announced charges on Monday against two men for violating U.S. export laws to supply Iran with materials for a drone strike that killed U.S. service members in Jordan earlier this year . <p> U.S. Attorney for Massachusetts Joshua Levy detailed charges against Mahdi Mohammad Sadeghi , a naturalized U.S. citizen living in Natick , and and Mohammad Abedininajafabadi , a Iranian citizen who was arrested in Milan , Italy . <p> Levy said that since 2016 , the two conspired to violate American export laws by giving sensitive American technologies to Iran . Sadeghi works for a U.S. semiconductor company based in Massachusetts . Abedininajafabadi is chief executive of a Tehran-based company with ties to the Iranian military , government and the IRGC , which the U.S. considers a terrorist organization . <p> Both are charged with conspiring to violate U.S. export laws ; Abedininajafabadi was also charged with conspiracy to provide material support to a terrorist organization . <p> Federal officials are seeking to extradite Abedininajafabadi from Italy to face charges in the U.S. <p> " As alleged in this criminal complaint , the grave potential damage from the leak of American technology overseas came to fruition , " Levy said at the Moakley Federal Courthouse in Boston Monday evening . <p> Abedininajafabadi 's company allegedly manufactured the navigation system used in the drone attack . <p> Sadeghi has been an employee at Analog Devices Inc . ( ADI ) in Wilmington since 2020 , according to a LinkedIn profile with his name . When asked about its employee and the allegations , the company issued a statement to WBUR : <p> " ADI takes its compliance obligations and role in national security very seriously , " stated Ferda Millan , of its external communications . " We have cooperated fully with federal law enforcement and will continue to do so throughout the proceedings . ADI is committed to preventing unauthorized access to and misuse of our products and technology . " <p> According to ADI 's website , Sadeghi received his Ph.D . in electrical engineering from the University of Michigan , Ann Arbor , in 2014 . His thesis involved unmanned air vehicles . <p> Advertisement <p> The LinkedIn profile shows a Sadeghi with the title of technology group product marketing manager , who focuses on the design , fabrication and validation of micro electronic mechanical systems . <p> The drone attack in northeastern Jordan on the military outpost known as Tower 22 in January 2024 killed three U.S. soldiers and injured more than 47 service members . <p> U.S. officials blamed the attack on the Islamic Resistance in Iraq , an umbrella group of Iran-backed militias that includes Kataib Hezbollah . <p> The one-way attack drone may have been mistaken for a U.S. drone that was expected to return back to the logistics base about the same time and was not shot down . Instead , it crashed into living quarters , killing the three soldiers and injuring more than 40 . <p> Tower 22 held about 350 U.S. military personnel at the time . It is strategically located between Jordan and Syria , only 6 miles from the Iraqi border , and in the months just after Hamas ' Oct. 7 attack on Israel , and Israel 's blistering response in Gaza , Iranian-backed militias intensified their attacks on U.S. military locations in the region . <p> Following the attack , the U.S. launched a huge counterstrike against 85 sites in Iraq and Syria used by Iran 's Revolutionary Guard and Iranian-backed militia and bolstered Tower 22 's defenses . <p> With reporting by WBUR 's Amy Gorel and Christine Willmsen , and The Associated Press <p> Editor 's note : a previous version of this story used a shortened version of Abedininajafabadi 's name listed by the Department of Justice .
