The WhatsApp group was centrally featured in the prosecution 's statements during the trial . <p> As the trial of several people accused of being involved in the riots that rocked Amsterdam last month started on Wednesday , a WhatsApp group emerged as one of the main vectors that spread calls for violence . <p> The group , known as " Buurthuis 2 " ( Community Center 2 ) , originally called " Free Palstine , " sic was one of the main sources of calls for violence against Maccabi Tel Aviv fans and Israelis in the city , according to reports from Het Parool . <p> The defendants in the trial are accused of various crimes ranging from incitement to manslaughter . <p> The prosecution is sourcing much of the evidence from the group , in which members posted messages organizing people to confront Maccabi fans . <p> Someone calling themselves " Abu Obaida , " the nom de guerre of Hamas 's spokesperson , called for members to do something against the Maccabi fans .
