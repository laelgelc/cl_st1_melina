<p> Progressive Jewish group Taste of Olam Haba and Little Rock Peace for Palestine , according to their Instagram accounts , disrupted the lighting of a Lubavitch of Arkansas public Hanukkiah because of the involvement of Huckabee , who is set to become US ambassador to Israel . <p> The activists carried poles with candles on the end , and chanted slogans throughout the lighting event . According to photographs published by the groups , some wore sweaters calling for " ceasefire now " in Gaza . <p> " Christian Zionists and their agenda have no place in our Jewish communities and in our government . As Mike Huckabee , the US President-Elect Donald Trump appointee as future Israeli ambassador , addressed our city , we stood united in rejecting his message of division . Instead , we spoke for unity , justice , and equality for all people between the river and the sea , " the two activist groups said on social media .
