<h> Management received hundreds of nominations from staffers passionate about celebrating colleagues <p> FOX News Media CEO Suzanne Scott and President &amp; Executive Editor Jay Wallace announced the recipients of the 2024 FOX News Media Spotlight Awards last Wednesday during an end-of-year town hall for employees . <p> The FOX News Media Spotlight Awards are an annual tradition for the company and management received hundreds of nominations from staff members passionate about celebrating colleagues who earned special recognition . <h> Unsung Hero Award <p> The Unsung Hero Award goes to employees who consistently go above and beyond with work that is vital to FOX News Media but often goes unnoticed . Three winners were selected and Hemmer joked he would continue his tradition of referring to winners as " Unsung " when he sees them around the office . <p> " Three of our absolutely critical and beloved members of this community , " MacCallum said . <p> Director of technical operations Latoya Lewis <p> Director of technical operations Latoya Lewis . <p> Lewis is known to " own " the control room and is clear , communicative and in command whenever breaking news occurs . Her attention to detail helps the entire department , and she 's also involved with the FOX News Media Career Launch Program . <p> Director of technical operations Alex Mitchner <p> Director of technical operations Alex Mitchner . <p> Mitchner " believes in people who believe in themselves , " according to colleagues . She 's known to provide opportunities for colleagues that they might not have found on their own and often mentors less experienced staffers . Mitchner has also played a key role in helping the team adapt to new technology . <p> Broadcast engineering supervisor Jason Oster <p> Broadcast engineering supervisor Jason Oster . <p> Oster received a " ridiculous number of nominations " because he often solves problems before anyone else realizes there was even an issue . The tireless professional plays a key role in keeping FOX News Media networks up and running . He 's the longest-serving winner , having started at Fox News in 1999 . <h> Rising Star Award <p> The Rising Star Award goes to employees who have shown exemplary work ethic and growth during the early stages of their careers . Four winners were selected and Baier promptly joked they would eventually be running the company . <p> " Congratulations to our rising stars , " Baier said . <p> Broadcast production engineer Frank Andriola <p> Andriola was nominated 11 times , indicating he is highly respected by his colleagues while helping maintain FOX News Media infrastructure . He makes it his mission to learn everything about new technologies and then teach it to others . <p> Fogliano started at the Washington , D.C. , assignment desk as a freelance guest greeter and quickly made a name for herself , pitching segments and booking guests . Within a few months , she was on the road for political coverage and is now a booker on " America 's Newsroom . " <p> Associate producer Meghan Tome <p> Associate producer Meghan Tome . <p> Tome has learned to manage multiple reporters across multiple live shots and picked up the job so quickly that managers have to keep finding new challenges for the rising star . She often shows up early , stays late and volunteers to help the Capitol Hill team on her days off . <p> Human Resources coordinator Jordan Wells <p> Human Resources coordinator Jordan Wells . <p> Wells is proactive and creative about finding ways to support her colleagues . She is constantly volunteering for projects and has developed a reputation as someone always willing to help . <h> Community Service Award <p> This award goes to an employee that has dedicated time and energy to his or her local community and is most active in company volunteer efforts . Three winners were selected this year . <p> " Thank you for your service to your community , " Perino said . <p> Senior manager of engineering Richard Hernandez <p> Hernandez has collected and delivered new clothes and toys to children living in orphanages in several Mexican cities . He 's created an annual toy drive at the L.A. Bureau , where his colleagues are inspired by his generosity . Thanks to Hernandez , kids ranging from age three to 17 get gifts each Christmas . <p> " Varney and Co. " senior producer Robert McNally <p> Senior producer Robert McNally . <p> McNally has been a member of the Coast Guard Auxiliary since 2012 . He is also a member of the Honor Guard and Color Guard , conducting ceremonies at events , participating in parades and supporting veterans ' funerals . <p> Overton coded a new system to manage all the editorial , graphics , guest bookings and other data that FOX Weather relies on every moment of every day . He has streamlined operations and earned the " Terminator " inspired nickname " Skynet . " <h> The Impact Award <p> The Impact Award goes to a person or department that significantly and positively impacted FOX News Media over the past year . Two winners were selected . <p> Scott said both groups have had an " incredible impact " on FOX News Media in 2024 . <p> " For more than a year now , these six amazing people have been the core of a team that has told a story of a nation at war . They have brought us constant updates on the hostages , taken us deep into the tunnels under Gaza and reported on the ongoing humanitarian crisis , " Scott said . <p> " And they did it all while living in a war zone , with their homes , their lives , and their loved ones all under constant threat , " Scott added . " This is their daily life , journalism during wartimes . We are so grateful for their work .
