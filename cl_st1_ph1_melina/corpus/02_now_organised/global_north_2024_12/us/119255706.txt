<h> John Bolton : Trump second term poses ' much higher chance ' of major international crisis <h> Share this : <p> In this file photo , U.S. President Donald Trump , left , is flanked by national security adviser John Bolton , right during a press conference at NATO headquarters in Brussels , Belgium , Thursday , July 12 , 2018 ( AP Photo/Markus Schreiber ) <p> The prominent foreign policy hawk said Trump 's lack of focus and inability to stick to policy decisions could trigger an overseas disaster . <p> " The risk of an international crisis ... is much more likely in a second Trump term , " Bolton said in a Christmas Eve interview with the Guardian . " Given Trump 's inability to focus on coherent decision making , I 'm very worried about about how that might look . " <p> Bolton , who served for 17 months in the first Trump administration , scoffed at Trump 's vow to quickly end conflicts in Ukraine and Gaza . <p> " ( Putin ) believes he knows how to play Trump , that Trump 's an easy mark , " Bolton said . " If you do n't understand how the person you 're talking about on the other side views you , that 's a real lack of situational awareness that can only cause trouble . " <p> Bolton admitted that he wrongly believed Trump would become a more disciplined leader after he was first elected to the White House in 2016 . <p> Those hopes were dashed once Bolton started working alongside Trump and observed him firsthand . <p> " He does n't know much about foreign policy . He 's not a big reader , " Bolton said . " He does n't think these facts are important . He thinks he looks the other guy across the table in the eye and they make a deal and that 's what 's important . " <p> During the presidential campaign , Bolton said he could n't support Trump but refused to endorse Kamala Harris either . <p> Despite the harsh criticism , Bolton praised Trump 's picks of Sen. Marco Rubio to be Secretary of State and Rep. Mike Waltz for national security adviser . He slammed ex-Rep . Tulsi Gabbard , Trump 's nominee for director of national intelligence , as " really dangerous . " <p> Bolton has been isolated in Republican power circles as Trump has pushed the party far away from his muscular foreign policy views . <p> But Bolton predicted that the GOP as a whole will eventually return to its hawkish roots . <p> " Trump is an aberration and , when he leaves the political scene , the party will snap back , " Bolton said .
