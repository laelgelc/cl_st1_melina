<p> The Consulate General of Israel in Atlanta has created a new initiative in which participants can add names and photos of those still held captive in Gaza to create unique ornaments to raise awareness of and call for the release of the hostages . <p> " As Christmas approached this year , a time when families come together , we wanted to use the opportunity to raise awareness to the fact that there are still 100 hostages , among them seven Americans , who should also be home with their families " , Anat Sultan-Dadon , Consul General of Israel to the Southeastern United States , told The Jerusalem Post . <p> The consulate has reached out to several prominent churches in Atlanta who have agreed to display the special ornaments on their Christmas trees . In addition , they produced an explanatory sheet through which individuals can also print the ornaments and hang them on their Christmas trees . <h> Details of the ornaments <p> The ornaments include a photo of each of the 100 hostages still held captive by Hamas , which will be hung on the Christmas tree using a yellow ribbon associated with the struggle for their release . <p> " The First Baptist Church of Atlanta , a mega church , was the first to agree to the initiative , " Anat Sultan-Dadon told the Post .
