Zarif on Nov. 9 called on Donald Trump to reassess his stance on Iran . <p> Share <p> Iran urges Trump to change ' maximum pressure ' policy <p> share <p> TEHRAN , IRAN -- <p> Iran signaled an openness toward Donald Trump Saturday , calling on the president-elect to adopt new policies toward it after Washington accused Tehran of involvement in a plot to kill him . <p> Vice President for Strategic Affairs Mohammad Javad Zarif urged Trump to reassess the policy of " maximum pressure " he employed against the Islamic republic during his first term . <p> " Trump must show that he is not following the wrong policies of the past , " Zarif told reporters . <p> His remarks came after the United States accused Iran of conspiring to assassinate Trump . <p> The Foreign Ministry described the American accusations Saturday as " totally unfounded . " <p> Tehran and Washington severed diplomatic relations shortly after the Islamic revolution in 1979 . <p> 2015 nuclear power accord <p> Zarif , a veteran diplomat who previously served as foreign minister , helped to seal the 2015 nuclear accord between Iran and Western powers , including the United States . <p> However , the deal was torpedoed in 2018 after the United States unilaterally withdrew from the deal under Trump , who later reimposed sanctions on Tehran . <p> In response , Iran rolled back its obligations under the deal and has since enriched uranium up to 60% , just 30 percentage points lower than nuclear-grade uranium . <p> Tehran has repeatedly denied Western accusations that it seeks to develop a nuclear weapon . <p> Zarif said Saturday that it was Trump 's political approach toward Iran that led to the surge in enrichment levels . <p> " He must have realized that the maximum pressure policy that he initiated caused Iran 's enrichment to reach 60% from 3.5% and increased its centrifuges , " he said . <p> Provocations <p> " As a man of calculation , he should do the math and see what the advantages and disadvantages of this policy have been and whether he wants to continue or change this harmful policy , " Zarif said . <p> In December 2017 , Trump recognized Jerusalem as Israel 's capital and the following year moved the American embassy there . <p> Trump also recognized Israeli sovereignty over the Golan Heights , which Israel captured from Syria in 1967 and later annexed . <p> During his first term , Trump also ordered the killing of revered Iranian military commander Qasem Soleimani , who led the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps foreign operations arm , the Quds Force . <p> Soleimani was killed in a drone strike while he was in the Iraqi capital , Baghdad , in January 2020 . <p> The Islamic Republic has not recognized its archnemesis Israel since the U.S.-backed shah was toppled in 1979 . <p> On Thursday , Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei said he hoped the president-elect 's return to power would allow Washington to " revise the wrong approaches of the past , " although he avoided mentioning Trump by name . <p> On Tuesday , election day in the United States , Trump told reporters he was " not looking to do damage to Iran . " <p> " My terms are very easy . They ca n't have a nuclear weapon . I 'd like them to be a very successful country , " he said after casting his ballot . <p> Iran insists that it uses nuclear technology for safe and civilian purposes . <p> Trump 's election victory came after Iran and Israel attacked each other directly , raising fears of a further regional spillover of the conflicts in Gaza and Lebanon .
