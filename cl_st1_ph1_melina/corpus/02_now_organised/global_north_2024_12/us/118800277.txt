" <p> Pope Francis met Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas at the Vatican on Thursday , their first face-to-face encounter in three years , as the pontiff has become more vocal in his criticism of Israel 's military campaign in Gaza . <p> The two men held private talks for a half-hour , the Vatican said , but offered few other details about the meeting . <p> A statement said Abbas also met afterward with the Catholic Church 's top diplomatic officials to discuss the " very serious humanitarian situation in Gaza , where it is hoped that there will be a ceasefire and the release of all hostages as soon as possible . " <p> Abbas said in a statement that he thanked the pope " for his positions in support of achieving a just peace in Palestine based on the two-state solution . " <p> Abbas is in Rome for a brief visit this week . He is also due to meet Italian President Sergio Mattarella and Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni on Friday . Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas meets Pope Francis at the Vatican , December 12 , 2024. ( credit : Vatican Media/ ? Handout via REUTERS ) <p> The pope , as leader of the 1.4-billion-member Roman Catholic Church , is usually careful about taking sides in conflicts , but has recently been more outspoken about Israel 's campaign in its ongoing conflict with the terror group Hamas . <p> In November , Francis suggested the global community should study whether Israel 's campaign in Gaza constitutes a genocide of the Palestinian people . The comment , in a forthcoming book , drew a public rebuke from Israel 's ambassador to the Holy See . <h> The last meeting in 2021 <p> Abbas and Francis last met in person in November 2021 . During their visit together on Thursday , Francis gifted the president a bronze artwork inscribed with the words " Peace is a fragile flower , " a gift the pope has also given other world leaders . <p> Abbas ' trip to Rome comes as Lebanese Prime Minister Najib Mikati is also due to make a visit to Italy . Mikati is scheduled to meet Francis on Friday . <p> The pope has also criticized Israel 's airstrikes in Lebanon as going " beyond morality . "
