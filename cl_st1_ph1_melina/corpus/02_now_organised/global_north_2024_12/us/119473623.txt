<p> Countries around the world are ringing in the New Year Tuesday , with celebrations briefly outshining conflict in some parts of the globe . <p> Nations in the South Pacific Ocean were the first to wave goodbye to 2024 . Midnight in New Zealand is 18 hours ahead of Eastern Time . <p> Across Asia , millions readied for the Year of the Snake , seen as the year of rebirth in the Asian zodiac . <p> In Japan , which observes the zodiac cycle from Jan. 1 , stores were seen selling snake-themed products while temples and homes underwent a thorough cleaning before much of the nation shut down in celebration . <p> Thailand had fireworks nationwide , including a show in Bangkok with the city 's Chao Phraya River as a beautiful scenic backdrop . The country 's tourism board livestreamed its celebration on YouTube , with musical performances in the lead up to the midnight finale . <p> Fireworks light up the Sydney Harbour Bridge and Sydney Opera House during the early New Year 's Eve celebrations.David Gray / AFP - Getty Images <p> The mood was somber in South Korea after Sunday 's Jeju Air flight crash at Muan that killed 179 people . Most celebrations were cut back or canceled while the country observed a period of nationalmourning . <p> In Israel , New Years is marked by the War in Gaza entering its 15th month . Scores of hostages remain in captivity under Hamas , while more than 45,000 civilians have died in Gaza since the fighting began , according to the Hamas-run health ministry . <p> Lebanon is reeling from a years-long severe economic crisis and heavy damage after Israel launched an offensive on the militant group Hezbollah in early September . <p> Many Syrians , meanwhile , are expressing cautious optimism for the coming year after the overthrow of dictator Bashar al-Assad . <p> But a stormy forecast is expected to dampen the mood in many parts of the United Kingdom . <p> Heavy rain and winds forecasted for Scotland have prompted the cancelation of Edinburgh 's famous Hogmanay celebrations , which usually include a street party , a rock concert and fireworks . <p> London , meanwhile , will celebrate with a parade through the city center featuring 10,000 performers , while fireworks will be staged over Westminster and Big Ben along the River Thames . <p> In Brazil , Rio de Janeiro is expected to host more than 2 million people along Copacabana Beach to watch fireworks erupt from ferries parked offshore and concerts by Brazilian popstars like Anitta and Caetano Veloso . <p> Auckland , New Zealand , was one of the first cities to welcome the new year as thousands thronged downtown or climbed the city 's ring of volcanic peaks for a fireworks vantage point -- and a light display recognizing Auckland 's Indigenous tribes . <p> In Australia two hours later , more than 1 million people gathered at the Sydney harbor for the traditional fireworks . Some partygoers , eager to secure a good vantage point , arrived nearly 20 hours ahead . Later , in Malaysia 's capital Kuala Lumpur , spectacular fireworks were launched near the city 's famous Petronas Towers . <p> Celebrations kicked off at 9 p.m. local time ( 5 a.m . ET ) , with the first display of fireworks created by the Indigenous group We Are Warriors . At midnight , a second display over the Harbour Bridge lasted close to 20 minutes and was viewed on television by nearly 25 million people around the world . <p> In all , it takes 26 hours for the entire world to welcome the New Year across 39 different local time zones . <p> Astha Rajvanshi <p> Astha Rajvanshi is a reporter for NBC News Digital , based in London .
