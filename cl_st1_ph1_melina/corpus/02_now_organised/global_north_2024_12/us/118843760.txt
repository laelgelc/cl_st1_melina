<h> Tower Hamlets school faces criticism over planned " fast for Gaza " to raise money for UNICEF as Jewish families raise concerns about their children 's safety in the school . <p> An illustrative image of a class in an elementary school. ( photo credit : SHUTTERSTOCK ) <p> A London secondary school has asked students and staff to " come together in a collective fast " to support the people of Palestine , according to the school 's website . <p> The " fast for Gaza " is set to take place on Friday , December 13 , at George Green 's School in Tower Hamlets , a school where more than half of students qualify for free school meals . <p> The school 's website featured a post promoting the fast , along with two bake sales and two sports tournaments , with the aim of raising money for UNICEF . <p> An anonymous parent of a Jewish student reportedly told The Jewish Chronicle that her child has been hiding their identity and has been made to feel that being Jewish at the school is " subversive . " <p> " The school organizing something like this causes a sense of shame for the Jewish kids . The school has a point of view , and it is against Jewish students , " the parent was quoted as saying . <p> " The school has decided who is good and who is bad . Judaism has been portrayed as bad . No one is going to go out and announce they are Jewish at that school , " the JC further cited the parent as saying . <p> According to the parent , a Palestinian flag had been placed outside the school for the month after October 7 . The parent also reportedly added that there had been incidents in which swastikas were inscribed in some classrooms and stairwells . <h> Previous fast <p> Jon Ryder , the school 's principal , told the publication that the " collective fast event " was " organized in response to student voices on this issue and follows on from a similar event we ran a year ago , which raised several thousand pounds . " <p> " As a Unicef Gold Award Rights Respecting school , we take any acts of discrimination incredibly seriously and will always investigate and take appropriate action if we are made aware of it , in line with our behavior policy , " he reportedly added .
