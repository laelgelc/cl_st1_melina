<h> Hanna Katzir , Israeli hostage survivor who spent 49 days in captivity , has died <p> In this photo released by the Israel Ministry of Foreign Affairs , Hanna Katzir is shown . <p> Israel Ministry of Foreign Affairs/Facebook <p> Hanna Katzir , an Israeli hostage survivor who was released last year after 49 days of captivity in Gaza , has died , officials said . <p> Katzir , 78 , struggled with a " complex medical condition " for " many months " after she was released , Kibbutz Nir Oz said in a statement . <p> During Hamas ' surprise terror attack on Israel on Oct. 7 , 2023 , Katzir was kidnapped from her home and her husband , Rami , was killed in their safe room at Kibbutz Nir Oz , the Hostages and Missing Families Forum Headquarters said . <p> In this photo released by the Israel Ministry of Foreign Affairs , Hanna Katzir is shown .
