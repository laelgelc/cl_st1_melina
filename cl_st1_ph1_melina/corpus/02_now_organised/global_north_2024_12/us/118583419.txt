We 've seen it in China , we 've seen in Venezuela , we see it in Gaza . ... And we 've yet to see it for us , " he said . <p> The Tices are also hoping that President-elect Donald Trump will take up the cause when he returns to the White House next month . Trump expressed interest in Austin Tice 's disappearance during his first term in the Oval Office . <p> Tice was last seen in a video blindfolded and being held by armed men in Syria in 2012 .
