For the past year , Israel has been fighting one such group to its south -- Hamas in Gaza -- and another to its north -- Hezbollah in Lebanon . Israel is wary of a similar group in Syria . <p> Israel captured the Golan Heights from Syria in the 1967 Mideast war and later annexed the strategic territory .
