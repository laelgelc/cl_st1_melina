As the petition before the FDA notes , the current vaccine , manufactured by Sanofi , was not tested against a placebo ; scientists and doctors almost universally agree that withholding a lifesaving vaccine would be unethical . <p> The Sanofi vaccine , given as a shot , is made using a technique similar to the one Salk used . It is an " inactivated " polio vaccine , meaning it is made using strains of inactivated , or killed , polio virus . <p> A different polio vaccine , first developed by scientist Albert Sabin , a competitor to Salk , contained a live but weakened form of the polio virus . The Sabin vaccine , administered orally , replaced the Salk vaccine in 1962 and was in use for decades in the United States . But the oral vaccine caused eight to 10 cases of paralytic polio each year . In 1999 , federal regulators decided to replace it with the current vaccine , which is safer . <p> Advertisement <p> The polio virus is still circulating worldwide . In 2022 , an unvaccinated man in Rockland County , New York , was paralyzed by polio . Earlier this year , the virus paralyzed a child in the Gaza Strip , the first case in the region in 25 years , which prompted a mass vaccination campaign against polio . <p> " We still need to give the polio vaccine as long as these strains are in the environment , " said Dr. Paul Offit , a vaccine expert at Children 's Hospital of Philadelphia . If the vaccine is eliminated , Offit said , " these strains will do just what they did in Gaza and do just what they did to this man in Rockland County . "
