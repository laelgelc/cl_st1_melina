<h> Israeli team in Doha talks on potential Gaza deal , says official <h> The talks are currently focused on bridging gaps between Israel and Hamas on the deal U.S. President Joe Biden outlined on May 31 . <p> An Israeli technical team is in Doha for working-level talks with Qatari mediators on " remaining issues " in a Gaza ceasefire and hostage release deal , an official with knowledge of the talks told Reuters on Monday . <p> Smoke rises from Gaza , amid the ongoing conflict in Gaza between Israel and Hamas , as seen from the Israeli side of the border , December 16 , 2024 . ( REUTERS ) <p> The talks are currently focused on bridging gaps between Israel and Hamas on the deal U.S. President Joe Biden outlined on May 31 , the official said . <p> Efforts by Egypt , Qatar , and the United States to reach a truce in Gaza and a release of hostages have gained momentum in recent weeks , though there has been no breakthrough . <p> The three countries have , for more than a year , led rounds of so-far fruitless talks to broker an end to the 14-month-long war in Gaza . <p> In previous rounds , disagreements over new demands that Israel introduced about its future military presence in Gaza obstructed a deal , even after Hamas accepted a version of the proposal Biden introduced in May . <p> A round of talks in mid-October failed to produce a deal , with Hamas rejecting a short-term ceasefire proposal .
