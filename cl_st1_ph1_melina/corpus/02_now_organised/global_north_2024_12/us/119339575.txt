<p> CLEARWATER -- On Thursday , as the sun set behind the Clearwater Memorial Causeway Bridge , Clearwater Mayor Bruce Rector lit the attendant candle on a cookie-themed menorah , part of a celebration at Coachman Park of the eight-day Jewish holiday . <p> Before lighting the menorah , Rector expressed hope for the release of 100 Jewish hostages held in Gaza for nearly 450 days . <p> Rabbi Levi Hodakov , spiritual leader of the Tabacinic Chabad Center and event organizer , explained the menorah tradition : " It is lit each night to commemorate the miracle of the oil , which lasted eight days instead of one . " <p> The event featured a performance by the Simply Tsfat band from Israel , the ( edible ) cookie-themed menorah , cotton candy , caricatures , a meet-and-greet with three Tampa Bay mascots , a gelt drop and dreidel hunt by the Clearwater Fire Department , and classic Hanukkah treats like latkes and donuts . <p> Hanukkah began at sundown Wednesday and will end at nightfall on Jan. 2 . <h> More <h> ONLY AVAILABLE FOR SUBSCRIBERS <p> The Tampa Bay Times e-Newspaper is a digital replica of the printed paper seven days a week that is available to read on desktop , mobile , and our app for subscribers only .
