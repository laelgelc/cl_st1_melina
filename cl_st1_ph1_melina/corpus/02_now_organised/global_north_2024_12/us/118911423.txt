<h> Jewish a cappella group celebrates Hanukkah with a ' Wicked ' message of hope <h> Six13 's Wicked-themed Hanukkah song takes on a deeper meaning this year . <p> Wicked : A New Standard for the Fantasy World ( photo credit : SHUTTERSTOCK ) <p> Six13 , a six-man a cappella vocal group known for revamping popular songs with Jewish-themed lyrics , released a Wicked-themed Hanukkah medley that features a message of hope amid the Jewish people 's darkest days . <p> The group , based in New York , releases music videos every Hannukkah and Passover , and has featured themes such as Mamma Mia ! , Taylor Swift , and Star Wars . <p> Unlike other years , however , ' A Wicked Chanukah ' opened with the lines " This year has changed our history , we will never be the same , " as footage from rallies supporting the release of the hostages and hostages being reunited with their families played . <p> The opening sequence , to the tune of Defying Gravity , melded the original story of Hannukkah with the current ongoing conflict , featuring photos of the Jewish people overcoming adversity through the ages and encouraging Jews globally to live proudly , ' Defining Destiny . ' <p> The video then segues into the history of Hanukkah to the tune of Popular , as the group shows Galinda and Elphaba how to spin a dreidel and light a menorah -- all in shades of pink and green , of course . <h> Holding space <p> The song then returns to Defining Destiny for the finale , featuring a photo of a Chanukiah in Nazi Germany , fading into IDF soldiers lighting one inside Gaza just last year , as the group delivers their final message , " No enemy there ever is or was is ever gon na bring us down . " <p> Many commenters thanked the group for their inclusion of the hostages in their video , saying this year 's song made them extremely emotional . <p> " There is so much power in our shared history and our Jewish identity , " one user wrote , " I wish more people would hold space for that . Am Yisrael Chai , Happy Hannukkah ! "
