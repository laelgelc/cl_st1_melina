Omer Neutra , 21 , died on October 7 of last year , and his body was taken by Hamas to the Gaza Strip , the IDF confirmed on Monday . <p> New intelligence information that came to light allowed a special military commission of rabbis , scientists , and lawyers to finally confirm his death around 14 months later , despite long-standing suspicions that he was dead . <p> Neutra was a lone soldier from New York , serving as a tank platoon commander in the 77th Battalion of the 7th Brigade . <p> He was abducted to the Gaza Strip on October 7 along with members of his tank crew . <p> Tank gunner Nimrod Cohen from Rehovot , who is currently held in Gaza , tank loader Oz Daniel from Kfar Saba , and driver Shaked Dahan from Afula , whom the IDF has confirmed as killed in Hamas captivity were among Neutra 's crew . ( credit : Hostages and Missing Families Forum ) <p> On Sunday , Neutra 's parents spoke with The Jerusalem Post about the possibility of a hostage deal during President-elect Donald Trump 's candidacy . <p> " We 're just hoping that he uses his leverage . You know , each side has their own leverages , " Orna Neutra , Omer 's mother , said , adding , " We 're hoping that the combined effort will finally make something move . " <p> " Omer loved sports , played soccer , basketball , and volleyball , and served as captain of his school 's sports teams , " the Hostages Families Forum said of him . <p> " His family and friends described him as a warm , optimistic , and people-loving person who ' lights up the room the moment he enters , ' " the forum added . <p> Neutra 's " bravery and sacrifice will never be forgotten , " Garin Tzabar , the program through which Neutra enlisted , said of him . <p> " We will continue to demand that Omer is brought home to be laid to rest in the land he fought to defend . Our thoughts and prayers are with his family , friends , and loved ones during this unimaginable time , " the program further noted . <h> Hostages currently held in Gaza captivity <p> Hamas is currently holding around 50 live and 50 dead hostages of the original 250 it kidnapped . <p> Around 80 were returned in a hostage deal in November 2023 , another eight were rescued , and many others were killed , or their bodies were recovered .
