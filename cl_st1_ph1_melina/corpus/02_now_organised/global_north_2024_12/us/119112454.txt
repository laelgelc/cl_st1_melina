Of those , 76% returned to combat duties after receiving treatment from mental health officials attached to their units stationed near combat zones . <p> So many are suffering from the consequences that war has ; not only war , but watching friends , family , and loved ones be decimated on October 7 . <h> Suicide rates <p> Eliran Mizrahi , a 40-year-old father of four , reported for duty to the Gaza Strip shortly after the October 7 massacre . He did not come back the same , his family told CNN in October . <p> Mizrahi struggled with PTSD for six months after his return and died by suicide shortly before he was supposed to be redeployed . <p> Shirel Golan , a survivor of the Hamas massacre at the Nova music festival , was found dead in her apartment on her 22nd birthday later that month . <p> According to her family , Golan suffered from post-traumatic stress disorder due to the horrific events she experienced at the festival on October 7 , when Hamas invaded Israel , massacring some 1,200 , including in the Nova Music Festival area , and kidnapping around 250 . <p> Her family said they did not receive adequate assistance from state authorities to get her the necessary treatment to deal with her experiences . <p> Indeed , the state has not been prioritizing support for the survivors of mass casualty events such as the Nova Massacre . <p> In November , it was revealed that the state would stop funding psychological assistance to the families of hostages who have returned from captivity . <p> According to the law , as soon as a hostage returns , the captive 's family is no longer entitled to psychological assistance financing .
