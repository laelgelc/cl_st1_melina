<h> Marsiling-Yew Tee GRC raises over S$600,000 for aid to Gaza <h> December 08 , 2024 , 11:25 AM <p> The residents of Marsiling-Yew Tee Group Representation Constituency ( GRC ) raised over S$600,000 in a one-month campaign for the Rahmatan Lil Alamin Foundation ( RLAF ) . <p> On Dec. 6 , Marsiling-Yew Tee Member of Parliament ( MP ) Zaqy Mohamad shared that S$600,478 was raised as part of the GRC 's humanitarian aid campaign . <p> The money was donated to Rahmatan Lil Alamin ( Blessings to All ) Foundation ( RLAF ) in its latest fundraising campaign , " Humanitarian Aid for Gaza 2024 " . <p> " What a journey it has been ! We are truly humbled by the overwhelming support we 've received , " Zaqy wrote in a Facebook post on Dec. 6 <p> Prime Minister Lawrence Wong added in a Facebook post on Dec. 8 that the funds will help deliver essential health , relief , education , and medical supplies to the people of Gaza . <p> As of 12 pm on Dec. 6 , RLAF said it had raised over S$2,535,753 for the " Humanitarian Aid for Gaza 2024 " campaign . <p> " We are profoundly touched by the generosity and compassion of everyone who has contributed , as well as the community partners and mosques who have played an essential role in spreading awareness and encouraging others to give . " <p> The fundraising campaign will end on Dec. 13 . <p> Singapore delivered the fifth tranche of humanitarian assistance and aid to Gaza on Nov. 2 , bringing the total contributions since Oct. 7 , 2023 , to approximately S$18 million in November 2025.
