<h> December 03 , 2024 , 09:34 PM <p> In his speech , Maliki , who is also Minister in the Prime Minister 's Office and Second Minister for Education , said that more is needed to be done to address the dire humanitarian conditions in Gaza . <p> While in Cairo , Maliki also met with the Palestinian prime minister and foreign minister , Mohammed Mustafa , Egypt 's foreign minister , Badr Abdelatty , as well as other foreign dignitaries present at the conference . <h> Humanitarian relief and ceasefire <p> During his conference speech Maliki spoke about Singapore 's concerns about the humanitarian situation in Gaza , its call for an immediate humanitarian ceasefire , and preparations and hopes for the future , should a ceasefire be called . <p> These points were elaborated on during a doorstop interview that the minister held after the conference . <p> During the doorstop , he elaborated on the humanitarian situation , noting that winter was " fast approaching " , and that the people in Gaza would need better shelter and warm clothing , in addition to the already insufficient food and essential supplies . <p> Maliki also noted that this was true for the remaining hostages in Gaza , who were taken hostage during the Oct. 7 2023 attacks on Israel by Hamas more than a year ago . <p> During the doorstop , he reiterated Singapore 's call for an immediate humanitarian ceasefire , and also the immediate , safe , and unconditional release of all remaining hostages . <h> S$18 million for relief efforts <p> During his speech and the doorstop , he said that Singaporeans and the Singapore government had collectively donated more than S$18 million in cash and in-kind donations for relief efforts in Gaza . <p> Maliki had gone to Egypt in November 2023 to hand over the first tranche of that aid , while Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan was present for the handover of the third tranche , which had been accompanied by an aircraft from the RSAF in March 2024 . <p> He paid tribute to the various regional partners that had helped Singapore 's relief efforts , such as Egypt , Jordan , the United Arab Emirates , Saudi Arabia , and Cyprus . <p> He also encouraged Singaporeans to continue contributing to fundraising efforts by reputable organisations such as the Rahmatan Lil Alamin Foundation and the Singapore Red Cross . <p> Maliki shared that the conference had spoken not just about raising aid for Gaza , but also the difficult logistics of delivering that aid to Gaza , but also within Gaza . <h> Massive future needs <p> Maliki also spoke about the future . <p> He said that efforts towards a ceasefire were ongoing but difficult , but even should such a ceasefire be achieved , " the future needs of Gaza will be massive " . <p> Singapore stood ready to join the international community " in supporting the Palestinian people as they seek to rebuild their lives " after the ceasefire is reached . <p> Maliki touched on some of the efforts that had been made by Singapore through its Enhanced Technical Assistance Package ( ETAP ) . <p> ETAP supported the Palestinian Authority , which is the governing body that controls the Palestinian territories of the West Bank , and its capacity-building efforts . <p> The programme would be expanded from an annual offering of three postgraduate scholarships in Singapore , to five undergraduate and five postgraduate scholarships , which Singapore hoped would contribute to the PA 's human capital development efforts , in preparation for eventual statehood . <h> 60 years of diplomatic relations <p> Maliki also met several local and foreign dignitaries while in Cairo , including Abdelatty . <p> Abdelatty posted about the meeting on social media , describing Singapore and Egypt diplomatic ties as strong , and that the two friendly nations aspired to celebrate the 60th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations in 2026 . <p> He also hoped to advance various areas of bilateral cooperation to broader horizons . <p> Singapore is the fifth largest Asian investor in Egypt , with over US$ 700 million ( S$941 million ) in investments in the country . <p> Abdelatty welcomed the continued and enhanced cooperation in the cultural field , particularly the provision of Egyptian expertise to Singaporeans and students at the Al Azhar University , " supporting efforts to promote the moderate approach to Islam in Singapore " . <p> Meanwhile , the Singapore Cooperation Programme has also trained about 700 Egyptian officials . <p> Maliki also met with the Prime Minister and Foreign Minister of Palestine , the foreign ministers of Bahrain , Jordan , and Lebanon , and Malaysia 's deputy foreign minister .
