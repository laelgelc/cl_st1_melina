<p> It was a plot twist that stands out as the lowest point in what has been a challenging year overall -- the sour cherry on top of a struggle sundae . <p> The year started out with my own health issues . <p> In January , about a month after turning 41 , I started waking up some mornings feeling more exhausted than I had ever felt before . On these days , I 'd feel utterly drained of not just my physical energy but my very life force . The only thing that could restore me back to normalcy was a full day of sleep . <p> I 'm a single mother , so when my son is around , I feel like I have to be 100 per cent functional . But these periods of total drain were making it impossible . <p> Not only was it tough to enjoy my time with him through play and outings , I found myself also becoming extremely irritable and cranky , which then triggered my mum guilt . <p> Then my Achilles heels started hurting . A lot . All the time . <p> Each time I stood up or put any weight on them , they would burn . I started having to hobble my first few steps before being able to walk normally . <p> I went for a health screening . No issues flagged . I was , apparently , perfectly fine . <p> I knew what was happening : Perimenopause . <p> I 'd reached that time of my life when declining estrogen levels mess with all sorts of bodily functions and affect one 's mood and mental health . <p> Like countless generations of women before me , I had no choice but to suck it up and deal with it . <p> In 2024 , the writer started experiencing perimenopause , which often drained her of energy and caused physical pains that made it hard for her to function , especially as a single mother . But no , I quickly realised my nervous system was not yet ready to handle the anxiety-inducing mechanisms of modern dating -- and perhaps may never be . <p> Within a month , I was off the dating apps and on a mission to reclaim my peaceful solitude . I retreated to the comfort I found in novels , movies , daily meditations , and in blasting Linkin Park while smashing plates in The Fragment Room with my best friend . <p> Looking at each one of these developments , none were exactly minor or insignificant . But none were particularly devastating or destructive either . <p> They were just relentless -- one immediately after another , no pauses or breaks , some overlapping and blending into others to form a frustrating patchwork of big life changes that made it hard to find any sense of clarity or stability . <p> For CNA TODAY 's Yasmine Yahya , the many big life changes she experienced in 2024 seemed to hit her one after another with no pauses or breaks , making it hard to find any sense of clarity or stability . ( Photo : iStock ) <h> AND YET ... <p> But even as I document this litany of minor tragedies , I know in my heart that I have it good . <p> Take a look at the headlines on any given day . So many around the world are suffering , in so many different ways . The challenges I faced felt almost trifling by comparison . What did some pain in my heels matter , held up next to the bloodshed we 're seeing in Gaza , Ukraine and Sudan ? <p> And even as each one of these events in my life unfolded , they taught me something valuable about life . <p> The day I found out about my mother 's cancer diagnosis , I was in the office surrounded by my colleagues . <p> Some people might find this incredibly awkward in a place of work , but when I broke down sobbing at my desk , they instantly formed a protective bubble around me . <p> They listened , soothed , said all the right words . Tissues , tea and pastries appeared out of nowhere as if by magic . That 's women for you . <h> Related : <p> My mother underwent surgery to remove her tumour . ( Illustration : iStock ) <h> Related : <h> IT 'S NEVER ALL BAD <p> Perhaps the best thing that happened this year was something I decided to do for myself , which is the thing that has helped me put this whole year into perspective . <p> I signed up for the Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction course at Singapore Management University , an eight-week programme that teaches you how to practise mindfulness in daily life to better understand yourself , regulate your emotions and respond to stress . <p> Each week we learnt a new method of meditation . About halfway through the course -- coincidentally , on my birthday -- we spent six full hours in total meditative silence . <p> Sounds intense ? That 's because it is . <p> But it turned out to be the best way I could have spent my birthday -- giving myself the gift of undivided inward attention , unbothered by my phone , my work or other people . <p> About four hours in , I was contemplating a tree in the courtyard of the Yong Pung How School of Law , when a sudden wind passed through , sending a symphony of small , yellow leaves through the air . <p> It was the most mundane of occurrences . Yet , that moment of deep silence gave me fresh eyes to see beauty where I least expected it . <p> The impermanence and unpredictability of life is exactly what makes it so enchanting , so worth living regardless of what comes at you . In that moment , I did n't just understand this truth -- I felt it . <p> So was 2024 still a struggle sundae ? <p> Kind of , yeah . But I 'm now learning to appreciate even the sourest bits of it -- how they can make the sweet ones that much better , how they show me that my palate 's a little tougher than it was before , and the part they play to create a richer , fuller taste . <p> I do n't know what 2025 will bring .
