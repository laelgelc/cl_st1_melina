<h> Most Enthralling Creative Work <h> JUDGE RANALD MACDONALD 'S COMMENTS <p> It was a privilege and a considerable challenge to select the winning entry this year . Thank you to all the writers as each should be proud of their compelling and powerful contributions . <p> It makes me feel confident about the essential role writing has to play at this concerning time of AI ( not IA because THAT is a badge of honour ) where ethics , fairness and integrity in what is being presented are essential . <p> I found it interesting , but understandable , that so many entries concerned the Israeli/ Palestinian conflict . Though human rights , race relations , the environment , the fate of whistleblowers and a powerful piece on scam victims , also featured . <p> On " scamming " there are a couple of lines in victim Kim Sawyer 's piece which will remain with me : ' Scammers need a facilitator , that facilitator is a bank . Banks may not be the thieves , but they do drive the getaway car . ' <p> But the best and most evocative article in my judgement is another article by Kim Sawyer -- ' The perils of indifference : History repeats itself on Gaza ' . <p> Beautifully written , it features some excellent and apposite quotes from the remarkable Nobel prize winner , Holocaust survivor Elie Wiesel . One , in particular , is worth highlighting : ' Indifference is more dangerous than anger and hatred ... indifference is not a response . Indifference is not a beginning ; it is an end . Indifference is the friend of the enemy . ' <p> Dr. Sawyer for me is a worthy winner in the Most Compelling Article category . Certainly , I was compelled ! <h> JUDGE JO DYER 'S COMMENTS <p> Karen Throssel 's ' Changing the words ' is a stark invocation of the fraught existence to which Australia condemns its workers . <p> With spare , direct language , ' Changing the words ' contrasts the monotony of the life of a wage slave with the dangerous precarity of the underemployed , posing confronting questions about the impotence of all workers in a system skewed against them , and the unhappiness , anxiety and risk such powerlessness engenders .
