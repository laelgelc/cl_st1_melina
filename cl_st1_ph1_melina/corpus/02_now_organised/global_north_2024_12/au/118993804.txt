<p> Palestinians , Arabs and Muslims described hostility , threats and discriminatory practices such as restrictions on cultural expression and prayer spaces . <p> Many did n't want to speak out for fear of academic repercussions . <p> Africans and Asians encountered severe racism and often had to moderate and self-censor how they expressed themselves , while international students reported exclusion , social isolation and feeling like they were reduced to " cash cows " . <p> Race Discrimination Commissioner Giridharan Sivaraman said universities played a critical role in providing academic qualifications and " are gateways to opportunity that can either address inequality or exacerbate it " . <p> " However , structural racism has been legitimised by Australia 's colonial history and remains embedded in our society , " he said in the report 's foreword . <p> " Universities are not inoculated from racism by the intellectual endeavour that takes place within them . ( James Ross/AAP PHOTOS ) <p> There had been significant increases in reports of anti-Semitism , Islamophobia and anti-Palestinian and anti-Arab racism at universities and " each of those experiences is unique and not to be conflated one with the other " , Mr Sivaraman said . <p> The findings fit in with research and data from Jewish and Islamic community groups that have reported significant spikes in reports of anti-Semitic and Islamophobic abuse , harassment and intimidation since the war in Gaza . <p> Racism carried many negative implications , including poorer life outcomes , an impact on mental and physical health and challenges with academic performance , retention and progression . <p> Staff and students told the commission about low rates of racial literacy , including people not recognising racism , a lack of safety and support on campus and dissatisfaction with complaint mechanisms . <p> Jewish students were staying away from campus due to concerns over their safety , while African students spoke of discrimination in accessing workplace placements due to bias . <p> " Consultations also suggest that conversations about racism are shut down and there is a reluctance to discuss institutional and structural racism , " the commission 's report said . <p> The commission will put out a national survey and start focus groups in January before delivering its final report with a list of recommendations to the federal government in June 2025 . <p> Previous research highlighted racism endured by Arabs and Muslims following the 9/11 terror attacks , the exploitation of international students in the pandemic and Indigenous people feeling culturally unsafe , the commission said .
