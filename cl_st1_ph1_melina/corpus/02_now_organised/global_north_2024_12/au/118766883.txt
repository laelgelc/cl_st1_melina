<h> Save articles for later <p> So farewell to 2024 . That was the year that was , or for so many of us that endured it , that was the year that was n't . <p> Historians will no doubt mark 2024 as one for the books : Syria fell , Trump rose again , the war in Gaza raged on , and , er , Charles visited us for the first time as King . <p> But for the rest of us , it all seemed rather a dull grind , a year of brain rot and enshittification , as the dictionary dorks who decide the word of the year put it in either an encapsulation of the zeitgeist or a cheap marketing ploy . <p> But this Christmas , CBD gives thanks to the higher powers for people with big houses , in what became a mainstay of this column . Without other people 's houses to write about , vast swaths of the media ecosystem would shrivel away . <p> Anthony Albanese and the house he bought at Copacabana on the Central Coast.Credit : Domain / Andrew Ellinghausen <p> Even Prime Minister Anthony Albanese seemed a little too distracted by the property market -- we guess he 's a Sydneysider after all -- buying that Copacabana clifftop home , and finally offloading ( after much struggle and a price drop , as we reported ) his unsightly Dulwich Hill investment property . <p> Only this week were we able to report that fun guy Andy Lee 's popularity among his Melbourne neighbours had taken a dive due to the " horrendous noise " from his tunnelling a 7.7-metre drop on the site of his historic crumbling mansion Ravenswood on the banks of the Yarra in Hawthorn . <p> In May , CBD was proud to reveal that Rupert Murdoch 's niece and queen of Melbourne Penny Fowler and AFL commentator Hamish McLachlan had secretly bought each other 's South Yarra houses in an upper-class property swap . <p> Closer to home , the old Cranbrook chums behind prestige property reality series Luxe Listings had a spectacular falling-out that ended up before the Supreme Court . <p> Then there was the Point Piper mansion where Bruce Lehrmann was spotted hanging out just days before the Federal Court found he had raped Brittany Higgins . <p> Advertisement <p> The former Liberal staffer 's failed multimillion-dollar defamation lawsuit against Network 10 and Lisa Wilkinson ended with Lehrmann -- " the most hated man in Australia , " as his new lawyerZali Burrows told CBD -- and Seven West Media becoming an industry-wide joke . <h> Political animals <p> In Canberra , the Albanese government sputtered its way to the end of the year with enough fumbles to make Peter Dutton look like a contender . <p> But before Dutton gets too big for his Gina Rinehart-owned Rossi boots and starts thinking about the Lodge , he 'll have to overcome the Liberal Party 's women problem . We 're not sure hosting $5000-a-head fundraisers in male-only clubs is going to help that cause . <p> In June , political types took to the catwalk for Midwinter Ball at Parliament House , the annual meeting of political , media and business creatures . <p> The theme was " love is in the air " -- topical for the debut of politics ' newest couple , Victorian Jewish Labor MP Josh Burns and pro-Palestinian Animal Justice Party Victorian state MP Georgie Purcell . <p> Portraits of a rich lady . <p> NDIS Minister Bill Shorten turned proud dad as he accompanied his daughter , Georgette , also known as Gigi , to the ball , proudly describing her as a " stunner " . Gigi returned the favour , regaling hacks with stories about her embarrassing dad . <p> But our favourite moment was when Prime Minister Rachel Griffiths ( if you watch the TV drama Total Control ) late in proceedings grabbed a mini bottle of vodka from the wine bucket in the middle of the table and poured herself a neat vodka before noticing your columnist and putting a finger to her lips . Do n't you know who we are , PM ? <p> Scott Morrison finally quit politics , only for the Liberals to cancel his farewell party because nobody wanted to come . Meanwhile , his one-time vanquished nemesis Shorten 's retirement was met with a flurry of goodwill . <p> Shorten is heading for the vice chancellor role at the University of Canberra . His farewell public event is on December 18 in Thornbury , where some items of memoraBILLia will be auctioned , as the program puts it . Still bringing the zingers ! <p> Morrison at least had plenty of jobs to keep him busy , notably getting hired by billionaire cardboard box king Anthony Pratt , along with former Victorian premier Dan Andrews , as CBD brought you in an exclusive copied immediately by our rivals . <p> As for the other formers -- when he was n't playing the part of globe-trotting conservative intellectual , Tony Abbott was enlisted to fight a bike path in North Sydney . Julia Gillard finally saw the theatrical adaptation of her political life . Kevin Rudd turned the new Washington , DC , embassy into a kind of glitter-strewn frat house . But only Malcolm Turnbull got to share a set with adult entertainment industry figure Stormy Daniels as part of a British TV panel for the US presidential election . <p> That same night , Australia 's richest person , Gina Rinehart , was popping champagne at Mar-a-Lago to celebrate Donald Trump 's victory . It was another banner year for Rinehart , who has played art connoisseur , fashionista and even model for her own Driza-Bone raincoats in an advertising campaign we certainly did n't see coming . <p> Not to be outdone in the eccentric billionaire miner stakes , Clive Palmer kept fighting arcane legal battles and promising to rebuild the Titanic . The quintessential Queenslander and secret Victorian showed us his softer side by buying his old Melbourne childhood home for $4.5 million , well above the market rate . <h> We told you so <p> We do n't want to blow our own trumpets here at CBD Towers but felt it was our duty to remind readers of our soothsaying abilities when it came to prominent jobs . <p> It was CBD that brought you news that former investigative reporter of this parish , Linton Besser , would be the new host of Media Watch . <p> Similarly , we picked that Jillian Segal would be appointed to the hot seat as antisemitism envoy of the prime minister . <p> More recently we tipped that James Glenday would take over as host of ABC News Breakfast , to fill the gap left by departing legend Michael Rowland . <h> Final thoughts <p> As always , our dear readers could n't look away from meltdowns at our most expensive private schools . The failed revolt over Newington going co-ed inevitably wound up in court . Sydney Grammar cancelled Craig Foster . The less said about the Scots College castle the better . <p> The year closed with the sad news former federal cabinet minister Marise Payne and former NSW cabinet minister Stuart Ayres had quietly split after meeting on the 2007 federal election campaign trail . <p> Just this week , though , Burns and Purcell told us their own love match was still going strong despite trying political times . " But we are still very much happily together , spending time between our communities and supporting each other every day . " <p> If only more of the political , business , sporting , media , celebrity , cultural and influencer creatures we spend far too much time hanging around could be " very much happily together " , the world would be a better place . But we would have nothing much to write about and be out of work . <p> Just enough space left to thank all those aforementioned creatures , as well as colleagues , tipsters and you , dear readers , and wish you a very happy Christmas and New Year before we return in mid-January with exciting changes to delight and torment you all . <p> Kishor Napier-Raman is a CBD columnist for The Sydney Morning Herald and The Age .
