It 's unclear if it was a Range Rover Sport like this one , or another model . The most recent was published in December for $130,502 . <p> It is understood the contract was to purchase a Land Rover to replace an eight-year-old BMW for use in Los Angeles . <p> Over in Singapore , Tesla is the car of choice for Aussie diplomats . The department published a contract with Tesla Motors Singapore for $71,096 in December .
