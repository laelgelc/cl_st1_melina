<h> Israeli troops kill 20 in strikes on Gaza , medics say <h> Topics <p> Israeli troops have killed at least 20 Palestinians , most of them in the northern Gaza Strip , in air strikes and other attacks on targets that included a school sheltering displaced Gazans , medics and residents say . <p> They said at least 11 of the dead were killed in three separate Israeli air strikes on Gaza City houses . <p> The others were killed in the towns of Beit Lahiya , Beit Hanoun and Jabalia camp . <p> Residents said clusters of houses were bombed and some were set ablaze in the three towns . <p> The Israeli army has been operating in the towns for more than two months . <p> In Beit Hanoun , Israeli forces besieged families sheltering in Khalil Aweida school before storming it and ordering them to head towards Gaza City , the medics and residents said . <p> Medics said several people were killed and wounded during the raid on the school while the army detained many men . <p> The number killed was not immediately clear . <p> There was no immediate comment from the Israeli army . <p> Palestinians accuse Israel of carrying out ethnic cleansing to depopulate the areas at the northern edge to create a buffer zone . <p> Israel denies this and says the campaign targets Hamas militants and aims to prevent them from regrouping . <p> The war began when the Palestinian militant group Hamas stormed into Israel on October 7 , 2023 , killing 1200 - mostly civilians - and taking more than 250 hostages back to Gaza , according to Israeli authorities . <p> Israel then launched an air , sea and land offensive that has killed almost 45,000 people , mostly civilians , according to authorities in the Hamas-run Gaza Strip , displaced almost the entire population and left much of the enclave in ruins . <p> A bid by Egypt , Qatar and the United States to reach a truce has gained momentum in recent weeks but there has been no news of a breakthrough .
