<p> The former stonemason was diagnosed with silicosis in 2019 but his claim for compensation is being dragged out because one of the companies he is suing -- Israel-based Caesarstone -- has hidden behind the war in Gaza , his lawyer claims . <p> Mr Bucklow lodged his claim for compensation in 2021 against his former employer Pacific Stone and several companies which sold engineered stone under the brand names Essastone , Silestone , Smartstone , Quantum Quartz and Caesarstone . <p> He had been a stonemason for 17 years and regularly installed engineered stone kitchen benchtops , an artificial product containing a high quantity of crystalline silica . <p> Two years ago the NSW Dust Diseases Tribunal made an order for the defendants to hand over information key to Mr Bucklow 's case , but his lawyer Jonathon Walsh told 7.30 the Israel-headquartered Caesarstone could not meet the deadlines set by the court . <p> Ceasarstone has been accused of using the Israel-Gaza war as a delaying tactic . ( ABC News : Carl Saville ) <p> Mr Walsh believes that Hamas 's attacks on October 7 and the subsequent war are being used as a delaying tactic in the litigation . <p> " The war in Gaza ... has apparently caused them not to be able to discover digital documents in Israel for the benefit of an Australian proceeding , " Mr Walsh told 7.30 . <p> " I have doubts that that is the main reason why Caesarstone has n't been able to comply with their discovery orders , simply because the internet has been on this entire time . <p> " Their company has been selling its products . It has been litigating and being litigated against all across the globe . " <p> Mr Bucklow says the delays from Caesarstone are hurting him and his family . <p> " They 're just delay , delay , delay and you know , we 're just stuck here in the middle , in limbo , " he told 7.30 . <p> Joshua Bucklow ( right ) says his children have been helping him financially . ( ABC News : David Sciasci ) <p> " Our bills just get higher and higher and it 's an embarrassing thing to say , but our kids have been paying our bills for the last three years . " <h> ' You 're full of dust ' <p> Breathing in crystalline silica dust can cause silicosis , an incurable disease where the lungs are scarred and impaired . <p> Joshua Bucklow took this photo of dust at a site where engineered stone was cut . ( Supplied : Joshua Bucklow ) <p> " You 'd be cutting out hot plates , sinks . You 'd be cutting out , you know , over 10 a day , and at the end of the day ... you 're just full of dust . It 'd be up your nose . It 'd be in your mouth . " <p> Mr Walsh claimed delays with Mr Bucklow 's case were reminiscent of the asbestos era , when manufacturer James Hardie was accused of searching for loopholes and using stalling tactics to minimise compensation payments to workers suffering from asbestos-related lung diseases . <p> Lawyer Jonathon Walsh says he is reminded of the early asbestos cases when it comes to seeking compensation for silicosis . ( ABC News : Chris Gillette ) <p> He told 7.30 documents eventually handed over by Caesarstone then had to be translated here . <p> " A few hundred items of documents produced by Caesarstone were in Hebrew when they were discovered to us , and it was a tortuous process to try and get them translated into English , " Mr Walsh said . <p> " The behaviour that I observed by Caesarstone in particular , in Josh 's case , is quite reflective of the bad days of the early asbestos cases of the mid-to-late 80s and 1990s . <p> " The same processes were undertaken where the discovery of relevant documents relevant to litigation was absolutely tortuous , and it seems like we 've come full circle . " <p> Caesarstone declined to comment when approached with these accusations by 7.30 . <h> Caesarstone exposed to millions in lawsuits <p> Caesarstone was founded in Israel in 1987 and trades on the NASDAQ stock exchange . <p> In a report to the US Securities and Exchange Commission the company said as of December last year it was subject to lawsuits from 172 " injured persons " globally , including 76 in Australia . <p> It said it would settle some claims but " vigorously defend " others , with its total exposure estimated to be about $US25.7 million ( $39.6 million ) . <p> The company has described itself as " Australia 's leading supplier of engineered stone for over 30 years " . <p> It said since it set up its Australian subsidiary in 2008 it had " provided the independent stonemason industry with information relating to the safe handling " of its product and " what safety measures should be considered to reduce the exposure to silica dust " . <p> But Mr Walsh claimed the company should have done more to warn against the dangers of silicosis and owed a duty to Mr Bucklow to " safeguard " him from injury . <p> " Caesarstone should have acknowledged their products were dangerous on day one , " Mr Walsh said . <p> " They knew or should have known on that date how dangerous their products were , and for them to say otherwise is an absolute derivation of a basic duty of a manufacturer of any product . " <h> No-one wants the blame <p> Joshua Bucklow says he would be covered in dust after cutting engineered stone . ( Supplied : Joshua Bucklow ) <p> Mr Bucklow said he only learned about the dangers of silica dust in 2019 . When working he would at times wear a mask , but believed they were ineffective . <p> " You can wear your mask as much as you want , but as soon as you stop cutting and you take off your mask , the dust is still there , " he said . <p> Mr Walsh said his client had been " put through the wringer for three years " , with his case now likely to go to trial because the multiple defendants were in dispute with one another over how much they each were potentially liable . <p> There is no precedent in Australia as to the liability of manufacturers , employers and suppliers in silicosis claims . Mr Bucklow 's claim could prove a test case if it proceeds to trial and a judgment . <p> " The employers and the suppliers are fighting because each do n't want to concede ground as to how much they think they should be liable to workers like Josh , " Mr Walsh said . <p> " It actually does n't matter to Josh or to me how much each of the defendants are apportioned in terms of their liability because at the end of the day Josh is seeking damages against all the parties and should be compensated accordingly . "
