<h> The Friday Poem : ' Not Waving But Dying ' by Kim Cope Tait <h> Not Waving But Dying <p> The day we moved in to the house on the peninsula I stood before the picture window that faces the harbour . Oh , I said , I 'm going to die here . It was n't macabre , no fantail arrived . And I was n't afraid . I just knew I had landed at last in the place of my dying . I lifted my palm to the sea , just so . <p> I did die in that house -- to my old life . And a fantail did arrive the day that I announced my leaving . She flew in through the open door . The man I was leaving waved frantically , fear of the literal rising in his throat , turning grief to terror in an instant . I extended my hand to her softly . She did not alight there , of course , but paused mid-flight as if considering .
