Simpson in Washington Wednesday . <p> Simpson , along with Yukon Premier Ranj Paillai and Nunavut Premier P.K. Akeeagok , were scheduled to also meet Thursday with representatives from Greenland , which Trump has also made noise about trying to acquire . <p> Opportunities for Arctic collaboration between Canada and the United States was the focus of a talk with the three premiers at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ where Canadian leaders continue their diplomatic push against threatened U.S. tariffs . <p> The three territories have critical minerals essential for U.S. defence and advancement , as well as the geographic position to be a crucial partner in continental safety . <p> " This is a moment where people are now taking notice and it 's providing real opportunities for economic development in the north , but also the social development , " Simpson said . <p> All 13 of Canada 's premiers were in the United States capital Wednesday for the first time in history -- part of a full-court press against Trump 's threats to impose steep tariffs on Canadian goods . <p> The threats of devastating duties come as Trump continues to expand on ideas around American imperialism , promising to redevelop Gaza , take back the Panama Canal , annex Greenland and make Canada the 51st state . <p> Repeated comments about Canada and Greenland suggest that the president 's gaze is increasingly northbound . Trump has talked about Arctic nations in terms of access to critical minerals and the American desire to push against the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Expansionist discourse is ringing alarm bells in Canada and Europe . Greenland is a semi-autonomous Arctic territory that is n't part of the European Union but whose 56,000 residents are EU citizens , as part of Denmark -- a NATO ally . <p> Greenland also has cultural , linguistic and family ties to the Inuit in Canada .
