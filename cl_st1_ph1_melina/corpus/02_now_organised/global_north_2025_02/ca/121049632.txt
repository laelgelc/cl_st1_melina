<p> The following op-ed was submitted by Collingwood resident and retired judge Norman Douglas . <p> Rich Uncle Milburn Pennybags was the original name of that mustachioed old man in a tuxedo and top hat who has also been referred to as ' Mr. Monopoly . ' <p> The board game , made popular in 1935 , has undergone some changes in the 21st century , but when I played it back in the 1950s and ' 60s , it was fun . I loved getting the " get out of jail free " card so I could sail around the board without worrying if I landed behind bars . <p> I think that was the beginning of my interest in the law . <p> My childhood friends and my brothers and I put the game away as we got into our teens . <p> On Monday night , though , as I watched U.S. President Donald Trump 's startling announcement that he intends to take over the Gaza Strip , it hit me -- our modern Pennybags really believes life is a Monopoly game . <p> The 28 properties @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ were based on streets , land , railroads and utilities in or near Atlantic City . The goal is still to take control of other people by enriching yourself and impoverishing the other guy . <p> The winner is the one with all the money and property , hotels , railroads and utility companies . It 's fun when your opponent ends up in jail . And since you have the get-out-of-jail-free card , you have no restraints . <p> As a matter of fact , the new Mr. Monopoly has thousands of those cards , and new cards called " executive orders " that forbid you from passing " GO " and can even kick you off the board completely . <p> There 's one that simply says " You 're Fired .
