<h> Oscars 2025 : Real-life politics , from Gaza to Ukraine , take the stage in best documentary category <p> Oscar nominated documentary No Other Land turns hand-held cameras on the Israeli military 's destruction of Masafer Yatta , a group of farming villages in the southern West Bank , delivering harrowing footage of homes , schools and wells being bulldozed and protestors being beaten and shot.Antipode Films <p> Oscar season flips the usual Hollywood paradigm : An industry obsessed with new releases and opening weekends must now nurture a group of films for months on end , somehow keeping them in the public consciousness without allowing them to grow stale . Best documentary nominees about continuing wars are the trickiest of all to keep fresh -- when the subject is on the news every night , how do you maintain engagement without risking outrage fatigue ? <p> Two war documentaries are the front-runners to win their category on Sunday . It 's co-directed by two Israelis , Yuval Abraham and Rachel Szor , and two Palestinians , Basel Adra and Hamdan Ballal . Abraham and Adra are also its subjects -- the former is labelled a traitor by other Israelis ; the latter is under constant threat of violence and arrest . <p> In the second front-runner , Porcelain War , we spend time with Ukrainian artists who became soldiers after the Russian invasion . One of its co-directors , the Los Angeles-based filmmaker Brendan Bellomo , managed to transport cameras to the other co-director , Slava Leontyev , a ceramicist/soldier on the front lines . The film shows us what becoming a citizen soldier actually looks like , while also highlighting the resilience of art and nature . <p> Both documentaries are well-made , urgent and heartbreaking ; both have won plaudits from critics ' groups and prizes at film festivals .
