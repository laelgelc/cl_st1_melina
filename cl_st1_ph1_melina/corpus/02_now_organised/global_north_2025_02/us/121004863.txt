<h> State Department lays out plans for $7 billion-plus arms sale to Israel as Netanyahu visits DC <h> The sale is another step in Trump 's effort to bolster Israel 's weapons stocks <p> The State Department has formally told Congress that it plans to sell more than $7 billion in weapons to Israel , including thousands of bombs and missiles , just two days after President Donald Trump met with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu at the White House . <p> The sale is another step in Trump 's effort to bolster Israel 's weapons stocks . In late January , soon after he took office , he lifted the hold on sending 2,000-pound bombs to Israel . The Biden administration had paused a shipment of the bombs over concerns about civilian casualties , particularly during an assault on the southern Gaza city of Rafah . <p> Trump told reporters that he released them to Israel , " because they bought them . " <p> According to the State Department , two separate sales were sent to Congress on Friday . One is for $6.75 billion in an array @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ It includes 166 small diameter bombs , 2,800 500-pound bombs , and thousands of guidance kits , fuzes and other bomb components and support equipment . Those deliveries would begin this year . <p> The other arms package is for 3,000 Hellfire missiles and related equipment for an estimated cost of $660 million . Deliveries of the missiles are expected to begin in 2028.
