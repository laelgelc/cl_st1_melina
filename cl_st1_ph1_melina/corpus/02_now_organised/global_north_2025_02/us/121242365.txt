<p> More than 35,000 people , both Jewish and non-Jewish , traveled to Israel last year for educational trips , despite the country being engaged in a war on multiple fronts . Looking ahead to 2025 , experiential Israel education groups plan to boost participation by 50% , according to a recent gathering of the Israel Educational Travel Alliance ( IETA ) , a coalition of more than 100 organizations dedicated to bringing visitors to Israel . <p> The convening in Jerusalem earlier this month was the coalition 's first-ever meetup in Israel . IETA was initially founded after the COVID-19 pandemic broke out to help groups focused on educational travel to Israel respond to the global crisis .
