Its sanction-plagued economy is running on fumes . The value of its currency is spiraling , inflation stands around 30% , and a broad range of Iran 's people are understandably growing angrier . <p> The latest public frustrations have focused on an energy crisis that has forced sporadic blackouts and the closure of schools and businesses to conserve natural gas , which supplies most of the country 's power and heat .
