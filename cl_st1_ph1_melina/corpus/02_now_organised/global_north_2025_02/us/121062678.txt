<p> Recently freed American hostage Keith Siegel kept hope alive during his 484 days in captivity after learning half-way through that his son had survived Hamas ' brutal Oct. 7 terrorist attack , his family says . <p> Siegel is now reuniting with his family and recovering from his 15-month ordeal . Ronen Harish / GPO <p> As Siegel continues to recover after losing a significant amount of weight , his siblings say he 's glad to resume his vegetarian diet after having to give it up under Hamas . <p> " He 's craving tofu , " his sister Lucy Siegel said . " He 's had it for breakfast , lunch and dinner . " <p> So far , the terror group has given up 21 hostages , including five Thai citizens who were kidnapped during the Oct. 7 attack . The first phase of the cease-fire deal calls for 12 more hostages to be released in the next three weeks . <p> There are 76 hostages remaining in Gaza -- around 30 of whom are presumed dead , according to Israeli officials .
