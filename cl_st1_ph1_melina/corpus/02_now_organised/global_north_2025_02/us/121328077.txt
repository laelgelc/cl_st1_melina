<p> Egyptian President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi met with World Jewish Congress President Ronald Lauder and spoke with the Crown Prince of Jordan , Hussein bin Abdullah by telephone on Sunday . <p> Sisi and Lauder met along with the Egyptian General Intelligence Chief Hassan Rashad at the presidential palace in Cairo to discuss the hostage deal and help facilitate humanitarian aid into the Gaza Strip , the president 's office announced . <p> During the meeting , they discussed Egyptian efforts to implement the ceasefire agreement in Gaza . This included discussions on ensuring the release of hostages and the facilitation of humanitarian aid into the territory . <p> They also discussed ways to restore stability to the Middle East more generally , during which Lauder expressed his appreciation for Egypt 's " unwavering efforts to restore stability in the region . " <p> Sisi called on all parties to " adhere to their responsibilities and commitments so as to ensure upholding the ceasefire .
