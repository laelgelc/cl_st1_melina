Since the 18th century , thinkers associated with conservatism and classical liberalism have emphasized the importance of having an independent civil society , the informal networks in a country that are adjacent to the political sphere . Businesses and schools , libraries and neighborhood associations , are crucial elements of that sector . From Baron de Montesquieu and Edmund Burke in the 18th century to Alexis de Tocqueville and John Stuart Mill in the 19th , political philosophers have underscored that freedom depends on the pillars of civil society not being subsumed by those with governmental authority .
