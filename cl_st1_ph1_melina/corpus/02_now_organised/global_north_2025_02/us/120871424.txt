He also provided them with food and made sure they showered , despite also emotionally manipulating them , the girls told KAN . <p> In many ways , the senior official took on the role of " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cops . " <p> KAN wrote that the other Hamas operatives treated the female observers like soldiers and treated them very differently to civilian hostages .
