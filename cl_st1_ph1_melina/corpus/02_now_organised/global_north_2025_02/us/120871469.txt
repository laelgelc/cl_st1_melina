<p> A delegation of representatives from the Thai government visited the Western Wall in Jerusalem on Sunday , the Western Wall Heritage Foundation announced . <p> The visit came as part of the envoys ' visit to the Jewish State amid the release of the five Thai nationals from Gaza captivity last week . <p> The Thai representatives included Maris Sangiampongsa , Thailand 's Foreign Minister , and his deputy , Russ Jalichandr , the country 's military chief of staff , General Songwit Nunphakdi , and Thailand 's ambassador to Israel , Pannabha Chandraramya . <p> The representatives were given a detailed explanation of the holy site and prayed for the return of the remaining hostages . <p> They were kidnapped from the Gaza border area on October 7 while working in agriculture there . <p> Rintalak Suttisak , 43 , and Sahaot Banawat , 30 , two Thai nationals who were killed on October 7 , are still in captivity . <p> Following the release of the five Thai citizens , Chandraramya told the Jerusalem Post , " Our government worked together with all parties involved -- we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and of course the US -- we did everything that every other government involved would do . " <p> According to Israeli media reports , Israel 's Interior Minister Moshe Arbel said that the five released would be the recipients of Israeli resident status .
