<p> Capping off a stratospheric rise to pop 's upper echelons , Chappell Roan was named best new artist at the Grammys 2025 . The singer beat out a surprisingly competitive field , which also included Benson Boone , Sabrina Carpenter , Doechii , Khruangbin , RAYE , Shaboozey , and Teddy Swims . All of the night 's nominees took to the stage throughout the night , with Roan earning an enthusiastic standing ovation for her performance of " Pink Pony Club . " <p> In her acceptance speech , the singer , born Kayleigh Amstutz , thanked her listeners and shared a political message . " I told myself if I ever won a Grammy and I got to stand up here in front of the most powerful people in music , I would demand that labels and the industry profiting millions of dollars off of artists would offer a livable wage and healthcare , especially to developing artists , " she said , before talking about her early years in Los Angeles when she was dropped from a recording contract and struggled financially .
