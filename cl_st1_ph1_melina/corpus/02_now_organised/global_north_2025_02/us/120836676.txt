<p> Just a few weeks ago , not canceling the Grammys ceremony seemed like a bad idea . Music 's biggest night was scheduled to unfold shortly after the devastating California wildfires razed entire sections of neighborhoods like Altadena and the Pacific Palisades . Multiple Hollywood events were postponed or scrapped entirely ; it did n't feel like the time or , quite frankly , the place to celebrate . <p> But it 's actually a good thing that the Grammys stuck to their guns . The 2025 ceremony turned out to be one of the best awards shows in recent memory -- an unexpected delight featuring an incredible array of performances , memorable speeches , and , most importantly , a creative way to give back to the communities impacted most by the fires . <p> But once the show started , any red-carpet awkwardness became a distant memory .
