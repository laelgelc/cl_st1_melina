<p> In 1867 diamonds were discovered near Kimberly , South Africa . At first just the province of individual fortune seekers ( both black and white ) much as the California Gold Rush 30 years before , the discovery spawned a " new class of mining capitalists , " and by 1889 South Africa became home to a mining industry , rather than a haven for men carrying shovels and dreams and shortly after became a monopoly owned by DeBeers Consolidated mines . <p> The crux of all of these events circled around Dutch/English imperialism ( even to the extent of providing troops to protect capitalist interests ) and were controlled by it politically . Over the many years since , through wars , national extremism , segregation and apartheid , the great wealth the nation of South Africa owned was parsed out to investors whose only real concern was whether they could secure the largest share among their peers .
