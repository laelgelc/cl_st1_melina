<h> Letters : New Year 's attack on Bourbon shartters illusion that we are immune to terror <p> My wife and I have n't been to the French Quarter for New Year 's Eve in several years . <p> I 've been expecting some kind of terror-violence to erupt here during the Jazz Festival or Mardi Gras . However , that 's not why we do n't go on New Year 's Eve ; it 's just too many people . We go to the Quarter for dinner , to show friends around , for books and music and just to beat the streets . I go on Mardi Gras because it 's Mardi Gras Day , and death lives on your shoulder anyhow . People fall off of ladders and die . Cars smack bicyclists from their bikes . <p> I hope there is never a crowd stampede at such a time . <p> During the 1970s age of airliner hijacking , I thought someone someday would commandeer a plane and use it as a bomb . Then it happened in 2001 . Thanks to all the people who continue to live there and the bookshops , cafes and the remaining neighborhood groceries , bars and good restaurants and hot pizza that keep it real . I do not urge anyone never to go there . But I feared this was coming , and coupled with the truck bombing in Las Vegas with its obvious symbolism , it 's impossible not to see the alienation and rage and the dead . A coincidence has meaning : these attacks did n't happen alone . Such disasters happen every day in Gaza , in Darfur , in Ukraine . Now we know . It 's a delirious world . War is a disease and a disaster .
