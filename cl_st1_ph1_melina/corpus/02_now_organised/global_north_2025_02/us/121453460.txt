<p> A new report by Justice for Jews from Arab Countries ( JJAC ) sheds light on the exodus of more than 135,000 Jews from Iraq , which began in 1951 . <p> The report , released on Tuesday , comes amid discussions on population transfers in the Middle East following US President Donald Trump 's Gaza relocation proposal . <p> It outlines the 2,500-year-old Jewish presence in Iraq , which ended abruptly due to violence and persecution that began in the 1940s . <p> JJAC 's research estimates that assets and property seized from Iraqi Jews are valued at over $34 billion today . " Ignoring the persecution and expulsion of Iraqi Jews erases 2,500 years of history , " said JJAC co-president Sylvain Abitbol . " This report ensures their story is remembered .
