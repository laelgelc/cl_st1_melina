<h> Crowd gathers at State House , marches through Boston Common in protest of President Trump <p> BOSTON ( WHDH ) - Hundreds of people gathered at the Massachusetts State House before marching through Boston Common Wednesday , protesting against President Donald Trump . <p> Many protesters expressed anger regarding Trump 's second term in office and his policies . Some chanted " our body , our choice " and " trans lives matter " and held signs stating " Save Our Democracy " and " Stop The Coup . " <p> The protests come in response to the president 's slew of executive orders . Many were also upset by his recent comments regarding " taking over " Gaza . <p> Demonstrators also focused on Elon Musk , the man Trump tapped to head his Department of Government Efficiency . <p> " I 'm here because Elon Musk is where his not supposed to be . He is in our pocketbooks . He is in our confidential information . He has no legal jurisdiction to do so . I 'm mad as hell . Do n't like anything that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and I needed to do something , " one protester said . <p> Musk has caused an uproar in Washington , D.C. , by sidelining employees in a handful of agencies . <p> " They should be angry . An unelected bureaucrat has access to every single American 's personal , identifiable information . I did n't vote for Elon Musk , " another protester said . <p> Wednesday 's protest was part of a coordinated nationwide effort . <p> " Just get people galvanized , wake them up . What -- 76 million people did n't vote for him ? You only need a few more . I think people on the center and people on the right , this is not what they signed up for and I think some of them are going to come around as well , " said a demonstrator . <p> Police were monitoring the demonstration .
