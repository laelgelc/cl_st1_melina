<h> Letters : Arabs leaders choose violence over peaceful coexistence <h> Share this : <h> Arabs leaders choose violence over peaceful coexistence <p> Editor : On the very weekend that a handful of hostages were being returned to Israel by Hamas , including a father but not his two infant children , a pair of letters expressed anti-Israeli pronouncements that echoed the terrorists ' narrative and justified Hamas ' intent to destroy the Jewish state ( " Hamas pursues a righteous cause , " Feb 1 ; " Israel and its leaders have committed war crimes in Gaza , " Feb. 2 ) . <p> Contrary to the first letter , there is no " righteousness " in either Hamas ' aims or methods . The brutality of the Oct. 7 assault on innocents has been well documented for its bloody cruelty and lack of humanity . The second writer ignores the fact that Hamas chose to start a war in which the civilians under its rule were intentionally left unprotected while only terrorist fighters could hide in tunnels . <p> Moreover , the false equivalency of likening the hostages @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is an affront to decency and common sense . So is the accusation of " genocide " against all Palestinians when facts show that the Arab population of Israel has increased by millions since Israel 's independence , and the ratio of civilians to terrorists killed in Gaza is far lower than other world conflicts , particularly those involving the difficulty of urban warfare . <p> The real agenda of both writers can be seen when they harken back to World War I and the breakup of the Ottoman Empire that ultimately spurred both the League of Nations and later the United Nations to approve of a Jewish state . Alas , the rejection of a Jewish state has led generations of Mideast Arabs to choose wars , terrorism and deceptive propaganda rather than constructively building a peaceful community alongside their Jewish neighbors . However , peaceful coexistence , rather than repeated anti-Jewish actions , needs to be encouraged , but for some reason both writers have sadly opted to eschew that course .
