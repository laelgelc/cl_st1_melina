<h> Women Ruled the 2025 Grammys -- and Thank God for That <p> In 2018 , Neil Portnow , the president and CEO of the Recording Academy -- the music industry organization responsible for the Grammy Awards -- was asked by reporters after the ceremony why just 17 of the evening 's 86 awards had gone to women . Portnow 's response ? <p> " It has to begin with women , " he said , before suggesting that female artists needed to " step up . " ( Thankfully , just a few months later , it was announced that Portnow would step down , in part due to those comments . ) <p> In the years since , the Grammys have made progress in fits and starts : In 2021 , women swept the top four awards , while in 2024 , every single televised award was presented to a female artist .
