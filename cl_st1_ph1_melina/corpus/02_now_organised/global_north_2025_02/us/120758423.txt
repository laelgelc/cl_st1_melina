<p> As an Israeli who lived for many years in the United States , including in San Diego , I have received a lot of messages from Americans asking me my opinion of the recent ceasefire and hostage deal reached between Israel and Hamas on Jan. 16 , following 467 days of war . <p> Many , particularly those not well-versed in Israeli history , are surprised that for most Israelis , me included , this deal was met with a mix of joy , dread and concern . Joy -- because every Israeli wants our hostages back . Dread -- because every Israeli wants every one of our hostages to come home , and this deal does n't do that .
