<p> Homer wrote about the Trojan War ; Alfred Lord Tennyson , the Crimean War ; Walt Whitman , the Civil War ; Wilfred Owen , World War I. Their poems are part of world history and culture . Poets should and must document the destruction and horrors of war . <p> But can poetry ameliorate a war or hasten a peaceful resolution ? Perhaps , but only if poets and readers can slip behind enemy lines , or if the poetry of the " enemy " can cross those lines .
