<p> Sixty percent of Israelis , including 56% of Arab Israelis , oppose integrating the Palestinian Authority into a future arrangement in Gaza , according to a recent survey by the Jerusalem Center for Security and Foreign Affairs ( JCFA ) . <p> The poll was conducted among a representative sample of more than 700 Jewish and Arab Israelis aged 18-65 . Some 62% of the respondents , including 68% of Jews and 25% of Arabs , said they were concerned about an attack originating from the West Bank similar to the October 7 massacre . <p> The survey was carried out by Dr. Menachem Lazar 's Lazar Research Institute . It reflected a shift in public opinion regarding a potential Palestinian state . <p> Following the October 7 massacre , 67% of Israelis , including 75% of Jews , opposed establishing a Palestinian state along the 1967 lines , according to the survey .
