<p> At first , Thursday 's festivities in Gaza seemed like just another sordid spectacle in a 16-month exhibition of debasement . A label affixed to the latter 's coffin declared that she had been " arrested " on October 7 , presumably for the crime of existing while Jewish . All four corpses were handed over to the Red Cross for transfer to Israel as part of the ongoing cease-fire deal . <p> Then Israeli coroners concluded that the two children had been murdered by their captors and that the woman 's body was n't their mother 's after all .
