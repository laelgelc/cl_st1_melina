<p> The Cunios , a documentary feature that tells the moving story of one Argentine-Israeli family 's extraordinary , harrowing experiences at Kibbutz Nir Oz on October 7 , is in the works as Keshet International , a global content distributor founded in Israel , and International Emmy Award-winning production company Anima Films , are teaming up to find pre-sale partners . <p> David Cunio , his wife , Sharon , and their twin 3-year-old daughters were taken hostage during the October 7 , 2023 , Hamas attack in which a quarter of Nir Oz 's population was murdered and kidnapped , several of whom are still held hostage in Gaza . <p> While Sharon and the girls were released in the November 23 deal , David is still held captive . David 's brother , Ariel Cunio , was kidnapped from Nir Oz with his partner , Arbel Yehoud . Ariel is still in Hamas captivity and like David , is not expected to be released in the first stage of this hostage deal . <p> Arbel Yehoud was released on Thursday .
