" <p> Egypt will host an emergency Arab summit on February 27 to discuss what it described as " serious " developments for Palestinians , according to its Foreign Ministry on Sunday . <p> The summit comes amid regional and global condemnation of US President Donald Trump 's suggestion to " take over the Gaza Strip " and create a " Riviera of the Middle East " after resettling Palestinians elsewhere . <p> On Saturday , the Islamic Republic 's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi noted that Islamic countries must adopt a united stance on the issue , according to the state-run Islamic Republic News Agency ( IRNA ) . <p> " The scheme to forcibly relocate Palestinians from Gaza is not only a big crime that equals genocide , but also has dangerous ramifications for the stability and security of the region and the world , " he reportedly claimed . <h> An ' extraordinary meeting ' <p> " The Organization of Islamic Cooperation must @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by holding an extraordinary meeting with the presence of the foreign ministers of the member states to defend the legitimate rights of the Palestinian people , " Araghchi was further cited as saying .
