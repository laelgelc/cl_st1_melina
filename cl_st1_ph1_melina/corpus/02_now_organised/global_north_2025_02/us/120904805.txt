<p> Palestinians refer to their 1948 expulsion as the Nakba , Arabic for catastrophe . Some 700,000 Palestinians -- a majority of the prewar population -- fled or were driven from their homes before and during the 1948 Arab-Israeli war that followed Israel 's establishment . <p> After the war , Israel refused to allow them to return because it would have resulted in a Palestinian majority within its borders . Instead , they became a seemingly permanent refugee community that now numbers some 6 million , with most living in slum-like urban refugee camps in Lebanon , Syria , Jordan and the Israeli-occupied West Bank . <p> In Gaza , the refugees and their descendants make up around three-quarters of the population . <p> Israel 's rejection of what Palestinians say is their right of return to their 1948 homes has been a core grievance in the conflict and was one of the thorniest issues in peace talks that last collapsed 15 years ago .
