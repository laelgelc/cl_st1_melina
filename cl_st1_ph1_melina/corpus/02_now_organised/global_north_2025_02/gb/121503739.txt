There would be requests from schools and synagogues and memorial events and political receptions and media organisations . And she would try and do as many as she could , a great deal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ her health declined . <p> It was an opportunity to tell her story . And to explain how the day fell at a moving time for her and the family . The week of Holocaust Memorial Day is also the week each year when we mark the death of my grandmother , which took place on January 25 , 1945 . <p> After Mum 's own death the duty to tell her story fell to her family . And this year we duly appeared at commemoration events , at community meetings , at the Treasury , on Newsnight , on the Today programme , on Times Radio and even in a prison . <p> And on each occasion we were heard with respect and thoughtful understanding . Sometimes the audience was familiar with the Holocaust , and we were there only to remind them . Sometimes they were hearing the story for the first time . And they were left in no doubt about what had happened and why it mattered . <p> Which is why I have read with astonishment that some Jews have turned against HMD . They @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have one . Some Jews have even ( I assure you this has actually happened ) lobbied government to stop funding it . <p> I mean , oh my goodness ! What on earth are they thinking ? Let me try and stay as even-tempered as possible as I try to counter this astonishing nonsense . In order to gather myself , I 've waited until now to respond . <p> The objection being made is that HMD makes reference to other genocides and commemorates them too . This , it is ( extraordinarily ) argued , makes the event almost pointless , leading people to think that what happened to Jews is a run-of-the-mill slaughter that has happened to plenty of others . Better not to have it at all , the critics suggest . <p> Well , first of all it is , in my view , entirely appropriate that other genocides are marked . My grandfather , Alfred Wiener , was one of the most important figures in shaping understanding of the Holocaust . He did much to make clear the Nazi project was to eliminate the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ understand this . <p> Yet Alfred always drew attention to the broader consequences of the Nazi ideology . He saw himself as defending civilisation against brutality . He , without hesitation , regarded the prevention of other genocides as part of Holocaust commemoration . Indeed he had always asserted that the reason the Nazis had to be stopped was not just because of what they were doing to the Jews , but because of the result of this for everyone . <p> Second , in so far as there is concern about mentions of Gaza , the argument is quite simple . What has happened in Gaza is awful and depressing and the correct subject for endless debate and review . But what it is and was not is a genocide . To feel that we should abandon HMD and refuse to mention other genocides because someone might claim Gaza is a genocide is a total surrender . <p> In any case , HMD was not dominated by Gaza . We appropriately and sensitively noted deaths in the conflict , but did not allow it to overwhelm the commemoration . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , a triumph of diplomacy and leadership . <p> Which leads to my final point . Those people suggesting the genocide against Jews was drowned out ? What were they listening to or watching ? Not the same things as me , clearly . <p> A wonderful event on the BBC seen by millions , the King in Poland , the Prince and Princess of Wales meeting dozens of survivors , thousands of local events , buildings lit in purple all over the country , Pearl &amp; Dean adverts in cinemas everywhere , and simply acres of media coverage . <p> I got to tell our family story over and over again , reaching millions of people . And each time I did so I was able to explain the impact this has had on Jewish people and our need for freedom and security . <p> Holocaust Memorial Day is a magnificent achievement . Let 's make sure we continue to give it the support it deserves .
