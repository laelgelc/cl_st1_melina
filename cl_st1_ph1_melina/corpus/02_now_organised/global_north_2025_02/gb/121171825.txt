<p> DUBAI : U.S. President Donald Trump is scheduled to attend a meeting of global financiers and tech executives hosted by Saudi Arabia 's sovereign wealth fund in Miami later in February , according to several people with knowledge of the event . <p> According to the people , who spoke to Reuters on condition of anonymity , Trump is scheduled to deliver an address at the gathering . <p> Among other attendees poised to attend the FII Priority summit are TikTok CEO Shou Zi Chew , Oracle CEO Safra Catz , as well as Yasir Al-Rumayaan , the governor of the Public Investment Fund , the $925 billion Saudi sovereign fund , according to the FII website . The public website does n't list Trump as an attendee . <p> A representative for FII in Riyadh declined to comment . Representatives for the U.S. embassy in Riyadh did n't immediately respond to a request for comment . <p> Trump 's possible participation at the event would come after Saudi Arabia was among Arab states that swiftly condemned his plans for rebuilding Gaza as a " Riviera of the Middle @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ enclave . <p> Trump enjoyed close ties with Gulf states during his first tenure as president , including Saudi Arabia which has invested $2 billion with a firm of Jared Kushner , Trump 's son-in-law and former aide . <p> The Trump Organization plans to build a Trump Tower in the Saudi capital Riyadh as part of real-estate expansion in the region including in the Emirati capital Abu Dhabi . <p> Trump has called on Saudi Arabia and OPEC to bring down the cost of oil . He has also said Riyadh should increase a planned U.S. investment package to $1 trillion from an initially reported $600 billion . <p> U.S. exports of goods to Saudi Arabia were significantly higher in value than Saudi foreign direct investment in the U.S. in recent years , government data showed .
