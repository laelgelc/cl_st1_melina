<p> Even among the violence of October 7 , the kidnapping of infant Kfir Bibas , four-year-old Ariel Bibas , and their mother Shiri Bibas was an act of particular depravity . As campaign groups in Britain were making preparations for shows of support for Palestine , the Bibas family were carried into Gaza . <p> Today , their bodies appear to have been returned to Israel after one final indignity . Four coffins were paraded on a stage in Khan Yunis before a baying crowd , marked with propaganda slogans declaring the " date of arrest " of their occupants , beneath a poster depicting Israeli prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu as a blood-drenched vampire . The coffins appear to have been locked with no keys provided . Military engineers were reportedly checking for traps prior to formal identification . <p> It is a reminder , if any were needed , that there can be no coexistence or peace with Hamas , and that the terrorist organisation must be rooted out and destroyed , along with those who offer it shelter and succour .
