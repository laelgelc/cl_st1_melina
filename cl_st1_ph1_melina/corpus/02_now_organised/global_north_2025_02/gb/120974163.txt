<h> Aberdeen Uni announces Palestine scholarships plan <p> Aberdeen University is to offer scholarships to Palestinian students affected by the Israel-Gaza conflict . <p> The university has announced plans to join the Higher Education Scholarships for Palestinians ( HESPAL ) scheme . <p> The programme , which is run by the British Council , has awarded 242 bursaries to Palestinian academics and researchers since its inception in 2010 . <p> The University of Aberdeen also boasts a wider ' At-Risk &amp; Displaced Persons Scheme ' , which provides financial support to students and academics fleeing international conflict around the world . <p> " Higher education institutions in the UK have a long tradition of providing a ' safe haven ' for scholars who find themselves at risk in their home country and are forced to flee from conflict and persecution , " the university 's website states . <p> " At the University of Aberdeen , we are committed to supporting these individuals as demonstrated by our Inclusive theme within our Aberdeen 2040 Strategy and our commitment to becoming a University of Sanctuary . <p> " The University has committed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Fellow per year on a rolling basis .
