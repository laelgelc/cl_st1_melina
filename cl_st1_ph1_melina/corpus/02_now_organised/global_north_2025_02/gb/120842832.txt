I have been voting SNP since before many of the current Cabinet were born . I view the news , from recent opinion polls , that we are probably going to see another SNP minority government with very mixed emotions . I can already begin to hear the hollow chants of " both votes SNP " in my ears and smell the political carrots dangling in the air . <p> It looks like , short of an unforeseen and game-changing event , the years from 2026 to 2031 will sadly be a repeat performance of the same political play that has been performed on the Holyrood stage from 2014 to the present day .
