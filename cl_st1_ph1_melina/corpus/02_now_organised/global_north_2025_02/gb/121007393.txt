<p> DUBAI , UAE , Feb. 6 , 2025 /PRNewswire/ -- Dubai Humanitarian , the world 's largest humanitarian aid hub , leading efforts to provide aid to those affected by the crisis in Gaza , has released data from the Humanitarian Logistics Databank that reveals more than $137million worth of humanitarian aid was distributed to 106 countries during 2024 . <p> 2024 was marked by significant global crises , with the data demonstrating the heavy impact that conflicts and natural disasters have had on children .
