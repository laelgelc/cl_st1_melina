<p> Euro area government bond yields dropped to multi-week lows on Wednesday as investors feared a possible disinflationary impact of U.S. tariffs , which could lead the European Central Bank to deepen its easing path . <p> Investors also await updated estimates of the so-called neutral rate ( r* ) that the ECB staff will publish on Friday . <p> R* is the interest rate level that keeps the economy in balance , with full employment and stable inflation , and is usually the rate target for central banks . <p> " Friday 's revision could conclude a tighter range with a higher minimum , " Citi said , recalling that in Davos , ECB President Christine Lagarde had indicated 2.25% and 1.75% . <p> Money markets priced in an ECB deposit facility rate at 1.85% in December from 1.95% late Friday before the tariff announcements by U.S. President Donald Trump . <p> Some analysts argued that the demand shock facing euro zone exporters in case of higher U.S. import duties was likely more significant than the inflationary effect of potential European Union retaliatory tariffs . <p> German two-year yields @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ were down 3 bps at 2.03% .
