<h> Times letters : Surrendering the Chagos Islands to Mauritius <p> Sir , I am perplexed by suggestions that the UK may be under some obligation under international law to hand over the Chagos Islands to Mauritius . First , as even the Foreign Office recognises , the opinion of the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) was purely advisory , not binding . Second , it was based on a UN General Assembly resolution . Such resolutions are not legally binding . Third , when the UK signed up to the ICJ our declaration specifically said that the government of the UK " accepts ... the jurisdiction of the International Court of Justice ... other than ... any dispute with the government of any country which is or has been a member of the Commonwealth " , as is Mauritius . Hence any opinion of the ICJ would be triply non-binding . Ministers never mention these facts .
