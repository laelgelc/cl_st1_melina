But does Anneliese Dodds 's resignation from the government prove that they have grown a spine ? <p> Unloved by the powerbrokers of the Labour right , lukewarm on hard-left ideologues like Tony Benn , Jeremy Corbyn and John McDonnell , yet nonetheless susceptible to their economic radicalism , the soft left has always suffered from a lack of political definition -- and , to be frank , a modicum of self-respect . <p> The clue is in the name . When coined in the 1980s it described a more open and pluralistic cadre of left-wing MPs who bemoaned Benn 's ideological excesses and habit of denouncing colleagues as apostates .
