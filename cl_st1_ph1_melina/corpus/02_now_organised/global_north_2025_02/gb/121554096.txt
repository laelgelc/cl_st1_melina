<h> Bus nearly hits Southampton pro-Palestine protestors <p> A peaceful march by Palestinian supporters was inches from disaster after a bus came to a stop right next to members of the group stood in the road in the city centre . <p> Over a hundred members of Palestine Solidarity Campaign Southampton and Bournemouth Palestine Solidarity Movement ( BPSM ) joined forces in Guildhall Square today at 12.30pm for a demonstration through the city . <p> Onlookers applauded and cheered as the groups marched a red cloth - which was almost half a kilometre long - down Above Bar Street and into Southampton city centre . <p> The march started and ended at Guildhall Square .
