Good stationery may be one of the pleasures of life , but is anyone actually buying much any more ? Of course , people will always need pens , string , bubble wrap and so on , yet the heyday of stationery has definitely passed . There was a time , when people still wrote letters to each other or used writing implements as a matter of course , that it was a large part of our shopping , especially if you were a school kid .
