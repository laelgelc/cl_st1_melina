Now your partner of 80 years is leaving you . <p> Europe and the US probably wo n't remarry after Donald Trump 's presidency , though they may become friends . How can Europe get through the divorce , and rebuild a life ? <p> The first job is to distinguish short-term issues of the break-up from longer-term ones . The short-term fallout will go away when Trump does . These include his designs on Greenland , and his support for far-right European parties -- policies that he originated , and that future US presidents are unlikely to follow . On uniquely Trumpian issues , Europeans need to rebuff Trump for less than four years .
