Image : AP <p> Cuts to international aid budget ' immoral , unnecessary and shameful for Britain ' <p> YORK Green Party are shocked and appalled by the cut to international aid announced by the government . In 2021 , when Boris Johnson 's government announced a 28 per cent cut in aid , Keir Starmer said " the cut is wrong , because investing 0.7 per cent on international aid is in Britain 's national interest " . But now the government is cutting aid by a further 40 per cent . <p> It comes at a time when need is increasing -- because of war and conflict in too many places including Myanmar , Sudan and Palestine and because of the devastating effects of the climate emergency . <p> It is unacceptable , unnecessary @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
