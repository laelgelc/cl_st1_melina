<p> I 'm no fan of Donald Trump , but boy has he given me a week full of chuckles . I am not saying that his plan to take over Gaza , temporarily " inviting " its people to leave for neighbouring countries so he can redevelop the strip , is a good one . It certainly does n't seem realistic . <p> But the screaming fury and hand-wringing it has prompted among the " international community " -- which , when it splutters , always splutters about its fellow splutterers -- has been an absolute pleasure to watch . <p> The fury took on a more pointed , and even funnier form , with the yells and cries about the noble pursuit of defending human rights against " atrocities " after Trump sanctioned the head honchos of the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) for putting out a warrant on Bibi Netanyahu and singling out the US for its alleged complicity in Israel 's " genocide " in Gaza ( aka its entirely just war against Hamas ) . <p> In its typical " international community " style @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fewer than 79 wrote that Trump 's " measures increase the risk of impunity for the most serious crimes and threaten to erode the international rule of law , which is crucial for promoting global order and security " . <p> As ever , the lead authors were from as random , and toothless , a selection of countries as you could hope to find : Slovenia , Luxembourg , Mexico , Sierra Leone and Vanuatu . The United Kingdom , to nobody 's surprise , plus France , Germany , Canada , Brazil and Bangladesh also signed , no doubt with terribly serious , downturned mouths and many shakes of the head .
