<h> Times letters : How the US and the world can rebuild Gaza <p> Sir , President Trump wants to create a Levantine Riviera ( " Trump plan to ' take over ' Gaza provokes international outcry " , Feb 6 ) . All land in Hong Kong is held as leasehold . When the government " sells " land , developers pay a premium -- an amount equivalent to freehold value -- in return for a long lease , and the terms include ground rent paid to the government and passed on to unit owners when the developer sells units . China granted a 99-year new territories lease to Britain in 1898 . Perhaps the US could obtain a 99-year lease of Gaza , sharing premiums and ground rents with the Palestinians in the form of a sovereign wealth fund . The Americans would then start selling sites . <p> Land premiums were sky-high in Hong Kong , and the Mass Transit Railway was built from the proceeds of premiums paid for development rights above Tube stations .
