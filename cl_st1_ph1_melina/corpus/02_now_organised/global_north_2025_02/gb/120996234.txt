<h> Hamas names next hostages to be released <p> Hamas has released the names of the next hostages to be freed on Saturday in the Gaza Strip in exchange for Palestinian prisoners held by Israel . <p> They are male civilians Eli Sharabi , Ohad Ben Ami and Or Levy . <p> So far , 18 hostages have been freed since the ceasefire began on 19 January with Israel in return releasing 383 prisoners . Hamas says another 183 are to be returned on Saturday . <p> Some 33 hostages and 1,900 prisoners are due to be freed by the end of the first stage of the ceasefire in three weeks ' time . Israel says eight of the 33 are dead . <p> Hamas seized 251 hostages and killed about 1,200 people when it attacked Israel on 7 October 2023 , triggering the war . <p> At least 47,500 Palestinians have been killed in Israel 's offensive , according to Gaza 's Hamas-run health ministry .
