<p> The movement for the " liberation " of Palestine has always been marked by a lack of originality and fresh thinking . All too often , the goal appears to be stasis , rather than success . So it 's of little surprise that a truly novel solution -- the proposed relocation of Gazans to other parts of the Middle East , as now suggested by Donald Trump -- was immediately dismissed as offensive and unworkable . <p> No matter how provocative Trump 's proposal might be , however , it has hardly arrived in a vacuum . Nearly 16 months into the Israel-Hamas war , no side has delivered a realistic plan for if ( or even when ) the fighting stops . Instead , Israel has committed to a precarious ceasefire , with an ever-cunning Hamas now taunting the world with creepy hostage-release ceremonies and worrying displays of rearmament . <p> As Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu prepares to meet president Trump in Washington this week , Hamas terrorists continue to emerge from their tunnels looking surprisingly healthy . From Greece and Turkey to India and Pakistan , population transfers have often accompanied major wars . But nothing even close to a forced " transfer " is on the table here .
