He , his wife and their two daughters woke to explosions near their home on Kibbutz Nahal Oz on October 7 , 2023 . They hid for their lives in their safe room but incredibly survived thanks to Tibon 's brave parents , who drove down from Tel Aviv to rescue them . <p> The seasoned journalist , who still lives in temporary accommodation with his family , shares their experience in his book The Gates of Gaza .
