<p> The Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) have confirmed that 86-year-old Shlomo Mansour , a hostage from Kibbutz Kissufim , was killed on October 7 and his body has been held captive by Hamas in Gaza since . <p> The news of Mansour 's death was shared this morning by Kibbutz Kissufim in a statement . <p> " With a heavy heart , we kibbutz members received the news this morning of the murder in Hamas captivity of our dear friend , Shlomo Mansour , 86 , who was kidnapped from his home in Kibbutz Kissufim in the Hamas terrorist attack on October 7 , 2023 , " the statement says . <p> The kibbutz has not disclosed further details surrounding Mansour 's death , including the circumstances or exact timing . <p> " This is one of the most difficult days in the history of our kibbutz . Shlomo was much more than a community member to us -- he was a father , a grandfather , a true friend and the beating heart of Kissufim , " they say . <p> " His smile , modesty and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ hearts are broken that we were unable to bring him back to us alive . The entire community grieves his loss and is united in grief and pain , " the Kibbutz says . <p> Mansour had worked as a chicken coop manager in the kibbutz . His wife , Mazal , managed to escape during the attack . <p> Born in Baghdad , Iraq , Mansour survived the 1941 Farhud massacre before emigrating to Israel with his family . <p> He was part of the larger wave of Jewish emigration from Iraq , with his family being among 135,000 Jews -- or 95 percent of the community -- who fled to Israel after emigration was permitted . <p> Mansour 's story is intertwined with the history of many Iraqi Jews who , like him , sought new lives in Israel after facing persecution .
