<h> Scotland must understand it 's role in the British Empire in this new imperialism era <h> What was Scotland 's role in the British Empire ? Let 's understand it <p> DEBATES over the legacy of the British Empire constantly simmer in public discourse , and this week , Scotland sparked an eruption . <p> A new YouGov poll has revealed that Scots are more likely to see Scotland as having been a subject of British imperialism , rather than the active partner it -- unfortunately -- was . <p> A staggering 40% of Scots believe Scotland was " more of a subject country in the British Empire " , while 29% perceive it as having been " more of a partner " with England . Scots played pivotal roles in the colonial machinery , bolstering the rank of administrators , establishing tobacco farms and sugar plantations , and engaging in military service and missionary work . <p> The British Empire has scarred Scotland 's built environment with statues and buildings memorialising both slave plantation owners and anti-abolitionists . Perhaps most striking is the sobering reality that more Jamaicans bear the surname Campbell than Scots themselves . <p> Just because Scots " built the Empire " , does n't mean we should take pride in it .
