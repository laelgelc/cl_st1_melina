<h> Pope 's condition ' critical but stationary ' <p> Pope Francis remains in a critical but stable condition after spending eleven days in hospital . The Holy Father is fighting a complex respiratory infection and bilateral pneumonia , and doctors are either unable or unwilling to offer an overall prognosis . <p> The Vatican stated in a medical bulletin issued later in the day on 25 February that " the clinical conditions of the Holy Father remain critical , but stationary " . <p> " There have been no acute respiratory episodes , and his hemodynamic parameters continue to be stable , " the statement said .
