<p> Only NATO countries , Japan , South Korea and Israel have the top fighter aircraft in the US arsenal , the F-35 . The plane , which features advanced electronics and sensors , is billed as a multirole platform that can be used to achieve air superiority and conduct strike missions . It is competing in India against Russia 's 5th-generation stealth-like jet , the Su-57 . <p> While both the Su-57 and the F-35 are competing against one another to fill India 's need for a significant number of new jets , the reality is the aircraft are considerably different . <p> The F-35 is optimized for stealth , although it falls short of the stealth protection of its big brother , the F-22 . Congress decided years ago that the F-22 could not be exported , leaving the Japanese in the lurch as they sought the fighter to fend off China .
