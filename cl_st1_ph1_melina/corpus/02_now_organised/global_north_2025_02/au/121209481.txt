<h> Record number of journalists killed in 2024 : report <p> Camera IconEight-five journalists and media workers were killed in the Gaza war in 2024 , a new report says . ( EPA PHOTO ) Credit : AAP <p> A record number of journalists were killed in 2024 , according to a report by US non-profit organisation Committee to Protect Journalists ( CPJ ) . <p> At least 124 journalists and media workers were killed , with nearly two-thirds of them Palestinians killed in Israel 's war in Gaza , the CPJ said . <p> " The toll of conflict on the press is most glaring in the unprecedented number of journalists and media workers killed in the Israel-Gaza war , 85 in 2024 , and 78 in 2023 . " <p> The CPJ said the number of conflicts globally - whether political , criminal or military in nature - had doubled in the past five years . <p> " All of the 2024 killings point to the increased dangers facing reporters and media workers - and the threat that poses to the flow of information worldwide . " <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the killing of 39 other journalists and media workers in 16 nations , with the deadliest being Sudan , Pakistan , Mexico , Syria , Myanmar , Iraq and Haiti . <p> It is the highest toll since the CPJ began collecting data more than three decades ago . The previous record was 113 journalists killed in 2007 , during the Iraq War . <p> " Conditions can grow more lethal for the press when those who kill journalists are not held to account . And fewer journalists means less information for citizens seeking the truth , " the CPJ warned . <p> Forty-three of those killed in 2024 were freelancers , " another grim new record for self-employed members of the press who often face the most danger because they have the fewest resources , " the CPJ said . <p> Thirty-one of those freelancers were killed in Gaza , more than double the 14 killed in 2023 . <p> Under international humanitarian law , journalists are considered civilians and targeting them in a conflict is a war crime .
