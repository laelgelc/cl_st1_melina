Bernard Moylan , Bronte <h> Oxygen for hospitals <p> While a 12 per cent rise in Commonwealth funding for public hospitals is very welcome , it will have little effect on the decade-long unconscionable and worsening waiting times for elective surgery and outpatient specialist consultation ( " PM throws $1.7b lifeline to save hospitals " , February 6 ) . The only figure that matters is 50 per cent , the original Medicare proportion of public hospital funding which the Commonwealth must re-establish . Out-of-hospital care , mostly for the less severe end of the disease spectrum , is funded by the Commonwealth and uncapped . Public hospital care , mostly for severe disease , is funded by the state and capped . Not a clever country . A single funding authority would provide a solution .
