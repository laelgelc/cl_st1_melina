<p> On January 19 , a ceasefire and prisoner exchange deal between Hamas and Israel took effect , initially set for 42 days , with negotiations continuing for subsequent phases . <p> The agreement was mediated by Egypt and Qatar , with US support . <p> Israel has killed nearly 47,500 Palestinians , mostly women and children , in its carnage in Gaza and wounded over 111,000 . <p> More than 11,000 people remain missing . <p> The Israeli genocide caused a massive shortage of basic necessities , including food , water , medicine and electricity , while displacing almost the entire population of the enclave . <p> In November 2024 , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former defence minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza . <p> Israel also faces a genocide case at the International Court of Justice over its war on the enclave .
