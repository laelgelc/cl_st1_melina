Will Trump open fire ? <p> The president has already imposed tariffs on China , Canada and Mexico -- the latter two since paused pending talks . The outbreak of any trade war between the US and EU would inevitably lead to similar dialogue . <p> A deal could be done relatively quickly . It 's important not to panic .
