By 2050 , one-in-four people on the planet will be African , creating a large market opportunity for investors . McKinsey estimates the region has $3 trillion in consumer spending potential . Fin-tech innovation , renewable energy solutions and a cultural renaissance are the norm not the exception . It should lead new alliances to fight for these programmes in negotiations over the EU 's budget . <p> But the State should also reimagine foreign assistance . Using part of the Apple windfall it could create a " people and planet fund " to invest strategically in transitioning the global energy system , building resilience to climate change and fighting poverty . It could identify innovations developed by Irish firms and support their deployment in emerging markets , create jobs and support sustainable growth in those countries . In turn , innovations from these countries could be brought back to Ireland . <p> Profits from these investments would subsidise future investments and generate returns for the Irish taxpayer .
