<h> US landlord stands in court over anti-Muslim-motivated murder trial <p> The attacker is charged with murdering a Palestinian child and assaulting his mother in a hate crime , with the Muslim community fearing increased violence . <p> AP <p> The trial of Joseph Czuba centres on accusations of a hate-motivated attack against a Palestinian family . / Photo : AP <p> The trial of a suburban Chicago landlord accused of murder , attempted murder and a hate crime in a 2023 attack on a Palestinian American woman and her young son is set to start on Tuesday . <p> Joseph Czuba , 73 , is charged in the fatal stabbing of six-year-old Wadee Alfayoumi and the wounding of Hanan Shaheen on October 14 , 2023 . <p> Authorities said the family was targeted because of their Islamic faith and in response to Israel 's war on Gaza following the Hamas attack on October 7 , 2023 . <p> Opening statements start on Tuesday morning in a trial that is expected to last about a week . <p> Czuba has pleaded not guilty to three counts of first-degree murder , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ His defence attorney , George Lenard , declined to comment before the end of the trial . <p> The attack on the family in Plainfield , nearly 65 kilometres southwest of Chicago , has renewed fears of anti-Muslim racism in the Chicago area 's large and established Palestinian community . <p> " We firmly pray and hope that Mr. Czuba will be locked up for the rest of his life , so we can send a message that hate crimes against anyone on the basis of their religion and national origin are not tolerated , " Joe Milburn , a staff attorney for the Council on American-Islamic Relations , told reporters on Monday ahead of jury selection .
