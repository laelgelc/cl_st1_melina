<h> More grain ships divert from Red Sea this week after attacks <p> More ships carrying grain were diverted from the Suez Canal to sailings around the Cape of Good Hope this week after attacks on vessels in the Red Sea , shipping analysts said on Friday . <p> " Another 16 vessels were confirmed diverted this week , taking the total grain cargoes diverted to some 3.9 million tons , up from 3.0 million tons last week , said Ishan Bhanu , lead agricultural commodities analyst at data provider and analysts Kpler . <p> About 7 million metric tons of grain cargoes normally transit the Suez Canal into the Red Sea each month . <p> The Houthis , who control much of Yemen , have launched drone and missile attacks on ships in solidarity with Palestinians in Gaza . The Houthis say they have been targeting vessels that had Israeli links or had been sailing to Israel . <p> " Many of the diverted ships are carrying U.S. grain cargoes showing caution with this freight , " Bhanu said . <p> " One ship which sailed from the U.S. Gulf for China actually transited the Suez Canal southbound @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ north again and transiting the canal northbound , then sailing past Gibraltar .
