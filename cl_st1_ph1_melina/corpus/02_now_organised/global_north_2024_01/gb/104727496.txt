U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude settled at $71.65 a barrel , down 12 cents or 0.17% . <p> Both contracts slipped more than 10% in 2023 to close out the year at their lowest year-end levels since 2020 . <p> Brent had climbed 10% and WTI by 7% last year , supported by supply concerns following Russia 's invasion of Ukraine . <p> A Reuters survey of 34 economists and analysts forecast Brent crude will average $82.56 in 2024 , down from November 's $84.43 consensus , as they expect weak global growth to cap demand .
