<p> Liverpool City Council has reiterated no form of racism " will be tolerated in our city " . <p> As part of a speech delivered at the Town Hall for Holocaust Memorial Day , the Lord Mayor of Liverpool , councillor Mary Rasmussen , said the council is committed to working with and supporting all faith and community groups across the area . <p> The pledge comes after Merseyside Police recorded a rise in anti-Semitic hate crimes - the force recorded four incidents in both 2021 and 2022 but last year recorded 20 . Islamophobic offences also rose from six to 10 in the same period . <p> Councillor Mary Rasmussen said : " Liverpool City Council has also declared that antisemitism , Islamophobia and all other forms of racism will not be tolerated in our city and rejects any and all inflammatory rhetoric and intimidation of communities affected by the situation in Israel and Gaza . The council resolves to continue to work with and support all of our faith and community groups . " <p> The Lord Mayor spoke at a service for Holocaust Memorial Day - with this year marking the 79th anniversary of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ national day of remembrance for all the victims of theHolocaust and other more recent Genocides such as Cambodia , Bosnia , Armenia , Rwanda and Darfur . <p> This year 's theme is Fragility of Freedom - reflecting that in every genocide , those who are targeted for persecution have had their freedom restricted and removed before they themselves are murdered . <p> Merseyside Police Chief Superintendent Zoe Thornton , head of local policing for Liverpool , told the ECHO : " We take all reports of hate crime seriously because we know the devastating impact it has on victims , and targeted communities . Nationally there has been a significant rise in anti-Semitic hate crimes and a rise in Islamophobic hate crime , which is believed to be linked to the ongoing conflict in Israel and Palestine following the Hamas terror attack on October 7 . <p> " Here on Merseyside , we have seen rises in anti-Semitic and Islamophobic hate crime , although it has not been as significant as it has been in other parts of the country .
