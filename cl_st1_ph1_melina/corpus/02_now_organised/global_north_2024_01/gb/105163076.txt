It also underlined the risks to shipping in the vital waterway amid Israel 's war on Hamas in the Gaza Strip . <p> The attack happened 70 miles south east of Aden , where the drone smashed into the vessel , said United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations , an arm of the Royal Navy that oversees Middle Eastern waterways . <p> The ship 's captain reported a fire onboard had been extinguished , it said . " Vessel and crew are safe and proceeding to next port of call , " it added . <p> Brigadier General Yahya Saree , a military spokesman for the Houthis , identified the ship as the bulk carrier Genco Picardy .
