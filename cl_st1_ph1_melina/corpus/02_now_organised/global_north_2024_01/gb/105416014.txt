<h> Two missiles seen exploding off Yemen : maritime monitors <p> Two missiles exploded in waters south of Yemen on Friday , maritime monitoring agencies said , following months of attacks on shipping by the country 's Huthi rebels . <p> A Panama-flagged oil tanker " reported seeing two blasts " in the Gulf of Aden , said risk monitor Ambrey , a report that was corroborated by the British navy 's United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations . No damage was reported . <p> Ambrey said the missiles exploded about a mile from the India-affiliated oil tanker and 200-300 metres ( 650-1,000 feet ) above the waterline . UKMTO said they detonated in the water . <p> " The target was unclear at the time of the report .
