<h> Oman , Norway review bilateral relations <p> Muscat : The Sultanate of Oman and the Kingdom of Norway held a session of political talks on Tuesday . <p> The Omani side was headed by Sheikh Khalifa Ali Al Harthy , Undersecretary of the Foreign Ministry for Political Affairs , while the Norwegian side was headed by Andreas Motzfeldt Kravik , State Secretary of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs . <p> During the session , the two sides reviewed bilateral relations .
