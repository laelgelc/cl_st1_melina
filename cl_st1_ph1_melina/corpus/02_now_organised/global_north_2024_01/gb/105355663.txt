<p> A Greater Manchester MP has called for an ' immediate ' and ' sustainable ' ceasefire in Gaza . Gorton MP Afzal Khan , who quit the Labour frontbench in November over the party 's stance on the Middle East conflict , will present a petition to Parliament today ( January 24 ) . <p> It follows four months of fighting between Israel and Hamas which controls Gaza and is considered a terrorist group in the UK . Tens of thousands of Palestinians have been killed in this latest conflict triggered by an unprecedented attack by Hamas killing around 1,200 . <p> Mr Khan 's petition calls for a ceasefire from both parties in the conflict to prevent further bloodshed and damage so that a peaceful two-state solution is achieved in the region . It argues that a ceasefire is needed to prevent the humanitarian crisis from intensifying . <p> The petition also condemns the actions of the Israeli army and urges Hamas to immediately release Israeli hostages .
