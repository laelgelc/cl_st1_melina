<p> The European Commission said on Monday it would review whether it could continue to fund the U.N . Palestinian refugee agency ( UNRWA ) in light of the allegations that staff members were involved in the Oct. 7 attacks by Hamas in southern Israel . <p> Several countries , including EU members Germany , Italy , the Netherlands and Finland , paused funding for UNRWA after allegations by Israel that 12 of its 13,000 staff in Gaza were involved . <p> U.N . officials urged them to reconsider a pause in funding , pledging that any staff found involved in the Hamas attack would be punished and warning that aid for some two million people in Gaza was at stake . <p> " The European Commission will determine upcoming funding decisions for UNRWA in light of the very serious allegations , " the commission said in a statement . <p> " The Commission will review the matter in light of the outcome of the investigation announced by the UN and the actions it will take .
