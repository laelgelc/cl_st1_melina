<h> India sees December exports boost , but all eyes on Red Sea <p> Higher exports helped India 's merchandise trade deficit come in lower than expected at $19.8 billion in December , data showed on Monday , though officials signalled that the turmoil in the Red Sea could have an impact on this month 's figures . <p> Economists polled by Reuters had expected a merchandise trade deficit of $21 billion in December . <p> " There has been a demand upturn in December ( leading to higher exports ) . This provides us a cushion as there are uncertainties for trade in January and going forward due to the Red Sea crisis , " Trade Secretary Sunil Bharthwal said . <p> Nearly 80% of India 's merchandise trade with Europe passes through the Red Sea where shipping has been attacked by Yemen 's Iran-backed Houthi militants , in a spillover from Israel 's more than three-month war with Hamas in Gaza . <p> Trade ministry official Satya Srinivas told reporters India 's December exports were not impacted by the Red Sea crisis , but some more recently consignments had been put on hold . <p> India 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ imports were $58.25 billion , government data showed on Monday . In the previous month , merchandise exports were $33.9 billion , while imports stood at $54.48 billion . <p> Merchandise exports for the period April-December totalled $317.12 billion , while imports for the same period reached $505.15 billion , the data showed . The figures compared with $336.30 billion in exports , and $548.64 billion in imports for the same period last year . <p> In December , services exports were $27.88 billion , while imports were $13.25 billion .
