<h> Children 's word of the year revealed by Oxford University Press <h> ' Climate change ' named Oxford children 's word of the year <p> More than 3,000 children aged six to 14 were asked for their word of the year ( Image : PA ) <p> " Climate change " has been declared the children 's word of the year by Oxford University Press ( OUP ) . <p> The award was based on feedback from more than 3,000 children aged six to 14 , with the words " war " and " coronation " following close behind . <p> A subsequent survey by market research agency Opinium , involving 1,000 children , revealed that " climate change " was chosen by 33 per cent of respondents , focusing on environmental transformation and global warming . <p> War and coronation were also popular choices ( Image : Canva ) <p> " War " was picked by a total of 31 per cent of children , while 24 per cent went with " coronation " . <p> Helen Freeman , director of Oxford Children 's , said : " The choice of ' climate change ' not only @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environment , but the research also reveals their willingness to engage with meaningful change . <p> " Whether it 's understanding how children use slang phrases or what word reflects the past year , it 's imperative we continue to support children 's language development so they have the means and vocabulary to express themselves . " <p> Children provided insight into their choice of " climate change " , commenting on environmental threats and their apprehensions about the future . <p> Coronation ranked highly ( Image : Olivia Christie ) <p> Feelings of sadness , fear and worry were often associated with the term by the children . <p> Meanwhile , the word " war " was chosen by 31 per cent of children , with ongoing conflicts in Europe , the Middle East , Israel , Gaza , and Ukraine cited as influences . <p> 13 per cent of children pointed to such news as the reason behind their selection , and 23 per cent saying either the conflict in Israel and Gaza or Russia 's invasion of Ukraine was the reason for their choice . <p> Amy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Plastic , said : " We also must n't ignore the findings from the language research which demonstrate young people 's desire for action . <p> " Young people not only recognise that climate change is an issue , but they 're also seeing the importance of tackling it . <p> " No-one will feel the negative effects of climate change more than the next generation -- or the positive effects of the action we take against it now . " <p> " Coronation " , evoking a happier sentiment , was chosen by 24 per cent of children as their word of the year , inspired by the crowning ceremony of the King in May . <p> The 2023 title went to " Queen " following Queen Elizabeth II 's passing and in 2022 , it was " anxiety " . <p> OUP also revealed that " bro " , " lol " ( laugh out loud ) and " slay " were shortlisted from a survey by Childwise , following a study on children 's colloquial use of language . <h> Comments : Our rules <p> We want @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ our community - a place where readers can debate and engage with the most important local issues .
