<h> Global shares slip again as rate cut euphoria fades <p> Global stock markets extended a New Year slide on Wednesday , while the dollar held firm , as market optimism about early U.S. interest rate cuts ebbed and the latest escalation of hostilities in the Middle East weighed on sentiment . <p> MSCI 's broad index of world equities slipped 0.3% to its lowest level in almost two weeks , while U.S. stock futures pointed to a weak open for Wall Street . <p> In a further sign that recent blistering gains for stocks and bonds were faltering , U.S. Treasury yields rose while European shares fell 0.7% and Asia Pacific shares outside Japan shed over 1% . <p> Caution was rising ahead of the release of minutes from the U.S. Federal Reserve 's December meeting , due at 1900 GMT on Wednesday , as well as a slew of important U.S. data this week . <p> Fed officials in December predicted 75 basis points ( bps ) of rate cuts in 2024 , driving money market bets for around double that amount of cuts that prompted a cross-market year-end rally . <p> " We had that whacking @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ convinced themselves there would be a soft ( economic ) landing , cooling inflation and a rapid pivot to rate cuts , " AJ Bell investment director Russ Mould said . <p> " But if you get an unexpected hard landing or an inflationary boom , you might get a slightly different script , so I guess people are now pausing for reflection .
