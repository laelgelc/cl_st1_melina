<p> Jordan 's King Abdullah said on Monday that Israel had created a whole generation of orphans by a " brutal " war in Gaza where he said around over 30,000 people , mostly women and children , had been killed or were missing as a result of the conflict . <p> In remarks at the Kigali Genocide Memorial in Rwanda , where the monarch spoke of " unspeakable crimes " during that African conflict , Abdullah said a lesson to be drawn was that Israel 's " indiscriminate aggression " in Gaza would never guarantee its security .
