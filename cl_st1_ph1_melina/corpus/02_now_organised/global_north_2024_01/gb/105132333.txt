<h> Gaza and Ukraine cast clouds over Davos <h> This week 's World Economic Forum will underscore how much of the world 's attention is on political developments away from Europe <p> Political elites and business titans have begun gathering in Davos this week for a World Economic Forum that will be the first barometer for global sentiment at the start of an already turbulent year . <p> Davos jamborees are not always the most representative gauge for the state of global affairs -- last year 's event , for example , was dominated by animus between American and European officials over green industrial policy , hostility that soon petered out ; the forum 's economists are now hailing activist industrial policy as a source for global " growth spots " -- but much of the world 's attention this week will be elsewhere rather than on the Swiss mountain town and its networking , political addresses and Alpine parties .
