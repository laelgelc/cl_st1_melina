<p> Two British Royal Navy mine-hunter vessels were involved in a collision in a port in Bahrain , the Royal Navy said . <p> No one was injured in the accident which occurred in Bahrain Harbour on Friday , it said . An investigation into the incident has been opened . <p> The Royal Navy is working with the U.S. Navy in the Gulf region in an effort to protect Red Sea shipping from an upsurge in attacks by Houthi forces based in Yemen . <p> The Houthis say they acting in solidarity with the Palestinian group Hamas , which is locked in conflict with Israel in Gaza .
