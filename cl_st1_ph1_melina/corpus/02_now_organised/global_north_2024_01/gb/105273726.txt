<h> Thousands march through city centre in peaceful call for end to anti-Semitism <p> A sea of people gathered with flags and posters for the peaceful demonstration ( Image : Jake Lindley / Manchester Evening News ) <p> Thousands marched through Manchester city centre on Sunday afternoon ( July 20 ) as they made a peaceful call for an end to anti-Semitism . <p> People gathered with flags and posters in hand for the demonstration , that organisers said was to be one of the city region 's largest single gatherings of Jewish people and supporters ever . <p> The march , organised by the Jewish Representative Council of Greater Manchester , set off from Manchester Cathedral at 2pm , along Deansgate before ending at Castlefield Bowl for a number of speeches . <p> The Union Jack and the flag of Israel were waved in the air as those taking part held up posters calling for an end to Jewish hatred , that read : " Say no to anti-Semitism " and " Enough is Enough , call out Jew hatred " .
