<h> Israeli hostage killed during failed rescue bid : army <p> Israeli hostage Sahar Baruch was killed last month during a rescue attempt by special forces in Gaza , the Israeli military says , without giving details . <p> Hamas said on December 8 that a hostage it named as Sa'ar Baruch had been killed during an attempted rescue operation . <p> " At this point , it is not possible to determine the circumstances of Sahar 's death , and it is not known whether he was murdered by Hamas or killed by our forces ' fire , " the military said in a statement . <p> Baruch was among the 240 hostages seized by Hamas gunmen when they stormed into southern Israel on October 7 . <p> More than 100 have since been released under agreements brokered by Qatar and other countries and one hostage was rescued by Israeli forces . <p> At least 123 remain in Gaza , of whom at least 23 have been declared dead by Israeli authorities .
