<p> Last week , the Israeli Supreme Court ruled on two major bills pushed by Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's far-right government as part of its judicial overhaul . The decision to strike down the first and delay activation of the second until after the next general election delivered a historic victory for Israel 's democratic forces . <p> The first bill would have eliminated the Supreme Court 's power to block ' extremely unreasonable ' government decisions . Both bills were approved by the Knesset during the summer as amendments to Israel 's Basic Laws , which serve as the country 's de facto constitution . <p> These rulings mark a watershed moment for Israeli democracy .
