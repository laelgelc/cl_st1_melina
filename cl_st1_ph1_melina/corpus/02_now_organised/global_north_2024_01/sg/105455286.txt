<h> In pictures : COP28 <h> Over the span of two weeks , a record-breaking number of participants gathered in Dubai for the annual UN climate change meeting . This year 's talks were dominated by negotiations over the language on fossil fuels and mobilising finance for a loss and damage fund . <p> As world leaders and negotiators descended on Dubai for COP28 , climate campaigners , corporate leaders and industry lobbyists alike seized the opportunity to get their voices heard . The result was a plethora of pavllions , panel discussions , press conferences and ( permitted ) protests that filled the halls and streets of Dubai 's Expo City . <p> While the United Nations said it expected more than 70,000 delegates to take part in this year 's climate change meeting , subsequent news reports pegged the actual number at over 100,000 people -- making it the largest COP gathering since its first meeting in 1995 .
