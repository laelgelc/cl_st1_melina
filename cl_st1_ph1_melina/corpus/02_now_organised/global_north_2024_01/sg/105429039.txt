PHOTO : MINISTRY OF NATIONAL DEFENSE , CHINA/ CHINA DAILY <p> January 26 , 2024 <p> BEIJING -- China 's defense ministry denied on Thursday an allegation that China has provided weapons or equipment to the Palestine-Israel conflict zone . <p> Wu Qian , a spokesperson for the Ministry of National Defense , made the remarks in response to a media query regarding Israeli claims that Hamas has been found using Chinese-made weapons in the Gaza Strip . <p> The spokesperson stressed that China has always been prudent and responsible in terms of military items export . <p> China is committed to three principles for arms export : it should be conducive to the legitimate self-defense @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ peace , security and stability of the region concerned and the world as a whole ; and it should not be used to interfere in the internal affairs of the recipient country , said the spokesperson . <p> The military also said that the Chinese military will continue conducting regular actions , including patrols , around Taiwan island , aiming to strengthen the ability to defend national sovereignty and territorial integrity . <p> When asked about Global Firepower 's latest military power ranking , Wu said capability , defense policy , and strategy all matter when judging a military . <p> " Even a superpower will collapse if addicted to war . Forgetting the war will be dangerous even though the world is peaceful , " he added .
