<h> Dawn <p> ISLAMABAD -- Caretaker Prime Minister Anwarul Haq Kakar on Monday said Baloch militant outfits were being funded by Indian intelligence agency Research and Analysis Wing ( RAW ) for carrying out terrorist acts in the country . <p> Speaking at a press conference in Lahore , the prime minister addressed those who had called for militants to be presented in courts , saying that close to 90,000 people were killed in the country yet " not even nine people have been convicted " . <p> " The criminal justice system is not right . They commit 80-90 murders after taking money from India . I announce that they do all this with funding from RAW . Deny that they do n't , " he said . <p> Commenting on the police action against Baloch protesters in Islamabad , the premier acknowledged that it should not have happened .
