<p> For all the focus on recent changes in the political mood on college campuses , the downfall of Harvard President Claudine Gay turns out to be a story about some of the oldest values of academia . <p> Gay , a political scientist , resigned today , making her the second president of an Ivy League institution to bow out in the past month . University of Pennsylvania President Liz Magill stepped down on December 9 , but the cases are not as similar as they might initially seem . Magill 's departure stemmed directly from the shaky December 5 congressional testimony by a panel of college presidents about anti-Semitism and the Israeli-Palestinian conflict , and was viewed as a victory for Elise Stefanik , the Republican representative who led the questioning . <p> What appear to have doomed Gay were the allegations of plagiarism lodged against her .
