However , the Nazis carried out their killings of part of a national genocidal project which mobilized all the elements of a modern , massive nation-state , a state whose population , generally speaking , knew that Jews were being removed from society . Unlike Hamas which is attempting to cause a reaction , the Nazi state had no need to publicize its killing of Jews ; it had the support of both government and people . Indeed , making its crimes public would have had the undesired effect of warning the victims . <p> The depiction of the Nazis being deployed in recent weeks has several false elements .
