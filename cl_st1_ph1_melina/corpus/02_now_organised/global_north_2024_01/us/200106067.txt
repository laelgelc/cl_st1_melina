<h> Bodies of 2 hostages recovered in Gaza , Israel says <p> The Israeli military says it has recovered the bodies of two hostages in Gaza and that they were brought back to Israel on Tuesday . <p> In a statement , the Israel Defense Forces said the two have been identified as Eden Zakaria , 27 , and Ziv Dado , 36 . <p> The IDF said Zakaria was at the music festival in southern Israel that came under attack on Oct. 7 when Hamas militants stormed the site . Dado , a soldier in the Israeli military , was a logistics supervisor in the Golani Brigade 's 51st Battalion and was taken hostage during the attacks , the statement said . <p> The Israeli military said it recovered the bodies of two hostages during an operation in the Gaza Strip , Dec. 12 , 2023 .
