<p> The videos keep coming : armed fighters taunting men with their hands tied behind their backs , bodies piled in an open grave . The videos are from Sudan , where two generals began a struggle for power last spring that has killed more than 12,000 people , displaced 6.6 million , and turned the nation into a battlefield , one that has flooded social media with images and videos that give the impression of a country burning alive . The Washington , DC -- based Sudanese women 's rights activist Niemat Ahmadi ca n't stop watching the footage . Nor can her friend and fellow activist Sadya Eisa Dahab .
