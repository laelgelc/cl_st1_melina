<h> UK latest country to pull funding for UNRWA for group 's links to Oct. 7 Hamas attack <p> The United Kingdom has joined other nations in halting funding for the United Nations Relief and Works Agency in Gaza , known as UNRWA . On Friday , the agency opened an investigation and announced several employees were fired due to their alleged involvement in Hamas 's attack on Israel on Oct. 7 . <p> " The UK is appalled by allegations that UNRWA staff were involved in the 7 October attack against Israel , a heinous act of terrorism that the UK Government has repeatedly condemned , " the Foreign and Commonwealth Office said in a statement . <p> Israel accused 12 employees of the UNRWA of participating in Hamas 's attack on Israel , which resulted in at least 1,200 people dead and more than 250 hostages taken .
