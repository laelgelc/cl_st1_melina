The suit was filed to the ICJ -- the main judicial body of the United Nations which settles international disputes among member states and answers requests for advisory opinion on legal questions . <p> More than 22,000 Palestinians have been killed since Hamas attacked Israel on Oct. 7 and killed 1,200 people and kidnapped some 200 others .
