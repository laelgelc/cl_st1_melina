Months later , while living in a refugee camp in the Philippines , they were hired to play extras in Francis Ford Coppola 's Vietnam War film " Apocalypse Now , " which came out 45 years ago and won two Oscars . <p> Though my parents played a variety of characters -- translators , Viet Cong , drivers , POWs -- they had no face time and no speaking parts . <p> They escaped a war only to be cast in a reenactment that placed them at the margins of their own story . <p> My mother , Hoa Le , owned two sets of clothes then : one to wear , one to wash .
