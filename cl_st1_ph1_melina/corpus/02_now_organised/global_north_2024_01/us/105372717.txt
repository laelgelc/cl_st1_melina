<h> U.S. crude oil rises 1% after winter storm hits domestic production <p> U.S. crude oil prices rose about 1% Wednesday after domestic production fell substantially in the wake of severe winter storms this month . <p> The West Texas Intermediate futures contract for March gained 72 cents , or 0.97% , to settle at $75.09 a barrel . The Brent futures March contract gained 49 cents , or 0.62% , to settle at $80.04 a barrel . <p> Production in the U.S. fell by an estimated 1 million barrels per day to 12.3 million bpd total for the week ending Jan. 19 , according to the Energy Information Agency . Commercial crude inventories in the U.S. dropped by 9.2 million barrels during the same period . <p> A blast of Arctic weather hit oil output in the U.S. last week particularly in North Dakota , the third-largest crude producing state in the nation .
