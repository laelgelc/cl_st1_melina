<p> Anti-Israel protesters vandalized and defaced the Los Angeles National Cemetery , where nearly 90,000 veterans who served the country from World War I through Vietnam are buried . <p> Video posted online showed a demonstrator spray-painting " Free Gaza " with an upside-down red triangle on the entrance to the National Cemetery as a protest shut down a major boulevard outside the US Federal Building on Saturday . <p> Demonstrators wearing traditional Palestinian keffiyehs were seen waving Palestinian flags and holding signs accusing President Biden of being an enabler of genocide and Zionists of being Nazis . <p> They chanted , " From the river to the sea , Palestine will be free , " a slogan that the Anti-Defamation League has labeled antisemitic , saying it calls for the elimination of the state of Israel . <p> Protesters also shouted " Long live Palestine , " " Long live intifada " and " Biden , Biden you 're a liar , we demand a cease-fire . " <p> City council candidate Sam Yebri , who is Jewish , accused the protesters of being antisemitic . <p> " Today , pro-Hamas extremists in LA sunk to a new @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ National Cemetery , where 85K American heroes who sacrificed their lives for our freedom are interred , " he wrote . <p> " They even used a Nazi concentration camp symbol of an upside down red triangle . <p> " The anti-America crowd returned not to clean up their vile crime but to add a pro-genocide against Jews postscript : ' intifada , ' " Yebri continued . <p> " Jews are becoming increasingly unsafe in America because our leaders refuse to acknowledge the danger posed by these growing extremist forces in America , " he claimed .
