<h> Other Papers Say : Dissuade freeway protests <p> Public officials at every level of government have a responsibility to ensure highway protests do not become regular occurrences . <p> This will take law enforcement coordination and a clear message that those seeking to influence national and international politics must not have unfettered ability to inflict pain on local residents . <p> Demonstrators calling for a cease-fire in the Israel-Hamas war blocked traffic on Interstate 5 in Seattle for hours on Jan. 6 . All northbound Interstate 5 lanes were blocked between Interstate 90 and Mercer Street . <p> Washington State Patrol Public Information Officer Rick Johnson first posted about the demonstration just before 1:30 p.m . At 3:40 p.m. , Washington State Patrol , with the assistance of the Seattle Police Department , issued a dispersal order for protesters to leave the freeway , according to the agency 's social media feed . Demonstrators left about a dozen vehicles on Interstate 5 . The State Patrol said tow trucks removed them and the freeway reopened shortly after 6 p.m . <p> Freeway protests are distressingly common .
