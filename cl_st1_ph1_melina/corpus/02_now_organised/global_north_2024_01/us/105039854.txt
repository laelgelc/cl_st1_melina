Griffin on Thursday said he and his 16 colleagues are still deliberating what those new rules might be . <p> Two issues are likely to influence the outcome . <p> First off , a Cleveland man who was cut off at council 's microphone last year has sued the body , saying its previous public comment rules amounted to unconstitutional restrictions on free speech , in part because they discriminated against particular viewpoints .
