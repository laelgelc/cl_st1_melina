<h> Part of a thinning generation of Silicon Valley female leaders , Sandberg stepped down as chief operating officer of Meta in 2022 -- after more than 14 years in the role <p> Former Meta chief operating officer Sheryl Sandberg is leaving the company 's board of directors after 12 years , marking the end of her leadership of the transformative -- and often controversial -- social media giant . <p> Sandberg , who helped turned the company from a viral social media site for college kids into one of the most profitable digital-advertising businesses in the world , announced she was stepping down from her role as COO in 2022 . She left Meta during a difficult financial period marked by multiple rounds of layoffs and revenue losses . <p> Since Sandberg 's departure from the C-suite , the company has evolved into a dramatically different entity . Meta chief executive Mark Zuckerberg , after laying off tens of thousands of workers , has sought to remake its culture to become more efficient and focused .
