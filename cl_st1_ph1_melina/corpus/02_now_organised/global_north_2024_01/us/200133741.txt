<h> Gov . Hochul , Chuck Schumer call for release of Hamas-held hostages <p> Gov . Kathy Hochul delivers remarks at the rally for Israeli hostages still held by Hamas terrorists in Gaza , marking 100 days in captivity , on Friday , Jan. 12 , 2024 in Manhattan , New York . ( Susan Watts / Office of Governor Kathy Hochul ) <p> Gov . Hochul and Sen. Chuck Schumer on Friday called for the release of Hamas-held hostages , joining a rally in Midtown Manhattan almost 100 days after the Oct. 7 terror attack in Israel . <p> " I want the hostages brought home , " Hochul said at the rally . " I want them brought home now . And I want the rest of the world to start saying the same thing . " <p> Two hostages held by Hamas are New Yorkers , according to Schumer 's office . " The people of Israel have a right to defend themselves , and they have a right to bring the hostages home . " <p> Hochul also said she was " sick and tired of the way this has been covered , " suggesting that the news media has not sufficiently focused on the hostages .
