The freedom to report the news is not only a cornerstone of democracy , but also a fundamental human right . According to Article 19 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights , the right " to seek , receive and impart information and ideas through any media and regardless of frontiers " should be protected . Barring international media from a conflict or indeed any other event is a dangerous precedent . Journalists must be able to exercise our duty to bear witness , no matter how difficult or dangerous that might be .
