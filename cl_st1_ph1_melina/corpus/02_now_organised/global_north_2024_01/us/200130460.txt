<p> The Israel Defense Forces revealed Thursday what it described as an elaborate Hamas tunnel in Gaza that has been " confirmed to have held Israeli hostages . " <p> Israel 's military said , " Millions of shekels are estimated to have been invested in excavating the tunnel " underneath the southern city of Khan Younis " and equipping it with air ventilation systems , electrical supply and plumbing . " <p> One million Israeli shekels are worth about $267,000 . The IDF wrote in a post on X that the tunnel was found to be " connected to an extensive network beneath a civilian area . " <p> A video released by the IDF purportedly shows soldiers walking through the tunnel , which has electrical wires leading down multiple staircases . In one room , a toilet and plumbing can be seen .
