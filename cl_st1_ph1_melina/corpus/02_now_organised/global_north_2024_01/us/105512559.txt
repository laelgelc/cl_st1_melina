<p> Two prominent Democratic senators are urging the Biden administration to use American leverage over Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to temper his devastating U.S.-backed campaign in Gaza , HuffPost has learned . Elizabeth Warren ( D-Mass. ) and Brian Schatz ( D-Hi. ) sent a letter to Secretary of State Antony Blinken on Tuesday asking for specific actions that President Joe Biden will take to change Israel 's course and " to advance an independent , self-governed Palestinian state . " <p> Their message comes after Netanyahu earlier this month said Israel can not accept a Palestinian state between the Jordan River and the Mediterranean Sea . Biden has for months resisted calls to do more to pressure Netanyahu , instead privately trying to win Israeli restraint .
