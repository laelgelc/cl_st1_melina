<h> History Suggests U.S. Airstrikes Against the Houthis Will Backfire <p> On Thursday , U.S. and British forces carried out air raids on targets throughout Yemen , hoping to damage the Ansar Allah militia , otherwise known as the Houthis . The strikes were retaliation for attacks on unarmed ships in the Red Sea , as well as U.S. naval vessels , that have crippled global shipping . <p> History suggests that the strikes are a mistake . They continue a pattern of the world misunderstanding and underestimating the Houthis . These militiamen have overcome significant geographical and technological hurdles to establish control over the Yemeni highlands , while striking Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates to the point that they were willing to negotiate . The strikes also ignore a long pattern of outside powers trying , and failing , to achieve military objectives in Yemen . <p> Air strikes may hurt the Houthis militarily in the short term .
