The Fed , ECB and BoE are expected to commence rate cuts as early as the first half of 2024 as inflation continues to normalize toward central banks ' target range and labor costs moderate . Consumers also appear well-positioned to weather a mild economic downturn , which could lead to a pickup in demand .
