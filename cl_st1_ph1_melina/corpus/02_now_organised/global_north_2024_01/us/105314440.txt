<h> Some Palestinians Want to Leave Gaza . Let Them . <p> Recently , I reached out to a prominent Palestinian activist to learn about his experiences in Gaza since the October 7 Hamas attack on Israel . He told me that his apartment had been destroyed , and that he lives in a tent with his family . They are under the near-constant threat of bombings , are often hungry , and are worried about starvation and sickness . He wants to leave the enclave -- but right now , he ca n't . <p> Several other Palestinians I 've talked with also want to leave Gaza , and have also encountered closed borders . Gaza is central to Palestinian history , and I would like people there to survive and thrive right where they are .
