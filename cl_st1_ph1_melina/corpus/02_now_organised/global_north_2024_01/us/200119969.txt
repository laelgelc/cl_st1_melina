( AP Photo/Lindsey Wasson ) <p> The Associated Press <p> SEATTLE -- Protesters calling for a cease-fire in the Israel-Hamas war blocked northbound traffic on Interstate 5 in Seattle for several hours Saturday . <p> Additional demonstrators on a nearby overpass cheered in support of the blockade , which began around 1:15 p.m. , the Seattle Times reported . The state transportation department said on the social media platform X that traffic at one point was backed up more than 6 miles ( 9.7 kilometers ) , and the agency asked drivers to use alternate routes . <p> Seattle police posted on social media at 3:40 p.m. that officers gave a dispersal order , though @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ including hail , moved through the area , and protesters left the freeway around 4:45 p.m. , according to the Times . <p> Trooper Rick Johnson with the Washington State Patrol said via X that while people had abandoned the freeway , 12 vehicles were left behind . He said the roadway would reopen when they were towed . <p> Hamas ' Oct. 7 attack from the Gaza Strip into southern Israel killed around 1,200 people , and some 250 others were taken hostage . It was the worst such attack in Israel 's history . <p> Since then the Health Ministry in Hamas-run Gaza said Saturday a total of 22,722 Palestinians have been killed in Israel 's subsequent assault . The count does not differentiate between combatants and civilians . <p> Israel has held Hamas responsible for civilian casualties , saying the group embeds itself within Gaza 's civilian infrastructure . Still , international criticism of Israel 's conduct has grown because of the rising civilian death toll .
