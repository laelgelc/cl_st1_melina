<p> It 's a story -- the attempted genocide of Europe 's Jews by the Nazis and their willing executioners -- that has been told many times before . But , say the creators of the new German TV drama The Interpreter of Silence , it 's a story that needs to be told again and again . <p> The five-part limited series , which bowed on Hulu in the U.S. and on Disney+ worldwide Nov. 15 , is up for this year 's Critics Choice Awards in the best foreign-language TV series category . The period drama will go up against the South Korean series Bargain , The Glory , Mask Girl and Moving , the French crime series Lupin , and the Italian mafia drama The Good Mothers . <p> Set in Frankfurt in 1963 , The Interpreter of Silence follows the events of Eva Bruhns , a 24-year-old German woman , played by Katharina Stark , who gets a job as a Polish-to-German interpreter in the Auschwitz trials , the first prosecution of former SS officers and Nazi functionaries to take place in post-war Germany .
