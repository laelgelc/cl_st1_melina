Biden previously joined union members on their picket line in Michigan when they were striking for better pay from the country 's largest automakers . <p> " I 'm honored to have your back and you have mine , " Biden said . Shawn Fain , the union president , said that Donald Trump , the likely Republican nominee , " does n't care about the American worker .
