They teach students to think critically about history . They help educators to develop their pedagogical skills , and they enable scholars to access archival documents . They provide diversity training to professionals in all types of industries . <p> After Hamas ' terrorist attacks in Israel on Oct. 7 , most institutions of Holocaust remembrance made statements of solidarity with the victims , as did many organizations around the world .
