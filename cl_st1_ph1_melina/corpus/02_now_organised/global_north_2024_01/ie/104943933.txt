<h> Colin Murphy : Fight against antisemitism should not stifle public debate <p> Smoke rises following an Israeli bombardment in the Gaza Strip . Photo : AP/Ariel Schalit <p> Colin Murphy <p> Sun 7 Jan 2024 at 03:30 <p> In his play The Merchant of Venice , Shakespeare tells the story of a merchant who borrows from a craven Jewish money-lender , Shylock , who demands a " pound of flesh " should the merchant forfeit . The merchant is a man who spits at Jews in the street , yet is considered the most honourable man in Italy . At the end of the play , which turns into a romantic comedy , Shylock is humiliated and forced to convert . <p> This is as good a place as any to start a discussion of antisemitism . It is a virus with deep roots in western , Christian culture , even if , thanks to the subtle processes of cultural formation , such as storytelling , it is often unconscious .
