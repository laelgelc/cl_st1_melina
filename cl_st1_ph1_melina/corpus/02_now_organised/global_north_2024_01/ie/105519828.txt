<p> In March , 38 representatives of the State will " bring Ireland 's message " to 86 cities in 48 countries . <p> The cadre , which includes every minister , 20 junior ministers , the Ceann Comhairle , the Cathaoirleach of the Seanad and the Attorney General , will head to the US , to Australia , to Brazil and China and Japan and Singapore and Cardiff as Ireland grabs the global spotlight for a week . <p> The scale of the exodus is a guaranteed point of contention and conversation every single year as focus shifts to the cost ( around ? 175,000 in 2022 ) and the perception among some sections of the country that the trips are little more than jollies taken on the taxpayer dime as ambassadors across the globe lay out the Ferrero Rocher and champagne . <p> But that cost is also relatively small - last year events were held in nearly every mission across the globe , with the 896 events costing less than ? 700 each on average for a total of ? 618,152 .
