<h> Solidarity rally for Palestine this Saturday in Galway City <p> An emergency solidarity rally with the people of Palestine will take place in Galway City this Saturday , February 3 at 2pm . <p> The rally , assembling at the Skeff side of Eyre Square , will once again demand an immediate and robust political response from the Irish Government as Israeli occupation forces continue their indiscriminate attacks in Gaza , where famine is looming . <p> The demo will include testimony from a Gazan who will provide a first-hand account of the dire situation on the ground , with Dr Angy Skuce , a member of Healthcare Workers for Palestine , discussing the heinous crimes committed against the healthcare system in Gaza . <p> Additionally , Eoghan Fox , President of the Galway Council of Trade Unions , will highlight the importance of the Boycott , Divestment , and Sanctions ( BDS ) movement in advocating for Palestinian rights . <p> The rally will also include a march to draw attention to complicit companies operating in Galway that support Israel . <p> The IPSC calls on all concerned citizens in Galway city and county to join in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Over 26,000 Palestinians , including more than 10,000 children , have been killed since October , with the Israeli government being accused of genocide . <p> About 85% of Gazans have been forcibly displaced , while the entire population is food insecure . <p> The Galway ISPC is calling on Ireland to support South Africa 's case at the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) and push for implementation of the Court 's binding provisional measures . <p> They said that the Irish Government must act now to prevent genocide , in accordance with its obligations under the Genocide Convention , by imposing lawful sanctions on Israel and on complicit corporate entities , as well as ending the bilateral arms trade and enacting the Occupied Territories Bill and the Illegal Israeli Settlements Divestment Bill .
