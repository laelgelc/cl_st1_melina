<h> Israel pulls troops from Gaza <p> Israeli soldiers ride a military vehicle near the border with Gaza . REUTERS <p> Thousands of Israeli soldiers are being shifted out of the Gaza Strip , the military said yesterday , in the first significant drawdown of troops since the war began as forces continued to bear down on the main city in the southern half of the enclave . <p> Toppling the Islamist faction remained an objective of the offensive in the Palestinian enclave , a military official said , adding some of the five brigades withdrawn will undergo training for a possible flare-up of a second front against Hezbollah in Lebanon . <p> Since launching the war in retaliation for the cross-border Hamas rampage of October 7 , Israeli officials have said they would wage it in three main stages . The first was intense shelling to clear access routes for ground forces and encourage civilians to evacuate . The second was the invasion that began on October 27 . <p> With tanks and troops having now overrun much of the Gaza Strip , the military is moving to the third stage , said the military . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ intense mopping-up missions against the terrorists , " the military said . <p> Two of the brigades being withdrawn were made up of reservists , a military official added , and described the move as designed to " re-energize the Israeli economy . " <p> Some of the troops pulled out of Gaza in the south were said to be preparing for rotation to the northern border with Lebanon , where Hezbollah has been exchanging fire with Israel in solidarity with the Palestinians . Israel has warned that if Hezbollah does not back down , a full-on Lebanon war looms . <p> Both Hamas and Hezbollah are backed by Iran , whose militant allies in Syria , Iraq and Yemen have also been carrying out longer-range attacks against Israel .
