<p> China 's commerce ministry has said it is closely following developments in the Red Sea , state media reported , after attacks by Huthi rebels on ships in solidarity with Palestinians in Gaza . <p> " It is hoped that the relevant parties will proceed from the overall interests of regional security and stability as well as the common interests of the international community , " ministry spokesman He Yadong said Thursday , according to state news agency Xinhua . <p> Beijing hopes those involved will " restore and ensure the safety of waterways in the Red Sea and jointly maintain the smooth flow of global production and supply chains as well as the normal order of international trade " , Xinhua reported He as saying . <p> " We will also strengthen coordination with relevant departments , closely follow relevant developments , and provide timely support and assistance to foreign trade companies , " He said .
