<h> GUEST BLOG : GEOFFREY MILLER -- New Zealand 's huge shift in the Middle East <p> A decision to provide intelligence support for future US and UK airstrikes on Yemen is highly symbolic . <p> The Prime Minister , Christopher Luxon , announced the deployment of the New Zealand Defence Force ( NZDF ) troops to support the US-led military response to the attacks on commercial shipping from Houthis in Yemen that began on November 19 . <p> In announcing the contribution , Luxon played down its uniqueness , saying ' choosing to support action in the Middle East is not unusual for New Zealand ' . This was immediately echoed by his foreign minister , Winston Peters , who argued the ' support for maritime security in the Middle East is not new ' . <p> A ' fact sheet ' released by the Government compared the NZDF contribution to other multilateral efforts in the Middle East such as the UN Truce Supervision Organisation ( UNTSO ) mission that has been ongoing since 1954 . <p> The fact sheet also pointed to New Zealand 's role in the Bahrain-based Combined Maritime Forces ( CMF ) , a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ since 2008 and continuously since 2013 . <p> The CMF brings together 40 countries in four separate Combined Task Forces ( CTF ) . While all of these overlap to some extent , New Zealand has traditionally focused its contribution of up to 12 personnel on CTF 150 , which concentrates more on criminal activities such as piracy , narcotics and smuggling . <p> To date , New Zealand has not been involved in CTF 153 , ' Red Sea Maritime Security ' , a relatively new mission that was established in April 2022 . CTF 153 is now serving as the umbrella for ' Operation Prosperity Guardian ' , a new naval patrol mission involving over 20 countries announced in December by US Defence Secretary Lloyd Austin . <p> TDB Recommends NewzEngine.com <p> New Zealand 's announcement made no mention of Operation Prosperity Guardian . <p> Instead , the NZDF contribution appears to be for the much smaller and far more elite coalition that is backing joint US-UK airstrikes on Yemen . <p> US Central Command ( Centcom ) listed Australia , Bahrain , Canada and the Netherlands as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ conducted earlier this week . <p> They are the same countries that participated in the first wave of joint airstrikes , against 60 Houthi targets , on January 11 . <p> New Zealand signed a joint statement in support of that operation , but was not otherwise involved in it . <p> Following Tuesday 's announcement , New Zealand can now expect to be included on the list of supporting countries for future airstrikes . So far , the aim of these has been to destroy Houthi infrastructure such as missile systems , radar units and other military facilities . <p> In between the joint missions , the US has also conducted smaller airstrikes against Houthi infrastructure on its own , according to Centcom . <p> The bombings constitute the sharper , more hawkish end of Washington 's military response to the Houthis . <p> will be one of just a handful of countries that are bombing Yemen . <p> As Richard Harman points out , many countries willing to participate in Operation Prosperity Guardian , such as Singapore , are the Middle East . <p> Unlike Australia , New Zealand strongly opposed the US-led invasion of Iraq in 2003 . <p> Wellington did deploy troops to the anti-ISIS coalition in February 2015 -- with then suggestion our ongoing support for maritime security in the Middle East is connected to recent developments in Israel and the Gaza Strip , is wrong ' . <p> However , the Houthis have clearly linked
