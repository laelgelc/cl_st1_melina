<h> Weaponizing The Crime Of Genocide <p> All too often the murder of people begins with the murder of language . The evil process that led to the Nazi Holocaust began with an assault on words . Deportations to concentration camps were described as " resettlement " , gas chambers as " showers " , and the diabolical project to annihilate an entire people was branded as " the final solution " .
