<p> An influx of new releases from Kelly Clarkson , Miley Cyrus and Kelsea Ballerini reimagine the divorce album in all its complexity . While the music industry has long been youth-obsessed , there may be something to the fact that these musicians are all women in their 30s and 40s and consequently possess a kind of self-assuredness and rich , emotional maturity . In a culture where relatability is currency , relationship stories with the weight and wisdom of age register as fresh . If all popstars are teenaged , where does that leave the rest of us ?
