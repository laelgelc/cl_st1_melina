<p> I vividly remember seeing empty and damaged store locations for Best Mart 360 , which is a public food retail company . For months , it faced an unprecedented boycott campaign from pro-democracy groups due to its CEO 's alleged affiliations with Beijing . <p> Then the COVID-19 pandemic arrived . The subsequent enforcement of Beijing 's National Security Law meant , ironically , that protesters ' calls for a boycott lost widespread public attention within months . Between 2019 and 2023 , Best Mart 360 's revenue has only increased year-over-year . <p> Now , in Canada , pro-Palestine groups have enlarged and strengthened Boycott , Divestment , Sanctions ( BDS ) movements against companies that have ties with the Israeli state . The movement follows the aftermath of the October 7 attack on Israel waged by Hamas , which set the stage for the bloodiest conflict in the region in recent decades . It led to Israel directly engaging in a military counter-offensive in the Gaza Strip , which has since led to over 26,000 deaths in Gaza , as of January 27 .
