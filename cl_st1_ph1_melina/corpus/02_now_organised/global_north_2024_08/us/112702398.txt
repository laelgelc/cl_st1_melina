<h> Why is Christian Science in our name ? <p> Our name is about honesty . The Monitor is owned by The Christian Science Church , and we 've always been transparent about that . <p> The Church publishes the Monitor because it sees good journalism as vital to progress in the world . Since 1908 , we 've aimed " to injure no man , but to bless all mankind , " as our founder , Mary Baker Eddy , put it . <p> Here , you 'll find award-winning journalism not driven by commercial influences -- a news organization that takes seriously its mission to uplift the world by seeking solutions and finding reasons for credible hope . <p> Last week , Ghada Abdulfattah evacuated her home . Our correspondent in Gaza has evacuated before , but she 's always been able to move between family homes . For The Christian Science Monitor , this is a profound question . We were founded on the principle that spirituality makes these qualities inexhaustible .
