<p> For the first time in recent years , a majority of Americans oppose sending U.S. troops to defend Israel if it comes under attack by its neighbors , according to a nationwide poll released Tuesday . <p> The survey by the Chicago Council on Global Affairs found that 55 percent of Americans opposed sending U.S. troops to defend Israel if its neighbors attacked it , while 41 percent supported doing so .
