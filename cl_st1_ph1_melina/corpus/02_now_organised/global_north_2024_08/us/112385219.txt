It 's just off the midrachov , the pedestrian main street , filled with small shops and eateries . Maura 's first job in Israel was as a landscape architect in Jerusalem . <p> The Ruskins moved to Zichron Ya'acov , wanting to forge a new path , both personally and professionally .
