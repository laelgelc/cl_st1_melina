" <h> The Battle of Ammunition Hill <p> The Battle of Ammunition Hill was one of the fiercest and most significant battles during the Six Day War , particularly in the context of the battle for Jerusalem . It took place on June 6 , 1967 , and was a crucial part of the larger effort to reunify the city , which had been divided under partial Israeli and partial Jordanian control since 1948 . <p> The battle quickly devolved into brutal close-quarters combat . Israeli paratroopers had to fight their way through a labyrinth of trenches and bunkers , often engaging in hand-to-hand combat with the Jordanian soldiers . <p> The fighting was intense , with both sides suffering heavy casualties .
