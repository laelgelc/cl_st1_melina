<p> JERUSALEM -- More than 40,000 Palestinians have been killed in Israel 's military campaign in the Gaza Strip , the local Health Ministry said Thursday -- a bleak indicator of the war 's toll even as a full count remained out of reach amid a near-total collapse of the enclave 's health-care system . <p> The official figure of 40,005 killed since October does not distinguish between civilians and combatants . But the Gaza Health Ministry , which has operated for years under the Hamas-led government , says the majority of the dead are women and children . At least 92,401 have also been injured over more than 10 months of war . <p> Israel launched its assault on Oct. 7 after Hamas militants attacked southern Israel , killing about 1,200 people and taking about 250 others to Gaza as hostages . More than 300 Israeli soldiers have also died in combat since a ground invasion began in late October . <p> Displaced Palestinians plead for peace ahead of Gaza cease-fire talks in Qatar on Aug. 15 , as the death toll reaches 40,000 , the local Health Ministry said .
