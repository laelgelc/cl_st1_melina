<h> Supreme Court , justices deserve our complaints <p> A recent letter writer criticized people for attacking the Supreme Court when it overturned Roe v. Wade and claiming that was wrongly decided , and instead of complaining , we should vote . However , the writer 's premise being incorrect , we should continue complaining . Roe followed the court 's own 1965 decision in Griswold v. Connecticut ( never reversed ) that held that we have a constitutional right to privacy , whether it be the right to use contraceptives ( Griswold ) or a woman 's right whether or not to terminate her pregnancy ( Roe ) . We should complain when Republicans denied President Obama the right to appoint a Supreme Court justice ; when President Trump 's three appointees misled Congress and Americans when they claimed that Roe was established law and then could not wait to overturn it ; and when they ignored 50 years of precedence -- never has the court taken away a constitutional right by overruling itself .
