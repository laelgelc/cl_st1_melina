<p> Relatives of the captive Bibas family released a video on Thursday urging organizers of the Netherlands ' annual Redhead Days Festival to advocate for the release of their loved ones from Gaza - who have become well known for their striking orange hair . <p> Kfir and Ariel , now 1 and 5 years old , are the youngest hostages to have been abducted by Hamas on October 7 - along with their parents . <p> The festival has a 20 year history , and redheads from over 80 countries gather at the annual meet up . People attend the annual Redhead Days Festival in Tilburg , Netherlands , August 27 , 2023.
