<h> Israel Is Buying Google Ads to Discredit the UN 's Top Gaza Aid Agency <p> Back In mid-January , Mara Kronenfeld was googling the name of the nonprofit she runs , which raises money in the US on behalf of the leading humanitarian aid provider in Gaza . It read like a promo from the UN agency , but the link directed to an Israeli government website . Kronenfeld says she had found the beginnings of a months-long online advertising campaign by Israel to discredit and defund UNRWA . <p> About the time Kronenfeld encountered the ads , Israel had accused 12 UNRWA staffers of participating in the deadly attack by Hamas extremists on Israel last October .
