<h> Statue of Anne Frank in Amsterdam defaced with pro-Palestinian graffiti for a second time <p> The statue commemorating Anne Frank , one of the most famous victims of the Holocaust , was defaced with pro-Palestinian graffiti for the second time on Sunday . <p> The statue is located in Merwedeplein , near the Anne Frank House in Amsterdam . <p> According to images published on X , the base of the statue was spray-painted with the slogan " Free Gaza " while the girl 's hands were painted with the same red color , AFP reported . <p> Police have opened an investigation into the most recent defacement , which likely occurred overnight Saturday into Sunday . An Amsterdam police spokesman told AFP that no suspect had yet been identified . <p> Frank , who died aged 16 in a Nazi concentration camp in 1945 , is an icon of the Dutch Jewish community . Her diary has become one of the most influential accounts of the Holocaust , which wiped out around three-quarters of the country 's 140,000 Jews during the Nazi German occupation . <p> The Amsterdam house where the Frank family took refuge for two @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after which they were sent to the Bergen-Belsen concentration camp -- has become a museum dedicated to her story . <p> Sunday marks 80 years since the Nazis arrested her family on August 4 , 1944 . <p> It is the second time in less than a month that the statue of one of the world 's most recognizable victims of the Holocaust has been targeted , according to television station AT5 and confirmed to AFP by the police source . <p> AT5 said the statue in a southern Amsterdam park was vandalized on July 9 , after which the municipality called for increased protection of the site with video surveillance cameras and night-time lighting . <p> Antisemitic incidents have increased across the world since the beginning of Israel 's war with Palestinian militants in the Gaza Strip sparked by Hamas ' October 7 attacks . <p> The October 7 attacks resulted in the deaths of 1,197 people , mostly civilians .
