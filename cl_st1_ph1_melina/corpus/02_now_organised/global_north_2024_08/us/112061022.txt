" The proposed negotiations start Aug. 15 in either Cairo or Doha , Qatar . <h> Who said what <p> " It is time to release the hostages , begin the cease-fire and implement this agreement , " President Joe Biden , Egypt President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi and Qatari Emir Tamim al-Thani said in the joint statement .
