<h> Namibia barred the MV Kathrin from docking over suspicions of carrying military cargo for Israel , aligning with its support for Palestine during the Gaza conflict . <p> The vessel MV Kathrin , en route from Vietnam , was blocked from docking in Namibia on suspicion of carrying military cargo for Israel 's ongoing war against Hamas in the Gaza Strip , the BBC reported on Tuesday night . <p> The ship was stopped due to " explosive material destined for Israel , " Namibian Justice Minister Yvonne Dausab claimed . The ship , which left from Vietnam , had requested to dock at Walvis Bay before continuing north towards the Mediterranean . <p> Walvis Bay , Namibia 's largest commercial port , is located on the western side of the African continent . It handles nearly 900 vessels and about eight million tonnes of cargo each year , the BBC cited the Namibian Ports Authority ( Namport ) .
