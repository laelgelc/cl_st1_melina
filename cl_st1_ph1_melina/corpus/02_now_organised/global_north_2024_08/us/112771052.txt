<p> A second United States aircraft carrier , the USS Abraham Lincoln , arrived in the Middle East on Wednesday escorted by guided missile destroyers , the U.S. Central Command said . <p> The carrier , equipped with F-35 fighter jets , added to the U.S. ships already in the region , including the USS Theodore Roosevelt aircraft carrier , which arrived in mid-July . Defense Secretary Lloyd Austin ordered the Lincoln to transit from the Indo-Pacific to the Middle East earlier this month as the Biden administration prepares to defend Israel against an expected Iranian attack . <p> In addition to the Lincoln , Austin ordered the USS Georgia , a guided missile submarine , to the Middle East in a rare acknowledgment of U.S. submarine whereabouts .
