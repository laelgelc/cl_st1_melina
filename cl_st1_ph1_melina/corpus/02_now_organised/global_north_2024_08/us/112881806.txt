<h> Is Joe Biden Really The Most Pro-Union President Ever ? <p> Joe Biden likes to think of himself as the " most pro-union president " in U.S. history . As his time in the White House winds down and his legacy begins to take shape , there 's no doubt that he has a strong case to make for himself . <p> Biden , sometimes called Union Joe , has spoken unabashedly about the value of collective bargaining throughout his one term as president . He 's stacked labor agencies with pro-union appointees who are making it easier for workers to organize . He 's openly scolded employers for trying to prevent their workers from forming unions . And he 's even stood alongside striking autoworkers on a picket line -- a first for a sitting president . <p> Advertisement <p> Yet union membership is hovering near a historic low following a decades-long decline , with barely 1 in 10 workers belonging to a union .
