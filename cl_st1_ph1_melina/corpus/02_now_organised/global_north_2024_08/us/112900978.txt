<p> CHICAGO -- Jesse Jackson sat in a wheelchair near the back wall of a darkened restaurant at the Hyatt Regency McCormick Place . It was late Thursday morning , the final day of the Democratic National Convention . Slowed by Parkinson 's disease at age 82 , the reverend was eating , or trying to eat , a breakfast of strawberries , grapes and cantaloupe . <p> With determined effort , he would stab his fork into a piece of fruit , then lift it slowly toward his mouth .
