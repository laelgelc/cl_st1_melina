<h> A Powerful Lobbying Group Is Coming for Another Member of the Squad <p> Progressive congresswoman and " Squad " member Cori Bush is in the fight for her political life in Missouri , hoping a grassroots campaign can fend off a well-funded primary challenge from local prosecutor and Democrat Wesley Bell . A key difference between these two Democrats is the backing of the influential American Israel Public Affairs Committee : Bell has it , and Bush does not . The primary , which will be decided by voters on Tuesday , is another test of political might for the outside group determined to oust as many critics of Israel 's war in Gaza from Congress as possible this election cycle . <p> Bush is the incumbent , holding Missouri 's 1st Congressional District 's seat since 2021 , after wresting it from 10-term incumbent Democrat William Lacy Clay in a 2020 primary . In the process , she became the first Black congresswoman to represent Missouri and the first activist from the Black Lives Matter movement to earn a seat in Congress .
