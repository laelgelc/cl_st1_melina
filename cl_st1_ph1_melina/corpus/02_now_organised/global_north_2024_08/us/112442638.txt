It was Biden 's first joint event with Harris since the president dropped his reelection bid and endorsed his second-in-command . Thursday 's focus was on one of Biden 's legislative victories -- the Inflation Reduction Act . <p> Harris cast the tiebreaking vote on the 2022 law , which authorized Medicare to negotiate drug prices , among an assortment of other reforms .
