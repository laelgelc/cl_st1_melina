<h> Services for seniors at risk without funds <p> A significant issue has emerged from the recently passed Pennsylvania budget that threatens essential services for seniors in Lehigh County . Key services such as home-delivered meals , personal care assistance and senior center operations are at risk due to a budget shortfall . The Department of Aging had requested a 5% increase of $15.2 million from the Pennsylvania Lottery Fund to support these services and address critical responsibilities , including investigating neglect , abuse and financial exploitation . <p> Without this additional funding , Pennsylvania 's Area Agencies on Aging , including our Lehigh County Office of Aging and Adult Services , will face a deficit of approximately $404,000 -- 5.5% of our budget . This shortfall forces us to contemplate creating waiting lists and potentially reducing or eliminating vital services such as community centers , home-delivered meals , transportation and home care aides .
