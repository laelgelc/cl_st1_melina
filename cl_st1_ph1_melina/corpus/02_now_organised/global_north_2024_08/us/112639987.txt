<p> CHICAGO ( CBS ) -- The second night of the Democratic National Convention was also set to be the second night of protests in Chicago on Tuesday . <p> The group Behind Enemy Lines is set to protest at 7 p.m. outside the Israeli Consulate , located in the Accenture Tower at 500 W. Madison St. in the West Loop -- 1.75 miles due east of the United Center where the convention is under way . <p> Across the street , a pro-Israeli counterprotest was planned for the same time . <p> Chicago Police were on the scene ahead of time , using the intersection of Madison and Clinton streets as a staging area . Police officers on bicycles were lined up outside the entrance . <p> Metra announced Tuesday afternoon that the entrance to the Ogilvie Transportation Center from the Accenture Tower will be closed for the evening due to the planned protest . <p> Commuters on the Union Pacific lines should enter through the French Market doors a block north on Clinton Street , Metra said . <p> Businesses nearby also closed early due to " unforeseen circumstances .
