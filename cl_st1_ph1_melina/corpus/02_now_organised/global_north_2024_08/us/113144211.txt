Tim Walz ( D-MN ) , sits down for a CNN interview that airs at 9 p.m . EST . It will be Harris 's first extended question-and-answer session with the news media since her campaign began . <p> With more than a month passed and several news cycles come and gone , the list of questions Harris faces is almost limitless .
