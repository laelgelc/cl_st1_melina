<p> Rep. Ilhan Omar , D-Minn. , handily won her primary race on Tuesday against an opponent , Don Samuels , who was boosted by fundraising from pro-Israel donors . <p> Elected in 2018 , Omar is now almost certain to clinch re-election in November to represent Minnesota 's deep-blue 5th District . She defeated Samuels in the 2022 primary . <p> Omar told supporters on Tuesday night that she was " incredibly honored " by the results . " I am honored to represent the people who welcomed me and my family as refugees to this incredible state , " she said . <p> The victory by Omar , a leading pro-Palestinian advocate in Congress and a vocal critic of Israel , comes on the heels of primary losses for Reps . Cori Bush and Jamaal Bowman , fellow progressive " Squad " colleagues who have also condemned Israel 's military assault on Gaza . <p> Bush and Bowman were defeated by opponents backed by the pro-Israel group AIPAC . The United Democracy Project , a super PAC affiliated with the group , poured millions of dollars into both races , buying ads attacking the incumbents .
