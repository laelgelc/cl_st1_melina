<p> It 's been three years since the Taliban took control of Afghanistan . They have transitioned from insurgency to authority , imposed their interpretation of Islamic law and sought to reinforce their claim to legitimacy . <p> Despite no international recognition as the country 's official rulers , the Taliban enjoy high-level meetings with major regional powers like China and Russia . They even attended United Nations-sponsored talks while Afghan women and civil society were denied a seat at the table . It was a triumph for the Taliban , who see themselves as the country 's only true representatives . <p> There 's no domestic challenge to their rule , and no overseas appetite to support one . Wars in Ukraine and Gaza draw the international community 's focus , and Afghanistan does n't represent the same terror threat it once did .
