<h> China urges citizens to take ' caution ' in Lebanon travel <p> China 's embassy in Beirut urged citizens to " travel with caution " should they visit Lebanon , warning they face " higher security risks " as fears of a regional conflict soar . ( AFP ) <p> China 's embassy in Beirut urged citizens to " travel with caution " should they visit Lebanon , warning they face " higher security risks " as fears of a regional conflict soar . <p> In a statement issued Monday evening Beijing time , the embassy warned citizens the situation in the country was " grave and complex " . <p> " The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Chinese Embassy in Lebanon remind Chinese citizens to closely monitor the evolution of the local situation and to travel with caution in Lebanon in the near future , " the embassy said on its official WeChat account . <p> As Israel 's war against Iran-backed Hamas in Gaza nears the 11th month , the Tehran-aligned " Axis of Resistance " is widely expected to retaliate after the killing of two senior figures .
