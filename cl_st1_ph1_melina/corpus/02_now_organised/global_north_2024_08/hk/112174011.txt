<h> Israel steps up evacuation tempo after school strike <p> Israel expanded evacuation orders in Khan Younis in the southern Gaza Strip overnight , forcing tens of thousands of Palestinian residents and displaced families to leave in the dark as explosions from tank shelling reverberated around them . <p> The Israeli military said it was attacking militants from the Hamas group - which administered Gaza before the war - who were using those areas to stage attacks and fire rockets . <p> On Saturday , an Israeli airstrike on a school where Palestinians were sheltering in Gaza City killed at least 90 people , prompting an international outcry . <p> The Israeli military said it had struck a Hamas and Islamic Jihad militant command post , an allegation the two groups rejected as a pretext , and killed 19 militants . <p> In Khan Younis in the south of the Gaza Strip , the evacuation instruction covered districts in the center , east and west , making it one of the largest such orders in the 10-month-old conflict , two days after tanks returned to the east of the city . <p> The announcement was posted on X and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " For your own safety , you must <p> evacuate immediately to the newly created humanitarian zone .
