<p> In the phone call , Wang repeated Beijing 's denunciation of the assassination of Hamas chief Ismail Haniyeh in Tehran on July 31 , saying the strike had violated Iran 's sovereignty and threatened regional stability . <p> Iran and the Palestinian Islamist group Hamas have accused Israel of carrying out strike that killed Haniyeh . <p> Israel has not claimed or denied responsibility for the killing , which has fueled concern that the war between Israel and Hamas in the Gaza Strip was turning into a wider Middle East war . <p> " China supports Iran in defending its sovereignty , security and national dignity in accordance with the law , and in its efforts to maintain regional peace and stability , and stands ready to maintain close communication with Iran , " Wang was quoted as saying . <p> Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian on Sunday nominated Abbas Araqchi as the country 's foreign minister . Araqchi had been Iran 's chief negotiator in nuclear talks from 2013 to 2021.
