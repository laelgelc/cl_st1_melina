<p> When Democratic presidential candidate Kamala Harris announced Tuesday morning -- just over three months till Election Day -- that she 'd chosen Minnesota Governor Tim Walz as her running mate , she responded to the call of the progressive wing of her party . She brought in a folksy Midwesterner -- and former high school football coach -- to balance her identity as a Californian and Washington , D.C. insider . But mostly , she rejected the safety of electoral math for something that might be even more powerful -- a sense of fun . Smart leaders know that fun is one of the simplest and most powerful elements you can bring to any company , or any project . <p> Walz , of course , is most famous these days for pinning the word " weird " on the Republican ticket Harris is hoping to beat . Walz deployed that word again and again when describing presidential nominee Donald Trump and vice presidential nominee JD Vance . It 's fun to say , and even more fun to use against people and ideas you profoundly disagree with .
