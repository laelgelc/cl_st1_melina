<h> Israeli military says it has recovered the bodies of 6 hostages in a Gaza operation <p> JERUSALEM ( AP ) -- The Israeli military says it has recovered the bodies of six hostages taken in Hamas ' Oct. 7 attack that started the Gaza war . <p> The military said in a statement Tuesday that its forces recovered the bodies in an overnight operation in southern Gaza . It identified the hostages as Yagev Buchshtab , Alexander Dancyg , Avraham Munder , Yoram Metzger , Nadav Popplewell , and Haim Perry , without saying when or how they died . <p> The recovery came as the the United States , Egypt and Qatar are trying to mediate a cease-fire agreement between Israel and Hamas that would see the release of scores of hostages held by the militant group . <p> Hamas is still believed to be holding around 110 hostages captured in the Oct. 7 attack . Israeli authorities estimate around a third of them are dead .
