<h> US approves $20 billion in weapons sales to Israel amid threat of wider Middle East war <p> WASHINGTON ( AP ) -- The U.S. has approved $20 billion in arms sales to Israel , including scores of fighter jets and advanced air-to-air missiles , the State Department announced Tuesday . <p> Congress was notified of the impending sale , which includes more than 50 F-15 fighter jets , Advanced Medium Range Air-to-Air Missiles , or AMRAAMs , 120 mm tank ammunition and high explosive mortars and tactical vehicles and comes at a time of intense concern that Israel may become involved in a wider Middle East war . <p> However , the weapons are not expected to get to Israel anytime soon , they are contracts that will take years to fulfill . Much of what is being sold is to help Israel increase its military capability in the long term . <p> The Biden administration has had to balance its continued support for Israel with a growing number of calls from lawmakers and the U.S. public to curb military support there due to the high number of civilian deaths in Gaza .
