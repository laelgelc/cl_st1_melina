<p> CIVICUS , a global civil society alliance , is concerned about the ongoing erosion of civic freedoms in Australia due to increasing anti-protest laws , the stifling of protests and efforts to silence whistleblowers in recent years . <p> These actions , highlighted in a brief published today , are inconsistent with commitments made by Australia to the UN Human Rights Council . They also contravene its international human rights obligations guaranteed in the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights ( ICCPR ) , which the country is a state party to . Some of the provisions in the laws are unnecessary , disproportionate and are inconsistent with Australia 's international human rights obligations .
