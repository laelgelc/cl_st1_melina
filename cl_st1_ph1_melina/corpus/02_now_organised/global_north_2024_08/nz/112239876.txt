<h> This Is The Foreseeable Future <p> Predicting the future used to be a fool 's errand . Even half a century ago , things were a lot more uncertain than they are now . Which is a paradox because the verities of the past have never been more uncertain . <p> There 's a loss of faith in science , yet there 's a widespread belief that technology will save us . Democracy increasingly serves only the very rich , yet we 're told our vote counts more than ever . <p> Organized religion is as outdated as the red robes of Roman Catholic cardinals , yet the importance of the inner life has never been clearer . Capitalism has become an all-consuming economic system for which borders are irrelevant , yet nationalism and protectionism are rearing their ugly heads . <p> All told , the comforting emotionally held belief that nothing can or will fundamentally change has become doctrine .
