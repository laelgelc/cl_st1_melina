<h> Federal Judge Rules Against Coalition To March On The DNC 2024 <h> A federal judge has ruled that the Coalition to March on the DNC must remain two blocks away from the convention . <p> The Democratic National Convention will be held in Chicago from August 19th to 22nd . On Monday night a federal judge in Chicago ruled against the Coalition to March on the DNC 2024 , who were appealing the city 's denial of their proposed march route . <p> The city initially attempted to keep them in Grant Park three miles from the United Center , where the convention will be held . It eventually conceded to their demand to rally in Union Park , which is 15 minutes away . The coalition wanted to march for 2.3 miles on wide streets that pass alongside the convention center , but the city 's route confines them to 1.1 miles , largely on narrow streets and several blocks from the convention center . <p> A circuit court has been set aside for individual and mass arrests during the convention . <p> I spoke to Coalition organizer Faayani Aboma Mijana . <p> ANN GARRISON : Who @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ FAAYANI ABOMA MIJANA : Our coalition is over 200 organizations from 21 states around the country , and our principal demand is to stand with Palestine and end US military aid to Israel .
