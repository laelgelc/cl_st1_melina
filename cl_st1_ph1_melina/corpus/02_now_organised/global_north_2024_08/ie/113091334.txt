<p> The event will feature 15 open mic slots and readings from special guests Ahmed Alnaouq , Walaa Sabah , Olivia Fitzsimons , Lianne O'Hara , Nithy Kasa and Janet Moran . All proceeds from the open mic will go towards the youth-led Palestinian nonprofit We Are Not Numbers based in the Gaza Strip . <p> Scheduled for this Sunday , 1 September , from 7-9pm on Zoom , the evening will comprise readings from special guests and 15 open mic slots . <p> The event will feature such guests as Ahmed Alnaouq , a London-based Gazan who served as the original project manager of We Are Not Numbers and now acts as Steering Committee member . A couple weeks ago , Alnaouq hosted a conversation with Pink Floyd 's Roger Waters and English author Matt Kennard discussing the ongoing crisis in Palestine and more . <p> Also among the special guests is Northern Irish writer Olivia Fitzsimons , whose debut novel The Quiet Whispers Never Stop was shortlisted for the Kate O'Brien and Butler Literary Awards . <p> Current project manager of We Are Not Numbers , Walaa Sabah , will make an appearance . Sabah was the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Spirit of Mother Jones Festival in Cork , as well as special guest at the second Open Mic for Gaza . <h> Advertisement <p> Poet and playwright Lianne O'Hara will also make an appearance , as will Congolese-Irish poet Nithy Kasa , Dublin actor and playwright Janet Moran . <p> As well as readings and talks from special guests , the 15 open mic slots are for up to 3 minutes per person . If you would like to join the open mic , you can express interest via the sign-up form , with a short bio . Those wishing to donate to the cause are encouraged to do so via the same form . <p> The youth-led nonprofit project We Are Not Numbers ( WANN ) are based in the Gaza Strip . It strives to share the stories behind the numbers of Palestinians in the news and advocates for their human rights .
