<p> A large crowd gathered in Cork city today for the 41st weekly Palestine solidarity march , consisting of approximately 800 people . <p> The march , organised by the Cork branch of the Ireland Palestine Solidarity Campaign , was led by children togged off in sports kit , carrying footballs and other sports gear , and commemorated the 69 Palestinian Olympians who were killed by Israeli airstrikes or as a result of Israel blocking the transit of food and medical aid into Gaza . <p> Children taking part in the march carried placards with slogans such as ' What if it was us ? ' and ' Please help us protect our friends in Palestine ' . <p> Speaking at the march , Kathy Glavanis , co-chair of the Cork branch of the Ireland Palestine Solidarity Campaign said : " As the Olympic games unfold in Paris , today in Cork we marched in our hundreds to remember the Palestinian athletes whose lives have been brutally taken by Israeli attacks . <p> " Four hundred Palestinian athletes have been killed since October 2023 , including 69 Olympians . <p> " To see not only the careers but also @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a tragic way is nothing short of horrific . " <p> Figures from the Palestinian Olympic Committee and the Palestine Football Association indicate that approximately 400 Palestinian athletes have been killed since last October while gyms , pitches , stadiums and other sporting infrastructure across Gaza have been razed to the ground . <p> Cork people in their droves continue to organise and attend multiple actions every week to show their solidarity with Palestine .
