<p> Cork musician Martin Leahy has released his new single ' Not In Gaza ' and its accompanying video , which includes artwork from Cork artist Niamh Leonard . <p> The track is released in collaboration with Cork Health Care Workers For Palestine , who march every Saturday in Cork City . The words of their chants are included on the song . <p> ' Not In Gaza ' was written after Leahy attended a talk given by the surgeon Nick Maynard , who had recently returned from working in Gaza , and where he offered a harrowing account of what he had witnessed there . <h> Advertisement <p> The song recalls the story of Hind Rajab , a six year old Palestinian girl who was killed by Israeli forces along with six of her family members and the two paramedics who were coming to her rescue . An excerpt from her last call to the PRCS is included at the end of the track . <p> " As the situation in Palestine worsens by the day , " Leahy comments , " the world turns away and it fades from the news .
