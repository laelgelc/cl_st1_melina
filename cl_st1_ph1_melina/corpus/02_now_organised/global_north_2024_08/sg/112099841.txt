" <p> Commercial ships operating near Yemen have been coming under attack from the country 's Houthi movement over the past nine months in a response to the conflict in Gaza . <p> Several leading shipping companies have been avoiding the region altogether , taking longer routes around Africa rather than using the Suez Canal . This is likely to deliver a significant boost to bunker demand and freight markets while the current situation continues .
