<p> Fights broke out , a supermarket closed and public transport was at a standstill in Manchester following a day of protests . Hundreds of people descended on the city centre as a number of demonstrations were held on Saturday ( August 3 ) . <p> In the morning , close to 150 people joined one protest marching under the banner ' Stand Up For Your Country : Enough is Enough ' . They were met with a counter rally which featured around 350 demonstrators . <p> Officers did their best to keep the demonstrators apart with two rows of policemen and women deployed to separate the groups . However , video footage captured by the Manchester Evening News showed scuffles breaking out in the Gardens . <p> Metal fences hurled while punches were thrown by a number of people in the melee .
