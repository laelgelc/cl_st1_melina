<p> Egypt and Rwanda have agreed to establish an Egyptian logistics zone in Rwanda , a move that will facilitate trade and investment between the two countries and strengthen their bilateral ties . <p> The agreement came during a meeting between Egyptian Foreign Minister Bader Abdelatty and his Rwandan counterpart , Olivier Nduhungirehe , in Kigali on Monday . <p> The logistics zone , located near the Rwandan border with Tanzania , will provide Egyptian companies with easier access to the Rwandan market . Abdelatty also expressed his country 's commitment to supporting Rwanda in key sectors , including water resource management and healthcare , and praised Rwanda 's decision to allocate a logistics zone for Egypt . <p> The meeting discussed the progress of the Magdi Yacoub Rwanda-Egypt Heart Center , which is expected to become a regional hub for healthcare services in central and eastern Africa .
