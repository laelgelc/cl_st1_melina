<p> Originally set to take place in Highfield Community Centre in Hall Green on August 16 , the charity have now decided to cancel the sleepout due to ' the current climate of fear and violence ' . It comes as there are rumoured riots in Birmingham this weekend . <p> ISRA-UK released a statement saying : " It is with a heavy heart that ISRA-UK is announcing the cancellation of our Big Summer Solidarity SleepOut for Gaza , due to the current climate of fear and violence spreading across cities all around the country . <p> " We understand how important this fundraising event is for many of you who were looking forward to participating on Friday 16th August , but with far-right riots expected to be taking place in Birmingham on that weekend , we regret we can not guarantee the safety of all our attendees . <p> " The violence across the UK over the last few days has been driven by division and hatred , as well as ignorance and misinformation . While we reject any attempt to divide our communities or our society in this way , we must remain vigilant against any threats @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ their friends and neighbours . <p> " Safety of our fundraisers is of the highest priority for us , especially at an event being held in an open space overnight . For this reason , we are cancelling our SleepOut for Gaza event planned for August , but are aiming to reschedule this for Saturday 5th October . <p> " We apologise for any inconvenience caused by current events , but do hope you can join ISRA-UK for our charity SleepOut for Gaza later this year . " <p> All registered participants have been offered a place at the rescheduled event in October . The sleepout was in response to nearly a million Palestinian children thought to have been displaced since the start of the Israel-Hamas conflict . <p> The Birmingham based charity has been distributing food parcels and fresh hot food to families in Gaza since the beginning of the conflict .
