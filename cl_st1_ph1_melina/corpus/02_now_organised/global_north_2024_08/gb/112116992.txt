<h> Peaceful anti-racist protest held in Cardiff <p> A protest against the war in Gaza takes place weekly ( Image : Richard Swingler Photography ) <p> A peaceful anti-racist protest took place in Cardiff on Saturday afternoon . A protest against the war in Gaza has been taking place in Cardiff every Saturday since the war broke out in October . <p> This weekend the usual protesters were also joined by those holding placards opposing the far-right racist riots that have been taking place in parts of England and elsewhere in the UK over the last week . The demonstration , organised by Stand Up to Racism , took place at 1pm starting at the Aneurin Bevan statue on Queen Street . <p> The protest today was part of Stand Up to Racism 's national day of protest . Other organisations including Cymru Students for Palestine , Black Lives Matter Cardiff &amp; Vale and Cardiff Stop the War were also involved . <p> The protest on Saturday was peaceful , with people holding placards referencing the recent riots in England .
