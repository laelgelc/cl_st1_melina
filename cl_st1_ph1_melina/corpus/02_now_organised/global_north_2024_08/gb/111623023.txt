<p> This week marked 300 days since Hamas unleashed its brutal October 7 attack on Israel , sparking a bloody conflict . <p> But , already , war-torn Gaza has endured more than enough death to last a lifetime . Over 39,000 have been killed in the stricken enclave , the territory 's health ministry figures show . Around 1,200 perished in Hamas ' assault on Israel nearly a year ago -- with about 250 taken hostage . Some have been rescued ; others who have died , tragically , will never see home . <p> As war rages on , Palestinians are starving . One in five in the strip -- over 495,000 people -- face " catastrophic levels of acute food insecurity " , according to recent figures . Hamas ' top political leader was assassinated in an airstrike in Iran on Wednesday blamed on Israel . It came only hours after Israel targeted a top commander in Hezbollah , an ally of Tehran , with a hit in Lebanon .
