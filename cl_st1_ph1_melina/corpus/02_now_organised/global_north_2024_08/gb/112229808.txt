U.S. gold futures gained 0.4% at $2,483.70 . <p> Investors are pricing in a 49% chance of a 50 basis point ( bps ) rate cut by the Federal Reserve in September , the CME Group 's FedWatch tool shows . The U.S. producer and consumer prices numbers due on Tuesday and Wednesday will be scanned for further cues . <p> " The inflation point , will , in my view , define if it is a 25 bps or 50 bps cut . So the outcome of the inflation data will result in some higher volatility in gold prices , " UBS analyst Giovanni Staunovo said . <p> " For gold to reach a new record high , inflation needs to be below market expectations .
