U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude futures increased by 23 cents , or 0.3% , to $77.21 per barrel . <p> Both benchmarks fell more than 1% on Wednesday after U.S. crude inventories rose unexpectedly and on easing worries about a wider Middle East conflict . <p> U.S. consumer prices rose moderately in July and the annual increase in inflation slowed to below 3% for the first time in nearly 3-1/2 years , reinforcing expectations the Federal Reserve will cut interest rates next month . <p> " We saw a correction in Asia trade as the oil market was oversold on Wednesday , " said Yuki Takashima , economist at Nomura Securities , adding that investors are betting the Fed could start cutting rates next month . <p> Investor worries over Iran 's potential response to the killing of the leader of the Palestinian Islamist group Hamas last month supported prices .
