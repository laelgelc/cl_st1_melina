<p> Oil prices steadied on Wednesday , with benchmark Brent holding above $77 a barrel , after steady sell-offs driven by expectations of reduced Chinese demand and diminishing concerns the conflict in the Middle East could spread and disrupt supply . <p> Brent crude futures were up 14 at $77.34 a barrel by 1142 GMT . U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude was 10 cents higher at $73.27 . <p> Since peaking above $82 on Monday last week , Brent had shed 6.2% of its value by the end of trading on Tuesday , closing at a two-week low of $77.20 . WTI fell 7.5% in the same period . <p> As expectations swirl economic weakness in China , the world 's biggest crude importer , will subdue demand , stocks in the United States , the world 's biggest producer and consumer of oil , are set to rise . <p> U.S. crude oil stocks were forecast to have risen last week , according to market sources citing American Petroleum Institute figures on Tuesday . Gasoline and distillate stocks fell , however , according to the sources . <p> Official U.S. government inventory estimates are set to be released @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Meanwhile , U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken wrapped up a trip to the Middle East intended to help broker a ceasefire agreement in Gaza . <p> Blinken and mediators from Egypt and Qatar have raised hopes for a U.S. " bridging proposal " that could shrink the gaps between the two sides in the 10-month-old war . <p> " Hopes of a cease-fire between Israel and Hamas have weighed on oil , along with lingering demand concerns , " ING commodities strategists said . <p> " While weaker Chinese demand has been well reported , refinery margins around the globe have been under pressure for much of August , suggesting that these demand concerns are not isolated to just China , " they said . <p> China 's economic struggles have contributed to weak processing margins and low fuel demand that has curbed operations at state-run and independent refineries . <p> Imports of crude oil from Russia fell in July by 7.4% from a year ago , while fuel oil imports retreated for a third straight month , customs data showed this week . <p> ( Reporting by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Jeslyn Lerh in Singapore ; Editing by Shri Navaratnam , Kim Coghill , Sharon Singleton and Barbara Lewis ) <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
