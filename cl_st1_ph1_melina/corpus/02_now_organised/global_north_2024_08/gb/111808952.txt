<h> Oil prices slip , US recession fears offset Mideast supply worries <p> SINGAPORE : Oil futures extended losses in a volatile session on Monday as fears of a recession in top oil consumer the United States offset supply worries stemming from mounting tensions in the Middle East , the world 's largest oil producing region . <p> Share markets also tumbled across Asia as U.S. recession worries sent investors rushing from risk assets while wagering that rapid fire rate cuts will be needed to rescue growth . <p> Brent crude dropped $1.04 , or 1.4% , to $75.77 a barrel by 0605 GMT , while U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude was at $72.43 a barrel , down $1.09 , or 1.5% . <p> Brent and WTI had tumbled more than 3% on Friday , with both contracts marking their fourth straight week of losses - biggest losing streaks since November . <p> U.S. recession fears , stemming from Friday 's weak July payrolls report , only " adds to Chinese demand concerns that have been lingering in the oil market for some time " , ING analysts led by Warren Patterson said in a note . <p> Oil @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to phase out voluntary production cuts from October , which means supplies will rise later this year , analyst say . <p> A Reuters survey showed on Friday that OPEC oil output rose in July despite production cuts by the group . <p> However , oil prices were supported by geopolitical risks in the Middle East as fighting in Gaza continued on Sunday , the day after a round of talks in Cairo ended without result . <p> Israel and the United States are bracing for a serious escalation in the region after Iran and its allies Hamas and Hezbollah pledged to retaliate against Israel for the killings of Hamas ' leader Ismail Haniyeh and Fuad Shukr , a top military commander from Lebanese armed group Hezbollah last week . <p> " The risk of a wider regional war , while I still think is small , ca n't be ignored , " Sydney-based IG market analyst Tony Sycamore said .
