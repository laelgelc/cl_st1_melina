<h> War and immigration make this Feast Day of St Maximilian Kolbe poignantly relevant <p> Every saint 's story and life 's testament still resonates today no matter how sizeable the passage of time between us and them . But the story of Saint Maximilian Kolbe , whose Feast Day is celebrated today , seems particularly relevant given current events around the world . <p> This includes especially the recent atrocities committed by Hamas in Israel on 7 October 2024 , as well as the subsequent war in Gaza with its attendant horrors , compounded by combat in an urban environment leading to appalling numbers of civilian casualties . <p> Both the suffering of the Jewish people and of refugees affected by war featured prominently in Kolbe 's life , and were issues that he was willing to risk his life for and , ultimately , die for at the hands of the Nazis during World War II . <p> During the German occupation of Poland , Kolbe refused to sign a document that would recognise him as a German -- based on his German ancestry -- and thereby give him a degree of protection , instead remaining at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after about 3,000 Polish refugees ( of whom more than half were Jewish ) . <p> The monastery also acted as a major religious publishing centre , producing publications that were strongly critical of the Nazis . <p> In 1941 , Kolbe was arrested by the Gestapo and eventually transferred to Auschwitz .
