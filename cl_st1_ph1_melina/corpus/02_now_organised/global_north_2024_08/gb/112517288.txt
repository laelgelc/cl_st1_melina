<p> People curious to try some traditional folk dancing with a live band are in luck because a ceilidh is taking place next week . <p> Ceilidh , a word derived from Scottish Gaelic and Old Irish , is pronounced " kay-lee " and is a social event with folk music and traditional dancing . The Ceilidh is happening on Friday , August 23 at Crowle Community Hall . <p> Ann Hindley , the organiser , encouraged people who have never attended a ceilidh before to come along and try the dances and not worry if they make mistakes . <p> She said : " You have a caller and all the dances are explained beforehand and it 's called all the way through so you 've got instructions all the way through . It can make a mess of it - but people just enjoy it . " <p> Folk music will be played live by the " very good ceilidh band " Pierrot , said Ann . She added : We are asking people to people their own drinks and food to share . " What 's happening in Gaza at the moment is horrendous and they need money , " she said . <p> Ann said the recent riots have also been upsetting . She said : " What 's been happening in the last couple of weeks in places in Rotherham and other places nearby is absolutely terrifying , particularly for refugees . It 's those sorts of things really and putting effort into supporting them .
