<h> The military confirmed the killing of Deif a day after Hamas chief Ismail Haniyeh was reported dead in Tehran . <p> The Israeli military on Thursday announced that Hamas military chief Mohammed Deif had been killed in a strike it carried out last month in Gaza 's southern area of Khan Yunis . <p> The military 's confirmation it had killed Deif comes a day after the killing of Hamas chief Ismail Haniyeh in Tehran , which was announced by Iranian Revolutionary Guards and Hamas . <p> " The IDF ( Israeli army ) announces that on July 13th , 2024 , IDF fighter jets struck in the area of Khan Yunis , and following an intelligence assessment , it can be confirmed that Mohammed Deif was eliminated in the strike , " a military statement said . <p> " Deif initiated , planned , and executed the October 7th massacre , " the military said of the Hamas attack on southern Israel that resulted in the death of 1,197 people , according to an AFP tally based on official Israeli figures .
