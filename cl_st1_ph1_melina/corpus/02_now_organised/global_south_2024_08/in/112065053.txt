Thakur highlighted that all political parties have united in their concern for the welfare of Indian nationals in Bangladesh . He said that Prime Minister Narendra Modi had tweeted his congratulations to Muhammad Yunus and the Bangladesh 's Interim Government , emphasising the need for ensuring peace , security , and development for Hindus and minorities in the country . <p> But Thakur criticised the Leader of the Opposition ( LoP ) Rahul Gandhi and Congress chief Mallikarjun Kharge for their tweets congratulating Muhammad Yunus , accusing them of neglecting to address the safety and security of Hindus and other minorities .
