<h> Speak up against minority-targeting <h> After Sheikh Hasina 's removal as Bangladesh 's Prime Minister , attacks on Hindus have increased . India and Israel have expressed concerns , while other global powers remain silent . The protection of minorities and adherence to a rules-based global order are essential . Speaking up against violence should not be seen as politically inconvenient , even if the affected communities lack significant influence . <p> Silence from capitals of major global powers-G7 and Western countries-on the targeted attack on Hindus in Bangladesh since Sheikh Hasina 's ouster as PM has been voluble . India and Israel have been the only countries to voice concern . Multilateralism ca n't be all about convenience . It is easy to look away when those threatened are a minority in most countries . Silence becomes expedient when communities under attack are not politically or electorally significant compared to the perpetrators of attacks .
