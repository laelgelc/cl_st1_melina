<h> Hamas Targets Israel With ' M90 Rockets ' , Explosions Heard In Tel Aviv <h> Explosions were heard in Tel Aviv but there were no reports of casualties . <p> Hamas ' armed al-Qassam Brigades said on Tuesday it targeted the Israeli city of Tel Aviv and its suburbs with two " M90 " rockets . <p> " A short time ago , one launch was detected that crossed the territory of the Gaza Strip and fell into the maritime space in the center of the country . No policy alerts were triggered . At the same time , another launch was detected that did not cross into Israel , " the Israeli Air Force said . <p> Explosions were heard in Tel Aviv but there were no reports of casualties , Israeli media reported . <p> Hamas is sticking to its demand that Gaza truce talks focus on a deal already discussed with Israel and mediators . Meanwhile , Israeli airstrikes killed 19 Palestinians in the central and southern Gaza Strip on Tuesday , medics said . <p> The US said on Monday that it expected peace talks slated for Thursday to go ahead as planned , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reported that US Secretary of State Antony Blinken planned to set off on Tuesday for discussions in Qatar , Egypt and Israel . <p> The Israeli government said it would send a delegation to Thursday 's talks , but Hamas , the Palestinian militant group that ran Gaza before the war , requested a workable plan to implement a proposal it has already accepted rather than more talks .
