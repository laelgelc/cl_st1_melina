These " do-and-die " unmanned aerial vehicles , equipped with home-built engines , are capable of flying up to 1,000 kilometers , marking a significant stride in India 's defense capabilities , reported NDTV . <p> The Indian kamikaze drones will each be approximately 2.8 meters long with a wingspan of 3.5 meters , weigh around 120 kg , and carry an explosive payload of 25 kilograms . Powered by a 30-horsepower Wankel Engine , designed and developed by NAL , these drones can fly at speeds of up to 180 kilometers per hour . <p> Dr. Abhay Pashilkar , Director of NAL , is leading the research on these indigenous drones .
