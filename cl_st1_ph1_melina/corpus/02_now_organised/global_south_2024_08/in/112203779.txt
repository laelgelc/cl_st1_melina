<h> Philippe Lazzarini , Commissioner of the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees ( UNRWA ) , reported today that more than 75,000 Palestinians have been displaced in the southwestern region of Gaza in recent days . <p> Philippe Lazzarini , Commissioner of the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees ( UNRWA ) , reported today that more than 75,000 Palestinians have been displaced in the southwestern region of Gaza in recent days . <p> In a post on X , Lazzarini stated that the mass displacement of Palestinians continues unabated . He explained that recent events have forced over 75,000 people to flee their homes in southwestern Gaza . Additional Israeli evacuation orders issued last night have compelled even more residents to relocate . <p> Lazzarini noted that these newly displaced individuals are seeking refuge in overcrowded shelters without specific destinations , having lost everything and requiring all forms of assistance .
