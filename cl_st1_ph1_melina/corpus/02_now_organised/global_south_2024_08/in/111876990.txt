<h> US Urges Mid-East Peace Amid Rising Tensions <h> U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken has informed Iran and Israel about a consensus in the Middle East to avoid further conflict . After discussions with Australian officials , Blinken announced that ceasefire and hostage negotiations regarding the Gaza war are nearing completion . <p> U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken has communicated to Iran and Israel that there is a widespread consensus in the Middle East to prevent any escalation of conflict . Blinken made these remarks on Tuesday , after a high-level meeting with Defense Secretary Lloyd Austin and their Australian counterparts . <p> During the meeting , Blinken highlighted the importance of maintaining peace in the region . He emphasized that the talks aimed at achieving a ceasefire and securing a hostage deal in the ongoing Gaza war have reached their final stage . <p> This development follows intensive diplomatic efforts to mitigate tensions and foster stability in one of the world 's most volatile regions .
