<h> Air India Suspends All Flights To And From Israeli Capital Tel Aviv With Immediate Effect <h> Air India said that the step has been taken in the best interest of the passengers and the crew . <p> New Delhi : The political and military situation in the middle east is not only unstable but also dangerous due to the ongoing armed clashes between Israel , Gaza-based Hamas , Lebanon-based Hezbollah , and Yemen-based Houthis . <p> The current situation is very tense because of the alleged assassination of Ismail Haniyeh , the political leader of Hamas , in Tehran , Iran on 31 July , 2024 . The killing of Haniyeh has been said blamed on Israel . Under the circumstances , there is a very real chance of a full-blown war breaking out between Iran and Israel . <p> Under these conditions , Air India , the flag carrier airline of India , has announced suspension of all flight to and from Israeli capital Tel Aviv with immediate effect until further notice . The decision was announced on Friday , 09 August 2024 . <p> The airlines said that it is continuously monitoring the situation and offering @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ travel to and from Tel Aviv . <p> Air India said that the step has been taken in the best interest of the passengers and the crew . The airlines has shared 24/7 helpline for more specific information . <p> Air India posted on X : " In view of the current situation in parts of the Middle East , scheduled operation of our flights to and from Tel Aviv are suspended with immediate effect until further notice . We are continuously monitoring the situation and are offering a full refund to our passengers with confirmed bookings for travel to and from Tel Aviv . Safety of our guests and crew remains our topmost priority . For more information , please call our 24/7 Contact Centre at 011-69329333 / 011-69329999. "
