In light of this , Aranyani Bhargav , a Bharatanatyam dancer from Bengaluru , and Donovan Roebert , a South African dance scholar and activist , started the Indian Dancers for Gaza 's Children ( IDGC ) , to raise awareness and support for these young lives . This humanitarian movement is launching a series of solidarity events worldwide to raise awareness and funds for severely injured children in Gaza due to the ongoing conflict . <p> When Arpita Shirodkar , a software engineer and Kathak artiste from Panaji , came across their post on Instagram , she was inspired to get involved . " However , it is only in the past year that the ongoing global conflicts in Ukraine , Gaza , and elsewhere have prompted us to question where our humanity has disappeared .
