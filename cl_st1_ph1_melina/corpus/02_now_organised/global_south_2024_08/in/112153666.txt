<h> Assam CM Warns of Declining Hindu Population Amid Bangladesh Crisis <p> GUWAHATI : Amid ongoing unrest in Bangladesh Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma has voiced serious concerns regarding the declining Hindu population in both Assam and Bangladesh . Sarma warned that a similar demographic shift is anticipated in neighboring states like West Bengal and Jharkhand . <p> Referring to official census data up to 2011 , Sarma pointed out significant decline in Hindu population . " The census report reveals that from 1951 to 2011 Hindu population in Assam decreased by 9.23 per cent . Meanwhile , in Bangladesh it saw sharper decline of 13.5 per cent , " the Chief Minister stated . He emphasized that this trend is not just limited to Assam and Bangladesh . It could also impact other eastern regions bordering Bangladesh . <p> Sarma 's comments come at time when Bangladesh is experiencing volatile political climate .
