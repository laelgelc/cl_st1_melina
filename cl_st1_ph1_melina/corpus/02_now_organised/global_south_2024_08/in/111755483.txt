It is the story of Rajaram Deb , a man in search of transcendental revelry and ecstasy . Set in colonial Calcutta , the novel starts with Rajaram awaiting two men to carry him away to a celebration that he is supposed to never forget . It tries to understand what Zionists mean when they say ' Palestine is a land without a people ' .
