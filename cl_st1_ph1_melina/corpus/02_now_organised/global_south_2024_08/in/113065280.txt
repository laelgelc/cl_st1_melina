<h> Video : Family members of rescued Israeli hostage run to greet him at hospital <p> The Israeli military rescued a 52-year-old hostage from a tunnel in the southern Gaza Strip on Tuesday . The hostage , Qaid Farhan al-Qadi , worked as a guard at a packing factory in Kibbutz Magen before he was abducted by Hamas terrorists on October 7 last year . <p> A video shared by Israel 's official X handle showed family members of Qaid Farhan Alkadi running to greet him at the hospital . He is said to be in good health . <p> The rescue operation was led by the IDF Southern Command , the Shin Bet security agency , and the IDF 's 162nd Division . Al-Qadi was found inside a tunnel by commandos of the Israeli Navy 's elite Shayetet 13 unit .
