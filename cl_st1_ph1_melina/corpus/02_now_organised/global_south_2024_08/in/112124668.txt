While Israel has not officially claimed responsibility for the assassination , Iran has , of course , pointed fingers at Tel Aviv . <p> This is unsurprising given Israel 's long history of targeted killings of Palestinians , including Hamas leaders , even during this round of conflict . His murder is humiliating for Iran , which has promised to avenge him . <p> Israel is bracing for retaliatory strikes , even hinting at a preemptive strike on Iran .
