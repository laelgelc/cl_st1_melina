<p> Israeli Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir 's visit to the Al-Aqsa Mosque , known to Jews as Temple Mount , sparked controversy on Tuesday . Ben-Gvir advocated for Jews to be allowed to pray at the site , challenging longstanding rules . Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu quickly denied any changes to these rules and rebuked Ben-Gvir , underscoring the deep divisions within the government . <p> U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken issued a statement condemning Ben-Gvir 's visit , describing it as a blatant disregard for the historic status quo in Jerusalem 's holy sites . Blinken warned that such provocative actions could exacerbate tensions and derail diplomatic efforts aimed at achieving a ceasefire and regional stability . <p> The visit comes at a particularly sensitive time , with the Gaza war ongoing and risks of broader conflict involving Iran and its regional proxies . Ben-Gvir 's actions highlight the internal discord within Netanyahu 's coalition , which has faced repeated clashes over policy concerning the Gaza war , the West Bank , and judicial power .
