<h> World <p> GAZA -- Hamas has criticised the new ceasefire proposal for Gaza , presented in Doha last week , as catering to Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's conditions , particularly his refusal to agree to a permanent ceasefire . <p> The group claimed the proposal aligns with Netanyahu 's demands , including rejecting a permanent ceasefire , maintaining control over the Netzarim Corridor , Rafah Crossing , and the Philadelphi Corridor , and setting new conditions on the prisoner exchange , which Hamas said hindered the deal 's completion , Xinhua news agency reported . <p> Hamas accused Netanyahu of " being fully responsible " for the mediators ' failure , obstructing an agreement , and endangering Israeli hostages by " continuing his aggressive actions against Gaza . " <p> The group reiterated its commitment to the agreement reached on July 2 , based on US President Joe Biden 's announcement and a UN Security Council resolution , urging Egyptian and Qatari mediators to enforce the agreed terms . <p> A new round of Gaza ceasefire negotiations took place in Doha on Thursday and Friday , following which the three mediators issued a joint statement announcing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that relevant parties would continue their efforts in the coming days to negotiate the details of the deal 's implementation . <p> Netanyahu 's office expressed gratitude for the mediators ' efforts and hoped they would lead Hamas to accept the terms of a proposal made in late May . <p> Meanwhile , Hamas , which did not participate directly , accused Israel of adding new conditions to a previously supported proposal , and accused the US administration of trying to create a " false positive atmosphere , " expressing scepticism about the negotiations ' outcome .
