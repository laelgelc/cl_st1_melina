Consequently , in 2024 , Booker Prize-winning author Arundhati Roy became one of the accused under this law , for an alleged speech about Jammu and Kashmir in 2010 . <p> The most significant negative impact on human rights groups worldwide from the September 11 attacks arose from the expansion of the mandate of the Financial Action Task Force ( FATF ) , initially established in 1989 by the G7 to develop policies to combat money laundering . <p> It expanded its mandate to include " terrorism financing " in 2001 .
