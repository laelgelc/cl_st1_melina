What has been happening in Gaza for the past 10 months is the most horrific atrocity , injustice , and genocide against humanity since World War II . <p> " ... for them , in Ukraine , they call it genocide , a crime against humanity , a war crime , but when it happens in Gaza , everyone acts as if they do n't understand , ( instead saying ) they have rights to self-defend ... to me , this is the hypocrisy of the highest order , " he said . <p> Mohamad made the remarks during the simultaneous opening of the Umno Women , Youth , and Puteri assemblies here on Wednesday ( Aug 21 ) night . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said the party remained steadfast in advocating for the Palestinian cause to liberate their homeland and free Jerusalem and the Al-Aqsa Mosque . <p> The opening ceremony was also attended by Umno 's top leaders and the heads of its three wings . <p> The Umno General Assembly will take place from Wednesday until Saturday ( Aug 24 ) at the Kuala Lumpur World Trade Centre , involving 6,433 delegates from across the country . - Bernama
