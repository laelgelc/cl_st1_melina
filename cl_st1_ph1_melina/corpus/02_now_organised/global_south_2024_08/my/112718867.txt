<p> SUBANG : A total of 127 Palestinian citizens , including 41 injured as a result of Israeli attacks at the Gaza Strip , safely arrived in Malaysia . <p> The Palestinians were flown in two Royal Malaysia Air Force ( RMAF ) A400M aircraft from Cairo , Egypt , and arrived at the Subang Air Force base between 2pm and 2.30pm on Friday ( Aug 16 ) . <p> Defence Minister Datuk Seri Mohamed Khaled Nordin said the mission to bring the Palestinians to Malaysia was made on a humanitarian basis and to show solidarity towards Palestinians and the nation 's stand to combat inhumane acts or genocide .
