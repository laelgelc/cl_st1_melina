<h> Cops bring calm to Craig Town <p> KINGSTON , Jamaica -- The Kingston Western police say they have managed to break the backbone of an ongoing war in the Craig Town community and restore calm . <p> Head of the division , Senior Superintendent Michael Phipps , said the police have managed to disrupt the operation of the two warring gangs in the community through a series of proactive activities . <p> " The Kingston Western police , with the support of our JDF ( Jamaica Defence Force ) counterparts and specialised operations through intense policing activities , have managed to impact the general Craig Town area of the division , " Phipps said . <p> " We have had some activities which included interventions , curfews , targeted and snap operations where the main perpetrators of violence in the area were our main focus . " <p> The police activities and intense investigations have led to the arrest and charge of a 17-year-old student for a double murder in the community two weeks ago . <p> The community had been under siege after the Gaza and 110 gangs began warring for turf , leading to a wave @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ double murder , which occurred in broad daylight . <p> Phipps promised the police would keep a presence in the community . These efforts will continue , " he assured .
