<h> Turkey restores access to Instagram after 9-day block <p> ISTANBUL -- Turkey said on Saturday it had restored access to social media platform Instagram after the company agreed to cooperate with authorities to address the government 's concerns . <p> Turkey blocked access to the social media platform on Aug. 2 for failing to comply with the country 's " laws and rules " and public sensitivities . <p> A top Turkish official accused Instagram of blocking condolence posts over the assassination of Ismail Haniyeh , leader of the Palestinian militant group Hamas . <p> Turkey has denounced Israel 's attacks on Gaza , called for an immediate ceasefire and criticized what it calls unconditional support for Israel by the West . <p> The nine-day ban triggered protests from users and small businesses who reach their customers through the platform . <p> Turkey ranks fifth in the world in terms of Instagram usage , with more than 57 million users , following India , the United States , Brazil and Indonesia , according to data platform Statista . <p> " As a result of our negotiations with Instagram officials , we will lift the access block ... after @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ catalog crimes and on censorship imposed on users , " Transport and Infrastructure Minister Abdulkadir Uraloglu said in a post on X. <p> Catalog crimes in Turkish law include acts such as murder , sexual assault , drug trafficking , abuse and torture . <p> " Significant gains have been achieved in increasing security in the digital environment in Turkey , legal compliance , protection of user rights and the development of a fair inspection mechanism , " Mr. Uraloglu said . <p> Instagram parent Meta had agreed to comply with Turkish law and ensure efficient removal of posts and content if they carry elements of certain crimes or " terrorism propaganda , " Mr. Uraloglu said . <p> Instagram was not immediately available for comment when contacted by Reuters . <p> " Live metrics show Instagram is being restored across Turkey 's main internet providers after national restriction spanning nine days . This is the country 's longest ban of a major social media platform in recent years , " Internet monitor NetBlocks said . -- Reuters
