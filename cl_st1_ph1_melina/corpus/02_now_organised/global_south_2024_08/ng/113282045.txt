<p> The Israeli military has confirmed the killing of Wissam Khazem , a leader of the Palestinian armed group Hamas , during ongoing operations in the occupied West Bank . Khazem , along with two other fighters , was reportedly killed in an overnight strike near Zababdeh , southeast of Jenin . According to a military statement , Israeli forces " encountered and eliminated " Khazem in a vehicle before targeting " two additional terrorists " as they attempted to flee . <p> The Palestinian health ministry has verified the deaths , bringing the total number of Palestinians killed since the start of the operation to at least 19 . This military action , described as one of the largest in the West Bank in two decades , has prompted international concern . The UK Foreign Office expressed alarm on Friday , stating it was " deeply worried by the methods Israel has employed and by reports of civilian casualties and the destruction of civilian infrastructure .
