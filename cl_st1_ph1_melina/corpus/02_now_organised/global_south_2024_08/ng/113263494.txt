While the movie has some good points , taking a closer look , you will realise that Hollywood and other major movie industries worldwide have overused those points . For instance , a major plot of the film is the kidnapping of the daughter of the first female governor of the Central Bank of Nigeria . Tell me you have yet to see a movie where an influential person 's child got abducted . <p> Tokunbo tells the story of a car smuggler who got entangled in the kidnapping of the daughter of a government official . A failure to pull through with the abduction will spell grave consequences for his own family . <p> What are the drawbacks of Tokunbo ?
