<p> DOHA : Hamas on Tuesday condemned claims by US President Joe Biden that it was backing away from a Gaza truce deal , calling his remarks a " green light " for Israel to continue aggression in the besieged Palestinian enclave . <p> The " misleading claims ... do not reflect the true position of the movement , which is keen to reach a ceasefire " agreement , the Palestinian group said in a statement . <p> It called Biden 's remarks -- which were made on Tuesday as the US president prepared to leave Chicago after a keynote address to the Democratic National Convention -- an " American green light for the Zionist extremist government to commit more crimes against defenceless civilians " . <p> The statement came as US Secretary of State Antony Blinken was in Egypt Tuesday for talks on a Gaza ceasefire . <p> Blinken , who met Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu in Jerusalem on Monday , will afterwards head to Qatar , which along with Egypt is brokering negotiations for a truce in the 10-month-old Israeli aggression in Gaza . <p> Hamas said Biden 's remarks reflect a clear " American bias @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ war of extermination against defenceless civilians in the Gaza Strip " . <p> The group said it is committed to a ceasefire framework outlined by Biden on May 31 which he said had been proposed by Israel . <p> It described the latest US modifications to that plan as " a coup against " the previous framework , accusing Washington of " acquiescing " to conditions set by Netanyahu . <p> " Netanyahu was always the one obstructing an agreement and setting new conditions and demands , " Hamas said , calling on " the US administration to reverse its policy of blind bias towards the Zionist war criminals . " <p> It called on the " mediators to assume their responsibilities " and oblige Israel to accept the original proposal .
