<p> JERUSALEM : Israel 's outgoing head of military intelligence took responsibility for his country 's failures to defend its border on Oct. 7 at his resignation ceremony on Wednesday . <p> Major General Aharon Haliva , a 38-year veteran of the military , announced his resignation in April and was one of a number of senior Israeli commanders who said they had failed to foresee and prevent the deadliest attack in Israel 's history . <p> " The failure of the intelligence corps was my fault , " Haliva said at the ceremony on Wednesday , and he called for a national investigation " in order to study " and " understand deeply " the reasons that led to the war between Israel and Hamas . <p> The Oct. 7 attack badly tarnished the reputation of the Israeli military and intelligence services , previously seen as all but unbeatable by armed Palestinian groups such as Hamas . <p> In the early hours of the morning of Oct. 7 , following an intense rocket barrage , thousands of fighters from Hamas and other groups broke through security barriers around Gaza , surprising Israeli forces and rampaging through communities in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ killed in the attack , most of them civilians , and about 250 were taken into captivity in Gaza , according to Israeli tallies .
