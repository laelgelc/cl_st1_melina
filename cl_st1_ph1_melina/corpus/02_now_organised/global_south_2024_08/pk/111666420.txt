<h> Nation observes mourning day in solidarity with Palestine <p> ISLAMABAD -- The nation is observing mourning day across the country today in solidarity with the Palestinian brothers and sisters and to condemn the ongoing Israeli brutalities . <p> The decision was taken in a consultative meeting of the government allied parties held in Islamabad under the chair of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif to discuss the deteriorating situation in Palestine . <p> In an a joint declaration , it was also announced that the funeral prayer in absentia for chief of Political Bureau of Hamas Ismail Haniyeh , who was martyred in Tehran on Wednesday , will be offered after Friday prayers across the country . <p> The meeting , also decided to present a resolution in the Parliament to express full solidarity with the people of Palestine . <p> The participants of the meeting strongly condemned the ongoing Israeli brutalities in Palestine for last nine months , and expressed full solidarity with the Palestinian brothers and sisters . <p> The declaration , also urged the international community , including the United Nations to break their silence and immediately get stopped the ongoing genocide of the oppressed Palestinians by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ crimes . <p> Speaking on the occasion , Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif said silence of the international community over the ongoing Israeli barbarism in Palestine is a matter of grave concern . <p> The Prime Minister said assassination of Hamas leader Ismail Haniyeh is condemnable . He said Israel has grossly violated the resolutions adopted by world bodies , including the United Nations Security Council and the decision of the International Court of Justice . <p> He said the International Court of Justice has declared Israel 's oppression as genocide . <p> Shehbaz Sharif said preventing Israel from this genocide is a big challenge for the modern world . <p> He , however , appreciated the countries , which have condemned the ongoing Israeli oppression in Gaza .
