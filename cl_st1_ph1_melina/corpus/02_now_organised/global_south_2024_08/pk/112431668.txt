<p> GENEVA : The UN human rights chief Thursday deplored the " grim milestone " of 40,000 Palestinians reported killed in Israel 's 10-month assault on Gaza , accusing the country 's military of breaking the " rules of war " . <p> United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights Volker Turk blamed Israel for the soaring death toll in the territory , where it launched a fierce assault in response to unprecedented attacks by Hamas in October . <p> The health ministry in Hamas-run Gaza said on Thursday that the death toll from the conflict had reached at least 40,005 people , with another 92,401 wounded . The ministry does not provide a breakdown of civilian deaths . <p> " Today marks a grim milestone for the world , " Turk said in a statement . <p> " Most of the dead are women and children . This unimaginable situation is overwhelmingly due to recurring failures by the Israeli Defense Forces to comply with the rules of war , " he added . <p> " The scale of the Israeli military 's destruction of homes , hospitals , schools and places of worship is deeply shocking . "
