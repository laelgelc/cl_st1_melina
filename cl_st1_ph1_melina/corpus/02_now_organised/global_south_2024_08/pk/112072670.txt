<p> ISLAMABAD : Chief of Army Staff General Syed Asim Munir on Thursday vowed to prevent anarchy in Pakistan , stating , " The greatest sin in the sight of Allah is creating chaos in the land , " and affirming that those who do not follow Shariah and the Constitution are not considered Pakistanis . <p> While addressing at the National Ulema Convention , the army chief highlighted the critical challenges Pakistan is facing and called for unity in combating internal and external threats . <p> His speech was characterised by a strong emphasis on the importance of adhering to both Islamic teachings and the Constitution , while condemning those who reject these principles . <p> Reflecting on Pakistan 's longstanding support for Afghan refugees , General Munir reminded the audience that Pakistan has hosted millions of Afghans for over 40 years .
