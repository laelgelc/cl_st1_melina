<h> Dedication stressed for country 's stability <p> LAHORE:Post Graduate Medical Institute Principal Prof Al-Fareed Zafar Monday said it is our collective responsibility to work with dedication for the stability of the country . <p> Addressing a moot titled " Hum Sab Ka Pyara Pakistan " at Lahore General Hospital , Prof Al-Fareed said Pakistan is a great country and we are fortunate to have four seasons and natural resources . He said work with dedication and honesty was key to stabilise the country . He said all relevant institutions should make joint efforts to promote education , development of health sector and eradicate poverty . <p> MS Dr Faryad Hussain , Zumard Khursheed , Rubina Inam , President YDA Dr Haseeb Thind and a large number of medical professionals also attended the moot . The PGMI Principal said resources and the talent of the youth of the country are our asset .
