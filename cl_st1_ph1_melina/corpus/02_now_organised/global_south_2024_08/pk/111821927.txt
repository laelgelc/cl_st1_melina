CDT ( 1458 GMT ) , with prices earlier trading around their lowest since January . U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude was down 84 cents , or 1.14% , at $72.68 . <p> " The oil and product trade is going to be cautious as the market tries to get a handle on how bad the global market meltdown is going to be , " wrote Phil Flynn , senior market analyst for Price Futures Group . <p> Slumping diesel consumption in China , the world 's biggest contributor to oil demand growth , is also weighing on oil .
