<p> Russian President Vladimir Putin on Tuesday told Palestinian Authority President Mahmud Abbas that Moscow was " concerned " about civilian deaths in Gaza . <p> " We are concerned above all about civilian losses , " Putin said , according to images shown on Russian state television . " We are doing everything ... to support Palestine and the Palestinian people , " he said . <p> The only way to create " long-lasting , reliable , stable peace in the region " is implementation of all UN resolutions and the " creation of a fully-fledged Palestinian state , " he added . <p> Abbas told Putin : " We feel that Russia is one of the dearest friends of the Palestinian people " . <p> The leaders held talks after a weekend strike on a Gaza school building left at least 93 people dead , including 11 children , according to the Gaza civil defence agency . <p> Israel 's military said the raid had " eliminated " 31 fighters . <p> Moscow for years tried to balance relations with all major players in the Middle East -- including Israel and the Palestinians . <p> But since @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Putin has moved closer to Israel 's foes , Hamas and Iran . <p> The Gaza war began with the Hamas October 7 attack on Israel which resulted in the deaths of 1,198 people , mostly civilians , according to an AFP tally based on Israeli official figures . <p> Israel 's military offensive in Gaza has killed at least 39,929 people , according to a toll from the territory 's health ministry , which does not provide a breakdown of civilian and fighters deaths . <p> The Kremlin has repeatedly criticised Israel 's response to the October 7 attacks and called for restraint .
