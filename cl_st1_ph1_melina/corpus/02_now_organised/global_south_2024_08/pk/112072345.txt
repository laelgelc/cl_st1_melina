In an interview with Time magazine , Netanyahu expressed deep regret for the security lapse and the consequences that followed . <p> " I unequivocally apologize for what happened , " Netanyahu stated . " I am profoundly sorry for the events that transpired and for not being able to prevent them . " <p> This apology marks a significant departure from Netanyahu 's previous stance , where he had not publicly acknowledged any security failures . Reflecting on the attack , Netanyahu said , " Even today , when we look back , we think about what we should have done differently and how we failed to act .
