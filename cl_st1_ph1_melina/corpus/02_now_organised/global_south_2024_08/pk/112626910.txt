<h> Israeli soldier , two Hezbollah fighters killed in clashes <p> Beirut , Lebanon - An Israeli soldier and two Hezbollah fighters were killed Monday in clashes , Israel 's military and the Lebanese group said , the latest in a wave of cross-border violence . Hezbollah has exchanged regular fire with the Israeli army in support of its ally Hamas since the Palestinian group 's October 7 attack on Israel sparked the Gaza war . The Israeli military said Monday a member of its Bedouin Trackers Unit " fell during combat in northern Israel " . Hezbollah said two of its fighters were " martyred " , after Lebanon 's health ministry reported that an Israeli strike left two people dead in the border village of Hula . Israel 's military said air forces struck in the Hula area in south Lebanon . Hezbollah had said earlier it launched a " simultaneous air attack " with " explosive-laden drones " on two Israeli military positions -- the Yaara barracks near the border , and a base near the coastal town of Acre , around 15 kilometres ( 10 miles ) from the frontier .
