The plan includes a cease-fire , a hostage-prisoner exchange and the reconstruction of Gaza . <p> Around @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Israel launched a brutal onslaught against Gaza that began Oct. 7 following an attack by the Palestinian resistance group , Hamas . <p> The assault has sparked increased regional tensions , with the latest escalation occurring last Wednesday when Hamas political chief Ismail Haniyeh was assassinated in the Iranian capital Tehran , one day after an Israeli strike in a suburb of Beirut killed a senior military commander from the Lebanese group , Hezbollah . <p> Kirby said the US is engaged in " intense diplomacy " to prevent a broader escalation in the Middle East and is watching events between Iran and Israel " extremely closely .
