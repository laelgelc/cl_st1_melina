<h> OIC to discuss crimes by Israeli occupation forces against Palestinians and its aggression against sovereignty of Islamic Republic of Iran by Israel <p> ISLAMABAD - Organisation of islamic Oooperation ( OIC ) has accepted the request from Palestine and Iran for an urgent meeting of the executive Committee of OIC at the level of foreign ministers on 7th August 2024 at Jeddah . <p> According to the agenda of the meeting exclusively available with The Nation , emergency meeting of executive Committee of OIC at the level of foreign ministers is going to discuss the the continued crimes by Israeli occupation forces against Palestinians and its aggression against the sovereignty of islamic Republic of Iran by Israel . The executive committee meeting would be opened by the chair and secretary general of OIC . Later the sessions would be closed door which would be addressed by members including Palestine , Iran and other members including Pakistan .
