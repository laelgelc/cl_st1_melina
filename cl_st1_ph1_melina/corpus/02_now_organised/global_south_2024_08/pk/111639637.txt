Countries like Pakistan had a good excuse for doing nothing -- they had problems at home , and their economies were tanking . <p> No idea how some of the potent powers like China , Russia , and Turkey rationalized their unwillingness to intervene -- even though it was in their larger interest . The American factor and the risk of a conflagration might have afforded them a ruse to watch this Greek tragedy from the sidelines . Protests in the Western world were very heartwarming , but as one must have noticed , they did not persuade even their own governments to revise the policies .
