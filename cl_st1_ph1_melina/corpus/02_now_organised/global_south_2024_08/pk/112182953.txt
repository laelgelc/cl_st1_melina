<p> Pakistan has strongly condemned the recent Israeli military attack on Al-Tabeen School in Gaza , where more than 100 Palestinians were martyred . <p> In an official statement , Foreign Office Spokesperson Mumtaz Zahra Baloch expressed Pakistan 's deep concern and condemnation of the attack . She highlighted that the assault , which targeted a school sheltering displaced individuals , resulted in over 100 civilian deaths and numerous injuries . <p> " The attack on a school providing refuge to the homeless is a horrific , inhumane , and cowardly act , " Baloch stated . She further emphasized that targeting civilian populations and facilities constitutes a grave violation of international humanitarian law and is considered a war crime . " Israel must be held accountable for these war crimes and the ongoing genocide in Gaza , " she added . <p> Baloch also called upon the international community , the United Nations , and Israel 's allies to take immediate action to halt the genocide of Palestinians in Gaza and to ensure the safety and protection of the people of Gaza . <p> It is worth noting that just yesterday , Israel targeted over 250 Palestinians during @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Al-Daraj area of northern Gaza , resulting in the martyrdom of more than 100 individuals . Reports indicate that Israeli forces dropped three bombs on the school , each weighing 2,000 pounds .
