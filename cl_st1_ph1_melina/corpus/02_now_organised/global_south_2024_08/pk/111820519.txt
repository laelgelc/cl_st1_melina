<h> Elusive peace <p> By News Desk <p> August 05 , 2024 <p> The assassination of Ismail Haniyeh , the political leader of Hamas , occurred in Tehran . Although Israel has not yet commented on Haniyeh 's assassination , there is speculation that the motive behind the killing may have been to benefit Zionist interests . Haniyeh , a key figure within Hamas , was elected as the head of its political bureau in 2017 . Known for his pragmatic approach , Haniyeh played a crucial role in negotiating ceasefires during previous Israel-Gaza conflicts and maintained close ties with Hamas 's allies . <p> He was also recognized for fostering relationships with leaders of various Palestinian factions , including those who were rivals to Hamas . Haniyeh 's death marks a significant milestone in the ongoing conflict with Israel , with potential ramifications for both regional politics and the internal dynamics of Palestinian leadership . This tragic event underscores that peace remains a distant dream .
