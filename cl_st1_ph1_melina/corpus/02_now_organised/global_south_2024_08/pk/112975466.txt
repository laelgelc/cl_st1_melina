<h> UN Report evidence of Israeli genocide against Palestinians <p> ISLAMABAD -- Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Saturday demanded immediate action from international community on the recent report of the UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs ( OCHA ) regarding the forced eviction of Palestinians from their land and child malnutrition . <p> The Prime Minister said the latest UN report is fresh evidence of the Israeli genocide against the oppressed Palestinians . <p> He said the UN report testifies that there is not a single day in which innocent Palestinian blood is not shed . It says the Palestinian children are the biggest target of Israel . <p> The Prime Minister said the forced eviction of two hundred and fifty thousand Palestinians from their homes in the month of August is a challenge to international institutions , international conscience and international law . The pace of Israeli oppression can be gauged from the unjust eviction of one hundred and forty six Palestinians from their homes in just two days . <p> He said the report of the United Nations agency confirms that on the one hand , the genocide of Palestinians with deadly weapons is continuing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as a weapon . Only one percent of children in northern Gaza and six percent in southern Gaza are receiving food . <p> Expressing serious concerns over the massacre of Palestinian people , the Prime Minister said reports of international organizations are a serious indictment against Israel . He said the murderers of humanity should be punished and the oppressed be protected . <p> The Prime Minister expressed Pakistan 's commitment to speed up delivery of food to Palestinians , especially children .
