<p> Sharing an Instagram story about the young girl , who has lost her hair under stress and trauma caused by Israel 's military aggression in Gaza , Malala demanded quick ceasefire to protect innocent beings . <p> " This is the trauma Palestinian girls are facing under Israel 's bombardment -- no peace , no school . We need a ceasefire now and protect these children 's lives , " she wrote . <p> The Pakistani Nobel-laureate has times and again raised her voice for the rights of Palestinian children and condemned Israel 's attacks on Gaza , particularly on educational institutes . <p> In an interview with Al-Jazeera , Sama Tabil , 8 , told how once she used to adore her hair and now she was ridiculed for her baldness . <p> With teary eyes , she expressed her desire to travel abroad to get treatment so she could " be beautiful again " .
