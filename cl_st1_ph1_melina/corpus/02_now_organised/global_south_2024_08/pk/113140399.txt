<h> Pseudo peace process <p> By News Desk <p> August 28 , 2024 <p> This letter refers to the news report ' Sisi , Biden agree Israel and Hamas need to show flexibility in Gaza talks ' ( August 24 , 2024 ) . The fact is that Joe Biden has been blaming only Hamas for the absence of a ceasefire . And that is understandable because , with his term ending in a few months , concentrating on appeasing Israelis to earn a massive retirement package from pro-Israel lobbies is important to him . That is why , while feigning for a ceasefire , he keeps approving military aid packages for Israel . This is hardly an inducement towards ceasefire . Moreover , the real solution is not the ceasefire but the establishment of a Palestinian state . If the US president and all his top officials can not even force Israeli forces to vacate Palestinian land , they will be unable to make any progress towards peace .
