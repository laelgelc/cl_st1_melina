U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude futures rose 95 cents , or 1.2% , to $77.93 . <p> " The U.S. retail sales data , along with the U.S. unemployment claims number , provided a plethora of positive news for the markets , " said Naeem Aslam of Zaye Capital Markets . <p> Both benchmarks fell more than 1% on Wednesday after U.S. crude inventories rose unexpectedly . <p> Earlier , data showing U.S. consumer prices rose moderately in July , reinforcing expectations the Federal Reserve will cut interest rates next month , lent support to the market . <p> Oil prices were also propped up by worries over Iran 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Palestinian group Hamas last month .
