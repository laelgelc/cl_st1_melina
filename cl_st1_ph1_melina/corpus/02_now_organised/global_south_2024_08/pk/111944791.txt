<p> ISLAMABAD -- Ministry of Foreign Affairs issued a travel warning advising Pakistani citizens to leave Lebanon and refrain from traveling there due to the current security risks . <p> Pakistani nationals who are currently in Lebanon are urged to return while commercial flights are still available and exercise heightened caution if they must stay , especially in areas at risk . <p> This advisory comes amid escalating conflict , with Hezbollah engaging in frequent skirmishes with Israeli forces in support of Hamas . The violence escalated following a Hamas attack on Israel on October 7 , which triggered the latest conflict in Gaza . <p> MoFA shared contact details for the Pakistani Embassy in Beirut to assist nationals in the region . <p> The embassy can be reached at +961-81669488 and +961-81815104 via cell/WhatsApp or by email at parepbeirut@mofa.gov.pk . <p> This advisory highlights the government 's concern for the safety of its citizens amid ongoing regional instability .
