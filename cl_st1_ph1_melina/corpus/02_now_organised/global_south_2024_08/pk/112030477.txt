<h> Turkiye joins the ICJ 's Israel " genocide " case from South Africa <h> ISTANBUL : According to the foreign ministry , Turkiye officially requested on Wednesday to join South Africa 's genocide complaint against Israel at the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) in The Hague , regarding Israel 's actions during the Gaza War . <p> In May , Turkiye declared that it would take the required legal steps and join the case , formally known as presenting a statement of official intervention . <p> Hakan Fidan , the foreign minister of Turkey , announced on Wednesday that the formal request had been made . <p> In a post on X , Fidan stated , " The international community must do its part to stop the genocide and exert the necessary pressure on Israel and its supporters . " <p> He went on , " Turkiye will try his hardest to do that . " The ultimate determination of the case 's admittance will be made by the court . <p> In December , South Africa filed a lawsuit against Israel , charging it with orchestrating a genocide in Gaza . <p> The International Court @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ must protect Palestinians against genocide perpetrated by its troops and abstain from any actions that would violate the Genocide Convention . <p> Defending in court that its actions in Gaza constitute self-defense and targeting Hamas fighters who assaulted Israel on October 7 of last year , killing 1,200 Israelis and foreigners , Israel has repeatedly rejected the case 's allegations of genocide as unfounded .
