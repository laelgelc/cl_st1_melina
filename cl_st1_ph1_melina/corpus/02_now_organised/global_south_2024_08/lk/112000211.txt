<h> USD 590,000 received by ' Gaza Fund ' handed over to Palestinian government <p> Colombo , August 8 ( Daily Mirror ) - An additional sum of USD 590,000 has been received by the ' Children of Gaza Fund , ' which was established by President Ranil Wickremesinghe to provide basic needs for children displaced due to the current military situation in the Gaza Strip . <p> The President 's Media Division ( PMD ) said that the funds were officially handed over to the Palestinian government yesterday ( 07 ) at the Presidential Secretariat under the patronage of President Ranil Wickremesinghe . <p> The official documents were presented by President Wickremesinghe to the Palestinian Ambassador to Sri Lanka , Dr. Zuhair M.H. Zaid , and the Resident Representative of the UNDP in Sri Lanka , Azusa Kubota . <p> Following a proposal by President Ranil Wickremesinghe during the Cabinet meeting on March 4 , 2024 , the government of Sri Lanka agreed to provide financial assistance of USD 1 million to cover the essential expenses of nearly one million children trapped in the Gaza Strip due to the war .
