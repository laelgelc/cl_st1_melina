<p> Israel -- The Israeli military said on Thursday it had " eliminated " a Hamas militant in an air strike in Gaza , which Al Jazeera reported had killed two of its journalists . <p> On Wednesday , Al Jazeera said its Arabic channel correspondent Ismail al-Ghoul and cameraman Rami al-Rifi were " targeted by Israeli occupation forces " in an air strike during their coverage of Gaza 's Al-Shati refugee camp . <p> In a statement issued on Thursday , the military said its fighter jets " struck and eliminated Ismail al-Ghoul , a Hamas military wing operative " . <p> Ghoul had " participated in the October 7 massacre " . <p> " As part of his role in the military wing , Ghoul instructed other operatives on how to record operations and was actively involved in recording and publicising attacks against ( Israeli ) troops . "
