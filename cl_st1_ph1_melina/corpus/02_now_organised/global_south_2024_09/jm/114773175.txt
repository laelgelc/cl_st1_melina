<h> Gangs importing gunmen in bloody St Andrew turf war -- Cops <p> KINGSTON , Jamaica -- An ongoing war between two gangs in Craig Town , St Andrew , has taken a wicked twist as the syndicates have now been importing gunmen from other communities to aid in the conflict , according to the police . <p> The 110 and Gaza gangs have been involved in a bloody turf war over recent months , resulting in several murders and shootings . <p> But police said the war has taken an even more deadlier twist as the gangs , who members are known to each other , have sought and acquired help from gunmen from outside communities . <p> The outside gangsters are unknown to their rivals and are being used to carry out attacks and shootings in the community , the police disclosed . <p> This was apparently the tactics used on Sunday when 61-year-old Vincent Lyons , otherwise called ' Shortman ' , the father of one of the alleged gang leaders , was shot dead near his business place on Madden Street in the community . <p> That attack , police investigations have unearthed , was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ brought into the community by the Gaza gang . This gang has also imported men from Waltham Park Road . <p> The 110 gang , the police shared , have brought in men from Denham Town , Hannah Town and Allman Town . <p> Fearing reprisals and counter reprisals from Lyons ' killing , lawmen on Monday imposed a 48-hour curfew in the community . <p> The police also listed 11 men from the area as persons of interest . Detectives said these men can help with their investigations into the ongoing war . <p> Head of the Kingston Western Police Division , Senior Superintendent Michael Phipps , said crime fighters will rigorously target the gang members and their out of town cronies . <p> " Our immediate strategy is really aimed at going after the violence influencers within the community with a special focus to target the person , especially those who are imported into the area to use violence against residents , " Phipps shared . <p> " Of course , our efforts will be supported by the JDF and Specialized Operations division . And we want to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ security forces operatives within the space , " he said . <p> SSP Phipps was adamant that he expected persons named as persons of interest to report to the Denham Town police no later than 6 pm Tuesday .
