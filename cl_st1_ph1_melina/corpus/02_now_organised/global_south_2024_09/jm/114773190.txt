<h> Police presence heightened in Craig Town after father of alleged gang leader killed <p> There is heightened tension and police presence in Craig Town , St Andrew , following the killing of a man said to be the father of a gang leader from the community . <p> The deceased , identified by police as 61-year-old Vincent Lyons , otherwise called ' Shortman ' or ' McCalla ' , a bar operator from the area , was shot dead near his business place on Sunday . <p> Lyons , lawmen have revealed , is the father of Rusheed Lyons , who is being sought in connection with gang-related activities in the community . The younger Lyons , the police said , is the alleged leader of the notorious 110 gang . <p> The 110 and Gaza gangs , located in the said community , have staged a bloody turf war over recent months , resulting in several murders and shootings . <p> Reports indicated that around 8:00 pm , the senior Lyons was standing along the roadway talking to a friend when he was pounced upon by men armed with guns . Police , who were quick on the scene , assisted Lyons to the Kingston Public Hospital , where he died while undergoing treatment . <p> Police said 14 spent casings were found after they processed the crime scene . <p> The police believed Vincent Lyons ' killing would have brought heavy reprisals and as such the lawmen have imposed a 48-hour curfew in the community . <p> The police have also listed 11 men from the area as persons of interest . The police said these men could help with their investigations into the ongoing war . <p> The men are being asked to report to the Denham Town Criminal Investigations Branch ( CIB ) by 6:00 pm Tuesday .
