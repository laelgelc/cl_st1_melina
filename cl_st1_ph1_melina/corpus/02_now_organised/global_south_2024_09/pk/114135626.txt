Israeli-Palestinian conflict . <p> " Together , we want to identify the concrete actions that will enable us to make progress towards this objective , " Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez wrote on social network X. <p> " The international community must take a decisive step towards a just and lasting peace in the Middle East , " the Socialist premier added . <p> Sanchez welcomed participants at his official residence before the start of the meeting at the foreign ministry in central Madrid , hosted by his top diplomat Jose Manuel Albares . <p> Asks international community to take a '
