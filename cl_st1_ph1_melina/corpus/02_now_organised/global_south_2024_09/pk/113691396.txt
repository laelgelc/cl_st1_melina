The Palestinian Civil Defense confirmed the casualties but added that recovery efforts are ongoing . <p> The Israeli military has not commented on the airstrikes . <p> The ongoing Israeli offensive in Gaza has pushed the Palestinian death toll to 40,878 , according to health officials in Gaza .
