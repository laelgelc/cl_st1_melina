Photo : AFP <p> Explosions targeting walkie-talkies associated with Hezbollah have resulted in at least nine fatalities and over 300 injuries across various Lebanese cities on Wednesday . <p> This incident follows a previous attack attributed to Israel , which left more than 2,800 people injured and claimed 12 lives due to exploding pagers . <p> Hezbollah confirmed that the recent blasts specifically targeted devices used by the group . According to a senior security official , the explosions were relatively small , similar in scale to those from the previous day . <p> In addition , multiple solar power systems in homes throughout Lebanon reportedly exploded , injuring at least one girl in the town of al-Zahrani . <p> Images circulating on social media showed destroyed solar panels and other electronic devices , although it remains unclear whether these incidents were directly linked to the walkie-talkie explosions . <p> Hezbollah and the Lebanese government have placed blame on Israel for the initial attacks , with Hezbollah vowing a " fair punishment " in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ devices amidst the wreckage . A video captured a Hezbollah member during a funeral for those killed in the earlier blasts when an explosion occurred , sending bystanders fleeing . <p> While Israel has not officially claimed responsibility for the attacks , reports indicate that explosives may have been planted in numerous pagers used by Hezbollah . In response to the situation , the Israeli military has repositioned its 98th Division , which recently operated in Gaza , to northern Israel and is conducting limited reserve recruitments in the area . <p> In the afternoon , the IDF reported that several rockets were fired from Lebanon into western Galilee , although they struck open land and caused no injuries . Additionally , an Israeli fighter jet intercepted a drone reportedly launched from Iraq early that morning .
