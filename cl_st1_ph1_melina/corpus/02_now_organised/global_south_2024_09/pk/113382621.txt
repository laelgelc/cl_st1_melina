<p> JERUSALEM : Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said on Monday that Hamas " executed " six hostages whose bodies were recovered from Gaza , shooting them " in the back of the head " . <p> " These murderers executed six of our hostages , they shot them in the back of the head , " he said during a press conference , rejecting the notion that Israel should respond with " concessions " in Gaza ceasefire talks . <p> The killing of the six hostages , announced by Israeli officials on Sunday , has spurred an outpouring of grief in Israel as well as anger at the government for not sealing a deal that would have secured the hostages ' release . <p> Israel 's health ministry said on Sunday that the six hostages were shot at close range shortly before their bodies were recovered from the Gaza Strip . <p> As thousands of protesters gathered in Tel Aviv on Monday for a second consecutive day of demonstrations criticising the government , Netanyahu sought forgiveness for not being able to save them but also appealed for unity . <p> He called on the international community to apply @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the unprecedented October 7 attack on southern Israel . <p> " We say yes , they say no all the time , but they also murdered these people and now we need maximum pressure on Hamas , " he said . <p> " Hamas has to make the concessions . " <p> Netanyahu also insisted on Monday that Israeli forces must retain control over the Philadelphi Corridor along the Egypt-Gaza border , which has emerged as a primary sticking point in talks mediated by the United States , Egypt and Qatar . <p> Hamas is demanding a complete Israeli withdrawal from the area as part of the stalled talks . <p> " The achievement of the war 's objectives goes through the Philadelphi Corridor , " Netanyahu said . <p> " Control of the Philadelphi axis guarantees that the hostages will not be smuggled out of Gaza , " he added . <p> " I will not give in to pressure . " <p> Of 251 people taken hostage during the October 7 attack , 97 remain in Gaza , including 33 the Israeli military says are dead .
