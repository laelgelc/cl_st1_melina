<p> Pakistan strongly condemned on Thursday the recent attacks on Lebanon 's Hezbollah , saying that the use of cyber and electronic means to commit terrorism was reprehensible . <p> " We condemn terrorism in all forms and manifestations and offer our deepest condolences to the family of the victims , " Foreign Office ( FO ) spokesperson Mumtaz Zahra Baloch said in her weekly press briefing . <p> She further said that these terror attacks are a manifestation of Israel 's alarming adventurism in the region which has endangered regional peace and security . <p> " Pakistan reaffirms its support to Lebanon 's sovereignty and territorial integrity and calls upon the world to take urgent steps and hold Israel accountable .
