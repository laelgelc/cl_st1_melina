" The message was also heard on at least one Lebanese radio station , where Israel managed to seize control of the airwaves . <p> Israel was able to send the calls and texts by hacking into Lebanon 's telecommunications systems , a practice they have perfected over the last decade in Lebanon and in Gaza , according to two Israeli intelligence officers .
