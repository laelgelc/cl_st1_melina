<h> Water-related issues and suggestions <p> Editor , The state 's capital Shillong has been hit particularly hard by the water crisis with multiple local media reports suggesting that people are now being forced to ration water . For the ones who can afford it , purchasing water from tankers still remains an easier resort , but for the majority , the only option is resorting to alternative , often unhygienic , sources . This includes local streams and rivulets where effective effluent treatment does not exist . Even Sohra , once the world 's wettest place , has been suffering . As per the report , a water loss of 20.5 percent takes place pre-consumption in the process of water getting treated and then transmitted to the main reservoir . Following that , the measure of unaccounted-for water -- or water that has been produced and is wasted even before it reaches the consumer -- is steep where losses are more than 50 per cent . This means that in most situations , leakages and siphoning go largely untraced .
