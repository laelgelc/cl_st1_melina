He said a shaft hidden in a residential home in Rafah , southern Gaza , led to the tunnel buried 20 meters underground . <p> The video shows a low , narrow passageway with no bathroom and poor ventilation , according to Xinhua news agency . <p> " All the evidence inside the tunnel has been collected for investigation . Hundreds of thousands of Israelis took to the streets , demanding that Netanyahu secure a deal to bring home the remaining hostages . <p> Hamas took about 250 hostages in a surprise attack against Israeli towns adjacent to Gaza on October 7 , 2023 .
