<p> Washington : Indian and American officials held the US-India 2+2 Intersessional Dialogue in the national capital on Monday . During the crucial meeting the dignitaries discussed matters pertain to the Indo-Pacific region , and the critical situation in Ukraine , and Gaza . This interaction comes ahead of the meeting between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and US President Joe Biden . The Indian delegation was co-chaired by Ministry of External Affairs Joint Secretary for the Americas Nagaraj Naidu along with Indian Ministry of Defense Joint Secretary for International Cooperation Vishwesh Negi . While , the US delegation was co-chaired by Assistant Secretary of State for the Bureau of South and Central Asia Donald Lu and Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Indo-Pacific Security Affairs Jedidiah P. Royal . <p> Taking on X ( formerly Twitter ) , the Ministry of External Affairs ( MEA ) spokesperson , Randhir Jaiswal said , " India and the U.S. held the 2+2 Intersessional today with officials from India 's Ministry of External Affairs and Defense and the U.S. State and Defense Departments . " <p> According to the state department , the dialogues shared priorities , including defense cooperation , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and industrial and logistics coordination . <p> " The officials discussed a range of issues across the Indo-Pacific and worldwide , including support for a just and durable peace in Ukraine , as well as support for a ceasefire and humanitarian assistance in Gaza , " the State Department said . " Assistant Secretary Lu and Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary Royal reiterated the United States ' commitment to further enhancing ongoing partnerships and expanding people-to-people ties " , it further added .
