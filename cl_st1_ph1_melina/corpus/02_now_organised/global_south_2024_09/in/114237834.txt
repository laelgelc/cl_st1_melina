<h> India 's Ministry of External Affairs has condemned remarks by Iran 's Supreme Leader Ayatollah Khamenei about the treatment of minorities in India . The ministry described the comments as misinformed and unacceptable , urging countries to examine their own records before making observations about others . Khamenei had expressed concern over the suffering of Muslims in India , Gaza , and Myanmar . <p> AgenciesIran 's supreme leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei <p> India took a firm stance against remarks made by Iran 's Supreme Leader Ayatollah Khamenei regarding minorities in the country . <p> " We strongly deplore the comments made regarding minorities in India by the Supreme Leader of Iran . These are misinformed and unacceptable .
