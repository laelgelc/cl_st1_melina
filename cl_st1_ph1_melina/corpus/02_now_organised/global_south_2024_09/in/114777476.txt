This summit was seen as crucial for multiple reasons . First , it was the last one to be attended by Biden , a key architect of the grouping 's institutionalisation , and Japanese Prime Minister Fumio Kishida , who played a pivotal role in adding political heft to the forum . Second , 2024 marks 20 years since the formation of the grouping . Third , and most importantly , the Wilmington summit further consolidated the grouping by expanding key areas of cooperation in the Indo-Pacific .
