He advocated firstly , for the association of ideas with language and secondly , for the association of one idea with other ideas as well . So , whenever we try to understand utilitarianism through the lens of ' greatest good for greatest number ' we must also keep in mind that the ' association principle ' of Bentham 's philosophy was somewhere complementary to the utility factor .
