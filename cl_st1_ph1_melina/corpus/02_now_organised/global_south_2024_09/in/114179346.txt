<h> Elias Khoury , outspoken Lebanese novelist , no more <h> Lebanese novelist Elias Khoury , known for his dedication to the Palestinian cause and his outspoken criticism of Middle Eastern dictatorships , has died at 76 . Khoury , a prominent intellectual and author , was also a vocal supporter of Arab uprisings and served as editor-in-chief of the Palestine Studies magazine until his death . <p> AgenciesElias Khoury <p> Beirut : Lebanese novelist Elias Khoury who dedicated much of his writings to the Palestinian cause and taught at universities around the world , making him one of Lebanon 's most prominent intellectuals , has died . He was 76 . <p> Khoury , a leading voice of Arab literature , had been ill for months and admitted and discharged from hospital several times over the past year until his death early Sunday , Al-Quds Al-Arabi daily that he worked for said . <p> The Lebanese writer , born and raised in Beirut , was outspoken in defense of freedom of speech and harsh criticism of dictatorships in the Middle East . <p> In addition to his novels , Khoury wrote articles in different Arab media outlets over @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Arab world . <p> Two days after the Israel-Hamas war broke out on October 7 , Khoury wrote an article in Al-Quds A-Arab daily titled " It 's Palestine . " He wrote that " the biggest open-air prison , the besieged Ghetto of Gaza , has launched a war against Israel , occupied settlements and forced settlers to flee . " <p> Born in Beirut on July 12 , 1948 , Khoury had been known for his political stances from his support of Palestinians to his harsh criticism of Israel and what he called its " brutal " settling policy in Palestinian territories . <p> He studied at the Lebanese University and later at the University of Paris , where he received a PhD in social history . " The Catastrophe began in 1948 and it is still going on , " he once wrote referring to Israel 's settlement policies in occupied Palestinian territories . The " nakba , " or " catastrophe " is a term used by many Arabs to describe the displacement of hundreds of thousands of Palestinians when Israel was created in 1948 . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ broke out in the region in 2011 and toppled several governments . <p> " The question is not why the Arab revolts broke out , " he wrote after uprisings that toppled long-serving leaders like Egypt 's Hosni Mubarak and Tunisia 's Zine El Abidine Ben Ali . " The question is not how people tore down the wall of fear but how fear built Arab kingdoms of silence for five decades . " <p> Khoury , who belonged to a Greek Orthodox Christian family , took part in Lebanon 's 1975-90 civil war and was wounded in one of the battles . From 1992 until 2009 , Khoury was the editor of the cultural section of Lebanon 's leading An-Nahar newspaper . Until his death , he was the editor-in-chief of the Palestine Studies magazine , a bulletin issued by the Beirut-based Institute for Palestine Studies . <p> His first novel was published in 1975 , but his second , Little Mountain , which he released in 1977 and was about Lebanon 's devastating civil war was very successful .
