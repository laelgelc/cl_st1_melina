India also advised countries to look at their own record before commenting on conditions of minorities in India . <p> The Ministry of External Affairs on Monday rebuked Tehran over the recent remarks made by Iran 's Supreme leader Ayatollah Khamenei regarding Muslims in India . In dia issued a statement condemning the remarks and said , " We strongly deplore the comments made regarding minorities in India by the Supreme Leader of Iran . These are misinformed and unacceptable . " <p> In its statement , the MEA also advised countries making statements on minorities in India to look at their record . The statement read , " Countries commenting on minorities are advised to look at their own record before making any observations about others . " <p> India 's statement came hours after Iran 's Ayatollah Khamenei said that one can not consider oneself a Muslim if they are oblivious to the sufferings of Muslims in Myanmar , Gaza and India . " <p> Khamenei gave the statement while he faced backlash at home for curbing women 's rights and imposing a burqa and hijab . Amini 's death sparked a series of protests . Eventually , she became the symbol of resistance for women 's rights activists .
