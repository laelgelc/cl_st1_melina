<h> World <h> Israel to intensify attacks on Lebanon : Military chief <p> JERUSALEM -- Israel 's military chief Herzi Halevi said on Tuesday that Israel is about to intensify its airstrikes on Lebanon . <p> " We should not give Hezbollah a rest " and " should keep on working with full force , " Halevi said in a statement . <p> " Today we will accelerate the offensive operation , " he said , adding that Israel will also continue its military operation in the Gaza Strip , Xinhua news agency reported . <p> On Monday , the Israeli army launched intensive airstrikes on villages and towns in Lebanon , prompting Hezbollah to launch retaliatory missile strikes targeting several Israeli military targets in northern Israel . <p> According to Lebanese officials , the death toll from intensive Israeli airstrikes on Lebanon since Monday has reached 558 , including 50 children and 94 women , with injuries totalling 1,835 , whereas about 16,500 residents in Lebanon have been displaced to Beirut and other areas in the country . <p> The two sides resumed crossfire on Tuesday , with Israeli forces unleashing another wave of airstrikes on Hezbollah targets @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ southern Lebanon , and Hezbollah attacking several airports , headquarters , and sites in northern Israel with artillery and missiles . <p> Since October 8 , 2023 , Hezbollah and the Israeli army have been exchanging fire across the Lebanese-Israeli border , raising concerns of a broader conflict as the war between Hamas and Israel continues in the Gaza Strip .
