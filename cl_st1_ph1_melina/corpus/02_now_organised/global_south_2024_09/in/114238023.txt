<h> India has strongly condemned comments made by Iran 's Supreme Leader regarding the treatment of minorities in India , calling them misinformed and unacceptable . These are misinformed and unacceptable . Countries commenting on minorities are advised to look at their own record before making any observations about others , " according to a strongly worded MEA statement that was issued after the Iranian Supreme Leader 's sudden remarks on X " sufferings of Muslims in India " and clubbing it with Gaza and Myanmar . <p> In remarks that took the Indian government by surprise Iranian Supreme leader Ali Khamenei wrote on X , " We can not consider ourselves to be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Muslim is enduring in #Myanmar , #Gaza , #India , or any other place . The enemies of Islam have always tried to make us indifferent with regard to our shared identity as an Islamic Ummah . " <p> Sources told ET that the government here has been taken by surprise over the remarks as there are no major irritants in the relationship and Iran remains critical to India 's outreach to Afghanistan and Eurasia . India also played a key role in Iran 's entry into both BRICS and SCO . <p> In fact , in May India signed a deal with Iran for management rights of Chabahar Port for 10 years .
