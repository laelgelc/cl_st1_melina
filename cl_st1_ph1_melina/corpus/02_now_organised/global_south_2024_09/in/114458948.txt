<p> Nasrallah addressed Israeli officials ' promises to return to their homes thousands of Israelis displaced by exchanges of fire across the border with Lebanon . <p> " You will not be able to return the people of the north to the north , " he said , warning that " no military escalation , no killings , no assassinations and no all-out war can return residents to the border " . <p> The " only way " to return the displaced to the north is to " stop the war on Gaza , " he said . <p> Hezbollah is an ally of Palestinian militant group Hamas , which on October 7 launched an unprecedented attack on Israel that sparked Gaza 's deadliest ever war . <p> Up until now , the focus of Israel 's firepower had been on Gaza . <p> But Israel 's northern border with Lebanon has seen exchanges of fire between Israeli troops and Hezbollah militants almost every day since October 8 . <p> The violence has killed hundreds of people , mostly fighters , on the Lebanese side , and dozens on the Israeli side .
