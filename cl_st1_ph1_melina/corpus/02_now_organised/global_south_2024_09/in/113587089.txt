<p> American historian Allan Lichtman , known as the " Nostradamus " of presidential election predictions , has accurately forecast the winners of nine of the last 10 US presidential races . <p> This time , Lichtman has predicted that Democratic presidential candidate and Vice President Kamala Harris will defeat her Republican challenger , former President Donald Trump , in the race to the White House . <p> His forecast comes just days before a scheduled presidential debate between Trump and Harris on September 10 . <p> The 77-year-old historian is one of the few pundits to have accurately predicted both Donald Trump 's 2016 victory and Joe Biden 's 2020 win . <p> His track record spans four decades and 10 presidential races , with his only miss being in 2000 , when he predicted Democrat Al Gore to defeat Republican George W Bush . Gore did win the popular vote but lost the electoral college by a razor-thin margin . <p> To accurately forecast these races , Lichtman employs a model called ' The Keys to the White House ' , which uses 13 keys -- in the form of true-or-false questions -- to assess the state @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ presidential election . <p> According to this model , if six or more keys are unfavourable to the White House party , it is predicted to lose the election . Conversely , if fewer than six keys are unfavourable , the party is expected to win . <p> In a video with The New York Times , Lichtman backed Harris to defeat Trump in November 's presidential election , noting that eight keys are in favour of the Democrat , compared to three in favour of the Republican . <p> " Kamala Harris will be the next president of the United States -- at least that 's my prediction for this race , but the outcome is up to you , so get out and vote , " he said in the video . <p> Lichtman 's keys are based on whether the White House party gained seats in the House during the most recent midterm elections , avoided a primary contest , and if the incumbent president is seeking re-election . <p> Additionally , the model considers whether there is no third-party candidate running , the short-term and long-term @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ policy changes , there is no significant social unrest or major scandal , the White House has achieved foreign policy success without experiencing foreign policy failure , and whether the incumbent candidate and challenger possess the necessary charisma . <p> Lichtman said Trump benefitted from three keys that came up false . <p> The first key involved the White House gaining seats during the anticipated red wave in the 2022 midterms , defying expectations . However , the party lost control of the House , making this key false . <p> Similarly , Lichtman noted that if the incumbent president were not seeking re-election , it could negatively impact the Democrats ' standing , which makes this key false as well . <p> Additionally , the poll historian pointed out that the key requiring the incumbent party 's candidate to be highly charismatic has not been met by Harris , rendering this key false . <p> " To meet this key , a candidate must be a once-in-a-generation figure with broad inspirational appeal .
