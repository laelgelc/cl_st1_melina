When not in use , the device emits static noise . To talk , the user presses a push-to-talk button , which switches the device from speaker to microphone mode , eliminating the static . <p> When someone speaks , their voice is converted into radio waves and transmitted at the speed of light . The receiving handset converts the waves back into sound . Once finished speaking , the user says " over " and releases the button , allowing the device to return to listening mode .
