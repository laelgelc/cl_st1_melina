On March 11 , the Philippine Senate recognized the enduring friendship and cooperation of Manila and Seoul . In the esteemed commendation formalized by the Senate Resolution No. 946 , the Philippines commemorated Manila 's active role in the inter-Korean peace process and reconciliation efforts . <p> During the Korean War in 1950s , 7,440 Filipino soldiers served in the Republic of Korea to secure the battered peninsula . This was meant to fortify America 's commitment to a free and open Indo-Pacific region that is connected , prosperous , secure , inclusive , and resilient .
