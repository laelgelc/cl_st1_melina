Yet , as we confront democratic backsliding , violent conflicts , and an accelerating climate crisis , the question remains : can multilateralism , in its current form , deliver meaningful change ? <p> Civil society , once again invited to the sidelines , must be given a larger role if this pact is to address our most pressing problems and avoid becoming another tick-box exercise . <p> One of the biggest criticisms of modern multilateralism is its inability to confront the growing trend of democratic backsliding . Democracies around the world have seen threats to their institutions , often through the rise of populist leaders who undermine the rule of law and suppress fundamental freedoms .
