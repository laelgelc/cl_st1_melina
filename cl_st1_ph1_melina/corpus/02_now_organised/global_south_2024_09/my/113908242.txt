<p> CAIRO , Sept 10 -- Israeli airstrikes on a tent camp for displaced Palestinians killed and wounded 65 people in southern Gaza , the enclave 's civil emergency service said early today , as the Israeli military said it had targeted a Hamas command centre . <p> Residents and medics said a tent encampment near Khan Younis in the Al-Mawasi area , a designated humanitarian zone , was struck by at least four missiles . The camp is crowded with displaced Palestinians who have fled from elsewhere in the territory . <p> The Gaza civil emergency service said at least 20 tents caught on fire , and missiles caused craters as deep as nine meter . It said the 65 victims included women and children but did not provide a breakdown of deaths and injuries . <p> There was no immediate comment from the Gaza health ministry , which compiles casualty figures .
