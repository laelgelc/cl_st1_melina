<h> Explosions heard near site of funeral for Hezbollah members in Beirut <p> BEIRUT ( Lebanon ) : An explosion was heard on Wednesday near the area where the funeral for Hezbollah members killed in yesterday 's pager explosion was being held in Dahieh , a neighbourhood in southern Beirut , Lebanon 's capital , Anadolu Agency reported . <p> Anadolu correspondents at the scene reported hearing the explosion , while eyewitnesses said the blast could have been caused by devices involved in yesterday 's explosion or other pagers . <p> No official statement has been made regarding the incident . <p> Meanwhile , Israel 's state television KAN claimed that new explosions had occurred in pagers in Lebanon . Local sources also suggested that radios may have exploded in the incident . <p> The pagers , wireless telecommunications devices , exploded Tuesday in several areas in Lebanon , including the capital Beirut , which local media suggested was an Israeli breach of the system . <p> The Israeli intelligence agency Mossad planted explosives in the batteries of pager devices that detonated Tuesday in Lebanon , killing 12 people and injuring around 2,750 others , including 200 in critical @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The pager blasts came amid mounting border escalation between Israel and Hezbollah , which have been engaged in cross-border warfare since the start of Tel Aviv 's deadly war on the Gaza Strip , which has killed over 41,200 people , mostly women and children , following a Hamas incursion on Oct 7 last year . -- BERNAMA-ANADOLU
