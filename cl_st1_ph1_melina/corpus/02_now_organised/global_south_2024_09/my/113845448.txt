<p> KUALA LUMPUR : The global community should make a collective voice to stop the Gaza war , which has destroyed educational institutions in Palestine , said Datuk Seri Dr Wan Azizah Wan Ismail . <p> Dr Wan Azizah , who is the wife of Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim , said they should be united and vocal in fighting the injustices faced by Palestinian teachers and students . <p> " As we gather here today , united by our commitment to protect education , I urge the key participants of this conference to make a powerful , joint statement . <p> " Together , our voices can resonate globally , demanding an end to the violence that erodes the foundation of a peaceful future , " she said in her remarks at the Fifth Observance of the UN International Day to Protect Education from Attack , themed The Human Cost of War , in Doha , Qatar , on Monday ( Sept 9 ) . <p> She stressed that there were no two ways about it and that education must be recognised as fundamental to humanitarian assistance as medical care is . <p> " Just as we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ protect young minds . She added that education is not a luxury but a fundamental right and a lifeline , " she added . <p> Dr Wan Azizah added that education is as crucial to a child 's survival as food and medicine . In rebuilding communities , it must be at the forefront , a beacon of hope amidst the ruins . <p> The conference focused on the devastating impact of war on children and the loss of education opportunities , potentially for entire generations who have been displaced due to prolonged and widespread conflict . <p> The participants included global leaders , experts , advocates , civil society and youths uniting to support global action to safeguard education from attacks . <p> According to the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees ( UNRWA ) , over 625,000 Palestinian children in Gaza have been deprived of education since the start of the Israeli onslaught on the besieged enclave on Oct 7 , 2023. -- Bernama
