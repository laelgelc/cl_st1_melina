<h> Pakistan , Afghanistan condemn Israeli aggression against Lebanon <p> NEW DELHI : Pakistan and Afghanistan on Tuesday condemned Israeli aggression against Lebanon , saying the Zionist regime 's actions may lead to a regional conflagration . <p> Israel has killed more than 550 people , including 50 children and dozens of women , in its bombardment of various areas of Lebanon , including the capital Beirut , since Monday , according to the Lebanese Health Ministry . <p> " This act of aggression against the Republic of Lebanon is a grave violation of the UN Charter and international law . It is a dangerous escalation that has further endangered peace and security in an already volatile region , " Pakistan 's Ministry of Foreign Affairs said in a statement . <p> " We call upon the international community to take urgent steps to hold Israel to account for its alarming adventurism in the region and its acts of aggression and genocide , " it said . <p> The Afghanistan government in a statement said the failure to respond to the Zionist regime in Gaza emboldened it to attack Lebanon .
