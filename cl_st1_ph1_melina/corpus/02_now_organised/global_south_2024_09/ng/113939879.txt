<p> Hamas has rejected the U.S. accusation that it is the main obstacle to reaching a ceasefire in the Gaza Strip . <p> In a statement by the senior Hamas official , the organisation said the accusation is " a blatant bias in favor of the Israeli position . " <p> The Hamas official , Izzat al-Rishq said that John Kirby the White House National Security Communications Advisor 's remarks suggesting the movement has changed some of its conditions for a ceasefire are " baseless . " <p> He said the U.S. administration believed that " blaming Hamas is less costly in light of the U.S. elections . " <p> He added that " the world knows that Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is the one who added new conditions and demands , not Hamas .
