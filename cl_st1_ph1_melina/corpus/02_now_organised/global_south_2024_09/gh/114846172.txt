<p> President Akufo-Addo has reaffirmed Ghana 's commitment to good governance . <p> According to him , the country 's efforts in enacting laws and policies that ensure accountability and the protection of all citizens under the rule of law can not be overemphasised . <p> He said during his address at the United Nations General Assembly in New York on Wednesday , September 25 , 2024 . <p> " Ghana continues to be a beacon of good governance in Africa , we have enacted laws and implemented policies that uphold the principles of accountability and ensure that every Ghanaian , regardless of their background , is protected by the rule of law , " he said . <p> Shifting the focus to global issues , President Akufo-Addo highlighted the international community 's struggle to address ongoing conflicts , particularly the Russian invasion of Ukraine and the war in Gaza and Lebanon . <p> He critiqued the UN Security Council for its inability to respond effectively . <p> " Take the Russian invasion of Ukraine , for instance .
