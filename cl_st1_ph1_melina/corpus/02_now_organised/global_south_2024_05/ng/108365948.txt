<h> Fidelity Bank renovates Nasarawa PHC clinic , donates water facility <p> As part of its Corporate Social Responsibility initiatives , leading financial institution , Fidelity Bank Plc , has renovated the Primary Healthcare Clinic along the Workers Village in the Tudun Amba Community of Lafia Local Government Area of Nasarawa State . <p> The bank also provided water facilities to ameliorate issues of water scarcity being experienced in the community in recent times . <p> Speaking at the inauguration ceremony , the Divisional Head , Brand and Communication Plc , Fidelity Bank , Meksley Nwagboh , said the dignity of every human person is a shared responsibility . The bank decided to embark on the project as a means of improving the living condition of people in the community and as part of the bank 's social responsibility to its host community . <p> Expressing gratitude to the leadership of the community for giving the bank the opportunity to execute the project , Nwagboh said , " What we are doing today is not different from what we have been doing in communities , local governments and states across the country over the years .
