<p> Before the attacks started , the Israeli army had called on the Palestinian civilian population to temporarily evacuate from eastern Rafah . <p> Avichai Adraee , a spokesperson of the Israeli army , said in a press statement that " for all people who live in the neighborhoods of Al-Salam , Al-Jeneina , Tabba Zaraa , and Al-Byouk in the Rafah area in blocks 10-16 , 28 , and 270 , the army will work with extreme force against terrorist organizations there . " <p> Israel considers Rafah the last major stronghold of Hamas in the Gaza Strip . Located in the southernmost part of the Strip , Rafah shelters approximately 1.4 million Palestinians . <p> Israel launched a large-scale offensive against Hamas in Gaza on October 7 , 2023 , following Hamas 's surprise attack on southern Israel that killed about 1,200 people .
