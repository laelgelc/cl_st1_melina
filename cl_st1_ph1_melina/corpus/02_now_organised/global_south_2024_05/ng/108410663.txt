<p> The Minister of Industry , Trade and Investment , Mrs. Doris Nkiruka Uzoka-Anite , has advocated for extending gas concessions currently given to electricity companies to cement manufacturers to help address rising prices . <p> She made this statement during an investigative hearing by the House of Representatives Joint Committee on Solid Minerals , Industry , Commerce , and Special Duties in Abuja on Tuesday . The minister was represented by the Permanent Secretary of the ministry , Mr. Nura Rimi . <p> Mr. Rimi emphasized that cement manufacturers should receive the same gas pricing benefits as electricity distributors . <h> RelatedStories <p> Mrs. Uzoka-Anite said that a recent meeting with the Minister of Works , Mr. David Umahi , and major cement manufacturing companies led to the recommendation . <p> She stated , " There is a need for a friendly gas pricing . The meeting recommended that gas to power concession given to the electricity companies should also be enjoyed by the cement companies . " <h> Challenges faced by cement manufacturers <p> She highlighted several challenges faced by cement manufacturers , including the cost of gas , high import duties on spare parts , inadequate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ smuggling of cement to neighbouring countries . <p> She noted that both the cement manufacturers and the government recognize that the current high price of cement is abnormal in some areas nationwide . <p> At the hearing , it was unanimously agreed that retail prices for cement should not exceed N8,000 per 50kg bag , depending on the location , and cement manufacturers must establish a price monitoring mechanism to ensure compliance . <p> Manufacturers agreed to implement this mechanism and to sanction any distributors or retailers who fail to comply . <p> She stated that Nigeria is self-sufficient in cement manufacturing , with major players such as Dangote Industries Ltd , BUA Group , and Lafarge Holcim churning out enough to even export . <h> Efforts to re-engage cement manufacturers <p> She added that the Ministry is discussing how to re-engage cement companies that have halted construction activities , understand their challenges , and find ways to bring them back into the sector . <p> Rep.
