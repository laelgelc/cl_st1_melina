It looks like Europe is about to join the 143 UN member states who have already recognised the Palestinian state . <p> This is not what Israel wanted after the vicious October 7 attack by Hamas and indeed the immediate reaction of Israeli PM Benjamin Netanyahu was this was rewarding terrorism . <p> In truth , Israel 's disproportionate attack on Gaza has forced nations to reconsider their unqualified support for Israel . If Israel had merely assassinated the Hamas leadership , the world would have turned a blind eye . But blowing up 60 percent of the buildings in Gaza and killing 35,000 Palestinians was too much . <p> The only way to break the cycle of violence is the two-state solution , the very thing that Netanyahu has fought against all his political career .
