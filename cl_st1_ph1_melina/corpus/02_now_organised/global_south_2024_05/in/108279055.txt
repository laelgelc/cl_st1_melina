<h> Israel 's defense minister announced the intensification of a ground operation in the southern Gaza city of Rafah , with additional troops joining to destroy tunnels used by Hamas . The minister , Yoav Gallant , stated that Hamas was being worn down , lacking the ability to reorganize , maintain reserves , or treat targeted terrorists . The campaign aims to further weaken Hamas , emphasizing the destruction of tunnels and the organization 's logistical limitations . <p> " This operation will continue as additional forces will enter the area . Several tunnels in the area have been destroyed by our troops and additional tunnels will be destroyed soon , " Defense Minister Yoav Gallant said in a statement . <p> " This activity will intensify - Hamas is not an organization that can reorganize , it does not have reserve troops , it has no supply stocks and no ability to treat the terrorists that we target . The result is that we are wearing Hamas down , " he said .
