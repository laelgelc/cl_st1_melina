<p> Celebrities such as Priyanka Chopra , Rashmika Mandanna , Amy Jackson , and others expressed their support for Palestine , condemning civilian casualties and advocating for a ceasefire in Gaza . They shared the viral phrase ' All Eyes On Rafah ' on their Instagram stories . Amy Jackson 's notable appearance at this year 's Cannes Film Festival was accompanied by sharing pictures on her Instagram handle . She used her platform to voice support for Palestine , criticizing film festivals and media outlets for silencing advocates and condemning the recent attacks on Rafah . Jackson passionately called for a ceasefire in Gaza and urged people not to overlook the suffering of Palestinians . Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu acknowledged a ' tragic mistake ' after a strike in Gaza , resulting in numerous Palestinian deaths and drawing criticism from global leaders .
