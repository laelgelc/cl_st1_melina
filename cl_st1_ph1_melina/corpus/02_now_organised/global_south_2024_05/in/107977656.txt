( File ) <p> Jerusalem : <p> Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's cabinet decided on Sunday to shut down Al Jazeera 's operations in Israel for as long as the war in Gaza continues , on the grounds the Qatari television network threatens national security . <p> " The incitement channel Al Jazeera will be closed in Israel , " Netanyahu posted on social media following the unanimous cabinet vote . <p> A government statement said Israel 's communications minister signed orders to " act immediately , " but at least one lawmaker who supported the closure said Al Jazeera could still try to block it in court . <p> The measure , the statement said , will include closing Al Jazeera 's offices in Israel , confiscating broadcast equipment , cutting off the channel from cable and satellite companies and blocking its websites . <p> The network @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ critical of Israel 's military operation in Gaza , from where it has reported around the clock throughout the war . The Israeli statement did not mention Al Jazeera 's Gaza operations . <p> Israel 's parliament last month ratified a law allowing the temporary closure in Israel of foreign broadcasters considered to be a threat to national security . <p> Al Jazeera made no immediate comment on Sunday , although it has previously rejected accusations that it was a threat to Israel 's security and said the shutdown was an effort to silence it . <p> The law allows Netanyahu and his security cabinet to shut the network 's offices in Israel for 45 days , a period that can be renewed , so it could stay in force until the end of July or until the end of major military operations in Gaza .
