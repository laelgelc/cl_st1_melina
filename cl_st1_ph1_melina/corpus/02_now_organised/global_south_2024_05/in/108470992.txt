It adjudicates legal disputes submitted by member states and provides advisory opinions on legal questions referred by authorized UN organs and specialized agencies . <p> The ICJ is composed of 15 judges elected to nine-year terms by the UN General Assembly and Security Council . ICJ decisions are binding on parties to a case , but the court has no enforcement powers and depends on the Security Council to compel compliance . The ICJ is distinct from the International Criminal Court , which tries individuals accused of crimes such as genocide and war crimes .
