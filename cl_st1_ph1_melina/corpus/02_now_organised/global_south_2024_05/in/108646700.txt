<p> Nobel Peace Prize laureate Malala Yousafzai is all set to make her acting debut with a cameo in the second season of the British sitcom ' We Are Lady Parts ' . <p> Her initial appearance from the show , which has gone viral on social media , shows the Pakistani activist donning a cowboy hat and riding a horse . <p> Malala will appear in the season 2 episode titled ' Malala Made Me Do It , ' alongside Anjana Vasan , Sarah Kameela Impey , Juliette Motamed , Lucie Shorthouse , and Faith Omole . The episode features a country song with the same title . <p> ' We Are Lady Parts ' premiered its second season on May 30 <p> In a New York Times interview with showrunner Nida Manzoor , the director revealed that Malala was invited to join the show through a letter . <p> " I was like , ' What will it look like ? Do I have dialogue ? How many times do I have to do it ? How long will it take ? ' " the Nobel Peace Prize winner told the publication . <p> " When I @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was completely beyond my wildest imagination . And the funny thing was , I did n't have lines , so it made things a lot easier , " she added . <p> Yousafzai praised the series for its positive portrayal of Muslim girls , noting that it allows viewers " to connect with them and relate to them . " <p> " Oftentimes , when we look at conflicts , wars , oppression against people , it always begins with dehumanizing another group , " she said .
