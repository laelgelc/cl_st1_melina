<p> In its earlier report , the SIAM had said that the exports of two wheelers , three wheelers , and commercial vehicles will fall in 2023-24 owing to the Russia-Ukraine and Israel-Gaza conflicts , a slowdown in the global economy , and foreign exchange crises in key markets . <p> The overall automobile exports from India for fiscal year 2024 reported a 5.5% decline in FY24 to 4,500,492 units from the previous year 's 4,761,299 . <p> Overall , the Indian automobile industry 's export performance offered a mixed picture , with passenger vehicles showing a modest increase while other vehicle segments , such as commercial , two-wheelers , and three-wheelers , faced declines . <p> However , the rise in passenger vehicle exports was led by Maruti Suzuki with a shipment of 2,80,712 units , a 10% increase from the previous year . <p> Hyundai Motor India also played a significant role , exporting 1,63,155 units , a 7% increase compared to last year 's 1,53,019 units .
