<h> Dr Farooq condemns Israeli offensive in Rafah <p> Srinagar , May 29 : The Jammu and Kashmir National Conference president Dr Farooq Abdullah on Wednesday condemned Israel 's recent offensive in the southern Gaza city of Rafah , resulting in multiple casualties . Condemning the fresh offensive by Israel , Dr Farooq said , " No condemnation is enough for the massacre being carried out by the Israeli army in Rafah . Such brute aggression and genocidal acts targeted against civilians including children are in gross violation of international humanitarian law . Israel should heed the overwhelming calls of the international community to cease its attacks on innocent people in Gaza particularly in Rafah . " He impressed upon the Government of India to show steadfast dedication to the cause of Palestine by extending humanitarian assistance to the war-torn people there . " India has always stood with the people of Palestine and now is the time to do it .
