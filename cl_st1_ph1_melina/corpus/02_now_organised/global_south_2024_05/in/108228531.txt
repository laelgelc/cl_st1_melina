<h> Former Indian Army colonel Col Waibhav Anil Kale was killed in an attack on a UN vehicle in Gaza during the Israel-Hamas conflict . The incident involved mistaken identity by Israel Defence Forces . Close to 190 UN personnel have died in the conflict , with most casualties from the UN Relief and Works Agency . <p> Col Waibhav Anil Kale had joined the United Nations Department of Safety and Security two months ago and had recently been posted to Gaza . Known to have extensive counter insurgency experience during his service in the Indian Army , the retired officer was driving a UN vehicle towards the European Hospital in Gaza when it came under fire . <p> An inquiry has been initiated by Israel into the incident and the UN has strongly condemned the attack .
