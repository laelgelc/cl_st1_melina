<p> New Delhi , May 28 : The Polit Bureau of the Communist Party of India ( Marxist ) ( CPI-M ) ) has strongly condemned the recent Israeli airstrike on a tent camp in Rafah , which resulted in the deaths of 45 people , including 20 women and children . The camp housed displaced individuals who have been severely affected by ongoing conflicts . The party in a statement issued here said , " Despite the International Court of Justice 's directive to cease the offensive on Rafah , Israeli armed forces have continued their attacks , causing immense civilian casualties . The overall death toll in Gaza has now exceeded 36,000 , with many more still trapped under rubble . " The CPI(M) has urged all democratic and peace-loving citizens of India to protest against Israel 's actions and demand an immediate halt to the Rafah offensive . It also called for the enforcement of a ceasefire to prevent further loss of life and suffering .
