<h> Did Israel kill India 's Pathankot hero in Gaza firing ? <p> Col Waibhav Anil Kale ( retd ) joined the UN barely a month ago . The shots that killed him appeared to have come from an Israeli tank , says a senior official of the global body , which has launched a probe into the matter <p> India says it is " deeply saddened " over the death of Colonel Waibhav Anil Kale ( retd ) , who was serving as an observer with the United Nations in Gaza , even as a senior official of the global organisation has stopped short of calling it an attack by the Israeli Defence Forces ( IDF ) . " We extend our heartfelt condolences to his family and dear ones . Our Permanent Mission to the UN in New York and our Missions in Tel Aviv and Ramallah are extending all assistance in the repatriation of mortal remains to India and continue to be in touch with relevant authorities regarding the investigation into the incident , " the ministry of external affairs said in a statement .
