<p> An Ontario mother withdraws her Jewish daughter from Burlington Central High School due to escalating antisemitism . Incidents include verbal taunts , a van amplifying pro-Palestinian rhetoric , and an " Arts &amp; Culture " event featuring a Palestine booth and erasing Israel from maps . The school 's dismissive response prompts concerns about the safety of Jewish students and calls for action against antisemitism . <p> A mother in Ontario has decided to pull her Jewish daughter from Burlington Central High School . This decision comes amidst concerns about the rising antisemitism . Anissa Hersh , a resident of Burlington , witnessed , what she perceived to be , the school 's endorsement of threatening antisemitic messages . This prompted her to express concerns about the safety of her daughter . <p> On October 7 , 2023 , Hersh recalled an instance at a school event . Hersh observed what she denoted to be a Gaza protest . She saw a booth labeled " Palestine , " along with a map that relabeled Israel as Palestine . Moreover , there were several other messages erasing Jewish history . A disturbed Hersh then approached the school administration . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ minister , Stephen Lecce , shared in Hersh 's concerns . He recognized the need for tackling antisemitism within the school system . The Halton District School Board is responsible for Burlington Central High . They acknowledged the incident and vowed to investigate . A spokesperson of the board also reiterated their commitment to fighting all forms of racism and discrimination . <p> When the school suggested homeschooling as an alternative , Hersh 's frustrations grew . Despite reassurances from authorities , Hersh remained concerned about the school 's inclination to suggest an alternative solution , rather than deal with the root cause of the issue . She expressed worry over her daughter 's future education , and further questioned whether they would need to relocate to ensure her safety and well-being . <p> Concerns over the safety of Jewish students continue to mount . Community leaders and educators alike are called to create an inclusive environment , free from racism and discrimination .
