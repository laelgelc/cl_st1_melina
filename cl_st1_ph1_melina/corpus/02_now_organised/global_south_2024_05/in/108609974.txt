<h> Kani Kusruti looked beautiful in an ivory bodycon dress , but the highlight of her outfit was the unique watermelon clutch that she flaunted on the red carpet . <p> Actress Kani Kusruti , who played the lead role of a nurse in the Grand Prix-winning film All We Imagine as Light , recently grabbed the audience 's attention with her appearance at the 77th Cannes Film Festival . The Malayalam actress looked beautiful in an ivory-bodycon dress , but the highlight of her outfit was the unique watermelon clutch she flashed on the red carpet , showing solidarity with Palestine ahead of the screening of her film at the prestigious film festival . <p> The red , green and black beaded clutch was crafted by Kusruti 's friend and Kochi-based designer , Diya John , at Salt Studio . On Instagram , the studio shared a video that depicted the making of the watermelon-shaped clutch .
