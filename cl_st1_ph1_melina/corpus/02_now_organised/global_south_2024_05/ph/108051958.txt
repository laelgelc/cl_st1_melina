<h> Rollback : Gasoline down by 75 ? , diesel by 90 ? , kerosene by P1.05 per liter <p> Local oil companies will slash pump prices by up to over a peso per liter for the second straight week effective Tuesday , May 7 . In separate advisories , firms announced the price of gasoline would go down by 75 centavos per liter and diesel by 90 centavos per liter . Kerosene , mainly used as a heating oil and transport fuel , will decrease by P1.05 per liter . Cleanfuel will be the first to implement the pump price rollback on Tuesday at 12:01 a.m. while Shell Pilipinas and Seaoil will follow at 6 a.m . Other industry players are expected to make the same pump price announcement .
