" <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The ministry highlighted that the negligence exhibited by Israeli security forces proves Israel 's ongoing efforts to impede the distribution of humanitarian aid to the people of Gaza through various means . <p> The UN Security Council is urged to seek guarantees from Israel for the smooth delivery of humanitarian aid to prevent the worsening of the humanitarian catastrophe in Gaza .
