<p> Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar on Wednesday arrived in Gambia to represent Pakistan at the 15th Organisation of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) Summit being held in Banjul on May 4-5 . <p> Dar announced his arrival in Gambia in a post on social media platform X , erstwhile Twitter . <p> Dar was received by the Gambian Minister of Petroleum and Energy Nani Juwara , Foreign Secretary of Pakistan Ambassador Muhammad Syrus Sajjad Qazi and Ambassador of Pakistan to Senegal accredited to Gambia Saima Maymunah Sayed . <p> The Foreign Office announced in a press release on Wednesday that Dar would also attend the OIC Council of Foreign Ministers ' Meeting preceding the summit on May 2-3 . During this meeting , ministers will discuss and finalise the agenda and outcome documents for the summit . <p> The summit is being convened at a critical juncture for the Muslim Ummah , particularly as the conflict affecting the people of Gaza persists without resolution . It presents an important opportunity for OIC leaders to address the dire situation in Gaza and present a unified stance on the Palestinian question . <p> According to the press @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ minister and foreign minister will speak about Pakistan 's perspective on the ongoing genocide in Gaza , the right to self-determination of the people of Jammu &amp; Kashmir , imperatives of solidarity and unity of the Ummah , rising Islamophobia , issues of climate change , terrorism , and other contemporary global challenges .
