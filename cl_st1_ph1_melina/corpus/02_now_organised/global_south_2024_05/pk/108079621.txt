<h> Talks between Israel , Hamas deadlocked : Reports <p> Unresolvable disagreements between the Israeli and Hamas delegations in indirect talks for a hostage swap and cease-fire in the Gaza Strip are preventing a deal from being reached , Israeli media reported Thursday . <p> The Israeli Broadcasting Authority cited " informed sources " on the progress of the latest round of negotiations , which began in the Egyptian capital Cairo last Tuesday . <p> The media outlet said the Israeli delegation left Cairo on Thursday evening and returned to Israel . <p> It quoted unnamed informed sources as saying that there are " unsolvable disagreements " between the two parties , without revealing the nature of the disagreements . <p> According to the broadcasting authority , Israel 's security cabinet will meet on Thursday evening to discuss how to proceed following the departure of its delegation from Cairo . <p> Earlier on Thursday , Egypt 's private Al-Qahera News channel quoted a high-level Egyptian source as saying that the Hamas and Israeli delegations left Cairo on Thursday without discussing a new date . <p> Hamas said late Monday that it had accepted a Gaza cease-fire proposal drawn up @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ truce offer accepted by Hamas did not meet its key demands and decided to push ahead with an operation in Rafah in southern Israel to apply " military pressure on Hamas with the goal of making progress on freeing the hostages and the other war aims . " <p> Israel has killed more than 34,900 Palestinians , mostly women and children , and injured over 78,500 others following an attack by the Palestinian group Hamas on Oct. 7 last year which claimed 1,200 lives . <p> Over seven months into the Israeli war , vast swathes of Gaza lay in ruins , pushing 85% of the enclave 's population into internal displacement amid a crippling blockade of food , clean water and medicine , according to the UN . <p> Israel stands accused of genocide at the International Court of Justice .
