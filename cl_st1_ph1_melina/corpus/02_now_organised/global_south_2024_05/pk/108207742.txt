<h> Egypt to join genocide case against Israel at top UN court <p> Egypt said Sunday it will join a genocide lawsuit filed by South Africa against Israel at the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) over its deadly offensive in the Gaza Strip . <p> In a statement , the Egyptian Foreign Ministry said the move comes " in light of the escalating severity and scope of the Israeli assaults against Palestinian civilians in Gaza and the systematic targeting of civilians and destruction of infrastructure in the strip . " <p> " These actions constitute a flagrant violation of international law , humanitarian law , and the Fourth Geneva Convention of 1949 regarding the protection of civilians during wartime , " the ministry said . <p> Egypt called on Israel , the occupying power , to comply with its obligations and implement interim measures requested by the ICJ to ensure the provision of humanitarian aid in Gaza . <p> It also demanded the UN Security Council and stakeholders to urgently intervene to reach a cease-fire in Gaza , halt military operations in Rafah and provide protection for Palestinian civilians . <p> More than 35,000 Palestinians have been @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ onslaught on the Gaza Strip since a Hamas attack last Oct. 7 that killed nearly 1,200 people . <p> Last week , Palestinian resistance group Hamas accepted a proposal drawn by Egypt and Qatar for a cease-fire in Gaza . <p> But Israel said the truce offer accepted by Hamas did not meet its key demands and decided to push ahead with an operation in Rafah , home to more than 1.5 million displaced people , to apply " military pressure on Hamas with the goal of making progress on freeing the hostages and the other war aims . " <p> More than seven months into the Israeli war , vast swathes of Gaza lay in ruins amid a crippling blockade of food , clean water and medicine . <p> An interim ruling by The Hague-based court in January said it is " plausible " that Tel Aviv is committing genocide in Gaza , ordering it to stop such acts and take measures to guarantee that humanitarian assistance is provided to civilians in Gaza .
