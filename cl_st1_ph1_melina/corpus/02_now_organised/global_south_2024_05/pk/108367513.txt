<h> Land of the free ? <p> By News Desk <p> May 20 , 2024 <p> The US government has allowed Americans to burn sacred texts , burn flags of other countries and even openly bad mouth police and government employees -- all in the name of free expression . But now it is enacting a law that restricts criticism of Israel . This is being done because the people of the US are protesting against their own government 's act of financially and militarily supporting Israel 's genocidal war in Gaza . <p> This whole situation highlights some serious faults in the democratic system of governance , for which the US serves as a blueprint . Both its major parties , despite being answerable to the people , are trying to stifle their opposition to Israel 's actions seemingly under the influence of powerful lobbies .
