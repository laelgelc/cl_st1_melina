The incident occurred as the staff members were en route to the European Hospital in Rafah . <p> UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres expressed deep sorrow upon learning about the death of the UN Department of Safety and Security ( DSS ) staff member and the injury sustained by another DSS staffer in the attack . This unfortunate event underscores the dangers faced by UN personnel operating in conflict zones , as they carry out crucial humanitarian missions . <p> The spokesperson for the Secretary-General , Farhan Haq , condemned all attacks on UN personnel and called for a comprehensive investigation into the incident .
