<h> Ishaq Dar to lead Pakistan delegation to 15th OIC Summit in Banjul <p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : The Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Mohammad Ishaq Dar will lead the Pakistan delegation at the 15th OIC Summit being held in Banjul , the Gambia on May 4-5 , 2024 . <p> Ishaq Dar will also attend the OIC Council of Foreign Ministers ' Meeting preceding the Summit on May 2-3 , 2024 where the ministers will discuss and finalize the agenda and outcome documents for the Summit . <p> The 15th OIC Summit is being convened at a critical time for the Muslim Ummah as the war on the people of Gaza continues unabated . <p> It would be an important occasion for the OIC leaders to deliberate upon the dire situation in Gaza and project a strong , collective and unified stance on the Palestinian question . <p> At the Summit , the deputy prime minister and foreign minister will speak about Pakistan 's perspective on the ongoing genocide in Gaza , the right to self-determination of the people of Jammu &amp; Kashmir , imperatives of solidarity and unity of the Ummah , rising Islamophobia , issues @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ challenges . <p> The deputy prime minister will underline the need to find collective solutions to challenges confronting the Muslim Ummah . <p> On the sidelines , Ishaq Dar will hold bilateral meetings with leaders and Foreign Ministers participating in the Summit .
