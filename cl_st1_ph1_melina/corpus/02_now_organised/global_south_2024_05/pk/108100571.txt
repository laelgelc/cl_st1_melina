<h> UN General Assembly approves resolution seeking full membership for Palestine <p> 03:23 PM 11 May , 2024 <p> NEW YORK -- The UN General Assembly has overwhelmingly supported the Palestinian bid to become a full UN member by passing a resolution . <p> It urged the Security Council to give " favourable consideration " to Palestine 's request . It has passed overwhelmingly with 143 countries in favour while nine states , including US and Israel , opposed it and 25 abstaining . <p> The resolution " determines that the State of Palestine ... should therefore be admitted to membership " and it " recommends that the Security Council reconsider the matter favorably . " <p> The Palestinian bid for full UN membership comes seven months into a war between Israel and Hamas in the Gaza Strip . <p> Riyad Mansour , Permanent Observer of the observer State of Palestine , recounted the devastating impacts of the ongoing war in Gaza , with over 35,000 Palestinians killed , a further 80,000 injured and over two million displaced . <p> " No words can capture what such loss and trauma signify for Palestinians , their families , their communities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> He added that the Palestinians in Gaza have been pushed to the " very edge " of the Strip " to the very brink of life " with " bombs and bullets haunting them " . <p> Mr. Mansour highlighted that despite the attacks and destruction , the flag of Palestine " flies high and proud " in Palestine and across the globe , becoming a " symbol raised by all those who believe in freedom and its just rule " . <p> Ambassador Munir Akram of Pakistan said there will come a day when Israel will be held accountable for the crimes committed against Palestinians , especially in Gaza . <p> The insults hurled today are " the arrogance of the aggressor " reflecting the impunity of the occupier , he said , explaining his delegation 's position ahead of the vote on the draft resolution . <p> He also expressed hope that the international community will appropriately respond in that regard . <p> The Ambassador underscored that the resolution 's adoption will determine the widespread support for Palestine to be accorded full UN membership .
