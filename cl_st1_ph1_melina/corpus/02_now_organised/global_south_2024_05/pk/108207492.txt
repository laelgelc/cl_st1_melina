<h> Training program for the blind and visually impaired , engaging workshops under ' ARA ' and ' Stitching Hope for Gaza ' initiatives <p> SHARJAH - The Kalimat Foundation ( KF ) has successfully completed its participation in the 15th Sharjah Children 's Reading Festival ( SCRF 2024 ) , which lasted for 12 days . During the festival , KF 's stand served as a centre for edutainment activities and workshops . <p> These activities were part of its ' ARA ' initiative , which aims to empower blind and visually impaired children and integrate them into society . Additionally , KF 's stand supported its ' Stitching Hope for Gaza ' initiative , a recent effort to aid children in Gaza . <p> ' Seeing Through Their Voices ' Training Program <p> During the 12-day cultural celebration , KF launched the ' Seeing Through Their Voices ' training program to empower blind and visually impaired children and integrate them into society by developing their public speaking and communication skills . Celebrating identity and heritage <p> As part of the ' Stitching Hope for Gaza ' initiative , KF showcased its new merchandise collection inspired by the traditional Palestinian Keffiyeh and Tatreez , with all proceeds of this new line dedicated to supporting children in Gaza through the Emirates Red Crescent 's ' Tarahum -- For Gaza ' relief campaign . <p> The foundation also organised a special workshop with the Palestinian embroidery expert Marwa Bakri .
