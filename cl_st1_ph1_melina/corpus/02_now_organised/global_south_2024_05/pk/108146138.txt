<h> Israeli army seizes control of Palestinian side of Rafah crossing <p> The Israeli army on Tuesday said that its forces have seized control of the Palestinian side of the Rafah border crossing with Egypt . <p> In a statement , the Israeli army said its 401st armored brigade took " operational control " of the Rafah crossing from the Palestinian side . <p> Israeli forces are conducting a " sweep operation " in the areas of eastern Rafah it seized , it added , claiming to kill 20 Palestinian gunmen . <p> The spokesman of the Palestinian border crossing authority confirmed earlier that the border was closed from the Palestinian side . <p> Video footage shared by Israeli media outlets , including the Times of Israel , showed an Israeli tank at the Rafah crossing . <p> Following the Palestinian Hamas group announcement of accepting a Qatari-Egyptian proposal for a cease-fire in Gaza , Israel 's war cabinet decided to push ahead with an operation in Rafah in order to apply what it said " military pressure on Hamas with the goal of making progress on freeing the hostages and the other war aims .
