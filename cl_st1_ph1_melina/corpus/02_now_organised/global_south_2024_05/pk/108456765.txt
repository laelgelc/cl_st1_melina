<h> JI rejects Punjab 's defamation bill <p> LAHORE - Jamaat-e-Islami has vehemently rejected the Punjab Defamation Bill 2024 , denouncing it as a draconian piece of legislation . In a display of solidarity with journalists , the JI Emir Hafiz Naeem ur Rehman has pledged unwavering support to the media community in their protest against the grave encroachment on freedom of speech . <p> Expressing his stance during a media briefing along with the delegation of Joint Action Committee ( JAC ) at Mansoorah on Thursday , Rehman minced no words in his condemnation of the bill . <p> " Formed on the basis of a rigged election , the current government lacks any semblance of legitimacy . Yet , it shamelessly proceeds to endorse laws that blatantly aim to stifle freedom of speech , " he said . Earlier , the JAC delegation , which comprises representatives from the Pakistan Broadcasters Association ( PBA ) , All Pakistan Newspapers Society ( APNS ) , Council of Pakistan Newspaper Editors ( CPNE ) , Pakistan Federal Union of Journalists ( PFUJ ) , Association of Electronic Media Editors and News Directors ( AEMEND ) , and Lahore Press @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sought the backing of JI against the bill . <p> Rehman emphasized that the Punjab government must engage in dialogue with journalists and promptly revoke the recently passed legislation . He asserted that resorting to coercion to suppress media freedom was unacceptable . <p> Expressing gratitude for the solidarity extended by JI , the JAC affirmed its determination to oppose the bill vigorously , even resorting to legal recourse should the government persist in upholding the black law . Meanwhile , during a separate presser held at Mansoorah on Thursday morning , the JI Emir addressed several pressing issues . He dismissed the necessity of holding fresh elections in light of the existence of legal documents in the shape of Form 45 . He advocated for the establishment of a judicial commission to probe election rigging , with results based on Form 45 . <p> Rehman further demanded the release of all political prisoners , emphasizing the paramount importance of upholding the constitution . He stressed the imperative for all to adhere to the rule of law , asserting it as fundamental for steering the country in the right direction @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Hasan , warning that such actions could exacerbate political instability . <p> Rehman called for a transparent investigation into the attack on the " Save Gaza Camp Islamabad , " which resulted in the loss of two lives . He urged the government to voice robust support for the people of Palestine and suggested Islamabad recognize Hamas as the legitimate representative of the Palestinian people . <p> Rejecting the privatization of schools in Punjab , Rehman argued that it is the state 's responsibility to ensure free education for its citizens . He pledged the JI 's commitment to advocating for farmers ' rights and standing in solidarity with them . <p> Rehman criticized the IMF 's ability to address the national economy 's challenges . He accused the rulers of prioritizing their own lavish spending while burdening the impoverished masses with unfair taxes under the IMF 's directives . He also called for a review of Independent Power Producer ( IPP ) agreements .
