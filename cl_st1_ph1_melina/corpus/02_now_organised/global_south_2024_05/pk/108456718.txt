<h> PM Shehbaz urges UNSC , world community to implement ICJ 's verdict on Gaza <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has urged the United Nations Security Council ( UNSC ) and the international community to immediately implement the decision of the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) to stop the Israeli operation in Gaza . <p> The prime minister welcomed the decision of the ICJ on the case filed against the genocide in Palestine . <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif paid tribute to thirteen judges who ruled in favour of oppressed humanity and to South Africa for filing the petition . <p> He said Pakistan had supported South Africa 's petition , and will continue to fight the case of all Palestinians . <p> The prime minister said following the court decision , the UN 's commission of inquiry should be given immediate access to Gaza and Rafah . <p> The prime minister further said decision to open roads , provide immediate food and medical aid should be implemented so that the oppressed people can get relief .
