<h> Turkey cuts trade ties with Israel amid rising diplomatic tensions <p> Turkey has severed all trade relations with Israel , with sources stating that it has halted all commerce and imposed restrictions on revenues and imports from Israel . <p> The move by Turkey has drawn strong protests from Israel , with the Israeli Foreign Minister condemning Turkey 's actions , alleging that President Erdogan is breaking all agreements by closing ports and harbors for Israeli revenues and imports . <p> Israel 's Foreign Minister remarked that such dictatorial behavior is expected from dictators , asserting that Israel will establish trade relations with other countries and focus on local production . <p> Turkey has yet to make an official announcement regarding the cessation of trade ties with Israel . <p> It is noteworthy that last month , Turkey imposed restrictions on Israeli products in response to the ceasefire declaration in Gaza by Israel . <p> The Turkish Ministry of Commerce previously stated that these restrictions will remain in place until Israel ceases hostilities in Gaza and allows unhindered access for humanitarian aid .
