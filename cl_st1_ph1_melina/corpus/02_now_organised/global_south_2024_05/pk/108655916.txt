<p> UN experts demand decisive int'l action against Israel over continued assault on Rafah city <p> May 31 , 2024 <p> ( File photo ) <p> The UN experts have demanded a decisive international action against Israel over its continued assault on Rafah city of Gaza enclave . <p> More than fifty UN experts have strongly condemned the strike on the makeshift camp , stressing these barbaric attacks are a flagrant violation of international law . <p> The experts said that such barbaric attacks on civilians flagrantly breach the laws of war , criticizing the international community for its failure to halt Israel 's war machine in the Palestinian territory . <p> Meanwhile , Spain has also rejected Israel plans to impose restrictions on the activities of its consulate in Jerusalem in response to Madrid 's recognition of a Palestinian state . <p> Spanish Foreign Minister Jose Manuel Albares said this action is against the international law .
