<p> WASHINGTON : Accepting a ceasefire deal with Israel should be a " no-brainer " for Hamas , but the motivations of the Hamas 's elusive Gaza-based leadership remain unclear , US Secretary of State Antony Blinken said . <p> Hamas has announced that its delegation will return Saturday to Cairo to resume long-running talks brokered by Egypt and Qatar that would temporarily halt Israel 's aggression in return for freeing hostages . <p> " We wait to see whether , in effect , they can take yes for an answer on the ceasefire and release of hostages , " Blinken said late Friday . <p> " The reality in this moment is the only thing standing between the people of Gaza and a ceasefire is Hamas . " <p> Noting that Hamas " purport to represent " the Palestinian people , Blinken said : " If it is true , then taking the ceasefire should be a no-brainer. "
