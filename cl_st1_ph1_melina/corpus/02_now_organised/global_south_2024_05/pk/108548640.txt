With this cap increase , we will be ready to help more people as the situation evolves , " Immigration Minister Marc Miller said . <p> A spokesperson for Miller said 448 Gazans had been issued a temporary visa , including 254 under a policy not related to the special visa program , and 41 have arrived in Canada so far .
