<h> Yemen 's Houthis launch attacks at six ships in three seas <p> Houthi military spokesman , Yahya Sarea , delivers a statement during a pro-Palestinian rally in Sanaa , Yemen May 3 , 2024 . PHOTO : REUTERS <p> DUBAI : <p> Yemen 's Houthis launched attacks at six ships in three different seas , including the Marshall Islands-flagged bulk carrier Laax that was damaged after reporting a Houthi missile strike off the coast of Yemen , the Iran-backed group said on Wednesday . <p> The Laax was attacked on Tuesday . The Houthis also launched attacks against the Morea and Sealady vessels in the Red Sea , the Alba and Maersk Hartford in the Arabian Sea and the Minvera Antonia in the Mediterranean , military spokesperson Yahya Saree said in a televised speech . <p> The Laax , which was carrying a cargo of grain , was hit by five missiles fired from Yemen , but the vessel was still able to sail to its destination and the crew were safe , the ship 's security company , LSS-SAPU , told Reuters on Wednesday . <p> " The vessel has sustained damage , she is not taking @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ wounded onboard , " a LSS-SAPU spokesperson said . <p> The spokesperson with LSS-SAPU , which was responsible for evacuating the crew from the Rubymar ship which sank after being hit by a Houthi missile earlier this year , said Laax 's Greece based owner had no connection with Israel or the United States . <p> The vessel last reported its position on May 28 with a destination of Bandar Imam Khomeini in Iran , LSEG shipping data showed . <p> The Houthis , who describe their attacks as acts of solidarity with Palestinians in Israel 's war in Gaza , have launched repeated drone and missile strikes in the Red Sea region since November , later expanding to the Indian Ocean . <p> They promised to attack any ships sailing towards Israeli ports , even in the Mediterranean . <p> The group has managed to sink one ship , the Rubymar , seized another vessel , killed two crew members and disrupted global shipping by forcing vessels to avoid the nearby Suez Canal and reroute trade around Africa .
