<h> Two Palestinians open fire at Jerusalem bus stop , killing three <p> Israeli officials work at the scene of a violent incident in Jerusalem November 30 , 2023 REUTERS/Ronen Zvulun <p> Two Palestinian attackers opened fire at a bus stop during the morning rush hour on Thursday at the entrance to Jerusalem , killing at least three people and wounding eight others , Israeli police said . <p> " The terrorists arrived at the scene by car in the morning , armed with an M-16 rifle and a handgun , " police said . " The terrorists began shooting at civilians before subsequently being killed at the scene . " <p> The shooters came from East Jerusalem and were stopped by off-duty soldiers and another civilian who was nearby , police said . <p> Security camera footage aired by Israel 's Channel 12 television showed the moments of the attack . A white car is seen stopped beside a crowded bus stop . Two men then step out , guns drawn , and run at the crowd as people scatter . Shortly afterwards the Palestinian attackers are gunned down . <p> " This attack is further proof for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the murderous terrorism that threatens our citizens , " cabinet minister Benny Gantz wrote on social media platform X. <p> A large number of first responders and security forces converged on the area that was crowded with morning commuters , and police said they were working to reopen the street . <p> U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken , who was visiting Tel Aviv , said Thursday 's shooting was a reminder " of the threat from terrorism that Israel and Israelis face every single day ... My heart goes out to the victims of this attack . " <p> The violence came as Israel and Hamas struck a last-minute agreement on Thursday to extend their six-day ceasefire in Gaza by one more day to allow negotiators to keep working on deals to swap hostages held in the coastal enclave for Palestinian prisoners .
