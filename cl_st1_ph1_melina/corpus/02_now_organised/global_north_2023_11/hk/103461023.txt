<h> Israelis capture Hamas power bases <p> The Israeli army said yesterday it had captured parliament and other institutions run by Hamas in Gaza City , as its forces deepened an offensive in Palestinian territory . <p> Military units " took over the Hamas parliament , the government building , the Hamas police headquarters and an engineering faculty that served as an institute for the production and development of weapons , " it said . <p> That came as Palestinians trapped inside Gaza 's biggest hospital Al-Shifa were digging a mass grave to bury patients who died during the Israeli encirclement and said no plan was in place to evacuate babies despite Israel announcing an offer to send portable incubators . <p> Israeli forces have surrounded Al-Shifa hospital in Gaza City , which they say sits atop an underground headquarters of Hamas militants . <p> Hamas , Gaza 's ruling group , denies fighters are present and says 650 patients and 5,000-7,000 other civilians are trapped inside the grounds , under constant fire from snipers and drones . It says 40 patients have died in recent days , including three premature babies .
