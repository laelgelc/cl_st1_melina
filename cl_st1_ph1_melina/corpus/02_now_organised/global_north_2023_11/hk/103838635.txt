<h> Hostage release truce extended <p> An Israel-Hamas truce in the Gaza Strip stretched into a fifth day yesterday as the two sides completed the release of Israeli hostages and detained Palestinians and looked poised to free more as the pause in fighting was extended by two days . <p> Hamas took 240 hostages during an October 7 incursion into southern Israel that killed 1,200 people , prompting Israel to retaliate by bombing the coastal enclave and launching a ground offensive in its north . <p> Israel said 11of its nationals had returned from the Gaza Strip on Monday , bringing to 69 the total of Israeli and foreign hostages the Palestinian group has freed since Friday under the truce . <p> Israel has added 50 more female prisoners to its list of Palestinians for potential release if additional hostages are freed .
