<h> KnowYourStuffNZ Celebrates Two Years Of Legality <p> On November 25 , KnowYourStuffNZ is celebrating the two-year anniversary of the Drug and Substance Checking Legislation Act 2021 . This Act made drug checking permanently legal in Aotearoa New Zealand , a world first . <p> " When this bill was passed , we became the first fully-legal and publicly-funded drug checking organisation in the world , " says founder and Chair of the Board of Directors Wendy Allison . <p> " It was a great step forward , as it means we can now offer our staff , volunteers , and clients assurance that they will not be arrested for the simple act of trying to keep themselves or others safer when consuming recreational drugs . " <p> KnowYourStuffNZ was founded in 2015 by Wendy Allison and her friends . They operated in a legal grey area until 2020 , when temporary legislation was put in place to allow drug checking services to operate over the 2020-21 festival season . In 2021 the legislation was made permanent . <p> Since its inception , KnowYourStuffNZ has grown from a small volunteer-run organisation to a national network of drug checking services @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , drug checking services are now offered at clinics and pop-up events across the country . <p> " In the 2022-23 season we ran over 100 events and tested nearly 4000 samples , " said Operations Manager Emma Carroll . <p> The biggest change under this new legislation has been the provision of drug checking services outside of festivals .
