<h> NZCTU Presents Briefing For The Incoming Government <p> The New Zealand Council of Trade Unions has today provided its briefing for the incoming government . <p> NZCTU President Richard Wagstaff said since 2017 , the country has made significant progress on a range of economic and social issues . <p> " Under the last Government , many measures improved . Child poverty has fallen . Unemployment reached record lows . The minimum wage increased by 44 percent . Benefits were increased and linked to wages rather than inflation . Paid parental leave was extended to 26 weeks , and sick leave was doubled . All of these changes helped to deliver a more equitable Aotearoa and helped to ensure that some of the poorest New Zealanders had a real boost in their quality of life . We hope that the progress made to date continues under this new government . <p> " New Zealand needs to become the best country in the world to be a worker , by creating good work , and building a more productive , sustainable , and inclusive economy .
