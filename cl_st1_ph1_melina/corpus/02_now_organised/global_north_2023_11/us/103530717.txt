<h> Newsletters <p> A new poll found that Jewish people overwhelmingly support President Joe Biden and oppose former President Donald Trump . <p> The results of the recent National Survey of Jewish Voters from the Jewish Electorate Institute found strong liberal attitudes among Jews , support for Israel , and support for Biden 's handling of Israel 's war with Hamas . The results cast doubt on some conservative commentators ' claims that the reactions of some Democrats to the war in Gaza would drive many Jews into the arms of Republicans . <p> Among Jews , only the Orthodox had a generally positive opinion of Trump , though only 9% of Jews in the United States are Orthodox . The majority -- Reform , Conservative , or no Denomination -- expressed overwhelmingly liberal attitudes , including hostility to Trump and the Republican Party generally . <p> Overall , 68% of American Jews supported Biden , while only 22% supported Trump . Attitudes were largely consistent across age groups . <p> Biden 's job approval rating among Jews was 66% , far higher than his approval among the general population .
