<p> " Blackouts " -- his second novel after his award-winning " We the Animals " -- combines fictional storytelling with hidden queer history . " <p> Central to the deathbed conversation between the two men is the real-life 1940s publication " Sex Variants : A Study of Homosexual Patterns " and the research 's " devastating history .
