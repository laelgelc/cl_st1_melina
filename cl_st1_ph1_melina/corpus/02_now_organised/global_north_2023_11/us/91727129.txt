<p> Democratic Rep. Rashida Tlaib of Michigan accused President Joe Biden of supporting " the genocide of the Palestinian people , " in a video posted Friday . <p> The roughly one and half minute video posted to X , features footage from protests in Tlaib 's home state of Michigan , as well as California , New York and battleground states like Pennsylvania and Ohio where people held Palestinian flags and chanted phrases like " Free Palestine ! " <p> " Mr. President the American people are not with you on this one , " Tlaib says . <p> The video closes with Tlaib saying , " We will remember in 2024 , " followed by the text , " Joe Biden supported the genocide of the Palestinian people , " written in all capital letters . <p> The White House declined to comment.Biden 's campaign did not immediately respond to a request for comment . <p> The post comes a day after Tlaib , the only Palestinian American in Congress , said that Americans " do not support funding for war crimes " and took direct aim at Biden in a statement . <p> " As the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Biden is cheering on Netanyahu , whose own citizens are protesting his refusal to support a ceasefire , " Tlaib said in the statement . " We must be laser focused on saving lives , no matter their faith or ethnicity . " <p> Biden said last week that the flow of humanitarian aid into Gaza needed to " increase , " a day after Secretary of State Antony Blinken said " humanitarian pauses must be considered " so food , water , medicine and other essential humanitarian assistance can flow into Gaza . <p> Tlaib has repeatedly called for a cease-fire in the war in the wake of Hamas attacks that involved seizing hostages and killing about 1,400 people in Israel . <p> Tlaib has criticized U.S. funding for the Israeli military , arguing that the government has supported Israel without considering humanitarian guardrails . She delivered remarks at an Oct. 18 rally with demonstrators outside the Capitol calling for a cease-fire.
