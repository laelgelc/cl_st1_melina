The drying facility will enable the city to remove more water and heat the biosolids to a degree that removes bacteria and pathogens , said Utilities Director Adam Steurer , creating a high-quality , nutrient-rich product . <p> Then the biosolids can safely be used for other purposes , such as fertilizer . The plan is to use biosolids on greenspaces and not food crops , Steurer said , and the city will work to identify agricultural partners as the project moves forward . <p> The storage building will be used to temporarily store water-treatment residuals at the Water Treatment Facility before disposal . <p> Once the drying facility and storage building are in use , the city will save money @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be passed on to customers .
