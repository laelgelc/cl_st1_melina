<p> In the early 1980s , Lynne Littman was preparing her feature debut . Littman knew the subject matter would be a tough sell . <p> " I thought people would make fun of it , " Littman , 82 , says over Zoom from her Los Angeles home , recalling being worried about what ticket-buyers might say .
