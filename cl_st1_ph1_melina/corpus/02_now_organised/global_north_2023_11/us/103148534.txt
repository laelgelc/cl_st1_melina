<h> Newsletters <p> The IDF began its ground offensive into Gaza last week after several delays . Israeli forces launched a three-pronged attack into the Gaza Strip , with the apparent intent of completely enveloping the highly dense Gaza City , where fighting is expected to be fiercest . The first phase of this plan was declared completed on Thursday . <p> " Our troops have completed the encirclement of Gaza City , the center of Hamas activity , " IDF spokesman Rear Adm . Daniel Hagari said in a Thursday press conference , the Times of Israelreported . <p> He added that a ceasefire was " off the table . " <p> The IDF 's move to encircle Gaza City was conducted incrementally , taking nearly a week and a half to move the width of the strip , which is just a few miles . The pincer in the south , moving east to west , appears to have reached the sea , splitting the Gaza Strip in half . <p> As most IDF advances have been confined to open fields outside the city itself , favorable to IDF armor supremacy , losses have been minimal -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ In contrast , Israeli Defense Minister Yoav Gallant claimed the IDF has killed " thousands of terrorists , " largely through airstrikes . <p> As fighting nears the urban sprawl of Gaza City , the IDF reported an increased intensity of fighting . The IDF claimed it had killed 130 terrorists in recent ground battles .
