When it comes to misinformation , Musk depends on X 's users to deal with the problem in the form of Community Notes , a program that allows approved users to add context to posts that contain inaccuracies or falsehoods . <p> Unfortunately , for Musk and company , this crowdsourced solution does n't appear to be working . But , Community Notes is failing . <p> According to a report from NBC News last month , and a recent report Bloomberg last week , X often failed to show Community Notes fact-checks on viral posts spreading falsehoods in a timely manner .
