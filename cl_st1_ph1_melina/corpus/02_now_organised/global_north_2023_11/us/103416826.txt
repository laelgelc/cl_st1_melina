<p> The Gaza Strip has all the harrowing pitfalls soldiers have learned to expect from urban warfare : high-rise ambushes , truncated lines of sight and , everywhere , vulnerable civilians with nowhere to hide . <p> But as Israeli ground forces inch their way forward in Gaza , the bigger danger may prove to be underfoot . <p> The Hamas militants who launched a bloody attack on Israel last month have built a maze of hidden tunnels some believe extend across most if not all of Gaza , the territory they control . <p> And they are not mere tunnels . <p> Snaking beneath dense residential areas , the passageways allow fighters to move around free from the eye of the enemy . There are also bunkers for stockpiling weapons , food and water , and even command centers and tunnels wide enough for vehicles , researchers believe . <p> Ordinary-looking doors and hatches serve as disguised access points , letting Hamas fighters dart out on missions and then slip back out of sight . <p> No outsider has an exact map of the network , and few Israelis have seen it firsthand . <p> But photos and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tunnels suggest the basic outlines of the system and how it is used .
