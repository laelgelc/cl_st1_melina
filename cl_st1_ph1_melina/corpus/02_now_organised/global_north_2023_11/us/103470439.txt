<h> AVS says two of the three dog deaths ' most likely ' poisoned , as field reopens to public after investigations <p> SINGAPORE -- Investigations have found no evidence of contamination at a field in Parry Avenue , despite authorities asserting that two of the three dogs that died after visiting the field in October were " mostly likely " poisoned . <p> The Animal and Veterinary Service ( AVS ) and the Singapore Land Authority ( SLA ) said in a joint statement on Saturday ( 11 November ) that testing of samples taken from the field - a regular spot for dog owners - found no substances that could have killed the two dogs on 5 and 6 October . <p> Furthermore , post-mortem findings found no evidence that the third dog experienced poisoning . It was also not reported to have seizures , and is likely to be unrelated to the other two cases . <p> No other related cases have also been reported in the vicinity or other parts of Singapore , and the field was reopened to the public on Sunday , after being cordoned off for investigations . <p> Dog owners had @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ October , claiming that their dogs had experienced symptoms of poisoning , such as multiple seizures and vomiting . <p> According to multiple news reports , the suspected poisoning cases were alleged to not only be at the Parry Avenue field , but also along the nearby Phillips Avenue and Sandilands Road . <h> Third case unrelated to other two cases <p> As two of the deceased dogs were cremated before investigations , no samples were available for laboratory analyses to determine the cause of illness and death . <p> However , considering clinical information on hand , AVS and SLA said that poisoning was the " most likely cause of demise " . <p> After interviewing the owner of the third dog , which died on 5 October , the authorities determined that the dog did not enter the Parry Avenue field , but had walked in the vicinity of the field . <p> Clinical signs of the third dog were found to be unlike the other two dogs , and it did not suffer from seizures .
