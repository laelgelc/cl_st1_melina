<p> According to Merriam-Webster Dictionary , a deepfake is " an image or recording that has been convincingly altered and manipulated to misrepresent someone as doing or saying something that was not actually done or said . " Deepfakes are so ubiquitous and convincing , that the public needs to be equipped with critical media literacy skills to avoid being persuaded by these falsehoods . <p> The existence of these deepfakes should at least lead us to be skeptical if not outright suspicious of the veracity of any news-related videos until factually confirmed by trusted sources or corroborating evidence . When any party releases a video , whether it be Israel 's footage after a hospital raid or Palestinian videos showing the damage from Israel attacks , audiences should be skeptical and demand transparently sourced , factual documentation . <p> This occurred early after the Oct. 7 Hamas attacks , when President Joe Biden confirmed , and later denied , seeing photos of Hamas beheading babies .
