<p> Israel said 11 hostages , all women and children , have been released from Hamas captivity in the Gaza Strip late Monday , the final day of an initial four-day truce between the warring sides . <p> Early Tuesday , 33 Palestinians held in Israeli prisons were freed and driven to the occupied West Bank city of Ramallah . <p> Hours earlier , Qatar 's Foreign Ministry said Israel and Hamas agreed to extend their truce for another two days . <p> Monday 's releases bring the number of Israelis freed under the truce to 50 , along with 19 hostages of other nationalities . So far , 150 Palestinians have been released from Israeli prisons . <p> Roughly 240 hostages were captured by Hamas in its Oct. 7 attack in southern Israel that ignited the war . One was freed by Israeli forces and two were found dead inside Gaza . <p> Israel has said it would extend the cease-fire by one day for every 10 additional hostages released .
