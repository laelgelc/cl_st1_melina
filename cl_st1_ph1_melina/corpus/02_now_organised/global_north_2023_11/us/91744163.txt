<p> Amid the dual threats of escalating violence and ongoing repression , a six-year-old interactive site started in Canada has emerged to provide the world a rare glimpse of the perspective of members of Gaza 's LGBT community . <p> Queering the Map , a moderated platform founded in 2017 by Lucas LaRochelle in Montreal , allows LGBT-identifying users to make anonymous geotagged posts .
