<p> The sight of dogs ravaging war-torn streets has become an all too familiar sight . Sandra Tabet 's debut feature film " Rabies " ( Rage ) -- a development project at the Atlas Workshops -- returns to early 1990s in Beirut , in the aftermath of the Lebanese Civil War ( 1975-1990 ) , when rabies began to spread rapidly amongst ravenous dogs , leading to some parts of the city being overrun . <p> Combining horror genre codes with a real-world setting , " Rabies " follows 60-year-old history teacher Julia , who tries to find a cure for her 30-year-old son Ghassan , who after being bitten by a rabid dog slowly transforms into a violent monster . <p> What is the core inspiration for this project ? Even though I 've been living in France for two and half years , Beirut remains constantly in my thoughts . When I began writing " Rabies " I did n't know I was writing a horror film . I was simply trying to explore my feeling of being trapped inside the city I love . <p> What attracts you to the horror genre ?
