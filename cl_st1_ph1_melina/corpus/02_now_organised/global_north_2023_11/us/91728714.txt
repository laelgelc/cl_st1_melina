In a competitive contest , particularly in a small town such as Uvalde or a state House special election , the margin between the first- and second-place candidates may be a relatively small number of votes . This may slow the race-calling process as a handful of absentee , provisional or other untallied ballots could play a decisive role in determining the result . <p> In races subject to a runoff , determining whether a candidate has cleared the required majority vote threshold could delay reporting a final winner . If the leading candidate hovers near the 50 percent mark , the race might not be called until additional votes are counted .
