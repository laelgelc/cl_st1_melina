<p> Initially established in 1948 to house Palestinians who fled or were forced from their homes during the Arab-Israeli War , the camps are now home to many of Gaza 's 1.7 million registered refugees , according to the United Nations . Even before the war , the camps were cramped , impoverished and lacked basic infrastructure . <p> On Tuesday , Israeli airstrikes hit the Jabalya refugee camp in the north . More than 100 people were killed and hundreds more injured , according to the director of a local hospital . The attack was condemned by aid organizations and diplomats . Israel 's military said the strike killed a senior Hamas commander and attributed the death of civilians to " the tragedy of war . " On Wednesday , another strike on the camp left dozens wounded , the hospital director said .
