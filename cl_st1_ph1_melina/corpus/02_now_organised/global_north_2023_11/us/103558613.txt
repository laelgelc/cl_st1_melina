<p> A man was shot and killed Thursday afternoon outside the Howard Street station in Rogers Park , hours after a Chicago Transit Authority train crash injured 38 people near the station . <p> Kenneth Sandy , 30 , was fighting with another person , who pulled out a handgun and fired shots around 2 p.m. in the 1600 block of West Howard Street , Chicago police and the Cook County medical examiner 's office said . <p> He was shot in the torso and was pronounced dead at the scene , police said . No arrests were reported . <p> Less than four hours earlier , a Yellow Line train crashed into a slow-moving snowplow on the tracks near the Howard station , critically injuring three people .
