<p> " When they told her she was being released , she did n't want to go . She told them , ' Either we leave together , or I stay here , and no one gets released , ' " a family member of Kirsht told Israel Hayom on Wednesday , one day after the 36-year-old was released on the fifth day of the cease-fire deal between Israel and Hamas . <p> Kirsht had been held in the Gaza Strip for 53 days with her husband , Yagev Buchshtab , the relative explained . <p> " She was with Yagev throughout the entire captivity . It was easier for them , " they said . <p> Their Hamas captors , however , refused to allow the couple to walk free side by side . <p> Kirsht was subsequently released alongside 11 other hostages -- including Merav Tal , whom she was spotted putting her arm around moments after cocking her head and giving one of the Hamas terrorists a steely stare .
