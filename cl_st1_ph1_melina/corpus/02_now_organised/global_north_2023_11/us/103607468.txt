<p> The Washington Post backed away from its claim Saturday evening that Israel and Hamas had reached a tentative deal that would free at least 50 hostages in exchange for a five day pause in fighting on both sides , after a U.S. National Security Council spokesperson tempered the claim online . <p> In an alert around 8:30 p.m . Saturday evening , the Post reported that Israel and Hamas had reached a " tentative U.S.-brokered deal " that would pause the deadly conflict in Gaza and allow some women and child hostages to be free . At 9:27 p.m. , National Security Council spokesperson Adrienne Watson reposted the story on X , formerly Twitter , with her own clarification : <p> " We have not reached a deal yet , but we continue to work hard to get to a deal , " Watson wrote . <p> The article was subsequently updated to say that the U.S. was " close " to a deal with Israel and Hamas to free some of the hostages , but no correction or clarification was immediately added to the story .
