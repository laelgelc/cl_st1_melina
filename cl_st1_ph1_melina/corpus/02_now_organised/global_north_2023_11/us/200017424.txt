<p> Boardwalk Empire and Boiling Point star Stephen Graham is to receive the Richard Harris Award at this year 's BIFAs . The award recognises an outstanding contribution by an actor to British film . Previous winners include Riz Ahmed , Judi Dench , Vanessa Redgrave , Daniel Day-Lewis , Helena Bonham Carter , John Hurt and Emma Thompson . Graham 's film and TV career has included hits such as This Is England , Snatch , Boardwalk Empire , Rocketman , Venom : Let There Be Carnage , The Virtues , and Roald Dahl 's Matilda the Musical . In 2020 , multi-BAFTA nominee Graham co-founded Matriarch Productions with his wife and fellow actor Hannah Walters . Matriarch 's first project was Phillip Barantini 's 2021 BIFA-winning movie Boiling Point , which also spawned a TV series . Matriarch is currently working on A Thousand Blows with Disney+ .
