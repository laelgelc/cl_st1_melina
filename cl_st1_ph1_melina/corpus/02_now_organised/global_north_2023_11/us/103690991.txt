Ten hostages will be released on each day of the truce , Israeli officials said . <p> Although some details remain undisclosed , the deal -- brokered by Qatari , Egyptian and U.S. senior leaders as well as diplomats in tense , on-and-off-again talks -- is expected to slow Israel 's invasion and bombing of Gaza while pausing a war that threatens to consume the Middle East and spread to Iran and Lebanon , where Hezbollah fighters have been shelling northern Israel for weeks . <p> Advertisement <p> Final steps to approve the deal came in marathon sessions of the Israeli war and security Cabinets that began Tuesday and dragged into Wednesday .
