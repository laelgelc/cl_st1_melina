In the summertime , it 's cleaned to prepare for the possible rupture of late August fires known to envelop homes and the wilderness in a haze of smoke . The canyon reminds me of being raised in Australia ; brush fires abundant , the smell of ash would often steel the air into a strange metallic taste and smell .
