<p> Decades before the latest eruption of war in Israel and Gaza that began with Hamas ' Oct. 7 massacre -- and well before Internet algorithms amplified misinformation -- the Israeli-Palestinian conflict was already a source of confusion , competing claims , Rashomon-like narrative clashes . Even basic facts seem to defy confirmation or debunking . <p> Nonetheless , we are obligated to try to understand , and books are the best place to start . This in itself is heartening , but it 's not to say that what follows is anything like a comprehensive or even a representative list .
