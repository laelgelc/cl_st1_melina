<p> BEAR , Del. -- President Biden , arguably the nation 's biggest Amtrak fan , visited a train maintenance shop in his home state of Delaware on Monday to showcase more than $16 billion in federal investments for rail travel along the busy Northeast Corridor , saying of long-delayed improvements , " we 're finally getting it done . " <p> The president highlighted spending to modernize 25 passenger train projects between Boston and Washington at an event in Bear , about 12 miles from Biden 's home in Wilmington . <p> " Why , in the United States of America , do we not have the best rail system in the world ? " Biden asked . Noting that better train service in some of America 's busiest locales would help ease car traffic and potentially reduce fossil fuel use , he added , " There 's so much more we can do to better the environment and quality of life . " <p> Amtrak runs about 800,000 trips daily on its Northeast Corridor , the White House says , which makes it the nation 's busiest rail corridor .
