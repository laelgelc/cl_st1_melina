Its bungling of the matter led Professor Morgan Marietta , the event 's organizer , to resign as chair of the school 's political science department . <p> Advertisement <p> The Oct. 18 event was an opportunity for students to ask questions of UTA Middle East scholar Brent Sasley . But it degraded almost immediately after Marietta 's opening remarks , where he described Hamas a terrorist group and repeated President Joe Biden 's description of a brutal Oct. 7 attack on Israeli civilians as " pure evil . " <p> Instead of listening and waiting for a chance to respond , students constantly interrupted both instructors , accusing them of sympathizing only with Israel . At least one student who clapped in support of what the instructors said was screamed at to get out . <p> This has become an all too common theme on college campuses , where @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ shout down or intimidate speakers or students with whom they politically disagree . The administration decided to require Marietta to jump through a series of administrative hoops before hosting another public event .
