<p> They call for humanitarian relief , aid and a political solution in Gaza . <p> All that existed up to Oct. 6 . The United States and other countries have poured hundreds of millions of dollars into Gaza to help the Palestinians . It all went into the hands of Hamas , which turned it into missiles and training for the Oct. 7 massacre of innocent people . <p> Two things I never hear these people talk about . First , how does Israel not get attacked again , by a group who wants it extinguished , if it goes back to the status quo of Oct. 6 and before ? Second , I never see the protesters ' signs completed . The signs say , " Free Palestine . " What they never say is , " from Hamas .
