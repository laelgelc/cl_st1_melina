American college campuses serve as havens of some of the most entrenched anti-Israel sentiment , with radical pro-Palestinian activists seemingly unmoved even by the torture and dismemberment of some Jewish victims . Among the most stalwart pro-Israel voices in America are , as usual , evangelical Christians . To cite one example , the Ethics and Religious Liberty Commission ( ERLC ) of the Southern Baptist Convention ( SBC ) issued an " Evangelical Statement in Support of Israel , " with signatures from major leaders in the SBC as well as other top evangelical figures .
