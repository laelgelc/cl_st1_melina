The New Jersey State Police removed the device , Ocean County Prosecutor Bradley D. Billhimer and Lakewood Township Police Chief Gregory Meyer said in a joint press release Sunday afternoon . <p> The spot is across the street from a synagogue , according to the news outlet . <p> The press release issued Sunday did not note the proximity of the discovery to the synagogue or describe the device itself . <p> " The device was found to be inert and nonexplosive , " Billhimer said .
