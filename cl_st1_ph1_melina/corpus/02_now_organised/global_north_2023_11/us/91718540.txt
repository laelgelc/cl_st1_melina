<h> One Israeli and one Palestinian cry together for peace <p> Rami Elhanan and Bassam Aramin belong to a club nobody wants to belong to . <p> Elhanan and Aramin joined the Parents Circle -- a community for families who have lost close relatives to violence in the Gaza Strip -- over a decade ago after they lost their young daughters to fighting in the region . <p> As casualties mount in the latest war , Elhanan , who is Israeli , and Aramin , who is Palestinian , are thinking about the parents who now join them in grief . Elhanan says he shares one message to these families , which is the slogan of the Circle : " It 'll not stop unless we talk . " <p> Choosing anger will only cause more suffering from the shared circumstances , Aramin said . It 's up to parents like them " to use this pain as a power " to foster connection , not devastation . <p> " Let us look forward , for other kids , " Aramin said .
