<p> Cynthia Nixon , the Sex and the City star and former New York gubernatorial candidate , announced that she is on a hunger strike along with other activists in protest of Israel 's attacks on Gaza , which came following Hamas ' brutal terrorist killings and kidnapping of Jewish residents . <p> Nixon protested the death of civilians in Gaza during Israel 's counter-bombing campaign on the Hamas-held area . <p> " As the mother of Jewish children whose grandparents are Holocaust survivors , I have been asked by my son to use any voice I have to affirm as loudly as possible that ' never again ' means ' never again ' for everyone , " Nixon said during a protest in front of the White House on Monday . <p> " In seven weeks , Israel has killed more civilians on a tiny strip of land than was killed in 20 years of war in the entire country of Afghanistan , " she said , citing reports from Gaza officials on the death toll . <p> " I am sick and tired of people explaining this away by saying civilian casualties are a routine toll of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ There is nothing routine about these deaths , " she said . <p> The hunger strike is going on all week , but Nixon told the Guardian that she would interrupt her fast for work commitments . <p> Nixon called on President Joe Biden , " who has experienced such devastating personal loss , " to " connect with that empathy which he is so well known and to look at the children of Gaza and imagine that they were his children . " <p> Nixon 's call for a ceasefire reflects the divisions on the left -- and , to an extent , within the entertainment industry -- over calls for a ceasefire following the Oct. 7 attacks .
