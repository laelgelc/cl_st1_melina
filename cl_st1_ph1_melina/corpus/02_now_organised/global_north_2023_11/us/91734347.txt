<h> Ryanair 1H Rev EUR8.58B <p> Ryanair Holdings said net profit for the first half of fiscal 2024 rose as traffic grew 11% to 105.4 million passengers and that it will pay a dividend of 400 million euros ( $429.3 million ) in 2024 . <p> The airline said that for the period ended Sept. 30 , net profit was EUR2.18 billion , up from EUR1.37 billion the year before . <p> Revenue was EUR8.58 billion compared with EUR6.62 billion the year before . <p> The Irish low-cost carrier said that it will pay an interim and a final dividend of EUR200 million each in February and in September respectively . <p> " The board ... will retain the flexibility to consider , when or if appropriate , the return of surplus cash to shareholders through special dividends and/or share buybacks , " it said . <p> The airline backed its traffic guidance for the fiscal year of 183.5 million passengers but said this was dependent on Boeing meeting delivery commitments , and that it expects pre-exceptional profit after tax for fiscal 2024 to be in the EUR1.85 billion to EUR2.05 billion range .
