<p> SACRAMENTO , Calif. -- California Democratic Party convention speakers called for a cease-fire in Gaza on Saturday , channeling a progressive stance that has opened fractures within the party . <p> Delegates cheered and chanted " cease-fire now ! " after Dr. Sara Deen , a Muslim leader from Southern California , called for a halt to Israel 's military operation from the main stage of a party convention in Sacramento . Deen was joined by Rabbi Steven B. Jacobs , an interfaith leader who condemned abuses by both Hamas and the Israeli government . <p> " We stand together acknowledging the thousands of Palestinians and Israelis dead in Israel and Gaza and the occupation , " Jacobs said , condemning Israeli hostages held by Hamas and " Palestinian political prisoners held without charges in Israel . " <p> The speech did not reflect an official party position .
