She was one of 19 Australians who made it out of Gaza through the Rafah crossing with Egypt last weekend . <p> The mother of five travelled to Gaza City on October 3 -- just four days before Hamas militants attacked Israel , killing 1,400 people and seizing more than 200 hostages . <p> Since then , Israel has been at war with Hamas in Gaza .
