<p> ( Reuters ) - A humanitarian assessment team visited Al Shifa Hospital in northern Gaza and saw signs of shelling and gunfire in what was described as a " death zone , " the World Health Organization ( WHO ) said on Sunday . <p> The WHO-led team , which included public health experts , logistics officers and security staff from various U.N . departments , was able to spend only an hour inside the hospital on Saturday due to security concerns , WHO said in a statement . <p> The team described the hospital as a " death zone " and said the situation was " desperate , " with the hospital basically not functioning as a medical facility due to scarcity of clean water , fuel , medicine and other essentials . <p> " Signs of shelling and gunfire were evident . The team saw a mass grave at the entrance of the hospital and were told more than 80 people were buried there , " the WHO statement said . <p> The hallways and hospital grounds were filled with medical and solid waste , and patients and health staff expressed fear for their health and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and 291 patients , including 32 babies in critical condition , remaining in Al Shifa , WHO said . <p> " WHO and partners are urgently developing plans for the immediate evacuation of the remaining patients , staff and their families , " it said . <p> " Over the next 24 -- 72 hours , pending guarantees of safe passage by parties to the conflict , additional missions are being arranged to urgently transport patients " to other hospitals in the south of Gaza . <p> The Israeli military did not immediately comment on the WHO statement or the visit . <p> The remaining 2,500 internally displaced people who had sought refuge on Al Shifa grounds were gone after the Israeli Defense Forces issued evacuation orders on Saturday , WHO said . <p> Israeli forces seized Al Shifa in their offensive across north Gaza last week , saying it concealed an underground Hamas command centre .
