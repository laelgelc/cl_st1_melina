<h> How Israel 's Iron Dome works <p> The missile defence system is one of the most important tools in Israel 's arsenal and has saved countless civilian lives over various conflicts in the last decade , analysts say . It is highly effective . <p> The Israel Defence Forces said the system boasted a 95.6 per cent success rate during a rocket salvo fired by Islamic Jihad in May . <p> Development on the Iron Dome first began in 2007 . After tests in 2008 and 2009 , the first Iron Dome batteries were deployed in 2011 . <p> The system has been upgraded several times since . <p> The Iron Dome is designed to shoot down incoming projectiles . <p> It is equipped with a radar that detects rockets and then uses a command-and-control system that quickly calculates whether an incoming projectile poses a threat or is likely to hit an unpopulated area . <p> If the rocket does pose a threat , the Iron Dome fires missiles from the ground to destroy in the air . <p> To those on the ground , a direct interception sounds like a loud bang and can sometimes be felt @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ carry 11 kilograms of high explosives , IHS Jane 's said . <p> Its range is from 4km to 70km . <p> It is important to note that unlike the air defence systems designed to stop ballistic missiles , the Iron Dome targets unguided rockets that remain at low altitudes -- the type often fired by militant groups in Gaza .
