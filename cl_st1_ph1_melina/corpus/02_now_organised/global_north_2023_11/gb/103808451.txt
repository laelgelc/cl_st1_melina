<h> Thank you for subscribing ! <p> A protest group staged a peaceful blockade at a Birmingham manufacturer . Members of community union Acorn UK linked arms and held banners outside avionics manufacturer Meggit in Longbridge on Monday morning , November 27 , claiming the firm was involved in supplying parts to Israeli armed forces . <p> Similar action took place at sites in Bristol and Leeds . Acorn claimed all the companies it targeted made or provided components and systems for military aircraft used in the bombardment of Gaza . <p> It coincided with a vandal attack claimed by campaign group Palestine Action in which red paint was thrown and smashed windows at number 55 Colmore Row in Birmingham city centre . Officers arrested four men on suspicion of criminal damage and they remained in custody . <p> Paul Barnes , West Midlands Area Organiser , Acorn UK , said : " We believe that , through non-violent demonstration and protest , we can highlight activity we believe should be called out .
