<h> Blackburn with Darwen Labour councillors in Gaza ceasefire call <p> TWO Blackburn with Darwen Labour councillors have tabled a motion to the authority 's annual Policy Council calling for a permanent ceasefire in the conflict between Israel and Gaza . <p> The resolution from Bastwell and Daisyfield ward 's Cllr Shaukat Hussain and Wensley Fold ward 's Cllr Sabahat Imtiaz will be debated on Thursday . <p> The move follows eight former Labour councillors leaving the party over leader Sir Keir Starmer 's stance on the Gaza conflict and forming a new ' 4BwD Independent Group ' group on the council with former Tory councillor Altaf ' Tiger ' Patel who quit the Conservative Party over its attitude to the conflict . <p> The current ceasefire is scheduled to expire after Monday , as Israel and Hamas prepare for a fourth exchange of militant-held hostages for Palestinians imprisoned by Israel . <p> Israel has said it would extend the ceasefire by one day for every 10 additional
