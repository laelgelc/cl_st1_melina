<h> Article bookmarked <p> A political resolution that addresses the roots of the conflict is the only way to defend Israel 's borders and citizens <p> ( AP ) <p> More than five weeks have passed since Hamas 's brutal attack on Israel . Whole families were executed , toddlers were slaughtered , women were raped , victims were tortured and dismembered . Approximately 1,200 people were killed , most of them civilians , and 240 Israelis and other nationals were taken hostage in Gaza . <p> Since then , Hamas has been firing rockets daily at cities and towns in Israel , while Israel has engaged in aerial and artillery bombardments of the Gaza Strip . On 27 October , Israel commenced its ground invasion , which is still ongoing . <p> According to the UN , around 11,000 people have been killed in the Gaza Strip thus far , among them more than 4500 children . In addition , 1.5 million people have been displaced , and 45 per cent of Gaza 's housing units have been destroyed or damaged .
