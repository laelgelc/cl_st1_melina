<h> In Pictures : Pro-Palestinian rally held at US Consulate in south Belfast <p> 06 November , 2023 08:11 <p> In Belfast on Saturday afternoon , pro-Palestinian activists marched from Queen 's University to the US consulate building in the south of the city . <p> A rally was held at a barrier on the road leading to the consulate , while five organisers were permitted to walk up to the gates of the building to hand a letter of protest to a police officer - who took it inside . <p> The rally heard speeches and chants condemning Israel 's actions in Gaza and US support for the Israeli stance . <p> At the close of the event , several children left teddy bears close to the barrier to signify the young lives lost in the conflict . <p> A Pro-Palestinian march to the US Consulate took place in Belfast on Saturday .
