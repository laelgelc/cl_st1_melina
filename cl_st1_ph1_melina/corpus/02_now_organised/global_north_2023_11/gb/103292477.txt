<h> Israel says it does not intend to ' reoccupy ' Gaza or control it for long time <p> WASHINGTON - Israel does not intend to " reoccupy " Gaza or control it for a long time , a senior Israeli official said in Washington , as Israeli forces pressed their offensive against Hamas militants in the Palestinian coastal enclave . <p> " We assess that our current operations are effective and successful , and we 'll continue to push , " the Israeli official told reporters late on Tuesday , speaking on condition of anonymity . " It 's not unlimited or forever , " the official added , without giving a specific timeframe . <p> Israel has so far been vague about its long-term plans for the Hamas-run Gaza Strip , should it succeed in its air , land , and sea operation to vanquish Hamas following a deadly Oct. 7 rampage in southern Israel by the Islamist militants . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu told ABC news earlier this week that Israel would seek to have security responsibility for Gaza " for an indefinite period " .
