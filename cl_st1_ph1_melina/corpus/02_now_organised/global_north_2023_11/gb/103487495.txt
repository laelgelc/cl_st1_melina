<h> Thank you for subscribing ! <p> Hundreds of people attended a protest at Birmingham 's main railway station over the war in Gaza . It was the second sit-down protest held at Birmingham New Street Station in as many weeks since the conflict broke out on October 7 . <p> Hamas gunmen attacked Israel from the Gaza Strip , killing about 1,200 people and taking more than 200 hostages . Israel has responded with air strikes on Gaza and has launched a ground offensive . More than 11,000 people have been killed , according to the Hamas-run health ministry . <p> People have gathered in Birmingham city centre and at New Street Station for regular protests calling for a ceasefire . <p> The protest began shortly before the stated start time of 4.30pm on Wednesday ( November 15 ) with a small group sitting down and chanting and holding up signs ' Gaza - Stop the Massacre ' . <p> The protest took place during the evening rush hour but routes around the station were accessible and passengers just mildly inconvenienced while the protest took place . Some passengers did express their annoyance . <p> The nearest the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ approached the group . There was one other minor skirmish when another man approached -- but the protest was largely peaceful . <p> The protesters aired their views blaming Rishi Sunak at one stage . Stop the War leaflets from the Socialist Party were handed out . The chanting was punctuated by station announcements over the tannoy of various alterations to train times and platforms . <p> Some train passengers stopped and watched the protest as they were passing through . At one stage there were around 70 or 80 people on the floor , lying down at one stage . At around 6pm some of the protest group started to drift off but the protest continued to beyond 6.30pm -- with chanting and some speeches . <p> Palestinian flags were being waved although one of the protestors said a flag had been removed from one section of the station .
