<p> Israel has confirmed for the first time that a total of 11 Americans are currently held hostage by Hamas with three set to the released . <p> National Security Adviser Jake Sullivan said this week the US does not have a " precise number " on the total hostages currently in the hands of Hamas . Israel and Hamas have now agreed on a truce in the form of a four-day ceasefire for hostages to be released , taken in the militant incursion into Israel on October 7 . <p> Qatar , acting as a mediator , announced the deal with Hamas later confirming the ceasefire would begin at 10am on Thursday . Both sides will let go of women and children first , and the supply of humanitarian aid flowing into the besieged territory will be ramped up . <p> President Joe Biden welcomed the deal , saying Netanyahu has committed to supporting an " extended pause " to make sure that the hostages are released and humanitarian aid can be sent to Gaza .
