<p> People were seen carrying placards calling for an end to bombing in Gaza and " Free Palestine " and they marched through the city to Kings Square . The march was organised after more than a month of aerial bombardment that has killed around 14,000 , following the October 7 Hamas terror attack on Israel . <p> More than 1,400 Israelis were killed in the assault , and more than 240 were taken hostage , according to Israeli officials . <p> Steve Gower , one of the people who spoke at the square , said : " I , as others , were privileged to speak at the Gloucester , Palestine demonstration and march today . " Shoulder to shoulder with 1,000 plus of Gloucestershire 's finest , from all walks of life , " he said on social media after the march . <p> " We brought Gaza to Gloucester for a moment in time . Each and every one of us gave Gaza a voice , many of us will never forget and are proud of our day in the sun . We are all Palestinians .
