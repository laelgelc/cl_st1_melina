And he knows only too well the sense of panic and fear that Israeli Jews would have felt a month ago when they fled from Hamas terrorists -- because he felt exactly the same on November 9 , 1938 . <p> That day , and the devastating night that followed , is now known as Kristallnacht -- ' The Night of Broken Glass ' -- when Jewish homes , businesses and synagogues across Germany and Austria were attacked by the Nazis . <p> Some 91 Jewish people were killed and more than 30,000 were rounded up and sent to prison camps .
