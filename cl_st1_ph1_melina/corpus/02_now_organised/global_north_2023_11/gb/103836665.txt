<p> ABU DHABI -- President His Highness Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan welcomed His Excellency Anwaar-ul-Haq Kakar , Prime Minister of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan , who is on a working visit to the UAE . <p> During the meeting at Qasr Al Bahr in Abu Dhabi , His Highness and the Prime Minister of Pakistan discussed cooperation between the UAE and Pakistan , with an emphasis on expanding opportunities to serve the interests of both nations . Particular focus was given to economic , investment , and developmental areas , alongside agriculture , food security , and energy , among other areas of cooperation that drive sustainable development and contribute to the prosperity of both countries . This engagement underscores the deep historical ties and mutual friendship that bind the UAE and Pakistan.The two leaders also reviewed regional and international issues of mutual interest , including developments in the Middle East and the crisis in the Gaza Strip . <p> The meeting underscored the critical need for sustainable channels to deliver humanitarian aid to the Gazan people and the urgency of establishing an enduring ceasefire .
