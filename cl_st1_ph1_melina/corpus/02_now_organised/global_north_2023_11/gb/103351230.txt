At least eight Palestinians had been killed by Israeli settlers <p> AMMAN - The U.N . High Commissioner for Human Rights said on Friday that Israel must take immediate measures to protect Palestinians in the occupied West Bank as they find themselves targeted by more violence since the Israeli-Hamas war erupted last month . <p> Volker Turk said at least 176 Palestinians , including 43 children and one woman , had been killed in incidents involving Israeli security forces since the beginning of October . At least eight Palestinians had been killed by Israeli settlers . <p> Before the start of the Gaza war between Israel and the Islamist group Hamas on Oct. 7 , it was already the deadliest year on record for Palestinians in the Israeli-occupied West Bank , with about 200 killed . <p> " I ... appeal as a matter of urgency for Israeli authorities to take immediate measures , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the West Bank , who are being on a daily basis subjected to violence from Israeli forces and settlers , ill treatment , arrests , evictions , intimidation and humiliation , " Turk told reporters in Jordan 's capital Amman . <p> The worsening violence in the West Bank has fuelled concerns that the Palestinian territory could become a third front in a wider war , in addition to Israel 's northern border where clashes with Lebanese Hezbollah forces have taken place . <p> " It is Israel 's duty to ensure that all incidents of violence are promptly and effectively investigated , and that victims are provided with effective remedies , " Turk said . <p> " Continued widespread impunity for such violations is unacceptable , dangerous , and it is in clear violation of Israel 's obligation under international human rights law .
