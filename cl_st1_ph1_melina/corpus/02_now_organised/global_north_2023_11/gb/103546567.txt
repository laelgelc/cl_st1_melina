<h> How Tallinn Black Nights is putting sustainability , diversity and inclusion at the forefront of everything it does <p> " The first thing I do when I wake up is check my phone , " says Saara Huimerind-Tuksam , the first sustainability , diversity and inclusion officer for Industry@Tallinn &amp; Baltic Event . When an attendee submits an online incident report , it is Huimerind-Tuksam who reads it . <p> Her role is to provide welfare support to the 700 industry attendees of the Tallinn Black Nights Film Festival ( POFF ) , taking place this month in Estonia .
