<h> IBBY calls for Gaza ceasefire and Israeli hostages release <p> The International Board on Books for Young People ( IBBY ) has spoken out on the Middle East crisis , saying it " joins the worldwide call for an immediate ceasefire and release of hostages in Gaza " . <p> The Switzerland-based non-governmental organisation also called for others in similar fields , including publishers , to speak out . <p> IBBY said : " For all of us who have committed a lifetime to serving children , we can not bear the continuing loss of children 's lives . We are reeling from news of the rapidly escalating conflict in the region , recognising that in this horrific crisis of death and destruction , it is innocent children who suffer the most . As a global community represented in over 80 countries , we stand together in our demand to end this humanitarian crisis . " <p> The organisation added that it extended the call " to all organisations and individuals who work in the fields of information , education and publishing " to " join us in speaking up for the rights of all children and youth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ obligation to protect all the children of our world and condemn all forms of violence against them . " <p> English PEN called for a ceasefire in October , saying : " English PEN calls for an immediate ceasefire to protect the lives and human rights of writers , journalists and the many other innocent people affected by the ongoing conflict in Israel and the Occupied Palestine Territories . We urge the UK government to do the same . "
