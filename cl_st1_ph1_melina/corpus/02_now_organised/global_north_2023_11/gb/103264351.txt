<p> Some schools in the UAE will offer an extended weekend to students to celebrate the Indian festival of Diwali . However , on-campus celebrations will be muted as the Israel-Hamas war rages on . <p> In a circular sent to parents , Gems Our Own Indian School said it would remain closed on Friday , November 10 , and Monday , November 13 , on the occasion . This would translate into a four-day weekend for the students . <p> Lalitha Suresh , principal of the school , said the four-day break will give families time to celebrate Diwali over an extended weekend . <p> " Diwali celebrations in school will be low-key this year , with activities limited to class assemblies that are educational in nature . All students have been appraised to be conscious of the regional situation , and they have been encouraged to celebrate Diwali by donating towards the Emirates Red Crescent campaign , " she told Khaleej Times . <p> She was referring to the ' Tarahum - for Gaza ' relief campaign that the UAE launched to help Palestinians affected by the war in Gaza .
