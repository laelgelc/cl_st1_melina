<p> ABU DHABI -- The Joint Operations Command at the Ministry of Defence announced its intention to build three desalination plants in Rafah , Gaza Strip , as part of the ' Gallant Knight 3 ' humanitarian operation . <p> Following the directives of President His Highness Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan , ' Gallant Knight 3 ' aims to provide support for the Palestinian people . <p> The production capacity of each plant is 200,000 gallons per day with a total of 600,000 gallons , benefiting 300,000 people per day . <p> The initiative symbolises the UAE 's historic stance of support and solidarity with the brotherly people of Palestine , particularly in light of the challenging circumstances they are facing . <p> ' Gallant Knight 3 ' was launched on 5th November under the leadership of the Joint Operations Command at the Ministry of Defence in cooperation and coordination with the Emirates Red Crescent , the Khalifa bin Zayed Al Nahyan Foundation , the Zayed Charitable Humanitarian Foundation , and other UAE institutions to provide humanitarian support to Palestinian people in Gaza . <p> The initiative also provide volunteering opportunities for doctors registered with the Ministry @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Dhabi , and for volunteers registered with the Emirates Red Crescent and other humanitarian and charitable entities in the UAE . <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
