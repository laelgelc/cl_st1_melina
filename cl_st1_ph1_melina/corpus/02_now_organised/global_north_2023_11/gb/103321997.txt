<h> In a first , pope to have day of private meetings with world leaders in Dubai <p> VATICAN CITY - Pope Francis will have nearly an entire day of bilateral meetings with world leaders attending the COP28 climate conference in Dubai next month , according to a programme released by the Vatican on Thursday . <p> It will be the first time in his papacy that the pope , 86 , who is also a head of state , has so many high-level , one-on-one meetings with counterparts and others in such a short amount of time . <p> The pope 's position on climate change is well known , so the event will give him an opportunity to connect in person with Arab and other world leaders to discuss the wars in Gaza and Ukraine . <p> Francis will be in Dubai from Dec. 1-3 . The conference runs from Nov. 30 - Dec. 12 . <p> After giving his speech to the conference on the morning of Dec. 2 , the pope will have two sessions of private meetings for the rest of the day . <p> Organisers in Dubai have not yet given a list @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ one-on-one meetings had been confirmed . <p> Buckingham Palace has said King Charles will give the opening address . British Prime Minister Rishi Sunak is expected to attend . <p> Two U.S. officials told Reuters last week that President Joe Biden was unlikely to attend but stressed that a final decision has not been made . <p> Last year , Egyptian President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi , Jordan 's King Abdullah II , and French President Emmanuel Macron were among the attendees . <p> Dozens of countries plan to push for the world 's first deal to phase out CO2-emitting coal , oil and gas at the Dubai meeting . <p> It will be the first time a pontiff has been at the U.N . climate meeting since they began in 1995 and he is expected to drive home a recent appeal for action to curb global warming . <p> In a major document on Oct. 4 , Francis appealed to climate change deniers and foot-dragging politicians to have a change of heart , saying they can not gloss over human causes or deride science while the planet " may be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ known as an Apostolic Exhortation and titled Laudate Deum ( Praise God ) , was a follow-up to Francis ' 2015 encyclical on the environment " Laudato Si " ( Praise Be ) . <p> Failure in Dubai , Francis said in the document , " will be a great disappointment and jeopardise whatever good has been achieved thus far " . <p> ( Reporting by Philip Pullella ; Editing by Nick Macfie ) <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
