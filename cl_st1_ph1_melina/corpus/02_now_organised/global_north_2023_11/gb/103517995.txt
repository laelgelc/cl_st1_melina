He also called for an immediate ceasefire and securing humanitarian corridors for the entry of urgent relief aid to Gaza . <p> The minister made the remarks during phone calls with his Czech and Brazilian counterparts . During his phone call with Czech Foreign Minister Jan Lipavsky , Prince Faisal stressed the importance of an immediate ceasefire .
