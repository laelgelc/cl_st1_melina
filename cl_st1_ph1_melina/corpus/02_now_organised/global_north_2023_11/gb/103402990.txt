<p> Hundreds of pro-Palestinian protesters marched through the streets of Manchester calling for a ceasefire . Palestinian flags stretched as far as the eye could see as demonstrators made their way down Oxford Road on Sunday ( November 12 ) . <p> It followed a vigil at Whitworth Park to remember those killed in Gaza during the ongoing conflict in the Middle East . Around 300 people were in attendance at the park for the vigil , which was organised by groups including Manchester Palestine Action . <p> Candles were lit while the names of children who have lost their lives in the conflict were read out loud . " It 's important to remember them but by remembering them we need to honour them , " one speaker said . <p> " Today we have to remember the 11,000 people who have been killed . " The rain poured down as the vigil moved away from the park and began marching down Oxford Road . <p> The police presence was increased with several police vehicles following the demonstration .
