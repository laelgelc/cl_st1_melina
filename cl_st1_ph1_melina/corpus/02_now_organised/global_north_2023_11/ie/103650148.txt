<p> A total of 240 hostages are being held in Gaza , including at least 30 children , according to the Israeli Embassy . <p> While the group 's leader did not provide any more information , a Hamas official gave some details of the negotiations to Al Jazeera TV . <p> Issat el Reshiq explained that negotiations were focused on how long the truce would last , arrangements for the delivery of aid into Gaza and the exchange of Israeli hostages held by Hamas for Palestinian prisoners in Israel . <p> Both sides would free women and children and details would be announced by Qatar , which is mediating in the negotiations , he said . <p> Sources from Hamas and Islamic Jihad also confirmed that their groups had agreed to the terms of a truce deal . <p> The agreement includes a five-day truce , comprised of a ceasefire on the ground and limits to Israeli air operations over southern Gaza . <p> Under the agreement between 50 and 100 Israeli civilian and foreign hostages would be released - excluding military personnel .
