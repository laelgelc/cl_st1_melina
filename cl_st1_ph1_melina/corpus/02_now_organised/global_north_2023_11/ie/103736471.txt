<h> Two outdoor events to show solidarity with Palestine this weekend <p> Two outdoor events for people to show solidarity with people in Palestine will take place in Galway city centre this weekend . <p> This evening , Friday , images will be projected onto the iconic Galway hookers at the Claddagh Basin as the sun sets , with people gathering at 4.30pm . <p> There will be a light art installation piece and projections onto the sails of the Galway hookers , along with live music by Palestinian and Irish artists including Roisin El Cheriff , Hala Jaber and Katie O'Connor . <p> Participants are urged to bring a candle and dress warmly if they want to join a call for peace and the liberation of the Palestinian people . <p> On Saturday afternoon , a rally for Palestine will take place through the streets of the city , beginning at Courthouse Square at 2.30pm . <p> People will march through the city centre before being addressed by Palestinian and Irish speakers , and some more live music , at the Spanish Arch . <p> Participants are urged to bring a flag , a banner , and a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and protesters will cooperate with Garda ? and stewards . <p> Members of the Galway Palestine Solidarity Campaign ( GPSC ) highlighted that a temporary cessation to the horrific onslaught on Gaza will do little to ease the suffering children . <p> Israeli forces have killed nearly 15,000 people in Gaza over the past seven weeks .
