<p> Christy Moore has announced an upcoming Dublin gig which will raise vital funds for Palestine . <p> The musician will perform at Vicar Street next year in aid of the Gaza Emergency Regional Fund . The concert will be held in memory of Mahmoud Abu Nujaile , Ahmad Al Sahar , and Ziao Al-Tatari , three medics who were killed in a strike on a Gaza hospital . <p> Contributions will help support Medecins Sans Frontieres , known for providing life-saving medical care in crisis zones .
