<h> Nelofer Pazira-Fisk : Echoes of collective trauma call for humanity to be heard amid the terror in Gaza <p> People search through buildings that were destroyed during Israeli air raids in the southern Gaza Strip . Photo : Getty <p> Nelofer Pazira-Fisk <p> Yesterday at 03:30 <p> " Somebody had to make a start . What we wrote and said is also believed by many others . They just do n't dare express themselves as we did . " These words were uttered by a 22-year-old German student , Sophie Scholl , as she was convicted of high treason for distributing anti-Nazi leaflets at Munich University in February 1943 . That same month she was executed by guillotine . <p> Scholl was part of a non-violent resistance group called the White Rose . On the day she died , she said : " How can we expect righteousness to prevail when there is hardly anyone willing to give himself up individually to a righteous cause ? "
