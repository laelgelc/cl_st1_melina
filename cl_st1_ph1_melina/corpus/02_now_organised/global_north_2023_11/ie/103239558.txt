How to relearn listening <p> So how can we resolve , like my young daughter Lulu has , to become better listeners ? <p> ? Stay focused on them <p> You know that moment when another person is talking and you begin to finish the resolution of their story or the punchline to their joke in your head ? That jump to the conclusion moment when your brain switches back to your world and how you view it . You may even begin internally constructing your own story to share before the other speaker has crossed their verbal finish line . <p> These are precisely the times I want to encourage you to try disrupting your old pattern of thinking by mentally shouting the word " focus " . I said mentally , not aloud . You might not have been listening . <p> Engage your body <p> Body language can make a huge difference <p> To help you retain that refreshed focus , whether you are meeting in person or virtual , remember to tell your face and your body to provide important indicator cues to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ occasionally to non-verbally demonstrate understanding , agreement or just plain , " I 'm staying with you " and following along with your delivery of your personal or professional perspective . <p> Also engage your eyes . Look at the other speaker . Do n't stare at them blankly , of course . Rather , remind yourself to sneak a glance down at your phone or over their head or shoulder at that interesting painting on the wall or whatever else may distract your attention . <p> Also tell the rest of your face to get into the act . Smile with your mouth or furrow your brow depending on the emotion of the words they are saying . <p> These mechanics might seem theatrical in nature to some of you .
