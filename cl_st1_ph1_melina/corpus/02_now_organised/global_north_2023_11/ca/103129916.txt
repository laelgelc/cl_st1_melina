<h> ' A more beautiful side of Israel ' : This Canadian-led photography collective is raising money for kibbutzim attacked by Hamas <p> This portrait of Emily , a 19-year-old lone soldier from Uzbekistan in the Israel Defense Forces , is one of dozens of photographs contributed to " Photographers for Israel " , a new fundraising initiative launched out of Hamilton , Ont . ( Photo courtesy of Brant Slomovic ) <p> When Niv Shimshon woke up to the horror of what happened in Israel on Oct. 7 , 2023 , he immediately contacted his friends and family back in his home country . The Israeli-born photographer -- who moved to Canada 10 years ago , now living in Hamilton with his wife and two young children -- could only donate a bit of money to his family 's kibbutz and offer words of support . <p> Feeling helpless , he decided to take action .
