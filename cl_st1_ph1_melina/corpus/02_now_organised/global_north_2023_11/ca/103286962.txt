<h> PBBM : At least 40 Filipinos ' safely , successfully ' exit Gaza <p> MANILA -- At least 40 Filipinos were able to " safely and successfully " exit Gaza through the Rafah border crossing to Egypt , President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr . announced Wednesday . <p> In a video message uploaded on his official Facebook page , Marcos thanked the Department of Foreign Affairs ( DFA ) and the embassies of Israel , Jordan , and Egypt for the safe passage of the Filipinos from the Gaza Strip . <p> Marcos said their successful exit from Gaza was made possible through the DFA 's coordination with the embassies in Israel , Jordan , and Egypt . <p> He said the Filipinos would be repatriated in the coming days . <p> " Ikinagagalak kong ibalita na 40 sa ating mga kababayan ay ligtas at matagumpay na nakatawid ng Rafah crossing sa Egypt . Sila ngayon ay patungo sa Cairo , kung saan sila magmumula para makauwi nang tuluyan sa ating bansa sa susunod na mga araw ( I am happy to announce that 40 of our countrymen have safely and successfully crossed the Rafah crossing in Egypt @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from there they will return to our country in the next few days ) , " he said .
