During that time he hit 28 more home runs than any other hitter , while ranking fourth in the majors in OPS ( .945 ) and sixth in fWAR ( 33.3 ) . <p> Bautista led the majors in home runs twice , won three Silver Slugger awards , and was a top-10 MVP vote-getter four times .
