<h> IDF conquers western Gaza Strip : Israel Defence Minister <p> Tel Aviv , Nov 17 ( IANS ) Israel 's Defence Minister Yoav Gallant has said that Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) has made significant advancement in the Gaza Strip and has conquered the western part of the enclave . <p> He said that all threats related to Hamas have been eliminated in the western part of Gaza . <p> Gallant , a retired General of the IDF , was speaking to soldiers during his visit to the 36th Armoured Division of the IDF . <p> He said that the Israeli military has got specific and significant details regarding the misuse of Al-Shifa hospital during its raids there . <p> It may be noted that IDF has entered the Al-Shifa hospital and has reportedly neutralised five Hamas men who had fired at the advancing troops from inside the hospital premises . <p> The IDF said that some laptops that they could get hold of from inside the hospital had revealed the presence of Israeli hostages .
