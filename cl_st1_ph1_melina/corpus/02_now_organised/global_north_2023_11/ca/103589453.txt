<h> Poilievre walks political tightrope as pressure mounts over Israel-Hamas war <p> Prime Minister Justin Trudeau has faced controversy over statements about the conflict in Gaza , but Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre has stayed out of the fray , even while taking some surprising stances , by sticking to his ideologies and staying on message , Conservative strategists say . <p> As Prime Minister Justin Trudeau faces criticism for his public comments on the Israel-Hamas war , Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre has walked a tightrope between expressing unwavering support for Israel and defending the rights of Canadians to rally in support of Palestine -- even those that support Hamas . <p> The Conservative leader has so far successfully navigated the political minefield the conflict has become , even as he has been working hard to court Muslim voters in Canada , by sticking to his ideologies and staying on message , Conservative strategists say . <p> In the days following the Oct. 7 assault by Hamas militants from Gaza that Israel says killed 1,200 people , both Poilievre and Trudeau strongly condemned those who took part in public demonstrations in Canada that appeared to celebrate the bloody massacre .
