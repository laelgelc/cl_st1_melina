" And here they can stay for a long time . There is a room with air conditioning inside . " <p> Israel has long accused Hamas of using the Shifa hospital complex as a command and control center as part of a wider strategy that seeks to hide its forces among the civilian population @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the hospital site has been at the center of accusations of war crimes on both sides , with Palestinians accusing Israel of targeting hospitals and Israel saying the sites were being used to shelter armed fighters . <p> Journalists were driven in Israeli military vehicles to the hospital complex in the northern Gaza Strip past a landscape of buildings destroyed or vacated during Israel 's nearly seven-week-old invasion of the Palestinian enclave . <p> Graced with arches , the tunnel was a well-built structure lined with stone and concrete .
