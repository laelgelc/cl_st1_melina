<h> Treasure Trove : David Matlow looks at history being written today and the call to release the hostages in Gaza <p> November 26 , 2023 <p> By David Matlow <p> All of the items included in the Treasure Trove since its inception in February 2021 have been related to our past . Today 's treasure relates to the history that is being written today . <p> We ca n't do anything about the past except learn from it but we can do something about the historic times we are in now . We can play a role in trying to write a better end to the story . <p> This card ( the size of a business card ) was handed out at the March for Israel held in Washington D.C. on Nov. 14 . It calls for the return of all of the hostages kidnapped by Hamas terrorists on Oct. 7 who are being held in Gaza . <p> Prepared by the Orthodox Union , on the back is the prayer for the redemption of captives . Maimonides ( the 12th century Torah scholar , philosopher and physician ) said " There is no greater mitzvah than @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ being hungry , thirsty , unclothed , and they are in danger of their lives too . " <p> Although some of the hostages have been released , many more have been held in Gaza for more than 50 days . Each of us has the opportunity to fulfill the mitzvah of pidyon shvuyim ( redemption of captives ) and work towards the return of all of the hostages . There are many ways to do this including writing to the Red Cross or your member of parliament , attending rallies including the one in Ottawa on Dec. 4 , hanging posters , and keeping the hostages in our thoughts and prayers . <p> We can each do something . We do this with the hope that we can make a difference , that we can play our role in history , and that we can help bring them all home now .
