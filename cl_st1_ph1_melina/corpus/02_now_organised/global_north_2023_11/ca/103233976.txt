<h> Trapped Amidst Conflict : Canadian Nationals Unable to Exit Gaza <p> Escalation in Gaza is seeing more dead and more reaction from International leaders <h> Canadians Caught in Crossfire <p> In the aftermath of devastating conflict , Canadian citizens find themselves in an increasingly precarious situation within the Gaza Strip . Despite the rising death toll and widespread destruction , escape remains elusive for many . <h> Dire Statistics and Stalled Evacuations <p> The Palestinian Ministry of Health in Gaza has reported a staggering number of casualties , with over 9,700 lives lost , including nearly 4,800 children . The conflict has ravaged the densely populated areas of the territory , leaving behind ruins and despair . <h> Canadians Await Rescue <p> Today , the Canadian government acknowledges the presence of its citizens -- permanent residents and their families -- who are caught in the turmoil . They face an uncertain future as the Rafah border crossing , their only passage out , remains closed . Global Affairs Canada has yet to provide a timeline for their evacuation , leaving nearly 450 individuals in limbo .
