Aharon 's brother Avichai , was wounded in the attack on Kibbutz Kfar Aza in southern Israel . Avichai 's wife , Hagar , and their three children , Ofri , 10 , Yuval , 8 , and Oria , 4 , were kidnapped and held hostage in Gaza .
