<h> ' Slight improvement ' in Pope 's condition <p> The Holy See Press Office provides an update on the Pope 's health : no new episodes of asthma-like respiratory distress ; a slight reduction in oxygen flow and levels ; and improved test results . His mild kidney insufficiency is not a cause for concern . Pope Francis has resumed work and , in the evening , called the parish in Gaza . <p> The Holy Father 's clinical condition , despite its severity , has shown a slight improvement . <p> No episodes of asthma-like respiratory distress occurred today , and some laboratory tests have shown improvement . The monitoring of his mild kidney insufficiency has not raised any concerns . Oxygen therapy continues , although with slightly reduced flow and oxygen levels . <p> Given the complexity of the clinical picture , the doctors are prudently maintaining their cautious prognosis . <p> In the morning , the Pope received the Eucharist , while in the afternoon , he resumed his work . <p> In the evening , he called the parish priest of Gaza to express his @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of God , who in these days have gathered to pray for his health . <p> It has been reported that the Holy Family parish in Gaza had sent a video , and the Pope called to thank them . <p> It has also been confirmed that the news spread by a French outlet about an apartment that Rome 's Isola Tiberina -- Gemelli Isola Hospital reportedly made available to Pope Francis for a supposed transfer is false . In reality , it is an apartment for private patients. --Vatican News
