( AP Photo/Nasser Nasser ) <p> RAMALLAH ( Palestine ) : Thirty-two Palestinians were released from Israeli jails on Saturday ( Feb 1 ) as part of the fourth batch of the prisoner swap deal between Israel and Hamas , Anadolu Agency ( AA ) reports . <p> The freed prisoners were handed over to the International Red Cross from the Ofer Prison facility near Ramallah in the central occupied West Bank , in line with the terms of the ceasefire and prisoner swap deal that took effect on Jan 19 . <p> According to an Anadolu correspondent , hundreds of Palestinians gathered near the Ramallah Cultural Palace to welcome the freed prisoners , waving Palestinian flags and chanting in support of the Palestinian resistance and Gaza . <p> Despite earlier warnings by the Israeli army , the crowd gathered to receive @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Israeli orders . <p> Many of the released prisoners appeared in poor health and with frail bodies , with some barely able to walk . <p> They were taken to hospitals in Ramallah for medical checks and treatment , the Anadolu correspondent added . <p> Earlier , the Al-Qassam Brigades , the armed wing of Hamas , released three captives from Gaza and handed them over to the Red Cross . <p> A total of 183 Palestinians are expected to be released on Saturday from Israeli jails , including 111 who were arrested by the Israeli army in Gaza after Oct 7 , 2023 , according to the Hamas-run Prisoners ' Information Office . <p> Among those freed , 18 Palestinians were serving life sentences , while 54 were serving long-term sentences , the office said . - Bernama-AA
