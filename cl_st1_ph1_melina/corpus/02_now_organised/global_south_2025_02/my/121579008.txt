<p> BANGI : Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim has expressed pride in his Cabinet ministers but emphasised the need for further improvements , citing ongoing geopolitical tensions and the rising cost of living as key challenges . <p> Speaking at a Cabinet retreat , Anwar said the session aimed to assess ministerial performance , address public concerns and make necessary adjustments to government policies . <p> " There are several indicators that are encouraging , which make me proud . However , we must tackle certain challenges and make adjustments to our projections , " he said during a press conference here on Saturday ( Feb 22 ) . <p> He said Malaysia 's economic policies must adapt to the complexities of global politics , including developments in the United States under President Donald Trump , responses from Europe and the evolving situations in China , Gaza and Ukraine . <p> " While statistics show low inflation , we acknowledge public concerns over rising prices of essential goods . <p> " We must explore ways to raise wages , including revising civil servant salaries and the minimum wage system @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on the people , " he said . <p> When asked if the retreat addressed underperforming ministers , Anwar clarified that the session was designed not only for ministers to present their views but also to receive feedback from independent reports , academics and civil servants . <p> " This retreat is not just an opportunity for ministers to give their opinions - they must also listen to assessments from independent observers , " he said . <p> Ministers were then given the chance to outline their commitment to improving performance , Anwar added . <p> " I will not say everything is perfect , but overall , I am proud of their dedication . <p> " They take their responsibilities seriously , heed the feedback given and are taking the necessary steps to enhance their performance , " he said .
