<p> KUALA LUMPUR : Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim held a meeting with Cuban Foreign Minister Bruno Eduardo Rodriguez Parrilla to discuss potential areas of cooperation that could be maximised for the benefit of both nations . <p> In a Facebook post on Friday ( Feb 14 ) , Anwar said the meeting also coincided with the 50th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Malaysia and Cuba , which began on Feb 6 , 1975 . <p> " We had the opportunity to exchange views and discuss cooperation synergies in the halal industry , healthcare , investment and trade , Asean , Bricks as well as the situation in Gaza . <p> " During the meeting , I also extended an invitation to Cuban President Miguel D ? az-Canel to visit Malaysia to further strengthen our longstanding diplomatic relations , " he said . <p> Parrilla was in Malaysia for a four-day official visit from Wednesday ( Feb 11 ) . <p> This marks his first visit to Malaysia since assuming the role on March 2 , 2009. -- Bernama
