<p> PUCHONG : Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim on Friday reiterated that the Malaysian government remains consistent and will continue to be vocal and firm in defending Palestinian rights . <p> He said the government also remains firm in upholding the rights of Palestine and its people to rebuild the country independently . <p> " The Gaza issue , everyone knows , is very troubling , but we ( Malaysia ) are firm in our stance with our friends , with Arab and Islamic countries to support Palestine . <p> " Our stance is that no other country has the right to determine the fate and rights of the Palestinian people , " he told reporters after performing Friday prayers at the As-Salam Mosque in Puchong Perdana here on Friday ( Feb 14 ) . <p> On Feb 4 , United States ( US ) President Donald Trump reportedly said that the US will " take over " the Gaza Strip , shortly after proposing the permanent resettlement of Palestinians elsewhere . <p> Anwar had , on Jan 29 , announced that Malaysia would build a school , hospital @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reconstruction of Gaza through a collaboration with the people and private sector . <p> He said the reconstruction of Gaza would also involve joint efforts by the Japanese and Malaysian governments , which intend to establish and launch funds through the East Asia programme . <p> Anwar 's remarks followed the three-phased ceasefire agreement reached between the Hamas group and Israel , with the first phase spanning six weeks from Jan 26. - Bernama
