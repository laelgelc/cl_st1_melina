<h> China opposes forced displacement of people in Gaza <p> February 6 , 2025 <p> China has all along believed that the Palestinians governing Palestine is the fundamental principle of post-conflict governance of Gaza . Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Lin Jian said on Wednesday . <p> " We oppose the forced displacement of the people in Gaza , " he said while responding to a question during his regular briefing held here . <p> He said that the Chinese side hoped that relevant parties will take the opportunity of the ceasefire and post-conflict governance in Gaza to bring the Palestinian question back to the right track of a political settlement based on the two-State solution , so as to realize lasting peace in the Middle East . <p> According to media reports , US President Donald Trump said the U.S. will take over and own the Gaza Strip after resettling Palestinians living there to neighboring countries , like Jordan and Egypt .
