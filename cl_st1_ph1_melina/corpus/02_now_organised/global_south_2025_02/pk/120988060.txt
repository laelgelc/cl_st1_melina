<h> Israelization of Kashmir <p> The striking parallels between the situations in Israel and India-particularly in the contexts of Palestine and Kashmir-highlight a concerning reality : India seems to be veering toward the Israelization of Kashmir . <p> At the core of Israel 's ideological framework lies a dual belief system . The first tenet posits that Jews are the chosen people of God . The second claims that God has bestowed upon them the land stretching from the Nile to the Euphrates . This belief leads to the conviction among many in Israel that the territories detailed in the Torah inherently belong to Jews alone . This concept transcends religious boundaries ; even non-religious Jews in Israel tend to embrace the idea of these biblical lands . <p> This is reminiscent of the Hindu philosophy of Akhand Bharat , which similarly views the whole region as Mother India , rejecting the notion of separate nations within its domain . <p> The belief in biblical lands is not merely the ideology of a few extremists in Israel ; it enjoys official recognition . Likewise , the concept of Bharat Mata @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Parivar , receives backing from the Indian government . <p> The concept of Bharat Mata , far from being a mere invention of the Sangh Parivar , receives backing from the Indian government . <p> Maps presented in the Knesset claim territory from the Nile to the Euphrates , just as the unveiling of India 's new Parliament included a depiction of Akhand Bharat that encompasses Pakistan , Bangladesh , Nepal , Myanmar , and a significant part of Afghanistan . <p> Both the biblical notion of Zionism and the idea of Hindu nationalism are endorsed at the highest levels of government , evidenced by their presence in parliamentary displays . <p> The two prominent global conflicts , Palestine and Kashmir , both feature prominently in the injustices faced by Muslims . In both cases , international law appears to support those who are oppressed , yet powerful nations choose to overlook these facts , leaving the United Nations paralyzed , and tracing the roots of these conflicts back to British involvement . <p> India 's current policies in Kashmir mirror those implemented by Israel in the occupied Palestinian territories @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> The parallels are striking : similar to Israel 's actions of displacing Palestinians and appropriating their lands , India is applying tactics in Kashmir that resonate with Israel 's approach . <p> Recent Indian legislation has been aggressively aimed at diminishing the Muslim majority in occupied Kashmir while attempting to confiscate state lands-a level of hostility reminiscent of the Israeli experience in Palestine , where similar strategies have been employed . It 's also noteworthy that Indian legislation reflects an inclination to draw from Israeli practices , with reports of collaborations between the two nations . Observers have illuminated India 's engagement with Israel under the term " West Bank formula , " hinting at plans that involve land appropriation reminiscent of Israeli settlements in Palestinian territories . <p> This continuity in strategy raises alarms about potential parallels , especially in light of past propositions from the Trump administration regarding the resettlement of Gaza 's population . Such discussions could equally apply to Kashmir , suggesting a potential mutual alignment of strategies by India and Israel .
