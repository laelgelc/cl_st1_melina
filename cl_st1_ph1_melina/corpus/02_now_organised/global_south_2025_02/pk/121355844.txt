<h> Gold prices soar Rs1,700/tola <p> Gold prices rose on Monday , following an increase in international markets . In the local market , the price of gold per tola went up Rs1,700 to Rs303,200 . Similarly , the price of 10-gram gold increased Rs1,458 to Rs259,945 , according to the All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association ( APSGJA ) . <p> The rise comes after a notable decline on Saturday , when gold dropped Rs4,700 to Rs301,500 per tola . <p> In the global market , gold prices climbed . APSGJA reported that the international gold rate reached $2,900 per ounce ( including a $20 premium ) , marking a $17 increase in a single day . <p> The surge was primarily driven by the global economic uncertainty and shifting investor sentiment . According to APSGJA President Mohammad Qasim Shikarpuri , market fluctuations are heavily influenced by geopolitical tensions and economic policies . " Trump 's threats are leading the market into a state of fear . Furthermore , political uncertainty , such as developments in Gaza and other international issues , continued to fuel market volatility . <p> Shikarpuri emphasised that gold prices were expected to remain high as long as economic and geopolitical instability persisted . However , if global policy directions became more stable and positive , gold prices could register a correction . <p> Locally , soaring gold prices have made it difficult for the general public to purchase the metal , with investment activity dominating the market . In Pakistan and globally , investors are turning to gold as a hedge against inflation and economic downturn , given its liquidity and reliability . <p> For now , gold remains the preferred asset for investors seeking financial security amid economic turmoil .
