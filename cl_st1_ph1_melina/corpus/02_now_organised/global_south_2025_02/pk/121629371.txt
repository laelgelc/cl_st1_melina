<h> OIC FMs meet on Gaza in KSA on March 7 <p> ISLAMABAD - An extraordinary meeting of the Council of Foreign Ministers of OIC countries on Gaza situation will be held in Jeddah on 7th of next month . <p> The meeting has been convened after hectic diplomatic efforts by Pakistan in the past three weeks . <p> Highly credible diplomatic sources told The Nation that the meeting would adopt a strong communiqu ? at the end of the moot which will likely reject the relocation of Palestinians from Gaza . The CFM would discuss different proposals by member countries on future roadmap for Gaza including permanent ceasefire and rehabilitation of Palestinians . <p> Arab leaders are scheduled to meet on the Gaza situation on 27th of this month and Gulf leaders on March 5 which would follow extraordinary CFM meeting on March 7 in Jedda , the sources said . <p> Turkish President in his recent visit to Pakistan had discussed fresh proposals on Gaza with the Pakistani leadership .
