<p> Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar says Pakistan shall continue to support the establishment of a sovereign , independent , and contiguous Palestinian state based on pre-1967 borders , with Al-Quds Al-Sharif as its capital . <p> He stated this during a telephonic conversation with Foreign Minister of Iran Seyyed Abbas Araghchi . <p> They exchanged views on the situation in Middle East with a particular focus on the continuing plight of Palestinians in Gaza . <p> Commenting on the proposal to displace the people of Gaza , the Deputy Prime Minister termed it as deeply troubling and unjust . <p> He emphasized that the Palestinian land belongs to the Palestinian people and the only viable and just option is the two-state solution , in accordance with the UN Security Council resolutions . <p> Ishaq Dar also conveyed Pakistan 's support for convening of an Extraordinary OIC meeting of Foreign Ministers to deliberate upon this issue . <p> Both sides agreed to maintain close contact on these developments in the days ahead .
