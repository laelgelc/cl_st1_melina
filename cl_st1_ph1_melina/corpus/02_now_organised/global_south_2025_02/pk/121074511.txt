<h> Gulf markets end mixed amid renewed tariff threats <p> Stock markets in the Gulf ended mixed on Sunday as investors grew anxious about President Donald Trump 's revived trade tensions with China and potential tariffs on other nations . <p> Trump said on Friday that he planned to announce reciprocal tariffs on many countries by Monday or Tuesday of next week , a major escalation of his offensive to reshape global trade relationships in the United States ' favour . <p> Trump 's plan to take over the war-ravaged Gaza strip and develop it economically has sparked concerns among Arab states . <p> Saudi Arabia has strongly rejected Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's comments about displacing Palestinians from their land . In a statement released on Sunday , the Saudi foreign ministry emphasized its categorical opposition to such remarks . <p> In Qatar , the index fell 0.3% , hit by a 0.3% fall in the Gulf 's biggest lender Qatar National Bank . <p> Elsewhere , dairy company Baladna declined about 2% after posting a slight decrease in fourth-quarter earnings .
