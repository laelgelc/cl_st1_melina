<p> Pakistan 's 24th relief consignment for people of Gaza arrives in Egypt <p> February 16 , 2025 <p> Pakistan 's 24th relief consignment carrying 55 tons of relief supplies for the people of Gaza has reached El-Arish International Airport , Egypt . <p> The consignment was dispatched on the directions of the Prime Minister Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif , by the National Disaster Management Authority in collaboration with Al-Khidmat Foundation to provide humanitarian and relief assistance to the brotherly people of Gaza Strip . <p> The relief consignment includes bell and winterized tents as well as tarpaulin sheets . <p> Officers from Embassy of Pakistan , Cairo received the relief consignment , and handed over to Egyptian Red Crescent Society for onward dispatch to Palestinians in Gaza . <p> More consignments from the Government and people of Pakistan are on their way and will be delivered soon to the Palestinians inside Gaza .
