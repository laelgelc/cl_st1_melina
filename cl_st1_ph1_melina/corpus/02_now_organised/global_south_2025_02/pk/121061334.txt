<h> Dar reaffirms support for Palestinian state in talks with Iran 's FM <p> Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar reaffirmed Pakistan 's support for the establishment of a sovereign and independent Palestinian state during a telephone conversation with Iranian Foreign Minister Seyyed Abbas Araghchi on Sunday . <p> According to a statement from the Foreign Office , the two leaders discussed the ongoing crisis in the Middle East , particularly the situation in Gaza . Dar reiterated Pakistan 's position in favor of a Palestinian state based on pre-1967 borders , with Al-Quds Al-Sharif as its capital . <p> The foreign minister also condemned proposals to displace Gaza 's population , describing them as concerning . He emphasised that Palestinian land belongs to its people and called for a resolution to the conflict through the implementation of a two-state solution , in line with UN Security Council resolutions . <p> Additionally , Dar expressed Pakistan 's support for convening an Extraordinary Meeting of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) Foreign Ministers to address the issue . <p> Both officials agreed to maintain close coordination in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ consistently advocated for a peaceful resolution to the Palestinian issue based on international law and justice . <p> Earlier this week , Foreign Office issued a strong response to US President Donald Trump 's remarks regarding Gaza , calling them unjust and unacceptable . <p> During the weekly media briefing , Foreign Office spokesperson Shafqat Ali Khan reaffirmed Pakistan 's firm stance on Palestine , stating that its policy has remained unchanged since 1947 . <p> " Any statement suggesting the forced removal of Palestinians from their land is unjust .
