<p> The Foreign Office on Thursday voiced strong concern and disapproval over proposals to displace Gaza 's population , labeling them " troubling and unjust . " It called for unified international efforts to support Gaza 's reconstruction . <p> " The Palestinian land belongs to the Palestinian people and the only viable and just option is the two-state solution , in accordance with the UN Security Council resolutions " , the FO said . <p> Pakistan 's statement followed U.S. President Donald Trump 's proposal to permanently resettle Palestinians from devastated Gaza to neighboring countries . <p> During key discussions with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu , Trump referred to Gaza as a " demolition site , " sparking reactions from Pakistan and other nations . <p> Trump repeated his call for Jordan , Egypt and other Arab states to take in Gazans , saying Palestinians there had no alternative but to abandon the coastal strip , which must be rebuilt after nearly 16 months of a devastating war between Israel and Hamas.
