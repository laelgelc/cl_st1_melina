<h> Saudi Arabia rejects Trump 's proposal on Gaza <p> RIYADH -- Saudi Arabia rejected US President Donald Trump 's proposals regarding Gaza , saying that the relations with Israel could not be normalized without the establishment of a Palestinian state . <p> According to a foreign news agency report , Saudi Arabia 's response came after Trump suggested that Gaza residents should relocate to Egypt or Jordan . <p> Reacting to Trump 's recent statement about US control over Gaza , Saudi Arabia firmly rejected any attempt to displace Palestinians from their land . <p> In an official statement , the Saudi government reaffirmed that it would not establish relations with Israel without a Palestinian state . <p> During a press conference at the White House alongside Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu , US President Donald Trump announced plans to take control of Gaza , declaring , " The United States will take over Gaza , and we will own it , " . <p> Trump further stated that the US would stabilize Gaza and create job opportunities .
