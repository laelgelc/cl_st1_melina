<h> ' We are witnessing worst phase of country 's media history ' <p> A senior journalist Zahid Hussain delivers a lecture at the Institute of Business Administration 's Centre of Excellence in Journalism ( CEJ ) on February 11 , 2025. -- Geo TV/Kashif Suri <p> Although the current curbs on media freedom in Pakistan are not as strong as they were during the era of Ziaul Haq , we are going through the worst phase of the country 's media history . <p> This was stated by journalist Zahid Hussain as he delivered the sixth Razia Bhatti Memorial Lecture at the Institute of Business Administration 's Centre of Excellence in Journalism ( CEJ ) on Tuesday . <p> The title of the lecture was " Is the age of objective journalism over ? " Hussain , who worked for various prestigious Pakistani and international media organisations in his over-30-year journalistic career , including Newsline , Herald , Associated Press , Wall Street Journal and Times of London , admitted that objectivity was no longer a sought-after value in the current state of Pakistani media . Attempting to answer what was meant by objectivity in journalism , Hussain said it meant bringing all the facts to the readers and letting them decide on their own without trying to influence them . He said objectivity was the most desirable thing for a journalist who must leave aside their likes and dislikes and concentrate wholly on the facts while reporting . <p> Hussain lamented that a majority of reporting that was being done by the print and electronic media lacked objectivity as the boundary between ' opinion ' and ' reporting ' had been blurred . He cited many reasons for that , including the decline in the institution of the editor as media house owners , many of whom had no training or expertise in media ethics , were mostly real estate tycoons or industrialists who had launched television channels to protect their interests . <p> He recalled that he started his journalistic career during Zia 's dictatorship when draconian laws were in place to curb the freedom of press . He added that social media had made it possible for everyone to express their views , and in order to curb this freedom , the Peca law was recently amended . <p> He , however , noted that social media also had many drawbacks and it was difficult to ensure objectivity there . Hussain then diverted to the international scenario . He said many international media groups were not as free as they appeared to be .
