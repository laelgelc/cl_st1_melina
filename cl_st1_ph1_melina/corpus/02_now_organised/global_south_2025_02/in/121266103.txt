A new ten-year framework , 2025-35 , has been adopted , emphasising co-production . A defence procurement agreement is also imminent . The last covers both increased buying by India of oil and gas , to partly address the trade imbalance , and civil nuclear energy , especially the development of small modular reactors . India had already tweaked its domestic law , reducing the civil liability of foreign suppliers .
