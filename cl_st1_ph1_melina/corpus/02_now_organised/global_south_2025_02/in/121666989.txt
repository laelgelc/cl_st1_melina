<p> The White House has officially reversed a key policy that linked US arms sales to international human rights standards . The decision , made by President Donald Trump , scraps an order issued by his predecessor , Joe Biden , which was aimed at preventing US weaponry from contributing to human rights abuses abroad . <p> Biden 's National Security Memorandum-20 , signed in February 2024 , sought assurances that US weapons would n't be used in violation of international law , a move prompted by concerns over Israel 's actions during its war in Gaza . The memorandum had called for verifying that US arms were n't being used in ways that violated humanitarian laws , particularly amid Israel 's military operations in Gaza . <p> Trump 's reversal of this policy comes alongside his approval of more than $7.4 billion worth of arms sales to Israel , including bombs , missiles , and other military equipment . This action effectively removes the last of Biden 's policies addressing the Gaza conflict . <p> Senator Chris Van Hollen criticized the decision , calling it " shameful " and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Senator Jim Risch , a Republican , supported the move , arguing that the original policy had undermined US-Israel relations .
