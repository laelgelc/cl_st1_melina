<p> Ambassador of Israel to India Reuven Azar speaks to Times Now in an exclusive interview . <p> Ambassador of Israel to India Reuven Azar on Monday said that common threat between India and Israel is the " fight against radicalism and terrorism . " <p> Speaking to Navika Kumar , Group Editor-in-Chief of Times Now Navbharat and Times Now , he said , " Our Prime Ministers -- Prime Minister Modi and Prime Minister Netanyahu -- share a great chemistry , a great relationship . I think there are two things about this relationship that are very promising for the future . The first is that , because of the increase in global competition , we have to cooperate much more closely . We can give India the edge it needs to compete technologically in the international arena , and India can give us the supply chains that we need in order to find solutions for our economy . " <p> " And this is becoming evident in the last year , in which we had a smooth continuation of our trade relations , " he said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and we will continue to do even more so in the future , is this common fight against radicalism and terrorism .
