<p> Hamas on Saturday released six living hostages at different locations across the Gaza Strip , including the city of Rafah in the south and the Nuseirat refugee camp in the central part of the enclave . The six Israelis are Eliya Cohen , Omer Shem Tov , Omer Wenkert , Hisham Al-Sayed , Tal Shoham , and Averu Mengistu . <p> Earlier in the day , Hamas handed over two Israeli hostages to the Red Cross . Tal Shoham and Averu Mengistu were paraded on stage before being handed over to Red Cross officials . Later , three more Israeli hostages were handed over to the Red Cross . Three Israeli men -- Omer Wenkert , Omer Shem Tov , and Eliya Cohen -- were escorted by masked , armed Hamas fighters and made to pose on a stage before hundreds of Palestinians in the central town of Nuseirat . <p> A sixth hostage , Hisham Al-Sayed , 36 , was the last to be released , according to Israeli officials and the Hostages and Missing Families Forum . <p> The convoy carrying the freed Israeli hostages has left @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's custody in the Gaza Strip .
