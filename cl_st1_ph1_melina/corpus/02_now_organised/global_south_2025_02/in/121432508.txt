However , challenges remain in economic relations due to Trump 's tariff policies and migration issues . This reflects the importance the US under Trump continues to attach to its relations with India . It also reflects the affinity , both personal and ideological , that Modi and Trump appear to share . <p> There were significant and substantive outcomes , as reflected in the joint statement issued on Feb 13 during Modi 's visit . The challenge will be on the economic and trade relationship given Trump 's obsession with tariffs and reducing trade deficits with partner countries . Even in the presence of Modi , Trump did n't hesitate to harshly criticise India on its high tariffs , and called for the imposition of reciprocal tariffs generally . <p> India may have gained some breathing space by holding out the prospect of a limited bilateral trade agreement by autumn this year . More consequential will be India 's willingness to buy more US oil and LNG . Optics of the visit were positive . But there was a sour note on the migration issue . <p> Just prior to the visit , GoI was embarrassed by deportation of the first batch of illegal Indian migrants in humiliating and inhumane circumstances . These were widely publicised in the American and Indian media . The visit was followed within days by another such deportation . In both cases , US military aircraft were used . It 's not clear why GoI did n't insist on using Indian aircraft , or even send a ship to evacuate them . The orderly management of the global commons through established international institutions and multilateral processes is important to developing countries like India . Some may believe that the US walking out of the Paris climate agreement and reviving the fossil fuel industry may take the pressure off India to reduce its carbon emissions . But this would be very short-sighted . <p> Climate change-related disasters , and rising costs of adaptation , would surpass any gains from a delayed transition away from fossil fuels . The US is rejecting any international governance of AI , and this may pose a danger of global proportions . <p> US foreign policy under Trump is creating an unsettling churn in geopolitics . POTUS appears to have regressed to a past when territorial expansion was a marker of power . He wants to own Gaza , take over Greenland and make Canada the 51st state of the US . The US has reached out directly to Russia to negotiate an end to the Ukraine war with neither Ukraine nor Europe being at the table .
