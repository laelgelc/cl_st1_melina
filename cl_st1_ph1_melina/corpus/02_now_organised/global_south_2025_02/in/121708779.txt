<h> Heartbreak in Israel : An emotional farewell to Shiri , Kfir , and Ariel Bibas after Gaza captivity <p> ET Online 26 Feb 2025 , 05:35 PM IST <p> Thousands gathered for the emotional funeral procession of Shiri Bibas and her children , Kfir and Ariel , whose tragic deaths in Gaza deeply impacted Israel . Despite the family 's request for privacy , the public showed their respect along the route , holding flags and orange balloons . The bodies were returned as part of a ceasefire agreement with Hamas . As the convoy passed , Israel 's national anthem played in tribute . The route led to kibbutz Nir Oz , where the Bibas family had lived . Yarden Bibas , Shiri 's husband , released in a prisoner exchange , emotionally stated , ' We wo n't recover until the last hostage returns home ' .
