<h> Maharashtra News Live : Maharashtra govt mandates use of Marathi in all government offices <p> The Maharashtra government has issued a government resolution requiring the use of the Marathi language in all government , semi-government offices , and corporations under its jurisdiction . According to the resolution , employees must communicate in Marathi with all visitors , except those from outside India or non-Marathi speaking states . If any government official fails to follow this directive , complaints can be filed with the office in charge , and disciplinary action will be taken .
