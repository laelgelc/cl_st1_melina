<h> Hamas announced on Friday that they will release three more Israeli hostages on Saturday in view of the Gaza deal with Israel . <p> Jerusalem : Hamas announced on Friday that they will release three more Israeli hostages on Saturday in view of the Gaza deal with Israel . In exchange , Israel is expected to free 183 Palestinian prisoners . This breakthrough is part of an ongoing agreement aimed at easing tensions in the region . The three Israeli hostages , identified as Ohad Ben Ami , Eli Sharabi , and Or Levy , were announced by Hamas for release in exchange for 183 Palestinian prisoners and detainees . The exchange is part of a fragile ceasefire deal aimed at ending the war in Gaza . <p> The three Israeli hostages were taken captive during the Hamas-led attack on October 7 in the year 2023 . Ohad Ben Ami and Eli Sharabi were kidnapped from Kibbutz Be'eri , while Or Levy was abducted from the Nova music festival . <p> In exchange for the release of the Israeli hostages , Israel is expected to free 183 Palestinian prisoners @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ serving long sentences , and 111 detained in the Gaza Strip during the war . <p> However , the exchange has been marred by accusations of ceasefire breaches and delays in aid deliveries . Hamas has accused Israel of delaying the entry of humanitarian supplies , including food , tents , and mobile homes , agreed upon under the truce deal . <p> " This demonstrates clear manipulation of relief and shelter priorities , " Hamas said in a statement . <p> Israel has denied the accusations , saying it has allowed thousands of trucks , including tents and shelters , to enter Gaza . <p> Despite the challenges , the ceasefire has held , and work on the second stage of the multi-phase agreement has begun . An Israeli negotiating team is expected to fly to Doha on Saturday to discuss the release of around 60 male hostages and the withdrawal of Israeli troops from Gaza .
