<p> Cristiano Ronaldo has been one of football 's greatest players for nearly two decades . Since starting his professional career at Sporting Lisbon , he has played for top clubs like Manchester United , Real Madrid , Juventus , and now Al-Nassr . With five Ballon d'Or awards and five Champions League titles , Ronaldo 's success on the pitch is undeniable . <p> However , beyond his football achievements , Ronaldo is also known for his generosity . Since tattoos require a waiting period before donating blood , Ronaldo has chosen to keep his body ink-free .
