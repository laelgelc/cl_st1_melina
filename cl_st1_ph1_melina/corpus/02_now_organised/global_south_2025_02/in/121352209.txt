A UNCTAD ( UN Trade and Development ) Rapid Assessment Report covering the most crucial period of the Red Sea Crisis stated that by February 2024 , container tonnage crossing the Suez fell by 82 per cent . Between December 2023 and February 2024 , the overall ship tonnage entering the Gulf of Aden fell by more than 70 per cent . <p> Consequentially , the vessel tonnage passing the Cape of Good Hope increased by 85 per cent , ascompared to December 2023 . The proposed canal also connects the Red Sea with the Mediterranean . Project details indicate that it will run from the Gulf of Aqaba via the port city of Eilat , passing through Arabah Valley for about 100 km North . Thereafter , it will take a westward turn north of the Gaza Strip , connecting to the Mediterranean . <p> Estimated to be about 257 km long , it will be shorter than the Suez Canal and may be built with a deeper draft to enable the transit of larger ships .
