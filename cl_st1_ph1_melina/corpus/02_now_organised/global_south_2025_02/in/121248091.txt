<p> WASHINGTON D.C : Prime Minister Narendra Modi arrived in Washington D.C. today , for a two-day visit , where he is set to meet key US leaders , including President Donald Trump , tech mogul Elon Musk and Vivek Ramaswamy , a close aide of President Trump . <p> PM Modi reached Washington D.C. on Wednesday night and is staying at the Blair House , the official guest residence for visiting dignitaries . His visit aims to strengthen India-US ties through discussions on trade , defense , and technology . <p> His meeting with Elon Musk is scheduled before his official talks with President Trump at the White House . The two have met before , with Modi touring Tesla 's San Jose facility in 2015 . <p> The Indian Prime Minister 's visit comes at a time when the US is facing trade tensions , mass deportations of illegal immigrants , and diplomatic challenges related to the Gaza conflict . <p> His talks with Trump are expected to cover trade deals , energy cooperation , and defense procurement , including liquefied natural gas and combat vehicles .
