<p> Gold is hovering at record highs early in 2025 , and the precious metal is getting a boost from investors looking for safe havens in an uncertain political environment . <p> While it was presumed that gold would keep rallying through 2025 , recent Trump administration policies have sped up gains , ING analysts said this week . Gold rose to over $2,880 an ounce on Wednesday , a record that put it up nearly 10% for the year . <p> ING now expects the yellow metal to reach $3,000 an ounce this quarter , a level Wall Street once considered a year-end target . <p> Bullion is generally treated as a shield against macroeconomic and geopolitical risks , and investors have reinforced this notion already this year by flocking to gold amid a spike in trade uncertainty stemming from President Donald Trump 's tariff maneuvering . <p> Trump announced and then delayed 25% tariffs on goods from Canada and Mexico .
