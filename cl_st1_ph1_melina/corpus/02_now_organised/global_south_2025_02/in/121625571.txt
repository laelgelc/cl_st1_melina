<h> Pope 's health condition improves slightly , resumes some work : Vatican <p> Raindrops are are seen on candles adorned with a picture of Pope Francis outside the Agostino Gemelli Polyclinic where the Pontiff is hospitalised in Rome on Monday . -- Photo:AP <p> Rome:Pope Francis showed slight improvement in laboratory tests Monday and resumed some work activities , including calling a parish in Gaza City that he has kept in touch with since the war there began , the Vatican said . <p> The Vatican 's evening bulletin was more upbeat than in recent days . It said the 88-year-old Francis , suffering from pneumonia in both lungs , had n't had any more respiratory crises . The slight kidney insufficiency detected on Sunday was of no concern . He is continuing to receive supplemental oxygen and doctors say his prognosis remains guarded . <p> He received the Eucharist in the morning and resumed working in the afternoon . " In the evening he called the parish priest of the Gaza parish to express his fatherly closeness , " the statement said .
