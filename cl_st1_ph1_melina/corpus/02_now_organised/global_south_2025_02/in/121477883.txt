<h> Israeli Defence Forces demolished the home of Amar Odeh in the West Bank city of Salfit , an alleged Palestinian terrorist responsible for killing two Israelis <p> Gaza : The Israeli Defence Forces ( IDF ) demolished the home of Amar Odeh , a Palestinian terrorist responsible for killing two Israelis in a stabbing spree last summer . The swift demolition action occurred in the West Bank city of Salfit , where Odeh 's family resided . The Israeli Army has also released a video of the entire operation of planting explosives and eventually blowing away the house to rubble . <p> According to reports , Odeh went on a rampage in the central city of Holon on August 4 , last year , killing Rina Daniv and Avraham Somechi , and wounding two others . He was subsequently shot dead by a police officer at the scene . <p> The IDF 's demolition of Odeh 's home is part of Israel 's policy to raze the homes of Palestinians accused of carrying out deadly attacks .
