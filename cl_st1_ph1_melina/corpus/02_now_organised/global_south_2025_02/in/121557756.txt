The tariff programme will definitively increase prices for American consumers and , in all likelihood , lead to a shortage of inputs for US industry . And , of course , the deporting of " large " numbers of immigrants will add to cost pressures as employers struggle to fill jobs . <p> More significantly , the push-me-pull-you tariffs will dramatically disengage global supply chains , which have become critical components of large-scale and high-end manufacturing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ worse , lower growth . This forced change of life -- however necessary -- for Europe will increase anti-Americanism as a renewed era of " the ugly American " takes hold in the world . <p> Importantly , American arrogance is sure to push Europe towards a closer relationship with China .
