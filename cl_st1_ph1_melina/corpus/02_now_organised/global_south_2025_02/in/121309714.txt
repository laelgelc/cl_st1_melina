Usually , this individual is alone , looking like the stock image of a festival attendee , a hanger for the tote with the festival logo , his frame confined to a blurred silhouette on the festival 's Instagram page . He has a recognisable feature that emerges when the author 's reading or presentation opens the floor for questions from the audience . His anticipating hand will go up , yearning for the mic , calling its attention with urgent , flailing motions . If not granted , sometimes the mic is snatched . <p> He will start politely enough . In Satyajit Ray 's short story , ' Patol-babu , Film Star , ' the titular character , once an aspirational theatre actor , stumbles into a minor role in a movie , where he has the scarcest piece of dialogue : " Aah ! There 's little room for negativity in literary journalism in this country .
