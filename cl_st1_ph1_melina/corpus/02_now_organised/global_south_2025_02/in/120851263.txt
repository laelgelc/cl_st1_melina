Despite the recent reiteration , the plan was promptly rejected by Jordan , Egypt , and Palestinian leaders , who criticized it as potential ethnic cleansing . <p> Amid heightened tensions , Trump 's remarks have reignited existing fears among Palestinians about being forced from their homes permanently .
