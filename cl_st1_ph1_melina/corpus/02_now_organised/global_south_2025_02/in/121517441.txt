<h> Trump says USAID program in India involved ' kickbacks ' to US officials <p> TOI Correspondent from Washington : The flap over the $21m USAID program for " voter turnout " in India continued to reverberate in both countries with US President Donald Trump calling it a " kickback scheme " and suggesting that it benefited Democratic operatives in America more than India . In a speech at the Republican Governors Association meeting in Washington DC on Thursday , Trump hammered away at the purported profligacy of previous administrations , insisting money appropriated by USAID to study political processes and health and education outcomes in countries such as India , Bangladesh , Nepal , and Moldova , was wasteful expenditure . " Can you imagine all that money going to India ? I wonder what they think when they get it . It 's a kickback scheme . It 's not like they spend it . They kick it back to the people who send it I would say in many cases , " Trump said about the now gutted programs.While not exactly a " kickback , " the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ spending in such programs goes towards administrative costs , including salaries and travel of US operatives detailed to execute the missions . But a former official familiar with how the " system " works explained that Trump also misses the larger point of the need for US investment in understanding other countries and their processes as part of its foreign policy toolkit . <p> The Washington establishment -- abhorred by Trump and his MAGA flock and dubbed " deep state " -- has long done this through a complex web of state institutions , NGOs , and non-profit organizations such as the Consortium for Elections and Political Process Strengthening ( CEPPS ) , which was allocated funds to study " democratic practices and institutions around the globe , " including in this instance , in India and Bangladesh . Founded in 1995 , CEPPS is associated with the National Democratic Institute ( NDI ) , which in turn in affiliated with the National Endowment for Democracy ( NED ) , whose founder once said the organisation does today what was done covertly 25 years ago by the CIA . Most @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Republicans dubbed " never-Trumpers " or " antiTrumpers . " While USAID insiders say its main mission is international development and it has nothing to do with the NED or CIA , MAGA activists say they all drink from the same fount -- US taxpayer dollars -- and are delighted with the Trump-Musk DOGE minders shutting off the money spigot . But old Washington hands in the foreign policy field , while acknowledging that there is plenty of waste and fraud in foreign aid , say shutting off funds and gutting programs will kill " domain expertise " which the US needs to retain its global primacy . Among the examples they cited was Washington 's ability to draw on regional language experts -- including those who had learned Telugu , Tamil , Bengali , and Gujarati -- to understand the political ecosystem that was throwing up national leaders in 1990s , notably when Narasimha Rao , Deve Gowda , and Narendra Modi were catapulted to the national stage . " Why are we caring about India turnout ? We got enough problems . We want our own turnout , " he said on Thursday .
