<p> Struggling to hold back the tears , Hamas captivity survivor Yarden Bibas bid farewell to his wife and two young children who were killed when held hostage in Gaza and brought to burial in Israel on Wednesday ( February 26 ) . After a funeral procession through southern Israel , where thousands of Israelis lined the roads with Israeli flags and orange balloons -- a symbol of baby Kfir and toddler Ariel 's ginger hair -- the single coffin holding the three bodies arrived at the cemetery . Standing at their fresh grave , Bibas addressed his young family , saying he loved them and was sorry for not being able to protect them . Israelis mourned the family , seen as a symbol of the trauma suffered by Hamas October 7 , 2023 attack .
