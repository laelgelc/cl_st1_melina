<h> Sydney Nurses Face Suspension Over Antisemitic TikTok Video <h> Two nurses in Sydney have been suspended for making antisemitic threats against Jewish patients in a TikTok video . Authorities are investigating the incident , which has drawn widespread condemnation . The video was initially shared by an Israeli TikTok user , leading NSW Health Minister and police to take action . <p> Two nurses in a Sydney hospital have been suspended following the release of a TikTok video where they threatened to harm Jewish patients . The video has triggered both police and health department investigations . <p> The footage , shared by Max Veifer , a TikTok user from Israel , shows a confrontation involving a man and a woman in medical attire who made threatening comments against Jewish individuals .
