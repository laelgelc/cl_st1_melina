How often does it happen that those who aggressively level all kinds of damning charges against those they seek to discredit are in reality themselves guilty of the very wrongs they purport to deplore ? And how often do those thus put in this false position foolishly fall into the trap by going on the defensive and trying to justify themselves rather than hurling those self-same accusations back at those who make them ? <p> When it comes to misdirection , few have proven to be quite as adept as the Media Review Network ( MRN ) . It is further characterized by the grossest of both history and contemporary realities . A standard rhetorical ploy , for example , has been to portray Hamas as just another liberation movement no different from what the ANC had been in the Apartheid era .
