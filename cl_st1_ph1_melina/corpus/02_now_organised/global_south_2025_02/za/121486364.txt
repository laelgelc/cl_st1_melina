The intention of this law is to allow the government to expropriate any property ( land being one such property ) for the purposes of building infrastructure . <p> Through this law , for instance , it is possible for the government to expropriate property in Soweto ( which is an exclusively black township ) if such expropriation will aid the delivery of infrastructure such as a road , railway line or healthcare centre , for instance . <p> The law is not about white land or farms , as has been claimed by voices resistant to transformation in South Africa .
