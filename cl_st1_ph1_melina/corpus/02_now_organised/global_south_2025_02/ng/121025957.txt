<h> International Criminal Court condemns US sanctions move <p> The International Criminal Court on Friday condemned an executive order signed by the United States President Donald Trump imposing punitive sanctions , countering that the order sought to " harm its independent and impartial judicial work " . <p> The court was established by the Rome Statute , negotiated within the UN , but it is a fully independent court set up to try the gravest crimes . <p> Such crimes included crimes against humanity . <p> Thursday 's executive order said the US Government would " impose tangible and significant consequences " on ICC officials who work on investigations that threaten national security of the US and allies , including Israel . <p> The directive followed the decision by ICC judges to issue arrest warrants in November for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former defence minister Yoav Gallant , which accuses them of alleged war crimes in relation to the conduct of the war with Hamas on Gaza . <p> The ICC also issued a warrant for a former Hamas commander , Mohammed Deif . <p> Neither the US @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are 125 states parties to the Rome Statute , which came Executive Order seeking to impose sanctions on its officials and harm its independent and impartial judicial work , " said the court in a press release , adding : " The Court stands firmly by its
