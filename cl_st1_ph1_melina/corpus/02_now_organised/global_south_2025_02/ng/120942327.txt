<h> Malaysia rejects Trump 's proposal to resettle Palestinians <p> KUALA LUMPUR- Malaysia has strongly opposed any proposal that could lead to the forceful displacement or movement of Palestinians , Malaysia 's Foreign Ministry said on Thursday . <p> Such actions constitute ethnic cleansing and are clear violations of international law and multiple UN resolutions . <p> The ministry stated in a statement , urging the international community to seek a credible and permanent solution that is acceptable . <p> " Any attempt , whether direct or indirect , to unilaterally and forcefully impose solutions that disregard the Palestinian people 's right to self-determination and infringe on their freedom is unacceptable . <p> " This is unjustifiable and will only further deepen one of the longest conflicts in the region , ' ' the statement said . <p> U.S. President Donald Trump said on Tuesday that the U.S. would take ownership of the Gaza Strip and redevelop it after Palestinians are relocated elsewhere . <p> Malaysia 's Foreign Ministry said that this is drawing a global backlash over the proposal . ( Xinhua/NAN )
