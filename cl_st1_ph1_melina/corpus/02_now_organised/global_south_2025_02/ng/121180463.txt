This concept of representative government has been positioned as the best and globally accepted . Where democracy works , it is people-centred . <p> Citizens wield absolute powers to choose who leads in every tiers of government . To this end , people are at liberty to vote any candidate of their choice without let or hindrance . United States of America , Britain and most European countries practice this form of government and have been at the fore front of ensuring that this ideology takes over global governments . <p> However , some countries have no regard for the western kind of democracy .
