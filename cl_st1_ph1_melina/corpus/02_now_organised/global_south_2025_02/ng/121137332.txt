<h> Arab states reject trump 's plan for Gaza <p> CAIRO ( Sundiata Post ) -- Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty said Arab states reject U.S. President Donald Trump 's widely condemned plan to displace Palestinians from Gaza and take control of the enclave . <p> During a meeting with U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio in Washington , Abdelatty emphasised the need to expedite Gaza 's reconstruction while Palestinians remained there . <p> A statement from the U.S. State Department following the meeting did not directly address Trump 's plan but emphasised the importance of close cooperation for Gaza 's governance and security , stating that Hamas should never again govern Gaza or threaten Israel . <p> According to Egypt 's foreign ministry , Abdelatty expressed hope for working with the new U.S. administration to achieve " comprehensive and just peace and stability " in the region . <p> He also met with U.S. Middle East envoy Steve Witkoff , where he reiterated similar points . <p> The Palestinian leadership , supported by neighbouring Arab states , has rejected any proposal to displace Palestinians from Gaza , a region @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ This has been a long-standing concern since the Israel-Gaza conflict intensified in October 2023 . <p> His later proposals for a U.S. takeover of Gaza and the potential permanent displacement of Palestinians have been widely criticised , with rights advocates and the United Nations calling it ethnic cleansing . <p> The ongoing Israeli military assault , which has killed more than 47,000 Palestinians in the past 16 months , has caused immense suffering , displacement , and a hunger crisis in Gaza .
