<p> GENEVA -- More than $50 billion will be required to rebuild Gaza after the 15-month Israel-Hamas conflict in the Palestinian enclave , according to an assessment released by the United Nations , the European Union and the World Bank ( WB ) on Tuesday . <p> The Interim Rapid Damage and Needs Assessment ( IRDNA ) said that $53.2 billion is needed for recovery and reconstruction over the next 10 years , with $20 billion needed in the first three . <p> Israel 's campaign in Gaza was launched in response to the Hamas-led attack on Oct. 7 , 2023 in which 1,200 people were killed and more than 250 were taken hostage , according to Israeli tallies . Israel 's operation has killed more than 48,000 people , according to Gaza health officials , and left the enclave in ruins . <p> Years of rebuilding work , including clearing unexploded ordinance and millions of tons of rubble lie ahead . <p> The report , issued amid a fragile ceasefire that began last month , warned that conditions were not yet in place for large-scale recovery and reconstruction work @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ enclave would be run after the war and what security arrangements would be in place . <p> " The speed , scale , and scope of recovery will be shaped by these conditions , " it said . <p> The IRDNA said that more than 292,000 homes had been destroyed or damaged and 95% of hospitals were non-functional , while the local economy had contracted by 83% . <p> More than half the total estimated cost of rebuilding , or $29.9 billion , would be required to repair damage to buildings and other infrastructure , including housing , which would require around $15.2 billion to rebuild , it said . <p> Another $19.1 billion would be needed to make up for social and economic losses , including health , education , commerce and industry sectors devastated in the conflict , it said . -- Reuters
