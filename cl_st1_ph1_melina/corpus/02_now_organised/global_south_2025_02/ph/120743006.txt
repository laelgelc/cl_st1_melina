<p> In 2005 , Aziz Abu Sarah -- a Palestinian then living in east Jerusalem -- met with a Jewish friend who was visiting Israel from the US . Although it was the tail end of his tour , his friend had n't met any Muslims or Arabs on his trip , and he was harboring anti-Palestinian views . " He was so one-sided , even though he knew me , " says Abu Sarah . It was a revelation that planted the seed for Mejdi Tours , a tour operator intended to bridge the divide between two at-odds populations in a country the size of New Jersey . <p> It would take four years , and the collaboration of Jewish-American co-founder Scott Cooper , to create the company , at the heart of which is a " dual narrative " philosophy , meaning that each tour group is guided by a pair of locals who represent opposing religious , cultural , political or ethnic perspectives .
