<h> Philippines sends humanitarian aid to Gaza <p> OCD Administrator Ariel Nepomuceno led the turnover of essential supplies , including 8,323 bonnets , 3,187 pairs of gloves , and 20 blankets to the Jordanian Consulate on Friday . <p> The Philippines ' donation will support the Humanitarian Air Bridge Campaign of Jordan . <p> " We stand in solidarity with all those impacted by this tragedy and recognize the urgency of the situation , " Nepomuceno said . <p> " We are hopeful that today 's ceremony will not only celebrate the successful partnership we have forged but also inspire other Philippine agencies to join future rounds of this important campaign . Together , we can continue to make a meaningful impact on the lives of those in need , both here and abroad , " Nepomuceno added . <p> More than half a million people have returned to north Gaza since the ceasefire between Israel and Hamas took effect last month .
