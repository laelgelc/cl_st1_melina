Governance starts at the top . Good corporate governance is ultimately the responsibility of the board . As is often rightly said -- companies do not fail , boards do . It starts with having the right men and women in the board with rich and diverse backgrounds . Diverse in the terms of gender , age , cultural background , education , professional experience , length of service . <p> A diverse board is not just about representation . It is a matter of resilience . The more diverse perspectives we have in scanning the horizon , the better prepared we are for what comes next . When leaders from different backgrounds , disciplines , and experiences come together , they collectively bring unique insights that help organizations think through complex risks , challenge assumptions , and seize opportunities . Governance is more than compliance . My 2017 column mentioned that in the institution I was with , governance went beyond formal rules . " For us it is all about imbibing and nurturing a culture of integrity , fairness , accountability and transparency cascaded from the Board , its management , and to all our employees . " <p> I am sure here in the ICD you are making progress towards nurturing such a culture in all the companies you monitor , as well as in your own practices . <p> Culture determines behavior . Without the right governance culture , even the best policies and structures will fall short . <p> We need only look at past crises to see why this matters .
