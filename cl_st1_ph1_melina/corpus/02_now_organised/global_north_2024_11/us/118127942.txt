The crimes and human rights abuses of autocracies such as China , Iran , and North Korea , among others , have largely been ignored . But Israel is targeted because the ICC intends to delegitimize the country . <p> When the court announced in May that it was seeking arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant , international legal expert Eugene Kontorovich rightly warned that the ICC was seeking " to create moral equivalence between Hamas and Israel .
