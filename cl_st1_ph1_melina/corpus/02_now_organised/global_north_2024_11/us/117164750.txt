<p> Hamtramck , Michigan , Mayor Amer Ghalib said " neglect " from the Biden-Harris administration on the issue of the war in Gaza led him to endorse former President Donald Trump . <p> With only two days left until Election Day , both Trump and Vice President Kamala Harris are battling to win over Michigan . The state has 16 electoral votes up for grabs and a history of swinging to either party . <p> " During the whole year of a war in the Middle East , I supported the uncommitted movement in Michigan to send a strong message to the current administration to take our concerns into consideration , but nothing was happening . In fact , they completely neglected us and negligence became the norm and everything else is an exception , " Ghalib said on Fox News 's Sunday Morning Futures with Mario Bartiromo . " Harris added insult to the wound by having Liz Cheney be her top military adviser , which brings memories back to the Arab American community about the invasion of Iraq based on false claims of having weapons of mass @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this is very disappointing to the Arab American community . " <p> The uncommitted movement encouraged Democratic voters to vote " uncommitted " in Michigan 's primary election as a form of protest against President Joe Biden 's support of Israel . Shortly after the state 's Democratic primary , Biden publicly called for a ceasefire in Gaza , which Ghalib said is his main priority . <p> Former Wyoming Republican Rep. Liz Cheney and her father , former Vice President Dick Cheney , have both endorsed Harris for president . Dick Cheney served as vice president during the war in Iraq .
