<p> Tensions over abortion rights erupted in Boston on Saturday , as hundreds of counter-protesters clashed with anti-abortion marchers as they rallied through the city in one of the first large-scale local demonstrations over the issue since the presidential election . <p> Nine people were arrested on disorderly conduct charges during the protests , which began outside a Planned Parenthood location in Allston before winding their way through the city to the Parkman Bandstand on Boston Common . Among the people arrested was someone who threw a bottle of what was believed to be urine at police , according to an officer at the scene . <p> The intensity of the demonstration and counter-protest reflected the high stakes many placed in women 's reproductive rights since the US Supreme Court overturned Roe v. Wade two years ago . The reelection of Republican Donald Trump , who has supported rolling back abortion access , has only heightened the political divide around reproductive rights . <p> In the lead-up to the election , Trump moderated his language and said he would not support a national abortion ban .
