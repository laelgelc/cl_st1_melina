( Chris Dehnel/Patch ) <p> VERNON , CT -- College credits are piling up in Rockville High School . <p> So is valuable life experience . <p> When the Rockville High School Class of 2024 graduated in June , many of its members moved on to the next stage of their lives with much more than a high school diploma . More than half had earned college credit through their dual enrollment courses with area colleges and through more traditional Advanced Placement classes and tests . <p> Those numbers are expected to increase when the current senior class graduates in June , according to school administrators . More and more students are taking classes that earn themselves college credit while also fulfilling high school graduation requirements .
