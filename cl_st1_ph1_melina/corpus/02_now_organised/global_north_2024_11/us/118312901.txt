<p> BOSTON ( WHDH ) - Hundreds of pro-Palestinian protesters took their message to Black Friday shoppers in the Back Bay on Friday , swamping the Prudential Place and Copley Place as part of a nationwide demonstration . <p> The protest was meant to highlight the group 's dissatisfaction with the US providing financial support to Israel in their fight against Hamas in Gaza . Many waved flags and held signs urging shoppers not to support an economy that indirectly supports the ongoing war in the Middle East . <p> Boston police say the protesters were not in the mall for very long and there were no arrests . <p> The Prudential Center and Copley Place did n't immediately respond to requests for comment . <p> This is a developing news story ; stay with 7NEWS on-air and online for the latest details . <p> ( Copyright ( c ) 2024 Sunbeam Television .
