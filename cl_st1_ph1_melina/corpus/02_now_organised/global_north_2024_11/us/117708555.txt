<p> The Democratic Party raised a billion dollars for the 2024 elections . They had a clear and consistent pitch : This was a last-ditch crusade to save America from an existential threat to its democracy . <p> And what did donors get for piling their wads of cash onto the collection plates ? A sprinkling of holy water on a towering inferno . <p> Consider , for example , efforts to hold Donald Trump accountable through the criminal justice system . He faced a kaleidoscope of legal jeopardy after leaving office in 2021 . Democrats put themselves on the front lines of these battles , telling us that swift and sure justice was needed to protect democracy from its enemy-in-chief . <p> Almost four years later , only one criminal trial , in New York state court , had even reached a verdict . No sentencing before the election , of course . <p> The wheels of justice turned slowly indeed -- giving Democrats plenty of time to solicit additional funds for their mission of salvation .
