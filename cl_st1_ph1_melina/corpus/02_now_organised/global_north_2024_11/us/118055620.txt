<h> The program officially launched in mid-November upon the enrollment of 17 new immigrants to Israel at Sapir College . <p> The Tekuma Regional Council is working in collaboration with Israel 's Aliyah and Integration Ministry , the Jewish Agency , and Sapir Academic College , in order to launch a targeted aliyah program . New immigrants to Israel between the ages of 18 to 35 will study at Sapir College in Sderot , and live in the surrounding Tekuma region . <p> The Sapir B'Aliyah program , a joint effort between the Tekuma council , the Jewish Agency , Sapir Academic College , and the Ministry of Aliyah and Integration , aims to develop a flourishing student community in the immediate vicinity . <p> New Olim will be able to live at the Jewish Agency 's Ibim Absorption Center , which will act as an integral resource for immersion and integration of young immigrants to the region , continuing recovery efforts in a post October 7 world . <p> Director General of the Prime Minister 's Office and head of the Tekuma Administration , Yossi Sheli , stated : @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on creating continuity and strengthening the young community here , while attracting a new and diverse population to experience life in the region . <p> " This initiative will reinforce the local youth population and bolster Sapir Academic College as a leading institution of higher education . Tekuma Administration is working closely with civil society organizations and national institutions as we promised at the outset of this endeavor . " <h> Kicking off at Sapir College <p> The program officially launched in mid-November upon the enrollment of 17 new immigrants to Israel at Sapir College . Students are pursuing both undergraduate degrees , as well as participating in academic preparation programs . <p> Their involvement in this particular initiative will also allow these new immigrants to receive extended Hebrew ulpan support while undergoing their studies . <p> The makeup of the participants in this program include those who hail from Ethiopia , the former Soviet Union , and many other nations . Those participating exhibit an interest and commitment in rebuilding Israel 's recovering south . <p> At a cost of NIS 1.4 million , the local administration , in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ministry , covers tuition assistance and living stipends of NIS 4,000 per student for one year . The Jewish Agency will handle the remaining aspects . <p> This program aims to invest in the region and rebuild the economic foundation , helping young Olim find a place in a once-flourishing community . <p> Major General ( res . ) Doron Almog , Chairman of the Jewish Agency , commented on the program . " The Ibim Absorption Center serves as an incubator for immigrants and young people who choose to build their lives in Israel . Immigration is a tremendous engine for growth , resilience , and innovation in all areas of life . It brings hope . The combined efforts in Ibim , located in the Gaza envelope , are driving significant momentum for recovery and development . I look forward to welcoming another million immigrants in the coming years to help build a stronger and better Israel . This is true Tekuma ( rebirth ) . "
