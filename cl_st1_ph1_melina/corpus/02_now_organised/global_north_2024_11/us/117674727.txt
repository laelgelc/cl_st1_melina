<h> The ongoing mental health crisis about diaspora Jews calls for building resilience , not pathologizing , spiritual and mental health leaders said at a recent Mental Health IsReal event <p> Rising global antisemitism and the ongoing war in Israel have led Jews around the world to experience heightened anxiety . A gathering in New York City two weeks ago put on by Mental Health IsReal ( MHI ) , a new initiative for Jewish mental health started after the October 7 attacks , aimed to address that crisis . <p> Dennis Prager -- co-author of the 1983 bestseller Why the Jews ? -- had a message of tough love for the attendees : Stop being surprised by Jew hatred . It has been with us from time immemorial . <p> " Vulnerability and pain is not unique to Jews , but we are the only ones targeted for extinction , " Prager told the crowd . <p> He admonished the audience for their ignorance about the rise in antisemitism .
