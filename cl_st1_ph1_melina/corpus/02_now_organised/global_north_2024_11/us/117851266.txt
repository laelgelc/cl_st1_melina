<p> Trust in the police is in decline , research by the Israel Democracy Institute presented at the institute 's Security and Democracy conference on Tuesday found . <p> " Trust in the police has undergone major upheavals over the last year and a half , " the institute said , adding that this trust was at a record low for the last decade during protests against the government 's attempted judicial reform in June 2023 .
