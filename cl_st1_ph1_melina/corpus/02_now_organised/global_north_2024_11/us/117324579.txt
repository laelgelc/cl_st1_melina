These theatrical juxtapositions , however inane , have a way of sticking in your brain . <p> But not everyone gets the reality-TV component of his act . Across different cities and states , it was clear that people stood for hours at Trump rallies because they still obsess over Trump the man , and because Trumpism has become something like a religion . Trump makes a significant portion of the country feel good , either by stoking their resentments or simply making them believe he hears their concerns . In the end , though , he 's also the one feeding their fears . <p> It can be easy to write off American politics as a stadium-size spectacle that 's grown only cringier and uglier over the past decade . Another was from the Jersey Shore and said she believed that people would egg her house if she put a Harris sign in the front yard . Another , who was from Texas , summed up the risks posed by Trump more succinctly than almost anyone I 've spoken with over the past two years of covering the campaign : " He 'll let people get away with promoting hate and violence in our country , and I think that is my biggest fear .
