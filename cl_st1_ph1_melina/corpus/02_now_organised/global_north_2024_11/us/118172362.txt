That they , like all people around the world , are entitled to the protection of the law , " the British barrister said . <p> The allegations stem from a counterinsurgency campaign that Myanmar 's military began in August 2017 in response to an insurgent attack . Hlaing , who heads the Myanmar Defense Services , is said to have directed the armed forces of Myanmar , known as the Tatmadaw , as well as the national police to attack Rohingya civilians . <p> Khan was in Bangladesh where he met with members of the displaced Rohingya population .
