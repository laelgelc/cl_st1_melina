<p> " Europe is sitting on an explosive barrel and it must wake up before it 's too late , " Diaspora and Antisemitism Minister Amichai Chikli said , following the release of his ministry 's new report into European antisemitism in the year following the October 7 massacre . <p> The report , Chikli said , is extremely pertinent given " the horrifying violence against Jews which again broke out on European soil , " referring to the Amsterdam attacks on Israelis on Thursday . <p> The dossier , which is mainly based on open-source intelligence , presents the trends of European antisemitism , and was commissioned by the National Command for Combating Antisemitism in the Diaspora Ministry . <p> For context , the European Union Agency for Fundamental Rights ( EUFRA ) recorded an increase of up to 400% in antisemitic activity since the outbreak of the war .
