The Israeli authorities have moved forward with its implementation , despite widespread condemnation from the international community and some of Israel 's allies . <p> The United Nations itself has denounced the move saying it will have " devastating consequences " as it is the main agency delivering aid to Gaza . While the UNRWA ban will undoubtedly amplify the suffering of Palestinians , it is also a spectacular own goal for Israel . <p> That is because it will elevate the two and a half million Palestine refugees in Gaza and the West Bank to a new level of international protection under the mandate of the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees ( UNHCR ) whose preferred solution for protracted refugee situations is voluntary repatriation : the right of return . <p> This is precisely the opposite @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ far-right cabinet , in particular , were hoping to achieve when they set out to destroy UNRWA . The second sentence of the article makes this explicit .
