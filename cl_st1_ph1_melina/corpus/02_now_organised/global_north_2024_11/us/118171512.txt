<p> The warrants issued by the International Criminal Court against Israel 's prime minister and former defense minister represent many historic firsts . They would be the court 's first prosecutions of leaders in a liberal Western democracy , and represent the first time anyone has been charged with the " crime of starvation " ; the first time the court has accused a country of war crimes during a defensive war against an external invader ; and the first prosecution of a non -- member state at the bequest of a member that is not generally recognized as a state . <p> For all of these juristic innovations , the warrants also represent something entirely familiar : an international institution , created to serve high and noble purposes , succumbing to the temptation of pursuing an anti-Israel agenda .
