<h> Letter : Hamas is responsible for despair in Gaza <h> Share this : <h> Hamas is responsible for despair in Gaza <p> Editor : On Oct. 6 , 2023 , the world tends to forget , there were no Gazan deaths , no one living in tents and no talk of famine and starvation . The next day , Hamas invaded Israel accompanied by hundreds of rockets , killing and raping Israelis . The news of the invasion was met with jubilation on the streets of Gaza . Candy was handed out to the cheering crowds as Hamas paraded their " spoils of war , " dead Israeli soldiers and children , women and elderly hostages . I remember the interviews with the Gazans who were proud of the invasion , as it kindled a spirit of national pride . <p> However , this momentary euphoria has been replaced with despair . Who is to blame for the death and the suffering of Gaza ? Only a person of twisted mind would say Israel . It is Hamas . The news has forgotten the 100 hostages , men , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ being held starving in underground tunnels and all that is reported is the dire straits of the Gazans . It is clear that if the hostages would be released , the war and the suffering would end . My question is , where are the mass demonstrations by the suffering people of Gaza demanding that Hamas surrender ? Why are the Gazans not helping to find and free the hostages ? <p> Is the answer that they hate Israel and are faithful to Hamas ? If that is the case , they are enemies of Israel and then it is grotesque to hold Israel responsible for providing food and medicine to sympathizers of Hamas . Rather , have those who are concerned of their welfare force Hamas to surrender now .
