<h> The initiative aims to strengthen ties between faith , military service , and the land of Israel . <p> A new initiative , the " Oz LeYehuda " program , pairs IDF soldiers with community volunteers for Torah study during weekends and leave , reflecting the values of Yehuda Cohen , a Givati Brigade commander who was killed in combat in Gaza . <p> The program is one of two projects launched to honor Cohen 's legacy , alongside the Yehuda Lookout Point in the northern Jordan Valley . <h> " Oz LeYehuda " program <p> The program was inspired by Cohen 's belief in the spiritual power of learning to enhance military service .
