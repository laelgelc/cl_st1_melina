<p> New Jersey 's interim U.S. Senator George Helmy recently returned from a humanitarian trip to Jordan in light of the Israel-Hamas war and voiced concerns about Israel 's move to ban a humanitarian arm of the United Nations . <p> Helmy , a member of the Senate Foreign Relations Committee , visited Jordan last Monday and met with the deputy commissioner of the United Nations Relief and Works Agency ( UNRWA ) , the primary U.N . agency responsible for coordinating aid into Gaza and the West Bank . An Israeli bill banning the agency is expected to take effect in three months . <p> Helmy is the first Coptic Christian to serve in the U.S. Senate and is also the only Arab-American in the upper chamber . He was appointed by Gov . Phil Murphy to replace Sen. Bob Menendez stepped down after the longtime Democrat was convicted of federal corruption charges . Helmy is holding the seat while the election between Democrat Andy Kim and Curtis Bashaw plays out .
