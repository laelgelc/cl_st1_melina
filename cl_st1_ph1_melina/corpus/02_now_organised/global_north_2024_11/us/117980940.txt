<h> Israel stands by as aid convoys come under attack in Gaza <p> Tel Aviv -- For the past year , hungry Gazans have waited in desperation for more food to enter the besieged territory from Israel . After aid trucks have been allowed in , there have been episodes of looting . <p> But now , armed criminal gangs are intercepting entire convoys . <p> Truck driver Abu Ahmad was in the worst incident so far when more than 100 trucks were attacked on Saturday . The gangs shot through the windows of his truck , he told CBS News , saying they would kill any driver who did n't stop . <p> Ahmad said Israeli tanks were nearby and an Israeli drone watched the entire attack unfold . <p> However , Israel 's military says it is not responsible for protecting the aid convoys , a premise with which former Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Olmert disagrees . <p> " The Israeli government does n't want it to happen .
