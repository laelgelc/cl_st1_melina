Despite some early claims to the contrary , this did n't determine the outcome , either in the popular vote or in the electoral college . However , the shift in Arab and Muslim American sentiment toward Trump and away from Kamala Harris , if not all other Democrats , tells a fascinating part of the 2024 election saga . <p> The swing in Michigan was quite dramatic .
