My country has elected a felon whose former top aides have described him as a fascist and " the most dangerous person to this country . " Yet in an election that was n't even close , voters not only chose him but also picked a Republican Senate to empower him further . <p> This will be a test of our country and of each of us , so let me offer a manifesto for how ordinary Americans of my ilk can respond . <p> 1 . I accept Donald Trump 's victory . If we are to stand up to Trump , we must first resist the impulse to be like Trump . We lost . We were outvoted .
