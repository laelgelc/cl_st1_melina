Israel claimed to have killed Deif in July , however . It is unclear whether he is still alive . <p> Effectively , the defendants are now internationally wanted suspects and ICC member states are under legal obligation to arrest them . <p> Israeli officials slammed the move , calling it " anti-Semitic " .
