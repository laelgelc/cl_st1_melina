And it has taken root within many of our own families . <p> Blood may be thicker than water , but for many American families it seems like it is not as thick as politics . Or , if not politics specifically , then the cultural rifts have been widened and inflamed within our political debate . <p> Maybe our national healing can start with our families , around the holiday table . <p> There are mixed views within my own family , just like there are in millions of American families .
