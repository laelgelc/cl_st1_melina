<h> Vaccination schedule delayed <p> In a statement , COGAT said that the " coordination will ensure that the population can safely reach medical centers where the vaccines will be administered . " <p> The polio campaign began on Sept. 1 after the World Health Organisation ( WHO ) confirmed in August that a baby was partially paralysed by the type 2 polio virus , the first such case in the territory in 25 years . <p> The final phase of the campaign was intended to reach an estimated 119,000 children under 10 years old in northern Gaza with a second dose of the oral polio vaccine . However , achieving this target is now unlikely . <p> COGAT said there will be an assessment upon the campaign 's completion to see if the vaccination schedule will be extended .
