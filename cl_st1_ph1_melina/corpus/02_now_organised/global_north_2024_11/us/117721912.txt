<p> Last week , a United Nations-backed group , the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification 's Famine Review Committee , warned that parts of the Gaza Strip were at risk of " imminent " famine . Since the war began , more than a year ago , it has been difficult to get aid into the territory . This summer , there had been slight progress in increasing the amount of food and medicine arriving , but , in August , those efforts began to stall as Israel stymied delivery operations . Aid organizations and the United Nations have made clear that the humanitarian emergency is especially acute in Gaza 's north .
