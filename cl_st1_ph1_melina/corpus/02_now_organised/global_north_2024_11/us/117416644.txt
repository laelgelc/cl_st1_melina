Come January , that number will remain the same , even though history was made on Tuesday night . <p> Donald Trump , the Republican president elected in 2016 , will return to the White House . He has vowed to round up immigrants and begin mass deportations . He has claimed he can stop the war in Ukraine and the Israeli wars in Gaza and Lebanon with phone calls . He has spoken frequently in recent months about " the enemy within " and expressed his willingness to weaponize the Department of Justice and even employ the U.S. military to quash dissent from political critics to the media to average citizens alike . <p> It portends a very dark future .
