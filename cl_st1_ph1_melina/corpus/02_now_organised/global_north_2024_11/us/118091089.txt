<h> Israeli strikes kill dozens in Lebanon as Israel accuses Hezbollah of firing a barrage of some 250 rockets <p> Tel Aviv -- Israel said a barrage of hundreds of rockets was fired at the north of the country Sunday by Iran-backed Hezbollah militants in neighboring Lebanon .
