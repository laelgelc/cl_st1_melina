Fenigstein started the festival in 1981 when he was a student at the Berklee College of Music in Boston , thinking it would be nice to show some Israeli films there at a time when virtually none were shown in America . The festival has grown into a huge event that has been held in many cities over the years , including New York , Miami , and Chicago , in addition to Los Angeles .
