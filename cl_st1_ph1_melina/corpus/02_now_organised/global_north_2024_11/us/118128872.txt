To date , the conflict has killed thousands and caused over 1 million Lebanese to flee their homes . As the winter months set in on the Lebanese mountains Raja'a , whose name means hope , grew more desperate . Her phone calls have become more frequent . " Firas my dear , do you think peace is near ? I 'm cold . In its wake , Hezbollah grew exponentially stronger as Iran furnished it with ballistic missiles and precision-guided munitions while U.N . peacekeepers and the Lebanese Armed Forces looked the other way . <p> To address these concerns , the U.S. has reportedly provided Israel a side-letter of assurance that consents to continued aerial reconnaissance over Lebanon , and Tel Aviv 's right to strike at emerging threats if the monitoring and enforcement mechanism fail .
