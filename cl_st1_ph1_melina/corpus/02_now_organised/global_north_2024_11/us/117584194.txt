<h> Staff Sergeant Gary Lalhruaikima Zolat , 21 , from Afula , was among three other IDF soldiers killed in an explosion in northern Gaza . <p> Staff Sergeant Gary Lalhruaikima Zolat was killed in battle while fighting in the northern Gaza Strip along with three other soldiers from the Shimshon Battalion ( 92 ) . <p> Zolat was 21 years old from Afula and served in the Kfir Brigade . <p> " He was a good boy . Loved his family and friends in the army . Loved animals . He wanted to be a warrior , " his father told Ynet . " I spoke to him on Sunday night , and he said he was fine . He said his leg hurt , but he could do whatever he needed with his friends . " <p> An explosion killed the four IDF soldiers , and the IDF is investigating whether it was an anti-tank missile or an explosive device . <p> He leaves behind his parents , two sisters , and a brother . He moved with his family in 2014 to Afula from Sderot . IDF @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2024.
