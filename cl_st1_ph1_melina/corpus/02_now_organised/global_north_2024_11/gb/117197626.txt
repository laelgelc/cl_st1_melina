After 7 October , he helped start a movement to withhold a vote for Joe Biden at the Democratic Primary in Michigan over his stance on Gaza , resulting in over 100,000 people voting ' Uncommitted ' on their ballot papers . <p> Michigan has historically been a battleground for Republicans and Democrats .
