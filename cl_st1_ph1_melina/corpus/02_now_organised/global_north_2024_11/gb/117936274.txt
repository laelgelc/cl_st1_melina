<p> The Republic of Ireland 's bishops are calling on voters to remember the right to life -- at every stage -- as the country prepares for elections . They have framed this need within the requirement for better Church-State dialogue in the face of increasing " diminution " of respect for life , both at the start and end of the natural life span . <p> " The current structures for Church-State dialogue are not functioning for the greater good of society and are in need of review , " the Irish bishops say in a statement issued on 20 November . <p> " Any such structures must be made work so that the voice of Churches and other faith communities can be heard and contribute to a national reawakening based on the reality of people 's lives , and not ideology or special interests . As a Church , we are open to engaging with Government and with other Churches and faiths on this question .
