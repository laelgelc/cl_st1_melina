<p> From their home in Nabatieh 's historic city centre , Amina Bitar and her family have watched as homes and shops around them have fallen into piles of blackened rubble . Just across the street , a giant crater could be seen where her neighbour 's home once was . To the left , two cars were stuck in a pile of charred debris , their window panes blown out and doors left ajar . <p> Despite the strength of the nearby blasts , the herbs potted on the terrace were still alive .
