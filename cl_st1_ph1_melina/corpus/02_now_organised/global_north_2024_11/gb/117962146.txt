<h> What happened <p> The International Criminal Court Thursday issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant , and Hamas military leader Ibrahim Al-Masri , saying the three men were likely responsible for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza and Israel . <p> Hamas killed about 1,200 Israelis and abducted 250 others in its Oct. 7. attack . Israel 's known death toll in Gaza topped 44,000 yesterday , according to the Gaza Health Ministry , with more than half of the dead noncombatants . <h> Who said what <p> The ICC said it found " reasonable grounds " to believe Netanyahu and Gallant were " criminally responsible " for the " war crime of starvation as a method of warfare " and the " crimes against humanity of murder , persecution and other inhumane acts .
