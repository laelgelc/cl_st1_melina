<h> Medo Halimy planted something every day in the Al-Mawasi encampment , until he was killed by an Israeli airstrike . His declaration of hope wo n't be forgotten <p> His name was Medo Halimy . When he was 19 he made a garden in the refugee camp where he lived . Under a brutal Gazan sun he sowed seeds which his little brother had given him . When the seedlings were big enough , he transplanted them into the sandy soil , watering them in with dark brown fertiliser from a plastic bottle . <p> By last month , Medo 's garden had expanded to a handful of plants . Melon , rocket , lima bean , mint . He was in the Al-Mawasi encampment , half a minute from the beach , when an Israeli airstrike hit . He was bleeding from his head , his friend Talal said . A few hours later Medo was dead . <p> We talked about Medo in the vegetable garden I work at this week . We said his name as the sun trickled over the old stone wall and on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fleeces and wondered what it meant to plant a garden in Gaza . My clever colleague , our head grower , hosts workshops where people learn to feed themselves . On top of the practical stuff , he 's taught me that you plant a garden for tomorrow . Today is a consequence of all the days that came before it . <p> Medo was growing as an act of resistance . He posted videos of his progress on Instagram . He wanted to plant something every day " until the genocide was over , " he said in a clip uploaded on August 10 . In his last video , Medo made a low polytunnel to keep his seedlings safe . His melon plant had just put out yellow flowers . It was working , this alchemic combination of dark brown feed and care . <p> Medo kept posting videos , racking up hundreds of thousands of followers . Before he died he 'd been working on a secret project , showing his notebook where he 'd sketched a grid of blacked-out squares . They looked like raised beds and I wondered if he 'd been able to fund them . Malnutrition is rife in Gaza ; in raised beds he could grow lots more nutrient-rich herbs and beans . <p> After Medo died , his youngest fans mourned him with dedicated vegetable patches in their Animal Crossing video games . Al-Mawasi , where he lived , is one of the most fertile regions on the planet . The sea is rich with minerals and fish . The land has everything you need to run a farm . <p> Except it is impossible to run a farm in Gaza . Al-Mawasi is supposed to be a safe zone but nowhere is safe . Some 70 per cent of land is ruined and almost 60 per cent of the agricultural land has been damaged . Every greenhouse pane is smashed . <p> Medo made a declaration of hope with his guerrilla gardening . It was enough . It will be remembered .
