<p> Jews and gay people should hide their identity in parts of Berlin with large Arab populations , the German capital 's police chief has warned . <p> " There are areas of the city , we need to be perfectly honest here , where I would advise people who wear a kippah or are openly gay to be more careful , " said Barbara Slowik . <p> " There are certain neighbourhoods where the majority of people of Arab origin live , who also have sympathies for terrorist groups , " she said , adding that they were often " openly hostile towards Jews " . <p> She told the Berliner Zeitung newspaper that " violent crimes against Jewish people are few and far between , but every act is one too many " . <p> A fortnight ago , a youth football team from Makkabi Berlin , a Jewish sports club , reported being " hunted down " by youths carrying sticks and knives after a match in an Arab neighbourhood of the city .
