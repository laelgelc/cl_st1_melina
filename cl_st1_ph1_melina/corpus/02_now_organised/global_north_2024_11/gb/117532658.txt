" . <p> The announcement followed media reports about a document outlining accusations against Mr Khan , understood to include unwanted sexual touching and " abuse " . <p> In a statement , Mr Khan said he " had previously called for an investigation in relation to this matter " . <p> " I welcome the opportunity to engage in this process , " he said , adding he would continue " all other functions as prosecutor " while the investigation is ongoing . <p> Any issues relevant to the investigation into allegations against him would be handled by deputy prosecutors not reporting to him , "
