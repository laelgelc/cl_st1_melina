<h> Islamic Jihad releases video of Russian-Israeli hostage <p> Palestinian Islamic Jihad released a proof-of-life video on Wednesday of Russian-Israeli hostage Alexander ( " Sasha " ) Troufanov , who was abducted from his family home during the Hamas terrorist attacks in southern Israel on October 7 , 2023 , and has been held captive in the Gaza Strip for 404 days . <p> In the undated video , the third one of Troufanov that the terrorist organisation has published , he says his age is 28 , although he turned 29 on Monday . <p> In the video , Troufanov says that he and the other remaining captives ( there are 101 , 97 of whom were kidnapped on October 7 and many of whom are believed to have died ) are running out of food and basic hygiene products . <p> He also pleads for the Israeli public to continue to push for a hostage deal and voices his fear of being accidentally killed by the Israel Defence Forces . <p> The two previous PIJ videos of Troufanov were released in May . <p> Trufanov is an engineer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ acquired by Amazon in 2015 . His friends have tried to get the tech giant to issue a statement about his plight . <p> During the Hamas-led October 7 massacre , Trufanov was taken hostage from Kibbutz Nir Oz along with his mother , Yelena Trufanov ; his grandmother Irena Tati ; and his girlfriend , Sapir Cohen . His mother and grandmother were freed by Hamas on November 29 , 2023 , at the request of Russian President Vladimir Putin , while Cohen was released as part of a ceasefire deal that same month . <p> He also appeared in a propaganda video released by Hamas on Oct. 30 , alongside two other women who were released during the November ceasefire . Alexander 's father , Vitaly Trufanov , was murdered in the Hamas terror onslaught on Nir Oz , along with some 40 other residents of the kibbutz.
