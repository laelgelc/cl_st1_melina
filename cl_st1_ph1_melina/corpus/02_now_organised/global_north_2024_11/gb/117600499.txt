<p> A bill that would have allowed the government to strip tax-exempt status from any non-profit it deemed to be supporting " terrorism " was narrowly blocked in the House of Representatives on Tuesday evening . <p> The bill was widely feared as a tool that Donald Trump could use to punish dissent , and critics argued the measure could have disproportionately targeted groups that criticize Israel and support Palestinian causes -- and that it was designed to do just that . <p> The vote failed with 145 votes against it , falling short of the two-thirds required to circumvent regular House rules .
