How exactly does it work ? I defy any Labour politician to give a convincing answer to this simple question . <p> For an illustration of the dysfunction at play here consider the position taken by Anas Sarwar , leader of Scottish Labour , on the question of cuts to winter fuel allowance for the elderly . <p> Last week Sarwar said a Scottish Labour government at Holyrood would reinstate the payment , albeit using devolved powers to taper the level of support given to the wealthiest households . <p> You might conclude from this bold statement of principle by the party leader that Scottish Labour MPs at Westminster refused , en bloc , to back the cut when it was first announced by Rachel Reeves , the chancellor , in July . <p> You would be mistaken .
