PHOTO : UNSPLASH <p> November 27 , 2024 <p> HONG KONG -- More than 1,000 medical workers in Gaza have been killed in Israeli strikes since October last year , local authorities have said , as Tel Aviv is accused of waging a " campaign of extermination " in north Gaza . <p> Experts said now that arrest warrants have been issued against Israeli and Hamas officials , the international community " should do more to stop the killing of civilians " and damage to critical infrastructure . <p> Hussam Abu Safia , director at Kamal Adwan Hospital in Beit Lahia , who was injured in an Israeli attack on Saturday , renewed an appeal for global intervention in a letter published in English on Telegram , Al Jazeera reported . <p> " After the failure of the occupying army to evacuate the north , they have now begun to directly target @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , we have been bombed directly , " Al Jazeera quoted Safia as saying . <p> The attacks included the hospital 's reception , emergency departments , electricity generators , oxygen station and water network . <p> Safia also accused Israeli forces of using a new type of weapon . " Specifically a quadcopter that drops bombs containing tiny fragments that are nearly invisible to the naked eye . These projectiles penetrate the bodies of our workers , causing severe bleeding and damage to internal organs , " he was quoted as saying , adding that Israeli forces were waging a " campaign of extermination " in northern Gaza . <p> Israeli army spokesperson Avichay Adraee posted a new evacuation order for some blocks in the Shejaiya neighborhood in the Gaza Strip on X on Saturday , alleging that " terrorist organizations " were once again firing rockets toward Israel in these areas . <p> Israel 's operation in Gaza and Lebanon has caused widespread damage to medical facilities , aid distribution channels and civilian infrastructure , killing and injuring essential workers in the fields of healthcare and journalism . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ over 300 other medical personnel had been arrested , tortured and executed in prisons , according to a WAFA News Agency report . The ongoing conflict has so far killed more than 44,000 Palestinians and 1,200 Israelis and foreigners . <p> Hospitals damaged <p> Ahmad Al-Farra , head of the pediatric department at Nasser Medical Complex and director of Al-Tahrir Hospital for Children and Maternity , told China Daily that nearly 35 hospitals and medical centers had been damaged by Israeli forces . <p> " They evacuate the hospital ( of staff and medical supply ) , they try to destroy everything , even the generator , even the water supply , even the oxygen supply for these hospitals , " said Al-Farra . <p> He also appealed for support for the health sector and said they would work on launching support on global platforms , which would include health organizations and health activists . <p> " ( This is ) to protect it from the Israeli forces , to be protected from the damage that we expect to continue , " said Al-Farra . <p> Hadi Rahmat Purnama , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Centre for International Law Studies at the Faculty of Law at Universitas Indonesia , in Jakarta , told China Daily that the continuous targeting of medical personnel was " outrageous " as they " should be protected during armed conflict " , citing the Geneva Conventions as the legal basis for protecting medical personnel . <p> He said Israel " has made indiscriminate attacks against Palestinians , which amount to war crimes " , and that the majority of the international community has condemned these Israeli attacks , which are " not proportional " . <p> " The US and European states have not done enough to prevent Israel from holding their attack against the Palestinians . The casualties are beyond necessary to weaken Hamas , " said Purnama . <p> In another development , Lebanon 's Hezbollah movement fired heavy rocket barrages at Israel on Sunday , and the Israeli military said houses had been destroyed or set alight near Tel Aviv after a powerful Israeli airstrike killed at least 29 people in Beirut the day before . <p> On Monday , Israeli Ambassador to Washington Mike Herzog told @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ between Israel and Hezbollah could be reached " within days " . <p> Among the issues that remain is an Israeli demand to reserve the right to act should Hezbollah violate its obligations under the emerging deal . The deal seeks to push Hezbollah and Israeli troops out of southern Lebanon .
