<p> KUALA LUMPUR - Malaysia said on Nov 8 that six of its peacekeepers serving under the United Nations were injured in an explosion in Lebanon . <p> The defence ministry said in a statement shortly after midnight that the blast happened on Nov 7 near the Saida Stadium , but did not give details on what caused the explosion . <p> The UN has said that five Malaysian peacekeepers were among those wounded in an Israeli air strike in South Lebanon on Nov 7 , in a raid that also killed three civilians . <p> Israel launched a barrage of strikes after Lebanon 's Hezbollah said it carried out a missile attack targeting a military base near Israel 's Ben Gurion International Airport on Nov 6 . <p> Malaysia 's defence ministry said the explosion had " targeted another civilian vehicle headed towards Beirut but also caused damage to a bus carrying " the Malaysian UN peacekeepers , injuring six of them . <p> It described the injuries as " minor " , including one peacekeeper who suffered a fracture in the left arm and was taken to hospital @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ monitor this situation closely and will provide updates regarding the incident as they become available , " the statement said . <p> The armed forces " remains committed to ensuring the safety and well-being of its personnel while carrying out peacekeeping missions under the United Nations banner , " it added . <p> Hezbollah and Israel have been at war since late September , when Israel broadened its focus from fighting Hamas in the Gaza Strip to securing its northern border .
