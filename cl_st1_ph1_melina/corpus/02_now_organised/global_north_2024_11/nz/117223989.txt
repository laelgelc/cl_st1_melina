The brutal bombardment by Israel has displaced an entire nation of Palestinians from their homes and destroyed almost all infrastructure and essential services . UNWRA continues to be a critical lifeline for Palestinians trapped in this humanitarian catastrophe . <p> For more than seven decades , UNRWA has played a crucial stabilising role in the region , delivering essential services in healthcare , education , and relief to millions of Palestinian people in their occupied homeland and registered Palestinian refugees in neighbouring countries .
