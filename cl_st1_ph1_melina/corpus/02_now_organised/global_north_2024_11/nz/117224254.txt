<h> The Mighty Aotearoa Alternative Awards 2024 Winners Announced <p> After a triple run of chaotic annual award show evenings across the motu , the Student Radio Network Awards returned as The Mighty Aotearoa Alternative Awards on Saturday -- dedicated to showcasing the best musicians , broadcasters , and supporters of alternative music in typically insane student radio style . Otautahi 's own RDU 98.5FM took on the hosting reigns this year , seeing Liam Donnelly and Bex Pemberton present all national awards and sharing the love across Aotearoa , with every station simulcasting from their respective studios and venues . We squeezed into the 95bFM watch party at BIG FAN in Morningside , enjoying the MAAA-exclusive Hazy beers from Three Boys Brewery and taking in sets from multiple award nominee Geneva AM and our very own Power Nap . <p> The national awards this year reflected the current state of Aotearoa , with a variety of the nominees and winners championing indigenous rights from here to the other side of the world . Te Tohu Puoro o te Reo Maori - Favourite Song featuring Te Reo Maori was won @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Marlon Williams , Caru and Dera Meelan took home the award for Te Totahi Toa - Favourite Single for ' See No Evil ' feat . Church and deadforest , and Christoph el Truento 's Dubs from the Neighbourhood won Te Tohu Kopae Puoro Rorotu - Favourite Album . <p> Tamaki favourites Mokotron and SKILAA won awards for Te Tohu Kaipuoro Toa - Favourite Solo Act and Te Ropu Rorotu - Favourite Group respectively , with the former having his mahi also recognised for Te Tohu Hopunga Puoro Rorotu - Favourite EP/Mixtape as part of Tautoko Gaza . The compilation was produced by Aotearoa Artists in Solidarity with Palestine late last year and released exclusively on Bandcamp , with all proceeds going to Medical Aid for Palestinians . With contributors like Babe Martin , Citacsy , Molly McLennan , and the aforementioned Mokotron all in the room , their win received the most rapturous applause from the Tamaki camp . <p> Speaking of camp , Ian Jorgensen ( aka Blink ) of Camp A Low Hum took out the prize for He Tohu Kahurangi - Outstanding Achievement Award for his @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of service to the music community in Poneke and around Aotearoa . The winners of Te Tohu na te Marea i Kowhiri - People 's Choice Award was won by Otepoti upstarts IVY , and longtime Poneke hometown heroes Mermaidens had their song ' I like to be alone ' top the 2024 SRN Top 10 , after the track was nominated for the APRA Silver Scroll earlier this year . <p> Each SRN station -- 95bFM , Radio Control 99.4FM , RadioActive.FM , RDU 98.5 FM and Radio One 91FM -- had their own round of regional awards , check out the full list of winners HERE . Up in Auckland , we had the privilege of seeing Aotearoa 's leading skate brand-affiliated radio show Arcade Radio take out the award for He Tohu Kahurangi i te Pahotanga ki a 95bFM - 95bFM 's Outstanding Achievement in Broadcasting after ten years on air . Rob Bollox , aka The Bard of Bollox won Te Tohu Tiketike o te Hapai i a 95bFM - Outstanding Contribution to 95bFM in recognition of his time as the presenter of The Mind Trench @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and much more , including being one of the friendliest faces you can come across in the station . Lastly , Thee Golden Geese rightfully won the award for Te Waiata Rorotu 2024 ki a 95bFM - 95bFM 's Favourite Song of 2024 with their anthem dedicated to the station , ' 95bFM Top Ten ' after one of the most infamous rises to the top of the charts that student radio has ever seen .
