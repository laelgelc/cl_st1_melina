THE CANADIAN PRESS/Darryl Dyck <p> DELTA , B.C. -- Police in British Columbia say truck traffic to a major Metro Vancouver container port terminal has been restored after being disrupted by pro-Palestinian protesters for hours . <p> Delta Police say on social media platform X , formerly known as Twitter , that inbound and outbound traffic at GCT Deltaport is running again , although truck volumes may remain heavy until a backlog is cleared . <p> A statement from terminal operator GCT says the protest disrupted truck traffic for about three hours on Friday , but " terminal operations continued without interruption .
