" <p> The city also raised concerns about the suspension of sewage treatment facilities , warning that untreated wastewater could flood the streets , exacerbating the risk of environmental and health disasters , and facilitating the spread of diseases and epidemics . <p> Calling for immediate action , the city government urged the international community and human rights groups to " urgently intervene to end the Israeli war on Gaza , which has destroyed all aspects of life .
