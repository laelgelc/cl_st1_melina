<h> Talks with rabbinic council to resume <p> A year after the suspension of discussion between the Canadian Conference of Catholic Bishops and the Canadian Rabbinic Caucus , the two parties are expected to resume official communication . <p> With the potential to mark a significant restoration in ecumenical relations between the Church and the Canadian Jewish community , both parties will look to regain the effective bond shared prior to last October 's dialogue breakdown when they meet in Ottawa next month . <p> It was the CCCB 's lacklustre response in regards to the events of Oct. 7 , when Hamas fighters broached the Israeli border , slaughtered upwards of 1,200 Israelis and took more than 200 captives back to Gaza , that led to the suspension of talks between parties . <p> " The statement was generally seen by the Jewish community as being very weak and not addressive of the issue at hand which was that Jews were brutally slaughtered , in some cases raped and taken hostage , " said Fr .
