<h> Protest blocks access to Deltaport for three hours Friday <p> Traffic to a major Metro Vancouver container port terminal was restored after being disrupted by pro-Palestinian protesters for hours . Jack Morse has the story . <p> By Jack Morse <p> Posted November 30 , 2024 1:52 pm . <p> Last Updated November 30 , 2024 2:11 pm . <p> Traffic to a major Metro Vancouver container port terminal began flowing nearly three hours after it was disrupted by pro-Palestinian protesters starting around 8 a.m . <p> Part of the protest on the road into Deltaport was aimed at Dallas Leung , a UBC board member and vice-president of Global Container Terminals , which operates the port . <p> " The board of governors continues to invest UBC 's endowment in companies complicit in Israeli apartheid and genocide , and these include weapons companies like Raytheon , Lockheed-Martin that make bombs that drop in Gaza , and shipping and logistic companies , like Zim , that pass through Deltaport , and facilitate these weapon shipments , " said Sam , one of the protesters . <p> UBC 's president @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ own the weapon companies , but 10 per cent of its endowment -- about $2.8 billion -- is invested in a managed fund that includes ownership in those stocks . <p> The protester 's chants also included calls for the destruction of the state of Israel . <p> Police were on the scene . According to an afternoon release , officers told protesters to clear the road and continue their protest without blocking traffic . <p> Outbound traffic was restored by 10:20 a.m. and inbound traffic started getting into the port again around 11 a.m . <p> There were no arrests at the protest and police say none are expected . An investigation is ongoing . <p> Global Container Terminals says terminal operations at the port continued without interruption during the protest , despite demonstrators blocking truck access . It says there was no delay to shipping operations at the port .
