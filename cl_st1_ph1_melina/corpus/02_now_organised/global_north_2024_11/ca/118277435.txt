<p> At 8am on Thursday Nov 28 , roughly 30 students packed up their signs , blankets , snacks and water and walked out of the Henry Hicks Academic Administration Building on Dalhousie University 's Studley Campus . They had been there for over 40 hours , since a board of governors meeting on Tuesday afternoon , and had decided to leave " on their own terms , " the group posted in a story on Instagram . " We have not given up . " We remain unwavering and determined in our commitment and demands for @dalhousieu to fully divest from the state of Israel and for a free Palestine from the river to the sea , " posted the group . <p> The students who occupied the building were members of the Students for the Liberation of Palestine - Kjipuktuk , or SLPK coalition , including Dal students .
