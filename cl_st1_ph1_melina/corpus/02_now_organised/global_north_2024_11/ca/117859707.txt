<h> FedUp Concordia calls for food sovereignty <h> Students will be able to vote for sustainable food measures at the upcoming CSU by-elections <p> FedUp Concordia is a group advocating for food sovereignty on campus . Photo Soraya Yale-Ramanathan . <p> Students at Concordia University will be able to vote on FedUp Concordia 's food sovereignty proposal in the upcoming Concordia Student Union ( CSU ) by-elections . <p> FedUp Concordia is a new group on campus formed by students frustrated with the quality and ethics of the food served at the university . Its goal is to move away from corporate food ownership at Concordia and switch to student-led initiatives . <p> The advocacy group had presented its referendum question on food sovereignty at a CSU council meeting in September . If the referendum question passes at the by-election , FedUp 's measures will be included in the CSU 's official Positions Book . <p> FedUp calls for three goals to be added . <p> The first goal calls for Concordia to create new regulations in accordance with the National Student Food Charter , which puts emphasis on values @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ second asks that Concordia supports and fosters more student-led food initiatives , like the Hive Caf ?
