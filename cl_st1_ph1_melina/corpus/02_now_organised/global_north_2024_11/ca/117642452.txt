Israel launched a stepped-up air and ground campaign in late September after cross-border clashes in parallel with the Gaza war . <p> U.S. ambassador Lisa Johnson met with Berri , a Hezbollah ally and the typical conduit for diplomacy with the group , on Thursday to submit Washington 's first written proposal in at least several weeks , two senior Lebanese political sources said . <p> " It is a draft to get observations from the Lebanese side , " one of the sources told Reuters .
