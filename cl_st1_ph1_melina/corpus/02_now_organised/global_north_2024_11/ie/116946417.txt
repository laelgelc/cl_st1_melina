<h> Hamas official says group rejected Gaza truce proposal <h> A Hamas official confirms the group 's rejection of a proposed short-term truce in Gaza from Egypt and Qatar , citing the lack of a lasting ceasefire and unmet conditions like the Israeli army 's withdrawal and the return of displaced persons . This follows recent meetings discussing a potential truce involving hostage exchange and increased aid . <p> IANS <p> A Hamas official said Friday the group received a proposal from mediators Egypt and Qatar for a short-term truce in Gaza and rejected it for not including a lasting ceasefire . <p> " The proposals do not include a permanent cessation of aggression , nor do they entail the withdrawal of the Israeli army from the Gaza Strip or the return of displaced persons , " the official , a member of Hamas 's political bureau , told on condition of anonymity as he was not authorised to speak publicly on the issue . <p> A senior Hamas leader , Taher al-Nunu , had already warned on Thursday that the group would reject any proposal for a temporary halt @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ group had not received any formal proposal at the time . <p> Meetings between Mossad head David Barnea , CIA Director Bill Burns and Qatar 's prime minister in Doha , which concluded on Monday , discussed proposing a " short-term " truce of " less than a month " , a source with knowledge of the talks told AFP on Wednesday .
