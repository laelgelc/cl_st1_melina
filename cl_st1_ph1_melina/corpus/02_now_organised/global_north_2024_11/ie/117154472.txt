<h> Palestine Festival of Literature issues boycott letter to book trade as lawyers for Israel claim it is discriminatory <p> An open letter organised by the Palestine Festival of Literature ( PalFest ) and circulated by Fossil Free Books has called on the publishing industry to boycott Israeli institutions that " have remained silent observers " to the ongoing crisis in Gaza . <p> The boycott letter -- which is still open -- has been signed by 445 writers , publishers , literary festival workers and other book workers . It asks signatories to pledge not to " cooperate with Israeli institutions including publishers , festivals , literary agencies and publications " . <p> In response to the letter , UK Lawyers for Israel ( UKLFI ) , an association of lawyers supporting Israel , has sent a separate document to trade bodies and publishers , alleging that the " boycott is plainly discriminatory against Israelis " . <p> Referencing the UK Equality Act 2021 and other anti-discrimination laws , UKLFI called on the Society of Authors ( SoA ) , the Publishers Association ( PA ) and the Independent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " the legal risks in participating in , assisting or supporting the discriminatory boycott proposed by the authors of the letter " . <p> The PalFest letter , seen by The Bookseller , references the " emergency " situation faced by Palestinians in Gaza and the " years of displacement " experienced by Palestinians . It claims that " culture has played an integral role in normalising these injustices " . <p> In response to this , it has outlined that those who have signed the letter " will not co-operate with Israeli institutions " that are " complicit in violating Palestinian rights " or that " have never publicly recognised the inalienable rights of the Palestinian people as enshrined in international law " . <p> In UKLFI 's letter to the industry , Jonathan Turner , the chief executive of UKLFI , has accused the signatories of the PalFest letter of discrimination , saying that they " do not impose similar conditions on publishers , festivals , literary agencies or publications of any other nationality " . <p> The Bookseller has contacted PalFest and Fossil Free Books for comment @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @
