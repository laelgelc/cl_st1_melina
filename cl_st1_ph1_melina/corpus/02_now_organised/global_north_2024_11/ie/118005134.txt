<h> UN Security Council not fit for purpose <h> A cycle of inhumanity and injustice <p> Letter of the Day <p> Sat Nov 23 2024 - 05:00 <p> A chara , -- On November 18th , Russia , as a permanent member of the United Nations Security Council ( UNSC ) , vetoed a resolution calling for an immediate ceasefire in Sudan where a civil war has killed tens of thousands of civilians since last April ; 14 of the 15 members of the UNSC had voted in favour of the motion . <p> Two days later the United States , as a permanent member of the UNSC , vetoed a resolution calling for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza where tens of thousands of civilians have been killed since October of last year ; 14 of the 15 members of the UNSC had voted in favour of the motion . <p> The UN was founded in 1945 as , in its own words , " an international organization designed to end war and promote peace , justice , and better living for all humankind " . <p> Unfortunately the organisation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Russia , China , UK , France ) each having a permanent veto -- seems deliberately structured to ensure that this noble design is rarely honoured . <p> Given that powerful states aim to achieve their ends by suspending legality in the interests of " national security " or " reason of state " , the world needs a genuinely transgovernmental international organisation that overrules such Machiavellian stratagems . <p> Given that any attempt to transform the UN into such an organisation would itself be vetoed by the permanent members , it would appear that humanity has allowed itself to be trapped in a cycle of inhumanity and injustice .
