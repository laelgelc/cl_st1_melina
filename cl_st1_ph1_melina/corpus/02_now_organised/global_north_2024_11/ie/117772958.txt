<h> Tommy Tiernan to launch John Tiernan 's book of short stories <p> Athlone native John Tiernan will launch his first book titled ' Tell Tale Townie ' in the Shamrock Lodge Hotel , Athlone , on November 19 at 8pm . <p> ' Tell Tale Townie ' features 22 short stories which hang delicately between anecdotal nostalgia and brilliantly witty works of fiction . <p> John is aware that the tales are ' of a different time ' , however he hopes that they will still serve to conjure up long forgotten memories for locals . <p> " They were written in a different time -- politics was different , schooling methods were different , the things that were accepted were different . <p> " Many of the stories are humorous -- but there are some that are more serious . " <p> John is a retired public servant , and former Roscommon County Manager . He lives with his wife , Mary , at the Hill of Berries . " <p> Breaking out of the rhythm of political life meant John had to find a new rhythm , which saw him leaning into the joy of comedy . <p> " My intention was always to build in an element of humour , " John said .
