<p> On Monday , Israel formally notified the United Nations of its intention to sever all ties with the UN relief agency for Palestinian refugees ( Unrwa ) , the organisation that coordinates much of the delivery of aid within Gaza . <p> It follows the Israeli parliament 's vote last week to ban Unrwa from operating in Israel and Palestinian territories and prohibiting all Israeli state contact with the agency . <p> The new laws followed allegations that members of Unwra participated in the 7 October Hamas attacks . <p> The ban is not set to take effect for another three months but Israel has already contacted the UN secretary general officially withdrawing itself from a 1967 co-operation agreement with Unrwa . <p> John Whyte , an Irish aid worker who us Unrwa 's head of projects and currently based in Gaza , joins In The News to discuss the consequences of this ban for Palestinians and whether the agency can continue to operate without Israeli co-operation .
