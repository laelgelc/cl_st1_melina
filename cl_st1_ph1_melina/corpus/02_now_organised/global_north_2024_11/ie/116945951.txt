<h> Deputy Carthy calls for safety of Irish peacekeepers <p> Camp Shamrock is the primary base of Irish peacekeepers deployed to South Lebanon . <p> Following the attack , understood to have been a Hezbollah rocket intended to strike Israel , Matt Carthy TD stated " the only mechanism " to secure the " safety and wellbeing of peacekeepers and civilians " in the region is through the " urgent delivery of a ceasefire and the full implementation of international law . " <p> Deputy Carthy said the safety of the men and women of the Irish Defence Forces deployed as part of the United Nations missions to south Lebanon " should be the chief priority of government . " <p> " Israel has now expanded its genocidal campaign from Gaza to Lebanon and the international community , including the Irish government , must reconsider their failed soft touch , largely rhetorical , rebukes of a rogue Israeli regime . <p> " Israel acts with impunity because it has never been held to account , " he said . "
