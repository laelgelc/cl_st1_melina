It was driven by lies , misinformation , fake news and polls , hate speech and vilification , and many attempts at voter suppression . The campaign has sliced and diced American society and foreshadowed the most dangerous and uncertain global environment since World War II . <p> The division has been by gender and age , within political parties , between neighbours , within families . <p> The contest was between two personalities , which highlighted some fundamental issues across America . Trump , a convicted felon and perceived by many as the racist , misogynist male against Harris as a Black woman , the daughter of immigrants .
