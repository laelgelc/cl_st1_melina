<h> For Steve McQueen , whose film Blitz is released this month , history is not about retelling , but revealing powerful stories that have never been told . By So Mayer . <h> Truth matters to Blitz director Steve McQueen <p> " When I was doing the research for Small Axe , I came across this photograph of a Black child on a railway station with an oversized coat , a large suitcase and a cap , standing waiting to be taken away by the train . " Steve McQueen cups his hand before him , as if still holding the photograph that led to his epic new film Blitz . Set in 1940 during the intense Luftwaffe bombing of London , it 's a large-scale drama with an intimate focus on George ( Elliott Heffernan ) , a pre-teen Black boy from the East End whose mother , Rita ( Saoirse Ronan ) , decides reluctantly to evacuate him to the countryside , setting him on an epic journey back to her . <p> As with McQueen 's first feature film Hunger ( 2008 ) , which won @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Un Certain Regard prizes , and his Academy Award-winning 12 Years a Slave ( 2013 ) , Blitz offers a dazzling , fresh perspective on mainstream histories : " Not a retelling , " he corrects me , " telling : telling what 's been unseen . " From political prisoners in Northern Ireland and a free Black man taken as a slave , to the wardens , munitionettes , dancers and firemen of wartime London , McQueen 's unerring eye and ear have placed tales of fierce survivors amid devastation on the big screen . <p> He recently returned to the gallery work that made his name , winning the Turner Prize in 1999 -- he is the only artist to have won a Turner Prize and an Academy Award -- with Grenfell ( 2023 ) . Grenfell 's wordless excoriation of government , council and developer neglect is echoed in the film 's thread about tube stations being locked to prevent their use or overcrowding as shelters . <p> Blitz is " made about a time in 1940 , but it 's very much about 2024 -- I do n't even have to tell you that " , McQueen says , gesturing around us to evoke the bombs whose whistling menace , falling in close-up , punctuates the film , inescapably paralleling the news from Gaza , Lebanon and Ukraine .
