We pursue that aim in a variety of ways : through our research @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ policymakers , business people , academia , media and the broader community . <p> We recognise that a lively and sophisticated discussion of the key global issues affecting Australia 's future requires a broad range of contributors . One of the most important of these is the Australian media . <p> At a time when the media industry is going through a period of great change and uncertainty , we believe it is vital that Australian journalists continue to report and comment on events in the world that impact on Australia . It is for this reason that we have decided to establish this media award . <p> The Lowy Institute Media Award will be given to an Australian journalist or journalistic team who either through a single piece of reporting or commentary , or through a series of reports , has deepened the knowledge , or shaped the discussion , of international policy issues in Australia in the course of the last year . <p> A panel of eminent judges will nominate candidates whose work over the last twelve months they feel best meets the award criteria , specifically @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ issues to Australia 's engagement with the world . <p> Influence -- the degree to which the journalist or journalistic team 's work informed or shaped public discussion of international policy . <p> Originality -- the extent to which the work brought attention to a new international issue or idea , or re-examined an existing issue from a new perspective . <p> Quality -- a high standard of writing or broadcast . <h> Past Media Awards <p> 2023 Lowy Institute Media Award <p> The winners of the 2023 Lowy Institute Media Award were Kate Geraghty and Anthony Galloway for their work reporting on the conflict in Ukraine across two series of articles for The Sydney Morning Herald and The Age . <p> The other finalists were : <p> Stephen Rice of The Australian for a series of reports about Cambodian political influence in Australia ;
