<p> Australia has voted to recognise Palestinians ' " permanent sovereignty " over their natural resources in a significant foreign policy shift that breaks with the US and Israel . <p> Votes in favour of two United Nations draft resolutions on Wednesday ( Thursday AEDT ) are the latest in a series of more pro-Palestinian votes Australia has taken at the global body , angering local pro-Israel groups but draw praise from Palestinian advocates . <p> The draft resolution in the UN 's Second Committee recognised the " permanent sovereignty of the Palestinian people in the Occupied Palestinian Territory , including East Jerusalem , and of the Arab population in the occupied Syrian Golan over their natural resources " . <p> Votes on both resolutions will now go to the UN 's General Assembly .
