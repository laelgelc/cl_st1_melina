<p> JERUSALEM ( Nov 16 ) : An Israeli army official said today footage relating to hostages seized by Hamas was found on computers at the Al-Shifa hospital during an ongoing special forces raid Hamas said severely damaged the medical facility . <p> According to Israeli authorities , around 240 people were taken hostage after the Hamas attacks on Israel on October 7 , which killed an an estimated 1,200 people , mostly civilians . <p> Israel 's bombardment and Gaza ground offensive have killed more than 11,500 people , mostly civilians and including thousands of children , according to Hamas government officials . <p> The Al-Shifa hospital -- the largest in Gaza -- has become a focus of the operation by the Israeli military , which says it is used as a base by Hamas , a charge the Islamist movement denies . <p> " Soldiers are proceeding one building at a time , searching each floor , all while hundreds of patients and medical staff remain in the complex , " the Israeli army official said . <p> Intelligence materials including equipment " belonging to Hamas " had been found , they said . <p> " During @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ abducted from Israel were found on computers and other technological equipment . " <p> The official said the items belonging to Hamas had been taken for " further examination and investigation " . <p> An AFP journalist in the hospital said hundreds of soldiers were taking part in the operation , encircling buildings and using armoured bulldozers to push through walls . <p> The Hamas health ministry said the raid had destroyed medical services in the hospital , where the UN estimated 2,300 patients , staff and displaced Palestinians were sheltering before Israeli troops moved in on Wednesday .
