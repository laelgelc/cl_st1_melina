<h> Malaysia sends second batch of humanitarian aid to Palestine <p> The shipments were part of 50 tonnes of goods totalling more than RM7 million collected through Ops Ihsan. -- Bernama photo <p> SEPANG ( Nov 11 ) : Malaysia 's second shipment of humanitarian aid for Palestinians through Ops Ihsan departed for El-Arish International Airport , Egypt via a special chartered cargo flight last night . <p> The cargo plane , carrying 20 tonnes of medical supplies and baby goods , took off at about 11 pm from the Cargo Terminal of Kuala Lumpur International Airport ( KLIA ) here . <p> The first shipment , involving goods weighing 20 tonnes , was made on Nov 3 . <p> The shipments were part of 50 tonnes of goods totalling more than RM7 million collected through Ops Ihsan . <p> The remaining 10 tonnes of goods are expected to be delivered soon . <p> Once the aid arrives in Egypt , it will be handed over to the Egyptian Red Crescent Society , acting as a partner to bring them into Gaza through the Rafah border . <p> Deputy Prime Minister Datuk Seri Fadillah Yusof who witnessed the sending-off @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ non-governmental organisations ( NGOs ) , airlines and logistics personnel who made the humanitarian aid mission a success . <p> Meanwhile , he said Malaysia is ready to send more rubber gloves to the medical teams in the Gaza Strip . <p> " We ( Malaysia ) were informed that they are in urgent need of rubber gloves and we are still waiting for confirmation of the actual amount they need , " he told reporters .
