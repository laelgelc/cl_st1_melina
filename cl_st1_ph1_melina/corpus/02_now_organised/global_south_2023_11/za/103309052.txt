Picture : @DIRCOZA/Twitter <p> CAPE TOWN - Minister of International Relations and Cooperation Naledi Pandor said the killing of thousands of Palestinian civilians by Israel is intolerable . <p> She told Parliament that South Africa can not watch another genocide unfold in the absence of action by the United Nations ( UN ) to bring an end to the month-long conflict . <p> Delivering a statement on the Israel-Gaza war for debate in the house on Tuesday , Pandor outlined a seven-point plan of action to ease the tensions , including an immediate ceasefire and access to humanitarian aid to Gaza .
