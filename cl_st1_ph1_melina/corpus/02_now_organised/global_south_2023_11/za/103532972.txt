<p> CAPE TOWN - Political parties are divided over whether or not South Africa should sever diplomatic ties with Israel over the killing of thousands of Palestinians in Gaza . <p> Economic Freedom Fighters ( EFF ) leader Julius Malema tabled the motion in a mini-plenary of the National Assembly . <p> He said that the call to boot the Israeli ambassador to South Africa from the country was not against Jewish people but rather against the actions of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and the violation of Palestinians ' human rights . <p> The Freedom Front Plus ' Corne Mulder : " South Africa will not be in a position to play any role whatsoever in terms of any mediation or any chance to try and play a constructive and a positive role to bring this conflict to an end . " <p> The EFF 's motion will be voted on by a full sitting of the House next week .
