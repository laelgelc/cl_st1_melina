<p> Cultural organisation Sammilita Sangskritik Jote ( SSJ ) held a protest seminar against the barbaric attacks of Israeli forces on innocent Palestinians in Gaza on Saturday afternoon in the premises of the central Shaheed Minar of the capital . <p> Presided over by Sammilita Sangskritik Jote President Ghulam Quddus , speakers at the event stated that influential countries that assist and collaborate with Israel are equally accountable for the humanitarian catastrophe brought about by the heinous attacks on civilians in Gaza . <p> The cultural elites also condemned political parties , especially Bangladesh 's Islamic political alliances , for failing to condemn the killings of innocent Palestinians . <p> Mentioning Bangladesh 's state towards this conflict and the people 's support of the Palestinians seeking liberation is a remarkable gesture of humanity . The speakers expressed their gratitude to the government , thanking Premier Sheikh Hasina for taking the necessary steps . <p> Sammilita Sangskritik Jote President Gholam Quddus announced that on Tuesday evening , the SSJ will host a lamp-lighting event at the central Shaheed Minar in honour of those who died in Palestine as a result of Israeli attacks and recent political unrest in Bangladesh .
