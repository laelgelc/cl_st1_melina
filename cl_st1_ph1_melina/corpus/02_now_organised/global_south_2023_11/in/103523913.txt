<h> US-Based Reporters Condemn ' Israel 's Killing of Journalists in Gaza ' <p> The statement of condemnation signed by 1,265 media personnel has appealed to newsrooms in the West to refrain from ' dehumanising rhetoric that has served to justify the ethnic cleansing of Palestinians ' . <p> New Delhi : Journalists from the United States have issued a statement of condemnation against " Israel 's killing of journalists in Gaza " and appealed to newsrooms in the West to uphold " integrity " in covering Israel 's atrocities against Palestinians . <p> According to the Committee to Protect Journalists ( CPJ ) , 36 journalists are among 11,000 Palestinians killed in Israel 's ongoing war in Gaza . Besides deaths , several journalists have been injured , detained , gone missing , or have seen their family members killed . The CPJ describes it as the " deadliest conflict for journalists " since 1992 when it first began tracking such deaths . <p> The statement of condemnation , which was first brought out on November 6 , was signed by 1,265 reporters , editors , photographers , producers , and other workers in newsrooms around the world @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to urge an end to violence against journalists in Gaza and to call on Western newsroom leaders to be clear-eyed in coverage of Israel 's repeated atrocities against Palestinians , " the statement said . <p> It also condemned the biased coverage of the ongoing war and longstanding conflict between Israel and Palestine in the Western media .
