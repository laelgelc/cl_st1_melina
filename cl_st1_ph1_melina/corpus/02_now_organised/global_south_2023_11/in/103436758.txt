<h> ' Hamas Has Lost Control Of Gaza ' , Israel Defence Chief Gallant Says <p> Making the rounds on social media is a photo of IDF soldiers posing in the building of Hamas ' parliament . In the picture , the team of soldiers can be seen proudly waving Israeli flags in the terrorist organisation 's legislative council building . This is following Israeli Defence Minister Yoav Gallant 's statement that Hamas had " lost control of Gaza . " <p> " There is no power of Hamas capable of stopping the IDF . The IDF is advancing to every point . The Hamas organization has lost control of Gaza , terrorists are fleeing south , civilians are looting Hamas bases , they have no confidence in the government , " Gallant had continued . <p> The Palestinian Legislative Council building in Gaza has only served Hamas 's lawmakers since the terror group 's takeover of the Gaza Strip in 2007.
