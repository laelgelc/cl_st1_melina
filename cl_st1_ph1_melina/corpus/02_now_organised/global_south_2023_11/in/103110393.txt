<p> Israel Gaza Conflict : At least 50 Gazans were killed and several others injured in an Israeli airstrike on the densely-populated Jabalia refugee camp in northern Gaza , which Israel said killed several Hamas operatives , including a senior leader involved in the planning and execution of the October 7 attacks . Palestinian health officials said that at least 50 Palestinians were killed and 150 wounded in the airstrikes which left large craters surrounded by wrecked concrete buildings . More than 8,525 Palestinians , including 3,500 children , have been killed in Israeli airstrikes since October 7 . The Israeli offensive is in retaliation to the deadly Hamas attack that killed at least 1,400 Israelis , mostly civilians , and resulted in the abduction of over 200 civilians and soldiers by the Palestinian militant group .
