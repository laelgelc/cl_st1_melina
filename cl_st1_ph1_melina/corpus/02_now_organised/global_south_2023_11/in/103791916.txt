<p> New Delhi : Foreign Secretary Vinay Kwatra on Sunday discussed with Iranian Foreign Minister Hossein Amir-Abdollahian in Tehran ways to boost connectivity through strategic Chabahar port and the current situation in West Asia arising out of the Hamas-Israel conflict . <p> The foreign secretary was in Tehran to co-chair a meeting of India-Iran Foreign Office Consultations ( FOC ) along with Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister for political affairs Ali Bagheri Kani . <p> On Kwatra 's meeting with Amir-Abdollahian , External Affairs Ministry spokesperson Arindam Bagchi said both sides agreed to further strengthen the ongoing cooperation in various spheres . <p> " Foreign Secretary Vinay Kwatra called on FM @Amirabdolahian of Iran in Tehran today , " Bagchi said on X. <p> " Discussed bilateral matters , connectivity projects including Chabahar port , and shared perspective on current challenges in the region . Both sides agreed to further strengthen the ongoing cooperation in various spheres , " he said . <p> Kwatra 's visit to Tehran assumes significance as it came amid growing global concerns over the hostilities between Hamas and Israel . The two sides agreed on a four-day ceasefire from Friday . <p> On the FOC @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the two sides reviewed the entire gamut of bilateral relationship , including connectivity projects such as Chabahar port , political engagements , trade and economic relations , capacity building initiatives and people-to-people ties . <p> " They also exchanged views on current regional and global issues , including developments in Afghanistan and Gaza , " the MEA said in a statement . <p> " The Indian side appreciated Iran 's participation in the Voice of Global South Summits held this year , " it said .
