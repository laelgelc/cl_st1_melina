<h> Israel-Hamas Truce : Hamas Delays Second Round Of Hostage Release <p> On the second day of ongoing Israel-Hamas truce the armed wing of Hamas said that it had decided to delay the second round of hostage releases until Israel is committed to letting aid trucks enter northern Gaza . Hamas ' al-Qassam Brigades added that the hostage releases would be delayed if Israel does not adhere to the agreed terms for the release of Palestinian prisoners . <p> Till now there has been no immediate Israeli response to the statement issued by Hamas ' armed wing . Except for last-minute changes , 13 Israeli hostages were expected to be freed , an Israeli military spokesperson had told France 's BFM television station earlier in the day . In return for the release of 13 Israeli hostages , 39 Palestinian prisoners would be released . Reported news agency Reuters . <p> In the first exchange on Friday , 13 Israeli women and children - out of around 240 hostages captured by Hamas fighters on a killing spree in southern Israel on Oct. 7 - were released .
