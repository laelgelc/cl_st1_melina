<h> For news , views , interviews . <p> The United States , Israel , and Hamas are close to an agreement on a five-day pause in fighting in the Gaza Strip to facilitate the release of hostages , The Washington Post reported on Sunday , citing sources . <p> The conflict between Israel and Hamas started on October 7 . Image Credit : IBNS <p> The six-page agreement provides for a cessation of all combat operations by the parties to the conflict for at least five days and the release of 50 or more hostages , the report said , adding that the release of the hostages could begin in the coming days . <p> The pause will be monitored from the air , the newspaper reported . <p> The agreement outline was drawn up during weeks of negotiations in Doha between Israel , the US , and Hamas , indirectly represented by Qatari mediators , the report read , citing Arab and other diplomats . <p> It is still unclear , however , whether Israel will agree to suspend the offensive in the Gaza Strip temporarily , provided the conditions are right , the newspaper said .
