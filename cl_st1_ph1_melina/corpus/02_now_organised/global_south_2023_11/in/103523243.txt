<p> New Delhi/Tel Aviv : Israeli President Isaac Herzog on Thursday accused Iran of trying to derail the India-Middle East-Europe corridor . Herzog said that there is an " Iranian evil effort " to undermine the entire regional situation . <p> " About a month and a half or two months ago , at the G-20 summit , where Prime Minister Modi was there , US President Joe Biden announced a special corridor of energy and communications and business and science from Israel all the way down to India , through Saudi Arabia and the Gulf and Jordan , " the Israeli President told news agency ANI in an exclusive interview . <p> " An amazing vision that connects all of us together , that will connect Europe to Southeast Asia and even America all the way to Australia . That 's a grand vision that gives India a huge strength on the way . But somebody decided that they want to derail this effort of inclusion of Israel and this huge peace vision of President Biden , " Herzog added . <p> Israeli President 's statement : <p> The Israeli President said that Iran was working to undermine @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ region . " This empire of evil , of Iran , is working extra hours to undermine any inclusion of Israel in the region and any peace process . And we all , all nations in the world must fight Iran for pursuing this evil approach , " he stated . <p> Earlier , the Israeli President blamed Iran for the October 7 Hamas attack . After the attack , Herzog in a statement had said that Hamas militants were supported and directed by their proxy commanders in Iran to carry out out an unprovoked , heinous attack against the Jewish state . <p> Notably , after the Hamas attack , Israel launched an offensive against the Hamas in Gaza .
