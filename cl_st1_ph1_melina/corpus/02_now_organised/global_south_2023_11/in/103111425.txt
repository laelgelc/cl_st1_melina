<p> The Israel-Hamas conflict has extended into its 26th day , with Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu strongly rejecting a ceasefire with Palestine . The toll from the war has now exceeded 10,000 lives lost , including 8,796 in Gaza and 1,400 in Israel . In a concerning development , Iran-backed Houthi forces in Yemen have initiated hostilities against Israel by launching multiple missiles and drones towards Israeli territory . Houthis claimed responsibility for these attacks , with their military spokesperson stating that they would continue until Israeli aggression ceases . This escalation has raised concerns , as the Houthis are a part of the " Axis of Resistance " that opposes Israel and the United States . Previously , the Houthi group had demonstrated its missile and drone capabilities during the Yemen war in attacks on Saudi Arabia and the UAE.
