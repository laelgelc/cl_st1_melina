That requirement has now been eliminated as the current war with Hamas has claimed over 1,400 lives in the country . <p> The Israel Health Ministry has allowed parents to circumvent usual legal procedures to retrieve the sperms of their sons - civilian or military - who were killed during the ongoing war against Hamas . The ministry has instructed the families to retrieve the sperm before burial . <p> According to the Times of Israel , sperm has been received from 33 men during the course of the war since last month . Four of these fallen people were civilians while the rest were soldiers .
