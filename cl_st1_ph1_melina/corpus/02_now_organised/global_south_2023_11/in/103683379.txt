The first hostages to be released are women and children . " The government of Israel is committed to bringing all of the hostages home . Tonight , the government approved the outline for the first stage of achieving this goal , " the office said in a statement.Media reports ahead of the vote said Israel would free some 150 Palestinian prisoners and allow additional humanitarian aid into Gaza as part of the deal , but the Israeli statement made no mention of either of these elements .
