A total of 145 countries voted in favour of the resolution , while only seven countries voted against it. ? <p> New Delhi : India voted in favour of a United Nations General Assembly resolution condemning Israeli settlements in Palestine . The resolution was adopted on Thursday . It condemned activities of Israeli settlers in " Occupied Palestinian Territory , including East Jerusalem , and in the occupied Syrian Golan " . <p> Notably , the resolution was adopted amid the ongoing war between Israel and Hamas in the Gaza Strip . A total of 145 countries voted in favour of the resolution , while only seven countries voted against it . <p> " By its terms , the Assembly would condemn settlement activities in the Occupied Palestinian Territory , including East Jerusalem , and in the occupied Syrian Golan and any activities involving the confiscation of land , the disruption of the livelihood of protected persons , the forced transfer of civilians and the annexation of land , whether de facto or through national legislation , " the UN said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ voting . The countries that voted in favour of the resolution are - the United States , Israel , Canada , Hungary , Marshall Islands , Federated States of Micronesia and Nauru . <p> " The Resolution in the UNGA did not include any explicit condemnation of the terrorist attacks of October 7 . An amendment was moved to include this aspect , prior to the vote on the main resolution , " reported news agency PTI quoting a source as saying . <p> Last month , India abstained during voting on a UN resolution calling for a ceasefire between Israel and Hamas in the Gaza Strip . That resolution was adopted with 120 countries voting in its favour . <p> Militant group Hamas attacked Israel on October 7 . Around 1,400 Israeli 's were killed in the attack . In retaliation , Israel launched airstrikes and ground operation against Hamas in the Gaza Strip . In the Israeli assault around 9,000 Gazans have been killed so far .
