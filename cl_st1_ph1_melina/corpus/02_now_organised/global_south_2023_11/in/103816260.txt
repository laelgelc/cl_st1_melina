The journey is not an end in itself , but a means to an end . Nowhere is this truer than when it comes to the matter of conducting military operations as part of the grand strategy of a nation . If the ends -- the desired aims or outcomes -- are uncertain or ill-defined , then there is every possibility of getting bogged down in a never-ending and unwinnable battle . <p> In military appreciations , this boils down to the Ends-Ways-Means template . The end , or aim , is a military or politico-military objective aligned with a political aim .
