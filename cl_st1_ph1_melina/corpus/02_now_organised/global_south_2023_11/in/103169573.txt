<p> New Delhi : Several people were killed after an Israeli strike hit a convoy of ambulances that was on its way from al-Shifa Hospital in Gaza on Friday , Palestinian health authorities said , news agency AFP reported . A statement issued by the Hamas government said the ambulances , which were transporting the injured people , were heading towards south in Rafah . <p> " Several citizens were killed and dozens wounded in an Israeli strike at the entrance to Al-Shifa hospital in Gaza city , " the health ministry said in a statement . <p> According to the health ministry spokesperson , the Israel strike targeted the ambulance convoy at multiple locations outside the door of al-Shifa Hospital . He said it was targeted at the hospital gate and at Ansar Square a kilometre away , news agency Reuters reported . <p> It is to be noted that the al-Shifa Hospital in Gaza is facing severe over-crowding as the bed occupancy rate stood at 164 per cent , according to the World Health Organization . <p> The Palestinian health ministry said around 16 hospitals in Gaza are not functioning due to lack of fuel and damage @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the fuel shortage could risk the lives of the critical patients in other hospitals . <p> Meanwhile , as the Israel Hamas war entered its 28th day , Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said there would be no temporary ceasefire in Gaza till all the hostages are released . <p> " Israel rejects a temporary ceasefire that does not involve the return of our hostages , " PM Netanyahu said in a televised address .
