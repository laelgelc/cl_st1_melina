<h> ' Hamas went too far with the October 7 attack ' <p> Hamas so far is defiant in the face of Israel 's continuing ground offensive in Gaza . Paola Caridi , author of Hamas : From Resistance to Regime , in a conversation with TOI , explains why the militant outfit is deeply worried about getting the terrorist tag <p> How do you explain Hamas 's October 7 attack ? Why now ? Were you surprised by the scale of the attack ? I think everybody was taken by surprise . I did n't imagine such a scale of the Qassam Brigade 's attack , crimes of war , inside Israel . Regarding why , in my understanding there are various reasons that are not linked to that precise moment , but to the previous history of Hamas till that moment . For 16 years , from 2007 till 2023 , Hamas was sealed with the Israeli blockade of Gaza . Even before that Gaza was not a free place , but the last 16 years have been tough .
