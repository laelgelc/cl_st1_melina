In order to overcome the terror menace United Nations adopted Global Counter-Terrorism Strategy in 2006 . It holds that terrorism is unacceptable in all its forms and manifestations , and member states resolved to take practical steps , individually and collectively , to prevent and combat terrorism . The main features of this strategy are : * Measures to address the conditions conducive to the spread of terrorism . * Measures to prevent and combat terrorism . * Measures to build States ' capacity to prevent and combat terrorism and to strengthen the role of the United Nations system in that regard . * Measures to ensure respect for human rights for all and the rule of law as the fundamental basis of the fight against terrorism .
