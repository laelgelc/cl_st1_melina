<p> On Friday , in Gaza , a four-day cease-fire in the Israel-Hamas war began . It was a part of an agreement that Qatar helped broker . 24 hostages were freed . These include 10 Thai citizens , one Filipino citizen , and 13 Israeli citizens . Later in the day , Israel released 39 Palestinian prisoners . <p> As the truce began , additional aid started flowing into Gaza . In Gaza , 200 trucks of aid would enter daily . " When the truce starts , 130,000 litres of diesel and four trucks of gas will be delivered every day to Gaza , " Egypt had said . <p> On its Telegram channel , Hamas confirmed that all hostilities from its forces would stop .
