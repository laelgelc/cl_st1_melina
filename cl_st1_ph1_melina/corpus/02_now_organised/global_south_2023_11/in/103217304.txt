<h> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu rejects calls for a ceasefire , stating that there will be no ceasefire without the return of hostages . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu vetoed calls for a ceasefire yet again on Sunday as the war with Hamas neared the one month mark .
