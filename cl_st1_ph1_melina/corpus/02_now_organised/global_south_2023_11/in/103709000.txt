Most are Israeli citizens or dual nationals and would comprise only women and children . There are no suggestions of the elderly being part of this deal . The 50 hostages will be released in batches of 10 during the four-day ceasefire . <p> For its part , Israel would release about 150 Palestinian women and children who are in its jails .
