This shifts the spotlight on Qatar as to why it does what it does . <p> Iran 's role in bankrolling Hamas was not a secret . It is open and as clear as daylight . What is murkier is Qatar 's role . It has crafted an image globally as pro-western and different from the theocratic Arab states through its soft power mechanisms and a global television network Al Jazeera . <p> Al Jazeera is critical about everything that happens elsewhere but it goes hypocritically ultra-soft when it comes to Hamas and other terrorist outfits in the region and their ideological affiliations .
