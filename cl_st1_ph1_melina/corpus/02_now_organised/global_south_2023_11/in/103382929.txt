<p> London : Three lakh people take part in pro-Palestine protest calling for ceasefire in Gaza <p> 1 minute ago <h> London : Three lakh people take part in pro-Palestine protest calling for ceasefire in Gaza <p> Nov 12 , 2023 , 12:18AM ISTSource : TOI.in <p> About 3 lakh people attended the pro-Palestine protest in London calling for ceasefire in Gaza . This is the biggest rally held in London since the beginning of the Israel-Hamas war . 92 counter-protesters were arrested from near the protest site , say police . Met Police says it is also looking for two people seen wearing Hamas headbands . Some protesters chanted ' from the river to the sea ' seen as call for erasure of Israel . The protest stretched to almost 2.5 miles as per the police . About 2,000 officers had been deputed to ensure that the protest remains peaceful .
