<p> Israel has released 33 Palestinians from prison as part of a ceasefire deal with Hamas . This occurs within the framework of a 4-day truce agreement between Israel and Hamas , which involves the reciprocal release of hostages . Hamas had previously released 24 hostages , comprising 13 Israelis , 10 Thais , and one Filipino national . <p> Qadura Fares , who heads an advocacy group for prisoners , said 33 prisoners freed in the West Bank were handed to a team from the International Committee of the Red Cross . He said the remaining six were being freed from a Jerusalem lockup . <p> The releases on both sides were part of a deal for a four-day truce in the Israel-Hamas war that began Friday . <p> Qatar has led weeks of intense negotiations , coordinating with the US and Egypt , to reach an agreement for the freeing of 50 civilian hostages from Gaza in return for the release of Palestinian prisoners , a four-day truce and access for humanitarian aid . <p> Over the next four days , 150 Palestinian prisoners and 50 Israeli hostages are to be freed .
