<p> Delivering the Sunday sermon at a Syrian Christian church this weekend in Kanjirappally , Kerala , the parish priest took an unexpected detour from the scripture into the conflict raging in the Middle East . The faithful , who were otherwise distracted by the symphony of raindrops thudding on the roof , were suddenly all ears as the vicar proceeded to speak about the Promised Land or Canaan -- roughly covering the area from the Jordan River to the Mediterranean Sea -- which , he asserted , rightfully belonged to the Jews , based on the Old Testament . <p> The clergyman went on to state that all Abrahamic religions , which have the Torah as part of their scripture , should gracefully accept the fact . He was only echoing the general unease among the laity .
