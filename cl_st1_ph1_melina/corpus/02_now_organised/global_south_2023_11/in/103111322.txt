( Reuters ) <p> Paris-based Reporters Without Borders ( RSF ) on Wednesday called on the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) to probe the killings of 34 journalists in the war between Israel and Hamas , accusing both sides of committing possible war crimes . " The scale , seriousness and recurring nature of international crimes targeting journalists , particularly in Gaza , calls for a priority investigation by the ICC prosecutor , " said Christophe Deloire , head of the group . <p> In a statement , the media watchdog said it has filed a complaint with the ICC 's prosecutor regarding eight Palestinian journalists it said were killed in Israel 's bombardment of civilian areas in Gaza , and an Israeli journalist killed during Hamas 's bloody October 7 attack in southern Israel . <p> " The attacks suffered by Palestinian journalists in Gaza correspond to the international humanitarian law definition of an indiscriminate attack and therefore constitute @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Even if these journalists were the victims of attacks aimed at legitimate military targets , as the Israeli authorities claim , the attacks nevertheless caused manifestly excessive and disproportionate harm to civilians , and still amount to a war crime under this article , " the RSF said . <p> " The Israeli journalist 's death constituted the intentional homicide of a person protected by the Geneva Conventions , which is a war crime under article 8.2. a. of the ICC 's Rome Statute , " the group added . The media watchdog said the complaint cited " the deliberate , total or partial , destruction of the premises of more than 50 media outlets in Gaza " since the war began . This complaint comes as Israel claims that it makes every effort to avoid killing civilians and accuses Hamas of putting them at risk by operating in residential areas . <p> This is RSF 's third complaint to the ICC prosecutor about war crimes against Palestinian journalists in Gaza since 2018 .
