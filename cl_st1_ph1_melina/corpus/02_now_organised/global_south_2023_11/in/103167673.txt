<p> Hezbollah Chief Hassan Nasrallah Makes First Speech On Israel-Gaza Conflict English News N18VHassan Nasrallah , the leader of the Lebanese Shia Islamist group Hezbollah , is addressing his followers in his first public comments since the Israel-Hamas war broke out.The speech is likely to indicate the group 's next moves , as its fighters and the Israeli army are engaged in intense attacks on the Lebanon-Israel border.This has raised fears the area could become another front in the conflict.So far , however , the violence has largely been contained.Since Hamas carried out the 7 October attacks on Israel , killing more than 1,400 people , Lebanon has been on edge , closely watching Hezbollah.The group has intensified its attacks on Israel , which is retaliating . But both sides have apparently taken steps to avoid a dangerous escalation , and most strikes have been limited to the border area
