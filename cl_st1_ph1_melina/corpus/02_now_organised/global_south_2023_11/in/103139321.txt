<h> Cong root of all problems in country : Yogi <p> ALWAR ( Rajasthan ) , Nov 1 : Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath on Wednesday compared the ideology of Congress to that of the Taliban and said that the party is the root of every problem in the country . During a public meeting held in support of Bharatiya Janata Party ( BJP ) candidate Yogi Balaknath from Tijara assembly constituency here , Yogi said that the remedy for such Taliban like ideologies lies in the mace ( gada ) of Lord Hanuman . He pointed to Israel 's targeted actions in Gaza as a clear example , where terrorists are being eliminated selectively . He lamented that this Taliban-like mindset has blemished the longstanding traditions of Rajasthan , a region celebrated for its devotion and power . The CM asserted that had Congress been in power , the Ram Mandir issue would never have been resolved . " However , under the leadership of the Prime Minister , a magnificent temple of Ramlala is now being constructed in Ayodhya , signifying a period of development in the region , " he said . " Yogi highlighted the achievements of the Central Government and the good governance and development initiatives in UP . He said that after the formation of the BJP Government in Rajasthan , the realisation of the concept of Rama Rajya will be complete .
