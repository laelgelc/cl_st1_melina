<h> Less breeding in shaky times <p> Nowadays , everybody I meet seems to be bemoaning the fact that Gen Z does n't want to procreate . In my yoga group , on the litfest circuit , at cocktail parties , it is the same familiar lament , " Aiyyo , I want to be a grandmother but my useless son/daughter simply wants to adopt a cat/dog/goldfish . " As a mother of three 20-somethings , I can confirm that this indeed seems to be the sitch . Of course , I am generalising wildly and speaking only from personal experience but it really looks like let alone procreate , kids today do n't even seem to want a committed relationship , or a long-term career . They all seem to float and drift and flit , or focus too hard and burn out . The older auntiejis and unclejis are happy to lump the blame of the grand old institution of marriage collapsing squarely on feminism . " Girls want to dress up and go out and earn money and party , " they say , like these are somehow bad things to want .
