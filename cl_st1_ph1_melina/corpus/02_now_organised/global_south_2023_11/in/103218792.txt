<h> ? ? ? 44,000 roads repaired in state so far ? ? ? <p> Sunday 5th November , 2023 <p> Lucknow : The state government on Saturday said of the 85,000 roads that were identified for upgradation during the 2023-24 financial year 44,000 have already been repaired.While 24,000 carriageways have been made pothole-free the remaining 20,000 have got extra layers of bitumen for resurfacing.A government spokesperson said 51,000 road stretches required patch repairs so that the potholes and cracks that had emerged could be repaired , the remaining 34,000 require renovation and restoration.Public works department ( PWD ) has been instrumental in maintaining and upgrading the maximum number of roads . The official added that 768 highways have also been repaired during the drive to improve commute between various parts of the state.Besides PWD , urban development , housing , rural development , mandi parishad and National Highways Authority of India are other agencies that are working in a collective manner to improve the road network .
