<h> 10 vending zones , 5k stalls to come up in IMT sectors <p> Sunday 12th November , 2023 <p> Gurgaon : The Manesar municipal corporation ( MCM ) identified 10 street vending zones in the IMT sectors and wrote to the Haryana State Industrial &amp; Infrastructure Development Corporation ( HSIIDC ) seeking permission to set up the vending zones . Officials said 5,000 vendors would be given stalls . A committee headed by joint commissioner Dinesh Kumar has been formed to identify the street vendors in Manesar and allocate them street vendingsites.MCM commissioner Ashok Kumar Garg recently held a meeting where he announced the allocation of sites to street vendors and the provision of permanent shelters . However , he added that the street vendors should not encroach upon the roads , green belts , etc. till MCM allocates these sites .
