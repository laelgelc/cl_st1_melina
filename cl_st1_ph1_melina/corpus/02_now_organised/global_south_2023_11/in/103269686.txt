<p> In recent times , Europe and the US have witnessed a surge in protests related to the Israel-Palestine conflict . <p> Thousands of people have rallied in cities like Berlin and London to oppose antisemitism and express support for Israel . However , there have also been demonstrations demanding relief for Palestinians in besieged Gaza . <p> These protests have been marked by instances of antisemitic rhetoric and actions , causing concern among Jewish communities . In Paris , despite a ban on pro-Palestinian demonstrations , activists have continued to rally in support of the Palestinian cause . Meanwhile , in the US , the legacy of the Vietnam-era antiwar movement is being echoed in these protests . <p> The situation has escalated to such an extent that authorities have had to step up protection for Jewish communities .
