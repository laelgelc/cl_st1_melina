<p> New Delhi : The Virtual G20 Summit was held on Wednesday under the chairmanship of Prime Minister Narendra Modi . While addressing the summit , PM Modi welcomed the release of Israeli hostages by Hamas . <p> " We welcome the news of the release of hostages . We hope all the hostages will be released soon , " said the Prime Minister on the release of 50 hostages held in Gaza . He emphasised that the Israel-Hamas conflict should not become a regional conflict . <p> " No One Can Accept Terrorism , Death Of Civilians Should Also Be Condemned , " he added . PM Modi said that the nations should have mutual trust to overcome challenges . " In today 's world which is full of challenges , it is mutual trust that binds us , connects us with each other , " the Prime Minister stated . <p> PM Modi 's address : <p> While speaking on the growth of Arttificial Intellligence , PM Modi highlighted that the technology should be developed by keeping in mind the need of the people .
