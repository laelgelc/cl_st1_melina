<h> UNRWA official says ' started receiving half of fuel needed in Gaza ' <p> The UN agency for Palestinian refugees UNRWA on Sunday said it has begun receiving half of the fuel it needs to run its daily operations in the Gaza Strip , which will allow it to operate at a minimum level . <p> Spokesperson Adnan Abu Hasna said in a statement that Gaza continues to face a humanitarian crisis due to Israeli attacks on the territory since Oct. 7 . <p> " Finally , the agency has been allowed to obtain 60,000 liters of fuel daily . This quantity represents half of our needs to meet the minimum requirements of our operations , " he said . <p> " These quantities will be distributed to water desalination stations , to generate power for hospitals , bakeries , and to handle sewage water , as well as the collection of solid waste , " the spokesperson added . <p> He said " many streets in the Gaza Strip are flooded with sewage water due to insufficient fuel to pump the water , and residents will only receive two-thirds of their drinking water needs .
