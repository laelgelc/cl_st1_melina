<h> Sigh of relief for Gazans as Hamas-Israel truce takes place <p> 07:25 PM 24 Nov , 2023 <p> Source : UN website <p> GAZA -- The four-day truce between Israel and Hamas came into effect today ( Friday ) , marking the first pause in Israel 's massive bombardment of the Gaza Strip following October 7 attack by Hamas on southern Israel . <p> The holdup came after weeks of talks involving Israel , Palestinian fighting group , Qatar , Egypt and the United States . <p> The four-day break in fighting to be accompanied by release of 150 Palestinian prisoners and 50 Israeli hostages . <p> It also offers an opportunity for aid to filter into the war-ravaged enclave where nearly 15,000 people have been killed in the Israeli bombing , including more than 6,000 children . <p> Trucks with relief supplies continued to enter Gaza through the Rafah crossing from Egypt on Friday after the entry into force of a four-day pause in fighting , UN humanitarians said . <p> UN humanitarian affairs coordination office ( OCHA ) spokesperson Jens Laerke told reporters in Geneva that the hope regarding the agreement between Israel and Hamas announced @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , that it enables us to reach the people who need us and that it will be extended into an actual humanitarian ceasefire in the long run " . <p> " We hope the agreement ... will bring respite to the people of Gaza and Israel and some relief to the hostages and detainees who will be released , and to their families , " Mr. Laerke said . <p> Over 1.7 million people in Gaza are estimated to be internally displaced and about one million of them are staying in more than 150 UNRWA shelters across the Strip . <p> Hamas and other Palestinian gunmen seized around 240 hostages during unprecedented raids into southern Israel on October 7 which killed 1,200 people , most of them civilians , according to Israeli authorities . <p> The attack prompted a relentless Israeli campaign of bombing and a ground offensive in Hamas-run Gaza .
