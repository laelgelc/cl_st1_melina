<p> OSLO : Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Stoere urged Israel on Monday to release the full tax transfer it is withholding from the Palestinian Authority ( PA ) , saying the payment was " critical " for the welfare the Palestinian population . <p> Norway is the chair of the international donor group to the Palestinian territories , known as the Ad Hoc Liaison Committee . It was a facilitator in the 1992-93 talks between Israel and the Palestine Liberation Organization that led to the Oslo Accords providing for limited Palestinian self-rule . <p> On Nov. 2 , Israel said it would proceed with a tax revenue transfer to the PA in the Israeli-occupied West Bank but would withhold funds bound for Hamas-ruled Gaza , where the PA helps cover public sector wages and pay for electricity . <p> Israel 's decision came after an internal cabinet debate over whether to make the transfer as Israel battles Hamas that rule the Gaza Strip . <p> On Nov. 6 , the PA said it would not accept a partial transfer from Israel .
