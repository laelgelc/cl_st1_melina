<h> WHO chief tells UNSC one child killed in Gaza every 10 minutes <p> UNITED NATIONS : The World Health Organization ( WHO ) has painted a dire picture of the war-ravaged Gaza to the United Nations Security Council , revealing that on an average , a child is tragically killed every 10 minutes , nowhere and no one is safe in the bombardment-battered Palestinian enclave . <p> World Health Organization ( WHO ) Director General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus told the United Nations Security Council on Friday that half of Gaza 's 36 hospitals and two-thirds of its primary healthcare centres are non-operational , with those still functioning stretched beyond their capacities . " The healthcare system in Gaza is " on its knees " , " he revealed . <p> " Hospital corridors crammed with the injured , the sick , the dying . Morgues overflowing . Surgery without anaesthesia . Tens of thousands of displaced people sheltering in hospitals , " Tedros told the 15-member council . <p> The situation escalated following an October 7 attack in southern Israel , prompting it to vow to eradicate Hamas , the ruling power in Gaza .
