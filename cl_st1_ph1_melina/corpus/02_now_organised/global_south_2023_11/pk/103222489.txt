<h> Thousands march in Washington , London , Paris and Berlin for Palestinians <p> LONDON : Thousands of pro-Palestinian protesters rallied in London , Paris and Berlin on Sunday , calling for a ceasefire in Gaza after Iranians took to the streets against the United States and Israel . <p> Amid ongoing bombardments by Israel after the deadly Hamas attack on its territory last month killed 1,400 people , the British capital saw a large turnout in support of Palestinians for the fourth consecutive week . <p> Israel 's bombing campaign since the October attack by Hamas has killed nearly 9,500 people , mostly women and children , according to the Hamas-run health ministry in the Gaza Strip . <p> Police estimated that about 30,000 attended the rally in Trafalgar Square , central London . <p> They said it had arrested 11 people , including one for displaying a placard that could incite hatred . <p> One group carried a bundle of fabric , representing a dead baby killed during the Israeli bombing campaign . <p> Sama Dababneh , 26 , a Jordanian business consultant said she was tired of all upsetting images from Gaza .
