<h> Israeli military approves plans to continue ground operation in Gaza <p> Israel 's military chief approved on Wednesday evening operational plans to continue the ground operation in the Gaza Strip . <p> " Herzi Halevi , the chief of staff , held a session today ( Wednesday ) to approve plans for the subsequent stages of the fighting at the Southern Command headquarters , " the Israeli army said in a statement . <p> Halevi " approved the operational plans for the subsequent stages of the ground maneuver , " the army said , without providing further details . <p> " We know what needs to be done , and we are ready for the next step , " the statement quoted Halevi as saying . <p> On the other hand , local daily Yedioth Ahronoth quoted Defense Minister Yoav Gallant as saying : " The forces of the army in the air , on land , and at sea are ready to resume fighting immediately . "
