<h> Western hypocrisy <p> By News Desk <p> November 12 , 2023 <p> The Western world order , which is purportedly based on democratic and liberal values , has a different approach when it comes to the Palestine and Kashmir issues . Amidst the ongoing Israel-Hamas war , the West has not stopped the Israeli atrocities but has shown sympathy with Israel and claims that Israel has a right to defend its land . As a result , scores of innocent men women and children are being killed in Gaza and the West Bank . However , when it comes to the Russia-Ukraine war , the Western leaders begin to strike a different tune . <p> Upholding human rights and international law in the face of the Russian invasion of Ukraine is a priority , but not in the case of the Israeli attacks on Palestine . Apparently , the lofty principles of the West only apply to its own people and not those of the Global South . The west deserves nothing but opprobrium for this racist and bigoted approach in Palestine .
