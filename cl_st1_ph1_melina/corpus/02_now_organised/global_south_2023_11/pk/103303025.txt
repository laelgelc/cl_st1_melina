<h> Will our boycott help ? <p> By News Desk <p> November 08 , 2023 <p> Ever since the start of Israeli aggression in Gaza where the state has subjected the trapped people to relentless bombing , Palestine supporters around the world have asked people to boycott Israeli companies or those organizations that are in favour of Israel . Such calls have reached Pakistan as well . And while most people are looking for alternatives to their essential food items , many believe that this boycott is useless . The economic boycott is the best way to send a strong message to governments that have blood on their hands . We are already seeing the power of protests around the world . Leaders are giving statements about respecting people 's lives . We have to keep fighting and keep telling the world that we stand with Palestine . And we have to send a positive answer to calls for boycott .
