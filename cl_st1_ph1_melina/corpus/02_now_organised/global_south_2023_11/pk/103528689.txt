<h> FO urges international institutions to investigate Israel 's aggression as war crimes <p> Pakistan on Thursday called on international institutions to take Israel hostility on unarmed civilian population of Gaza as war crimes . <p> Foreign Office spokesperson Mumtaz Zahra Balch said during weekly press briefing that the Israeli aggression in Gaza continues for over a month unabated . The Israeli army is committing atrocities on Palestinian people . Attacks on hospitals are a serious violation of international humanitarian and war laws , she added . <p> She said Caretaker Prime Minister Anwar-ul-Haq Kakar has strongly condemned the Israeli aggression and brutality and reiterated his full support for Palestine . <p> She said that the caretaker premier had attended the Nov 11 OIC summit held in this regard . " Pakistan welcomes the declaration of the joint summit of the OIC and the Arab League . Pakistan condemns Israel 's attacks on hospitals in Gaza and calls on the international community to take notice of its aggression , " she added . <p> Talking about illegal immigrants , the spokesperson said that 58,368 Afghans have left Pakistan after the Taliban came into power .
