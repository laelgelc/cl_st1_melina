<h> Gaza death toll since October 7 climbs to 14,532 , including more than 6,000 children <p> The number of people killed in Israeli air and ground attacks on the Gaza Strip since the Oct. 7 Hamas offensive has risen to 14,532 , including over 6,000 children and 4,000 women , the media office in the besieged enclave said on Wednesday . <p> The number of deaths among medical personnel reached 205 , including doctors , nurses , and paramedics , it said in a statement . <p> As many as 25 members of the civil defense teams and 64 journalists were also among those killed . <p> The office put the number of injuries at more than 35,000 , saying more than 75% of them are children and women . <p> The number of destroyed government buildings reached 102 , along with 266 schools , of which 67 are no longer in service . <p> As many as 85 mosques have been completely destroyed , while 174 are damaged . Three churches were also targeted .
