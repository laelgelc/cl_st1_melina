<p> BRUSSELS : The European Union said on Tuesday a review of its development aid to Palestinians , ordered after the Oct. 7 Hamas attack on Israel , found no evidence of funds going to the group and that its assistance would continue . <p> The EU is the biggest provider of such aid to Palestinians . It has earmarked some 1.2 billion euros ( $1.3 billion ) for its programmes for the period between 2021 and 2024 . <p> The European Commission , the EU 's executive body , announced the review two days after Hamas attacked Israel from Gaza , killing 1,200 people and taking around 240 hostage , according to Israeli tallies . <p> Officials said the review was ordered as a precaution , not because they had any indications EU cash was going to Hamas . <p> " The review found no indications of EU money having directly or indirectly benefited the terrorist organisation Hamas , " said Commission Executive Vice President Valdis Dombrovskis . <p> Development aid is used for projects designed to have a long-term impact , such as paying the salaries of officials at the Palestinian Authority , which governs the West @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ agency UNRWA . <p> It is separate from humanitarian aid , meant for urgent needs for essentials such as food , water and shelter . <p> " The review found that the control system in place has worked . As a result payment to Palestinian beneficiaries and UNRWA will continue without any delays , " Dombrovskis told reporters . <p> The Commission said , however , that it would not proceed with plans to provide 75.6 million euros ( $82.5 million ) for Gaza infrastructure projects that were not " feasible in the current context " . That money will now go to other projects . <p> Israel launched heavy bombardment of Gaza after the Oct.7 attacks as part of a campaign to defeat Hamas . <p> The enclave 's Hamas-run government says at least 13,300 Palestinians have been confirmed killed - including at least 5,600 children - during Israel 's aerial blitz and invasion .
