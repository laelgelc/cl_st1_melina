<p> The COAS welcomed the dignitary and highlighted that visit by H.E . Imam-e-Kaaba to Pakistan is a matter of honour for the people of Pakistan . <p> The Army Chief further mentioned that the Muslims from all across the world have boundless reverence for Harmain Shraifain and profound respect for their custodian . <p> H.E . Imam-e-Kaaba said Islam is a religion of peace &amp; brotherhood and there is no space for misconstrued interpretations of Islam . <p> The dignitaries condemned the ongoing atrocities in Gaza Conflict and the oppression of the Muslims in IIOJK and expressed solidarity with the people of Palestine and Kashmir . <p> The COAS remarked that Pakistan and Kingdom of Saudi Arabia have strong strategic relations based on exemplary historic religious and cultural ties and unique reverence of Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in the hearts of the Pakistanis apart from the unanimity of views amongst the two brotherly countries . <p> At the end , H.E . Imam-e-Kaaba prayed for the peace , stability and unity of Ummah.
