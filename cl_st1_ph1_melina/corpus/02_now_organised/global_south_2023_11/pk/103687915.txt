<p> JERUSALEM -- After six weeks of relentless fighting , Israel and the Palestinian group Hamas have agreed to ceasefire for a pause in fighting in Gaza and the release of some hostages . <p> Reports in international media suggest that over 50 hostages will be released in a four-day truce , and there will be no fighting in the given duty . Under the deal , groups of 12-13 hostages will be released per day . <p> Tel Aviv has decided to release Palestinian captives and allow more humanitarian aid to enter the besieged enclave . <p> Under the Doha-mediated deal , hundreds of trucks of humanitarian , medical and fuel aid will enter Gaza . The starting time of the truce would be announced within the next 24 hours , per reports . <p> It was reported three Americans held by Palestine groups are likely to be among at least 50 hostages to be released . <p> Meanwhile , the death toll in Gaza has crossed 14,000 mark since Israel-Hamas conflict started last month .
