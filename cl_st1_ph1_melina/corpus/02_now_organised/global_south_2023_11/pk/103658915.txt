<h> Reiterates govt pledge to protect children from all kinds of abuse , violence , homelessness , abduction n Launches App for recovery of missing children <p> ISLAMABAD - Caretaker Prime Minister Anwaar- ul-Haq Kakar has reaffirmed unwavering commitment to safeguard the children from all kinds of harm including abuse , violence , homelessness and abduction . <p> The prime minister said this at the launch of Zainab Alert , Response and Recovery mobile App ( ZARRA ) at an event here on Monday on the occasion of Global Children Day . <p> The ZARRA App can be used to report about missing and abducted children . The App will allow users to upload a photo of the child with complete information about child 's disappearance and alerting relevant departments for prompt action . <p> The App , which is connected with all the police stations in the country , will also allow users to track the progress on investigation process and update . <p> Speaking on the occasion , Prime Minister Anwaar-ul-Haq Kakar expressed the confidence that the initiative will serve as an impregnable fortress to safeguard the children .
