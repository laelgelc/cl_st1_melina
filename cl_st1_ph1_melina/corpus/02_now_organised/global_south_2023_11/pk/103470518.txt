<h> Israeli airstrikes level 12 dwellings in Jabalia , causing casualties <p> Several Palestinians were killed and others injured as Israel carried out airstrikes that flattened 12 homes late on Monday in the northern Gaza Strip . <p> The official Palestinian TV reported on Telegram that " dozens of martyrs have been recovered , and 12 houses were entirely destroyed by the Israeli airstrikes on the Jabalia Services Club area . " <p> It added that " 31 martyrs have been recovered up to this moment . " <p> Images shared on Telegram by the broadcaster purportedly show the aftermath of the Israeli strikes , with casualties and injuries in a residential area within the Jabalia Services Club . <p> For more than a month , the Israeli army has been attacking all parts of the Gaza Strip , while its ground operation has focused on isolating its northern part and establishing a heavy military presence . <p> Israel has been carrying out relentless air and ground attacks on the Gaza Strip -- including on hospitals , residences and houses of worship -- since the Palestinian resistance group Hamas launched a cross-border attack on Oct. 7.
