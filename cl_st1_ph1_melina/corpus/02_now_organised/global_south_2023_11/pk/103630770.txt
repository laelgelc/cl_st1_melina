<p> UNITED NATIONS : A UN protectorate in Gaza would not solve the conflict there , the body 's Secretary-General said Monday , calling instead for a " transition period " involving Arab nations and the United States and leading to a two-state solution . <p> Antonio Guterres said it was " important to be able to transform this tragedy into an opportunity " -- which , for him , meant moving " in a determined and irreversible way to a two-state solution . " <p> This means , after the current war between Israel and Hamas fighters in Gaza ends , " a strengthened Palestinian Authority , assuming responsibilities in Gaza , " he said . <p> But the Palestinian Authority can not go into Gaza backed by Israeli tanks , he added -- meaning the " international community needs to look into a transition period . " <p> " I do not think that a UN protectorate in Gaza is a solution , " however , Guterres said . <p> Instead , he called for a " multi-stakeholder approach " that would see the US act as the " main guarantor " of Israel 's security , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " Everybody needs to come together to create the conditions for the transition , allowing for a strengthened Palestinian Authority to assume responsibilities in Gaza , " and from there to a two-state solution , he said . <p> Guterres also again denounced the killing of civilians -- especially children -- in Gaza as Israel wages a relentless air and ground campaign in retaliation for the October 7 attacks by Hamas . <p> For seven years now he has published a " list of shame " of parties to armed conflict who commit grave violations against children .
