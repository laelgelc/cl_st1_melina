<p> BEIRUT : The US embassy in Lebanon on Friday said there had been 12 hours of calm along the Blue Line marking the border with Israel , where daily exchanges of fire had been taking place between armed group Hezbollah and Israel . <p> " Twelve hours of calm on the Blue Line have given us all hope and renewed energy to build a better tomorrow for Lebanon , " the embassy said on X. <p> Residents told Reuters that the frontier had been quiet since just before 0700 local time on Friday morning , apart from a small spray of fire from the Israeli side . <p> It followed a temporary truce brokered by Qatar between Israel and Hezbollah 's ally Hamas in Gaza . <p> The truce did not formally extend to Lebanon , but Lebanese parliament speaker Nabih Berri , who heads the Shi'ite Amal Movement which is allied to Hezbollah , told reporters on Thursday that " what will happen in Gaza will happen in Lebanon . " <p> " If there is a disruption ( to the truce ) in Gaza , there will be a disruption in Lebanon , given that what @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Gaza , " Berri said . <p> A total of 87 Hezbollah fighters have killed since violence broke out along the border following Hamas ' attack on Israel on Oct. 7 , including seven in neighbouring Syria . <p> More than a dozen Lebanese civilians , including children and journalists , have also been killed in Israeli shelling on southern Lebanon . <p> The exchanges of fire had been ramping up in the days leading up to the Hamas-Israel truce .
