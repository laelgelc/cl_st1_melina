<h> 4-day humanitarian pause takes effect in Gaza <p> The four-day humanitarian pause between the Israeli army and Hamas came into effect on Friday morning in all areas of the Gaza Strip , temporarily stopping attacks for prisoner exchange and aid . <p> The pause commenced at 7 a.m. local time ( 0500GMT ) . <p> Israel estimates that at least 239 Israelis are being held by Hamas following the cross-border attack by the Palestinian group on Oct. 7 . <p> Israel launched relentless air and ground attacks on the Gaza Strip following a cross border attack by the Palestinian group Hamas on Oct. 7 . <p> The Palestinian death toll from Israeli attacks on the Gaza Strip has soared to 14,854 , the government media office in the blockaded enclave said Thursday . The victims include 6,150 children and over 4,000 women , while more than 36,000 people have been injured , it said .
