It came as a ray of hope to break the endless cycle of indiscriminate brutality being inflicted by the Israeli forces on Palestinians . Over 8,400 Palestinians have been killed which includes mostly women and 3500 children . But Israel has not only rejected this resolution but also called it a " dark day for the United Nations and mankind " . While Israel is still defiant , the loss of Palestinians lives continues unabated . Some US politicians have acknowledged that violence against Palestinian civilians is a tragedy . But there has been no end to this brutality .
