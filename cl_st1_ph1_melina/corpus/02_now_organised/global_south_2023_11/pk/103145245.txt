<p> Casualties from Israeli airstrikes on the Jabalia refugee camp in the Gaza Strip have risen to 1,000 , according to the Health Ministry . <p> Early Thursday , ministry spokesman Ashraf Al-Qedra said on Facebook that there were " 1,000 killed , injured , and missing in the massacres in Jabalia , " located in the northern Gaza Strip . <p> Gaza 's Interior Ministry said on Tuesday that an Israeli airstrike resulted in " 400 casualties , including killed and injured , and the complete destruction of a residential neighborhood in the Jabalia camp . " <p> On Wednesday evening , Israeli fighter jets carried out another raid on the camp , causing extensive damage to dozens of homes . <p> This week the Israeli army has expanded its air and ground attacks on the Gaza Strip , which has been under relentless airstrikes since a cross-border attack by Hamas on Oct. 7 . <p> More than 10,300 people have since been killed in the conflict , including at least 8,796 Palestinians and more than 1,538 Israelis .
