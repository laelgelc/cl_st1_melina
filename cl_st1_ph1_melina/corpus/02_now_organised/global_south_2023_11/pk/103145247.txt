<h> UN denounces ' latest atrocity ' after strikes on Gaza refugee camp <p> UNITED NATIONS , UNITED STATES - The United Nations on Wednesday decried strikes on Gaza 's largest refugee camp that killed scores of people in attacks that Israel said targeted a Hamas commander . <p> " This is just the latest atrocity to befall the people of Gaza where the fighting has entered an even more terrifying phase , with increasingly dreadful humanitarian consequences , " Martin Griffiths , the UN humanitarian chief , said in a statement . <p> He said " the world seems unable , or unwilling , to act , " adding " this can not go on . We need a step change . " <p> A strike on the Jabalia refugee camp killed at least 47 people Tuesday -- including a Hamas commander involved in the October 7 attacks , according to Israel . Israel said its warplanes had hit a " vast " tunnel complex at the site , killing " many Hamas terrorists " , including local battalion commander Ibrahim Biari .
