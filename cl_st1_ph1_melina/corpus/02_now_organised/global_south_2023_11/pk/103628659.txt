The team saw a mass grave at the entrance of the hospital and was told that more than 80 people were buried there , " said a WHO report released on Sunday . <p> " Patient deaths have occurred over the previous two to three days @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said . " Lack of infection control measures and antibiotics has resulted in severely infected wounds , particularly in trauma patients , " it added . <p> The report expressed deep concern , urging immediate efforts to restore functionality to Al Shifa and other hospitals .
