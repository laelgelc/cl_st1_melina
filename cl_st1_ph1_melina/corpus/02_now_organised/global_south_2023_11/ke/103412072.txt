<h> India and US push defence deals amid ' global challenges ' <p> NEW DELHI , India Nov 12 -- India and the United States announced progress on key defence deals and said they would expand their growing partnership in the face of geopolitical challenges as their top diplomats and senior ministers met on Friday . <p> US Secretary of State Antony Blinken and Defence Secretary Lloyd Austin met Indian Foreign Minister Subrahmanyam Jaishankar and Defence Minister Rajnath Singh in New Delhi as part of their annual " 2+2 Dialogue " , focused on the Indo-Pacific region . <p> The two countries which were once on opposite sides of the Cold War are now working on landmark deals including for the US to supply and manufacture engines for Indian fighter jets . <p> Indian Defence Secretary Giridhar Aramane said that deal between the aerospace unit of General Electric and India 's state-owned Hindustan Aeronautics was on track . <p> " We are finalising the commercial arrangements and the necessary legal requirements are being put in place , " he told reporters after the talks . <p> A more than $3 billion deal for India to buy 31 armed drones @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is waiting for the
