The theory is that by raising interest rates and making it more expensive to borrow , consumers will spend less and that would lead to slower price rises . <p> The bank had faced criticism , with some suggesting that holding interest rates at higher levels could put the US economy at risk of entering a recession . <p> But the economy grew by a better-than-expected 4.9% from July to September .
