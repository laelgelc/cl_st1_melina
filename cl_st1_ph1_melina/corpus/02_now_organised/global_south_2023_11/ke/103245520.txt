<h> Honduras says it recalled ambassador from Israel due to grave humanitarian situation in Gaza <p> The Honduran government has recalled the republic 's ambassador to Israel for consultations due to the grave humanitarian situation in Gaza , Foreign Minister Enrique Reina said . <p> " Taking into account the grave humanitarian situation facing the Palestinian civilian population in the Gaza Strip , the government of President Xiomara Castro has decided to immediately recall Mr. Roberto Martinez , Ambassador of the Republic of Honduras to Israel , to Tegucigalpa for consultations , " Reina wrote on X , formerly known as Twitter . <p> In recent days , Honduras has become the third country in Latin America to recall its diplomatic representative from Tel Aviv . Earlier , Colombia and Chile took similar steps .
