<h> Hamas govt says 195 killed in Israel strikes on Gaza camp this week <p> The Hamas government in Gaza says 195 people had been killed in Israeli strikes this week on Jabalia refugee camp , the biggest in the Palestinian territory . <p> " The victims of the first and second massacres in Jabalia exceed 1,000 " including " martyrs and wounded " , said a statement from the Hamas government press office , referring to strikes on Tuesday and Wednesday . <p> " We have recorded 195 martyrs , 120 missing under the rubble , and 777 wounded , " it said . <p> The Hamas-run health ministry says 8,796 people have died , mostly women and children , since Israel launched a massive ground and air assault following the October 7 Hamas attacks it says claimed 1,400 lives , also mostly civilians .
