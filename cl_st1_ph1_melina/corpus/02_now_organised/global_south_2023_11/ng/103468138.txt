<h> OIC selects Nigeria as special delegate to halt Gaza conflict <p> ABUJA- The Federal Republic of Nigeria has been selected by the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) and the Arab League as part of a special delegation mandated to initiate immediate international action to halt the ongoing war in Gaza . <p> The Minister of Foreign Affairs , Amb . Yusuf Tuggar , in a statement said the message was communicated to Nigeria by the Royal Embassy of Saudi Arabia in Nigeria on Tuesday in Abuja . <p> Tuggar said the the decision was taken in furtherance to the resolution adopted at the Joint Arab Islamic Extraordinary Summit on Israeli aggressions against the Palestinian people , which took place in Riyadh on Saturday . <p> According to the minister , the resolution that bears Nigeria in the inclusion in the peace team states the following : <p> " Assign the Foreign Minister of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia , in its capacity as the presidency of the 32nd Arab and Islamic Summit , along with counterparts from Jordan , Egypt , Qatar , T ? rkiye , Indonesia , Nigeria , and Palestine . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ organizations to initiate immediate international action on behalf of all member states of the OIC and the Arab League to formulate an international move to halt the war in Gaza . <p> " To pressure for a real and serious political process to achieve permanent and comprehensive peace in accordance with established international references .
