<h> More people at risk of death from disease than bombings in Gaza -- WHO <p> The World Health Organization ( WHO ) on Tuesday expressed fear that more people could die from diseases than from bombings in the Gaza Strip if the health system in the enclave was not restored . <p> A WHO official , Margaret Harris , who addressed journalists in Geneva , Switzerland , said the United Nations health agency was concerned about a rise in outbreaks of infectious diseases , particularly diarrhea <p> She said : " Eventually we will see more people dying from disease than from bombardment if we are not able to put back together this health system . " <p> Harris said more than 15,000 people have been killed in Israel 's bombardment of Gaza , with around 40 percent of them children . <p> " There are no medicines , no vaccination activities , no access to safe water and hygiene and no food . We saw a very high number of cases of diarrhea among infants , " she added .
