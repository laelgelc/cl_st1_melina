<h> Hamas releases hostages after accusing Israel of truce violations <p> After a seven-hour delay , Hamas turned over 13 Israeli prisoners and four Thai nationals to the International Committee of the Red Cross ( ICRC ) , citing violations by Israel of the terms of the truce . <p> On Saturday , the second day of the ceasefire in hostilities between Israel and the Palestinians , the impasse was broken through mediation by Qatar and Egypt . <p> It is currently anticipated that Israel will free 39 Palestinian detainees from its facilities . <p> Majed al-Ansari , a spokesperson for Qatar 's Ministry of Foreign Affairs , said in a post on X that 13 Israelis and four foreign nationals had been handed over to the ICRC . They were on their way to the Rafah border crossing between southern Gaza and Egypt before travelling to Israel . <p> " After undergoing an initial medical assessment , they will continue to be accompanied by IDF Israeli Army soldiers as they make their way to Israeli hospitals , where they will be reunited with their families , " it said . <p> The Israeli captives included six adult women @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from the Israeli prime minister 's office . The hostages were released after spending 50 days in captivity , it said . <p> Hamas spokesperson Osama Hamdan had said earlier that the aid deliveries permitted by Israel had fallen short of what had been promised and were not reaching northern Gaza , which was the target of Israel 's offensive . <p> Only 65 of 340 aid trucks that had entered Gaza since Friday had reached northern Gaza , which was " less than half of what Israel agreed on " , Hamdan said from Beirut .
