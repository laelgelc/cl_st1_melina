<h> Israel agrees to four-hour daily ceasefire in Gaza <p> Israel has agreed to a four-hour military humanitarian ceasefire in northern Gaza . <p> The White House spokesman , John Kirby , announced the ceasefire in a media briefing on Thursday in Washington . <p> The United States President , Joe Biden , had previously appealed to the Israeli Prime Minister , Benjamin Netanyahu , to allow a ceasefire in the fighting that broke out following the October 7 attack by the Palestinian militant group , Hamas . <p> The death toll in the one-month-old conflict has exceeded 10 , 000 with over 9,000 victims , mostly women and children , identified in Gaza . <p> The White House spokesman described the ceasefire as a " significant step forward " as the Israeli continues its aggression on Gaza , a city of 2.7 million Palestinians .
