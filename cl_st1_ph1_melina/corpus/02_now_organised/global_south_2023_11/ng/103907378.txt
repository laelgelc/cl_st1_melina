<h> Israeli-Hamas ceasefire agreement extended <p> The ceasefire between Israel and Hamas that was due to expire at 7am on Thursday , has now been extended shortly before it was scheduled to elapse , the Israel Defense Forces said on Telegram . <p> " In light of the mediators ' efforts to continue the process of releasing the hostages and subject to the terms of the framework , the operational pause will continue , " the IDF wrote . <p> The Palestinian militant group , Hamas has also announced its agreement to extend the truce in Gaza for a seventh day , while Qatar , which has been mediating between the two sides , also confirmed that another temporary truce had been agreed . <p> In a statement on its own Telegram channel , Hamas said it was willing to extend the truce deal with Israel for four days and release more Israeli hostages in exchange for Palestinian security prisoners . <p> Meanwhile , a new batch of 61 Israeli hostages and 20 foreign nationals have been already released by Hamas and more are set to be released Wednesday during the truce .
