<p> The House of Representatives has rejected a motion seeking to request President Bola Tinubu to schedule a date for the conduct of the national census . <p> Nigeria last had its national census conducted in 2006 . <p> In the motion moved by Dominic Okafor and Patrick Umoh from Anambra and Akwa Ibom states , respectively , it sought to urge the President to declare a date for the conduct of the census to facilitate accurate economic planning . <p> The 2023 census was initially scheduled to take place across the country between May 3 and 5 , 2023 , but former President Muhammadu Buhari postponed the exercise . <p> Lai Mohammed , former Minister of Information and Culture , had said that a new date for the census would be determined by the new administration . <p> While leading the debate on the motion , Okafor , the lead sponsor , said census provides accurate population data that is crucial for development planning , policy formulation , resource allocation , and government programme implementation . <p> The lawmaker stressed that the census is crucial at this time when the federal and state governments are working on giving @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " Data collected in the 2006 population census is far from reality in 2023 and can not validly form a solid base for the formation of national economic policies that will aid the federal government in administering viable national planning , " he said . <p> Okafor said the National Population Commission , NPC , spent N200 billion out of the N800 billion budgeted for the planning and mid-execution of the census . <p> " If the population and housing census fails to take place within 12 months , the information gathered across the country would become stale , a waste of resources and the country would have to increase costs of gathering fresh information . " <p> In his contribution , Ahmed Jaha from Borno state , opposed the motion , noting that census is not feasible because of the insecurity ravaging many parts of the country . <p> He argued that many people displaced in the north-west , north-east and other parts of the country as a result of insecurity are not liable to be counted . <p> " In Nigeria , we are not ready @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " he said <p> The lawmaker said he is strongly against the motion , adding that it should be rejected . <p> Ademorin Kuye from Lagos state , supported the motion , urging his colleagues to support it . <p> He said for there to be proper planning at the state and federal levels , a census should be conducted . <p> The lawmaker said Jaha did not take time to look at the request of the motion appropriately . <p> He added that the concerns raised were in existence when the money was appropriated for the conduct of the census . <p> Inua Garuba from Gombe state also opposed the motion , saying there should not be a census when there is no security . <p> " Some few kilometres away from some state capital nobody will have the courage to start conducting census of people , " he said . <p> Kama Kama from Ebonyi state backed Garuba 's position , saying some places in the south-east are not accessible . <p> Idris Wase from Plateau state said while the census would lead to good planning for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ addressed first . <p> " We are deceiving ourselves if we have not secured ourselves but want to conduct the census . The situation is not right , " he said . <p> The motion was rejected when it was put to a voice vote by Tajudeen Abbas , Speaker of the House . <p> A Nasarawa lawmaker , Gaza Jonathan proposed that all the requests should be expunged except the one which calls for the investigation of the National Population Commission , NPC , for the money expended on the planned census . <p> Jonathan 's amendment was voted for when it was subjected to a voice vote by Abbas .
