<p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu summoned his war cabinet on Tuesday amid growing signs of an imminent deal on the release of hostages who were taken by Hamas militants to the Gaza Strip more than six weeks ago . <p> " We are making progress . I do n't think it 's worth saying too much , not at even this moment , but I hope there will be good news soon , " Netanyahu told reservists without providing further details . <p> His office said that " in light of developments in the matter of the release of our hostages , " he would convene his war cabinet at 6 p.m ( 1600 GMT ) followed by meetings of his wider security cabinet and the full cabinet . <p> Two far-right members of Netanyahu 's government said they would vote against the deal when the full cabinet meets at 8 p.m. ( 1800 GMT ) , though they were not expected to tip the vote . <p> Talk of an imminent hostage deal has swirled for days . ' And then I 'll know that it 's really over and I can breathe a sigh of relief and say that 's it , it 's over , " she said . <p> Tarshansky was kidnapped from her home in Kibbutz Beeri , one of the communities worst hit by Hamas . <p> In a sign it was expecting hostages to return soon , Netanyahu 's office said it was convening the directors-general of all the relevant government ministries to prepare for their treatment and aid .
