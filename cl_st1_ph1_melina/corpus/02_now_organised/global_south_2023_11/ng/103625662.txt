<h> Casualties as Israeli troops open fire on another hospital in Gaza <p> The Israeli army besieged an Indonesian hospital in the north of the Gaza Strip and opened fire from tanks , the Al Jazeera TV channel reported . <p> According to the channel , Israeli tanks " positioned themselves around the Indonesian hospital and opened fire on the building . " As a result of the shelling , there are fatalities and casualties among the patients and medical staff . <p> Earlier , Israeli troops were reported to have fired heavy artillery shells at the Indonesian hospital .
