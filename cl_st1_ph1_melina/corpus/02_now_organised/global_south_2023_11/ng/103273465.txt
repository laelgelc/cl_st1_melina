<p> Exactly after a month of the raging war between Israel and Hamas militia in Gaza commenced , the Senate , Tuesday , urged the federal government to join other countries in prevailing on the United Nations ( UN ) to ensure cease fire between the warring territories . <p> It also sought for two states solution between Israel and Palestine for lasting peace that had eluded the areas over the last 70 years . <p> Senate 's resolutions were sequel to a motion sponsored to that effect by Senator Suleiman Kawu Summaila ( NNPP Kano South ) and 28 others . <p> Senator Kawu in the motion tater read on his behalf by Senator Adamu Aliero ( PDP Kebbi Central ) , noted that the Israeli-Palestinian conflict is a complex and long-standing crisis that has been ongoing for over 100 years . <p> The battle according to him , is rooted in competing claims to the land of Israel and Palestine and has been marked by violence , displacement , and suffering on both sides . <p> On the current war , Senator Kawu said many international communities have condemned the ongoing conflict and that if urgent steps @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ may spread to neighbouring countries and then to the allies of Israel and Palestine , which may further result in the loss of more lives and properties . <p> Being a global embarrassment , the Senate in its resolutions , urged the federal government to collaborate with other countries of the world in making the United Nations , enforce cease fire between the warring enclaves and also proffered two states solution for the incessant wars .
