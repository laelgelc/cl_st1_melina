<h> Israeli-Hamas Deal : More trucks with humanitarian supplies head to Gaza <h> The truce deal , which was brokered and announced by Qatar on Wednesday , involves a four-day pause in fighting between both sides . <p> More trucks carrying humanitarian supplies moved through Egypt 's Rafah border crossing to the Gaza Strip on Saturday , the second day of a temporary truce agreed to by Israel and the Islamist Hamas movement . <p> Seven fuel trucks , including four loaded with cooking gas , passed through the crossing on Saturday , an Egyptian official said . <p> In addition , 100 trucks carrying food and medical aid bound for Gaza also crossed through Rafah , Raed Abdel-Nasser , the head of the Red Crescent in Egypt 's northern Sinai , told dpa . <p> The Palestinian Red Crescent , meanwhile , said its teams received 196 trucks of relief supplies via Rafah on Friday from its Egyptian counterpart . <p> The truce agreement , which was brokered and announced by Qatar on Wednesday , involves a four-day pause in fighting between both sides .
