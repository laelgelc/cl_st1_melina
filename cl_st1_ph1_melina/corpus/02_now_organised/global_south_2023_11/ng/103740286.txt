<h> Calm at Lebanese-Israeli border after Gaza ceasefire begins <h> Hezbollah has not officially announced that they would abide by the temporary Gaza ceasefire . <p> The Lebanese-Israeli border was calm on Friday after the first pause in fighting in nearly seven weeks began in Gaza . <p> " No Hezbollah attacks have been registered since the truce started , " a Lebanese security source told dpa . <p> A source from the Hezbollah an Iran-backed militant group said that there were still Israeli warplanes and " intelligence drones " hovering over areas of southern Lebanon , but no strikes were recorded . <p> Hezbollah has not officially announced that they would abide by the temporary Gaza ceasefire , but according to Israeli media reports , the military had been ordered to respond only in the event of new attacks from Lebanon .
