<h> DFA hopes Gaza truce will help free 17 OFW hostages <p> THE Department of Foreign Affairs said the 17 Filipino seafarers taken hostage by Houthi-Yemeni forces in Yemen are safe . <p> " The positive news is that all indications are they are all safe and not physically , " DFA Undersecretary Eduardo De Vega told The Source of CNN Philippines . <p> He said one of the seafarers was able to contact his family in the Philippines and assured them that they are all safe . <p> De Vega said unfortunately , the seafarer was not able to reveal as much details as the government would like to . <p> The Department of Migrant Workers , he said , is in charge of the general welfare of the seafarers , including coordinating with their families in the Philippines , their manning agencies and the shipowner . <p> The DFA is also on deck , dealing with finding political and diplomatic solutions to release the 17 Filipinos . <p> De Vega said he hopes the four-day truce between Israel and Hamas would help facilitate the release of all the 25 crew of Galaxy Leader , a Japanese ship @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ vessel in the Red Sea . <p> " The way we look at it , the truce is a positive development which may lead to the Houthi rebels freeing our 17 hostages , " De Vega added . <p> Senators on Thursday weighed in on the continuing war between Israel and Hamas in the Gaza Strip , with one pushing a ceasefire and two others urging government to move quickly to free the 17 Filipino hostages of the Houthis . <p> In a statement , Majority Leader Joel Villanueva offered " prayers for the safety of the 25 individuals , including 17 Filipino seafarers " in Yemen . <p> He urged the Department of Foreign Affairs , Department of Migrant Workers ( DMW ) , and other concerned agencies to " exhaust all efforts to bring our kababayans home . " <p> " The establishment of the DMW , which we principally sponsored and authored in the Senate , will provide a faster and more efficient service to our Overseas Filipino Workers OFWs , " Villanueva assured . <p> Moreover , he added , " we ensured the funding of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to our OFWs in need . " <p> Meanwhile , Deputy Minority Leader Risa Hontiveros also urged the DFA and DMW to utilize all diplomatic channels and resources available to facilitate the immediate release of the 17 OFW sailors . <p> " We call on the Department of Foreign Affairs to leverage its diplomatic relationships and engage with international partners to address this pressing issue , " Hontiveros said . <p> Moving forward , Sen.Hontiveros underscored the need for a comprehensive and improved policy framework to effectively manage recurring hazards that OFWs , specifically seafarers , faced in the course of their work . <p> Meanwhile , , Sen. Robinhood Padilla , a Muslim , supported calls for an end or at least a cease-fire in the Israel-Hamas conflict . <p> " I am one with the whole world in praying for the realization of a ceasefire -- or " urgent and extended " humanitarian pauses -- between Israel and the militant group Hamas , which is stated in Resolution 2712 of the United Nations Security Council , " he said .
