<h> Israel confirms killing Hamas 's ' head of government ' in Gaza <p> Israel confirmed on Tuesday that several senior Hamas officials and mid-level commanders , including Issam al-Daalis , head of the Hamas-run government in Gaza , were killed in its latest airstrikes . <p> Al-Daalis was the head of Hamas 's Government Administrative Committee in the Gaza Strip , a role comparable to that of a prime minister . <p> The Israeli military and Shin Bet domestic security agency said in a joint statement that al-Daalis succeeded Rawhi Mushtaha as " the head of the Hamas government " after Mushtaha was killed by Israeli forces in July 2024 . <p> The statement said several Hamas officials were killed over the past 24 hours in " dozens " of airstrikes . It said that " based on the information available at this stage , it can be determined with high probability " that three other Hamas members were killed .
