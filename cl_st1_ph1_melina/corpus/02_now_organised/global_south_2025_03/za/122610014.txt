<p> The South African Communist Party ( SACP ) strongly condemns the recent bombardment of Gaza by the apartheid Israeli regime , marking a continuation of the regime 's genocidal campaign against the people of Palestine . Apartheid Israel 's bombardment of Gaza since Tuesday 18 March 2025 has led to the death of over 400 people , the majority of whom are women and children , wounding hundreds more . <p> Thus far , the apartheid Israeli regime has murdered more than 61,700 people in Gaza since it began its genocide on 7 October 2023 . Tens of thousands more Palestinians remain missing under the rubble and presumed dead . Many more have been heavily injured . <p> The SACP reiterates its call for intensified unity among justice-loving people across the world , including co-operation of the people , governments and international bodies to isolate the apartheid Israeli settler regime economically , politically , culturally and otherwise . In particular , the world working-class movement should develop a leading role in this struggle for a just world order . <p> United States imperialist military aggression equally condemned <p> --&gt; @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ United States for its military aggression in Yemen , which has led to the death of over 53 people , wounding hundreds more . The continuous military bombardment of Yemen by the imperialist forces has everything to do with the control and exploitation of Yemeni resources , including the subjugation of its people . The violation of the Yemeni people 's right to self-determination sits at the core of the US imperialist aggression in Yemen as well as everywhere else as it seeks to control the entire region . <p> In solidarity with the people of Yemen , the SACP calls for unity of the working-class and all justice and peace loving people across the world to defend the right to national sovereignty . The people of Yemen have the right to determine their destiny , free from foreign aggression and control . <p> The SACP reiterates its support for the entire axis of resistance in the Middle East , including Iran , against aggression by imperialist forces and apartheid Israeli regime .
