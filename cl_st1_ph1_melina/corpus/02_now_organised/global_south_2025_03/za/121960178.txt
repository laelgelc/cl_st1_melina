<h> Record number of journalists killed in 2024 <p> by TINTSWALO BALOYIJOHANNESBURG , ( CAJ News ) -- THE International Federation of Journalists ( IFJ ) reports that 122 journalists were killed worldwide in 2024 . <p> Ten were killed in Africa . <p> The global figure represents among the five deadliest years since the IFJ began its Killed List in 1990 . <p> The Gaza strip accounted for almost half of journalists killed worldwide , with 64 casualties in 2024 . In the Asia Pacific region , 22 journalists were killed last year , an 83 percent increase from the year before . <p> Four media workers were killed in Europe . <p> In Latin America and the Caribbean , there was a fall in the number of killings , with nine journalists murdered compared to 11 in 2023 . <p> In the Middle East and the Arab World , there were 77 killings recorded by the federation . <p> Meanwhile , the situation is worrying in Sudan , where six journalists have been killed this year . <p> Anthony Bellanger , IFJ general secretary , said , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , where the international community has largely abandoned the country .
