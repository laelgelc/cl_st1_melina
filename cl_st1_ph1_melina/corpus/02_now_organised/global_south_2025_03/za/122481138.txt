<p> UCT 's research enterprise is heavily reliant on funding from international sources . Of these , US federal agencies contribute by far the largest share . The US National Institutes of Health ( NIH ) contributed a significant proportion of research revenue to UCT in 2024 , most of which was awarded as direct or subaward grants or contracts for research on HIV/AIDS , TB , HIV-associated TB , pediatric infectious diseases , population health and psychiatry in the Faculty of Health Sciences ( FHS ) - areas of global research leadership for UCT . <p> The recent turmoil wrought within the NIH by actions of the Trump administration has raised serious concern over the future of funding to foreign institutions . The risk of federal funding cuts is exacerbated by the Executive Order issued on 7 February 2025 according to which " foreign aid or assistance " to South Africa specifically is to be halted .
