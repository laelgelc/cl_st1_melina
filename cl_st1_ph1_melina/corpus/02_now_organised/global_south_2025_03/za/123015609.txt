Illustration : KAREN MOOLMAN <p> Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky is slated to visit SA towards the end of April . While each day 's tumult in Trumpland seems to exceed the one before , Ukraine and SA have respectively provoked two of the most explosive recent Trump-ordered exits : the directive to Zelensky to leave the White House after the Oval Office shouting match , and the expulsion of SA ambassador to the US , Ebrahim Rasool . <p> Zelensky and Ramaphosa may have cause to rethink the SA visit . The sheer spite and conspiracy-inclined mindset of the Trump administration may have each speculating whether they can afford the ire such a visit might provoke from Trump . SABC senior international news editor Sophie Mokoena wrote recently on X that the number two demand of SA from the US is that we stop Zelensky 's visit to the country . <p> We should n't . And not from some desire to thumb our noses at Trump @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that urge ) . We should roll out the carpet for Zelensky because revulsion at naked aggression and oppression , solidarity with the embattled people of his country , and respect for his wartime leadership of the country argue for such honour . But even if we do n't see it that way , we should do so in our shameless self-interest . <p> As is now trite , a torch has been put to what we have taken to be the rules-based international order , even if it has often only functioned as fac ? ade . Yet SA , even at its most ambitious approximation in any foreseeable future , can only hope to be a strategically located middle-power . Unless it pins its hopes on being a favoured flunkey of an existing or emerging power , SA 's security and sovereignty rest on preservation of , and reform to , multilateralism , the rules-based order and the norms and principles underpinning this system . <p> At the centre of this system is the principle of territorial sovereignty -- denoting that a state has exclusive authority and control @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , without external interference . It is also the principle most implicated , and its foundational status compromised , by Russia 's invasion of Ukraine . Any future " peace " for Ukraine that requires of the country that it cede parts of its territory , will be bought at the cost of that principle 's continued force . <p> Whatever might be required by SA 's previously declared position of neutrality vis- ? -vis Ukraine and Russia , this is the moment to declare its unambiguous support for Ukraine retaining the maximum possible geographical area and people within it . If nothing else , failure to do so chimes inconsistently with SA 's much scrutinised position on Israel and Gaza : if Russia can take territory and displace and dispossess its inhabitants , why exactly are Israel 's actions to be reviled ? <p> But more existentially , for the purposes of its own territorial integrity , dilution of the importance of this principle makes the demands of AfriForum , Solidarity , the Cape Independence group , and the more extreme flanks of the DA far more potent . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ceded in satisfaction of Russia and under the pretext of nominal protection of Russian-speaking minorities in Ukraine , is a world susceptible to demands that chunks of SA are peeled off in satisfaction of aggrieved minorities ' demands for self-governance . <p> As the world resets -- and unless SA 's future trajectory is to be determined simply as flotsam of that tidal wave -- it will need to consciously define its placement . Unquestionably , our global standing is not what it once was . Our domestic record , and many of the actions we 've taken on the international stage , have in no way met the highest hopes and expectation of a democratic SA . <p> But degraded though our image may be , our record as a country that avoided civil war in the face of historic injustice , and more specifically a racial conflagration , and secured a constitution that guarantees a nation-state fashioned by constitutional norms and values , rather than immutable racial or ethnic ties , continues to exert a powerful influence on the global imagination . Especially now . <p> That is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reaction to SA 's International Court of Justice application regarding the situation in Gaza and an intensive targeting of the country for its role , despite the numerous other parties -- such as Belgium , Chile , Ireland , Mexico and Spain -- that have also joined the application . <p> And whatever one might feel about the wisdom of Rasool 's utterances that led to his expulsion , it 's hard to see that their veracity might be credibly contested . While we might only be a tawdry pastiche of what the " rainbow nation " ideal was meant to represent , the disproportionate amount of time and attention being spent vilifying SA by those in the US suggest SA is being made to function ideologically as even more of a failed state than it really is . <p> Whether because of demographic trends , as Rasool enumerated , or for other reasons , there appears to be a vested interest in holding out a narrative that white minorities face only grave peril and persecution within a multiracial nation state -- with SA being held up as the exemplar @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ only to provoke unnecessary enmity from the US . But there is no question we should be unapologetic in looking to frame ourselves as staunch upholders and reformers of the existing multilateral system . In this respect we will have to , quite clearly , be a counterpoint to what the US administration is intent on collapsing . <p> The impending visit by Zelensky , and the opportunity to demonstrate greater support and solidarity for Ukraine , allows us an opportunity to repair somewhat our damaged international standing .
