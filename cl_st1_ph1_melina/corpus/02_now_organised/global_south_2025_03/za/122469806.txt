<p> Johannesburg -- South Africa considers improving its relationship with the United States a " priority " , President Cyril Ramaphosa said Monday after Washington expelled Pretoria 's ambassador . <p> Tensions between the two countries boiled over on Friday when US Secretary of State Marco Rubio posted on X that ambassador Ebrahim Rasool was " persona non grata " . <p> Rubio linked his post to an article from conservative news outlet Breitbart which wrote that , in an online seminar Friday , Rasool had described Trump 's Make America Great Again movement as " a white supremacist response to growing demographic diversity in the United States " . <p> Rubio called him " a race-baiting politician " who hates President Donald Trump . <p> " We have noted the displeasure that has been expressed by the United States , particularly about the remarks he made , " Ramaphosa told journalists , adding he was waiting for a " full report " from Rasool . <p> We have noted the displeasure of the US with regards to remarks made by Ambassador Rasool .
