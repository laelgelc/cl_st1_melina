<h> DA calls for urgent overhaul of SA 's foreign policy <p> The DA says that in light of rising tensions with the US , South Africa 's foreign policy must focus on driving economic growth and creating jobs rather than being influenced by the ANC 's historical alliances . <p> Photo : Twitter <p> They are calling for a review of foreign policy to ensure it prioritises the country 's domestic needs . <p> The DA 's Emma Louise Powell said the party believes the time has come for a more consensus-based approach to appointing ambassadors . <p> She said that the country 's embassies should be staffed by qualified professionals . <p> " The ANC , a 39% party , no longer enjoys an outright majority and is no longer at liberty to single-handedly determine South Africa 's foreign policy agenda without consulting its partners in government . <p> Section 231 ( 1 ) of the Constitution clearly states that the negotiation and signing of all international agreements are the responsibility of the National Executive , in other words , the multiparty Cabinet , established and regulated in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of Intent . " <p> The DA 's statement comes amid diplomatic tensions between South Africa and the US , which boiled over on Friday when US Secretary of State Marco Rubio posted on X that US Ambassador Ebrahim Rasool was " persona non grata " . <p> Rubio linked his post to an article from conservative news outlet Breitbart , which wrote that , in an online seminar Friday , Rasool had described Trump 's Make America Great Again movement as " a white supremacist response to growing demographic diversity in the United States " . <p> Rubio called him " a race-baiting politician " who hates President Donald Trump . <p> Rasool 's expulsion is the latest development in tensions between Washington and Pretoria over several policy disagreements , including South Africa 's case against Israel at the International Court of Justice over the war on Gaza . <p> President Cyril Ramaphosa said South Africa would seek to engage with the United States by dispatching envoys from the business community and government . <p> " This is our responsibility as a government to advance the interests of our @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ South Africa , " he said .
