<h> OPINION <h> UCT opts for suicide <p> William Saunderson-Meyer <p> 21 March 2025 <p> William Saunderson-Meyer writes on council 's extraordinary refusal to modify its anti-Israel resolutions <p> JAUNDICED EYE <p> The Nelson Mandela legacy is finally depleted . The ' Madiba magic ' that opened international doors and wallets , and froze criticism in its tracks , is no more . Truth is , Donald Trump killed Nelson Mandela . <p> The halo of righteousness under which the African National Congress swaggered on the world stage since 1994 lies shattered . Already for some time , international obsequiousness has been wearing thin -- even the right-on Joe Biden administration became impatient with South Africa 's anti-Western posturing -- but it was who Trump finally drew the curtain on perhaps the longest-running illusion in modern history . <p> The American president 's freezing of billions in aid to South Africa , with more sanctions looming , has a simple moral . Actions have consequences . <p> The United States accepts that it is our sovereign right to consort with the scum of the earth , to defend and befriend @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ every turn . Equally , the US will make its own cost-benefit analyses . Rightly or wrongly , cleverly or foolishly , it has decided that based on present behaviour , it 's ending what has been an extraordinary display of generosity towards South Africa . <p> This is a sea change that the ANC and its supporters need to get to grips with so that they stop making fools of themselves and South Africa . It 's slow at happening . Instead , there has been self-pity and anger -- much rending of garments and gnashing of teeth -- directed at the USA , Trump , Elon Musk , Afrikaners , AfriForum and every other scapegoat imaginable . <p> --&gt; <p> However , there has been no sign of adapting foreign policies , modifying contentious legislation or moving away from the race-based criteria that have become ubiquitous in South Africa . After all , if the Expropriation and BELA acts are indeed so benign in intention as their defenders claim , what possible objection could there be to scrapping or rewriting a couple of clauses that endanger your entire @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ On the contrary , there has been a defiant doubling down by the ANC . Its leaders WILL fete Iranian despots . The Budget WILL find further millions for anti-Israeli court actions . Cyril Ramaphosa WILL co-author an article in Foreign Policy accusing Israel of systematic genocide and Trump of ethnic cleansing . <p> That 's not unexpected behaviour . The ANC is notoriously hide-bound ideologically and hence resistant to change . Even if Ramaphosa wanted to make major adaptations and compromises , it likely could only be done at the cost of again splitting the party . <p> But far more extraordinary than ANC 's predictable actions is when an autonomous university chooses self-immolation in preference to rescinding a motion that puts it at odds with most of its staff and alumni , as well as a critical mass of the donors who have poured billions into making it the leading academic institution on the African continent . That is what 's playing out at the University of Cape Town ( UCT ) . <p> --&gt; <p> As I wrote earlier this year ( * ) , anti-Israeli sentiment @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ array of non-governmental organisations and among many of the staff and administrators at our universities . It 's well-orchestrated and implacable , totally indifferent to the collateral damage caused locally , often to their own institutions . <p> As was detailed in High Court papers filed in February by historical studies Professor Adam Mendelsohn , who is contesting the legality of a UCT Council ( UCTC ) resolution enforcing an academic boycott of Israel , the financial damage caused to the university is potentially astronomical . Mendelsohn charges the UCTC executive committee with deliberately keeping Council members in the dark about the fact that it had been warned that hundreds of millions of rands earmarked by two donors for UCT would not be forthcoming if the academic boycott resolution was passed . <p> Mendelsohn -- since suspended as head of department by UCT , that great citadel of free thought , for challenging them in court -- also cited an internal UCT risk assessment that concluded that any ' reasonably significant ' cut in the university 's contract research funding would have ' catastrophic consequences ' . Using 2018 figures @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ losing almost 30% of its contract research revenue , R330m a year , while the ' worst case ' would be double that . <p> Since then , the Trump 2 era has dawned . February 's USAID freeze has been followed by whispers last week that the US National Institutes of Health ( NIH ) is similarly about to cut research ties and funding . This would be calamitous not only for UCT , the largest single recipient of NIH funding outside North America , but also for the Medical Research Council , which would lose 28% of its scientific budget . <p> --&gt; <p> From a report tabled by UCT Vice-Chancellor Professor Mosa Moshabela at a heated UCTC meeting this past weekend , during which he outlined the damage caused by the anti-Israel resolutions , it transpires that the true situation is far worse than anyone comprehended . A leaked extract ( ** ) of Moshabela 's report , published on Politicsweb , details the potential carnage faced by UCT . <p> US federal agencies currently fund 155 projects at UCT through grants or sub-awards , of which @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this amount , R660m is budgeted as income for the current financial year . On top of this , if the funding is ended , there is an additional indirect cost to UCT of R204 million . <p> Most of these NIH grants are linked to UCT 's flagship Institute of Infectious Disease and Molecular Medicine and focus on HIV and TB research . Their loss places at risk critical research infrastructure , UCT 's global research ranking , and the substantial state subsidy that the university gets for every journal article published . <p> The loss of intellectual capital would be staggering . UCT estimates that the salaries of 475 academic and research support staff depend wholly or in part on NIHI funding . The NIH also supports 61 postgraduate students and 20 postdoctoral fellows . <p> --&gt; <p> Moshabela warns that the attrition of academic capacity will also have negative knock-on effects that will reduce the subsidy and fee income on which UCT is ' significantly reliant for its financial sustainability ' . There would be ' negative impacts ' on the university 's scientific visibility and its @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ membership of prestigious organisations like the International Association of Research Universities . <p> Columbia University has been hit by a US$400 million federal funding cut because of what the US government perceived as a failure to squelch antisemitism on campus . Moshabela reports that UCT was warned by the US Consulate that , similarly , its resolutions on Gaza risked ' triggering US sanctions targeted specifically at UCT ' . <p> The impact will be ' immense and sustained ' , said Moshabela . ' Beyond funding cuts , there is the possibility that individual UCT staff members may face direct sanction through ... visa denials or restricted participation in US activities . ' <p> All this academic carnage happens against the backdrop of an already parlous and deteriorating situation at UCT . <p> Fundraising has cratered . No retreat , no surrender .
