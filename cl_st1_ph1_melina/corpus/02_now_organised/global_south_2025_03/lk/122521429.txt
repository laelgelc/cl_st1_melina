<p> The attacks follow a deepening humanitarian crisis in Gaza , where Israel has blocked the entry of all aid and goods for more than two weeks . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu 's office said the attack was in response to Hamas 's " repeated refusal " to release remaining captives in Gaza , and the group 's failure to agree on ceasefire proposals from US presidential envoy Steve Witkoff and other the mediators . <p> The White House confirmed that President Trump was consulted ahead of the Israeli attack . <p> Hamas blasted " Netanyahu and his extremist government " for overturning the January 19 ceasefire and " exposing prisoners in Gaza to an unknown fate " . <p> Hamas has also called for the " free people of the world " to protest the devastating attacks on the Palestinian territory .
