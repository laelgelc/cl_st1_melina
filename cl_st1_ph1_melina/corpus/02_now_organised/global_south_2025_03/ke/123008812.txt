<p> Ranil calls for army doctors to be sent to Myanmar , set up medical campsPosted on March 31st , 2025 <h> Daily Mirror Online <p> Former President Ranil Wickremesinghe has urged the Sri Lankan government to dispatch a medical team , including military personnel , to Myanmar to assist those affected by the recent earthquake . <p> Issuing a special statement , Wickremesinghe emphasized Sri Lanka 's long-standing ties with Myanmar and stressed the importance of providing humanitarian relief . <p> My proposal to the government is to send a medical team , including doctors from the army 's medical corps , along with essential medicines and equipment , to set up a temporary hospital in Myanmar , " he said . <p> When there was an earthquake in Nepal , we deployed an army team and provided the second-largest aid package after India . Today , countries like India , Thailand , Malaysia , China , and even Australia -- despite being farther away -- have extended assistance to Myanmar . We must also take action , " he urged . <p> Wickremesinghe further suggested mobilizing medical supplies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stating , We can collect the necessary medicines and send them to Myanmar . This is about maintaining our regional ties and playing a responsible role in humanitarian aid " . <p> The former President also referenced Sri Lanka 's past contributions to global crises , highlighting a recent donation to Gaza . <p> Last year , I allocated one million dollars from Sri Lanka for Gaza .
