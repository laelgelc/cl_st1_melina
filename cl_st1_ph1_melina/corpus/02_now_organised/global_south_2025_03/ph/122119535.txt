<h> Slovenia opens embassy in the Philippines <p> A year ago , the Slovenian government decided to open its first embassy in the ASEAN region in Manila . Next week , Tanja Fajon , Slovenian Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Foreign and European Affairs , will pay an official visit to the Philippines and officially open the embassy . <p> The visit of Minister Fajon and the opening of the embassy herald enhanced cooperation in areas of common interest . We will promote stronger economic ties , more investment , trade growth and people-to-people contacts . We look forward to stronger cultural cooperation . <p> In this context , we congratulate the Philippines on its status as Guest of Honor at the 2025 Frankfurt Book Fair , a status Slovenia enjoyed two years ago , which we saw as a tribute to the nation 's entire culture , not just literature . We firmly believe that we could strengthen cooperation in science , R&amp;D and sport . <p> Basketball and Slovenian star Luka Doncic are very popular here .
