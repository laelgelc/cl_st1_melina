<h> What is the International Criminal Court , which had former Philippine President Duterte arrested ? <p> THE HAGUE , Netherlands -- The International Criminal Court ( ICC ) , where detained former Philippine President Rodrigo Duterte is being taken after his arrest in the Philippines , is a court of last resort for the most serious international offenses , such as genocide and crimes against humanity . <p> The 79-year-old former President has become the first former Asian leader to be arrested by the global court . <p> Duterte was expected to arrive Wednesday , March 12 , 2025 , in the Netherlands after his arrest the previous morning on an ICC warrant accusing him of crimes against humanity over deadly anti-drug crackdowns he oversaw while in office . <p> What is the International Criminal Court ? <p> The ICC handles allegations of war crimes , crimes against humanity , genocide and aggression , and takes on cases when nations are unable or unwilling to prosecute crimes on their territory . <p> The court 's newest member , Ukraine , formally joined in January , bringing the number of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ States , Russia and China -- are among nations that are not members . <p> The ICC , which has a budget this year of more than 195 million euros ( $213 million ) , does not have a police force and has to rely on the cooperation of states to arrest suspects . <p> Judges at the court have issued 60 arrest warrants and convicted 11 people .
