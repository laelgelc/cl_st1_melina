<p> GENEVA , Switzerland -- The UN Human Rights office accused Israel on Friday , March 28 , of violating international law by forcibly displacing Palestinians in Gaza under " mandatory evacuation orders . " <p> The Israeli army has issued what the UN describes as 10 mandatory evacuation orders , covering large areas across Gaza , since it resumed its war against Hamas on March 18 , breaking a two-month-old ceasefire amid rows over terms for extending it . <p> " These evacuations fail to comply with the requirements of international humanitarian law , " UN human rights spokesperson Thameen Al-Kheetan said in a statement on Friday . <p> Israel 's mission to the UN in Geneva did not immediately respond to a request for comment . Israel has previously denied violating humanitarian law in Gaza , blaming Hamas militants for harm to civilians by operating among them . Hamas denies this . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former defense minister have been indicted alongside Hamas leaders by the International Criminal Court in The Hague on war crimes charges , which Israel rejects . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the evacuated population , nor ensure that these evacuations are conducted in satisfactory conditions of hygiene , health , safety and nutrition , " Al-Kheetan 's statement added . <p> Over half of northern Gaza appears to be under such orders , it said , while those who have been newly displaced from the south of the enclave in the Rafah area and forced to go to coastal Al Mawasi were not guaranteed safety there . <p> " We are deeply concerned about the shrinking space for civilians in Gaza who are being forcibly displaced by the Israeli army from large swathes of territory , " it added . <p> Since Israeli airstrikes resumed on March 18 , at least 855 Palestinians have been killed and 1,869 injured , according to the UN , which cited figures from the Hamas-run Gaza health ministry . -- Rappler.com
