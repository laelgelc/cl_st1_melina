<h> US cuts $377 million in UNFPA funding , threatening lifesaving support for women and girls <p> The United States has cut $377 million in funding to the UN reproductive and sexual health agency , United Nations Population Fund ( UNFPA ) , potentially leading to " devastating impacts " on women and girls . <p> The United Nations Population Fund ( UNFPA ) stated on Friday that the cuts would end lifesaving support for women and girls . <p> Nearly all of UNFPA 's grants ( 48 as of now ) with USAID and the U.S. State Department have been terminated , the organization was informed on Wednesday . <h> RelatedStories <p> The USAID grants were designated to provide critical maternal healthcare , protection from violence , rape treatment , and other lifesaving care in humanitarian settings . <p> This included UNFPA 's efforts to prevent maternal deaths , ensure safe childbirth , and address the horrific violence faced by women and girls in crisis zones such as Gaza , Sudan , and Ukraine . <p>
