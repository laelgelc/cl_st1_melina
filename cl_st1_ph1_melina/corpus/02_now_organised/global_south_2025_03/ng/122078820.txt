<h> Trump cancels $400m grants for Columbia University over antisemitism allegation <h> Trump cancels $400m grants for Columbia University over antisemitism allegation <p> President Donald Trump of the United States on Friday cancelled grants and contracts totalling $400 million to Columbia University in New York City due to its inaction in addressing the persistent harassment of Jewish students . <p> According to the General Services Administration , in a joint statement with the Department of Justice , the Department of Education , and the Department of Health and Human Services , the cancelled funds would be deducted from the $5 billion in grants committed to Columbia . <p> Columbia has been at the centre of campus protests , with demonstrators calling for an end to US support for Israel in response to civilian deaths and the humanitarian crisis caused by Israel 's assault on Gaza . <p> There have been allegations of both antisemitism and Islamophobia in protests and counter-protests . <p> Columbia has previously stated that it has made efforts to tackle antisemitism . <p> " Cancelling these taxpayer funds is our strongest signal yet that the federal government @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ like Columbia that does not protect Jewish students and staff , " said Leo Terrell , who leads the Justice Department 's antisemitism task force .
