<h> Israeli attack on Beirut blatant violation of ceasefire -- Iran <h> Israeli attack on Beirut blatant violation of ceasefire -- Iran <p> Iran has condemned the Israeli strikes on Beirut , calling them a " blatant violation " of the ceasefire . <p> Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei made this known in a statement on Saturday . <p> " The excuses made by the Zionist regime for justifying the aggression against Lebanon are totally baseless and unjustifiable , " Baqaei said . <p> He also called on the international community to " make decisive plans to counter the occupying regime 's constant law-breaking in the region -- from Gaza to Lebanon and Syria -- and the regime 's growing threats against international peace and security . " <p> The Israeli military carried out several airstrikes on southern Beirut on Friday after a rocket attack was launched at Israel from southern Lebanon . <p> Hezbollah has denied involvement in the incident and has not responded to the Israeli assaults .
