<h> India expresses concern over situation in Gaza , calls for release of all hostages <p> India on Wednesday expressed deep concern over the situation in Gaza after Israel 's fresh attack left over 400 people dead . <p> In a statement , the Ministry of External Affairs ( MEA ) said , " We are concerned at the situation in Gaza . It is important that all hostages are released . We also call for the supply of humanitarian assistance to the people of Gaza to be sustained . " <p> India 's comments came as Israel resumed air strikes in Gaza on Tuesday , triggering uncertainty over the fragile ceasefire that it sealed with Hamas in January . <p> The Enforcement Directorate ( ED ) will question RJD chief Lalu Prasad Yadav in Patna over the land-for-jobs scam . The probe centers on alleged railway job allocations in exchange for cash during his tenure as Railway Minister ( 2004 -- 2009 ) . ED will also investigate properties acquired by the Yadav family in Delhi and Bihar . This follows the recent questioning of Rabri Devi and Tej Pratap Yadav.
