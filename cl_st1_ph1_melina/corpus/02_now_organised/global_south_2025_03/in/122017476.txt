<h> Why subsume the Jaintia identity ? <p> Editor , The ethnic identity of the Jaintias has been under question since the 1950s . This time it has been debated in the ongoing Budget Session of the Meghalaya Legislative Assembly . The MLA of Mawlai initiated the debate during the Short Duration Discussion on the statements made by Cabinet Minister Kyrmen Shylla on the proposed construction of the Railways in Jaintia Hills . Surprisingly while participating in the debate , the Nongkrem Legislator misrepresents the Minister 's statement to advance his agenda , the agenda of ' cultural assimilation ' . Today , the people of ' Ka Ri Khadar Dolloi ' have accepted and are proud of the term Jaintia as the generic name for the Bhois , the Biates , the Hadems , the Pnars and the Wars who are the present inhabitants of Jaintia Hills . So do n't start the process of assimilating ethnic identities .
