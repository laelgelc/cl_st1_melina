<p> The ongoing conflict in Gaza continues to escalate , with Israeli airstrikes killing at least 51 people in the last 24 hours . Among those killed was Ismail Barhoum , a member of Hamas ' political wing , who died in a strike on Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis where he was being treated . <p> In Lebanon , Israeli strikes killed at least eight people following an alleged rocket attack on Israel , which Hezbollah has denied . These actions come amid continued Israeli operations in Lebanon , despite a ceasefire agreement reached with Hezbollah four months ago . <p> Gaza 's Health Ministry reports that over 50,000 Palestinians have been killed in the ongoing war , although experts suggest this is likely an undercount due to the collapse of Gaza 's health system and the large number of people trapped under rubble . Meanwhile , the U.S. has carried out new strikes on Yemen , where the Houthi rebel group continues to target ships in an effort to pressure Israel to end its actions in Gaza .
