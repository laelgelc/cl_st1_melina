<h> India voices concern over Gaza situation , calls for humanitarian aid <p> New Delhi : India on Wednesday said it is concerned over the situation in Gaza and called for supply of humanitarian assistance to people of the conflict-hit Palestinian territory . India 's comments came as Israel resumed air strikes in Gaza on Tuesday triggering uncertainty over the fragile ceasefire that it sealed with Hamas in January . <p> According to the Hamas-run health ministry , over 400 people were killed in the Israeli bombing . " We are concerned at the situation in Gaza . It is important that all hostages are released , " the Ministry of External Affairs ( MEA ) said . " We also call for the supply of humanitarian assistance to the people of Gaza to be sustained , " it said in a statement .
