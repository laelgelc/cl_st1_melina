The Trump administration 's geopolitical outlook signals such a fundamental change . <p> There is no doubt that the first of those conflicts whose winding down will trigger more positives for the world is the Ukraine war , now into its third year . However , the US President has correctly focused on Ukraine first . West Asian affairs involving Gaza , Iran , Syria and Israel are parts of a larger legacy issue .
