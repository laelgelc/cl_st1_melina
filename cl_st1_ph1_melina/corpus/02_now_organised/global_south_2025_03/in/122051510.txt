<p> US President Donald Trump issued a warning on Wednesday regarding the potential for increased devastation in Gaza if all remaining hostages are not released . He also urged Hamas leaders to escape . Firmly supporting Israel amid a precarious ceasefire , Trump stated that he is " providing Israel with all necessary resources to complete the mission , " as his administration accelerates the delivery of billions of dollars in military aid . He emphasized on his social media platform , " Release all hostages immediately , and return the bodies of those you have killed , or it will be the end for you , " following his meeting with the recently freed hostages . <p> This serves as your final notice . For the leadership , the moment to depart Gaza is now , while the opportunity still exists . Trump also emphasized that there would be consequences for Gaza as a whole , where nearly the entire population has been displaced due to Israel 's unyielding military operations in response to the attacks by Hamas on October 7 , 2023 . <p> " To the People @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it will not materialize if you continue to hold hostages . If you persist , your fate is sealed ! " His remarks come in the wake of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's warning of " unimaginable consequences " should Hamas fail to release the remaining hostages taken during the October 7 incident . <p> The initial phase of the ceasefire concluded over the weekend , following six weeks of relative tranquility characterized by the exchange of Israeli hostages for Palestinian prisoners incarcerated in Israeli facilities .
