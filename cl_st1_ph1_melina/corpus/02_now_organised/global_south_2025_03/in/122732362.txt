<p> Israel 's military launched a strike on Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis , the largest medical facility in southern Gaza on Sunday night , killing at least two people and causing a major fire , as reported by Associated Press . The attack took place shortly when the hospital had already been full of casualties after a sudden wave of Israeli airstrikes earlier in the week . Victims included Hamas political bureau member Ismail Barhoum and a 16-year-old post-surgical patient who was being treated at the facility , according to Gaza 's health ministry . <h> Deadly attack on Nasser Hospital <p> Israel 's ministry has acknowledged the strike , stating it targeted a Hamas militant hiding inside the hospital premises . Officials have restated that civilian harm is the result of Hamas operating in densely populated areas . Similar to many other healthcare centres in Gaza , Nasser Hospital has faced repeated damage during the war , limiting access to critical assistance . <p> According to the Gaza Health Ministry , more than 50,000 Palestinians have been killed so far , including more than 15,600 children .
