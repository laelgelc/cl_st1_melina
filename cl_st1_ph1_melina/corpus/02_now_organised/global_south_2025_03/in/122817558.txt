<h> Israel orders evacuation of parts of Gaza City <p> JERUSALEM , Mar 26 : The Israeli military has ordered the evacuation of parts of Gaza City as it steps up its renewed offensive against Hamas . <p> The latest orders issued Wednesday apply to Zeitoun , Tel al-Hawa and other neighbourhoods where Israeli forces have carried out previous operations during the 17-month war . The military said it will soon respond to rocket fire from the area and ordered residents to move south . <p> Israel ended its ceasefire with Hamas last week when it launched a surprise wave of strikes that killed hundreds of Palestinians . <p> Israel has vowed to increase military pressure until the militants return the remaining 59 hostages -- 24 of whom are believed to be alive . Israel has also demanded that Hamas disarm and send its leaders into exile . <p> Hamas has said it will not release the remaining hostages without a lasting ceasefire and full Israeli withdrawal from Gaza . <p> The war was triggered by Hamas ' October 7 , 2023 , attack into Israel , in which Palestinian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ abducted 251 . <p> Israel 's retaliatory offensive has killed over 50,000 people , according to Gaza 's Health Ministry , which does not say how many were civilians or combatants .
