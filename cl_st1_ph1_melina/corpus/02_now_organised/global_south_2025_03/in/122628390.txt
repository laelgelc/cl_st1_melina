<h> Israel strikes military sites in east , south Lebanon <h> Four missiles were fired in the Nabatiyeh area of southern Lebanon <p> An Israeli army Merkava main battle tank deploys at a position in northern Israel along the border with southern Lebanon . ( Photo by AFP ) <p> Jerusalem : Israel 's military said it struck military sites in east and south Lebanon on Thursday , in its latest attack despite a November ceasefire that ended a war against militant group Hezbollah . <p> " A short while ago , the IDF ( military ) struck a military site containing an underground terrorist infrastructure site in the Bekaa area in Lebanon , as well as a military site containing rocket launchers in southern Lebanon in which Hezbollah activity has been identified , " the military said in a statement . <p> Lebanese state media on Thursday reported Israeli strikes on the country 's south and east . <p> The state-run National News Agency said " enemy aircraft " struck " the eastern slopes of the mountain range within the town of Janta in the Bekaa , " as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , west of Baalbek " , also in the east . <p> Four missiles were fired in the Nabatiyeh area of southern Lebanon , NNA said . <p> No casualties were immediately reported . <p> The November 27 truce largely halted more than a year of fighting between Iran-backed Hezbollah and Israel , including two months of open war during which Israel sent in ground troops . <p> Hezbollah had said it was acting in support of Hamas militants fighting Israel in Gaza . <p> Israel has continued to carry out strikes on Lebanese territory since the truce agreement took effect . <p> Under the agreement , Israel had been expected to withdraw from Lebanon by February 18 after missing a January deadline , but it has kept troops at five locations it deems " strategic " . <p> The ceasefire also required Hezbollah to pull back north of the Litani River , about 30 kilometres ( 20 miles ) from the border , and to dismantle any remaining military infrastructure in the south .
