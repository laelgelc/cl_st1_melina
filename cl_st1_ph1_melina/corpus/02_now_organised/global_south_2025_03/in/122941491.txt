<p> The Mahatma -- champion of non-violence , father of a nation . But what if we told you that his final days were spent in disillusionment , surrounded by the very bloodshed he fought his whole life to prevent ? That his philosophy , tested against the fires of Partition , was reduced to a whisper ?
