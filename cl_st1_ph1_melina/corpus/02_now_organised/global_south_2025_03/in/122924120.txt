Israel 's military warned residents to evacuate parts of Beirut ahead of the strike , claiming that Hezbollah uses civilians as human shields , the report stated . <p> Hezbollah has denied launching rocket attacks on northern Israel and accused the country of seeking a pretext for continued strikes on the Lebanese territory .
