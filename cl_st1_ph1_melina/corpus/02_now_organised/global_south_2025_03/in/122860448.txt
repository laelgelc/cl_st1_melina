<h> Senator Bernie Sanders plans to introduce votes blocking $8.8 billion in arms sales to Israel due to the humanitarian crisis in Gaza . Despite low chances of passing , the move seeks to pressure American and Israeli governments to protect civilians amid escalating violence . <p> U.S. Senator Bernie Sanders has announced a bold plan to push for votes next week aimed at halting arms sales to Israel , valued at $8.8 billion , due to the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Gaza . The senator accused Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu of violating U.S. and international law during the conflict . <p> According to Palestinian officials , the crisis has resulted in over 50,000 Palestinian deaths and left much of Gaza in ruins . Despite bipartisan Congressional support for Israel historically making it difficult to pass such resolutions , Sanders ' initiative aims to spur discussions on civilian protection . <p> In a related development , former President Donald Trump recently bypassed Congressional oversight to approve military sales to Israel , while Senator Sanders and other progressives criticize the U.S. administration 's limited response to alleviate Palestinian suffering .
