<h> Egypt Proposes New Plan to Restore Gaza Ceasefire Deal <h> Egypt proposes a new Gaza ceasefire plan , with Hamas releasing hostages and Israel agreeing to withdraw . Both the US and Hamas have agreed , but Israel has not responded . <p> Egypt has put forward a new proposal to restore the Gaza ceasefire deal , following a spike in violence after Israel resumed air and ground operations against Hamas last week . The plan , which was agreed upon by both Hamas and the United States , suggests that Hamas release five Israeli hostages each week . In return , Israel would implement the second phase of the ceasefire after the first week . <p> The Egyptian proposal also includes a timeline for the full release of all hostages , with Israel agreeing to a complete withdrawal from Gaza , supported by US guarantees .
