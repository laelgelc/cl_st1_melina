<h> India concerned over situation in Gaza ; calls for humanitarian aid to affected people <p> New Delhi : India has expressed concern about the situation in Gaza following Israel 's resumption of bombing in the Palestinian territory , which has broken the fragile ceasefire with Hamas . India called for humanitarian aid to be provided to people affected by the conflict and emphasised the importance of releasing all hostages currently held by Hamas . <p> " We are concerned at the situation in Gaza . It is important that all hostages are released , " the Ministry of External Affairs ( MEA ) said . <p> " We also call for the supply of humanitarian assistance to the people of Gaza to be sustained , " it said in a statement . <p> India 's comments came as Israel pounded Hamas targets in Gaza on Tuesday , triggering uncertainty over the ceasefire that came into effect on January 19 . <p> According to the Hamas-run health ministry , over 400 people were killed in the Israeli bombing .
