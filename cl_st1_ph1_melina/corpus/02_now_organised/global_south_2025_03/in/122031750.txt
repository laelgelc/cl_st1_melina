<h> US holds direct talks with Hamas , Trump issues ultimatum , ' release hostages or you are dead ' <p> ET Online 06 Mar 2025 , 02:05 PM IST <p> US President Trump issued a final ultimatum to Hamas on March 5 , 2025 , threatening further destruction of Gaza if all hostages are not released . Strongly backing Israel , he stated that the US would provide all necessary support to complete the mission . Trump warned Hamas leaders to flee Gaza now while they still can and demanded the immediate release of hostages and return of the bodies of the murdered victims . He also warned of serious repercussions for Gaza , highlighting the ongoing displacement caused by Israel 's military actions . Trump urged the people of Gaza to release the hostages for a chance at a brighter future or face deadly consequences .
