<p> The White House has released footage of former President Donald Trump meeting with eight freed Gaza hostages , who shared their harrowing experiences and expressed deep gratitude for his efforts in securing their release . During the emotional meeting , they thanked him for pushing to bring them and others still in captivity home . Trump strongly condemned Hamas , calling their actions a " reign of terror " that must be stopped . Shortly after the meeting , he posted on X , urging Israel to " finish the job " and issued a final warning to Hamas , demanding the immediate release of all remaining hostages . Calling the crisis " sick and twisted , " he slammed Hamas for holding onto the bodies of deceased victims . Israeli officials report that at least 24 hostages , including American citizen Edan Alexander , are still alive in Gaza , while the bodies of 35 others remain in Hamas ' custody .
