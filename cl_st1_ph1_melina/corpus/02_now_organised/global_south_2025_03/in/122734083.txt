<p> Gaza : The Israeli precision airstrike in Gaza has reportedly killed a Hamas leader , also termed as the newly appointed Prime Minister of Hamas . The hospital strike killed two people , including the 16-year-old boy who had undergone surgery two days ago , the territory 's Hamas-run Health Ministry said .
