The ice cream man has promised <p> Short Bio : Batool Abu Akleen is a Palestinian poet and painter whose work transcends borders . At 15 , she won the Barjeel Prize for her poignant poem I Did n't Steal the Cloud . Displaced from Gaza City after October 7 , 2023 , Batool continues to write and share her powerful poetry , reflecting resilience and displacement . Her poetry has been translated into multiple languages , including Italian and English , and has been published in renowned magazines worldwide . Currently , she serves as the Poet in Residence with Modern Poetry in Translation ( MPT ) . Those poems are part of her forthcoming poetry collection , to be published next year by Tenement Press , England .
