<p> The US Air Force 's F-35 fighter jets emit as much CO2 as an average UK petrol car does in one year for every 100 nautical miles flown . <p> With 750 bases worldwide , the US military 's emissions are the largest , rivalling the entire annual output of smaller nations like Portugal and Denmark . <p> Although India is among the top three emitters of greenhouse gases , its emission index per military person is low . <p> While the G20 countries are jointly responsible for 80 percent of global emissions , South Asia with 25 percent of the global population is responsible for nine to 10 percent with India alone accounting for seven percent . Bangladesh and Pakistan have been collectively responsible for less than 1.5 percent . <p> Despite having the largest armed forces in the world , South Asia is responsible for marginal climate damages due to military operations . <p> South Asia is in a unique position to claim its rightful reparation from the rich world for implementing UN-mandated Climate Action goals .
