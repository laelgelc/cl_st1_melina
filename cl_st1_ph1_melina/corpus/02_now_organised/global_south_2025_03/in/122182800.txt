Trump 's gratitude towards Pakistan for apprehending Islamic State -- Khorasan Province terrorist Sharifullah -- involved in the 2021 Kabul airport attack that killed 13 American soldiers and dozens of Afghan civilians -- is seen as recognition of the Pakistani military 's role in combating terrorism . <p> The excitement comes from the rare acknowledgement in a speech where Trump offered few kind words to other allies , including India and the European Union .
