<h> Pope calls parish in Gaza as his condition remains stable on Ash Wednesday <p> As his clinical condition remained stable with no new respiratory episodes , Pope Francis called the Catholic parish in Gaza and carried out several work activities , according to the Holy See Press Office on Wednesday evening . <p> " The Holy Father increased his respiratory and active motor physiotherapy . He spent the day in his armchair . <p> " Given the complexity of the clinical situation , the prognosis remains guarded . <p> " This morning , in the private apartment located on the 10th floor , the Holy Father participated in the rite of the blessing of the Sacred Ashes , which were imposed on him by the celebrant . He then received the Eucharist . <p> " Afterwards , he engaged in several work activities . During the morning , he also called Father Gabriel Romanelli , the parish priest of the Holy Family Church in Gaza . <p> " In the afternoon , he alternated between rest and work .
