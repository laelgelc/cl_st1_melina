Most buildings in the densely populated area have been reduced to rubble and much of the population has moved multiple times to escape the conflict . <p> " Out , out , out , Hamas get out , " chanted those seen in one of the posts published on X , apparently from the Beit Lahiya region of Gaza yesterday . It showed people marching down a dusty street between war-damaged buildings . <p> The posts began circulating widely late yesterday .
