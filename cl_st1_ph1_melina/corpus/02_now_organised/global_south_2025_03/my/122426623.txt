<h> UNRWA opens 130 temporary education centres in Gaza <p> RAMALLAH : The United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees ( UNRWA ) announced that it has opened 130 temporary education centres in Gaza , enabling direct learning for approximately 47,000 children across the Gaza Strip , the Palestine News and Info Agency ( WAFA ) reported . <p> This came in a statement posted by UNRWA Commissioner-General Philippe Lazzarini on his X account on Saturday , highlighting the agency 's role in providing direct learning services to children in Gaza , who have faced killing and displacement over nearly 16 months of genocidal warfare perpetrated by Israel . <p> Lazzarini emphasised that UNRWA remains the largest provider of emergency learning and psychosocial support services in Gaza . <p> He stressed that " education restores some hope to the children of Gaza , helping them recover , " noting its role in overcoming " the unspeakable traumas that children are experiencing .
