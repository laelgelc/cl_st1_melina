<p> Fahmi says security agencies will monitor the movements of all 15 Palestinian nationals , and they will be subject to strict conditions regarding their activities in Malaysia . -- Bernama photo <p> PUTRAJAYA ( March 19 ) : The government will impose strict monitoring and stringent security conditions on the 15 Palestinian nationals who will be relocated to the country . <p> MADANI government spokesperson Datuk Fahmi Fadzil said Foreign Minister Datuk Seri Mohamad Hasan had informed the Cabinet of the matter during today 's meeting . <p> According to Fahmi , security agencies will monitor the movements of all 15 Palestinian nationals , and they will be subject to strict conditions regarding their activities in Malaysia . <p> " They are not here ( Malaysia ) yet , but the review process on various aspects is underway , " he said at the weekly press conference today . <p> Fahmi explained that the process of bringing in the 15 individuals aligns with the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) decision to relocate 380 Palestinians- formerly detained by Israel-to several member countries . <p> " Around 380 detainees @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Therefore , several countries , such as Qatar and Turkiye , agreed to take in 100 Palestinian nationals each , while Malaysia accepted 15 , " he said . <p> Yesterday , Mohamad said Malaysia was ready to accept 15 former Palestinian detainees released by Israel as part of the conditions for the Gaza ceasefire agreement . <p> He said Malaysia has long participated in and supported Palestine 's struggle for independence , and this decision is seen as appropriate without placing any burden on the country . <p> According to Mohamad , all 15 individuals have been detained by Israel for 20 to 30 years without trial . -- Bernama
