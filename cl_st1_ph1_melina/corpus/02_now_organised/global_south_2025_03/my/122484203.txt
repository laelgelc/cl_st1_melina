<p> KUALA LUMPUR , March 17 -- Eight humanitarian workers from the Malaysian Consultative Council of Islamic Organizations ( Mapim ) were killed in an Israeli airstrike in Beit Lahiya , northern Gaza , on Saturday . <p> According to an Astro Awani report , the attack occurred around 1:30 pm local time as the aid workers were setting up tents for displaced families affected by the ongoing conflict . <p> This attack on humanitarian workers is a heinous war crime , " said Mapim chief activist Datuk Sani Araby in a statement condemning the strike . <p> Mapim then urged Malaysians to perform Qunut Nazilah prayers for the safety of those in Gaza . <p> Sani said that Mapim and its partner , Al-Khair Foundation , were deeply saddened by the deaths , which include three media personnel documenting aid efforts .
