Multilateral systems , including the United Nations , are faltering as geopolitical rivalries fuel a " might-makes-right " landscape . Meanwhile , autocratic leaders and tech oligarchs exploit economic inequality to undermine democratic norms , criminalize dissent , and target climate activists , Indigenous communities , and LGBTQ+ rights defenders . <p> The report calls for sweeping reforms to address systemic flaws . Key proposals include overhauling the UN Security Council to curb veto powers , enacting a global wealth tax on the ultra-rich , and banning fossil fuel lobbyists from climate talks .
