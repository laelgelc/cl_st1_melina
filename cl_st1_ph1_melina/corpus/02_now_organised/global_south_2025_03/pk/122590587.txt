<p> Miami Beach Mayor Steven Meiner said on Wednesday he dropped his threat to evict an art house cinema from city property for screening Oscar-winning No Other Land , a film about the Israeli displacement of Palestinians in the Israeli-occupied West Bank . <p> The move came after multiple rights advocates and artists criticised Meiner 's threat and said it would violate free speech . <p> Meiner said earlier he sought to evict and halt future grant payments to the non-profit O Cinema in South Beach .
