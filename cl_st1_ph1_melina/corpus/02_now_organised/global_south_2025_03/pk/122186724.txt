<h> Israel set to cut off power supply to Gaza <p> JERUSALEM - Israel has threatened to cut off its electricity supply to Gaza . The full effects of that are not immediately clear , but the territory 's desalination plants receive power for producing drinking water . Sunday 's announcement comes a week after Israel cut off all supplies of goods to the territory to over two million people . It has sought to press Hamas to accept an extension of the first phase of their ceasefire . That phase ended last weekend . Israel wants Hamas to release half of the remaining hostages in return for a promise to negotiate a lasting truce . <p> Hamas has pressed to start negotiations on the ceasefire 's more difficult second phase instead . Hamas yesterday said it wrapped up the latest round of ceasefire talks with Egyptian mediators without changes to its position , calling for an immediate start of the ceasefire 's second phase . The new letter from Israel 's energy minister to the Israel Electric Corporation tells it to stop selling power to Gaza .
