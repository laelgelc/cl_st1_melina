<h> 404 Palestinians killed as Israel resumes genocide in Gaza <p> At least 404 Palestinians were killed and 562 others injured in Israeli airstrikes in the Gaza Strip on Tuesday , breaking a ceasefire that lasted nearly two months , the Health Ministry said . <p> " Many victims are still under the rubble and efforts are underway to recover them , " the ministry added in a statement . <p> The Israeli army said early Tuesday that it conducted airstrikes in the Gaza Strip , the largest since the ceasefire with the Palestinian group Hamas took effect on Jan. 19 . <p> The Gaza Government Media Office said entire families were among the victims , who were killed together in the Israeli attacks , noting that ambulances and civil defense teams are unable to bring all victims to hospitals .
