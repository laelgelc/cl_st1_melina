<h> Gaza is Bleeding While Muslims Feast <p> We are witnessing the barbaric Israeli bombardment of Gaza , where innocent people are being killed and displaced once again . The same tragedy is unfolding in Lebanon and Yemen . Yet , in stark contrast , the Muslim world carries on with lavish iftar and sehri feasts -- camel meat in the Arab world and even ostrich meat in Karachi and other cities . <p> How is this Ramadan not compromised when our fellow Muslims are starving , suffering , and dying ? While the oppressed struggle for survival , Muslim leaders hold conferences that yield no meaningful outcomes . In reality , these rulers bear responsibility for the bloodshed of their Muslim brethren . It is time to reflect on our priorities and take concrete action to support those in need . The suffering in Gaza and other conflict zones can not be ignored while the rest of the Muslim world indulges in excess .
