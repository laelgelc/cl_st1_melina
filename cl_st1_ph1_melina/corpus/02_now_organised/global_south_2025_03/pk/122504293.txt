<p> The Houthis , who have targeted Israel before , condemned the resumption of strikes on Gaza on Tuesday , vowing escalation in support of Palestinians after threatening earlier this month to attack Israeli ships in the Red Sea . <p> The wave of Israeli strikes in Gaza , by far the deadliest since the truce took effect , killed more than 400 people , according to the health ministry in Hamas-run territory . <p> The Houthis ' supreme political council condemned in a statement " the Zionist enemy 's resumption of aggression against the Gaza Strip " . <p> " The Palestinian people will not be left alone in this battle , and Yemen will continue its support and assistance , and escalate confrontation steps , " it added . <p> The Houthis , part of Iran 's " axis of resistance " against the United States and Israel , have launched scores of drone and missile attacks on passing ships and Israeli territory during the Gaza war , claiming solidarity with the Palestinians .
