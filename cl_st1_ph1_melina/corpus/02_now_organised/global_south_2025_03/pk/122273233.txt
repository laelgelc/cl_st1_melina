<p> Pakistan has welcomed the EU 's support to the Arab and OIC plan for reconstruction of Gaza and peace in Palestine based on the Two-State solution . <p> In a statement at the Security Council meeting on the EU 's engagement with the UN , Pakistan 's Ambassador to the UN Munir Akram lauded the EU for its humanitarian support to the suffering people of Gaza and the West Bank , as well as its support to UNRWA . <p> Munir Akram recalled Pakistan 's excellent relations with the EU and said that Pakistan and the EU enjoy a robust , comprehensive and wide-ranging partnership . He stated that the EU is Pakistan 's largest trade and investment partner . <p> He noted that the GSP plus has played an important role in the growth of Pakistan-EU trade and served as a win-win model for economic cooperation .
