<h> Fake Deal with Gazans <p> Israel has aggressively resumed the second phase of its war on Gaza , targeting civilians with relentless attacks . This is despite the principles outlined in the UN Charter , which call for maintaining global peace and preventing future conflicts . <p> It is disheartening to see the silence of Muslim countries , especially the Arab world , which remains a passive observer . They watch the suffering of Gazans as if it were a spectacle , indifferent to the human tragedy unfolding before their eyes . <p> The mothers , fathers , and children of Gaza urgently plead for the world to stop Israel from escalating this brutal war . The tragedy they endure today echoes the horrors of the Holocaust under the Nazis , during which six million people were killed . History will remember these crimes against the people of Gaza if the world remains silent . The international community must act decisively to prevent further bloodshed . Gazans will never forget and will always be grateful to any nation that stands in their defence against Israeli aggression .
