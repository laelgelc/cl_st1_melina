<h> Why Trump ? <p> By News Desk <p> March 16 , 2025 <p> This letter refers to the news report ' Hundreds , potentially Trump , nominated for Nobel Peace Prize this year ' ( March 6 , 2025 ) . How has Trump been nominated ? In light of his plans or threats to remove and relocate the entire population of Gaza , basically an open call for ethnic cleansing , this can only be considered a blow to the prize 's integrity . How can Palestinians be forcibly ousted from their own land ? Almost all other world leaders are against this plan . I hope the Nobel Peace Prize committee will reject Trump 's nomination for this coveted prize .
