<h> US travel ban looms if Pakistan does n't address ' deficiencies ' <p> Donald Trump 's administration in Washington is considering issuing sweeping travel restrictions for the citizens of dozens of countries as part of a new ban and Pakistan has been placed in a group that would be considered for a partial suspension of visa issuance if their governments " do not make efforts to address deficiencies within 60 days " , according to sources familiar with the matter and an internal memo seen by Reuters . <p> The memo lists a total of 41 countries divided into three separate groups . <p> A US official speaking on the condition of anonymity cautioned there could be changes on the list and that it was yet to be approved by the administration , including US Secretary of State Marco Rubio . <p> The New York Times first reported on the list of countries . <p> The move harkens back to President Donald Trump 's first term ban on travelers from seven majority-Muslim nations , a policy that went through several iterations before it was upheld by the Supreme Court @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ January 20 requiring intensified security vetting of any foreigners seeking admission to the U.S. to detect national security threats . <p> That order directed several cabinet members to submit by March 21 a list of countries from which travel should be partly or fully suspended because their " vetting and screening information is so deficient " . <p> Trump 's directive is part of an immigration crackdown that he launched at the start of his second term . <p> He previewed his plan in an October 2023 speech , pledging to restrict people from the Gaza Strip , Libya , Somalia , Syria , Yemen and " anywhere else that threatens our security . " <p> The State Department did not immediately respond to a request for comment from Reuters .
