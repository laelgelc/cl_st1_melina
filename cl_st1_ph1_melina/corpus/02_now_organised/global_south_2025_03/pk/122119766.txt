<h> Absurd and unethical <p> By News Desk <p> March 08 , 2025 <p> The world has categorically rejected and condemned US President Donald Trump 's plan to take over Gaza and forcibly displace Palestinian people to neighbouring countries from the coastal territory . Any plan to forcibly deport Palestinians against their will is a war crime and , when committed as a part of a widespread and systematic attack on the civilian population , it would constitute a crime against humanity . Most world leaders consider this proposal and plan unlawful , appalling , and absurd . Taking control of Gaza for the sake of redeveloping it through forced displacement of Palestinians from their homeland is tantamount to ethnic cleansing . Using the language of threats and intimidation against Palestinians will not serve the cause of durable peace in the region . The stability and resolution of the Israel-Palestine conflict in the Middle East lies in the establishment of two states .
