<h> PM Shehbaz calls for unity to promote lasting peace in Gaza , Ukraine <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has emphasized the need for collective efforts to build lasting peace in Gaza and Ukraine . <p> He was talking to High Commissioner of the United Kingdom Ms. Jane Marriott , who paid a courtesy call on him in Islamabad today . <p> The Prime Minister conveyed his greetings to King Charles III and wished him a quick recovery to full health . He also reiterated his invitation to King Charles III for undertaking a Royal visit to Pakistan at his earliest convenience . <p> The Prime Minister also expressed satisfaction at the progress made in bilateral ties between the two countries . <p> The High Commissioner said the UK is committed to strengthening its ties with Pakistan and to continue partnering in the country 's national progress and development .
