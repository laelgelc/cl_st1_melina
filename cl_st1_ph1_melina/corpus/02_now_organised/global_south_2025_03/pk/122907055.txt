<p> President Donald Trump hosted his first Ramadan iftar dinner of his second term at the White House on March 28 , 2025 . During the event , he expressed gratitude to the Muslim American community for their support in the 2024 election . Trump acknowledged the " hundreds of thousands " of Muslim Americans who helped him win last year . <p> Trump also used the occasion to emphasize his administration 's commitment to peace in the Middle East . He highlighted the success of the Abraham Accords , which normalized relations between Israel and several Muslim-majority countries . He assured the Muslim community that his administration would continue working on lasting peace in the region . <p> The president 's remarks also included his promise to end the Israel-Gaza conflict . He referenced the temporary ceasefire just before taking office and reaffirmed his support for Israel 's actions in Gaza .
