<p> Pakistan has drawn the international community 's attention to the plight of thousands of Palestinians including women and children who remain arbitrarily detained , routinely subjected to torture and held in inhumane conditions in Israeli prisons . <p> During UN Security Council briefing on the Middle East , including the Palestinian Question , Pakistan 's Permanent Representative to the UN Ambassador Munir Akram urged the global community not to turn a blind eye to the suffering of the Palestinian people , emphasizing that justice and human rights can not be applied selectively . <p> Munir Akram strongly condemned the resumption of Israeli attacks on Gaza and the indiscriminate killing of Palestinian civilians through bombardments , airstrikes , and artillery fire . <p> He highlighted the significance of the upcoming June Conference , co-chaired by France and Saudi Arabia , as a crucial opportunity to advance the peaceful resolution of the Palestinian issue and implement the two-state solution in accordance with international law and UN resolutions .
