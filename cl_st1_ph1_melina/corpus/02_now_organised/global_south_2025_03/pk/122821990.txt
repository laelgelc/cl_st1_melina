<h> Gaza death toll nears 50,200 as Israeli attacks continue unabated <p> At least 39 more Palestinians were killed in Israeli airstrikes in the Gaza Strip , pushing up the death toll since October 2023 to 50,183 , the Health Ministry said on Wednesday . <p> A ministry statement said that 124 more injured people were also transferred to hospitals , taking the number of injuries to 113,828 in the Israeli onslaught . <p> " Many victims are still trapped under the rubble and on the roads as rescuers are unable to reach them , " it added . <p> The Israeli army launched a surprise aerial campaign on the Gaza Strip on March 18 , killing 830 people and injuring nearly 1,800 others despite a ceasefire and prisoner exchange agreement that took hold in January . <p> Last November , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza . <p> Israel also faces a genocide case at the International Court of Justice for its war on the enclave .
