" <p> Earlier , on March 23 , Israel 's military had encircled the Tal al-Sultan area in Rafah , warning civilians to leave in order to dismantle terrorist infrastructure and eliminate militants there . <p> Rafah , along Gaza 's southern border with Egypt , had been a target of a major Israeli offensive a year ago . <p> Following the resumption of intense bombing on March 18 and a new ground offensive , Israel 's military campaign has led to at least 50,357 deaths in Gaza , mostly civilians , according to Hamas-run Gaza 's health ministry . <p> The war , which began after Hamas 's October 7 , 2023 , attack on Israel , has claimed over 1,218 Israeli lives .
