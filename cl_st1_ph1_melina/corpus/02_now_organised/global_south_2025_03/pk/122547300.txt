It is a campaign of ethnic cleansing and genocide , not peaceful coexistence . The world must recognise that this will not stop at Gaza 's borders . The violence has already expanded to the West Bank , Syria , and Lebanon , and it will continue to spread so long as Israel is allowed to act with impunity . <p> In full support of Israel , the United States has adopted the same brutal approach , indiscriminately bombing Yemen -- the only nation with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the ceasefire agreement .
