<h> Massive protests in Karachi show solidarity with Palestinians <p> April 14 , 2025 <p> On Sunday , tens of thousands of people marched in Karachi to support Palestinians amid the ongoing conflict in Gaza . Participants filled Shara-e-Faisal Road , proudly waving Palestinian flags and holding images of Hamas leaders Ismail Haniyeh and Yahya Al-Sinwar . To symbolize the children lost in Gaza , they placed dolls wrapped in white shrouds along the pavement . <p> The rally , organized by Jamaat-e-Islami , saw many women and children participating . Supporters from Christian and Hindu communities also joined the march . Banners declared messages like " Stop bombing Gaza " and " Down with Israel , " while a giant 100-foot Palestinian flag captured everyone 's attention . <p> As the crowd shouted " Labbaik ya Gaza " and " Aqsa , we are here , " leaders from Jamaat-e-Islami , including Hafiz Naeem-ur-Rehman , stressed the determination of the Palestinian people . Many protestors , like Ali Mustafa , held signs asserting their unwavering support for Palestinian liberation . <p> Meanwhile , a Palestine Solidarity Conference took place @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Maulana Fazal-ur-Rehman criticized Israel and accused global powers of enabling its actions . Since October 2023 , nearly 51,000 Palestinians have died in the conflict , prompting calls for justice and accountability .
