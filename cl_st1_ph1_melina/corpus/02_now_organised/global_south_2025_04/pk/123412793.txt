<p> KARACHI : Ten activists of the Tehreek Labbaik Pakistan ( TLP ) were arrested on Tuesday evening after a mob ransacked an outlet of an international fast-food chain in the Defence Housing Authority ( DHA ) , police said . <p> DIG-South Syed Asad Raza told Dawn that the incident occurred at around 6pm when a group of 40-50 activists of the TLP , armed with sticks and stones , attacked an international fast-food outlet on Korangi Road and tried to vandalise it . <p> " Police responded immediately and dispersed the crowd , " he said , adding that 10 suspects had been apprehended . <p> Regarding the possible aim of the attack , the officer said " their motive was to protest against US / Israel policies in Gaza " . <p> DIG Raza said that the situation had been brought under control and authorities are focusing on arresting the remaining protesters and their organisers . <p> He said the police had also beefed up security at other outlets of international fast-food chains . <p> Earlier on Monday night , another branch of the same fast-food chain @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said Bahadurabad SHO Naveed Soomro . <p> He added that they had attacked the restaurant when a party rally was passing through the area . <p> The restaurant manager lodged a rioting case FIR against over a dozen unknown persons at the Bahadurabad police station .
