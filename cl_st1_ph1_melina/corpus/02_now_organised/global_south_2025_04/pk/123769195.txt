<h> Violent protests harm Pakistan , not help Gaza : Azma Bukhari <p> Punjab Information Minister Azma Bukhari has strongly condemned the violent protests held under the guise of solidarity with Gaza , warning that such actions are damaging Pakistan rather than aiding the Palestinian cause . <p> Speaking at a press conference in Lahore , she said , " Harming Pakistan wo n't help Palestinians , " and emphasized that the recent attacks on food franchises are affecting the livelihoods of around 25,000 Pakistanis . <p> " Those injured and killed in these incidents were Pakistanis . Whose cause are we really serving ? " she questioned . <p> Bukhari accused extremist elements of attempting to derail Punjab 's development and suggested the unrest may be influenced by foreign forces . She warned that no one would be allowed to take the law into their own hands , adding , " Islam stands for peace , not chaos . "
