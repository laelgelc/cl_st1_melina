<p> TORONTO -- The organizations of Muslim voters organized a debate among candidates of all parties as Canada is all set for election 2025 , the local media reported on Monday . <p> However , the Conservative candidates did not participate in the debate . <p> According to the details , Muslim voter organizations in Canada jointly invited electoral candidates from all parties contesting for the Canadian Parliament in Milton and surrounding areas to participate in a debate . <p> Organized under the auspices of the Muslim Advisory Council of Canada , each participating candidate in the debate was given two minutes to present their stance and respond to questions in turn . <p> In this debate , two candidates each from the Liberal Party and the NDP , along with one independent candidate , participated . They presented their viewpoints and answered multiple questions on various topics , including Gaza .
