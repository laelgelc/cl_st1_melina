<p> Seven individuals are reproted to have arrested in Sambhal district , Uttar Pradesh , India , for putting up posters bearing the slogans " Free Gaza , Free Palestine " on shop walls in the Narauli area . <p> These posters also called for a boycott of Israeli goods and urged members of a particular community to buy only from shops run by people of the same faith . <p> The arrests were made after police identified the accused using CCTV footage and information from shop owners where the posters were displayed . The arrested individuals were named Asim , Saif Ali , Rahees , Matloob , Fardeen , Arman , and Arbaz . <p> The police registered a First Information Report ( FIR ) under section 126 of the BNS code .
