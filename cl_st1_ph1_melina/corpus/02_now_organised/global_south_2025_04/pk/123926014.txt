<p> At least 50 more Palestinians were killed in Israeli airstrikes in Gaza in the last 24 hours , bringing the death toll from Israel 's genocidal war since October 2023 to 51,355 , the Health Ministry said on Thursday . <p> A ministry statement said that 152 more injured people were also transferred to hospitals , taking the number of injuries to 117,248 in the Israeli onslaught . <p> " Many victims are still trapped under the rubble and on the roads as rescuers are unable to reach them , " it added . <p> The Israeli army resumed its deadly offensive on the Gaza Strip on March 18 and has since killed nearly 2,000 people and injured over 5,200 others , shattering a ceasefire and prisoner exchange agreement that took hold in January . <p> Last November , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza . <p> Israel also faces a genocide case at the International Court of Justice for its war on the enclave .
