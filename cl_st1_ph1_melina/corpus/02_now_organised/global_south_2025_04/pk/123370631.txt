<h> JI delegation meets PM Shehbaz over power relief , Gaza concerns <p> A three-member delegation of Jamaat-e-Islami ( JI ) , led by Ameer Hafiz Naeem-ur-Rehman , met Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif in Islamabad today and expressed gratitude for the recent reduction in electricity prices . <p> During the meeting , Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif informed the delegation that the government is working diligently to implement long-term reforms in the energy sector . He emphasized that the goal of these reforms is to provide relief to the common people and improve their quality of life . <p> The Prime Minister reaffirmed his government 's commitment to prioritizing public welfare and ensuring that Pakistan continues on its path of progress despite ongoing challenges . <p> Both Shehbaz Sharif and Hafiz Naeem-ur-Rehman also voiced deep concern over the international community 's silence regarding Israel 's continued bombing and oppression of Palestinians in Gaza . The Prime Minister reiterated Pakistan 's unwavering support for the people of Gaza and its commitment to raising the issue of Israeli aggression at every international forum .
