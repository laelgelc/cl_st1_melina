<h> Punjab to launch Air Punjab and Pakistan 's first bullet train : Azma Bokhari <p> Punjab 's Information Minister Azma Bokhari on Saturday announced two major infrastructure projects -- the launch of a provincial airline named Air Punjab and Pakistan 's first bullet train from Lahore to Rawalpindi -- while sharply criticising India 's leadership over tensions in Kashmir . <p> Speaking at a press conference in Lahore , Bokhari described the airline and high-speed rail as " historic initiatives " by a provincial government . <p> " Air Punjab will initially lease four aircraft and operate domestically . Within a year , we hope to acquire an international air license , " she said . <p> She also announced a bullet train project between Lahore and Rawalpindi , stating the route will cover the distance in just 2 hours and 20 minutes . Additional routes across the province are also planned . <p> " This is the first time in Pakistan 's history that a provincial government is launching its own airline and bullet train network , " Bokhari said .
