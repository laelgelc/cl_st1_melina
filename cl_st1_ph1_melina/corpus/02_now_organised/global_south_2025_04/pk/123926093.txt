<h> State minister tells NA panel that PB licence was issued to each MP with PM 's approval <p> ISLAMABAD - Minister of State for Interior Talal Chaudhry on Wednesday informed the National Assembly panel that the government had allowed issuance of prohibited bore ( PB ) licences but these would be issued in a limited number on the recommendation of parliamentarians . <p> Briefing the NA Standing Committee on Interior and Narcotics Control , the minister of state clarified that one PB licence was issued to each parliamentarian with the approval of the prime minister . He added that under the policy , non-prohibited bore ( NPB ) licenses were issued to close associates of parliamentarians . He further said special permissions for issuing NPB licenses in Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa would be sought from the PM . " Arms licenses for businessmen and taxpayers are under consideration , " he added . The committee held its meeting under the chair of MNA Raja Khurram Shahzad Nawaz in the Parliament House . The minister of state stated that as part of the national counterterrorism initiative , unregistered vehicles would not be allowed to ply on roads . The chair directed that a report on this matter should be submitted in the next meeting .
