<h> Unholy Silence <p> At a time when Christians around the world gathered to mark Easter , worshippers in Jerusalem faced intimidation , aggression , and restriction -- at the hands of Israeli forces . The Holy City , sacred to three major religions , has once again become a battleground -- not just for territory , but for the basic right to exist and express faith . That this happened at the Church of the Holy Sepulchre , one of the most revered sites in Christianity , and during Easter no less , speaks volumes of the impunity with which the Israeli state operates . <p> Meanwhile , Pope Francis -- frail but resolute -- called for a ceasefire in Gaza . His words echoed a growing global cry for justice , even as Western powers continue to look the other way , offering political cover , economic support , and military aid for a genocide being live-streamed in real time . The absurdity is almost theatrical : while European capitals light candles for Easter peace , their governments fan the flames of war .
