<p> Minister for Religious Affairs and Interfaith Harmony Sardar Muhammad Yousaf has urged intending pilgrims to uphold national image during Hajj journey by strictly following Saudi laws . <p> He expressed these views in an exclusive interview with Radio Pakistan 's Correspondent Abdul Rehman Cheema in Islamabad . <p> The Minister advised pilgrims to strictly adhere to all rules , regulations and instructions issued by the Saudi authorities as these are designed for the smooth conduct of Hajj . <p> Congratulating the pilgrims for being chosen for this sacred journey , he pointed out that they also serve as ambassadors of their country . He advised them to remain fully focused on prayers during their stay at the Holy land . <p> Talking about his recent visit to the Saudi Arabia , Sardar Muhammad Yousaf commended the exceptional arrangements made by the Saudi leadership for the guests of Allah .
