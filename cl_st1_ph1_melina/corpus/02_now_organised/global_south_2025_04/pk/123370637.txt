<p> UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres on Tuesday rebuffed a proposal by US President Donald Trump and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to relocate the people of Gaza to other countries , calling it a violation of international law . <p> In response to Anadolu 's question about their widely denounced plan , Guterres said : " Palestinians must be able to live in a Palestinian state , side by side with an Israeli state . That is the only solution that can bring peace to the Middle East . " <p> He further stated that forcibly relocating Palestinians " is something that is against international law . " <p> Asked about Netanyahu claiming the people of Gaza are being " locked " in the enclave , Guterres noted the urgent need for medical evacuations and stressed that " everything must be done to increase the number of medical evacuations. "
