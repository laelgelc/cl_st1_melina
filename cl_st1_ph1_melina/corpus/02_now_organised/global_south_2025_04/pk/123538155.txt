<p> Irish rap group Kneecap has criticised Coachella 's official live stream for censoring their pro-Palestinian and anti-establishment messaging during their performance at the festival 's opening on Friday . <p> Known for their politically-charged music , the Belfast-based trio -- comprising rappers Mo Chara and M ? gla ? Bap as well as masked DJ Pr ? va ? -- delivered a set that stood out amid the usually apolitical landscape of the California festival . <p> But while festival-goers got the full experience , audiences tuning in from home appear to have missed key moments . According to SFGATE , the live stream omitted several of the band 's political statements , including one about Palestine . <p> One such moment included a jab at the controversial former British prime minister , Margaret Thatcher . In a video posted on X ( formerly Twitter ) , the group can be heard proclaiming , " If anybody was wondering , Margaret Thatcher 's still dead , " followed by chants of " Maggie 's in a box . " That moment , however , did n't appear on the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ online . " Not the only thing that was cut -- our messaging on the US-backed genocide in Gaza somehow never appeared on screens either , " the group wrote on X the next day , accompanied by a Palestinian flag emoji . " Back next Friday , Coachella , and it 'll be sorted . " <p> Though according to the outlet , some pro-Palestinian sentiments did make it through -- including one of the MCs shouting " F*** it , free Palestine " after a track , which drew loud cheers and was included in the stream . <p> A moment also captured on the stream showed an audience member waving a Palestinian flag . <p> The band , however , suggests a clear decision was made to censor more explicit political statements . <p> Kneecap was n't the only act to speak up for Palestine at Coachella . Veteran punk rockers Green Day used their headline slot on Saturday to make a lyrical alteration during their performance of ' Jesus of Suburbia ' . <p> Frontman Billie Joe Armstrong sang , " Runnin ' away from pain @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ broken home , " replacing the original 2004 lyric , " Runnin ' away from pain when you 've been victimised . " <p> The band also called out Trump supporters in their song ' American Idiot ' , changing a lyric to " I 'm not a part of the MAGA agenda " during their set . <p> Last month , Armstrong made a similar tweak while performing in Australia , changing another line from the same track to , " Am I retarded , or am I just JD Vance ? " -- a jab at the vice president of the US.
