<p> The National Assembly was informed on Monday that the Ministry of Religious Affairs and Interfaith Harmony is considering requiring Hajj Muavineen to fully focus on serving pilgrims , rather than performing Hajj themselves . <p> During the Question Hour in the National Assembly , Minister for Parliamentary Affairs Tariq Fazal Chaudhry said the Ministry is finalizing its recommendations in this regard . He explained that this measure aims to ensure that Hajj Muavineen remain available to assist and guide the Hujjaj throughout their pilgrimage in Makkah . The Minister said under this strategy Hajj Muavineen will not be permitted to wear Ahram while providing their services to the pilgrims during the Hajj days . <p> He further informed that the Ministerial Staff termed as Seasonal Staff are deputed for seasonal Hajj duties in Pakistan and Saudi Arabia . He said experienced staff is mostly deployed at Madinah Munawarrah and Jeddah Airports , Mission operation Offices , Lost and Found Cell Accommodation , Transportation , Complaint Management , Haram Guide and other duties .
