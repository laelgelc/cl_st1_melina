<h> NA body condemns India 's action on IWT , Israel 's Gaza attacks <p> ISLAMABAD - The National Assembly Standing Committee on Parliamentary Affairs on Thursday unanimously passed a resolution condemning India 's suspension of the Indus Waters Treaty and downgrading of diplomatic ties , calling the actions a serious provocation . <p> Chaired by Rana Iradat Sharif Khan , the committee also adopted a separate resolution denouncing the killing of civilians in Gaza , characterising Israel 's actions as ethnic cleansing . It extended condolences on the passing of Pope Francis , acknowledging his global influence . <p> The Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs briefed the committee on expanding the Prime Minister 's Public Affairs and Grievance Wing , with a focus on outreach in remote regions . Plans to integrate provincial public grievance systems , including AJK and GB , with the Pakistan Citizen Portal were also discussed . <p> Since the 16th National Assembly 's inception , 44 assurances have been made by various ministries , the committee was informed .
