<h> Israel 's Netanyahu arrives in Hungary , defying ICC warrant <p> Budapest - Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu arrived in Budapest early Thursday on his first trip to Europe since 2023 and in defiance of the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) ' s arrest warrant against him . Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orban extended an invitation to Netanyahu last November , a day after the ICC issued the arrest warrant over alleged war crimes in Gaza . Orban vowed the EU member would not execute the warrant , despite being an ICC member , saying the court 's decision " intervenes in an ongoing conflict ... for political purposes " . " Welcome to Budapest , Benjamin Netanyahu ! " wrote Hungary 's Defence Minister Kristof Szalay-Bobrovniczky on Facebook as Netanyahu began his visit , and after greeting him at the airport in capital Budapest . Netanyahu was welcomed with military honours , after which he will hold talks with Orban . A joint news conference is expected around 12:30 pm ( 1030 GMT ) .
