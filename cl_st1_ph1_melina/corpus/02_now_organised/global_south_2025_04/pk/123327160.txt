<h> US cancels visas for hundreds of international students over pro-Palestine protests <p> April 8 , 2025 <p> The US government has canceled visas for around 450 international students , including many from Pakistan . This decision affects students from several prestigious universities , including Harvard , Stanford , and UCLA . The sudden action has left many students confused and vulnerable to immigration consequences . <p> Reports indicate that the visa cancellations specifically target students who participated in pro-Palestine demonstrations . These protests were held in response to Israeli actions in Gaza . Many affected students come from Muslim-majority countries , raising concerns about potential bias in the decision . <p> The abrupt visa revocation came without prior notice and has alarmed universities and human rights activists . They are now working alongside students to secure legal assistance and to appeal the cancellations . The impacted students , feeling abandoned by the system , are facing a difficult situation . <p> Human rights advocates are voicing strong opposition to this move by the Trump administration .
