<h> Karachi markets to remain closed today in solidarity with Gaza <p> Major markets across Karachi are observing a shutdown today ( Monday ) in a show of solidarity with the people of Gaza , following a call by the city 's trade associations . <p> According to trade leaders , prominent commercial hubs including Sarafa Bazaar , Jodia Bazaar , the Medicine Market , Mithadar , and other wholesale markets in the Old City area have suspended operations for the day . <p> Rizwan Irfan , President of the Karachi Electronic Dealers Association , appealed to traders to close their businesses as a symbolic protest . Karachi Tajir Ittehad leader Jamil Paracha echoed the sentiment , stating that the city 's business community stands united with Gaza . <p> Trader representative Shakir Fancy confirmed that all major wholesale markets would remain closed in protest against Israel 's ongoing military aggression in Gaza .
