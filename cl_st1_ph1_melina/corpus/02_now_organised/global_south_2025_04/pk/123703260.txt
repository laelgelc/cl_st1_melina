<h> Gaza aid blockade <p> The world continues to bear silent witness to the genocide in Gaza . Even though the death toll has now passed 51,000 , with over 100,000 injured and millions displaced , no countries have taken any meaningful action against Israel . Though the US and Germany stand out for their almost blind support of Israeli atrocities , several other democratic states that claim to value human rights have also done little more than pay lip service to the issue . <p> While a handful of Latin , South American , and African countries have withdrawn their diplomats , none of the countries that Israel actually cares about has made any effort to sanction the State of Israel or any of its political or military leaders . <p> In fact , several countries have rejected or criticised South Africa for pursuing a case against Israel in the ICJ . While former US President Joe Biden 's administration used whataboutism regarding violence by Hamas to justify its refusal to condemn violence by Israel , the Trump administration has gone all-in , with President Donald Trump repeatedly calling @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ his allies , and even most of his rivals . <p> But beyond the bombs and bullets , even Israel 's weaponisation of starvation has generated little more than a peep . At least a dozen major humanitarian organisations are begging Israel to end its aid blockade , but not a single Western country capable of threatening Israeli interests has gone beyond finger-wagging . Intentionally starving millions of people is a war crime . <p> Those enabling it , from Washington to Brussels , are accomplices . If this was happening in most other countries in the world , a US-backed coalition would have deposed the government by now . Instead , even after the ICJ ruled that Israeli actions in Gaza plausibly constitute genocide , weapons sales continue unabated , and the US has gone as far as sanctioning the ICJ and threatening its lawyers and officials , along with the government of South Africa . <p> With the US beyond redemption and Europe divided , the only certainty is that Palestinians will continue to die unnecessarily .
