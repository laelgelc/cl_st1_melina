<p> Senator Irfan Siddiqui of the Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz ( PML-N ) has accused Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi of replicating Israeli policies in Kashmir . In a social media post , Siddiqui stated that Modi is attempting to transform the region into " another Gaza , " drawing parallels to Israel 's actions in Palestinian territories . He criticized Modi 's government , claiming it has become " the largest killing ground for Muslims and minorities " rather than the world 's largest democracy . <p> Siddiqui pointed out India 's repeated threats to cut off water to Pakistan , warning of severe consequences . He emphasized that any dam construction choking Pakistan 's rivers would be treated as an act of war .
