<h> Gaza death toll passes 50,500 mark amid ceaseless Israeli attacks <p> At least 100 more Palestinians were killed in Israeli airstrikes in the Gaza Strip in the last 24 hours , bringing the overall death toll from Israel 's genocidal war since October 2023 to 50,523 , the Health Ministry said on Thursday . <p> A ministry statement said that 138 more injured people were also transferred to hospitals in the last 24 hours , bringing the number of injuries in the Israeli onslaught to 114,776 . <p> " Many victims are still trapped under the rubble and on the roads , with rescuers unable to reach them , " it added . <p> The Israeli army launched a surprise aerial campaign on the Gaza Strip on March 18 and has since killed 1,163 people and injured over 2,700 others despite a ceasefire and prisoner exchange agreement that began in January . <p> Last November , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza .
