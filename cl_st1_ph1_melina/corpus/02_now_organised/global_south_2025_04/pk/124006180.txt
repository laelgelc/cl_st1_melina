Lives depend on it , " Tedros said . <p> The World Food Programme ( WFP ) earlier confirmed that its supplies inside Gaza have been depleted , further worsening the already dire humanitarian conditions . <p> Gaza has faced severe restrictions on humanitarian access since the escalation of hostilities in October 2023 .
