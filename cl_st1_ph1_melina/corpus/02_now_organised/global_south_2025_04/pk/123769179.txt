<h> JI Chief Hafiz Naeem announces April 26 nationwide strike in support of Gaza <p> Jamaat-e-Islami ( JI ) chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman has announced a complete nationwide strike on April 26 to express solidarity with the people of Gaza . <p> Speaking at the Gaza Million March in Islamabad , Hafiz Naeem criticized Pakistan 's rulers for their inaction , stating , " While the rulers are asleep , the Muslim Ummah is wide awake . <p> " He strongly condemned any attempts to normalize ties with Israel , warning that alliances with Israel or the United States would lead to dire consequences . " Palestine is not a political issue -- it is a matter of faith , " he emphasized . <p> He called for the opening of a Hamas office in Pakistan and praised the Palestinian people for their sacrifices . He also highlighted increasing international support for Gaza , citing recent developments in Spain and California . <p> Calling the Palestinian resistance a global symbol of hope for the oppressed , Hafiz Naeem urged Muslim leaders to set aside internal differences and unite .
