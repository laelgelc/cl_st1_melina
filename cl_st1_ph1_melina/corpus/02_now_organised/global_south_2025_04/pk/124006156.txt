<p> In Peshawar , business hubs such as Qissa Khwani Bazaar , Khyber Bazaar , Peepal Mandi , and Saddar observed a complete shutdown , with a protest camp set up at Rampura Gate . <p> Rawalpindi 's Raja Bazaar , Murree Road , and Saddar areas also witnessed deserted streets and closed shops , while business centers in Murree and Pakpattan were similarly shut . In Balochistan , markets in Quetta , Mastung , Kalat , and Khuzdar remained closed to express support for Gaza . <p> JI Ameer Hafiz Naeem ur Rehman said the strike demonstrated the nation 's strong support for the Palestinian cause , declaring that Pakistanis would not tolerate the ongoing genocide in Gaza . <p> Traders ' Alliance Distances Itself from Strike <p> Meanwhile , Karachi 's All City Traders ' Alliance distanced itself from the strike , stating that markets like the Karachi Timber Market would remain open , having already shown solidarity earlier this month . <p> Some traders also filed complaints with the Sindh IG , alleging they received threats to close their businesses and requested police protection .
