<h> Red Crescent demands probe after paramedics killings in Gaza <p> UNITED NATIONS - The Palestinian Red Crescent Society ( PRCS ) has called for urgent answers over the whereabouts of a missing paramedic , coupled with a full independent investigation into the Israeli killing of 15 humanitarian workers in Gaza on 23 March . Last Sunday , a joint PRCS and UN humanitarian coordination office ( OCHA ) mission uncovered a shallow grave in Rafah . The bodies of eight PRCS paramedics , six civil defence workers , and one UN staff member were found . They had been killed by the Israeli military while attempting to reach victims of shelling on 23 March . " They were humanitarians . They wore emblems . They should have been protected , " the International Federation of Red Cross ( IFRC ) ' s Permanent Observer , Dylan Winder , told reporters during a briefing at UN Headquarters in New York .
