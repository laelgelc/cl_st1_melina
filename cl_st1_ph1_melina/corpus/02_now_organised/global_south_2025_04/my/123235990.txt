<p> RAMALLAH , April 5 -- The Israeli army said yesterday that its soldiers shot dead a Palestinian who threw stones at troops near the occupied West Bank village of Husan , whose mayor told AFP the boy was 17 . <p> The army said that on Thursday evening " several terrorists hurled rocks toward Road 375 adjacent to Husan ... Soldiers who were operating in the area responded with fire toward the terrorists , eliminated one terrorist and hit an additional terrorist " . <p> Husan mayor Jamal Sabateen said the army had opened fired on youngsters throwing stones in the village west of Bethlehem . <p> " The Israeli army opened fire on them -- killed one and injured another . The army took them , and up until now , they have n't been returned , " Sabateen told AFP . <p> The West Bank-based Palestinian health ministry reported the killing of the young man " by Israeli gunfire " and identified him as Yussef Zaoul . <p> Husan sits seven kilometres ( four miles ) west of Bethlehem , in a region usually quieter than the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ militancy where the Israeli military has been operating for months . <p> Yet in recent weeks , Palestinian sources pointed to several Israeli raids and dozens of arrests in the area , including in the city of Bethlehem . <p> The health ministry also reported the death of 42-year-old Hussein Hardan on Friday . <p> It said Hardan was killed " by Israeli gunfire " in Jenin at dawn . <p> When questioned by AFP , the Israeli military said it had operated to apprehend " a terrorist " . <p> During the operation , the man attempted to flee and soldiers opened fire , killing the man , the army said . <p> Since the start of the Gaza war in October 2023 , violence has soared in the West Bank . <p> Israeli troops or settlers have killed at least 917 Palestinians , including militants , in the territory since then , according to health ministry figures . <p> Palestinian attacks and clashes during military raids have killed at least 33 Israelis , including soldiers , over the same period , according to official figures .
