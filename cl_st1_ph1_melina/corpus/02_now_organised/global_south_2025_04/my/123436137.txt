<h> ' Denying Palestinians right ' : Hamas launches appeal against UK ban <p> LONDON , April 10 -- UK-based lawyers said they have asked the British government to lift a ban on the Palestinian militant group Hamas , a petition that has drawn sharp criticism from opposition politicians . <p> The legal submission argues the ban contravenes Britain 's human rights commitments , with the militants insisting on " the legitimacy of the struggle of the Palestinian people for independence , territorial integrity , national unity and liberation . " <p> The military wing of Hamas was designated a " terrorist group " by the UK in 2001 . The United States and the European Union consider Hamas a terror group . <p> Hamas 's proscription was extended in 2021 to include the political wing , with the group considered a " complex but single terrorist organisation " , according to the government website . <p> In the UK , belonging to , encouraging and expressing support for a proscribed organisation , among other acts , are criminal offences . <p> The Home Office said it did not comment @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ interior ministry was announced by London-based firm Riverway Law on yesterday . <p> It added the right to resist " foreign occupation by all available means , including armed struggle , is moral , legitimate and explicitly enshrined in international law . " <p> Hamas militants stormed over Israel 's border with the Gaza Strip to carry out the October 7 , 2023 attack against Israel in which 1,218 people were killed on the Israeli side , mostly civilians , according to an AFP tally based on Israeli official figures . <p> In retaliation , Israel vowed to crush Hamas and has relentlessly bombarded Gaza , with the death toll since the start of the war now at more than 50,000 people , mostly civilians , according to the territory 's Hamas-run health ministry . <p> Riverway Law said it was instructed by Mousa Abu Marzouk , a senior Hamas official . <p> By banning Hamas " Britain is effectively denying the Palestinians the right to defend themselves " , the lawyers said in a statement on X. <p> The submission argues that the proscription of Hamas is " disproportionate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " to Britain 's national security . <p> The plea says the ban goes against Britain 's human rights duties , including under the European Convention on Human Rights ( ECHR ) -- which has become a target of UK 's political right since Britain left the European Union in 2020. -- AFP
