<p> PETALING JAYA : Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim expressed deep sorrow over the death of Pope Francis , praising the Pope 's enduring legacy as a moral beacon of compassion , humility , and justice in a tumultuous world . <p> " As the first pope from the Global South , Pope Francis 's commitment to interfaith understanding , environmental stewardship , and the dignity of the marginalised extended far beyond the reach of the Catholic Church , " Anwar stated in a Facebook post on Monday ( April 21 ) . <p> Anwar highlighted Pope Francis as a steadfast advocate for peace , noting that his efforts to build bridges across faiths and cultures earned respect worldwide , including in the Muslim community . <p> " He spoke with moral clarity on the immense suffering in Gaza , reminding the world that we must show the same compassion to all victims of violence , wherever they may be . <p> " He also championed the dignity of migrants and refugees , urging the international community to respond to their plight with humanity and conscience .
