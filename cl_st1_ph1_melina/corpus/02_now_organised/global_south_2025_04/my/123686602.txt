<p> LONDON : King Charles III on Thursday paid tribute to humanitarian workers who " risk their own lives " to protect others in war-torn countries , in a message broadcast to mark Easter . <p> " One of the puzzles of our humanity is how we are capable of both great cruelty and great kindness , " said the British monarch , who is also head of the Anglican Church , in a message published by Buckingham Palace . <p> " This paradox of human life runs through the Easter story and in the scenes that daily come before our eyes -- at one moment , terrible images of human suffering and , in another , heroic acts in war-torn countries where humanitarians of every kind risk their own lives to protect the lives of others , " he added . <p> The king , who is still receiving weekly treatment for an unspecified cancer diagnosed in early 2024 , and Queen Camilla are due to attend Maundy Thursday services at Durham Cathedral in north-east England , before attending a traditional Easter Mass in Windsor on Sunday . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ did not mention any particular conflicts . <p> However , his message comes at a time when the number of humanitarian workers killed reached a record high of 280 in 2024 , according to the UN , as conflict rages in Gaza , Sudan and Ukraine . <p> The king also stressed the similar duty of care for strangers demanded by Judaism , Islam , " in other religious traditions and in the hearts of all those who seek the good of others " . <p> " There are three virtues that the world still needs - faith , hope and love . And the greatest of these is love , " he added . <p> " It is with these timeless truths in my mind , and my heart , that I wish you all a blessed and peaceful Easter . "
