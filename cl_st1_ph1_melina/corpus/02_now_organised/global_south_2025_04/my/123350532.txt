<p> KANGAR : All mosques in Perlis have been directed to recite Qunut Nazilah ( supplication for protection during times of calamity ) during the final raka'ah of the five daily prayers starting from Fajr prayer on Wednesday ( April 9 ) , until further notice . <p> The Raja Muda of Perlis , Tuanku Syed Faizuddin Putra Jamalullail , said the order was issued with the consent of the Raja of Perlis , Tuanku Syed Sirajuddin Jamalullail , following ongoing Israeli military attacks and the blockade of humanitarian aid to the people of Gaza . <p> " With the consent of His Royal Highness Tuanku Syed Sirajuddin Almarhum Tuanku Syed Putra Jamalullail , and after closely monitoring the situation in Gaza involving military aggression and aid restrictions by Israeli forces and illegal settlers , it is hereby ordered that all mosques in Perlis recite Qunut Nazilah in all five daily prayers beginning April 9 ( 10 Syawal 1446H ) , " he said in a statement on Tuesday ( April 8 ) . <p> The statement was issued by the Perlis Islamic Religious and Malay Customs Council ( MAIPs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ least 44 Palestinians were killed in ongoing Israeli airstrikes on Gaza , according to Anadolu Agency .
