<p> PARIS , April 24 -- The Cannes film festival said yesterday that the screening of a documentary about Gaza photojournalist Fatima Hassouna at the event next month would honour her work after the " horror " of her death in an Israeli air strike last week . <p> " Put Your Soul on Your Hand and Walk " by Iranian director Sepideh Farsi is to be shown at ACID Cannes , at this year 's May 13-24 festival , which runs parallel to the main competition . <p> The film features conversations between Farsi and Hassouna , 25 , as she documents the impact of Israel 's devastating war on the Palestinian territory . <p> Hassouna was killed along with 10 relatives in an air strike on her family home in northern Gaza last Wednesday , the day after the documentary was announced as part of the ACID Cannes selection . <p> The Israeli military , which media freedom group Reporters Without Borders ( RSF ) has accused of carrying out a " massacre " of Palestinian journalists , claimed it had targeted a Hamas member . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ deep sorrow at this tragedy , which has moved and shocked the entire world , " the festival said in a statement on Hassouna sent to AFP . <p> " While a film is little in the face of such a tragedy , its screening at the ACID section in Cannes on May 15 will be , in addition to the message of the film itself , a way of honouring the memory of the young woman , a victim like so many others of the war , " it added . <p> Just before her death , Hassouna wrote on social media that " if I die , I want a loud death . I do n't want to be just breaking news , or a number in a group . " <p> " She was such a light , so talented . When you see the film you 'll understand , " Farsi told Hollywood news website Deadline after her death . " I had talked to her a few hours before to tell her that the film was in Cannes and to invite her . " Her name joins those of nearly 200 journalists killed in 18 months . This carnage must stop , " it wrote on the Bluesky social media website . <p> Also at Cannes , Palestinian twins Tarzan and Arab Nasser will showcase their latest film " Once Upon a Time In Gaza " , a tale of murder and friendship set in the war-torn territory , in the secondary " Un Certain Regard " section . <p> An attack by Hamas militants on Israel on October 7 , 2023 set off the war . The attack resulted in the deaths of 1,218 people , mostly civilians , according to an AFP tally based on official Israeli figures . <p> Israeli forces have since killed more than 51,000 people in Gaza , according to the territory 's Hamas-run health ministry . -- AFP
