Security concerns ( 44 per cent ) and socio-political instability ( 33 per cent ) are the primary factors driving the desire to leave , while a low birth rate among Christians further contributes to the shrinking community in Israel . <p> Attacks against Christians have risen sharply in recent years , with 111 confirmed incidents in 2024 , up from 89 in 2023 . These attacks , often attributed to ultra-orthodox and national-religious groups , include physical assaults , vandalism of church property , and verbal harassment . Most of the victims are clergy or individuals wearing visible Christian symbols .
