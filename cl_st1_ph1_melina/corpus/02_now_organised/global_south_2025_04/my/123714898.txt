His dream was to represent Palestine at the Asian Games in Jakarta . But his journey , like that of many in his homeland , was interrupted by violence . <p> Alaa was born and raised in the Gaza Strip , a place often marked by conflict and hardship . In May 2018 , while Israel celebrated the 70th anniversary of its founding , Palestinians observed Yawm al-Nakba , the Day of Catastrophe -- commemorating the 1948 exodus of hundreds of thousands of Palestinians . <p> During that same period , the international spotlight briefly turned to the region as the Giro d'Italia cycling race began in Israel . But his dream was cut short when he was struck in the leg by an Israeli sniper . The bullet , designed to inflict maximum damage , shattered his leg . With medical facilities overwhelmed and borders sealed , two weeks passed before doctors were forced to amputate to save his life . <p> His dream of Jakarta was lost -- but not his spirit . <p> The SunbirdsWith extraordinary resilience , Alaa transformed personal tragedy into a new mission .
