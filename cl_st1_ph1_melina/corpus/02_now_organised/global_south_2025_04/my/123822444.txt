<p> KUALA LUMPUR : Pope Francis , the head of the Roman Catholic Church who passed away today , will be remembered not only as the first pope from the Global South , but as a moral voice of compassion , humility and justice in a turbulent world , said Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim . <p> Anwar posted on Facebook today that Pope Francis ' commitment to interfaith understanding , environmental stewardship and the dignity of the marginalised resonated far beyond the Catholic Church . <p> " Pope Francis was a steadfast advocate for peace and his efforts to build bridges across faiths and cultures earned the respect of many , including in the Muslim world . <p> " He spoke with moral clarity on the immense suffering in Gaza , reminding the world that we must show the same compassion to all victims of violence , wherever they may be , " he said . <p> Anwar added that the Pope also championed the dignity of migrants and refugees , urging the international community to respond to their plight with humanity and conscience . <p> He said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to mind the words of the Bard : " His life was gentle , and the elements so mix 'd in him that Nature might stand up and say to all the world , ' This was a man ' . " <p> On a very personal note , Anwar said he had the honour of visiting Pope Francis during his early days at the Vatican . <p> " I am deeply saddened by the passing of His Holiness Pope Francis . <p> " On behalf of the government and people of Malaysia , I extend our deepest condolences to the Holy See , to Catholics around the world , especially to the Catholic community in Malaysia . <p> " May his legacy endure , " he said . <p> According to international news reports , Pope Francis , the first pontiff from Latin America , died on Monday aged 88 . <p> He had long battled various health issues , including ' asthma-like respiratory crises ' associated with thrombocytopenia.
