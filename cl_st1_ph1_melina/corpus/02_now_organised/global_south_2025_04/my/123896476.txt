<p> With deep sorrow , we join the world in mourning the loss of Pope Francis -- a towering religious leader whose life embodied the highest ideals of compassion , justice and human dignity . <p> Pope Francis modelled unwavering courage . His advocacy transcended religious boundaries and went on to become a global voice of conscience . <p> He tirelessly reminded us of our shared responsibility for humanity and the planet . He did not only express his empathy for the suffering and the marginalized , he was vocal in condemning economic inequality and challenging systems of oppression . <p> Pope Francis ' willingness to speak uncomfortable truths to power has inspired millions beyond the Catholic community . <p> His final Easter message - a thunderous condemnation of violence in Gaza delivered through labored breath , encapsulated his life 's work ; moral clarity that pierced through diplomatic platitudes .
