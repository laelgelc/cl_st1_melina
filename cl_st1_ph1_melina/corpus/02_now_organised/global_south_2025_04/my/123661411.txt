<p> Kuala Lumpur : Malaysia 's homegrown coffee brand , ZUS Coffee , and MAPIM ( Malaysian Consultative Council of Islamic Organisation ) served 10,000 Iftar meals for families in Gaza during the month of Ramadan , to break their fast with warmth , nourishment , and dignity despite ongoing hardships in Gaza . <p> The ZUS Coffee community has raised RM266,000 through the ZUS CARES initiative , featured in the ZUS Coffee App . This contribution was officially handed over to MAPIM to aid this programme , ensuring that families receive support during the sacred month of Ramadan . <p> Advertisement <p> The ZUS CARES initiative has been ongoing , enabling customers to support causes that matter . It is a built-in donation feature in the ZUS Coffee App that lets customers donate to verified causes at checkout . <p> While this donation handover took place during Ramadan , the spirit of giving among Malaysians has been evident long before , with continued support over time . <p> " Ramadan was a time of reflection , gratitude , and giving .
