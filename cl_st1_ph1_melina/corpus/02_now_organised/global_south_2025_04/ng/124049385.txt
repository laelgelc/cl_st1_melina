Electing a new pope is a big deal amongst the Catholic , and they do not joke with age-long rituals . The election process is highly secretive and nothing will be certain until white smoke pouring from the chimney of the Sistine Chapel tells the world that a new pope has been picked . <p> When a pope dies or resigns , cardinals aged under 80 are eligible to enter a secret conclave to choose the new head of the nearly 1.4 billion-member Roman Catholic Church from amongst themselves .
