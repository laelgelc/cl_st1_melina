We will not be part of passing this policy , " Hayya said . <p> Recall that Egyptian mediators have been working to revive the January ceasefire agreement that halted fighting in Gaza before breaking down last month , but there has been @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ blaming each other for the lack of a deal . <p> DAILY POST reports that the latest round of talks on Monday in Cairo to restore the ceasefire and free Israeli hostages ended with no apparent breakthrough . <p> According to Hayya , Hamas accepted a proposal by the mediators , Qatar and Egypt , to release some hostages in return for Palestinians jailed by Israel , and begin talks on implementing the second phase of the ceasefire agreement , which includes ending the war and the withdrawal of Israeli forces from Gaza . <p> He accused Israel of offering a counterproposal with impossible conditions .
