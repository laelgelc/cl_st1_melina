<h> You Saw Evil In Gaza , Condemned It -- Fani-Kayode Mourns Pope Francis <h> Former Minister of Aviation , Femi Fani-Kayode , has mourned the passing of Pope Francis . <p> Naija News reported that Jorge Mario Bergoglio , Pope Francis , died at the age of 88 years , in the Vatican , on Monday . <p> In a statement on Monday , Fani-Kayode lauded the late Pope 's strong condemnation of the crisis in Gaza , Palestine . <p> He wrote : " Sad to hear that Pope Francis has passed on . He died one day after Resurrection Day and a day after he met with American Vice President JD Vance . <p> " He fought a good fight and tried his best to bring peace and hope to the world . His strong condemnation of the genocide in Gaza stands him out as one of the most compassionate , courageous , respected and credible men of God and world leaders . <p> " He saw evil and called it out regardless of the consequences . For this alone he won the respect and admiration of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the Vatican , the Catholic Church and to the Body of Christ as a whole . We have lost a great and worthy Prince of the Kingdom and soldier of Christ . <p> " Yet despite this great loss the gates of hell shall not prevail against the Church . Our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ has conquered death ; therefore , to live is Christ and to die is gain . <p> " May the soul of Pope Francis , a gentle , humble , great and deeply courageous man , rest in peace . "
