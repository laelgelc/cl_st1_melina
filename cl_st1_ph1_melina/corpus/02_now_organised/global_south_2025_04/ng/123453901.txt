Preliminary reports suggest that their ethnic identity as Hausa-Fulani may have played a decisive role in their tragic fate . It appears the attackers , driven more by emotion than reason , allowed the victims ' cultural and linguistic affinity with the criminal elements terrorizing the region to distort their moral judgment . In their misguided pursuit of justice , they tragically discarded the foundational principle that every individual is presumed innocent until proven guilty . <p> The atrocity was met with widespread condemnation and a genuine outpouring of grief that transcended Nigeria 's ethnic and religious divides .
