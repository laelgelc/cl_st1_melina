( Xinhua/Peng Ziyang ) <p> Hungarian Foreign Minister Peter Szijjarto presented a bill on Tuesday to the National Assembly proposing Hungary 's formal withdrawal from the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) . <p> Speaking during a general parliamentary debate in Budapest , Szijjarto recalled that Hungary had been an active participant in the establishment of the ICC , signing the Rome Statute in 1999 and ratifying it in 2001 . <p> However , he added that the statute had never been incorporated into Hungarian law . <p> The Hungarian parliament must make the final decision on withdrawal , after which he would formally notify the United Nations secretary-general .
