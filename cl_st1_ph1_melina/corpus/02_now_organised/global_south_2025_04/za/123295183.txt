In Cape Town 's historic Bo-Kaap , residents have painted over 30 murals in solidarity with Palestine as part of the Angel for Gaza initiative . These powerful artworks , often depicting scenes of loss and resistance , aim to raise awareness about the humanitarian crisis in Gaza and serve as a visual form of protest . Despite facing pushback from city authorities over by-law compliance , the community remains resolute , using their walls to voice support and draw parallels between their own history of resistance and the Palestinian struggle .
