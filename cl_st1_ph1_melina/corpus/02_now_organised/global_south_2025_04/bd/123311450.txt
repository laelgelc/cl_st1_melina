<h> SUST shuts down for a day in solidarity with Gaza , holds protest rally <p> Shahjalal University of Science and Technology ( SUST ) suspended all academic and administrative activities on April 7 in solidarity with the global " No Work , No School " initiative , protesting the ongoing Israeli military aggression in Gaza . <p> The shutdown was officially declared in a notice signed by the university 's deputy registrar , stating that the move was a mark of protest against the " brutal occupation and genocide " being carried out by Israeli forces in Palestinian territory . <p> Around noon , hundreds of SUST teachers , students , officers , and staff gathered in front of the university 's central library and brought out a protest rally . They called on the international community to take decisive steps to stop the humanitarian catastrophe in Gaza . <p> The protest rally was part of a broader student-led campaign on campus . On April 5 , general students of the university had already announced a boycott of classes and exams to protest the escalating violence in Palestine .
