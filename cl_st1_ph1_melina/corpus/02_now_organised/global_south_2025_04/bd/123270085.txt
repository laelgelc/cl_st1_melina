<h> Swadhinata Concert postponed for Gaza tragedy <p> Shobar Agey Bangladesh Foundation , a pro-BNP social and cultural organisation , announced on Monday night the postponement of its planned Swadhinata Concert for a temporary period , expressing solidarity with the oppressed Palestinians in Gaza and Rafah . <p> Organisation 's president Shahiduddin Chowdhury Annie and general secretary Rakibul Islam Bakul made the announcement in a press release . <p> " We have decided to temporarily postpone the concert , which was scheduled to take place simultaneously in Dhaka , Chittagong , Khulna , and Bogra on April 12 , by expressing our solidarity with the oppressed people of Gaza and Rafah , " he said . <p> Earlier , a delegation from Shobar Agey Bangladesh , led by Shahiduddin Chowdhury Annie , met DMP Commissioner Sheikh Md Sajjad Ali at his office , seeking the law enforcement 's full cooperation to ensure the smooth organisation of the concert . <p> Talking to reporters after the meeting , Annie said they have decided to hold their scheduled Swadhinata Concert at four venues simultaneously on April 12 , instead of the previously @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ protests against the mass killings and severe human rights violations in the Gaza Strip . <p> " Swadhinata Concert of Shobar Agey Bangladesh was initially scheduled for April 11 , not only in Dhaka but also in Chattogram , Khulna , and Bogura . But we will all unite this Friday to participate in a procession protesting the heinous and barbaric genocide in the Gaza Strip , " he said . <p> On March 27 , Shobar Agey Bangladesh Foundation announced it would hold the ' Swadhinata Concert ' on April 11 in four divisional cities , including Dhaka , from 3:00 pm to 11:00 pm . <p> This organisation had previously organised the " Bijoy Concert " at Manik Mia Avenue in Dhaka last December to commemorate Victory Day .
