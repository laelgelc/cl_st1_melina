<p> Several Bangladeshi musicians have officially joined forces to produce a new song , " Akashe Urche Mrito Lash " ( Where Lifeless Bodies Fly to the Sky ) . The lyrics , brimming with support for Palestine against genocidal atrocities , has been penned by Tushar Rahman . <p> The song 's overall composition was crafted by Jahid Nirob . Its title appears to be inspired by a recent viral video from Gaza , which captured a haunting scene of deceased bodies being hurled skyward by intense explosions in the conflict-stricken area . <p> The sound designed by Suman Parvez and recorded in the production studio Sound Of Silence is a quiet yet heartfelt protest . As per the production team , " The cries of the Palestinians deserve to be heard , and we carry the responsibility of listening . If our small voices can contribute , even in the slightest way , to the effort of saving them , that alone gives our protest meaning . Pray for Gaza . Stand for justice . Stand with humanity .
