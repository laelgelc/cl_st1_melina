<h> GUB holds peaceful protests in solidarity with Gaza <p> In a powerful demonstration of solidarity , the students , administrative staff , and faculty members of Green University of Bangladesh ( GUB ) took part in the " Global Strike for Gaza " movement on April 7 . Held peacefully on campus , the protests featured a human chain and heartfelt prayers , expressing unwavering support for the people of Palestine amid the ongoing humanitarian crisis . <p> With placards , Palestinian flags , and powerful messages , participants raised their voices against the ongoing injustice and violence in Gaza .
