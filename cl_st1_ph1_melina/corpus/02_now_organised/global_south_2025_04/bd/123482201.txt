<h> ' Except Israel ' to be reinstated on Bangladeshi passport <p> The government has instructed the authorities concerned to reinstate the " except Israel " clause on Bangladeshi passport , reversing a 2021 decision that had removed the words . <p> Security Services Division of the Home Affairs Ministry issued a letter on April 7 to the director general of the Department of Immigration and Passports to take necessary steps to reinstate the ' except Israel ' condition on Bangladeshi passport as was done previously . <p> ' Palestine Solidarity Movement Bangladesh ' held a ' March for Gaza ' rally on Saturday where speakers demanded the restoration of the ' except Israel ' clause and the cancellation of all agreements with Israel . <p> Earlier , Bangladeshi passport use to say : " This passport is valid for all countries of the world except Israel . "
