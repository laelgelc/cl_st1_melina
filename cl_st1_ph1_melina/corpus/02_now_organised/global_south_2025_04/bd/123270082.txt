<h> Schools , universities closed across Bangladesh in solidarity with Palestinians <p> Classes and examinations at various educational institutions across Bangladesh were suspended on Monday in solidarity with the Palestinian people , amid ongoing Israeli attacks on Gaza . <p> Dhaka University , Jahangirnagar University , and several other institutions , including North South University , Independent University , Bangladesh , BRAC University , and East West University , had officially announced on Sunday that all academic activities would remain suspended for the day in protest against Israel 's indiscriminate attacks on civilians in Gaza . <p> In response to a global strike titled " The world stops for Gaza : no work no school until the genocide stops " , observed on April 7 , 2025 , Dhaka University students announced on Sunday that all academic activities would be suspended on Monday to show solidarity with the people of Gaza . <p> A notice issued by Dhaka University authorities on Sunday stated that all classes and examinations would remain suspended , and university offices would be closed from 9am to 11am . <p> Jahangirnagar University issued a similar announcement @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to 12pm , with academic activities suspended for the entire day . <p> At Rajshahi University , students announced a rally in front of the university 's Senate Building on Paris Road from 11am to 12pm . <p> Across the country , many schools and colleges also remained closed , although not all issued formal notices . Students in districts including Cumilla , Pirojpur , and Barishal boycotted classes and exams , and staged demonstrations in support of Palestine . <p> The day 's other protest programmes in Dhaka included march by the National Olama Mashayekh Aimmah Council at North gate of Baitul Mukarram after Zuhr prayers , protest march by Bangladesh Jamaat-e-Islami across the country , protest by Jamaat-e-Islami Dhaka Metropolitan Northat Mohakhali Rail Gate at 4pm , solidarity rally organised by March for Palestine at Raju Sculpture , University of Dhaka at 4pm and " Palestine Resistance Week " by BRAC University studentsat Main gate , cafeteria , and campus of BRAC University from 9:00 AM -- 5:00 PM ( April 6 -- 9 ) .
