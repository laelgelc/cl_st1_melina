Beside her in photo is her chief of staff Joshue Santiago . ( JERRY S. TAN ) <p> BY virtue of a declaration made by President Ferdinand R. Marcos , Jr . of a ' Period of National Mourning ' from April 24 up to April 26 , 2025 in connection with the passing of Pope Francis , Manila Mayor Honey Lacuna enjoined all government offices in Manila , including Manila LGU offices and barangays , to fly the Philippine flag at half-mast during the said period . <p> Lacuna said that at the Manila City Hall , a special flag ceremony will be held on Saturday to raise the Philippine flag at half-mast in the morning and then lower it at sunset in the afternoon of the same day . <p> According to Lacuna , while President Ferdinand R.
