It was Ahmed al-Sharaa . <p> In Washington , London , Brussels -- they cheered behind their curtains . The narrative was ready . " Moderate Islamist defeats dictator . ' Some even called it a ' miracle . ' <p> But social constructivism warns us not to take these labels at face value .
