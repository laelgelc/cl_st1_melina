<h> The most trusted Pope <p> The word trust , pagtitiwala in Filipino , is a puny word for public regard for Francis the Pope and bishop of Rome . What about gratitude , admiration , appreciation , amazement , wonder , and , best and simplest of all , love ? I think all those words apply , but trust happens to be the consistent term used in 11 Social Weather Stations ( SWS ) surveys that cover the last three Popes . ( See " First Quarter 2015 Social Weather Survey : Pope Francis sets all-time trust record of net +81 , " www.sws.org.ph , 4/8/15 ) <p> This survey question is an SWS standard for all public figures , political or non-political , Filipino or foreign . ( We should get to Donald J. Trump in due time . ) It has two degrees of trust and two degrees of distrust , around a middle or neutral state of trust .
