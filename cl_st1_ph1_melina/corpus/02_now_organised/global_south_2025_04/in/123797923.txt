<p> The inception of Salaam Cola aligns with growing calls to boycott Coca-Cola due to its operations in the Atarot Settlement Industrial Zone in East Jerusalem , an area considered an illegal Israeli settlement under international law . Coca-Cola has faced criticism for its association with such settlements , leading consumers and businesses to seek alternatives that align with their ethical stances . <p> Within a year of its founding , Salaam Cola has widened in 14 countries across four continents , offering a bold alternative in the global soft drink market . <p> Salaam Cola 's mission resonates with various communities and businesses , particularly those aiming to support Palestinian causes . For example , Pakistani street food cafes and other food joints in the US have started stocking Salaam Cola , not just for its taste but as a statement of solidarity towards Palestinians . <p> Drawing inspiration from Salaam Cola , other brands like Cola Gaza and Palestine Drinks have emerged , each donating a portion of their profits to Palestinian charities .
