<h> Trump Needs to Back Off or Americans May Pay the Price <h> There is no evidence that Trump will succeed on any of the major fronts he has opened , writes Vivek Katju . <p> Donald Trump holds almost supreme power in the United States . Not only did he , as he often proudly proclaims , win all the seven swing states but he also won a majority of votes cast nationwide . The Senate and the House of Representatives are controlled by his Republican party , and the party is overawed by him . US institutions are bending to his will as he proceeds to literally , and figuratively , cut them to size . <p> The lower Federal judiciary is offering some resistance , but the Supreme Court is generally not standing in the way of Trump 's policies and programmes and their execution . <p> The Democratic party has been largely marginalised for the present . Protests such as ' Hands Off ' over the weekend are unlikely to impact Trump 's thinking . Yet , despite all this , has he gone too far @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to undertake in the domestic and foreign policy spheres ? To put it differently -- has he bitten more than he can chew ? <h> Immigration Policy Rejigs : A Mixed Bag <p> Trump contested the election on the peg of reinvigorating America and emphasised he was the only one who could do so . He promised to Make America Great Again ( MAGA ) . To do so he focused on illegal immigration , US loss of manufacturing and trade imbalance , and a loss of US influence and reputation abroad . <p> No sooner was he sworn in , Trump signed a slew of executive orders which included the establishment of the Department of Government Efficiency ( DOGE ) , run indirectly by his current crony Elon Musk . Musk 's team is running amok in not only downsizing US government bureaucracy but virtually eliminating some government departments altogether . But while this is occurring -- though with less intensity now -- Trump 's main focus has been on four areas : <p> Sending back illegal immigrants and tightening the screws on the entry of illegal immigrants <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ukraine war <p> Eliminating US trade imbalance and restoring the country as a manufacturing centre <p> How has he fared in these areas so far and what are the prognoses ? <p> A recent media report indicates that US has deported around 1,00,000 illegal immigrants over a period of around nine weeks since 20 January . This works out to around 50,000 deportations per month . The number of undocumented persons in the US is estimated to be around 11 million . At the current rate of deportations , Trump will succeed in sending back only about 10 percent of the total undocumented population in the US . <p> Will this count as success ? Where Trump is succeeding , though , is in ensuring a dramatic fall in the entry of illegals into the US since he assumed office . <p> After all , Trump can not really succeed in eliminating undocumented workers from the US because sections of the US economy can not do without them . His record on immigration will , at best , be a mixed one . <h> Israel-Gaza , Russia-Ukraine : ' Peace @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ agreement led to the release of 25 Israeli hostages and eight bodies and the release of about 2,000 Palestinians from Israeli custody . It also led to the stoppage of Israeli bombing and the withdrawal of its forces from some areas of Gaza . This phase was to lead to negotiations for the second phase which would witness the release of the remaining hostages estimated to be about 34 hostages and 24 bodies . <p> It was also expected to result in the return of a majority of displaced Gazans to their homes . The second phase has failed to take off . Israel has resumed bombing of Gaza . The exchange of hostages has remained suspended . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu met Trump on 7 April . The two leaders said new negotiations were on to get all the hostages out . Trump has separately warned Iran to expect to be subjected to unprecedented aerial bombing if it does not enter into a new nuclear deal with the US . On its part , Iran has refused to bend to Trump 's pressure and is not willing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Trump 's plan to turn Gaza into a Middle East Riviera by sending its people for resettlement to Egypt and Jordan has proved to be a non-starter till now . Trump is tenacious and may seek to pursue it at a later stage . If he does so , Egypt and Jordan will have no choice but to resist it . It may also inflame the Arab street . <p> Trump had proclaimed that he would soon end the Ukraine war . He has worked hard on Russian President Vladimir Putin to agree to a ceasefire . <p> For this purpose , it is being assumed that he has accepted that Russia would retain the Ukrainian territories that it currently occupies . He has also stopped the supply of all US weapons to Ukraine after his disastrous meeting with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy in the White House in early March . Trump has turned his back on Europe in his approach to the Ukraine war . <p> Trump had clearly hoped that after getting the benefit of the doubt from the US , Putin would soon agree to a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ And finally , not only a bilateral understanding would be reached between the two nations but also that Europe and Russia would agree on new terms for European security . All that is not happening because Putin , in the first place , wants Zelenskyy to go . Besides , with the balance of advantage on the battlefield currently with Russia , Putin has little incentive to rush into a ceasefire . <p> Trump is now frustrated with Putin and making this publicly known . His administration is threatening secondary sanctions on Russia . Whether the latter will succumb to these threats remains dubitable . <h> Liberation Day Disaster <p> Trump has gone ahead and imposed reciprocal tariffs and called the day he did so as ' Liberation Day ' . He obviously believes that US markets are of such great importance to all countries that they will ensure balanced bilateral trade , and the important players will make investments in the US . The immediate economic fallout for the US and the rest of the world has been disastrous . <p> Stock markets , including American , have plunged @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ inevitably have to pay more for imported products . <p> All countries are trying to cope with the new situation through different strategies stretching from reducing their tariffs to US imports to imposing countervailing tariffs on them . On his part , Trump has called on Americans to endure short term pain for long-term gain . The question , though , is if his step will result in any real gain for the US , including in decisively increasing in the manufacturing sector . That is still an imponderable . <p> There is no evidence that Trump will succeed on any of the major fronts he has opened . The chances are that he will have to compromise on all four . But then , Trump is unconventional and will declare victory in all these areas , even when there is none . <p> ( The writer is a former Secretary West , Ministry of External Affairs .
