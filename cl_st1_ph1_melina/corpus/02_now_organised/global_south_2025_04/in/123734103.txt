<h> In Short <p> Thousands protested Trump policies across US <p> Events included marches , food drives , and teach-ins <p> Concerns raised about civil liberties under Trump rule <p> Thousands of Americans gathered in cities and towns nationwide on April 19 to protest US President Donald Trump 's policies . The demonstrations , which coincide with the anniversary of the American Revolutionary War 's opening battles in 1775 , carried a mix of civic resistance and historical symbolism . <p> The events ranged from large marches in major cities to quieter community actions like food drives , teach-ins , and volunteer work . In Manhattan , protesters marched from the New York Public Library toward Central Park , chanting , " No fear , no hate , no ICE in our state , " as they passed Trump Tower . <h> What 's Fuelling The Protests <p> Demonstrators in front of the White House raised similar concerns about the country 's direction and the protection of civil liberties .
