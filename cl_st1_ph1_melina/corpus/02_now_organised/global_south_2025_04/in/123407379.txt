<h> ' US close to getting Israeli hostages in Gaza back , ' said Donald Trump as he claimed of having dealing with Hamas <p> Palestinians inspect the damage at the site of an Israeli airstrike(AFP) <p> President Donald Trump on Thursday , April 10 , announced that the United States was close to getting all Israeli hostages being held in Gaza back to Israel . Donald Trump also claimed that he was dealing with both Israel and Hamas , but he gave no other details about the talks . <p> " Getting close to getting hostages in Gaza back , " Trump announced , adding that he was dealing with both Hamas and Israel to secure peace in the region . <p> Donald Trump had met Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu at the White House on Monday , April 7 , and emphasised that end of war in Gaza was " not-too-distant future " . <p> When asked on his election promise to end war in Gaza , Donald Trump said , " I would like to see the war stop , and I think the war will stop @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ too-distant future . " <p> The US President had also said that the progress was on to secure release of all prisoners held by Hamas in Gaza . <p> The development has come as Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) detonated 35 buildings and tunnels in a 24 hour period in military operations in Gaza overnight and this morning . <p> An IDF Spokesperson said , " IDF forces continue to operate in the Gaza Strip and destroy dozens of terrorist infrastructures in the area , the Air Force attacked approximately 35 terrorist targets over the past 24 hours . " <p> " Over the past 24 hours , the forces eliminated a number of terrorists , located and destroyed terrorist infrastructures and continued to operate to establish operational control in the area . " <p> Israel attacked Gaza Strip after Hamas-led fighters launched a multi-front strike on southern Israel on October 7 in 2023 , killing some 1,200 people and taking 251 hostage , according to Israeli tallies . Since then , Israel has so far killed more than 50,000 Palestinians , according to local health authorities .
