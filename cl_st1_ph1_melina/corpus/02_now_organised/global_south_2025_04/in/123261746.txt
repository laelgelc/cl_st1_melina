His report noted severe weight loss and muscle wasting , and cited complaints from Ahmad about inadequate food since at least December . <p> Ahmad collapsed and died last month in Megiddo Prison . An Israeli investigation is ongoing . Physicians for Human Rights Israel said Ahmad is the youngest Palestinian prisoner to die since the Gaza war began . He was detained during a September raid in the West Bank for allegedly throwing stones . <p> Solomon observed the autopsy on March 27 at Abu Kabir Forensic Institute , which has not released its findings .
