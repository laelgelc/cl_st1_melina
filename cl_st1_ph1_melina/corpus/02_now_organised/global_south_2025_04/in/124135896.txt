<h> Letters to Editor <p> Updated - April 29 , 2025 at 09:47 PM . <p> Focus on job security <p> This refers to the recent reports highlighting employees ' reluctance to switch jobs amid prevailing global economic uncertainties ( April 29 ) . The ongoing geopolitical tensions , inflationary pressures , and slowing economic growth in major economies have triggered cautious behaviour among both employers and employees . Many organisations , especially in the tech sector , are planning lay-offs , either to cut costs by replacing high-salaried staff with lower-paid but skilled recruits , or due to increased automation and AI-driven operational efficiencies . <p> For employers , shrinking profit margins and unpredictable market conditions are key drivers behind workforce optimisation strategies . On the other hand , employees are choosing job security over career mobility , leading to stagnation in roles and additional workload . <p> Rather than passively clinging to their current positions , employees are encouraged to proactively focus on reskilling and upskilling , particularly in areas influenced by AI and emerging technologies . <p> Srinivasan Velamur <p> Chennai <p> Honour ethics , if not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ plagiarism ' in arbitration ' ( April 28 ) .
