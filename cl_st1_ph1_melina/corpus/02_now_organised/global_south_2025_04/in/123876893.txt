It promoted a normative and legalistic approach to international politics , rooted in the belief that ethical principles and cooperative institutions could foster peace and progress among nations . Idealists argued that a state 's internal values -- democracy , justice , and the rule of law -- should shape its foreign policy . <p> US President Woodrow Wilson epitomised idealist thinking through his Fourteen Points ( 1918 ) , which called for open diplomacy , self-determination , and the establishment of a general association of nations , ultimately leading to the League of Nations . <p> Idealism was rooted in Enlightenment optimism about human rationality and moral advancement , suggesting that international relations could be governed by law and reason rather than by power and conflict . <p> However , idealism faced significant criticism during the interwar years .
