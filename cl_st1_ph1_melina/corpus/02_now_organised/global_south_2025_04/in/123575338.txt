The police tracked the individuals through CCTV footage and has pledged swift arrests in the matter <p> Rawalpindi : Anti-Israel Protestors in Pakistan 's Rawalpindi stormed into and vandalised a KFC outlet packed with customers in the Rawalpindi Military Garrison town on Sunday ( April 13 ) . A group of protestors also harassed the customers and shouted slogans inside the KFC . <p> The police on Monday said they had traced the miscreants involved in vandalising the international fast food outlet in Rawalpindi 's Saddar area . The police tracked the miscreants through CCTV footage and have assured swift action into the matter . A video clip of the incident is making rounds on the internet . <p> In the viral video , some men can be seen abusing customers and vandalising the restaurant , asking customers to leave . People can be seen fleeing the spot in fear . Palistine 's flag can also be spotted in the video . <p> According to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ manager of the KFC Saddar branch , a group of 10 -- 12 armed men stormed the premises around 8:30 pm on Sunday , 13 April , harassed customers , and shouted slogans . " When we tried to stop them , they hurled abuses and threatened to kill us , " the complainant stated . <p> Reason For Protest ? <p> KFC and other US brands are being boycotted in Pakistan , Bangladesh and some regions due to perceived links to Israel and the ongoing conflict in Gaza . <p> A similar incident took place in neighbouring Bangladesh , over the same issue . Here , outlets of international brands such as Bata , KFC , Pizza Hut , and Puma , perceived by the agitators as having ties to Israel , were vandalised and plundered .
