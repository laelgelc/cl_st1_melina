" <p> Following the Mass , parishioners carried a large portrait of Pope Francis in a prayerful procession by singing hymns . " <p> St Mary 's Basilica in Secunderabad celebrated a special Mass . The preacher recalled the Pope 's habit of addressing crowds as " brothers and sisters " and his appeals for peace in conflict zones , including Gaza . Parishioners reflected on his 2019 gesture of kneeling to kiss the feet of South Sudanese leaders to urge unity , and his visits to children with AIDS and cancer at Gemelli Hospital . <p> Church officials said similar memorial services were held in parishes throughout the archdiocese.
