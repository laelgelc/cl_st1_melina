<p> U.S. airstrikes on Yemen 's Ras Isa oil terminal resulted in at least 30 fatalities and wounded 80 others , according to Houthi-run media . The strikes , carried out under President Donald Trump 's ongoing military directive , targeted infrastructure controlled by the Iran-aligned Houthi movement . <p> U.S. Central Command confirmed the operation at the Red Sea port in Hodeida governorate but did not report any deaths or injuries . " This facility supported illicit revenue used by the Houthis for regional destabilization , " CENTCOM said . " The objective was to degrade resources fueling Houthi operations , not to target civilians . " <p> The Houthis claimed medical responders and port workers were among the casualties . Their official SABA agency labeled the strike as an attack on Yemen 's national sovereignty and accused the U.S. of hitting a civilian port used by the population for essential fuel . <p> The Ras Isa facility , linked by pipeline to Marib , remains under Houthi control amid a broader suspension of Yemen 's oil exports due to the conflict . Several failed attempts were made against U.S. warships . <p> Washington has linked the air offensive to countering both the Houthis ' naval threat and Iran 's nuclear activities , framing the operation as part of a wider regional security effort .
