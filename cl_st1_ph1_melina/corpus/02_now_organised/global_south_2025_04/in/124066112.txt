Seventy years after the Bandung Conference , India will do well to use this moment to translate its multipolar intentions into reality <p> Multipolarity is gaining salience around the world . It has been on the agenda of developing countries since 1955 , when the Asian-African Conference was held in Bandung , Indonesia ( April 18 -- 24 ) . Seven decades on , the relevance of its equal-power-distribution concept is being acknowledged even by proponents of unipolarity , reflecting an understanding of a world in profound transition .
