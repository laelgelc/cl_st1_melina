<h> US Conducts Fresh Airstrikes On Yemen <p> Sanaa : The US military carried out 10 airstrikes across northern Yemen , targeting multiple locations . <p> The strikes reportedly hit Al-Salem district in northern Saada province , Al-Munira district in western Hodeidah province , and the Vocational Institute in Al-Sawma'ah district of central Al-Bayda province on late Saturday night , Xinhua news agency reported quoting Houthi-run al-Masirah TV . <p> No casualties have been reported so far . On Friday , the US Central Command said on X that its operations against Houthis are continuing . <p> The United States resumed its air campaign against Houthi forces on March 15 , stating that the strikes were aimed at deterring the group from launching attacks against Israeli and US naval assets in the Red Sea . <p> The Houthis , who control much of northern Yemen , said they would halt their operations if Israel ends its offensive in Gaza and permits the delivery of critical humanitarian aid into the Gaza Strip . <p> Earlier on Thursday the death toll from US airstrikes on the Red Sea port city of Hodeidah @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Houthi-controlled health authorities reported . The majority of the victims were women and children , they said , adding that the death toll increased after the badly injured died in the hospital . <p> The tragedy occurred when US military warplanes struck homes in the Amin Muqbil residential neighbourhood , according to health authorities and local residents , who also shared video footage on social media following the airstrikes .
