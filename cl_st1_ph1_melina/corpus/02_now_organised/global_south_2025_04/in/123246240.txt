<h> UK Foreign Secretary David Lammy condemned the detention and refusal of entry of two British MPs by Israeli authorities as unacceptable and deeply concerning . The MPs , part of an unofficial parliamentary delegation , were barred due to plans to document Israeli security actions and spread anti-Israel rhetoric . <p> Lammy further emphasised the UK government 's commitment to supporting the MPs and reiterated its focus on achieving a ceasefire and engaging in negotiations to " stop the bloodshed , free the hostages and end the conflict in Gaza . " <p> " It is unacceptable , counterproductive , and deeply concerning that two British MPs on a parliamentary delegation to Israel have been detained and refused entry by the Israeli authorities .
