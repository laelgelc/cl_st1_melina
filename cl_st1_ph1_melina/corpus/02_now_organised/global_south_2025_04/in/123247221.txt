<h> Diplomatic Push for Peace in Gaza : UAE and Israel in Talks <h> The UAE 's foreign minister met with his Israeli counterpart in Abu Dhabi to discuss a Gaza ceasefire and hostage release . The meeting underscores the UAE 's ongoing diplomatic ties with Israel , established formally in 2020 under the Abraham Accords , despite the ongoing conflict in Gaza . <p> The United Arab Emirates Foreign Minister , Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed , held discussions with Israel 's Gideon Saar in Abu Dhabi . The meeting focused on securing a ceasefire in Gaza and the release of hostages , as reported by the UAE state news agency . <p> The UAE made history as a significant Arab nation to formalize relations with Israel through the U.S.-brokered Abraham Accords in 2020 . This diplomatic relationship has persisted despite the Israeli conflict in Gaza . <p> These talks highlight the UAE 's commitment to maintaining diplomatic channels with Israel while advocating for stability and peace in the region .
