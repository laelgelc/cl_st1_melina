<h> Recipe for Disaster -- Power of a Few Persons to Take Decisions that can Kill Millions <h> Share : <p> One aspect of recent world affairs that should cause increasing concern is the increasing possibility that just a few persons with very high power concentrated in their hands , acting largely on their own , can take decisions that will end up killing millions of people . <p> This worrying possibility consists of three parts . Firstly , a lot of power is concentrated in a few countries . Secondly , within these countries there are frightening tendencies of power to make highly destructive decisions getting concentrated in a few persons . Thirdly , there are increasing signs of non-transparency as well as breakdown of democratic checks and balances leading to more arbitrary decision-making . <p> Of course , in the course of history , very excessive power has existed at times in the hands of certain persons with strong destructive tendencies , with horrible results .
