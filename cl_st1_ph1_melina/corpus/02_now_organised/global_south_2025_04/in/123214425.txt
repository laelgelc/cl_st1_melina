<p> Microsoft 's 50th anniversary celebration was disrupted on Friday when a pro-Palestinian protest broke out during a keynote by AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman . The interruption marked the latest flashpoint in growing backlash over the tech industry 's involvement in supplying artificial intelligence tools to the Israeli military amid the war in Gaza . <p> According to the Associated Press , Suleyman had been outlining new product updates and a long-term vision for Microsoft Copilot when the protest began . Suleyman paused his address while Aboussad continued , " You claim that you care about using AI for good but Microsoft sells AI weapons to the Israeli military .
