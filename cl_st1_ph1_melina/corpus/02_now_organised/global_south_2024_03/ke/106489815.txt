<h> CS Duale delivers Ruto 's message to Egyptian President <p> Defence Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale on Wednesday delivered President William Ruto 's message to his Egyptian counterpart President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi . <p> In his message , President Ruto emphasized on Kenya 's commitment to further strengthening the outstanding relations between the two countries in all fields . <p> President El-Sisi expressed Egypt 's pride in its historical relations with Kenya , as a model of cooperation between brothers on the continent . <p> The meeting tackled issues of joint cooperation , in light of the two countries ' keenness on bolstering the partnership and cooperation in military and security areas . <p> The discussions also touched on regional and international developments , notably the situations in the Gaza Strip , the Red Sea , and the Horn of Africa , as well as their implications for peace and security in the African continent . <p> The Kenyan Defense Minister commended Egypt 's wise role in consolidating security and stability in the continent , affirming his country 's commitment to coordinating and cooperating with Egypt for their common benefit and to promote joint African action across the continent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ El-Sisi during his three-day visit to Egypt . The meeting was also attended by Minister of Defense and Military Production , General Mohamed Zaki.
