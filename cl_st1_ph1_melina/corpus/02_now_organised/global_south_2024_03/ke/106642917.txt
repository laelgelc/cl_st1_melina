<p> King Mohammed VI of Morocco , who is also the Chairman of the Al Quds Committee , has given His Very High Instructions to deploy a humanitarian food aid operation for the Palestinian population in Gaza and the Holy City of Al Quds , according to a statement from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs , African Cooperation , and Moroccan Expatriates . <p> The aid , which coincides with the advent of the holy month of Ramadan , aims to alleviate the suffering of Palestinian populations , notably their most vulnerable sections , according to the statement . <p> As a result , the King , has ordered approximately 40 tons of food , including staples , to be sent to the people of Gaza . <p> In addition to the institutional relief , which is mostly provided through the Mohammed V Foundation for Solidarity , the King has funded a significant portion of the aid that has been sent , especially that which is provided to infants and small children , using monies from his own personal reserves . <p> The ministry pointed out that since the outbreak of armed hostilities more than five months ago , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ unprecedented land route , its humanitarian aid , which will be delivered directly to recipient populations . <p> As a result , 1,000 lunches would be provided daily to Palestinians residing in the city , and 2,000 food baskets will be given to 2,000 Al Quds households . <p> The help also covers Jerusalem Hospital 's establishment of an emergency coordination room . <p> Moroccan aid is transported by direct land route .
