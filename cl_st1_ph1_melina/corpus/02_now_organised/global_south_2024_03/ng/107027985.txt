<h> Senate President Akpabio returns to Nigeria after IPU meeting in Geneva <p> Abuja ( Sundiata Post ) -- President of the 10th Senate , Godswill Akpabio has arrived Abuja with his delegation after the Inter-Parliamentary ( IPU ) meeting in Geneva , Switzerland . <p> Sundiata Post recalls that Akpabio was accompanied by the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives , Hon Benjamin Kalu and Senator Asuquo Ekpenyong for the meeting that took place at the International Conference Centre of Geneva , ( ICCG ) Switzerland from Saturday , 23rd to 27th of March , 2024 . <p> Senate President said that from the engagement in IPU , Nigeria will gain parliamentary co-operation adding that the image of Nigeria has improved dramatically . <p> According to Akpabio , a lot of bilateral discussions with other parliamentarians in Africa and across the globe took place which will bring co-operation in diverse ways . <p> The Senate President said his statement on Gaza triggered a lot of reactions . He stated that after his call for hostilities in Gaza , everything happening on both parts now are tinting towards ceasing of hostilities .
