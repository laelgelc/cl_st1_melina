<h> Russia , China veto US Security Council bid on Gaza ceasefire <p> Russia and China on Friday vetoed a US-led draft resolution at the Security Council on a ceasefire in Gaza . <p> The United States , Israel 's main ally which has vetoed previous ceasefire calls , put forward the resolution which for the first time would have supported " the imperative of an immediate and sustained ceasefire " and condemned the October 7 attack by Hamas . <p> Russia and China exercised their vetoes , Algeria also voted against and Guyana abstained . Moscow accused Washington of a " hypocritical spectacle " that does not pressure Israel . <h> Related Posts <p> The other 11 Security Council members voted in favour , including permanent members France and Britain . <p> Russia 's ambassador , Vasily Nebenzia , said that the United States was doing nothing to rein in Israel , mocking Washington for speaking of a ceasefire after " Gaza has been virtually wiped off the face of the Earth . " <p> " We have observed a typical hypocritical spectacle . The American product is exceedingly politicized , with the sole purpose being to play @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of some kind of a mention of a ceasefire in Gaza , " he said . <p> The resolution will " ensure the impunity of Israel , whose crimes are not even assessed in the draft . " <p> The draft links a ceasefire to ongoing talks , led by Qatar with support from the United States and Egypt , to halt the war in return for Hamas releasing hostages .
