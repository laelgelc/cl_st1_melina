<h> Missile Attack From Lebanon Kills 1 , Injures 7 In Northern Israel <p> One person was killed and seven others were injured in an anti-tank missile attack in northern Israel from southern Lebanon on Monday , according to the country 's national ambulance service . Two of the injured were in serious condition , Magen David Adom said in a statement . Israeli Army Radio said the anti-tank missile struck an orchard in Galilee in northern Israel . The Israeli public broadcaster KAN said the dead person and the injured were all foreign laborers , apparently from Thailand . Tension flared along the border between Lebanon and Israel amid intermittent exchanges of fire between Israeli forces and Hezbollah , in the deadliest clashes since the two sides fought a full-scale war in 2006 . The border tension comes amid an Israeli military offensive in the Gaza Strip that has killed over 30,534 people following a cross-border attack by the Palestinian group Hamas on Oct. 7 last year .
