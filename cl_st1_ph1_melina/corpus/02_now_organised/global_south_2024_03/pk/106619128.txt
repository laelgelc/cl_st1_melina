country or current regional tensions , experts said on Tuesday . <p> The Security Belt-2024 joint exercise kicked off on Monday local time after Chinese warships arrived in waters near Chabahar , Iran and rendezvoused with participating Iranian and Russian naval vessels , China Central Television ( CCTV ) reported on Tuesday . <p> It marks the fourth joint naval exercise by the three countries since 2019 . <p> According to an annual schedule and consensuses reached by the three countries , the joint exercise held near the Gulf of Oman started on Monday , and will last until Friday , CCTV reported . <p> With the theme of jointly building peace and security , the exercise mainly features anti-piracy and search and rescue training courses , and is organized in three phases , namely the
