<p> My mother was recently travelling from Abbottabad to visit me in Lahore . On the bus , she found herself seated next to an overwhelmed mother and her four children . Before she knew it , she was acting loco parentis to two of the children , who were glued to her for the entire ride . <p> My mother asked one of the daughters which city she preferred , Lahore or Abbottabad . The girl , unable to distinguish between the two , deflected by saying , " aap ko pata hai Pakistan ko Allah miyan ne apne haathon se banaya hai ?
