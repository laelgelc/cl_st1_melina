<p> RAWALPINDI , Pakistan : The Chief of Army Staff ( COAS ) General Syed Asim Munir on Tuesday presided over the 263rd Corps Commanders ' Conference ( CCC ) held at GHQ in Rawalpindi , according to the Inter Services Public Relations ( ISPR ) . <p> The forum paid rich tribute to the supreme sacrifices of Shuhadas including officers and men of the Armed Forces , Law Enforcement Agencies ( LEAs ) and the citizens who have laid their lives for ensuring peace and stability in the Country . <p> The forum resolved that terrorists , their facilitators and abettors , working on behest of hostile forces to destabilise Pakistan , will be dealt with full might of the state . <p> The COAS directed commanders to continue consolidating the gains of efforts against terrorism and militancy . <p> The forum expressed concerns over continued repression of Kashmiris in Indian Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir ( IIOJK ) and condemned the unabated human rights violations by India . <p> The forum reaffirmed that Pakistan shall continue to support Kashmiris politically , diplomatically and morally at all levels . <p> The forum also expressed complete solidarity with the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ war crimes being perpetrated in Gaza . <p> The COAS said that " the Palestinian people have unequivocal diplomatic , moral and political support of the Pakistani nation and we will continue to support the principled stance of our brethren for enduring resolution of the Palestinian issue " . <p> The forum appreciated efforts of civil administration , LEAs and security forces for providing all out assistance during general elections in the light of the Election Commission of Pakistan 's ( ECP ) guidelines for creating a safe &amp; secure environment for the electoral process , despite overwhelming odds . <p> The forum noted that the armed forces of Pakistan at a great peril to their primary responsibility , provided security environment for the conduct of general elections as per the given mandate , and had nothing to do with the electoral process . <p> However , the forum expressed dismay that some vested small segments of polity and media especially social media have been maligning Armed Forces of Pakistan with unsubstantiated allegations of interference which is highly deplorable .
