<p> As Muslims began the holy month of Ramadan , the UN chief on Monday reiterated his call for a cease-fire in Gaza , and urged actions to avoid further " preventable " deaths . <p> " Yet even though Ramadan has begun -- the killing , bombing and bloodshed continue in Gaza , " Antonito Guterres said at a press encounter . <p> " My strongest appeal today is to honor the spirit of Ramadan by silencing the guns -- and removing all obstacles to ensure the delivery of lifesaving aid at the speed and massive scale required , " said Guterres . <p> He also called for an " immediate release of all hostages " taken by Hamas . <p> " We must act to avoid more preventable deaths , " he said . <p> " We have witnessed month after month of civilian killing and destruction at a level that is unprecedented in all my years as Secretary-General , " he added . <p> On life-saving aid , Guterres said it is " coming in trickles -- if it comes at all , " and asserted that " international humanitarian law lies in tatters .
