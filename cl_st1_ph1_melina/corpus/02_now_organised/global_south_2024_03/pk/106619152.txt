I do n't think anyone can guarantee success . The only thing I think you can guarantee is that the alternatives are worse . " <p> Burns ' statement came hours after mediator Qatar said Israel and Hamas were not close to a deal on a cessation of hostilities in Gaza and releasing hostages .
