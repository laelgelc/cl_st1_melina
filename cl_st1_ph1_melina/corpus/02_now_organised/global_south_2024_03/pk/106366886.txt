<h> OIC to hold meeting on Palestine next week <p> The Organization of Islamic Cooperation said on Friday it will hold an " extraordinary meeting " of its Council of Foreign Ministers on March 5 " to discuss the continued Israeli aggression against the Palestinian people . " <p> The pan-Islamic body in a statement said the session will convene at its headquarters in the Saudi Arabian city of Jeddah . <p> Last November , the OIC and the Arab League held a joint summit in Riyadh , which adopted a number of decisions including assigning a committee to tour world capitals and seek to stop the Israeli war against the Palestinian people . <p> Israeli attacks in the Gaza Strip since the Oct. 7 Hamas incursion have killed more than 30,000 people , most of them women and children . The initial Hamas attacks killed about 1,200 people . <p> The onslaught has also caused mass destruction , displacement and conditions for a famine in the besieged Palestinian enclave .
