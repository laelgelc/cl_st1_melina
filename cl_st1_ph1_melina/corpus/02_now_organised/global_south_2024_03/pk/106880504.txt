Seven major categories of online offences have been identified , including inciting extremism and violent terrorism through social media content . <p> Under the act tabled in the Canadian parliament on February 24 , 2024 , the Digital Safety Commission of Canada will be established . The commission will be authorized to ensure that social media platforms are transparent , responsible and accountable . It also aims to protect minors from the negative effects of social media , block access to unwanted indecent content within 24 hours , and keep records of social media activities .
