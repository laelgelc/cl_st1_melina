<p> Hamas recently made a shocking revelation regarding the Israel-Hamas truce deal , citing significant disagreements during negotiations for a Gaza truce . Talks on a truce and potential exchange of hostages and inmates persisted in Doha this week . Senior Hamas official Sami Abu Zuhri blamed Israel for the truce talks ' failure , revealing a " bridging proposal " by the United States . The proposal entailed releasing jailed Palestinians in exchange for Hamas freeing hostages in a new Gaza truce , with Israel suspending its offensive for six weeks if a deal was reached . Negotiations were indirect , with an Israeli delegation led by Mossad Chief David Barnea and CIA Director William Burns mediating . However , reports emerged of the U.S. urging Qatar to pressure Hamas further , potentially leading to Hamas ' expulsion if no progress was made .
