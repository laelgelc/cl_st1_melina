<p> New poll shows young Americans angry over President Joe Biden 's Gaza war handling US election <p> 1 hour ago <h> New poll shows young Americans angry over President Joe Biden 's Gaza war handling US election <p> Mar 06 , 2024 , 01:00AM ISTSource : TOI.in <p> President Joe Biden faces significant backlash ahead of the 2024 US polls . A recent survey reveals 60% of US voters expressing dissatisfaction with Biden 's handling of the Gaza war . A majority of Americans believe Biden has not done enough to assist Palestinians in Gaza . Biden 's disapproval ratings have surged by 8 points since the December 2023 survey . He is particularly disliked among young Americans who feel he lost control of the Gaza situation . Meanwhile , Donald Trump is gaining momentum in caucuses , securing a string of victories . The former president , heading into Super Tuesday , finished first at 12 caucus sites , solidifying his position .
