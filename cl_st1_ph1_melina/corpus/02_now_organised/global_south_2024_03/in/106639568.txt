<p> Israeli forces open fire on Palestinians waiting for food , Gaza government office reports . The death toll of Palestinians waiting for aid rises to 400 , with 1,300 injured , as per Gaza government sources . Palestinian news agency WAFA reports at least a dozen killed and over 20 injured in Israeli bombings on Palestinians in northern Gaza . Over 31,000 Palestinians have been killed and over 72,700 injured since October 7 , 2023 , with no response or statement from the Israeli army on the recent incident . With no end to the war in sight , Palestinians begin fasting for the holy month of Ramadan , while hunger and humanitarian crises worsen across the Gaza Strip . The US , Qatar , and Egypt had hoped for a cease-fire before Ramadan . The Gaza Health Ministry reports 67 Palestinians killed by Israeli strikes in the past 24 hours .
