<p> Qatar 's foreign ministry spokesperson Majed Al-Ansari expressed cautious optimism about the prospect of a ceasefire and hostage deal between Israel and Hamas , despite negotiations being far from reaching an agreement . Despite ongoing talks involving US , Qatari , and Egyptian mediators , the Muslim holy month of Ramadan commenced without the desired truce and hostage exchange . Al-Ansari highlighted the challenges , stating that both sides have yet to find common ground on key issues . Efforts are underway to reach a resolution within the confines of Ramadan , although no specific timeline was provided . Qatar remains committed to establishing a permanent ceasefire in Gaza rather than a short-term truce . The situation on the ground remains complex , with tensions stemming from the conflict initiated by Hamas 's attack on Israel in October .
