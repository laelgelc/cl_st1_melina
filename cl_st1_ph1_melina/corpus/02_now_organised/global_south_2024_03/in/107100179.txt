<p> Egypt 's rejection of Israel 's proposal to deploy Arab or joint forces to the Gaza Strip has garnered attention as a significant diplomatic development . According to a high-ranking official speaking to Sky News , Cairo firmly declined Israel 's suggestion , indicating a clear stance on regional security matters . The proposal , put forth by Israeli Security Minister Yoav Galant , aimed to enhance security around the ongoing construction of a seaport in Gaza under U.S. supervision . Meanwhile , President Biden 's announcement of the U.S. constructing a port to facilitate aid delivery to Gaza aligns with efforts to address humanitarian concerns in the region . Secretary of State Blinken 's discussions with Arab countries , including Cairo , regarding ' Arab troops ' underscore the complex geopolitical dynamics and diplomatic efforts underway in the Middle East .
