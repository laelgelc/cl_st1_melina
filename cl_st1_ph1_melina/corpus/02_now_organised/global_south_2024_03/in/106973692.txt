<h> Israel strikes deep into northeast Lebanon <h> Israel conducted a deep strike into Lebanon , targeting Hezbollah in the Hermel area , the most extensive raid since hostilities began over the Israel-Hamas war . The strike followed ongoing clashes along the Israel-Lebanon border , with Hezbollah supporting Hamas . The Israeli military targeted Hezbollah 's aerial unit in Zboud , deep inside Lebanon , responding to an attack on its aerial control unit in northern Israel . Hezbollah claimed multiple attacks on Israeli targets , including a missile strike on the Meron air control base .
