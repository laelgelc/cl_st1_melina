<p> A disturbing video depicting the Israeli army shooting down Palestinians has surfaced online , shared by Al-Jazeera . The footage captures the shocking incident near Al-Nabulsi roundabout , where IDF troops are seen opening fire on men walking along Gaza 's Al-Rashid Street . The Palestinian men , upon noticing the troops , waved a white fabric , a common symbol of non-aggression . Despite this , Israeli soldiers can be seen shooting at one of the men . The video further shows Israeli soldiers using a bulldozer to bury both men , sparking strong condemnation from Hamas , which called it a brutal act of extermination . Hamas has urged the UN to hold Israel accountable for war crimes against Gaza civilians . In response , the Israeli army stated that a thorough examination is necessary to assess the incident 's details . These developments unfold amid eight recent Israeli attacks across Gaza , resulting in the forceful displacement of nearly 2 million people .
