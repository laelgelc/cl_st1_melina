<p> Chinese diplomat Wang Kejian met Hamas political leader Ismail Haniyeh in Qatar , marking the first meeting between a Chinese and Hamas official since the October 7 attacks . This encounter followed Wang 's visits to Israel and the occupied West Bank , where he became the first known envoy Beijing sent to either location since the conflict began . During the meeting , Wang and Haniyeh discussed the Gaza conflict and other pertinent issues , with China 's ambassador to Qatar , Cao Xiaolin , also in attendance . Haniyeh commended China 's role in international forums like the UN Security Council and the International Court of Justice . Hamas previously met with Cao in Qatar last month , although China 's Foreign Ministry and its embassy in Qatar have not disclosed details about these engagements . Wang 's visit aligns with China 's efforts to elevate its status as a mediator for peace and increase its global presence . However , China has refrained from condemning Hamas or labeling it a terrorist group .
