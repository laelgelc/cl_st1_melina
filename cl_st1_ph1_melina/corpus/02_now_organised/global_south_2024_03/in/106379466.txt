Houthi leaders now accuse UK Prime Minister Rishi Sunak , holding him responsible for the ship 's assault . Mohammed Ali al-Houthi , from Yemen 's Supreme Political Council , shocked the world on ' X ' by blaming Sunak and the UK government , connecting the attack to their support for Israel 's actions in Gaza .
