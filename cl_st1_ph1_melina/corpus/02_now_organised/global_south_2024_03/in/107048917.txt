<p> In one video circulating online , an Israeli soldier is shown holding lingerie and grinning while dangling it over the open mouth of a fellow soldier who holds a gun . This video is just one of many posts depicting Israeli troops in Gaza displaying lingerie and mannequins , as reported by Reuters . <p> Younis Tirawi , a self-described Palestinian reporter , reposted several of these images , prompting further scrutiny . Reuters verified eight of these posts on Instagram or YouTube . Ravina Shamdasani , a spokesperson for the UN Human Rights Office , condemned the sharing of such images , calling it " demeaning to Palestinian women , and all women .
