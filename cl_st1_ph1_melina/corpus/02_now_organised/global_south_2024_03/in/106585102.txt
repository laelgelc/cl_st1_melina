<h> Video : Celebrities wear their politics at Oscars 2024 <p> At the 2024 Oscars , a striking symbol of solidarity and political activism adorned the lapels of many celebrities as they walked the red carpet . <p> The red pins , representing Artists4Ceasefire , were not just fashion statements but powerful calls for peace amidst the devastating Israel-Hamas conflict that had claimed over 30,000 lives since October . Notable figures like Grammy winner Billie Eilish , Oscar nominee Mark Ruffalo , and comedian Ramy Youssef , among others , chose to wear these pins to spotlight the urgent need for a ceasefire , the release of hostages , and the delivery of humanitarian aid to civilians in Gaza . <p> The pin , featuring an outline of a hand around a black heart , symbolized collective support for ending the violence and compassion for those affected by the war .
