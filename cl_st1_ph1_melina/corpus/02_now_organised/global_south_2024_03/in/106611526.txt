<p> Recent reports indicate escalating tensions in the Israeli-Palestinian conflict as the Israeli Army reportedly attempted to assassinate Hamas leader Marwan Issa . The IDF carried out an airstrike on a building believed to be his hiding place , and Hamas is investigating whether Issa was killed in the attack . Issa , ranking behind only Gaza strongman Yahya Sinwar and Mohammed Deif , was the apparent target of the strike in Nuseirat Camp . The death toll rose in the Gaza Strip amid intensified military operations by Israel , including raids on March 9 in Gaza 's Nuseirat Refugee Camp . Israeli forces engaged Hamas militants in ongoing strikes , revealing tunnels and a weapons cache in southern Gaza as military and intelligence efforts intensified to dismantle Hamas infrastructure . Both IDF and Hamas scrambled to confirm the death of a senior militant following the recent strike .
