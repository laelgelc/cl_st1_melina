<h> Gaza Truce Talks : Hamas ' demands clash with Israel 's conditions <p> Mar 05 , 2024 , 01:00AM ISTSource : TOI.in <p> As of now , negotiators are working to finalize another truce between Israel and Hamas . Hamas has submitted a list of 20 prisoners to Israel , with the fate of the truce depending on Israel 's response . The ceasefire deal aims to ensure at least 40 days of peace , including resettlement efforts for displaced Gazans . The war , which saw Hamas launching a surprise attack on Israel on October 7 resulting in over 1200 deaths and 250 hostages , prompted an all-out military campaign by Israel . With over 30,000 killed in Gaza and more than 1 million internally displaced , UN agencies warn of famine . The ceasefire , if achieved before Ramadan , would bring much-needed relief to Gazans .
