<p> Singapore -- Oil prices slipped on Friday on the possibility of a nearing Gaza ceasefire that could ease geopolitical concern in the Middle East , while a stronger dollar and faltering US petrol demand also weighed on prices . <p> Brent crude futures fell 42c , or 0.5% , to $85.36 a barrel by 2.03am GMT . US crude futures shed 40 cents , or 0.5% , to $80.67 a barrel . <p> Both contracts are set to end the week little changed after rising more than 3% last week . <p> Oil was trading lower on reports of a UN draft resolution calling for a ceasefire in Gaza and as another round of profit-taking kicked in , IG analyst Tony Sycamore said in a note . <p> US secretary of state Antony Blinken said on Thursday he believed talks in Qatar could reach a Gaza ceasefire agreement between Israel and Hamas , easing geopolitical risks in the region . <p> Blinken met Arab foreign ministers and Egyptian President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi in Cairo as negotiators in Qatar centred on a truce of about six weeks . <p> In the US , the world 's top oil consumer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ slipped below 9-million barrels for the first time in three weeks , indicating a possible slowdown in crude demand . <p> But consultancy FGE said preliminary weekly data for the first half of March that showed on-land crude and main product stocks at major oil hubs globally falling by almost 12-million barrels , compared with the 2015 to 2019 average draw of 6-million barrels , could be bullish for oil .
