That can mean variations such as eating every other day , eating five days a week and then fasting for two days or limiting daily eating to certain hours . <p> Time-restricted eating , where people condense all of their eating into a daily window of 10 hours or less , is the most popular form of intermittent fasting . Diners will delay breakfast until 10 a.m. or noon and then eat dinner by 6 p.m. or 8 p.m. , forgoing food the rest of the time . <h> How is it supposed to help ? <p> The theory behind time-restricted eating is that it supports the circadian rhythm , or the body 's internal clock .
