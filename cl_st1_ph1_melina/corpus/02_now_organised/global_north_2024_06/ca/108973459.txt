<p> Ellin Bessner , host of The CJN Daily podcast , was admittedly nervous ahead of Sunday 's 55th annual Walk with Israel , held by UJA Federation of Greater Toronto . For weeks , pro-Palestinian protest groups in the city had been threatening to disrupt the important Jewish solidarity march -- the first one since the deadly Hamas attack on Oct. 7 . <p> It was stunning watching the record turnout of an estimated 50,000 people -- and also seeing the massive police presence that kept a lid on trouble . <p> But by the time Bessner and her family completed the nearly five-kilometre walk on June 9 , her anxiety over the Middle Eastern war and rampant domestic antisemitism fell to the wayside and joy took over , even if only for a short time . <p> On today 's special feature episode of The CJN Daily , Ellin invites listeners to join her on the walk and meet some of the people she met along the way : Israeli visitors Rami and Vered Gold , who survived the Hamas massacre at Kibbutz Be'eri ; Michael Gilmore of Kehillat Shaarei Torah , the Toronto synagogue targeted by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ public school teacher in Millbrook , Ontario ; Noah Shack , UJA 's head of combating antisemitism , and others .
