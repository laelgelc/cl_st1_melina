The country stands ready to contribute to initiatives that foster stability , security , and peace in the region , " it said . <p> Phase 1 of the proposed approach includes an " immediate , full , and complete ceasefire with the release of hostages including women , the elderly and the wounded , the return of the remains of some hostages who have been killed , and the exchange of Palestinian prisoners " . <p> Phase 2 seeks to see a permanent end to hostilities " in exchange for the release of all other hostages still in Gaza , and a full withdrawal of Israeli forces from Gaza " . <p> Meanwhile , a " major multi-year reconstruction plan for Gaza " would @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ hostages still in the enclave would be returned to Israel .
