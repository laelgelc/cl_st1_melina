<h> ' State of Palestine ' applies to join South Africa 's case at top UN court accusing Israel of genocide <p> THE HAGUE , Netherlands ( AP ) -- Palestinian officials have applied on behalf of the " State of Palestine " at the top U.N . court for permission to join South Africa 's case accusing Israel of genocide in Gaza . <p> The request published Monday says that Israel 's ongoing military operation is " part of a systematic effort to wipe Palestinian society and its culture and social institutions from the map . " The request to the International Court of Justice was signed by Palestinian Authority foreign ministry official Ammar Hijazi . <p> South Africa filed its case with the world court late last year accusing Israel of breaching the genocide convention in its military assault that has laid waste to large swaths of Gaza . Israel denies it is committing genocide in its military operation to crush Hamas triggered by the deadly Oct. 7 attacks in southern Israel . <p> The court has issued three preliminary orders in the case calling on Israel to do all it can to prevent deaths in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , halt its offensive in Rafah . <p> It is unclear how long the court 's judges will take to rule on the request . If granted , Palestinian officials will be able to address the court in writing and during public hearings .
