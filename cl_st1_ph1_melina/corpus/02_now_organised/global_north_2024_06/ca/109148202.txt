The deaths will likely fuel calls for a cease-fire and heighten Israeli public anger over ultra-Orthodox exemptions from the military . <p> Last month , Israel 's Supreme Court ordered an end to government subsidies for many ultra-Orthodox men who do n't serve in the army . A new draft law has n't been passed , but the coalition of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu this week voted in favor of extending exemptions for religious men .
