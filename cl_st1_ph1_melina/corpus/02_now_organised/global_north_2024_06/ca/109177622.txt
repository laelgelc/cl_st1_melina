<h> Israeli military announces ' tactical pause ' in attempt to increase flow of aid into hard-hit Gaza <p> JERUSALEM ( AP ) -- The Israeli military on Sunday announced a " tactical pause " in its offensive in the southern Gaza Strip to allow the deliveries of increased quantities of humanitarian aid . <p> The army said the pause would begin in the Rafah area at 8 a.m. ( 0500 GMT , 1 a.m. eastern ) and remain in effect until 7 p.m. ( 1600 GMT , noon eastern ) . It said the pauses would take place every day until further notice . <p> The pause is aimed at allowing aid trucks to reach the nearby Israel-controlled Kerem Shalom crossing , the main entry point for incoming aid , and travel safely to the Salah a-Din highway , a main north-south road , to deliver supplies to other parts of Gaza , the military said . It said the pause was being coordinated with the U.N . and international aid agencies . <p> The crossing has suffered from a bottleneck since Israeli ground troops moved into Rafah in early May .
