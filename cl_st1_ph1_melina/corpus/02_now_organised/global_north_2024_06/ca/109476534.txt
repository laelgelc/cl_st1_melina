<h> Canada 's inflation surprises higher in May , casting doubt on a July rate cut <p> Canada 's annual inflation rate reaccelerated unexpectedly to 2.9 per cent in May while measures of core inflation also increased , according to Statistics Canada , reducing the odds of a Bank of Canada rate cut in July . <p> The increase came as a surprise to analysts , who had expected inflation to cool to 2.6 per cent from 2.7 per cent in April , according to a survey from Reuters . Statistics Canada said on Tuesday that acceleration was largely due to higher prices for services , which increased 4.6 per cent in May after a 4.2 per cent increase in April . The services price growth was led by cellular services , travel tours , rent and air transportation . <p> The Bank of Canada 's closely watched measures of core inflation also edged up in May , again surprising analysts . CPI-median increased from 2.6 per cent in April to 2.8 per cent in May , while CPI-trim was up from 2.8 per cent in April to 2.9 per cent in May .
