Hezbollah militants subsequently launched more rockets in a single day than at any point so far during the Israel-Hamas war . Yet there have only been 28 casualties , including 18 soldiers , because for the past eight months , the border towns have sat largely empty . After Oct. 7 , an estimated 60,000 Israeli residents fled or were ordered to evacuate their homes .
