<h> Understanding UTAM 's investments in light of O4P 's divestment report <p> A May 2024 report issued by the student activist group U of T Occupy for Palestine ( O4P ) claims that the university is indirectly investing billions of dollars in companies that are profiting from Israel 's military operations in Gaza . <p> O4P , the student group behind the occupation of Simcoe Hall and the encampment on King 's College Circle , has been calling on U of T to disclose and divest all of its investments in companies contributing to Israel 's military aggression in Gaza . This includes arms manufacturers , automotive manufacturers , and surveillance technology developers . <p> Here is a breakdown of what O4P 's calls for U of T to " disclose and divest " means for the university . <p> UTAM -- U of T 's investment manager <p> U of T created the University of Toronto Asset Management Corporation ( UTAM ) in 2000 to manage the investment of U of T 's endowment , its expendable funds investment pool ( EFIP ) , and -- up until 2021 -- its pension fund . " <p> As of December 31 , 2023 , UTAM held approximately $8.2 billion in total assets under its management , with $4.2 billion coming from the endowment and four billion dollars from EFIP . <p> UTAM follows the " manager of managers " approach in its long-term investments of the university 's endowment portfolio . This means that , rather than directly investing in company securities such as stocks , UTAM allocates the vast majority of its endowment funds to external investment managers . He wrote that the investment of the university 's endowment " does not hold any direct investments in companies .
