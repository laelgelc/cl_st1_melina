<p> 6 June 2024 . At least 70 dead people and over 300 wounded , the majority of whom are women and children , have been brought to Al-Aqsa hospital in Gaza in the last days following heavy Israeli strikes in the Middle Area of the Gaza strip . <p> " The odour of blood in the hospital 's emergency room this morning was unbearable . There are people lying everywhere , on the floor , outside ... bodies were being brought in plastic bags . The situation is overwhelming " , says Karin Huster , Medecins Sans Frontieres/Doctors Without Borders ( MSF ) medical team leader in Gaza . <p> Medical teams in Al-Aqsa hospital - one of the only remaining functional health facility in the Middle Area - are currently trying to deal with a huge influx of patients , many of them arriving with severe burns , shrapnel wounds , fractures , and other traumatic injuries . <p> " With the insane escalation of violence in various locations of the Gaza strip over the last 48 hours and while the Rafah crossing point has remained closed for a month , the health system has been stretched @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " - Karin Huster . <p> The repeated mass casualties incidents are unacceptable and the horrific toll on civilians shows the total disregard for human lives in Gaza .
