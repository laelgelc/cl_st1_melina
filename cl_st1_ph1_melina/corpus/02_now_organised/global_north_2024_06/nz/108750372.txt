<h> Israeli airstrikes near city of Aleppo kills several people , Syrian state media say <p> Israeli airstrikes around the Syrian city of Aleppo killed several people early Monday , Syrian state media reported . <p> The state-run SANA news agency gave no specific toll . It said the strikes were around the southeastern edge of Aleppo . <p> " The aggression led to a number of martyrs and some material losses , " SANA said . <p> Israel did not immediately acknowledge the strikes and rarely does when it comes to Syria . <p> Syria and Israel have been at war since Israel 's founding in 1948 . Syria 's President Bashar Assad has been backed by Iran in his country 's yearslong war , and Israeli strikes previously have targeted Iranian positions and equipment . <p> The strikes also come while Israel is fighting Hamas in the Gaza Strip . Israel separately has been striking targets in Lebanon as well as Hezbollah continues its cross-border fire into the country .
