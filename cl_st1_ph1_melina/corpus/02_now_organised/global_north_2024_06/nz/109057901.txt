Will Alexander , a teacher and former actor in the popular TV show " Shortland Street , " said in a statement on June 4 that he had been contacted by supporters in Palestine : " Enough people have lost their lives ... As my health is being affected , they are asking me to stop . " <p> Alexander told the Spinoff he had been feeling shaky and dizzy since day four of his zero-calorie diet and by last week had lost 12 kilograms .
