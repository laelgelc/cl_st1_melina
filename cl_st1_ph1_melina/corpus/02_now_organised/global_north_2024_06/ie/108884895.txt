<h> President Higgins awarded FAO Agricola medal <p> President Michael D. Higgins has been awarded with the ' Agricola Medal ' of the United Nations Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) at a ceremony in ? ras an Uachtar ? in today ( Friday , June 7 ) . <p> President Higgins was presented with the medal by the director-general of the FAO , Dr. Qu Dongyu . <p> The medal bears the Latin name for farmer , and is presented to international figures who have undertaken " outstanding efforts " in advancing the cause of global food security , poverty alleviation and nutrition . <p> Speaking at the event , director-general Qu said : " It is the highest recognition by FAO to a head of state who has committed his life , his experience , his achievements , his influence and his political will and courage to better global agrifood systems " . <h> Agricola medal <p> On receiving the Agricola medal , the president said that the objective when the FAO was founded in 1945 was for " eliminating hunger and improving nutrition " . <p> " This objective remains as urgently demanding of action @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " President Higgins said . <p> The president spoke about new data from the 2024 Global Report on Food Crises ' ( GRFC ) , which shows that 2024 is the fifth consecutive year of growing numbers facing high levels of acute food insecurity . <p> " Acute malnutrition in food crises continues to deteriorate , with over 36 million children under five years of age in 32 food-crisis countries affected , 10 million of whom were severely malnourished in need of urgent treatment , " the president said . <p> " Conflict is now the main driver of food insecurity , affecting 135 million people across 20 territories . <p> " Economic shocks were the second driver of food insecurity , affecting 75 million people , while climate change-related weather extremes accounted for food insecurity among a further 72 million people , " President Higgins said . <h> President Higgins <p> The president spoke about the war in Sudan causing hunger crisis , along with the reported to the United Nations . Yet , each time , famine has returned , " President Higgins said . <p> He added that sustainable intensification has been advocated by many , but that this sometimes " overlooks " the need to employ local wisdom in food security models . <p> " A recalibration towards more holistic , inclusive , and sustainable approaches is necessary to ensure that the development of African agriculture benefits all stakeholders and preserves the continent 's rich biodiversity and agricultural heritage . <p> " The modes of production of food have huge and diverse cultural significance , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ smallholder farmers who depend on rain-fed agriculture for their sustenance , each unpredicted drought or flood can spell disaster , pushing them further into poverty and hunger , " the president said . <p> " We now need best ecological practices in agriculture , including agro-ecology , to become widespread . <p> " This is substantially different from mere adjustments to the productionist agronomy model , a colonially imposed food system which has exacerbated food insecurity by creating over-dependence on a small number of staples and an over-reliance on imported fertiliser , pesticide and seeds . <p> " Increases in food production must be sustainable , even as we continue to lose land to environmental degradation and climate change , " the president said .
