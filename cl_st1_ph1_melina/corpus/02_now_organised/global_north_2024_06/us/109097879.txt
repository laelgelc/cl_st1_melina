The building was taken over Wednesday afternoon , trapping people inside . ( Myung J. Chun / Los Angeles Times via Getty Images ) <p> The protesters smashed windows and left behind graffiti , copiers and furniture . They also flipped over at least one vending machine . <p> The occupation of the building forced some university staff members , including the university 's new president , Berenecea Johnson Eanes , to shelter in place . <p> Los Angeles Police Department officers arrived on campus around 10 p.m . KTLA reported that administrators were able to leave the premises Thursday morning . <p> Protesters told the outlet that their actions were a response to " radio silence " from university administrators regarding divestment from Israel and the ongoing war against Hamas . <p> " We do n't think property is sacred , " one protester said . " We believe that lives are more important than property . Especially when our money goes to this school . This is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ way . " <p> As a result of the incident , classes have been moved online for at least the next 24 hours . <p> University spokesperson Eric Frost Hollins told the Los Angeles Times that the CSU LA Gaza Solidarity Encampment had emailed them to inform them of a planned sit-in . The occupation occurred even though Cal State had set up a special area for peaceful demonstrations . <p> " Unfortunately , this action went in a different way today , " the university told the Los Angeles Times .
