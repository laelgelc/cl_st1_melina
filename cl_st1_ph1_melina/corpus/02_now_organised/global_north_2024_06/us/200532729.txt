<p> South African President Cyril Ramaphosa was reelected by lawmakers for a second term Friday after his African National Congress party struck a dramatic late deal to forge a coalition with the main opposition and other parties . <p> Ramaphosa won convincingly in a Parliament vote against surprise nominee Julius Malema , the leader of the far-left Economic Freedom Fighters . Ramaphosa received 283 votes to Malema 's 44 in the 400-member house . <p> The 71-year-old Ramaphosa secured his second term with the help of lawmakers from the second-largest Democratic Alliance party and others after the ANC lost its 30-year parliamentary majority in a landmark election two weeks ago .
