<h> Creators for Palestine Raise $1.5 Million on Twitch <p> Creator for Palestine -- a group of Twitch streamers , YouTubers , and TikTokers -- raised over $1.5 million dollars this weekend for relief efforts in Gaza . The event was started by YouTuber Nikki Carreon , per the Washington Post . " we were able to do this after I started a groupchat in the fuckin shower with 3 people in it in hopes to earn 2,000 dollars for Palestinian relief , " Carreon tweeted . She also shouted out folks like Frogan , Hassan Khadair , Sarah Schauer and others for organizing the event/getting the stream going . The Try Guys offered their studio for the livestream 's physical locale . The group of 100+ content creators did a livestream on Hasan Piker 's Twitch stream and the Try Guys YouTube channel . Proceeds are going to orgs like Medical Aid for Palestinians , HEAL Palestine and UNRWA .
