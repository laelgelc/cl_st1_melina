<h> No Gaza refugees <p> President Joe Biden wants to assuage his anti-Israel critics by allowing Gazan refugees into the United States . But a new Department of Homeland Security Office of Inspector General report reveals how he has already failed to track the 77,000 Afghan refugees admitted into the country . It shows his administration is not capable or willing to handle any more refugees . <p> When the Afghan government fell apart after Biden removed U.S. troops in a rout against the advice of his generals , the administration sought to minimize the killing of American allies by flying out as many Afghan refugees as possible . Some 97,000 were flown to the U.S. , and 77,000 were granted humanitarian parole by DHS . <p> This allowed them to apply for immigration benefits including resettlement assistance and work authorization . Parole status is good for two years as long as the parolee maintains good behavior , gets vaccinated , and reports address changes . <p> To get benefits , including work benefits , parolees must apply with the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Service .
