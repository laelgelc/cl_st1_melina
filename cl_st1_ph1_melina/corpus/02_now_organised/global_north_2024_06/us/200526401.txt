<h> Rachel Goldberg-Polin : It Will Take A Diplomatic Solution To Bring The October 7 Hostages Home <p> Rachel Goldberg-Polin , the mother of Hersh Goldberg-Polin , who has been held hostage by the Palestinian militant group Hamas , poses for a photo next to a poster of her son during an interview in Jerusalem on May 29 , 2024 . The number 236 that Rachel Goldberg-Polin taped above her heart marked the number of days her son Hersh had been held hostage in Gaza when she met AFP at her Jerusalem office . Hamas militants kidnapped 252 people on October 7 , of which 105 were freed during a truce in the war in late November . ( Photo by Ahikam SERI / AFP ) ( Photo by AHIKAM SERI/AFP via Getty Images ) <p> Following the weekend 's military raid , some 120 hostages remain in Gaza . Among them is Hersh Goldberg-Polin , a 23-year-old Israeli-American citizen who was captured at the Re'im music festival on October 7 . His mother , Rachel Goldberg-Polin , has become a tireless advocate for the release of all hostages , and on Tuesday she spoke to Forbes about her @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and the " most exquisite torture " hostage families have experienced for the last eight months . <p> Wearing the number 249 taped to her t-shirt -- to mark the number of days Hersh has been held hostage -- Goldberg told ForbesWomen editor Maggie McGrath that " the way to get all of these hostages released ... is when these things are diplomatically negotiated . That is truly the way forward . " <p> When asked what she wants the general public to understand about her experience since her son was taken , Goldberg replied : " You can hold two truths . This does n't have to be as divisive as I often hear it being . You can be heartbroken and extremely concerned about innocent civilians who are caught in Gaza , and you can be heartbroken and severely concerned about civilians in Gaza who were dragged there on October 7 . You can hold both of those . "
