<p> Modelling sisters Gigi and Bella Hadid have pledged to donate $1m to support Palestinian relief efforts . <p> The BBC reports that a spokesperson for Bella states that the money will be shared between four humanitarian organisations with a focus on children and families affected by the ongoing conflict in the region . <p> The sisters ' father Mohamed Anwar Hadid is Palestinian , and they have both previously expressed their support for citizens affected by the crisis . <p> Bella appeared at the recent Cannes Film Festival dressed in a red and white outfit made of traditional Arabic cloth , and she wrote on Instagram that it was a " beautiful way to represent the history , labour of love , resilience and , most importantly , the art of historic Palestinian embroidery . " <p> Gigi wrote on Instagram after the October 7 attack by Hamas : " My thoughts are with all those affected by this unjustifiable tragedy , and every day that innocent lives are taken by this conflict -- too many of which are children . <p> " I have deep empathy and heartbreak for the Palestinian struggle and life under occupation , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ While I have hopes and dreams for Palestinians , none of them include the harm of a Jewish person . " <p> The BBC reports that more than 36,000 people have been killed in Gaza since October , quoting figures from the Hamas-run health ministry .
