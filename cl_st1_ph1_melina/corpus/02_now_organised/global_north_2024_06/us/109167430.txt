<h> Biden 's foolish plan for post-war Gaza <p> The Biden administration 's plans for post-war Gaza represent a significant departure in the U.S.-Israeli relationship and could endanger American lives . <p> According to a recent Politicoreport , the White House is " coalescing around plans for an interim ' Palestinian Council ' to govern Gaza and a security coalition in which the U.S. military will play a major role . " If true , such a plan would be disastrous for the United States , Israel , and Palestinians . <p> The United States has spent decades bogged down in state-building in the Middle East with very little to show for it . After Iraq and Afghanistan , American popular opinion is unlikely to sustain such an effort , and taxpayers are unlikely to be willing to pay for it . Moreover , Washington faces a growing array of security problems , including a Russia that menaces its neighbors in Europe and a China that seeks to dominate Asia and beyond . <p> Indeed , the challenge posed by Beijing is immense .
