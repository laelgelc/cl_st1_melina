( AP Photo/Rafiq Maqbool ) <p> ASSOCIATED PRESS <p> MECCA , Saudi Arabia ( AP ) -- Muslim pilgrims have been streaming into Saudi Arabia 's holy city of Mecca ahead of the start of the Hajj later this week , as the annual pilgrimage returns to its monumental scale . <p> Saudi officials say more than 1.5 million foreign pilgrims have arrived in the country by Tuesday , the vast majority by air , from across the world . More are expected , and hundreds of thousands of Saudis and others living in Saudi Arabia will also join them when the pilgrimage officially begins on Friday . <p> Saudi officials have said they expect the number of pilgrims this year to exceed those in 2023 , when more than 1.8 million people performed Hajj , approaching pre-pandemic levels .
