<h> Israel rescues four hostages taken by Hamas on Oct. 7 <p> Four Israelis taken hostage by Hamas on Oct. 7 during the attacks at the Nova music festival were rescued by security forces Saturday morning . <p> The rescued hostages are Noa Argamani , 25 , Almog Meir Jan , 21 , Andrey Kozlov , 27 , and Shlomi Ziv , 40 . They have all been taken to Sheba Tel-HaShomer Medical Center near Tel Aviv , and are reported to be in good condition . <p> Once reunited with her father and dying mother , Argamani talked with Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu over the phone . Video footage from the attack in October , showed Argamani reaching out for her boyfriend as she was being taken away on the back of a motorcycle by two Hamas terrorists . <p> She appeared in a series of propaganda videos released by Hamas in January .
