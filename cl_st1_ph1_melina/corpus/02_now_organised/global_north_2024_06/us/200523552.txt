For some , like Goucher College , which received a $55 million bequest from alumna Anica Donnan Rawnsley and Drake University , which was gifted $28 million from alum Greg Johansen earlier this year , it provides a source of vital sustaining future revenue . For other well-heeled schools , like Dartmouth College , which has an endowment of nearly $8 billion and received a $150 million scholarship bequest from the late alum and telecommunications CEO Glenn Britt in March , the donations add to the institution 's largesse and afford bragging rights . <p> So it comes as no surprise that as commencements wrap up , college event planners turn their attention to reunion weekends -- one of the key events in the ongoing courtship between schools and their alumni . Happy alumni , feeling nostalgic and proud of their alma maters , are the primary targets for colleges ' annual giving campaigns as well as occasional capital fundraisers . In 2022 , private gifts to the top 100 private , non-profit colleges on the Forbes Top Colleges list amounted to more than $16 billion . According to the Council for Advancement and Support of Education , direct donations from alumni made up 20% of all gifts to colleges in fiscal year 2023 . And when one considers the money coming from wealthy alums ' donor-advised funds , the proportion is significantly higher . <p> Reunion weekends " used to be very heavily oriented towards fundraising , " says David Flinchbaugh , senior vice president at Graham-Pelton , a higher ed fundraising consultancy . Today , the pitch is more subtle and few colleges collect checks during reunion weekends , but the events are valuable in other ways -- alumni bring their kids ( read : prospective students ) to campus , make connections with the college @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and facilities which they are more likely to support later on . " Reunions now are much more about engaging alumni in the overall life of the institution , " Flinchbaugh says . " In other words , it used to be just come back and see your friends . Now it 's much more about what has changed at the institution , what 's going on with faculty , what 's going on in terms of career services for students , and what 's going on in the life of the students .
