<h> Kehlani Raised $555,000 for Gaza , Sudan , and Congo <p> Kehlani is putting her money where her mouth is after supporting Palestine in her music video for " Next 2 U. " The performer raised over $555,000 in aid for Gaza , Sudan , and Congo with merch from the new single . " This song is about protection , something that institutions have failed to do for the people of Palestine , Congo , and Sudan , " she wrote on Instagram . " No one got us the way we got each other . Me &amp; my team feel overwhelmed with gratitude for yall showing out for this fundraiser . " Kehlani sold T-shirts that were made in Bethlehem and screen-printed in Ramallah , both on the West Bank . Along with benefitting Palestinian families amid the Israel-Hamas war , the proceeds also go to families in Sudan , which is experiencing a civil war , and Congo , which is facing attacks by armed rebel groups .
