<p> Benjamin Netanyahu 's maneuvers always boil down to a combination of two motives . One is the prevention of a Palestinian state . The other is political survival . At times , the two motives tug against each other . More often , especially of late , they run in the same direction . <p> That is the case now , when Netanyahu is the unpopular leader of a wartime government .
