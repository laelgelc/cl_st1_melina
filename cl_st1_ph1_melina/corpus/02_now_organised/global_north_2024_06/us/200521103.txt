For most of the 2024 races , voters wo n't know for sure who will qualify to make the ballot until qualifying -- which starts Monday and runs through noon Friday -- ends . Without a doubt , some candidates will drop out , and there are always a few last-minute surprise additions . <p> But Florida 's congressional races are already set . Along with judicial , state attorney and public defender candidates , they qualified in March . A quick glance through those races reveals something strange going on . With the exception of hardcore mischief-makers like North Florida 's Matt Gaetz , it 's nearly unheard of for an incumbent member of Congress to draw a serious opponent from their own party . Yet this year , 16 of Florida 's 28 congressional districts have incumbents being " primaried .
