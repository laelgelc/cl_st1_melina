<p> A group that tracks antisemitism in Germany said Tuesday it recorded an overall increase of more than 80% in incidents last year , with well over half of the total coming after Hamas-led militants attacked Israel in early October . <p> The RIAS group said it recorded 4,782 antisemitic incidents in 2023 , ranging from anti-Jewish comments to attacks . That compared with 2,616 in 2022 . <p> The group said that 2,787 of last year 's recorded incidents -- more than the previous year 's total -- took place after the Oct. 7 attack by Hamas on Israel , which triggered the ongoing war in Gaza .
