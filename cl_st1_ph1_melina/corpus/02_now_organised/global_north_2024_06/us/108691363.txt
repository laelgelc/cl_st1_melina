<p> The 10 Arab and Iranian photographers featured in " Louder Than Hearts " at the Middle East Institute 's gallery are not only all women , but their subjects are almost entirely female as well . This emphasis feels fitting for the first exhibition curated by Rania Matar , a photographer known for her nuanced , intimate portraits of women and girls in the Middle East and the United States . <p> It opens with six photos from her series " Where Do I Go ? Fifty Years Later " commemorating the Lebanese civil war , which began in 1975 . Matar -- who was born in Lebanon to Palestinian parents and came to the United States at age 20 , during the war -- photographed young women who left the country in recent years amid its economic crisis and the aftermath of the 2020 Beirut port explosion . <p> " I almost look at them as self-portraits of my younger self , " Matar said in an interview in Washington after the show opened .
