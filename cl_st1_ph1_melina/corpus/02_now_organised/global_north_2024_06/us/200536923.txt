Palestinians in Gaza marked a somber Eid al-Adha amid the rubble and worsening food and water shortages . Aid organizations warned that parts of the territory may already be suffering from famine . <p> In the run-up to the holiday , many Muslims may fast or embark on the Hajj pilgrimage . <p> AP <p> The pilgrimage , one of the five pillars of Islam , involves walking to Mecca and circumambulating the cube-shaped Kaaba , the most sacred Islamic site . This year , 1.8 million people from around the world are making the journey to Mecca , according to Saudi authorities .
