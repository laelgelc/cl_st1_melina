' s World Food Programme halted deliveries because of security concerns . Aid groups have warned that the risk of famine is growing . <p> Officials at the meeting , which occurred at an Israeli military base near Ashdod , included Lt. Gen. Patrick Frank , commander of U.S. Army Central Command , Maj. Gen. Yaron Finkleman , commander of the IDF 's Southern Command , and a humanitarian coordinator for the U.N .
