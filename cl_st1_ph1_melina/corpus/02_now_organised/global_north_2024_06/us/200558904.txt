<p> Iran will hold a runoff presidential election on Friday , July 5 , after an initial vote saw reformist candidate Masoud Pezeshkian and hard-liner Saeed Jalili each fall short of securing an outright win in an election with the lowest turnout ever in the Islamic Republic . <p> Iranian state television reported the results that triggered a second round of voting , with the two candidates neck and neck in the race to replace hard-line President Ebrahim Raisi , who died in a helicopter crash in May . <p> After counting over 24.5 million votes , Pezeshkian had 10.41 million while Jalili held 9.47 million . <p> The historically low turnout of 39.96% is itself a gauge of the Iranian electorate 's waning support for its Shiite theocracy after years of economic turmoil and mass protests . <p> Voters faced a choice between the three hard-line candidates and the little-known reformist Pezeshkian , a heart surgeon .
