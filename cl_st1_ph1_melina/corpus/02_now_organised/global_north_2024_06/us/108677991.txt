Although this is controversial today , the UN convention does not state that asylum must be claimed in the first safe country reached after leaving the country of origin.How do you get asylum in Britain ? There are schemes allowing refugees from Ukraine and Hong Kong to come to live in Britain . But technically , to claim asylum you have to be in the UK .
