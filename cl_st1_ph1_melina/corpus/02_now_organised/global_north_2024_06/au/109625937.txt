Everyone has caught Assange fever , it seems . Despite his wife 's reasonable assertion he needs time to " let our family be a family " , we 're all waiting to see what he 'll do next . Assange for PM ? After all , one of the storylines that 's fallen by the wayside somewhat over the years is Assange 's failed Senate bid back in 2013 , where he launched a Wikileaks political party from the Ecuadorean embassy , at one point posting a Farnham parody video pointing fun at Tony Abbott , Kevin Rudd and Julia Gillard . <p> In fact , he only launched the party after taking umbrage at Julia Gillard labelling Wikileaks " illegal " , despite advice to the contrary from the AFP . He basically mounted a political party out of spite . <p> Of his political leanings , Assange said he " could be described as a libertarian " , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of understanding before acting " , as he puts it . <p> Assange noted " very little difference between Liberal and Labor -- especially once they get into government . Labor suffers more from cronyism , while the Liberals care more for big business " . <p> He slammed the government for " the betrayal of the rights and interests of people by political insiders , operating in their own interests " and wished to fight for " right of citizens to live their lives free from state interference " . <p> He also said he would be " a fierce defender of press freedom ... even though I have known only too well smear by unethical media " ( which sounds quite Shakespearean ) and would " absolutely " use parliamentary privilege to squash court-imposed gag orders . <p> Sounds like a contender .
