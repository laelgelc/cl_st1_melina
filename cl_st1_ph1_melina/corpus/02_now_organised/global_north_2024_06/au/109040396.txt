<h> Aid doctors : Gaza toll shows failure of international humanitarian law <p> With the world watching on as a humanitarian crisis in Gaza worsens -- compounded by a strained medical system , water and power shortages and extreme hunger -- about 37,000 people are estimated to have died as a result of 247 days of the Israel-Palestine conflict . <p> Aid organisations have called attention to what they say is a flagrant disregard for the rules of war and no accountability . And civilians have been left in a desperate state of suffering , with more than 79,000 Gazans injured and an estimated 10,000 still trapped under rubble . <p> Melissa Coade is The Mandarin 's news editor based in Canberra 's parliamentary press gallery . She has had various government , communications and legal roles , and has written for the Law Society of NSW journal ( LSJ ) and Lawyers Weekly .
