PHOTO : THE DAILY STAR <p> June 4 , 2024 <p> DHAKA -- Eminent photographer Shahidul Alam has returned his honorary doctorate degree from the University of the Arts London for supporting the Israeli occupation of Palestine . <p> Shahidul was awarded the degree in 2022 in recognition of his contribution to advancing the struggle for rights through photography , reads a press release issued by Drik Picture Library Limited yesterday . <p> " At the time of receiving the degree , the University of the Arts London was committed to freedom of expression . The students of the university showed solidarity with Palestine and demanded that the university administration support the ceasefire in Gaza . This reassured me , " Shahidul said in the release . <p> " However , this reassurance was lost when I saw that the administration , especially the current vice-chancellor of the university , has a different position . Students have repeatedly said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a partner in Israel 's occupation , discrimination and ongoing genocide through Israeli or Israeli-affiliated organisations and institutions . " <p> Given the circumstances , Shahidul no longer wants to be associated with the University of Arts London . <p> He said he had formally informed the university of returning the honorary doctorate .
