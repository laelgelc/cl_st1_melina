Negotiators from 200 parties came together with a landmark agreement to increase climate action before the end of this decade to keep within the 1.5-degree Celsius target . <p> But despite the first Global Stocktake finding the world is off track in meeting the goals of the Paris Agreement , consensus on other key issues remained elusive , including the level of ambition in emission reduction targets . While some countries committed to aggressive targets in line with scientific recommendations , others stopped short , citing debt and ballooning costs as barriers to pushing for more ambitious goals . <p> Despite calls for bold action , COP28 lacked clear commitments and timelines for a full coal phase-out . For one , the Azerbaijani Government nominated an all-male organising committee for the conference , following the longstanding tradition of ignoring the important role that women play in climate action . This resulted in a strong and immediate backlash from the international community , prompting the government to add 12 women to its 41-member committee . <p> COP29 is hosted in an authoritarian petrostate known for its human rights violations against environmentalists and journalists . Amnesty International and others have called on Azerbaijan to commence meaningful reforms prior to COP29 and place human rights at its core . <p> Fossil-fuel lobbyists might also be granted access to the negotiations , similar to what happened at COP28 . The presence of the world 's biggest polluters will have a significant influence on the COP presidency and participating parties . <p> There is also a worrying trend of some parties attempting to remove the language referencing the special circumstances of least developed countries and small island developing states . <p> The principle of special needs and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is more than necessary to advance climate justice .
