<h> The Yomiuri Shimbun <p> TOKYO -- Russia 's aggression against Ukraine has become prolonged , and support for Ukraine must not ultimately be a temporary effort . <p> It is highly significant that the Group of Seven advanced nations have established a framework for sustained support . <p> A G7 summit was held in Italy . It was only natural that support measures for Ukraine were discussed as the most important topic . <p> The G7 leaders agreed to establish a new fund to support Ukraine . <p> Under the agreement , Japan , the United States , the United Kingdom and Canada will first provide loans to the fund , which will then be used for Ukraine 's reconstruction and military buildup . Ukraine will not be obligated to repay the loans ; the proceeds from investments made with the Russian central bank 's frozen assets , which are managed by the countries of the European Union , will be used to repay the loans . <p> The administrations of some Western countries are facing harsh criticism due to " aid fatigue " for Ukraine and inflation . The establishment of the fund is intended to avoid @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ future due to the domestic circumstances in certain countries . <p> Germany , France and Italy will not be directly involved in the fund for the time being , but they plan to provide financial assistance through the EU . Japanese loans will be kept in separate accounts within the fund to ensure that they are not used for military purposes . <p> Russia has reacted against the latest G7 decision , suggesting it will retaliate . <p> However , it is only natural that Russia , which has destroyed the land of Ukraine and taken many lives , should be held responsible for providing compensation for the damage and suffering . The G7 , which has touted the importance of the rule of law , must unite to support Ukraine and ensure Russia 's aggression ends in failure at any cost . <p> Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy also attended the summit . <p> During his meeting with Zelenskyy , Prime Minister Fumio Kishida signed a bilateral document with the Ukraine leader stipulating cooperation in the provision of nonlethal equipment and the removal of land mines , among other measures @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ support has significantly helped Ukraine move closer to peace . <p> Japan is limited in its direct military assistance . At the same time , it has experienced many disasters and has accumulated knowledge of recovery and reconstruction . Japan should continue to provide assistance by taking advantage of these unique strengths . <p> The G7 also agreed to fully support the ceasefire proposal presented by the United States regarding the fighting between the Israeli military and the Islamist group Hamas . <p> The group called on Israel to refrain from conducting military operations in Rafah , the southernmost part of the Palestinian territory of Gaza , to which many displaced people have fled . <p> Israel 's stance of not hesitating to take the lives of Gaza residents can not be overlooked . Japan should also step up its efforts to urge Israel not to do so .
