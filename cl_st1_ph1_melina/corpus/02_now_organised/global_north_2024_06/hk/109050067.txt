<h> Lebanon front hots up over killing of commander <p> The Israeli military confirmed yesterday that it had killed senior Hezbollah commander Taleb Abdallah as well as three other fighters in a strike on a command and control center in southeastern Lebanon . <p> In response , Hezbollah had fired dozens of missiles toward northern Israel in a significant escalation of the conflict that has seen near daily exchanges of fire since the start of the war in Gaza last October . <p> Israeli jets hit a number of launch sites in southern Lebanon after projectiles were fired towards northern Israel , its military said . <p> It said the jets had hit a launcher in the area of Hanine , while a separate strike had hit four infrastructure sites in the area of Yater.
