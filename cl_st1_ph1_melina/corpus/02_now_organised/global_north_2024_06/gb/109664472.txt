<h> Swindon candidate : ' If I was mainstream I would be an MP by now ' <p> Liberty is the central theme of Debbie Hicks ' campaign to get elected to Parliament . <p> Ms Hicks , a 49-year-old teacher is the independent candidate standing in Swindon North at the general election who has entered an alliance with the independent in Swindon South Martin Costello . <p> She has come up with what appears to be disparate set of policies ; she 's against Net Zero targets and would oppose the imposition of ultra-low emission zones in Swindon but would allow the council to take over empty houses to provide shelter for the homeless , for example . <p> But she says the underlying them is civil liberties , and she is very concerned that they have been curtailed and will be so even further . <p> " We 're losing the freedom of speech , the Online Safety Act is an attack on freedom of speech on the internet , we are losing the freedom to assemble and protest in the Police Crime , Sentencing and Courts Act . It gives too many powers to stop protests @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Rights Act is one of the biggest examples . We are losing the sense of proportionality in the case of activists . <p> " It does n't matter what the cause is , whether it 's Just Stop Oil , or Gaza protesters . I certainly do n't approve of what Just Stop Oil want , I do n't agree with them , but we must all have the freedom to protest . " <p> Ms Hicks was convicted in 2022 of public order offences relating to Gloucestershire Royal Hospital and Covid-19 regulations . <p> She says : " Yes , I have two convictions because , look at me I 'm so dangerous .
