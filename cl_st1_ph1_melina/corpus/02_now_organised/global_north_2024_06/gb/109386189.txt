<p> Thousands of people of all ages paraded through the streets of Cardiff city centre today dressed in bright colours and waving flags and banners as the annual Pride Cymru event returned to the capital . This year 's event marked the 25th anniversary of Pride Cymru in Cardiff and , as always , provided a space for people to celebrate the LGBTQ+ community and its allies . <p> Hundreds of people marched with their friends and family members , whilst others joined groups from sports teams , workplaces and social groups as the bright and colourful parade made its way along the streets . Of course , many shopfronts and businesses en-route were adorned with flags and rainbow-print too , whilst spectators also got into the Pride spirit . <p> After the parade , the party continued in Cardiff Castle with a full line-up of live entertainment including local favourites Miss Kitty and Polly Amarous and 1990s chart-toppers Vengaboys ! <p> We 've got lots of photos from this year 's Pride Cymru event in the updates below . Today , it 's a day for both celebration and protest and aims to give people in Wales a place to " celebrate our common humanity , celebrate LGBTQ+ culture " and " give people the chance to know more about the community " . <p> The annual event typically takes place in June as the LGBTQ+ community refer to the month as Pride Month . It 's organised by its namesakes , Pride Cymru , a volunteer-led charity that works to " promote the elimination of discrimination , be it on the grounds of sexual orientation , gender , race , religion or ability .
