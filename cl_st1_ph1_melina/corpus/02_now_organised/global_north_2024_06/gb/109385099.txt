<p> Twenty-two people have died in an airstrike which damaged a Red Cross building in Gaza . <p> The victims had been taking shelter in the compound , which was struck by heavy-calibre projectiles and the shelling killed the group . " <p> It added that all parties had an obligation to take precautions to avoid harm to civilians and humanitarian facilities .
