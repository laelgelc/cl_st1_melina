<h> Credo : In destructive times reflect on the wonder of creation <p> Jonathan Wittenberg <p> Saturday June 22 2024 , 12.01am , The Times <p> I often think of Chagall 's painting , Solitude . In the background , a small town burns , perhaps a refence to pogroms in Belarus from where the artist hailed . In the right-hand foreground a gentle-faced calf holds a violin ; on the left , an elderly Jew clutches a Torah scroll . It is as if man and animal are saying to each other : " Despite everything , we still have our music , you and I. " <p> These are cruel months . Amid rising antisemitism , many of us Jews feel deeply alone , even shunned , in campuses and workplaces . We fear and worry for our friends and relatives in Israel .
