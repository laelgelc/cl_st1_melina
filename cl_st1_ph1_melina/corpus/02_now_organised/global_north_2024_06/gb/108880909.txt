<h> Egypt will have access to about $820mln subject to IMF Board approval , fund says <p> WASHINGTON : The International Monetary Fund said on Thursday that it reached a staff-level agreement with Egypt on the third review of an expanded IMF loan program , which would disburse about $820 million to Cairo upon board approval . <p> The agreement follows a May 12-26 IMF mission to Cairo to review Egypt 's reform performance under an Extended Fund Facility loan that was expanded to $8 billion in March from an original $3 billion loan agreed in December 2022 . <p> The IMF said in a statement that Egypt 's efforts to restore macroeconomic stability are making some progress , despite a difficult regional environment marked by spillovers from the war in Gaza and Red Sea shipping disruptions that have hurt Suez Canal receipts . <p> " While geopolitical tensions and their impact on Egypt remain challenging , the authorities have stayed the course to preserve macroeconomic stability through fiscal discipline , tight monetary policy , and a shift to a flexible exchange rate regime , " IMF Egypt mission chief Vladkova Hollar said in a statement . <p> Hollar @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ divestment of state enterprises and maintain tight monetary policy to control inflation . <p> The IMF said its Executive Board would consider the staff agreement and disbursement in the coming weeks .
