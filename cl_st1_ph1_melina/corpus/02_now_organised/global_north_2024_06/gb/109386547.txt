And Bristol Central , known as the ' most left-wing constituency in Britain ' , is where they believe they stand the best chance of winning a second seat in parliament . <p> Carla Denyer , the Greens ' co-leader , is the candidate here . Debbonaire is out in Bristol door-knocking four times a day while Denyer , a former city councillor , must also focus on the Greens ' national campaign .
