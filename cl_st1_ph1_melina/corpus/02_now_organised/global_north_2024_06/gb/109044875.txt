The Conservatives have been in power for 14 years and , while they are nominally centre-right , many of the party 's policies and positions will hardly strike the average voter as sensible and centrist . Maybe if the Tories really had stuck to the centre ground they would n't be 21 points behind in the polls and heading for electoral wipeout on 4 July . <p> Nowhere is the Tories ' refusal to adopt a sensible centrist position clearer than in the gender debate .
