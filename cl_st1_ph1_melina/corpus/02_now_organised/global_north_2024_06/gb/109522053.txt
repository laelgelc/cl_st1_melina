<h> Yousaf warns Starmer against ' betrayal of people of Palestine ' <h> Yousaf warns Starmer against ' betrayal of people of Palestine ' <p> The former first minister urged the Labour leader to immediately recognise Palestine as a state if he won the keys to Downing Street after the July 4 election ( Andrew Milligan/PA ) <p> The failure of Sir Keir Starmer to immediately recognise Palestine as a state would be a " betrayal " , former first minister Humza Yousaf has said . <p> Leading a Holyrood debate -- the first time he has spoken in parliament since stepping down from the top job -- Mr Yousaf urged Sir Keir to act quickly if he wins the keys to Downing Street after the July 4 election . <p> Labour has already pledged to recognise Palestine in its manifesto if it takes power . <p> " In just over a week 's time , Keir Starmer will likely be the Prime Minister of the UK , " he said . <p> " My appeal to him and the government he will lead is not to equivocate , do not deny the people of Palestine their inalienable right @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , ensure that the UK joins our neighbours and allies in Ireland , Norway and Spain and immediately recognise the state of Palestine . <p> " Anything less will be a betrayal of the people of Palestine who have been let down for far too long . " <p> The Labour manifesto agrees with Mr Yousaf that Palestinian statehood is an " inalienable right " , adding : " It is not in the gift of any neighbour and is also essential to the long-term security of Israel . <p> " We are committed to recognising a Palestinian state as a contribution to a renewed peace process which results in a two-state solution with a safe and secure Israel alongside a viable and sovereign Palestinian state . " <p> The former first minister -- whose wife 's parents were trapped in Gaza during the bombardment following the October 7 attack -- went on to question " where is our humanity ?
