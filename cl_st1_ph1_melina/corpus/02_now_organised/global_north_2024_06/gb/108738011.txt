<h> Pro-Palestine protest brings traffic in Cardiff to standstill <p> A pro-Palestine protest brought rush-hour traffic in Cardiff city centre to a standstill on Monday evening . Footage posted to social media showed demonstrators sitting on Park Place , Boulevard de Nantes and Stuttgarter Strasse , in opposition to the Israel-Hamas war in Gaza which has killed more than 36,000 Palestinian civilians . <p> Campaign group Cardiff Stop the War said the protest was part of a weekly blockade for Rafah , the southernmost city in Gaza from which more than one million sheltering Palestinians have been forced to flee due to Israeli bombardment . <p> The group said protesters were dressed in black " to mourn the Gaza dead " and called the war a " genocide " . Stagecoach South Wales and Megabus said some of their services had been suspended due to the protest , while motorists faced delays as roads in the Welsh capital were blocked off . <p> South Wales Police advised motorists to avoid the area . A spokesperson for South Wales Police said in a post on social media : " We 're aware of an protest taking place in the vicinity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Cardiff city centre .
