<h> Oil steady as US demand flags but Middle East risk mounts <p> LONDON - Oil prices were largely steady on Thursday as a surprise build in U.S. stockpiles fuelled fears about slow demand from the world 's top oil consumer and countered supply concerns stoked by escalating conflict in the Middle East . <p> Brent crude oil futures were up 43 cents , or 0.5% , at $85.68 a barrel by 0850 GMT . U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude futures rose 42 cents , or 0.5% , to $81.32 . <p> Both benchmarks had settled slightly higher on Wednesday . <p> The U.S. Energy Information Administration ( EIA ) reported a 3.6 million barrel jump in the country 's crude oil stocks last week . Analysts polled by Reuters had expected a drawdown of 2.9 million barrels . <p> U.S. gasoline stocks also rose , increasing by 2.7 million barrels .
