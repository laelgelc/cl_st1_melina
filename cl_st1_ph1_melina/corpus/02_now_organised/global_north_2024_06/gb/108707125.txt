<h> Michael Douglas pays solidarity visit to southern Israel <h> Michael Douglas pays solidarity visit to southern Israel <p> Actor Michael Douglas on Sunday paid a solidarity visit to an Israeli kibbutz that was hit hard in the October 7 Hamas attack which sparked Israel 's war against the Islamic militant group . <p> Douglas met members of Kibbutz Be'eri and visited burnt-out homes destroyed in the attack . <p> Be'eri was among the hardest hit communities , with about 100 people killed and 30 others taken hostage . <p> Douglas said he met families of hostages and visited the scene of a music party where more than 300 people were killed . <p> Michael Douglas is a legend of the silver screen . What he saw today in the south of Israel however , is no movie set . It is the scene of one of the worst crimes against humanity , carried out against peace-loving people by brutal Hamas terrorists . <p> He also met Israel 's president , Isaac Herzog , who presented him with an Israeli dog tag necklace and small pin of a yellow ribbon -- symbols of solidarity with the dozens of hostages @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a very difficult time .
