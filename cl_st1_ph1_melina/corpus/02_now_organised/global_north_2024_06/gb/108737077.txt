<p> Defence has taken centre stage for the upcoming General Election , driven by conflicts like the Ukraine war and Gaza confrontation . Sir Keir Starmer launched Labour 's defence policy , emphasizing the government 's duty to protect citizens . Labour plans to spend 2.5% of GDP on defence and field fourteen candidates with military backgrounds . The policy highlights an unshakeable commitment to NATO and maintaining Britain 's nuclear deterrent , despite Starmer 's past opposition . Labour also promises to establish a Military Strategic Headquarters to improve defence procurement . Critics question funding details and the feasibility of these pledges , comparing them to vague promises .
