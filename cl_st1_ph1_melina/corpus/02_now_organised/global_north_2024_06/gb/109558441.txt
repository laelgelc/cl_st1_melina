<p> Remittances remained a crucial source of external finance for developing countries in 2023 <p> After a period of strong growth during 2021-2022 , officially recorded remittance flows to low- and middle-income countries ( LMICs ) moderated in 2023 , reaching an estimated $656 billion , a report said , citing World Bank 's latest Migration and Development Brief , released on June 26 . <p> The modest 0.7% growth rate reflects large variances in regional growth , but remittances remained a crucial source of external finance for developing countries in 2023 , bolstering the current accounts of several countries grappling with food insecurity and debt issues , Emirates News Agency , WAM , said , citing the report . <p> In 2023 , remittances surpassed foreign direct investment ( FDI ) and official development assistance ( ODA ) . <p> Looking ahead , remittances to LMICs are expected to grow at a faster rate of 2.3% in 2024 , although this growth will be uneven across regions . <p> Potential downside risks to these projections include weaker than expected economic growth in high-income migrant-hosting countries and volatility in oil prices and currency exchange rates . <p> " Migration @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ development , " said Iffath Sharif , Global Director of the Social Protection and Jobs Global Practice at the World Bank . <p> " Many countries are interested in managed migration in the face of global demographic imbalances and labour deficits on the one hand , and high levels of unemployment and skill gaps on the other . <p> " We are working on partnerships between countries sending and receiving migrants to facilitate training , especially for youth , to get the skills needed for better jobs and income at home and in destination countries .
