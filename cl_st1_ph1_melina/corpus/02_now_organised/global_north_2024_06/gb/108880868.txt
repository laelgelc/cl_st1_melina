<p> GENEVA - Unemployment in the Gaza Strip has reached nearly 80% since the war with Israel erupted last October , the United Nations labour agency said on Friday , bringing the average unemployment rate across Palestinian territories to more than 50% . <p> Unemployment in the Gaza Strip has reached 79.1% , while the West Bank has seen joblessness hit nearly 32% , the International Labour Organization ( ILO ) said in its fourth assessment of the impact of the war on employment . The figures give a combined unemployment rate of 50.8% . <p> " This excludes Palestinians who have given up on finding a job , " said Ruba Jaradat , ILO Regional Director for Arab States . <p> " The situation is much worse .
