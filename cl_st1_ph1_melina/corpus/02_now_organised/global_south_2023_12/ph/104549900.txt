<h> DOH records 4 more fireworks injuries before New Year <p> People watch fireworks illuminate the sky at Bonifacio Global City in Taguig , Metro Manila on December 22 , 2023 . <p> AFP / Jam Sta . Rosa <p> MANILA , Philippines -- The Department of Health tallied four new fireworks-related injuries on Sunday , bringing the total to 12 ahead of New Year festivities . <p> Two of the new cases were boys , aged 11 and 17 , who used illegal fireworks like piccolo and boga , the DOH said in a report . <p> A 23-year-old male and 49-year-old female who lit kwitis -- a legal and regulated firecracker -- at home also sustained injuries . <p> The health department urged mayors and barangay captains to organize community fireworks displays to prevent injuries related to fireworks . <p> " It 's better to see and hear fireworks not from home , but from a safe distance and in the company of healthy family and friends , " the agency said in Filipino . <p> The DOH also reminded Filipinos to safeguard the mental and physical health of their pets during holiday festivities , noting that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> It said that pets must be kept indoors during fireworks displays or placed in a quiet room to provide a comforting atmosphere . <p> Sen. Sherwin Gatchalian reiterated Sunday his call for a ban on the sale , distribution , and use of firecrackers and other pyrotechnic devices . In 2022 , he filed a bill seeking such a ban , which remained pending at the committee level . <p> Gatchalian noted that even with the issuance of a Duterte-era executive order limiting the use of firecrackers only to community fireworks displays , injuries related to firecracker use persist . <p> " Year after year , victims of firecrackers persist .
