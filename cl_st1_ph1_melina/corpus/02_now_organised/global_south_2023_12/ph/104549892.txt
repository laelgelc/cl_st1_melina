<h> Merry poor Filipinos <p> It was supposed to be a feel good news , especially to set the celebratory mood on Christmas day . <p> If we are to believe the official government figures , the ranks of poor Filipinos have decreased a bit in numbers during the first six months of 2023 . The Philippine Statistics Authority ( PSA ) reported last Friday that the poverty incidence in our country has reached 22.4 percent from January to June . This is lower than the 23.7 percent recorded in the same period in 2021 , the PSA noted . <p> This is translated to about 25.24 million Filipinos of the total population of 110 million people all over our country . This is our poverty incidence as of the first half of 2023 . Based from the same PSA survey , the number of Filipinos who ca n't buy their basic food needs reached 8.7 percent .
