<h> WHO reports on the Gaza situation by the numbers <p> IN his opening remarks at the Special Session of the World Health Organization Executive Board on December 10 , 2023 , WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus described the event as " a meeting we would rather not be having . " <p> " As I have said repeatedly , I deplore the barbaric and unjustifiable attacks by Hamas on Israel on the 7th of October , which killed more than 1,200 people . I am appalled by reports of gender-based violence during the attacks , and by the mistreatment of hostages . I am relieved that 114 hostages have been released , and I repeat my call for the remaining hostages to be released . I well understand the anger , grief and fear of the Israeli people following the horrific attacks two months ago , " Tedros said . <p> " I also understand the anger , grief and fear of the people of Gaza , who had already suffered through 16 years of blockade , and are now enduring the destruction of their families , their homes , their communities and the life they knew , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : " More than 17,000 people are reported to have died in Gaza , including 7,000 children -- and we do n't know how many are buried under the rubble of their homes . More than 46,000 injuries have been reported . Some 1.9 million people have been displaced -- almost the entire population of the Gaza strip -- and are looking for shelter anywhere they can find it . But nowhere and no one is safe in Gaza . " <p> As more and more people move to a smaller and smaller area in Gaza , overcrowding , combined with the lack of adequate food , water , shelter and sanitation , are creating the ideal conditions for disease to spread . On average , there is one shower unit for every 700 people , and one toilet for every 150 people , according to the WHO chief . <p> Tedros told the Board that Gaza 's health system is on its knees and collapsing .
