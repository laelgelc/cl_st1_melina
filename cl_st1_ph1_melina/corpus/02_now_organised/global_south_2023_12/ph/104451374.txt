<h> SFA , Japanese FM agree to expand cooperation in key areas of concern <p> THE foreign ministers of the Philippines and Japan graced the related events of the commemorative summit for the Asean-Japan Friendship and Cooperation 's 50th year . <p> Foreign Affairs Minister Yoko Kamikawa of Japan welcomed Secretary of Foreign Affairs ( SFA ) Enrique A. Manalo on December 16 . The former stated that the two countries have made significant achievements -- including linkages in areas such as security , economy , and people-to-people exchanges , while close Japan-Philippines and Japan-United States-Philippines coordination are realized via mutual summit-level visits between the two Asian nations . <p> In response , Manalo appreciated that he was warmly received by the Japanese side and stated that he would like to further develop relations of their countries , in cooperation with Kamikawa . <p> The Japanese minister also mourned the victims of the earthquake and December 3 explosion in Mindanao , then extended her heartfelt sympathy to those who were affected . <p> Bilateral relations <p> KAMIKAWA remarked that she would like to further enhance cooperation in
