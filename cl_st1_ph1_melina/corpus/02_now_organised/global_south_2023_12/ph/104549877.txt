No bettor won the jackpot of P542,209,191.20 . <p> STAR / File <p> MANILA , Philippines -- The jackpot of the Grand Lotto 6/55 is estimated to soar to a whopping P553 million by the next draw on Wednesday night , the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office ( PCSO ) said yesterday . <p> The Grand Lotto 6/55 draw last Saturday yielded a winning combination 25-21-07-49-32-22 . No bettor won the jackpot of P542,209,191.20 . <p> However , there were 27 who correctly guessed five of the six digit winning combination . The winners can each claim a P100,000 prize . Tickets will be sold on Dec. 26 to Dec. 30 and on Jan. 2 , 2024 onwards . <p> Fighting raged Friday between Israel and Hamas in Gaza as the UN Security Council approved a much-delayed resolution to boost aid to the besieged Palestinian territory where civilians are struggling to find food .
