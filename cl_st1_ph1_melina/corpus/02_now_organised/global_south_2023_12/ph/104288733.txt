<h> I Am His Hands <p> Jia Haixia lost his sight in the year 2000 . His friend Jia Wenqi lost his arms as a child . But they 've found a way around their disabilities . " I am his hands and he is my eyes , " Haixia says . Together , they 're transforming their village in China . <p> Since 2002 the friends have been on a mission to regenerate a wasteland near their home . Each day Haixia climbs on Wenqi 's back to cross a river to the site . Wenqi then " hands " Haixia a shovel with his foot , before Haixia places a pail on a pole between Wenqi 's cheek and shoulder . And as one digs and the other waters , the two plant trees -- more than 10,000 so far . " Working together , we do n't feel disabled at all , " Haixia says . " We 're a team .
