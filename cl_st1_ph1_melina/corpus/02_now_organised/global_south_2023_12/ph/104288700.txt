<h> Beyond the reach of law <p> The Presidential Anti-Organized Crime Commission ( PAOCC ) stumbled into what could be the veritable source of the unimpeded sending of text scams in our mobile phones . But what is more glaring and disconcerting is why these text scams continue to proliferate several months after we have already a law that mandated the registration of subscriber identity modules ( SIMs ) in mobile phones , tablets and other digital devices . <p> Retired police general and now head of PAOCC Undersecretary Gilberto Cruz disclosed in our Kapihan sa Manila Bay news forum last Wednesday about having uncovered the sale of pre-registered SIM cards online using the popular social media platform of Facebook . The Facebook " Marketplace " is an e-commerce platform that is free to the public and connecting sellers and buyers . One can find unique goods , from home decor to trendy fashion , or any product or service one looks for . <p> But what shocked the veteran police sleuth like the PAOCC chief is seeing pre-registered SIM cards being sold online . <p> " If you type ' registered SIM cards ' in Facebook Marketplace , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ used SIM cards , " Cruz exclaimed .
