Department . <p> He added that it is Washington 's expectation and willingness that Israeli forces " shift to more targetted operations , with a smaller number of forces , that 's really focused in on dealing with the leadership of Hamas , the tunnel network , and a few other critical things " . <p> Advertisement <p> " It is vitally important how Israel conducts its operations , " he said . <p>
