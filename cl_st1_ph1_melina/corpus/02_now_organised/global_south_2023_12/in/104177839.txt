A fine of Rs 10,000 each was also imposed on the accused.Those convicted are Abdul Majid Kazi , 48 , his son Mohammad Siddiqui Kazi 37 , and Mohammad Usman Sheikh 48 . The order was delivered by the Adhoc district judge and additional sessions judge Vasudha L Bhosale.Additional public prosecutor Vinit Kulkarni told the court that on January 21 , 2016 , the victim , RTI activist Sajid Ansari , then aged 34 , was brutally killed by the accused who attacked him with knife . The accused were angry as the victim had complained to the civic corporation about the gate of a housing complex which was allegedly illegal and was pulled down by the corporation , said Kulkarni.As many as eight prosecution witnesses were examined to prove the charges against the accused and make the case watertight , leading to their conviction . ? ?
