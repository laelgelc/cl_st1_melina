<p> IDF had resumed combat operations against Hamas after accusing latter of violating a temporary truce . Soon after Hamas fired missiles toward Israeli territory , IDF ' restarted ' combat operations against militant group . In the hour before the truce was set to end , Hamas-affiliated media also reported sounds of explosions and gunfire in Gaza . The seven-day pause began on Nov 24 and was extended twice , allowing for the exchange of dozens of hostages . Notably , a temporary truce between Israel , Hamas militants expired on Nov 30 , with neither side announcing a deal to extend it . Earlier , Israel and Hamas had started a four-day ceasefire on Nov 24 after 49 days of bloodshed . Hamas released 6 more Israeli hostages to Egypt via Rafah crossing on Nov 30 , as a one-day extension to ceasefire .
