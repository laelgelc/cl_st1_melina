<p> Over the past few weeks , soldiers from the Nahal Brigade in the 162nd Division have been operating in the northern Gaza Strip . The forces encountered and killed numerous Hamas operatives , destroyed Hamas infrastructure and located large quantities of weapons . During their operations , the 931st Battalion of the Nahal Brigade encountered Hamas fighters who had positioned themselves inside a civilian residence . The fighters were on the third floor of a building and launched grenades at the troops . After arriving at the building , the soldiers encountered the fighters and killed them with precise fire . During scans made afterwards in the area , a camera was located on the body of one of the fighters . Footage was found on the camera of fighters planting explosive devices and setting up booby traps aimed at attacking IDF troops .
