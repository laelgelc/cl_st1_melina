" He is trying to remove the mistrust and hatred between communities . We were part of this effort earlier and we will be part of it again , " he added . Abdullah said he had no expectations from Defence Minister Rajnath Singh 's Jammu and Kashmir visit but hoped that incidents like the civilian deaths in Poonch will not be repeated . " I have no expectations from his visit . Can he bring back the dead ? Does he have that power ? But he can do this much that such injustice is not repeated , " he added .
