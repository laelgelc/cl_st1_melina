<h> IS ISRAEL ATTEMPTING TO ETHNICALLY CLEANSE PALESTINIANS FROM GAZA ? <p> It 's difficult not to be emotionally overwhelmed when you visit a Nazi extermination camp . I 've visited two : Auschwitz and Majdanek , both in Poland . Along with many other camps , these played a major role in the systematic murder by Nazi Germany of some six million European Jews between 1941 and 1945 , an attempted " ethnic cleansing " known as the Holocaust . <p> Auschwitz , to the west of the delightful Polish city of Krakow , was constructed in 1941 and from the following year until late 1944 , freight trains delivered Jews from all over German-occupied Europe to its gas chambers . Of the 1.3 million sent to Auschwitz during that period , 1.1 million were murdered , mostly Jews . Records show that 865,000 were gassed on arrival , the remaining were murdered by starvation , exhaustion , disease or beatings . Others were killed during medical experiments . <p> While more than 2 million people visit Auschwitz each year to view and reflect on the horrors displayed in the various buildings , many fewer visit Majdanek , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ city of Lublin in north-east Poland . <p> When you enter , you feel as if the Nazis have just left . Finding a safe homeland was an idea rooted in Jewish history , but modern legal attempts began only in 1839 . <p> It was n't until 1881 that the first wave of Jewish migration to Ottoman-ruled Palestine began , following pogroms in Eastern Europe .
