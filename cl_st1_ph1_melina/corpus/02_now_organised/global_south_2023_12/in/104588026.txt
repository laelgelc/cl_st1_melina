<h> An excerpt from ' The Shortest History of Israel and Palestine : From Zionism to Intifadas and the Struggle for Peace , ' by Michael Scott-Baumann . <p> On May 14 , 1948 , David Ben-Gurion proclaimed the birth of the new State of Israel , " a Jewish state established by and for the Jewish people . " The next day , armed forces from Syria , Iraq , Transjordan , and Egypt entered Palestine . Consequently , the State of Israel was born in war and its first aim was survival . <h> Israel 's War of Independence , as it is known in Israel , was to consist of three phases of fighting , interspersed by UN-sponsored ceasefires . <p> In the south , an Egyptian army of ten thousand men crossed the border near the coast and attacked some isolated Jewish settlements in what was deemed to be part of the new Arab state . In the north , Syrian forces crossed the border but were resisted by Jewish settlers and Haganah forces , the latter now formed into the Israeli Defense Forces ( IDF ) . Abdullah of Transjordan moved his Arab Legion to defend the Old City , the mostly Arab East Jerusalem . It was his army that the Israelis wanted most to defeat , for two main reasons : first , they wanted to gain control of the Old City in order to protect the Jewish holy places and the Jewish minority that lived there ; and , second , the Israelis knew that the Arab Legion was the most effective and best-trained Arab army -- if they could defeat it , then the other Arab armies would collapse . <p> However , the Israelis were not able to overcome the Arab Legion , although they did gain control of West Jerusalem and were able to feed and protect the Jewish population in that part of the city . In the ten days of fighting during this second phase of the war , Israel improved its position and kept the upper hand for the rest of the war . <p> During the second truce , in September , Count Bernadotte , the special UN mediator from Sweden , produced a peace plan . It gave added land to the Arabs in the south and more land to the Israelis in the north , but Jerusalem was still to be an international city under UN control , and the Arab refugees were all to have the right to return home . <p> The next day , Bernadotte was assassinated by the Stern Gang . The new Israeli government wanted to maintain international support and ordered the dissolution of the Stern Gang and Irgun . Some of their members were then incorporated into the IDF . <p> In mid-October , the Israelis broke the ceasefire and concentrated on defeating the Egyptians in the south . They had survived their first great test and were confident of their future as an independent nation .
