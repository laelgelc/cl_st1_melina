<p> What is the media message of 2023 ? There is n't any . Just that the moon became a star and news stars fell from grace while the unusual achieved stardom . <p> Otherwise , it was another year of living dangerously . The media was under attack , reminding us of the important role it plays . <p> If journalists associated with the NewsClick website were investigated by the Enforcement Directorate and had their electronic equipment seized -- its promoter faces charges under UAPA -- the opposition INDIA bloc boycotted 14 television news anchors . <p> Far from standing together , united , to oppose such challenges to press freedom , many in the media remained silent or , worse , supported the actions . In 2023 , the media is split wide open like the political class : there 's the ' Modi media ' and then there 's the ' Lutyens media ' -- other journalistic enterprises live precariously between the two . <p> And shame on the media for almost entirely ignoring the tragic ethnic violence between the Meiteis and Kukis in Manipur .
