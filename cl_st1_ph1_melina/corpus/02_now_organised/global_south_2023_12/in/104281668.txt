<p> Russian President Vladimir Putin , in a year-end press conference , declared that there will be no peace in Ukraine until Moscow achieves its goals , emphasising the unchanged objectives of de-Nazification , de-militarisation , and a neutral status for Ukraine . Putin dismissed the need for mobilisation , highlighting ongoing recruitment efforts . Addressing concerns about the Israel-Hamas War , Putin remarked that Gaza is the largest graveyard for children globally . The president , in power for 24 years , faced the media , addressing issues from the Ukraine conflict to domestic matters . Ordinary citizens could phone in questions in a new format , with around 2 million questions submitted ahead of the broadcast . The press conference followed a challenging year for Putin , who , unlike the previous year , chose to face the media amid military setbacks and shifts in Ukraine and increased scrutiny from both international and domestic perspectives .
