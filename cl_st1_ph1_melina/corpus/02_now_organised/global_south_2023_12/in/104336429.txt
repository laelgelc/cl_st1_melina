<h> Why villa tourism is taking over Goa <h> For some time now independent villa rentals have emerged as a distinct hospitality sector , but one that 's largely overlooked . Covid turbocharged this by driving the demand for second homes which easily become villa rentals when not occupied by owners . <p> The Goa winter tourist season has started - or has it ? Every December Goa is filled with lamentations about lack of tourists , less charter flights from the UK and Russia and beach shacks facing ruin from empty tables . And , somehow , by March , another successful season is wound up , with everyone planning for the next . <p> This year , like every year , certain factors are cited as likely to undermine the season . The Ukraine and Gaza wars have cut off two sources of regular Goa visitors while the UK 's stagnant economy means fewer British tourists . And Indian tourists , who flocked to Goa when Covid made global travel hard , are now heading to Thailand , Vietnam , Greece , Croatia and other foreign destinations . <p> But Israelis mostly moved away from Goa sometime @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ war . Many UK tourists balance higher costs in Goa with the higher heating bills they have to pay at home , and duly come again to Goa . <p> Indian tourists are looking beyond domestic destinations , but there are always those who ca n't plan in advance and then , at the last minute , find visas impossible to get ( just try getting a Schengen or UK visa at short notice ) , international tickets priced too high ( especially when families are involved ) or can only take time out for a few days . Goa , and other Indian destinations , still work for last-minute planning and short trips . <p> So why the current round of Goa-is-gone groaning ? One reason might be that too much weightage is being given to hotel and resort bookings . Builders in Goa have perfected a style of villas designed cookie-cutter style , each identical and crammed against each other , but focussed internally around a small central pool , so occupants do n't see their neighbours . They do n't really work for long-term living , but are fine for short breaks , which is what most of their buyers want - and which they are happy to offer others , for a price . <p> Builders help by offering management and concierge services , which run the properties and help put them up for rent . The pitch is that , after several years , the property pays for itself with rentals . The eternal battles over Goa taxis are a problem , but car rentals are easily done , and many families now drive down themselves , or send their car ahead with a driver . Restaurants now do substantial business in food delivery to villas - Swiggy bikers are regularly seen in villages , and the older system of ' pilots ' , local guys on bikes who can be hired , has neatly adapted to these needs as well . <p> Given enough notice , many restaurants are happy to do full meals to groups in villas - they save on overheads and work to prearranged menus . Many other services are also available now from people who moved to Goa during the pandemic , such as activity sessions for young children . Villa holidays are also ideal for the many Indian families who now want to travel with their dogs , who get to enjoy walking on Goa lanes or beaches . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ who take up several villas in a complex . Their crammed nature then becomes an advantage , with families close enough to interact , but also have some privacy . Other groups , like friends planning college or school reunions , are also realising the advantage they provide over hotels - not least the fact that scrutiny over what happens in villas is so much less . <p> Demand for villa rentals , especially around New Year 's , has reached the point where residents in desirable properties are being offered large sums to hand over their homes for that period to professional villa managers to rent out . It 's not surprising that this might be an attractive proposition since it involves getting paid , substantially , to leave Goa at a time when it 's most chaotic and unappealing for long-term residents . <p> Villas are becoming de facto hotels , with none of the regulations that might apply to actual hotels .
