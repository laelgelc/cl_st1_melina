The situation that this august body has been deliberating upon has many dimensions . There is the terrorist attack on Israel on 7 October and the concern for the hostages taken at that time . There is an enormous humanitarian crisis and large scale loss of civilian lives , especially of women and children . There is the issue of observing international humanitarian law in all circumstances .
