<p> As a Jewish girl growing up in provincial England then Italy , I grew accustomed to anti-Semitism . " Do n't be Jewey ! " in the playground when I would n't share sweets . " Steen ? And over and over : " You do n't look Jewish " though my blonde , snub-nosed looks make me a ringer for my Polish grandmother . One elderly Italian lady told me that she thought Jews struggled with Nazis because they were similar . <p> Such occasions are upsetting but I have never felt unsafe . This is not the experience of many Jews . If you attend synagogue and if your appearance marks you as Jewish , such badges of belonging make you a target .
