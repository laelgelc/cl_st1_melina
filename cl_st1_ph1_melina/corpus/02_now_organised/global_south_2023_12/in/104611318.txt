<p> New Delhi : India and Russia have agreed to establish the Chennai-Vladivostok maritime corridor , in a breakthrough for ties between Moscow and New Delhi , Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov announced Wednesday . <p> " We have paid special attention to the matters of building up practical cooperation . In addition to the agreements that have been reached yesterday Tuesday ... Today we have agreed upon a number of steps that will allow us to expand cooperation including in the context of the upcoming launch of the International North-South Transport Corridor , the establishment of the Chennai-Vladivostok route and the cooperation on exploration of the Northern Sea route , " Lavrov said during a joint press conference held with India 's External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar in Moscow . <p> Jaishankar reached Moscow Monday for a five-day visit to Russia . <p> The International North-South Transport Corridor ( INSTC ) and the Chennai-Vladivostok , or Eastern Maritime Corridor , would ensure transportation corridors between both the Western and Eastern coasts of India with Russia . <p> While Lavrov Wednesday spoke of the " launch " of the INSTC , the corridor has already been used last year @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> The INSTC was established in September 2002 between Iran , India and Russia . The 7,200-kilometre-long multi-modal transportation corridor facilitates the movement of freight between Afghanistan , Armenia , Azerbaijan , Russia , Central Asia and Europe , besides Iran and India as reported by ThePrint earlier . <p> Both India and Iran back the project , with New Delhi proposing to include Chabahar port , which it has been developing with Iran , to the INSTC framework . The first India-bound Russian cargo -- sent by train for the first time using the transportation corridor -- reached Iran last year , having covered roughly 3,800 kilometres through Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan before being sent to India over sea . <p> Jaishankar and Lavrov also agreed to expand the " legal framework " of ties between the two countries .
