<h> How does a ' theatre-holic ' , Quasar Thakore Padamsee , curate plays that reflect global concerns while entertaining audiences at one of South Asia 's biggest arts festival <p> The sixth edition of the Serendipity Arts Festival in Goa , one of the largest multidisciplinary cultural events in South Asia , will open with a play on a war that has changed the global landscape . Afghanistan Is Not Funny is a true account of British **26;332;TOOLONG Henry Naylor and award-winning photographer Sam Maynard 's visit to Kabul during the war on terror . Its selection is a part of the theatre programming by Mumbai-based curator Quasar Thakore Padamsee that reflects an intersection of global concerns with works of artistic merit . With Serendipity , set to begin December 15 , Thakore Padamsee spoke to The Indian Express about the role of a curator at a time when artists and audiences are seeking to articulate crisis at multiple levels , from political to environmental . It is imperative that the programming that we do as curators or the things that we make as artists reflect our concerns , so that we become a conduit of information for people to access . War is not the only terrible thing that we must guard against . There are many other cautionary tales by storytellers . As curators , we are actively trying to highlight the need to be environmentally conscious in simple things .
