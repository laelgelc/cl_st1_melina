<p> The festive glow of Christmas has dimmed on the streets of Syria 's cities as main churches , in a show of solidarity with Palestinians enduring the Gaza war , have opted for subdued celebrations , focusing on prayers . Traditionally vibrant areas like Azizia in the northern Syrian city , renowned for its lively festive market and towering Christmas tree , now stand eerily deserted with no signs of holiday decorations in sight . The main square , once a hub of seasonal cheer , remains almost empty . The health ministry in Gaza reports a staggering toll , with over 20,000 casualties since Israel 's offensive , predominantly affecting women and children who grapple with displacement , hunger , and basic necessities.In Damascus , a city that once thrived with Christmas vibrancy , now wears a somber tone . The festive atmosphere is confined to a lone market , and even the Mariamite Cathedral , known for its grandeur , displays modest decorations and a small tree in its courtyard . This subdued Christmas reflects the stark reality of the ongoing conflict and the profound impact it has cast on the spirit of the season .
