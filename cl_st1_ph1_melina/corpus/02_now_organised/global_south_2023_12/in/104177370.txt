<h> Kuwait expresses regret over US veto on Gaza ceasefire resolution <p> KUWATI CITU , Dec 10 : Kuwaiti Foreign Minister Sheikh Salem Abdullah Al-Jaber Al-Sabah expressed on Saturday his country 's deep regret over the use of the veto in the United Nations Security Council against a resolution calling for an immediate ceasefire in the Gaza Strip and the rescue of the deteriorating humanitarian situation . <p> In the United Nations Security Council session on Friday , a resolution calling for an " immediate humanitarian ceasefire in the Gaza Strip " , which was proposed by the United Arab Emirates and widely supported internationally , failed to pass . Among the 15 Security Council member states , 13 voted in favor of the resolution , Britain abstained , and the United States used its veto . <p> Sheikh Salem Al-Sabah emphasized that " a ceasefire and the protection of civilians in the Gaza Strip are urgent humanitarian demands that must be achieved as soon as possible , " pointing out that he fears " failure to pass the resolution will be considered a new license for the occupying forces to continue their killings , destruction , and displacement @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ expressed serious concern about the Security Council 's failure to respond to the warning from the United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres to invoke Article 99 of the UN Charter . Through this article , the Secretary-General drew the attention of the Council to the fact that what is happening threatens humanitarian order and the collapse of international peace and security . <p> Sheikh Salem Al-Sabah reiterated his firm position , calling for continued political and diplomatic efforts to meet the humanitarian needs of Palestinian civilians . <p> Israel has been launching massive airstrikes and a ground offensive in Gaza in response to the Hamas Oct. 7 surprise attack on Israel that killed around 1,200 and in which over 200 were taken hostage . On Tuesday evening , the Hamas-run government media office in Gaza said that the Palestinian death toll had risen to 16,248 as a result of Israel 's attacks on the Gaza Strip . ( UNI )
