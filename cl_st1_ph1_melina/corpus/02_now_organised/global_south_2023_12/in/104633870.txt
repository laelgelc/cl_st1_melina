<h> Express View : Israel leaders must look beyond militarism <h> Friends and well-wishers of both Israel and Palestine -- in New Delhi , Washington , Berlin and beyond -- must push for an immediate ceasefire . It is only then that the difficult process of building peace can begin <p> Reports from Gaza indicate that at least 20,000 Palestinians have died since Israel began its attacks in response to the terror strikes and kidnappings by Hamas on October 7 . Over 8,000 of the dead are reportedly children and Hamas still holds more than 100 hostages in Gaza . The Israeli airstrikes on Christmas Eve and December 25 killed 100 civilians .
