<h> Abdullahs , Muftis should stop dancing to tune of Pak : Chugh <p> Excelsior Correspondent JAMMU , Dec 26 : BJP national general secretary , Tarun Chugh took strong exception to former J&amp;K Chief Minister , Farooq Abdullah 's statement in which he criticised the Narendra Modi-led Government for not engaging in peace talks with Pakistan and saying that India could meet the same fate as Gaza if a solution is not found through dialogue . Chugh said Abdullah is playing in the hands of Pakistan ISI and is behaving more like a PRO of the Pakistan Government . Instead of mourning the killing of Army personnel at the hands of Pakistan sponsored terrorists and condemning the nefarious designs of the ISI , Abdullah is dancing to the tunes of Pakistan. , he said . " It is time Abdullahs and Muftis stop doing the bidding of Pakistan ISI and let J&amp;K grow and progress with tourism and computers ' ' . Chugh said Prime Minister , Narendra Modi has ushered in a new era of development and progress in J&amp;K and there is no scope for Abdullahs and Muftis to disrupt it .
