<p> In the morgue of the Nasser Hospital , in southern Gaza , workers wrap the corpses of people killed in Israeli airstrikes in white cloth amid the stench of death . They record whatever basic facts they can about the dead : name , identity card number , age , sex . <p> Some of the bodies are badly mutilated . Only those that have been identified or claimed by relatives can go for burial and be included in the Gaza Health Ministry 's death toll for the war . The rest are stored in the morgue 's refrigerator , often for weeks . The toll stood at around 20,000 people on Thursday , amid renewed international calls for a fresh ceasefire in Gaza . The ministry says thousands more dead remain buried beneath the rubble . About 70% of those killed are women and children , it says .
