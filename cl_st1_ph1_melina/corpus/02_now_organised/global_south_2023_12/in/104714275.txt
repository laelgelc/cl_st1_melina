In his masterly treatise , " The Rise and Fall of the Great Powers " , Yale historian Paul Kennedy saw by 1986 that the United States faced the twin problems of Number One in relative decline : first , the longevity of remaining top dog and second , whether she can retain the economic and technological bases of her power .
