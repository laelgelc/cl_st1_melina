<p> The Indian stock market continued its pursuit of record highs , embracing the Santa Claus rally as benchmark indices Nifty and Sensex reached new peaks . The widespread buying across sectors , coupled with expectations of a rate cut , has sustained bullish sentiments . <p> In the Nifty50 index , Hindalco traded highest with 4.44 per cent gains and Ultra Tech Cement hit a record-high with 4.17 per cent gains , while Bajaj Auto and Tata Motors also gained 3.79 per cent and 2.97 per cent respectively . <p> Conversely , only ten or one-fifth of stocks traded in the red zone with state-run NTPC and ONGC trading lowest with around 1 per cent loss , while Adani Enterprises , UPL and Adani Power traded slightly lower . <p> " Strong global market undercurrent coupled with India 's robust macro performance in past few quarters gave investors ample ammunition to go ballistic on India 's equities as Sensex reached a new milestone level of 72,000 mark led by gains in banking , auto and metal stocks . The rally came despite worries over the ongoing conflict at Gaza and attacks on ships at the Red Sea , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and receding worries of recession in developed economies going ahead dictated the optimistic mood , " said Prashanth Tapse , Senior VP ( Research ) , Mehta Equities . <p> Sectoral MovementIn terms of sectoral performance , PSU Banks and Metals witnessed 2.06 per cent and 1.39 per cent gains respectively , while Pharma and FMCG rebounded to trade higher . Overall the buying was witnessed across the sectors . <p> Moreover , the more domestically focussed indices , mid-cap and small-cap traded higher with gains of 0.38 per cent and 0.45 per cent respectively .
