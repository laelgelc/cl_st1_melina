<p> The US military said Sunday it shot down two anti-ship ballistic missiles fired toward a container ship by Yemen 's Houthi rebels in the Red Sea . Hours later , four boats tried to attack the same ship , but US forces opened fire , killing several of the armed crews , the US Central Command said . No one was injured on the ship . <p> The Singapore-flagged MAERSK HANZGHOU reported they had already been hit by a missile Saturday night while transiting the Southern Red Sea and requested assistance , CENTCOM said in a statement . The USS GRAVELY and USS LABOON responded to the call for help , and the Denmark-owned vessel was reportedly seaworthy and no injuries were noted , the statement added . <p> This is the 23rd illegal attack by the Houthis on international shipping since Nov. 19 , CENTCOM said . <p> In another statement , CENTCOM said the same ship issued an additional distress call about a second attack by four Iranian-backed Houthi small boats .
