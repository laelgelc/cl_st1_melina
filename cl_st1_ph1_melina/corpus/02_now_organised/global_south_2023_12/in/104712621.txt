<h> Summary <p> To gain a glimpse of the kind of strains many countries will face in 2024 , it can help to focus on the challenges facing a single country . Consider Egypt 's recent experience . <p> When Russia invaded Ukraine in February 2022 , the sharp global increase in grain and fertilizer prices disrupted the Egyptian economy , which was already worn down by the covid-19 pandemic , water shortages , and debt . Then came Russia 's withdrawal from the Turkish-mediated deal to allow Ukrainian grain exports through the Black Sea , which pushed prices up once again . By the time Hamas launched its barbaric attacks against Israel on 7 October , Egypt 's annual inflation rate was nearing 40% . Since then , the Israeli reprisals and the unprecedented Palestinian casualties they have caused in Gaza mean that Egypt has been on the front line of a new security and humanitarian crisis .
