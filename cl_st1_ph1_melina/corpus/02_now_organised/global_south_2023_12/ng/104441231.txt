<p> According to the Hamas-controlled Ministry of Health , Israeli military actions in Gaza since October 7 have resulted in nearly 20,000 deaths and over 52,000 injuries . <p> Simultaneously , airstrikes have devastated much of northern Gaza , leading to the displacement of almost 1,900,000 people , constituting more than 80 percent of the enclave 's population , according to the United Nations . <p> Meanwhile , the UNICEF spokesperson James Elder has expressed deep concern about the safety of children and families in Gaza 's hospitals , citing the recent shelling of the largest remaining fully functioning hospital , Al Nasser in Khan Younis . <p> He raised concerns about the deteriorating conditions in safe zones , warning that they are becoming areas of disease due to the lack of water , sanitation , and shelter . <p> However , if this situation persists , James cautioned that child deaths from disease could surpass those caused by bombardments.
