<h> Syrian Air Defenses Thwart Israeli Rocket Attack Near Damascus <p> Syrian air defenses successfully repelled an Israeli rocket attack targeting locations in the vicinity of Damascus , as reported by Syrian State Media ( SANA ) . <p> A military source disclosed to SANA that most of the missiles were intercepted , minimizing the impact of the aggression . <p> According to the Syrian state news agency , the attack originated from the direction of the occupied Syrian Golan Heights , with the city of Damascus being the primary target . The assault resulted in material damages , and no casualties were reported . <p> Rami Abdel Rahman , director of the Syrian Observatory for Human Rights , told AFP that the Israeli air force struck " Hezbollah targets " in the Sayyida Zeinab neighborhood , located to the south of Damascus . Ambulances were dispatched to the area following the airstrikes . <p> This incident follows a day of renewed violence in the Gaza Strip , occurring after a week-long temporary humanitarian truce . <p> Israel 's airstrikes in Syria over recent years have been frequent , with the stated objective of countering the presence of Iranian-backed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a Lebanese militant group .
