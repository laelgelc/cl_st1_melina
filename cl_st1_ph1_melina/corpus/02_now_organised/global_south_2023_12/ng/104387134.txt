<h> Hundreds Of US Synagogues Receive Bomb Threats <p> Hundreds of synagogues and Jewish institutions across the United States received bomb threats by email this weekend , in a substantial acceleration of a months-long spree of hoax threats . <p> The Secure Community Network , which coordinates security for Jewish institutions nationwide , said early Sunday that it had tracked 199 threats over the past 24 hours , with nearly 100 in California and 62 in Arizona . Synagogues in at least 17 states plus Washington , D.C. , were affected , according to local media reports . <p> None of the threats were deemed credible after local investigations . But some of them caused significant disruption : A Boulder , Colorado , synagogue evacuated its Shabbat morning services on Saturday , for example , while a congregation in western Massachusetts canceled its Sunday religious school . <p> In Alabama , the state 's only Jewish lawmaker , Philip Ensler , posted a video to social media showing the moment that the Torah reading at his synagogue was interrupted and everyone in attendance was ushered outside . " This is exhausting , " he tweeted . " I pray @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ peace . " <p> Ensler , who is also executive director of the Jewish Federation of Central Alabama , later said in a federation statement that six of seven Jewish institutions in his area had received the threats . <p> The surge in threats comes at a time of high anxiety for American Jews amid a spike in reports of antisemitic incidents amid Israel 's war in Gaza . It also follows multiple arrests of people who have been charged with sending bomb threats targeting Jewish and other institutions , including a minor in California last week and a man in Peru in September . <p> Hundreds of synagogues have received bomb threats since the latest spree began over the summer , including during the High Holidays . The arrests do not appear to be blunting the threats . <p> " Unfortunately , there is reason to believe that this nationwide trend will continue in the foreseeable future , " the Jewish Federation of Northern New Jersey told community members in an email on Sunday afternoon sharing news of at least five threats in the area .
