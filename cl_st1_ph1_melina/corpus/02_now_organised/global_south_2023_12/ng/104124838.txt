Many of the men were without their battle fatigues as the IDF troops lined up others to proceed farther down the closed-off road . <p> In one clip , the Hamas members could be seen in the back of an Israeli military vehicle . <p> Israel 's Kan News , however , reported that the group of men were detained before the IDF could verify whether they @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Jihad . <p> IDF Spokesman Rear Adm . Daniel Hagari confirmed the arrests on Thursday and said the men were taken into custody in " centers of gravity " for the terrorist group . <p> " Whoever is left in those areas , they come out from tunnel shafts , and some from buildings , and we investigate who is linked to Hamas , and who is n't . We arrest them all and interrogate them , " Hagari said during a news conference .
