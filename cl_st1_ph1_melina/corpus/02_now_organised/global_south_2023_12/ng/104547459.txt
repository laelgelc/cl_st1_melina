<h> Biden Says He Did Not Ask For Cease-Fire In Call With Netanyahu <p> US President Joe Biden said that he did not ask Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to negotiate a cease-fire in Israel 's war with Hamas during a call Saturday , despite rising pressure to do so . Biden told reporters that he had a " long talk " with Netanyahu , not discussing its contents , later adding that he did not ask for a cease-fire , The Hill reported . <p> The international community has nearly unanimously urged the US and Israel to push for a cease-fire in Gaza , as the Palestinian death toll from the Israeli military offensive rises . " We are deeply concerned by Netanyahu 's current military strategy in Gaza . <p> The mounting civilian death toll and humanitarian crisis are unacceptable and not in line with American interests ; nor do they advance the cause of security for our ally Israel , " the letter reads . <p> " We also believe it jeopardizes efforts to destroy the terrorist organization Hamas and secure the release of all hostages , " the lawmakers continued .
