<p> ABUJA- The Minister of Solid Minerals , Mr Dele Alake has alleged that powerful Nigerians involved in illegal mining were the ones sponsoring banditry and terrorism in the country . <p> Alake said this when he appeared before the House of Representatives Committee on Solid Minerals for the 2024 budget defence in Abuja on Tuesday . <p> According to him , a lot of banditry and terrorism are sponsored by illegal miners ; they are not people who pick gold on the ground but powerful individuals in the country . <p> He said majority of the illegal miners were not foreigners but added that foreigners could be seen as symptoms . <p> " Nigerians are those powerful people behind them ; we are identifying them with both kinetic and non-kinetic means . We have encouraged those petty illegal miners to form cooperatives . " <p> He said the most disturbing aspect was that most of the foreigners engaged in illegal mining in the country had no proper immigration . <p> He said for mining to generate the requisite revenue , there was need to have a formal structure that the multinationals could deal with , just like the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , said the approach of the ministry under his leadership would be different from NNPCL , adding that the structure being proposed for Nigeria Mining Cooperation would be primarily private sector driven . <p> He added that " Nigeria will also have its equity , adding that this will mean that no government after can destabilize the structure . " <p> He said the conservative amount of minerals deposited in the country was over 700 billion dollars , adding Nigeria had not even accessed a quarter of what it has . <p> He urged the chairman of the committee to support the process , adding that this would help change mining in the country . <p> He also said the host communities where mining activities took place across the country would be engaged to forestall breakdown of laws and orders . <p> He pleaded with the committee to consider the amount budgeted for the ministry in the 2024 budget proposal . <p> " If we are given that amount of money , I can tell you that what the ministry will contribute will outweigh other ministries , including what we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ trillions to the coffers of this country as revenue if we are given such a budget as proposed , " he said . <p> According to him , we have 44 minerals in high demand but we have six that are in global demand . <p> He said Nigeria had the potential to become one of the destination hubs of the world 's economy through solid minerals . <p> He , however , said there was need to sanitize the operating environment for the investors . <p> The chairman of the committee , Rep. Gaza Gbefwi ( SDP -Nasarawa ) said the solid minerals sector remained the last hope for the country to attain the level of diversity it required . <p> He , however , said that the funds appropriated to the sector in the 2024 budget were grossly inadequate . <p> He added that the committee had taken proactive steps to put up motions and bills that would strengthen and reorganise the sector .
