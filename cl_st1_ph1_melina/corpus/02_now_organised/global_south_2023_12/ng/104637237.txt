<h> Plateau massacre : Self-defence is now or never <p> IN a haunting reminder that President Bola Tinubu 's seven-month-old administration has yet to exert a firm grip on security , terrorists massacred about 160 persons across three local government areas of Plateau State last weekend . <p> In the three-day rampage , the marauders wreaked havoc on several communities in Bokkos , Barkin Ladi , and Wase Local Government Areas between Saturday and Monday , drenching the Christmas festivities with blood and gore . <p> This is another tragic moment that exposes Nigeria 's weak and obsolete security system . Governor Caleb Mutfwang and other state governors must stop depending solely on the Federal Government to protect their people . <p> The massacres again exposed Nigeria 's dysfunctional security system , notably the failure of its quirky single policing system and the corresponding overwhelming of available law enforcement personnel . <p> It was also a grim replay of the New Year 's Day massacre in Benue State in 2018 when Fulani herdsmen massacred 73 persons . The terrorists slaughtered 113 persons in 18 Bokkos villages , Monday Kassah , the LG chairman reported . This toll is too heavy , surpassing the casualty figures of some ongoing wars around the world . <p> As usual , symptoms of a failing state were also evident where criminals will go on the rampage for days without a swift response from the security agencies . Yet , in these troubled communities , there are special joint security teams of military and police operating there . <p> Contrast that to the response of Israel , which came under surprise attacks from Hamas on October 7 that killed over1,200 persons . The response was swift and determined , with a vow to sustain the Israeli Defence Forces ' operations in Gaza until Hamas is destroyed . Tinubu and the Nigerian state should imbibe this template . <p> Nigeria 's North-Central states , especially Plateau and Benue , have been killing fields for years . Compounding this , the violence has assumed political , ethnic , and religious coloration .
