The 2024 budget proposals for the Ministry showed that out of total allocation of N29.24 billion , N24.91 billion has been earmarked for capital expenditure . <p> He said , government can not leave exploration in the hands of the private sector because if that is done , half of the information would be kept for pecuniary purposes to the detriment of the country 's economic and security interests . <p> " So it is proper for government to devote substantial amount of funding into exploration .
