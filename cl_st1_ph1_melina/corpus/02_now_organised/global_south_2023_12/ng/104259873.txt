<p> The Minister of Solid Minerals , Dele Alake , has alleged that powerful Nigerians involved in illegal mining were the ones sponsoring banditry and terrorism in the country . <p> He made this known to the House of Representatives Committee on Solid Minerals in Abuja on Tuesday . <p> He appeared before the House Committee for the 2024 budget defence . <p> According to Alake : " A lot of banditry and terrorism are sponsored by illegal miners . <p> " They are not people who pick gold on the ground but powerful individuals in the country . " <p> Alake said the majority of the illegal miners were not foreigners , but added that foreigners could be seen as symptoms . <p> He said : " Nigerians are those powerful people behind them . <p> " We are identifying them with both kinetic and non-kinetic means . <p> " We have encouraged those petty illegal miners to form cooperatives . " <p> Alake said the most disturbing aspect was that most of the foreigners engaged in illegal mining in the country had no proper immigration . <p> He said for mining to generate the requisite revenue , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the multinationals could deal with , just like the Nigeria National Petroleum Company Limited . <p> He , however , said the approach of the ministry under his leadership would be different from that of the NNPCL , adding that the structure being proposed for Nigeria Mining Corporation would be primarily private sector driven . <p> He added : " Nigeria will also have its equity . <p> " That this will mean that no government after can destabilise the structure .
