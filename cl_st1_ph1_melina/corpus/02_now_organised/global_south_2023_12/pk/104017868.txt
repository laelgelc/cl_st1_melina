Strong headwinds unleashed by multiple crises and unmet challenges make the future outlook a troubled one . Adding to uncertainty is a record number of elections next year in countries across the world including the US . <p> The shift to a more multipolar world will gather increasing momentum but with multilateral institutions under persisting stress . Global power shifts will continue while middle powers will play a growing role in shaping geopolitics . <p> Unpredictability will remain an overarching reality among key geopolitical trends , risks and challenges in 2024 .
