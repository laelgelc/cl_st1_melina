UN Palestine Refugee Agency ( UNRWA ) shelters in the south of the Strip are nine times over capacity and scores of people live outdoors , exposed to the harsh weather , or in makeshift shelters . <p> OCHA said that in overcrowded shelters , sewage can not be managed .
