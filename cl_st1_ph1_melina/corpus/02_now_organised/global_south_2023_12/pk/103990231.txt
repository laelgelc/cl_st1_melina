<p> Caretaker Prime Minister Anwaarul Haq Kakar on Sunday said Pakistan was a strong voice and a proactive advocate for climate finance for developing countries , adding that the same was acknowledged by the developed world in the United Nations ' 28th Conference of Parties ( COP28 ) . <p> The prime minister is currently in Dubai for COP28 .
