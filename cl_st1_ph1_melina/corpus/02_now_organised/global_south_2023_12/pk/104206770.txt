Alvi 's stance echoes Pakistan 's commitment to international instruments and the Constitution , emphasising the unfortunate plight of the Palestinian people.Israel 's actions in Gaza , including the killing of thousands , targeting civilians , healthcare facilities , and aid workers , demand urgent international attention . Equally distressing is the reign of terror in Indian Occupied Kashmir , yet again highlighting the failure of the international community to hold India accountable for its human rights abuses .
