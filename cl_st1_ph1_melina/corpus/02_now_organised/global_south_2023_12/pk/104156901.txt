Huge areas of the enclave have been razed to a wasteland , and over 80 percent of people have been displaced , and are facing shortages of food , fuel , water and medicine . <p> Amid no end to fight and end of casualties , Israel continues to pound Gaza from north to south , and the situation causes spiraling humanitarian nightmare .
