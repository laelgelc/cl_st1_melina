<h> Pakistan laments UN Security Council 's failure to call for a ceasefire in Gaza <p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : Pakistan has said that it is deeply disappointed that the UN Security Council has once again failed to call for a ceasefire in Gaza , even in the face of a human tragedy of epic proportions taking place there . <p> In a statement on Saturday , the Spokesperson of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Mumtaz Zahra Baloch said that despite the invocation of Article-99 of the UN Charter by the Secretary-General and his warnings of humanitarian catastrophe in Gaza , the Council has failed to perform its primary responsibility to maintain international peace and security . <p> The Spokesperson said that the collective punishment endured by the besieged people of Gaza is unprecedented and unacceptable . <p> Mumtaz Zahra Baloch said that continuation of Israel 's campaign in occupied Palestine will prolong human suffering , with massive civilian casualties and forced displacement of millions of people . <p> It could also trigger a wider and more dangerous conflict .
