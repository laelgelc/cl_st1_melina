<h> Afghan ties , Gaza to feature in envoys ' moot <p> The country 's ambassadors posted in key world capitals have been summoned by the government for a three-day in-house consultations on foreign policy challenges including the current state of relationship with Afghanistan , deteriorating situation in the Middle East , Kashmir dispute and other matters . <p> Sources on Sunday told The Express Tribune that ambassadors stationed in 20 key world capitals and international organisations would attend the conference to be held at the Foreign Office in the first week of January . <p> They further said caretaker Prime Minister Anwaarul Haq Kakar would attend the inaugural session . Relevant officials will join the diplomats for the three-day consultations . <p> The conference is an annual feature to discuss contemporary foreign challenges and form Pakistan 's policy accordingly . <p> This is the first time conference is taking place in person since 2019 . <p> The sources said ambassadors of Pakistan in the US , France , Germany , UK , India , Russia , China , Afghanistan , Iran , Turkey and other countries would attend the conference . <p> Permanent representatives to the UN and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the event . <p> The sources said the diplomats would present their respective briefings on the issues that directly affected Pakistan 's foreign policy and make recommendations at the end of the conference . <p> Pakistan 's charge d'affaires in Kabul will give his input on the current state of ties with the Taliban government . <p> The other issue figuring high on the agenda will be the current situation in Gaza . <p> Since the October 7 Hamas attacks , Israel has carried out relentless bombing and ground offensive in Gaza . <p> The massacre in Gaza has raised questions about the role of the Islamic world . <p> The relationship with New Delhi , with particular reference to the Indian Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir ( IIOJK ) , will be discussed in detail as well . <p> India has further strengthened control over the disputed territory after the recent decision of its Supreme Court , endorsing the Narendra Modi government 's August 5 , 2019 unilateral act of depriving IIOJK of its special status .
