<h> Pakistan , Iran FMs discuss Gaza situation in telephonic conversation <p> Foreign Minister Jalil Abbas Jilani on Monday held a telephone conversation with his Iranian counterpart Amir Abdollahian wherein they discussed the dire situation in Gaza as well as the recent terrorist attack that took place in Southeastern Iran . <p> During the conversation , they discussed matters of bilateral importance and the regional situation . <p> He emphasized the urgency of a ceasefire , lifting of the siege , provision of uninterrupted humanitarian assistance including medical supplies to the people of Gaza , and the resumption of diplomatic efforts for lasting peace in the Middle East based on a just solution to the Palestinian question . <p> Foreign Minister Jilani strongly condemned the terrorist attack in Southeastern Iran on December 15 , resulting in the deaths of several Iranian security personnel . He underlined Pakistan 's firm commitment to working closely with Iran in confronting the menace of terrorism , which was a collective challenge to regional peace and security .
