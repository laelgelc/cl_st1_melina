<p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has said that we are disappointed that after a brief pause , Israel has restarted bombing the Palestinian people , adding that the temporary pause offered a crucial respite to the suffering people of Gaza and allowed exchange of prisoners . <p> " We are deeply concerned at the escalating Israeli aggression against the West Bank and the escalation of hostile acts against the Palestinian population , " said the Spokesperson of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Mumtaz Zahra Baloch at her weekly media briefing in Islamabad on Friday . <p> " We strongly condemn the Israeli raid in the Jenin refugee camp and acts of violence and repression against the returning prisoners and their families , " she said . <p> The Spokesperson said that Pakistan reiterates its call for a durable and sustained ceasefire to enable supply of extensive and robust humanitarian assistance ; urgent medical aid to the injured ; and shelter to those who have been displaced as a result of indiscriminate and inhumane bombing campaign by Israeli occupation forces . <p> Mumtaz Zahra Baloch said that the international community should take immediate and decisive @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ terror against the Palestinian civilians . <p> The Foreign Office Spokesperson said that Pakistan supports the call for a comprehensive investigation into attacks against medical facilities , schools , mosques , churches , residential buildings and water facilities and urges full accountability for the war crimes being perpetrated in occupied Palestine . <p> " We reaffirm our support for our Palestinian brothers and sisters in this time of great ordeal and trauma for the Palestinian people , " she said .
