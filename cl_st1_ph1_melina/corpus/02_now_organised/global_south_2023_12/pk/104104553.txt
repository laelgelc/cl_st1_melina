<p> WASHINGTON : The ongoing conflict between Israel and Hamas in Gaza is expected to " severely depress " the Palestinian economy , the IMF said Thursday . <p> " We do expect the conflict to severely depress economic activity in both the West Bank and Gaza , where even before the conflict GDP growth was set to decline over the medium term , " the International Monetary Fund communications chief , Julie Kozack , told reporters . <p> She added that while the economies of the West Bank , Gaza and Israel will be the hardest-hit , " the ultimate impact will depend on the duration and intensity of the conflict . " <p> Israeli forces are continuing their operations inside the Gaza Strip , and have vowed to destroy Hamas for its unprecedented cross-border attack on October 7 , in which 1,200 people were killed and more than 200 hostages were seized , according to Israeli figures . <p> The death toll in Gaza has now soared above 17,000 , according to the Hamas-run health ministry .
