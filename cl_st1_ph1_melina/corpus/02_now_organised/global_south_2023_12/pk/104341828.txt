<p> During his ongoing visit to the United States , the Chief of Army Staff ( COAS ) General Syed Asim Munir held a meeting with the UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres at UN Headquarters in New York . <p> In a statement on Saturday , the Inter Services Public Relations ( ISPR ) said the UN Secretary General warmly welcomed the visit by the COAS and appreciated the contributions made by Pakistan Army and contingents of the Law Enforcement Agencies ( LEAs ) who have remained committed to the peace and stability of the world . <p> The COAS reaffirmed Pakistan 's continuous support to the United Nations in all its earnest efforts . <p> The COAS during his interaction particularly highlighted about the ongoing Kashmir and Gaza issues . <p> The Army Chief said that peace in South Asia will remain elusive until a peaceful solution to the longstanding Kashmir Dispute is found in accordance with UN Security Council resolutions and the aspirations of Kashmiri people . <p> General Asim Munir also condemned unilateral and illegal Indian efforts to change the status of Jammu &amp; Kashmir as it is in violation of UN Security Council Resolutions . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ issue and urged the UNSG to mobilize the international community for immediate cessation of hostilities in Gaza to prevent the unfolding human tragedy and highlighted that enduring solution to the issue lies in Two State Solution . <p> The COAS particularly shared deep concerns over the plight of the innocent civilians who are being brutally targeted and are not being provided with sufficient humanitarian relief . <p> The UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres acknowledged the concerns and thanked General Syed Asim Munir for the visit to the UN Headquarters .
