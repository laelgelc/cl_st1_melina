He went on to say that the oppressed Palestinians have been forced to live like hostages in an open prison for the last several decades . <p> PTI founding chairman pointed out that the oppressed Palestinians were first deprived of their lands , then their houses were confiscated , which was followed by imprisonment and forced migration . He added that now they were being ruthlessly and brutally massacred in their own homeland . <p> He stated that there was no example in the history of such injustice , as barbaric Israeli forces were hell-bent to erase the oppressed Palestinians from the face of the earth . <p> Imran Khan called upon the world community , especially those in the Western countries , to shun double standards and take immediate and tangible measures to ensure respect for international law and values . <p> PTI founding chairman urged that it was imperative to protect the beleaguered Palestinians from the brutality and fascism of Israel and they should be guaranteed their long-denied basic rights with security of life and property .
