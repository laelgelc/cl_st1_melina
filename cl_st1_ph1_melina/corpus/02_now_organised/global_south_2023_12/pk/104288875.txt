<p> PERTH : Usman Khawaja wore a black armband and put tape over messages on his shoes supporting people in Gaza in a muted protest during the first Test Thursday against Pakistan . <p> The opening batsman had wanted to wear the shoes during the match at Perth with the hand-written messages " Freedom is a human right " and " All lives are equal " clearly visible . <p> But Pakistan-born Khawaja , who is Muslim , was told that it flouted International Cricket Council rules on messages during matches that relate to politics , religion or race . <p> With Cricket Australia saying it expected the players to uphold the rules , Khawaja covered over the slogans with semi-transparent tape leaving the words -- in the colour of the Palestinian flag -- visible only in close-up . <p> According to local media , Cricket Australia said Khawaja was wearing the armband in line with a video he recently shared on Instagram from the children 's charity UNICEF from Gaza . <p> In the post Khawaja commented : " Do people not care about innocent humans being killed ?
