<p> LONDON : The latest artwork by British street artist Banksy showing three drones plastered across a ' STOP ' traffic sign in south London was removed by an unidentified man shortly after it was unveiled by its creator on Friday . <p> Pictures and videos posted online showed the man , with assistance from another person , using pliers to break the sign off its post and run off with it as passersby looked on . <p> Banksy posted a picture of the artwork on his website as well as on Instagram , where he has more than 12 million followers . It was not clear if its removal was part of the stunt . <p> Banksy usually provides confirmation of his work on social media , but gives few other details .
