Minister Jilani unequivocally condemned the attack , expressing solidarity with Iran and emphasising the imperative for collective efforts to address the global threat of terrorism . The Foreign Office 's assertion that terrorism is a menace to regional and global peace underscores the gravity of the situation , necessitating bilateral and regional cooperation .
