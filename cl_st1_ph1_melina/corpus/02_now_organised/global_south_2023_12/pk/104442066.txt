<h> UN Security Council vote on Gaza postponed for 2nd time <p> Voting on a UN Security Council draft resolution that seeks the suspension of hostilities in Gaza in order to deliver aid was postponed on Tuesday for the second time in a row . <p> The US is not yet convinced on the draft resolution , which had been scheduled to be voted on late in the day , according to information obtained by Anadolu from UN sources . <p> The members of the Security Council will vote on the draft resolution on Wednesday . <p> The text , which was drafted by the United Arab Emirates ( UAE ) , calls for the urgent suspension of hostilities to allow safe and unhindered humanitarian access and for urgent steps towards a sustainable cessation of hostilities . <p> Reaffirming that all parties to conflicts must adhere to their obligations under international law , it also requests that a UN monitoring mechanism be deployed expeditiously . <p> Earlier Tuesday , deputy US ambassador to the UN Robert Wood told reporters that the members were still working on the text with other players . <p> " The only thing I can @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ now , and we 'll see what happens later today . But that 's all I can tell you at this point , " he added . <p> The US vetoed a Security Council resolution on Dec. 8 that demanded an immediate cease-fire to halt the bloodshed in the Gaza Strip as the death toll continued to mount . <p> Israel 's air and ground attacks on the Gaza Strip since an Oct. 7 attack by the Palestinian group Hamas have killed more than 19,450 Palestinians , mostly women and children , according to health authorities in the enclave . <p> The war has left Gaza in ruins , with half of the coastal territory 's housing stock damaged or destroyed and nearly 2 million people displaced within the densely-populated enclave amid shortages of food and clean water . <p> Nearly 1,200 Israelis are believed to have been killed in the Hamas attack , while more than 130 hostages remain in captivity .
