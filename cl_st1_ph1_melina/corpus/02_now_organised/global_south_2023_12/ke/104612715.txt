<h> Prepare for future pandemics , WHO Chief urges in end of year message <p> The World Health Organization ( WHO ) Director General Dr Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus has urged countries around the world to adequately prepare for the emergence of future pandemics . <p> Tedros noted that the COVID-19 pandemic marked a turning point for the world following three years of crisis , pain and loss . <p> " In terms of emergency preparedness and response , gaps remain in the world 's readiness to prevent the next pandemic . But 2024 offers a unique opportunity to address these gaps , " said Tedros . <p> The WHO Chief revealed that governments are negotiating the first-ever global agreement to protect communities , countries and the world from the threat posed by pandemics . <p> The Pandemic Accord is being designed to bridge the gaps in global collaboration , cooperation and equity . <p> " The Accord , and plans to strengthen the International Health Regulations , represent monumental actions by governments to create a safer and healthier world , " he stated . <p> Tedros further highlighted 2023 as a year of " immense and avoidable suffering and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as well as the Ukraine war . <p> " The barbaric attacks by Hamas on Israel on the 7th of October left around 1300 people dead and over 200 taken hostage .
