" <p> Meanwhile , Israel 's foreign ministry has rejected the case with " disgust " describing it as a blood libel spread by South Africa . <p> The conflict has been raging for nearly 12 weeks since Hamas launched its attack on 7 October , leaving over 1,000 people dead , mostly civilians , according to Israeli officials . <p> The Health Ministry in Gaza said over 21,000 people have been killed in Israel 's retaliatory attacks .
