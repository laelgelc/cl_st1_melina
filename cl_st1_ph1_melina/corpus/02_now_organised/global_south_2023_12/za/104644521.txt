Picture : IBRAHEEM ABU MUSTAFA/REUTERS <p> Geneva -- A UN report published on Thursday deplored " rapid deterioration " of human rights in the Israeli-occupied West Bank , and urged Israel to end violence against Palestinians there . <p> The report , published by the Office of the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights ( OHCHR ) , said 300 Palestinians were killed in the West Bank since October 7 , the day Hamas gunmen went on the rampage in southern Israel and took hostages back to Gaza . <p> Most of the West Bank killings were during operations by Israeli security forces or confrontations with them . <p> At least 105 deaths could be attributed to Israeli operations involving air strikes or other military tactics in refugee camps or other densely populated areas . At least eight people were killed by Jewish settlers , it said . <p> There was no immediate comment from Israeli officials on the report .
