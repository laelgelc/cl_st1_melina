<p> In the age of smartphones , social media , and the 24-hour news cycle , it 's almost impossible to keep information from the public for long . We know more about most celebrities , athletes , and world leaders now than we ever have before . <p> One of the major remaining exceptions to this societal trend is the papal conclave . It is one of the most secretive traditions and events in the world . There 's no outward politicking . No man puts his name forward to become a candidate publicly . We will never know how any of the voting members cast their ballots . The votes are submitted on folded pieces of paper and then burned after each round of votes is tallied .
