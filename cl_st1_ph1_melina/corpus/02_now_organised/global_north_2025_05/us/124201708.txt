Gaza charity kitchens near collapse as aid blockade continues <h> Charity kitchens in central Gaza are struggling to meet soaring demand as food supplies dwindle under Israel 's continued blockade . <p> Dozens of Palestinians gathered at charity kitchens in Deir al Balah and Nuseirat on Wednesday , seeking what may be their only meal of the day as food supplies run low under an Israeli aid blockade nearing two months . <p> " There is nothing left , " said one displaced woman . " If these kitchens close , we have nowhere to turn . " <p> The blockade , in place since early March , has halted all imports , including food and medicine . <p> Israel says the measure is aimed at pressuring Hamas to release hostages . Meanwhile , aid agencies warn of a mounting hunger crisis , with thousands of children now malnourished .
