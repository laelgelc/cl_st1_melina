LOUIS -- Decorated gymnast Simone Biles is often referred to as the " GOAT , " an acronym for " greatest of all time . " But Biles encouraged Washington University students to strive for something different on Monday -- to instead be the GYOAT , or the " greatest you of all time . " <p> " You do n't have to be the greatest engineer , the greatest lawyer , the greatest entrepreneur or the greatest social worker , " Biles said . " But definitely be the greatest you that you can be . " <p> Biles , the most decorated gymnast in history , spoke at WashU as the university 's keynote commencement speaker on Monday .
