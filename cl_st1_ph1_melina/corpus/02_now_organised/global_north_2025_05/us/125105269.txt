And in many cases in Minnesota , making the 180-degree turn is perfectly legal . <p> But just because a U-turn is permissible does n't mean the risky maneuver should done , said Matt Moseley , supervisor of driving programs at AAA Minneapolis . <p> " We should always be asking ourselves : Is it legal , is it safe , is it worth it ? " he said . <p> That brings us to a question from Drive reader Kim Johnson , who wrote in a few months ago ( sorry for the delay , Kim ) .
