When Moses led the Israelites from Egypt , freedom did not immediately mean statehood . For 40 years , our ancestors developed the values , laws , and communal structures that would define them -- not as subjects of a kingdom or citizens of a republic but as keepers of a divine covenant . <h> Humility vs charisma <p> Moses himself embodies a leadership model strikingly different from what we see @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ chosen not for boldness or charisma but for humility . His defining characteristic was that of a shepherd who would leave the 99 to search for the one -- who prioritized the most vulnerable among his flock . <p> This stands in stark contrast to the rising tide of authoritarian leadership we witness across the globe in 2025 , where strength is often measured by dominance rather than compassion . Populists who have mastered the art of social media lead , rather than serving the people with humility . <p> The Torah repeatedly emphasizes our obligation toward the vulnerable : " You shall not oppress a stranger , for you know the feelings of the stranger , having yourselves been strangers in the land of Egypt " ( Exodus 23:9 ) . <p> This moral imperative forms the backbone of Jewish ethics . Our tradition insists that a society is judged not by its military might or economic prosperity but by how it treats its most marginalized members . This is not merely one aspect of our societal commitment but the very heart of our national mission . <h> Ingathering @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ancient dream -- a sovereign Jewish state in our ancestral homeland . Yet the Zionist vision is not merely about political independence or secure borders . It has encompassed the revival of Hebrew culture , the ingathering of exiles , and the creation of a society that should embody the ethical ideals of our prophetic tradition . <p> As David Ben-Gurion wrote in 1954 , Israel will be judged " by the moral character it imparts to its citizens , by the human values determining its inner and outward relations , and by its fidelity , in thought and act , to the supreme behest : ' and thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself . ' " <h> Hostages ' plight <p> Another year of independence , and we can not ignore the shadow cast by the ongoing war and the plight of the 59 Israelis still held captive in Gaza . Their absence from our national celebration represents an unfulfilled promise -- a reminder that our national mission remains incomplete while any of our people remain in captivity . <p> There is an attempt to create a dichotomy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the individual . Somehow , campaigning for the return of every hostage is to undermine the solidarity of the collective , because of the ransom to be paid . <p> This is to miss the very lesson that our first national leader taught . The shepherd is not fulfilling his duty if a single sheep is left behind . Moses is not enough alone : Abraham , Isaac , and Jacob were shepherds , as was King David . Rachel and Leah , the mothers of the 12 tribes , were shepherds , too . Indeed , the Almighty himself is compared to a shepherd in Psalm 23 . <p> This is not a bug but a core feature . <h> Liberty to live <p> True independence means not only freedom from external domination but the liberty to live according to our highest values . When we forget the Mosaic model of leadership -- one that prioritizes the lost and vulnerable -- we risk betraying the very essence of Jewish civilization that predated and transcends statehood itself . <p> The Talmud teaches us , " All Israel is responsible for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ commitment to each member of the flock -- all the more so the country 's leadership . This principle demands that we leave no one behind , that we count every captive as if they were our own family member . <p> We must rededicate ourselves to the values that defined us before we had a state . <p> We must demand from our leaders the humility of Moses , the moral courage of the prophets , and an unwavering commitment to bring home every last captive . <p> The State of Israel represents a miracle of history and persistence , and its ultimate purpose is to embody the timeless ethical vision that has sustained Jewish identity across millennia . <p> Only when we fully embrace this moral heritage -- only when every captive returns home -- can we truly celebrate our independence as complete . <p> The writer is a founding partner of Goldrock Capital and the founder of The Institute for Jewish and Zionist Research .
