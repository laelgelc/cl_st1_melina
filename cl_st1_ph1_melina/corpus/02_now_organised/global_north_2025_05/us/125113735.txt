But those seeking to understand history and current events should value the opinions of experts over laymen . <p> They should look to three types of people to gain their information . First are experts . These are people who have spent years studying a topic by reading , studying other experts , and teaching subject material . Degrees do n't establish expertise , but they are a marker of time spent studying the subject . <p> Second are insightful people .
