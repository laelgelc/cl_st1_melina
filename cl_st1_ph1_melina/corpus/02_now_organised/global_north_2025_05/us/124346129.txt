<p> Ono , 62 , announced Sunday he will step down as president and intends to take the same job at the University of Florida . He expects to assume that role this summer , he announced in a May 4 release through his UM office . <p> Prior to arriving in Ann Arbor in October 2022 , Ono was president at the University of British Columbia . He was hired soon after the university settled the Robert Anderson sexual abuse case , as well as the separate sexual misconduct case with former Provost Martin Philbert . <p> Ono 's predecessor Mark Schlissel was also fired for having an inappropriate relationship with a subordinate . <p> Ono started as the university 's 15th president with a ton of enthusiasm , taking selfies with Olympian Michael Phelps and giving cookies to the students protecting the bronze M in the Diag during Michigan-Michigan State week . <p> He brought that enthusiasm to a number of campus initiatives , but also soon weathered a number of challenges .
