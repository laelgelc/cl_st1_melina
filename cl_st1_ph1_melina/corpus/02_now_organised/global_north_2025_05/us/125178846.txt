Any changes in borders are supposed to occur peacefully and through mutual agreement . <p> This initially emerged in the 19th century , primarily in the Western Hemisphere , and evolved gradually to be reflected in the League of Nations and the United Nations , as well as agreements among leaders in the Organization of African Unity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ result , there has been a precipitous decline in territorial conquests since World War II .
