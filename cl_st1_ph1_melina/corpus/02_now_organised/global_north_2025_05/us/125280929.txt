<p> Abu Toha , 32 , is a visiting scholar at Syracuse University and a 2023 graduate of SU 's renowned MFA program in creative writing . <p> He returned to Syracuse last year through the university 's participation in the international Scholars at Risk program . <p> " It hurts to win a big prize while the suffering which I wrote about in the winning work continues , " Abu Toha told Syracuse News . <p> " It is my biggest hope that this achievement and recognition will be a step toward greater understanding of the decades-long plight of the Palestinian people , " Abu Toha added , " and that it will inspire people , especially those in power , to act and put an end to this tragedy . " <p> The Pulitzer Prize Committee characterized Abu Toha 's work as " essays on the physical and emotional carnage in Gaza that combine deep reporting with the intimacy of memoir to convey the Palestinian experience of more than a year and a half of war with Israel .
