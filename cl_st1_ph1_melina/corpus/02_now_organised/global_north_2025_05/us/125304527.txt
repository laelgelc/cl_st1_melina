Deadly rush at UN food warehouse as Gaza hunger crisis worsens <h> Hundreds of Palestinians forced their way into a UN food warehouse in central Gaza on Wednesday , desperate for aid as hunger grips the territory after months of border closures . <p> Crowds pushed and shouted at the warehouse entrance , with some tearing through the metal walls to reach supplies . Many emerged carrying 25kg bags of flour , navigating throngs of people still trying to get in . Two people were killed and several were injured . <p> The scramble for food shows a worsening crisis , with Gaza nearing famine after nearly three months of Israeli restrictions . <p> Uncertainty remains over who opened fire a day earlier at a separate aid site , where one person was killed and dozens injured .
