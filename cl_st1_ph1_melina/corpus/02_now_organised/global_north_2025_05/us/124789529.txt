An examination of the ' news events ' that were debunked by fact-checkers following Operation Sindoor shows the extent and nature of fake narratives in circulation . <p> One prominent example was the viral fake news about the ' destruction of Karachi port ' , shared by a user on X and viewed over 2.5 million times . The image was actually from an Israeli air strike on Rafah in Gaza . Another widely circulated and inflammatory post claimed that an Indian drone had struck an area near a mosque in Islamabad ; that was , in fact , a fire accident from the previous year . <p> A search on AltNews , a fact-checking platform , reveals the many hyper-nationalistic claims that have been circulating on social media handles in both Pakistan and India . These posts have often been accompanied by commentary -- subtle or explicit -- calling for war .
