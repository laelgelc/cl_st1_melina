Elishai Young fell in battle in the Gaza Strip in October 2024 . Young was a member of the Hebrew Israelite community in Dimona and , despite serving as an IDF tank soldier , he did not hold Israeli citizenship . <p> Young was killed in northern Gaza along with 20-year-old St.-Sgt . Ofir Berkovich , who served in the 52nd Armored Battalion , 401st " Iron Tracks " Brigade . <h> Honoring Staff Sergeant Elishai Young <p> " Recognizing the sacrifice of Elishai Young , who enlisted in the IDF and gave his life for the security of the State of Israel , is a moral and ethical obligation , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ citizenship is not just symbolic -- it is a clear declaration : Elishai was an inseparable part of the State of Israel , and his family will always be a part of us . This is a duty of honor , appreciation , and remembrance . " <p> At his funeral in Dimona , his mother eulogized him : " There is no one like Elishai . He went to the army , and whenever he had a few days or time , he would come to his family . He always said , ' Mom , I 'm coming home . I 'm coming back , do n't worry . Nothing will happen to me . ' He always told me , ' Be strong no matter what -- this is war . ' <p> " I want to say thank you to all the soldiers . What is happening in our world hurts . I want Elishai back in my arms . I want him back . "
