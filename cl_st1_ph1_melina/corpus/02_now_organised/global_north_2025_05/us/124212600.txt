This is not a flowery statement crafted for Independence Day . There is Jerusalem with its holy sites , accessible -- security issues aside -- to all faiths . The external enemies -- Hamas , Hezbollah , Iran -- are dangerous but not existential threats if Israel remains internally strong . The greatest danger is within . <p> First and foremost -- the political chasm that has opened . Democracy accommodates disputes , but what has happened here in recent years is beyond reasonable . The protest movement managed to delay it -- but not defeat it . <p> The danger remains .
