<p> Suha Arafat , the widow of Yasser Arafat , reportedly played a role in opening a channel between Hamas and the US to negotiate the release of Israeli-American hostage Edan Alexander , Hezbollah-affiliated network Al Mayadeen reported on Tuesday . <p> This comes following reports that the US and Hamas engaged in talks to free Alexander . <p> Meetings in March between US hostage envoy Adam Boehler and Hamas largely focused on Alexander 's release . Boehler , with President Donald Trump 's approval , held direct talks with Hamas , as the group has been designated as a terror organization by the US since 1997 . <p> Boehler said in March that US meetings with Hamas were " very helpful , " and that he did not rule out additional encounters with the Palestinian terror group .
