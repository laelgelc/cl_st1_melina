<p> The Knesset Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee decided on Tuesday to extend the deadline that enables the IDF to use emergency measures ( known as Tzav 8 orders ) to call up reservists for service , with haredi ( ultra-Orthodox ) Shas MK Yinon Azoulay casting the tie-breaking vote . <p> The committee voted 8-7 to extend the deadline from May 22 to May 29 . The opposition succeeded in striking down the measure on Sunday after several coalition MKs were absent and when Likud MK Amit Halevi voted against the measure over what he claimed were insufficient guarantees in the IDF 's operational plans to ensure soldiers ' safety . <p> Coalition whip Likud MK Ofir Katz sanctioned Halevi by removing him from the panel " until further notice , " Halevi 's spokesperson said . Katz himself replaced Halevi in the committee for Tuesday 's vote . <p> The coalition has struggled to pass the emergency measures in recent weeks .
