Witkoff as Special Envoy to the Middle East West Asia , U.S. President Donald Trump said that " Steve will be an unrelenting voice for peace . Admittedly , he works for a hands-on President who likes to do all diplomacy by himself . But that still does n't account for his effectiveness , which might be rooted in how he bagged this gig in the first place . <p> At one level , it helps to be the President 's long-time golf partner . It helps even more if you are a businessman from the same industry as the President ( real estate ) , and both subscribe to its distinctive set of values -- pragmatism , realism , and a belief that cracking deals and making money trumps all else , including ideology and abstract questions of right and wrong . <p> At another , Mr. Witkoff has trod the well-worn path of billionaire tycoons who begin by funnelling millions of dollars into a Presidential campaign , leverage their contributions to gain the candidate 's confidence and influence within the party , and after their chosen horse wins the race @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ administration from where they orchestrate a sweet convergence of interests between state policy and private business . Elon Musk is a prime example , and so is Witkoff . <p> However , unlike Musk and other megadonors of Mr. Trump , Mr. Witkoff is one of the few men outside the Trump clan who enjoys his absolute trust . It is this dynamic -- he can pick up the phone and call the President any time -- that sets Mr. Witkoff apart from other notional contenders for the role of one of the U.S. 's top diplomats , be they distinguished career diplomats or politicians with years of experience with the Senate Committee on Foreign Relations . <p> Mr. Witkoff 's interlocutors -- Vladimir Putin , Benjamin Netanyahu , Hamas -- value the fact that they are negotiating with someone who actually speaks for the U.S. President and not another functionary from the State Department . <p> Such a high level of trust is not bought with financial contributions alone .
