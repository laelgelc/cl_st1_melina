<h> IDF announces contracting co. employee killed in northern Gaza <h> David Libi leaves behind his parents and seven siblings . <p> David Libi , a contractor working on IDF engineering projects , was killed in Gaza on May 29 , 2025. ( photo credit : VladislavStarozhilov from Getty Images Pro via Canva ) <p> David Libi , a 19-year-old employee of a contracting company that conducted engineering work for the IDF on behalf of the Defense Ministry , was killed on Thursday in northern Gaza , the IDF and the Mateh Binyamin Regional Council announced . <p> Libi leaves behind his parents and seven siblings . <p> " With deep sorrow , we announce the fall in battle in the Gaza Strip of David Libi , of blessed memory , a resident of the community of Malachei HaShalom in Binyamin , " the West Bank council announced in a press release . <p> David Libi was a third-generation descendant of Binyamin 's pioneering community , and he is remembered as " a devoted builder and lover of the land , " the Mateh Binyamin Regional Council said . ( credit : IDF SPOKESPERSON 'S UNIT ) <h> David served in the IDF engineering corps <p> Libi was the eldest son of Eliav and Sarah Libi , who were dedicated members of the West Bank communities movement . In recent months , David served in the IDF engineering corps , operating heavy machinery used to destroy terror infrastructure , the Mateh Binyamin Regional Council noted . <p> David was on the front lines of the war in Gaza . He demonstrated unwavering dedication to the mission of defeating the enemy . Many terror infrastructure sites were destroyed thanks to his actions , the council wrote . <p> Professional teams from the Mateh Binyamin Regional Council are currently supporting the family , and funeral details will be announced at a later time .
