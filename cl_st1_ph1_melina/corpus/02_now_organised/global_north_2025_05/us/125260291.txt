So here 's the truth about Harvard and nearly every campus in this country : International students are needed not just for higher education but for the higher standard of living and higher ideals their schools deliver to America . <p> Harvard is at the crux of this crucial truth because the Trump administration is trying to revoke its ability to enroll international students . In addition , President Donald Trump has threatened the school 's tax-exempt status . <p> " It 's what they deserve ! " Trump posted on social media . <p> It 's not what the nation deserves . <p> In fact , the opposite : American higher education is world class and an extraordinarily successful export at a time when trade deficits are also a presidential obsession .
