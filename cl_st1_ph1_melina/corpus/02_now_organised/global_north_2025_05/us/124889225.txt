" <p> So far , Cannes ' 25 has made for cultural whiplash of the best kind . The festival presented a string of films that delved into the darkness that surrounds us these days , from Gaza to Ukraine to Donald Trump 's administration , but it also embraced the sheer escapism of Hollywood cinema and the artistic trailblazing of the French New Wave . This year 's Cannes contains all of those things -- and even in a uncertain year with the heart of the Palme d'Or race yet to be unveiled , the messages coming out of the Palais are all the better for being so contradictory . <p> You want the good news first ?
