<h> Letter : Storm-battered St. Louisans should feel kinship with Gazans <p> At a time when our country is torn apart by politics , it is refreshing to see St. Louis neighbors , friends and strangers come together to help the victims of the recent tornado and their families . At last count , there were five dead and more than 5,000 buildings damaged or destroyed . <p> As we think about the St. Louis victims , let us also remember Gaza . There , more than 47,500 have been killed , more than 111,000 injured and 450,000 buildings damaged or destroyed since the Israeli-Hamas war began on Oct. 7 , 2023 . <p> Each week brings death and destruction from bombs on a scale that dwarfs our recent tornado . Gaza , however , is not a natural disaster . We , as a nation , must unite together and demand that our political representatives stop this horror .
