Eyal Zamir on Tuesday night ordered the IDF 's Human Resources Command to initiate a new maximal drafting of haredim ( ultra-Orthodox ) to help with the widening invasion of Gaza and the ongoing war effort . <p> To date , the army has sent out 24,000 summons since summer 2024 for tens of thousands of draft-age eligible haredim , only a fraction of whom have agreed to join the military . <p> In addition , Zamir has emphasized the shift to sending a draft notice to every 16.5-year-old haredi male , something that has generally not been done in the past . <p> It was unclear what new measures Zamir wanted @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , whether in the area of sending more summons or greater readiness to arrest draft dodgers . <p> Further , Zamir directed a speeding up of the special programs slated to absorb haredim in a way that will allow them to maintain their haredi lifestyle .
