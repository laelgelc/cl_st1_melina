Partners last month in partnership with the Muslim American Leadership Alliance . <p> Meanwhile , a plurality ( 49% ) opposed President Trump 's plan to transform the Gaza Strip into the " Riviera of the Middle East , " with 26% saying they supported the idea and 16% not giving an opinion one way or another . <p> Many Muslim Americans support some of President Trump 's key policy objectives .
