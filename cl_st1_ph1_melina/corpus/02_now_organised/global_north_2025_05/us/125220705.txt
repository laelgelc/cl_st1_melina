<p> I have written a lot about the heart-piercing trials and tragedies of Palestinians for a long time . <p> I have treated every word of every column that has appeared on this page , devoted to Palestine 's precarious fate and the indefatigable souls who refuse to abandon it , as an obligation and a duty . <p> It is the obligation and duty of writers -- who are privileged to reach so many people in so many places -- to expose injustice and give pointed expression to gratuitous suffering . <p> I have made it plain throughout : Here I stand .
