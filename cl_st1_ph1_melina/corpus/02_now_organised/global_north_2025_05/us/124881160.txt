Between the solid concrete walls of the underground bunker , Cohn has run the Art Shelter Gallery , a space for contemporary art , since 2017 . <p> Not everyone in the neighborhood welcomes Cohn 's project . It was founded in 2003 by a group of artists , including Uri Zohar , Mordechai Arnon , and Ika Yisraeli . " They were prominent figures from the world of art and culture who began asking questions and looking for answers after the 1973 Yom Kippur War , a traumatic experience for Israel , " explains Cohn . <p> The once secular Zohar , Arnon , and Yisraeli , known in part for their wild public appearances , found what they were looking for in the yeshivot , the traditional religious schools . " They became very religious , and that was a shock in the Israeli cultural scene . " <p> Cohn 's Art Shelter Gallery is said to have been the inspiration for the award-winning cult seriesShtisel .
