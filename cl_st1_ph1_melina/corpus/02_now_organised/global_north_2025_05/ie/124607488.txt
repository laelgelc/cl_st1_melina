In our six medical schools , generations of students have fundraised and worked in countries in need of basic medical aid . <p> They leave a legacy of medical training and return home with a new understanding of the meaning of healthcare which they carry forward . <p> As we sit in the safety and familiarity of our homes and workplaces , watching with horror as the news unfolds , the lives of millions of people hang suspended waiting for the world to make up its mind .
