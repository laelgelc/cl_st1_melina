<h> Shadi Hamid : A genocide is happening in Gaza , we need to call it for what it is <p> Throughout history , atrocities have usually been committed under cover of darkness . The perpetrators know that what they are doing is wrong . They hide it . They deny it . They speak in euphemisms . But what happens when they no longer feel the need to hide ? What happens when they say the quiet part out loud ? This is what is happening in Gaza today . The mask has come off . <p> Ethnic cleansing has become the official policy of Israel . The nation 's leaders are admitting it , without apology . There was barely a pretense before . But now there 's not even that . And these admissions , combined with mass killing on the ground , point to something even more horrific : genocide.
