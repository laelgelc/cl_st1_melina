<h> Kathriona Devereux : A grand plan to deal with next pandemic , thanks to Irish Mike <p> The adoption of a global pandemic agreement that might prevent the deaths of millions in the future is a fitting milestone for Dr Ryan to depart on , writes KATHRIONA DEVEREUX . <p> Dr Mike Ryan , executive director of the World Health Organisation 's Health Emergencies Programme , during the covid pandemic - he has now stood down from the role after helping to draw up a Pandemic Accord <p> People from all over the world who care about the health of their nations are assembled in World Health Organisation ( WHO ) headquarters in Geneva for the annual World Health Assembly . This meeting is due to be momentous . <p> After three years of intensive negotiations , including a few very late-night sessions , 194 WHO member states reached a consensus last month on the final wording of a new global Pandemic Accord . It is due to be ratified this week . <p> Getting that many nations to agree on something as complicated and contentious as pandemic planning @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Dr Tedros Ghebreyesus and Dr Mike Ryan both wore green ties to mark the moment the entire text was ' greened ' . Negotiators and colleagues hugged , celebrating the end of a gruelling process and the beginning of a global commitment to pandemic preparedness . <p> Dr Ryan , who is now departing the WHO , played a crucial role in guiding the world , and Ireland , through the darkest days of covid-19 . I often wonder what Ireland 's pandemic response might have been if the man from the WHO urging us to stay at home had n't been a Sligo man . <p> Irish people do n't always acquiesce to authority , but we listened to every word from Mike Ryan . He was one of our own . <p> The WHO estimates that around 14.9 million excess deaths occurred globally in 2020 and 2021 - both directly from covid-19 and indirectly due to the strain it placed on health systems . <p> Although we do n't like to talk about covid times anymore , these deaths underscore the importance of preparing for future outbreaks @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not a matter of if , but when the next global pandemic will emerge . The Pandemic Accord aims to enhance prevention , preparedness , and response for whatever virus threatens us next . <p> The adoption of a global pandemic agreement that might prevent the deaths of millions in the future is a fitting milestone for Dr Ryan to depart on . <p> The agreement is also a much- needed shot in the arm for public health advocates at a time when decades of progress are unravelling . <p> The defunding of USAID and the U.S withdrawal from the WHO mean vaccine roll-outs are stalling and outbreak containment teams are reducing . Preventable diseases are resurging . <p> The climate crisis and senseless wars fuel further instability and humanitarian crises are multiplying . <p> There has only been one other public health treaty agreed by the WHO .
