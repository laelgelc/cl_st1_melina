<h> Pope Leo XIV calls for major aid package to reach Gaza <p> Pope Leo XIV has urged for humanitarian assistance to be delivered to the Gaza Strip and called for an end to the " heartbreaking " suffering of its people , as he led his first general audience in St Peter 's Square , reports Breaking News . <p> According to the Vatican , around 40,000 people were present at the audience , which followed closely after an estimated 200,000 attended Sunday 's inaugural Mass for the first American to become pope . <p> Formerly Cardinal Robert Prevost of Chicago , Pope Leo began the event by riding through the square in the popemobile , pausing to bless several infants along the way , reports Breaking News . <p> In addressing various groups of pilgrims , Pope Leo spoke in English -- his native language -- as well as in fluent Spanish and the traditional Italian used for papal communications . <p> " I renew my heartfelt appeal to allow the entrance of dignified humanitarian aid to Gaza and to put an end to the hostilities whose heartbreaking @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sick people , " he stated , reports Breaking News . <p> The Wednesday general audience is a long-standing tradition , offering the faithful a personal encounter with the pope . It typically includes a short papal reflection on a biblical or thematic topic , language-specific summaries from aides , and direct greetings to different groups . <p> The event , usually lasting over an hour , ends with the pope commenting on a pressing global issue or an upcoming occasion . <p> Leo began the gathering with his recurring phrase , " Peace be with you , " reports Breaking News . <p> In pursuit of peace , Pope Leo also reaffirmed on Tuesday the Vatican 's offer to host the next round of ceasefire talks between Russia and Ukraine . This was confirmed during a phone call with Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni , according to her office . <p> Meloni made the call following discussions with US President Donald Trump and other European leaders , who asked her to confirm the Holy See 's willingness to host the negotiations , reports Breaking News . <p> " Finding @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ welcome the next talks between the parties , the premier expressed profound gratitude for Pope Leo XIV 's willingness and his incessant commitment in favour of peace , " read a statement from Ms Meloni 's office late Tuesday , reports Breaking News . <p> President Trump had referenced the Vatican 's standing offer during a call on Monday with Russian President Vladimir Putin . <p> While preliminary talks could potentially take place in Rome , Italy would face legal constraints in allowing Mr Putin to attend in person , due to an active international arrest warrant issued by the International Criminal Court .
