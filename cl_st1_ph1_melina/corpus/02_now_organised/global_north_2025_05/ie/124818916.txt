<h> Karol Balfe : Dystopia is the living reality in Gaza and yet world leaders just watch on <p> An injured Palestinian boy weeps at the funeral of his two younger brothers , killed in an Israeli strike in Khan Younis yesterday . Photo : Reuters <p> Karol Balfe <p> Today at 03:30 <p> Dystopia , often fictionalised , is characterised by oppression , suffering , injustice , and totalitarian control . It is normally reserved for futuristic novels . But dystopia is the living reality in Gaza today . For its beleaguered population , it feels like the world is ending , over and over again . <p> This has been heightened by the relentless , calculated and crushing 10-week aid blockade which has seen starvation and famine tightening their grip on two million traumatised people . It has turned daily survival into a desperate act of endurance .
