<h> Watermelon Pictures to launch Watermelon+ , streaming service for Palestinian cinema <p> EXCLUSIVE : Production and distribution company Watermelon Pictures is launching Watermelon+ , a streaming service showcasing Palestinian and world cinema . <p> Watermelon+ will be available to worldwide subscribers from May 8 , and will host original productions , new releases and series alongside classic titles . Subscriptions are priced at $7.99 for a month , or $79.99 annually . <p> The company is founded by co-CEOs and brothers Badie Ali and Hamza Ali ; with Alana Hadid , sister of supermodels Bella and Gigi Hadid , as creative director . <p> It has conducted US theatrical releases for documentary The Encampments , chronicling the Columbia University Gaza Solidarity Encampment ; and Farah Nabulsi 's Palestinian thriller The Teacher . <p> The Teacher and Nabulsi 's Oscar-nominated short The Present will be available on Watermelon+ from launch , with The Encampments arriving later this summer . <p> Further library titles will include From Ground Zero , Palestine 's Oscar submission that was executive produced by Michael Moore and made the 10-strong shortlist for the international feature award @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Cameras . <p> " Our vision is to grow Watermelon+ into a must-have platform for underserved communities everywhere , a true home for bold , necessary storytelling that deserves to be seen , " said Badie . " Watermelon+ is n't just a platform for films -- it 's a launchpad for the next generation of underserved artists whose stories will shape the future .
