<p> Dunamaise Arts Centre , in association with Laois Ireland Palestine Solidarity Campaign ( IPSC ) , will present a powerful evening of cinema and solidarity with a special screening of No Other Land -- the 2025 Oscar Winner for Best Documentary Feature . <p> Created by a Palestinian-Israeli collective , No Other Land tells the urgent and deeply human story of Basel Adra , a young Palestinian activist documenting the mass expulsion of his community in Masafer Yatta by Israeli forces . Spanning years of resistance and unlikely friendship , the film follows Basel and Israeli journalist Yuval Abraham as they navigate their shared fight for justice amid the brutal realities of occupation . <p> Winner of the Panorama Audience Award at the 2024 Berlin International Film Festival and the 2025 Best Documentary Oscar , No Other Land is a raw , intimate act of creative resistance -- a vital piece of storytelling that echoes far beyond the screen . <p> This screening will also include a small fundraiser in support of Gaza relief efforts . Organised by Laois IPSC , the evening will feature a raffle and a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ toward providing urgent medical aid , food , and essential supplies to families affected by the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Gaza . <p> Laois IPSC , a local branch of the Ireland-Palestine Solidarity Campaign , continues to advocate for Palestinian rights through education , awareness , and action . This event is part of their wider efforts to stand in solidarity and support those impacted by the conflict . Join us for an evening of film and reflection -- and help make a small difference on a local scale .
