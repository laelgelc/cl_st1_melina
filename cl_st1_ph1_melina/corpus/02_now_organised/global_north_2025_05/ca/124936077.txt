<p> Mayor Olivia Chow backed a proposed bylaw that would create " bubble zones " to prevent protests that block access to places of worship , schools and child care centres , or target people based on their identity . <p> But a group of councillors is vowing to push for changes beyond what staff outlined , setting up a potential clash on the council floor during debate on Thursday . <p> Last week , municipal staff published a long-awaited bylaw that 's been the subject of heated debate at city hall since widespread protests broke out following the Oct. 7. 2023 Hamas attack on Israel and ensuing war in Gaza . <p> The staff proposal would create a 20-metre protest exclusion zone on city property -- such a roads and sidewalks -- outside any place of worship , daycare or school at the owner 's request .
