<p> The Gospel parable of the " wasteful sower " who casts seeds on fertile soil as well as on a rocky path " is an image of the way God loves us , " Pope Leo XIV told visitors and pilgrims at his first weekly general audience . <p> The parable can strike people as odd because " we are used to calculating things -- and at times it is necessary -- but this does not apply in love , " the pope told an estimated 40,000 people gathered in St. Peter 's Square May 21 . <p> Pope Leo read his full prepared text in Italian and also read the summaries of the talk in English and in Spanish . <p> At the end of the audience , Pope Leo drew attention to ongoing Israeli military operations in Gaza and its limitations on the delivery of humanitarian aid to the area . <p> " The situation in Gaza is increasingly worrying and agonizing , " he said .
