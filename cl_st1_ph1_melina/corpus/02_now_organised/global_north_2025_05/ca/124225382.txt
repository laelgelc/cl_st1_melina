A fire broke out but was brought under control , authorities said . <p> A nearby tugboat responded to a distress call from the ship , which was carrying 12 crew members and four civilians , Malta 's government said . The Freedom Flotilla Coalition said in an email there were no injuries among those on board its vessel . <p> Charlie Andreasson , who has been involved with the Freedom Flotilla for more than a decade , told The Associated Press that he had spoken to people on board who said there were two explosions and a fire broke out . <p> The group did not provide evidence that the fire was caused by drones , but in a video it shared an explosion can be heard .
