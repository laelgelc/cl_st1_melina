Yes ! is a constantly inventive , abrasive tour de force that makes most of the polished competition at Cannes look like made-for-TV movies . It 's a divisive political film eager to anger all sides , a no-win proposition slathered with an obscene messiness that aptly reflects the time in which we live , inside Israel and out . <p> A visibly exhausted Lapid sat down to chat on the beach the day before the film 's premiere , a week after he completed postproduction .
