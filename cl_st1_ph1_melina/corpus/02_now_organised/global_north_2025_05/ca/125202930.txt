<h> UN official says 47 were wounded , mostly by gunfire , when crowd overran aid hub in Gaza <p> Ajith Sunghay , head of the U.N . Human Rights Office for the Palestinian territories , told reporters in Geneva that it appeared Israeli army fire had caused most of the injuries . <p> On Tuesday , crowds of Palestinians overwhelmed a new aid distribution hub set up by an Israeli and U.S.-backed foundation . The crowd broke through fences and an Associated Press journalist heard Israeli tank and gun fire , and saw a military helicopter firing flares . <p> The distribution hub outside Gaza 's southernmost city of Rafah was opened the day before by the Gaza Humanitarian Foundation , which has been slated by Israel to take over aid operations . <p> The U.N . and other humanitarian organizations have rejected the new system , saying it wo n't be able to meet the needs of Gaza 's 2.3 million people and allows Israel to use food as a weapon to control the population .
