Wright created seven variations on this Prairie Style model . Each one came in a kit . <p> Turns out the Fallingwater architect was bullish on prefab homes . During the mid-1910s , Wright and his associates produced nearly 1,000 drawings for a project called American System-Built Homes , which sought to bring affordable -- and architecturally distinctive -- housing to the middle class . He promoted the kits in the Chicago Tribune and Ladies ' Home Journal , but fewer than 20 were actually assembled .
