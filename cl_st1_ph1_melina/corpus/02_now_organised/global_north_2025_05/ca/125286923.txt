<h> Canada calls on Israel to abandon plans for new West Bank settlements <p> OTTAWA -- Canada is calling on Israel to abandon plans unveiled Thursday to authorize 22 Jewish settlements in the occupied West Bank which it says would be illegal . <p> Israel captured the West Bank , along with Gaza and east Jerusalem , in the 1967 Arab -- Israeli war and the Palestinians want all three territories for a future state . <p> Global Affairs Canada says the proposed new settlements " violate international law and undermine prospects for lasting peace and security via the two-state solution . " <p> The Thursday decision would cover new settlements and legalize outposts already built without government authorization . <p> Defense Minister Israel Katz said Thursday that the decision " constitutes a crushing response to Palestinian terrorism , " while a spokesperson for Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas called the decision a " dangerous escalation " that could trigger more violence .
