<h> ' We 're not scared to live in our own country ' : 56,000 people participate in Toronto 's 2025 Walk With Israel <p> An enthusiastic participant on the Walk With Israel , Toronto , May 25 , 2025 . ( Credit : Lila Sarick ) <p> A record 56,000 participants turned out for Toronto 's UJA Walk with Israel on May 25 . The numbers exceeded the roughly 50,000 people who marched last year , UJA Federation of Greater Toronto said . <p> The annual event raised about $1.4 million , earmarked for projects in Israel , an increase from the $1.2 million raised last year . <p> Security was on the minds of walkers , as they strolled past schools and synagogues on the 4 km route that started at Temple Sinai and ended at the Prosserman JCC , with a festival with music , food and celebration . <p> Madison Rochwerg recalled going on the Israel walk as a child .
