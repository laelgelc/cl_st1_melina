<h> And the Bandt played out : Inside the Greens result <p> Greens leader Adam Bandt has lost his seat . At 2.15 on Thursday afternoon he held a media conference to announce he had just called Sarah Witty , his Labor opponent for the seat of Melbourne , to concede defeat . <p> Almost 24 hours earlier , ABC election analyst Antony Green had called the result , but Bandt held back , in the hope that absent and declaration votes , which broke solidly to him in 2022 , would do so again . <p> Fifteen years after Bandt won the inner-urban seat of Melbourne from the ALP , Labor has taken it back , along with at least two of the Greens ' other three seats in the House of Representatives . Maybe all four . <p> Bandt 's loss will stand as the most stunning outcome of last Saturday 's extraordinary election , even more than the loss of Dickson by opposition leader Peter Dutton . <p> Dutton 's seat was marginal , held by a narrow 1.7 per cent at the 2022 election .
