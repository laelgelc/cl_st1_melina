<h> ' What 's left ? ' : Dispatches from a devastated Gaza <p> The European Gaza Hospital in the wake of Israeli airstrikes , May 13 , 2025 ( Image : REUTERS/Hatem Khaled ) <p> My friend Mohammed , a paediatrician who remains working in Gaza , messaged me on WhatsApp last week . " Killing . Destruction . Displacement . Exodus . Hunger . High prices . There is no clean drinking water , no food , no healthcare , no education , no infrastructure , no clean air in Gaza , " he wrote . " What 's left ? What do they want from us ? " <p> Another doctor friend , Khamis , a neurorehabilitation and pain medicine specialist who also continues to care for his patients in Gaza , posted a video on Facebook recently of a displaced elderly woman gingerly navigating the stony rubble with her walking frame . " I ca n't walk , " she cries , looking around as if searching for someone to hear her . Her older male companion gently pulls her arm and keeps her advancing . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pushes his very sick young nephew in a child 's blue wheelchair , the boy 's eyes swollen shut , their life carried in a single backpack and some plastic bags . The uncle collapses in slow-motion to the dusty ground . The boy , whose father has just been killed , motions him impatiently to keep going . The boy 's grandmother is inconsolable . " We 're tired ! " she sobs , " They send us ' aid ' . We do n't want your food . We just want the war to stop . We 're tired . We 're tired . All that we 've been through . We 're not animals . We 're people . Where are all the other nations and the world ? " <p> These scenes and messages came as the Israeli Occupation Forces ordered the forceful displacement of the residents of Khan Younis , a major city in southern Gaza , and those in northern Gaza , as it prepared for a new and " unprecedented " military ground offensive -- Operation Gideon 's Chariots . Hundreds of innocent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ weeks alone in some of the worst single-day death tolls since the beginning of the conflict in October 2023 . Four major hospitals -- Kamal Adwan Hospital , Indonesia Hospital , Hamad Hospital for Rehabilitation and Prosthetics , and the European Gaza Hospital -- have had to suspend medical services due to direct targeting or proximity to attacks . Flimsy amounts of humanitarian aid including food and baby formula have been permitted by Israel to enter . <p> This week , two people were fatally crushed and two others died of gunshot wounds after a crowd desperately forced its way into the World Food Programme warehouse in central Gaza , " tearing away sections of the building 's metal walls " . Sigrid Kaag , the acting UN special coordinator for the Middle East , told the UN security council the limited aid being allowed into Gaza is akin to " a lifeboat after the ship has sunk " . <p> As of this week , Israel has deployed all of its standing infantry and armoured brigades to Gaza , amounting to tens of thousands of troops on the ground @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Arabic language professor , described in a distressed message a few nights ago what this looks like for his family : " Sounds of explosions so loud every 15 minutes , the house shook several times , it 's like a mini earthquake every time , I keep thinking for how long our house will hold before it falls under one of those man-made tremors , and people are hungry , like they barely find what keeps them alive ... " <p> On a visit to Gaza for palliative care research in 2020 , I spent time in the European Gaza Hospital . I chatted to cancer patients and healthcare workers , wandered its courtyards of green shrubs and vibrant red native Australian callistemon flowers , and sat on outdoor benches where patients shared cigarettes and conversation . On May 13 , 2025 , warplanes dropped several massive bombs on the European Gaza Hospital in Khan Younis , killing and injuring dozens , and putting the hospital -- and vital services unavailable anywhere else in Gaza , including cancer care , neurosurgery and cardiac care -- completely out of service @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ care , having been forcibly displaced from multiple hospitals during the assault . They were all forced to evacuate , again . <p> One colleague posted a photo to Facebook of the outdoor wooden bench where they would sit on coffee breaks , now showered with shrapnel . My friend Randa , a cancer and palliative care nurse , sent messages via Facebook Messenger in the aftermath : " What good are my words in a society that has become deaf and blind ? We are being exterminated . There is no safe place . Everyone here is dying -- either from airstrikes , shelling or hunger . There is nothing to eat and nowhere to take shelter . " <p> " Yesterday , my brother 's children became motherless . What was their fault ? And today , my friend survived , but what kind of survival is it , when she lost all her children ? " <p> Mohammed wrote again to me this week , " Life has been taken out of service . The future has been executed . And humanity has been assassinated . " <p> He shares an account from his daughter , who asks , " How do you exist in a world that tells you , over and over , that your pain is too complicated , too political , too inconvenient ? ... What if the only constant is the fight to keep breathing ? " <p> Randa continued her message , shifting to past tense : <p> " ...
