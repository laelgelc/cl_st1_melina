<h> Where the election will be won and lost <p> The Albanese government will be returned with an increased majority , there will be an expanded cross bench in the parliament , and the Liberal Party will win the lowest proportion of seats in the house since its foundation in 1944 . <p> This is the prediction of Paul Smith , director of public affairs and public data for YouGov , on the basis of the company 's final poll before today 's election , released late on Wednesday night . <p> According to YouGov 's MRP method -- MRP standing for multi-level regression and post-stratification , a complex and purportedly more accurate way of measuring electoral opinion that models thousands of simulated outcomes on a seat-by-seat basis -- the Coalition has just a 1 per cent chance of forming a majority government . <p> A Labor majority is a 97.3 per cent probability , and a hung parliament , in which neither party commands a majority , a 2.7 per cent probability . <p> According to the company , Labor will win 52.9 per cent of the vote after @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ figures , Labor will increase its numbers from the current 77 lower house seats to 84 .
