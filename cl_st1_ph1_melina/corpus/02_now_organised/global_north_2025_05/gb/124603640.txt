Israel is unpopular even in European countries that have historically been its allies , such as Germany , signaling growing discomfort with its government 's conduct in Gaza and the West Bank . <p> The data used in this story came from an online survey conducted by Nira Data and the Alliance of Democracies between April 9-23 , 2025 .
