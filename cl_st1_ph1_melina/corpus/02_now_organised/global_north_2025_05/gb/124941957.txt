It does not necessarily reflect the view of The Herald . <p> A Liberal Democrat with over 40 years of council activity has been elected as the new political leader of Pendle Council . <p> Lib-Dem David Whipp was voted into the top job at Pendle Council 's annual meeting , where he announced plans for a new cross-party executive with other councillors . <p> It comes as Pendle Council seeks a new chief executive , is being recommended to include more people on work around a potential Lancashire-wide council shake-up and as Reform UK takes control of Lancashire County Council . <p> David Whipp is a Lib-Dem borough councillor for the Earby and Coates ward . But he was also elected to Lancashire County Council in this month 's local elections for the Pendle Rural division . David Whipp won the support of most councillors for the leader 's role . Currently at Pendle , there are 11 Conservatives , 10 Independents , nine Lib-Dems , two True Independents and one Reform UK councillor -- newly-elected Marion Atkinson . <p> Speaking at the meeting , Coun Whipp said : " Can I thank the council very much for this . I hope to work in the fashion that I 've worked for this council for the last 45 years , in an inclusive and consensual way with all parties for the benefit of the borough . " <p> He chose Independent Coun Asjad Mahmood as deputy leader . The pair have worked together in past executives including with Coun Mahmood as council leader . But earlier this year , Coun Whipp resigned as deputy leader at the time over the Pendle taxi licensing and safety row . <p> Now , Coun Whipp needs to form a new executive .
