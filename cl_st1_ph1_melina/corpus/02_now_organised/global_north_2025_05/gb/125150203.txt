<h> Leicester joins global kite festival to show ' action and solidarity ' <h> It was a peaceful way to increase awareness according to organisers <p> People could be seen flying kites with the Palestinian flag in Victoria Park ( Image : Devon Winters ) <p> Kites flew high in Leicester over the weekend as part of a global festival . Organisers said the kites were a " peaceful " way to help show solidary amid the ongoing plight of Palestinians . <p> The Kites 4 Palestine event took place at Victoria Park on Sunday ( May 25 ) , with a host of kites flying high in the sky . A number of Palestinian flags were also raised as part of the event which aimed to raise awareness of the ongoing plight affecting Palestinian people in Gaza . <p> An event spokesperson said : " The event is designed to be a peaceful way to increase awareness about the current situation in Gaza . The weather was just right for this event , as a strong wind was blowing . Leicester was among a number of places which took place in the Kites 4 Palestine global event . Last year 's festival saw kites raised at locations includding London , New York , Tokyo and Cairo . <p> A spokesperson revealed the kite was a symbol of solidarity . Whether you fly a kite , donate a toy , or raise money , you 're showing them that they are not forgotten . "
