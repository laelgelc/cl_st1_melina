Now it 's from having the right political opinions : is immigration destroying this country or contributing to it ? Is VAT on private education crushing aspiration or merely taxing the rich ? Our opinions are confirmed to us by the political podcasts we listen to -- the 21st-century version of religious sermons -- and where once we might have worn religious imagery on necklaces , now we tell the world who we are by posting our political opinions .
