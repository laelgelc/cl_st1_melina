They are not strangers to me . <p> In my extended family , some new arrivals have Czech , Filipino and Estonian blood in their veins along with my mix of Irish , Welsh , Scots and English . These children are n't strangers to me . <p> As a child , a teenager , a young man in his twenties , then as a freshly-minted husband , a new dad approaching 30 , and a writer entering mid-life , I saw Britain as flawed -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bring us all increasingly closer together . <p> Over the last 10 years , though , that collapsed . We 've been driven apart by politicians , and vast swathes of the British media , who separate us on the grounds of race , gender , religion and sexuality . There 's power in division . But only mourning now for the notion of togetherness . <p> So I 'm estranged from the ruling spirits of this country , the politicians and the pundits , who say we must anatomise into ever-more separate categories of identity . <p> The dam holding back that sense of estrangement burst this week . That a Labour Prime Minister would seek to claim that the people who share this island with me are strangers broke its banks . <p> Keir Starmer knew what he was saying and who he was saying it to , he did n't accidentally chose to mimic the words of Enoch Powell . <p> A party I grew up believing in , a party which my family supported from its formation a century ago , is now dead . I 've asked my friends and my neighbours and my family . <p> There 's hurt for sure , but there 's also a sense of fear , a sense of fear which does n't wish to be spoken of too loudly in case by naming the fear it walks into their lives . <p> We 've been turned into an island of cruelty , not of strangers . I see and hear the same sense of fear among trans friends . They feel hounded , shamed ; they feel utterly alone , that nobody speaks for them . <p> They can not even use a public bathroom , any more . If they speak for themselves -- if they lose their temper , which they have done , with those who degrade and belittle them -- they are labelled abusers . <p> Defend the trans community at your peril . Men who defend the trans community are labelled misogynists , groomers , rape apologists . <p> None of this is true . Just as it 's not true that refugees and migrants are destroying Britain . This country is being destroyed by obscene income inequality . That 's the simple truth . <p> Britain has chosen to put the weakest in the firing line : the foreign outsider , the sexual outsider . <p> Cruelty has made us an absurd country . In order to appease concocted fear around the " foreign other " we 're now going to destroy our own care system . <p> Labour does n't even believe what it says . What do we represent ? <p> Is there a debate needed around immigration ? Yes , for pity 's sake , yes . The effective open borders policy which the Tories developed can not stand . But Starmer is not debating , he is scapegoating . He is unleashing demons . <p> Was there a debate needed around any clash between women 's rights and trans rights ? Yes , of course . Maybe I 'm what can now be called " old-fashioned " . <p> Whatever I am , I 'm a stranger today to the mood unfolding across Britain . I 'm from a now-distant time when we walked towards a united future , when we tried very hard to deal with our differences and conflict in a way which granted dignity and respect to all . <p> However , politics and the media -- two industries in which I 've been immersed my entire adult life -- have proved so incompetent at navigating the challenges which life presents , that the path this country now walks is one of hatred and cruelty . <p> So my estrangement feels profound .
