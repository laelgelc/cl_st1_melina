Everyone is now scrambling to work out what might succeed it . A new multi-polar order ? Spheres of influence ? A worldwide version of the 19th-century Concert of Europe ? By far the most plausible answer , however , is a prolonged and dangerous period of global disorder . <p> Of course there never was a golden age of universal liberal international order .
