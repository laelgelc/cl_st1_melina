<p> While mentoring underprivileged youth at north London 's Arsenal Football Club in 2016 , I noticed regular and alarming newspaper reports about the deaths of local young men in knife-related stabbings and high levels of male suicide . <p> I talked to the young men about childhood issues , such as absent fathers , gangs , childhood poverty , a dearth of male role models and mental health issues . Existential issues relating to the " growing pains " of becoming an adult in challenging inner-city London was a common theme . And so the project Young Men , Worst Fears was born . <p> It seemed that the question " what is your worst fear " ignited their imagination and caused them to drop their guard . It was as if they were unravelling .
