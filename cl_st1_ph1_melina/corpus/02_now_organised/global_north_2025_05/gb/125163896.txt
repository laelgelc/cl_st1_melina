Distress and despair <h> Israel 's safety needs to be defended , but for Netanyahu 's government to drive out the Palestinians would be a disaster <p> ' How do you feel about Israel and Gaza ? " I get it everywhere I go , and I admit , I rather resent it . I 'm British . I live in Pinner . And the truth is that , British though I am , what happens to Israel is of vital importance to every Jew and being Jewish is vital to me . So how do I feel about Israel and Gaza ? All right , I 'll tell you . <p> Last week , I attended a seminar about Belsen , the place which had starved my grandmother to death . The Jews accepted this division , the Palestinians did not . Instead they began a war to drive out the Jews . Seemingly undaunted by repeated defeats and the disaster this has brought upon everybody , they have never ended this war . They are still fighting it . <p> Earlier this year , desperate to find some common ground , to initiate a dialogue , I wrote that I understood why Palestinians had initially rejected the UN decision . In Gaza the murderous cult of Hamas has seized power , invaded Israel and enjoyed the support of protest activists for their campaign . <p> The political result of this has been predictable . People like me , inside and outside Israel , who retain a belief in a two-state solution look foolish , impractical and credulous . It is therefore unconscionable to drive out Palestinians from Gaza and for Israel to settle the West Bank . <p> Hamas launched a war against Israel , with its horrendous attack . They then hid among civilians , in tunnels and near schools and hospitals . They knew that Israel would have to come after them , for its own safety . And Hamas wanted as many children as possible to die , for propaganda purposes . They had tunnels and used them to hide themselves and the hostages , rather than the civilian population . The blame for the civilian dead lies with them . <p> But for Israel to move from a war to eliminate Hamas to one that aims to drive out Palestinians from Gaza altogether is horrendous and must be opposed most robustly . It is also simply unacceptable to use the general withholding from civilians of food , water and other supplies as a way of waging war . <p> To find myself needing to write these things is profoundly painful . The more it rises the more Jews become desperate to keep Israel safe . And the more we strive to keep Israel safe , the more antisemitism rises . Round and round we go , spiralling downwards . You ask how I feel about Israel and Gaza . That 's how .
