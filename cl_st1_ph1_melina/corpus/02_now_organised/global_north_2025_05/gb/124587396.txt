<h> Pope Leo sings Marian prayer at first Sunday address to pilgrims <p> ROME -- In his first Sunday Regina Coeli address during Easter , Pope Leo XIV gave pilgrims a surprise and sang the traditional Marian prayer , and prayed for increased vocations to the priesthood and consecrated life . <p> Speaking to pilgrims from the central loggia of St Peter 's Basilica , Leo noted that his address coincided with Good Shepherd Sunday , saying it was " a gift " that his service as pope began on that day . <p> " On this Sunday , we always hear proclaimed at Mass a passage from the tenth chapter from the Gospel of John , where Jesus reveals himself as the true Shepherd : who knows and loves his sheep and gives his life for them , " he said . <p> Leo noted that it also marked the World Day of Prayer for Vocations , which has been observed for 62 years , and that the weekend also coincided with the Jubilee of Bands and Popular Entertainment as part of the broader Jubilee of Hope . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for their music and performances , saying , " they enliven the feast of Christ the Good Shepherd : the One who guides the Church with his Holy Spirit .
