The Palestinian left , since the 1960s , has understood that Palestine 's liberation is inseparable from that of the wider region . <p> This is partly due to the regional nature of Zionism itself : a military outpost used to suppress challenges to western power and influence . Israel 's actions across the Levant over the past 16 months provide a contemporary example of this . <p> It is also a reflection of class relations inside Palestine .
