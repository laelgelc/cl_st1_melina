Even if MPs can agree on the text of the Bill itself , it nonetheless faces significant procedural hurdles . This is because there is very little House of Commons time for private members ' Bills -- typically just 13 Fridays per annual session for all Bills . This in practice means they must usually complete their report stage in a single five-hour sitting to stand any chance of passing.The key challenge is that MPs could table large numbers of amendments , and speak at length , meaning that the Bill runs out of time .
