<p> In his speech to the summit in Dublin Castle , Mr Martin reiterated the Government 's support for a two-state solution to the Israeli-Palestinian conflict . <p> " The horrific and unjustified attack by Hamas on Israel on 7 October 2023 has been followed by the brutal waging of war by Israel in Gaza , that has inflicted appalling suffering on Palestinians in Gaza while increasing tensions in an already damaged and fragile region , " he said . <p> " There must be an immediate ceasefire , the release of all remaining hostages and the resumption of unhindered humanitarian assistance to the people of Gaza and at an unprecedented scale . <p> " It has been over 50 days since food , medicines and other essential supplies have not been allowed enter Gaza . Israel 's continuing humanitarian blockade is pushing Gaza closer to a hunger crisis . This must be lifted immediately . <p> " It is simply wrong , in principle and in law , to inflict hunger and suffering on a civilian population , whatever the circumstances . This behaviour clearly constitutes a war crime @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the mass displacement of people in Gaza or to talk of permanent occupation . Not only is it an affront to decency and to international law , history tells us it offers no solution . <p> " Ireland remains convinced that the implementation of a two-state solution is the only way to establish lasting peace and security for both Israel and Palestine , and the wider region . We will continue to work with international partners to step up our efforts to achieve that goal . "
