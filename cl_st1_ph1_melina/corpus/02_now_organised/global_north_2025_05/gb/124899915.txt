The company made several business deals in the region during Trump 's first term and Trump 's son-in-law , Jared Kushner , inked a $2 billion investment with the Saudi crown prince after leaving the White House . But in his second term as president , any " past conflict-of-interest concerns about Trump seem petty , as he strikes deals with Middle Eastern sovereign wealth funds and members of the ruling families of the Arab world , " said Mother Jones . <p> In one notable instance , Trump 's son Eric " signed a deal with representatives from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ links to the Saudi royal family , to build a Trump-branded resort just north of Doha , Qatar , " said Mother Jones . Trump has also " declined to duplicate his first-term pledge to not advance his personal business interests from the White House .
