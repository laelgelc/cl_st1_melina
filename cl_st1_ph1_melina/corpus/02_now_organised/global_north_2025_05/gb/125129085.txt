<h> Oxford man kayaking to London in support of Palestinians <p> This article is brought to you by our exclusive subscriber partnership with our sister title USA Today , and has been written by our American colleagues . It does not necessarily reflect the view of The Herald . <p> An Oxford resident has begun a paddle from his home city to London in support of a Palestinian charity . <p> Dr Neil McCulloch , a keen kayaker , started his ' Paddle for Palestine ' on Saturday , May 24 . <p> Dr McCulloch plans to paddle 25km each day , travelling from Donnington Bridge to Westminster , a total distance of 200km , to raise funds for Medical Aid for Palestinians . <p> He said : " We have all seen on TV the tragic devastation and loss of life in Gaza as a result of relentless bombardment and the withholding of humanitarian aid . <p> " I felt I had to try and do something to help , so I set out to do this paddle and set up a JustGiving page . <p> " The response @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 3,000 , three times the original target . " <p> Dr McCulloch also hopes to bring the issue to the attention of East Oxford MP Anneliese Dodds . <p> He said : " The government needs to do far more to stop this awful war and to ensure access to humanitarian aid for the Palestinian people , as required by international law . <p> " I 've therefore written to Anneliese Dodds and , as her constituent , asked to meet with her when I arrive at Westminster . <p> " If I 've paddled 200km to get there , the least she can do is meet me to explain what she is doing to pressure the government to take action to end the moral outrage we witness on our TVs everyday .
