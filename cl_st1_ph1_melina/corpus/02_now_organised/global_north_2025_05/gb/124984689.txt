<h> Jewish memorial in Palmeira Square in Hove attacked again <p> This article is brought to you by our exclusive subscriber partnership with our sister title USA Today , and has been written by our American colleagues . It does not necessarily reflect the view of The Herald . <p> A Jewish memorial in the city has been attacked again . <p> The memorial in Palmeira Square in Hove was reportedly targeted on Wednesday ( May 14 ) at 3.30pm . <p> Heidi Bachram , an organiser from the Palmeira Memorial Group , told The Argus that " it 's extremely distressing " to see the memorial damaged again . <p> She said : " There have been over 40 attacks on it including vandalism , theft , and graffiti . Four people have been arrested for committing crimes there . <p> " The abuse has been relentless .
