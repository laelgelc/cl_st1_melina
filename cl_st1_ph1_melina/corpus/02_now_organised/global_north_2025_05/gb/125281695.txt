<p> Gadot has become a focal point of pro-Palestine protests due to her high profile and IDF record . After winning the Miss Israel pageant in 2004 , she was conscripted into the army and served as a combat trainer , specialising in gymnastics , callisthenics and hand-to-hand techniques . <p> Fewer than three weeks of her two year service was spent in uniform .
