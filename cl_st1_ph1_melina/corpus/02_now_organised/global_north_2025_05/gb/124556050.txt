<h> The Documentary Podcast <h> BBC OS Conversations : Inside Gaza <p> May 10 , 2025 <p> 23 minutes <p> Available for over a year <p> After 19 months of war , Israel says it is preparing to expand its military campaign in Gaza . The aim is to defeat and dismantle Hamas , regarded as a terrorist organisation by many countries and bring home the remaining 59 hostages . They have been held in captivity since Hamas gunmen attacked Israel on 7 October , 2023 . There have been several strikes on Gaza within the last week alone , with reports of dozens of people killed and wounded . We hear from women and men in the Palestinian territory about the bombings , the food shortages and their fears for another looming crisis ahead . Aid agencies have warned that , since Israel cut off humanitarian aid on 2 March , after the collapse of a two-month ceasefire , mass starvation is imminent .
