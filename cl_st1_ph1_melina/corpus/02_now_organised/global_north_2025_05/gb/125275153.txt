<p> PRO-PALESTINIAN activists targeted the London-based landlords of an Israeli weapons factory this morning . <p> The Palestine Action protesters threw red paint on the registered address of Discovery Park Ltd and shattered the windows , to demonstrate against the company 's involvement in arming the genocide in Gaza . <p> The company leases property to Instro Precision in Kent , which is owned by Elbit Systems , Israel 's biggest weapons producer . <p> Instro was granted more than a dozen weapons export licences for the shipment of arms to Israel last year . Most fell under the category " ML5b , " which includes target acquisition and related systems . <p> A Palestine Action spokesperson said : " Discovery Park Ltd are the landlords of a Kent-based Israeli weapons factory which is exporting targeting gear for the Israeli military to commit genocide in Gaza . <p> " As part of our commitment to the Palestinian people , we will not allow companies on our doorstep to profit from mass murder . Discovery Park must evict Elbit , or expect Palestine Action . "
