Unfortunately , Israel 's response has gone too far and its actions have caused terrible humanitarian disaster ; and the restrictions imposed on the delivery of humanitarian supplies are completely unacceptable . <p> " In our opinion , it may even be a likely breach of international humanitarian law . So it can not be justified , " he added . <p> Earlier in May , Israel partially ended an 11-week long aid blockade on Gaza .
