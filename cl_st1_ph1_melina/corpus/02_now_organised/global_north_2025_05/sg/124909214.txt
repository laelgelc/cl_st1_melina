<p> INTERNATIONAL : Today , the worldwide hunger predicament is getting worse at a frightening rate , with approximately 300 million people staggering on the brink of starvation , according to the latest Global Report on Food Crises ( GRFC ) published by The Guardian . <p> Monitoring severe food insecurity across 53 nations , the report discloses that 295.3 million individuals -- virtually a quarter of the inhabitants in the observed regions -- are confronted with high levels of critical food insecurity in 2024 . This escalating food insecurity is driven by a lethal mixture of intensifying battles and regional conflicts , climate disasters , and cuts to philanthropic support . <p> An additional 13.7 million people were strapped into continuing hunger last year , and according to the warnings of trend experts , it is rising beyond control . " Intensifying conflict , increasing geopolitical tensions , global economic uncertainty , and profound funding cuts are deepening acute food insecurity , " the GRFC cautioned in its study . <h> Gaza , Sudan , and beyond -- epicentres of catastrophe <p> The most severe starvation , categorised by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as a " catastrophe , " has more than doubled , affecting multitudes of people in the Gaza Strip and Sudan . More than 95% of those suffering this dreadful level of food deprivation were located in these two areas . In Sudan , where civil war has devastated infrastructure and banished millions of people , starvation has been publicly and formally declared , with over 24 million people now suffering from serious food insecurity . <p> Circumstances in Gaza have likewise worsened . The failure of an armistice and the sustained barricade on charitable support have left more than half of Gaza 's 2.1 million inhabitants in disastrous food insecurity . " Following the closure of all crossings into the Gaza Strip in early March ... food access has been severely restricted , " the report stated . The latest IPC evaluation published Monday established that Gaza 's residents remain at " critical risk " of famine . <p> Other countries in calamitous conditions include Haiti , South Sudan , and Mali , where continuing undernourishment and food scarcities continue amidst political volatility and aggression . <h> Climate change @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ report highlights the increasing role of climate change in exacerbating universal deprivation and is compounding the pain and chaos brought about by regional tensions . Severe famines are predicted across East Africa , predominantly in Ethiopia , Somalia , and Kenya , along with parts of Afghanistan and Pakistan , compromising agricultural production and food accessibility . <p> Similarly upsetting is the sweeping drop in humanitarian subsidies , specifically from USAID . The sudden termination of assistance initiatives this year has halted food services and amenities for more than 14 million families in helpless countries like Yemen , Sudan , and Haiti . Even before these subsidies were slashed , approximately half of the nations studied were already undergoing severe food issues . <p> - Advertisement - <p> As the world wrestles with economic ambiguity and geopolitical conflict , the GRFC 's results are an unambiguous cue -- without vital and continuous global action , the hunger predicament will remain an escalating issue , and millions more will be pushed beyond the edge .
