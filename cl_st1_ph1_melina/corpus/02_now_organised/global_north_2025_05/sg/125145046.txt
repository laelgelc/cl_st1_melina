<p> More than 50 people have been killed in Israeli air strikes across Gaza overnight , including one that hit a school sheltering displaced Palestinians . It is the latest in an expanded assault by the Israeli military aimed at defeating Hamas . Meanwhile , the head of a controversial US and Israeli-backed aid distribution programme for Gaza has resigned . Jake Wood quit as executive director of The Gaza Humanitarian Foundation . He said it was impossible to do his job while staying in line with principles of neutrality and independence .
