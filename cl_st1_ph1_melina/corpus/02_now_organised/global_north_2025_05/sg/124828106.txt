<p> JERUSALEM/CAIRO -- Israel and Hamas resumed ceasefire talks on Saturday in Qatar , both sides said , even as Israeli forces ramped up a bombing campaign that has killed hundreds of people over 72 hours , and mobilised for a massive new ground assault . <p> Palestinian health authorities said at least 146 people had been confirmed killed in the third day of Israel 's latest bombing campaign , one of the deadliest waves of strikes since a ceasefire collapsed in March . Many hundreds more wounded were being treated in hospital , and countless others were still buried under rubble . <p> Israel says it is mobilising to seize more ground in Gaza in a new campaign dubbed " Operation Gideon 's Chariots " , which follows a visit this week to the Middle East by US President Donald Trump .
