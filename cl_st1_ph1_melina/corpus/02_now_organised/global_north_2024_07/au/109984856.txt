<p> No-one remembers Joe Bullock . Amid all the coverage of Fatima Payman , not one political journalist recalled another WA Labor senator who was faced with his party supporting a position he could n't in good conscience hold . He resigned from the Senate in 2016 after Labor reached a compromise position on marriage equality at its 2015 conference : that it would be the subject of a conscience vote for two terms but then support for marriage equality would be required . Bullock , a conservative Baptist , said he could n't go to the 2016 election , even though he was n't up for election that year , backing marriage equality .
