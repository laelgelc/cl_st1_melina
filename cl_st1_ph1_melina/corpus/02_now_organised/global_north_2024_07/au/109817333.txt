Is such a system fit for purpose ? <p> No party , of course , would survive long if every internal dispute were put on public display . Public opinion often reflects displeasure in the constant bickering that goes on between parties , let alone within parties . <p> So how does a modern political party balance the growing divergence of views within its ranks with the reality that the public will often mark it down if it does not display discipline and solidarity ?
