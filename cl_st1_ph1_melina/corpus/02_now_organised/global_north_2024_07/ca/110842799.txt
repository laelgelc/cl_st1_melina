<p> GAZA CITY , Palestine -- A new health crisis unfolded in the bombarded Gaza Strip with the detection of poliovirus in sewage , the Gaza Health Ministry revealed on Thursday . <p> A ministry statement noted that " tests on sewage samples , conducted in coordination with UNICEF , confirmed the presence of the poliovirus . " <p> The statement added that the presence of the virus in sewage , which flows through areas with displaced persons and residential zones due to infrastructure destruction , " represents a new health crisis . " <p> The situation is exacerbated by severe overcrowding , scarce and contaminated water supplies , accumulated garbage , and the Israeli blockade on hygiene supplies . <p> The ministry warned that the detection of the virus in sewage puts thousands of residents at risk of contracting polio . <p> Separately , the Israeli Health Ministry confirmed on Thursday that it found components of the type 2 poliovirus in sewage samples from Gaza . <p> The ministry said in a statement published by Channel 12 that the sample results matched those of the World Health Organization . <p> The samples tested in a WHO-accredited laboratory in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ area , the statement added . <p> Polio is a highly contagious viral disease that attacks the nervous system and can cause total paralysis within hours . <p> According to the WHO , the escalation of hostilities by Israel in Gaza has led to a continuous rise in deaths and injuries , severe overcrowding in shelters , and the disruption of health , water , and sewage systems , which could accelerate the spread of infectious diseases . <p> Israel , flouting a UN Security Council resolution demanding an immediate ceasefire , has faced international condemnation amid its continued brutal offensive on Gaza since an Oct. 7 attack by Hamas . <p> More than 38,800 Palestinians have since been killed , mostly women and children , and over 89,400 injured , according to local health authorities . <p> Over nine months into the Israeli onslaught , vast tracts of Gaza lie in ruins amid a crippling blockade of food , clean water and medicine . <p> Israel is accused of genocide at the International Court of Justice , whose latest ruling ordered it to immediately halt its military operation in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ million Palestinians had sought refuge from the war before it was invaded on May 6.
