<p> NEWS RELEASE SIMCOE COUNTY 4 PALESTINE *********************** Simcoe County 4 Palestine welcomes one and all to the Simcoe County Kite Flight in Solidarity with Gaza . Community members are invited to this family-friendly event to raise awareness and honour the children of Gaza . <p> The kite has long been a symbol of defiance and hope for the people of Palestine . It broadcasts an unmistakable message of freedom flying high , often in the colours of the Palestinian flag . <p> In 2011 , the children of Gaza broke the world record for most kites flown in a single group , flying 12,350 kites at Al-Waha beach . This was a demonstration of their resilience at a time when Gaza was under harsh blockade . <p> Over the past eight months , the kite has continued to be a powerful symbol of freedom and resistance . <p> Free kite kits will be offered to be decorated and flown , and attendees are welcome to bring their own kite from home . Drinks and baked goods will be offered , community members are encouraged to bring lunch and stay for a picnic . <p> This event is in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Barrie Mosque , Christians for Just Peace Barrie , and Food Not Bombs Barrie . <p> Local artist Nicholai Melamed will be offering portrait drawings -- by a suggested $10 donation to Simcoe County 4 Palestine to continue with organizing and advocacy efforts .
