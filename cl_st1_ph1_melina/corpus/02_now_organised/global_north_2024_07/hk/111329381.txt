AFP <p> Israeli Defence Minister Yoav Gallant yesterday vowed to " hit the enemy hard " after rocket fire from Lebanon killed 12 young people in the Israeli-annexed Golan Heights and again raised fears that the war in Gaza will spread . <p> Iran warned Israel any new military " adventures " in Lebanon could lead to " unforeseen consequences . " <p> The rocket strike on the Druze town of Majdal Shams hit a football pitch and killed young people aged 10 to 20 , Israel 's military said . <p> Israel 's army called it " the deadliest attack on Israeli civilians " since the October 7 attack that began the war in Gaza and triggered regular exchanges of fire across the Lebanese border . <p> Israel blamed Lebanon 's Hezbollah movement for the rocket fire but the Iran-backed group - which has regularly targeted Israeli military positions - said it had " no connection " to the incident . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ an Israeli strike on a school housing thousands of displaced Palestinians killed at least 30 people . " <p> Israel 's military said later yesterday it hit Hezbollah targets " both deep inside Lebanese territory and in southern Lebanon .
