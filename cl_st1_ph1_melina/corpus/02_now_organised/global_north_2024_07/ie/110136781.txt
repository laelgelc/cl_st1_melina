<h> ActionAid calls on people of Cavan to support those displaced in Gaza <p> ActionAid Ireland has launched an urgent appeal to people in Co Cavan for support for Gaza and other countries devastated by conflict and crisis around the world . <p> ActionAid Ireland CEO , Karol Balfe , says it 's important to be also mindful of the needs in other countries around the world suffering the devastating consequences of conflict and other crises including in Ethiopia , Afghanistan , Bangladesh and Lebanon . <p> The CEO said a stark reminder of the horrors the people of Gaza are going through came over the weekend with the Israeli air strike on a UN-run school at Nuseirat refugee camp where thousands of displaced people were sheltering. 16 people are reported to have been killed . <p> Ms Balfe added that despite huge challenges with supplies getting through to the stricken region because of Israeli border blockades , limited food , water and medical aid " is beginning to reach people in need " . <p> " ActionAid , through our local partners , has direct access to ensure donations go straight to those most in need . These @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , mothers and fathers who have been living in hell . " she said . <p> " The conflict in Gaza is only one of many humanitarian emergencies around the world due to war , climate destruction , migration , hunger and inequality . ActionAid staff are on the ground in more than 60 countries working directly with women and children to provide life-saving aid and access to the support services they need . "
