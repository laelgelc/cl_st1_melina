<p> The International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) has given Jordan access with $130 million after it said the country showed resilience , reflecting sound-macro-economic policies and structural reforms in recent years . <p> The fund said it has completed the first review of Jordan 's Extended Fund Facility ( EFF ) programme , and said it is off to a strong start . <p> The country will have access to total funding of SDR 926.370 million , or special drawing rights , which is an international reserve asset , equivalent to $1.2 billion , during the programme , of which the $130 million is the latest phase . <p> The IMF said Jordan 's economy continues to show resilience including in the face of the challenges posed by the Israel -- Gaza conflict and the disruptions to trade in the Red Sea , reflecting strong progress made in recent years thanks to macro-economic policies and structural reforms helping it weather successive external shocks . <p> Growth of 2.4% is expected in 2024 following 2.6% in 2023 , and is expected to pick up pace in 2025 , the IMF said . <p> Disclaimer : This article is provided @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tax , legal or investment advice or opinion regarding the suitability , value or profitability of any particular security , portfolio or investment strategy .
