<p> Bristol University students who set up a protest camp at their university campus two months ago are now facing eviction . The students have been protesting against the war in Gaza and demanding the university ends its links with defence companies . <p> The university Vice Chancellor , Evelyn Welch released a statement yesterday ( Monday , July 1 ) explaining why legal action was being taken . Those currently camped at Royal Fort Lodge received a document which requires them to attend Bristol County Court on Redcliff Street on Friday , July 5 at 3.45pm . <p> Students at the Bristol encampment told Bristol Live that they would remain at Royal Fort Lodge until " the university ends its complicity " with the war in Gaza .
