<p> Over 2,500 roads in Coventry now have no street lights on for hours at night . It comes amid a public outcry over the new council " part-night " lighting scheme . <p> The move involves turning off street lamps from midnight to 5.30am every day except on Fridays and Saturdays , when it happens an hour later . Councillors backed the policy in February and since mid-July it has been in place across the city . <p> Overall 2539 city streets are being left in the dark for at least four hours overnight , according to a council list . We broke this down by each of the 18 council areas and found some have more roads affected than others . <p> A full list of the streets affected can be found below . The council says the change will save it ? 700,000 in energy costs per year , and also cut carbon emissions and light pollution . <p> But at the time of writing , 30 July , more than 1,000 people have signed a petition demanding the council bring back nightly street lights .
