<p> The Electoral Commission is to review claims that candidates and supporters were subject to intimidation , abuse and ' thuggery ' on the general election trail in Birmingham , we can confirm today . <p> The survey of evidence and experiences on the election trail comes amid multiple complaints from candidates , activists and voters in the run up to and during polling day and on results night , including harassment , verbal abuse and threats . A dossier of evidence is being pulled together by Birmingham City Council to aid the commission , which is the independent body which oversees elections and regulates political finance in the UK . " We work to promote public confidence in the democratic process and ensure its integrity , " says its website . <p> Council leaders are urging anyone with examples of and evidence of intimidation or threats to contact them . Concerns about the ' aggressive ' campaign in the city were highlighted by senior Labour MPs Jess Phillips and Shabana Mahmood at the election count , who both delivered impassioned speeches about the ' unacceptable ' abuse experienced .
