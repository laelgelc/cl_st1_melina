<p> A man from Surrey has pleaded guilty to making anti-Semitic posts on X , formerly Twitter , that included calls for a " Jew-free world " . <p> Peter Kandalaft , 57 , from Addlestone , sent multiple tweets following Hamas ' attack on Israel on October 7 and Israel 's subsequent invasion of Gaza . <p> Prosecutor James Cable told Westminster Magistrates ' Court on Monday that the posts included " let 's smash the Jews " , " let them Jews die and pile them high " and that " we all need a Jew-free world and world peace " . <p> One was sent in response to an image of a dead person in an Israel Defence Forces uniform , Mr Cable said .
