<p> The Green Party co-leader Carla Denyer has won the Bristol Central general election contest , becoming Britain 's second , and Bristol 's first Green MP , in a historic victory over Labour ' s Thangam Debbonaire . <p> Ms Denyer polled 24,539 votes for the Greens , beating the 14,132 votes for Labour - a result that was a more resounding victory for the Greens than any poll or forecast beforehand suggested it would be . <p> The victory came after a fierce battle for the Bristol Central seat which saw unprecedented levels of campaigning , election flyers , visits from senior politicians and a string of celebrity endorsements on both sides . <p> The defeat for Ms Debbonaire , who would have been the new Secretary of State for Culture , Media and Sport in Keir Starmer 's new Labour cabinet , ends a nine year spell as an MP in Bristol , having been elected in the Bristol West constituency back in 2015 . <p> Boundary changes , which made the new constituency smaller and concentrated on the city centre , St Pauls , Redland and Clifton , helped the Green Party , who won @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> And with the pre-election polls suggesting a narrow victory for Carla Denyer , the prime message from the Greens - that voters had a ' free-hit ' to vote Green without risking Labour losing to the Conservatives in Bristol Central - broke through to voters , who backed the Green Party co-leader . <p> Thangam Debbonaire , the Labour MP for Bristol West from 2015 to 2024 , arrives at the General Election count on July 5 , 2024 .
