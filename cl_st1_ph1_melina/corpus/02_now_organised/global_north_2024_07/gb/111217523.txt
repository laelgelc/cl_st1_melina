<h> Fern Press acquires The World after Gaza by Pankaj Mishra <p> Fern Press , an imprint of Vintage , has acquiredThe World after Gaza -- a treatise on Gaza , colonialism and the Shoah by Pankaj Mishra . <p> UK and Commonwealth rights , excluding Canada and India , were bought by Michal Shavit , publishing director , from Peter Straus at RCW Literary Agency . The World after Gaza will publish on 6th February 2025 . Rights have sold in the US , France , Germany , Italy , the Netherlands , Norway , Spain , Sweden and India . <p> According to Fern Press , The World after Gaza takes the war in the Middle East and the bitterly polarised reaction to it within , as well as outside , the West , as the starting point for a " broad re-evaluation of two competing narratives of the last century : the West 's triumphant account of victory over Nazi and communist totalitarianism and the spread of liberal capitalism , and the ' darker peoples ' , in W E B Du Bois 's words , frequently thwarted vision of racial equality " . <p> Shavit @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ urgent and powerful reckoning with one of the most seismic and catastrophic conflicts of our times . Incisive , thought-provoking and deeply moving , it provides a new understanding and critically important historical and ? cultural ? lens on the Israel-Hamas war by one of the most important thinkers at work today . " <p> Mishra says : " I am really grateful to have such engaged and astute publishing allies in the joint struggle to understand our turbulent and distressing time . " <p> Mishrais the author ofAge of Anger : A History of the Present , From the Ruins of Empire , Run and Hide and several other books .
