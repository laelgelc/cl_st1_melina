<p> It has been an almighty year for Irish film . Irish-language Kneecap scooped the audience award at Sundance ahead of opening the Galway Film Fleadh tonight ( July 9 ) ; Claire Keegan 's Small Things Like These was the first Irish independent film to open the Berlinale ; the country was well represented at Cannes ; and Cillian Murphy became the first Irish-born actor to win best actor at the Oscars . <p> Galway Film Fleadh festival heads , CEO and co-founder Miriam Allen and director of programming Maeve McGrath , are anticipating this energy will underscore this year 's event , which runs until July 14 . <p> " The power of Irish film is so strong , " says McGrath . " That 's why you see decision makers beating the path here to Galway .
