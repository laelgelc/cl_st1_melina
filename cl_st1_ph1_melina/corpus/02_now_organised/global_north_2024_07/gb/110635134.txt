<p> Protesters have blocked the entrance to the UK arm of an Israeli defence firm in Bristol . At 6.30am this morning , Monday , July 15 , members of the Palestine Action group attached themselves to a vehicle obstructing the entrance to Elbit Systems UK , at the Aztec West business park . <p> In the early hours of this morning , the group claims that dozens of Palestine Action activists broke into a company called Hydrafeed , in Milton Keynes . They said that this was an attempt to disrupt the supply of machinery to Elbit 's Instro Precision factory in Sandwich , Kent . <p> Elbit Systems UK , based at Aztec West , provides military equipment to the British Armed Forces and does not manufacture any equipment at its Bristol site for the Israel Defence Forces . A spokesperson for the company said claims that the Bristol facility supplies the Israeli military are " false " . <p> Palestine Action is a direct-action network of groups and individuals formed with the intention to disrupt the activities of Elbit Systems and other companies that the group claims contribute to Israeli military action in Gaza . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a partner of the Israeli weapons maker has done a serious moral and financial miscalculation as Palestine Action will break every link of Elbit 's supply chain , which involves targeting Elbit 's suppliers and of course , shutting Elbit down . We will not allow any institution to continue facilitating the bloody massacres of the Palestinian people . " <p> Last month , seven people were arrested after attaching themselves to two cars and blocking access to Elbit Systems UK at its Bristol base . According to pro-Palestinian protesters , the blockade lasted for nine hours and police had to drill through concrete that had been used to weigh down the two vehicles used . <p> An Elbit Systems UK spokesperson said : " We provide critical support and advanced technology to the British armed forces from our site at Aztec West 600 .
