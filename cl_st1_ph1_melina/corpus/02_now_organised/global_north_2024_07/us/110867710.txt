<h> #Breaking : Twitter Adds Unserious Emoji to Serious Hashtag <p> The app formerly known as Twitter thought it was doing something fun , something for the Summer Olympics beginning on July 21 , when it added a dancer emoji at the tail end of " #Breaking " to celebrate the 2024 games ' brand-new sport . Instead , its pictogram of a dancer performing a windmill move appears next to sobering news like " #Breaking*insert dancing emoji* : Worldwide outage is affecting airlines , banks , media , and offices from U.S. to Australia , " or " #Breaking*insert dancing emoji* : Massive protest in #Bannu , asking for peace . " Search the hashtag , and find graphic images of victims in war-torn Gaza , riots in London , and missile attacks in Crimea , all led by a tiny drawing of a text-size Olympian with his legs in the air . Twitter always felt like laughing at a funeral anyway .
