<p> Lawmakers in Congress have n't left themselves much choice if they want to fund the government and prevent a government shutdown in a few months : They 'll need to kick the can down the road . <p> The House left Washington last week , and the Senate will soon follow , with scant progress made toward passing the 12 annual spending bills , or appropriations , that keep vital agencies and government programs running . Current federal financing expires Sept. 30 , the end of the 2024 fiscal year . <p> Without new funding or an extension , vast portions of the government -- including functions of the Transportation Department , Internal Revenue Service and National Park Service -- would go offline . Military service members would miss paychecks but would be required to remain on the job . <p> The Republican-controlled House has passed five of the dozen bills -- all on virtually party-line votes with such steep budget cuts and culture-war policy provisions that they stand no chance of becoming law .
