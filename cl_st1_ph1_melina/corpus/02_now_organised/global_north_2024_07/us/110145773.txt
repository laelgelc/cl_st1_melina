<h> Here 's What a Hasan Minhaj -- Hosted Daily Show Would Look Like <p> Hasan Minhaj fans who were disappointed the comedian was not named the permanent host of The Daily Show will no longer have to endure a political climate without his commentary . The once-leading candidate for the job -- who was taken out of the running in 2023 after a widely discussed New Yorker article set off a public debate about factual inaccuracies and exaggerations in his Netflix stand-up specials Homecoming King and The King 's Jester -- is back with a new self-produced digital series , Hasan Minhaj Does n't Know . The show 's first episode , an interview with Senator Elizabeth Warren , premiered on YouTube July 8 . <p> Co-created by Prashanth Venkataramanujam and showrun by Scott Vrooman , who were both collaborators on Minhaj 's previous Netflix talk show , Patriot Act , Hasan Minhaj Does n't Know will see the comedian interviewing the " biggest names in politics , culture , and tech , " according to a release . " I talk to the man , " Warren says when asked about the incumbent 's age and ability . " The man is sharp . The man knows what he 's talking about . He does the job . " <p> Elsewhere in the interview , Minhaj gives Warren a few tips about how the Democratic Party can improve its political messaging by studying professional wrestling , pushes the senator to affirm her commitment to calling for a cease-fire in Gaza , and asks her whether the moral trade-offs of presidential politics are spiritually corrosive . " It 's a dumb show for curious people who are looking for answers but ca n't find them , not even from their host , " says the release .
