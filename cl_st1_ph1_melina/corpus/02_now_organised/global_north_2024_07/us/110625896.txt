<h> 57 years ago , the Newark Rebellion sparked change , but was it enough ? Lawrence Hamm <p> By Lawrence HammJuly 12 , 2024 at 10:07 am EDT <p> Lawrence Hamm is the founder and chairman of the Newark-based People 's Organization For Progress and author of Lawrence Hamm , A Life in the Struggle with Annette Alston and a former candidate for U.S. Senate . He says his neighborhood was occupied by the National Guard during the 1967 Newark Rebellion . ( Photo courtesy of Bud Lee Picture Maker/Bud Lee Picture Maker ) <p> I 'll never forget the sound of glass crunching under my feet . Just a few blocks from my home on Springfield Avenue , there was glass everywhere and the smell of burnt wood lingered in the air . It was two weeks after the Newark Rebellion , near the end of July . I was 13 and about to start my first year at Arts High School in September . <p> The night the Rebellion began , on July 12 , 1967 , the non-stop screeching sirens of police cars and fire trucks could be heard and seen in our part of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ just half a block up , and the dry goods food warehouse directly across the street from our home was broken into . <p> We lived in a three-floor tenement building and we had a porch where we sat and watched everything unfold from the second floor . My mother , grandfather , grandmother , and I were there . I asked my grandfather about what was going on , and that night was the first time he talked to me about racism and the oppression of Black people , or as he said , " colored people " and " negroes . " <p> My grandfather , Claude Cobb , shared his experiences from the army -- he served in WWI -- and the South . He hated it so much that he told my mother he would get up off the train if she sent his body to be buried down there after he died . He also talked about what he experienced up here in the North . <p> A few days later , units of soldiers arrived in Newark . They were called National Guardsmen , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to me . I remember seeing them come up 16th Avenue in trucks , Jeeps , and small tanks called " half-tracks . " They wore uniforms and helmets , carrying rifles with bayonets and guns . They cleared people off the streets and went door to door , searching our apartments and cars . We could not leave our homes . We were under military occupation . <p> The Rebellion was sparked by an incident of police brutality . Newark police officers beat John Smith so badly that he was thought to be dead . The Black cab driver survived , but his beating was the fuse , and the oppressive conditions in which Black people lived in Newark were the dynamite . The fuse had been lit , and this time there was an explosion . <p> It was called the Newark riots , but we stopped using the term " riots " more than 50 years ago . We call it a rebellion because that term more accurately describes what happened . It was a collective response to racial oppression . <p> Back then , Dr. Martin @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ millions on a racist war in Vietnam , the government should spend that money at home to eliminate poverty . <p> Today people are saying we should stop spending billions abroad on Israel 's genocidal war in Gaza , and the war in Ukraine , and spend that money on our domestic needs . <p> This month also marks the anniversary of the appointment of the Kerner Commission to determine the causes of the rebellions in Newark and other cities throughout the nation . In 1968 , it issued a report that said America was moving toward two societies , one Black and one white . <p> Today , I will call on the Biden administration to assemble a new Kerner-type commission to assess where we are today on the issue of racial equality , justice , and progress for Black people in the United States . <p> The People 's Organization for Progress ( POP ) has observed the anniversary of the Rebellion since the group was founded 42 years ago . It is dedicated to the 26 people killed during the unrest . <p> Lawrence Hamm , founder and chairman of the Newark-based People 's Organization For Progress , is the author of Lawrence Hamm , A Life in the Struggle with Annette Alston and a former 2020 and 2024 candidate for US Senate .
