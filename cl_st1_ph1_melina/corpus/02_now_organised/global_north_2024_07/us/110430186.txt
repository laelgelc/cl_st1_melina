<p> According to data collected by the Cooperative Election Study , the 46th president has lost ground among adherents of nearly every major religious group -- Latter-day Saints in particular -- since 2021 . <p> That year , nearly a third of U.S. members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints said they approved of Biden 's job as president .
