While the post garnered 1,400 reactions and nearly 250 comments , nobody claimed the ritual object . <p> Similar posts have cropped up in the more than eight months since Israel began its ground invasion of Gaza at the end of October . More broadly , Israeli military looting has been an issue during the war . <p> In February , IDF Chief of Staff Herzi Halevi called on soldiers " not to take anything that is not ours -- be it a souvenir or a piece of military equipment .
