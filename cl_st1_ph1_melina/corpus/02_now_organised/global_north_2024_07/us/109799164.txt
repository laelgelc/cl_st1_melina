This evolution has placed French Jews in a complex position , torn between the party 's pro-Israel stance and lingering concerns about its nationalist rhetoric . <p> On the other side of the political spectrum , the emergence of the New Popular Front coalition presents its own set of challenges . Traditionally , Jewish institutions were associated with the Socialist Party and the presidential party . The recent Socialist Party 's entry into an alliance with far-left elements , some of which have been criticized for anti-Zionist and even antisemitic sentiments , is deeply troubling for many in the Jewish community and has been perceived as abandonment . The coalition 's presumed sympathy for Hamas and its use of the Palestinian flag as a rallying symbol have exacerbated fears of rising anti-Semitism disguised as anti-Zionism .
