Linoy Ashram in Rhythmic Individual All-Around Gymnastics and Artem Dolgopyat in the Men 's Floor Routine Gymnastics finished in first place in their respective disciplines . Avishag Semberg took third place in Taekwondo -49kg , and the Judo Mixed Team also grabbed a bronze for their efforts . <p> In this edition of the Games , there will be 88 Israeli athletes competing in 16 different sports , with 54 men and 34 women . However , 18 of the men are from the soccer team .
