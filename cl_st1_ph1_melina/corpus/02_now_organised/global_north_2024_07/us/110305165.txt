<p> Whoever wins the race to represent Florida 's 22nd District will gain a seat in Congress and a very high-profile constituent : Donald Trump . <p> Democratic Rep. Lois Frankel has held this seat since 2013 . Her closest race was her first one in 2012 , when she beat Republican Adam Hasner by nine points . <p> Three Republicans hope to change that : Deborah Adeimy , Dan Franzese and Andrew Gutmann .
