Biden , for all his faults , is a decent human being who has dedicated his life to public service . Trump has shown himself to be driven only by self interest and ego . It 's not even close . <p> But I am concerned that Biden is becoming a bit Trumpian in his denial of something Americans can see with their own eyes : that his ability to lead the nation for another four years is an open question . It was readily evident in last month 's presidential debate and it was evident in Thursday 's press conference . <p> Biden did OK in Thursday 's press conference .
