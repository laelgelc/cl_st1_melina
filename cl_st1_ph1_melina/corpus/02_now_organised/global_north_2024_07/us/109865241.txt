<p> A dozen former US government officials who quit over US support for Israel 's war in the Gaza Strip on Tuesday accused President Joe Biden 's administration of " undeniable complicity " in the killing of Palestinians in the enclave . <p> In a joint statement , the 12 former government officials said the administration was violating US laws through its support for Israel and finding loopholes to continue shipping weapons to its ally . <p> Both the White House and the State Department had no immediate comment on the statement . <p> There has been mounting international criticism of Israel 's conduct in Gaza and of US military and diplomatic support for its ally in the Gaza war . <p> The resignations of the 12 US officials reflects some dissent within the government over its support for Israel .
