<h> Daily Herald opinion : Abhorrent violence disrupts tenor of campaign , but not our system <p> Milwaukee will host the 2024 Republican National Convention this week at the Fiserv Forum . Associated Press Photo <p> The Republican National Convention in Milwaukee had never held much promise for political deliberation . The abhorrent violence in Butler , Pennsylvania , only diminishes further the prospects for a respectful , productive start to the party 's presidential campaign . <p> Indeed , merely stooping to discuss matters of political consequence seems disrespectful to the moment . Gun violence has disrupted the fundamental process of our democracy . An innocent bystander apparently has been killed along with the suspected shooter . <p> The life of a former president has been assaulted and threatened . <p> These are events that can only bring shame to our nation and disappointment in the mad extremes to which misguided partisans can fall prey . <p> And , they are likely events that change the tenor and the dynamics of the rest of the political campaign .
