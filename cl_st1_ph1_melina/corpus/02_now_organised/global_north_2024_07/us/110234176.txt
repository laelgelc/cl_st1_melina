<p> If you looked at the U.S. military budget without knowing otherwise , you 'd probably guess we were in World War III . <p> Our military spending is now the highest it 's been at any point since World War II -- and Congress keeps adding more . The House of Representatives just passed legislation that will take military spending to $895 billion , while the Senate Armed Services Committee passed a bill that would total $923 billion . <p> Those totals do n't even include the military aid to Ukraine and Israel that was included in the $95 billion war package Congress passed this spring . We 're teetering closer and closer to a $1 trillion military budget . <p> Adjusting for inflation , the last time the national security budget topped $1 trillion was in 1945 , the final year of World War II . <p> Unlike a world war , there 's nothing happening today that can justify this level of spending .
