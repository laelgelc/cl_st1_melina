<h> Behind the Bylines : In this interview , the ' Jerusalem Post ' military correspondent Yonah Jeremy Bob reflects on his career , and on the importance of journalism . <p> Israel is a country in conflict . That has always been true , and the IDF has always been a dominant feature in the local news cycle . As such , one of the most important roles in Israeli journalism is the military correspondent . <p> It is a role that covers some of the most important news stories to come out of the Jewish state , and during the ongoing Israel-Hamas war that 's more true than ever . <p> It takes a highly skilled journalist to handle such a demanding job . It also requires keeping up with the rapid pace of military news and going into battle zones to see the war firsthand . For The Jerusalem Post , that skilled journalist is Yonah Jeremy Bob . <p> An accomplished reporter and author who has been working with the Post for years , covering everything from Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's trial and the International Criminal Court 's legal cases against Israel to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the war with Hamas . <p> In our " Behind the Bylines " column , we look at the people behind the articles that keep our paper -- and website -- running . As the Israel-Hamas war continues to rage , Yonah managed to make time in his ultra-busy schedule to sit down with In Jerusalem and speak about his journalist journey , and what makes his work so important .
