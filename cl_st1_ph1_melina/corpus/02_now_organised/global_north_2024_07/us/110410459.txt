<p> COLUMBUS , Ohio -- Green Party presidential candidate Jill Stein has filed petitions to appear on Ohio 's presidential ballot this November as an independent candidate , according to state and party officials . <p> Stein , a Massachusetts physician and environmental activist , is the fifth 2024 presidential hopeful who has made moves so far to run in Ohio 's general election on Nov. 5 . <p> Ohio Secretary of State spokesman Ben Kindel confirmed that Stein 's campaign submitted petition signatures from registered Ohio voters on Tuesday .
