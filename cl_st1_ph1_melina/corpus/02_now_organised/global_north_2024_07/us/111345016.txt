<p> Colorado military officials say the United States Marine Corps Memorial was vandalized with anti-Israeli graffiti . <p> On Thursday , officials issued a press release , saying that the USMC memorial was vandalized after it was desecrated with graffiti reading " FREE GAZA . " Officials say the incident happened overnight . <p> USMC Memorial <p> " The defacement of this Memorial is not just an attack on a monument , but on the values and sacrifices it represents , " said Paula Sarlls , president of the USMC Memorial Foundation . " We urgently need the community 's help to restore the Memorial to its rightful state . " <p> Following the incident , the USMC Memorial Foundation has turned to the community for support and has asked for volunteers , along with donations to help clean up the graffiti . <p> The foundation also hopes to get the graffiti cleaned up ahead of the memorial 's 47th anniversary celebration , which is scheduled for August 24.
