With handmade signs decrying the " racist and reactionary Republican agenda , " demonstrators took to the streets to try and shout their message across the U.S. Secret Service blockades and fences surrounding the official RNC venues . <p> " It 's hot , but I 'm glad we 're here , " says Lisa Taylor , who marched along with other members of the Progressive Labor Party . <p> Organizers predicted more than 5,000 people from around the U.S. would participate in the show of solidarity against Donald Trump , the Republican presidential nominee .
