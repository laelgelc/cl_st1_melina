<p> The landmark decision last week ordering the government to begin drafting ultra-Orthodox men could lead to the collapse of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's governing coalition as Israel wages war in Gaza . <p> Tens of thousands of men rallied in an ultra-Orthodox neighborhood to protest the order . But after nightfall , the crowd made its way toward central Jerusalem and turned violent . <p> Israeli police said protesters threw rocks and attacked the car of an ultra-Orthodox Cabinet minister , pelting it with stones . Water cannons filled with skunk-scented water and police mounted on horses were used to disperse the crowd . But the demonstration was still not under control late Sunday . <p> Police spray ultra-Orthodox Jewish men with foam after the men blocked a street in Jerusalem on Sunday.Mahmoud Illean / AP <p> Military service is compulsory for most Jewish men and women in Israel . Many held signs criticizing the government , with one saying " not even one male " should be drafted . <p> The ultra-Orthodox parties are key members of Netanyahu 's governing coalition and could potentially force new elections if they decide to leave the government in protest . <p> Party leaders have not said whether they will leave the government .
