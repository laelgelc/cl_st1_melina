At the convention 's closing , Franklin wrote one of the finest speeches of the early American experiment , urging compromise and a unanimous vote to support the Constitution . <p> That September , the Philadelphia socialite Elizabeth Willing Powel asked Franklin , " What have we got , a republic or a monarchy ? People use fluid intelligence when faced with unfamiliar information that must be organized , and when they have to solve a new problem or navigate a familiar one in unusual circumstances .
