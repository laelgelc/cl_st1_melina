<p> President Biden took one of the most consequential steps imaginable on Sunday : He ended his bid for a second term . After more than half a century of admirable public service , relinquishing power was n't easy . It required a push from the Democratic establishment but also a measure of self-awareness that is too often absent from U.S. politics . <p> " I believe it is in the best interest of my party and the country for me to stand down , " Mr. Biden wrote in an open letter . The president offered his " full support and endorsement " for Vice President Harris to replace him as the nominee . He said he will address the nation " later this week in more detail . " <p> Mr. Biden campaigned in 2020 as a " bridge " to the next generation of Democratic leaders .
