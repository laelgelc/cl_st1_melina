We at Israel 's Next Generation are engaging in the field closest to his heart -- the young people of Israel , our future generation . <p> The Rebbe expressed his hope for them on many occasions , citing their youthful energy . <p> Through them , he intended to bring about the world-embracing revolution that he , indeed , went on to carry out . <p> Loving the orders and loving the rebellion ! <p> In the early ' 50s , Ma'ariv journalist Geula Cohen , who had been a member of the pre-state underground and who later became an MK and deputy minister , met with the Rebbe and published his words in the newspaper . Let them rebel against stubbornness , weakness of mind , despair , and lack @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ young people at the helm . <p> The Rebbe challenged those who claim that attempting to change the thinking of the youth is contending with a human being who lacks the ability to choose , therefore making it useless . <p> The Rebbe maintained the exact opposite . It is the adults who are usually already set in their ways . Fixed on a lifestyle they have been practicing for decades , they will not easily change their ways and will also tell themselves that the previous decades of their lives were less significant , even considering them a mistake . <p> Young people , in contrast , come with a youthful , healthy , and open mind . They are but at the start of their way in life , filled with the vigor and the ability to grasp the most meaningful road to promoting the ideals they will be raised upon at this age . <p> Therefore , it is necessary to invest precisely in this age group , believe in them , and understand that they are , in fact , the future of our people in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ simple sense of " the next generation begins with young people " but also in the sense that they can bring new vitality , allowing the growth of entirely new things . <p> What we are witnessing this year , a year of war in the Land of Israel and the world as a whole , is that fighting belongs to young people ! <p> The war in the Land of Israel belongs to young soldiers . <p> The most horrendous thing that happened on October 7 happened at the Nova festival , predominantly attended by young people . <p> We must take their energy and channel them in the direction of values , of Jewish pride , all the while striving for world peace and justice . <p> We need to strengthen them and be an am segula -- a treasured people . <p> Unfortunately , the recent months have shown us that our enemies are ready to teach us by force what we , the people of Israel , do learn on our own .
