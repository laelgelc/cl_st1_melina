<p> Denis O'Reilly is a lifetime Black Power member and chairman of the Consultancy Advocacy and Research Trust . He lives at Waiohiki , Hawke 's Bay , where he chairs the Waiohiki Community Charitable Trust . <p> OPINION : In response to the great potato famine , my Irish great-grandfather and whanau sailed from Liverpool to Lyttleton on the good ship Otaki . <p> In February 1876 , our whanau eventually settled on seemingly unoccupied land near Fairlie . <p> It was not until I read Buddy Mikaere 's book about the charismatic prophet of the 1870 's Hipa Te Maiharoa from Arowhenua that I learned of my people 's naive role in the alienation of Maori land and contribution to settler colonisation .
