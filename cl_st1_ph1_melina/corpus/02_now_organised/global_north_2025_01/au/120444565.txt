<h> Editorial Beneath the soil of history <p> The words were painted to the right of the childcare centre 's doors . It is not possible to say if they were written before or after the firebombing , only that when they were noticed the building was already well alight . In black and jagged letters , they said : " Fuck the Jews . " <p> The childcare firebombing follows the burning of the Adass Israel Synagogue . It follows the torching of cars in Jewish neighbourhoods , twice in as many months . It follows the vandalisation of Australia 's largest Jewish school in Melbourne and the spray painting of swastikas on the Allawah Synagogue in Sydney . <p> These are abhorrent acts . There is no doubting their intention . They exist to engender fear , to reanimate ancient hatreds . The message is as blunt as the writing on the wall : " Fuck the Jews .
