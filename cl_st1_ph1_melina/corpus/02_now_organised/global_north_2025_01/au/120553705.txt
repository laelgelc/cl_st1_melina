<p> The Roy Morgan poll 's 52-48 result is similar to other recent surveys : both a Newspoll published on Monday and a YouGov poll from earlier in January had the coalition ahead by 51 per cent to 49 per cent on a two-party-preferred basis . <p> The Roy Morgan survey of 1567 voters was conducted on January 20-26 . <p> Roy Morgan CEO Michele Levine said the Greens had registered their lowest level of support since late 2023 during a week in which key issues included anti-Semitism , a ceasefire in the Gaza Strip and the annual debate about Australia Day . <p> " Greens leader Adam Bandt has been largely silent on the rising problem of anti-Semitism in the community and last week credited Australian street protests with helping bring about the current ceasefire in the conflict between Israel and Hamas in Gaza , " Ms Levine said . <p> Mr Albanese has yet to announce a date for the election but it must take place by May 17.
