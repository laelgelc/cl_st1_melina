<p> Israel and Hamas have finally agreed to a temporary truce in Gaza some 467 days after the horrors of October 7 , 2023 . The news has been welcomed globally and is especially welcome for the welfare of ordinary Palestinians in Gaza . <p> The hope with any ceasefire is that it will lead to a permanent cessation of hostilities between two implacable foes in the world 's most volatile and unstable region . The reality , however , is that a negotiated settlement of the war is unlikely to form the enduring basis for peace in the Middle East .
