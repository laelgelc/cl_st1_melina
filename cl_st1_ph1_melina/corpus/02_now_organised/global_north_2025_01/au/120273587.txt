<p> Donald Trump has often been dismissed as a hip-shooter devoid of strategic sense or policy vision . For better or for worse , Trump was one of the United States ' most revolutionary presidents during his first term , and that appears likely to be true of his second . <p> In the Middle East , Trump initiated the normalisation of Arab-Israeli relations . The so-called Abraham Accords between Israel and the United Arab Emirates , Bahrain , Morocco and Sudan in 2020 -- 21 laid the groundwork for an unprecedented regional security architecture . He says he will continue this process during his second term , bringing about the normalisation of diplomatic relations between Israel and Saudi Arabia . <p> In East Asia , Trump decisively broke from the US 's longstanding policy of engagement with China .
