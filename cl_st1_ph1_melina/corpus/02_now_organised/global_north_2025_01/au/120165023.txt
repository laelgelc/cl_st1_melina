<h> Israel and Hamas reach ceasefire deal in Gaza <h> Ceasefire in Gaza <p> At 12.15pm on Sunday , Israel and Hamas are due to start a 42-day ceasefire in Gaza as part of a deal that involves the initial release of 33 hostages and about 1000 Palestinian prisoners and could eventually end their 15-month-old war . <p> Announcing the deal on Wednesday , Joe Biden , whose United States presidency ends on Monday , welcomed the breakthrough , saying it followed " one of the toughest negotiations I have ever experienced " . <p> " After more than 400 days of struggle , a day of success has arrived , " he said . <p> The deal , which followed talks in Qatar that involved officials from the US , Egypt and Qatar , will involve three phases . <p> In the first phase , Hamas will release 33 hostages -- most of whom are believed to be alive -- including women , children , those over 50 years old and the wounded .
