" <p> Guterres told the meeting he is " deeply concerned about an existential threat to the integrity and contiguity of the occupied Palestinian territory of Gaza and the West Bank " , as he addressed what he called " Israeli administrative changes " over the past two years that have streamlined and accelerated that country 's settlement expansion in the territory . <p> With Donald Trump returning to the US presidency on Monday , pro-settlement Israelis have revived their controversial aspiration of formally annexing swaths of the West Bank . <p> The territory was part of the British colony of Mandatory Palestine , from which Israel was carved during the 1948 Arab-Israeli war , with Jordanian forces taking control of the West Bank during the same conflict . <p> Israel conquered the territory from Amman in the 1967 Arab-Israeli war and has occupied it ever since .
