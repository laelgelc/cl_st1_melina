<h> What Is The World Health Organization And Why Does It Matter ? <p> When the plague , cholera and yellow fever rippled deadly waves across a newly industrialised and interconnected world in the mid-19th century , taking a global approach to health became an imperative . Doctors , scientists , presidents and prime ministers urgently convened the International Sanitary Conference in Paris in 1851 , a precursor to what is now the largest of its kind : the World Health Organization , known as WHO . <p> From laboratories to battlefields , the United Nations specialised health agency has been dedicated to the wellbeing of all people since 1948 . It is guided by science and supported by its 194 member nations , including the United States , a co-founder that on Monday announced plans to withdraw . <p> What has WHO done for the world ? The short answer is -- a lot . They routinely evacuate injured patients and provide lifesaving equipment , supplies and services in conflict or disaster-ravaged areas . <h> Tracking and addressing health crises <p> Every day and through the night , teams of WHO experts sift through thousands of pieces of information , including scientific papers and disease surveillance reports , scanning for signals of disease outbreaks or other public health threats , from avian flu to COVID-19 . <p> WHO mobilises to prevent , detect and respond to infectious disease outbreaks while also strengthening access to essential health services . <p> That includes bolstering hospital capacity to do everything from delivering new babies to treating war injuries and training healthcare workers . <h> Eliminating diseases around the world <p> A wide range of diseases and conditions are ripe for elimination given the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ diseases , sexually transmitted infections , diseases passed from mother to child and those that vaccines can prevent . <p> The UN health agency supplies essential medicines and medical equipment while working to enable -- and where possible , strengthen -- laboratory capacity to diagnose diseases . <p> In 2024 , WHO Member States achieved several milestones in tackling these major global health challenges .
