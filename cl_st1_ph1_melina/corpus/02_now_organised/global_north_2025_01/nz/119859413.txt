<p> Isabella Rossellini was considered cinema royalty when she was born in 1952 , the daughter of actress Ingrid Bergman and director Roberto Rossellini . But she quickly dispelled any notions of being a nepo baby . After becominga hugely successful model for Lancome , she made her US acting debut in 1985 's White Nights ; the following year she garnered global attention in David Lynch 's Blue Velvet , delivering a shattering performance as a woman alternately victimised and worshiped by a psychotic gangster played by Dennis Hopper . <p> Now 72 , Isabella has become the kind of actor who signals artistic gravitas , offbeat humour and bold experimentation in any production she 's in .
