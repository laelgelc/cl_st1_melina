<p> There 's a lot more to the ideal chocolate biscuit than meets the eye.Photograph : Robert Billington/The Guardian <p> I am a complete sucker for a chocolate biscuit , one of the most exciting things about a 1980s childhood . Before structured playdates , Roblox and MrBeast videos , our thrills came in a biscuit tin full of Penguins , Clubs and Breakaways . The more chocolatey the chocolate , the better . Bliss . <p> In recent years , the rise of the posh , extra-chocolatey biscuit has delighted me . An ideal fancy biscuit should feel heavy in the hand , need a good bite to break through the chocolatey shell , it should withstand at least three good dunks in tea , and it should come in a packet with at least one breathlessly outlandish claim along the lines of " most chocolatey yet " or " thickest coating ever " . You should feel instantly joyful when you 're eating the first one . After the third , ideally , you should feel a bit sick . <p> *** <h> Best bargain <h> Sainsbury 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ special-treat biscuits at a bargain price .
