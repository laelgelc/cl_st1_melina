<h> Buy something or leave , Starbucks says <p> The move is part of a turnaround plan as the firm faces flagging sales <p> Starbucks says it is reversing rules for its cafes in North America that allowed people to use their facilities even if they had not bought anything . <p> The changes , which are set to come into force from 27 January , are a U-turn from a policy introduced six years ago that allowed people to linger in Starbucks outlets and use their toilets without making a purchase . <p> The move is part of the " back to Starbucks " strategy - a plan announced by the firm 's new boss as he tries to tackle flagging sales . <p> Starbucks said there was no change to its policy in the UK , where customers can continue to use the facilities without purchasing anything . <p> The world 's biggest coffee chain says its new code of conduct - which also addresses harassment and bans smoking and outside alcohol - aims to make its stores more welcoming . <p> " These updates are part of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the cafe experience as we work to get back to Starbucks . " <p> The company said the new rules would be displayed at every store and staff would be instructed to ask anyone who violates the code of conduct to leave . That includes allowing employees to call the police when necessary . <p> The change ends a policy Starbucks introduced in 2018 after two black men were arrested at one of the company 's stores in Philadelphia . <p> The men , one of whom had asked to use the toilet , had said they were waiting for a friend and refused a manager 's request to leave . <p> The video of the incident went viral , and critics accused the chain of racial profiling and called for a boycott . <p> Starbucks apologised to the men , agreed a settlement , and to help counter the backlash announced it would welcome people into their cafes , whether or not they were customers . <p> Starbucks has been trying to boost flagging sales as it grappled with a backlash to price increases and boycotts sparked by the Israel-Gaza war .
