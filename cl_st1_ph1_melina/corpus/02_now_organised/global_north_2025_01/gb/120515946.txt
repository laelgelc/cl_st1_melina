Those three words carry great significance . It is the first time in more than a century that an American president has expressed that ambition . It is also the first moment since the United Nations was created that three of the five permanent members of its security council -- Russia , China and the US -- have the intent to expand beyond their current borders . Greenland seems to be first in the president 's sights : he reiterated to reporters at the weekend that he thought he would " get " it . <p> If you 've ever been to Greenland , as I have , you will know that several truths about it become immediately obvious . Instead , they would lose all their coastal cities . <p> Yet it is also impossible not to notice that Greenland has hosted major US bases before . There are some very long runways left over from the Second World War , in addition to a current space base . <p> Those runways make a simple point : America does not need to own Greenland or to threaten its allies in order to obtain strategic bases there , which are indeed desirable as the race for the Arctic between the big powers heats up . It has happened before by agreement and , as the Danish PM has made clear , could easily be settled on again . Bullying Denmark is unnecessary .
