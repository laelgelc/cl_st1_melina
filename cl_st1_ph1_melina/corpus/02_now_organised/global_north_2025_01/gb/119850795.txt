<p> Councillors Aftab Nawaz , Khizar Hussain , Pete Smith , Hajran Bashir , Sabina Ditta , Naheed Gultasib , Farhana Hassan and Saiqa Nasreen , along with two members of the public , have called on the council to condemn the ongoing conflict in Palestine . <p> Walsall has long shared a close friendship with a village in the West Bank named Kobar . Before the war escalated in October 2023 , Palestinians visited Walsall and were welcomed by the mayor , and on many occasions , Walsall residents visited Kobar . <p> Since October 7 , 2023 , after around 1,200 people were killed and 200 others taken hostage by Hamas terrorists , more than 45,885 Palestinians have been killed , with children making up over a third of the death toll . <p> The notice of motion put forward by the eight councillors states : " Residents of Walsall 's diverse communities , including some local Palestinian families , have looked on with horror , shock , and disbelief at the destruction in the Middle East . <p> Walsall Council House on Lichfield Street October 2024 Permission @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ confirmed their solidarity with the people in the Middle East , particularly the people of Gaza , by holding vigils , fundraisers and many rallies throughout the year . " <p> Walsall councillors will be asked to support the motion , which calls for the immediate return of Israeli and Palestinian hostages , urges the UK Government to recognise a Palestinian state based on pre-1967 borders with East Jerusalem as its capital , demands an immediate and unconditional ceasefire , and a reevaluation of arms sales to Israel . <p> The meeting is due to take place at 6pm on Monday January 13 , 2024 at the Council House .
