In 2022 , the Glasgow Caledonian University student drove an ambulance to the Polish-Ukrainian border to help refugees fleeing the Russian invasion , having previously donated more than 30 rescue vehicles to war zones in Burma , Syria , Iraq and Yemen . <p> Last year , Mr Javaid drove another ambulance to the Egypt-Gaza border to provide medical support and help evacuate civilians , taking the number of emergency transport he has delivered to trouble spots around the world to more than 40 . The international tourism and events student now plans to stage a festival in Glasgow 's Tollcross Park in August @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to help people in need . <p> He said he wants to buy another 15 ambulances for distribution , 14 of which will be sent to countries around the world while the last one will be donated to a care home in the UK .
