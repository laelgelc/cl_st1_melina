<p> I 've become addicted to Instagram recipe reels , and frequently delve into the amazing sped-up world of weird and wonderful online dish creation . But every now and then , a recipe catches my eye and I press play ... only to see that the creator is wearing a symbol of the " pro-Palestinian " movement : a watermelon , a keffiyeh , a pin with the Palestinian flag 's colours . I immediately lose my appetite : here , I assume , is someone who thinks Israel is in the wrong , not Hamas . Here is someone who does not think the lives of Israeli women , or children , or hostages , or all three , are worth anything . <p> I do n't want that from a video on how to make a fruit ice cream . It 's inappropriate . Politics ( if that 's what the pro-Palestine movement post-October 7 can be called ) does not belong everywhere . <p> But the idea that an understated symbol that remembers the hostages still imprisoned by Hamas in Gaza is political is perverse . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ were taken from their homes in Israel by the most brutal animals on earth .
