<p> Muscat -- The Muscat Stock Exchange ( MSX ) ranked as the third-best performing market in the GCC in 2024 , following the steepest decline in the region in 2023 . The MSX 30 Index posted a modest yearly gain of 1.4% and closed the year at 4,576.6 points . <p> GCC markets once again saw one of the smallest equity market gains globally in 2024 . The aggregate MSCI GCC Index reported a gain of 0.7% for the year , reflecting mixed performance at the country level , according to a research report by Kuwait-based Kamco Investment . <p> The Dubai Financial Market saw the largest gains , with a 27.1% surge , followed by a healthy 8.0% rise in Boursa Kuwait . Saudi Arabia also closed the year in the green , with a 0.6% gain after a volatile year , while Oman and Bahrain posted gains of 1.4% and 0.7% , respectively .
