<h> Israeli population tops 10 million for first time despite record number of residents leaving <p> A record number of Israelis moved abroad in 2024 , according to the Central Bureau of Statistics ( Getty ) <p> Israel 's population may have exceeded 10 million in 2024 for the first time , yet the ongoing war and domestic political turmoil have led to an unusually high number of residents leaving the country over the past year . <p> According to data published by the Central Bureau of Statistics ( CBS ) 82,700 veteran Israelis moved abroad , an increase from 55,000 the year before . <p> The figure is up from the previous decade , where on average 35,000 people left each year , according to the Times of Israel . <p> The war with Hamas in Gaza and Hezbollah in Lebanon , along with rocket and missile attacks from other Iranian-based proxies , has slowed population growth in the Jewish state . <p> The total grew by 1.1 per cent in 2024 , down from 1.6 per cent in the previous year and 2.2 per cent in 2022 , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the country . <p> Some 32,800 people immigrated to Israel last year , down 15,000 from 2023 , perhaps due to sharp decline in people making aliyah from the former Soviet Union , according to the Jewish News Syndicate . <p> The CBS announced that Israel 's population had reached 10,027,000 , topping 10 million the first time by the end of 2024 . <p> Around 181,000 children were born in Israel last year , 76% to Jewish mothers and 24% to Arab mothers . This is up from 179,000 last year . <p> Approximately 76.9% of the population ( 7.707 million people ) are Jewish , and 21.0% ( 2.104 million people ) of the population is Arab . <p> Two percent ( 210,000 people ) identify as " other " , a category which includes non-Arab Christians and those not classified by religion in the state 's population registry . <p> Meanwhile , 23,800 Israelis returned home in 2024 . <p> 51,400 people died in 2024 , slightly higher than last year 's figure of 49,500 deaths .
