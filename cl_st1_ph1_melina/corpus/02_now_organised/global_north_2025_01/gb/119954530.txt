<h> Gaza ceasefire deal is ' on the brink ' , says Joe Biden as he defends foreign policy record <p> A ceasefire is close in the Gaza Strip , outgoing US president Joe Biden said on Monday , as he sought to defend his foreign policy record . <p> A week before handing over to Donald Trump , Mr Biden touted his administration 's backing for Ukraine against Russia 's 2022 invasion and for Israel 's wars in the Middle East as being among his foreign policy successes . <p> In a rare State Department speech , Mr Biden said the United States was " winning the worldwide competition " and would not be surpassed economically by China , while Russia and Iran have been weakened . <p> " Compared to four years ago , America is stronger , our alliances are stronger , our adversaries and competitors are weaker , " he said .
