<h> Pope sends special inauguration telegram to Trump <p> Pope Francis has marked today 's inauguration of Donald Trump as the 47th president of the United States of America by sending him a special inauguration telegram . <p> In the 20 January telegram sent ahead of Trump starting his second term in office , the Pope said , " I offer cordial greetings and the assurance of my prayers that Almighty God will grant you wisdom , strength and protection in the exercise of your high duties . " <p> He added : " Inspired by your nation 's ideals of being a land of opportunity and welcome for all , it is my hope that under your leadership the American people will prosper and always strive to build a more just society , where there is no room for hatred , discrimination or exclusion . " <p> Set against a world facing various challenges , including war and political conflict , the Pope asked that God would guide Trump 's efforts " in promoting peace and reconciliation among peoples " . <p> " With these sentiments , I invoke @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ people an abundance of divine blessings , " the Pope said . <p> Trump 's election win in November 2024 to a second term as US president could usher in , according to some observers , a second round of tensions in the Vatican-US relationship , as he and Pope Francis hold vastly different views on issues such as immigration , climate change and engagement with China . <p> Pope Francis has already said it would be a " disgrace " for the incoming president to make good on his campaign promises to expel undocumented immigrants . <p> It is not yet known when Trump 's first visit to Italy and the Vatican during his second presidential term will take place , but with war still raging in Ukraine and a tenuous ceasefire agreement having just been implemented in Gaza , it likely wo n't be long before the two leaders meet in person . <p> Photo : US President Donald Trump shakes hands with Pope Francis during a special audience at the Vatican , Vatican City State , 24 May 2017 .
