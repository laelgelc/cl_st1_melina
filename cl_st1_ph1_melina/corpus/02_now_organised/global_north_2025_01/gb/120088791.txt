<h> ' It must not be a false dawn ' : Archbishop of York on Israel -- Hamas deal <h> Archbishop of York statement on ceasefire-hostage deal <p> Archbishop of York Stephen Cottrell said the ceasefire-hostage deal has been far too long in the making ( Image : Danny Lawson/PA Wire ) <p> THE Archbishop of York Stephen Cotrrell said the ceasefire-hostage deal between Israel and Hamas " has been far too long in the making , but it is welcome news for those caught up in this devastating violence . " <p> Mediators say Israel and Hamas have agreed to pause the fighting in Gaza , starting on Sunday ( January 19 ) , after 15 months of war and to begin exchanging dozens of hostages held there for Palestinians imprisoned by Israel . <p> Israeli prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu had yet to confirm that the deal had been finalised . <p> Archbishop Stephen said : " My prayers are with all the families being reunited over the coming days , and for those who are still held in captivity . <p> " My prayers too are with the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ deprivation and displacement . <p> " Now must be the time -- again , so long overdue -- for unfettered aid to reach the people of Gaza , and for the indispensable role of the United Nations Relief and Works Agency to be respected and protected by all parties . <p> " May this deal be a precursor to a wider , more durable and just settlement in the region : it must not be a false dawn . <p> " Please pray for Archbishop Hosam and our Anglican sisters and brothers in Palestine and Israel , and for all the Christians of the Holy Land as they bear the light of Christ in such dark times . <p> " Pray too for the courageous staff of the Anglican Al-Ahli Hospital in Gaza , and those in their care . <p> " Across the West Bank , this war is being felt not just in the daily grievous news from Gaza , but through widespread intensification of the occupation . <p> " I pray this deal acts as a catalyst to bring freedom , justice and dignity to the Palestinian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ long term security of both Palestinians and Israelis . <p> " We lift before God the peoples of the Holy Land , and for His justice , mercy and peace to be known by all .
