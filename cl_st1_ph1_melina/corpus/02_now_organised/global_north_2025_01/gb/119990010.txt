<p> China is building special landing barges whose only conceivable purpose is to carry People 's Liberation Army vehicles over Taiwanese beaches as part of a full-scale invasion of Taiwan . <p> Naval News was the first to report on the barges , as many as five of which are under construction at Guangzhou Shipyard in southern China . <p> The barges , which apparently take just a few months to complete , have been in development since at least 2022 , according to Naval News . They represent a new and critical capability for Chinese forces . Put simply , the barges connect ships to the shore -- virtually any ships . <p> In a decade of breakneck expansion , the PLA Navy has built up a substantial amphibious flotilla including eight Type 071 landing docks for hauling troops and supplies , at least three Type 075 assault ships for carrying troops and helicopters and , so far , one giant Type 076 assault ship that can also launch and land high-performance drones . The new assault barges are the key to linking car ferries and other civilian vessels to the sand of a contested beach . <p> The barge is essentially a floating pier . It beaches itself on the sand -- one end anchored on dry land , the other jutting into the surf . A ferry can pull up alongside , drop its ramp onto the top of the barge and offload its vehicles . But losing a few barges would n't doom a Chinese invasion of Taiwan . The vessels are simple , and Chinese industry has already proved it can build additional copies in a few months . <p> The new barges are the latest warning sign that an attack on Taiwan is probably imminent . With single-mindedness that should impress leaders in other countries , the Chinese have armed themselves for a devastating war of choice on a peaceful neighbour whose only sin is not being part of China . <p> If and when the invasion begins , the barges might be the reason it succeeds .
