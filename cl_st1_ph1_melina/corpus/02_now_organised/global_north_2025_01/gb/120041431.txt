Israel 's security cabinet is set to vote on the deal Thursday morning . After that , lists of Palestinian security prisoners to be freed will be published , to enable petitions against their release to the high court . The court is widely expected not to intervene in the deal . <p> The ceasefire , which was announced on Wednesday evening , drew hundreds onto the streets of Tel Aviv to celebrate and demand the return of all hostages .
