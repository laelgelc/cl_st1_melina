<h> The UN funding crisis : Tough choices on Ukraine and Gaza <p> The war in Ukraine is about to enter its fourth year . Will the incoming administration in Washington bring it to an end -- and what will that mean for the millions of refugees inside the war-torn country and the territories Russia occupies ? <p> Host Anne McElvoy talks to Tom Fletcher , United Nations ? undersecretary-general for humanitarian affairs and emergency relief coordinator , who has been in Ukraine to survey the toll the conflict has taken on the Ukrainian people and its infrastructure . <p> They also discuss the huge task facing the U.N . following a cease-fire in Gaza . As a former senior foreign policy adviser to three British prime ministers -- Tony Blair , Gordon Brown and David Cameron -- Fletcher has advice for Peter Mandelson , who is about to become Britain 's next ambassador to the United States , on how to handle Donald Trump .
