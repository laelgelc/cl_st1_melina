Any court may defer sentence for up to six months after conviction in circumstances where a defendant facing an immediate custodial sentence seeks to convince the court that he or she will not offend again . The court can tell the defendant that a prison sentence is indicated but can be avoided if the defendant complies with reasonable conditions , for example that the defendant commits no further offence and seeks to obtain ( and retain ) paid work . Any other reasonable condition likely to prevent further offences can be imposed .
