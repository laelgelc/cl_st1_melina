Among them is a copper flagon made in Syria and buried in Essex in the late 500s . It is believed that the flagon belonged to an English mercenary who went to fight for the Byzantines against the Sassanians in the 570s . The flagon 's looping handle would have held it tight to a saddle , so perhaps it came to England attached to the warrior 's horse as he rode home from his adventures in the East . <p> There are many spectacular objects in this exhibition .
