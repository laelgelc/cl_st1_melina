<h> Brian Taylor : Hail to the Chief -- we can only hope he thinks twice <h> Hail to the Chief -- we can only hope he thinks twice <p> Donald Trump is surrounded by U.S. Secret Service agents at a campaign rally in July after being shot ( Image : Evan Vucci ) <p> Churn is vital in democratic politics . It is right and good that , when the people have spoken , the powerful respond . By departing office , if they are defeated . Seldom , though , in my experience , has such a switch been greeted with so much apprehension . <p> No doubt Donald Trump 's ardent supporters are delighted at the prospect of his second term . Those he has elevated to high office , facing US Senate hearings this week , will be eager with anticipation . <p> Globally , the mood is one of disquiet . Of fear . It is not that his mandate is disputed -- although , of course , the President Elect did not grant such leeway when ousted from office four years ago . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , with an attitude which soured US politics then and since . Rather , it is concern as to how his character and his ideology will impact upon world affairs , ranging from existing conflicts to the climate and the economy . <p> Will he listen only to his instincts and those of his like-minded advisers ? Will he live up to his self-image as the heroic defender of America ? Or will external and global advice prevail ? Can he , in short , be constrained ? <p> Not a good start that his pre-inauguration aims included the potential annexation of Greenland . Open-mouthed European leaders seemed torn . Should they ignore this as ludicrous bombast or threaten military reprisals against this challenge to Denmark 's sovereignty over the icy territory ? <p> Do I believe that the USA will invade Greenland ? No , I do not . Wiser counsel will win through , linked perhaps to a restatement of America 's strategic interests . However , there are other issues where Trumpian rhetoric may well become reality . So anxieties remain in the Middle East . Enduring , entrenched anxieties . <p> As is also the case in Ukraine . It was no accident that Sir Keir Starmer visited Kyiv to sign a co-operation pact with President Zelensky just days before the Trump accession . It was perhaps his biggest , single pitch . His most effective attack upon Biden and Kamala Harris . <p> It is entirely understandable that current and aspirant migrants will feel apprehensive with regard to United States policy . Will there be a coherent , structured plan which controls migration while enabling the recruitment of workers for American firms and public services ? Probably not . Such a programme escaped Trump Mark One . But significant gesture action can be anticipated . <p> In this , Mr Trump is not entirely alone . In the Commons , the Liberal Democrat leader Sir Ed Davey urged visas for wealthy US investors seeking refuge from Trumpian policies . Which means , let us be blunt , to the exclusion and detriment of others . It is gut patriotism , old-style protectionism . And it seems certain to be pursued . The question is to what degree .
