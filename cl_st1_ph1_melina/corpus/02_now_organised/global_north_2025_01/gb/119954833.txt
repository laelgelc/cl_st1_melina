<p> Fifteen months into the war in Gaza , where more than 46,500 Palestinians have been killed and almost all of the population of 2.1 million are living in makeshift accommodation , conditions continue to deteriorate in the face of famine and cold . <p> At least 1.9 million people -- or about 90% of the territory 's population -- are displaced . Many have been forced to relocate repeatedly , some as many as 10 times . <p> Over the past three months , Israeli ground operations have concentrated on the northern third of the territory , where Israel says it is trying to prevent Hamas from regrouping and Palestinians accuse Israel of seeking to permanently depopulate a buffer zone . <p> At least 5,000 Palestinians have been killed during Israel 's 100-day siege of northern Gaza , according to the Gaza government 's media office , although that figure does not distinguish between combatants and civilians .
