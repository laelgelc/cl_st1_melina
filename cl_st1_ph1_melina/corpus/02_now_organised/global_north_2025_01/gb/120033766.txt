<p> After Bashar al-Assad 's spectacular defenestration last December , he has joined a growing list of fallen Middle Eastern despots : Reza Pahlavi of Iran , Gaddafi of Libya , Mubarak of Egypt , Saleh of Yemen , and Ben Ali of Tunisia , to name a few . <p> Who might be next ? As our new Policy Exchange report argues , the Islamic Republic of Iran could be the frontrunner . The regime has suffered an annus horribilis , and is now staring down the barrel of another Trump presidency . <p> However , the Iranian regime is in an exceedingly weak state . Herein lies an opportunity for the UK . With a tougher approach , we can press the advantage further on a regime which constitutes the second gravest state threat to national security , a decades-long terroriser of our regional partners , and the ultimate oppressor of the Iranian people . <p> To say the regime has had a bad year would be an understatement . Its " forward defence " survival strategy has been severely damaged . Senior Revolutionary Guard Corps officials are reportedly bemoaning how dire financial and military constraints preclude another operation against Israel . <p> At home , conditions are no better .
