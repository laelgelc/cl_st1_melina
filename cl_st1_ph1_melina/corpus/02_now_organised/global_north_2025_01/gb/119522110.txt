<h> King Harald says his family has been ' put to the test ' in New Year 's speech <p> It has n't been an easy year for the Norwegian Royal Family -- to put it mildly -- and King Harald addressed the controversies up front in his annual New Year 's Eve speech , saying that the royals " have been truly put to the test " this year . <p> But while there have been hardships , King Harald said , the year has also made them all aware of " what matters in life . " <p> The King talked mostly about how happiness and sadness , goodness and badness walk together in life and that nobody can escape the difficulties . " At the core of being human is that none of us are spared pain and hardship , " he said . " Each of us has to find ways to cope with it , and try to get through it . " <p> He added : " I 'm not a psychologist . " <p> And once again , King Harald turned to hope as the central core of his speech . Even when we make mistakes , he said , we pick ourselves up , learn from them , and move forward . " This drive is something very special to us humans . And I think it 's about hope .
