" <p> The British-Iraqi journalist also shared insights from some of her acclaimed work in the field , which included reporting from Gaza in 2021 , the last English-language interview with Yahya Sinwar ( the former Hamas leader who was killed in 2024 ) , as well as her reports on Sheikh Jarrah , the Russia-Ukraine war and an upcoming MEE documentary on Lebanon . <p> Middle East Eye delivers independent and unrivalled coverage and analysis of the Middle East , North Africa and beyond .
