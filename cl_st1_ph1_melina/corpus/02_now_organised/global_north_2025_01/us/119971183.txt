Before their arrival over the weekend , a working group had made progress , officials say . <p> The talks involve several issues that have long been sticking points -- including which hostages and prisoners will be freed , as well as when and to where . Hamas says the only deal it will sign must include an end to the conflict . <p> Hamas is considered a terrorist group by the U.S. and many other governments . <p> Israel has threatened to escalate its offensive against Hamas if there is no deal by Trump 's inauguration -- a possible reference to ground operations in areas of Gaza that troops and tanks have so far skirted , in the belief that hostages are being kept there and could be harmed . <p> Phased ceasefire <p> Biden officials have for months sought to bridge the gap by working on a phased ceasefire that would start with a pause of about six weeks and involve a partial exchange of hostages for prisoners along with a boost in aid . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The problem has been disagreement between Hamas and Israel on whether the first phase guarantees an end of war . Qadura Fares , who oversees prisoners for the Palestinian Authority -- a rival of Hamas but indirectly involved in the latest talks -- said in an interview that those serving life sentences for violent crimes would be deported to Qatar , Egypt and Turkey . <p> The first hostages freed would be women , the ill and injured and those over 50 .
