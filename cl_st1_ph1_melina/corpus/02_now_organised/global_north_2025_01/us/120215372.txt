<h> Israelis , Palestinians prepare for long-sought truce in Gaza <p> Jan. 18 , 2025Updated Sat. , Jan. 18 , 2025 at 7:50 p.m . Israel 's Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said an agreement with Hamas to pause the war in Gaza has been finalized , suggesting it was on track to begin on Sunday . ( Abir Sultan/Pool/AFP ) <p> The mediating country Qatar on Saturday announced a time for the ceasefire agreement between Israel and Hamas to take effect the next day , setting off final preparations for a truce that much of the world hopes will end 15 months of destruction in the Gaza Strip . <p> The deal should go into effect at 8:30 a.m. local time Sunday , said Majed al-Ansari , a spokesperson for the Foreign Ministry of Qatar , which spent months alongside the United States and Egypt struggling to broker an agreement . <p> Israel 's government approved the deal early Saturday after hours of deliberations and amid internal rifts in the governing coalition of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu . The approval cleared a final obstacle , raising hopes for Israelis who @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ survived one of the most intense bombing campaigns of the 21st century . <p> " It 's a mix of joy , sadness and longing for a new beginning , " said Mariam Moeen Awwad , 23 , who has been displaced from her home in northern Gaza six times since the war began . <p> Awad had planned to move into her newly furnished apartment with her husband in November 2023 . The war derailed those plans , leaving the couple in an overcrowded property and eager to return home , she said , " if it 's even still there . " <p> In Israel , authorities have started preparations to welcome home dozens of hostages , without knowing whether they will return malnourished , traumatized or dead . <p> In his first remarks since the ceasefire 's approval , Netanyahu said in an address Saturday night that 33 hostages would be released in the first phase of the deal , " most of them alive . " <p> Defending the deal , he argued that Israel had made major strategic gains over the past several months , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ I pledged to you , we have changed the face of the Middle East , " he said . <p> Three reception points have been established to receive the hostages along the Gaza border , according to an Israeli military official . Those will be staffed by Israeli soldiers as well as doctors and psychologists , said the official , who spoke on the condition of anonymity in accordance with protocol . <p> The hostage release is expected to be the first such major exchange since a weeklong ceasefire early in the war . <p> " The ones who were freed back then were already poorly nourished , " Hagar Mizrahi , a senior Israeli Health Ministry official , said of the hostages freed during the 2023 truce . " Imagine their situation now , after an additional 400 days . We are extremely worried about this . " <p> Of the women , older men and other hostages set to be returned , many are believed to have been held in Hamas ' network of tunnels in Gaza , under conditions likely to leave physical and psychological scars . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ begin recuperating in privacy . <p> " Last time , we saw the Red Cross transferring the hostages , and some of them were running to the relatives , hugging them , " said Einat Yehene , a clinical psychologist working with the Hostage Families Forum , an advocacy group . " It 's not going to be easy and similar this time , given the physical and the emotional conditions we expect . " <p> In exchange , hundreds of Palestinian prisoners are to be freed . The total number of prisoners to be released and their identities were among the many contentious points involved in the negotiations for a deal . <p> The new deal also calls for allowing 600 trucks carrying aid to enter Gaza daily and negotiations on the withdrawal of Israeli forces from the territory and a permanent end to the war . <p> Those negotiations are likely to be bitter and difficult , like the months of talks that yielded this recent ceasefire agreement . The agreement also allows Israeli forces to remain in a buffer zone along Israel 's border with Gaza and Gaza 's border with Egypt , he added , at least during the initial phase . <p> " If we need to go back to fighting , we will do it in new ways and with great might , " he said . <p> Another uncertainty in how the deal might unfold arises from the chaotic , ruined conditions within Gaza , where tens of thousands of people have been killed since @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ without homes , clean water or ready supplies of food or medicine . <p> Israel 's campaign has left a power vacuum across much of Gaza , and lawlessness has proved a dangerous factor in efforts to get aid to people in need . Organized looting has repeatedly stripped trucks of supplies , including from a convoy of 100 trucks holding U.N . aid late last year . <p> Israel has continued striking Gaza since the ceasefire was announced , and over the past 24 hours , 23 Palestinians were killed and 83 others wounded , Gaza 's Health Ministry said Saturday morning . More than 46,000 people have been killed in Gaza since the war began , according to the ministry , which does not distinguish between combatants and civilians . <p> Desperately needed aid is expected to pour into Gaza once the ceasefire begins .
