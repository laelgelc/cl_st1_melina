" But we look at this as an opportunity ... to repair the world and hopefully bring together more good than hate . " <p> The synagogue has since covered the words temporarily with paper until they can find a paint to match the previous coat , Argoff-Treseder said . <p> The synagogue was one of two houses of worship in the Bankers Hill area that were vandalized with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> St. Paul 's Episcopal Cathedral was also damaged with black paint that covered areas of the building 's stucco with profanity and crosses . <p> San Diego Police Department officials confirmed that they are investigating both incidents . <p> " There 's no word on who it is , but we 're still early in the investigation , " Lt.
