<p> Negotiations are ongoing between Israel , Hamas , and hostage deal mediators to secure the release of Arbel Yehoud from captivity in Gaza before the next round of hostage releases on Saturday , Israeli officials told The Jerusalem Post on Sunday . <p> Civilian hostage Yehoud , 29 , was scheduled to be released last Saturday as part of the original terms of the hostage and ceasefire deal agreements , which stated that female civilians were to be released earlier than female soldiers . <p> On Saturday , Hamas released four of the five female soldiers held by the terror group , violating the terms of the hostage deal . <p> Both Hamas and Palestinian Islamic Jihad , whose terrorists are allegedly holding the hostage , claimed " internal problems " brought about a delay to Yehoud 's release . Now , Israel is working to secure her release before Saturday , an official told the Post . <p> Earlier on Sunday , Islamic Jihad officials told Reuters that an agreement has been reached and that Yehoud " will be released according to the agreement before Saturday .
