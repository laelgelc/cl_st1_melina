<p> DEIR AL-BALAH , Gaza Strip ( AP ) -- Mediator Qatar says an agreement has been reached to release an Israeli civilian hostage and allow Palestinians to return to northern Gaza . The deal eases the first major crisis of the ceasefire between Israel and Hamas . Qatar 's statement early Monday says Hamas will hand over the civilian hostage , Arbel Yehoud , along with two other hostages before Friday . And on Monday , Israeli authorities will allow Palestinians to return to northern Gaza .
