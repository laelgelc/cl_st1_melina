<p> The Hebrew version of the document has been presented to the security cabinet . <p> " The purpose of the agreement is the release of all Israeli hostages held in Gaza -- civilians and soldiers , whether alive or not -- taken at any time , in exchange for an agreed-upon number of Palestinian prisoners held in Israeli prisons , and the establishment of lasting quiet , leading to a permanent ceasefire , the withdrawal of Israeli forces from the Gaza Strip , and the rehabilitation of the Strip , " the document stated . <p> The document also reveals the details of the deal being negotiated . It states that by the seventh day , Hamas must provide information on the number of hostages to be released in the first humanitarian stage . <p> According to KAN , while in this document Israel demands the deportation of at least 50 Hamas terrorists either abroad or to Gaza , it is now demanding a higher number . <h> Ending the war ? <p> In the first stage , Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has already approved initiating post-war reconstruction in Gaza which will include rebuilding infrastructure , clearing rubble , and bringing in at least 60,000 caravans and 200,000 tents .
