<p> Major-General Ghassan Alian , head of the Coordinator of Government Activities in the Territories ( COGAT ) , visited the family of St.-Sgt . Oron Shaul after IDF and Shin Bet ( Israel Security Agency ) troops retrieved the long-lost soldier 's body from the Gaza Strip , the military said early Monday morning . <p> Alian served as the chief of the Golani Brigade during the 2014 Operation Protective Edge when Shaul 's body was kidnapped . <p> Alian conveyed his condolences to the family and emphasized the military 's commitment to returning the hostages home . <p> Head of the Golani Brigade , Adi Ganon , who served as deputy commander to the 13th Battalion in the Golani Brigade at the time , was also present . He stated , " This morning , I had the privilege of visiting the Shaul family home and talking about the feelings and memories I carry with me from my time as the deputy commander of 13th Battalion during Operation Protective Edge . " <p> " Coming full circle , troops of Battalion 51 took part in the moving rescue @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Your Oron , our Oron , has returned home , " he said , addressing the family .
