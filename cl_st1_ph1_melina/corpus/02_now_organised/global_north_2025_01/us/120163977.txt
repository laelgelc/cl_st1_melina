<p> ORLANDO ( AP ) -- The owner of a hotel in Orlando 's tourist district has reached an agreement with the U.S. Justice Department after the agency sued alleging discrimination when the resort canceled a planned conference for an Arab American group in the weeks after the attack which triggered the war in Gaza . <p> The Justice Department 's complaint filed Thursday alleged that the owner of DoubleTree by Hilton Hotel Orlando at SeaWorld had discriminated on the basis of national origin when it canceled hosting an annual summit held by the Arab America Foundation in November 2023 , almost a month after the Hamas attack on Israel on Oct. 7 , 2023 . <p> There had been no threats or specific risks to the hotel related to the summit or any other non-discriminatory reason to cancel the meeting for the Arab American cultural and educational group , the complaint said . <p> " Simply stated , amidst an ongoing war in the Middle East , the hotel did not want an Arab group -- in this instance , the Foundation -- to have its event at the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the first time in years that the DoubleTree had canceled an event against the wishes of the customer . " <p> The owner , AWH Orlando Property , denied the allegations and did n't admit liability . Attorneys for the owner said in an emailed statement on Friday that both parties reached the agreement to avoid a prolonged legal process . <p> " We are deeply committed to inclusivity and to fostering a welcoming environment for all , " the statement said . " As part of this commitment , we continue to implement proactive measures , including regular training on diversity , equity , and inclusion , while striving for continuous improvement and best practices . " <p> AWH Partners , which lists the hotel in its online portfolio , said in response to an emailed inquiry that it has " no control , say , input , or involvement " in the management or operations of the hotel since it is a passive , limited-partner investor . <p> Under the terms of the Justice Department agreement , the hotel must issue a statement within a month to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ guests regardless of race , color , religion or national origin . The hotel also must adopt a written anti-discrimination policy and reach out to at least five Arab or Arab American groups to let them know the hotel is open to all members of the public , according to the agreement . <p> The agreement , which was reached immediately after the Justice Department 's complaint was filed , must be approved by a federal judge .
