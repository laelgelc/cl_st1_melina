For example , when the International Criminal Court 's chief prosecutor , Karim Khan , filed war-crime charges against Benjamin Netanyahu last year , the Israeli prime minister said , " Khan takes his place among the great antisemites in modern times . " <p> Such misuse threatens to weaken the stigma attached to accusations of antisemitism at a time when Jews around the world need protection . It lies in a mistaken understanding of the best way to oppose the persecution of Jews . <p> The " working definition " of antisemitism that Harvard just adopted to guide its assessments facilitates this misuse .
