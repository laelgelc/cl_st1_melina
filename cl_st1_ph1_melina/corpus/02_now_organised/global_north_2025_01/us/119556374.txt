<p> The hostage-ceasefire negotiations expected to take place in Cairo on Friday have a high chance of being successful , senior Hamas official Mousa Abu Marzook told London-based Qatari newspaper Al-Araby Al-Jadeed on Thursday . <p> " There is a great chance for the negotiations to succeed this time , " he told the publication . <p> According to Al-Araby Al-Jadeed , a Hamas delegation arrived in the Egyptian capital on Wednesday and is set to travel to Doha , Qatar , on Thursday for continued negotiations . The talks are reportedly scheduled to pick back up on Friday . <p> Hamas spokesperson Jihad Taha told the Qatari newspaper that the delegation in Cairo was set to meet with Egyptian , Qatari , and Turkish mediators in order to complete previous negotiations . <h> ' Overcoming obstacles ' or creating them ? <p> The Hamas delegation aims to " to overcome the obstacles and conditions that the Israeli side has recently set with the aim of continuing the aggression against our people , " Taha said.Hamas terrorists take part in a military parade to mark the anniversary of the 2014 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Gaza Strip , July 19 , 2023.
