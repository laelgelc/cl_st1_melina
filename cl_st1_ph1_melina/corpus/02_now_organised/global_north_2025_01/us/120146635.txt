It involves Hamas releasing Israeli captives , in exchange for Israel releasing thousands of Palestinian prisoners , and Israel allowing a surge in humanitarian aid . <p> A ceasefire that was elusive all through 2024 suddenly clicked into place a few days before the Trump inauguration . How did it happen ? What does it mean for Israel and Netanyahu ?
