Alongside the hostages , 1,167 Gaza residents , including all women and children under 19 , will be freed . <p> However , Israel continued its airstrikes on Saturday , and Gaza 's Health Ministry said 23 bodies had been brought to hospitals over the past 24 hours . <p> " What is this truce that kills us hours before it begins ? " asked Abdallah Al-Aqad , the brother of a woman killed by an airstrike in the southern city of Khan Younis . Health officials said a couple and their two children , aged 2 and 7 , were dead .
