" Penguin/Christian Sinibaldi <p> From 2016 through 2022 , Scottish-born author Ali Smith engaged in a rather remarkable project , releasing five consecutive novels born out of current events . In addition to their kaleidoscopic narratives , these books -- the Seasonal Quartet and its aptly titled " Companion Piece " -- garnered deserved attention because they offered , despite the often glacial pace of modern publishing , almost real-time commentary on issues that were still in the news , such as the UK 's Brexit vote , the pandemic , and the ascension of right-wing nationalists in Western democracies like England and the United States . Smith 's latest title , " Gliff , " fits into this framework as well , serving as the extrapolated endpoint of her work over the past decade .
