<h> Sisi says Egypt will not participate in ' act of injustice ' displacing Palestinians <h> Sisi said Egypt would work with the new US president to reach peace between Israel and Palestinians based on a two-state solution . <p> By REUTERS JANUARY 29 , 2025 15:45 Updated : JANUARY 29 , 2025 16:18 <p> Abdel Fattah al-Sisi ( photo credit : REUTERS ) <p> Egypt will not participate in the displacement of Palestinians , an " act of injustice " that would threaten Egyptian security , President Abdel Fattah Al-Sisi said on Wednesday , his first public response to Donald Trump 's call for Cairo to take in residents of the Gaza Strip . <p> Speaking at a press conference with visiting Kenyan president William Ruto , Sisi said Egypt would work with the new US president to reach peace between Israel and Palestinians based on a two-state solution . <p> " Regarding what is being said about the displacement of Palestinians , it can never be tolerated or allowed because of its impact on Egyptian national security , " Sisi said . <p> EGYPTIAN PRESIDENT Abdel Fattah al-Sisi @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Sisi recently stated that he will not cooperate with a US-led coalition against Iran that would counter Tehran 's attack on Israel . ( credit : Iran 's Presidency/West Asia News Agency/Reuters ) <h> Trump 's comments <p> Trump said on Saturday that Egypt and Jordan should take in Palestinians from Gaza , which he called a " demolition site " following 15 months of war ignited by Hamas 's October 7 2023 attacks that made most of its 2.3 million people homeless . <p> Any suggestion that Palestinians leave Gaza , the territory they want for a state , has been anathema to the Palestinian leadership for generations and repeatedly rejected by neighboring Arab states since the Gaza war began in Oct. 2023 . <p> Jordan is already home to several million Palestinians , while tens of thousands live in Egypt . The foreign ministries of Egypt and Jordan have both rejected Trump 's suggestion in recent days .
