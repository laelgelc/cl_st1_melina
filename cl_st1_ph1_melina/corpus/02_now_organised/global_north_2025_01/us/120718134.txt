<p> Zionism is an idea , a concept , and it has never been one thing that people universally agree upon . From its very beginning , Zionism was a subject of debate and contention . There is a saying that where you have two Jews , you have at least three opinions . Zionism is a work in progress , an open concept , and in many ways it is a struggle . It is a struggle over identity , purpose , and values . It is a debate about what it means to build a national home for the Jewish people in the Land of Israel , and it continues to evolve with each generation . <p> The origin of Zionism is the ancient yearning of Jews to return to Zion , a longing expressed in prayers , songs , and rituals for thousands of years . However , in its modern rebirth in the late 1800s , Zionism emerged alongside many national aspiration movements awakening across Europe and beyond . It evolved in parallel with its twin movement -- the Palestinian national movement .
