<h> Rep. Bell 's vote to sanction ICC is unseemly after AIPAC support ( Letter ) <p> Newly sworn U.S. Rep. Wesley Bell , D-St . Louis , was one of 45 Democratic House members who joined Republicans to impose sanctions on the International Criminal Court for having charged Israeli leaders with war crimes over the country 's devastating military assault on Gaza . The bill , which still must be debated in the Senate , would punish members of the Court , as well as any non-U.S . citizens who materially or financially support the ICC . <p> The Court has played a key role in holding accountable governments for egregious violations of international law , around the world and across ideological lines . Voters in St. Louis ' First District should recall that Rep. Bell 's primary victory over incumbent Rep. Cori Bush in the Democratic primary was heavily financed by pro-Israeli PACs like The American Israel Public Affairs Committee ( AIPAC ) , whose support induced him to abandon a Senate campaign to seek a House seat .
