<h> A political path back for the Democrats ? Who 's to say ? <h> In a time of ecological and economic collapse , the party has to be radically different <p> David Scharfenberg finishes his electoral postmortem , " How the Democrats come back " ( Ideas , Jan. 5 ) , by laying out a clear path for the party : " Nominate a candidate without woke baggage . " <p> The idea here is that the progressive elements of the party have scared off common-sense , hard-working people in the middle who just want a stable economy and a noncrazy cultural landscape . If the Democrats just lay off the wokeism , they can win back these regular folks while reaping the benefits of demographic changes that are already trending their way . <p> It 's a tempting interpretation , but it misses the bigger picture of ecological and economic collapse , something that more and more people are becoming aware of , even if only subconsciously . Trumpism has mass appeal not because it is a set of sensible economic proposals .
