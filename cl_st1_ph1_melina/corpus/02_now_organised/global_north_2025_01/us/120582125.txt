<h> Letter : Make cease-fire permanent , end Israeli occupation <h> Share this : <h> Make cease-fire permanent , end Israeli occupation <p> Editor : North Branch Friends Meeting understands the long-held Quaker commitment to peace as " a living testimony , " which " requires us to honor that of God in every person , and therefore to avoid not only physical violence but also more subtle forms of violence -- be they psychological , economic , or systemic " ( PYM -- Quaker Testimonies ) . This testimony calls us to express solidarity with the recommendations for Friends included in " A Different Future Is Possible : Shared Quaker Vision for Peace in Israel and Palestine . " <p> The recently achieved cease-fire should serve not as a conclusion but instead as a call to further action that addresses the root causes of conflict in the past , present , and future . It is our fundamental obligation to act in the world to counter violence wherever it may occur . In response to the ongoing violence in Israel and Gaza , we agree with and would supplement @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " asks us to act in the following ways : <p> Urgently support a permanent cease-fire and efforts to build systemic peace and justice by amplifying our voices in our communities at the local , state , and national levels . <p> Encourage decision-makers on all sides in working for a sustainable and holistic peace . <p> Organize and participate as best we can to achieve a just and lasting peace , perhaps through teach-ins , actions , and protests . <p> Actively support an end to Israel 's occupation and equal protection and rights for all people . <p> Divest from corporations profiting from militarism , including the occupation of Palestine . <p> Support those in Israel and Palestine who are working for peace . <p> Remember that our work for peace calls us to action not only in the Middle East but anywhere where violence is being perpetuated .
