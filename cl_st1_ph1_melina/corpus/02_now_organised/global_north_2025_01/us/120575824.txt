<h> Victory and defeat in Gaza <p> The author in her displacement tent and a photo of the first glass of Arabic coffee she had in a long time . Images taken in December 2024 and January 2025 in the Gaza Strip Courtesy of Hadeel Awad <p> A ceasefire has finally come . After 15 months of relentless genocidal war , we are finally able to breathe a sigh of relief . Many of us have also been able to return to our homes or what is left of them . <p> While we enjoy our bomb-free time , the world appears to be engaged in a fierce debate about who won . Is Israel triumphant ? Or is Hamas the one that can declare victory ? Or are the heroic Palestinian people the winners ? <p> I am a nurse , not a pundit , so I have no answers to offer . But let me tell you , dear reader : The world should not be deceived by our survival . Staying alive in Gaza is not synonymous with heroism . We just kept going in a circle , every day , returning to the beginning . <p> Every day , every family had to go out in search of drinking water , water for washing , food and something to make a fire with -- the very basics .
