<p> International Holocaust Remembrance Day , observed Jan. 27 , brings the weight of history , the horror of genocide , and a profound reckoning with the worst of human nature . Amid the great darkness of the Holocaust , there is always a sliver of hope and testament to the best in human nature -- those individuals who risked everything to save Jews , defying unthinkable evil . In the words of the Yad Vashem memorial to the victims of the Holocaust in Jerusalem , they are the " Righteous Among the Nations . " <p> Yad Vashem honors these heroes for their moral fortitude and courage . They came from all corners of society . Some hid Jews . Some forged identity documents to facilitate escapes . Some provided false employment to protect Jews from deportation . Some attempted direct rescue operations , hoping to smuggle even a few Jews out of ghettos or across borders . They demonstrated a profound commitment to humanity in the face of extreme adversity . Under Nazi rule , Germans faced severe consequences for aiding Jews , yet some still chose to resist . They smuggled Jews to safety , falsified documents , and hid them in their homes .
