<p> ISTANBUL -- Tens of thousands of people gathered on Istanbul 's Galata Bridge on New Year 's Day on Wednesday to express solidarity with Palestinians in Gaza . <p> Demonstrators waved Turkish and Palestinian flags and chanted " Free Palestine " in the protest , organized by the National Will Platform , a coalition of more than 300 pro-Palestinian and Islamic groups . <p> Bilal Erdogan , the son of President Recep Tayyip Erdogan , addressed the crowd , urging support for Gaza and condemning Israel 's actions there . He referred to the recent ouster of Syrian President Bashar Assad by rebel forces . <p> " Muslims in Syria were determined , patient and they achieved victory . After Syria , Gaza will emerge victoriously from the siege , " he said . <p> Drone video showed thousands of people filling the bridge and the adjacent Emin ? n ? and Sirkeci districts . <p> President Erdogan has been a fierce critic of the Israeli offensive in Gaza .
