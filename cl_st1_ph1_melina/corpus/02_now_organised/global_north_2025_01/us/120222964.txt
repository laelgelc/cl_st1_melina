<p> Once access is achieved , the attackers replace desktop backgrounds with an image bearing the colors of the al-Aqsa Martyrs Brigade -- a group associated with Hamas . The background also includes text in English and Hebrew declaring , " Israel has been defeated . Over a year of bombings on Gaza , but the resistance is stronger than ever . The struggle will continue until every piece of Palestinian land is liberated . October 7 was just the beginning . " <p> In some cases , attackers have also gained control of printers , printing copies of the message , and have even corrupted organizational files . <p> Preliminary investigations suggest the attack resembles methods used by the Handala hacking group , which has previously targeted Israeli websites and stolen sensitive data . As part of their strategy , the group verifies the geographical location of the targeted computer by attempting to access the Israeli Home Front Command website , which is accessible only within Israel . Once confirmed , they proceed with their attack . An illustrative image depicting a cyberattack.
