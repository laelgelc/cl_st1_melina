<h> Biden 's dark foreign policy legacy <p> President Joe Biden 's legacy as an architect of the international order may be his most consequential one . While his domestic policy vision began with ambition , his efforts largely failed to change the policy landscape fundamentally , and much of his agenda either did not make it into law or is likely to be reversed . At the same time , Biden leaves the global arena significantly different than he found it -- and in ways that are likely to endure . The decisions he made regarding war , peace and diplomacy will not only shape his successor 's policy options but will have long-lasting effects on the United States ' global stature and its relationships with its allies and adversaries . <p> Unfortunately , Biden 's foreign policy legacy is largely a dark one .
