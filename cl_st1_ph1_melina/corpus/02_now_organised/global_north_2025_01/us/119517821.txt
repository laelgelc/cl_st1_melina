<p> Israel 's population reached 10 million people before the end of 2024 , according to a report released by the Central Bureau of Statistics . <p> Of the 10 million citizens in Israel , approximately 76.9% of them ( 7.707 million people ) are Jewish , and 21.0% ( 2.104 million people ) of the population is Arab . Two percent of the population ( 210,000 people ) identify as other . <p> The CBS noted that the " other " category includes non-Arab Christians and those not classified by religion in the state 's population registry . <p> Overall , the population in Israel grew by 1.1% ( 129,600 people ) in 2024 . The natural increase ( number of births minus the number of deaths ) was 112,000 . <p> This growth rate is slightly lower than in 2023 , when the population grew by 1.6% , according to the previous year 's CBS report . Tuesday 's report attributed this to the high number of Israelis who emigrated abroad . In 2023 , 55,400 people emigrated abroad , far beyond the annual average of 37,100 for the decade before the Israel-Hamas War . <p> Additionally , 40,600 Israelis emigrated abroad in the first seven months of 2024 , meaning that 2,200 more people left the country each month than in the year prior . In 2023 , approximately 39% of the emigrants came from the country 's wealthier districts , 28% left from the North , and 15% from the South . <p> Israel saw an influx of 32,890 new immigrants in 2024 . Combined with those who returned after emigrating and family reunifications , the country 's net migration -- the difference between the number of people entering and leaving Israel -- was negative 18,200 . <p> Some 181,000 babies were born in Israel this year , 76% to Jewish mothers and 24% to Arab mothers . <p> The CBS report stated that 51,400 deaths were recorded in 2024 , nearly 1,800 more than in 2023 .
