He leaves behind a remarkably weak foreign policy record . <p> Yes , there was one big achievement : Biden ended the war in Afghanistan . He also reaffirmed America 's commitment to NATO and brought the United States back into the Paris Agreement on climate change . <p> During Biden 's presidency , though , the United States achieved no other geopolitical breakthroughs .
