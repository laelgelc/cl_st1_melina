The government has stressed the necessity of the deal to bring hostages home . <p> After months of intense negotiations , Israel is set to implement the first phase of a hostage release agreement on Sunday , January 19 , 2025 . This initial phase will see the release of 33 hostages over 42 days . The hostages include women , children , elderly individuals , and those with urgent medical needs . <h> Timeline and details <p> The initial phase begins with the release of three hostages on January 19 . Each week , additional hostages will be freed , with a focus on women and children . The final group in this phase is expected to include long-term captives Avera Mengistu and Hisham al-Sayed . <h> Terms of the agreement <p> The deal includes the controversial release of approximately 1,000 Palestinian prisoners , including 200 described as having " blood on their hands .
