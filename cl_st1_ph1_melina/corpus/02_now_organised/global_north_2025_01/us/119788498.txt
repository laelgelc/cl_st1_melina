<h> Despite war , 600 students from 64 countries come to Ben-Gurion University <h> Despite global headlines focusing on conflict , international students find profound experiences in Israel . <p> By PELED ARBELI JANUARY 8 , 2025 13:27 <p> International students at Ben-Gurion University 's campus who chose to study in Israel during the war ( photo credit : Dani Machlis , Ben-Gurion University ) <p> One might expect that during times of conflict , students seeking to study abroad would remove Israel from their list of preferred destinations . However , approximately 600 students from 64 countries chose to continue or begin their academic journey at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev ( BGU ) , located in southern Israel . This region includes areas near the Gaza Strip , which experienced devastating attacks on October 7 , 2023 , during the Israel-Hamas war . <p> Despite global headlines emphasizing the conflict , international students are discovering something even more meaningful during their time in Israel : solidarity , purpose , and community .
