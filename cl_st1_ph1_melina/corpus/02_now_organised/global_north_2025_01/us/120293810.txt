<h> Trump withdraws U.S. from World Health Organization <p> President Donald Trump moved quickly Monday to withdraw the United States from the World Health Organization , a move that public health experts say will undermine the nation 's standing as a global health leader and make it harder to fight the next pandemic . <p> The move was not unexpected . Trump has been railing against the WHO since 2020 , when he attacked the agency over its approach to the coronavirus pandemic and threatened to withhold United States funding from it . But after he lost the 2020 election the threat did not materialize . <p> Leaving the WHO would mean , among other things , that the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention would have no access to the global data that the agency provides . When China characterized the genetic sequence of the novel coronavirus in 2020 , it released the information to the WHO , which shared it with other nations . <p> More recently , the WHO has become a target of conservatives over its work on a " pandemic treaty " to strengthen pandemic @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ surveillance of pathogens , rapid sharing of outbreak data , and building up local manufacturing and supply chains for vaccines and treatments , among others . <p> Talks on the treaty broke down last year . In the United States , some Republican lawmakers viewed the agreement as a threat to American sovereignty . <p> Lawrence O. Gostin , a public health law expert at Georgetown University who helped negotiate the treaty , said that a United States withdrawal from the WHO would be " a grievous wound " to public health but an " even deeper wound to American national interests and national security . " <p> Founded in 1948 with help from the United States , the World Health Organization is as an agency of the United Nations . The WHO 's mission , according to its website , is to " confront the biggest health challenges of our time and measurably advance the well-being of the world 's people . " <p> That includes bringing aid to war-torn areas like the Gaza Strip and tracking emerging epidemics like Zika , Ebola and COVID-19 . The annual budget @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ States has typically contributed an outsize share . <p> According to Gostin , it will take some time for the United States to withdraw .
