<p> International Criminal Court ( ICC ) Chief Prosecutor Karim Khan objected on Tuesday to Israel 's appeal of the war crimes arrest warrants that were issued against Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former defense minister Yoav Gallant . <p> The warrants constitute the darkest legal crisis Israel has ever encountered and have led to diplomatic bouts between Israel and other states , some of which have been more aggressive in seeking to arrest IDF soldiers than others -- although the ICC itself has not gone after soldiers just yet . <p> On November 21 , the ICC 's Pre-Trial Chamber , a lower court , approved the warrants . <p> A few weeks later , in mid-December , Israel appealed to its Appeals Chamber -- the institution 's top court -- to reverse the decision . <p> The appeal rested mainly on Articles 18 and 19 of the Rome Statute , which established the court and to which Israel and the US are not signatories -- raising , both directly and indirectly , a plethora of errors it said the lower court committed in its decision .
