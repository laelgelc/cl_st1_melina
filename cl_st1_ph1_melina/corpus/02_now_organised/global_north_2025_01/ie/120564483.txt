<h> US Senate blocks bill to impose sanctions on ICC <p> The Senate voted 54-45 in favour , falling short of the needed 60 votes in favour to advance . <p> Reuters <p> The ICC issued arrest warrants in November last year for Netanyahu and his former Defence Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza . / Photo : Reuters Archive <p> The US Senate has blocked a bill to sanction the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) for its arrest warrants against Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former defence minister over the carnage in besieged Gaza . <p> The chamber voted 54-45 in favour of the bill on Tuesday , meaning the measure , which had already passed the Republican-majority House of Representatives , could not get the 60 " yes " votes needed in the 100-member Senate to advance . <p> The vote was largely along party lines , with almost every Democrat and Independent voting against it . Senator John Fetterman was the only Democrat to vote with Republicans to advance the measure .
