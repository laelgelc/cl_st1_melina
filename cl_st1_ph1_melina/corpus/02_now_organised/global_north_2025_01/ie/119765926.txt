<p> Palestinian filmmaker and producer Rashid Masharawi sat down with Screen International to discuss From Ground Zero , a collection of 22 short films made during the Gaza war that has been shortlisted for the Oscars ' international feature category . Watch above . <p> The acclaimed director , who curated the anthology of shorts from emerging Palestinian filmmakers , explains how the project came together , why Michael Moore has come on board and what an Academy Award nomination would mean for those involved . <p> From Ground Zero captures life in Gaza amidst the ongoing war , blending approaches that include animation , documentary , and fiction . <p> The film received its North America premiere at Toronto International Film Festival and was released in US theatres on January 3 through Watermelon Pictures .
