<p> The 90 Palestinian prisoners freed by Israel on Sunday as part of the Gaza ceasefire deal included 69 women and 21 teenage boys , according to Hamas . None had been convicted of murder . Among those released , 76 were from the West Bank and 14 from East Jerusalem , which were occupied by Israel in 1967 . All but seven were arrested after the October 2023 attack on Israel by Hamas . <p> Under the first phase of the six-week ceasefire agreement , 33 Israeli hostages -- including the three women released by Hamas on Sunday -- are set to be exchanged for 735 Palestinian prisoners and more than 1,000 Gazan civilians , mainly women and children , captured by Israel during the war . <p> The most prominent prisoner freed was Khalida Jarrar ( 61 ) , who had been held in administrative detention since December 2023 . A leading member of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine ( PFLP ) , she was accused of planning a 2019 bombing that killed a 17-year-old Israeli girl in the West Bank .
