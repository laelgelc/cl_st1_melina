<p> Abu Nabhan 's wife , Beraa Ziyad Abdulhafiz Hamide , told Anadolu that her husband was just 25 years old . She said he had previously lived in the Al-Jadid Refugee Camp , northwest of the Nuseirat Refugee Camp in central Gaza . <p> " Saed and I have a 15-month-old baby named Anas . Our child was born about a month before the Israeli attacks began , " she said . <p> A total of 31 members of Saed 's family were killed by Israeli forces , she added . The Israeli onslaught , which continues since the Oct. 7 , 2023 Hamas attacks , have to date claimed over 46,000 lives in the blockaded enclave . <p> His friends said Abu Nabhan had little time to spend with his child due to the dire conditions in Gaza . They remembered him as a cheerful person .
