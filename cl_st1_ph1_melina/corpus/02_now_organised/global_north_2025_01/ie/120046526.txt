<h> Watch : A look back at 15 months of war in Gaza <p> The 7 October attack by Hamas left hundreds of Israelis massacred , and 250 taken hostage . It triggered an overwhelming response . <p> The Israeli military unleashed a devastating air campaign , followed by a fuel and food blockade , followed by a land invasion . <p> One part of Gaza after another was evacuated , but civilians appeared to be mercilessly shunted into firing zones . Over 70,000 tonnes of ordinance were dropped on Gaza , laying waste to entire swathes of civilian areas . <p> Over 46,000 people have been killed , at least half women and children . Famine has been a regular danger . <p> Hamas continued to fire rockets into Israel , while to the North Hezbollah launched missiles from Lebanon . <p> Eventually that prompted a partial invasion of southern Lebanon and yet another deadly air campaign that killed thousands . <p> Despite international outrage , a genocide case at the International Court of Justice , and arrest warrants against the Israeli prime minister and defence minister , Israel @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ decapitating both Hamas and Hezbollah , killing the Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar , and the Hezbollah leader Hassan Nasrallah . <p> Hostilities with Iran , the main sponsor of proxy groups fighting Israel , exploded into the open after Israel killed a senior Iranian in Damascus .
