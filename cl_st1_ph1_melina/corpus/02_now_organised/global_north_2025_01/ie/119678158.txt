<h> Robbie Keane appointed new manager of Hungarian giants Ferencvaros <p> ROBBIE KEANE HAS been confirmed as the new manager of Hungarian champions Ferencvaros . <p> Keane flew to Budapest on Sunday evening , and was confirmed as manager on Monday afternoon . Keane will now fly to Spain to take charge of a training camp , with the Hungarian league season currently on a mid-season break . <p> Keane replaces Dutchman Pascal Jansen , who left the club at the end of 2024 to take charge at New York City in Major League Soccer . Jansen leaves Ferencvaros in a strong position , second in the league but a single point off the league leaders , and with a game in hand .
