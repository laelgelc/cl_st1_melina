<p> Talks aimed at cementing a truce in Gaza between Israel and Palestinian resistance group Hamas are ongoing , with " technical meetings " taking place between the parties , mediator Qatar 's Foreign Ministry has said . <p> " The technical meetings are still happening between both sides , " ministry spokesman Majed al-Ansari said Tuesday , referring to meetings with lower-level officials on the details of an agreement . <p> " There are no principal meetings taking place at the moment . " <p> Mediators Qatar , Egypt and the United States have been engaged in months of talks that have failed to end Israel 's war on Gaza . <p> Ansari said there were " a lot of issues that are being discussed " in the ongoing meetings , but declined to go into details " to protect the integrity of the negotiations " . <p> Hamas said at the end of last week that indirect negotiations in Doha had resumed , while Israel said it had authorised negotiators to continue the talks in the Qatari capital . <p> A previous round of mediation in December ended @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with Hamas accusing Israel of setting " new conditions " . <p> In December , the Gulf emirate expressed optimism that " momentum " was returning to the talks following Donald Trump 's election victory in the United States . <p> A month earlier , Doha had said it was putting its mediation on hold , and that it would resume when Hamas and Israel showed " willingness and seriousness " .
