<h> The Irish sports stars who refuse to stay silent on the Israeli-Palestinian conflict <p> ACCORDING TO figures provided last month by the Ministry of Health in Gaza , more than 45,000 people have now died as a result of the conflict with Israel . <p> That number included 17,000 children and as the war enters its 15th month , there appears to be no end in sight . <p> Given the experience of bloodshed under British colonial rule , it is a conflict that has resonated with Irish citizens . <p> Inevitably , many people prefer not to speak on this issue -- several individuals approached for this article either declined or did not respond to requests for comment . <p> But some within the Irish sporting community have refused to stay silent . <p> Shelbourne assistant boss and former Irish international footballer Joey O'Brien is one man who has consistently protested the treatment of Palestinians in recent months , wearing t-shirts , hats and badges to show solidarity with the oppressed nation . <p> " It 's a personal thing , " he tells The 42 . It 's an absolute disgrace , they 're trying to wipe out a population . " <p> The former West Ham and Bolton player has been following the conflict for years but one story in particular hit home . <p> It concerned a husband whose wife had recently given birth to twins .
