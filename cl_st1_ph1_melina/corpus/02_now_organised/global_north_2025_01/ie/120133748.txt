<h> Houthi militia expected to halt Red Sea shipping attacks <p> A protest staged in solidarity with the people in the Gaza Strip , in Sana'a , Yemen , on Wednesday . Photo : Mohammed Hamoud/Getty Images <p> Renee Maltezou , Jonathan Saul and Yannis Souliotis <p> Today at 03:30 <p> Maritime security officials said yesterday they were expecting Yemen 's Houthi militia to announce a halt in attacks on ships in the Red Sea , after a ceasefire deal in the war in Gaza between Israel and the militant group Hamas . <p> The experts pointed to an email , seen by Reuters , from the group postponing a planned security briefing that had been due to take place in the coming days as a possible signal . Speculation has mounted that Houthi leader Abdul-Malik al-Houthi may announce a pause off the back of the Gaza deal .
