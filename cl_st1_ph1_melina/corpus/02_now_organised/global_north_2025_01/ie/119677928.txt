<h> More than 100 killed in Israeli strikes on Gaza over weekend , as efforts for ceasefire continue <p> Palestinian girls at the site of an Israeli strike on a house in Gaza City yesterday . Photo : Reuters <p> Nidal Al-Mughrabi <p> Today at 03:30 <p> Israeli airstrikes killed at least 14 Palestinians in three separate attacks in the Gaza Strip yesterday , taking the weekend death toll to 102 , Palestinian medics said , as US and Arab mediators stepped up efforts to conclude a ceasefire deal . <p> Health officials said an Israeli airstrike killed five people in a house in the Nuseirat camp in central Gaza , while another airstrike killed four others in Jabalia in the northern edge of the enclave , where Israeli forces have been operating for three months .
