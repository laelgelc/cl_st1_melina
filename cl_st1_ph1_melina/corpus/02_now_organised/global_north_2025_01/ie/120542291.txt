<p> Punk band Chewie ( formerly Chewing on Tinfoil ) have announced a headline show at The Academy , Dublin on Friday , October 17 . <p> The group have been a core part of Dublin 's underground punk scene for nearly two decades . They pride themselves on being a DIY band that has done everything themselves- organising international tours , promoting their often sold-out gigs , and releasing music under their own label . <p> Their most recent album , Inarticulate , was released in May 2023 , however for their upcoming October show , Chewie plan to revisit a project they produced ten years previous . Marrowbone Lane , a twelve track record from 2013 , will be played in its entirety as the band takes the stage at the Academy . <p> Chewie have plan to re-release a version of the 2013 album later this year , re-mastered from the original recordings .
