This is the most significant failure in Israel 's history . Nevertheless , were an election to be held today , he and his coalition partners would not win a majority in Israel 's parliament . <p> What do you see as his political future ? <p> This is the most serious challenge by far that Netanyahu has faced in his long career . Hamas will be enforcing order on an angry , devastated and impoverished Palestinian population for a long time to come . <p> Has the ceasefire worked as intended so far ? <p> It is very early days , but yes , it is working .
