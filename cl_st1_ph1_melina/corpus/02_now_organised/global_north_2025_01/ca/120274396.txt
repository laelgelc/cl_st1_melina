<h> Israel releases 90 Palestinian prisoners under Gaza ceasefire deal <p> FILE : Bringing them home : The Israeli Air Force and the " Yas'ur " helicopters from the 118th Squadron ( " Night Birds " ) , are Prepared for the Process of Receiving the Hostages Returning From the Gaza Strip . ( Photo : Israeli Air Force/Facebook ) <p> RAMALLAH , Palestine -- Israel released 90 Palestinian detainees early Monday from Ofer Military Prison in the occupied West Bank as part of a prisoner exchange agreement with Hamas . <p> Palestinians gathered in the town of Beitunia , near Ramallah , in the occupied West Bank to welcome them . <p> Israeli soldiers intervened by firing tear gas and sound bombs intermittently at the crowd , who were using fire to stay warm due to the cold . <p> As the time neared 1 a.m. , a large number of Israeli military jeeps and bulldozers raided Beitunia . <p> After firing tear gas and sound bombs , Israeli military jeeps began to drive the Palestinians away from the area . <p> Shortly after the raid on Beitunia , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ International Committee of the Red Cross ( ICRC ) departed from Ofer Military Prison . <p> It proceeded from Beitunia towards Manara Square in Ramallah , where the Palestinian detainees would be handed over . <p> The 90 Palestinian detainees released included 69 women , eight male minors and 12 men sentenced for relatively minor offenses . <p> Israeli media reported that 78 of the Palestinians released from Ofer Military Prison were from the occupied West Bank , while 12 were from occupied East Jerusalem . <p> According to the agreement between Israel and the Palestinian group Hamas , 90 Palestinian detainees were released in exchange for three Israeli women detainees who had been freed the previous day . <p> The military wing of Hamas , the Izz ad-Din al-Qassam Brigades , delivered the three Israeli women detainees to a Red Cross team in the evening , marking the first phase of the ceasefire and prisoner swap agreement .
