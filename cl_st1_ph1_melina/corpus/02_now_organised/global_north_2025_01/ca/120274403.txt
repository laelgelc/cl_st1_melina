Increasingly , many doctors find they must speak up and be advocates before a world that seemingly cares little for the lives and rights of their patients . <p> In some cases , that advocacy has been in the form of civil disobedience against policies and laws seen to be unjust or inequitable . On the one hand , medicine is a profession of norms , rules , regulations , standards and tradition . Admittedly , it is difficult to codify " acceptable " civil disobedience , since it is , by definition a transgressive act . Acts of civil disobedience are anchored in individual morality . <p> In a culture where morality is increasingly individualized , agreeing on universal parameters to describe civil disobedience is likely an impossible undertaking .
