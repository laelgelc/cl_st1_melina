<h> Pro-Palestinian rally planned in Kitchener following ceasefire deal <p> A rally to stand with the Palestinian community is set to take place Friday evening in front of Kitchener City Hall . <p> The Palestinian Youth Movement ( PYM ) is organizing the rally . The group says the message of the rally is that there is still a lot of work to be done in Gaza . <p> Nancy Yasmin , an organizer with PYM said while their group is relieved to see a ceasefire deal in place , a lot of people overseas are still struggling in the ongoing conflict . <p> " The purpose of this rally is to recommit ourselves to the liberation of Palestine , " said Yasmin . " Even if the ceasefire was actually enacted this second , there are over one million Gazans displaced in tents , hospital , school court yards in the cold winter . "
