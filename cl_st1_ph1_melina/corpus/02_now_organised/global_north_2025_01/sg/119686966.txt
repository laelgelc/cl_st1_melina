More than 95,000 people injured in a land where half of all hospitals have been damaged or destroyed . <p> Beyond the broken bodies and obliterated buildings , another chilling statistic : 96 per cent of children feel their death is imminent , and almost half want to die , according to a study released this month . <p> And the death toll keeps rising as 2.3 million people criss-cross the blasted land , seeking shelter from the daily Israeli airstrikes that pound an area just 45km long and 10km wide on the edge of the Mediterranean Sea . <p> Experts warn of imminent famine in parts of Gaza , a claim Israel has said is based on " partial , biased data .
