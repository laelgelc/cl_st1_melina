<p> JERUSALEM : The Israeli military said on Sunday ( Jan 5 ) it had intercepted a missile launched from Yemen , the latest in a series of recent attacks . <p> " Following the sirens that sounded a short while ago in Talmei Elazar , a missile launched from Yemen was intercepted prior to crossing into Israeli territory , " the military said in a statement posted to Telegram . <p> Yemen 's Houthi rebels , who control much of northern Yemen including the capital Sanaa , said in a statement on Sunday they had launched " a hypersonic ballistic missile " targeting a power station south of the Israeli city of Haifa . <p> On Friday , Israel 's military said it had shot down a missile and a drone launched from Yemen , where Iran-backed rebels have stepped up their attacks since a November ceasefire in Lebanon between Israel and another Iran-backed group , Hezbollah . <p> Like Hezbollah -
