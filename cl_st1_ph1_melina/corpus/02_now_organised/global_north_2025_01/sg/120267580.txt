<h> Yemen 's Houthis to Narrow Scope of Ship Attacks After Gaza Ceasefire <p> Yemen 's Houthi movement is set to narrow the scope of its attacks on commercial shipping . File Image / Pixabay <p> Yemen 's Houthi movement is set to narrow the scope of its attacks on commercial shipping following the ceasefire deal implemented in Gaza over the weekend . <p> Attacks from now on will focus on Israel-flagged or wholly Israeli-owned ships , the Houthis ' Humanitarian Operations Coordination Center said on Sunday . <p> For more than a year , the Houthi movement has been carrying out attacks on commercial ships in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden . Most leading shipping companies have diverted their vessels away from Yemen as a result , meaning the Suez Canal can not be used and ships must consume more fuel on longer voyages around Africa . <p> There is unlikely to be an instant mass shift of shipping companies to use Suez again , but this may emerge gradually over the year if the ceasefire holds .
