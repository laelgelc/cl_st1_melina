<h> Joy and relief for parents of Thai hostages released by Hamas <p> Ms Wiwaew Sri-uan , mother of Thai hostage Watchara Sri-uan , reacts with her relative as her son is released from Gaza on Jan 30 . PHOTO : REUTERS <p> Thanks for sharing ! <p> Udon Thani natives Watchara Sri-uan and Surasak Lamnao are among five Thai hostages being released on Jan 30 after being detained for more than a year since the Hamas raids on Israel on Oct 7 , 2023 . <p> The elated parents of Mr Watchara -- Tom and Wiwaew Sri-uan -- said they would not allow their son to work abroad again . <p> Ms Wiwaew said that she had received the good news about Mr Watchara 's release from her granddaughter on Jan 29 . <p> The parents of Mr Surasak -- Mr Chanda Prachanan and Ms Khammee Lamnao -- could not contain their happiness after hearing the good news . Ms Khammee said she wants to meet her son at the airport , so she can give him a big hug . <p> She said through all these painful months @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ She thanked officials for their encouragement and Hamas for releasing her son . <p> Mr Viras Tanna , father of Buri Ram native Phongsak Tanna who is one of five hostages released on Jan 30 , said he was so happy that he could not sleep . <p> He said that he had followed up with several agencies and media outlets , hoping that his son was still alive as there was no official confirmation . <p> Mr Sarayut Thongsri from Buri Ram Provincial Labour Office revealed that five Buri Ram natives had died and three others were still detained . <p> Mr Phongsak is the third hostage released , following two Buri Ram natives who had been released earlier , Mr Sarayut said , adding that 809 Buri Ram natives still worked in Israel .
