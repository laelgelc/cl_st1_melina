His executive orders have also run afoul of the courts , with some overruled and their implementation delayed . One was his order to terminate home-born citizenship and then , more recently , he refused to accept the judge overturning his order invoking the Alien Enemies Act -- which he has disputed signing -- to deport certain Venezuelan citizens to prison in El Salvador . The act has been used previously only when the US was at war . <p> Trump has also taken every opportunity to disparage the judicial process in the US , including attacking judges and juries , and most recently threatening to pursue the lawyers involved in the criminal cases against him and extract pardons for his activities . The aim is to politicise the judicial process for some populist advantage . But the importance of the separation of powers can not be overstated : it is fundamental to democracy under the rule of law . The latter means all authority and power must come from the ultimate source of the law . A strong and stable judiciary is also fundamental to our economic system , which operates within a consistent set of legal parameters that can be relied upon to be impartially enforced , with fairness and integrity , to protect people 's rights and freedoms . The funding of the courts and judicial appointments must be fully transparent and accountable .
