<p> With press freedom under threat around the world , it is disturbing to learn that RCMP officers recently came to The Ubyssey 's office " seeking contact information for sources in a previous story , who they anticipated might stage a demonstration . " I commend The Ubyssey 's journalists for standing their ground . <p> The RCMP 's visit to UBC 's student newspaper is not an isolated incident , rather it represents an escalation of already excessive surveillance . Last year , dozens of faculty members decried the maximalist response by campus security and police to protest and speech against Israel 's war on Gaza and system of apartheid . Nowadays it is not uncommon for several UBC Campus Security staff to film ( sometimes inconspicuously ) related demonstrations . Often RCMP officers are present , too .
