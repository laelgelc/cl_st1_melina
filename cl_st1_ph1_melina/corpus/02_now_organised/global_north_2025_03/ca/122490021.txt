( AP Photo/Abdel Kareem Hana ) <p> By Julia Frankel , The Associated Press <p> Posted Mar 18 , 2025 04:49:13 PM . <p> Last Updated Mar 18 , 2025 05:02:08 PM . <p> JERUSALEM ( AP ) -- Israel 's wave of predawn airstrikes across Gaza shattered two months of relative calm during a ceasefire with Hamas . Tuesday was one of the deadliest days in Gaza since the war began , with over 400 Palestinians killed and hundreds more wounded . <p> The first phase of the ceasefire saw Hamas release 33 Israeli hostages in exchange for Israel releasing nearly 2,000 Palestinian prisoners . The deal also called on Israel to ramp up delivery of humanitarian aid into Gaza . After those exchanges wrapped up , Israel cut off all aid to Gaza to pressure Hamas to extend the ceasefire .
