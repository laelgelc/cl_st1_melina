" The assembly , held in a hybrid format at 6:00 PM EST on the second floor of the University Centre , attracted significant participation . In-person attendance was capped at 300 due to fire code regulations , with additional members joining via Zoom . After extensive discussion , the motion passed , initiating a three-day undergraduate student strike scheduled for April 2 to 4 , 2025 : the first approved SSMU strike motion in the history of the student society .
