He was held overnight at an immigration detention center in New Jersey before being moved to an immigration facility in Jena , Louisiana . <p> Judge Jesse Furman in Manhattan called the legal challenge an " exceptional case " in need of careful legal review to determine whether the government " violated the law or exercised its otherwise lawful authority in an arbitrary and discriminatory manner .
