<h> Ellen Coyne : Every now and then , a deluge of emails about Gaza brings my inbox to a standstill and I understand why <p> Every now and then , the deluge of emails come and bring my inbox to a standstill . Over the course of the war in Gaza , dozens if not hundreds of people across Ireland have been running a campaign aimed at journalists like me . The emails to myself and many of my colleagues across TV , radio and newspapers are coordinated to come in one undeniable wave -- so many emails that it 's difficult to work , and impossible to avoid them . <p> Sometimes it 's because our coverage of what is happening in Gaza is too scant , other times it 's because it 's too sterile . The writers tell us that they think the kind of language used by the media is biased or even negligent . While the body of the email is almost always the same , the subject lines vary . They plead with us to " do the right thing " , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " do n't look away " .
