<p> Proceeds will support the the Stay Human School 's day-to-day operations and provide essential resources to the community . <p> Stay Human Collective have announced a fundraising concert for Gaza at Dublin 's Whelan 's , set for April 30 . <p> The lineup will include Sack , Mundy , Brian Brannigan , Alice Jago , Novatone , Stefan Murphy and comedian Kevin Gildea as MC . All proceeds will go to the Stay Human School in Jabalia , Northern Gaza , which provides education , food , water , baby supplies and financial support for the community . Tickets are available now . <p> " Education offers a form of hope in the midst of the trauma and suffering these children have and are enduring , " said spokesperson and concert organiser Moira Cassidy . " The school is basically just four walls in a bombed-out area in Gaza , but since opening in September it has provided not just education , but distraction from the horrors they are enduring .
