They have been triggered by a barrage of missiles , launched from Yemen , purportedly by Ansar Allah , also known as the Houthis . The Israel Defence Forces claim to have intercepted two missiles launched from Yemen , while Israeli emergency services say that at this time , there have been no injuries reported . <p> This threat is only a new one in the most technical of senses . Israel already lives within the range and under the constant threat of missile attack -- from Lebanon , Gaza and the West Bank , from Iran itself and from Iranian proxies in Iraq ( and formerly Syria , before the fall of the Assad regime ) . Iran 's ballistic missile and drone programmes are extremely capable and have absorbed a lot of military spending over the past decade . Iranian deliveries of missiles and drones to its regional proxies have been consistent .
