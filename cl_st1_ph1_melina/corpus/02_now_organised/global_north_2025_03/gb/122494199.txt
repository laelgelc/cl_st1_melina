<h> Evening of poetry in Dorchester to support Palestinians <p> From left to right : Naomi Wilcox-Lee , Peter Roe of The Jawbone Collective , Molly Dunne -- current Bard of Dorchester at the poetry night in Dorchester ( Image : DPSC ) <p> Poets came together in Dorchester to support Palestinians suffering from the war between Israel and Hamas . <p> An evening of poetry in solidarity with Palestine was held at the popular Dorchester venue and record shop , Vinyl Van . <p> The event was organized and hosted by Naomi Wilcox-Lee in partnership with Dorset Palestine Solidarity Campaign ( DPSC ) and brought together twelve local poets who shared their own work , and pieces by Palestinian poets . <p> Among the performers were former and current Bards of Dorchester , Peter Roe and Molly Dunne . Peter presented a selection of poems submitted for a special " Gaza Collection " . <p> Naomi , who is co-chair of Dorset PSC , described the idea behind the event : " We held our first Poems for Palestine event just over a year ago . <p> " Poetry @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ giving voice to our feelings , amplifying the experiences of oppressed peoples , and helping us to imagine a brighter future . <p> " We did all these tonight . It was an emotional and cathartic evening . All our love to you .
