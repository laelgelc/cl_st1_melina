<h> Worcester Cathedral : VE Day to be marked with concert <p> This article is brought to you by our exclusive subscriber partnership with our sister title USA Today , and has been written by our American colleagues . It does not necessarily reflect the view of The Herald . <p> AS wars rage in Ukraine and Gaza , Worcester will be remembering a conflict that was a lot closer to home when the city 's cathedral hosts a concert to celebrate VE Day which brought World War II in Europe to an end in May 1945 . <p> The event on Saturday , May 3 at 7.30pm will feature the Band of the Mercia Regiment with the Oakville Singers from Droitwich and includes a segment inviting audience participation for ' The Songs that Won the War ' . <p> Organiser David Hallmark said : " The programme will contain famous works by ( Edward ) Elgar and ( William ) Walton and provide an opportunity for the local community to reserve 600 seats for a special occasion to remember World War II and its impact on all families at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ukraine and Sudan and the impact on those millions desperate for peace in our time as soon as possible . " <p> The Mercian Regiment band comprises Army Reserve musicians from across the area and from diverse civilian backgrounds . <p> It plays a vital role in regimental , ceremonial and charitable events , taking part in a wide variety of concerts and parades at local , national and international levels . <p> Captain Ian Johnson took on the role of director of music in 2019 following his retirement from the Band of the Scots Guards and under his direction the Band of the Mercian Regiment continues to uphold its reputation for musical excellence representing the Reserve Forces and the wider Mercian community with distinction . <p> Capt Johnson has a wealth of experience from a distinguished military career that has seen him serve both as a musician and a medic . <p> His passion for jazz and contemporary music has influenced his approach to band leadership , adding a dynamic and modern dimension to performances . <p> He said : " The Band of the Mercian Regiment is a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Reserve , balancing their military commitments with civilian careers . <p> " While they are all accomplished musicians , each member brings a wealth of professional expertise from diverse fields , including teaching , law enforcement , commercial insurance underwriting and highly-specialized scientific disciplines such as laser and optical science . <p> " The band is also proud to include a professor of practice in science and innovation , showcasing the remarkable range of skills within its ranks . " <p> The Oakville Singers is a chamber choir based in Droitwich with members drawn from across the South Midlands . <p> It takes its name from Selly Oak and Bournville where the choir began in 1965 under Roger Gay . <p> It has sung at most of the cathedrals in UK and in Europe at Chartres , Brussels , Cologne and Aachen . <p> Ivan Oliver took over as musical director of the choir in 2005 . <p> He is now organist at the parish church of St John the Baptist at Claines , near Worcester . <p> The choir works with schools giving children the opportunity to take part @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Oakville has raised many thousands for charitable causes . Most recently it performed at Coventry Cathedral . <p> Mr Hallmark added : " This occasion provides an opportunity to raise funds for The Worcestershire Soldier At The Commandery project which has the charity number 276510 . <p> " The display in the Worcester City Art Gallery and Museum in Foregate Street since 2001 is to be relocated to The Commandery from 2026 . <p> " The display presents the Worcestershire history of 330 years of military and social legacy to the community . <p> " The project now seeks the final phase of financial support of ? 55,000 and welcomes contributions from individuals and organisations and businesses across Worcestershire .
