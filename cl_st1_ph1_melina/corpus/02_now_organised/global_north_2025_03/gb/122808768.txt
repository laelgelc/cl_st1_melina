Some event or action , often unpredictable , sparks rapid change . <p> Slow and steady has been the SNP 's governing strategy . But now is the time for a sudden leap ; a leap towards independence . I intend to describe how that might be accomplished . <p> When the SNP took over power in Holyrood , the initial strategy was to prove the party to be competent and credible , holding to a left-of-centre position .
