<p> Demonstrations at a global trade union conference in Belfast over the inclusion of an Israeli delegation are aimed at encouraging unions across Europe to put pressure on their governments over Gaza , it has been claimed . <p> Pro-Palestinian protestors have taken to the stage in protest at the Uni Europa Conference , which is taking place at Belfast 's Waterfront Hall and is organised by the European regional body of the UNI Global Union . <p> The three-day conference has attracted around 700 delegates from across the continent . <p> The protestors waved Palestinian flags and chanted slogans including " we are all Palestinians " while on the stage , to oppose the participation of members of Histadrut , which represents trade unions in Israel . <p> Protestors also demonstrated outside the venue with flags and banners . <p> The demonstrations included members of the Trade Union Friends of Palestine group , and the Boycott , Divestment , Sanctions ( BDS ) Belfast organisation . <p> The protests against the Israeli Histradut delegation at the UNI Europa Conference .
