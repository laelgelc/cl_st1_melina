<p> The Trump administration moved this week to deport Mahmoud Khalil , a recent Columbia University graduate of Palestinian heritage who once led campus protests against Israel 's war in Gaza . Critics say Khalil 's arrest is a threat to the First Amendment and to the rights of Americans everywhere . <p> Khalil is a lawful permanent resident -- a green card holder -- who " is n't charged with a crime , " said NPR . The Trump administration instead said he should be deported for protest activities that it " equates with antisemitism and support for terrorism " committed by Hamas . One obstacle , however , is that legal experts say green card holders have free speech rights . The Constitution " does not distinguish between citizens and non-citizens " when it comes to the First Amendment , said Georgetown Law professor David Cole . If the government can not punish a citizen for speech , it also can not " deport a foreign national for their speech .
