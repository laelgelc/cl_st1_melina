<h> This International Women 's Day we pay tribute to the global face of the hostage campaign <p> International Women 's Day ( IWD ) , celebrated each year on March 8 , began in 1911 , came under the auspices of the United Nations in 1975 and since 1996 has been given an annual theme . In the aftermath of October 7 , UN Women , the world 's largest women 's humanitarian organisation , an entity set up to fight gender-based violence , had nothing to say about the atrocities committed against Israeli women and girls that day . <p> It then took seven whole weeks of lobbying by Israeli feminist groups for the UN Women 's executive director Sima Sami Bahous to even acknowledge the depravity , the sexualised torture , women and girls suffered in the Jewish state on October 7 .
