Israel said it had intercepted one rocket , while the other failed to cross the border . <p> Hezbollah said it was not involved . Israel said the attack was a ceasefire " violation " , while the office of Lebanon 's president Joseph Aoun also said Israel 's attack was a " violation of the agreement " . <p> The strike came after the Israeli military issued an evacuation warning for a building in the Hadath area in the Lebanese capital . Videos showed people leaving in panic .
