<p> CAIRO - Egypt is expected to sign a memorandum of understanding with the European Union on previously agreed financing worth 4 billion euros ( $4.3 billion ) in the second half of the year , Planning Minister Rania al-Mashat said on Wednesday . <p> The financing is part of a 7.4 billion euro funding package agreed between Egypt and the EU in March last year , as European states sought to shore up Egypt 's economy and limit future migration across the Mediterranean . <p> Mashat told a press conference that a visiting EU delegation had been involved in talks about the funding with Egyptian authorities since the start of March . She said she expected the financing to be adopted by the bloc 's 27 members in June . <p> Egypt received the first tranche of the package , amounting to 1 billion euros , in January .
