<p> To say that the 2009 premiere of Seven Jewish Children , Caryl Churchill 's play that covered decades of Israeli history in 10 minutes , caused controversy is an understatement . <p> Subtitled A Play for Gaza , it was hastily written in response to Operation Cast Lead , the Israeli military 's incursion into the Palestinian territory shortly after Hamas took power . As many as 1,400 Gazans died , as did 13 Israelis during the three-week conflict . <p> Churchill wrote seven gnomic scenes -- starting with the Russian pogroms of 1903 and the Holocaust , then onto the foundation of Israel and up to the present -- in which Jewish adults debate how much an unnamed and unseen female relative should or should not be told about violence in the world . <p> Each line in the poetical script starts with " Tell her " or " Do n't tell her " . The first scene includes the line " Do n't tell her they 'll kill her " as , we deduce , the family is hiding from persecution .
