<h> Oil prices rise on demand outlook strength , weaker US dollar <p> Oil prices rose on Thursday , boosted by a strong outlook for demand in the United States after fuel inventories fell more than expected , and a weaker U.S. dollar . <p> Brent crude futures rose 43 cents , or 0.6% , to stand at $71.21 a barrel by 0423 GMT , their highest level since March 3 . U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude ( WTI ) gained 38 cents , or 0.6% , to $67.54 . <p> U.S. government data showed a higher-than-expected drawdown last week in distillate inventories , including diesel and heating oil , which fell by 2.8 million barrels , outstripping a drop of 300,000 barrels expected in a Reuters poll . <p> Global oil demand averaged 101.8 million barrels per day ( bpd ) , an annual increase of 1.5 million bpd , the analysts said . <p> U.S. crude inventories , rose 1.7 million barrels , however , exceeding expectations for an increase of 512,000 barrels in an earlier Reuters poll . <p> A weaker greenback also contributed to oil gains @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of February . <p> " Throughout the week , the weakness of the dollar appeared to provide some support for dollar-denominated oil prices , " said Phillip Nova senior market analyst Priyanka Sachdeva . <p> Oil investors stay hopeful of the prospect of the Federal Reserve easing interest rates by 50 basis points by year 's end , she added . <p> Global risk premiums rose after Israel launched a new ground operation on Wednesday in Gaza after breaking a ceasefire of nearly two months . <p> The United States kept up airstrikes on Houthi targets in Yemen in retaliation for the group 's attacks on ships in the Red Sea .
