<h> Close to $1m raised for Gaza during Ramadan : Masagos <p> SINGAPORE -- Close to $1 million has been collected in an ongoing fund-raising campaign for humanitarian relief in Gaza , as contributions flowed in from the Malay/Muslim community and other Singaporeans throughout the month of Ramadan , said Minister-in-charge of Muslim Affairs Masagos Zulkifli . <p> In his Hari Raya Aidilfitri message on March 30 , he thanked the contributors for their generosity towards the campaign conducted by local charity Rahmatan Lil Alamin Foundation ( RLAF ) . <p> The donations will help provide food , medical supplies and essential goods to the people of Gaza . <p> RLAF 's campaign , Aid For Gaza , started on Feb 24 , and will run till April 6 . <p> Israel launched its invasion of Gaza in response to the Oct 7 , 2023 , attack in southern Israel by Palestinian militant group Hamas . Singapore 's humanitarian aid to Gaza has amounted to more than $19 million since then . <p> In it , he also highlighted the Malay/Muslim community 's strong unity in supporting those in need @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Ramadan is the month for the community to reflect , worship and increase their good deeds , he said . <p> " I am glad to see the strong spirit of unity within our community . We come together in acts of worship , strengthen ties and help those in need , " said Mr Masagos . <p> One such effort by a mosque is an initiative called Our Community Pantry at Masjid Al-Iman in Bukit Panjang . <p> The mosque works with community partners to provide food supplies to underprivileged families regardless of race or religion , underscoring the community 's core values of collaboration and helping one another , said Mr Masagos . <p> In 2025 , Hari Raya Aidilfitri , also known as Hari Raya Puasa , falls on March 31 . It marks the first day of Syawal -- the 10th month of the Islamic calendar . <p> " This Syawal , let us preserve and build upon the noble values of compassion , concern and generosity that we embraced during Ramadan , " Mr Masagos said . <p> " Together , we can make @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be proud of -- one that embodies our vision of ' Masyarakat Gemilang , Masa Depan Cemerlang ' , or ' Community of Success , Thriving Future ' .
