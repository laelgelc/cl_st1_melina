<p> Doctors without Borders has accused Israel of using humanitarian aid as a bargaining chip , days after Israeli authorities cut power to the only water desalination plant left in Gaza . Yemen 's Houthi rebels have vowed to resume attacks on Israeli ships travelling through the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden , until shipments of aid are allowed back into Gaza . Meanwhile , mediators are seeking to bridge differences between Israel and Hamas , as fresh rounds of negotiations begin in Doha .
