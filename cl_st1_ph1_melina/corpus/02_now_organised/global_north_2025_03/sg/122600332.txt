<p> At least 70 Palestinians have been killed across Gaza , as Israel relaunched ground operations in one of its deadliest bombing campaigns on the enclave . The renewed offensive , which has entered its third day , marks the end of a two-month ceasefire . Hamas said talks with mediators to stop Israel 's attacks are still ongoing . It added that Israel 's incursion into the Netzarim Corridor , which separates northern Gaza from the south , constitutes a " new and dangerous " violation of the truce .
