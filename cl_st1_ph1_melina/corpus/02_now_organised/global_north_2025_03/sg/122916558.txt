<p> ISLAMABAD : Pakistani rice exporter Waqar Rice Mills has been milling , processing and supplying Pakistani rice to countries in the Gulf for the past four decades . <p> The firm wants to ramp up exports to the region , but access has been limited . <p> Exporters holding face-to-face meetings with importers are limited by visa restrictions imposed on Pakistani visitors by nations including United Arab Emirates , Qatar and Saudi Arabia , said the mill 's director of international sales Mohsin Aziz . <p> The company is among Pakistani firms whose Gulf export ambitions face high interest rates and a lack of loans . <p> " We could achieve it simply if the government , first of all , provides very , very cheap loans . And also , if they could reduce the policy rate back to 3 to 4 per cent where it was back in 2013 to 2018 , " said Aziz . <p> The price of rice is another challenge for Pakistan 's exporters . <p>
