PHOTO : AFP <p> March 20 , 2025 <p> SINGAPORE -- Singapore is gravely concerned about the resumption of hostilities in Gaza , which has resulted in an increasing number of civilian casualties , said a spokesperson for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs ( MFA ) on March 19 . <p> Urging all parties to " resume negotiations towards a permanent ceasefire " , the spokesperson said all civilians must be protected according to international humanitarian law . <p> All hostages should also be released immediately and the delivery of humanitarian supplies to Gaza should not be impeded , the statement added . <p> MFA said a negotiated two-state solution is the only viable path to achieving a " comprehensive , just and durable " solution to the conflict , adding that Singapore will work closely with the Palestinian Authority to enhance its capacity for administration and reconstruction .
