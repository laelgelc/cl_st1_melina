<h> David Parker on what Trump and a whiplash world mean for New Zealand <p> A wide-ranging conversation with the opposition spokesperson on foreign affairs . <p> Even before the second Trump term began , the world was a volatile place . But since January 20 , across eight whiplash weeks , the pace of change has been astonishing . Donald Trump 's America First geopolitics , melding expansionist and isolationist instincts , has created a febrile , volatile atmosphere in foreign affairs . <p> In the coming days , Winston Peters will meet Marco Rubio , the US secretary of state , in the most important engagement between New Zealand and Washington so far in Trump 's second term .
