We have avoided violent conflict in and around the Fujian geopolitical faultline by not , so far , trying to find and impose final tidy solutions . <p> Likewise , to find peace in the world 's current military hotspots , we have to accept and negotiate around the current realities of those situations . Most importantly @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : ' if you are in a hole , stop digging ' .
