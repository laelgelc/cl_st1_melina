<p> The conversation on settler colonialism must not be limited to academic discussion . It is a political reality , clearly demonstrated in the everyday behavior of Israel . <p> Israel is not merely an expansionist regime historically ; it remains actively so today . Additionally , the core of Israeli political discourse , both past and present , revolves around territorial expansion . <p> Frequently , we succumb to the trap of blaming such language on a specific set of right-wing and extremist politicians or on a particular US administration . The truth is vastly different : the Israeli Zionist political discourse , though it may change in style , remains fundamentally unchanged throughout time . <p> Zionist leaders have always associated the establishment and expansion of their state with the ethnic cleansing of the Palestinians , later referred to in Zionist literature as the " transfer . Ultimately , the depopulation of Palestine occurred through force , culminating in the Nakba , the Catastrophe of 1948 . <p> The discourse of the erasure of the Palestinian people has been the shared foundation among all Israeli officials and governments , though it has been expressed in different ways . It has always had a material component , manifesting in the slow but decisive takeover of Palestinian homes in the West Bank , the confiscation of farms , and the constant construction of " military zones .
