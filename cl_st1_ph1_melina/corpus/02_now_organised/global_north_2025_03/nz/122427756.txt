On the north side of that statue a counter rally numbering 500 people was taking place and was opposing what Ms Swarbrick was trying to achieve . The counter-rally stood in solidarity with the people and State of Israel . <p> The land Ms Swarbrick claims that Israel has " an unlawful presence in " is the indigenous , ancestral and historical land of the Jewish people , and not to acknowledge that is biased , racist and unfair .
