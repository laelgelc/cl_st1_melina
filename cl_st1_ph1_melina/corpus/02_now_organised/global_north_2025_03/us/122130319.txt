( credit : NATIONAL PHOTO ARCHIVE/GPO ) <h> A non-signatory <p> The NPT is the primary regulator of nuclear weapons in International Law and was created in the wake of the increasing nuclearisation of the world in the 1960s and initial treaties blocking nuclear testing in 1963 . <p> The treaty divides the world into nuclear-weapon states and non-nuclear-weapon states . Nuclear states are responsible for preventing the creation or proliferation of nuclear weapons among non-nuclear states , while non-nuclear states are required to prevent the creation or proliferation in their own states . <p> Non-nuclear states are required to register all nuclear facilities with the IAEA . Israel is not a signatory to the treaty and thus has not registered itself in either category . <p> Israel initially supported the NPT in 1968 ; however , it withdrew its support following the Soviet invasion of Czechoslovakia , which caused the treaty to be delayed . <p> The only other countries not party to the treaty are India , Pakistan , North Korea @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ only one to have signed and then withdrawn . <p> The development is reported to have caused a rift between then-prime minister David Ben-Gurion and then-US President John F.
