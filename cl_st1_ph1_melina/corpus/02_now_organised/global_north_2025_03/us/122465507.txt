<p> The Purim parade in Jerusalem on Sunday featured the Israeli police band and a yellow ribbon , symbolising hostages held in Gaza amid ongoing conflict . <p> Jerusalem hosted its annual Purim parade on Sunday , celebrating the Jewish holiday that commemorates the biblical story of surviving a plot to exterminate Jews in Persia . <p> The event featured the Israeli police band and participants carrying a giant yellow ribbon symbolising hostages held in Gaza . <p> This year 's Purim observance carried added significance for many , as it followed the 7 October 2023 attacks and the ongoing conflict in Gaza . <p> " It feels like really special ( Purim this year ) because , there 's still like 70 or so ( hostages ) in Gaza and they ca n't celebrate Purim so it 's almost like we 're celebrating for them by setting up the symbol , and by advocating for their release , just to bring them home , " said one of the participants .
