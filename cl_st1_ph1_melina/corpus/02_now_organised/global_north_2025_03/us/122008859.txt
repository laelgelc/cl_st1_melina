<p> Incoming IDF Chief of Staff Lt.-Gen . Eyal Zamir told the high command on Thursday night that 2025 will be a year of war focused on Gaza and Iran , with other fronts also being given attention . <p> He said that he will emphasize the concept of " victory " as an important value , as , in recent years , the IDF had been focused on relative victory or deterrence . <p> Additionally , he has decided that all efforts will be made to return the hostages from Hamas , though no specific policy changes have been named . <p> Moreover , he has decided that a new IDF budgetary framework will be set in the second half of 2025 .
