<p> With Michael Dukakis poised to clinch the Democratic nomination for president in 1988 , Colorado 's Jewish newspaper turned its attention to his wife . " What kind of role model would Kitty Dukakis be for our Jewish children ? " the Intermountain Jewish News asked in an editorial that May . <p> The newspaper 's concern was that Kitty was Jewish and the Massachusetts governor was not -- making their union an example of the intermarriage trend that was accelerating and igniting concern in Jewish communities across the Diaspora . <p> " We 're talking about a woman who married out , who reportedly does not even raise her children exclusively in the Jewish tradition , and who might well be projecting all this from the most visible arena in the world , " the editorial said . <p> In the end , Kitty Dukakis -- who died Sunday at age 88 from complications of dementia -- did not have a chance to set the tone from the White House .
