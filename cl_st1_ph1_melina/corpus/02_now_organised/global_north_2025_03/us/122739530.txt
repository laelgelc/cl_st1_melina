<h> Green Party Candidate Jill Stein Faces Charges for Protest Actions <p> Prosecutors in St. Louis have formally charged former Green Party presidential candidate Jill Stein and one other demonstrator for their actions during a pro-Palestinian protest at Washington University last spring , The St. Louis Post-Dispatch reported . <p> Stein was charged Friday with first-degree trespass and fourth-degree assault ; she has been accused of kicking a police officer and hitting him with a bicycle during a protest last April that resulted in the arrest of more than 100 people , most of them outsiders . <p> According to court documents , Wash U police ordered the demonstrators to disperse , but they refused . When officers moved in to make arrests , Stein linked arms with other protesters .
