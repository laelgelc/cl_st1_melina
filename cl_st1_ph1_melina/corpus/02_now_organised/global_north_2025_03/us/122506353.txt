<h> IDF issues evacuation warnings to residents of the Gaza Strip <p> The deterioration of the ceasefire agreement between Israel and the terrorist organization Hamas culminated in a wave of airstrikes from Israel early Tuesday morning . After the bombing campaign , Israel Defense Forces issued an evacuation warning to residents in several areas of the Gaza Strip , a sign that more airstrikes were imminent . <p> In a post on X , Col. Avichay Adraee , IDF spokesman for the Arab media , highlighted on a map the " dangerous combat zones " from which Gaza residents should immediately evacuate , warning those who remain that doing so " puts your lives and the lives of your family members in danger , " the Times of Israel reported . <p> " The IDF has launched a strong offensive against terror organizations , " Adraee said in a translated version of the evacuation warning posted to X. " These designated areas are considered dangerous combat zones . For your own safety , you must evacuate immediately to known shelters in western Gaza City and in Khan Younis .
