This unique " exorbitant privilege " is what has enabled Washington to run both trade and fiscal deficits without any major drag on the economy . <p> Trump has increasingly realised the importance of protecting this system , threatening 100 percent tariffs and other action against countries that seek to de-dollarise and embrace the Russia and China-backed " BRICS " organisation . <p> Trump today sees his task as not just one of reordering fiscal policy to support US domestic manufacturing , but one of establishing new rules of the international monetary order as well .
