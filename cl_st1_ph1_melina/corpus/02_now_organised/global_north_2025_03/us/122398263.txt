<h> Large protest outside Fox News DC bureau organized on Bluesky <p> A large protest outside Fox News 's Washington , D.C. , bureau on Saturday was organized on Bluesky , a left-leaning alternative to X. <p> The protest , " Media March , " was scheduled to start at noon on Saturday with the tagline , " TRUTH OVER PROPAGANDA . " It was a part of three protests under DESCEND DC , with a protest on the National Mall Friday and one set to be at the Heritage Foundation on Sunday . <p> Protesters were seen arriving in vans . Chanting and horns were heard from inside the building . <p> Signs displaying the messages " Fox News ruined my parents , " " Fox lies America dies " were present , as well as others saying " reject fascism , " " deport Muskkk , " and " free Luigi . " Some protesters held up flags in solidarity with transgender people , Ukraine , and Gaza . <p> Footage from the Media March protest against Fox News in Washington , D.C. <p> In a video reviewed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of protesters were seen marching in front of the building and chanting , " No justice , no peace ! " <p> Bluesky was launched in February 2023 , and gained in popularity among left-leaning activists and politicians during the 2024 campaign cycle . Many critics of President Donald Trump and Elon Musk ditched Musk-owned " free speech platform " X in favor of the new heavily-censored social media platform . <p> Fox News came under criticism in the wake of the 2020 presidential election after the news outlet was sued for $1.6 billion by Dominion Voting Systems for allegedly publishing election misinformation . They settled for $787.5 million . <p> The company 's D.C. bureau is located in the Hall of the States Business Center , which they share with NBC and C-SPAN.
