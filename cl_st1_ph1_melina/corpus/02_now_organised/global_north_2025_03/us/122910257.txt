Until recently , the USRAP helped nearly 100,000 refugees resettle in the United States every year since the enactment of the Refugee Act in 1980 . It is estimated that a total of 3.7 million refugees have been resettled in the U.S. since 1975 . <p> U.S. humanitarian assistance not only helps save the world 's poor and persecuted , it benefits the U.S. in return . Many of the children saved by U.S. humanitarian efforts have since been resettled to the U.S. and now contribute , by serving in our military , working as nurses in our hospitals , teaching in our schools and constructing our roads .
