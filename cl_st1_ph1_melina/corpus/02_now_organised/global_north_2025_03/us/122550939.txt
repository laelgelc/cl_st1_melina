We have been threatened with being " cleaned out " , with mass death , with " all hell breaking loose " on us . <p> The thing is , we have already been through hell . I , like two million other Palestinians in Gaza , survived the genocidal inferno from October 7 , 2023 to January 19 , 2025 . <p> If I have to be honest , I did not survive by hanging on to life . No , I survived by dropping the " f " in life and holding on to " lie " . <p> The more I lied to myself , the more I sustained my fragile existence . <p> I still remember the first lie I told myself . I felt we were going to die one way or another in what was to follow . She felt the same way ; that 's why she was crying . <p> But how can one exist in total acceptance of imminent death ? Human beings by nature want to live . So I started lying to myself again . <p> Soon after , when Israel bombed the Baptist Hospital on October 17 , killing hundreds of people , I lied .
