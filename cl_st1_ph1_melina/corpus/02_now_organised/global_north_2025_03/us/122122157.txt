Dozens of bodies exhumed from mass grave in northern Gaza <p> Efforts to exhume bodies from a mass grave in northern Gaza are ongoing , with 40 bodies retrieved early this week now set to be buried in official cemeteries . <p> Rescue teams in northern Gaza have begun exhuming bodies from a mass grave in northern Gaza , according to the Palestinian news agency Wafa . <p> The remains were buried in a mass grave in Beit Lahia , due to the Israeli siege on the north of the enclave that was imposed at the time of their deaths . <p> On Tuesday , 40 bodies were recovered and will now be reburied in official cemeteries . <p> The war has claimed at least 48,440 lives , according to Gaza 's health ministry , while the Gaza government media office estimates the toll exceeds 61,000 , saying thousands of people who were missing beneath rubble were presumed dead .
