<h> " Stop the Genocide ! " protest in Downtown Albuquerque <p> Over 50 people participated in the " Stop the Genocide ! " protest organized by the Party for Socialism and Liberation . The protesters shut down Central Avenue as they marched , calling for an end to all U.S. aid to Israel .
