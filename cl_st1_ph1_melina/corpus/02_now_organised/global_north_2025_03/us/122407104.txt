<p> ( JTA ) -- Miami Beach Mayor Steven Meiner is pushing to evict an independent theater from its city-owned space and cut its funding after it screened " No Other Land , " an Oscar-winning documentary about Palestinian displacement in the West Bank . <p> Meiner , who is Jewish , is introducing legislation to revoke O Cinema 's lease and withdraw more than $40,000 in city grant funding , describing the film , which has faced criticism from both pro-Israel and pro-Palestian advocates , as antisemitic . The proposal will go before the city commission at its next meeting on Wednesday . <p> " No Other Land " premiered last Friday at O Cinema , which operates out of the Miami Beach Historic City Hall . <p> Several days before the screening , Meiner contacted O Cinema CEO Vivian Marthell , urging her not to show the film . <p> " The City of Miami Beach has one of the highest concentrations of Jewish residents in the United States , " Meiner said in his letter to Marthell .
