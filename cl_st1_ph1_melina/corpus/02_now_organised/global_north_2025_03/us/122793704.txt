<p> A Korean American Columbia University student , who is a legal permanent US resident and has participated in pro-Palestinian protests , sued the administration of President Donald Trump on Monday to prevent her deportation , a court filing showed . <p> Yunseo Chung , 21 , has lived in the US since she was seven , but her legal team was informed two weeks ago that her lawful permanent resident status was being revoked , according to the court filing in the US District Court for the Southern District of New York . <p> The Trump administration says her US presence hinders its foreign policy agenda , according to the lawsuit . <p> Chung has not yet been arrested . Immigration agents have made multiple visits to her residences looking for her . <h> Deporting foreign pro-Palestinian protesters <p> Trump has pledged to deport foreign pro-Palestinian protesters and accused them of supporting Hamas , posing hurdles for US foreign policy and being antisemitic . Human rights advocates have condemned the government 's moves . <p> Columbia protester Mahmoud Khalil , who was arrested this month and is legally challenging his detention , is also a lawful permanent resident . Trump , without evidence , accused Khalil of supporting Hamas , which Khalil denies . <p> Actions against Chung " form part of a larger pattern of attempted US government repression of constitutionally protected protest activity and other forms of speech , " Monday 's lawsuit said . <p> " The government 's repression has focused specifically on university students who speak out in solidarity with Palestinians and who are critical of the Israeli government 's ongoing military campaign in Gaza . " <p> The US Immigration and Customs Enforcement agency had no immediate comment .
