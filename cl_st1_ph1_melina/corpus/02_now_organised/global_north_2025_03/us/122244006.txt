<p> Judges Yitzhak Amit , Noam Sohlberg , and David Mintz instructed the government to address the electricity supply issue within its broader response regarding the suspension of humanitarian aid to Gaza . The decision follows the government 's move to stop selling electricity to the Strip , amid concerns that it may also cut off water supplies . <p> A petition was submitted to the court requesting a temporary injunction , arguing that the decision violates Israel 's humanitarian obligations under international law . The petitioners claim that as an " occupying power , " Israel is required to ensure the provision of basic needs to Gaza 's civilian population . <p> They further contend that Israel is leveraging its control over essential resources to exert political pressure on Hamas , resulting in direct harm to civilians . <p> They also accused Israel of severely restricting humanitarian aid from passing into the Gaza Strip in recent months , including blocking electricity and water .
