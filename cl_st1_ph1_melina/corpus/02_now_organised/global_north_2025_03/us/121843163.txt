<h> Ramadan in Gaza : Ruins and unshakable faith <p> Children play in a neighbourhood decorated for the Muslim fasting month of Ramadan in Khan Younis in the southern Gaza Strip on February 28 , 2025 Eyad Baba/AFP <p> Ramadan has come to devastated Gaza . While the rest of the world embarks with a festive mood on a month of fasting and prayer , we do so with grief and sorrow . <p> The echoes of war still ring loud . There is no certainty that this ceasefire will last . People are anxious about what happens next . They fear the war may come back . <p> The memory and trauma of what we have witnessed and experienced over the past year hang heavy in our minds . <p> Last year was not the first time for us to observe Ramadan during a war .
