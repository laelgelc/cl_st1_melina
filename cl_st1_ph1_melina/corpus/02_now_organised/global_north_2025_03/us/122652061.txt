<h> VCU eliminates its DEI office , following Trump 's order <p> Virginia Commonwealth University has eliminated its office of DEI , in an effort to comply with President Donald Trump 's directive that calls for government agencies to stop all forms of discrimination based on race or gender . <p> VCU 's board of visitors on Friday voted 11-4 to eliminate the Division of Inclusive Excellence , putting at risk the jobs of employees who work directly on initiatives of DEI , or diversity , equity and inclusion .
