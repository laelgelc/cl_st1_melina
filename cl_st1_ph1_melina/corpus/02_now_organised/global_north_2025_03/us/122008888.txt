<h> The official launch event will take place this Friday at 7-10 a.m. , with participants being able to ride independently . <p> Keren Kayemeth LeIsrael -- Jewish National Fund ( KKL-JNF ) announced this week the opening of the Yad Mordechai Forest Singletrack , a new 20-kilometer off-road cycling trail designed for riders with basic skills . This initiative aims to expand leisure and sports opportunities in the region while offering a unique riding experience amid the natural beauty of southern Israel . <p> The official launch event will take place this Friday 7-10 a.m .. Participants will have the opportunity to ride independently , and at 9 a.m. , a festive gathering will be held at the Nabiah Mar'I Active Recreation Campsite . KKL-JNF representatives and guests will deliver remarks , and light refreshments will be served . Participation is free , but registration in advance is required . <p> The new singletrack consists of three distinct loops , designed to offer a safe and enjoyable ride while preserving the surrounding natural environment .
