<p> The Trump administration 's Education Department announced Monday that it 's investigating 60 universities -- including Portland State -- for allegedly failing to protect students from discrimination against Jewish students . <p> The department has criticized universities for tolerating an " explosion of antisemitism " since the start of the ongoing Israel-Hamas war , sparked when Hamas killed 1,200 Israelis in a shock attack on Oct. 7 , 2024 . More than 46,000 Palestinians have died in the war since then , leading to widespread protests at college campuses across the U.S. , including at PSU , in response to Israel 's offensive in Gaza . <p> The department 's Office for Civil Rights warned the schools listed -- which includes PSU -- of " potential enforcement actions , " with Education Secretary Linda McMahon saying in a statement that " public investments funded by U.S. taxpayers ( are ) contingent on scrupulous adherence to federal antidiscrimination laws .
