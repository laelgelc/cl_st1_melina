<p> After converting to Islam in high school , David Hunsicker , who grew up in Heidelberg Township , wondered how the Lehigh Valley Muslim community would grow . <p> But the community he wanted and needed -- Al-Maqasid -- formed in 2012 , and in 2017 moved to its campus in Lower Macungie Township , just a short drive from his family 's home . <p> " Never in a million years did I think that some of the premier scholars of North America were going to decide to establish a seminary here in what was my backyard , " said Hunsicker , who is now an enthusiastic fan of Al-Maqasid 's community and educational programming , which came together Friday night in the weekly iftar meal during Ramadan . <p> The iftar is the evening meal where Muslims break their fast during the monthlong celebration of Ramadan -- a " celebration , " Hunsicker said . Some people spend iftar at home with families , but at Friday 's Al-Maqasid event , over 100 members of the organization gathered to pray , eat and play .
