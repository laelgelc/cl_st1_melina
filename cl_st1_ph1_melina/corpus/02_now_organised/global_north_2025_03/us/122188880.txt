In a joint letter Thursday , the same day as the webinar , the ADL , the Academic Engagement Network , Hillel International , the American Jewish Committee and the Jewish Federations of North America condemned the event 's use of this term . <p> " Language used in the event 's description -- including ' scholasticide ' and ' exterminationist ' -- suggests the adoption and promotion of a one-sided and inflammatory narrative which deviates from the mission of the AAUP , " the letter said .
