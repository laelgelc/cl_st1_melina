<h> UIS , Israel Backed Aid Group Begins Gaza Operations <p> A controversial new aid distribution group backed by the US and Israel has begun working in Gaza . <p> The Gaza Humanitarian Foundation said lorry loads of food had been delivered to secure sites on Monday and that distribution had begun . Hundreds of Palestinians collected food parcels from a site in southern city of Rafah on Tuesday . <p> The GHF , which uses armed American security contractors , aims to bypass the UN as the main supplier of aid to the 2.1 million people in Gaza , where experts have warned of a looming famine after an 11-week Israeli blockade that was recently eased . <p> A UN spokesman said the operation was a " distraction from what is actually needed " and urged Israel to reopen all crossings . <p> The UN and many aid groups have refused to co-operate with GHF 's plans , which they say contradict humanitarian principles and appear to " weaponise aid " . <p> They have warned that the system will practically exclude those with mobility issues , force further @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ aid conditional on political and military aims , and set an unacceptable precedent for aid delivery around the world . <p> Israel says an alternative to the current aid system is needed to stop Hamas stealing aid , which the group denies doing . <p> In a statement sent to journalists on Monday night , GHF announced that it had " commenced operations in Gaza " and delivered " truck loads of food to its Secure Distribution Sites , where distribution to the Gazan people began " . <p> " More trucks with aid will be delivered on Tuesday , with the flow of aid increasing each day , " it added . <p> Handout photos showed three lorries laden with pallets of supplies at an unspecified location and just over a dozen men carrying away boxes .
