<h> Israeli air strike kills Hamas leader Mohammed Sinwar <p> Netanyahu confirmed the development on Wednesday while briefing the Knesset , Israeli parliament . <p> " We changed the face of the Middle East , we pushed the terrorists from our territories , we entered the Gaza Strip with force , we eliminated tens of thousands of terrorists , we eliminated ( Mohammad ) Deif , ( Ismail ) Haniyeh , Yahya Sinwar and Mohammed Sinwar , " he told lawmakers . <p> Sinwar was a brother to Yahya , former Hamas leader . He became the group 's de facto leader after the Israeli military killed Yahya last October . <p> Advertisement <p> The strike is the latest in a string of killings that have eliminated a handful of Hamas senior officials . <p> Sinwar was one of Israel 's most wanted targets . He was the focus of an Israeli strike on a hospital in southern Gaza earlier this month . <p> Last week , Netanyahu said it was likely the Hamas leader had been killed .
