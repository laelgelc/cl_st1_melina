<h> Rescuing northern Nigeria from terrorism <p> THE spike in terrorism in the North , especially in the North-East and North-Central , is alarming . Boko Haram and ISWAP have become deadlier . They now use drones and improvised explosive devices in their attacks . In all this , there appears to be no immediate solution in sight . <p> Nigeria has been under siege from Boko Haram forces since 2009 ; Fulani herdsmen 's rapine and banditry aggravate the situation . <p> In recent times , these terrorists have killed thousands of innocent Nigerians . In its 2024 Crime Experience and Security Perception Survey , the NBS reported that 614,937 people were killed in Nigeria between May 2023 and April 2024 . <p> Among them were the over 140 victims of the Christmas 2023 killings in Bokkos , Mangu and Barkin Ladi villages in Plateau State . A month later , the invaders massacred 30 other persons in the three LGAs . <p> SBM Intelligence reported that over 2,300 people were killed in the first quarter of 2024 . It recorded 706 deaths within the period . The North-West followed with 552 deaths while the North-Central recorded 273 deaths . The South-South , South-West and South-East recorded 185 , 163 and 121 deaths , respectively . <p> Borno State , which is the epicentre of the Boko Haram Islamic insurgency , is worse off . With 515 reported killings within the period under review , the state tops the list of terrorism-affected states . In January , ISWAP terrorists killed 20 soldiers and 40 farmers in the state . <p> As Governor Babagana Zulum of Borno put it recently , terrorists now attack and kidnap people almost daily in many communities . <p> April was particularly traumatic for the people of Plateau and Benue . Though the Nigerian Air Force has intervened on some occasions , it is time it fully collaborates with the Army in prosecuting the war . <p> In 2021 , the government of President Muhammadu Buhari acquired some A-29 Tucano fighter jets to help in fighting insurgency .
