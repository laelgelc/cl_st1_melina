His assertions , which smack of denominational jealousy , were products of political mischief taken too far . <p> To say that " You know that going to see the Pope is just a matter of your lobby . How well you can lobby , how many friends you have out there in the Vatican that can lobby for you ... probably with the correct inducement , " is one of the most irresponsible things of anyone to say about the Vatican .
