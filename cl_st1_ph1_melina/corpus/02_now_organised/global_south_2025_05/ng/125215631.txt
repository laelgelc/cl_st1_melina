<h> Hamas leader , Mohammed Sinwar killed in recent airstrike -- Netanyahu <p> The strike marks another high-profile assassination in Israel 's ongoing campaign targeting Hamas leadership . Despite such hits , the militant group maintains a strong grip on power in the Gaza Strip . <p> Mohammed Sinwar was the brother of Yahya Sinwar , the former Hamas leader who was killed by Israeli forces in southern Gaza last October . <p> Netanyahu made the announcement during a speech in the Knesset , Israel 's parliament . <p> " We changed the face of the Middle East , " he said . " We pushed the terrorists from our territories , we entered the Gaza Strip with force , we eliminated tens of thousands of terrorists , we eliminated ( Mohammad ) Deif , ( Ismail ) Haniyeh , Yahya Sinwar and Mohammed Sinwar . " <p> The Israeli army previously released a video allegedly showing Mohammed Sinwar traveling in a car through a tunnel near the Erez crossing , close to the Israel-Gaza border , on December 17 , 2023.
