<h> The rubble called Gaza <p> It has been 19 months since the war on Gaza reconvened and , in that time , deaths of more than 53,000 Palestinians have been documented . This bars those who were buried without being registered or those who went missing beneath rubble . <p> At least 94% of all hospitals in the Gaza Strip have been damaged or destroyed . Almost 90% of all school buildings have been smashed up and rendered inoperative . Even if some of the schools are operational , there are barely any children to attend them because Israel has killed at least 17,000 Palestinian children . <p> Yet , despite all that brutality , Israel has still found room to commit even graver acts of violence .
