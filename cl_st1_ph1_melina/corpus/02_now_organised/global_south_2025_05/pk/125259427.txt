<h> Education Blockade <p> U.S. President Donald Trump 's decision to halt new visa appointments for students and exchange visitors marks yet another move in the country 's steady drift toward isolationism . While the suspension is described as temporary , U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has made it clear that the pause is aimed at expanding social media screening of applicants -- an escalation that aligns with broader efforts to suppress dissent within American academic institutions . <p> This policy 's intent and impact are plain to see . It effectively targets students who have expressed solidarity with Palestine or taken public positions critical of U.S. foreign policy . By keeping such voices out of the country , the American state strengthens its control over the domestic narrative and shields itself from the rising global condemnation of its ally , Israel . As the slaughter in Gaza continues with tacit Western support , access to U.S. universities -- long seen as centres of intellectual openness -- is now being conditioned on political silence or ideological conformity .
