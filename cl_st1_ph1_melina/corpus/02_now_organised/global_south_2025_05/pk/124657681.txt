<h> WHO says 57 children died from effects of malnutrition in Gaza since March 2 <p> At least 57 children in Gaza have died from the effects of malnutrition since the start of the aid blockade on March 2 , the World Health Organization ( WHO ) said Tuesday , citing the Palestinian Health Ministry . <p> Speaking at a UN press briefing in Geneva , Richard Peeperkorn , WHO representative for the West Bank and Gaza , described the situation as " one of the world 's worst hunger crises , " driven by the " deliberate obstruction " of humanitarian aid , including food and medical supplies . <p> The latest analysis by the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification ( IPC ) showed the entire population of Gaza -- some 2.1 million people -- is at " critical risk of famine , " after 19 months of war , displacement , and aid restrictions . <p> From April 1 to May 10 , 93% of the population , 1.95 million Gazans , was classified in IPC Phase 3 or above , meaning " crisis or worse .
