<p> LAHORE:The Punjab government , under the leadership of Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz , has taken several revolutionary measures for the welfare of the working class . He praised Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz 's measures taken for the welfare of working class . " The protection of the rights of workers is being ensured perfectly in Punjab " . <p> He prayed for the end of atrocities on Muslims in Gaza , Palestine and Kashmir .
