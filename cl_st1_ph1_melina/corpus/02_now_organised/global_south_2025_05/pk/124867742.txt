<h> Austria 's JJ wins Eurovision 2025 with opera-techno fusion <p> BASEL - Austria 's JJ won the 2025 Eurovision Song Contest on Sunday , with his operatic song " Wasted Love " triumphing at the world 's biggest live music television event . After votes from national juries around Europe and viewers from across the continent and beyond , JJ gave Austria its first victory since bearded drag performer Conchita Wurst 's 2014 triumph . After the nail-biting drama as the votes were revealed running into Sunday morning , Austria finished with 436 points , ahead of Israel -- whose participation drew protests -- on 357 and Estonia on 356 . " Thank you to you , Europe , for making my dreams come true , " 24-year-old countertenor JJ , whose real name is Johannes Pietsch , said after his triumph in the Swiss city of Basel . " Love is the strongest force in the world . Let 's spread more love , " the Austrian-Filipino singer said . " Wasted Love " saw him hit the high notes while mixing opera and techno .
