None of the threats is larger than the loss of glaciers in the Upper Indus Basin ( UIB ) , deemed the " third pole " of the planet , as it holds the largest concentration of ice after the north and south poles . Modi 's gambit to take war to the globe 's third-largest concentration of ice is madness . <p> Water played no role in the Pahalgam incident . But the unprovoked water threat by Modi will bring the headwaters of the Indus , Jhelum , and Chenab rivers potentially to war in perilous proximity to the UIB glaciers .
