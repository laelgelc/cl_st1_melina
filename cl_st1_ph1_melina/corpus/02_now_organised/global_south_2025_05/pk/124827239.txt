<h> US embassy in Tripoli denies claims of Palestinian relocation to Libya <p> May 18 , 2025 <p> TRIPOLI -- The US Embassy in Libya has strongly denied media reports claiming that Washington is planning to relocate Palestinians from Gaza to Libya . In a statement posted on X , the embassy said , " The report of alleged plans to relocate Gazans to Libya is untrue . " <p> This clarification came after NBC News reported that the Trump administration was working on a controversial proposal to resettle up to one million Palestinians from Gaza to Libya . The report cited unnamed sources , including two individuals with direct knowledge and a former US official . <p> However , the embassy 's statement firmly rejected any such relocation plans . Meanwhile , Libya 's internationally recognized Government of National Unity has not issued an official comment on the matter . The report has stirred concern in the region , particularly among Palestinians . <p> Palestinians have long opposed any idea of being forced to leave Gaza . Many compare such proposals to the 1948 Nakba , when hundreds of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ argue that forced relocation would amount to ethnic cleansing . <p> Trump has previously floated similar ideas , suggesting countries like Egypt and Jordan could accept Palestinian refugees . Those proposals were quickly rejected by both nations , as well as by the United Nations and several Arab states , who condemned them as unjust and inhumane . <p> During a recent visit to Qatar , Trump repeated his interest in taking control of Gaza and turning it into a " freedom zone . " He also said he hoped to see Gaza transformed into the " Riviera of the Middle East , " sparking further backlash from Palestinian leaders and human rights groups .
