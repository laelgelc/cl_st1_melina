<h> Continued Silence <p> It seems perverse that the nations of the world -- from the moralising states of the European Union to the affluent Arab powers of the Middle East , as well as numerous governments across North Africa and South America -- have chosen silence over the bombing of an aid flotilla bound for Palestine , a vessel on which renowned activist Greta Thunberg was present . Yet , these same governments are swift to condemn the attacks carried out by Houthi rebels on commercial shipping in the Red Sea . Where is the logical consistency ? Where is the moral clarity in their positions ? These two incidents make it abundantly clear that neither exists . <p> Israel 's cabinet has openly approved what it calls a plan for Gaza 's " conquest " -- a chilling term that now accompanies calls for what amounts to ethnic cleansing and the forced removal of the population from the Gaza Strip . Aid convoys are targeted , starvation is used as a weapon , and attacks on Yemen , Lebanon , and Syria continue with impunity .
