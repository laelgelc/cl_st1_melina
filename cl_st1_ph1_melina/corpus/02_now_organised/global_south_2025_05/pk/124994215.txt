<h> Growing Israeli aggression <p> A few weeks ago , Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu declared a new ground offensive in Gaza . Since then , the world has witnessed a devastating escalation in violence -- fresh airstrikes , razing of civilian infrastructure , and indiscriminate killing of Palestinians in a besieged territory where safe zones simply do not exist . Hundreds have perished , many of them women and children . <p> After months of global silence and diplomatic fence-sitting , major players like the UK , France and Canada have finally voiced support for Gaza . But sympathy , though necessary , is not enough . The European Union 's decision to review trade ties with Israel signals a long-overdue shift . <p> Economic pressure -- not diplomatic platitudes -- is what Tel Aviv understands . That pressure must intensify . Because yesterday , Israeli forces fired at a diplomatic delegation near the Jenin refugee camp in the occupied West Bank . It is time to stop dressing up genocide as self-defence . Arms sales to Israel must end . Sanctions must begin .
