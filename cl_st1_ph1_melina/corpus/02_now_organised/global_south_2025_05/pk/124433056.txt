<p> After an attack that killed 26 civilians in Pahalgam , in Indian Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir ( IIOJK ) on April 22 , online users began circulating video clips claiming they show recent developments following heightened tensions between India and Pakistan . <p> The claims are false . The viral videos predate the April 22 attack . <h> Claim <p> Indian social media accounts are sharing a 32-second video clip with the logo of the Qatar-based channel Al Jazeera , alleging that the news outlet reported that the Indian army had staged an attack in Pakistan 's city of Sialkot on April 30 . <h> Fact <p> The claim is false . Geo Fact Check 's investigation found that the video is unrelated to Pakistan and is actually from Gaza . It was first posted following an Israeli airstrike on the Indonesian Hospital in Gaza on November 9 , 2023 . The footage was reported by Al Jazeera English ( timestamp 5:06 ) here and by AFPhereon the same day . <h> Claim <p> A 39-second video circulating on X ( formerly Twitter ) claims to show Indian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ coastline . <h> Fact <p> This claim is also false . The video is from Russia , not Pakistan . It was first posted on April 28 , 2020 , by the official YouTube channel of the Russian Ministry of Defense . The footage shows a training exercise by Russian air forces over the Baltic Sea . The original video can be viewed here . <p> Verdict : The clips circulating online are unrelated to the April 22 attack in Pahalgam . One is from Gaza , filmed in November 2023 , and the other shows a 2020 Russian military drill over the Baltic Sea .
