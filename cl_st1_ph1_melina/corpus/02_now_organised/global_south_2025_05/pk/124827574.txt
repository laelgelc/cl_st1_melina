With each passing day , India finds itself increasingly isolated , abandoned by allies now preoccupied with their own affairs , and forced to confront the failure of its foreign policy . At present , the only government openly expressing solidarity with India is the Taliban regime in Afghanistan , whose foreign minister recently conveyed condolences to his Indian counterpart in a rare and ironic gesture of support . <p> That irony has not been lost on political commentators around the world . The BJP , India 's ruling party , has long vilified Muslims -- both within its borders and beyond -- using " Taliban " as a slur to paint them as extremists or terrorists . And yet , here is the same Indian government grasping the hand of the very group it once reviled , not out of principle , but out of geopolitical desperation . <p> This episode lays bare the hollowness of India 's claimed moral high ground .
