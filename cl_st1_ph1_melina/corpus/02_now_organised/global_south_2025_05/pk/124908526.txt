Since the Palestinian group Hamas attacked Israel on October 7 , 2023 , Israel 's retaliatory offensive in Gaza has ravaged large swathes of the Palestinian territory and killed tens of thousands of people . <p> Israel has vowed to " take control of all " the besieged territory of more than two million inhabitants , where United Nations agencies have warned of famine following Israel 's implementation of a two-month total blockade . <p> Aid started to trickle into the Gaza Strip on Monday , following widespread condemnation of the siege .
