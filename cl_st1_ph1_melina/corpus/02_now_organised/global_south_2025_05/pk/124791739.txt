Hunger and malnutrition are spreading faster than our ability to respond , yet globally , a third of all food produced is lost or wasted , " he said . <p> Conflict and violence were the primary drivers in 20 countries and territories , where 140 million people faced acute hunger , the report found . <p> Extreme weather was to blame in 18 countries and " economic shocks " in 15 nations , together affecting a total of 155 million people . <p> The deteriorating situations in Gaza , Myanmar and Sudan outweighed improvements in Afghanistan and Kenya . <h> ' Failure of humanity ' <p> The report warned that the outlook was " bleak " for 2025 as as major donor countries have substantially reduced humanitarian funding . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is a failure of humanity , " Guterres said . <p> " Hunger in the 21st century is indefensible .
