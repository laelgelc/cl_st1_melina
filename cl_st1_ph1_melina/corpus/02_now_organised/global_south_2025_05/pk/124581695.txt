" Nevertheless , it is urgently necessary that Hamas is disarmed and that it can no longer have military control over Gaza . " <p> He said that Germany would do whatever it takes to guarantee Israel 's security , but this does not mean that his country can not criticise Israel 's course of action , adding that this " must not lead to antisemitism . " <p> " I am not sure whether all of Israel 's strategic goals can be achieved in this way ( through a military campaign ) and whether this will serve Israel 's security in the long term , " Wadephul said .
