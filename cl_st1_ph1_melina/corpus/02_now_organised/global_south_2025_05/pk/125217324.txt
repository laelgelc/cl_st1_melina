<h> Crime of Genocide <p> In legal terms , what we are witnessing today is genocide . In real , immediate , and live-streamed terms , it is genocide unfolding before our eyes -- arguably more visible and sustained than any the world has seen in recent history , even more openly conducted than the horrors of the Holocaust . <p> First , the act -- what legal scholars call actus reus . The evidence is overwhelming . Israel has reduced Gaza to rubble through indiscriminate and disproportionate bombing . Civilian infrastructure -- hospitals , schools , homes -- has been obliterated . The scale of suffering is staggering , with thousands dead and many more wounded . Beyond the bombs , Israel is using siege tactics to starve the population -- cutting off aid , medicine , water , and food , weaponising basic human needs as tools of destruction . These are not acts of war ; they are instruments of extermination . <p> The most chilling images emerged just yesterday : Palestinians fenced in , corralled into camps where food is thrown at them as if they @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the Holocaust -- they are the construction of modern-day concentration camps , where Gaza , already described by many as the largest open-air prison , is now being carved into smaller , more controlled pockets of suffering . <p> Second comes the mens rea -- the intention to commit genocide . That too is now in plain sight . At Israel 's annual Flag March in Jerusalem , slogans of " Death to Arabs " and calls to burn villages were chanted openly . Crowds marched through Muslim and Christian quarters , harassing civilians and demanding the expulsion of Palestinians . These were not isolated extremists -- they were a reflection of a vast majority political and social consensus rooted in eliminationist rhetoric . <p> If this is not intent , then the very concept of genocide loses all meaning . The world can not claim ignorance . It is happening in full view , and history will remember not only those who committed these crimes -- but also those who stood by in silence .
