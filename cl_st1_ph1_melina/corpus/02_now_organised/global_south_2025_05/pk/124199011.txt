Unfortunately , there is little meaningful debate about what these increases are meant to achieve -- just a vague hope that they will somehow improve traditional military capabilities . Likewise , we must strive for fairer economic relations between the Global North and the Global South . <p> On 1 May , International Labour Day is celebrated in around 150 countries , though not in the USA and Canada , where similar celebrations take place on the first Monday in September . In over 80 countries , including Pakistan , 1 May is a public holiday . The day honours the vital contributions of workers worldwide and raises awareness of labour rights , fair wages , and decent working conditions . Because so many issues still need addressing , the day is often marked by rallies and demonstrations , typically organised by labour unions and left-leaning political parties such as the Labour Party , Social Democrats , and Socialist parties in Western Europe . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and other social gatherings .
