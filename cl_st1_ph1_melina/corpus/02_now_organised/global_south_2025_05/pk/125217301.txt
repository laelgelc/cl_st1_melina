<h> NA human rights body to probe alleged drone strike in Waziristan <p> ISLAMABAD - The National Assembly Standing Committee on Human Rights on Tuesday expressed serious concern over the killing of four family members in an alleged drone strike in Hurmuz , Mir Ali , North Waziristan on 19 May , and announced the formation of a sub-committee to investigate the incident . <p> The meeting , chaired by Sahibzada Muhammad Hamid Raza at the Ministry of Human Rights , was told that residents of the area were still protesting with the victims ' bodies , demanding justice . The matter was raised by PTI lawmaker Sher Afzal Marwat . <p> A four-member sub-committee comprising Shahida Rahmani , Sher Afzal Marwat , Ali Muhammad Khan , and Zeb Jafar was constituted to visit the area and submit a report . <p> Meanwhile , the committee reviewed the escalating human trafficking crisis , with an estimated 10,000 Pakistanis reportedly trapped in the forests of Cambodia and Burma . Additional Director General Abbas Ahsan said three major boat accidents involving Pakistani migrants had occurred this year . He added that disciplinary action had been taken against 12 FIA officials , including four in Grade-20 , for alleged involvement or negligence . <p> The FIA also reported freezing over Rs720 million in accounts linked to trafficking agents , many held under the names of relatives .
