<h> UN chief ' alarmed ' over Israeli plans to deepen Gaza occupation <p> UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres on Monday conveyed his concern over reports of a planned expansion of Israel 's occupation in Gaza , warning of more deaths of civilians and more destruction . <p> " I can tell you that the secretary-general is alarmed by these reports of Israeli plans to expand ground operations and prolong its military presence in Gaza , " his spokesperson Farhan Haq told a news conference . <p> Stressing that Israel 's reported plans " will inevitably lead to countless more civilians killed and the further destruction of Gaza , " he added : " What 's imperative now is an end to the violence , not more civilian deaths and destruction . " <p> " Gaza is , and must remain , an integral part of a future Palestinian state , " he said , reiterating Guterres ' call for an immediate and permanent ceasefire as well as the release of all hostages . <p> Earlier in the day , Israel 's Security Cabinet unanimously approved a plan to expand its @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ inside the enclave . <p> In a statement , Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's office said the Cabinet approved " the operational plan " submitted by army chief Eyal Zamir to " defeat ( Palestinian group ) Hamas " and for the return of Israeli captives held in Gaza . <p> According to the statement , the plan requires the army " to conquer Gaza and hold the territory under its control .
