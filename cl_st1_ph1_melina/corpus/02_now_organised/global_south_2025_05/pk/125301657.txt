<h> White House says Israel signed off on latest Gaza ceasefire proposal <p> WASHINGTON - Israel signed off on the latest US ceasefire proposal for Gaza before it was sent to the Palestinian Hamas group , White House spokeswoman Karoline Leavitt says . US President Donald Trump and special envoy Steve Witkoff submitted the proposal to Hamas after Israel signed off on it , she says . Leavitt appears to be referring to the meeting Witkoff had on Tuesday with visiting Strategic Affairs Minister Ron Dermer , where the two went over the latest US proposal . " I can also confirm that those discussions are continuing , and we hope that a ceasefire in Gaza will take place so we can return all of the hostages home , " Leavitt says at a briefing . Asked if Hamas had accepted the proposal , she responds , " Not to my knowledge .
