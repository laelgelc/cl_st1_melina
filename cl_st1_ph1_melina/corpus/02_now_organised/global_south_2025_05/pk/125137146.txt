<h> PM Shehbaz offers dialogue with India on water , trade , and terrorism <p> May 26 , 2025 <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has stated that Pakistan is open to resolving disputes with India through peaceful talks . During a press conference in Tehran , he stressed that Pakistan wants dialogue on key issues like water sharing , trade , and counter-terrorism . However , he warned that any act of aggression would be met with a strong response . <p> The prime minister made these remarks after meeting Iranian President Dr. Masoud Pezeshkian . He described the visit as heartfelt , calling Iran Pakistan 's " second home . " Upon arrival , Shehbaz received a guard of honor and held bilateral talks covering regional security and mutual cooperation in trade and investment . <p> Shehbaz Sharif thanked the Iranian president for expressing concern over regional tensions and showing support for the Pakistani people . He praised Pakistan 's military for its bravery and reaffirmed that Pakistan remains committed to peace but will defend itself if threatened .
