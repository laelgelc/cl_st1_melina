<h> Italy demands Israel stops strikes , blasts expulsions of Gazans <p> Italy 's foreign minister on Wednesday again urged Israel to stop its strikes on Gaza , while warning that expelling Palestinians from the territory " is not and never will be an acceptable option . " " The legitimate reaction of the Israeli government to a terrible and senseless terrorist act has unfortunately taken on absolutely tragic and unacceptable forms , that we ask Israel to stop immediately , " Antonion Tajani told parliament , referring also to Hamas 's October 7 , 2023 , attack on Israel . " The bombings must stop , humanitarian assistance must resume as soon as possible , respect for international humanitarian law must be restored , " he said . " Hamas must immediately free all the hostages which are still today in its in hands , and who have the right to return to their homes . " Tajani also condemned US President Donald Trump 's plan for US control of Gaza and the forced displacement of the Palestinians living there .
