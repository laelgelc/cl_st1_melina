<p> After ending in a draw on the battlefield , the epic Indo-Pak tussle has moved to the battlefield of peaceful diplomacy , where it belonged from day one . The foes are now hoping for a clear win there . <p> A draw often seems like a loss to the stronger side and a win to the upcoming side . In starting a war , India may have hoped to inflict one-sided , humiliating losses on Pakistan before global umpires intervened , fearing nuclear escalation . It would have wished to avenge the huge loss of face caused by the Pahalgam terrorist attack both at home and globally and to ensure deterrence in the future . It clearly failed to land the knock-out punch that could help it regain ' honour ' .
