<h> Descent into Hell <p> The only difference between Auschwitz and Gaza is that the world still has a chance to stop the genocide in Gaza . The images emerging over the past two nights are harrowing -- scenes of horror , devastation , and inhuman cruelty . Gaza is a strip of land turned to dust , sealed off from food and water , where children waste away like shadows , their bodies skeletal , their cries unheard . Entire families lie buried under the ruins of their homes . And still , the bombs keep falling -- relentless , calculated , and backed by much of the world . <p> The rage this has provoked is no longer simmering -- it has boiled over . A world that allows such a genocide to unfold in real time , without intervention , is a world teetering on moral collapse .
