<p> The United Nations has issued a dire warning : up to 14,000 babies in Gaza could die within 48 hours unless immediate humanitarian aid is allowed into the region . UN humanitarian chief Tom Fletcher described the situation as " catastrophic , " highlighting that the delivery of just five aid trucks earlier this week is grossly inadequate compared to the needs of Gaza 's 2.3 million residents . <p> The World Health Organization ( WHO ) further emphasized the gravity of the crisis , stating that more than 2 million people in Gaza are facing starvation . WHO Director-General Dr. Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus reported that tonnes of food aid are blocked at the border , while people inside Gaza are starving . <p> International condemnation of Israel 's blockade and military actions continues to mount .
