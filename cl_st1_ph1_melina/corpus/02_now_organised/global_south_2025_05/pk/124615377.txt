<p> Beijing - The Pakistan Embassy in Beijing and the Pakistan Women 's Association of Beijing ( PWAB ) , led by Ms. Marium Mahmood , spouse of Ambassador Khalil Hashmi , jointly organized a fundraiser titled ' Dil Se Pakistan ' at the Embassy to support children suffering from Thalassemia . <p> The event witnessed generous contributions from sponsors and was attended by members of the Chinese community and diplomatic corps . Addressing the gathering , Ambassador Hashmi appreciated PWAB 's efforts and acknowledged the support of donors in aiding children battling the chronic blood disorder . <p> The ambassador also touched upon pressing global and regional issues , highlighting violations of international law , particularly the killing of civilians in Gaza and India 's unprovoked aggression against Pakistan . He condemned the disinformation campaigns and baseless accusations leveled by India .
