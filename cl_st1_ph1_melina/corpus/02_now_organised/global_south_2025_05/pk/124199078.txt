<h> The Ongoing Tragedy in Gaza <p> October 7 , 2023 , marked a devastating chapter in modern history when Hamas launched a surprise attack on an Israeli music festival , killing 1,140 people and taking 240 hostages . In retaliation , Israel vowed to eliminate Hamas . However , the response has escalated into what many now describe as a campaign of ethnic cleansing , turning Gaza into a nightmare of destruction and despair . <p> This devastation is not accidental -- it is systematic . Over 52,000 Palestinians have been killed since October 7 , many of them women and children sheltering in residential areas . Schools , hospitals , and refugee camps have not been spared . The long-standing Israeli blockade has only worsened the humanitarian crisis , cutting off access to basic necessities like food , water , medicine , and electricity . <p> Despite the enormity of the tragedy , the international response remains disheartening .
