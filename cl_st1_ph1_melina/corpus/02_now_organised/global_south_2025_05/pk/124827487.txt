<h> Iran 's Khamanei says US ' must and will leave ' region <p> Tehran - Iran 's supreme leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei said Saturday that the United States " must and will leave " the region , following a visit to Gulf Arab states by President Donald Trump . " With the determination of the region 's nations , America must and will leave this region , " Khamenei said in a televised address , criticising what he called a failed US model aimed at keeping Gulf states dependent on US support . The remarks from the Iranian supreme leader come a day after Trump concluded a high-profile Gulf tour aimed at strengthening alliances and securing multi-trillion dollar investments . On his tour , Trump condemned Iran 's leadership for its role in the region and threatened to cut Iran 's oil exports to zero .
