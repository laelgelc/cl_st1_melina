<h> Unilateral Declaration of Illegality <p> It is deeply ironic that India has unilaterally suspended the Indus Water Treaty following the tragic killing of tourists in Indian-occupied Jammu and Kashmir . The treaty , signed in 1960 , has long governed the distribution of river waters between India and Pakistan . This move amounts to a Unilateral Declaration of Illegality and is a shocking breach of an internationally recognised agreement . <p> Such an action is unbecoming of a country that claims to uphold democratic and secular values . India 's decision sets a troubling precedent , undermining the spirit of international cooperation and mutual respect in bilateral agreements .
