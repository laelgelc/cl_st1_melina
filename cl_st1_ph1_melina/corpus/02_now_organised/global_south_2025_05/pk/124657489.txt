<h> Deals and Diplomacy <p> US President Donald Trump should have arrived in Saudi Arabia after managing to end genocide of Palestinians by Israel . Still , he chose to visit the Gulf , and the toothless Arab leaders had little choice but to welcome the Israeli ally with grand investment deals ready like dishes on a table . <p> Trump began a four-day tour across the Gulf with business on his mind , not regional crises like Gaza or tensions with Iran . Accompanied by powerful business leaders , including Tesla 's Elon Musk , he landed in Riyadh to attend the Saudi-US Investment Forum . <p> He is also visiting Qatar and the UAE , skipping Israel for now . But it does not make any difference to Israel as the bully state knows that the Trump-led America will toe its policies , not matter how much bloodshed they entail . <p> At the forum , Saudi officials spoke proudly of stronger ties with the US beyond just oil , highlighting plans for vast investments . Trump 's main focus remains business : arms deals worth over $100 billion and investments possibly reaching a trillion dollars . <p> Despite Trump 's hopes of expanding Israel 's ties with Arab countries under the Abraham Accords , real progress seems far off due to Israel 's hard stance on Gaza and Palestine .
