<h> Pakistan expresses solidarity with Palestine at ICJ <p> The Hague - Pakistan 's Ambassador to the Netherlands Syed Haider Shah has stressed the need for providing unrestricted humanitarian access to Gaza . He made this oral statement before the International Court of Justice in The Hague on Thursday . The statement was made in the public hearing on the request for an advisory opinion on the Obligations of Israel in relation to the Presence and Activities of the United Nations , Other International Organizations and Third States in and in relation to the Occupied Palestinian Territory . Syed Haider Shah also called for holding Israel accountable for the imposition of collective punishment through the denial of humanitarian aid to millions of civilians . <p> The Ambassador highlighted the atrocities being committed by Israel in the Occupied Palestinian Territory , particularly in Gaza . He emphasized that Israel 's conduct towards the UN Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees reflected one of the most egregious breaches of the privileges and immunities of an international organization in the history of the UN .
