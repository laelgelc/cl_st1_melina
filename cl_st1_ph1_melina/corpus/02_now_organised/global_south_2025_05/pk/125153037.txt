<h> Malta to officially recognize State of Palestine next month <p> VALLETTA -- Malta has announced its decision to formally recognize the State of Palestine , becoming the latest European nation to take this significant diplomatic step amid growing international support for Palestinian statehood . <p> Prime Minister Robert Abela made the announcement on Sunday during a political event , where he addressed both global and domestic issues , with a strong emphasis on the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Gaza . <p> " We can not remain silent spectators to the human tragedy unfolding in Gaza , " said Prime Minister Abela . " Recognizing Palestine as a sovereign state is not only our moral responsibility but also a matter of justice and human dignity . " <p> He confirmed that Malta would officially recognize Palestine after the upcoming United Nations conference scheduled for June 20 . <p> Malta had previously expressed support for Palestinian statehood , having voted in favor of granting full UN membership to Palestine at the UN Security Council in April 2023 . However , formal recognition had not been extended until now . <p> With @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ member states recognizing Palestine will rise further . Currently , 147 out of 193 UN members acknowledge Palestine as a sovereign state . <p> In recent years , a growing number of countries including Ireland , Norway , Spain , Slovenia , Armenia , the Bahamas , Trinidad and Tobago , Jamaica and Barbados joined the list of nations recognizing Palestine . <p> Despite this momentum , several key Western powers including the United States , the United Kingdom , Germany and Australia have yet to officially recognize Palestinian statehood . However , change may be on the horizon ; French President Emmanuel Macron recently indicated that France could make a decision on the matter in the coming months .
