<p> GAZA : Palestinian journalist Moataz Raja was assassinated in an Israeli airstrike while reporting on the conflict in Gaza , Al Jazeera reported on Thursday . <p> Raja 's death came amid a surge in Israeli attacks that killed at least 55 people in the past 24 hours . <p> The Gaza Journalists Syndicate said Raja was killed instantly when an Israeli aircraft targeted the civilian vehicle he was traveling in on al-Nafaq Street in Gaza City . Since October 7 , 2023 , the syndicate has documented the deaths of at least 221 journalists in Gaza . <p> In the latest wave of violence , at least 23 people died in airstrikes in the central Gaza area of Bureij . Another 10 Palestinians lost their lives while seeking aid from a US-backed organisation , according to Gaza 's health ministry .
