<p> At the United Nations , Pakistan has reiterated the call for a permanent and unconditional ceasefire in Gaza . <p> The call was made by Pakistan 's Permanent Representative to the United Nations Ambassador Asim Iftikhar Ahmed during a UN Security Council briefing in New York on the Humanitarian Situation in Gaza . <p> The ambassador emphasized that the UN Security Council must resolve the Palestine issue based on the two-state solution , along the pre-1967 borders , with Al-Quds Al-Sharif as the capital of a sovereign and independent Palestinian State . <p> He said the UNSC must uphold its resolutions , end the occupation of Palestinian territories , and act with urgency to bring long-awaited justice to the people of Palestine <p> The ambassador said Gaza Reconstruction Plan must be fully and actively supported , and no forced displacement of Gazans be tolerated . <p> He also called for lifting of blockade in Gaza and allow unhindered access to aid convoys and medical teams .
