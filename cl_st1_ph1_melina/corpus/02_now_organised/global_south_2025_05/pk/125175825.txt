<h> Eid and Gaza <p> As Eid approaches , Muslims across the world prepare for celebration -- buying animals , clothes , and gifts , anticipating a day filled with prayer , joy , and family . But in Gaza , our brothers and sisters remain trapped in a nightmare . Their Eid is marked not by festivity , but by loss . While we dress in vibrant colours , their clothes are soaked in blood . While we embrace our loved ones , they bury theirs . <p> How can there be joy in Eid when their homes are rubble , their children starving , and their streets lined with graves ? Israel 's ongoing assault has robbed them not only of life and shelter , but of hope . <p> As the world watches in silence , Gaza bleeds . Let us not forget them amid our own celebrations .
