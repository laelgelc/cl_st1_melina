<h> Yemen government names finance minister as new PM <p> DUBAI : Yemen 's internationally recognised government named finance minister Salem bin Buraik as its new prime minister on Saturday , after his predecessor quit saying he was unable to fully exercise his powers . <p> Outgoing premier Ahmed Bin Mubarak had disputed for months with Rashad al-Alimi , who heads the Saudi-backed Presidential Leadership Council , two ministers and a member of the PLC told AFP . <p> Alimi named Bin Buraik prime minister in a decision published by the official Saba news agency . No other ministerial changes were announced . <p> Analyst Mohammed Albasha , of the US-based Basha Report Risk Advisory , posted on X that Bin Buraik is seen as non-confrontational -- " a sharp contrast to his predecessor , with whom much of the cabinet , and even the president , had fallen out " . <p> After Iran-backed Huthi rebels seized the capital Sanaa in 2014 , Yemen 's government withdrew to Aden in the south .
