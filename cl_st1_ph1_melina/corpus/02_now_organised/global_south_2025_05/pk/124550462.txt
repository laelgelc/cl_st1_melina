<h> Exceptional arrangements in place for Hajj pilgrims : Minister Sardar <p> Minister for Religious Affairs and Interfaith Harmony Sardar Muhammad Yousaf has expressed hope that this year 's Hajj will be exemplary as compared with previous years . <p> Talking to the State Media in Makkah today , he said exceptional arrangements have been in place by Pakistani Mission for the intending pilgrims . <p> The Minister said all camps in Mina will be air-conditioned to ensure a comfortable environment . The camps will be equipped with sofa-cum-beds , allowing Hujjaj to use them for both sitting and sleeping . Ample storage space will also be provided in the form of elevated storage racks for luggage . <p> He said Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif especially sent him to Saudi Arabia to oversee arrangements for the guests of Allah . <p> He highlighted that 100 percent accommodation for Pakistani pilgrims has been arranged in the Markazia area of Madinah , which is at walking distance from Masjid Nabawi ( SAWW ) . <p> Lauding the dedication of entire Hajj mission team , Sardar Yousaf urged them to leave no stone @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pilgrims . <p> He also expressed gratitude to the Saudi authorities for their continued support and cooperation in ensuring smooth arrangements for Pakistani pilgrims . <p> The Minister once again advised the intending pilgrims to strictly follow the rules , regulations and instructions issued by the Saudi government , as these are designed to ensure the smooth and safe conduct of Hajj . <p> He urged them to pray for the peace , progress , and prosperity of Pakistan , the unity of the Muslim Ummah , and for the oppressed Muslims of Gaza and Indian Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir ( IIOJK ) . <p> Regarding the Hajj flight operation , Sardar Yousaf said in the first phase , Pakistani pilgrims are being transported to Madinah , while the remaining pilgrims will be airlifted directly to Makkah . <p> Earlier , the Minister received the guests of Allah , coming from Mandinah , in Makkah 's hotel . On the occasion , DG Hajj Abdul Wahab Soomro , Director Aziz-ullah Khan , Coordinator Zulfiqar Khan and officials of Saudi Company Al Rajihi were also present .
