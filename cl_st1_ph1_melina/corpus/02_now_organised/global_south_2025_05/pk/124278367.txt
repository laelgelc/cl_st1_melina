<h> Gaza rescuers say 29 killed in Israeli strikes <p> Gaza City , Palestinian Territories - Gaza 's civil defence agency said Israeli strikes killed at least 29 people Friday in the Palestinian territory , devastated by war and under a total Israeli aid blockade for two months . <p> Israel resumed its military campaign in the Gaza Strip on March 18 after the collapse of a ceasefire that had largely halted the fighting . <p> Nine people were killed when an Israeli air strike hit a home in Bureij refugee camp in central Gaza , civil defence official Mohammed al-Mughayyir told . Another six people were killed in a separate strike targeting the Al-Masri family home in the northern city of Beit Lahia , he added . <p> In Gaza City , a strike on a community kitchen claimed the lives of six more , the civil defence agency reported . <p> Elsewhere across the Gaza Strip , at least eight additional fatalities were reported in similar attacks , the agency said .
