That 's the limit to the brave front they can put up , as long as it does n't upset their armed oppressors enough to open fire . These uniformed individuals then cite impassive demolition papers to justify bearing down on homes made of love and labour in Masafer Yatta in Palestine . <p> A woman watches her quarters , her belongings , every corner of memory in her precious home being crushed to dust . Not subdued by aloof demands of relocation , she protests , " Where do we go ? We have no other land . That 's why we suffer for it . In that spirit , Palestinian activist Basel Adra picked up a camera to film his documentary , No Other Land , the moment he believed that the beginning of the end had become unavoidable . <p> It 's harrowing enough to watch social media to spell out the atrocities for you within a minute or two . But as I sat for over two hours at the screening for No Other Land organised by The Second Floor ( T2F ) , I was compelled to face the spine-chilling reality with an intimacy that was difficult to evade . And that 's exactly the point . <p> Following a non-linear structure , the story of Masafer Yatta through Basel 's eyes begins in 2019 . In addition , Basel 's own memories from 1999 slip into the narrative time and again to put into perspective that Israel 's brutality following the October 7 , 2023 attack was n't merely a response but one aided by a veneered pretext . <p> Letting it sink in <p> Be it class debates or close-knit settings discussing the Palestinian struggle @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ twitchy mind allows me to . I was rendered speechless , as the cousin accompanying me would say . <p> Basel 's sturdy sense of hope along with the smiles that his people sport as defense are as heart-rending as they are warm , and just as soul-crushing as when Basel himself admits to Yuval that he 's losing energy . <p> It felt futile -- speaking from a distance , watching from a distance . Then I saw Harun Abu Aram . <p> Mirrored fragments <p> The documentary shows Harun , an unarmed resident of Al-Tuwanah ( a village to the south of Hebron ) , resisting as Israeli troops seize his electric generator . Moments into the heated escalation , the troops shoot Harun , cruelly subjecting him to lifelong paralysis . <p> As time prolongs his suffering , Harun 's mother hopes for one of two @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ her son to full health in exchange , or for death to relieve her son once and for all . Her second prayer is answered . <p> Watching Harun and his family struck me with heart-sinking unease and thoughts that are n't easy to confront or pen down . Even after the screen faded to black , their plight stayed with me , haunted me to some capacity . It followed me back home to my bedridden aunt . <p> For months , I have been struggling to put into words how I feel . When you 're informed from the distance of a phone call that a loved one -- a particularly lively one -- has bled from the inside , the first companion to comfort you is denial . There are no tears , no adrenaline rush , no grief -- just restless pacing and sleepless midnights that fade to tomorrows . Over and over again . <p> When you see her again , you can barely recognise her . She looks the same , smooth-skinned and relaxed as she always has . But she 's no longer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ dissolving flimsy debates with a harmless joke . She just rests . That 's all she prefers doing these days . <p> The denial tricks you into thinking it 's gone . But then you revisit old texts and send new ones for no real reason . Some days , you surprise yourself at your eagerness to initiate drawn-out conversations despite knowing that she ca n't respond . Every now and then , you recall a vivid memory because at least in your mind , her verve is eternal . <p> Regularly , you squeeze her hand , fix her scarf , help her move , and cling onto hope . But now , you sit with the reminder that hope is a luxury in a war-torn world -- and your smaller world is safely tucked away from it . <p> Denial is not a helpful instrument for Harun 's loved ones , who wait with bated breath for both good and bad news . Where they possess a wealth of love , they are robbed of resources . There is no comfort zone for them to retreat to . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ deceptive . <p> It hurts differently when you watch Harun 's family reserve the warmest blankets for him ; it hurts close to home , even . But it can never hurt quite the same because no one can snatch those blankets from you or ruthlessly bury them under rubble . Because many of us did n't grow up surrounded by echoes of the same desperate question : " Why is it only illegal for us ?
