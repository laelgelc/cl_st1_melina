<h> Kazi Salahuddin Ahmed 's solo exhibition ' Debris ' opens at AFD <p> The solo painting exhibition titled " Debris " by Kazi Salahuddin Ahmed opened its doors at La Galerie , Alliance Fran ? aise de Dhaka , Dhanmondi , yesterday . The event boasts almost 30 acrylic works on board paper , offering viewers a glimpse into the artist 's latest explorations . <p> Eminent artist Monirul Islam attended the inauguration ceremony as the chief guest . Mustafa Zaman , director of the Department of Fine Arts , Bangladesh Shilpakala Academy , also joined the occasion as the special guest . <p> The artist Kazi Salahuddin Ahmed has lived through the 1971 war . Today , he is forced to witness entirely man-made destruction in regions such as Syria , Russia , Kashmir , and Myanmar . <p> These tragedies , driven by the pursuit of power and control , have profoundly shaped his worldview . Over the decades , these experiences have left a lasting impression on his artistic journey , influencing both the themes and emotional depth of his work . <p> Art critic Mustafa @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " ' Of Real and Emotional Debris : The complete eraser of Gaza which was once full of life , the continued miseries of people in Kashmir , or the hopelessness faced by the displaced Rohingyas trying eke out a life in camps in Chattogram , all this jostle the mind as one tries hard to think of the future of the human race . " <p> " It is also hard to leave behind the legacy of Bangladesh 's multiple failures in sculpting a future . Here , the people 's dream for political stability has always remained a chimaera . The fall of the repressive regime has certainly made way for all to focus on a future beyond the given structure , but it seems that things keep falling apart , and we are only left with emotional debris . I navigate through a landscape where the edifices of our collective dream lie in ruin , hence the title ' Debris ' , " he added . <p> The highly accomplished artist Kazi Salahuddin Ahmed held his first exhibition in 1987 . He participated in many group exhibitions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ abroad , particularly in France , the United States , India , Pakistan , the United Kingdom , China , Japan , Australia , Nepal , Iran and Yugoslavia . <p> The open-to-all exhibition will run until May 31 ( Saturday ) , from Monday to Saturday , between 3pm to 9pm.
