<p> Israeli forces raided multiple foreign exchange businesses across the occupied West Bank on Tuesday , killing one Palestinian and injuring dozens in a widespread military operation that drew strong condemnation from Palestinian groups . <p> Residents reported that Israeli forces stormed the areas using live ammunition , tear gas , and smoke bombs . In Nablus , troops raided the Al-Khaleej Exchange Company and a nearby gold shop , while in Jenin , Israeli soldiers fired smoke bombs in the city center . Streets were also sealed off in Tubas and Bethlehem . <p> According to the Ministry of Health based in Ramallah , one man was killed and eight others were injured by live fire during the raid in Nablus .
