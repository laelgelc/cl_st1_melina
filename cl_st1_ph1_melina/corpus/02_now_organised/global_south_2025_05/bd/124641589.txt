<h> Cannes Film Festival kicks off amid global uncertainty <p> The 78th Cannes Film Festival , running from May 13 to 24 this year , once again transforms the French Riviera into a global celebration of cinema . <p> Filmmakers , producers , sales agents , and journalists from around the world converge to showcase and discover new films , emphasising that storytelling transcends borders . However , this year 's festival unfolds under the shadow of proposed US tariffs on foreign films , announced by President Donald Trump . <p> The 100% tariff proposal has sparked concern across the international film community , potentially impacting the distribution of Cannes-selected films in the lucrative US market . <p> Star-studded red carpet <p> Yet again , the festival 's red carpet will undoubtedly shine with Hollywood luminaries . Tom Cruise is set to attend the premiere of " Mission : Impossible -- The Final Reckoning " .
