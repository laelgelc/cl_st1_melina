<p> Israel has no intention of taking long-term responsibility for the civilian population in Gaza , even as its military continues operations across the territory , former Israeli National Security Advisor Gen Yaakov Amidror ( Retired ) told CNBC-TV18 . <p> " We do n't want to be responsible for the civilian life of the Palestinians , " he said , indicating that a third party will likely be brought in to manage Gaza 's civilian affairs once Israeli forces secure military control . <p> Amidror justified the intense military campaign , including airstrikes on civilian infrastructure , by accusing Hamas of embedding itself among civilians . <p> " This what they call a school , was a headquarters of Hamas , " he claimed . Israel has come under renewed international criticism after bombing a school in Gaza , killing 36 people , including children . <p> The presence of Israeli hostages continues to shape operational tactics . Amidror said about 20 to 30 hostages remain in Hamas custody and that military operations have avoided certain areas to ensure their safety . <p> Outlining Operation Gideon Chariots , Amidror @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ gaining near-total military control of Gaza and then clearing Hamas infrastructure , including destroying an estimated 100 kilometers of underground tunnels . He added that a few thousand Hamas fighters are still active inside Gaza and will be targeted in the second phase . <p> The ultimate goal , he said , is the total elimination of Hamas ' military capability . " Now Israel is doing what is needed to destroy and eliminate this terror organisation , " Amidror stated . <p> He reiterated that the war was triggered by the October 7 Hamas attack that killed 1,200 Israelis , many of them civilians . " We are speaking about terrorists who came to butcher families in their houses , attacking civilians celebrating at a festival , " he said . <p> " With all due respect to the European Union , European countries , and all those leaders who condemn Israel , the responsibility for the security of Israel is not in the hands of the European countries . It is in the hands of the Israeli government , " he asserted .
