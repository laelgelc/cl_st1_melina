Following relentless airstrikes that have left hundreds of Palestinians dead , Operation Gideon 's Chariots is now in full swing , begun even as Israel and Hamas concluded a second day of ceasefire talks in Doha . The intention , according to the Israeli Defense Forces , is to expand " operational control " in the Strip while seeking to free the remaining Israeli hostages . In the process , it hopes to achieve what has , to date , been much pie in the sky : defeating Hamas and seizing control of the enclave . <p> The mendacious pattern of the IDF and Netanyahu government has become clearer than ever . To claim otherwise was antisemitic . <p> Then comes the large dollop of corrective , inconvenient reality , be it a film , a blatant statement , or some item of damning evidence .
