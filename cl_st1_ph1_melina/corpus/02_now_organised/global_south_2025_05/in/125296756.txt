<p> Smoke rises from Gaza after an explosion near the Israel-Gaza border Reuters <p> The US proposed a 60-day ceasefire plan for the Israel-Gaza conflict on Friday , that also proposes an exchange of hostages and prisoners on both sides , a Reuters report said . This proposal comes in the wake of as many as 14 Gazan deaths from Israeli airstrikes , as of Friday . <p> The proposal , which has received Israel 's assent , is said to be guaranteed by US President Donald Trump -- as well as mediators Egypt and Qatar . It also promises humanitarian aid packages to Gaza from the United Nations , the Red Crescent and other agreed channels , as soon as Hamas agrees to the ceasefire . <p> This would also lead to the release of 28 Israeli hostages -- alive and dead -- in exchange for 1,236 Palestinian prisoners as well as the remains of 180 dead citizens .
