The day is significant becuse ' communications ' is an important and integral dimension of the Church . ' Communications Day ' is one of the oldent ' special days ' observance by the Church . The message for this year , was written by the late Pope Francis on the theme ' Share with gentleness the hope that is in your hearts ( cf. 1 Pet 3:15-16 ) ' <p> In his message , Pope Francis sets the agenda of what communications should be today ! He makes a fervent appeal for ' diarming communications ' saying among other things , " too often today , communication generates not hope , but fear and despair , prejudice and resentment , fanaticism and even hatred . As a result , we witness , often helplessly , a sort of atomization of interests that ends up undermining the foundations of our existence as community , our ability to join in the pursuit of the common good , to listen to one another and to understand each other 's point of view .
