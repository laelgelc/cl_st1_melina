<p> Strikes hit multiple areas , including a family home and a shelter in northern Gaza , killing 22 people -- more than half of them women and children . In central Gaza , 13 people were killed in Deir al-Balah and 15 in Nuseirat refugee camp . Ten more died in Khan Younis , in the south . <p> The Israeli military did not comment but maintains that it targets militants and blames Hamas for civilian casualties due to its operations in populated areas . <p> Israel has resumed a major offensive in Gaza , aiming to free remaining hostages and dismantle Hamas . The conflict began on October 7 , when Hamas-led militants killed about 1,200 people in Israel and abducted 251 . Israel 's response has killed over 53,000 Palestinians , according to Gaza 's Health Ministry . <p> On Monday , Israel allowed a small number of aid trucks into Gaza for the first time in over two months . UN agencies said the volume was insufficient compared to the 600 trucks per day that entered during a ceasefire earlier this year .
