The 2017-2021 blockade imposed by Saudi Arabia , the United Arab Emirates , Bahrain , and Egypt represented a major diplomatic crisis , sparked in part by Qatar 's independent foreign policy . The eventual lifting of the blockade signalled not only the failure of efforts to isolate Qatar but also the strength of its global networks and alliances . <p> Labour rights remain an area of international concern .
