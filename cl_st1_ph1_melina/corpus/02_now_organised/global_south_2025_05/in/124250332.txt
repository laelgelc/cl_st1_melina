<h> One Hundred Days that Shook US Foreign Policy <h> Share : <p> NEW YORK -- We are barely 100 days into US President Donald Trump 's second term , but much is already clear . Trump 2.0 is starkly different : more confident and surrounded by a team determined to implement a far more sweeping agenda . Those staffing the administration -- amplifiers more than restrainers , enablers more than guardrails -- spent the past four years preparing for this moment . <p> Trump 2.0 is an activist , imperial presidency , at home and abroad . He seems to be everywhere , dominating public space and private conversations alike in much of the world . The contrast with his predecessor President Joe Biden could not be starker . <p> The administration 's principal policy goal thus far has been to make good on Trump 's campaign pledge to secure the United States ' southern border .
