<h> Hamas has accepted a ceasefire proposal mediated by the U.S. This deal involves the release of ten Israeli hostages in exchange for a 70-day truce . The agreement also includes a partial withdrawal of forces from Gaza . Further , Israel will release a number of Palestinian prisoners . This development raises hopes for a possible end to the conflict . <p> IANS <p> Hamas has agreed to a proposal by U.S. special envoy Steve Witkoff for a Gaza ceasefire , a Palestinian official close to the group told Reuters on Monday , paving the way for a possible end to the war . <p> The new proposal , which sees the release of ten hostages and 70 days of truce , was received by Hamas through mediators . <p> " The proposal includes the release of ten living Israeli hostages held by Hamas in two groups in return for a 70-day ceasefire and a partial withdrawal from the Gaza Strip , " the source said . <p> The proposal also sees the release of a number of Palestinian prisoners by Israel , including hundreds of those serving lengthy prison terms .
