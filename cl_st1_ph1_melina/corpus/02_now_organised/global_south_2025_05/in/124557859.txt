" This haunting quote underscores a truth as old as civilization itself : the impermanence of power . It resonates profoundly today as the world witnesses simultaneous convulsions of war and unrest -- from the bloodied trenches of Ukraine to the decimated hospitals of Gaza , from the chaos in Sudan to the rising military tensions between India and Pakistan . Each of these conflicts , in its own way , reflects the arrogance of political establishments that cling to illusions of permanence , failing to grasp the cyclical nature of history . <p> Echoes from the Past : A Historical Warning <p> Empires like Rome , the Ottoman Caliphate , the British Raj , and the Soviet Union once seemed indomitable . They boasted military supremacy , economic strength , and sprawling territories . Yet today , they survive only in ruins and textbooks . The illusion that such control ensures longevity is precisely what history warns against . <p> Russia , Ukraine , and Crimea : The Cost of Expansionism <p> Russia 's annexation of Crimea in 2014 and its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022 stem from a desire to resurrect imperial influence . Yet this ambition has triggered devastating consequences : thousands dead , a shattered economy , and an isolated Russia . The Kremlin 's gamble reveals the old fallacy -- that land can be held forever , and that people can be subjugated without end .
