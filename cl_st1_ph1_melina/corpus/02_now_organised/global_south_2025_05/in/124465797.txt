<p> India was retaliating for a terror attack on tourists in Indian-controlled Kashmir on April 22 , which killed 26 civilians , most of them Indian . New Delhi has blamed a Pakistan-based militant group for the incident . <p> Pakistan has vowed revenge for the airstrikes , calling them an " act of war " . <p> If a full-scale war does break out between the two nuclear powers , it would n't be the first time they have fought over the disputed region of Kashmir . In fact , the two sides have been in conflict over Kashmir since 1947 . <p> The people of Kashmir , meanwhile , are stuck in the middle of this geopolitical rivalry , trapped in a security state with little hope for the future . <h> Life before the April 22 terror attack <p> Before the attack on the tourists last month , Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi 's government had made repeated claims that " normalcy " was returning to the region . <p> However , Kashmir remains one of the most heavily militarised zones in the world and the people @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ justified on the grounds of counter-terrorism . <p> In 2019 , the Modi government revoked Article 370 of the Indian constitution , which had granted a special status to the state of Jammu and Kashmir , along with a high degree of autonomy . <p> The revocation of this article brought Jammu and Kashmir , now a " union territory " , under the full control of the Modi government in New Delhi . <p> This decision was made on behalf of Kashmiris , not in consultation with them . Speaking with Kashmiris in 2020 as part of my ongoing research on the region , there was a huge sense of betrayal at the move . <p> One of my interview subjects claimed Indian security forces were " instilling fear and psychological warfare " in Kashmir . Another said " it 's no exaggeration to say after every three kilometres , there 's a checkpoint " manned by Indian security forces . The situation worsened during the COVID pandemic , with increased lockdowns and curfews . <p> Some hope did return last September when Kashmiris were able to vote in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The election meant the new local assembly would have the power to make and amend laws , debate local issues and approve decisions for the territory , particularly in education and culture . <p> Police say some 1,900 Kashmiris have been detained and questioned since the attack . This number will no doubt continue to rise . <p> It is no wonder Kashmiris were saying " everyone lives in fear " , even before India launched missile strikes on its neighbour . <p> Possible retaliation from Pakistan -- or a wider war -- now looms , with Kashmiris again on the front lines . <h> Calls for India to follow Israel 's lead <p> There is a very big concern that right-wing Indian media outlets and social media posts are now encouraging the Indian government to respond to the terror attack in the same way Israel has retaliated against Hamas in Gaza . <p> Some commentators are portraying the April 22 attack as India 's version of the October 7 Hamas attack on southern Israel , which could become a dangerous precedent for what the future holds for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ India 's right to " self-defence " . <p> In addition , the rise in right-wing rhetoric increases the likelihood of Islamophobic attacks taking place against Kashmiris , as well as Muslims in India more broadly .
