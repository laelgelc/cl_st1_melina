<h> Hamas agrees to Gaza ceasefire proposal by US envoy <p> Hamas agreed to a proposal by US special envoy Steve Witkoff for a Gaza ceasefire , a Palestinian official close to the group told Reuters . <p> The Palestinian militant group Hamas has agreed to a Gaza ceasefire proposal by the US special envoy , Steve Witkoff , on Monday ( May 26 ) , news agency Reuters reported citing a Palestinian official . As per the new proposal , ten hostages will be released and there will be a total of 70 days of truce . The mediators handed over the proposal to the Palestinian militant group . <p> " The proposal includes the release of ten living Israeli hostages held by Hamas in two groups in return for a 70-day ceasefire and a partial withdrawal from the Gaza Strip , " the source said , Reuters reported . <p> The new proposal also sees the release of several Palestinian prisoners by Israel . <p> Israel has not released any immediate comment yet . He said that the war could only end when Hamas is " eliminated " .
