" The fires , fanned by strong winds , have spread rapidly through the Jerusalem Hills , threatening surrounding communities and prompting road closures , including the critical Tel Aviv -- Jerusalem highway . <p> Shmulik Friedman , commander of Jerusalem 's Fire and Rescue District , said the blazes may be the largest in Israel 's history , adding that firefighting operations would continue through the week . According to the fire authority , at least 163 ground crews and 12 aircraft are currently engaged in battling the flames .
