<h> A U.S. judge has ordered the release of Rumeysa Ozturk , a Tufts University student from Turkey , held in a Louisiana detention facility . Ozturk 's detention followed her co-authoring an op-ed critical of Tufts ' stance on Israel 's war in Gaza . The case highlights First Amendment rights concerns . <p> In a significant ruling , a federal judge has ordered the immediate release of Rumeysa Ozturk , a Turkish student at Tufts University , from a Louisiana immigration detention facility . Ozturk was detained after co-authoring an opinion piece critical of her university 's response to Israel 's war in Gaza . <p> Judge William Sessions noted that Ozturk 's detention raised substantial First Amendment rights concerns , warning it could suppress free speech among non-citizens . The hearing in Vermont also highlighted the Trump administration 's controversial deportation efforts against pro-Palestinian activists . <p> Ozturk 's lawyers argued her detention was unlawfully punitive . Now set to return to Massachusetts , Ozturk faces ongoing legal battles as her case brings renewed attention to constitutional rights and the treatment of international students under the Trump administration .
