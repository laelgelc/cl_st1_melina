<p> Gaza : A senior Hamas official said that a US proposal for a ceasefire in the Gaza Strip remains under review , even though it does not meet the core demands of the movement and the Palestinian people . <p> Basem Naim , a member of Hamas 's political bureau , said in a statement , on Thursday , that the movement had received the Israeli response to the US proposal brokered last week by US Special Envoy to the Middle East Steve Witkoff , Xinhua news agency reported . <p> According to Naim , the Israeli position fails to address key Palestinian demands , including a complete cessation of hostilities and the lifting of the long-standing blockade on Gaza . <p> He said the proposal would allow for the continuation of " the occupation " and humanitarian suffering , even during any temporary truce . <p> " Nevertheless , the movement 's leadership is evaluating the proposal with a sense of national responsibility , particularly in light of the ongoing violence and humanitarian crisis facing the Palestinian people , " Naim said . <p> Hamas stated earlier that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was assessing it in a way that reflects the interests of the Palestinian people and aims to achieve lasting peace and relief for Gaza residents .
