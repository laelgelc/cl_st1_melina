<h> Israeli Strikes Kill 75 in Gaza as New Offensive Expands <p> SRINAGAR : At least 75 people were killed in Israeli strikes across Gaza overnight and into Sunday , according to hospitals and medics . The strikes come as Israel expands its offensive under a new operation named " Gideon 's Chariots , " more than 19 months into the war . <p> In Khan Younis , over 20 people died in airstrikes targeting homes and tents sheltering displaced people , Nasser Hospital reported . In northern Gaza 's Jabaliya camp , a strike killed nine people from one family . Another strike in the same area killed 10 , including seven children and a woman , Gaza 's civil defence said . <p> In Gaza City , Shifa Hospital reported that 15 children and 12 women were among at least 43 killed in multiple strikes . Central Gaza also saw attacks , with 12 deaths reported from Deir al-Balah and Zweida town . Nuseirat camp was also hit , killing two , Awda Hospital said . <p> The Israeli military gave no comment on the latest strikes . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ceasefire that would include the release of Israeli hostages , but not end the war . Hamas demands a full withdrawal of Israeli forces as part of any agreement . <p> Israel has also kept a blockade in place since mid-March , halting all imports into Gaza , including food and medicine . The move has worsened the humanitarian crisis . <p> The war began on October 7 , 2023 , when Hamas-led militants killed 1,200 people in southern Israel and took 251 hostages . Since then , more than 53,000 Palestinians have been killed , according to Gaza 's Health Ministry , which does not distinguish between civilians and fighters . <p> Meanwhile , Israel said it intercepted a missile launched by Yemen 's Iran-backed Houthi rebels early Sunday . Sirens sounded across parts of Israel . The Houthis claimed to have fired two ballistic missiles , including a hypersonic one , at Israel 's main airport . Israel responded with an airstrike on Yemen , its eighth since the war began .
