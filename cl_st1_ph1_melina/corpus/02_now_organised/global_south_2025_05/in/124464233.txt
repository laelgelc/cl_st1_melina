India has retaliated with missile attacks in Operation Sindoor . <p> Together with the other three permanent members of the United Nations ( UN ) Security Council they have condemned the terrorist assault and grilled Pakistan , currently a non-permanent member of the Council , on its role in launching or aiding the attack . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the US at a stronger individual level ?
