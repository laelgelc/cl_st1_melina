<h> Share : <p> Beirut -- The violent crackdown by security forces affiliated with the Southern Transitional Council ( STC ) on a peaceful protest by women in Aden , Yemen , is deeply concerning . <p> The use of force to disperse demonstrators calling for improved basic services is a clear violation of the rights to freedom of opinion , expression , and peaceful assembly . It reflects a repressive security approach and highlights the urgent need for accountability , as well as the protection of all citizens ' rights -- regardless of gender -- without fear or intimidation . <p> Euro-Med Monitor received testimonies from several women in Yemen who said they took to the streets to protest the severe decline in basic services , including healthcare , education , water , and electricity , amid the authorities ' ongoing failure to addressthe breakdown .
