<h> Israeli Army Intensifies Campaign Against Hamas <h> The Israeli army plans to escalate operations against Hamas in Gaza , aiming to gain more ground and dismantle terrorist structures , according to Chief Eyal Zamir . This initiative is part of a structured plan to respond to Hamas ' defiance and ensure their defeat . <p> The Israeli army is set to intensify its military operations targeting the militant Islamist group Hamas in the Gaza Strip . This strategic decision aims to capture additional territory within the embattled region . <p> According to Israeli army chief Eyal Zamir , the operations follow a structured plan designed to neutralize Hamas ' influence . Zamir emphasized that the group will face consequences due to its resistance and hostile actions . <p> The army 's strategy involves expanding its maneuvers , capturing more ground , and systematically dismantling the terrorist infrastructure until Hamas ' defeat is assured , as highlighted during Zamir 's field tour in Gaza .
