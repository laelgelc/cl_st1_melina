<p> Gaza School Airstrike : At least 36 Palestinians , including many children and women , were killed in an Israeli airstrike on a school sheltering displaced families in Gaza City . Civil defence teams recovered charred bodies from the rubble of the Fahmi al-Jarjawi school , which was hit without warning and engulfed in flames . Spain is now urging the global community to consider sanctions on Israel , as it hosts foreign ministers from 20 European and Arab countries to push for a two-state solution Israel claims over 200 airstrikes were carried out in Gaza in just 48 hours , targeting what it described as terrorist infrastructure . The Gaza Health Ministry reports at least 53,939 Palestinians have been killed since the war began , while the Government Media Office places the toll at more than 61,700 , including those presumed dead under the rubble . The humanitarian crisis in Gaza is worsening by the hour . This is the latest on Israel 's ongoing war in Gaza .
