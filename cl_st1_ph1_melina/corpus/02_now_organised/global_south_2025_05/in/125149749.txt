<h> At Least 25 Dead After Israeli Strike on Gaza School <p> SRINAGAR : At least 25 people , mostly women and children , were killed in an Israeli airstrike on a school being used as a shelter in northern Gaza , according to the territory 's Health Ministry . More than 55 others were injured , said Fahmy Awad , head of the ministry 's emergency service . He said the school was struck three times while people were sleeping , and a father with his five children were among those killed . <p> The Israeli military said it targeted a command and control centre inside the school used by Hamas and Islamic Jihad to coordinate attacks . It said militants were gathering intelligence at the site . The military added that Hamas 's presence in civilian areas contributes to civilian casualties . <p> Israel renewed its military campaign in Gaza in March after the breakdown of a ceasefire with Hamas . It has pledged to continue operations until Hamas is dismantled and the remaining hostages from the October 7 , 2023 attack are returned .
