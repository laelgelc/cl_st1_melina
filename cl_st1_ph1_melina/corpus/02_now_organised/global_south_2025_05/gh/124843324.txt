<p> European leaders have intensified calls for Israel to cease military operations in Gaza as the humanitarian toll mounts , with casualties surpassing 53,000 since October 2023 . <p> European Council President Antonio Costa condemned the violence , stating he was " shocked " by Israel 's " disproportionate force " and systematic violations of international law . In a social media post on Saturday , Costa demanded Israel lift its blockade to allow " safe , swift , and unhindered " aid access , stressing , " What 's happening in Gaza is a humanitarian tragedy . " <p> Norway , Iceland , Ireland , Luxembourg , Malta , Slovenia , and Spain issued a joint statement Friday , urging Israel to " immediately reverse its current policy " and warning of starvation risks for thousands without urgent intervention . The coalition also reaffirmed support for a two-state solution , pledging to pursue peace through the United Nations .
