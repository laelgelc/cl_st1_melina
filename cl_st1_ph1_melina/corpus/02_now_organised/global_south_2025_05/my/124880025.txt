<p> Pope Leo XIV blesses the image of Our Lady of Good Counsel at the end of Mass on Sunday , May 18 ( @Vatican Media ) <p> By Devin WatkinsAt the conclusion of the Mass of Inauguration of his pontificate on Sunday , Pope Leo XIV prayed the Regina Caeli and appealed for people suffering due to war . <p> He thanked the 200,000 people and numerous delegations attending the Eucharist , expressing his gratitude to the representatives of nations , Churches , and various religions . <p> " In the joy of faith and communion , we can not forget our brothers and sisters who are suffering because of war , " said Pope Leo . <p> He recalled that " the surviving children , families , and elderly " in Gaza have been reduced to starvation , as the Israel-Hamas war continues . <p> In Myanmar , noted the Pope , " new hostilities have cut short innocent young lives . " <p> And , he lamented , " martyred Ukraine awaits negotiations for a just and lasting peace .
