<p> NICOSIA , May 2 -- A group of activists organising an aid boat for Gaza said it was attacked today by Israeli drones in international waters off Malta as they headed towards the Palestinian territory . <p> " At 00:23 Maltese time , the Conscience , a Freedom Flotilla Coalition ship came under direct attack in international waters , " the group said in a statement . <p> " Armed drones attacked the front of an unarmed civilian vessel twice , causing a fire and a substantial breach in the hull , " it added , blaming Israel . <p> " Israeli ambassadors must be summoned and answer to violations of international law , including the ongoing blockade and the bombing of our civilian vessel in international waters . " <p> Israel has not commented on the allegation . <p> The strike , the activists said , appeared to target the boat 's generator . It left the boat without power and at risk of sinking , the statement added . <p> There was no indication of any casualties . The statement said a vessel was dispatched from Cyprus @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and that Italian authorities said they had sent another boat to the scene . <p> The group said activists from 21 countries were on board . They are on what it called a " mission to challenge Israel 's illegal and deadly siege of Gaza , and to deliver desperately needed , life-saving aid " . <p> Israel has since March 2 blocked all aid deliveries to Gaza , and resumed intense military operations in the territory in mid-March , with a two-month ceasefire in the Israel-Hamas war in tatters . <p> Hamas 's attack on Israel in October 2023 , which sparked the Gaza war , resulted in the deaths of 1,218 people , mostly civilians , according to an AFP tally based on official figures . <p> The health ministry in Hamas-run Gaza said on Thursday that at least 2,326 people have been killed since Israel resumed strikes , bringing the overall death toll since the war broke out to 52,418. -- AFP
