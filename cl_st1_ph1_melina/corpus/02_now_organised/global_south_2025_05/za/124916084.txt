<p> Cape Town -- President Cyril Ramaphosa is in Washington for a working visit and is set to meet US President Donald Trump . <p> The visit comes amid strained relations , particularly after the US granted asylum to 49 white South Africans based on false claims of racial persecution and " genocide , " which South Africa disputes . <p> The meeting aims to reset and strengthen bilateral ties , focusing on economic and trade relations . <p> Ramaphosa 's spokesperson , Vincent Magwenya , said the president is confident and not apprehensive , although some topics may spark robust discussions . <p> " He 's looking forward to it , he 's highly enthused , and we 're looking forward to a very successful meeting aimed at resetting the relationship between South Africa and the United States . He 's not apprehensive at all . <p> " We do n't think President Trump invited President Ramaphosa for that kind of treatment . There are issues of concern on the side of the US and on our side as well . It is possible that those issues may trigger @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of these engagements . But we believe that the kind of courtesy that we are accustomed to , that heads of state extend to one another , will be in full display tomorrow , " Magwenya said . <p> Key agenda items include the resettlement of white South Africans , aid cuts , and global conflicts such as Ukraine and Gaza . <p> Ramaphosa will also discuss business opportunities for Elon Musk 's companies , including Starlink , despite prior licensing controversies . <p> This marks Trump 's first hosting of an African leader at the White House . <p> South Africa currently chairs the G20 and will hand over leadership to the US in November .
