<h> From ruins to resilience : Gazan medical students triumph against adversity in SA <p> Twenty-seven final-year medical students from Al-Azhar University and Islamic University in Gaza were displaced from their universities after they were destroyed by Israeli missiles . But the students turned tragedy to triumph and have managed to complete their studies through the University of Cape Town and University of the Witswatersand . <p> Source : Supplied . Twenty-seven students from Gaza bid farewell to South Africa on the steps at UCT . They left Gaza after their universities were destroyed during the war , and studied at UCT and Wits . <p> The students arrived in South Africa in 2024 to complete their medical studies in order to graduate . Most of the students managed to escape to Egypt through the Rafa border to get to South Africa .
