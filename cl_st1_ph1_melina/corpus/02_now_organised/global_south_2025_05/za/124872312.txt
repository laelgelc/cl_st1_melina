The president of the Economic Freedom Fighters , Julius Malema , slammed Ramaphosa 's visit and called it ill-timed . <h> Cyril Ramaphosa jets to the US <p> According to SABC News , Ramaphosa is expected to address issues , including the alleged white genocide , of which Trump has accused the South African government . This was after Finance Minister Enoch Godongwana 's two attempts at tabling a budget speech were rejected and ultimately scrapped . <p> " You can not leave your country in crisis and go to America , where you will be ill-treated .
