<h> Qatar says Gaza ceasefire talks at ' delicate phase ' <p> DUBAI : Qatari Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani said on Wednesday talks on a Gaza ceasefire and a release of hostages are at a " delicate phase " . <p> " We are trying as much as possible to address this stumbling block , " he added , without giving further details . .
