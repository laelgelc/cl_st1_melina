<h> Herefordshire bowls club preparing for 100-year anniversary <h> Herefordshire bowls club preparing for 100-year anniversary <p> Some of the women players at St Martin 's Bowls Club ( Image : Submitted ) <p> WOMEN players at a Herefordshire bowls club are celebrating the club 's 100-year anniversary by hosting a series of charity matches taking place across the summer season , kicking off on April 30 . <p> Teams of women from local clubs will be competing for the Centenary Challenge Cup which will be presented to the winning team at the Grand Finale on 10 September 10 . <p> During the season the teams will raise money for the Save the Children Gaza Appeal . <p> There will be a range of other events to mark the anniversary of St Martin 's Bowls Club including several friendly bowling sessions with Herefordshire clubs and a celebration match with Bowls England players . <p> St Martin 's Bowling Club was opened in May 1924 and it was only the men who played and the women made the teas . <p> The 1920 's was a ' golden age ' for bowls but with the depression following the Wall Street @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> In 1944 wives and daughters were invited to join due to the post-war shortage of men . An independent women 's section was formed in 1958 with 17 members . <p> From 1976 the women competed in the County Women 's League , which sadly wound up in 2013 , after 37 years , due to insufficient numbers . <p> Today the club 's women 's section has 19 members who compete in friendlies , internal competitions and county matches . <p> Gone are the bowler hats and white skirts to be replaced with colourful team shirts and shorts or trousers . <p> Black woods/bowls have been replaced by rainbow colours and it is played in a more animated way than in the past . <p> Anyone interested in trying the game can contact the club 's secretary , Richard Walden , on 07870 269861 .
