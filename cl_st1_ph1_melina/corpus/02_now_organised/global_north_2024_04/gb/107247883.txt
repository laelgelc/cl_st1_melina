<h> What US-Japan defense shakeup will involve and why <p> WASHINGTON - The United States says next week 's summit between President Joe Biden and Japanese Prime Minister Fumio Kishida will bring a " historic " upgrade in defense ties between the long-time allies . On Wednesday in Washington , the leaders are expected to discuss plans to reform the U.S. military command in Japan to make it better able to function with Japanese forces in a crisis and to announce steps to allow more joint development , and potentially co-production , of military and defense equipment . <p> WHY DO THE COUNTRIES WANT TO IMPROVE MILITARY COORDINATION ? <p> Japan and the U.S. confront a range of threats that call for closer coordination . Topping the list is China 's growing military and the threat it poses to Taiwan , just over 100 km ( 62 miles ) from Japanese territory . It means commanders of U.S. forces in Japan have no strategic decision-making powers and that their authority is largely limited to managing bases and personnel , rather than working with Japanese counterparts .
