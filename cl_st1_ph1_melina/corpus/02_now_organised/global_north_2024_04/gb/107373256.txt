<h> Europe 's SMEs need one set of rules , report to say <p> Reuters/REUTERS <p> The report is being drafted by Enrico Letta , a former Italian prime minister , to find ways to bolster the European Union 's single market and so help boost its competitiveness against rival economies such as the United States and China <p> Europe 's export-oriented small businesses should be offered a single regulatory framework with the same rules governing their activities wherever they operate in the region , a report to be presented to national leaders next week will propose . <p> The report is being drafted by Enrico Letta , a former Italian prime minister , to find ways to bolster the European Union 's single market and so help boost its competitiveness against rival economies such as the United States and China . <p> Letta said Europe 's small and medium-sized enterprises ( SMEs ) should be able to opt for a newly created single framework instead of having to deal with different regulations and tax rules in the 27 countries of the European Union . <p> " I would call it Opt 28 , a sort of legal passe-partout @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to EU commissioners in Brussels on Wednesday . <p> SMEs are typically defined as companies with less than 250 staff and turnover of less than 50 million euros .
