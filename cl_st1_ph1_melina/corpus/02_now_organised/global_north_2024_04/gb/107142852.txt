By contrast , euro zone manufacturing activity contracted at an even steeper pace in March than in February , as demand continued to fall , data on Tuesday showed . <p> German inflation eased , data showed . Broader euro zone inflation data is due on Wednesday , and will be closely watched for indications about when the European Central Bank will cut rates . <p> European stocks rose in early trading , starting the second quarter on a positive note as financial markets re-opened after Easter public holidays .
