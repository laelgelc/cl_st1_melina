<h> Oman calls for self-restraint to spare region risks of war <p> MUSCAT : The Sultanate of Oman affirmed that it is following up , with great concern , the developments of the military escalation in the region and its serious repercussions . Oman called for self-restraint to spare the region and its peoples the risks of war . <p> The Sultanate stressed its position calling for adherence to international laws . It underlined the necessity of the Security Council 's assuming its responsibility towards maintaining international peace and security . <p> In a statement today quoted by Oman News Agency ( ONA ) , the Foreign Ministry of Oman reiterated the necessity of imposing an immediate ceasefire in the Gaza Strip .
