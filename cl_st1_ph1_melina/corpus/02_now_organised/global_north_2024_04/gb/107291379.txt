<p> Binyamin Netanyahu is facing the collapse of his fragile government as far-right cabinet members revolt over suggestions Israel is scaling down the war in Gaza . <p> The last division of the Israel Defence Forces ( IDF ) left southern Gaza on Saturday night after four months of fighting in and around the city of Khan Yunis . Although airstrikes continue and one IDF brigade has remained to secure a cordon south of Gaza City , bisecting the coastal strip , this is the smallest ground force Israel has had in Gaza since the first month of the war . <p> This is viewed in some quarters as a possible winding down of the war , or at least a prelude to a hostage-release deal with Hamas which would include a temporary ceasefire .
