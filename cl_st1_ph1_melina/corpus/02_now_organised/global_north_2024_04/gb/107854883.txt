<p> OR Books has acquired a new book by the late Palestinian poet and English literature professor , Refaat Alareer , who was killed in an Israeli bombing on 6th December , 2023 . <p> Co-founder Colin Robinson bought world rights to If I Must Die from a family friend of the poet , who administered the contract on behalf of his family . Early copies will be available in June and the book will be published in September 2024 . <p> Following his death , Alareer 's poem " If I Must Die " went viral on social media , and has been translated into more than 40 languages . The book , which takes its name from the poem , brings together Alareer 's poetry , his writing on literature and more personal correspondences . <p> The book is published with the support and cooperation of Alareer 's family , who are still living in northern Gaza .
