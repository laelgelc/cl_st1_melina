<p> The Joint Operations Command of the Ministry of Defence announced the implementation of the 33rd airdrop of humanitarian and relief aid as part of the " Birds of Goodness " operation . <p> Two C17 aircraft belonging to the UAE Air Force and two C295 aircraft belonging to the Egyptian Air Force participated in the airdrop operation . <p> The airdrop was carried out over inaccessible isolated areas in the northern Gaza Strip via four aircraft carrying 82 tonnes of food and relief aid , bringing the total amount of aid airdrops to 2,107 tonnes since the beginning of the operation . <p> This brings the total amount of aid sent by the UAE to northern Gaza to over 2,477 tonnes , including both land shipments through the Kerem Shalom crossing and airdrops via " Birds of Goodness .
