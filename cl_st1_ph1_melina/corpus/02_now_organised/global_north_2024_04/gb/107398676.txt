<p> ABU DHABI - The Joint Operations Command of the Ministry of Defence announced the successful completion of the 31st " Birds of Goodness " airdrop operation , delivering 81 tonnes of humanitarian aid and Eid clothing to northern Gaza . <p> Two C17 aircraft belonging to the UAE Air Force and two C295 aircraft belonging to the Egyptian Air Force participated in the airdrop operation . <p> The airdropped supplies encompassed essential food items alongside special Eid clothing parcels for families . These parcels contained clothes , toys , sweets , and various products for all family members . The mission aimed to address the needs of the Palestinian people in Gaza during Eid Al Fitr , fostering hope and joy while alleviating their hardships . <p> The operation targeted isolated areas in northern Gaza that are difficult to access by land .
