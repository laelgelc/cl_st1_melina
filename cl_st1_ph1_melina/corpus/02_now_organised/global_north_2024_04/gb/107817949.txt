<p> A woman and a man saddened by the deaths of children in Gaza stood on Pall Mall . One was part of a pro-Palestine march , condemned by critics as antisemitic . The other was part of a counter-demonstration , accused by some on the march of being " murderers " . <p> The handling of pro-Palestine protests , which have been held most weekends since October 7 , have been under scrutiny after a clip emerged that appeared to show a Metropolitan Police officer telling Gideon Falter , an antisemitism campaigner , that his " openly Jewish " presence at a pro-Palestinian march was " antagonistic " . <p> On Saturday 's march , campaigners said that the streets had been made unsafe for Jews , while a group of Holocaust survivors in the demonstration said that they had been warmly welcomed .
