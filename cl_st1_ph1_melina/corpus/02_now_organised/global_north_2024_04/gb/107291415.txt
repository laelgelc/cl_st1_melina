<h> Israel must realise how bad things look now <h> Netanyahu is in danger of destroying his country 's most precious asset : an aura of righteousness that its friends respect <p> Criticism of Israeli behaviour in Gaza misses the point . You do n't conduct a military operation to find terrorist needles in the haystack of a panicking population in a small and densely packed space without terrible collateral damage , jumpy troops , rogue incidents , misunderstandings and awful media headlines . <p> The Israel Defence Forces ' finding on the strike that killed seven World Central Kitchen aid workers this week was that this was one of those " misunderstandings " . On this at least , Binyamin Netanyahu is right . These things happen in war . From our own imperial history we British should know that . The Indian uprisings , the Boer wars , the Kenyan Mau-Mau ... for an occupying power , collateral damage comes with the territory .
