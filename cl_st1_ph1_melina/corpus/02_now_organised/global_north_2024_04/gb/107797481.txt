<h> ' We should not be cowered by fear ' - why this year 's festival of Passover is important to Gloucestershire 's Jewish community <p> Passover is one of the Three Pilgrimage Festivals that celebrates the Exodus of Israelites from slavery in Egypt . We speak to members of the Jewish community on the importance of the festival and also the concern of rising antisemitism <p> Here in Gloucestershire it is true that the county does not have a large scale Jewish community compared to most of the UK 's major citie . but the importance of one festival this week has been a matter of huge significance . <p> Passover is one of the Three Pilgrimage Festivals that celebrate the Exodus of the Israelites from slavery in Biblical Egypt . Passover means a festival of renewal where the Passover Seder is a long-standing tradition almost like a home from home ceremony . <p> " We talk about slavery and freedom in all its forms , the history of what it means to be Jewish and also we think about what freedom means , Anna said .
