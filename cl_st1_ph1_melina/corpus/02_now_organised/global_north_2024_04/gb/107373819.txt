<p> A once top-secret Cold War bunker which was prepared for members of the US congress in the event of nuclear war boasts 1,000 beds , a cafe and a 25-ton blast door . <p> The bunker is situated beneath The Greenbrier , a luxury four-star resort near White Sulphur Springs , West Virignia . The high-class resort and hotel has attracted wealthy businessman , politicians and bankers since it was built in 1858 . <p> During World War II , the hotel was briefly used as a detention centre for diplomats of enemy countries . <p> The UK has a series of nuclear bunkers dotted around the country that , despite never being used , still serve a purpose as Russia 's conflict with Ukraine rumbles on , horrific events unfold in both Israel and Gaza , and simmering tensions with North Korea and military manoeuvres around Taiwan keep people wary .
