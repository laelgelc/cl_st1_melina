<h> Brent oil futures above $89 as supply risks intensify <p> LONDON - Oil prices were stable on Wednesday , as investors mulled supply risks stemming from ongoing Ukrainian attacks on Russian refineries and the potential for a widening of the Middle East conflict . <p> Brent crude futures for June rose 16 cents , or 0.18% , to $89.08 per barrel at 0902 GMT , while U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude futures for May gained 11 cents , or 0.13% , to $85.26 a barrel . <p> Both Brent and WTI climbed 1.7% during the previous session to their highest since October . <p> " The rise in hostilities in both hotspots pushed the price of the two crude oil futures contracts to their highest levels this year , " PVM analyst Tamas Varga said of Tuesday 's rise . <p> Prices jumped higher on Tuesday after a fresh round of Ukrainian drone attacks on Russian refineries threatened to take even more of the country 's processing capacity offline . <p> Investors were also concerned that conflict in the Middle East could spread , after Iran vowed revenge against Israel for an attack on Monday that killed high-ranking @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ East involving more oil-producing nations could cause supply disruptions .
