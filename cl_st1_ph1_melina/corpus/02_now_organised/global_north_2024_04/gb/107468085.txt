<p> There are new calls for an Israel-Gaza ceasefire and a support request for Sefton residents impacted by the conflict as tensions continue to escalate in the region . <p> Sefton Council 's full council meeting will take place on Thursday April 18 and will hear a motion calling for an immediate and permanent end to fighting in the Middle East . <p> The motion has been submitted by newly independent Cllr Natasha Carlin and calls on Sefton council to write to the Prime Minister , Rishi Sunak and the Leader of the Opposition , Sir Keir Starmer to back an immediate end to the conflict . <p> Just last week , Cllr Carlin quit the Labour Party and released a statement detailing some of the reasons behind her decision .
