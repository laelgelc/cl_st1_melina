<p> CAIRO - Egyptian authorities have committed to stop sidestepping the finance ministry to borrow billions of dollars directly from the central bank , the International Monetary Fund said in a staff report prepared for a board meeting last month . <p> Egypt 's central bank as of February 2023 had lent as much as 765 billion Egyptian pounds ( $15.9 billion ) to state agencies other than the finance ministry , an apparent breach of a 2020 central bank law , said the IMF report , excerpts of which were seen by Reuters . It did not say when the lending started . <p> Egypt 's central bank , the finance ministry and the IMF did not immediately respond to requests for comment . <p> The IMF board meeting on March 29 approved an expansion of Egypt 's current loan programme to $8 billion , citing damage to the economy from the crisis in Gaza .
