<h> UAE : Central Bank projects 5.2% growth for 2025 <p> The UAE Central Bank has projected a 5.2 per cent GDP growth forecast for 2025 but revised down projection for 2024 . <p> According to the fourth-quarter 2023 report , the economy is projected to grow faster next year on the back of higher growth in the hydrocarbon sector . <p> The UAE 's economy will be driven mainly by the oil sector with 6.2 per cent growth followed by the non-oil sector at 4.7 per cent in 2025 . <p> " For 2024 , the Central Bank projects real output growth to rebound to 4.2 per cent , a downgrade from 5.7 per cent previously projected .
