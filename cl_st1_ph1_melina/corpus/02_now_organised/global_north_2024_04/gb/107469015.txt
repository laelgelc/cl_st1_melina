<p> ABU DHABI : President His Highness Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan spoke by telephone today with His Highness Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani , Emir of the State of Qatar . Their Highnesses discussed UAE-Qatar relations and opportunities for further collaboration to help achieve the development goals of both countries . <p> The two leaders also reviewed a number of regional and international issues of mutual concern , foremost among them the situation in the Middle East and the ongoing humanitarian crisis in the Gaza Strip .
