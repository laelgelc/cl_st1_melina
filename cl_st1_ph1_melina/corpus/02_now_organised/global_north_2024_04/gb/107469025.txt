<h> Oil prices fall after Iran attack as market draws down risk premium <p> BEIJING : Oil prices fell at Asia 's open on Monday , as market participants dialled back risk premiums following Iran 's attack on Israel late on Saturday which the Israeli government said caused limited damage . <p> Brent futures for June delivery fell 24 cents to $90.21 a barrel while West Texas Intermediate ( WTI ) futures for May delivery were down 38 cents at $85.28 a barrel by 1256 GMT . <p> The attack involving more than 300 missiles and drones was the first on Israel from another country in more than three decades . It had raised concerns about a broader regional conflict affecting oil traffic through the Middle East . <p> But the attack , which Iran called retaliation for an air strike on its Damascus consulate , caused only modest damage , with missiles shot down by Israel 's Iron Dome defence system . Israel , which is at war with Iran-backed Hamas militants in Gaza , has neither confirmed nor denied it struck the consulate . <p> While Israeli officials said the country 's war cabinet was in favour @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ part in any offensive against Iran .
