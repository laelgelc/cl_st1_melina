<h> Israel has been tricked into alienating its allies <p> The Sunday Times <p> Sunday April 14 2024 , 12.01am , The Sunday Times <p> Smoke billows after an Israeli bombardment on the Firas market area in Gaza <p> AFP/GETTY <p> Matthew Syed says Israel has been " rope-a-doped " by Hamas ( Comment , last week ) . He is right . Israel has increased the hostility of the Arab world towards it at a time when that hostility was declining . The real threat to Israel 's existence is not Hamas but the gradual loss of the support of its friends and allies around the world -- particularly that of the US , without which Israel will not survive . <p> Whatever the solution may be , it is not to bomb Gaza back to the Stone Age , however justifiable this might feel after the abhorrent events of October 7 . Apart from anything else , there is this to consider : it wo n't work.Peter Slot , London W4
