<h> Palestinian boy volunteers at hospital as Gaza war rages <p> Twelve-year old-Palestinian Zakaria Sarsak walks determinedly through a sea of people at the Al-Aqsa Hospital in Gaza . But he 's not there for treatment , he 's working as a volunteer , helping the medics by filling syringes and moving stretchers . <p> He decided he wanted to help those wounded in the Israeli bombardment that displaced him and his family from their home , and now spends much of his time at the hospital . <p> " I help bring injured people to Al-Aqsa Hospital . We also bring martyrs to the morgue , I go with the ambulance to bring casualties , " Sarsak said . <p> He makes his way through the corridors , wearing a blue fleece and a pair of protective gloves , and takes on tasks such as moving patients and helping with taking blood pressure readings . <p> " When we go to get injured people after attacks , I feel sad for children . We bring all injured people to Al-Aqsa Hospital , " he said . <p> The hospital is one of the few still tending to patients @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Hamas attacked Israel , killing 1,200 people and taking more than 200 hostages . <p> Israel 's offensive in retaliation to the attack by Hamas , which rules the Gaza Strip , has killed over 32,000 Palestinians and wounded over 75,000 people . <p> ( Writing by Michael Georgy ; Editing by Alison Williams ) <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
