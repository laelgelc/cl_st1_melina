<p> ABU DHABI - The Joint Operations Command of the Ministry of Defence announced the successful completion of the 30th " Birds of Goodness " airdrop operation , delivering 125 tonnes of humanitarian aid and Eid clothing to northern Gaza . <p> The airdrop , which took place on 10th April 2024 , was the largest to date and involved six aircraft : three C-17s from the UAE Air Force , two C-295s and one C-130 from the Egyptian Air Force . <p> The airdropped supplies encompassed essential food items alongside special Eid clothing parcels for families . These parcels contained clothes , toys , sweets , and various products for all family members . The mission aimed to address the needs of the Palestinian people in Gaza during Eid Al Fitr , fostering hope and joy while alleviating their hardships . <p> The operation targeted isolated areas in northern Gaza that are difficult to access by land .
