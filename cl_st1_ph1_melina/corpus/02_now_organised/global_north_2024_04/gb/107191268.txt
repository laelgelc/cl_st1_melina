It followed a series of delays by the government in presenting a proposal demanded by the court aimed at enhancing the military enlistment of ultra-Orthodox men . <p> The war in Gaza has brought to a head an unresolved conflict within Israel over the special treatment of the Haredim , an argument about the character of the Jewish state itself .
