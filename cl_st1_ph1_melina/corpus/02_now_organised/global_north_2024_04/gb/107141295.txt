<p> A woman spray painted the word " LIARS " onto the BBC Wales building in Cardiff during a pro-Palestine protest . The graffiti was sprayed on the walls of the building . <p> Deidre Murphy , 69 , was found guilty of criminal damage to a property value under ? 5,000 after the word was sprayed in red paint on the BBC 's Cardiff office in Central Square on October 28 last year . The protest opposed Israel 's bombing of the Gaza strip , with protestors meeting at the Aneurin Bevan statue before walking towards the BBC building . Murphy carried out the damage to the building during the protest . <p> The defendant pleaded not guilty to the charge but was found guilty following a trial at Cardiff Magistrates ' Court on Tuesday . Murphy , of Holtsfield , Murton , Swansea , was sentenced to a conditional discharge of two years , was ordered to pay compensation of ? 702 and court costs of ? 650.
