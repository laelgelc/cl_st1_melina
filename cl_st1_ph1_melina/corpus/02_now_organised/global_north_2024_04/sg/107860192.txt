<h> KFC Malaysia temporarily shutters outlets citing challenging economy <p> KFC Malaysia has temporarily closed outlets in the country , citing challenging economic conditions , after local media reported that the closings were because of boycotts over the fast food chain 's perceived links to Israel . <p> Malaysia , a majority-Muslim country , is a staunch supporter of the Palestinians , and some Western fast-food brands in the country , as in some other Muslim nations , have been targeted by boycott campaigns over Israel 's military offensive in Gaza . <p> " QSR Brands and KFC Malaysia has taken proactive measures to temporarily close outlets as means to manage increasing business costs and focus on high engagement trade zones , " it said in a statement late on Monday . The statement did not address the media reports . <p> It did not specify how many stores were affected , but local media reported over 100 outlets were temporarily closed . <p> Employees from the affected stores were offered the opportunity to relocate to outlets in areas with higher customer engagement , QSR Brands said .
