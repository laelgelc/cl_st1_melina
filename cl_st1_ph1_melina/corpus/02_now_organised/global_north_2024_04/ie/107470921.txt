They were instead charged with crimes against humanity . <p> Intentionality is an important concept , in law and moral philosophy , forming the necessary link between agency and action . Intentionality implies reason . <p> To say that an agent acted intentionally is to say an agent performed a given action because the agent had a reason for doing it .
