<h> Meet the Cork women supporting Gaza through ' craftivism ' <p> IN the kitchen of her home in Cobh , chef and food writer Lilly Higgins has a fridge magnet with the phrase : ' Art is the guarantee of sanity ' . These words have taken on a special significance in recent months , as she has used her creative talents to show solidarity with the people of Palestine . <p> Lilly is part of a community of artists in Cork who are channelling their skills into support for Gaza . Through this ' craftivism ' , they hope to shine a light on what is happening , while also celebrating the rich cultural history of Palestine . <p> Lilly has always been interested in the food and culture of the Middle East , and owns several cookbooks from Palestinian chefs . <p> " It 's cuisine I 'm drawn to , " she says . One of her sisters lives in Beirut , and Lilly has spent time in cookery schools in Jordan , " meeting people who do amazing work and listening to their stories .
