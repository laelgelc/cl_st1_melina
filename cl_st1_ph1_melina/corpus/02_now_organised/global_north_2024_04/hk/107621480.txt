( Reuters ) <p> Iranian President Ebrahim Raisi will make an official visit to Pakistan this week , Islamabad said on Sunday , as the two Muslim neighbours seek to mend ties after tit-for-tat missile strikes in January . <p> The visit , which Pakistan 's Ministry of Foreign Affairs said would take place from Monday to Wednesday , had been in doubt as Middle East tensions rose after Iran launched an unprecedented attack on Israel a week ago and central Iran received what sources said was an Israeli attack on Friday . <p> Pakistan has signalled since January that Raisi would visit , and the prime minister said last week the visit would take place " very soon " . <p> Tehran has played down Friday 's apparent Israeli attack and indicated it had no plans for retaliation , a response that appeared gauged towards keeping the Israel-Gaza war from expanding to a regionwide conflict .
