<h> UK reports possible pirate attack in Red Sea <p> The UK Maritime Trade Operations ( UKMTO ) said on Saturday that it had received a report of an incident 50 nautical miles northeast of the United Arab Emirates ' Fujairah and authorities were investigating . <p> Iran-aligned Houthis have staged attacks on shipping in the Red Sea region for months in solidarity with Palestinians in the Gaza war . <p> The commander of Iran 's Revolutionary Guard 's navy said on Tuesday that Israel 's presence in the UAE was viewed as a threat by Tehran and it could close the Strait of Hormuz - the entrance to the Gulf - if deemed necessary . Fujairah is located on the eastern side of the Strait of Hormuz . <p> Months of Houthi attacks in the Red Sea have disrupted global shipping , forcing firms to re-route to longer and more expensive journeys around Southern Africa , and stoked fears that the Israel-Hamas war could spread to destabilize the wider Middle East .
