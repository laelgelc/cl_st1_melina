<p> According to the World Bank Global Findex Database 2021 , 76% of adults worldwide have some form of account ownership . But , banks operate at their discretion , sometimes in a very arbitrary manner . In 2024 , still , having a bank account does not guarantee that from one day to the next , you might become unbanked . In light of this , perhaps keeping a crypto wallet with savings in stablecoins ( coins that are pegged to the value of a currency ) surfaces as a line of citizen defense against irascible institutions . <p> This denial of payment services does not only happen at the banking level , unfortunately .
