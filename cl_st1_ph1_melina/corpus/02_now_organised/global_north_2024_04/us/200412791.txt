<h> Iran-allied militia coalition claims its base in Iraq was hit by an airstrike <p> BAGHDAD -- An airstrike early Saturday hit a base used by the Popular Mobilization Forces , a coalition of Iran-allied militias in Iraq , the group said in a statement . <p> The attack on the Kalsu military base north of Babylon -- a former U.S. post that was handed over to the Iraqi military in 2011 -- wounded three PMF members , said two militia officials who spoke on condition of anonymity because they were not authorized to speak to the press . <p> It was not clear who was responsible for the purported attack , which came a day after a suspected Israeli strike in Iran , but the militia officials blamed U.S. forces . <p> A U.S. official said there were no U.S. airstrikes in Iraq . The official spoke on the condition of anonymity to provide details that were not public . <p> The PMF is a coalition of primarily Shiite , Iran-backed armed groups that joined in the fight against the Islamic State extemist group after it seized large sections of Iraq in 2014 .
