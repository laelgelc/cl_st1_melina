There were 101 Republicans and 210 Democrats who voted in favor , while Rep. Dan Meuser , R-Pa. , voted present . All 112 votes against it came from Republicans . <p> The passage of the bills comes weeks after the Senate passed a mammoth bill with aid for Ukraine , Israel and Taiwan , plus funding for border security .
