Known as Project Nimbus , the deal to provide AI and cloud services to Israel has become increasingly polarizing as its aggression in Gaza has escalated to what one UN expert denounced as genocide . <p> Since then , Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai has held fast in his steering Google into a different and perhaps less collaborative track .
