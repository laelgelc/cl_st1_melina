However , he then said , " Pause . " <p> During an Oval Office address last year , Biden again appeared to read a note from the teleprompter when he said , " Make it clear , " in the middle of remarks . <p> " We will have something that we do not seek -- make it clear : We do not seek -- we do not seek to have American troops fighting in Russia or fighting against Russia , " Biden said during a speech about the wars in Gaza and Ukraine in October .
