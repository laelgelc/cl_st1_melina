Sleiman headed the anti-Hezbollah Lebanese Forces Party in a predominantly Christian coastal area . <p> Lebanon 's army said on Monday a group of Syrians tried to steal Sleiman 's car the previous evening but ultimately killed him and took his body to neighbouring Syria .
