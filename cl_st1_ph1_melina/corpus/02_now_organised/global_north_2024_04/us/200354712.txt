<p> But is a cease-fire even a good idea ? Cease-fires are tricky business . They are easy to call for but hard to implement -- and sustain -- because the devil is in the details . <p> Cease-fires have a complex history . What often makes the difference between a durable cease-fire and a fragile one are the goals of each party -- sometimes it 's to stop violence to allow for humanitarian aid to be dispersed ; other times it is the start of advancing a longer-rangegoal to end the dispute . The length of a cease-fire also matters -- they can be hours , days , or months . <p> Advertisement <p> Whether or not a cease-fire works or fails depends on the objectives of each party .
