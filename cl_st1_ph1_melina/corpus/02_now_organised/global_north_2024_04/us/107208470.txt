So one Sudanese woman told Tom Perriello , who was appointed U.S. Special Envoy to Sudan recently . The people he met there told him the country was a " hell hole . " <p> For most Western observers , the scale and nature of the suffering in Sudan , like the origins and logic of the multi-layered conflicts causing it , exceed comprehension . One year into the most recent outbreak of warfare ( of many rounds since Sudan 's independence in 1956 ) , genocidal killing and famine have returned with a vengeance .
