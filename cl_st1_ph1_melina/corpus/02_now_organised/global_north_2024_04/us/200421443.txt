<h> The Cass Review Wo n't Fade Away <p> Anyone who reads the Cass Review , and who then reads most recent mainstream American media coverage of youth gender medicine , will be gobsmacked . <p> The review , spearheaded by the respected British pediatrician Hilary Cass ( and ably summed up in The Morning Dispatch last week ) , explains that youth gender medicine " is an area of remarkably weak evidence , and yet results of studies are exaggerated or misrepresented by people on all sides of the debate to support their viewpoint . The reality is that we have no good evidence on the long-term outcomes of interventions to manage gender-related distress . " <p> Cass and her colleagues arrived at this conclusion after an ambitious yearslong effort to interview clinicians , parents , and patients about their experiences with the National Health Service 's youth gender medicine system . She also commissioned a sizable bundle of independent systematic reviews evaluating both the evidence for puberty blockers and hormones , as well as the quality of recommendations published by influential groups like the World Professional Association for Transgender Healthcare .
