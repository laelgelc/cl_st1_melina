<h> Fewer college students blame Hamas for Oct. 7 attack on Israel : Poll <p> Fewer college students in the United States blame Hamas for the Oct. 7 attack on Israel that killed about 1,200 Israelis and resulted in more than 250 hostages , according to a poll . <p> According to a Generation Lab poll of two- and four-year students , the number of college students who blame Hamas for the attack has dropped from 52% in October of last year , just after the attack , to 41% , an 11 percentage point drop . <p> The news comes as protests in support of Palestinians in Gaza have ignited on campuses across the U.S. , including at Columbia University , Yale , and George Washington University . <p> Previously , only 12% said that the attack was a " justified act of resistance , " but that number has jumped 8 percentage points to 20% .
