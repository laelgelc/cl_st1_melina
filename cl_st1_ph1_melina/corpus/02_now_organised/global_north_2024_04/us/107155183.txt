" <p> To get through the COVID-19 pandemic and deal with the supply chain challenges directly afterward , the Medina-based company had to make some expensive changes to production and inventory . Demand for products had spiked during the pandemic , and it had to get more vehicles to dealers . <p> The company created rework lines to finish the snowmobiles , all-terrain vehicles and boats as parts arrived .
