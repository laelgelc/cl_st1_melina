<p> Diesel prices relative to crude have held fairly steady in recent weeks , but a report by the International Energy Agency has a wary -- though not apocalyptic -- recap for diesel consumers on how Ukraine 's attacks on Russian refineries might end that stability . <p> Crude and products markets are up overall in the past month . Brent , the world 's crude benchmark , settled on March 12 at $81.92 a barrel . It has crossed the $90 mark in recent days , primarily fueled by concerns that growing tensions between Israel and other countries , Iran in particular , could result in an escalation of military action that could disrupt production in Iran or elsewhere .
