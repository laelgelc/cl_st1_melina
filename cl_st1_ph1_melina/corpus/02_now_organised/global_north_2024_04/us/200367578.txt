<p> President Joe Biden puts Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on notice after Israeli strikes killed seven aid workers earlier this week in Gaza . In a sharp shift , Biden told Netanyahu an immediate ceasefire is needed and warned that future U.S. military support for Israel will come with conditions . <p> Meanwhile , Donald Trump , who is becoming increasingly critical of Netanyahu 's handling of the war , hit the campaign trail on Tuesday for the first time in nearly three weeks .
