<p> CIA Director William Burns is expected to travel to Cairo , Egypt , this weekend for a fresh round of hostage talks with top officials from Egypt , Qatar and Israel , multiple sources in the U.S. and the region familiar with the planning told CBS News . <p> The negotiations mark the latest effort to strike a deal that has remained elusive for months .
