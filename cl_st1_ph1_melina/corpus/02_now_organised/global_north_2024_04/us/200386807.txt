<p> The campaign was launched last week by UK film professionals Hanna Flint , Julia Jackman , Leila Latif , Sophie Monks Kaufman and Helen Simmons , and has immediately taken off with works from the likes of Jonathan Glazer , Spike Lee and Tessa Thompson .
