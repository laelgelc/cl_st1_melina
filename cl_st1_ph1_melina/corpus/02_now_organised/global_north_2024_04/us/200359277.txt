<p> Nearly 400 Apple workers have signed an open letter asking for Apple executives to " end their silence " on Palestinian suffering amid the war on Gaza . The group also alleged that employees who have expressed solidarity with Palestine have been " actioned against " or " wrongfully terminated . " <p> Apples 4 Ceasefire began their movement last week on social media and with the publication of the letter . The signatories range from retail workers to technical experts to operations specialists , working at Apple branches and offices globally : current and former employees from America , Scotland , Spain , Canada , and the UK have signed . <p> In the letter , the group criticised Apple for " the lack of care and understanding this company has given the Palestinian community , not only abroad suffering in Gaza , but also towards our own team members and anyone who supports them within our stores and offices . " <p> The group also claimed that Apple employees had been disciplined for showing solidarity with Palestine through accessories . By having its doors open in Israel and remaining silent on today 's attacks on Palestinian civilians , Apple has contributed to a systemic erasure that is now becoming a clear case of ethnic cleansing and racist violence . " <p> " If Apple wants to truly stand by its credo and make the world a better place , " Ra'ouf continued , " they need to lead the world like they have time and time again .
