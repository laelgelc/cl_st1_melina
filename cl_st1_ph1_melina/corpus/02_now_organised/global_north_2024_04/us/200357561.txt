U.S. Rep. Jan Schakowsky , Lincolnwood Mayor Jesal Patel , State Sen. Ram Villivalam and State Rep. Kevin Olickal attended the event , held at the Holiday Inn &amp; Suites Chicago North Shore . <p> " Islam teaches us to feel for what we want for our brothers and sisters the same ( as ) what we want for ourselves , " said Amiwala .
