Yet the top 10% of millennials have 20% more wealth than the top baby boomers at the same age . <p> " Millennials are so different from one another that it is not particularly meaningful to talk about the ' average ' Millennial experience , " wrote the study 's authors , Rob Gruijters , Zachary Van Winkle and Anette Eva Fasang .
