( AP Photo/Christophe Ena , Pool , File ) <p> The Associated Press <p> CAIRO -- President Abdel-Fattah el-Sissi of Egypt was sworn in for a third six-year term on Tuesday after being re-elected in a December vote in which he faced no serious challengers . <p> El-Sissi took the oath of office before parliament , which convened in the new administrative capital in the desert outside Cairo . <p> El-Sissi won 89.6% of the vote @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ than 67 million registered voters . He ran against three virtually unknown opponents . <p> The vote was overshadowed by the Israel-Hamas war in Gaza on Egypt 's eastern border , which has threatened to expand into wider regional turmoil . <p> El-Sissi was first elected as president in mid-2014 , then reelected in 2018 . A year later , constitutional amendments , passed in a general referendum , added two years to el-Sissi 's second term , and allowed him to run for a third , six-year term . <p> His victory in this latest election was widely seen as inevitable . His three opponents were marginal political figures who were rarely seen during the election campaign .
