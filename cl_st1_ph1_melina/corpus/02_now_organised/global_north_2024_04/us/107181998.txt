<h> Benny Gantz calls for early Israeli elections <p> Israeli Minister Benny Gantz called for the country to hold elections in September during a press conference on Wednesday evening . <p> This is the first time Gantz has called for new elections since joining the war cabinet following the Hamas attack on Oct. 7 that prompted the Israel-Hamas war . Moving elections up to September this year would bring a vote forward by two years . <p> " We must agree on a date for elections in September , towards a year to the war if you will , " Gantz said in a briefing . " Setting such a date will allow us to continue the military effort while signaling to the citizens of Israel that we will soon renew their trust in us . " <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has spoken out against the prospect of elections while the war is ongoing .
