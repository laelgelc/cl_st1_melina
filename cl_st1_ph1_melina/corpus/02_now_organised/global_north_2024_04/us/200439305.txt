<h> The Weeknd Pledges $2 Million to Provide Food for Humanitarian Support in Gaza <p> The donation was made in tandem with the United Nations World Food Programme ( WFP ) , which will provide 1,500 metric tons of fortified wheat flour to feed more than 157,000 Palestinians . It comes on the heels of a previous donation of $2.5 million from the Weeknd in Dec. 2023 . That funding provided 820 metric tons of food parcels to feed 173,000 Palestinians for two weeks .
