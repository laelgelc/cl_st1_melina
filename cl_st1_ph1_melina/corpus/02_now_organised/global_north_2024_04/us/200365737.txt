President George W. Bush did so in 2002 and Netanyahu himself did in 2009 . Admittedly , Netanyahu subsequently changed his mind , but the outside world will not let him undo a decision already on the books for 15 years . Attempting to backtrack on this agreement amounts to a doomed rear-guard effort . <p> Beyond that agreement , an eventual Palestinian state has become inevitable .
