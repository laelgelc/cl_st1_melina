( Shutterstock photo ) <p> TEHRAN -- A World Food Programme ( WFP ) official has sounded the alarm on the humanitarian situation in Gaza , warning that people in the besieged Palestinian territory are on the brink of famine and children there are dying of hunger . . " As we speak , a large number of children in Gaza are starving to death , " WFP executive director Cindy McCain said in an interview with CNN . <p> In response to the question whether the Zionist regime 's agreement to open a new crossing for the entry of humanitarian aid , McCain said that although this action is important it does not suffice the amount of aid needed in Gaza . <p> She said children in Gaza are currently dying of hunger and those that are not dying or have n't died yet are so emaciated . <p> A huge amount of humanitarian aid is waiting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that is why crossings for aid to enter Gaza are so important , McCain said . <p> McCain said that if these food items and aid do not reach the people of Gaza in time , many of them will die . <p> She noted that even if these aid reach the people they will experience the negative effects of malnutrition throughout their lives . <p> According to the United Nations Children 's Fund , more than 13,000 children have been killed since the beginning of the Zionist regime 's genocidal war at the Gaza Strip . <p> To date , one out of every three Palestinian children under the age of two years is malnourished due to severe famine , it added .
