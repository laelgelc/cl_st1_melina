THE CANADIAN PRESS/Nathan Denette <p> Former prime minister Stephen Harper is back on the front page of The Breach , courtesy of a fresh dispatch on the critical role that previously low-profile Toronto-based investment firm Awz Ventures is playing in the ongoing post-Oct. 7 conflict in Gaza . <p> " An investment firm led by ( Harper ) that is devoted to launching security companies in Israel has a new ' success ' story : helping that country 's military conduct secret mass surveillance of Palestinians in Gaza , " according to the latest bulletin , which is co-authored by managing editor Martin Lukacs and regular contributor Tim Groves , who reported on Harper 's involvement with the firm last year . <p> " The facial recognition technology the Israeli military is relying on was created by Israeli tech company , Corsight . Founded in 2019 , the company officially launched after receiving a $5 million investment from Awz Ventures . "
