<p> Sigh . It makes me nostalgic . My first engagement with politics came at Columbia University , in New York , in 1965 . I was 23 and had few or no political concerns . I was moodily crossing the campus in the rain and saw a hubbub between cops and antiwar students protesting a grad ceremony for students in the reserves . <p> I wandered over ; the police violence mesmerized me . Then Columbia 's prez , a former IBM CEO , came out to say , " You people are interrupting a university exercise . " I heard my voice say , " You should see the exercise we 're getting out here , " and got pushed to the front where I had an impromptu dialogue with him . He got tired of it , went back in , and the melee continued . I was tossed down the steps . <p> And that 's been part of my life ever since : politics , disruption .
