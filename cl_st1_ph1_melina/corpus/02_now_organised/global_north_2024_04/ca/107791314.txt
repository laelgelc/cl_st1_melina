THE CANADIAN PRESS/AP-Ismael Abu Dayyah <p> OTTAWA -- The World Central Kitchen plans to resume operations in the Gaza Strip Monday , four weeks after Israeli airstrikes killed seven of its aid workers including one Canadian . <p> The organization suspended food delivery in the region after the attack on three World Central Kitchen vehicles full of food from a nearby warehouse . <p> The aid group says the vehicles were clearly marked and their movements were known to the Israeli military when they were hit . <p> Jacob Flickinger , a 33-year-old military veteran from Quebec , was killed along with six of his colleagues . <p> Israel accepted responsibility and said the strikes were unintentional , and has pledged to change its rules accordingly . <p> The aid group says it plans to restart its operations @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ calls for an independent investigation into the deaths of its workers .
