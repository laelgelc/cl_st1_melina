<h> Oil prices fall on ceasefire hopes in Gaza <p> Oil prices decreased on Monday amid ceasefire hopes in the Middle East and growing uncertainties over the timing of the US Federal Reserve 's ( Fed ) interest rate cuts . ( Pexels Photo ) <p> ANKARA -- Oil prices decreased on Monday amid ceasefire hopes in the Middle East and growing uncertainties over the timing of the US Federal Reserve 's ( Fed ) interest rate cuts . <p> International benchmark Brent crude traded at USD87.45 per barrel at 10.45 a.m. local time ( 0745 GMT ) , a 0.86 percent decrease from the closing price of USD88.21 per barrel in the previous trading session . <p> The American benchmark West Texas Intermediate ( WTI ) traded at USD83.11 per barrel at the same time , a 0.88 percent fall from the previous session that closed at USD83.85 per barrel . <p> The likelihood of a ceasefire in Gaza increased as US Secretary of State Antony Blinken arrived in Saudi Arabia to discuss this possibility on Monday , drawing prices down at the start of the week . <p> Blinken will discuss efforts to reach a ceasefire in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Hamas is the stumbling block between the Palestinian people and the ceasefire , State Department spokesman Matthew Miller revealed in a statement . <p> He is also anticipated to discuss the path toward an independent Palestinian state that secures Israel 's security , measures to bring about long-term peace and stability in the region by preventing the conflict from spreading , and an increase in humanitarian aid for Gaza . <p> The possibility of a ceasefire in the region , home to major oil trade routes , supported price falls . <p> Additionally , uncertainties over when the US Federal Reserve ( Fed ) will start interest rate cuts continue to influence oil prices . <p> Money markets are still waiting for the US Fed to implement its first interest rate cut in the fourth quarter of the year , despite data released last week indicating otherwise in the US . <p> The possibility of a rate cut further supported downward price movements , as generally high-interest rates boost the value of the US dollar , making oil more expensive for holders of other currencies .
