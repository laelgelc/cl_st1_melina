<h> Temporary Pier in Gaza on Track to Be Operational in May <p> WASHINGTON -- NEWS -- A temporary pier the Defense Department is constructing off the Gazan coast to deliver humanitarian aid is on track to establish initial operations soon , the Pentagon announced today . <p> " We 're scheduled on track to meet our goal of early May , " said Deputy Pentagon Press Secretary Sabrina Singh during a news media gathering . <p> DOD originally announced its mission to construct the Joint Logistics Over-the-Shore , or JLOTS , capability on March 8 , with a goal of beginning initial delivery operations about 60 days from that date . <p> DOD officials announced on April 25 that construction of the pier had begun , and recent satellite imagery from Gaza 's coastline has shown construction activity in the area . <p> The components of the JLOTS include a floating pier , an approximately 1,800-foot-long causeway that will be attached to the shore , and a group of logistic support vessels and barges that will transport the aid from the pier to the causeway . <p> " Right now , you 're seeing construction of that floating @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to see construction of the causeway , " Singh told reporters . " Eventually , that causeway will be ... pushed into the coastline and secured by the . " <p> Since announcing the temporary pier would be used in the humanitarian aid mission , DOD officials have repeatedly emphasized that the IDF will provide force protection on and around the JLOTS . No U.S. boots will be on the ground as part of pier operations in Gaza . <p> " There is an integrated cell with the IDF and our U.S. military to ensure that there is deconfliction happening , " Singh said . " And that also helps with the coordination of JLOTS and the pier itself . So , we are confident that we are in a good place . " <p> When the pier is completed , officials anticipate it will initially facilitate the delivery of an estimated 90 daily truckloads of humanitarian aid into Gaza . Once fully operational , that number should jump to about 150 truckloads , or roughly 2,000,000 meals per day .
