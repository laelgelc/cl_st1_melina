<h> Laid-back Rabat , with its gorgeous gardens , colourful alleyways and unhurried souks is perfect for first-timers seeking a calm introduction to the country <p> First came the Phoenicians and the Carthaginians , then the Romans and the Berber Marinid sultans . Thousands of years later , the storks followed -- now hundreds of them sit in carefully constructed roosts on top of camel-coloured minarets , graves and ruined mosaics . The Unesco site of Chellah , pitched above the Boureg river estuary in Rabat , has been variously a mausoleum , a temple and a necropolis , shaped by the city 's custodians over a thousand years . Now it 's an overgrown bird 's nest . <p> On this Sunday morning in early spring , my friend and I are one of a handful of people in this cultural lasagne .
