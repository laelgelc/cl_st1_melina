<h> A top German court ruled that it would not be inhumane or degrading for the migrants , one born in Gaza and the other in Somalia , to return to another EU nation . <p> A top German court has ruled that two asylum seekers can be deported back to Greece despite the shortcomings in its immigration system . The Federal Administrative Court in Leipzig said it would not be inhumane or degrading for the migrants , one born in Gaza and the other in Somalia , to return . <p> Both men , in their 30s , were granted protection status in Greece . However , this did not give them enough access to state support . The Federal Office then rejected their German applications for asylum for Migration and Refugees , a decision upheld by an administrative court in Hesse . While the court acknowledged the Greek asylum system did have failings , it said the young , able-bodied men should be able to deal with them . <p> The court said the men would not face inhumane treatment upon their return to Greece .
