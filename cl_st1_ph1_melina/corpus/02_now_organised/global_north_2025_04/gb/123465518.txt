As a teenager , Mother Maria , as she is now known , had longed to meet God , and had set out to do so . She had tried the Lutherans . She had tried the Roman Catholics . Stories connect with our hearts , making them leap with joy or grow heavy with despair . They communicate truths of human longing and divine discovery . But the regret will not translate into a concerted effort to end the panoply of occupation . <p> Liberal democracy professes its commitment to the sanctity of loving relationships -- of motherhood -- and its entrepreneurs invent a thousand ways to part us from our cash to prove it .
