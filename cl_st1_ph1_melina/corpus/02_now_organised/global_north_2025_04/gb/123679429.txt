<p> Israeli strikes targeting displaced Palestinians in the so-called " humanitarian zone " of al-Mawasi in south Gaza 's Khan Younis overnight on Wednesday killed more than 15 people , mostly women and children . <p> In harrowing scenes , family members gathered around the charred remains of their relatives , including children as young as one year old , who were burnt beyond recognition . <p> Yusuf Abu Roos is a resident of the displacement camp . He told Middle East Eye that the sound of the strike rang out and , soon after , large fires spread throughout the displacement tents in al-Mawasi .
