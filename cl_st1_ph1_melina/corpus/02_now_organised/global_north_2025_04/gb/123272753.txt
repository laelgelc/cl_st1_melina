At its peak Kfa Aza kibbutz was where around 850 men , women and children lived and cultivated a verdant patch of land growing potatoes and wheat less than two miles east from the border with Gaza . <p> But the tranquil peace of the community was shattered on October 7 , 2023 , when around 250 Hamas militants stormed the complex in the early hours of the morning and unleashed hell on unsuspecting residents .
