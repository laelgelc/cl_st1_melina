<h> Share <h> Pope Francis leaves behind a divided church <p> Pope Francis 's death at the age of 88 was neither untimely nor a great surprise . Having made what appeared to be a miraculous recovery from double pneumonia and kidney failure -- and subsequently turning up at St Peter 's in a poncho just two weeks ago -- Francis appeared to have been granted a new lease of life . Despite this , he left this world on Monday morning , the day after administering the Urbi et Orbi blessing and the second day of the Easter Octave . As times go for a Pope to depart this life , he picked a suitable moment . <p> Many of the accounts of the Pope 's life , following his death , have bordered on the hagiographical -- and , indeed , so they should be . The Pope 's characteristic humility , emphasis on mercy , and strength in criticising those in high office are laudable features of an exceptional pontificate .
