<p> What are Donald Trump 's politics ? For years he was a Democrat but now claims to be a Republican . This matters because of the Republicans ' association with conservatism , demonstrated most notably in recent history by Ronald Reagan . It is worrying because many conservatives ( the lower-case ' c ' embraces many in the Reform party , and in other parties globally ) seem to feel they must express loyalty to President Trump and his policies ( or purported policies , as he is making it up as he goes along ) . Ironically , he feels no loyalty to them , or to any of their ideas unless they suit him . The conservative-minded should , therefore , be concerned about being tarred with his brush . <p> We conservatives should be horrified by this . We tend to believe in free trade and find his tariffs , like him , ignorant and preposterous .
