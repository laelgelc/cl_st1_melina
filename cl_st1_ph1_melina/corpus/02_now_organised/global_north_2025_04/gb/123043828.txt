<p> Gaza 's ongoing humanitarian crisis has created yet another problem for residents -- a desperate shortage of cash . <p> With the Israeli siege blocking supplies of fresh currency into the territory , Palestinians are having to mend their " tattered bills and rusted coins " to buy essential goods , said the Financial Times . <p> Gaza 's last working ATM closed in late 2024 , while most of its banks have either shut due to security fears or been destroyed by Israeli air strikes . Due to the lack of new cash coming into the enclave , much of the remaining currency has ended up in the hands of " powerful wholesalers " trading in goods from Egypt and Israel .
