It is a term named after American policy analyst Joseph Overton , who proposed that the political viability of an idea depends mainly on whether it falls within an acceptability range , rather than on the individual preferences of politicians using the term or concept . <p> According to Overton , the window frames the range of policies that a politician may recommend without appearing too extreme to gain or keep public office @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> What Stadlen said left me pondering what seems to be a chicken and egg situation ; does the media feed the public or does the public feed the media ?
