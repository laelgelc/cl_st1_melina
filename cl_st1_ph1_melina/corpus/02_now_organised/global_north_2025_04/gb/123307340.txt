<p> Are all Jews are rich ? Why do Jewish women shave their hair ? Is it antisemitic to say Zionists control the music industry ? <p> These are some of the questions on non-Jewish teenager 's minds -- questions to which UK Jewish Film 's education manager Rachel Burns must gives answers at the charity 's workshops in inner-city schools across the country . <p> The former English teacher has left children gobsmacked when she shares that even though she attends synagogue , her kids were bar mitzvahed , and the family say prayers at Friday night dinner , she does not believe in God .
