Once on the way up . Once on the way down . So great to be back . " <p> Despite its peaceful title , Namaste is anything but a gentle hour of observational comedy . <p> The funnyman said : " The literal meaning of Namaste is ' I bow to you ' . <p> " It 's what I 've been doing to audiences all my working life . They say ' thank you ' , not ' oh mate , that was hilarious ' . It 's making sense of things . <p> " You give them a glimmer so they have a handle on the chaos . " <p> His show moves between deep political insight and absurdist comedy with a pace and rhythm few performers can match . <p> " The world is in such a terrible state , " he says . " At the end of the day , the purpose of comedy is not just making people laugh . That 's not the end game . The means is comedy , but the end is to actually make sense of what 's going on . " <p> For Djalili , whose roots stretch across the Middle East , comedy has always been personal and political . <p> " People come @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ explains . <p> " A cultural stopgap . " And the journey I take the audience on , I could get cancelled for . But I 've been through this before . " <p> After 9/11 , Djalili found himself abruptly dropped from gigs and viewed with suspicion . <p> " I called my manager on September 12 and said , ' There 's a gig with Jack Dee tomorrow , I presume that 's not going ahead . Someone who is cancelled can joke their way out of it . I was never really cancelled for my jokes -- I was cancelled for who I was . " <p> Now , in an age of endless discourse about cancel culture , he 's embraced a new level of freedom . <p> " I 'm too old to care now , " he shrugs . " There 's a man I know of who was thrown off Twitter , banned from Facebook and had his Instagram taken away . The world literally decided he was finished . He is currently the President of the United States of America . That tells you all you need to know about cancel culture .
