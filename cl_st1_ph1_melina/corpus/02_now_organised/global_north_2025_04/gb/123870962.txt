<h> MPs have a duty to steer clear of sectarian politics <p> Tahir Ali , the Labour MP for Birmingham Hall Green &amp; Moseley , is the embodiment of a politician under the sway of sectarian interests . The streets of his constituency are littered with uncollected rubbish after weeks of bin strikes . Yet Ali , a member of Unite , the union behind the industrial action , has said almost nothing publicly about the dispute . His constituency has the joint worst child poverty rate in Britain , a staggering 55 per cent . <p> His political focus , however , in true sectarian style , has been elsewhere . He has submitted written parliamentary questions on Gaza . Most prominent recently is his public campaign in support of a new international airport in Kashmir , a region disputed by India and Pakistan . At a conference in Birmingham this month , Ali declared : " We will fulfil the demands of the Kashmiri people .
