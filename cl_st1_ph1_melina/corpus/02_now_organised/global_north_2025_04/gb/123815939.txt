" The operations to save these children 's lives are often carried out without anaesthetic due to the ongoing blockade of food and medical supplies being prevented from entering Gaza . " The UK is providing the weapons and logistical support that are being used to blow these children 's limbs off . " What sort of ' decent ' and ' respectable ' society allows this to happen in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Israel right now ! "
