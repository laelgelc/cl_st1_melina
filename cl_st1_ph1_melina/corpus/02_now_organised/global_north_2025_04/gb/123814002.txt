The process begins with a mass , before a long series of secret ballots inside the Sistine Chapel overseen by three cardinals selected as " scrutineers " . <p> Ballot papers are burned in a small , controlled fire inside the chapel . Black smoke billowing from the chimney to inform those outside that a new Pope has still not been selected . <p> White smoke , created by an additive in the fire , tells the world a new Pope has been chosen . The new Pope then chooses his papal name , dons white robe and red slippers , and appears before the crowds on main balcony of St Peter 's Basilica .
