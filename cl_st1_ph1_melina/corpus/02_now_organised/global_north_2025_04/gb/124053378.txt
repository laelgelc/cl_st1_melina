<h> Fifty-nine Marathon runners for 59 hostages held in Gaza <p> Fifty-nine London Marathon runners are currently making their way through the capital , dedicating their race to the 59 hostages still held captive in Gaza . <p> The hostages have been held in the Strip since October 7 , when Hamas terrorists launched an unprecedented attack on southern communities in Israel , murdering some 1,200 people and taking around 250 captive . The hostages have now been held in the Strip for 569 days . <p> A spokesperson for the Hostages and Missing Families Forum UK said : " Fifty-nine incredible runners are running to raise awareness and remind the world of our 59 hostages who are still not home . <p> " Please come join us to show your support for the hostages and their families . Bring your voices , your signs , and your hearts .
