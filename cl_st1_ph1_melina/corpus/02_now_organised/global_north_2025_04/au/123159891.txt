<h> Full program announced for the 2025 Palestinian Film Festival Australia <p> The 13th Palestinian Film Festival Australia is set to return this May with a bold and innovative program of new cinema from and about Palestine , offering a powerful and resonant exploration of resilience , love and freedom in the face of ongoing war and dispossession . <p> Running across five cities -- Sydney , Melbourne , Perth , Brisbane and Canberra -- from 1st to the 11th May , the Festival will once again spotlight a selection of vibrant new voices alongside acclaimed international works , continuing its mission to share Palestinian culture and art with Australia and the world . <p> " This year 's program is guided by a deep emotional pulse , " said Festival Director Naser Shakhtour . " It 's about the power of Palestinian cinema to speak from the rubble , to conjure memory , and to imagine freedom . These films reflect not just the struggles , but the resilience , beauty , and strength of Palestinian culture " . <p> Feature highlights include : <p> The Teacher , a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
