By Louisa Lim . <h> Janak Rogers ' unveils the rage in Australia Fair <p> In the 1920s , Australian children could play a board game called the White Australia Game . Players moved counters that represented white men and people of colour around a map of Australia . It had one simple aim , which also echoed government policy : " Get the Coloured Men Out and the White Men In . " <p> It 's one of the telling details revealed by Australia Fair : The Long Tail of the White Australia Policy ( SBS ) , an ambitious eight-part series interrogating Australia 's racist history of immigration and exclusion . <p> The podcast is uncomfortable but necessary listening as it walks through some of the most shameful passages of Australia 's history . " It 's good and they should really have it and keep out the coloured races , " says the first in clipped British tones . Another expresses support for the policy .
