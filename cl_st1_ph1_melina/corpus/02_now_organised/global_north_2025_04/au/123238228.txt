Both had flown to Israel from Luton on Saturday , Sky News said . <p> " I have made clear to my counterparts in the Israeli government that this is no way to treat British Parliamentarians , and we have been in contact with both MPs tonight to offer our support , " Lammy said . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the ceasefire and negotiations to stop the bloodshed , free the hostages and end the conflict in Gaza , " he said .
