<h> Cliff flag designed to keep conflict ' in the public eye ' <p> Motorists using the Cromwell Gorge road yesterday may have noticed a large Palestinian flag on cliffs overlooking Lake Dunstan . PHOTO : SUPPLIED <p> People driving through the Cromwell Gorge yesterday were given a very visual reminder that the conflict in Gaza continues , with the unfurling of a massive Palestinian flag . <p> A person involved said the 60sqm flag was handmade and had been set up on the cliff side on the other side of Lake Dunstan from State Highway 6 , about 6km from the Cromwell bridge . <p> The person , who asked to remain anonymous for fear of retribution , said they hoped it would inspire people to take action . <p> " We just really want to keep it in the public eye that this is going on . " <p> The only way for people in New Zealand to take action was to pressure the government to take action on the international stage , they said . <p> " Traditionally , we 've had quite a proud history of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ had nuclear free and all sorts of things in the past . However , we 've sort of really lost that voice and particularly under this government , " they said . <p> The Cromwell Gorge was chosen because of the amount of traffic through the area each day . <p> As well as the large flag on the cliff face , smaller flags with signs reading " Free Palestine " , to help people connect the flag with the issue , were placed on the roadside , but were removed by contractors at the request of NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi , which was concerned about driver distraction . <p> A spokesperson said it had no concerns about the flag as it was on land away from the highway . <p> The person involved in the flag display said it was planned to remove the cliff-side flag late yesterday . <p> " It 's just a one-day pop-up show . " <p> There were no further plans to display the flag in Central Otago , but the person would continue their work elsewhere .
