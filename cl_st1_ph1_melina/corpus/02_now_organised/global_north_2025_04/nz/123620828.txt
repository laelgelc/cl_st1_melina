This comes as the Government has just announced a massive hike in defence spending as part of the 2025 Defence Capability Plan ( DCP ) -- $9 billion of new money over the next four years . <p> To put it in perspective , Defence cost New Zealand about $6 billion in 2024-2025 . That is more than we spent on Justice , Corrections , Conservation , and Police combined .
