<h> Wellington Unites For A Joyous Eid Al-Fitr Celebration At Sky Stadium <p> Thousands of Wellingtonians gathered at Sky Stadium on 1 April to celebrate Eid Al-Fitr , marking the end of Ramadan with joy , culture , and community spirit . Families and friends , dressed in stunning traditional Eid outfits , filled the stadium with vibrant colours , laughter , and a deep sense of unity . <p> Hosted by NZEIDDAY Trust , this year 's event brought together a diverse crowd , including Hon Mark Mitchell , the Minister for Ethnic Communities , Members of Parliament , Wellington City Councillors , and thousands from the community , in what has become one of the capital 's most anticipated cultural gatherings . <p> With nearly 40 retail and food stalls , attendees indulged in a feast of delicacies from across the Muslim world .
