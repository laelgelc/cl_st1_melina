<p> " In the Jerusalem neighbourhood of Talpiyot lived an eccentric old man in a large villa . He visited the synagogue religiously whenever a Kiddush ( refreshments served after a Sabbath or festival service ) was served with cakes and kugel . <p> " At the synagogue everyone filled themselves with sweets , but this elderly man took twice as much , filling his pockets and his mouth . His fellow Jews smiled at his anxious hoarding and wondered how a man living in such a large house could be so desperate for a little cake ? Once a curious congregant asked him to explain this very strange behaviour . <p> " The elderly man replied heavily : ' In the concentration camps in Poland , there was never enough bread . I have never liberated myself from my fear that tomorrow there may not be any more food .
