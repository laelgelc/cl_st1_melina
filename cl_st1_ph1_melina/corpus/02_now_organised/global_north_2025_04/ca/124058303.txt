He became co-executive director , along with Yurman , in 2020 -- although the COVID-19 pandemic postponed their in-person management opportunity that summer . <p> The BB Camp located on Town Island , south of Kenora on Lake of the Woods in Northern Ontario , celebrated 70 years in 2024 .
