<p> Apparel retailer Frank and Oak says it is closing nine stores across Quebec , Ontario and British Columbia by May 7 . <p> Court documents show the Montreal-based company has 150 employees , but when asked about how many will lose their job as a result of the closures , spokesperson Elisabeth de Gramont said she did not have that information yet . <p> She says once the liquidation begins , all sales will be final , but the retailer 's e-commerce business will continue to operate . <p> ARTICLE CONTINUES BELOW <p> De Gramont attributed the closures to the legal process Frank and Oak began in December , when it filed a notice of intention , which helps companies restructure and avoid bankruptcy while providing creditors with some compensation . <p> As part of that process , Frank and Oak has said it is facing financial difficulties because COVID-19 weighed on the profitability of new stores , and supply chain issues , including conflict in Gaza , delayed shipments of inventory . <p> Frank and Oak began searching for an investor or buyer in mid-January .
