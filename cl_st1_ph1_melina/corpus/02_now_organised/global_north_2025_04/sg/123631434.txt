<p> Singapore is set to send a medical contingent to Egypt to support Gaza relief efforts , possibly in a few weeks ' time . Comprising personnel from the Singapore Armed Forces and the Health Ministry , they will provide medical care to Palestinians and other civilians from Gaza , who are being treated in Egypt .
