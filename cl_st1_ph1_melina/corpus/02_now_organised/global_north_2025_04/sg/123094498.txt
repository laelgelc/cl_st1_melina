<h> ' I have a very sharply different view on Israel , Palestine from Mr Calvin Cheng ' : Shanmugam <p> SINGAPORE -- Law and Home Affairs Minister K. Shanmugam has made clear that when it comes to the issue of Israel and Palestine , his views are very different from those of Mr Calvin Cheng . <p> Speaking to the media at Masjid Maarof in Jurong West on April 2 , he said , when asked to comment on a post by the former Nominated MP : " I have a very sharply different view on Israel , Palestine from Mr Calvin Cheng . " <p> He added : " I have previously said in November 2023 that Israel 's actions are illegal and oppressive to Palestinians , and that remains my view . " <p> His remarks come after a Facebook post by Mr Cheng on March 13 in which he offered to pay to relocate a group of activists to Gaza , as long as they do not return to Singapore . <p> Mr Shanmugam did not elaborate on the specifics of the post . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Calvin Cheng comments on a variety of matters -- I do n't see all of them . Those that I come across , I can say some I agree with , and some I clearly disagree with , " he said . <p> " What the facts really are , I do n't know . As I said , I have n't seen the blow-by-blow responses . <p> " But I would suggest it is best to keep to the truth , and also be sensitive in making comments on the Israel-Palestine situation , " Mr Shanmugam added . <p> He noted that a police report has been filed and there is a possibility of legal action . <p> Following Mr Cheng 's post , former Singapore Democratic Party chairman Jufrie Mahmood filed a police report on March 28 in which he accused the former NMP of being an Islamophobe , citing a previous example of derogatory language being used to describe a group of madrasah students . <p> Mr Cheng rebutted the accusation , calling it a " false attribution " as the remarks were made by another individual @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ taking legal action against Mr Jufrie , as well as several others , for making defamatory statements . <p> Noting that the police will follow through with the report , Mr Shanmugam said : " I will leave aside whether Mr Jufrie has put out falsehoods , and if he has , then that can be serious .
