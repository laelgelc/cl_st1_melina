<p> SINGAPORE -- Malay/Muslims in Singapore are making a difference today in global and regional events , such as responding to the call to support humanitarian aid efforts for Palestinians and Myanmar 's earthquake victims . <p> For instance , Singapore has so far contributed more than $20 million in humanitarian aid for Palestinians in Gaza , said Social and Family Development Minister Masagos Zulkifli on April 4 . <p> Nearly $2 million of that amount was raised over the last few weeks through the Aid for Gaza campaign organised by local charity Rahmatan Lil Alamin Foundation ( RLAF ) . <p> Speaking in Malay at the annual Hari Raya Get-Together , Mr Masagos said : " This afternoon , RLAF organised a fund-raising collection at all mosques in Singapore after Friday prayers for the earthquake victims in Myanmar . <p> " The collections will be channelled to the Singapore Red Cross . I encourage all of us to contribute generously where we can . " <p> This is one way for Malay/Muslims to work together and uplift one another , and go beyond the boundaries of Singapore , said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Much of this , he added , is due to the " extraordinary efforts " of M3 partners and the community .
