<p> BEIRUT : An Israeli strike on south Beirut killed at least three people on Tuesday ( Apr 1 ) , Lebanon said after Israel announced its second strike on the country 's capital in a fragile four-month truce . <p> The attack came without warning at around 3.30am ( 12.30am GMT ) during the Eid al-Fitr Muslim holiday . It came after Israel struck Beirut 's southern suburbs , a bastion of Hezbollah support , last Friday after issuing an evacuation warning . <p> The Israeli military said " the strike targeted a Hezbollah terrorist who had recently directed Hamas operatives and assisted them in planning a significant and imminent terror attack against Israeli civilians " , in a joint statement with the domestic Shin Bet security agency . <p> Lebanon 's health ministry said the strike killed three people and wounded seven others . <p> An AFP photographer at the scene said the top two floors of a multi-storey building were destroyed and that debris covered the street .
