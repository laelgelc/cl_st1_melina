<p> DUNAMAISE Arts Centre in association with Laois Ireland Palestine Solidarity Campaign ( IPSC ) will present an evening of cinema and solidarity with a special screening of ' No Other Land ' -- the 2025 Oscar Winner for Best Documentary Feature . <p> Created by a Palestinian-Israeli collective , ' No Other Land ' tells the urgent and deeply human story of Basel Adra , a young Palestinian activist documenting the mass expulsion of his community in Masafer Yatta by Israeli forces . <p> Spanning years of resistance and unlikely friendship , the film follows Basel and Israeli journalist Yuval Abraham as they navigate their shared fight for justice amid the brutal realities of occupation . <p> The screening on Tuesday 8 May at 8pm includes a small fundraiser in support of Gaza relief efforts .
