Ramani introduced Tolentino as a worthy recipient of the honour , given her outstanding contributions to discourse through her work at The New Yorker , Jezebel , and The Hairpin , coupled with her poignant essay collection " Trick Mirror : Reflections on Self-Delusion " . Tolentino has emerged as one of the foremost journalists of our time .
