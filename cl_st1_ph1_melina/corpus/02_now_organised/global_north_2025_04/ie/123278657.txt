<h> Thailand sees the Songkran festival generating over $3.7bn in 2025 <p> Thailand 's Songkran festival this year is expected to inject THB134.63bn ( $3.7bn ) into the economy , marking a 4.5% rise from the previous year , the government said on April 6 , The Nation reported . <p> Deputy government spokesperson Anukool Pruksanusak cited a survey by the University of the Thai Chamber of Commerce , which showed the projected figure was up from THB128.83bn in 2024 and nearly double the THB69 bn generated during the pandemic-hit year of 2020 . Only 5% of respondents said they planned to cut back on spending , the lowest figure in over 9 years . <p> Meanwhile , 72.1% reported plans to travel during the traditional Thai New Year , the highest level since 2019 . Despite a powerful earthquake striking the country on March 28 , consumer sentiment appeared largely unaffected . According to the survey , 74.5% of consumers reported no change in their spending habits . About 11.1% planned to reduce spending , while 14.4% expected to increase it . <p> Travel intentions also remained steady , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and 17.1% indicating they would spend more . Many respondents said they would carry out traditional Songkran celebrations but with added caution , avoiding high-rise buildings or potentially unstable structures , Anukool said . He added that business operators reported normal activity in the wake of the quake , with no disruption in sales , hotel bookings , or domestic travel . The revived tourism outlook is expected to further boost consumption during the holiday period . " The government has instructed relevant agencies to inspect the safety of hotels , tourist attractions , and buildings in high-risk areas , with strict safety protocols being implemented , " Anukool said .
