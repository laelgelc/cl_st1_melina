<h> Economic Exodus : Millionaires and tech workers leave Israel in waves following outbreak of war <p> At least 1,700 millionaires have departed Israel in the past year as economic conditions deteriorate following the Gaza war , according to a report by Henley &amp; Partners and South African data intelligence firm New World Wealth cited by Israeli media . <p> The figures quoted by The Daily Sabah reveal that Tel Aviv and Herzliya , which hosted 24,300 millionaires in 2023 , now have approximately 22,600 wealthy residents in 2024 . While the report did not explicitly state reasons for this exodus , previous Israeli media coverage has linked population departures to the economic and security impacts of the ongoing Gaza conflict . <p> Parallel to this wealth migration , Israel 's tech sector is experiencing its first workforce contraction in over a decade , Globes noted . The Israel Innovation Authority reported that between October 2023 and July 2024 , 8,300 tech employees left the country for extended periods of at least one year , representing 2.1% of Israel 's entire tech workforce .
