<p> The Palestine Liberation Organisation has announced the creation of a vice presidency under 89-year-old leader Mahmoud Abbas , who has not specified a successor . <p> The PLO Central Council 's decision came as Mr Abbas seeks greater relevance and a role in post-war planning for the Gaza Strip after having been largely sidelined by the ongoing Israel-Hamas war . <p> After a two-day meeting , the council voted to create the role of vice chairman of the PLO executive committee . <p> This position would also be referred to as the vice president of the State of Palestine , which the Palestinians hope will one day receive full international recognition . <p> The expectation is that whoever holds that role would be the front-runner to succeed Mr Abbas -- though it is unclear when or exactly how it would be filled .
