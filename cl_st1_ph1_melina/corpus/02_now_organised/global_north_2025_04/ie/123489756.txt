<h> Brighid McLaughlin : The legacy of trauma lives on in Belfast , but the once-deep wounds are slowly beginning to heal <p> A meeting with a tour guide convinced me that a still divided city can hope for a brighter future <p> Brighid McLaughlin with Lee in Belfast <p> Brighid McLaughlin <p> Today at 03:30 <p> Three weeks ago I was invited to speak at Imagine , Belfast 's Festival of Ideas and Politics . The fine attendance included a Church of Ireland minister and his wife , a Chicago lady involved in the Gaza-Ireland support network , two Protestants from Stormont , some ladies from Armagh who belonged to a book club , a man who worked in Glenveagh National Park , two solicitors , a self-confessed former Provo and , to my delight , several young people interested in writing . <p> One thing is for certain -- 20 years ago , these people would not have been in the same room .
