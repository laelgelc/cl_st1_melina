He had been elected in 2013 following the resignation of Pope Benedict XVI for ill health . <p> On April 20 , Easter Sunday , the Pope had written the special homily but was too frail to deliver it , giving it to one of his clergy to read aloud instead . He used it to call for more freedom and to condemn war . <p> " There can be no peace without freedom of religion , freedom of thought , freedom of expression and respect for the views of others , " said his Easter delivery . <p> " What a great thirst for death , for killing we see in the many conflicts raging in different parts of the world . Later he met American Vice-President JD Vance for a brief audience - with the potential for awkwardness as Francis had been an outspoken critic of Vance and Donald Trump 's deportation policies . <p> It was one of the first times since his five-week hospital stay that Francis had been seen in public .
