<h> Greenpeace boss arrested after group dumps blood-red dye into US Embassy pond to protest war in Gaza <p> The head of Britain 's Greenpeace branch was arrested alongside five other activists after they poured 80 gallons of blood-red dye into a pond at the US Embassy while protesting the war in Gaza Thursday . <p> Will McCallum , the environmental campaign group 's UK chief , arrived at the embassy with his group disguised as delivery riders on bikes , Greenpeace said . <p> The protesters could be seen hauling a red container with the label , " Stop Arming Israel , " which they dumped into the pond outside the embassy . <p> Areeba Hamid , co-executive director at Greenpeace , said that the dye was biodegradable and designed to wash away naturally . Greenpeace United Kingdom/AFP via Getty Images <p> Areeba Hamid , co-executive director at Greenpeace , defended the vandalism as a clear message calling on the US to stop providing weapons for Israel to use in Gaza . <p> " We took this action because US weapons continue to fuel an indiscriminate war that 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ blasted to rubble , and tens of thousands of Palestinian lives obliterated , " she said in a statement . <p> " As the biggest supplier of weapons to the Israeli military , the US government bears a heavy responsibility for the horrors unfolding in Gaza , " Hamid added . <p> More than 50,000 people have been killed in Gaza since the war began following the Oct. 7 , 2023 , terrorist attack that claimed more than 1,200 lives in Israel . <p> Israel has repeatedly refuted the estimate from the Hamas-run ministry of health , which does not differentiate between civilians and terrorists , but the numbers are backed by the United Nations . <p> Greenpeace found itself in hot water last month when a North Dakota jury found the group liable for hundreds of millions of dollars in damages for defamation and other claims brought by a pipeline company in connection with protests against the Dakota Access Pipeline .
