<p> WASHINGTON ( AP ) -- A crackdown on foreign students is alarming colleges , who say the Trump administration is using new tactics and vague justifications to push some students out of the country . <p> College officials worry the new approach will keep foreigners from wanting to study in the U.S. <p> Students stripped of their entry visas are receiving orders from the Department of Homeland Security to leave the country immediately -- a break from past practice that often permitted them to stay and complete their studies . <p> Some students have been targeted over pro-Palestinian activism or criminal infractions -- or even traffic violations .
