<h> Louis S. Texter <p> Louis was born Sept. 15 , 1935 , in Campbell , a son of Gaza J. and Christina Lisko Texter . <p> He was a 1953 graduate of Campbell Memorial High School . <p> Louis joined the U.S. Marine Corps on Aug. 12 , 1953 , and served until Aug. 11 , 1956 . <p> Louis married the former Florence Tarr on May 23 , 1959 . They were married until her passing on May 23 , 2014 . <p> Louis retired from Borden Dairy and also worked for Youngstown Sheet and Tube and Republic Rubber . <p> Louis was of the Catholic faith and a member of St. Joseph the Provider until its closing . He then joined Christ the Good Shepherd Parish . <p> He was a member of American Legion Post 560 . <p> Louis was a wonderful husband , father , grandfather , great-grandfather , brother , uncle and friend . Whenever you needed something , he was always there , no matter what . His great-grandson was his best friend . <p> Louis will be deeply missed and always remembered @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Samuel Horvath Jr. , both of Campbell . <p> He was preceded in death by his parents ; his wife , Florence ; his daughter , Christine Texter ; and his son , Kevin Texter . <p> The Texter family has entrusted Louis ' care to the Wasko Funeral Home in Campbell . <p> Calling hours will be 9:30 to 10:30 a.m . Thomas Kraszewski . <p> Louis will be laid to rest in St. John the Baptist Roman Catholic Cemetery . <p> Please visit www.waskofamily.com to view this obituary and to leave condolences with the family .
