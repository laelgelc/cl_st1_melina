<p> Clairo performed at Coachella last night ( Saturday , April 12 ) and her set featured a very special guest : Bernie Sanders . The U.S. senator from Vermont took the stage to introduce the singer and her band , making an impassioned call for youth activism and political engagement . " I 'm here to introduce Clairo not just because they are a great band , " Sanders said in his speech . " I 'm here because Clairo has used her prominence to fight for women 's rights , to try to end the terrible , brutal war in Gaza , where thousands of women and children are being killed .
