<h> IDF announces soldier killed in northern Gaza <h> Two additional soldiers were severely injured as well . <p> An IDF tank driver from the 79th Battalion in the ' Machatz ' Brigade , was killed in combat in northern Gaza , the IDF announced Thursday . <p> The soldier had not been named by press time but his family was been notified.Additionally , an officer in the Yahalom Unit and a reservist in the ' Machatz ' Brigade were severely wounded in the incident . <p> The soldiers were evacuated to a hospital to receive medical treatment , and their families have been notified . <h> IDF death toll since October 7 massacre <p> According to the IDF 's tally , this death raises the total of soldiers killed since October 7 of last year to 849 . <p> Some 408 of this number were killed since the start of the IDF 's ground offensive in the Gaza Strip on October 27 of that year .
