It has not said how long its forces will remain in its neighbors ' territories , where groups hostile to Israel are present . <p> But there are signs that Israel appears prepared to remain indefinitely , a visual analysis by The New York Times has found . <p> The military has set up watchtowers , prefabricated housing modules , roads and communication infrastructure , according to local residents and the United Nations .
