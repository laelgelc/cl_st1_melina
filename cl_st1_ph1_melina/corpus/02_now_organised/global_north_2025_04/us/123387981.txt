<p> U.S. to monitor immigrants ' social media for antisemitismEffective immediately , the government says it will begin screening immigrant social media for activity that officials think indicates support for antisemitism . <p> U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services has announced it will begin screening immigrants ' social media for evidence of antisemitic activity as grounds for denying immigration benefit requests . The screenings will affect people applying for permanent residence status as well as foreigners affiliated with educational institutions .
