Vatican Media/Getty Images <p> Pope Francis died of a cerebral stroke and heart failure , the head of the Vatican 's health department , Dr. Andrea Arcangeli , confirmed Monday , April 21 . <p> Francis , who was 88 , suffered a stroke and slipped into a coma before suffering an " irreversible cardiocirculatory collapse . " Arcangeli also noted some of the pope 's longstanding health issues , including high blood pressure , Type II diabetes , and a history of acute respiratory failure . <p> Francis had struggled with respiratory issues throughout his life .
