Catholicism was a part of my life in a structural sense : church most Sundays , Catholic class most Tuesdays and grace before dinner . <p> One day in early spring , after walking slowly to class under the beech trees that lined the streets of my hometown , we sat down , opened our booklets and learned that if you did n't believe in God -- our God -- you were going to hell .
