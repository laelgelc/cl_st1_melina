<p> On April 2 , Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu described Qatar as " a complex country . " The epithet seems a trifle inadequate . Qatar is close to mirroring Winston Churchill 's famous description of Russia -- " a riddle wrapped in a mystery inside an enigma . " <p> Dubbed " the wild card of the Middle East , " Qatar makes for an intriguing case study . This stand-alone gas-rich Gulf state -- the wealthiest country in the world on a per capita basis -- is best known to the general public as having won the hosting rights for the 2022 FIFA World Cup in somewhat dubious circumstances . <p> Qatar has long pursued a foreign policy that appears self-contradictory to the world in general , and positively infuriating to its Arab neighbors . While offering itself as a key US ally in the Middle East , it has also consistently backed hardline Islamists -- from Hamas in the Gaza Strip to the Muslim Brotherhood in Egypt , to wild-eyed jihadists in Assad 's Syria . <p> " We do n't do enemies , " a one-time @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to everyone .
