<p> For 12 hours last Thursday , the lookout post at the Urim base in the Gaza periphery was left with no guard and no security . As a result , 60 lookouts at the outpost were left unprotected , and the base 's gate was open and unsupervised , The Jerusalem Post confirmed Thursday . <p> In footage obtained by the N12 news site , the outpost can be seen abandoned , meaning anyone could theoretically enter without hindrance . <p> " Our base was completely abandoned -- without a guard and without a security force other than us , who were not all armed , " one of the female lookouts told N12 . <p> " We contacted the battalion headquarters , but for 12 hours , we received no response , during which time anyone could enter and exit freely . Finally , two reserve volunteers were sent to us -- fighters who do important work but can not sufficiently secure dozens of female lookouts and guard the entire outpost .
