<p> For the second time in less than 18 months , California 's Commission on Judicial Performance has publicly disciplined former San Diego Superior Court Judge Howard Shore , this time for making " in-court comments reflecting the appearance of racial bias " and misleading statements about his prior discipline . <p> The commission issued the public admonishment last week , a little more than two months after Shore retired .
