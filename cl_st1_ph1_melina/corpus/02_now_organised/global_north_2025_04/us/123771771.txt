<p> Thousands of Christians gathered in the cavernous Church of the Holy Sepulchre for a centuries-old Holy Fire ceremony . <p> Holding unlit candles , they packed into the sprawling 12th-century basilica built on the site where , according to tradition , Jesus was crucified and buried . <p> In near-total darkness , the Greek patriarch entered the Holy Edicule and emerged with two lit candles . The flame was passed from one candle to the next , the light overcoming the darkness in the rotunda . The flame was later transferred to Orthodox communities in other countries on special flights . <p> Eastern Orthodox Christians believe the light miraculously appears inside the Holy Edicule , built on the traditional site of Jesus 's tomb , while sceptics going back to the Middle Ages have dismissed it as a carnival trick for the masses . <p> Either way , the ceremony , which goes back at least 1,200 years , is a sight to behold .
