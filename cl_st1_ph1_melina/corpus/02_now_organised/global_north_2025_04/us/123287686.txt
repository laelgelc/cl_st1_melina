The Jewish state was hit with a 17% tariff after the White House found that the country has in place a 33% tariff rate on U.S. imports . <p> In footage of the meeting posted on X , Lutnick and Netanyahu are seen greeting each other warmly before the two sides discussed trade policy . No details on the meeting were made available , but Netanyahu 's office called it " warm , friendly and productive .
