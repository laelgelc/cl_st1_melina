Oh no . <p> I woke to a world without Pope Francis . That was the last thing this sad old world needed . <p> The first pope from the global south spent his final Easter defending the defenseless who are under attack . <p> Francis put his final day to good use . He celebrated one last Easter . He delivered one last homily ; read aloud by someone else because he had so few breaths left .
