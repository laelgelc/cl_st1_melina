<p> The New Yorker received three National Magazine Awards on Thursday , along with two ASME Awards for Design , Photography , and Illustration -- the most of any publication this year . The prizes , conferred as The New Yorker celebrates its hundredth anniversary , bring the total number of National Magazine Awards won by the magazine to fifty-nine . The awards are given by the American Society of Magazine Editors ( ASME ) , in association with Columbia University 's School of Journalism . <p> The writer Parul Sehgal.Photograph by David Surowiecki <p> In the Reviews and Criticism category , the prize went to a trio of pieces by the former staff writer Parul Sehgal . In examining books with varying styles and subjects -- about divorce , medical uncertainty , and the Israeli-Palestinian conflict -- Sehgal anatomized each within its literary and historical context , demonstrating a particular alertness to how the authors used or distorted language . For " A Safe Haven for Late Abortions , " the photographer Maggie Shannon documented efforts by a midwife and a physician to open and run an abortion clinic in Maryland , to which some patients had travelled because the procedure was illegal in their home states . The film , which received an Oscar nomination in January , uses footage from surveillance and police body cameras to examine the killing of Harith Augustus , a Black barber in Chicago who was shot by police on a city street . <p> In addition , The New Yorker earned two ASME Awards for Design , Photography , and Illustration , which recognize visual excellence . McGinley 's portraits of Yang received the ASME for Best Entertainment and Celebrity Photography , while the prize for Best Animated Illustrations went to the gleaming , unnerving figures illustrating " The Mormon TikTok Moms Are All of Us , " a critical look at a Hulu reality series .
