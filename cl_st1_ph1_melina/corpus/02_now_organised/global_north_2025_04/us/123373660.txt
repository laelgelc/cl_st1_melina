New York City , Chicago and Los Angeles received similar notices from the task force , which also has taken aim at Harvard and nine other universities . <p> The Department of Justice is sending a member of its Civil Rights Division to Boston to discuss " alleged harassment and discrimination against Jewish people " at schools in the city .
