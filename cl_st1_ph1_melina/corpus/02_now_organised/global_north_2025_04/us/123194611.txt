<h> Combat Intelligence battalions that have yet to receive personal weapons are expected to do so in the coming months . <p> Female IDF observers in Combat Intelligence Battalion 636 in the West Bank were issued personal weapons this week as part of the IDF 's ongoing efforts to implement lessons learned from the events of October 7 , N12 reported on Friday . <p> The initiative was announced as early as last year . The process of distributing personal weapons to female observers across all sectors is progressing , the report noted . <p> Combat Intelligence battalions that have yet to receive these weapons are expected to do so in the coming months . <p> In early December , the IDF 's Ground Forces Command released a document detailing the lessons learned from the October 7 massacre , which emphasizes strategies for improving combat capabilities , including enhanced protection for female observers . <p> As part of these improvements , the IDF has decided to move observation posts further from the borders in the north and Gaza . <h> IDF observers during training . The gate remained open , and no security personnel were present . <p> For 12 hours , 60 female observers were left without protection , as the base 's gate was left unsupervised.
