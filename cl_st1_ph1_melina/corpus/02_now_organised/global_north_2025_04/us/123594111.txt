<h> The festival will include premiere screenings of over 100 documentary films from Israel and abroad , including 36 new Israeli films . <p> Docaviv , the Tel Aviv International Documentary Film Festival , which will take place this year from May 22 to May 31 at the Tel Aviv Cinematheque and other venues around the city , announced that its opening-night movie will be Brandon Kramer 's documentary , Holding Liat , which is about one of the hostages kidnapped on October 7 , Liat Atzili . <p> Holding Liat , which premiered at the Berlin International Film Festival , where it won the Best Documentary Award , tells the story of how the family of Atzili , who returned home in the first hostage deal , coped with the ordeal . <p> Her partner , Aviv Atzili , was murdered , and his body is still being held in Gaza .
