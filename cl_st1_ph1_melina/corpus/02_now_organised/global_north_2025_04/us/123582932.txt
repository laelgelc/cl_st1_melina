Mahdawi had applied for U.S. citizenship and was arrested at an immigration appointment . <p> Luna Droubi , Mahdawi 's lawyer , said in an interview with Here &amp; Now that she has reason to believe that Mahdawi was set up to be arrested at his immigration appointment . <p> " Mr. Mahdawi wants to become a U.S. citizen .
