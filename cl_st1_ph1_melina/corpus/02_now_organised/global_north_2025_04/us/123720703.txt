<h> Richmond speaks up : Letters to the editor for the week of Apr. 18 , 2025 <p> Homelessness is a problem that disproportionately impacts LGBTQ+ youth , with 28% of LGBTQ+ youth and 38% of transgender youth experiencing homelessness at some point in their lives . While several nonprofit organizations exist to combat the issue , contradictions at the policy level make public life increasingly difficult for LGBTQ+ youth who do not have the support of their families of origin . Familial rejection is a major contributing factor , pushing youth onto the streets where they face increased risks of violence compared to other unhoused populations . <p> While the commonwealth passed legislation in 2020 prohibiting the practice of conversion therapy , recent attacks on providers of gender-affirming care make existing as a trans person increasingly unacceptable in our society .
