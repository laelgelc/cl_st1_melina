" Blind Spot " documents the surge in antisemitism at American colleges and universities in the wake of Hamas ' Oct. 7 , 2023 , attack on Israel . Interviews with dozens of Jewish students at Yale , Harvard , Columbia and other institutions attest to the threats and harassment to which many of them @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> One audience member conceded that the film preaches to the choir , and , indeed , " Blind Spot " makes no pretense of impartiality : The viewer learns that antisemitism is rampant and that the Oct. 7 attack was a savage atrocity . There 's no mention of the 50,000 Palestinians who have died in Israel 's attacks on Gaza . <p> Another asked if antisemitism is a problem at Southwestern U. The film 's faculty sponsor said , Yes , it 's a " serious " problem . Others said that SWU faculty members had signed on to antisemitic statements and asserted that they would not allow Zionists to sit in their classes . <p> Why were n't more Jewish students in the audience for the film ? Someone said that they were afraid to venture out of their dorm rooms for fear of being harassed and attacked for being Jews . <p> In short , our local university is a hotbed of antisemitism , just like Harvard , Yale and Columbia . <p> But is it really ? <p> Have Jewish students at SWU been @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ antisemitic graffiti been spray-painted on campus ? On the contrary . <p> The problem is that the first casualty of a controversial conflict is always nuance : Accusations of antisemitism often ignore the differences among antisemitism , which is a hatred of Jews simply because they 're Jews ; anti-Zionism , which argues that the imposed establishment of a country based on religion in the Middle East was n't the best idea to begin with ; and anti-Netanyahuism , which is a word I made up to describe disapproval of Israel 's current attacks in Gaza , which are arguably immoral , illegal , ineffective and , probably , counterproductive . <p> But there 's another wrinkle : On Apr. 2 , the New York Times reported on Rabbi Sharon Brous , who expresses an emerging skepticism among Jews about the Trump administration 's Taskforce to Combat Antisemitism .
