<h> The two nations are only a few hundred kilometers apart , but stand in stark contrast to each other . <p> The recent developments in the affair known as ' Qatargate ' have brought to the public eye one of the most striking relationships in the Middle East : that of Egypt and Qatar . One claim heard several times over the past few days accused that the Qataris had an interest in ' contaminating ' relations between Israel and Egypt , perhaps over the issue of mediation in Gaza and other ideological and geopolitical differences . <p> Regardless of the veracity of these claims , to understand why they are even possible , one must attempt to understand the history of relations between countries and the ideologies they espouse and drive .
