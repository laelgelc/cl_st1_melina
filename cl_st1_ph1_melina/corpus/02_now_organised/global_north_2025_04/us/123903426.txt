<p> TEL AVIV , Israel ( AP ) -- Antisemitic attacks have increased dramatically since the war in Gaza began , though the numbers declined slightly last year from a peak reached immediately after the Oct. 7 , 2023 , Hamas attack against Israel , according to an annual report about global antisemitism from Tel Aviv University . <p> " Contrary to popular belief , the report 's findings indicate that the wave of antisemitism did not steadily intensify due to the war in Gaza and the humanitarian disaster there , " said Uriya Shavit , the chief editor of the report . <p> He said there was a sharp increase in attacks against Jews from October to December 2023 , but that in 2024 , the number of incidents declined in almost every country around the world . <p> " The sad truth is that antisemitism reared its head at the moment when the Jewish state appeared weaker than ever and under existential threat , " he said . In Italy , there were 877 antisemitic incidents in 2024 , compared with 454 in 2023 , and 241 in 2022 . <p> The number of attacks also rose slightly in the U.S. and Argentina , but not as significantly . <p> The report also condemned the lack of punishment for perpetrators of antisemitic attacks . Many incidents are not reported to the police , and of those , very few result in arrests . For the first time in nearly 50 years of compiling data , the organization said that Israel-related incidents , including chants , speeches and signs at rallies protesting Israeli policies , made up more than half of the reported incidents in 2024 . <p> The ADL 's findings add to the intense , divisive debate among American Jews -- and others -- over the extent to which vehement criticism of Israeli policies and of Zionism , or the belief in Israel as the Jewish nation state , should be considered antisemitic.
