<h> Report : Waltz team set up 20 Signal chats on global issues <p> National Security Advisor Mike Waltz gestures today , before President Donald Trump ( not pictured ) delivers remarks at the White House in Washington , D.C. <p> WASHINGTON &gt;&gt; The team led by President Donald Trump 's national security adviser , Mike Waltz , set up at least 20 group chats on the commercial Signal app to coordinate official work on issues involving China , Gaza , Middle East policy , Africa and Europe , Politico reported today . <p> The news outlet cited four unidentified people who were personally added to Signal chats .
