<p> The IDF has killed 250 Hamas terrorists since it renewed hostilities in Gaza on March 18 , IDF Chief Spokesperson Brig. -Gen . Effie Defrin said on Thursday in his first press conference since taking office on March 27 . <p> Defrin also said that , more broadly , the military has struck over 600 targets in Gaza since March 18 , including 60 since a stronger invasion push on Wednesday . <p> He said these attacks , mostly from the air force , would pave the way for even more ground troops to enter deeper into Gaza . <p> Further , the new IDF spokesperson said that the military , often with the help of the air force , had assassinated 12 senior Hamas officials . <p> Describing the new actions taken on Wednesday , Defrin said , " We stepped up the invasion to a new level to serve the goals of the war : returning the hostages and dismantling Hamas 's military and political rule .
