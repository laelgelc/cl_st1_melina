Real Estate : A Love Story by Anat Malz , which won the Israeli Feature Film Competition at the Haifa International Film Festival in January , is a funny , bittersweet look at the pressures facing a young couple who are apartment hunting in Haifa after they are priced out of their beloved Tel Aviv . The movie uses their apartment-hunting journey to tell a story about their relationship -- a love story but a troubled one -- and is about the limits and compromises we all have to make in our lives .
