<h> Letter : Protesting plight of Palestinians is n't the same as backing Hamas <p> It 's distressing to witness international college students and professors targeted by ICE for speaking up for the innocent Palestinian victims in Gaza . <p> Nearly 70% of the war dead have been women and children . In no way does a call for the end of indiscriminate bombing and the provision of basic humanitarian aid in Gaza equate with support for Hamas . <p> In effect , most Palestinians are now hostages themselves as war rages between Hamas and Israel . It takes courage and empathy to speak up for the powerless when they are suffering .
