It will be the first summit of the expanded BRICS . <p> The BRICS cooperation mechanism is an important platform for cooperation among emerging markets and developing countries . Since its founding in 2006 , the mechanism has continuously strengthened its cohesiveness , consolidated its foundation for cooperation , expanded its scope of cooperation and improved its influence . It has become a positive and stable force for good in international affairs . <p> The mechanism has developed a multi-level framework for practical cooperation in dozens of areas , including economy and trade , finance , agriculture , culture , education , health , think tank and sister city development , becoming a constructive force in promoting global growth , improving global governance and promoting greater democracy in international relations . <p> By Jan. 1 this year , BRICS has expanded the number of its member countries to 10 , which account for nearly half of the global population and 1/5 of the global trade .
