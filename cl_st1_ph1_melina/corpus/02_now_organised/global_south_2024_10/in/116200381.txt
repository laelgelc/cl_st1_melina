His death is seen as a pivotal moment in the ongoing conflict , which has resulted in significant casualties on both sides . <p> By CNBCTV18.com October 19 , 2024 , 12:57:43 PM IST ( Published ) <p> 2 Min Read <p> An autopsy conducted by Israeli authorities has revealed that Yahya Sinwar , a prominent Hamas leader , died from a gunshot wound to the head . Chen Kugel , chief pathologist at the Israel National Centre of Forensic Medicine , confirmed the findings during an interview with CNN . <p> He said that Sinwar 's body was received with a severed finger , which the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) had removed for DNA analysis . Kugel expressed full confidence in the body 's identification and the cause of death , also mentioning additional injuries , including those from a tank shell . <p> The IDF released footage showing a tank firing at the building where Sinwar 's body was discovered , indicating @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ southern Gaza by the IDF 's 828 Brigade , operating in the Tel al-Sultan area of Rafah . <p> In videos shared by the IDF , two soldiers were seen standing next to a body they claimed to be Sinwar 's , which had one finger missing . However , CNN 's analysis showed earlier footage of Sinwar with all five fingers intact . <p> Sinwar was regarded as a key architect of the October 7 attacks on Israel , which resulted in around 1,200 fatalities , predominantly among civilians . Following a year-long manhunt , he was killed on October 16 , with his death announced the next day . <p> In footage captured shortly before his death , Sinwar is seen in a damaged apartment , injured and attempting to fight back against an approaching drone . <p> A statement from Hamas commemorated him as a hero , asserting that his death would only strengthen the movement 's resolve and that they would not concede to conditions for a ceasefire with Israel .
