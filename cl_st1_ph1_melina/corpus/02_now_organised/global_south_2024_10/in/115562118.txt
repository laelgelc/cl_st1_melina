<h> Will Escalation in the Middle East Harm the Indian Economy ? <h> Iran is nearer to India . Oil imports from the Middle East are quite critical for the Indian economy . <p> The Israel-Hamas war completes one year today without either side achieving its objectives . The war has now engulfed Lebanon as well . Israel has , besides pounding Beirut and South Lebanon with missiles and bombs , killed Hezbollah 's chief and other key operatives . <p> Iran jumped into the fray and launched more than 200 missiles at Israel , with some reaching their targets . Israel has promised to retaliate at the time of its choosing . There are serious apprehensions that Israel may successfully hit Iran 's oil and nuclear facilities . <p> Compared to Israel , Iran is closer to India . Oil imports from the Middle East are quite critical for the Indian economy . Millions of Indian labourers work in the region and send billions of dollars in remittances . <p> If the tensions escalate into a full-blown Israel-Iran war , how much is the Indian economy likely to be impacted ? <h> Oil <p> Globally , Iran produces @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ exports about two percent of global oil consumption .
