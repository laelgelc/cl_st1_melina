by United Nations Children 's Fund ( UNICEF ) on World Polio Day which is observed on October 24 . <p> In these fragile and conflict-affected countries , polio cases have more than doubled over the past five years . <p> The disease mainly affects children under five years of age but an unvaccinated person of any age can contract the disease . <p> In these countries , routine childhood immunisation rates have decreased from 75 to 70 per cent , significantly lower than the 95 percent required for the community immunity . <p> A global decline in childhood immunisation has led to an
