<h> Israeli Airstrike on Gaza School Kills 28 Civilians <p> GAZA : An Israeli airstrike on Thursday afternoon targeted a school in the Gaza Strip , killing at least 28 Palestinians and injuring over 54 , according to the Palestinian Health Ministry . The school was sheltering displaced civilians . <p> A destroyed locality in occupied Gaza ( Palestine ) in October 2023 . The destruction was in retaliation by Israel to the raids carried out inside the country by Hamas militants who control Gaza . ( File Image ) <p> The Palestine Red Crescent Society ( PRCS ) reported via social media platform X that its teams responded to the attack on Rafida School , located near the PRCS headquarters in Deir al-Balah . Emergency crews recovered the bodies , including women and children , with some victims severely dismembered , as reported by Xinhua news agency . <p> In a statement , the Israeli military claimed responsibility for what they called a " precise strike " on a command center inside the school compound , which they said was being used by militants to plan attacks on Israeli forces .
