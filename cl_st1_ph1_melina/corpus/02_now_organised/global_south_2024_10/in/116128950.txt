<h> Sinwar killed ; Hezbollah to escalate ; US hopes for ceasefire ; Netanyahu : War until hostages return <p> ET Online 18 Oct 2024 , 05:02 PM IST <p> Israeli forces have killed Yahya Sinwar , the Hamas leader behind the October 7 attack on Israel , during a gun battle in Gaza . Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has vowed to continue the war until all Hamas hostages are freed , despite Sinwar 's death . US officials labelled Sinwar the " chief obstacle " to a ceasefire , raising hopes that his death could lead to new negotiations . However , Hezbollah has vowed to escalate its confrontation with Israel , signalling the potential for a broader conflict . Meanwhile , the families of hostages have expressed mixed emotions , emphasising that their true relief lies in the return of their loved ones .
