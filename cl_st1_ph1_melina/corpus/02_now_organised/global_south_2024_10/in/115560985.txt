<p> NEW YORK , - Oil prices settled more than 3% higher on Monday , with Brent surpassing $80 per barrel for the first time since August as the increased risk of a region-wide Middle East war jolted investors out of record bearish positions amassed last month . <p> Brent crude futures rose by $2.88 , or 3.7% , to settle at $80.93 per barrel . U.S. West Texas Intermediate futures advanced by $2.76 , or 3.7% , to $77.14 per barrel . <p> Last week , Brent rose more than 8% and WTI advanced by more than 9% week-on-week , the most in more than a year , after Iran 's Oct. 1 missile barrage against Israel raised concerns that the response from Israel would aim at Tehran 's oil infrastructure . <p> If that happens , oil prices could rise by another $3 to $5 per barrel , said Andrew Lipow , president of Lipow Oil Associates . <p> Rockets fired by Iran-backed Hezbollah hit Israel 's third-largest city , Haifa , early on Monday .
