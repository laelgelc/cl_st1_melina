But in truth , most of their time was spent then meeting the needs of their communities and building a movement that could transform the lives of poor Black people . The Panthers ' survival programs were always meant to be launchpads for a wider movement to end poverty and systemic racism . <p> Indeed , the Panthers consciously called out the grim paradox of a nation that claimed there was never enough money to fight poverty at home , while it spent billions of dollars fighting distant wars on the poor of Vietnam , Cambodia , and Laos .
