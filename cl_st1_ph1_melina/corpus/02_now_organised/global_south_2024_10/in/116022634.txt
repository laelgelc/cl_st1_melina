<h> Opinion : Netanyahu and US election <p> Benjamin Netanyahu , the Israeli Prime Minister , has lambasted Emmanuel Macron , the President of France , for the latter 's call for an arms embargo on Israel . Bibi , as Netanyahu is popularly called , thinks that Israel will win ' with or without support " . This statement is significant . Bibi thinks he can show audacity as he can easily pressurise the Western bloc , particularly its leader the US to fall in line . <p> For America , this may have political repercussions in its election year . The US presidential election is just a month away and the complex Israel-America relation may have serious effects on its results with Arab-Americans , largely known to be supporters of the Democratic Party , veering away from Kamala Harris . <p> Which way their support will go is difficult to tell . It is unlikely that they would vote for Donald Trump who is a self-declared supporter of Israel . Their helplessness is palpable and understandable as they can not deny the centrality of the existence of Israel in the US foreign policy .
