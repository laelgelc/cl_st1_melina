After Iran , Saudi Arabia , and the United Arab Emirates ( UAE ) joined it in 2023 , the alliance that initially referred to Brazil , Russia , India , China , and South Africa gradually became a bigger body . <p> Russian President Vladimir Putin at the BRICS closing session in the southwestern Russian city of Kazan said all countries joining the bloc " share similar aspirations and values and a vision of new democratic global order .
