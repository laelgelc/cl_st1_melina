<p> A Palestinian reacts as a child is carried from the rubble of a building after an airstrike in Khan Younis , Gaza Strip . -- Photo:AP <p> Deir Al-Balah : An apparent Israeli airstrike early Sunday killed at least 18 people in central Gaza , Palestinian medical officials said . The strike hit a mosque sheltering displaced people near the al-Aqsa Martyrs hospital in the town of Deir al-Balah , the hospital said in a statement . <p> An Associated Press journalist counted the bodies at the hospital morgue . Hospital records showed that the dead were all men . Another two men were critically wounded , the hospital said . The Israeli military did not immediately comment about the strike on the mosque . <h> Also Read <p> The latest strikes add to the mounting Palestinian death toll in Gaza , which is now nearing 42,000 according to the Palestinian Health Ministry . The Ministry does not differentiate between civilian and militant deaths , but many of the dead were women and children .
