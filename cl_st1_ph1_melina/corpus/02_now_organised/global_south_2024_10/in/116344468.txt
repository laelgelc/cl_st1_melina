This will not deter me or the State of Israel from continuing our just war against our enemies in order to secure our future . " <p> The Israeli Prime Minister further said that anyone who tries to harm Israel 's citizens will pay a " heavy price " . <p> " We will continue to eliminate the terrorists and those who dispatch them .
