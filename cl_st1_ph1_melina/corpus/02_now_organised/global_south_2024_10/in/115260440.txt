<p> Chances are higher than ever that Israel will strike strategic targets in Iran in the coming days . On Tuesday , Iran attacked Israel with hundreds of missiles , most of which were intercepted with no major casualties reported . Iran said this was to avenge the killing of Hezbollah chief Hassan Nasrallah , IRGC commander Abbas Nilforoushan , and Hamas principal Ismail Haniyeh . In turn , Israel has said Iran will pay , and Iran has also vowed a " crushing " response to any Israeli attack . Violence could spiral quickly now , as the ripple effects of the Oct 7 Hamas attack on Israel , and Israel 's war on Gaza , are proving harder and harder to contain.The confrontation between Iran and Israel has been building for years , and international diplomacy has tried to keep it below the level of an all-out regional war . Iran officially rejects Israel 's right to exist and sponsors anti-Israel armed groups . Israel 's PM Netanyahu has always seen Iran as a more serious threat than the Palestinian issue , which he tried until recently to sideline .
