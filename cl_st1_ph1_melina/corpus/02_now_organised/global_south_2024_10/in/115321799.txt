<p> Aziz Salha , who took part in the Ramallah lynching in Oct 2000 , was eliminated in an IDF strike <p> Israel on Thursday stated that one of its airstrikes in Gaza killed Abdel-Aziz Salha , a Palestinian involved in the 2000 killing of two Israeli soldiers by a mob in the West Bank . Salha had participated in the storming of a Palestinian police station in Ramallah , where the two Israeli reservists , who had accidentally entered Palestinian Authority-controlled territory , were killed . Salha became infamous for waving his bloodied hands from the station window , an image that became symbolic of the second intifada , the Palestinian uprising against Israeli rule . <p> Taking to X , the IDF wrote : " We eliminated terrorist Aziz Salha , who took part in the Ramallah lynching in Oct. 2000 , in the area of Deir El Balah in central Gaza . Salha took part in the brutal lynching of Sergeant First Class ( Res . ) Yosef Avrahami and Corporal ( Res . ) Vadim Norzhich in Ramallah in 2000 . " <p> The killing of the reservists marked a major escalation in Israeli-Palestinian tensions as the peace process of the 1990s collapsed . <p> The military said Thursday that Salha was killed in an overnight strike on the central Gaza town of Deir al-Balah . The military identified him as a Hamas militant . <p> Salha was arrested by Israeli forces shortly after the killing of the two reservists , convicted and sentenced to life in prison . <p> He was among more than 1,000 Palestinian prisoners released in 2011 in exchange for an Israeli soldier held captive by Hamas in Gaza . One of the other released prisoners , Yahya Sinwar , was one of the masterminds of the October 7 attack and is now the top leader of Hamas.
