<p> The IDF has released unseen footage from the October 7 , 2023 attacks on Israel by Hamas fighters . One video shows troops of the elite Multidomain ' Ghost ' Unit operating in Kibbutz Re'im on the morning of October 7 . During the battle , commander of the unit Col. Roi Levy and officer Cpt . Yotam Ben Bassat were killed . In another video , scene of the battle at Sderot 's police station after Hamas fighters took control of the building and killed several officers , is seen . Meanwhile , IDF also announced death of a hostage in Gaza . The hostages forum claims that his body is still being held in Gaza by Hamas .
