<p> Stray dogs in Jabalya , northern Gaza , are scavenging the bodies of people killed in ongoing Israeli strikes . Fares Afana , the head of emergency services in the region , described the grim situation in a phone interview with CNN . " Stray dogs who are hungry are eating these bodies in the street , " Afana said . This has complicated efforts to identify the dead . <p> Israeli air and ground attacks have targeted the area for over 12 days , with at least 342 Palestinians reported dead .
