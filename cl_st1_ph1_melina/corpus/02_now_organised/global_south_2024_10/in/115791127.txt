<p> JPMorgan Chase shares climbed 5% on Friday after profit at the biggest U.S. bank beat expectations in the third quarter , fueled by gains in investment banking and rising interest payments . The prospect of further monetary easing by the Federal Reserve spurred an equities rally during the third quarter . The renewed economic optimism prompted companies to issue debt and equity and pursue acquisitions , buoying Wall Street businesses . Rival Wells Fargo 's earnings also surpassed estimates . JPMorgan 's investment-banking fees surged 31% , double management 's guidance of 15% last month . A strong showing in equities pushed trading revenue up 8% , higher than guidance of up to 2% that management had given last month . <p> " These earnings are consistent with the soft-landing narrative " of modest U.S. economic growth , Chief Financial Officer Jeremy Barnum told reporters . Net interest income - the difference between what the bank earns on loans and pays on deposits - grew 3% in the quarter .
