<h> It 's time to end the war in Gaza : Former Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Olmert <p> In an exclusive interview with India Today 's Consulting Editor Rajdeep Sardesai , former Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Olmert said that it 's time to end the war in Gaza . <p> Sharing his views on one year of the Hamas attack on Israel , Ehud Olmert said , " This has been a very difficult , a very painful , a very bloody year . I think it 's time to end it . And my first expectation is that Israel will decide to stop the war in Gaza . We have broken down the Hamas military power . <p> " We destroyed most of the tunnels on the ground in Gaza and killed most of its fighters . And there is so much that you can achieve in a military operation at this point now , as you have mentioned . There are 101 Israelis who are still held by Hamas as hostages , and if we need to stop the war in order to bring them back , then we have to do it .
