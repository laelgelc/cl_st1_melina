The conflict began with a Hamas attack on Israel on Oct. 7 , 2023 . <p> LAS VEGAS : US Vice President and Democratic presidential candidate Kamala Harris said on Thursday that de-escalation was needed in the Middle East , a region on edge for months amid Israel 's wars in Gaza and Lebanon .
