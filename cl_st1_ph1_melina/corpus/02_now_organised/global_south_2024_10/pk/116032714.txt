<p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Wednesday told the leaders of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation ( SCO ) countries that the international community should fulfil its responsibility of ensuring an unconditional and immediate ceasefire leading to the establishment of an independent state of Palestine . <p> " Let me say that we can not ignore the ongoing genocide in Gaza . The international community bears the responsibility of ensuring an immediate and unconditional ceasefire leading to the establishment of the State of Palestine based on pre-1967 borders with Al Quds as its capital , " PM Shehbaz was quoted as saying by the Prime Minister 's Office . <p> The prime minister said that the SCO meeting highlighted the member states ' commitment to a better and more sustainable future for future generations . <p> He congratulated Russian Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin on assuming the chairmanship of the Council and announced that the next CHG meeting would be held in Russia next year . <p> The prime minister also assured the Russian prime minister of Pakistan 's fullest support and reiterated unwavering commitment to SCO goals and objectives . <p> He urged the member states to prioritise the collaboration @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ challenges and work hand-in-hand to ensure that SCO remained the beacon of stability and development for the mutual benefit for the people in the region . <p> Before concluding the two-day meeting , he also thanked the participating countries for their commitment and support to ensure the smooth conduct of the meeting . <p> Besides , Mongolia is participating in the summit as an Observer State being represented by Prime Minister Oyun-Erdene Luvsannamsrai and Turkmenistan as a Special Guest represented by Deputy Chairman of the Cabinet of Ministers Rashid Meredov.
