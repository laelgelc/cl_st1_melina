<p> TEL AVIV -- The Israeli military has confirmed that a targeted airstrike has killed Gaza 's de facto Prime Minister , Rawhi Mushtaha , along with two senior ministers . The airstrike , which took place three months ago , targeted an underground tunnel in Gaza , identified as a command and control center by Israeli intelligence . <p> The Israeli military 's statement said the airstrike was based on accurate intelligence , confirming that Rawhi Mushtaha , a key figure in Hamas and a close ally of its leader Yahya Sinwar , was present in the tunnel . Mushtaha , who served as the political head of Hamas in Gaza , had shared a long history with Sinwar , with both having endured imprisonment in Israeli jails together . <p> Along with Mushtaha , Hamas Defense Minister Sami al-Sarraj and his deputy , Sami Oudieh , were also killed in the strike .
