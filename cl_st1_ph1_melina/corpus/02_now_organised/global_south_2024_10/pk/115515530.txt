<h> US official says Israel has not guaranteed it would not strike Iran 's nuclear facilities <p> A senior US State Department official said Israel has not assured the Biden administration it would refrain from attacking Iran 's nuclear facilities , according to CNN . <p> The official said Friday that it is " really hard to tell " whether Tel Aviv would retaliate on the one-year mark of an attack by the Palestinian resistance group , Hamas , last Oct. 7 against Israel . <p> " We hope and expect to see some wisdom as well as strength , but as you guys know , no guarantees , " the unnamed official said after CNN asked if Israel provided guarantees to the US on its military intentions toward Iran . <p> Earlier this week , Iran launched missiles against Israel in retaliation for the assassinations of Hamas leader Ismail Haniyeh and Hezbollah leader Hassan Nasrallah . <p> Israel continues air and ground attacks against Lebanon while conducting strikes in the Gaza Strip . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has vowed that Iran would " pay " for the attacks .
