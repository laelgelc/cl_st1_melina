<h> Pakistani envoy to US urges world to help resolve Kashmir dispute <p> Pakistan 's Ambassador to the United States , Rizwan Saeed Sheikh , Sunday urged the international community and human rights organizations to play their role in not only highlighting the plight of Kashmiri people in Indian Occupied Kashmir but also in facilitating a solution to the long-standing issue . <p> " The case is in our favour ; It 's only a matter of the right timing in international politics and economics , " he said , citing UN Security Council resolutions that grant the right of self-determination to the Kashmiri people to decide their future through a UN-supervised plebiscite . <p> " History is on our side , " the Ambassador told a large gathering at the Pakistan Embassy held to commemorate Kashmir Black Day . <p> The Day is commemorated annually on October 27 to mark the day in 1947 when India , without any legal justification , forcibly took control of the State of Jammu and Kashmir .
