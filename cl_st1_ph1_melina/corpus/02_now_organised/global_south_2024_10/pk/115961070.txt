<p> Over the last year , Israel has killed more than 42,000 Palestinians in Gaza and displaced nearly the entire population of 2.3 million people . <p> The Israeli assault on Gaza has compounded an already dire humanitarian crisis , with severe shortages of food , clean water , and medical supplies due to a prolonged blockade . <p> Efforts led by the United States , Egypt , and Qatar to mediate a cease-fire and secure a prisoner exchange between Israel and Hamas have failed due to Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's refusal to agree to a cessation of hostilities . <p> Meanwhile , Israel has extended its military operations to Lebanon , conducting air and ground raids as regional tensions continue to escalate . <p> Israel currently faces genocide charges at the International Court of Justice over its actions in Gaza .
