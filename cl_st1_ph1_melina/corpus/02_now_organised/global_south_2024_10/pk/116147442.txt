<h> US reiterates call for Israel 's full withdrawal from Gaza after conflict ends <p> The US " ultimately " wants to see Israel fully withdrawn from the Gaza Strip , the State Department reiterated Thursday . <p> That was after remarks by Israeli opposition leader Benny Gantz , who suggested the Israeli military would remain active in Gaza for years . <p> " We ultimately want to see Israel fully withdrawn from Gaza , " State Department spokesman Matthew Miller said when asked by Anadolu about Gantz 's comment . <p> After the Israeli military announced Thursday the killing of Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar in Gaza , Gantz , the head of the National Unity party and former War Cabinet minister , called the death an " important achievement , " but emphasized that does not mark the end of the war . <p> The Israeli military " will continue to operate in the Gaza Strip for years to come " and Israel must take advantage of Sinwar 's death " to bring back the hostages to replace Hamas ' rule , " Gantz wrote on X. <p> Asked if the US still opposes the reoccupation of Gaza @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in a speech in Tokyo last November , Miller confirmed : " Of course . " <p> While Miller declined to respond directly to Gantz 's comments , he reiterated that the US wants Israel to fully withdraw from Gaza . <p> " They have a right , as any country does , to address terrorist threats against its people . But what we want to see as an end to this war , the terrorist threat from Gaza eliminated , and we want to see ultimately a political path that establishes a Palestinian state that is not hostile to Israel , " he said . <p> " Of course , you would n't want to see and would n't need to see the IDF ( military ) operating in that environment , " Miller added .
