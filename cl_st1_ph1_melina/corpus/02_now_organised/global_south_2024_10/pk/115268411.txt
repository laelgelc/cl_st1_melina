<p> Hezbollah said Wednesday that its fighters clashed with an Israeli infantry force trying to infiltrate the town of Odaisseh in southern Lebanon . <p> In a statement , the Lebanese group said it inflicted losses on the Israeli force and forced it to retreat . <p> It marked the first direct clash between Hezbollah fighters and Israeli forces since Israel launched a ground operation in southern Lebanon on Tuesday . <p> Since Sept. 23 , Israel has launched massive airstrikes against what it calls Hezbollah targets across Lebanon , killing more than 1,073 people and injuring over 2,950 others , according to the Lebanese Health Ministry . <p> The top leadership of Hezbollah was killed in the Israeli assaults , including its leader Hassan Nasrallah . <p> Hezbollah and Israel have been engaged in cross-border warfare since the start of Israel 's war on Gaza , which has killed more than 41,600 people , most of them women and children , following an attack by the Palestinian group Hamas last October .
