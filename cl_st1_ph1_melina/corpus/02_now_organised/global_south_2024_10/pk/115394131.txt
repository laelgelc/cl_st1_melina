<p> The political leaders decided to express national solidarity with the Palestinian people by observing a " Day of Solidarity with Palestine " on October 7 . <p> Nationwide events , including gatherings and seminars , will be held to protest Israeli aggression and show support for Palestinians enduring the ongoing violence . <p> In the meeting , the leaders resolved to raise their voices against the brutalities inflicted on Palestinians , particularly in Gaza , and announced an All Parties Conference ( APC ) to discuss the crisis in depth . <p> The APC will be hosted by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif . <p> A committee was also formed to oversee the arrangements for the solidarity day and the APC . The committee includes Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar , Sherry Rehman , Khawaja Saad Rafique , and Nayyar Bukhari . <p> Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari welcomed the decision , condemning the " ongoing oppression of innocent Muslims in Gaza and Palestine .
