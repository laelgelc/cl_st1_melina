<h> PPP will actively participate in Gaza Million March : Khuhro <p> KARACHI - Pakistan Peoples Party ( PPP ) Sindh President Nisar Ahmed Khuhro on Friday said that the party workers would actively take part in the Gaza Million March under the auspices of Jamaat-e-Islami ( JI ) in Karachi on October 6 and in Islamabad on October 7 . He made the announcement in light of the directives of PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari . Khuhro said said that the PPP stood in solidarity with the people of Gaza , Palestine and Lebanon against Israeli aggression . Khuhro said that he strongly condemned the Israeli aggression against Gaza , Lebanon and Palestine , and urged the United Nations play its role in stopping the Israeli aggression . He appealed to all political parties to take a strong united stand against the Israeli aggression by keeping their political differences aside .
