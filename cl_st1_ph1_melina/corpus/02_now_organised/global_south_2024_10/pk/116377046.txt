<h> PM orders dispatching relief goods to Gaza , Lebanon <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Tuesday directed the authorities concerned to immediately dispatch two planeloads of relief goods to Gaza and Lebanon and called for using both land and aerial routes for the aid supply . <p> The prime minister , chairing a meeting to review the relief activities for Palestine and Lebanon , asked for improving coordination with Pakistan 's ambassadors in Palestine , Lebanon , Jordan and Egypt to ensure a better supply of relief goods . <p> Stressing for an accelerated pace of relief aid , he called for ensuring that the relief goods reached the deserving people . <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz reiterated his appeal to the countrymen and the expatriates to donate generously to the PM Fund Fund for Gaza and Lebanon with Account No . **29;1487;TOOLONG . He also instructed the relevant authorities to run an awareness drive to acquaint the people with the Relief Fund established by the government . <p> In the briefing on the relief activities , the prime minister was told that relief goods including winter tents , clothes , blankets , medicines and eatables were being dispatched @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Lebanon . <p> It was told that the Israeli attacks and siege were impeding the supply of relief goods to the affected areas . <p> The meeting was told that the government would also facilitate the NGOs desiring to send relief aid to Gaza and Lebanon .
