<h> Spanish PM urges international community to stop arming Israel <p> Rome - Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez on Friday called on the international community to stop selling weapons to Israel , after a meeting with Pope Francis at the Vatican . " I do believe that it is urgent that , in light of everything that is happening in the Middle East , the international community stops exporting weapons to the government of Israel , " he told reporters . " This is an appeal that I will make ... to the entire international community , " he said , adding that it was important " not to contribute in one way or another to the escalation of violence and to the war and its expansion in Gaza , the West Bank or , in this case , to Lebanon .
