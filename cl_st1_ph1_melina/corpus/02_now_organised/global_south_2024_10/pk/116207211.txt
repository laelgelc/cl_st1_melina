Today , those who wish to use the crossing known as Masnaa must climb down into and walk across a massive crater in the road , which is 10 metres ( 32 feet ) deep and 30 metres ( nearly 100 feet ) wide . Israel hit near the crossing on October 4 , accusing Lebanese group Hezbollah of using it to transport military equipment into Lebanon . <p> But the strike has made it harder for thousands of people trying to flee war in Lebanon to Syria . <p> The Israel-Hezbollah war erupted late last month after nearly a year of exchanges of fire over the war in Gaza .
