<h> Hafiz Naeem demands Palestine issue be raised at SCO summit <p> Jamaat-e-Islami ( JI ) Chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman urged that the ongoing Shanghai Cooperation Organization ( SCO ) summit in Islamabad should include discussions on the Palestine issue and condemn the violence in Gaza . <p> Speaking to the media in Faisalabad on Tuesday , Rehman stated , " The government convened the Gaza All Parties Conference ( APC ) on JI 's suggestion . The SCO summit must address the Palestinian issue , and the organization 's members should collectively condemn the atrocities in Gaza . If India supports Israel , it will find itself isolated . " <p> Rehman also stated that political parties , including Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf ( PTI ) , have the right to voice their concerns within the constitutional framework .
