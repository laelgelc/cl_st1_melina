<h> 16 more Palestinian students leave Cairo for Islamabad <p> CAIRO - The third batch of 16 Palestinian students from Gaza left Cairo for Islamabad on Wednesday to complete their studies in the country . They will complete studies in medical institutes of Pakistan under the directions of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and immense efforts of Global Relief Trust , Doctors of Rahman and Alkhidmat Foundation Pakistan , according to a statement issued by the Pakistan embassy in Egypt , Cairo . The first batch of the Palestinian students arrived at Lahore on Monday while in total , some 190 students will be arriving in Pakistan for medical education . In July , the Government of Pakistan had announced to allow medical students from Gaza to complete their medical education in Pakistan on humanitarian grounds . The decision was made to enable Gaza students to continue the final years of their medical education in the fields of cardiology , orthopedics , oncology , pediatrics and surgery to address critical needs in Gaza 's health care system .
