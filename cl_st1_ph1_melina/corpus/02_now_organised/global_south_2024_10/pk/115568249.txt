<h> Pakistan rallies in support of Palestinians on Solidarity Day <p> ISLAMABAD -- Pakistani government and people are observing Palestinian Solidarity Day to express support for Muslims facing severe brutality from Israeli forces . <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif is set to host the All Parties Conference in the capital city aimed at uniting voices against the ongoing oppression of unarmed Palestinians in Gaza . <p> Nationwide rallies and seminars are taking place in both small towns and major cities , highlighting atrocities committed by Israeli forces . PM Sharif reiterated Pakistan 's commitment to providing moral , diplomatic , and political support to Palestinians enduring relentless aggression . <p> In his message for Solidarity Day , Prime Minister emphasised that the government will continue to stand with the Palestinian people until they achieve an independent state , with East Jerusalem as its capital .
