<p> UNITED NATIONS : The UN secretary-general denounced Hamas and called for an immediate end to the " shocking violence and bloodshed " in Gaza and Lebanon in a statement Saturday ahead of the anniversary of the Palestinian group 's October 7 attack . <p> Monday marks one year since the devastating assault on Israel that sparked the ongoing war in Gaza , with Lebanon now also pulled into the fray and world leaders warning of a potential all-out regional crisis . <p> " This is a day for the global community to repeat in the loudest voice our utter condemnation of the abhorrent acts of Hamas , including the taking of hostages , " UN chief Antonio Guterres said in an anniversary message released Saturday evening . <p> While demanding the hostages ' " immediate and unconditional release , " Guterres also implored Hamas to allow the hostages to be visited by Red Cross personnel . <p> Hamas abducted 251 people on October 7 , 97 of whom are still captive in Gaza , including 33 the Israeli military has said are dead . <p> Guterres additionally voiced concern over the conflict spreading to Lebanon , where Israel @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ killing over a thousand people and forcing more than a million to flee their homes .
