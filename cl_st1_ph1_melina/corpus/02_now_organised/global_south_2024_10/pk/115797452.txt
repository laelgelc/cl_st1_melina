<h> Israeli attacks on Gaza refugee schools and Beirut leave 92 dead <p> Israel 's continued aggression has claimed the lives of 92 people in a series of attacks targeting refugee schools in Gaza and densely populated areas of Beirut , according to international media reports . <p> In Gaza , Israeli airstrikes hit refugee schools and hospitals , resulting in the deaths of more than 70 Palestinians . These attacks came amidst ongoing hostilities , as fierce clashes erupted between Palestinian resistance fighters and Israeli forces in northern Gaza . The confrontation led to the deaths of three Israeli soldiers . <p> In addition to the devastation in Beirut , Israel 's bombing campaign extended to the Lebanon-Syria border , where the Israeli military destroyed a field hospital run by the Iranian Red Crescent . These actions have drawn widespread international condemnation , particularly after reports of shelling near UN peacekeeping forces in the region . <p> The escalating violence has led to growing global outrage , with many nations calling for an immediate end to the attacks and a focus on diplomatic solutions .
