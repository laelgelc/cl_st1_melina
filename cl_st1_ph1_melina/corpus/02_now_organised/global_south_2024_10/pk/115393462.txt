<h> Iranian foreign minister arrives in Beirut amid Israeli escalation <p> Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi arrived in Beirut on Friday , marking his first visit to Lebanon since assuming his office . <p> The visit comes at a time of heightened tensions and ongoing Israeli attacks in the region . <p> According to the Lebanese National News Agency , Araghchi was received by Osama Khashab , the head of protocol at Lebanon 's Foreign Ministry , at Beirut 's Rafic Hariri International Airport . <p> Meanwhile , Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei announced that the Tehran delegation to Beirut , led by Foreign Minister Araghchi , is scheduled to meet with high-ranking Lebanese officials . <p> In a statement , Baghaei said : " Our delegation led by FM Araghchi and accompanied by two members of Parliament and Head of Iran 's Red Crescent Society will meet with Lebanese high-level officials shortly . " <p> " A package of 10 tons of foodstuff and medicine will also be delivered as part of our humanitarian assistance to Lebanon , " noted the official , adding that " Iran is steadfast in its solidarity with brave people of Lebanon @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ critical situation Lebanon faces and its dire implications for the future of our nations , " he added . <p> Lebanese officials have not yet commented on the agenda of Araghchi 's meetings , but analysts expect that talks will focus on the growing crisis along the Lebanon-Israel border , as well as Iran 's support for Hezbollah and the wider geopolitical implications . <p> Hezbollah and Israel have been engaged in cross-border warfare since the Israel 's war on Gaza started on Oct. 7 last year , which has killed nearly 41,800 people , most of them women and children . <p> In Israeli attacks in Lebanon , at least 1,947 people have since been killed , nearly 9,400 injured , and 1.2 million others displaced , according to Lebanese authorities . <p> The international community has warned that Israeli attacks in Lebanon could escalate the Gaza conflict into a wider regional war .
