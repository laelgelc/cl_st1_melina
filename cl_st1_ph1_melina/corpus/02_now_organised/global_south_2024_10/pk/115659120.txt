<p> Islamabad -- ( ) : A two-member delegation of the National Federation of Journalists ( NFJ ) met with the Iranian Ambassador to Pakistan , Reza Amiri Moghadam . The delegation was led by NFJ Secretary General Abid Siddique Chaudhry , accompanied by Joint Secretary Adnan Hameed . During the meeting , discussions centered on enhancing media cooperation between the two countries and strengthening the long-standing bilateral ties between Pakistan and Iran . <p> According to an NFJ spokesperson , Abid Siddique Chaudhry expressed condolences on behalf of the federation over the martyrdom of Hassan Nasrallah . He also recorded his sentiments in the condolence book , acknowledging Nasrallah 's decades-long association with the Palestinian cause . He remarked that Nasrallah 's martyrdom is a great loss to the Palestinian liberation movement , and this void can not be easily filled . <p> Chaudhry also briefed the Iranian Ambassador on NFJ 's objectives and highlighted his recent participation in the " Imam Khomeini Memorial " and " Gaza Conference " in Tehran .
