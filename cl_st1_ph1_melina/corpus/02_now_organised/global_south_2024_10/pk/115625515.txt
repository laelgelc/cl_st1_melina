<h> Pakistanis standing with innocent Palestinians : Hamza <p> LAHORE - Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz leader Hamza Shahbaz Sharif said on Monday that every Pakistani was standing with innocent Palestinians who had been subjected to unrelenting Israeli aggression and oppression . In his message issued here , he said that Israel was writing worst chapter of atrocities in the history . He said that state terrorism of Israel had spread to Lebanon and other countries of the region . Hamza Shahbaz said that silence of world on genocide of Palestinians was matter of great concern for humanity . He further said that Gaza infrastructure especially schools and hospitals had been specifically targeted and destroyed . The PML-N leader said that more than 41,000 people of all age groups had been martyred while those living in refugee camps had also been killed .
