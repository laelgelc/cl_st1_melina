<p> The UN voiced alarm over Israel 's announcement that over 120,000 weapons have been distributed to Israelis over the last year , with further distribution planned . <p> Speaking with Anadolu , Jeremy Laurence , spokesperson for the UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights , said Monday 's announcement of the mass arms distribution since Oct. 7 , 2023 , when Israel 's offensive on Gaza began , raised red flags . <p> Remarks made by far-right National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir , who made the announcement , " only serve to heighten these concerns , " Laurence said , stressing that their primary worry is a " sharp increase " in regional violence . <p> He cited a report from the UN Human Rights Office published last December which raised " serious concern " regarding the distribution of firearms by the Israeli military in the occupied West Bank . He noted that this coincided with an increase in settler-related violence , with over a third of the recorded incidents involving firearms , including shootings . <p> Laurence also referenced a September report by UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres , which recorded 1,350 attacks by Israelis @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ armed assaults . <p> Laurence indicated that the distribution of weapons to Israeli civilians , particularly in the context of growing settler violence , could escalate armed attacks on Palestinians . <p> He also mentioned an annual report by UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Volker Turk , submitted to the UN Human Rights Council in February , highlighting concerns over the use of inflammatory language by senior Israeli officials .
