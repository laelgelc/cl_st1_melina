<h> Hamas confirms death of leader Yahya Sinwar <p> Doha - Palestinian group Hamas on Friday confirmed its leader Yahya Sinwar had been killed , a day after Israel announced his death in Gaza . <p> " We mourn the great leader , the martyred brother , Yahya Sinwar , Abu Ibrahim , " Qatar-based Hamas official Khalil al-Hayya said in a recorded video statement broadcast by Al Jazeera . <p> Israel announced Sinwar 's death on Thursday , which Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu called a " heavy blow " to the Palestinian group Israeli forces have been fighting in Gaza more than a year . <p> In his statement , Hayya said Hamas would not release the captives until the war in Gaza ends . <p> The hostages " will not return ... unless the aggression against our people in Gaza stops , " the senior Hamas official said . He called on Israel to withdraw from Gaza and release Palestinian prisoners held in Israeli jails .
