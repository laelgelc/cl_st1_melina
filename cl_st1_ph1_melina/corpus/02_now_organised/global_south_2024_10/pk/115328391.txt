<h> Salahuddin 's Victory <p> 2 October should be remembered as the day when Muslim clemency and generosity triumphed over the fearful hearts of the Jewish and Christian populations of Jerusalem . It was on this day , in 1187 AD , that Jerusalem fell to the righteous sword of Salahuddin Ayubi , and its memory is forever etched in history . <p> Brave and magnanimous , kind and merciful , Sultan Salahuddin 's clemency surpassed his military prowess . Not a single sword was raised against women , the elderly , or children . Thousands of prisoners and their families were released from captivity , their lives and property spared by the great Sultan . As they left the city , many Christian and Jewish families lacked transportation , so Salahuddin ordered his men to provide them with camels , horses , and carts . <p> Salahuddin is revered in history by all three Abrahamic faiths . His popularity in the West equals that of Maulana Rumi . Working like a common soldier , he personally helped repair the walls of Jerusalem , carrying stones on his back .
