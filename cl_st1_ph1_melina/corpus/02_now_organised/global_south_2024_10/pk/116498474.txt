<h> US warns of attack threat in Sri Lanka surf hotspot <p> COLOMBO - The US embassy in Sri Lanka warned citizens on Wednesday of a possible attack on a popular surfing destination , prompting the island nation 's police to pledge more security for tourists . In a rare notice of an imminent threat , the embassy said it had " received credible information warning of an attack targeting popular tourist locations " in eastern Arugam Bay . <p> The warning comes after social media posts called for a boycott of Israeli-owned businesses in the area . <p> Protests by local Muslim groups against Israel 's wars in Gaza and Lebanon have drawn support from the wider community in the predominantly Buddhist South Asian nation . <p> Israelis accounted for less than 1.5 percent of the 1.5 million tourists who visited the island in the first nine months of this year -- or around 20,000 people altogether . <p> But Arugam Bay , a hotspot for surfing around 400 kilometres ( 250 miles ) east of Colombo by road , is a popular destination for Israeli tourists .
