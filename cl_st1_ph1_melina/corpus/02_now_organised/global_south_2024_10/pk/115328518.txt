<h> Bilawal announces support for JI 's Gaza million march <p> Chairman of Pakistan People 's Party ( PPP ) , Bilawal Bhutto Zardari , was invited to participate in the Gaza Million March by Ameer of Jamaat-e-Islami Pakistan , Hafiz Naeem-ur-Rehman , during a meeting between the two leaders . <p> A delegation led by Hafiz Naeem-ur-Rehman reached Zardari House in Islamabad for the meeting with Bilawal Bhutto . <p> Liaqat Baloch and Mayor of Karachi Murtaza Wahab were also present on the occasion . <p> Hafiz Naeem extended the invitation to Bilawal Bhutto for the Gaza March . <p> Speaking to the media after the meeting , Hafiz Naeem-ur-Rehman , alongside Bilawal Bhutto , condemned Israel for state terrorism , highlighting its recent attack on Lebanon . <p> He called on the government to focus on the Gaza situation . " On October 7 , we will take to the streets across the country at 12 pm to protest .
