<p> CAIRO : Egypt 's President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi announced Sunday a proposal for a two-day truce in the Israel-Hamas war that would include a hostage release and could pave the way for a " complete ceasefire " . <p> Sisi , whose government has been involved in mediation efforts to end the Gaza war , proposed a " two-day ceasefire " during which " four hostages would be exchanged for some prisoners in Israeli jails " , followed by more negotiations within 10 days aiming to secure " a complete ceasefire and the entry of aid " into the Gaza Strip , the president told a news conference in Cairo alongside his visiting Algerian counterpart . <p> International mediators seeking an end to the Gaza war , triggered by Hamas 's unprecedented October 7 , 2023 attack on Israel , have managed to secure a single , one-week truce in November . <p> Out of 251 hostages seized by Palestinian militants during the October 7 attack , 97 are still held in Gaza including 34 the Israeli military says are dead . <p> Earlier this month , Israeli forces in the Gaza Strip had killed Hamas leader Yahya @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ some analysts have said was an obstacle to reaching an accord . <p> Speaking in Cairo , Sisi said that " our brothers in the ( Gaza ) Strip face a very harsh siege " that has put them " on the brink of famine " . <p> " It is very important that aid enters as soon as possible " to alleviate the dire humanitarian needs in Gaza , Sisi added . <p> The Hamas attack that sparked the war resulted in the deaths of 1,206 people , mostly civilians , according to an AFP tally of official Israeli figures . <p> At least 42,924 Palestinians , a majority of them civilians , have been killed in the Israeli offensive on Gaza , according to figures from the Hamas-ruled territory 's health ministry , which the UN considers reliable .
