<p> Pakistan and Malaysia on Thursday agreed to enhance bilateral cooperation across multiple sectors , including trade , investment , defence , and agriculture . Both countries also reiterated their strong positions on regional issues such as Gaza and Kashmir . <p> Following a one-on-one meeting and delegation-level talks , the two prime ministers held a joint press conference , highlighting their commitment to strengthening collaboration in areas of mutual interest and addressing regional and international matters . <p> As part of their efforts to boost bilateral trade , both leaders announced that Pakistan would export halal meat worth $200 million annually , along with 100,000 metric tonnes of Basmati rice to Malaysia . <p> The total trade volume between Malaysia and Pakistan currently stands at $1.4 billion , encompassing products such as palm oil , textiles , chemicals , electronics , and apparel . Pakistan is Malaysia 's third-largest trading partner in South Asia . <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz expressed confidence in the quality of Pakistan 's exports . He assured that Pakistan would uphold the highest standards for its products .
