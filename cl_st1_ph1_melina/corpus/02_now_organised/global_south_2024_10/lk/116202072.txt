<h> The UN at 79 <h> Ripe for reform <p> United Nations ( UN ) Day , on 24 October , marks the anniversary of the entry into force in 1945 of the UN Charter . With the ratification of this founding document by the majority of its signatories , including the five Permanent Members ( US , UK , China , France and Russia ) of the UN Security Council ( UNSC ) , the United Nations officially came into being . <p> There is no other global organisation with the legitimacy , convening power and normative impact of the United Nations . No other global organisation gives hope to so many people for a better world and can deliver the future they want . Today , the urgency for all countries to come together , to fulfill the promise of the UN , has never been greater . <p> UN Day , celebrated every year , offers the opportunity to amplify the UN 's common agenda and reaffirm the purposes and principles of the UN Charter that have guided the UN for the past 79 years . <p> In 1945 , representatives of 50 countries met in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to draw up the United Nations Charter . The United Nations officially came into existence on October 24 , 1945 when the Charter had been ratified by a majority of signatories . <p> Createing a better world <p> The UN has many achievements to its credit in a variety of spheres . One major example is the almost total eradication of polio , the dreaded childhood disease . Apart from a few rare cases in strife-torn Afghanistan and Pakistan , polio does not occur anywhere else in the world . The same goes for smallpox , which is now confined to the laboratory . <p> The UN 's multitude of agencies have certainly made the world a better place . UNICEF does a lot of work in education , the International Labour Organisation ( ILO ) has managed to reduce child labour drastically and the World Health Organisation ( WHO ) has been in the forefront of managing global medical emergencies . Covid was a prime example , though even the intervention of the WHO could not prevent the hoarding of life-saving vaccines by the First World . One major shortcoming of the UN system is that the UNSC has hardly changed over the last eight decades . There is hardly any doubt that the UNSC as it is does not represent the true voice of the world , especially the Global South . Africa , for example , is severely under-represented at the UNSC .
