Behind the yellow line stands a slim senior lady in a simple black outfit , holding on to a cart , chatting casually with her companion . Across them , let 's show two other passengers , both watching the woman intently . The elderly man has this thought bubble : " Oh , wow , that 's Mrs. Billionaire CEO , waiting for her luggage .
