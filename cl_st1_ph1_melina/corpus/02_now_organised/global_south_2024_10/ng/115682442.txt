It was not just the question of calling people to bid for a permanent seat . It was a result of that war , and the decision of the United States on what it was going to do . <p> So , there were five seats out of 11 . The question of having 11 was amended by an increase of an additional four , making the membership of the UNSC 15 today . I should explain what the veto power means . The way that works is that out of the five mentioned major powers , if any of them does not agree on an issue that is being discussed , and gives a negative vote , that issue is dead . If it refuses to vote either way , then the approval of a substantive issue becomes a vote of nine out of 15 , with none of the permanent five saying NO . So , a veto is a position of power .
