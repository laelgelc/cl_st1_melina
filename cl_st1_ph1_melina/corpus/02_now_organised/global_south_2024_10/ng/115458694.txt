All these are packaged to attract a wider market of consumers , luring children and young people into the deadly habit of smoking or vaping . Evidence is growing that these newer tobacco products constitute gateways to nicotine addiction and the initiation of smoking among youth , giving high odds of turning youngsters into chronic smokers .
