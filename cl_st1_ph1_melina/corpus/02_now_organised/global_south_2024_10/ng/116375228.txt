With two weeks left before voters choose the next U.S. president , Trump leads Harris by 45 percent to 43 percent . <p> Trump is viewed as six points more favourable to Israel 's present government than Harris , despite his edge . Based on a survey of 500 Arab Americans , the poll 's findings are within a 5.93 percent margin of error . <p> According to a study conducted earlier this month by the Arab American Institute , Trump and Harris were nearly tied at 42 percent to 41 percent .
