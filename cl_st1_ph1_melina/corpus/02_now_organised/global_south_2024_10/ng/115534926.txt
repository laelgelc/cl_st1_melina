In this connection while all the security council members show strong support and full confidence for the SG , only the USA representative sits in silence like a grave . <p> Despite the advisory opinion of the International Court of Justice calling for an end to the transfer of arms , ammunition , and related equipment to Israel , and the resolution adopted by the General Assembly on September 13 that affirmed this demand , " Israel said on Thursday September 26 , that it had secured an $8.7 billion aid package from the United States to support its ongoing military efforts and maintain a military edge in the region .
