<h> Hamas Leader Behind October Attack Killed In Gaza <p> The Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) said it has killed a top leader of the Hamas group , Yahya Sinwar , in its operation in the south of Gaza . <p> In a statement on Thursday , the IDF said it has been in pursuit of Sinwarfor for over a year , having planned and executed the 7 October attack and was " responsible for the murder and abduction of many Israelis " . <p> Advertisement <p> " Yahya Sinwar was eliminated after hiding for the past year behind the civilian population of Gaza , both above and below ground in Hamas tunnels in the Gaza Strip , " it says . <p> The Israeli military says its operations in southern Gaza follow an intelligence " that indicated the suspected locations of senior members of Hamas " .
