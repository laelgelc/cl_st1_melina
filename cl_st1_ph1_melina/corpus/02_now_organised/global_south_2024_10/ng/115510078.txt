<h> Hamas celebrates October 7 attack on Israel as anniversary approaches <p> In a video message released Sunday , Hamas publicly lauded its October 7 attack on Israel , marking the upcoming anniversary of the assault that ignited the current conflict in Gaza . <p> " The crossing of the glorious 7th of October shattered the illusions the enemy had created for itself , convincing the world and the region of its supposed superiority and capabilities , " stated Khalil al-Hayya , a member of Hamas based in Qatar . <p> The attack last year led to the deaths of 1,205 individuals , primarily civilians , according to an AFP analysis of Israeli official statistics , which include those killed while being held hostage . <p> Since the onset of the war , at least 41,870 Palestinians , mostly civilians , have died in Israel 's military operations in the Gaza Strip , based on data from the Hamas-controlled health ministry .
