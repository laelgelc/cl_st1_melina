<p> JERUSALEM , Oct 6 -- The Israeli military said Sunday it deployed more troops to defend southern communities and areas bordering Gaza , ahead of the anniversary of the October 7 attack by Hamas . <p> " The IDF 's ( army ) Gaza Division has been reinforced with several platoons , with forces stationed to defend both the communities and the border area , " the military said . <p> " The soldiers are fully equipped to defend the region in coordination with local security forces , " it said in a statement . <p> Inside Gaza , the military said three divisions were working to " dismantle terrorist infrastructure and degrade Hamas 's capabilities " . <p> " The Southern Command remains at a heightened state of vigilance and readiness for the coming days , " commanding officer Major General Yaron Finkelman was quoted as saying . <p> Earlier , the military said its forces had surrounded the area of Jabaliya in central Gaza where Hamas was trying to rebuild its operational capabilities . <p> On October 7 last year , Hamas militants broke through the border from Gaza into Israel in what would become the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ end of the Jewish holiday of Sukkot , the militants launched their assault by land , air , and sea . <p> A year later , the confirmed death toll from the attack , including hostages killed in captivity , has reached 1,205 on the Israeli side , most of them civilians , according to an AFP tally based on official Israeli figures . <p> Militants abducted 251 hostages , 97 of whom are still captive in Gaza , including 33 the Israeli military has said are dead . <p> Hours after the attack Israel launched a lightning military offensive on Gaza , which has reduced large swathes of the territory to rubble , and nearly all of the 2.4 million residents have been displaced at least once as a humanitarian crisis worsened . <p> Israel 's relentless offensive on Gaza has so far killed at least 41,870 people , a majority of them civilians , according to the health ministry in the Hamas-run Palestinian territory . <p> The figures have been found to be reliable by the United Nations . -- EFP
