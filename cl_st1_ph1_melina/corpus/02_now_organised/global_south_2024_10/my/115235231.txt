Israel backs a local Christian militia called the South Lebanon Army ( SLA ) . <p> 1982 <p> Israel invades Lebanon all the way to Beirut in an offensive that followed tit-for-tat border fire . <p> Thousands of Palestinian fighters are evacuated by sea after a bloody 10-week siege of the Lebanese capital involving heavy Israeli bombardment of West Beirut . <p> Hundreds of civilians in the Palestinian refugee camps of Sabra and Shatila are massacred by Christian militiamen allowed in by Israeli troops after Lebanon 's newly elected Maronite Catholic president is killed by a car bomb . <p> Israel pulled back @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ south .
