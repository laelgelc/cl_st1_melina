Prayers were said . <p> On 7 October 2023 , about 1 100 Israelis were killed in an attack by Hamas . Since then , Israeli forces have killed at least 41 000 Palestinians in Gaza . According to Al Jazeera , at least 2 000 people have also been killed in Israeli strikes against Hezbollah in Lebanon . <p> Al Jazeera estimates that over two-thirds of the victims in the last year have been women and children . <p> Banners read ' No education as normal ' til boycott is formal ' and ' Never again for anyone . Not in our name .
