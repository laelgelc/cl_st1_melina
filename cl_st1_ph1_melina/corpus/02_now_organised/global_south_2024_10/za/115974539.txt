<p> Havana -- Thousands of Cubans , led by President Miguel Diaz-Canel and other leaders of the communist-run island , marched in Havana on Monday to express solidarity with Palestinians in Gaza . <p> The demonstrators , including some 250 Palestinian medical students living in Cuba , carried a large banner that read " Long live free Palestine , " while the president and his allies wore traditional keffiyeh scarves . <p> " We are here to support the just claim of the Palestinian people , for their sovereignty , their freedom ( ... ) and against the genocidal crusade that Israel practices towards the Palestinian people , " Michel Marino , a 20-year-old international relations student , told AFP . <p> The march had been due to take place on the anniversary of the brutal attack by Hamas on Israel , but it was postponed due to Hurricane Milton , which lashed Cuba and Florida last week . <p> The October 7 attack , which triggered the war in Gaza , resulted in the deaths of 1 206 people , mostly civilians , according to an AFP tally of official Israeli figures . The UN has described the figures as reliable . <p> " For a whole year our Gaza has not had a single day of calm , not a single day of peace and our people in the West Bank suffer daily aggression while the world remains paralyzed and unable to stop this tragedy , " said Mohammed Suwan , a Palestinian student , as he addressed the participants . <p> In June , the Caribbean island joined a lawsuit filed by South Africa against Israel before the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) over its military campaign in Gaza .
