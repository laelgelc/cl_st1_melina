On the contrary , Kamala Harris has struggled to break away in the race and secure a definitive lead . <p> According to the FiveThirtyEight daily election tracker , as of 23 October , Harris was ahead of Trump in the national polls by a mere 1.8% . <p> This slim margin , however , belies the more crucial reality of the swing states . In pivotal battlegrounds , such as Pennsylvania , Michigan , Wisconsin and Nevada , the two candidates are neck and neck , separated by less than half a percentage point .
