<p> The Goethe-Institut South Africa was abuzz with energy and passion as the Media Freedom Festival unfolded over the vibrant days , honouring the legacy of Black Wednesday . This annual event , which took place from October 16 -- 17 , pays tribute to October 19 , 1977 ( Black Wednesday ) , when the apartheid regime launched a brutal clampdown on media and political organisations , resulting in numerous detentions , bans , and newspaper closures . <p> Under the theme Media for Democracy : Ensuring Access , Accountability and Integrity , the festival brought together a diverse array of voices dedicated to advocating for media freedom and the protection of journalists . <p> The evening of day one focused on Building Global Solidarity -- The Media Freedom Coalition , featuring a distinguished panel including William Bird , the director of Media Monitoring Africa , and Deputy Minister in the Presidency Kenny Morolong , who joined via Zoom .
