<h> 85 per cent of children affected by polio in 2023 lived in fragile and conflict-affected countries <p> NEW YORK and NAIROBI , 24 October 2024 -- Of the 541 children affected by polio globally in 2023 , 85 per cent live in the 31 fragile , conflict-affected , and vulnerable countries , according to a new UNICEF analysis on World Polio Day . <p> Polio cases in these settings have more than doubled in the past five years , while routine childhood immunization has dropped from 75 to 70 per cent -- well below the 95 per cent needed for community immunity . <p> The data issue a stark warning that the life-threatening disease continues to thrive in areas where life is most precarious , and where conflict , natural disasters , humanitarian crises , other destabilizing factors make it difficult to deliver critical healthcare . <p> " In conflict , children face more than bombs and bullets ; they are at risk of deadly diseases that should no longer exist , " said UNICEF Executive Director Catherine Russell . Nowhere is this more evident than in conflict-affected areas , with 15 out of 21 such countries -- including Afghanistan , Democratic Republic of Congo , Somalia , South Sudan , and Yemen -- currently battling polio . <p> In recent months , UNICEF and partners have intensified emergency responses to surges in polio outbreaks .
