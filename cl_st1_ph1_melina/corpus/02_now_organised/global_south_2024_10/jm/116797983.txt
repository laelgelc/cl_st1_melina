<h> Fun galore at Scream Halloween party <p> It was full house at The University of the West Indies ( UWI ) Mona Bowl last Friday night as university students flocked the venue for the third staging of Scream , the annual Halloween party , promoted by Hourz Entertainment . <p> Patrons came dressed in several character costumes , animals , insects , and professions -- from construction workers to butterflies , goats , ladies of the night , Shakespearean outfits , nurses , the devil , and ship captains . <p> But the music stood out for the night .
