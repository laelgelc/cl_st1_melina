The Spanish flu outbreak of 1918-1920 killed between 20 and 50 million people worldwide . <p> Those numbers include an estimated 50,000 Canadians and some 680,000 Americans . <p> In comparison , the COVID epidemic still underway has taken an even greater toll on this side of the Atlantic . Some 56,000 Canadians have died , along with 1.2 million Americans . <p> Of course , the population of both countries is far higher now , meaning the per capita death rate is less .
