But a document from the Finance Ministry presented to the Knesset Finance Committee on December 25 suggests that the number is NIS 10 billion greater . <p> The Finance Ministry is also projecting that the war against Hamas will cost the country 's budget somewhere in the order of NIS 50 billion ( US$13.8 billion ) .
