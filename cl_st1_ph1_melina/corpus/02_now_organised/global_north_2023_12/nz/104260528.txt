<h> Chorus Of The Hebrew Slaves In Gaza <p> I was wandering up the Tip Track in Owhiro Bay today listening to a playlist of favourites . On came " Va , Pensiero " by Giuseppe Verdi - The Chorus of the Hebrew Slaves -- from Nabucco . It stopped me in my tracks . I have heard the piece innumerable times - including live at the Michael Fowler Centre sung by the Orpheus Choir . But this time was different . <p> Verdi wrote it in 1842 , not that long after Maori chiefs and the Crown signed The Treaty of Waitangi . At that time , Maori " owned " , to use the Pakeha concept , virtually 100% of the land ; today , by hook and by crooks , they hold less than 5% . Similar fates have , as we know , befallen indigenous peoples the world over , including the Palestinians . <p> If you do n't know Va , Pensiero , treat yourself and listen to it now .
