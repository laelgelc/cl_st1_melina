<h> PollyHill x Samara Alofa Unveil Debut Album ' AQUARIES ' <p> Tamaki Makaurau 's power duo of PollyHill ( Paloma Schneideman ) and Samara Alofa finally share their debut collaborative album AQUARIES -- eight aliencore tracks of electronic trip hop , alt RnB , jungle and psychedelic flavours expressing their shared love for the " wonderful and weird " . Out in the wild today are five new songs that follow the excellent releases of ' Void ' , ' Alien Sex ' and ' QUEERBAIT ' . There 's additions like the intoxicating final single ' HOME ' and tenderness of ' Pain ' , plus the ticking ' Acid Rain ' . Floating just outside the realms of heteronormative Earth , the queer multidisciplinary artists ( and flatmates ) created the album with the concept of a " domesticated alien " in mind -- of one who struggles to assimilate because that 's simply not who they are . Taking the production reins for the project , Schneideman and Alofa ( primarily handling vocal duties ) synergise into the musical stratosphere , where " sonic rebellion , cosmic healing , and queer joy " reach . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ part of TAUTOKO GAZA , a new Aotearoa compilation fundraiser for Medical Aid For Palestinians . Alofa and PollyHill will be performing tunes from AQUARIES together at Tamaki Makaurau 's Whammy Bar , with their friends Geneva AM , AJHoneysuckle , DJSNAKELEGS , Zeki and HALFQUEEN on 25th November .
