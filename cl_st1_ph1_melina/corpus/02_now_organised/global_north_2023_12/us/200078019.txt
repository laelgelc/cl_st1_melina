Lawmakers said they still need to flesh out just how far the legislation would go . <p> The measure now moves on to the lower house , the National Council . <p> Such a measure , if passed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ its European neighbors that have similar bans against incitement to hatred . <p> While the legislative effort has been in the works for months , it comes as much of Europe has seen a rise in antisemitism , following Israel 's muscular military response in Gaza after Hamas ' deadly Oct. 7 assault . <p> " There 's no place for symbols that make apologies for violence in our society , " said Federal Councilor Elisabeth Baume-Schneider , who heads the federal justice and police department , during a parliamentary hearing Wednesday . <p> She acknowledged the " tragic creativity " that some people have found to incite violence , hatred or recognition of Nazi symbols .
