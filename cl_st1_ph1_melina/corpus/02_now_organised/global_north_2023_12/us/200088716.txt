<h> Protesters demanding end to Israel-Hamas war march on Fifth Avenue in Manhattan <p> NEW YORK -- People demanding an end to the Israel-Hamas war marched on Fifth Avenue in Manhattan on Saturday , a busy last-minute shopping day . <p> It was one of several demonstrations across the country coinciding with Christmas weekend . <p> It also came one day after the U.N . passed a resolution to provide more aid to Gaza , but it did not call for an immediate cease-fire . <p> Meanwhile , Christmas celebrations in Bethlehem were canceled this year amid the war . The city in the occupied West Bank is typically full of tourists , decorations and shoppers this time of year . <p> A Palestinian Christian church is displaying a nativity scene with baby Jesus lying in the rubble . <p> " In the hometown of Christmas , this is what Christmas looks like , children being pulled from under the rubble , displaced families , destroyed homes . So we hope that the world pays attention to the plight of the Palestinians , " the church 's reverend said .
