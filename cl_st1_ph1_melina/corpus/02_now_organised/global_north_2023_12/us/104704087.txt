<p> One can argue whether Israel should be allowed to defend itself or remain a state . What is not debatable , however , is whether Israel has been performing genocide . This paper seems to support that belief -- based on many of the op-ed pieces it publishes . <p> The definition of genocide is the deliberate and systematic destruction of a group of people because of their ethnicity , nationality , religion or race . <p> If Israel was committing genocide , it would carpet bomb Gaza without risking the safety of its soldiers . It would be rounding up and killing 20% of its own population , which are Arabs , now known as Palestinians ( who incidentally make up that same percentage in their government ) . <p> Lastly , it would not bother letting its enemy know when and where it is advancing by dropping leaflets and calling civilians warning them to leave the area . <p> Alan Pollock <p> Waialae <p> EXPRESS YOURSELF <p> The Honolulu Star-Advertiser welcomes all opinions .
