" <p> The institute was encouraged by how much positive local-media coverage the events generated , taking it as a sign that a concerted campaign could lift the president 's lackluster approval ratings ahead of his reelection bid . Its leaders were eying a second round of activity this fall to amplify Biden 's record on lowering prescription-drug and child-care costs . <p> Since October 7 , however , those plans are on hold .
