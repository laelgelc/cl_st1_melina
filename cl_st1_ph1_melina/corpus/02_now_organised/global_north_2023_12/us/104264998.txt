The 9/11 terrorist attacks had that effect on the United States . Internationally , 9/11 prompted the U.S. wars in Afghanistan and Iraq , and counterterror operations worldwide . Domestically , the most obvious evidence of 9/11 's fallout is the now-familiar ritual of the airport security line as Transportation Security Administration agents daily check about 2 million U.S. travelers for weapons . Less apparent , but more far-reaching , was 9/11 's impact on the American psyche , causing a nation to rethink its security and relations with the world . <p> Just as 9/11 's terrible toll changed America , so too is the terrible toll of Hamas 's Oct. 7 terrorist attack changing Israel .
