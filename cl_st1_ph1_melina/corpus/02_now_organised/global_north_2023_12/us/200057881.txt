The Houthi group claims the attacks are a response to Israel 's ongoing military campaign against Hamas in Gaza . The most dramatic of these attacks took place last month when a group of armed Houthi militants seized the cargo ship , Galaxy Leader , in a helicopter-borne assault . The vessel had no Israeli crew on board , but its ownership is linked to Ray Car Carriers , founded by Israeli billionaire Rami Ungar . Last week , three commercial vessels in the region were hit by Houthi-launched missiles before the U.S. Navy shot down a drone launched by the rebels .
