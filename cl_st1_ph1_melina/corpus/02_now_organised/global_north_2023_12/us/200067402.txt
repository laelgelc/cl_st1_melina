<h> At the helm of Israel 's lethal eye in the sky <p> A drone operations room and Israeli made Hermes drones at Israel 's Palmachim Airbase <p> The Israeli-made Hermes 900 drone moved slowly across a cloudless sky at around 20,000 feet , just out of sight from the ground . <p> Its huge bulbous eye , tucked under the forward section of the anonymous grey craft , looked down on the battlefield below . For over 12 hours of constant flight , it relayed live images back to a small darkened control room below . <p> For the most part , say the pilots , the drones deployed over Gaza are gathering " battlefield intelligence " or providing " ground force protection " . They are an eye in the sky for the troops on the ground . <p> But the Hemes 900 , also known as Kochav or Star , carries a variety of guided bombs and can deliver " a precise legal strike when needed " . <h> ' Laser guided bombs ' <p> " We use small laser guided bombs with a payload of about a hundredth of a fighter jet " , one @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Tel Aviv told The Telegraph on Wednesday . <p> " We have two types . We can move it until the final seconds , and divert it into the sand if necessary . For every strike there must first be a designated abort area " . <p> The Telegraph was invited to Palmachim as criticism mounts over what Joe Biden this week called Israel 's " indiscriminate " bombing of Gaza , where more than 18,000 people are now reported to have died by the Hamas-run health authority . <p> Unguided " dumb bombs " account for up to 45 per cent of the 29,000 air-to-ground munitions that Israel has dropped on Gaza since Oct 7 , according to a recent US intelligence assessment obtained by CNN . <p> Since @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 1200 of their countrymen were killed , they have been working a rotation of four hours on four hours off , controlling the slow-moving , prop-driven drones which fly constantly above Gaza , just 25 miles to our south .
