<h> Get Our Newsletter <h> Follow Frontline <h> The FRONTLINE Interviews : <h> Netanyahu , America &amp; the Road to War in Gaza <p> To produce Netanyahu , America &amp; the Road to War in Gaza , FRONTLINE journalists interviewed Israeli , Palestinian and U.S. officials and insiders , as well as experts and observers of the decades-long Israeli-Palestinian conflict . They also drew on an archive of interviews conducted for the 2016 FRONTLINE documentary Netanyahu at War . These interviews offer first-hand accounts of the years of failed peace attempts , political maneuvering , and persistent outbreaks of violence leading up to the Oct. 7 Hamas attack on Israel and Israel 's retaliation in Gaza . The collection is part of FRONTLINE 's Transparency Project , which makes our source material available to the public . <p> Interviews for Netanyahu , America &amp; the Road to War in Gaza were conducted by James Jacoby , Anya Bourg and Lauren Ezell Kinlaw , and edited for the web by Lauren Prestileo .
