On Friday , that time arrived as Israeli aircraft continued bombing operations against Hamas infrastructure in the enclave after talks to extend a weeklong humanitarian truce broke down over a dispute about additional prisoner exchanges . Secretary of State Antony Blinken blamed Hamas for the breakdown , arguing that the terrorist group started sending rockets into Israel even before the pause was scheduled to expire . After it ended , the Israel Defense Forces did n't miss a beat , hitting 200 terrorist targets in Gaza . The Hamas-run Health Ministry reports that approximately 100 people have been killed thus far . <p> For those who believed the short-lived truce would snowball into a broader ceasefire leading to a termination of the war , the resumption of the fighting comes as a significant disappointment .
