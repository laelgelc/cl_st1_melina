<p> Thiessen chides Congress for not quickly passing Biden 's $105 million request for military aid mostly for Ukraine and Israel . From Vietnam to now Ukraine and Palestine/Israel , war is in , and diplomacy is out . <p> Our weapons make us complicit in the Gaza genocide and the destruction of Ukraine .
