( Reuters ) <h> WHO ARE THE HOUTHIS ? <p> The Houthis seized Yemen 's capital , Sanaa , in 2014 , launching a years-long , bloody war . The conflict soon became a proxy war between Saudi Arabia , which backed the Yemeni government in exile , and Iran , which backed the rebels . <p> The war caused a major humanitarian crisis that resulted in widespread hunger and misery in Yemen , the poorest country in the Arab world . The war has killed more than 150,000 people , including fighters and civilians , and created one of the world 's worst humanitarian disasters , killing tens of thousands more . <p> A ceasefire that technically ended a year ago has largely been honored . The Houthis currently control most of Yemen . <p> The group traces its history to a religious revival movement for the Zaydi sect of Shi'ite Islam in the late 1990s .
