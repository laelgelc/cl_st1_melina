Dozens of Palestinian security forces patrolled the empty square . <p> " This year , without the Christmas tree and without lights , there 's just darkness , " said Brother John Vinh , a Franciscan monk from Vietnam who has lived in Jerusalem for six years . <p> He said he always comes to Bethlehem to mark Christmas , but this year was especially sobering , as he gazed at a nativity scene in Manger Square with a baby Jesus wrapped in a white shroud , reminiscent of the thousands of children killed in the fighting in Gaza .
