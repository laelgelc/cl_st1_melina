<p> U.S. Capitol Police arrested about 50 people who were demonstrating inside a congressional office building Monday to demand a permanent cease-fire in the Israel-Gaza war . <p> The demonstration started around 10 a.m. and participants were arrested on charges of illegally protesting inside the Hart Senate Office Building , according to Tim Barber , a Capitol Police spokesman . Specifically , 49 were arrested under a D.C. code that prohibits crowding , obstructing or incommoding , which is often cited in the arrest of protesters during peaceful planned acts of civil disobedience . <p> The activists hung a banner that read " The People Choose Life " from Alexander Calder 's " Mountains and Clouds " sculpture in the Hart building and held another banner that equated sending additional aid to Israel with bombing Palestinians , according to videos of the protest . They wore shirts that said " Stop Weapons Now " and " Invest in Life " and chanted " Cease-fire now ! " as police used zip-tie handcuffs and escorted people out .
