<h> Las Dos Fridas <p> It is easy to see why the story of Diego Rivera and Frida Kahlo makes for excellent drama . Two famous painters -- one more famous in life , the other in death -- each iconic in their own way . There is Rivera : barrel-shaped , sleepy-eyed , one of Los Tres Grandes , the Big Three , who with his murals helped define Mexican national identity in the wake of the 1910 revolution . And there is the bird-like Kahlo , whose artistic origin tale begins with a nightmarish bus accident and ends with her making of her image -- and her many bodily devastations -- transcendent canvases . <p> Kahlo and Rivera were lovers and adversaries , confidants and intellectual partners , a viral artistic power couple before virality was a thing . They are figures who to this day can be conjured by first name alone . In other words , they are perfect material for an opera . <p> Daniela Mack as Frida Kahlo and Alfredo Daza as Diego Rivera stand before a figure representing Mictlantecuhtli , a deity of the underworld . <p> ( Cory Weaver / LA @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will stage the final performance of " El ? ltimo sue ? o de Frida y Diego , " ( The Last Dream of Frida and Diego ) , which had its world premiere last fall at the San Diego Opera .
