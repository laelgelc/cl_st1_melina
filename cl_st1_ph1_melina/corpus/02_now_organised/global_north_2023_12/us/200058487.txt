<h> Padma Lakshmi Is Transforming How Americans Think About Food <p> One of the best meals Top Chef alum Padma Lakshmi has ever eaten is Kabuli pulao -- an aromatic rice dish , cooked with lamb , carrots and raisins -- alongside members of the Afghan American community in Washington , D.C. She spoke with them about helping evacuees arriving after the Taliban 's 2021 takeover of Kabul , as well as how to get the recipe right , for her award-winning Hulu series Taste the Nation . Lakshmi has become known for showcasing this seamless blend of food and politics for American viewers . <p> Taste the Nation was born out of Lakshmi 's longtime advocacy around immigrants ' rights and her experience growing up as an Indian-American . It 's not just about food . " It 's really an excuse to dig deeper into these people 's lives , " she says . It 's also a way to platform a wider variety of cuisines , while amplifying the stories of immigrant communities .
