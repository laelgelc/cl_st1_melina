<p> A news broadcaster for a Turkish news channel was fired for featuring a Starbucks coffee cup on her desk during a live broadcast. ( iStock ) <p> " It is strictly forbidden for the announcer to present on TGRT News TV in a way that will covertly advertise any company , " the statement said . <p> " Our institution has an understanding that knows the sensitivities of the Turkish people regarding Gaza and defends them to the end . It is absolutely impossible to approve any action or publication contrary to this . We do not approve of this action of the presenter and director , whose employment contracts were terminated , and we strongly condemn it . For this reason , their employment contracts were terminated , " it continued . <p> The network reaffirmed its continued support for Gaza and the Turkish people . <p> Starbucks and its locations have faced boycotts and acts of vandalism over perceptions the coffee company is pro-Israel , because of its conflict with Workers United .
