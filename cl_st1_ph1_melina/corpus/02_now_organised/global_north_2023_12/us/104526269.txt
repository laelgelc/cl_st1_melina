<p> Israel and the West " shall soon await the closure of the Mediterranean Sea , the Strait of Gibraltar and other waterways , " Brigadier General Mohammad Reza Naqdi of the Iranian Revolutionary Guards warned . <p> The potential closure would be revenge for the United States and its allies for supporting " crimes " in the Gaza Strip as part of Israel 's retaliatory war in wake of Hamas ' Oct. 7 terror attack , the military commander said . <p> Iran backs Hamas , as well as Lebanon 's Hezbollah and the Houthi rebels from Yemen , but has no direct access to the Mediterranean itself -- making it unclear exactly how the Guards plan to close off the vast sea . <p> " The recent escalation in reckless Houthi attacks originating from Yemen threatens the free flow of commerce , endangers innocent mariners and violates international law , " Austin said in a statement from Bahrain . <p> On Friday , the White House confirmed that Iran was " deeply involved " in planning the Houthi attacks .
