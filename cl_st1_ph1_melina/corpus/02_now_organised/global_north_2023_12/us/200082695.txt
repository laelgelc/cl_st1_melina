Ross was forty-four , a giant as a filmmaker and photographer , widely published and lauded for his photographs and cinematography . Ross was relentlessly attentive . During the Sharia court proceedings , held in a brutally hot , tiny , pink room with a single window , he caught every glance , every tick .
