" We urge Congress to pass the NDAA quickly and President Biden to sign it when it reaches his desk . " <p> The must-pass bill dictates how the Pentagon can spend the money contained in a separate appropriations bill , which has yet to be approved by Congress two months into fiscal 2024 , which began Oct. 1 .
