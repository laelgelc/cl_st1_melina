<p> The Latin Patriarch of Jerusalem and other religious leaders arrived in Bethlehem on Sunday for their annual Christmas Eve procession , although the scene was much subdued from its typical fanfare . There were no decorations or lights , the traditional Manger Square Christmas Tree was absent , and no parades were traveling down the streets . <p> Local religious leaders issued a statement last month encouraging a scaled-back Christmas celebration , instead urging Christians to " stand strong " with those in Gaza and the West Bank during the war by " foregoing any unnecessarily festive activities .
