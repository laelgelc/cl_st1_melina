Electoral reform <p> Three citizen-initiated referendums to reform elections in Skokie were approved by voters in 2022 , and the village began implementing them this year . The referendums included making elections nonpartisan , introducing staggered terms for elected officials , and creating a new map that will split the village into four districts so that one village trustee would be elected from each . Two trustees would also be elected from within the entire village 's boundaries . <p> Skokie is in the midst of implementing three changes to their election ordinances . One of them will be to establish electoral districts , which are being drafted and proposed by outside consultants .
