<p> Susan Sarandon issued an apology for controversial comments she made at a pro-Palestine rally in New York last month . <p> At the Nov. 17 rally , the actor had said , " There are a lot of people that are afraid , that are afraid of being Jewish at this time , and are getting a taste of what it feels like to be a Muslim in this country , so often subjected to violence . " UTA subsequently dropped Sarandon as a client over the remarks . <p> In a post on Instagram late Friday , Sarandon wrote that " It was my intent to show solidarity in the struggle against bigotry of all kinds , and I am sorry I failed to do so . " <p> " Recently , I attended a rally alongside a diverse group of activists seeking to highlight the urgent humanitarian crisis in Gaza and call for a ceasefire , " Sarandon wrote . " I had not planned to speak but was invited to take the stage and say a few words . " <p> She continued , " Intending to communicate my concern for an increase in hate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ targets of rising antisemitic hate , ' are getting a taste of what it is like to be Muslim in this country , so often subjected to violence . ' This phrasing was a terrible mistake , as it implies that until recently Jews have been strangers to persecution , when the opposite is true . As we all know , from centuries of oppression and genocide in Europe , to the Tree of Life shooting in Pittsburgh , PA , Jews have long been familiar with discrimination and religious violence which continues to this day . I deeply regret diminishing this reality and hurting people with this comment . " <p> Sarandon concluded , " I will continue my commitment to peace , truth , justice , and compassion for all people . I hope that we can meet with love and willingness to engage in dialogue , especially with those with whom we disagree . " <p> The Oct. 7 terrorist attacks by Hamas on Israeli civilians and the ensuing Israel-Hamas war have exposed deep divisions in Hollywood .
