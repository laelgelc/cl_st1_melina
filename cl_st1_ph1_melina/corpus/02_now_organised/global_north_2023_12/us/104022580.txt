<h> Israel 's Impossible Dilemma <p> To no one 's surprise , Israel and Hamas have resumed fighting in Gaza after almost a week of temporary truces and prisoner exchanges . Despite American and other entreaties to limit civilian casualties , Israel appears determined to push into the south of Gaza , but its strategic thinking seems to end there , and to hold no plausible endgame in sight . As a consequence , the next phase of this vicious conflict will almost certainly lead Israel to an unenviable dilemma : whether to grant Hamas a small and ultimately hollow victory or a much larger and all-too-real one . <p> The next stages of the fighting seem clear . Israel will likely seize all of the significant aboveground urban areas in Gaza 's south , just as it did in the north . After that will come a major battle for control of Hamas 's extensive underground tunnel network , where most of the group 's fighters , leaders , equipment , and remaining hostages are presumed to be located .
