There are passages in Prophet Song that echo with 2020 's brutal police crackdowns on Black Lives Matter marches and former president Donald Trump 's increasingly autocratic and apocalyptic language . Some of the ghastliest scenes feel pulled from current reports of Israel 's relentless bombardment of the Gaza Strip , from Russia 's assault on Ukrainian sovereignty . None of these events had yet occurred when Lynch began writing Prophet Song four years ago .
