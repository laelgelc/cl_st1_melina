<p> Iran 's support for the Yemen Shia Islamist rebel group has been years in the making . However , President Joe Biden 's administration provided details on Dec. 22 of their involvement in the attacks against commercial vessels largely in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait . The narrow passageway leads into the Red Sea and the Suez Canal , and separates Yemen from Eritrea and Djibouti on the waterway 's African side . <p> In recent weeks , the Houthis have launched more than 100 one-way drones and ballistic missile attacks targeting at least 10 merchant vessels .
