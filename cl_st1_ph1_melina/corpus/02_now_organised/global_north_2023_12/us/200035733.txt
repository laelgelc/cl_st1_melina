<p> Film and TV production in Israel has been in virtual shutdown mode since Hamas ' October 7 attack but , nearly two months on , a group of producers are looking to try and roll the cameras again when possible . <p> The Israeli Producers Association ( IPA ) has launched a drive for donations and help in order to get projects back up and running once fighting stops . <p> A new group , titled Friends of Israeli Film &amp; TV Producers Association ( FIPA ) , was set up during the just-ended temporary ceasefire , with information distributed around producer circles in the nation . " We are working in emergency mode to bring back production and filming , " says a leaflet promoting FIPA . " This is the livelihood of thousands of Israeli families . And there are many more challenges in the short term and long term . " <p> The leaflet urges those who are willing to " please be a part of the efforts to keep telling the Israeli story .
