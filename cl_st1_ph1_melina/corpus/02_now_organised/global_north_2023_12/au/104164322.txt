<p> Thousands of protesters have come out in support of Palestine in a series of rallies held across the country on Sunday . <p> In Sydney 's CBD , NSW Police estimate about 6500 people flooded Hyde Park before supporters marched around the surrounding streets for the ninth consecutive week . A police spokesman said attendees were " pretty well-behaved , " and said there were no arrests and no incidents . <p> Attendees waved the green , red and white Palestinian flag , and held signs denouncing the violence on the Gaza Strip . One man held a placard of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu , with the words : The Butcher of Gaza . <p> One attendee was seen with fake blood smeared on his face .
