Or they are too earnest , daring to fracture the careful fictions spun for our delight and consideration by speaking out of place . <p> We turn to them for advice , insights into what it is to be human , for their anecdotes , as role models . But we expect them to leave themselves out of their work . We expect them to take on risks , to present the big ideas , to pose questions , to stimulate and to challenge . But we also require that they keep their mouths shut , to park their own beliefs and values at the stage door .
