<h> Australia pledges $265m to support refugees overseas as number of displaced people hits record level <h> Countries attending the largest international gathering on refugees have pledged a total of A$3.3 billion ( US$2.2 billion ) to help refugees as the number of people displaced globally hits record levels . <p> The Australian government has announced funding to displaced people overseas with $235 million to support displaced Rohingyas . A further $20 million people is being allocated to help those in Afghanistan and people forced to flee to neighbouring countries . An additional $10 million will help those in Sudan and those displaced to neighbouring countries .
