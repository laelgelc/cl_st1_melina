<h> Welfare recipients made to sweat on miscalculated debts <p> Welfare recipients have been forced to wait years as public servants responsible for Centrelink payments try to unscramble unlawful debts . <p> Two federal agencies have been warned to speed up their work to fix tens of thousands of miscalculated debt notices . <p> As many as 100,000 welfare debts were miscalculated by Services Australia and the Department of Social Services between 2003 and 2020 , the Commonwealth Ombudsman has found . <p> The departments ' " income apportionment " practices misinterpreted and unlawfully applied the Social Security Act from at least 2003 until December 7 , 2020 , when the law changed . <p> This resulted in some Centrelink customers ' employment income being assessed in the wrong fortnight and potentially affected a significant number of payments .
