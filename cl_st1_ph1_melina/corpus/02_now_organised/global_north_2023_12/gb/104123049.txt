<p> Zarrar Kahn 's Karachi-set thriller In Flames won the $100,000 Golden Yusr award for best feature film at the 2023 Red Sea International Film Festival , which announced its winners on Thursday evening ( December 7 ) . <p> A Canada-Pakistan co-production and Pakistan 's entry to the Oscars , In Flames is the story of a mother and daughter trying to survive after losing the family patriarch . It world premiered in Cannes ' Directors ' Fortnight . <p> Indian production Dear Jassi , directed by Tarsem Singh , won the $30,000 Silver Yusr . Based on the true story of an Indian couple who fell foul of the class system , the film world premiered in Toronto 's Platform section . <p> British-Palestinian director Farah Nabulsi received the jury prize for her Palestinian feature The Teacher , starring Saleh Bakri who also won best actor award for his role . <p> Nabulsi was emotional while receiving the two awards on stage , saying that in her moment of happiness of winning she could n't ignore what is happening to the Palestinian people in Gaza : " Saleh won his award for being an honest Palestinian and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said one thing , ' Stop genocide in Gaza , ' " <p> Uzbekistan 's Shokir Kholikov won the best director prize for family drama Sunday . Mouna Hawa won the best actress trophy for her performance in Jordan 's Oscar submission Inshallah A Boy . <p> Among other awards , Saudi actress Nour AlKhadra won the rising star prize , having starred in festival 's opening film HWJN by Yasir AlYasiri and the Matchmaker by Abdulmohsen Aldhabaan . <p> The Red Sea competition jury was headed by director Baz Luhrmann . <p> The awards ceremony took place at the Ritz Carlton theatre in Jeddah . Nicholas Cage received a Red Sea honouree award , joining the festival 's 2023 honouree line-up of Diane Kruger , Ranveer Singh , and Abdullah Al-Sadhan . Cage wore a red suit for the occasion .
