<h> Cairo , Jeddah and Dubai are top destinations for MENA travellers this winter <p> With winter travel taking off in the lead up to Christmas , airfares across the Middle East and North Africa region have witnessed a spike , with a 11.37% year-on-year increase in international flight bookings until January , according to online travel marketplace Wego . <p> However , as a possible impact of the geopolitical tensions in the region , following the onset of the Israel-Gaza war , data has further revealed that average flight prices on some traditionally popular routes have dropped this winter compared to 2022 , with Cairo experiencing the biggest decline in fares , resulting in an upward tick in bookings . <p> According to data , flights to Cairo from several GCC countries have witnessed some of the steepest price drops , averaging up to 35% cut compared to last year 's winter period .
