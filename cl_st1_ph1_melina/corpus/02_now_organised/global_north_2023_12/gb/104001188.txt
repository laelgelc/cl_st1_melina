<p> British-Israeli citizen Binyamin Needham celebrated his 19th birthday just a fortnight ago . He was named by officials today as being one of three killed in action . He had only been in the besieged Strip for two days when he sustained fatal injuries , the full details of which are yet to be revealed . <p> The teen -- who was born in Edgware , north London -- moved back to Israel with his parents Faye and Ammon 10 years ago . They settled near Haifa in the north of the country . Binyamin was the youngest of six , and known to his loved ones as the " baby of the family " . He was also a sergeant in the IDF 's Combat Engineering Corps of the 601st Battalion . He and two other IDF troops were killed Sunday . <p> He was fresh to the IDF after having just finished his apprenticeship and professional training .
