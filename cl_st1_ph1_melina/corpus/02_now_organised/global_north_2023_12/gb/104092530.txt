<p> Egypt is striving to accelerate the delivery of aid to the Gaza Strip , a senior official said on Thursday , after the amount of relief getting through to the Palestinian enclave dipped with the end of the Israel-Hamas truce on Dec. 1 . <p> Diaa Rashwan , head of the State Information Service , said Egypt would never allow the emptying of the Gaza Strip of its residents as Israel 's military campaign pushes them southwards towards the border with Egypt 's Sinai Peninsula . <p> Since the conflict in Gaza began on Oct. 7 , the Rafah crossing on its border with Egypt has been the only entry point for aid trucks carrying desperately needed supplies of food , medicines , water and fuel . <p> The number of trucks crossing daily has dropped in recent days to fewer than 100 , from nearly 200 when the week-long truce was in place .
