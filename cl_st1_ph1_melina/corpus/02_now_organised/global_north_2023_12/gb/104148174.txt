<p> A silent ' shine a light vigil for children of Gaza ' will be held in Nuneaton next week . The Palestine Solidarity Group of Nuneaton has organised the event outside the Town Hall on Wednesday ( December 13 ) . <p> In a poster , the group explains that they will be laying six coffins on the steps of the Town Hall , each said to " represent 1,000 innocent children who have been killed in conflict to date " . The silent vigil is to be staged from 5.30pm . <p> The poster invites families and children to join in the vigil to support and show solidarity for children in Gaza . It also requests , for safety reasons , that people do not bring candles for the vigil . <p> Instead , those who go along are being urged to use their phone lights or torches to take part in the silent vigil . Roads around the Town Hall may have to be closed during the event . <p> The vigil is being held half an hour before the final Nuneaton and Bedworth Borough Council full council meeting of the year .
