<h> GCC 's non-energy sectors to drive GDP expansion in 2024 <p> The GCC region is expected to defy the global slowdown in 2024 as non-energy sectors continue to drive growth and the pace of the Middle East 's GDP growth is predicted to rise to 3.2% next year , above the global GDP growth forecast of 2.1% , according to the latest Economic Insight report for the Middle East , commissioned by ICAEW and compiled by Oxford Economics , <p> According to the Q4 report , the Middle East economy will expand less than initially expected this year , with GDP growth expected at 1.3% . However , growth will improve in 2024 and outpace most advanced and emerging economies . <p> Projections for GCC growth this year have been scaled back to 0.7% due to a large negative contribution from the energy sector amid ongoing curbs in oil production . However , there are promising signs within the non-energy sectors , notably in Saudi Arabia and the UAE .
