<p> Crowds gathered in Liverpool City Centre for a protest against the death of medics in Gaza . <p> Men , women and children , gathered at St Luke 's Church , also known as the Bombed Out Church , at the top of Bold Street on Friday , December 1 at 7pm . People stood by the steps of the church and listened to guest speakers , who called for a immediate and permanent ceasefire in Gaza . <p> Candles were lit on the steps as people chanted and clapped . A number of people were wearing or waving Palestine flags and holding signs . <p> According to Health Workers for Palestine , a total of 254 medical personnel have died to date in the Israel-Gaza conflict . Along with the protest at the Bombed Out Church , people gathered up and down the country in cities including London , Birmingham , Leeds and Sheffield . <p> There were also planned protests nationwide in Australia , the USA and Netherlands .
