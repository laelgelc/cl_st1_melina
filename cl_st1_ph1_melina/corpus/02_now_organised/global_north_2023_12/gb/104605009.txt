<h> Increase in Kuwait 's import bill for goods <p> KUWAIT CITY , Dec 26 : Official statistics showed an increase in Kuwait 's import bill for goods by about 13.3 percent or KD1 billion in the first nine months of 2023 , reports Al-Anba daily .
