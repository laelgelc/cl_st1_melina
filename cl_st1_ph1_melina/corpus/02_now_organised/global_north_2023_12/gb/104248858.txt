<p> The United Nations called for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza on Tuesday night , with more than three quarters of countries in the 193-member general assembly demanding a halt to the violence , days after the United States vetoed a truce in the conflict . <p> The assembly voted overwhelmingly in favour of a ceasefire , with 153 countries in favour , 10 against and 23 abstentions . Arabic and Islamic nations had led the call for the resolution , which denounced the " catastrophic humanitarian situation in the Gaza Strip and the suffering of the Palestinian civilian population " . <h> Sponsored <p> The US vetoed a resolution in the UN security council last week that called for a ceasefire , and voted against the resolution , but does not have a veto in the general assembly .
