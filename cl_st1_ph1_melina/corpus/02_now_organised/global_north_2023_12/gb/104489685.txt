<p> LONDON - The entire 2.3 million population of the Gaza Strip is facing crisis levels of hunger and the risk of famine is increasing each day as the Israel-Hamas war grinds on , a U.N.-backed body said in a report published on Thursday . <p> That makes the proportion of households in the Palestinian enclave that are in hunger crisis , or suffering from high levels of acute food insecurity , the largest ever recorded globally , the report by the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification ( IPC ) said . <p> The humanitarian situation in Gaza has deteriorated rapidly since Israel began a major military operation on Oct. 7 , with heavy air strikes and a ground offensive laying waste to wide areas of the enclave since then , in response to a shock , deadly rampage into Israel by militants of Gaza 's ruling Hamas group . <p> Trucks bringing aid from Egypt have delivered some food , water and medicine , but the United Nations says the quantity of food is just 10% of what is needed for the territory 's inhabitants , most of whom have been displaced . <p> " There is a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the current situation of intense hostilities and restricted humanitarian access persists or worsens , " the IPC report said . <p> Distribution of aid within Gaza has been hampered by military operations , inspections of aid demanded by Israel , communications blackouts and shortages of fuel . <p> Some desperate Gazans have jumped onto aid trucks to try to grab scarce supplies of food and other goods .
