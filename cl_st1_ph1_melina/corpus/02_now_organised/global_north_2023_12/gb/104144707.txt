<p> Fierce fighting between Israel and Hamas , which erupted after the terror group brutally murdered 1,200 men , women , and children on October 7 , has led to the deaths of more than 17,000 Palestinians , according to Gaza 's Hamas-run health ministry . <p> The UK chose to abstain on a United Nations Security Council ( UNSC ) resolution demanding an immediate humanitarian ceasefire in Gaza - a motion that was vetoed by the US . <p> Speaking at the UNSC meeting on Friday , UK ambassador to the UN Dame Barbara Woodward Britain backs " further and longer pauses " to get aid to Palestinians and to allow the release of Israeli hostages . <p> But she argued to the council that " we can not vote in favour of a resolution which does not condemn the atrocities Hamas committed against innocent Israeli civilians " on October 7.
