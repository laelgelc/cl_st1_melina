<h> Nicola Sturgeon : This is right time to try harder to remember the spirit of Christmas <h> Nicola Sturgeon : Try harder to remember the spirit of Christmas <p> Nicola Sturgeon : This is right time to try harder to remember the spirit of Christmas ( Image : Newsquest ) <p> CHRISTMAS is a time when we are all supposed to be full of joy and bonhomie . <p> Instagram and other social media feeds are full of ' perfect ' pictures of fun nights out and family get-togethers . It is easy to believe that everyone is having a brilliant time . And many will be , which is fantastic . <p> For many , though , Christmas brings mixed emotions . <p> A heightened sense of loneliness for those who do n't have family and friends around . <p> For those who have lost loved ones , a reminder of the grief they feel . Conversely , an obligation to socialise with people that we might not normally choose to spend so much time with . <p> Massive pressure -- especially for those with kids -- to spend too much money .
