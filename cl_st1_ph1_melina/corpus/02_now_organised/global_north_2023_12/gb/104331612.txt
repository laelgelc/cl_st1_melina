<p> Hundreds of people took to the streets for the latest pro-Palestine march in Manchester . Protestors held placards and led chants as they made their way from Platt Fields Park in Fallowfield to the city centre , eventually stopping at St Peter 's Square . <p> There was a significant police presence as officers monitored protestors , with the march passing peacefully down Wilmslow Road , through the Curry Mile and then onto Oxford Road and finally culminating with speeches being made outside Central Library . Bus services were affected and trams were briefly halted to allow the protest to take place . <p> Along the way , protestors took aim at several multi-national businesses , starting chants about Pret A Manger , McDonald 's , Starbucks and KFC . The front doors of branches of McDonald 's and Pret A Manger were locked , and officers stood guard , as the march passed through . <p> At Pret A Manger on the corner of Portland Street , one protestor moved to the windows of the caf ? and displayed a placard at customers inside .
