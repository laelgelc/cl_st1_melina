<p> Whatever the " true " story of Melissa Caddick , who Google will tell you is Australia 's " conwoman of the century " , Vanishing Act ( ITV1 ) , a schlocky drama , does it little service . In real life , Caddick was a dodgy Aussie fund manager who set up a Ponzi scheme and revelled in spending the gains it brought her . She stole from friends , family and anyone with money ... and then in December 2020 she vanished . <p> It 's doubtful that such an obvious crook and chancer would have merited much further attention , were it not for the appearance of one of her running shoes , which washed up on a beach three months later and several hundred kilometres away from where she disappeared . In a flourish that could have been made for the true-crime age we currently inhabit , the shoe had her severed foot still in it . <p> Unfortunately , therefore , this means that by far the most noteworthy part of Caddick 's story is its ending , and its ending comes at the very beginning of this three-part drama .
