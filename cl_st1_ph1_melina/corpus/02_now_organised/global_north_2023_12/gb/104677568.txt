<h> Pro-Palestinian protesters gather at Trafford Centre <p> Pro-Palestinian protesters gathered at the Trafford Centredemanding a ceasefire in Gaza . Crowds of demonstrators flooded the shopping destination waving flags and clutching banners . <p> Stores including Zara and Marks &amp; Spencer were closed , with staffing putting the front shutters down on Saturday ( December 30 ) . A spokesperson for the Trafford Centre said : " There was a small demonstration targeting several retailers within our centre , which was managed peacefully by the relevant authorities and centre security teams . <p> " We are working closely with our brand partners to ensure the safety of our visitors and those who work at the centre . " <p> One protestor , who did not give his name , told the M.E.N that Zara was targeted in response to an advertisement by the retailer which the company pulled after receiving complaints it contained pictures resembling images from the Israel-Hamas war . <p> The Jacket campaign contained a series of images in which the model was pictured against a background of cracked stones , damaged statues and broken plasterboard . Zara said the campaign presented ' a series of images @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ created with the sole purpose of showcasing craft-made garments in an artistic context ' . <p> Some suggested they were similar to images emerging from Gaza . Zara said : " Unfortunately , some customers felt offended by these images , which have now been removed , and saw in them something far from what was intended when they were created .
