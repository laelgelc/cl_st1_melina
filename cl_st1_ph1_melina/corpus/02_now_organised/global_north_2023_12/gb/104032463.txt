" <p> Queen Nikkolah is the persona created by artist Laura Nsengiyumva ( Image : Facebook ) <p> The city of Ghent in Belgium has decided to cancel the appearance of Queen Nikkolah , a female " Santa Claus " who called for the liberation of Belgians from " colonial ideology " <p> Ghent mayor , Mathias de Clercq , caved into criticism and pressure , describing Queen Nikkolah , who was dressed in Palestinian flag colours , as " woker than woke . " <p> The original plan called for her to hand out gifts to children at the town hall , thereby challenging the traditional figure of Sinterklaas , the Belgian Santa Claus . Mr de Clercq said : " There is nothing wrong with Sinterklaas , as we know him , we should n't try to turn him into something else . " <p> Queen Nikkolah , created by artist Laura Nsengiyumva as an African alternative to Sinterklaas , aimed to provide children with revised versions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ New Flemish Alliance ( N-VA ) , Belgium 's largest party in the Dutch-speaking Flanders region , criticised the project as being " woker than woke " . <p> More than 300 children had registered to participate in the alternative event in Ghent , according to organisers . This year , Queen Nikkolah planned to wear Palestinian flags to raise awareness about the plight of children in Gaza amid the ongoing conflict between Israel and Hamas . <p> St Nicholas Day is celebrated on December 6 in Belgium and the Netherlands , with Sinterklaas traditionally accompanied by his Zwarte Piet , or " Black Pete " , a black-faced helper depicted with curly hair and bright red lips .
