<p> BERLIN - The German government will carry out a series of additional aid measures to support the civilian population in and from Gaza , a foreign ministry spokesperson said on Wednesday , adding that several ministries will work together to do so . <p> " This includes several relief flights that will take place before Christmas , " the spokesperson said during a regular news conference , adding that a flight operated by Germany 's air force is scheduled to depart on Saturday to Egypt . <p> The aim is to deliver incubators and ventilators for Egyptian hospitals treating infants from Gaza .
