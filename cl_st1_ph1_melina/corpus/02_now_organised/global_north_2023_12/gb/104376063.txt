<p> From the start of the war on October 7 , Israel has struggled to reconcile its key objectives , to destroy Hamas 's military and governing capabilities in Gaza and to rescue more than 240 hostages captured by the terrorist group . As the accidental killing of three hostages by Israeli troops on Friday has proven , doing both is proving to be extremely difficult . <p> The Israeli war cabinet and their generals decided on a strategy of pursuing the war with utmost ferocity while conducting negotiations with Hamas through its Qatari patrons .
