<p> The Bishop of Gloucester has published her annual Christmas message , this year focusing on the extreme suffering and death being experienced by children across Israel and Gaza since the October 7 Hamas attacks . Rt Rev Rachel Treweek wants to remind people about the message of the nativity story , and the child at the centre of it . <p> The Bishop of Gloucester 's Christmas message in full reads : " It is said that Christmas is for the children -- and amid the twinkling lights and Santas and food adverts , there is a tiny child -- Lying ' away in a manger ' over 2,000 years ago in Bethlehem , Palestine . The first Christmas . <p> " Although it 's not all sweetness . Let 's not forget King Herod . The nativity plays stop short of Herod 's slaughter of innocent babies , and Jesus and his parents fleeing into Egypt . It all seems rather poignant as I look to the Middle East this Christmas . <p> " There has still been the cry of a child -- well many .
