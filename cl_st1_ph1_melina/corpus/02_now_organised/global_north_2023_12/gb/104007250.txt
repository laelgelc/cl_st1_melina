<h> Gold prices scale record high as Fed rate-cut bets support <p> Gold prices extended gains to climb a record high on Monday , as Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell 's remarks elevated traders ' confidence that the U.S. central bank could cut interest rates early next year . <p> FUNDAMENTALS <p> * Spot gold was up 0.8% at $2,087.79 per ounce by 0145 GMT . Earlier in the session , bullion surged to an all-time high of $2,111.39 . <p> * U.S. gold futures for February delivery rose nearly 1% to $2,107.60 . <p> * Powell on Friday said " the risks of under- and over-tightening are becoming more balanced , " but the Fed is not thinking about lowering rates right now . <p> * The market viewed his comments as dovish , sending the dollar index and 10-year Treasury yields lower on Friday . <p> * A weaker U.S. dollar makes greenback-priced gold less expensive for other currency holders . <p> * While Powell reiterated that the Fed is prepared to tighten policy further if deemed appropriate , traders were convinced the rate-hike cycle was over , pricing in a 70% chance for a rate cut @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ CME 's FedWatch Tool shows . <p> * Euro zone inflation fell to 2.4% in November , data showed last week , providing fresh fuel to bets that the ECB will cut interest rates quicker than the bank has been suggesting . <p> * The Israeli military said its ground forces were operating against Hamas across the Gaza Strip on Sunday , its clearest indication yet that a planned ground offensive in the enclave 's heavily crowded south had begun as Israeli bombing killed and wounded dozens of Palestinians . <p> * Spot silver rose 0.3% to $25.49 per ounce , palladium advanced 0.8% to $1,008.40 per ounce , while platinum was down 0.2% at $932 . <p> ( Reporting by Harshit Verma in Bengaluru ; Editing by Sherry Jacob-Phillips ) <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
