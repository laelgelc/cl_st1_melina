<h> New York Times reporters take action over union ' interference ' <p> Senior reporters at The New York Times have formed a group to advocate for journalistic neutrality over concerns that the trade union representing staff at the paper could compromise their independence by taking stands on big political issues of the day . <p> The group , calling itself an " independence caucus " , is said to have been formed after the union , NewsGuild-CWA ( Communication Workers Union ) , which represents journalists at leading American newspapers but also some campaigning organisations , held a meeting on whether it should call for a ceasefire in Gaza . <h> Sponsored <p> Similar concerns were raised earlier this year after a union official in New York weighed in to support members of staff at The New York Times who had signed an open letter attacking the paper 's coverage of transgender issues .
