<p> It takes a while but slowly the children gathered around volunteer entertainer Ruaa Hassuna in a Gaza camp start clapping along as her music offers some respite from the horrors around them . <p> Smiles light up the faces of the children , huddled amid the tents outside the south Gaza town of Rafah which shelter hundreds of thousands of Palestinians driven from their homes by more than two months of relentless Israeli bombardment . <p> Hassuna is part of a troupe of more than a dozen volunteer entertainers who travel from camp to makeshift camp on a mission to provide children with some escape , however brief , from the death and destruction they have witnessed . <p> The 23-year-old plays the oud , a lute-like stringed instrument popular across the Middle East . Other volunteers entertain the children with slapstick , acrobatics , story-telling or dance . <p> " We use whatever means we can to remove the children from the war , " says the 23-year-old . " The aim of getting them to sing is to alleviate their stress .
