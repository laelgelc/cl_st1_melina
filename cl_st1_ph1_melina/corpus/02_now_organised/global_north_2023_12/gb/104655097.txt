<p> Cinema speaks a universal language . It is this language that allows us to peek into the life of a media baron in Florida ( Citizen Kane ) , feel the helplessness of a family in rural Bengal ( Pather Panchali ) and view a samurai 's murder from different vantage points ( Rashomon ) . When it comes to the Arab world , we have long relied on Western interpretations to understand the region . But today , the tables have turned . <p> Arab filmmakers are reclaiming the cultural space through films that talk about their lived realities , debunk Western stereotypes and showcase the complexity of an evolving society .
