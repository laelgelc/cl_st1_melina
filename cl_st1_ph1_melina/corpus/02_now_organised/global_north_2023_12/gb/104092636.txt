<h> How Egyptians will vote for their president <p> Egypt will hold a presidential election on Dec. 10-12 in which former army chief Abdel Fattah al-Sisi is poised to secure a third term that will extend his rule into a second decade . <p> Public attention has shifted from an economic crisis to the war in Gaza during the run-up to the polls . <p> Here are some facts about the vote . <p> VOTING <p> The vote in Egypt will take place from Dec. 10-12 . Voting for Egyptians abroad was held on Dec. 1-3 . <p> Results are due to be announced on Dec. 18 , with an absolute majority required to avoid a second round in early January . <p> Sisi was announced winner of the previous two presidential elections in 2014 and 2018 with 97% of the votes . <p> Approximately 67 million Egyptians above the age of 18 are eligible to vote , according to the election authority , out of a total population of 104 million . <p> People barred from voting include those under guardianship due to mental illness , those convicted of crimes including tax evasion and corruption of political life @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> CANDIDATES <p> The candidates running in the election are Abdel Fattah al-Sisi , president since 2014 ; Farid Zahran , head of the centre-left Egyptian Social Democratic party ; Abdel Sanad Yamama , head of the liberal Al Wafd party ; and Hazem Omar , head of the Republican People 's Party . <p> The most prominent potential opposition candidate , Ahmed al-Tantawy , withdrew in October , complaining that dozens of his supporters had been arrested and that officials and pro-government thugs had hampered his campaign . <p> Egypt 's National Election Authority ( NEA ) has said it was reviewing Tantawy 's complaints , and that such allegations were baseless . <p> OVERSIGHT <p> The NEA is responsible for overseeing and managing all elections and referendums in Egypt . <p> The election will be held under the supervision of judges and accredited local and foreign civil society organizations will be allowed to observe voting , according to the NEA . <p> PRESIDENTIAL TERM <p> Following constitutional amendments in 2019 , the presidential term was extended to six from four years . <p> The amendments changed an outright @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a bar on serving more than two consecutive terms . <p> An additional clause extended Sisi 's second term to six years from four , and allowed him to run for a third term under the new rules . <p> Sisi is the fourth former military man to establish his grip over Egypt since Gamal Abdel Nasser and other army officers toppled the monarchy in 1952 . <p> Nasser became president in 1954 and was succeeded on his death in 1970 by Anwar Sadat , who ruled until he was assassinated in 1981 . Sadat 's deputy , Hosni Mubarak , held power from 1981 until he was ousted in a popular uprising nearly three decades later . <p> Under Sadat , a two-term limit was imposed and then removed . <p> ( Reporting by Farah Saafan ; Editing by Aidan Lewis and David Evans ) <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
