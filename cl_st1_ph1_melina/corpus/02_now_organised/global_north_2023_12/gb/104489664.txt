<h> UAE Central Bank raises 2024 economy growth forecast to 5.7% <p> The Central Bank of the UAE ( CBUAE ) has revised the country 's 2024 growth projection from 4.3% to 5.7% as OPEC+ announced significant increases in oil production next year , the apex bank said in its latest Quarterly Economic Review report . <p> However , the CBUAE has revised its growth projections for 2023 from 3.3% to 3.1% , largely reflecting the extension of oil production cuts through the end of the year . <p> In the first week of December , OPEC+ oil producers agreed to voluntary output cuts totalling about 2.2 million barrels per day ( bpd ) for the first quarter of 2024 , led by Saudi Arabia rolling over its current voluntary cut . <p> The UAE also agreed to lower output by 163,000 bpd next year , from January until the end of March . <p> According to the CBUAE , oil GDP is expected to contract in 2023 by 3.4% in 2022 , as production cuts are forecast to be in place until the end of 2023 .
