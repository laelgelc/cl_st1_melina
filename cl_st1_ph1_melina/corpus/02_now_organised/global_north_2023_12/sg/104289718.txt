<h> Philippines votes in favour of UN resolution demanding for humanitarian ceasefire , release of hostages in Gaza <h> Charie Abarca <p> MANILA -- The Philippines has voted in favor of a United Nations ( UN ) resolution demanding an urgent humanitarian ceasefire and unconditional release of all hostages in Gaza . <p> The United Nations General Assembly on Tuesday afternoon met in an emergency special session to deliberate upon a pivotal measure that specifically demands the following : <p> Immediate humanitarian ceasefire between Israel and Hamas <p> Compliance of all parties with their obligations under international law <p> Unconditional release of all hostages , as well as ensuring humanitarian access in Gaza <p> Member states later adopted the measure with 153 votes in favor , 10 against , and 23 abstentions . Among those who voted in favor of the resolution is the Philippines . <p> Permanent Representative of the Philippines to the UN Antonio Lagdameo , during the emergency special session , said while the Philippines condemns the October 7 attacks perpetrated by Hamas , the country believes it is vital to adhere to international humanitarian law , especially the principles of proportionality and distinction in response @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ any military action considers the impact on civilians and strives to minimize , if not eliminate , collateral damage , " said Lagdameo . <p> " We are deeply concerned about the humanitarian situation in Gaza . The Philippines believes strongly that the humanitarian ceasefire is crucial to halt the loss of life and suffering . This ceasefire is a necessary step to facilitate the delivery of urgent humanitarian aid to all affected civilians , " he added . <p> Lagdameo called on all parties to comply with their obligations under international law , particularly regarding the protection of civilians , especially women and children . <p> " Finally , the Philippines seeks to contribute to a solution that respects the rights and needs of both Palestinian and Israeli civilians . We advocate for a peaceful resolution that upholds international law and leads to lasting peace and security in the region , " he added . <p> The Department of Foreign Affairs , on the other hand , has yet to detail what prompted the change in the country 's vote . <p> In October , the Philippines abstained from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ humanitarian truce .
