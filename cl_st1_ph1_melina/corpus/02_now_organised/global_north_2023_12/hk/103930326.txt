<h> Press group concerned over report of South China Morning Post journalist missing after China trip <p> Award-winning journalist Minnie Chan had attended a regional security forum in Beijing , which ended on October 31 . Staff at the paper told HKFP that managers had not been in touch about her whereabouts or welfare . <p> South China Morning Post ( SCMP ) reporter Minnie Chan has gone missing after a work trip to China , Japan 's Kyodo News reported on Thursday citing unnamed people close to the matter . A local press group has expressed concern over the apparent disappearance . <p> The award-winning journalist had attended the three-day Xiangshan Forum in Beijing -- a regional security dialogue which ended on October 31 . <p> Multiple calls and emails to the Alibaba-owned newspaper went unanswered on Friday . Staff at the paper , who did not wish to be identified , told HKFP that there has been no internal communication about Chan 's whereabouts or welfare . <p> According to Kyodo News , Chan 's friends were concerned she was under investigation by the Chinese authorities .
