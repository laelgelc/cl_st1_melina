<h> Sarah Carey : Let 's address our own prejudices first before we give out about the living hell of Gaza <p> Oliver Sears , whose mother and grandmother survived the Holocaust , spoke on radio this week about the complexity of life as a Jew . Photo : Sasko Lazarov <p> Sarah Carey <p> Today at 03:30 <p> When I heard about the Hamas attack on Israel , my heart sank . Not just for the savagery of the massacre -- the young people mown down at the rave , the slaughter of families , the mutilations and bloodied bodies of the raped women taken as hostages -- but for what was to come . Evil would beget evil . And so it has . <p> Netanyahu 's despicable government unleashed hell on Gaza , precisely as Hamas wanted . Now the suffering of Palestinians , bombarded by the IDF and exploited by Hamas and Arab neighbours for decades , dominates world attention . It leapfrogs Ukraine and the millions who carry on dying and ignored in the Democratic Republic of Congo , Sudan and Yemen .
