<p> Dozens of people filled the Ha'penny Bridge in Dublin this afternoon in as vigil was held for Palestine . <p> The famous bridge was thronged with people , many of whom were carrying Palestinian flags , while others carried banners in support of the region . The conflict has claimed the lives of more than 21,600 Palestinians , the majority of whom are women and children . <p> Cllr John Burtchaell was among the protesters in the capital this afternoon . The Fingal councillor said this was the latest Dublin demonstration against what he described as " genocide " carried out by Israel . <p> He said : " This is an unconscionable slaughter of innocent civilians by an apartheid state .
