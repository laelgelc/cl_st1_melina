<h> Ishaan Tharoor : Israel is struggling to destroy Hamas , but it is most certainly destroying Gaza <p> Palestinian children carry pots as they queue to receive food cooked by a charity kitchen in Rafah in the southern Gaza Strip . Photo : Reuters/Saleh Salem <p> Washington Post <p> Today at 03:30 <p> Amid mounting pressure on Israel from Western allies to rein back its campaign against Hamas , Israeli president Isaac Herzog held the line . " We intend to take over the entire Gaza Strip and change the course of history , " he said during an interview on Tuesday facilitated by the Atlantic Council , a Washington-based think tank . <p> Herzog said that the current conflict is a clash of " a set of civilisational values " and cast the Palestinian militant group , which carried out the brutal October 7 attack on southern Israel , as a " force of evil " .
