<h> Palestine solidarity protest to take place in Limerick this weekend <p> Ireland Palestine Solidarity Campaign at a recent demonstration in Dublin . <p> A LARGE crowd gathered in Limerick City last weekend for a protest in solidarity with Palestine , as the temporary ceasefire ended and bombing in Gaza resumed . <p> This week , the local group behind the protest are urging retailers to follow the example of sportswear brand O'Neills and " give Puma the boot " due to its financial links with Israel . <p> The appeal was launched by the Limerick branch of the Ireland-Palestine Solidarity Campaign ( IPSC ) who , as part of the campaign , are urging all local clothing stores to " remove PUMA products " as O'Neills have done . <p> A connected protest is being organised again this Saturday , gathering at 1pm on Bedford Row . <p> Limerick IPSC say this protest will be the first step in a boycott campaign . <h> Press Council <p> This publication supports the work of the Press Council of Ireland and Office of the Press Ombudsman , and our staff operate within the Code of Practice of the Press Council @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ or contact the Council , at www.presscouncil.ie , Lo-call 1800 208 080 or email : info@presscouncil.ie . <h> FREE MEDIA IRELAND <p> The Limerick Post is a member of Free Media Ireland , a network of free newspaper publishers committed to supporting local journalism and delivering engaging content while providing highly effective print advertising with unparalleled circulations .
