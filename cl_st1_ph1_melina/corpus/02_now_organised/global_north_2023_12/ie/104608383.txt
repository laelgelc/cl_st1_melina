<p> Iran has consistently denied any ambition to develop a nuclear weapons capability , insisting that its activities are entirely peaceful . <p> Iran appeared to have slowed its enrichment as a gesture while informal talks for a restored nuclear agreement resumed with the United States . <p> But animosity between the two countries has intensified in recent months , with each accusing the other of exacerbating the war between Israel and Hamas . <p> Iran suspended its compliance with limits on its nuclear activities set by a 2015 nuclear deal with major powers a year after then US President Donald Trump unilaterally pulled out of the agreement in 2018 and reimposed sweeping sanctions . <p> It has since built up its stocks of enriched uranium to 22 times the level permitted under the deal , according to a confidential IAEA report seen by AFP last month . <p> Eslami criticised what he called a " media frenzy " around the latest IAEA report , claiming it " sought to distract public attention " from the war in Gaza .
