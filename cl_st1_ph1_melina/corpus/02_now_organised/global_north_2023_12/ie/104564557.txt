<h> Colin Murphy : It 's not all bad news . Here are some reasons to be positive about the world this Christmas <p> Let 's remember that the world is getting richer and less unequal . Fewer babies are dying and people are living longer . Picture posed <p> Colin Murphy <p> Yesterday at 03:30 <p> A bleak midwinter ends an often bleak year . The horrors of Gaza . Refugee accommodation aflame . The hottest year on record . Grinding attrition in Ukraine . A new cold war with China . Forgotten wars in Yemen and Sudan . <p> But pause for a moment . The world is in a state of chassis -- but when has it ever been other ? It often seems like the years since 2016 have been a rollercoaster of geopolitical disasters , starting with Brexit . But only eight years earlier , the world lurched into a great recession . Seven years before that , 9/11 sparked wars in Afghanistan and Iraq . The 1990s witnessed the Balkan War , the Rwandan genocide and a host of other regional disasters .
