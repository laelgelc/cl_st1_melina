<h> Thank you for subscribing ! <p> Dublin City Council have voted tonight to fly the Palestinian flag over City Hall in solidarity to the people of Palestine . <p> The flag will be hoisted for a period of seven days from Tuesday , December 5 . The motion to fly the Palestinian flag was first brought forward in Dublin City Council on November 6 , however the vote failed to pass . Tonight , the Council agreed to rescind the previous decision . <p> A spokesman for Dublin City Council confirmed : " ... In light of the unique and unprecedented circumstances that prevail in Gaza , the City Council agrees to the deviation from the ' Protocol for the Flying of Flags from Civic Buildings ' and further agrees to fly the Palestinian flag above City Hall for a period of seven days from Tuesday , December 5 to demonstrate Dublin City Council 's ongoing solidarity with the plight of the people of Gaza .
