<p> The Ireland-Palestine Solidarity Campaign has announced a candlelight vigil at the GPO . <p> The purpose of this solemn gathering is to mourn the tragic loss of nearly 20,000 lives in what is described as Apartheid Israel 's ongoing genocidal war against the Palestinian people in Gaza . <p> The candlelight vigil signifies a quiet yet powerful act of remembrance , emphasizing the human cost of the conflict . The event 's slogan is " Mourn the dead . Fight for the Living " encapsulates its dual purpose -- to honour those who have perished and to galvanize collective action in support of the living . <p> This gathering at the GPO provides a platform for solidarity , shedding light on the persistent challenges faced by the Palestinian people .
