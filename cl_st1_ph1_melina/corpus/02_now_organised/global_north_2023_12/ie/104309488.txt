<p> A kind-hearted Dublin woman has launched a fundraiser to help the people of Gaza . <p> Karen Howley , from Raheny , is selling handmade earrings to raise vital money for those affected by the Israeli invasion of Palestine . Karen , 39 , the idea for the fundraiser came to her last Christmas . <p> Karen told Dublin Live : " Last year I made earrings for little Christmas presents for people just as little add-ons , and they really liked them . So my sister , who has a little cafe in Co Sligo , asked me if I could make some for the shop . " <p> Karen , who is a certified workplace and career coach , said she had no intention of making money out of the earrings and came up with the idea of setting up a fundraiser .
