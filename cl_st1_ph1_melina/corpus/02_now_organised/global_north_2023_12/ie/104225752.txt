<h> City Council passes motion endorsing call for ceasefire in Gaza <p> Galway City Council has passed a motion proposed by the Social Democrats which endorses a United Nations resolution which calls for an immediate humanitarian ceasefire in Gaza . <p> The motion also expresses support for the Boycott , Divestment and Sanctions ( BDS ) movement , as well as condemning the attacks by militant Palestinian groups on October 7 . <p> Social Democrat councillor Alan Curran proposed the motion due to the devastating aerial bombardment of the Gaza Strip , home to over 2 million Palestinians . <p> The IDF has killed more than 16,000 people in Gaza in just two months , with airstrikes wiping out entire families , neighbourhoods , refugee camps and schools . <p> Cllr Curran says there needs to be a cessation in military action today to stop further bloodshed . <p> " Galway City Council must make sure we are standing on the right side of history . Everyone with a voice for social justice and a platform of influence must use it to call for an end to this onslaught , " he said .
