<p> Abuja ( Sundiata Post ) -- Israeli warplanes have flattened an Iranian consulate in Damascus , Syria , killing six people including a senior commander of the Iranian Revolutionary Guards Corps , IRGC , Reuters reports . <p> Journalists reports that Israel has repeatedly hit Iranian targets during the six-month war in Gaza . <p> A Lebanese security source told Reuters that one of the casualties was Mohammad Reza Zahedi , a senior commander in the IRGC . <p> The Iranian state television acknowledged that several Iranian diplomats had been killed . <p> Iran 's Tasnim news agency said five people were killed in the Israeli strike , while Syria 's SANA state news agency reported an unspecified number of deaths and injuries . <p> Israel , however , declined to comment on the incident , following its usual practice . <p> An Israeli military spokesperson said : " We do not comment on reports in the foreign media . " <p> Israeli troops have ramped up airstrikes in Syria against Lebanon 's Iranian-backed Hezbollah militia and Iran 's Guards since the Iranian-backed Palestinian group , Hamas , launched an attack on Israel on October 7.
