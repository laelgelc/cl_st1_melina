<p> President of the Senate Godswill Akpabio is seeking Parliamentary Collaboration between the Nigerian Parliament and its Serbian counterpart . <p> Akpabio at a meeting with the President of the Serbia Parliament , Ms Ana Brnabic , in London , extensively discussed issues of mutual interest to both nations and how to improve parliamentary collaborations between Serbia and Nigeria . <p> The Meeting , which was at the request of Ms Brnabic , also discussed matters relating to the International Parliamentary Union(IPU) . <p> Senator Akpabio , was recently elected into the Executive Committee of the IPU , a role he has been playing successfully in the last few months , specifically his thought provoking appeal on the needless blood letting in Gaza , and the call for a ceasefire at the just concluded meeting of the body in Geneva , Switzerland .
