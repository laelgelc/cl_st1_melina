<h> Easter Message By Rev. Dr. Rufus Okikiola Ositelu <p> April 1 , 2024 <p> 37 3 minutes read <p> Beloved in Christ , Congratulations for the grace to celebrate another Easter in the land of the living . Glory be to God ( Hallelujah ) ! It is a time of gathering when people come together to enjoy quality time with those around them . Easter is also the celebration of God 's agape love for mankind . " For God so love the world that He gave His only begotten son , that whosoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life " . ( John 3:16 ; NKJV ) . In the same vein , Christ 's crucifixion and death on the cross confirm His love and sacrifice for believers . He paid the supreme price in order to reconcile us back to God after the fall of man in the Garden of Eden , thereby fulfilling God 's redemption plan for man . " Greater love has no one than this , than to lay down one 's life for His friends " ( John 13:4 ; NKJV ) . Our Saviour 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . We should praise God and live daily in the consciousness of the resurrection power . By it , He has nailed all our tribulations trials and challenges on the cross . He said , " it is finished " ( John 19:30 ; NKJV ) . His victory is our victory , we have been redeemed by His precious blood . He is RISEN ! ( Mathew 28:6 ) . Halleluyah ! I therefore , enjoin all believers to emulate Christ example of sacrifice and love in our relationships with one another . In spite of the challenging times Nigeria is going through , I urge you all to remain focused on our Lord Jesus Christ , the Author and Finisher of our faith.Permit me to use this occasion to call on government at all levels to give due attention to the following areas . <p> PEACE AND SECURITYThe constitutional duty of any Government is to protect lives and property of her citizens . At a time like this , Nigerian leaders should remember their oath of office and show more compassion and empathy to the populace and govern @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ development in an atmosphere devoid of peace . The need to beef up security in the land can not be over-emphasized . Innocent people are being killed daily . There is an urgent need to put an end to wanton destruction of lives and property , kidnapping and banditry in Africa and particularly in Nigeria . The recent killing of seventeen officers and soldiers in Delta State is tragic . The perpetrators should be brought to book . I will however like to commend President Bola Ahmed Tinubu GCFR for giving houses and scholarships to the deceased families as well as posthumous honours for the slain soldiers . All hands must be on deck to ensure Peace and Security . <p> ECONOMYThe rising cost of living is worrisome . The prices of goods and services are skyrocketing daily . There is an urgent need to bring down inflation . I call on Federal and State Governments to continually make policies and take critical steps to ease the burden of the masses.At this juncture , I will like to commend the Federal Government , Governors Senators and House of Representatives Members @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the economic situation of their people . I hereby urge other leaders to emulate this good gesture . We also thank God that the naira is begining to appreciate . <p> CORRUPTIONUnfortunately , corruption the hydra headed monster remains a big issue and continues to eat deep into the socio-economic and political fabric of the nation . I call on the Tinubu-led administration to combat it frontally and courageously so that Nigeria can move forward.I equally want to encourage Nigerians not to give up on God , but to keep hope alive . The future of Nigeria is bright and may Almighty God preserve us all till then and beyond . I enjoin my Prelate colleagues to continue to intercede earnestly for Nigeria , for peace , love , security and prosperity to reign in the land . There is equally the need to pray for world peace especially in war zones such as Gaza and Ukraine.Beloved in Christ , may you experience Joy , Love Peace and Prosperity this Easter 2024 in the Mighty Name of Jesus .
