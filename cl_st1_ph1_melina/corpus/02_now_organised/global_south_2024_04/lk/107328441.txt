<h> New legislation to be introduced to allow individuals to decide on their final rites ; President <p> 09 Apr 2024 - hitsCtrl.values.hits <p> President Ranil Wickremesinghe said new legislation will be introduced to allow individuals to decide on their final rites . He said a new committee would also be established to coordinate arrangements for the last rites of individuals in accordance with their religious customs or final wishes , including those within the Muslim community . He also expressed his commitment to enacting relevant legislation in this regard . These remarks were made during his attendance at the Ifthar ceremony held over the weekend at the Jumma Mosque in Katukele , Kandy .
