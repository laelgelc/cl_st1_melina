Olaf Scholz on Tuesday . <p> Xi made the remarks during a meeting in Beijing with Scholz , who visited Chongqing and Shanghai on the first two days of his three-day trip to China . <p> Xi has called on both China and Germany to be vigilant against rising protectionism , and view the issue of production capacity objectively and dialectically . <p> Noting the industrial and supply chains of China and Germany are deeply intertwined and the markets of the two countries are highly interdependent , Xi said mutually beneficial cooperation between China and Germany is not a " risk , " but a guarantee for the stability of bilateral relations and an opportunity for the future . <p> Xi stressed that China and Germany have enormous potential for win-win cooperation in traditional fields such
