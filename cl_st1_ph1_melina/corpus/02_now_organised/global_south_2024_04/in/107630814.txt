<h> Democracy in danger ? <p> The discourse surrounding democratic backsliding in India has reached a fever pitch due to the general elections . On one front , the Congress party daily asserts that elections are fixed . On the other front , the media , especially Western media , is painting a doomsday scenario for the world 's most populous democracy . The claims range from the usual ( EVMs are hacked ) to the outlandish ( 2024 general elections are the least free and fair in the nation 's history ) . Some have resorted to fear-mongering , suggesting that Narendra Modi would abolish elections in his third term . No democracy is without its flaws , and there are aspects of Indian democracy that warrant examination . However , those who vehemently oppose Narendra Modi prefer sensationalism fuelled by disinformation and distortion .
