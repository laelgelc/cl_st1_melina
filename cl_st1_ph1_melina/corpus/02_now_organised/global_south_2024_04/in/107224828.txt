Limiting crude 's gains were US jobs data that beat expectations and strengthened the dollar . <p> " The market now knows that some kind of retaliation from Iran will likely come - but it does n't know when and where and what - and that creates a great discomfort and nervousness , " said Bjarne Schieldrop , chief commodities analyst at SEB AB . <p> Global benchmark Brent and US benchmark West Texas Intermediate were both up less than 1% .
