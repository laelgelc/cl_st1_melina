Their offensive has nothing to show , except for the destruction of Gaza , around 33,000 civilian casualties , the displacement of over 1.7 million , a likely escalation of the war , increasing global isolation and a growing divide within Israel itself in the manner in which the war has been conducted and the direction in which it is headed . Israeli forces have rampaged the way from Gaza city in the north , then towards Khan Younis in the South , and now are poised for operations in the southernmost tip of Gaza -- Rafah , where they claim three Hamas battalions and the remnants of the Hamas leadership have been hemmed in .
