<h> Mukesh Aghi , President &amp; CEO , US-India Strategic Partnership Forum ( USISPF ) , tells ABP Live that the next Indian government will have to undertake ' aggressive reforms ' . <p> Mukesh Aghi , president &amp; CEO of the US-India Strategic Partnership Forum ( USISPF ) , has said that the Indian government that comes to power after the upcoming elections has to undertake " aggressive reforms " , especially in the taxation system , saying that it has become a cause of concern for American businesses with large-scale operations in the country . <p> On his recent visit to India , Aghi said India needs to assess as to why global businesses and companies are giving more preference to Thailand and Vietnam for setting up manufacturing units . <p> He also said that , once the new governments come to power both in India as well as the US -- the US presidential election is scheduled for this year too -- the two countries can start fresh negotiations for a free trade agreement ( FTA ) to facilitate more investments .
