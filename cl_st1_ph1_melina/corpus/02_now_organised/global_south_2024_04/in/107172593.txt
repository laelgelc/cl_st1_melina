<h> Jaishankar : India will definitely get permanent membership of UNSC <p> Apr 03 , 2024 , 12:25PM ISTSource : TOI.in <p> Union external affairs minister S Jaishankar explained the strategy that how India will get permanent membership of the United Nations . Jaishankar said that India needs to put pressure and effort to get the status of permanent member . He said , " Now I get a feeling that the world is looking for a change ... And that India should get a permanent seat at the UNSC ... That feeling I am seeing is growing every year . There are ongoing negotiations where every country is putting forward their view on this . There 's a Mexico model , a Lichtenstein model ... India , Japan , Germany and Brazil have put a united view . This will take forward our case . But we need to keep the pressure on ... The world is increasingly feeling that the UN has become weak . There was a deadlock in the UN on the Ukraine war . Even in the Gaza war there was a lack of consensus .
