<h> Fashion label Countrymade is inspired by familial stories of loss and longing <p> Brown , grey , black and white made up most colours in Countrymade by Sushant Abrol 's latest collection at the Lakme X FDCI fashion week . While the colours are the common template for menswear , Countrymade stands out for its sleek tailoring and textures . <p> Abrol 's collection was military-inspired . It centred around his brother , who passed away in 2019 . Abrol 's brother , Squadron Leader Samir Abrol died during a training sortie in a fighter jet crash three years ago . Sushant says his brother had encouraged him to start his own label . " He was three years older than me , and a class topper . So , there was pressure on me to perform well too . Everything of his used to be passed on to me , from old school uniforms to books , " he remembers fondly . <p> Countrymade 's latest collection is called ' The Road Back ' . It features combat helmets and sleek attache bags , which symbolise jeri-cans that carry fuel . " The collection is an ode @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is finding his way home . Home may or may not be how he left it , which is why the use of splattering of grey on the pieces , " Abrol , 34 , says . <p> The leather accessories or add-ons , he says , " has patina work done in grey , which symbolises cement and iron and steel " . The helmets have been wrapped in leather , that 's been worn out . The purpose of this is to portray the complex emotions that come with the journey . <p> " Metaphorically , the collection is about a boy taking the journey home -- something my grandfather , who was from West Punjab went through after the Partition . He wanted to revisit his familial home , which is now in Pakistan . But due to circumstances , he has n't been able to . " War is a recurring motif for Countrymade .
