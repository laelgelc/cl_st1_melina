<p> The video captures the harrowing discovery of two mass graves by the Hamas-run Health Ministry in northern Gaza . The first grave was unearthed at Gaza 's largest Al-Shifa Hospital , while another was found in Beit Lahia . Shockingly , 20 decomposed bodies were recovered from the grave in Beit Lahia , with nine more found at Al-Shifa Hospital . The bodies in Beit Lahia are believed to be from the same family . Some of the deceased individuals at Al-Shifa appeared to be patients , still in hospital gear , including one with a leg cast indicating recent injuries . Doctors at Al-Shifa reported witnessing executions outside the hospital 's main gate , prompting a thorough search for the mass grave . The scene paints a grim picture of the toll of conflict and violence in the region .
