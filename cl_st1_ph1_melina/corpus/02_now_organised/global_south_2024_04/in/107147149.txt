Abe urged us to reflect on the " confluence of the two oceans " -- the Indian and the Pacific . It ran into much bureaucratic and political resistance in Delhi . Sceptics in Delhi saw the Indo-Pacific as an " American plot " to " entrap " India into " containing " China . <p> It took over a decade after Abe 's call for India to formally embrace @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Narendra Modi at the annual Shangri La Dialogue in Singapore during the summer of 2018 . The Indo-Pacific is now well-established in the Indian discourse , and so is its institutional anchor , the Quad , which brings together Australia , India , Japan and the US . <h> From Eurasia to Europe <p> The idea of " Eurasia " -- the continental cousin of the maritime Indo-Pacific -- has not gained equal currency in the Indian strategic discourse but is now part of India 's new diplomatic vocabulary .
