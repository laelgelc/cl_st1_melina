<p> A 4.8 magnitude earthquake struck the state of New Jersey in the United States on Friday , causing a brief interruption during a UN Security Council meeting in neighbouring New York City . <p> During the meeting , Janti Soeripto , President and Chief Executive Officer of the " Save the Children " organization , was providing a briefing on the situation in war-torn Gaza when the tremors of the earthquake were felt inside the United Nations building . In a video shared by the UN , as the ground shook , one member humorously remarked , " You 're making the ground shake ! " <p> After a momentary pause , Soeripto resumed her briefing once the tremors had subsided . <p> The earthquake , which originated in New Jersey , rattled New York City with a magnitude of 4.8 . <p> Despite the noticeable shaking , there were no immediate reports of injuries or damage caused by the seismic activity .
