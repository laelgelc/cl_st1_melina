But Netanyahu remains stubbornly in post , refusing to budge even though the majority of Israelis want him gone . <p> Last Sunday marked 6 months since the horrific and brutal attack on innocent Israeli civilians when an estimated 3000 Hamas terrorists broke through the " impenetrable " fence surrounding Gaza , where some 2.3 million Palestinians are squeezed into an area of 365 square kilometres . The atrocities committed by the terrorists sickened the world as more than 1,200 Israeli 's were brutally murdered and some 250 were abducted as hostages .
