Meanwhile , only one brigade , Nahal , is presently in the Gaza Strip . <p> ANI Tel Aviv April 7 , 2024 7:57 pm <p> PHOTO : ANI <p> The Israel Defence Forces have withdrawn all ground troops from the southern Gaza Strip after a long four-month fight in the Khan Younis area , reported The Times of Israel . <p> The IDF withdrew their troops overnight on Saturday .
