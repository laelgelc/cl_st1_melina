<p> In response to escalating pressure from the United States , Israel withdrew its troops from southern Gaza , leaving only the Nahal unit to secure the Netzarim corridor . This corridor , dividing Gaza into north and south , enables the IDF to exert control over the region while facilitating aid access and preventing Gazans from returning north . Despite global calls for a ceasefire , Israel remains resolute , insisting that the IDF 's departure is not due to political pressure . Meanwhile , ongoing clashes resulted in the deaths of four IDF soldiers in southern Gaza , adding to the toll of 260 Israeli troops since the conflict began six months ago . Additionally , recent operations saw Hamas militants attacking Israeli troops from hidden tunnels in Khan Younis , prompting IDF responses aimed at neutralizing Hamas attack tunnels . The conflict has exacted a heavy toll on both sides , with over 33,000 Palestinian lives lost according to Gaza 's Health Ministry . Amid mounting international pressure , Israel 's leadership under Prime Minister Netanyahu stresses efforts to minimize civilian casualties while navigating complex diplomatic and military challenges in the region .
