<h> Opinion For Global Security , Avoid ' Double Standards ' <p> The wars in Gaza and Ukraine have renewed old debates about Western hypocrisy . Many have argued that the West 's support for Ukraine , often couched in terms of respect for international law , has been undermined by the same countries ' lacklustre support for Gaza . This has given rise to a larger foreign policy debate about the alleged double standards of the West . The claim is that Western countries are concerned with violations of international law only when it serves their own interests . <p> Norway has been clear that a real commitment to international law demands condemning not only Russia 's war of aggression against Ukraine but also calling out Israeli violations of international law in Gaza . <h> Upholding International Law <p> Avoiding double standards in our foreign policy has been a longstanding Norwegian priority . Successive Norwegian governments have , as a matter of principle , come to the defence of international law , regardless of who violates it . Whether it is the ongoing war in Gaza , conflicts on the African continent , Israel 's illegal occupation of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the Chagos Archipelago or Russia 's illegal war of aggression against Ukraine , Norway has been principled and clear . We have not shirked calling out any of these violations for what they are , regardless of who committed them . <p> Is this the right approach ? Some have been skeptical . It has been argued that countries should be careful not to criticise allies and partners when they violate international law . The argument is that the world is fraught with peril , and all states , perhaps especially smaller states such as Norway , should be careful not to alienate their allies and partners , even when they act inconsistently with international law . <p> This is , however , a mistaken argument . Real security depends ultimately on a peaceful international community equipped to resolve global challenges . That , in turn , requires that we work to ensure that international law is respected . Unless all countries are committed to international law , the system will collapse .
