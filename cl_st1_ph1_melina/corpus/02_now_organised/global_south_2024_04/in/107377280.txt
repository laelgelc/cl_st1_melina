<p> Washington : Strong economic activity in the United States and emerging markets is projected to help drive global growth by about 3% this year , the International Monetary Fund 's chief said Thursday , below the annual historic average and a warning sign about potential lackluster performances through the 2020s . " Without a course correction , we are indeed heading for the Tepid Twenties ' -- a sluggish and disappointing decade , " said Kristalina Georgieva , the organization 's managing director , in announcing the economic projection and longer-term outlook . <p> She said global economic activity is weak by past measurements and debt is up , posing major challenges to public finances in many parts of the world . <p> " The scars of the pandemic are still with us . The global output loss since 2020 is around $3.3 trillion , with the costs disproportionately falling on the most vulnerable countries , " she said . <p> The anticipated growth rate of just more than 3% is slightly above last year 's projection .
