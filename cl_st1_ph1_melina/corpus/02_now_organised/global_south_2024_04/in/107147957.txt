<h> El-Sissi won 89.6 per cent of the vote in the December election , with turnout of 66.8 per cent more than 67 million registered voters <p> President Abdel-Fattah el-Sissi of Egypt was sworn in for a third six-year term on Tuesday after being re-elected in a December vote in which he faced no serious challengers . El-Sissi took the oath of office before parliament , which convened in the new administrative capital in the desert outside Cairo . <p> El-Sissi won 89.6 per cent of the vote in the December election , with turnout of 66.8 per cent more than 67 million registered voters . He ran against three virtually unknown opponents . The vote was overshadowed by the Israel-Hamas war in Gaza on Egypt 's eastern border , which has threatened to expand into wider regional turmoil . <p> El-Sissi was first elected as president in mid-2014 , then reelected in 2018 . A year later , constitutional amendments , passed in a general referendum , added two years to el-Sissi 's second term , and allowed him to run for a third , six-year term .
