<h> PAS condemns Israeli attack on Iranian consulate in Syria <p> KUALA LUMPUR : PAS strongly condemned the Israeli attack on the Iranian Consulate building in Damascus , Syria , which claimed seven lives , including two top military officers , on Monday . <p> PAS president , Tan Sri Abdul Hadi Awang , in a statement , said that two of the victims were top military officers , namely a senior commander in the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps ' ( IRGC ) elite Quds Force , Brigadier General Mohammad Reza Zahedi and his deputy , General Mohammad Hadi Hajriahimi . <p> " PAS conveys its deepest condolences to the Iranian government for this tragedy . May God bless those who died . <p> " It is only appropriate that the Muslim world condemns Israel , which is not only the mastermind behind this attack , but is also committing war crimes in Gaza , " he said . -- BERNAMA
