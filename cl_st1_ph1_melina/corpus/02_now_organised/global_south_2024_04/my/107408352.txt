<h> Over 25 Palestinians killed in Israeli attack on house in Gaza on 3rd day of Eid <p> GAZA : least 25 civilians were killed and others injured in an Israeli airstrike on a house in the Gaza Strip Friday . <p> The Palestinian News Agency ( WAFA ) reported that the occupation aircraft launched intense air raids targeting a house belonging to the Tabatibi family in the Sidra area , in the Daraj neighbourhood in central Gaza City . This resulted in the killing of 25 civilians and wounding dozens . <p> " The occupation warplanes also targeted the northern areas of the Nuseirat camp in the central Gaza Strip , while the occupation army blew up a number of buildings north of the camp , " it said . <p> Medical sources , a number of civilians were wounded in an Israeli airstrike targeting a school housing displaced people in Nuseirat camp . They were subsequently transferred to Al-Aqsa Martyrs Hospital . <p> Ambulance and civil Defense crews managed to recover the decomposed bodies of 13 people from the Al-Balad area and the Al-Amal neighborhood in Khan Yunis Governorate , south of the Gaza Strip . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Nuseirat camp , resulting in the killing of one civilian and causing the injury of dozens of others . <p> According to medical sources , the number of civilians killed in the Gaza Strip since October 7 has risen to 33,545 , most of whom were women and children . The number of injured has so far reached 76,094. -- BERNAMA-WAFA
