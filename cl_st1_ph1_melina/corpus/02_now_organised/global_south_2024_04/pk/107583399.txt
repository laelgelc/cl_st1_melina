<h> US vetoes Palestine 's bid for full UN membership <p> The US vetoed a UN Security Council draft resolution Thursday demanding Palestine 's full membership in the United Nations . <p> The 15-member Council gathered in New York to vote on a draft resolution authored by Algeria recommending the admission of the State of Palestine for UN membership . <p> The membership was blocked with a vote of 12 in favor and two abstentions , including the UK and Switzerland . <p> ? ? ? ? ? ? ? Before the voting , Algeria 's envoy to the UN Amar Bendjama said it is time for Palestine to take its rightful place among the community of nations , and seeking UN membership is a fundamental expression of Palestinian self-determination . <p> " Today , the call of history resounds once again . And it is my honor to put before the council the recommendation to admit the State of Palestine as a full member of the United Nations . <p> " It is a critical step towards rectifying a longstanding injustice , " Bendjama said , urging every member to support the resolution . <p> Palestine was accepted @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2012 , allowing its envoy to participate in debates and UN organizations but without a vote . <p> States are admitted to membership in the UN by a decision of the General Assembly upon the recommendation of the Security Council , according to the UN Charter . <p> A council resolution needs at least nine votes in favor and no vetoes by the permanent members -- US , Britain , France , Russia or China -- to pass . <p> Palestine 's application for full UN membership comes amid a deadly Israeli offensive on the Gaza Strip since an Oct. 7 cross-border attack by the Palestinian group Hamas , which has killed nearly 34,000 Palestinians .
