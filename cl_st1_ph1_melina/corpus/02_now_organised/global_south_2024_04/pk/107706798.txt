At just seven months pregnant , her mother , Sabreen al-Sakani , reached the emergency unit in critical condition after she was fatally wounded in the head and abdomen at the weekend . <p> An Israeli air strike hit her family 's house in the east of the southern Gaza city of Rafah , witnesses told AFP . " It 's a miracle that she was still alive , despite her difficulties in breathing , " Sahib al-Shams , a surgeon and director of the Kuwaiti hospital in Rafah , told AFP . While examining Sakani , the hospital 's medical team realised she was pregnant . <p> They decided to go ahead with a C-section immediately , despite a lack of anaesthetics .
