<h> Pakistan Army underlines need for de-escalating Middle East tensions immediately <p> RAWALPINDI , Pakistan : The Corps Commanders have expressed concern over the escalating tensions in the Middle East and noted that a wider regional conflict could ensue if both sides did not de-escalate immediately . <p> In the 264th Corps Commanders ' Conference ( CCC ) presided over by the Chief of Army Staff ( COAS ) General Syed Asim Munir at Pakistan Army 's General Headquarters ( GHQ ) in Rawalpindi on Tuesday , the forum expressed solidarity with the people of Palestine and condemned serious human rights violations , war crimes and genocide being perpetrated in Gaza . <p> The forum also expressed serious concern over the ongoing Indian aggression in Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir ( IIOJK ) . <p> It reiterated that Pakistan will continue to provide political , diplomatic and moral support to our Kashmiri brothers and sisters . <p> The forum paid rich tribute to the supreme
