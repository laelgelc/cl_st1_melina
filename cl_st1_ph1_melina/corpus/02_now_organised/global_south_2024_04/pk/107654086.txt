<h> Parisians protest against Islamophobia amid Gaza war tensions <p> A crowd of around 2,000 people protested in Paris against racism , Islamophobia and violence against children on Sunday after a court allowed their demonstration to go ahead . <p> Bans on protests have been more frequent in France in recent months amid tensions stirred by Israel 's war on Hamas in Gaza . In a country that is home to large Muslim and Jewish communities , authorities have banned many pro-Palestinian demonstrations and public gatherings , citing the risk of antisemitic hate crimes and violence . <p> On Sunday , the protesters marched peacefully from the multi-ethnic Barbes neighbourhood towards Place de la Republique .
