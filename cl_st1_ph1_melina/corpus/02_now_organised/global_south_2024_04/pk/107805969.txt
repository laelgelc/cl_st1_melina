<h> Hamas says studying new Israeli truce proposal <p> JERUSALEM - Hamas said it was studying on Saturday the latest Israeli counterproposal regarding a potential ceasefire in Gaza , a day after media reports said a delegation from mediator Egypt arrived in Israel in a bid to jump-start stalled negotiations . <p> The signs of fresh truce talks come as the United Nations warned that " famine thresholds in Gaza will be breached within the next six weeks " unless massive food assistance arrives . Aid groups say Gaza 's already catastrophic humanitarian conditions would be worsened by an invasion which Israel vows to carry out against Hamas battalions that remain in Rafah , southernmost Gaza . <p> Rafah , on the border with Egypt , is crowded with hundreds of thousands of Palestinians displaced by nearly seven months of war between Israel and the Islamist movement . The area is regularly bombed already . Hospital officials said strikes in Rafah and elsewhere killed more than a dozen people overnight Friday-Saturday . <p> Among the dead were an entire family , their relative Mohammed Yussef said .
