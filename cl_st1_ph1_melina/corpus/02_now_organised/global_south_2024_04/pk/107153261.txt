<h> Newborns in Gaza ' too small ' to survive neonatal period : WHO <p> Infant mortality is on the rise in the Gaza Strip , with newborns frequently being born underweight , according to reports from the World Health Organization , which references healthcare professionals working on-site . <p> " At various medical facilities , especially maternity hospitals , doctors are reporting a significant increase in infants born with low birth weight who do not survive the neonatal phase due to their small size , " stated WHO spokesperson Margaret Harris during a briefing in Geneva . <p> She said that at Kamal Adwan , the only paediatric hospital in northern Gaza , " at least 15 malnourished children are coming in per day , and the needs are just getting ever more severe " . <p> Due to the extensive destruction in the area following six months of Israel 's offensive against Hamas , the World Health Organization ( WHO ) is unable to gather accurate statistics on child mortality .
