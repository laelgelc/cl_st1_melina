" <p> " Each day , this war claims more civilian victims , " said Griffiths , who will leave his post at the end of June due to health reasons . <p> The destruction wrought across the Gaza Strip by Israel since Oct 7 makes for grim reading -- over 33,000 people killed and , according to the World Bank , over one million Palestinians are without homes , close to 90 per cent of health facilities have been damaged or wrecked and schools have been destroyed or turned into shelters for the newly homeless . <p> " Rarely has there been such global outrage at the toll of the conflict with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ much impunity " , Griffiths said .
