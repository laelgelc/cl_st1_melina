<h> US okays $17 billion in military aid for Israel <p> WASHINGTON - The US House of Representatives on Saturday passed a $95 billion legislative package with broad bipartisan support providing security assistance to Ukraine , Israel and Taiwan , over bitter objections from Republican hardliners . <p> This included $17 billion in defense aid to Israel , and some $9 billion providing humanitarian relief to people in Gaza as well as other war-torn regions ( the final decision on allocation was up to the White House , with analysts expecting roughly $2 billion would go to Gaza ) . The legislation now proceeds to the Democratic-majority Senate , which passed a similar measure more than two months ago .
