The highly complex mission was conducted in collaboration with the acting Hospital Director , the agency said . <p> Prior to the mission , it said , WHO 's efforts to reach the hospital to evacuate patients and staff and conduct an assessment , were denied , delayed or impeded six times between 25 March and 1 April . <p> Like the majority of the north , Al-Shifa Hospital " is now an empty shell after the latest siege " , said WHO , with no patients remaining at the facility .
