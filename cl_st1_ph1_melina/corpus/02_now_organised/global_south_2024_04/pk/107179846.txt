<p> WARSAW : A friend of the Polish aid worker who was killed in Gaza this week paid tribute on Wednesday to a man he described as a hero whose passion was helping other people . <p> Damian Sobol was among seven people working for the charity World Central Kitchen ( WCK ) who were killed in an Israeli airstrike late on Monday , an incident that has provoked international outrage and condemnation . <p> " For me he is a hero , " Krzysztof Butra told Reuters in an interview . <p> Butra and Sobol had been friends since childhood , growing up together in the southeastern Polish town of Przemysl where they lived close to each other and played football together in the same team . <p> " He always said if not him , then who ? Someone has to go there , someone has to help these people , " Butra said of his friend 's commitment to aid work . <p> Butra said Sobol 's passion for volunteering had begun with Russia 's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022 , when Przemysl was flooded with refugees escaping the conflict across the border . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Turkey following the earthquake in 2023 that devastated much of the south of the country . <p> Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk said on Wednesday that the deaths of the aid workers and statements by Israeli officials - which many Poles viewed as inflammatory - had caused " understandable anger " and strained relations with Israel . <p> The Israeli ambassador to Poland Yacov Livne said Israel had " repeatedly expressed our deep regret , sorrow and condolences over the tragic death of World Central Kitchen workers , including Damian Sobol .
