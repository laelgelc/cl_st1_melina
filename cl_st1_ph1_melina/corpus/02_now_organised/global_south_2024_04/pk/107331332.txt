<h> World food prices rise for first time in 7 months : UN agency <p> Global food prices in March recorded their first monthly increase in seven months , driven by higher costs of vegetable oils and dairy products , the Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) , a Rome-based UN agency , has said . <p> The prices , however , posted a year-on-year decline during the month , according to the UN agency . <p> The February reading was the lowest for the index since February 2021 and marked a seventh consecutive monthly decline . <p> International food prices have fallen sharply from a record peak in March 2022 at the start of Russia 's invasion of fellow crop exporter Ukraine .
