<p> KARACHI : Gold rates hit an all-time high of Rs245,100 per tola ( 24K ) on Saturday , breaking the previous record of Rs 242,700 on Sept 2 last year . <p> A member of the All Sindh Saraf and Jewellers Association ( ASSJA ) said the gold rate rose by Rs4,900 per tola on the back of a $44 per ounce jump in the international market to $2,350 -- yet another all-time high . <p> According to the association , domestic gold rates are adjusted by a premium of over $20 as against the prices in international market . <p> However , the rates issued by ASSJA are not followed by jewellers in Karachi . A trader said some gold dealers were offering a rate of Rs239,000-240,000 as buyers can not further sustain the frequent price shocks . <p> He said that on Friday , the market in Dubai was lower than in Pakistan by Rs5,500 per tola , but on Saturday the two rates were almost equal . <p> Habibur Rahman , the chairman of Pakistan Gem Jewellers , Traders and Exporters Association ( PGJTEA ) , said that " high gold prices , a low @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ driven away buyers , especially during the last 10 days of Ramazan " . <p> He revealed that many shopkeepers had opted to keep their businesses closed after iftar this year . On the other hand , gold merchants used to keep their shops open for advance bookings and spot buying of jewellery for the post-Eid wedding season . <p> According to Mr Habib , the Gaza crisis and uncertainty over interest rate cut in June had combined to take the yellow metal prices to an all-time high . <p> He said many countries , including China , were piling up gold reserves while investors in Pakistan were putting their money in the bullion market to ensure future returns . He said the market was rife with speculations that investors would further shift towards gold as a safety net .
