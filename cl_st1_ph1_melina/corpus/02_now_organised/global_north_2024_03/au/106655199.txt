<h> ABC 2024 : Battle of wills over ideologies <p> Dr Lee Duffield , a veteran of ABC journalism and former Journalism academic , has been appraising the organisation and its work . <p> He has described one old ideology fast disappearing , a conservative aversion to new ideas and any airing of conflict in society ; and two main ideologies currently in force : one a professional ideal saying the ABC must stick to instructions to be a broad church for ideas and one accentuating " diversity " or " inclusivity " . <p> THE ABC 'S HANDLING of the recent two big " royal " occasions shows how it can fumble if swayed by ideology . At the Queen 's death , with ample advance warning , it set up a big team in London , paying homage to a royalist state ideology scarcely in force here and so overdoing the service provided to middle Australian folks very moved by the event . <p> That same week , British journalists rushed off to the shock counter-offensive by the Ukrainian army , with no warning .
