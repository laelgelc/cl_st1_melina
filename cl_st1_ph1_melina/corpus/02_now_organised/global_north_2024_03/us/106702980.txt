<h> Biden is learning that being progressive is good policy -- and good politics <p> There are at least four Biden presidencies happening within one administration . And so far , at the start of his reelection campaign , the president is leaning into the two more progressive presidencies and playing down one of the more centrist ones . That 's definitely good policy and probably good electoral politics . <p> There is the Elizabeth Warren version of the presidency , referring to the Massachusetts senator known for liberal economic ideas . When Democrats controlled Congress , they joined with President Biden to push through trillions of dollars in new spending . Biden appointees at federal agencies have been aggressive in limiting mergers of big companies and supporting labor unions .
