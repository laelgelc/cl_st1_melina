<p> ZURICH ( Reuters ) - The number of antisemitic incidents in Switzerland has leapt since the Oct. 7 attacks by Hamas in Israel and the Israeli government 's subsequent response against the Islamist group in Gaza , according to a report published on Tuesday . <p> The study by the Swiss Federation of Jewish Communities ( SIG ) and the Foundation against Racism and Antisemitism ( GRA ) showed the number of " real world " incidents shot up to 155 in 2023 - most of these after Oct. 7 - from 57 the previous year . <p> The " unprecedented increase " in cases included ten physical assaults , against one in the previous year , the study said , along with verbal insults , graffiti and antisemitic placards . <p> " As a result of the dreadful terror attacks by Hamas on October 7 , 2023 , antisemitism has also manifest itself in Switzerland in a way that we could not imagine , " SIG president Ralph Lewin and his GRA counterpart Zsolt Balkanyi-Guery wrote in a foreword to the annual report . <p> One of the most notorious recent cases occurred on March 2 , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ life-threatening injuries by a Swiss teenager , an Islamic State supporter of Tunisian origin . <p> The incident , described by SIG as the most serious antisemitic hate crime in Switzerland in two decades , caused widespread shock and concern , and led to enhanced security for Jewish sites in Zurich , where the attack took place . <p> About 114 of the antisemitic incidents in Switzerland last year occurred after Oct. 7 , the report said , when fighters from Hamas , which administers Gaza , killed 1,200 people in Israel and abducted 253 , according to Israeli tallies . <p> Israel 's retaliatory military campaign on the densely populated enclave has since killed more than 31,000 Palestinians , according to Gaza authorities . <p> Antisemitic incidents also picked up online , with 975 registered cases in 2023 , up from 853 the previous year . Nearly half of the 2023 incidents were logged after Oct. 7 , the study found .
