<h> UN aid chief Griffiths to step down in June for health reasons <p> March 25 , 2024 at 11:35 AM <p> GENEVA ( Reuters ) - United Nations aid chief Martin Griffiths , who recently pushed for more humanitarian access to Gaza , plans to step down at the end of June for health reasons , a U.N . spokesperson said on Monday . <p> Griffiths is a British diplomat who has headed the Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs ( OCHA ) since 2021 . <p> Farhan Haq , deputy spokesperson for the U.N . Secretary-General Antonio Guterres , did not disclose the nature of Griffiths ' health concerns . <p> " He advocated tirelessly for life-saving aid to reach those most in need and for the resources needed to do so , " Haq said in a statement . <p> " A skilled diplomat and mediator , he has played a key role in leading the humanitarian response of United Nations and partners and negotiating solutions to some of the most intractable crises . " <p> In a post on X , Griffiths said he had informed Guterres of his intention to step down in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " To everyone at @UNOCHA , it 's been the privilege of my life . I am deeply in your debt , " he wrote . <p> " To all partners and supporters , thank you for championing the cause of people in crises . " <p> Griffiths had previously served as the U.N . Secretary-General 's Special Envoy for Yemen , and as an adviser to all three of the U.N . Secretary-General 's Special Envoys for Syria , among other roles .
