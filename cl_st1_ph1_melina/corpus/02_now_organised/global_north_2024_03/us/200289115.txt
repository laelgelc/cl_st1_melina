Critics have placed the blame on the academy 's supposedly rarefied taste , which , after The Dark Knight missed out on a Best Picture nomination in 2009 , led the Oscars to expand the Best Picture field to 10 nominations in a bid to make room for more populist choices . <p> That has n't entirely worked out as planned ; with a few exceptions , including 2022 's Avatar and Top Gun sequels , voters have used the extra slots mainly to recognize more small-budget and international fare .
