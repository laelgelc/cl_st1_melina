Democratic congressman Adam Schiff , the early front-runner in California 's top-two primary for Senate , had a large war chest of about $50 million . Schiff lent him a hand via the now-familiar tactic of saturation ads attacking Garvey as a staunch conservative and trusty ally of Donald Trump . <p> This love-bombing was intended to consolidate a previously scattered Republican vote behind the former Dodgers and Padres star , and it worked like a charm . When the votes rolled in , Schiff comfortably ran first and Garvey was easily outpacing Schiff 's fellow Democrat and congressional colleague Katie Porter for the second general-election spot .
