<p> The Kuwait-based Alshaya Group , a private family firm holding franchise rights for a variety of Western companies including The Cheesecake Factory , H&amp;M and Shake Shack , issued a statement acknowledging the firings at its Middle Eastern and North African locations . <p> " As a result of the continually challenging trading conditions over the last six months , we have taken the sad and very difficult decision to reduce the number of colleagues in our Starbucks MENA stores , " the statement read . <p> Coffee shops across the region have been targeted by activists during the ongoing Israel-Hamas war in the Gaza Strip . REUTERS <p> Alshaya later confirmed it was firing about 2,000 employees , as first reported by Reuters . Many of its employees in the Gulf Arab states are foreign workers hailing from Asian nations .
