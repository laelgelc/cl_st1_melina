<h> US imposes sanctions on two Israeli settler West Bank outposts <p> Simon Lewis <p> March 14 , 2024 at 8:39 AM <p> By Simon Lewis <p> ( Reuters ) -The Biden administration imposed sanctions on two Israeli outposts in the occupied West Bank on Thursday , the latest move against settlement activity it says is an obstacle to peace between Israelis and Palestinians . <p> The U.S. Treasury Department added two entities , Moshes Farm and Zvis Farm , as well as three Israeli nationals , to its list of sanctioned entities , according to the Office of Foreign Assets Control 's website . <p> Axios reported late on Wednesday , citing three U.S. officials , that the outposts would be targeted with sanctions because they were used as a base for attacks on Palestinians by settlers deemed to be extremist . <p> The administration in February imposed sanctions on four Israeli men it accused of being involved in settler violence in the West Bank , signalling growing U.S. displeasure with the policies of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu . <p> The administration said at the time that Israel 's expansion of settlements in the West Bank was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ U.S. policy on the issue that had been reversed by the previous administration of Donald Trump . <p> Since the 1967 Middle East war , Israel has occupied the West Bank of the Jordan River , which Palestinians want as the core of a future independent state . Israel has built Jewish settlements there that most countries deem illegal . <p> Israel disputes this and cites historical and biblical ties to the land . <p> Israel has stepped up raids in the West Bank since the war began in October . United Nations ' records show at least 358 people in the Palestinian territory have been killed since Oct. 7 , a quarter of them children . <p> The death toll in Gaza has already passed 31,000 since Oct. 7 , according to Palestinian authorities in Hamas-run Gaza . Israel went on the offensive after Hamas fighters stormed into Israel on that day , killing some 1,200 people , Israel says . <p> ( Reporting by Simon Lewis in Washington , Rishabh Jaiswal and Shubham Kalia in Bengaluru ; Editing by Michael Perry and Howard Goller )
