Median scores for government restrictions peak , social hostilities involving religion tick down in 2021 <h> Government restrictions on religion , by region <p> The global median score on the Government Restrictions Index ( GRI ) rose from 2.8 in 2020 to 3.0 in 2021 , the highest it 's been since Pew Research Center created the index in 2007 . Both Europe and the Americas saw increases in their regional GRI scores , though their median scores remained lower than in the Asia-Pacific and Middle East-North Africa regions . Index scores remained the same from 2020 to 2021 in the Asia-Pacific , Middle East-North Africa and sub-Saharan Africa regions . <p> In Europe , the median GRI score ticked up from 2.9 to 3.1 , while the score for the Americas rose from 2.0 to 2.1 .
