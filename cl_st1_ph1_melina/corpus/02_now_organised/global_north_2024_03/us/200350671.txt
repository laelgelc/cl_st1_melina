<h> Israeli military says it killed a ' significant ' Hezbollah commander in airstrike <p> March 31 , 2024 at 11:17 AM <p> JERUSALEM , BEIRUT ( Reuters ) - The Israeli military said it killed a Hezbollah commander in an airstrike on a vehicle in Lebanon on Sunday , identifying him as Ismail Al-Zin , a commander in the anti-tank missile unit of Hezbollah 's Radwan Forces . <p> " Al-Zin was a significant source of knowledge regarding anti-tank missiles and was responsible for dozens of anti-tank missile attacks against Israeli civilians , communities and security forces , " the Israeli military said . <p> A source close to Hezbollah said Al-Zin was not a senior figure but confirmed he was in its elite Radwan unit . <p> Israel has now killed around 25 members of the unit , including at least three commanders , such as Wissma Tawil , a senior Hezbollah officer who played a leading role in directing its operations in southern Lebanon . <p> Hezbollah began launching rockets from hilltops and villages in southern Lebanon at Israel on Oct. 8 in support of its Palestinian ally Hamas , which carried out a cross-border attack @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ land , air and sea offensive on the Gaza Strip . <p> Israel 's shelling of Lebanon has killed nearly 270 Hezbollah fighters , but has also killed around 50 civilians - including children , medics and journalists - and hit both UNIFIL and the Lebanese army . <p> The U.S. and other countries have sought to secure a diplomatic resolution to the exchanges of fire between Hezbollah and Israel . Hezbollah said it will not halt fire before a ceasefire is implemented in Gaza .
