<h> Ukrainian Pysanky fundraiser returns to First Presbyterian in Elkhart <p> Mar. 10 -- ELKHART -- For the third year , an artistic fundraiser event returned to First Presbyterian Church in Elkhart . <p> The church hosted a Ukrainian Egg Decorating , or " Pysanky , " event Sunday afternoon . Pastor Sally Wicks said that this year the event has a combined focus . <p> " It 's for Ukraine and for those suffering from the Gaza/Israel war , " she said , shortly before the event got underway . <p> Wicks said that they were maxed out , with 123 people having signed up for the event and another 25 on a waiting list . <p> The event , previously hosted in the downstairs Contemporary Christian area , was moved to the church sanctuary due to renovations . <p> Cindy Vanderwerk , Elkhart , took part in the event for the first time . <p> " I think they 're beautiful and I want to see how others can do it , " she said about the Pysanky eggs . <p> Wicks said that Pysanky , or " Pysanka " in singular form , is an old @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was brought to Ukraine , eventually moving to Russia , Bulgaria and other Slavic nations . At the event , eggs were set up at tables , with about four dozen in reserve , and each participant used three components to decorate their egg -- a candle , a " kistka " and a small portion of bees wax . After the first portion , decorators would bring their eggs to the dyeing station , at the back of the sanctuary , and video monitors were set up around the sanctuary to help with instruction . <p> " My mother was an artist and she had a friend who was Ukrainian , " Wicks said during the introduction , reflecting on fond childhood memories of learning the art form while growing up in the Detroit area , eventually teaching it herself when she became a pastor .
