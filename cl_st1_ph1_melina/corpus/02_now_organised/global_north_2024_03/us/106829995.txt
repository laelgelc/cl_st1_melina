<h> Valencia 's Fallas Festival : Welcoming Spring with Fire <p> For hundreds of years , residents of Valencia , Spain , have celebrated the arrival of spring and paid tribute to San Jose , the patron saint of the carpenters ' guild , by building and then ceremonially burning huge monuments made of wood , cardboard , and paper . The monuments , or fallas , consist of ninots , or figures , many of which are caricatures that portray current events and celebrities . The two-week-long festival features parades , fireworks , and fiestas , and ends with the burning of hundreds of fallas , signifying cleansing and renewal . <p> A view of the burning Falla del Ayuntamiento , a 20-meter-long monument built of wood and cardboard , during the Fallas festival , in Valencia , Spain , on March 19 , 2024 .
