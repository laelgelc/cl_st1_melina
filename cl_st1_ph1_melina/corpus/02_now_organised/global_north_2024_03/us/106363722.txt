" <p> These are all worthy aims , but a moment of this gravity calls for something grander . Mr. Biden needs to rethink an exhausted format , step up to the bully pulpit and make a case less for himself than for his worldview . Mr. Biden 's is 38 percent . <p> It 's valid to castigate Republicans for killing the sensible bipartisan border deal , something Mr. Biden did during his visit to a Texas border town on Thursday -- but insufficient .
