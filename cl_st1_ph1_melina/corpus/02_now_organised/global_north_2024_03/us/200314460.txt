<p> NEW YORK -- Effy 's Cafe , an Israeli restaurant on the Upper West Side , was filled with longtime customers , politicians and community leaders in a show of solidarity Monday after it was targeted by vandals . <p> According to police , vandals spray painted two messages in green on the sidewalk outside the luncheonette on West 96th Street near Columbus Avenue on Saturday . <p> They said " Free Gaza " and " Form line here to support genocide . " <p> The restaurant has been a staple in the neighborhood for more than a decade . There was n't an empty table , and some customers held the Israeli flag in support . <p> " Devastated for the restaurant , devastated for our community , for our people , " said Brianna Zackai . <p> Manager Ben Zara was shocked when she got to work Sunday morning and also saw the front of the restaurant covered with red paint . <p> " I am scared . It was my first time seeing a vandalism attack like that . It has never happened to me , " said Zara.
