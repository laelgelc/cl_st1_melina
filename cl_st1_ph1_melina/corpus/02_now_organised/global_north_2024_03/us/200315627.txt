<h> During the Israel-Hamas war , Jews will soon celebrate Purim -- one of their most joyous holidays <p> DAVID CRARY <p> March 20 , 2024 at 12:16 AM <p> Purim is widely depicted as the most thoroughly joyful of Jewish holidays -- highlighted by celebrations that include costumes , skits , noisemakers and varying degrees of rowdiness . <p> It celebrates the biblical story of how a plot to exterminate Jews in Persia was thwarted , and thus is embraced as an affirmation of Jewish survival throughout history . For many Jews , it will have extra significance this year during a war in Gaza triggered by the Oct. 7 attacks on Israel in which Hamas killed 1,200 people and took about 250 others hostage . <p> WHEN IS PURIM ? <p> Purim is celebrated on the 14th day of the Hebrew month of Adar . This year , that means Purim begins on Saturday night and continues through Sunday .
