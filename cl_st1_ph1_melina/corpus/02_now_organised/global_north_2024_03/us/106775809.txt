What if abstraction is not the grand form it 's cracked up to be , but something we carry with us , half-illegible , inefficient and shabby ? <p> Perhaps legibility and sense are overrated in this mess we find ourselves in . Can we expose , or reverse , our rapid unspooling by the rearrangement of our uncomfortable parts ?
