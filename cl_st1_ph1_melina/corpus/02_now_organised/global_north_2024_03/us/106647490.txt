<h> Gaza on the Brink of Famine <p> The United Nations is warning that famine in Gaza is " almost inevitable . " Palestinians living in Gaza are struggling with extreme shortages of food , clean water , and medicine . Several countries , including Jordan , France , Egypt , the U.S. , the United Arab Emirates , and now Germany , are coordinating airdrops of humanitarian aid to help alleviate the crisis , and the U.S. military is working to a build a temporary port on Gaza 's coastline to bring in additional aid . Critics have pointed out that airdrops and a temporary pier are insufficient , dangerous , and haphazard operations compared with ensuring a steady and reliable supply of aid delivered by trucks , which might be achieved by a cease-fire agreement .
