Also the Alba MPs were thrown out by the Speaker when they protested about the neglect of Scottish issues . <p> So what should Scots do about the coming General Election ? Well , they can vote for Labour , who clearly under Starmer have no interest in Scotland . They can vote for the SNP , who are clearly desperate to hold on to their ? 80,000-a-year seats ( rumour has it that some of them would like to be Speaker ! ) .
