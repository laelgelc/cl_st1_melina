<p> Egypt 's non-oil private sector contracted at the sharpest rate in just over a year in February , with the ongoing Suez Canal disruptions , the foreign exchange crisis and a steep drop in customer sales resulting in the country 's S&amp;P Global Egypt Purchasing Managers ' Index ( PMI ) to deteriorate further in 2024 . <p> Operating conditions in the sector dropped to 47.1 in February , down from 48.1 in the previous month , with anything below 50.0 indicating a decline in business . <p> According to PMI data , this is the lowest reading for Egypt in 11 months , pointing towards a worsening of order book volumes in February and further indicating a weakened economy . <p> New orders fell at the fastest rate since March 2023 , with domestic sales faring poorly amid rising price pressures and supply-side challenges .
