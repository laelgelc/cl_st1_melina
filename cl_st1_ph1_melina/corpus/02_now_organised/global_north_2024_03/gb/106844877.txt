' s senior humanitarian and reconstruction coordinator for Gaza , as well as Curtis Ried , chief of staff of the U.S. National Security Council . <p> As famine looms over Gaza , agencies are increasingly looking for alternative routes to get aid into the enclave other than land crossings .
