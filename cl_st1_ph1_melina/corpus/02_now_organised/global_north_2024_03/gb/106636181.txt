Dr Syafiq Mughni , a top official from Muhammadiyah , told Khaleej Times how the organisation has been promoting religious harmony in Indonesia while undertaking peacekeeping efforts to resolve conflicts in Central Africa , as well as providing humanitarian aid to vulnerable populations in the Middle East region and Asia , including the Rohingya refugees . <p> " Our organisation was established in 1912 . We have been working to establish a culture of peace through education and preaching .
