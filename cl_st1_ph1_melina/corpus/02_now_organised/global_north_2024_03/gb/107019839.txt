<p> A Qatar Armed Forces aircraft arrived in the city of El Arish in the sisterly Arab Republic of Egypt , carrying 57 tons of aid , including food supplies provided by the Qatar Charity , in preparation to be delivered to Gaza , bringing the total number of aid planes to 88 . HE Minister of State for International Cooperation at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Lolwah bint Rashid Al Khater received the aircraft at El Arish Airport .
