<p> A group of women from Birmingham will deliver a truckload of hygiene and sanitary products to women in Gaza , who are reportedly using scraps of tent fabric to manage their periods . The emergency convoy mission is being led by local charity ISRA UK with the women travelling to the war-torn region during the holy month of Ramadan . <p> Approximately 1.4 million Palestinians have now been pushed into Rafah by Israeli bombing that has killed nearly 30,000 Palestinians . Displaced women living in the besieged region are also going weeks without showering amid dire humanitarian conditions . <p> On International Women 's Day today , Friday 8 March , ISRA-UK is preparing to send an all-female deployment to Egypt to prepare and pack a 40ft container with emergency hygiene packs for women and children . Travelling during Ramadan , the women 's group will be packing boxes of period pads , panty liners , wipes and mother and baby hygiene items and other toiletries which will be transported directly to Gaza through the Rafah border . <p> " We are on a mission to get hygiene products to the women and girls of Gaza , as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " said Saraya Hussain , who will be leading the deployment team . " The situation there is dire and desperate and the women need us . <p> " They 've requested our help and we need people to donate , help us fundraise for sanitary items and join our deployment group if they can commit to this endeavour . Palestinian women and girls in Gaza are said to be resorting to unsafe ways of managing their periods . <p> Relief charity ActionAid has heard that some displaced women living in Rafah are so desperate they are cutting out small pieces of the tents they rely on for shelter from the cold and rain to use as a substitute for period products , risking infection . <p> " Lacking clean water , women and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stay clean , " added Saraya .
