Two years after the start of Russia 's all-out war against Ukraine , it has become increasingly clear that both the EU and the United States are partners whose pro-Ukrainian consensus seems to reach limits when discussions touch on national interests in the electoral and/or financial sphere . <p> This dissonance at the Euro-Atlantic decision-making level favours Russia , which is preparing for new offensives in Ukraine in May . The prospect of a US presidency under Trump that favours transactional relations within Nato is a worrying sign for EU states , which need renewed and strengthened security guarantees that currently do not exist under the EU umbrella . <p> Meanwhile , the EU 's southern neighbours have not calmed down .
