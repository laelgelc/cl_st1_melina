<p> The European Commission , the Republic of Cyprus , the United Arab Emirates , and the United States have issued a joint statement endorsing the activation of a maritime corridor to deliver humanitarian assistance to Gaza . <p> Following is the full text of the statement : <p> " The humanitarian situation in Gaza is dire , with innocent Palestinian families and children desperate for basic necessities .
