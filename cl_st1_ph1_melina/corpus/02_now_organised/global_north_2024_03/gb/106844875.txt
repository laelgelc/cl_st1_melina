<h> In Egypt , Blinken and Sisi discuss Gaza ceasefire talks <p> U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken on Thursday discussed with Egyptian President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi the negotiations to secure an immediate ceasefire for at least six weeks in the war between Israel and Hamas and the release of all hostages kidnapped by the Palestinian militant group , State Department spokesperson Matthew Miller said . <p> The top U.S. diplomat is in Egypt after visiting Saudi Arabia a day earlier , as part of his latest Middle East tour .
