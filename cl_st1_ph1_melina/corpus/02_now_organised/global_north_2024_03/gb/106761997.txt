<p> The Israel-Palestinian conflict is seen pushing the rate of Palestinian unemployment in the occupied West Bank and Gaza to above 50% , the International Labour Organization said on Monday . <p> Already more than half a million jobs have been lost since Oct. 7 2023 , when Israel began a retaliatory military campaign in Gaza after Hamas militants launched deadly cross-border attacks , the new report showed . If the conflict continues until end-March then the unemployment rate will soar to 57% , it said . <p> ILO Regional Director for Arab States Ruba Jaradat said that the destruction of infrastructure and schools , hospitals and business in Gaza had " decimated entire economic sectors and paralyzed labour market activity , with untold repercussions on the lives and livelihoods of Palestinians for generations to come . " <p> In Gaza , some 200,000 jobs have been lost , accounting for about two-thirds of total employment in the enclave . <p> In the West Bank , the report described " near lockdown " conditions with more than 650 permanent and temporary checkpoints across the territory having significant negative effects on the economy .
