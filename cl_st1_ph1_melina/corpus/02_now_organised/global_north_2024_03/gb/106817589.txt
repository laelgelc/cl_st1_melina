<p> More than 31,923 Palestinians have been killed and 74,096 have been injured in Israel 's military offensive on Gaza since Oct. 7 , the Gaza health ministry said in a statement on Wednesday . <p> Some 104 Palestinians were killed and 162 injured in the previous 24 hours , the ministry added .
