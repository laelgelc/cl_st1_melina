<p> Israeli troops have killed the commander of Hamas 's rocket squads in central Gaza in a devastating airstrike . <p> They pinpointed Amar Atiya Darwish Aladini 's position after he was spotted from the air using surveillance drones . Aladini was behind Hamas rocket fire for years and played a key role in the 2008 war between Israel and Hazan militants . <p> But it is suspected he was a key commander leading Hamas rocket teams for " decades " according to sources . The Israeli Defence Force killed him in a joint operation with the domestic intelligence agency Shin Bet . It is also believed Israel has conducted strikes on several other Hamas and Palestinian Islam Jihad targets . <p> They were in response to a number of militant rocket releases aimed at the Israeli town of Sderot , close to Gaza . Israeli targets included apartments where fighters were gathering , weapons stores , rocket launch sites and tunnels . <p> The attack on Aladini is a major victory for the IDF , which is still preparing to assault the enclave of Rafah , where more than one million Palestinians are sheltering .
