<p> As on previous Saturdays in the past six months , there were two marches taking place in London yesterday . The first , a gathering of tens of thousands of full-throated , flag-waving supporters of an immediate ceasefire in Gaza gathered at Hyde Park Corner at noon , and shuffled peaceably and patiently in the sunshine in the direction of the American embassy at Vauxhall , over the river . <p> The second march was taking place mostly in the imaginations of right-wing commentators and politicians who increasingly choose to see these displays of solidarity with the Palestinian cause only as a provocation and a threat .
