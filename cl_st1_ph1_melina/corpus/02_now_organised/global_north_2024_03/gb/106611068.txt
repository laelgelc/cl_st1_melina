<h> Profile acquires Raja Shehadeh 's new book about Palestine and Israel <p> Profile Books has acquired the new book by 2023 National Book Award finalist and human rights campaigner Raja Shehadeh , What Does Israel Fear from Palestine ? . <p> Profile founder director Andrew Franklin bought world rights from Jenny Brown at Jenny Brown Associates , and the title will be released in June 2024 . US rights have been sold to Other Press . <p> In the book , Shehadeh examines how since the formation of the state of Israel in 1948 , " the Nakba ( or ' catastrophe ' as the Palestinians call it ) , there have been many opportunities to move towards peace and equality between Palestine and Israel -- but each has been rejected by Israel " . What Does Israel Fear from Palestine ? explores " what went wrong again and again , and why -- and how it could still be different " . <p> Shehadeh is a writer , lawyer , and the founder of the Palestinian human rights organisation Al-Haq .
