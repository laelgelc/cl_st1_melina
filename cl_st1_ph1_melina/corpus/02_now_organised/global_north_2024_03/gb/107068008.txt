Local people expressed dismay at the sudden disintegration of a familiar landmark they had known all their lives . The post-1945 international order is a bit like that bridge . It was always there . Its authority , rules and resilience were taken for granted . Now , alarmingly , the entire global edifice is in freefall as the usual supports are kicked away . <p> The sense of things breaking apart is profound -- and the negative ramifications are everywhere .
