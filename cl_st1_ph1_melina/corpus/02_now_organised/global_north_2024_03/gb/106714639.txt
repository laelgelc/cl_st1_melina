<p> A global humanitarian organisation in Sharjah on Friday launched a Ramadan drive -- seeking to bring hope to war-torn Gaza and Sudan . <p> Called ' I 'm Human ' , The Big Heart Foundation 's ( TBHF ) Zakat campaign allows residents and Good Samaritans to share their blessings with the scores of families and children stuck in the crossfires . <p> With a Dh100 donation , one can feed a family of five Palestinians who are besieged by the devastating Israeli war on Gaza and are cut off from sources of basic sustenance . It can also go to a displaced family of five in Sudan . <p> A Dh500 contribution puts a temporary roof over the heads of a 5-member family in Gaza or Sudan , while Dh7,000 zakat means a child from a low-income family in the UAE can go to school in Sharjah for a whole academic year . <p> TBHF has activated various donation channels , including SMS and online donations , as well as through Yalla Give .
