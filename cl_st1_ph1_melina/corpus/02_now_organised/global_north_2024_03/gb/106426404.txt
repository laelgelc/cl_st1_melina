<p> The ongoing Israel-Gaza conflict and a weakened domestic economy adversely impacted Lebanon 's private sector economy in February with overseas clients reluctant to place orders with businesses in the country , citing security concerns . <p> According to the latest BLOM Lebanon Purchasing Managers ' Index ( PMI ) survey , February closed at 49.1 , recording a slight dip from January 's 49.4 , with anything below 50.0 indicating a decline in business . <p> Weak demand conditions were a restrictive factor for businesses in Lebanon during February , with security concerns due to the war in Gaza furthering a dent in company sales . A challenging political and economic landscape also weighed on clients ' appetite to place new orders . Export performances also suffered due to the regional conflict . <p> The deterioration in the health of Lebanon 's private sector economy also resulted in a renewed drop in employment as business confidence fell to a seven-month low . <p> Supplier performance fractionally improved , leading stocks of inputs to rise again . Cost pressures also remained contained , with the rate of inflation holding close to January 's 28-month low . <p> Backlogs of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ staffing capacity .
