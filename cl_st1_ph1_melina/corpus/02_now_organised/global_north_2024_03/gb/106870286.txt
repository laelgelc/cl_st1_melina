<p> Riyadh : Gulf Cooperation Council ( GCC ) Secretary General Jasem Mohamed Albudaiwi affirmed the commitment of the GCC member states to the provisions of the bloc 's Declaration of Human Rights , which guarantees rights enshrined in their constitutions and national legislation , the GCC said in a statement . <p> Albudaiwi specifically referred to Article 2 of the declaration , which states that " people are equal in human dignity , rights , and freedoms , and they are equal before the law without discrimination based on origin , sex , religion , language , color , or any other form of discrimination . " <p> Albudaiwi 's statement was made on the occasion of the International Day for the Elimination of Racial Discrimination , observed on March 21 of each year . <p> He also stated that the leaders of the GCC countries believe in the dignity of human beings and respect for their rights , stressing their commitment to their protection as guaranteed by Islamic law , as well as the principles laid out in the United Nations Charter and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights . <p> " The International Convention on the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the GCC member states have ratified , has also played a role in shaping the GCC countries ' legislative and regulatory frameworks that are aimed at combating racial discrimination , hatred , and prejudice , as well as promoting a culture of tolerance , coexistence , justice , and equality in their societies , " His Excellency said . <p> According to the statement , he commended the role played by the GCC countries in the process of achieving lasting peace , which is primarily based on equality and non-discrimination .
