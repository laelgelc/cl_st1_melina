<h> UN Security Council demands immediate ceasefire in Gaza after US abstains from vote <p> Members of the United Nations Security Council vote on a Gaza resolution that demands an immediate ceasefire for the month of Ramadan leading to a permanent sustainable ceasefire , and the immediate and unconditional release of all hostages , at U.N . headquarters in New York City , U.S. , March 25 , 2024 . REUTERS/Andrew Kelly <p> Michelle Nichols <p> Today at 15:59 <p> The United Nations Security Council on Monday demanded an immediate ceasefire between Israel and Palestinian militants Hamas and the immediate and unconditional release of all hostages after the United States abstained from the vote . <p> The remaining 14 council members voted for the resolution , which was proposed by the 10 elected members of the body .
