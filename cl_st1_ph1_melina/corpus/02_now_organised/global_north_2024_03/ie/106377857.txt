<h> Joe Brolly : Dublin finally took the advice I gave to Con O'Callaghan 's father -- now they are back to their best <p> Con O'Callaghan is back to his best for Dublin . Photo by David Fitzgerald/Sportsfile <p> Joe Brolly <p> Today at 08:50 <p> Dublin 's opening two boring defeats in the league prompted me to ask the question , " Why wo n't they kick the ball in to Con O'Callaghan ? " For those games , he was a peripheral presence , tracking back and tackling round the middle third , taking a hand-pass at the last minute in a crowded defence . <p> What a waste . Like hiring the prize stallion from the stud and using him to give children donkey rides on the beach . Or asking Maradona to go in nets . I was chatting to his father and mother at a Gaza peace march in Dublin after the second league game and they were equally frustrated . " Why wo n't they give him early ball ? " I asked his dad . " No comment , " he said .
