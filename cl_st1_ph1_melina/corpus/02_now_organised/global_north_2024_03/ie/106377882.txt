<h> ' My heart is gone ' -- Mother of infant twins buried in Rafah , her husband was also killed <p> Mother of the Palestinian twins Wesam and Naeem Abu Anza , who were born during the conflict between Israel and Hamas and were killed in Israeli air strikes , reacts during their funeral , in Rafah in the southern Gaza Strip March 3 , 2024 . Photo : Reuters/Mohammed Salem <p> Bassam Masoud and Nidal al-Mughrabi <p> Today at 12:54 <p> Born a few weeks into the Gaza war , infant twins Wesam and Naeem Abu Anza were buried on Sunday , the youngest of 14 members of the same family whom Gaza health authorities say were killed in an Israeli airstrike in Rafah overnight . <p> Their mother , Rania Abu Anza , held one of the twins , its tiny body wrapped in a white shroud , to her cheek and stroked its head during the funeral on Sunday . A mourner held the second baby close by , pale blue pyjamas visible beneath a shroud .
