<p> Art-punks Stupid Son are tackling nightmares , malfunctioning window blinds and all of the tribulations that come with sleepless nights in their debut EP Comedy . <p> The seven-track record dropped this week following a whirlwind few months for the four lads as they released singles , nabbed a live radio session for BBC6 Music 's New Music Fix Daily and scooped up a slot at the Ones to Watch festival . <p> With Aaron Hegarty on sticks , Andrew Pierce taking guitar and vocals alongside Conall Loughney and Robert Broaders on bass , Stupid Son are one of the most exciting acts coming out of Ireland at the moment . <h> ' A bit tongue in cheek ' <p> Comedy is a concept record touching on the different facets of insomnia , but the title is a " bit tongue in cheek " and an " antithesis " to the record itself . <p> " You know in films , you would have a very happy song on during a fucked up scene , " Conall said .
