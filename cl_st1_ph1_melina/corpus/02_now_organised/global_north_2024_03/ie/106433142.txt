<p> Bohs will play the Palestine women 's football team in a historic fundraiser friendly in May . <p> The game will take place on May 15 to mark the anniversary of the Nakba . The club and the Palestinian FA have also agreed to work towards a second game in the future . <p> Funds raised will go towards humanitarian relief efforts in Palestine and facilitating the visit of the Palestinian team . A club statement read : " We would like to thank Palestinian Ambassador to Ireland Dr Jilan Wahba Abdalmajid for facilitating the talks that led to this game being agreed . Bohemian FC has been supporting Sport for Life Palestine through sales of our 2023 away shirt which fund access to sports for children in the city of Tulkarem in the West Bank . <p> " Given recent horrific events the work of the charity has been severely impacted and their activities now serve as an outlet to try reliving young children from the trauma they are going through . We will be announcing a series of events around the game in due course , as well as ticketing information , and hope to see a sold out Dalymount Park in solidarity with the women of the Palestinian national team and their nation . " <p> Bohs women 's team manager Ken Kiernan added : " I am delighted to have the opportunity to play this fixture . It will be great for our group to play at such an occasion , in what should be a sold out Dalymount Park , and test ourselves against women from another part of the world .
