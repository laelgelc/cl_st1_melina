<h> Person to Person : I moved here as a teenager to study music and forged a life for myself in Cork <p> I 'm an artist living in Cork . I work across disciplines such as sound art , field recordings , performance , composition , film , theatre , and visual art , and am the 2023/24 Music Network RESONATE Artist-In-Residence at Triskel Arts Centre . <p> I moved here as a teenager to study music in UCC in 2003 , discovered experimental music , and that was it . I mostly introduce myself as a sound artist . To put it very simply , I work a lot with various types of microphones and recording devices , recording the sounds of materials and processes , and then I arrange those sounds in ways that feel right to me . <p> I often amplify my body , my skin , my tongue , my hair and my saliva , or else I might record the sound of putting my body through various actions or stresses ( pushing , dragging , digging , submerging , etc ) . <p> My residency at the Triskel culminates in a concert @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ audio-filmic collaboration with the critic and filmmaker Maximilian Le Cain . <p> Where were you born ? <p> I was born in Galway and grew up in Tuam and Galway city .
