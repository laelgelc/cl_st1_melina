<h> Paraic O'Brien : How to distinguish the far right from genuinely concerned citizens <p> Asylum-seekers at Mount Street were moved to an alternative site . Photo : Arthur Carron <p> Paraic O'Brien <p> Today at 03:30 <p> I watched last year 's November 23 Dublin riots play out on social media from a hotel in East Jerusalem . I was in Israel reporting on the war in Gaza . That evening , I should have been absorbed in the conflict there , but all I could do was scroll through pictures and videos of Dublin on fire . I was ashamed of myself . Having reported on the rise of anti-immigrant sentiment in other parts of the world , I had n't properly noticed it happening at home . <p> Then , the week before last , I was dispatched by Channel 4 News in London to try to get under the skin of the anti-immigrant movement in Ireland . Our first stop was the encampment of 150 tents surrounding the International Protection Office on Mount Street , Dublin .
