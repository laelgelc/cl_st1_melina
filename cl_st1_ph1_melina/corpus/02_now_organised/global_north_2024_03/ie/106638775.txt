<p> All Irish acts that were previously scheduled for the SXSW Festival in Austin , Texas have announced they will not be appearing due to the festival 's links with the US Army and defence companies . <p> Acts including Dublin natives Gavin James , Sprints , Soda Blonde and Gurriers pulled out of the Republic of Ireland showcase alongside all other performers from across the country . Featured partners for the festival are the US Army , RTX , Collins Aerospace and BAE Systems - all who have provided or assisted the IDF and occupation of Palestinian territories . <p> Both the Music From Ireland official showcase and the Full Irish Breakfast official daytime showcase have been cancelled due to the decision . The acts met with Minister Catherine Martin and provided their reasoning why they would not be participating in the festival . <p> In a statement issued last night the Department of Tourism , Culture , Arts , Gaeltacht , Sport and Media said Minister Catherine Martin was in Austin on a trade mission to promote Ireland 's creative industries .
