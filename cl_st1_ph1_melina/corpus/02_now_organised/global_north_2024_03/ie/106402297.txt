<p> I WAS dropping Diarmuid to UCC on Monday morning . He attends twice a week . On this particular Monday , I knew that there was a farming protest outside the County Hall and farmers had been asked to gather in their tractors to lobby decision makers around the fact that agriculture is being asked to cut production by 25% to meet climate change targets . On our journey in , I deliberately avoided the spot where the protest would be but on my way home I could n't help swing by for a look . <p> I counted 60 tractors parked along the bus lane on the Carrigrohane Straight all of varying shapes and sizes and colours . None of them had drivers because they were gathered peacefully outside the County Hall . I contemplated blowing my horn in support of the farmers but the event was so quiet that I thought the blast of noise might appear like frustration rather than solidarity so I thought better of it and drove on .
