Her husband built a successful business , and Benchimol raised a family and earned international renown singing the ancient Sephardic hymns she had learned as a child in Alcazarquivir . <p> " It was a rich country , there were a lot of opportunities , " reminisces Benchimol , now 74 . " We had many friends and there was a real sense of brotherhood . There was never any racism against us .
