( Adrian Wyld/The Canadian Press ) <p> Canada welcomes the United Nations Security Council 's call for a ceasefire in the war between Israel and Hamas during Muslim holy month of Ramadan , Foreign Affairs Minister Melanie Joly said Monday . <p> Canada has been asking for a sustainable ceasefire since December , she said at a press conference in Ottawa . <p> " We 've clearly stated that the violence must stop and this ceasefire must be linked to hostages being released , Hamas laying down its weapons and also making sure that humanitarian aid can reach Gaza , " Joly said .
