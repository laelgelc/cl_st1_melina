<h> More Australians in favour of being independent middle power than US ally <p> Canberra , March 12 ( IANS ) More Australians believe the nation should aim to become an independent middle power than primarily an ally of the US , a poll has found . <p> Published by the online Australian edition of British newspaper ' The Guardian ' on Tuesday , the poll found that 38 per cent of Australians said the country 's role in global affairs should be as an independent middle power , Xinhua news agency reported . <p> Twenty-five per cent said the nation should do its best not to engage in world affairs and 17 per cent were unsure . <p> The poll found that 63 per cent of Australians described themselves as concerned about Israel 's military action in Gaza . <p> Thirty-seven per cent of Australians said Israel should withdraw permanently from military action in Gaza , twice as many than 18 per cent who supported it continuing . <p> Two thirds of participants in the poll said they believed the world is either very or somewhat divided and 10 per cent described it as united . <p> Sixty-eight @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and 57 per cent said it would undermine efforts to solve problems including climate change .
