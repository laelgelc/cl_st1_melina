<h> Former B.C. cabinet minister Selina Robinson talks about feeling heartbroken by the NDP government 's unwillingness to confront antisemitism in the province -- and in caucus <p> March 13 , 2024 <p> By Sam Margolis <p> Selina Robinson was the B.C. finance minister from 2020 to 2022 , after which she became the Minister of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills until Feb. 5 , 2024 . <p> A week after she left the governing NDP caucus in British Columbia because of what she described as the antisemitism within it , MLA Selina Robinson wonders where the leadership is going to come from to tackle the problem of antisemitism in the party , the public service and the province as a whole . <p> " The premier ( David Eby ) is now saying we need to be doing that work , but I was the one doing the work and you thought I was a liability , " she told The CJN . <p> On March 6 , Robinson released a scathing resignation letter , stating she was " heartbroken " by the lack of a strong response from Eby and her fellow caucus MLAs in the midst @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ antisemitic statements made by provincial NDP politicians . <p> Following her departure from the NDP caucus , Robinson said she had heard from members of the Jewish community who expressed their gratitude for the voice which she brought to government and for the positions she has taken . Many of the constituents in her suburban Coquitlam-Maillardville riding east of Vancouver have been equally supportive and , according to Robinson , " heartbroken " for her . <p> However , NDP MLAs have not contacted to her in the past week . " I suspect that they do n't know what to say , " Robinson said . " Colleagues from other parties have reached out to me to say that they are sorry I have experienced this . " <p> Robinson , a prominent Jewish politician in B.C. , was removed by Eby from her post as minister of post-secondary education in early February over remarks she made about the state of the land in Israel . Finding out that people were hurt or insulted by a statement , that described land that had been neglected by the Ottomans under their rule , hurt Arab Muslims or Palestinians , that was not my intent , " Robinson said . " I took responsibility for and apologized for those words and said I would engage with them to better understand how those words hurt them .
