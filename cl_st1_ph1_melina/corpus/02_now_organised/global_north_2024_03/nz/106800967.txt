It all stems from a lawsuit filed last November in the US District Court for the Northern District of California by the Center for Constitutional Rights , representing a number of Palestinian human rights organisations including Palestinians in Gaza and the United States . <p> The lawsuit sought an order from the court " requiring that the President of the United States , the Secretary of State , and the Secretary of Defense adhere to their duty to prevent , and not further , the unfolding genocide of Palestinian people in Gaza . " The relevant duty arose by virtue of the UN Genocide Convention of 1948 , which made obligations under it " judicially enforceable as a peremptory norm of customary international law .
