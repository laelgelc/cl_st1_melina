In their draft district plan , a whopping half a million hectares or nearly 70% of the Waitaki District land area is now captured under various regulatory classifications . This includes 300 SNAs and several landscape and cultural property overlays . <p> Many property owners have multiple classifications and some have 100% of their property captured under what is known as regulatory takings .
