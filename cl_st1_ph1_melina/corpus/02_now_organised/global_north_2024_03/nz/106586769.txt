<h> Maximum Vessel Lengths For Tory Channel Established <p> For the safety of vessels , their crew and cargo , the Marlborough Harbourmaster has issued a direction that sets a maximum length of 187 metres for any new vessels looking to use the Tory Channel/Kura Te Au , to access Marlborough ports at Picton and Shakespeare Bay . All vessels currently using the Channel are at or under this length . <p> Marlborough Harbourmaster Jake Oliver said the entrance to the Tory Channel is narrow and tidal , so is challenging to navigate , with the risk of an incident increasing with longer vessels . <p> " Safety is central to the work a Harbourmaster does , " he said . " The maximum length of 187 metres for the Channel is based on an indepth navigation safety risk assessment looking at past vessel crossings , vessel risk management documents and relevant Transport Accident Investigation Commission reports , " he said . <p> " We know from our review that vessels at and under 187 metres can travel through the Channel safely .
