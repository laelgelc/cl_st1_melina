<h> Family Court Associates Appointed <h> Hon Judith Collins KC <h> Attorney-General <p> Attorney-General Judith Collins today announced the appointment of the first six Family Court Associates , in a move designed to relieve pressure on the Family Court system . <p> " The associates will take responsibility for much of the initial work required to get cases to the point that a substantive decision can be made , including conducting mediation and settlement conferences and issuing pre-hearing directions . <p> " There are presently 16,000-plus active cases before the Family Court and the average age of those cases is more than 300 days . These associate roles will free up Family Court Judges to devote more time to hearing and determining substantive matters before the Court . " <p> The appointees will take up their roles on 2 April 2024 .
