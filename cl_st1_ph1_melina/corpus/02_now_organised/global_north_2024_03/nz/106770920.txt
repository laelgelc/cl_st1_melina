<h> Local Tamariki Help Choose Mangakino Basketball Court Colours <p> Taupo District Council is throwing an alley-oop to Mangakino basketballers with the construction of a new court beginning on Wednesday 3 April . <p> The planning for this project was a true community affair with local children voting for the colours used on the court . The winning colours were an eye-catching combination of purple and yellow , with many of the children naming the LA Lakers colours as the reason for their choice . <p> Construction , which includes a basketball court , adjustable-height hoops , fencing , shelter , and seating , is expected to be complete by mid-2024 . <p> Community engagement advisor Claire Dredge said more than 70 young people , and some of their parents , voted on the colours , which was an important part of the project . <p> " It 's these tamariki and rangatahi who are most looking forward to the basketball court , so we wanted to make sure they felt included in the planning . They were really enthusiastic about it when I visited them at the bus stop and the Mangakino Pool Party . <p> " Now @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ community asset they can be really proud of and look after for years to come . We also took on board feedback from nearby residents regarding noise and light disturbance and have taken steps to ensure any disruption is minimised . <p> " The turf used will reduce slipping and injuries , deliver excellent bounce and traction , and reduce the noise by 30 to 32 per cent .
