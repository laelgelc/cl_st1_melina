<h> Space Launch Takes Climate Research To New Heights <p> Space Minister Judith Collins and Climate Change Minister Simon Watts today welcomed the successful launch of MethaneSAT -- a milestone in the development of New Zealand 's space sector . <p> MethaneSAT is a state-of-the-art satellite designed to accurately detect methane emissions and contribute to global efforts to combat climate change . The mission is led by the United States ' Environmental Defense Fund , with New Zealand 's role including hosting the satellite 's mission operations control centre and leading an atmospheric science programme , studying global emissions from agriculture . <p> " Hosting and operating the mission operations control centre for the satellite here in New Zealand adds a significant piece of infrastructure to our space system and grows our capability to operate complex space missions , " Ms Collins says . <p> " The mission operation control centre , developed by Rocket Lab , will be based at the University of Auckland 's Te Punaha Atea Space Institute long-term , providing excellent opportunities for students to be part of mission operations activity . <p> " This mission demonstrates the value New Zealand brings to international science @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in this case supporting global efforts to combat climate change . " <p> Climate Change Minister Simon Watts says MethaneSAT puts New Zealand researchers at the forefront of work to detect and measure agricultural methane emissions from space . <p> " Global understanding of how space technology could be used to detect methane from agriculture is at a very early stage . New Zealand 's involvement in the mission allows us to test the ability to measure agricultural emissions from space , " Mr Watts says . <p> " In the long-term this research could help towards developing new ways to measure efforts to reduce methane from livestock . Exploring the potential of innovative technologies to help us combat climate change is an important part of New Zealand 's wider climate action .
