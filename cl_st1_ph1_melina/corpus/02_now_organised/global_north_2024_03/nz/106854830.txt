<h> Have Your Say On The Companies ( Address Information ) Amendment Bill <p> The Economic Development , Science and Innovation Committee is calling for submissions on the Companies ( Address Information ) Amendment Bill . The Companies Act 1993 requires the residential addresses of company directors to be made available to the public in a register . This bill would provide a mechanism for directors who have serious concerns for their personal safety to request that their residential address be replaced with a substitute address , such as a service address . <p> The bill would allow a director to submit an application to the Registrar declaring that the public availability of their residential address is likely to result in physical or mental harm to them or a person residing with them .
