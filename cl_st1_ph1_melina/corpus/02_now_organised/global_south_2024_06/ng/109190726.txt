Most significantly , the feast reminds humanity that attainment of success goes along with sacrifice of tangible and intangible capital . As an act of worship , Eid-el-Kabir , for Muslims , is a festival instituted by the Almighty through the agency of Prophet Ibrahim and Ismail ( upon them be peace and blessings of Allah ) . <p> The narrative captures the story of faith and the faithful . Prophet Ibrahim started out as a believer , a lonely believer in the Almighty at a time humanity chose to disbelieve in Him . He started out by asking his mother who his creator and god was . His mother told him it was his father , Azar . Prophet Ibrahim was not satisfied with her answer . The notion that his father was his god appeared confusing to him .
