<h> Sunday Special : Crispy Fried Plantain And Spicy Pepper Sauce Recipe <p> When it comes to Nigerian delicacies , only a few can evoke the feeling of Sunday dinner quite like fried plantains paired with pepper sauce . This greatly loved combination is the perfect comfort food , as it marries the sweetness of plantains with the spicy kick of pepper sauce . It is a true Nigerian signature . <p> Plantains have a long history of being a classic in West African dishes , popular for their nutritional content and versatile nature . Unlike their banana cousins , they are starchier , larger , and require cooking before eating . <p> The pepper sauce , or " ata dindin " as it is known in Nigeria , is made by blending chili peppers , onions , tomatoes , and a bunch of spices . Additionally , some people may add a touch of palm oil and crayfish for a unique flavor . <p> In this article , we shall relish the ultimate fried plantain and pepper sauce recipe for a Sunday treat . <h> Instructions <p> 1 . Heat the oil : In a large frying pan , heat about 1/2 inch of vegetable oil over medium-high heat until hot but not smoking . <p> 3 . Fry the plantains : Carefully place the plantain slices in the hot oil in a single layer . Avoid overcrowding the pan . Fry until golden brown on the bottom ( about 2-3 minutes ) , then flip and fry the other side until golden brown . Remove the fried plantains with a slotted spoon and transfer them to a plate lined with paper towels to drain excess oil . Sprinkle lightly with salt while they are still hot . <p> 3 .
