Both choices will further divide the ANC , rendering it highly unstable and further splits inevitable . Both choices will inflame political tensions and result in intense and enduring political acrimony in and beyond party politics , an acrimony that runs throughout society . On its outer edges that acrimony will understand itself in terms of a mutual and existential imperative to destroy the other side rather than a set of differences to be resolved through a democratic disputation .
