<p> Israeli Police released new footage showing the rescue of Noa Argamani from Hamas captivity in the Gaza Strip . Argamani has been one of the most recognizable faces of those abducted on Oct 7 , following a widely circulated video of terrorists taking her from the Nova music festival . Body cam footage shows members of the elite Yamam counter-terrorism unit storming an apartment in central Gaza 's Nuseirat . In the clip , an officer says to Argamani , " Everything is okay , Noa . We are taking you home . " Argamani is heard saying : " I am very excited , just very scared about the journey . " " Everything is ok . You are with us , it 's all ok , " an officer responds . Earlier , Israel released footage from the rescue of other 3 hostages who were held together in another location nearby and rescued in the same operation .
