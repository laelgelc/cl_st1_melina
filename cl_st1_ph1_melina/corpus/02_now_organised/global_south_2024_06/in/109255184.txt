After the third symbolic stoning Tuesday , pilgrims headed to Mecca to perform " tawaf , " circling the Kaaba in the Grand Mosque counterclockwise seven times . <p> That circumambulation , known as the Farewell Tawaf , marks the end of Hajj as pilgrims prepare to leave the holy city . Once the Hajj is over , men are expected to shave their heads , and women to snip a lock of hair in a sign of renewal . Most of the pilgrims then leave Mecca for the city of Medina , about 340 kilometers ( 210 miles ) away , to pray in Prophet Muhammad 's tomb , the Sacred Chamber . <p> The Sacred Chamber tomb is part of the prophet 's mosque , one of the three holiest sites in Islam , along with the Grand Mosque in Mecca and the Al Aqsa Mosque in Jerusalem . <p> Pilgrims cast stones at pillars in the symbolic stoning of the devil .
