<p> Israeli Minister Benny Gantz has announced his resignation from Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's emergency government , along with fellow cabinet member Gadi Eisenkot . The move comes amid a months-long assault on Gaza and marks a considerable setback to Netanyahu 's coalition . <p> In a televised news conference , Gantz cited Netanyahu 's refusal to accept a ceasefire and post-war plan for Gaza as the reason for his departure . " Netanyahu is preventing us from advancing toward true victory . That is why we are leaving the emergency government today , with a heavy heart but with full confidence , " he said . <p> Eisenkot , a former IDF chief , echoed Gantz 's sentiments in a resignation letter to Netanyahu . " Despite the efforts of many , alongside those of my colleague , the cabinet you headed was prevented for a long while from making determining decisions , which were needed to realise the war 's goals and improve Israel 's strategic position , " the former IDF chief wrote . <p> " Outside considerations and politics infiltrated into the discussions , " he said .
