Gantz had demanded that a small Cabinet be formed as a way to sideline far-right lawmakers in Netanyahu 's government . <p> The war cabinet members included Gantz , Netanyahu and Defence Minister Yoav Gallant . <p> Gantz had quit the coalition along with Gadi Eisenkot , one of the three observers in the body . Gantz had quit three weeks after laying down a deadline for Netanyahu to come up with a clear post-war strategy for Gaza .
