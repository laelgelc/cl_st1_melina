<p> SIPRI report projects that China 's armoury may reach over 1,000 warheads by 2030 . <p> Photo : REUTERS <p> China has rapidly increased its nuclear weapons armoury , now possessing 500 warheads , a significant jump from 410 in January 2023 , according to a report by the Stockholm International Peace Research Institute ( SIPRI ) . This growth makes China the fastest expanding nuclear power globally , surpassing the armoury of India ( 172 warheads ) and Pakistan ( 170 warheads ) . Additionally , some of China 's warheads are on high operational alert , a status previously seen only in the US and Russian armoury . <p> The current global nuclear weapon stockpiles : <p> Russia : 4,380 stockpiles <p> United States : 3,708 stockpiles <p> France : 290 stockpiles <p> United Kingdom : 225 stockpiles <p> Israel : 90 stockpiles <p> North Korea : 50 stockpiles <p> Of these , an estimated 3,904 warheads are deployed with missiles and aircraft , while the rest are stored .
