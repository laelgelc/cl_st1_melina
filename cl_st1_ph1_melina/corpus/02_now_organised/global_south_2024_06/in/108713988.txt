<h> World War II Remembrance Day observed <p> 02-Jun-2024 <p> <p> SENAPATI , Jun 2 : A program commemorating World War II Remembrance Day and the inauguration of a World War II Peace Museum was held in Shajouba village , Mao in Senapati district on May 29 . According to a statement , Jun Shimada , a war historian from Japan , inaugurated the World War II Peace Museum.Notably , " the trails of World War II traversed through this hamlet on March 30 , 1944 , amidst a fierce battle between the Imperial Japanese Army and the British Indian Army " , added the statement.In his inaugural address , Jun Shimada emphasized the imperative of fostering bonds of friendship and reconciliation . He drew poignant parallels with contemporary conflicts , citing the ongoing turmoil in Ukraine and Gaza , underscoring the potential for resolution through peace and understanding.The museum showcases relics , books , and paintings related to World War II , alongside war attire and implements once used by the Mao Naga warriors.
