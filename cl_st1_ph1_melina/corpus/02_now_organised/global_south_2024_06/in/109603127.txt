<h> The first US presidential debate of the 2024 election between Donald Trump and Joe Biden was conducted in Atlanta , Georgia . <p> US President Joe Biden and his Republican opponent Donald Trump both claimed credit for the strong economy under their presidency . The first presidential debate between both candidates was held today and was a highly anticipated event . The debate was watched by millions of viewers across the country . ' ' He has done a bad job and inflation is killing our country , ' ' Trump said , who was president from 2017 to 2021 . On the other hand , Biden alleged that ' ' The economy collapsed ' ' under his rule , as reported by Reuters . The two political leaders debated on a wide range of topics from immigration , abortion , the Ukraine-Russia conflict , Gaza war , and how both of them handled the economy in their terms . Both politicians presented their opinion and their vision for the future of the United States with a motive to woo the voters and increase their support base .
