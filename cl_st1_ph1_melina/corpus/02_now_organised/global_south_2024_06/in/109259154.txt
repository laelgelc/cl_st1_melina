<h> The jackfruit revolution <p> The narrative is important , and it is just not about the jackfruit that was hardly noticed while our focus was on mangoes . Today is Goa 's Revolution Day , the day we remember Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia . Others , like Dr. Juliao Menezes and the local patriots who braved the Portuguese administration and protested in Margao , are being forgotten . In a few days the feast of Sao Joao will be celebrated with as much righteous anti-semitism as the righteousness of Zionism in Gaza . When anything is steeped in tradition , it feels as natural as breathing or jumping in the well . <p> The jackfruit is the traditional fruit that is shared by the revellers on the feast , each according to his need and ability to eat . It does not spike the sugar levels in those who suffer from diabetes as mangoes do . There will be a ' Ponnsachem Fest ' or jackfruit festival at the Pharmacy College on June 18 , on the 18th June Road in Panaji this Saturday . <p> The name ' Jack ' is derived from the Malayalam word ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ' Jaca ' by the Portuguese who landed there in 1498 A.D. Later , Admiral Afonso de Albuquerque captured Goa in 1510 A.D. and the jackfruit found its way into European texts in 1563 through the Jewish botanist who lived in Goa , Garcia da Orta , after whom the Panaji Municipal Garden is named . <p> Incidentally , Garcia da Orta died without detection by the Grand Inquisition that he was a Jew masquerading as a Catholic . Later , his skeleton was exhumed and burnt at the stake as a heretic along with an effigy . And thus his connection with the jackfruit continues with the tradition on the eve of Sao Joao . <p> This jackfruit tree belongs to the banyan , breadfruit and mulberry family , Moraceae , and is native to the Konkan and Malabar regions . Perhaps the earliest use of the jackfruit tree was the use of its leaves as fodder for goats and cattle . They were then used to make leaf plates called patravali . The glossy leaves were later used to make cups for steaming idli and to make the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of grated coconut and jaggery . The leaves add their mild flavour to the steamed rice . <p> The jackfruit is a rage as ripe carpels , either fresh or freeze-dried , fried chips made from mature carpels , and vegetable made out of the tender jackfruit . The jackfruit 's fleshy perianth is frozen or pickled in brine and is then sold as ' Vegan Meat ' . Jackfruit chips and jackfruit leather , locally known as satt or panaspoli , are popular in Goa . Jam , jelly , milkshakes , nectar and wine are also made from ripe jackfruit . <p> The seeds of the ripe fruit are cooked with beans and other vegetables , especially during the monsoons . The jackfruit seed is a wonderful source of gluten-free-starch that enables gluten-intolerant persons to enjoy a roti , biscuits and bread . The seeds can be used to grow trees . <p> However , now named varieties of jackfruit are propagated by grafts which are available in Goa .
