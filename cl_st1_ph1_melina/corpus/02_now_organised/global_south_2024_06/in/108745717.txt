<h> world <h> Qatar and Saudi Arabia on Sunday condemned an Israeli parliamentary bill that seeks to label UNRWA , the main provider of aid for Palestinians in Gaza , a terrorist group , joining a growing number of nations opposed to the proposal . The bill , which passed a preliminary vote in parliament last week , is the product of increasingly tense relations between Israel and the United Nations agency for Palestinian refugees . Israel has accused the agency of militant links , claiming that hundreds of its employees are members of militant groups , including some who allegedly participated in the Oct. 7 attacks on southern Israel .
