<h> 2,500 Gazans unable to perform haj due to Rafah crossing closure <p> GAZA CITY : The ongoing war in Gaza and the Israeli occupation of the Rafah crossing , which connects Gaza to Egypt , have prevented 2,500 Palestinians from performing the Muslim haj pilgrimage this year , according to the Ministry of Endowments in the Gaza Strip . <p> This is a " clear violation of religious freedom , " said ministry spokesperson Ikrami Al-Mudallal , speaking to Anadolu Agency ( AA ) . <p> The conflict has disrupted the ministry 's usual Hajj preparations , including signing transportation contracts within Egypt and Saudi Arabia and booking accommodations in Mecca and Medina , Al-Mudallal added . <p> Al-Mudallal noted that " the closure of the Rafah crossing and the ongoing conflict have stopped 2,500 Gaza pilgrims , including accompanying missions , from travelling to perform haj . " <p> " This group represents 38 per cent of the total 6,600 Palestinian pilgrims , " he said . <p> Al-Mudallal said the ministry is in contact with the relevant authorities in Saudi Arabia and Egypt to address what they describe as a " significant violation " of Palestinian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ travel for haj . He assured that the pilgrims affected this year " would not lose their right to perform haj next year , with priority given to them , " especially since many have waited years for their turn and 70 per cent are elderly or ill . <p> This year , Saudi King Salman bin Abdulaziz 's royal gesture to host 500 pilgrims from the families of those killed and wounded in Gaza was allocated to families outside the Gaza Strip , according to Al-Mudallal . <p> " This gesture allowed those who had left Gaza to perform the haj , preserving Gaza 's right to the royal gesture , " he said . <p> On June 6 , the Saudi monarch ordered the exceptional hosting of 1,000 pilgrims from the families of Gaza 's killed and wounded as part of the Saudi Ministry of Islamic Affairs ' Haj and Umrah Guest Programme . <p> These pilgrims were chosen from those who had left Gaza either due to the war or for medical treatment . -- BERNAMA-ANADOLU
