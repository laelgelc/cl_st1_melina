<h> Palestinian death toll crosses 37,000 <p> GAZA : The number of Palestinians killed in the Israeli onslaught since Oct 7 has risen to 37,084 , as reported by Palestine News and Info Agency ( WAFA ) , citing local health authorities . <p> An additional 84,494 individuals sustained injuries , and the majority of the victims are women and children . <p> WAFA reported that in the last 24 hours , the Israeli occupation forces committed eight massacres against families in the Gaza Strip , resulting in the documented killing of at least 283 Palestinians and the injury of 814 others . <p> Meanwhile , ambulance and rescue teams are still unable to reach many casualties and dead bodies trapped under the rubble or scattered on roads across the war-torn enclave , due to obstacles created by Israeli occupation forces . -- BERNAMA-WAFA
