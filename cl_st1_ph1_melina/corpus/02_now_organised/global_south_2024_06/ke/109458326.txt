<h> King Mohammed VI dispatches 40 tonnes of medical aid to Palestine <p> King Mohammed VI , of Morocco who is also the Chairman of the Al-Quds Committee , has given His Very High Instructions to deploy a humanitarian medical aid operation for the Palestinian population of Gaza . <p> The Moroccan aid will be transported via the same unprecedented land route used for the food aid operation deployed on the Sovereign 's instructions last Ramadan , the press release states . <p> A significant portion of the assistance from the Sovereign 's personal money will be paid for by the King . <p> According to the press statement , the food aid mission that was deployed last Ramadan at the Sovereign 's request would use the same extraordinary land route for the delivery of the Moroccan supplies . <p> The medical aid ordered by the King of Morocco is part of a long tradition of active solidarity with Palestine , and a continuation of the Royal concern for the Palestinian people . <p> This aid , consisting of 40 tones of medical products and equipment for adults and children , is taking an exceptional route overland .
