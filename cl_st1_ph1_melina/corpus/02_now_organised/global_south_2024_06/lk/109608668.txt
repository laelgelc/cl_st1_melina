<h> Iranians vote in presidential election with limited choices <p> DUBAI , June 28 ( Reuters ) - Iranians voted on Friday for a new president following the death of Ebrahim Raisi in a helicopter crash , choosing from a tightly controlled group of four candidates loyal to the supreme leader at a time of growing public frustration and Western pressure . <p> The election coincides with escalating regional tension due to war between Israel and Iran 's allies Hamas in Gaza and Hezbollah in Lebanon , as well as increased Western pressure on Iran over its fast-advancing nuclear programme . <p> While the election is unlikely to bring a major shift in the Islamic Republic 's policies , its outcome could influence the succession to Ayatollah Ali Khamenei , Iran 's 85-year-old supreme leader , in power since 1989 . <p> Khamenei called for a high turnout to offset a legitimacy crisis fuelled by public discontent over economic hardship and curbs on political and social freedom . <p> " The durability , strength , dignity and reputation of the Islamic Republic depend on people 's presence , " Khamenei told state television after casting his vote .
