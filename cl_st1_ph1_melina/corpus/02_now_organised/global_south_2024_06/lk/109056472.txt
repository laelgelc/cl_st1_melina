<p> Foreign Minister Ali Sabry undertook an official visit to Russia at the invitation of his counterpart , Sergey Lavrov , Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Russian Federation from 10-11 June 2024 to attend the BRICS Foreign Ministers ' meeting with the developing countries held in Nizhny Novgorod , Russia . <p> The meeting was attended by high level representatives of 22 countries including 17 Foreign Ministers . <p> Minister Sabry , delivering his statement at the meeting , stated that Global South should collectively address global challenges of climate change , debt burden and food security . <p> He further stressed that the architecture of global governance , established in the aftermath of World War II has achieved significant milestones . " However , that it was increasingly evident that this structure must evolve to reflect the contemporary realities of our world " , he added . The Foreign Minister highlighted that voices and interests of emerging economies and developing nations must be integrated more fully into the decision-making processes of international institutions . <p> Emphasizing the need for the rules based order , the Foreign Minister stated that " it is imperative that we strive hard @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ suits the rich and powerful , but as an equitable and justifiable model for a sustainable world order . <p> This means upholding international law and ensuring the principles of fairness , justice , and respect for sovereignty guide our actions .
