, and it welcomes more like-minded partners to join the BRICS family , Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi said on Tuesday . <p> Wang , also a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China ( CPC ) Central Committee , made the remarks while attending the " BRICS Plus " dialogue at the foreign ministers ' level in the Russian city of Nizhny Novgorod . <p> Noting that the " BRICS Plus " dialogue is an important platform for BRICS countries and other emerging markets and developing countries , Wang said it has long added vitality and injected impetus into BRICS ' development .
