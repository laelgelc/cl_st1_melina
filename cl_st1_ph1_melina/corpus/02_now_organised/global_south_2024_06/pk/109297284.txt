<h> Mushahid Hussain becomes first Pakistani to address BRICS forum <p> Senator Mushahid Hussain Syed became the first Pakistani to address the official International BRICS Forum , hosted by current Chair , Russia , at the Russian Far Eastern port of Vladivostok , close to North Korea and Japan . <p> The 2-day forum was organised by the ruling ' United Russia ' Party . <p> The forum was informed that 29 countries , including Pakistan , are applicants for membership of BRICS , which now includes almost half the world 's population , contributing 30% of global GDP and 50% of global oil and gas producers amongst its members , a news release said . <p> Mushahid Hussain , who is currently Co-Chairman of International Conference of Asian Political Parties ( ICAPP ) , gave a keynote speech in which he expressed the hope that " Pakistan hopes to join BRICS as Pakistan wants to be part of the new emerging order of the Global South ' , adding ' Pakistan as a member of the UN Security Council during 2025-2026 will be a robust voice for peace , justice and the oppressed . " <p> Mushahid Hussain @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ economic and political order was already unraveling and organisations like BRICS and Shanghai Cooperation Organisation ( SCO ) would be the pillars of this emerging new global order , which will derive its strength from the United Nations Charter , international law and the Five Principles of Peaceful Coexistence , rejecting hegemony , military dictate and double standards . " <p> He also lauded a similar endeavour of President Xi Jinping for a ' Global Security Initiative ' . <p> Finally , Mushahid Hussain thanked Russia and China for their principled position on the Gaza Genocide , underlining Israel has lost the war politically , morally , legally , and diplomatically , and Israeli supporters in the declining West have also been exposed for their double standards and aiding and abetting the Gaza Genocide , which is a crime against humanity as stated by the International Court of Justice . <p> Mushahid Hussain Syed was introduced to the podium by Senator Andrei Klimov , Member of the Bureau of the Supreme Council of the United Russia Party and Head of its Foreign Relations .
