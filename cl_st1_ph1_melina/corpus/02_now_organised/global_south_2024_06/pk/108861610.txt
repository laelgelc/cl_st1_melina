<p> The Consulate Generals of Pakistan , Turkiye , and Indonesia in New York co-hosted a significant event on combatting Islamophobia . <p> In their opening statements , the Consul Generals emphasized their governments ' collective commitment to combatting Islamophobia , while also advocating for peace , tolerance , and inter-religious dialogue . <p> In his remarks , Consul General of Pakistan , Aamer Ahmad emphasized Israel 's genocidal actions in Gaza as a stark illustration of global Islamophobia and shed light on prevalent Islamophobic incidents worldwide , including hate speech , targeted attacks , and discrimination against Muslims . <p> He further brought into focus the systematic destruction of religious sites in India and other Islamophobic actions within the country . <p> He also emphasized the significant military presence of Indian occupation forces in Indian illegally occupied Jammu and Kashmir , as well as India 's discriminatory policies aimed at altering the region 's demographic composition from a Muslim majority to a Hindu majority through demographic engineering .
