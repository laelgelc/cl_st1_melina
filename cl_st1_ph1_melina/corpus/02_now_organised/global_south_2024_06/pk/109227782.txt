Inflation is so high that even sacrificial offerings have significantly decreased . " <p> Rashid also mentioned that PTI founder Imran Khan is correct in saying that negotiations with the current government are futile , as the real power lies elsewhere . <p> " The state of the country is visible to everyone . Trillions of rupees in taxes have been imposed while people can not afford basic utilities , " he said .
