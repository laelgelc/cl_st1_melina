<h> Dozens of students arrested in pro-Palestinian protest at Stanford <p> More than a dozen people were arrested at Stanford University in California on Wednesday after pro-Palestinian student protesters barricaded themselves inside the building that houses the school president 's office , the latest clash between US students and authorities over the Israel-Gaza conflict . <p> Approximately 10 students entered the building around 5:30 am on the last day of classes for the spring quarter , according to the student newspaper The Stanford Daily , while about 50 students linked arms and surrounded the building , chanting , " Palestine will be free .
