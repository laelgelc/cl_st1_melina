helicopter crash . <p> " We emphasise the importance of high participation ( in the election ) because it is the pride of the Islamic republic , " Khamenei declared in a televised speech three days ahead of the vote . <p> " In every election where the turnout was low , the enemies of the Islamic republic have denounced us , " he added . <p> Voters have the choice between six candidates , who
