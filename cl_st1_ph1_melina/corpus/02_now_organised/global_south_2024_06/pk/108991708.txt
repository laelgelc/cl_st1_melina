<p> Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar will depart on Monday for Jordan to participate in the high-level conference on the current situation in Gaza . <p> The conference , ' Call for Action : Urgent Humanitarian Response for Gaza ' , has been jointly organised by King Abdullah-II of Jordan , President of Egypt Abdel Fattah Al-Sisi , and Secretary-General United Nations Antonio Guterres , as per Radio Pakistan . <p> The conference aims to identify and develop a collective response to the dire humanitarian situation in Gaza , it was further reported . <p> Meanwhile , the United States stepped up pressure on Monday for a Gaza ceasefire with a call for a UN Security Council vote on a truce as it redeployed Washington 's top diplomat to the region scarred by eight months of war . <p> Secretary of State Antony Blinken 's regional tour was preceded by further bombardment of Gaza by Israeli forces , with witnesses reporting overnight strikes in the centre of the strip and helicopter gunfire on ravaged Gaza City .
