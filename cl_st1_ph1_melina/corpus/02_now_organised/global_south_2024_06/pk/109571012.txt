<h> Dar takes a stand against US resolution <p> The Pakistani government is mulling a tit-for-tat response to the US House of Representatives resolution demanding an investigation into the February 8 elections . <p> " In response to US Congress , we will also bring about a resolution . We must show our sovereignty , we must show our unity , we must show that we mean business . This resolution makes no sense , " Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar said while speaking on the floor of the National Assembly . The US House of Representatives , earlier this week , in an overwhelming majority , voted to demand a " full and independent investigation of claims of interference or irregularities " in the polls . <p> The resolution HR 901 was passed by a massive 368 against seven votes on Tuesday , which makes 85% of the total American lawmakers in the legislature . The move triggered a strong response from Islamabad , with the Foreign Office saying it " stems from an incomplete understanding of the political situation and electoral process in Pakistan " . <p> The resolution said it was to express support for democracy and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ democratic and electoral institutions , human rights , and the rule of law .
