<h> UN Security Council set to decide on US-backed Israel-Hamas ceasefire proposal <p> The United Nations Security Council will vote later on Monday on a United States ( US ) -drafted resolution backing a proposal outlined by US President Joe Biden for a ceasefire between Israel and Hamas in Gaza . <p> The US finalised its text on Sunday after six days of negotiations among the 15-member council . It was not immediately clear whether veto powers Russia and China would allow the adoption of the draft . <p> A resolution needs at least nine votes in favour and no vetoes by the US , France , Britain , China , or Russia to pass . <p> Biden laid out a three-phase ceasefire plan on May 31 that he described as an Israeli initiative . Some Security Council members questioned whether Israel had accepted the plan to end the fighting in Gaza . <p> The draft resolution welcomes the new ceasefire proposal , " which Israel accepted , calls upon Hamas to also accept it , and urges both parties to fully implement its terms without delay and without condition .
