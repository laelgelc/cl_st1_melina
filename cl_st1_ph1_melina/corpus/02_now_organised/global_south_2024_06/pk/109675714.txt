<h> PUC issues ' Code of Conduct ' for Muharram <p> LAHORE:Pakistan Ulema Council issued ' Paigham-e-Pakistan Code of Conduct ' to ensure peace during Muharram-ul-Haram <p> The Council also announced denouncing US resolution , supporting Pakistan 's resolution and appealed to all the religious and political parties to unite for the cause of Azm-e-Istehkam Pakistan ( Resolve for Pakistan 's Stability ) . Pakistan Ulema Council has issued Paigham-e-Pakistan Code of Conduct with the mutual collaboration of all the religious and political parties of the country to ensure peace , law and order situation in the country during the month of Muharram-ul-Haram . The code of conduct aims to create religious harmony , the council said . While addressing a press conference , Chairman Pakistan Ulema Council , President International Interfaith Council Hafiz Muhammad Tahir Mahmood Ashrafi and leading Ulema Mashaykh resolved that religious leadership of all the sects would fully endorse the Paigham-e-Pakistan Code of Conduct and any violation of this code of conduct would not be tolerated . <p> All the religious scholars and Ulema-Mashaykh of Pakistan completely refute the extremist and fundamentalist mindset . In view of Azm-e-Istehkam Pakistan , they demanded the government devise @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ extremism and terrorism . <p> Pakistan 's National Assembly has adopted a resolution in reply of US resolution , they said , adding the US should play its role in containing atrocities on the people of Kashmir and Palestine and Israel 's continuing atrocities on the people of Palestine . They urged upon the opposition parties to support this resolution for the cause of Kashmir and Gaza , they concluded .
