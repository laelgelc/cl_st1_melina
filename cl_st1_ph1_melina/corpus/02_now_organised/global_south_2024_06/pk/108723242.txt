<p> Two Palestinian teenagers were killed by Israeli gunfire in the occupied West Bank , the Palestinian health ministry said on Sunday . <p> The Israeli military did not confirm the deaths but said two suspects hurled explosives towards a local community , endangering civilians , and troops responded with live fire . <p> " Hits were identified , " the military said in a statement . <p> The Palestinian health ministry said a 16- and a 17-year-old were killed west of Aqabat Jaber refugee camp , near the West Bank city of Jericho . <p> Palestinian media said medicals teams were prevented from reaching one of the wounded and the other succumbed to his wounds on Sunday in a hospital in Jerusalem . One teen was shot in the head and the other in his chest , the Palestinian health ministry said . <p> The occupied West Bank , which Palestinians want as the core of a future independent state along with Gaza , has seen a surge in violence since the start of the war in Gaza last year , and a major crackdown by Israeli security forces which have made thousands of arrests .
