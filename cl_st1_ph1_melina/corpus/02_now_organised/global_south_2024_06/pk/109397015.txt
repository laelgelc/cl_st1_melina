<h> Israel ' pause ' in Gaza had no impact on aid supplies : WHO <p> Geneva - A daily " pause " the Israeli military declared in Gaza to facilitate aid flows has had no impact on deliveries of badly needed aid , the UN 's health agency said Friday . <p> " So overall , we the UN can say that we did not see an impact on the humanitarian supplies coming in since that , I will say , unilateral announcement of this technical pause , " said Richard Peeperkorn , the World Health Organization ( WHO ) representative in the Palestinian territories . " That is the overall assessment . " Over the weekend , the Israeli military announced a daily humanitarian " pause " in fighting on a key road in southern Gaza . <p> Days later however , a United Nations spokesman said " this has yet to translate into more aid reaching people in need " . According to the WHO , as of May 17 , only 750 people remained in the city of Rafah . Returning from Gaza , the UN Women 's Special Representative for the Occupied Palestinian Territory , Maryse Guimond , described the territory as a " world of devastation " . She added : " More than one million people in Gaza are in constant displacement " in the hope of finding somewhere safe . However , the WHO 's Dr Gargavanis said that " no place is safe " .
