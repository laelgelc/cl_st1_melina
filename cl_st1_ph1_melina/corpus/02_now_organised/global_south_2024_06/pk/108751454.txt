<h> By taking risks to criticize repressive governments or powerful , journalists , poets and writers work towards freedom of oppressed <p> Political dissent implies disagreement , criticism or protest against coercive decisions , unjust laws or imposed policies . It holds enormous power in times of occupation and oppression . By taking risks to criticize repressive governments or the powerful , journalists , poets and writers work towards freedom of the oppressed . <p> Engaging peacefully with these dissenting voices is constructive and leads to the strengthening of democratic principles , undoing injustices and social transformation . Dissent has been used as a political weapon in anti-colonial movements and against autocracies around the world . Pakistan itself was born of dissent and broke up on repressing the same . <p> In a country where social media platforms like X are already blocked and internet outages are a normal occurrence , the recently passed Punjab Defamation Bill , seemingly crafted to protect public officials , is a continuation of repressive laws and measures .
