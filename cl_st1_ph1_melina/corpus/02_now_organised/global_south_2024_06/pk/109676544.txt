<h> Fighting for third day in north Gaza as thousands displaced <p> Gaza Strip , Palestinian Territories - Explosions , air strikes and gunfire rattled northern Gaza on Saturday , the third day of an Israeli military operation that has uprooted tens of thousands of Palestinians and compounded what the UN called " unbearable " living conditions in the territory . <p> An AFP correspondent reported ongoing explosions from the Shujaiya area near Gaza City , with a resident saying bodies were visible on the streets . Israel 's military on Saturday said its operations were continuing in Shujaiya where fighting " above and below the ground " left a " large number " of militants dead . <p> A resurgence of fighting in the area comes months after Israel had declared the command structure of Hamas dismantled in northern Gaza . Last Sunday Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said the " intense phase " of the war was winding down after almost nine months , but experts see a potentially prolonged next phase .
