<h> Pakistan dispatches another consignment of relief goods for Gaza <p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : The Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Mohammad Ishaq Dar has said that in solidarity with the besieged people of Gaza , Pakistan will dispatch another consignment of relief goods for Gaza via Jordan . <p> In a post on X on Friday , the foreign minister said that the consignment consists of canned food , medical supplies and other relief goods and will be dispatched today from Karachi . <p> This is the 9th consignment of relief goods sent to the Palestinians in Gaza by the people and government of Pakistan since October last year . <p> In solidarity with the besieged people of Gaza , Pakistan will dispatch another consignment of relief goods for Gaza via Jordan .
