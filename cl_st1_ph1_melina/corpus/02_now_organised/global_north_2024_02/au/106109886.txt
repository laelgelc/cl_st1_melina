<h> Clementine Ford announces new podcast <p> Broadcaster , author , feminist and performer Clementine Ford has a new podcast called ' Untethered ... with Clementine Ford ' . <p> The announcement : <p> Best-selling author , performer , feminist and broadcaster , Clementine Ford has announced today the launch of her hotly anticipated new podcast series , aptly named Untethered ... with Clementine Ford which will launch on Wednesday 21st February . <p> Untethered ... with Clementine Ford is a weekly podcast exploring courage , choices and the catalysing moments in a person 's life that lead them to define themselves on their own terms . From choosing to remain childfree , cutting off toxic parents for good , unpacking white supremacy and embracing gender euphoria , Untethered invites speakers , thinkers and everyday folk to share first person stories of their own untethering . Whether you are desperate for some sage advice or looking to be inspired ... Untethered will nurture the hopes , dreams and goals you have for yourself and give you the toolbox to do so with confidence ! <p> After a hugely successful two years at the helm of Dear Clementine -- including @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and collecting one million downloads , fans of Clementine 's will revel in this new unfiltered , funny and engaging version of Clementine 's podcasting . <p> Clementine Ford said , " We are experiencing a profound shift in the way most people measure value . Making money , upholding oppressive systems , caring only for ourselves -- this is not the way to a happy life . Loyalty to these ideas is harming us all . What if we could just ... step off the treadmill ? Reject the narratives of conformity and expectation that keep so many of us from meeting our authentic selves ? What might seem scary in terms of getting started rarely seems so once you 've gotten going . The guests I 'm speaking to on Untethered come from all walks of life . Some of them will be familiar to listeners , while others may never have even listened to a podcast let alone appeared on one .
