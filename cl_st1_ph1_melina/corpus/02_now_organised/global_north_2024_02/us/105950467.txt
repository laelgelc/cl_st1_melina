<p> On Sunday , Feb. 4 , over 150 Chicagoans united in solidarity with the people of Israel at Temple Beth El in Northbrook for Jewish National Fund-USA and the Temple 's " Build Together : Stand with Israel " partnership event . <p> The event 's keynote speaker was Shahar Azani . An expert in Israel Education who now works to fight against BDS on college campuses and antisemitism globally , Azani serves as founder and principal at Fourth Dimension Strategies and as senior vice president at Jewish Broadcasting Services . He spoke to the crowd about how Israel has changed since Oct. 7 , countering antisemitism both on the ground and in the media , and Israel 's precarious situation on the global stage . <p> Local lay leaders also discussed how individuals can support the Jewish Homeland in the short and long term through Jewish National Fund-USA 's Israel Resilience Campaign ( **35;862;TOOLONG ) , which has raised nearly $60 million since Oct. 7 , 2023 , supporting the evacuation and relocation of tens of thousands of residents in Israel 's South while providing thousands more with housing , food , clothing , and other supplies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Build Together plan , a bold , strategic initiative to rebuild communities in the Israel Envelope ( formerly known as the Gaza Envelope ) and the lives of broken people . <p> " Jewish National Fund-USA has been with the communities of the Israel Envelope for over two decades , and they have n't left their side for over 120 days , " said Jewish National Fund-USA Chicago board member Bruce Werner .
