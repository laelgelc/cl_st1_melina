( Sina Schuldt/dpa via AP ) <p> The Associated Press <p> DUBAI , United Arab Emirates -- The U.S. military conducted new airstrikes targeting Yemen 's Houthi rebels , officials said Friday . <p> American forces destroyed four explosive-loaded drone boats and seven mobile anti-ship cruise missile launchers Thursday that could target vessels in the Red Sea , the U.S. military 's Central Command said . <p> " They presented an imminent threat to U.S. Navy ships and merchant vessels in the region , " Central Command said . " These actions will @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ more secure for U.S. Navy and merchant vessels . " <p> Since November , the rebels have repeatedly targeted ships in the Red Sea over Israel 's offensive in Gaza . But they have frequently targeted vessels with tenuous or no clear links to Israel , imperiling shipping in a key route for trade among Asia , the Mideast and Europe . <p> In recent weeks , the United States and the United Kingdom , backed by other allies , have launched airstrikes targeting Houthi missile arsenals and launch sites for its attacks . <p> A German frigate set sail Thursday to take part in the European Union mission to help defend cargo ships from Houthi attacks . <p> EU foreign ministers are expected to sign off on a Red Sea mission on Feb. 19 with seven countries ready to provide ships or planes . <p> The Hessen , carrying about 240 sailors , is expected to be in place once the EU mission has the official go-ahead and the German parliament has approved its participation .
