<p> College and university presidents are seemingly now expected by their many constituents to take positions on world events and unfolding crises , whether or not the proclamations would make any difference to what is happening on the ground . As parents , alumni and even politicians have exerted great pressure on higher education leaders to explicitly comment on the Hamas terrorist attack , the resulting military strikes by Israel in Gaza and the long-standing struggle for Palestinian self-determination , campus leaders often find themselves making many people unhappy . We are viewed as cowards if we stay silent and criticized for supporting the " wrong side " or being too neutral if we speak up . <p> How did we find ourselves here ?
