( Vincent Alban/Chicago Tribune ) <p> Last week , Chicago 's City Council passed a resolution calling for a cease-fire in Gaza . It was as close as these votes get , with Mayor Brandon Johnson breaking the tie . Groups such as the Jewish United Fund spoke out against it , in support of Israel , saying they speak for Chicago Jews . <p> Chicago Jews like us . You are sixth generation , born in the same Hyde Park hospital as your great-grandmother . This is the year of your bar mitzvah , which means this year , you take your place before the Torah and speak in our community with your own voice . <p> It is a hard time to become a Jewish @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have to , even when it is hard . You need to know that these groups do not speak for us or decide what it means to be Jewish . You need to know that being Jewish means standing for the good of the world . It does not mean standing for Israel . <p> Last month was Tu Bishvat , the birthday of the trees . At synagogue , we tasted fruit . I celebrated Tu Bishvat when I was a child . Your grandparents did too . That tradition is your inheritance . <p> The soldiers doing these things do them on behalf of a Jewish state . In my name and yours . That means what happens in Israel , though not your fault , is your problem . That is part of your inheritance too . <p> In the West Bank , Israeli settlers are building towns in places that do not belong to them . They have torn up 800,000 olive trees , from which Palestinian farmers made their living for centuries . Grandchildren picking fruit from the same trees their grandparents did , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , when I was your age , collecting tzedakah in blue boxes , sending coins to the Jewish National Fund to plant trees in Israel . This was a bar mitzvah project . We thought : a good deed . <p> But in Israel , even trees are warfare . That charity has planted Aleppo pines on disputed land for more than a century , growing forests that keep displaced Palestinians from places they once lived . <p> Destroy a tree to uproot a people . Plant a tree to claim their land . <p> I have never understood why the Torah tells us not to look at truth that hurts us . God commanded Lot to turn away from the destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah . In the Garden of Eden , God forbade Adam and Eve the tree of knowledge . <p> That ca n't be who we are . Some fruits are bitter . We ca n't pretend we do not taste them . <p> It is a gruesome lesson , that the government of a people you are part of can be so brutal . But @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ now is wrong . And it 's us , here in the diaspora , and not just soldiers there , who do this . It is our coins planting those trees . It is our support fueling Israel 's war . We can not look away . <p> You have to know how hard it is for some Jews to break with Israel . Our people have been through so much , and the idea of a Jewish state , safe and free from hatred , has seemed almost too good to be true . Because it is . We can not afford a haven for Jews in Israel if the cost is Palestinian land and life . <p> I do not support Hamas or its terrorist attack on Oct. 7 . I know antisemitism is real and that you will feel it in your life . You have already . I have too . The only person I ever hit , I hit because he mocked me for being Jewish . But as much as I can still feel that anger , decades later , I know hitting him @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ignorance any less . Just as I know bulldozing Gaza makes no Jew safer from those who hate us , whose power this war only grows . <p> I love my Jewishness . It is the most important thing about me . I love your Jewishness , too . Even as I write this , I can hear you upstairs practicing for your bar mitzvah : singing ancient words in ancient tune . Like I did , and our ancestors before me . <p> You did not make this world , but you receive it . That means you inherit work you did not ask for , to make right what others made wrong . We have to speak now , loudly , to make our leaders see , like the City Council resolution calls them to , that things are changing . That American Jews will not stand by Israel when Israel is wrong . <p> Even trees move , you know . Even the ones that have stood for generations . They open new leaves on new branches . They bend to find the light .
