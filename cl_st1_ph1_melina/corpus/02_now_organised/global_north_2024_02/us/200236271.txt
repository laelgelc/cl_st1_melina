As I listen to the opposing arguments , I 'm struck more often by what is n't being said than by what is . <p> Unfortunately , people who can not tell contrary truths usually wind up lying in the end . That is because most truths consist of contradiction . Minus the ability to say " on the one hand " and match it with " on the other , " our most strident positions amount to holding one hand behind our backs , fingers crossed as our noses grow longer .
