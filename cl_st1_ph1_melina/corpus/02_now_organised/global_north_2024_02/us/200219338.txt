<h> Jewish Identity with and Without Zionism <p> Although the prospect seems scarcely imaginable now , there was a time , not very long ago , when American Jews were free to have no particular thoughts or feelings about Israel . This was true not only of run-of-the-mill Jews but of intellectuals and writers as well . And it was n't merely that assimilation -- an act at once idealistic , pragmatic , and mortifying -- was more pressing to a Philip Roth or a Saul Bellow than one 's relationship , one way or another , to the nascent Jewish state . It 's that Israel , and Zionism , did n't seem like relevant objects of concern . This is no longer a tenable position .
