This includes hackers linked to Russia , China , North Korea , and Iran , who have been testing new ways to use these technologies against the U.S. , Israel , and European countries . <p> With half the world 's population set to vote in 2024 , the use of generative AI to target elections could be a " huge factor , " says Adam Meyers , head of counter-adversary operations at CrowdStrike .
