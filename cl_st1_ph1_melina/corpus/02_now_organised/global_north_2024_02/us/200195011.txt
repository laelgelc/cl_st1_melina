Once consensus is reached , the bill moves to the upper house , where the ruling party holds only seven seats . <p> Milei 's La Libertad Avanza party also holds a small number of seats in the 257-seat lower house , but was still able to garner enough support from like-minded allies , including the main center-right Juntos por el Cambio coalition of parties , to advance his agenda . <p> Earlier on Tuesday , the libertarian leader arrived in Israel as part of an overseas tour .
