<h> US to make ' additional strikes ' on Iran-backed targets : Jake Sullivan <p> As the United States and the United Kingdom launched a wave of strikes against the Houthis in Yemen this weekend , national security adviser Jake Sullivan said Sunday to expect additional strikes against Iran-backed targets . <p> Sullivan told Meet the Press that the U.S. intends " to take additional strikes " on Iran-backed targets . The U.S. and the U.K. launcheda third wave of airstrikes against the Iran-backed Houthi targets in Yemen on Feb. 3 . <p> When asked whether the U.S. has ruled out strikes in Iranian territory , Sullivan told Meet the Press on Sunday , " I 'm not going to get into what we 've ruled in and ruled out . " <p> At least 13 locations associated with the Houthis were targets of the latest strikes , with a focus on disabling their " missile systems and launchers , air defense systems , and radars , " according to Defense Secretary Lloyd Austin . <p> " This collective action sends a clear message to the Houthis that they will continue to bear further consequences if they do not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " Austin added in a Saturday evening statement . <p> The latest round of strikes comes as the Houthis have conducted dozens of attacks on commercial and military ships in the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden since November . <p> The Houthis , who control large portions of Yemen , began their spate of attacks last year in the weeks after Hamas militants invaded Israel on Oct. 7 . The Hamas attack killed more than 1,200 people and as a retaliation against Israel 's offensive in Gaza , which Hamas officials claim has resulted in more than 26,000 deaths , Israel Defense Forces say it has killed nearly 9,000 Hamas fighters , and that it has tried to limit civilian deaths . <p> In this image provided by the UK Ministry of Defence , RAF Typhoon FRG4s are prepared to conduct further strikes against Houthi military targets in Yemen , from RAF Akrotiri , Cyprus , Saturday , Feb. 3 , 2024 .
