Specifically Rafah , a 25-square-mile patch of land on Gaza 's border with Egypt that was , until recently , home to fewer than 200,000 people . Today , 1.4 million Palestinians are crammed together there , in search of safety from the Israeli bombs that have decimated the rest of Gaza . <p> They were told they would be safe there -- which they were also told about other locations in southern Gaza until the Israeli military forced them further south . Now they wait , trapped and starving , for an expected ground invasion . <p> I am an Arab Jew , and I have loved ones with friends and family in Israel and Palestine .
