<h> Aides to meet with local Arab American , Muslim leaders frustrated with the handling of the Israel-Hamas conflict <p> Top aides for President Joe Biden plan to meet with local Palestinian Americans while in Michigan on Thursday . <p> Dr. Yamaan Saadeh , a neurosurgeon at the University of Michigan , has been invited to meet with officials from the administration , where he plans to plead for his family to be evacuated from Gaza . <p> " There has to be a mechanism to allow families of Palestinian-Americans to leave Gaza , " said Saadeh . <p> Saadeh said his father , stepmom , and three sisters have been trapped in a war zone for months . They 're starving , homeless , and scared . <p> " While we 're grateful President Biden is sending senior aides , I think that 's the bare minimum , but it 's still not enough , " Saadeh said .
