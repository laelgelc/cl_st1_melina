<h> Gov . Kathy Hochul apologizes for remarks on Israel-Hamas war <p> NEW YORK -- Gov . Kathy Hochul apologized Saturday for remarks she made at a Jewish philanthropy event in New York City . <p> In a speech Thursday for the United Jewish Appeal-Federation of New York , the governor implied Israel has a right to destroy Gaza in response to the Oct. 7 attacks by Hamas . <p> She said , " If Canada someday ever attacked Buffalo , I 'm sorry , my friends ; there would be no Canada the next day . " <p> Hochul has since apologized , saying it was a poor choice of words . <p> She added , " While I have been clear in my support of Israel 's right to self-defense , I have also repeatedly said and continue to believe that Palestinian civilian casualties should be avoided and that more humanitarian aid must go to the people of Gaza . "
