It also includes $9 billion in humanitarian aid for Gaza , the West Bank and Ukraine , and $4.8 billion to deter Chinese aggression in the Indo-Pacific . <p> Cheney reiterated that Johnson should take action " to help the Ukrainian people . " <p> " And again , he 's gon na have to explain to future generations , to his kids , to his grandkids , whether or not he did what was right , whether or not he was a force for good and aided the cause of freedom , or whether he continued down this path of cowardice and doing what Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin want him to do , " she said .
