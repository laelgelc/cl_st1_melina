<p> DUBAI ( Reuters ) -The managing director of the International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) , Kristalina Georgieva , said on Sunday she was confident about the economic outlook despite uncertainties around war and geopolitics as the global economy has remained resilient . <p> In a speech at the World Governments Summit in Dubai , Georgieva said the IMF would publish a paper on Monday that shows phasing out explicit energy subsidies could save $336 billion in the Middle East , equivalent to the economies of Iraq and Libya combined . <p> She added that in addition to savings , eliminating regressive energy subsidies " discourages pollution , and helps improve social spending , " in a copy of the speech published on the IMF website . <p> In its latest regional economic update published last month , the IMF revised its GDP growth forecast for the Middle East and North Africa region downwards to 2.9% this year , due in part to short term oil production cuts . <p> " While uncertainties are still high , we can be a bit more confident about the economic outlook , because the global economy has been surprisingly resilient , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the conflict in Gaza , Georgieva warned of widening consequences . <p> " This exceptionally uncertain moment compounds the challenges of economies that are still recovering from previous shocks .
