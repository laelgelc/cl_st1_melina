<p> The Oscars are voted on by ( harrumph ) members of the Academy . In other words , people working in Hollywood . That means they 're ill equipped to judge the films they make , which are mostly not about Hollywood or films . They 're about life out in the world . <p> It 's people like film audiences who are equipped to judge the films . Why ? Because they have more direct experience of the subject matter . Having the skills to make films is no sign you have a platform from which to evaluate them . <p> So nominee Ryan Gosling , one of Hollywood 's Canadians , may 've pushed the envelope by saying Barbie 's star and director " pushed the envelope and made history , " and deserved nominations . Personally , I found the earnest messaging in Barbie , like the " iconic " speech by ( nominee ) America Ferrera , tedious . <p> On the other hand , the depictions of superficiality were eloquent , as was Barbie 's existential dismay at her sudden awareness of death and emptiness .
