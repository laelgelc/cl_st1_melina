According to Equifax Canada , as of the third quarter of 2023 we owe $113.4 billion in credit card debt alone . <p> But beneath their convenient and flexible surface , these BNPL programs harbour several pitfalls that can trap unwary consumers in a web of financial complications . <h> Buy now , pay later : the basics <p> These programs are fairly straightforward . They allow consumers to split the cost of a purchase into smaller payments -- typically four bi-weekly payments . Some programs also provide opportunities to finance purchases with monthly payments ( although these repayment plans typically impose a fair amount of interest ) @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ purchase a $200 pair of shoes , you would pay the first $50 payment up front , and then another $50 would be taken out every two weeks until the remaining $150 balance was settled . <p> Some of the biggest BNPL companies in Canada now are Klarna , PayBright , Sezzle , and Afterpay , just to name a few . <h> The good <p> Similar to credit cards , BNPL programs often attract criticism . However , also similar to credit cards , the problem does n't necessarily lie with the program , but with irresponsible use . Credit cards can be excellent tools when used responsibly , but can leave you buried in debt if used irresponsibly . <p> Before jumping into some of the pitfalls of BNPL programs , it 's only fair to mention the upside of them . <p> Namely , BNPL allows you to finance an otherwise hard-to-afford purchase and split it into manageable chunks . When using the pay-in-four repayment plan , you wo n't pay any interest either ; just a small processing fee , which is usually less than $2 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ credit cards , which charge monthly interest fees on any balance carried over into the next billing cycle . <p> This can really come in handy for dealing with emergency purchases , such as buying a new laptop for work/school or replacing a broken tool that you need for your small business . <h> The bad <p> Now , let 's dive into some of the not-so-great aspects of BNPL . <p> They can create a debt spiral <p> While BNPL can certainly be helpful when used to purchase necessities , many users take advantage of programs like Klarna and PayBright to spend outside of their means on more frivolous purchases . <p> BNPL programs ingeniously remove the immediate financial barrier to purchases , which can lure consumers into spending more than they can afford . <p> This illusion of affordability can lead to impulsive buying decisions , with the cumulative effect of multiple BNPL commitments fostering a habit of overconsumption . <p> This can lead you into a deceptive comfort zone , where the ease of accumulating debt under multiple BNPL companies becomes a downhill slope . <p> Each @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ leading to a scenario where the combined monthly payments exceed the consumer 's ability to pay . <p> For this reason , many BNPL programs impose maximum borrowing amounts . First-time users typically are n't given a big allowance , maybe only $200 to $300 at most . Then , as the user develops a good payment history and uses it more often , their maximum borrowing amount will increase , similar to the way a credit card line may increase . <p> Hidden risks and costs <p> While BNPL programs often advertise zero interest as their selling point , missed payments can trigger hefty fees and , in some cases , high-interest rates that escalate quickly . <p> Unlike traditional credit models , where terms are subject to strict regulations , BNPL agreements can catch consumers off guard with their penalty structures . This can turn an attractive short-term convenience into a long-term financial burden . <p> Consumer protection concerns <p> The BNPL sector operates in a relatively lax regulatory environment compared to traditional lending and credit services . This lack of oversight can leave consumers vulnerable to terms @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Consumers often find themselves caught up in repayment plans that they do n't fully understand , misled by the simplicity of the " pay later " promise . <p> The real terms , including penalties for missed payments and the specifics of interest accrual , can be obscured by marketing , leading to some unpleasant surprises down the line for some users . <h> How does BNPL affect your credit ? <p> Most BNPL programs do n't perform a hard credit pull . Initially , the BNPL company may perform a " soft " credit check , just to make sure that you do n't have a bad payment history . However , soft credit checks do n't directly affect your credit score . <p> That being said , BNPL can affect your credit . <p> If you repeatedly miss your scheduled payments or become delinquent on an amount you owe and it goes to a collection agency , the BNPL program may give you a negative mark on your credit report that can hurt your score for months or years to come . <p> Another downside of BNPL programs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ either . In this area , personal loans have a distinct advantage . <h> What 's the best alternative to BNPL ? <p> The best alternative to BNPL is to use a layaway program . Many stores offer to hold an item for their customers while they slowly chip away at the payments . This way , by the time you get the item , it 's already paid for . <p> I also suggest building an emergency savings fund , which will help you to be able to cover the cost of unexpected items , so you do n't have to worry about financing them with a credit card or BNPL program . <p> Tired of living cheque to cheque ?
