" Because all four great ape species used playful teasing , it is likely that the prerequisites for humor evolved in the human lineage at least 13 million years ago . " <p> The team had theorized that because human babies can tease others even before they can speak , as early as eight months old , similar behaviour could be exhibited by non-human animals . <p>
