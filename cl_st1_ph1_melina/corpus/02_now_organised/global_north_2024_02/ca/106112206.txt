( Submitted photo ) <p> On Jan. 30 , a striking piece of pop-up theatre was staged near Vancouver 's art gallery . A man dressed as a Hamas militant marched a woman in a white top and grey sweatpants down the street -- her hands tied together , her crotch blood-stained . Behind them was a placard : " This is what free Palestine looks like . " <p> It was a re-enactment of one of the most infamous videos shot on Oct. 7 , in which Hamas terrorists kidnapped an Israeli teenager and stuffed her into a black Jeep .
