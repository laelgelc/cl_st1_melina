<h> Hezbollah leader vows to continue attacks on Israel until Gaza war ends <p> Beirut , Feb 14 ( IANS ) Hezbollah leader Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah has said that the Lebanese armed group will not stop its attacks in border regions with Israel until the latter 's war on Gaza ends . <p> Nasrallah in a speech to commemorate Hezbollah fighters broadcasted by local TV channel al-Manar on Tuesday said that the group will " not stop the border offensive before the end of the Zionist war on Gaza " , Xinhua news agency reported . <p> Nasrallah noted that the group 's military front in southern Lebanon is aimed at weakening the Israeli enemy until it is convinced that it must stop its aggression in Gaza . <p> " This front will only stop when the aggression against Gaza stops within an agreement with the Palestinian resistance , " he said . <p> Nasrallah added that Hezbollah also has a national duty to protect Lebanon from " Israeli aggression , " and would continue to fight back if Israel 's attacks go on . <p> The Lebanon-Israel border has been witnessing increased tension since October 8 , 2023 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ toward Israel in support of the Hamas attack on Israel the previous day , prompting Israel to respond by firing heavy artillery toward southeastern Lebanon . <p> The confrontations between Hezbollah and Israel have killed 271 people on the Lebanese side , including 190 Hezbollah members and 44 civilians , according to Lebanese security sources .
