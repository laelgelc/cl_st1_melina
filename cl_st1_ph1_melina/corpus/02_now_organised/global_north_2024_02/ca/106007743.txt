( File Photo : G7/Facebook ) <p> ROME -- The G7 is calling for a suspension of fighting in Gaza to ensure both the release of Israeli hostages taken by Hamas and the provision of aid to Palestinian civilians , Foreign Minister and Deputy Premier Antonio Tajani said on Saturday . <p> In the Middle East , the G7 " aims to guarantee the security of the state of Israel , " said Tajani in a press conference at the Munich Security Conference . <p> " We are calling for the immediate release of hostages , we are working on de-escalation and we are calling for a suspension of fighting both to guarantee the release of hostages and to get aid to civilians who are victims of Hamas , " he added . <p> Separately , on the sidelines of the conference , Tajani said the " carnage " of Israelis and Palestinians must @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of two peoples and two states , we say this very clearly to everyone , " he said . <p> " It is obvious that some do not want this to be achieved , but we want peace and an end to this carnage that has led to the deaths of thousands of people , Israeli and Palestinian civilians . This carnage must end , " added Tajani . ( ANSA )
