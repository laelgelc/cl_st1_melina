Mr X eventually abandoned his car and was tracked to a house in Stratford . <p> Soon after Police arrived at the house , an officer fired three shots at Mr X after he was seen to point a shotgun out of a window towards Police . All three shots missed . Police surrounded the house , and Mr X surrendered 14 hours later . <p> The Authority found that the officer was justified under section 48 of the Crimes Act 1961 in deciding to shoot at the armed man in defence of himself and others around him . <p> The Authority also reviewed the planning of the operation to locate Mr X and the management of the subsequent siege .
