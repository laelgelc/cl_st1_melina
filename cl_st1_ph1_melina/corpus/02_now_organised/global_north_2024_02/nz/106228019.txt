<h> Running Hamilton Costs $5.60 A Day <p> Hamilton City Council is breaking down what it costs to run and maintain the city as it prepares to open the pages of its draft 2024-2034 Long-Term Plan . <p> It costs $5.60 per resident , per day to run the city . This covers everyday operating costs , like making sure rubbish and recycling is collected , maintaining roads and footpaths , and keeping taps running and toilets flushing . <p> The dollar figure is based on operating costs from the 2022-23 annual report and Hamilton 's estimated population ( 185,300 people in 2023 ) . <p> Diving deeper , Council has shown what Hamiltonians get for that figure in a year -- 22 billion litres of clean drinking water , 10 million m2 of green space maintained , and nearly 30 million kg of rubbish and recycling collected to name a few services . <p> A breakdown of the everyday costs of running Hamilton is available to read on Council 's website . <p> The cost to run a city is rising , and the challenge Hamilton faces is not unique .
