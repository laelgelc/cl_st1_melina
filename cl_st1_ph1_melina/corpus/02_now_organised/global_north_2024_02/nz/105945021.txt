<h> NGOs Raise NZ 's Human Rights Performance At UN <p> The New Zealand government 's performance was discussed at a briefing meeting last night for UN-based diplomats in Geneva , in advance of the fourth Universal Periodic Review ( UPR ) of New Zealand 's human rights record which will take place in April and May this year . <p> A combined NGO statement outlining some of the crucial issues around the constitutional and legislative framework , economic and social rights , human rights and climate change , the right to health , Indigenous peoples ' rights , disability rights , mass detention of migrants and asylum seekers , family violence and migrant women here in Aotearoa , along with New Zealand 's lack of acceptance of international human rights norms and its recent voluntary pledge was presented at the meeting .
