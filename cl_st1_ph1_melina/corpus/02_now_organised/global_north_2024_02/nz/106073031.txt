It amends the Social Workers Registration Legislation Act to delay the repeal of the experience pathway to social worker registration by two years . <p> It means that after 28 February 2026 all social workers will need to have a professional qualification to apply for registration . Currently , those without qualifications can apply for registration based on significant practical experience . <p> " Social workers play an important role in our communities .
