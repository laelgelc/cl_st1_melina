<h> Fr David Dowling Appointed Rector At Holy Cross Seminary Auckland <p> Father David Dowling has been appointed the new rector of Holy Cross Seminary in Auckland . <p> The appointment has been confirmed by the Vatican 's Dicastery for Evangelisation . Fr David is at present the Human Formator at Holy Cross . He will take over the role in April from the present Interim Rector , Bishop Emeritus Colin Campbell . <p> Holy Cross Seminary is the national seminary for the education and formation of diocesan priests for the Catholic Church in New Zealand . <p> Fr David is a priest of the Archdiocese of Wellington whose priestly journey began when he entered Holy Cross in 2001 after an 18-year career as a Customs officer . A born Wellingtonian and proud supporter of the Hurricanes , he attended St Patrick 's College , Wellington before his Customs career . He studied for the priesthood at Holy Cross from 2001 to 2007 and was ordained in Wellington on 1 September 2007 by Cardinal John Dew .
