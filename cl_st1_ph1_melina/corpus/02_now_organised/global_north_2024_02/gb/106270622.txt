<p> LONDON - Oil prices pulled back on Wednesday as the prospect of delays to U.S. interest rate cuts and a rise in U.S. crude stocks offset a boost from a potential extension to OPEC+ supply curbs . <p> Brent crude futures fell 90 cents , or 1.08% , to $82.75 a barrel by 0902 GMT . U.S. West Texas Intermediate futures ( WTI ) were down 92 cents , or 1.17% , at $77.95 . <p> Vandana Hari , founder of oil market analysis provider Vanda Insights , attributed the price falls to profit-taking plus a combined response to a surge in U.S. crude stocks and continuing hopes of Gaza ceasefire deal in the coming days . <p> Federal Reserve Governor Michelle Bowman had signalled on Tuesday that she was in no rush to cut U.S. interest rates , particularly given continuing inflation risks .
