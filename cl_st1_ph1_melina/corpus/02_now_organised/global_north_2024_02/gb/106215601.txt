<p> The number of MPs demanding Speaker Sir Lindsay Hoyle quits has risen to 77 . <p> Sir Lindsay is under pressure after being accused of helping Keir Starmer dodge a revolt on Wednesday last week . He sparked fury when he allowed a Labour amendment on an SNP motion calling for a ceasefire in Gaza to be heard . <p> Over the weekend a further six MPs have added their names to those calling for him to go . A motion of no confidence was put forward by Tory Sir William Wragg last week . <p> Sir Lindsay was in the Speaker 's chair at the start of the week 's proceedings this afternoon , but did not address the controversy .
