<p> The Gaza Strip 's second-largest hospital has been put " completely out of service " , a spokesperson for the Gaza health ministry said on Sunday . <p> " There are only four medical staffers currently caring for patients " inside the Nasser Hospital in the southern city of Khan Younis , Ashraf al-Qidra told Reuters . <p> " The Nasser medical complex is the backbone of healthcare in southern Gaza Strip . Its ceasing to function is a death sentence for hundreds of thousands of Palestinian displaced people in Khan Younis and Rafah , " he said . <p> Qidra said a lack of fuel and fighting around the facility had put it out of action . <p> Nasser was Gaza 's largest functioning hospital until Sunday and had been under siege this week in Israel 's war with Palestinian militant group Hamas .
