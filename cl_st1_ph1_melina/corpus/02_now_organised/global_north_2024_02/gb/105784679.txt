<p> Piers Morgan has quit TV to become star on YouTube . The Mirror reports he is leaving his TalkTV presenting job to take his show online instead . <p> The former Good Morning Britain presenter said he no longer wants to " to create my show for linear television " having grown frustrated with creating a show in a TV-format when the majority of his viewers watch via different methods . <p> Announcing the news on X , Morgan said : " Some personal news fire emoji " as he shared a link to a journalist reporting the news . The headline from Ben Smith reads : " News : Piers Morgan is ending his live broadcast show on UK 's TalkTV , and going full YouTube . He talked to me about it . " <p> Morgan , who claims he co-owns Piers Morgan Uncensored with News Corp , told Smith his recent debates regarding Israel 's bombardment of Gaza on his TV shows felt rushed on his hour-long TV show . " I find myself thinking , ' I wish we were n't just doing a TV show here , because if we were n't I @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the YouTube crowd love , " Morgan admitted . <p> The move to YouTube will mean Morgan is no longer regulated by Ofcom , but the divisive broadcaster has insisted that was n't what swayed his decision to go fully digital .
