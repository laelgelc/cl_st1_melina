<p> Copenhagen International Documentary Film Festival ( CPH:DOX , March 13-24 ) has unveiled the full programme for its 2024 edition , including seven films about the ongoing Israel-Palestine conflict . <p> The titles include the world premiere of Tal Barda 's I Shall Not Hate in the new Human Rights Competition , in which Nobel Prize-nominated Palestinian author and doctor Izzeldin Abuelaish continues to work for peace despite the loss of his three daughters in an Israeli attack on Gaza . <p> Also spotlighting the conflict are world premieres of Larissa Sansour and Soren Lind 's UK-Palestine co-production Familiar Phantoms and Jonathan Schaller and Philipp Schaeffer 's Silent Night , both in the New:Vision strand . <p> The Gaza war is part of one of the themes at this year 's festival , with a festival focus under the headline ' Conflicted ' .
