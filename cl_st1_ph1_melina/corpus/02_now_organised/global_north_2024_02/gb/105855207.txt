<p> Israel should stop and think seriously before taking any further action in Rafah , British Foreign Secretary David Cameron said on Monday , after airstrikes in the southern Gaza city that is the last refuge of about a million displaced civilians . <p> Local health officials said 67 Palestinians were killed and dozens wounded in the airstrikes , under the cover of which Israel freed two hostages . <p> Asked about the situation in Rafah and whether Israel had gone beyond international law , Cameron told reporters : " We think it is impossible to see how you can fight a war amongst these people . There 's nowhere for them to go . " <p> " We are very concerned about the situation and we want Israel to stop and think very seriously before it takes any further action . But above all , what we want is an immediate pause in the fighting and we want that pause to lead to a ceasefire .
