<p> An angry protester was forcibly removed from the Oldham council chambers by security after interrupting a full council meeting . The protester threatened councillors would see a " genocide at the ballot box " in reference to the conflict in Gaza . <p> Councillors were gathered to discuss the budget for 2024/25 , which sets the local authority 's spending priorities and cuts for the coming financial year , including setting the council tax and social housing rent . <p> The protester , who was in the public gallery , shouted : " We 've listened to a lot of talk about housing and social care and taxes but what we have n't heard is a stand against the genocide happening in Palestine . <p> " If you do n't want to recognise the genocide - we 'll show you a genocide in the ballot box . " <p> The meeting was paused as the gentleman was approached by two security guards who attempted to persuade the man to leave .
