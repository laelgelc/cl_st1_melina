<p> Activists have locked themselves to the entrance gates of a factory in Staffordshire in a protest over the conflict in Palestine . The group want to shut down the factory which makes drone engines . <p> It is the latest in a long list of protests at the UAV Engines site in Shenstone , near Lichfield . Activists have been staging protests in the area for more than three years - but they have gained even more traction since violence broke out in Gaza last year . <p> The factory is owned by Israeli-based defence contractor Elbit Systems . It makes repurposed Norton motorbike engines used in surveillance drones around the world . <p> And Palestine Action , which leads the protests , claims UAV Engines exports its products to Israel for use by its military . A spokesperson for the group said : " For the last 140 days , Elbit 's drones , munitions , arms and military technologies have been used to slaughter Palestinians in their thousands - but the company has a far longer history of facilitating atrocities . <p> " It is abhorrent that they are still allowed to operate in Britain . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to halt the corrupt , bloodthirsty industry fuelling death from Britain 's towns . " <p> One local woman in the area previously spoke to BirminghamLive about the impact the protests have had on the community . She said : " The worst was when they were living in the trees , opposite the factory . My daughter goes to the local primary school and she sees them dressed in suits . <p> " When they are doing ridiculous things , it 's not things I want my children to see .
