<h> Mati Diop 's ' Dahomey ' wins Golden Bear at 2024 Berlin film festival <p> Mati Diop 's documentary Dahomey , about artefacts being returned from Paris to present-day Benin , was awarded the Golden Bear for best film at the Berlin International Film Festival tonight ( February 24 ) . <p> The film , handled internationally by Les Film du Losange , is the second from the African continent to take the Berlinale 's top prize after Mark Dornford-May 's musical U-Carmen eKhayelitsha in 2005 .
