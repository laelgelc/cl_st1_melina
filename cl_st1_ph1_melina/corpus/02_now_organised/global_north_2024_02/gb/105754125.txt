<h> Jordan 's Abdullah to meet Biden and western leaders to lobby for end of Gaza war <p> Jordan 's King Abdullah began on Thursday a tour of major western capitals that will take him to the United States to meet President Joe Biden to lobby for an end to the war in Gaza , the royal palace said . <p> The monarch , who first visits France and Germany , would also hold talks in the United States with top Congressional leaders , politicians and administration officials . The statement did not give a date for the meeting with Biden .
