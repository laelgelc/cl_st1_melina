<h> The Rochdale by-election has turned into a circus . Voters deserve so much better <p> Rochdale residents have reluctantly played the role of extras in a Westminster psychodrama , but they just want an MP who will stand up for them . Political reporter Joseph Timan reflects on a bizarre and divisive election campaign <p> Azhar Ali , Simon Danczuk and George Galloway are among the Rochdale by-election candidates ( Image : Getty/PA/M.E.N . ) <p> Lots of people have lost faith in politics . Looking at the Rochdale by-election it 's not hard to see why . <p> Here we have , in sharp relief , much of what is wrong with British politics . Opportunism , divisive and racist rhetoric and a political culture detached from the every day concerns of ordinary people . <p> And for those who think Westminster 's macho culture is a thing of the past , look no further than this ballot paper . There are 11 men on it , but not a single woman . The Rochdale by-election is a microcosm of much that 's wrong in our wider politics . <p> The town is the scene of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ role of extras . National media have descended upon the streets over the last few weeks as voters decide who will replace the late Sir Tony Lloyd as their MP . <p> The widely-respected principled politician would be turning in his grave if he knew what the contest had become . Meanwhile his party has snatched defeat from the jaws of victory by ditching their candidate when it was already too late . Even if he wins , they lose . <p> In common with by-elections elsewhere , this contest had already attracted opportunists capitalising on anger and division as a route for a return to Parliament . <p> Ex-MP and Celebrity Big Brother contestant George Galloway has put the war in Gaza at the centre of his campaign , promising voters he will ' teach Labour a lesson ' , while Rochdale 's former MP Simon Danczuk is staging a political comeback of his own , less than a decade after he was caught sending lewd messages to a 17-year-old girl , suspended from the Labour Party and then beaten at the ballot box . <p> Journalists travelled to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in what became the biggest story in Westminster . Hacks will return to Rochdale on Thursday , expecting an election upset - but by Saturday morning , the town will be forgotten again . <p> Rochdale was once one of Britain 's wealthiest towns . But despite its rich industrial heritage and political history , for decades , this proud town and the problems its people face every day have all too often been ignored by the rest of the country - until it 's too late . <p> In some parts of Rochdale , more than half of children are living in poverty . Sir Tony , whose sudden death from leukaemia last month triggered the by-election , called the stark situation in the long-suffering constituency he represented for nearly seven years a ' scandal ' . <p> Another scandal reared its ugly head in Rochdale again recently . A damning report into grooming in the town published last month exposed ' lamentable ' failures by Greater Manchester Police and Rochdale council which left children ' at the mercy of their abusers ' . <p> Workers Party of Britain @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the by-election ( Image : M.E.N/PA/Getty ) <p> Former Manchester councillor Iain Donaldson , who now lives in Rochdale , is standing for the Lib Dems . Alongside the Official Monster Raving Loony Party , four independent candidates have entered the race in which the result is impossible to predict . <p> Some voters may still choose to vote for Azhar Ali , who will appear on the ballot papers next to Labour 's logo . However , without the hundreds of volunteers who would usually knock on Labour voters ' doors before the vote , his chances of winning are much slimmer . <p> The former MP Sir Tony Lloyd died last month ( Image : ABNM Photography ) <p> Mr Ali , who apologised for his ' false ' claims about Israel , is not the only candidate to face problems . Green Party candidate Guy Otten , decided to ' leave the stage ' a week earlier after ' regrettable ' social media posts he made ' a number of years ago ' came to light . <p> The national interest in who Rochdale residents elect to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ they face will not disappear . Whoever wins the by-election on Thursday may have as little as two months to make their mark before voters go to the polls again . <p> Convincing constituents to come out and vote is always a tall order at a by-election in February . But following the ' farce ' that this by-election has become , it 's an even bigger ask . <p> The winner must try and bring Rochdale 's diverse communities together to tackle the issues that face them all . As their predecessor put it when asked for his advice to aspiring politicians : " If it 's not about making people 's lives better , then do n't be a politician . " <p> That advice was true then , and it remains true . The people of Rochdale have a right to ask for at least that level of commitment from their politicians .
