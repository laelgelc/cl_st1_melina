<h> Israel 's Herzog says he had a ' good discussion ' with Qatari PM <p> Israeli President Isaac Herzog said on Saturday he met with the prime minister of Qatar , who has been trying to mediate a ceasefire in the Gaza war . <p> " I met with the Prime Minister of Qatar yesterday , " Herzog said at the Munich Security Conference , according to a statement from his office . " It was a good discussion . And I think he 's doing major efforts . He 's taken major efforts and devoting an enormous focus on this issue .
