Rather , it is the final proof that Britain 's institutions are in permanent decline . <p> Nothing works in Britain . <p> The nation is rotting from the top down and the ruling elites seem incapable of offering any solution . Instead , they cavil at anyone who wants to exit the carnival , particularly the uppity Scots . <p> Start with the British Parliament , that house of self-delusion . When I entered its precincts as an SNP MP in 2015 , despite a lifetime in politics , I was surprised by how ineffectual and obsolete it was .
