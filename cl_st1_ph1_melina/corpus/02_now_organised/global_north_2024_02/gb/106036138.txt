<h> Gold advances on weaker dollar and Middle East conflict <p> Gold prices hit their highest in almost a week on Monday as the U.S. dollar retreated slightly and conflict in the Middle East buoyed bullion 's safe-haven appeal . <p> Spot gold was up 0.2% at $2,016.45 an ounce by 1247 GMT at its highest since Feb. 13 . <p> U.S. gold futures rose 0.2% to $2,028.20 . <p> " Gold is taking benefits from the slide in the U.S. dollar and also the new escalation of tensions in the Middle East , " said Carlo Alberto De Casa , market analyst at Kinesis Money . <p> Israel expects to continue full-scale military operations in Gaza for another 6-8 weeks as it prepares to mount a ground invasion of the enclave 's southernmost city , Rafah , four officials familiar with the strategy said . <p> A UK-registered cargo ship reported coming under attack in the Bab al-Mandab Strait off Yemen on Sunday and the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency reported crew abandoning ship off Yemen after an explosion . <p> The dollar index was down about 0.1% .
