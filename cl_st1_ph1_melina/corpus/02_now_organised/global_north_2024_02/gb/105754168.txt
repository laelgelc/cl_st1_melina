<h> Inflation fell across GCC in 2023 , but Gaza conflict , Red Sea crisis could drive it up -- Kamco Invest <p> Consumer prices have broadly eased in most Gulf countries , with overall inflation in the region averaging 2.6% in 2023 , down from 3.3% a year prior , according to estimates by Kamco Invest . <p> Inflation rates in all countries across the GCC region declined in 2023 , with Bahrain posting the lowest at 0.1% , down from 3.6% in 2022 , followed by Oman with an inflation rate of 0.6% , down from 2.8% , said the report . <p> In Saudi Arabia , inflation rate was pegged at 2.3% in 2023 , compared to 2.5% in 2022 . Qatar 's inflation dropped to 2.9% from 5% , while in the UAE , inflation also fell to 3.3% from 4.8% . <p> Kamco Invest also noted that among the cities in GCC countries , only Dubai reported an increase in its average annual inflation rate , which edged up from 2.9% in 2022 to 3.3% in 2023 . <p> Image courtesy : Kamco <p> Overall , consumer prices in the GCC region trended lower @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ where high prices persist . <p> GCC 's inflation is lower than the global headline inflation , which the OECD estimated to decline to 7% in 2023 . <p> However , the region could face some challenges , especially since the Red Sea crisis and the Gaza conflict continue . <p> " There are new geopolitical challenges facing the global economy , especially the MENA economies , such as the ongoing war in Gaza and the disruption of global shipping lines in the Red Sea , " the report said . <p> Research firm BMI had said that the Red Sea crisis could drive up consumer prices in the Middle East , as most of the region 's imported goods ( 81.6% ) are transported using the routes that are exposed to the disruption . <p> Disclaimer : This article is provided for informational purposes only .
