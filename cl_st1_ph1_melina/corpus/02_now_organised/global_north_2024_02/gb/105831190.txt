<h> Saudi Arabia warns of severe consequences for assault on Rafah in Gaza <p> RIYADH -- Saudi Arabia has warned about the grave consequences of the assault and targeting of Rafah in the Gaza Strip , which has become the last refuge for hundreds of thousands of civilians displaced by the brutal Israeli aggression . The Kingdom firmly rejected and strongly condemned the forced displacement of these individuals . <p> Saudi Arabia has reiterated its call for an immediate ceasefire , stating that this persistent violation of international law and international humanitarian law underscores the urgent need for the United Nations Security Council to convene promptly .
