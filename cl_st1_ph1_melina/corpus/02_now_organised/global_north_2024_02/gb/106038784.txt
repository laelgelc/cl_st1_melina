<h> How the Berlinale is trying to navigate and engage with the Israel-Hamas war <p> One of the ways in which the Berlinale is addressing the large , complex issue of the Israel-Hamas war in Gaza is by channelling it into a small , safe space called The Tiny House . <p> The Tiny House project is a cabin-like structure , set up near the festival 's hub in Potsdamer Platz for three days from February 17-19 . Festival attendees and interested members of the public can come in and discuss their feelings around the issue , with the support of two moderators , Shai Hoffmann and Ahmad Dakhnous . <p> Hoffman , a German Jew with Israeli roots , is the architect of the project . With his German-Palestinian colleague Jouanna Hassoun , he was already working with children in schools to help create spaces for young people to discuss the Middle East conflict .
