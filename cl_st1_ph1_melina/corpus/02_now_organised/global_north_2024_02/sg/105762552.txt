After this , we need to create a road map and adhere to it . Our proposal could be approved or not . The process is still lengthy , " he said during a press briefing on Monday . <p> Nikkei reported on Sunday that Israel had expressed objection to Indonesia 's accession process to the OECD during the OECD ambassadors meeting on Jan. 29 and 30 , citing anonymous diplomatic sources . <p> Israel is not necessarily opposed to Indonesia 's membership but mentioned the situation in the Middle East and Indonesia 's criticism of the country 's military actions in Gaza .
