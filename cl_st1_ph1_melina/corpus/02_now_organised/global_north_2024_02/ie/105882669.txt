<h> Rosita Sweetman : Irish basketball players were right to show disdain for Israel <p> The Irish women 's basketball team went ahead and played an Israeli team on Thursday in Latvia , despite the squad being five players short -- players who had dropped out in protest at Israel 's genocide in Gaza -- and in the teeth of some rather blatant propagandising by the Israeli side . <p> Stung by the suggestion they were less than absolutely perfect in every way , they accused the Irish women of being antisemitic and " hating us " , posting photos on their official website of the squad posing with fully kitted-up IDF soldiers , an empty court with kit on the ground complete with a machine gun on top and a huge banner reading " Stand with Israel " .
