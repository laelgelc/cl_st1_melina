<h> Eilis O'Hanlon : Even Israel 's supporters believe it is reckless , but is it really committing genocide in Gaza ? <p> Pro-Palestinian activists and supporters pose before a National March for Palestine in London last month . Photo : AFP <p> Eilis O'Hanlon <p> Yesterday at 03:30 <p> Does it matter if Israel is committing genocide in Gaza ? To clarify : it matters greatly if Israel 's actions since it came under attack from Hamas on October 7 comply with international law prohibiting collective punishment or the deliberate targeting of civilians and humanitarian infrastructure . <p> By that reckoning , there is a strong case to be made that Israel has been reckless when it comes to upholding its obligations to the innocent . That is why many of the country 's firmest supporters are profoundly disquieted by what it is doing in Gaza .
