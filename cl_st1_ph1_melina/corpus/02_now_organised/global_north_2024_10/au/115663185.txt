<h> Punishing people because of their culture or background should be behind us . Yet it 's a mistake Australia keeps making <p> " Lest We Forget " is probably the most revered , reverberant phrase in the history of Australia 's Federation . <p> Lifted from a totally unrelated Rudyard Kipling poem , these three words enjoin Australians to keep vigil over our past , to honour the shedding of our own blood , to respect the sacrifices of those who died abroad resisting tyranny . To remember always , in our prosperity , what past generations learned through bloodshed and strife . <p> " Lest We Forget " was adopted after the end of World War I , to honour the Anzacs . <p> It 's a pretty good rule to live by , more generally . <p> And yet , there are some mistakes we seem doomed endlessly to repeat . <p> Australia is a nation of immigrants , of refugees .
