<h> Special envoy to combat Islamophobia announced <p> Mandarin Aftab Malik has been named by the federal government as a special envoy to combat Islamophobia , tasked with assisting with the ongoing social impacts in Australia of the Hamas-Israel conflict . <p> Malik , who is to advise on the most effective ways to combat Islamophobia , will begin his three-year term on October 14 . <p> Malik is a long-time public servant within the NSW premier 's department , working on projects to promote social cohesion and counter hate and extremism . He is also recognised by the UN Alliance of Civilisations as a global expert on Muslim affairs . <p> In his new role , the special envoy will listen to members of the Muslim community , religious discrimination experts and all levels of government as part of the government 's efforts to make Australians feel safe and included . <p> Jillian Segal was named the special envoy to combat antisemitism earlier this year . <p> In a joint statement on Monday , Prime Minister Anthony Albanese and Minister for Home Affairs , Immigration and Multiculturalism Tony Burke said the special envoy roles would bolster a $90 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ deadly situation in Gaza . <p> This follows more than a year of escalating deadly conflict in the region after the terrorist group Hamas killed nearly 1,200 Israelis and captured 251 hostages in a series of coordinated attacks last October . <p> An estimated 41,000 Palestinian civilians are believed to have died from the state violence . <p> " Promoting social cohesion and fighting against hate in Australia is more important today than it 's ever been , " the PM said . <p> " I look forward to working with Muslim leaders and the broader community to promote peace , understanding and inclusivity .
