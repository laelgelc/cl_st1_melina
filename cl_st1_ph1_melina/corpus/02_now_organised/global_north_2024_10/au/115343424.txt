<p> It was 2018 , and Dan Tehan , then federal education minister , was concerned about freedom of speech on university campuses . <p> The problem ? Left-wing student protesters were attempting boycotts of certain speakers they opposed , like Bettina Arndt . At the University of Sydney , riot police broke up a protest against an Arndt speech . <p> Coalition politicians talked of a " free speech crisis " on Australian campuses . Tehan claimed freedom of speech had to be protected at universities , " even where what is being said may be unpopular or challenging " . He announced a review of freedom of speech on Australian campuses , to be led by former High Court judge Robert French . <p> After French duly delivered his report , the Morrison government forced all the public universities to update their policies to be in line with the model he put forward . Nearly all of them adopted strengthened codes protecting free speech and academic freedom . <p> Fast-forward to 2024 , and the Coalition is a lot less concerned about free speech on campus .
