<h> Lulu Retail Kicks Off One Of UAE 's Biggest IPOs This Year <p> Lulu Retail Holdings , which runs one of the Middle East 's biggest hypermarket chains , kicked off an initial public offering on that bankers say could be the UAE 's largest this year . <p> The offering by the conglomerate that runs more than 240 stores in the six countries belonging to the Gulf Co-operation Council comes during a retail spending boom in the region that is spurring domestic listings by companies in the sector . <p> The listing , set to run from 28 October to 5 November , will offer more than 2.582 billion shares , which are expected to start trading on the Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange on 14 November , Lulu 's IPO document showed . <p> Two sources involved in the transaction said the offering could raise between $1.7 billion-$1.8 billion for the 25% stake .
