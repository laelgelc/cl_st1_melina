<p> President Michael D Higgins has said it is " time to stop this horror of history " after Israeli legislators passed laws that could threaten the work of the main UN agency providing aid in Gaza . <p> He called on all EU and UN member states to make " clear " their support for UNRWA and repeated his calls for a ceasefire and for the hostages to be released . <p> The Government has raised strong concerns about what Israel 's efforts to sever ties will mean for the already limited flow of aid to civilians in the Palestinian enclave . <p> " 100,000 people in northern Gaza , the majority women and children , are effectively trapped with no safe place to go to . Two of their three hospitals are destroyed and one is under siege , " Mr Higgins said in a statement . <p> " Older people are dragged from their hospital beds and the extraordinarily brave medical staff who have stayed with the most vulnerable are paying with their lives and the threat of arrest . " <p> He added : " Silence from those who have had the best of aspirations @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ catastrophe would be more than disappointing , it damages the Union . <p> " Given the circumstances of people starving to death , the placing under attack of the United Nations agency that is responsible for keeping them alive constitutes an appalling failure of diplomacy and the use of starvation as a weapon of war . <p> " It is time to stop this horror of history . Palestine and Israel will ultimately have to live together in spaces adjacent to each other . " <p> The Government issued a joint statement with Spain , Norway and Slovenia condemning the vote in the Israeli parliament that would ban UNRWA from operating in Israeli-controlled lands . <p> The Knesset also voted to cut formal ties between Israel and the organisation .
