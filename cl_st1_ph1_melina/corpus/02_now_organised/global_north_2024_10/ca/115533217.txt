THE CANADIAN PRESS/Sean Kilpatrick <p> OTTAWA -- Hundreds of people are gathering today in cities across Canada to remember the victims of Hamas 's Oct. 7 attack on Israel and the hostages that have still not yet made it home . In the nation 's capital , seas of Israeli flags are draped over participants in front of Ottawa city hall , with Canadian flags waving alongside them as they call for the release of hostages still being held in Gaza . They are expected to march alongside an increased police presence to Parliament Hill , where a Liberal MP and a Conservative MP will be among those delivering speeches at an event to mark the sombre anniversary .
