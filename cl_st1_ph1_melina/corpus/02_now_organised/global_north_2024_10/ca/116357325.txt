<h> Blinken arrives in Israel as US looks to renew cease-fire efforts after the killing of Hamas leader <p> TEL AVIV , Israel ( AP ) -- U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken arrived in Israel on Tuesday on his 11th visit to the region since the outbreak of the Israel-Hamas war . <p> He is expected to meet with top officials as the United States looks to revive cease-fire efforts after the killing of top Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar , but so far none of the warring parties appear to be backing down on longstanding demands . <p> Israel is still at war with Hamas more than a year after the militant group 's Oct. 7 attack , and with Hezbollah in Lebanon , where it launched a ground invasion earlier this month . Israel is also expected to strike Iran after its ballistic missile attack on Oct. 1 . <p> Blinken landed just hours after Hezbollah launched a barrage of rockets into central Israel , setting off air raid sirens in the country 's most populated areas but causing no apparent damage or injuries .
