<p> A community interfaithful vigil is being held on Monday for people to grieve together a year after violent events on Oct. 7 , 2023 , in Israel and to acknowledge the Palestinian civilians who were killed in Gaza . <p> The event is being held by Independent Jewish Voices Guelph ( IJV Guelph ) and Interfaith Justice Guelph . It will be on Oct. 7 at 6:30 p.m.at Market Square . <p> The event will include musical performances , candlelight vigil , food and speeches from community members and faith leaders . <p> It is being held the same date and time as the memorial event being hosted by the Jewish community at Branion Plaza . <p> When asked in an interview why IJV Guelph and Interfaith Justice Guelph was hosting its event the same day and time as the one at Branion Plaza , an IJV Guelph event organizer said it was a coincidence . They said their event was planned two weeks ago .
