<h> Opinion : Ethnic cleansing is happening in Palestine -- as it happened in Canada <p> The longstanding crisis in Palestine and Israel gained worldwide attention on October 7 , and has since been protested globally . While we , as Canadians , may feel a geographic disconnect from Palestine , I see the Israeli state 's ethnic cleansing of Palestinians to be similar to that of Indigenous peoples in Canada 's history . <h> The start of European invasion <p> Indigenous peoples in Canada existed before the first European invasion in 1497 . Over time , the Christian and European languages arrived and displaced and permanently altered the Indigenous ways of life and cultural traditions . <p> In contrast , over centuries , all three Abrahamic religions -- Judaism , Islam , and Christianity -- have declared spiritual connections to regions in the Middle East which encompasses modern-day Palestine , Israel , Jordan , and southern portions of Lebanon and Syria .
