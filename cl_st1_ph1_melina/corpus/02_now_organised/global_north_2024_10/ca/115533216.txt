THE CANADIAN PRESS/Sean Kilpatrick <p> OTTAWA -- Hundreds of people were gathering Sunday in cities across Canada to remember the victims of the Oct. 7 attack by Hamas on Israel and the ongoing war in Gaza . In the nation 's capital , a sea of Israeli flags could be seen draped over participants in front of Ottawa city hall , with Canadian flags waving alongside them as they called for the release of hostages still being held in Gaza . They were set to march to Parliament Hill , where Liberal MP Mona Fortier , Conservative MP Shuvaloy Majumdar and rabbis were to be among those delivering speeches at an event to mark the sombre anniversary .
