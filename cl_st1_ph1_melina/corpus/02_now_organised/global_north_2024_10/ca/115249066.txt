<p> Jon Allen is a senior fellow at the Munk School of Global Affairs and a former Canadian ambassador to Israel and Spain . <p> The past couple of weeks have brought a significant shake-up in the Middle East . After almost a year of fighting in Gaza and at its northern border with Lebanon , Israel has scored some sophisticated tactical victories . Israel has done significant damage to Hezbollah , its enemy in Lebanon , and its Iron Dome defence system , with help from its allies and neighbours , intercepted most of Tuesday 's missiles sent by Iran . However , for Israelis and Palestinians to ultimately be able to live in peace , they will have to find a way to share the land they inhabit . <p> Three weeks ago , the war in Gaza appeared to be winding down , although Palestinians there were still suffering daily bombings and the humanitarian situation continued to be dire . Hezbollah was firing rockets into northern Israel daily , and around 60,000 Israelis were unable to return to their homes .
