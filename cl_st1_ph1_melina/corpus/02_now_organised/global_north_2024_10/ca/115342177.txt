<h> Candlelight vigil to be held to commemorate lives lost in Israel-Hamas conflict <p> PRINCE GEORGE -- A candlelight vigil will be held on October 7 at 6:30 p.m. to commemorate the lives lost due to the Israel-Hamas conflict . <p> " We 're all human , and all of our lives are worth the same value . And that 's really what we want to get out of this vigil is that we want to recognize the shared humanity that we have across the globe , " says 2 Rivers 4 Palestine event organizer Shereen Sousa . " We have families in here in Prince George who are from Gaza . We have families here who are from Israel . We have families here who are from Lebanon . We are a global community here in Prince George . " <p> The vigil will be held at the bandshell at Lheidli T'enneh Memorial Park .
