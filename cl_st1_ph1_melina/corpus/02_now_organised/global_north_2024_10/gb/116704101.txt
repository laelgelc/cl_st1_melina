<p> In a joint statement on Monday evening , the governments of the four European nations said : " The Governments of Ireland , Norway , Slovenia and Spain condemn the approval by the Knesset of legislation to prevent UNRWA from operating in the Occupied Palestinian Territory . <p> " UNRWA has a mandate from the United Nations General Assembly . <p> " The work of the Agency is essential and irreplaceable for millions of Palestinian refugees in the region , and particularly in the current context in Gaza . <p> " The legislation approved by the Knesset sets a very serious precedent for the work of the United Nations and for all organizations of the multilateral system . <p> " Ireland , Norway , Slovenia and Spain will continue to work with donor and host countries to ensure the viability of UNRWA 's work and its humanitarian role .
