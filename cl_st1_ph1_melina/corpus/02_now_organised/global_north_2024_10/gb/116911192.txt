<p> Travellers are being given an essential warning to check the latest safety advice for their destinations before they take off . The Foreign Commonwealth and Development Office ( FCDO ) emphasises that journeying to areas advised against could not only invalidate travel insurance but also present serious risks . <p> These dangers may be due to potential political unrest , conflict , terrorism , or severe weather conditions . To pinpoint these high-risk locations , the FCDO provides a variety of travel alerts , including those recommending against " all travel " or " all but essential travel " . <p> According to a report by The Independent , out of the 226 countries and territories with FCDO advice pages , there are currently 68 marked as having no-go areas primarily because of security threats , health hazards , and laws that differ significantly from those in the UK .
