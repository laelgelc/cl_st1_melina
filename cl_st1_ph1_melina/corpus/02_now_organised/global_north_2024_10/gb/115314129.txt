<h> Israeli military says it killed a senior Hamas leader in Gaza three months ago <h> Israeli military says it killed a senior Hamas leader in Gaza three months ago <p> The Israeli military said on Thursday that it killed a senior Hamas leader in an airstrike in the Gaza Strip around three months ago . <p> It said that a strike on an underground compound in northern Gaza killed Rawhi Mushtaha and two other Hamas commanders , Sameh Siraj and Sameh Oudeh . <p> The military said the three commanders had taken refuge in a fortified underground compound in northern Gaza that served as a command and control centre . <p> It said Mushtaha was a close associate of Yahya Sinwar , the top leader of Hamas who helped mastermind the October 7 attack into Israel that triggered the war . <p> Sinwar is believed to be alive and in hiding inside Gaza . <p> Meanwhile , the Palestinian Health Ministry said 14 people were killed on Thursday in an Israeli strike in the Tulkarem refugee camp in the West Bank . <p> The Israeli military said it conducted a strike in Tulkarem , without elaborating .
