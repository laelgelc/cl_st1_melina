<p> UK filmmaker Asif Kapadia has " unequivocally " apologised for social media reposts about the Israel-Gaza conflict that caused documentary charity The Grierson Trust to withdraw his patronage . <p> In a statement , Kapadia said , " I 'm mortified by the hurt and offence that some of my retweets have caused . I now understand that they will be seen by many as antisemitic , or in the case of one even justifying violence . That was not my intention . <p> " Like many , I feel passionate about the fate of the Palestinian &amp; Lebanese people and the suffering they have endured over the years , but I am equally passionate about all anti-racism and condemn all forms of antisemitism . I unequivocally apologise for these ill-judged reposts which were posted in haste with a lack of due consideration . " <p> Kapadia was removed as patron of documentary charity The Grierson Trust today ( October 11 ) two days after his appointment to the role . <p> Describing some of Kapadia 's posts as " anti-Semitic " , the Trust said in a statement : " When we made the decision to appoint @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ posts , some of which appear to be no longer available , and we are sorry that our due diligence was not thorough enough . " <p> Known for documentaries including Senna , Amy and Diego Maradona , Kapadia 's latest film 2073 -- a docu-fiction hybrid -- is scheduled to have its UK premiere at BFI London Film Festival on Wednesday , October 16 .
