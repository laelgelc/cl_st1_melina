<h> Pro-Palestinian protest march through Hanley this weekend <p> A pro-Palestinian protest march is being staged this weekend . It begins at Hanley Park bandstand at midday on Saturday 19 October and ends in Albion Square , in Hanley . <p> The protest has been organised by The Stoke Palestine Solidarity Campaign as well as Friends of Al-Aqsa , North Staffordshire Trades Union Council , Stoke City &amp; North Staffs CND , Sumud Palestine UK , India Labour Solidarity ( UK ) , Leek Palestine Solidarity Campaign , the Radical Arts Collective , and the North Staffs Miners Wives Group . It will call for an end to the occupation of Palestine ; demand justice for the people of Gaza , the West Bank , and Lebanon ; and call for a ' Stoke Apartheid-Free Zone ' . <p> Jonas Marvin , from Stoke Palestine Solidarity Campaign , said : " It is absolutely critical that we continue the strong support for justice , peace and freedom that Stoke-on-Trent has shown towards the Palestinian people .
