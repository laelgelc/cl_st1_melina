<p> International Monetary Fund Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva said on Thursday that she will travel to Egypt in about 10 days to see first-hand the country 's difficult economic situation and drive home the need to stick with reforms . <p> Georgieva told a news conference that Egypt , which saw its IMF loan program expanded to $8 billion from $5 billion , earlier this year , has continued to suffer from conflicts in Gaza , Lebanon and Sudan , with a 70% loss of Suez Canal revenues . <p> " We have been very open to adjust the Egyptian program or any other program to what is best to serve people , " Georgieva said . " But let me say that we are not going to do our job for the country and for the people of the country if we pretend that action that needs to be taken can be foregone .
