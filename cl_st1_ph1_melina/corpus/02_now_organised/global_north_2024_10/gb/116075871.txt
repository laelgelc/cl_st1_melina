<p> BRUSSELS -- European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen has stated that the European Union aims to establish an economic corridor with Gulf countries to increase trade in renewable energy , data , and people-to-people exchanges . <p> Brussels hosted a Gulf-European summit on Wednesday , attended by Gulf and European leaders , including Saudi Crown Prince and Prime Minister Mohammed bin Salman . The summit primarily focused on the Middle East crisis , the ongoing conflict in Gaza and Lebanon , as well as broad economic issues . <p> Von der Leyen described her meeting with Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on the sidelines of the summit as " good . " <p> Following the meeting , she wrote on her social media platform X : " We want to strengthen EU-GCC cooperation by building an economic corridor to increase trade in renewable energy , data , and people-to-people exchanges . " <p> Von der Leyen also emphasized the importance of " working together to ensure the security we all need for economic growth .
