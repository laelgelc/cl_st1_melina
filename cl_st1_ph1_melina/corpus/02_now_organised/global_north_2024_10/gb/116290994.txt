<p> CAIRO - President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi warned on Sunday Egypt may be forced to re-evaluate its $8 billion programme with the IMF if international institutions do not take into account the extraordinary regional challenges the country is facing . <p> Egypt signed an $8 billion financial support package with the International Monetary Fund in March that requires it to reduce subsidies on fuel , electricity and other commodities and to allow its currency to float freely -- measures that have triggered public fury . <p> The IMF package unlocked billions of dollars of additional funds from the World Bank and the European Union . <p> On Friday , Egypt raised prices on a wide range of fuel products for the third time this year , with diesel and gasoline prices increasing by between 11% to 17% . In June , it raised the price of subsidised bread by 300% .
