<p> A new poll has found that former President Trump and Vice President Kamala Harris are essentially tied among voters in the battleground state of Michigan . <p> The Washington Post poll of 1,004 registered voters reveals that Trump is leading Harris there 47% to 45% . Among that group , those who said they were likely to vote for Harris over Trump 47%-46% . <p> The poll was conducted Oct. 24-28 and has a margin of error of 3.7% .
