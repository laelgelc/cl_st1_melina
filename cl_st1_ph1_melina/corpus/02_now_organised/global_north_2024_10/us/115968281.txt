Air Force/AP ) <p> Listen <p> 1 min <p> Share <p> Save <p> The Biden administration intensified pressure on Israel this week to improve dire humanitarian conditions in the Gaza Strip , warning that the United States would be forced to undertake new actions -- including a potential suspension of military assistance -- if food and other aid to Palestinians is not increased within a month . The admonition , in a letter sent Sunday to the Israeli government , comes amid concerns over civilian deaths , along with dwindling access by aid groups to Gaza 's north . The letter 's authenticity was confirmed by U.S. and Israeli officials , speaking on the condition of anonymity to discuss sensitive communications between the two governments . Since the spring , the amount of aid delivered to Gaza " has dropped by more than 50 percent , " the United States says .
