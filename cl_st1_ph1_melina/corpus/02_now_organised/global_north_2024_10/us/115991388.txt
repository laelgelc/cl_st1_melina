<p> Palestinian poet Mosab Abu Toha on life and loss in GazaMosab Abu Toha was able to escape Gaza , along with his wife and three young children . The award-winning poet talks about parenting in war and the devastation of leaving his family and friends behind . <p> Mosab Abu Toha and his wife and children are currently living in Syracuse , N.Y. , where he is a fellow at the University of Syracuse .
