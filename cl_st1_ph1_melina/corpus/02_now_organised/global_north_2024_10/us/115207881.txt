<p> Iranian officials said they launched more than 100 ballistic missiles in the attack , according to The New York Times , with others reporting the nation launched as many as 400 missiles . Air raid sirens rang out across Israel , and CNN reported that the Israeli security Cabinet is sheltering in an emergency bunker in Jerusalem . <p> The missile barrage comes after Israel launched several attacks -- including a recent ground invasion -- in Lebanon . The attacks were targeted at Hezbollah , the Islamic militant group allied with Iran , and last week killed Hassan Nasrallah , the leader of the group . Hezbollah is also allied with Hamas , the Palestinian terrorist organization that attacked Israel last Oct. 7 , leading Israel to launch a prolonged offensive in Gaza that has killed tens of thousands of Palestinians . <p> The Iranian National Guard called the missile attacks on Tuesday the " first wave of attacks on the occupied territories , " according to Al Jazeera . <p> U.S. officials reportedly warned earlier on Tuesday that Iran planned to strike Israel . " This morning , @VP and I convened our national security team to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Israel , " President Joe Biden wrote in a statement posted to X. " We discussed how the United States is prepared to help Israel defend against these attacks , and protect American personnel in the region . " <p> The National Security Council wrote in a statement that Biden and Harris " are monitoring the Iranian attack against Israel from the White House Situation Room , " and that Biden " directed the U.S. military to aid Israel 's defense against Iranian attack . " <p> Former President Donald Trump also responded to the attack . " The world is on fire and spiraling out of control , " he wrote before bashing Democrats and claiming that if Vice President Kamala Harris wins in November , " the world will go up in smoke . "
