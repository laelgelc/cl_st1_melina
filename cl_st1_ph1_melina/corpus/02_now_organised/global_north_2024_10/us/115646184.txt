During the current war , there have been instances in which Hezbollah 's media outlets have given false representations of its actions . <p> Thus , for example , after the assassination in July 2024 of the organization 's military commander in Beirut , Fuad Shukr , Nasrallah claimed that 340 rockets had been fired at Israel and that drones had hit two of Israel 's military bases , including Glilot . In actual fact , the bases were not attacked , and the large majority of the rockets were shot down .
