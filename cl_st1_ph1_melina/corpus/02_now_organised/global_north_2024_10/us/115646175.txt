<h> UN chief warns Netanyahu that blocking UNRWA would be ' catastrophe ' <p> By REUTERS OCTOBER 8 , 2024 19:46 <p> United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres said on Tuesday he has written to Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu , warning him that draft Israeli legislation to prevent the UN Palestinian refugee agency ( UNRWA ) from working in the Palestinian territories would be a " catastrophe . " <p> " Such a measure would suffocate efforts to ease human suffering and tensions in Gaza , and indeed , the entire Occupied Palestinian Territory . It would be a catastrophe in what is already an unmitigated disaster , " he told reporters . <p> In July , the Israeli parliament gave preliminary approval to a bill that declares UNRWA a terrorist organization and proposes to sever relations with the body . Israeli leaders have accused UNRWA of collaborating with Hamas militants in Gaza .
