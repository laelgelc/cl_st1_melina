Some might have actually voted for Trump , and even more might have stayed at home or skipped the presidential ballot line . <p> The picture is complicated by the presence of an even larger minor-party candidacy in 2016 , that of Libertarian Gary Johnson , who won 3 percent of the national presidential vote compared to Stein 's one percent .
