<h> Destruction in Gaza caused by Israel-Hamas war mapped using satellite data <p> Israeli military operations in Gaza have killed almost 42,000 people since Oct. 7 , 2023 , according to the Ministry of Health in the Hamas-run Palestinian territory , the majority of them women and children . Israel launched the war in immediate response to Hamas ' Oct. 7 , 2023 terrorist attacks , when the group fired hundreds of rockets and militants stormed across the Gaza border , killing some 1,200 people and taking 250 more as hostages . <p> In addition to lives lost , the United Nations estimates that the war has displaced 90% of Gaza 's roughly 2.3 million people . Many of them -- unable to leave the embattled enclave -- have been displaced multiple times within Gaza as they try to escape the Israeli airstrikes that have decimated its towns and cities . <p> As of January , the war had caused around $18.5 billion in damage to infrastructure in Gaza , according to the U.N . and the World Bank .
