<h> Protestors march down Front Street in Harrisburg to demand ceasefire , end to Pa. anti-BDS law <p> Protestors shut down Front Street in Harrisburg for over an hour Sunday afternoon , marching from the Governor 's Residence to the state Capitol to demand a halt of U.S. military aid to Israel and for Pennsylvania to end its law that prevents contractors from financially protesting the Israeli state . <p> The march -- which numbered about 300 people at its peak -- came on the eve of the one-year anniversary of an attack into Israel by Hamas , a militant group based in the Palestinian territory of Gaza .
