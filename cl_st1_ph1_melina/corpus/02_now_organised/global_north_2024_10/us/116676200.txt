<h> Egypt 's president says it has proposed 2-day cease-fire in Gaza , exchange of Israeli and Palestinian captives <p> Egypt 's president on Sunday said his country has proposed a two-day cease-fire between Israel and Hamas during which four hostages held in Gaza would be freed and some Palestinian prisoners would be released . <p> President Abdel-Fattah el-Sissi , speaking in Cairo , said the proposal also includes the delivery of humanitarian aid to the besieged Gaza Strip . This is the first time Egypt 's president has publicly proposed such a plan . <p> Egypt , along with Qatar and the United States , has been a key mediator for peace talks between Israel and Hamas since the war broke out more than a year ago . Months of negotiations sputtered to a halt in August . <p> El-Sissi said the proposal aims to " move the situation forward , " adding that once the two-day cease-fire goes into effect , negotiations would continue to make it permanent . <p> There has n't been a cease-fire in 11 months , since November 's weeklong pause in fighting in which 105 hostages were released in exchange for 240 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reactions from Israel or Hamas to Egypt 's proposal . <p> During a government memorial Sunday for the Hebrew anniversary of the Oct. 7 attack , Israeli Defense Minister Yoav Gallant said that " not every goal can be achieved through military operations . Iran 's supreme leader said the attack " should not be exaggerated nor downplayed , " while stopping short of calling for retaliation . It was Israel 's first open attack on its archenemy . <p> That exchange of fire has raised fears of an all-out regional war pitting Israel and the United States against Iran and its militant proxies , which include Hamas and the Hezbollah militant group in Lebanon , where Israel launched a ground invasion earlier this month after nearly a year of lower-level conflict sparked by the war in Gaza .
