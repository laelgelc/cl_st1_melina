<p> Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar sent a message to a senior operative saying that he believed it was time to revive suicide bombings , The Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday , citing Arab intelligence officials . <p> Sinwar , who assumed complete control of Hamas over the summer following the death of Ismail Haniyeh , was a chief architect of Hamas 's cross-border massacres in southern Israel on October 7 , 2023 . <p> The WSJ noted that Hamas largely stopped using suicide bombings nearly two decades ago , as some of its leaders feared that such tactics could politically isolate the group . <p> Yet since October 7 , Hamas leaders such as Sinwar and Khaled Mashaal have called for these radical terror attacks to continue . <p> In August , Mashaal spoke at a conference in Turkey , stating , " We want to return to martyrdom operations .
