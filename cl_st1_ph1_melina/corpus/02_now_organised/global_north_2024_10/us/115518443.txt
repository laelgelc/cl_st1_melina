But what Mr. Sullivan , the Arabs , and the Israelis overlooked was the Palestine question . <h> Two narratives <p> Israel believed that it had established a new status quo -- occupation without consequences . It has always sought to delink Palestinian militarism from its occupation of the Palestinian territories . Before October 7 , Israel had been treating Palestinian violence as a security nuisance . But after the Hamas attack , the first large-scale one in Israel proper since 1948 , the narrative shifted . Now , Israel is fighting an " existential war " against terror . Israel marched to Gaza with fire and fury . Over the past 12 months , the Israel Defence Forces ( IDF ) have killed more than 41,000 Palestinians ( more than 110 every day ) and injured nearly 1,00,000 Palestinians . Now , Israel is fighting a three-tier regional war in West Asia . <p> Israel has different objectives at each tier , which collectively make for its strategy to alter the balance of power in West Asia to further its advantage . At the bottom tier , Israel went to Gaza with two declared objectives -- to destroy Hamas and secure the release of hostages . In the middle , it wants to push Hezbollah from the border region of Lebanon and stop the Shia militia from launching rockets into Israel so that the displaced residents of the Upper Galilee region can return to their homes . At the top , it wants to weaken Iran , its main regional rival . Is this an achievable goal ? <p> After 12 months of fighting in Gaza , which has been under an Israeli blockade since 2007 and has been besieged by Israel since October 7 , 2023 , Israel is yet to meet its objectives in the 365 sq . km enclave , sandwiched between the Mediterranean Sea and Israel proper . Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu wants to obliterate Hamas , but now even the IDF says this is not an achievable objective .
