<h> Antisemitism remains all too common in Maryland READER COMMENTARY <h> Share this : <p> Rabbi Yanky Baron , holding the shofar , or ram 's horn associated with Rosh Hashana , discusses the need for the new security fence and gate installed at the Ellicott City Chabad House in response to the general uptick in antisemitic activity . He says many synagogues and Jewish religious schools in Maryland will look different after the installation of gated fencing and other security deterrents . The fresh attention to security measures was spurred by the rise of antisemitic activity that followed the Hamas attack on Israel on Oct. 7 . ( Amy Davis/Staff ) <p> I am grateful for and appreciate The Baltimore Sun 's editorial decrying the evil of antisemitism that appeared on the secular anniversary of the Hamas-led massacre of Jews and gentile alike in Israel ( " A tragedy a year ago in Israel has become a tragedy in the US , " Oct. 7 ) . Many American Jews have faced innumerable macro- and micro-terror events in the ensuing year . I personally have had my small yard sign announcing " We support the land of Israel @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have witnessed young men tearing down telephone pole posters of Jewish women and children held hostage in the hell-hole tunnels of Gaza and burning them on the sidewalks in front of the synagogues of my neighborhood out of meanness , hatred and ignorance . <p> Why is it that the state and judiciary will not tolerate a KKK or neo-Nazi party march in College Park celebrating Adolf Hitler 's birthday and the submission of the poor and weak to the power of the mindless mob , yet antisemitic marches calling for the destruction of the modern Jewish state , perhaps the first flower of redemption for all mankind , Jew and gentile alike , are allowed on our public campuses and streets ? When all other minorities and immigrants are protected from such trauma and abuse , only the Jews are treated with a different standard .
