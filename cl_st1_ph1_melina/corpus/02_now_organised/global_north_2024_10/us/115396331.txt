The study 's questions were asked as a part of PORI 's September face-to-face omnibus survey . <p> The survey was conducted in Hebrew and Arabic among 1,012 respondents across Israel via area probability sampling . One hundred primary sampling units were randomly selected , with households selected via random walk and respondents selected via the last-birthday method .
