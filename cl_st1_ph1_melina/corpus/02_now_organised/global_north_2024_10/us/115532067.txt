<p> Kibbutz Kfar Aza was one of the most impacted by October 7 . After Hamas either murdered or kidnapped 10% of its 700 residents , the survivors were displaced and have been scattered throughout the country ever since . KKL-JNF decided to shorten the already accelerated building schedule from ten months to four months . Three months ago , bulldozers and earth-moving equipment began to clear 365,000 cubic meters of earth to prepare the area for construction .
