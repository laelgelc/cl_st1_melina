His death , while a tactical success , offers Israel the chance for something far more consequential : a strategic breakthrough that can reshape the future of Gaza , Lebanon and perhaps even Israel 's domestic politics . <p> This is Israel 's opportunity to declare victory and move forward with a more decisive , long-term strategy that finally ends the cycle of conflict .
