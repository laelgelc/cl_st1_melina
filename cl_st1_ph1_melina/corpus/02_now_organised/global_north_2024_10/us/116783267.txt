<p> With the crisis continuing in Ukraine and Gaza , experts and policymakers are concerned about further increases in the prices of the components used for making petroleum-based chemical fertilizers . Recently , Senior Economist of the Food and Agriculture Organization Nicholas Sitko told The Hindu that India has to increase its own fertilizer production capacity . The Agriculture Minister of the country 's largest foodgrains producing Uttar Pradesh recently complained that the State has the stock of fertilizers for only 10 more days . In 2020-21 , the total consumption of fertilizers was about 629.83 LMT .
