The war in Gaza appears poised to continue indefinitely and probably expand , to the apparent delight of both Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar . Each must be surveying the wreckage in the region and anticipating the dark days ahead with determination and confidence . Each must think he is playing a sophisticated long game that the other will lose . <p> This is hardly the first time that the designs of right-wing Israeli leaders have coincided with those of Hamas .
