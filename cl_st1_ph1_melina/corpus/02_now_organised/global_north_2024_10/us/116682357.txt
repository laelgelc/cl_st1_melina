<h> ' After Sinwar 's death , we have nothing left to lose , ' Palestinian source in Qatar says -report <p> A Palestinian source based in Qatar has stated that following the assassination of senior Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar , there is a sense among Gaza officials that they have " nothing left to lose , " according to a report by Kan News correspondent Elior Levy on Sunday evening , as preparations for hostage negotiation talks are underway in Doha . <p> According to Levy 's report on Kan 11 , the source explained that this mindset will influence the Palestinian approach to negotiations , as they intend to stand firm on their conditions . <p> The source shared that after the discussions in Doha , consultation with the military council in Gaza will follow , which will guide the next steps . The source noted that the consultation process could take several days due to the complexity of communication between Doha and Gaza .
