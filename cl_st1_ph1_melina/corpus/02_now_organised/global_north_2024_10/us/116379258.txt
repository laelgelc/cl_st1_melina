<h> Jewish students assaulted , threatened and harassed at UCLA campus during anti-Israel protests : report <p> Jewish students and staffers were routinely assaulted , threatened and harassed as anti-Israel protests erupted on campus at the University of California , Los Angeles , according to the school 's antisemitism task force . <p> The group 's new 93-page report found that as protests over the war in Gaza broke out in UCLA , public displays of antisemitism ran rampant in the school , from a swastika being drawn in a classroom chalkboard to a sign being waved at an encampment that read : " Israelis are Native 2 HELL , " the Wall Street Journal first reported . <p> Officials also found a disturbing sculpture of a pig in March holding a bag of money beside a Star of David with a clock hanging above its head that read , " Time is Running out . " <p> The report also highlighted the violent clash that occurred on campus on the night of April 30 , where agitators stormed the protesters ' encampment , which led to bloody brawls and firecrackers being tossed at the demonstrators . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ night was a result of the " UCLA administration demonstrated a reluctance to take action against those in the encampment unless violence erupted . " <p> The task force said the school was more preoccupied with defending student 's right to protest than the safety of those on campus . AFP via Getty Images <p> The report ultimately slammed the school and administration for prioritizing its views on Freedom of Speech over the safety and wellbeing of students and staff . <p> UCLA officials told The Post that Darnell Hunt , the school 's interim chancellor who created the antisemitism task force in February , has received the report and is thankful for the months-long work . <p> " He is carefully reviewing the report and its recommendations to determine the best ways for UCLA to strengthen our ongoing efforts to combat antisemitism and protect the well-being of the entire Bruin community , " the school said .
