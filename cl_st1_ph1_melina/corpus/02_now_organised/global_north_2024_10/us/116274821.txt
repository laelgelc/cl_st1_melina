<p> Ever attentive to the outer world 's events , Paris got word of our war and decided to do something about it . " Let 's convene a conference , " said someone in the Quai d'Orsay , the 170-year-old Parisian palace of French diplomacy . Soon enough a date -- next Thursday -- was indeed set for yet another quixotic effort to pull Lebanon out of the abyss . <p> The planned gathering 's participants have yet to be officially announced at this writing , but analysts believe it will include Arab and Western government representatives alongside leaders of Lebanon 's religious sectors . <p> For its Lebanese participants , the conclave 's Parisian setting will surely constitute a welcome reprieve from the war that bedevils them as much as it bedevils us . Other than that , this umpteenth French attempt to mimic a superpower is likely to end up reflecting France 's journey from greatness through tragedy to farce . And that 's a shame , because events now actually offer Paris a once-in-a-century chance to play a major role in solving a major global problem .
