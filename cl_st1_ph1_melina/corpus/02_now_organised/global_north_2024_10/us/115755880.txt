<h> The Axe Files with David Axelrod <h> David Axelrod , the founder and director of the University of Chicago Institute of Politics , and CNN bring you The Axe Files , a series of revealing interviews with key figures in the political world . Go beyond the soundbites and get to know some of the most interesting players in politics . <p> Ilana Dayan , a prominent Israeli journalist , spoke with David last fall , just days after the October 7 massacre in Israel perpetrated by Hamas . One year later , David checked in with Ilana to talk about Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's staying power , reporting on Gaza , recognizing the US 's political calculations in its dealings with Israel , the need for both war and diplomacy in the Middle East , and what happens next between Israel and Iran .
