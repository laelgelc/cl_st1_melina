<p> Stretching for 120km along Lebanon 's southern frontier and Israel 's northern border , the so-called " Blue Line " is " a key to peace in the region " and one of the central elements of resolution 1701 since the war in 2006 , with UNIFIL peacekeepers being its temporary custodian , according to UN Peacekeeping . <p> Based on various historical maps , some dating back almost a century , the Blue Line is not a border , but a temporary " line of withdrawal " set by the UN in 2000 for the practical purpose of confirming the withdrawal of Israeli forces from southern Lebanon . <p> Whenever Israeli or Lebanese authorities wish to undertake any activities close to the Blue Line , UNIFIL requests that they provide advance notice , allowing the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to minimise any misunderstandings that could lead to increased tensions . <h> How is resolution 1701 implemented ?
