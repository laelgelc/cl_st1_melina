" They see that , at the moment , there are five-six-seven million Jews here , surrounded by hundreds of millions of Arabs . They have no reason to give in , because the Jewish state ca n't last . They are bound to win . In another 30 to 50 years they will overcome us , come what may . " <p> Morris is right . He is correct in the sense that Palestinians will not give up , that there can never be a situation where societies indefinitely survive and thrive within a permanent matrix of racial segregation , violence and exclusion - exclusion of the other , the Palestinians and the isolation of the self . <p> The very history of Palestine is a testament to such a truth .
