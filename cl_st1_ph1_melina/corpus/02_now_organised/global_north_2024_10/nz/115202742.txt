Every death and injury caused by the State of Israel is a crime against humanity . It should be condemned by everyone , " he added . <p> " We stand in solidarity with the people of Lebanon , who are the latest targets of US-EU imperialism who have armed and funded the State of Israel while it commits war crime after war crime .
