<h> UN 's top court to rule on Israel in Gaza war <p> The ICJ 's ruling will be politically significant if it goes against Israel EPA <p> The UN 's top court could issue emergency measures ordering Israel to halt its military operations in Gaza . <p> The session of the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) on Friday is part of a case brought by South Africa alleging that Israel is committing genocide . <p> Both countries testified when the case opened two weeks ago . Israel has vehemently rejected the allegation . <p> A ruling against Israel is not enforceable by the court but would be politically significant . <p> More than 25,000 Palestinians -- mostly women and children -- have been killed and tens of thousands injured , according to the Hamas-run health ministry in Gaza , since Israel began its offensive , triggered by an unprecedented attack on Israel by the group . <p> The Hamas attack on 7 October killed about 1,300 people , mainly civilians .
