<h> UN chief urges countries to reverse UNRWA funding suspension <p> Secretary-General Ant ? nio Guterres has appealed to the countries that suspended funding to the UN agency assisting Palestine refugees ( UNRWA ) to reconsider their decisions , to ensure continuity of its vital humanitarian operations . <p> In a statement on Sunday , the UN chief said that the Organization is promptly responding to the extremely serious allegations that several UNRWA personnel were involved in the 7 October terror attacks in southern Israel . <p> An investigation by the Office of Internal Oversight Services ( OIOS ) , the highest investigative body in the UN system , was immediately activated . <p> " Any UN employee involved in acts of terror will be held accountable , including through criminal prosecution , " Mr. Guterres said . <p> " The Secretariat is ready to cooperate with a competent authority able to prosecute the individuals in line with the Secretariat 's normal procedures for such cooperation , " he added . <p> UNRWA Commissioner-General Philippe Lazzarini had previously announced on 17 January a full , independent review of the agency . <p> Out of the 12 individuals implicated , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . One is confirmed dead , and the identities of the remaining two are being clarified . <h> Vital humanitarian efforts at risk <p> Over two million civilians in the Gaza Strip depend on lifesaving aid provided by UNRWA . The Agency operates shelters for over one million people and has been providing food and healthcare since the start of the conflict . <p> However , its current funding is insufficient to meet all requirements to support them in February . <p> Acknowledging the concerns of the countries that halted funds and expressing his own horror at the accusations , Mr. Guterres strongly appealed to the governments that suspended contributions to , at least , guarantee the continuity of UNRWA 's operations . <p> " The abhorrent alleged acts of these staff members must have consequences . But the tens of thousands of men and women who work for UNRWA , many in some of the most dangerous situations for humanitarian workers , should not be penalized , " he said .
