<h> US ' not seeing acts of genocide ' in Gaza , State Dept says <p> The US has not observed acts in Gaza that constitute genocide , State Department spokesperson Matthew Miller said on Wednesday , after SA launched genocide proceedings at the International Court of Justice over Israel 's military operation in the Palestinian enclave . <p> " Those are allegations that should not be made lightly ... we are not seeing any acts that constitute genocide , " Miller said at a regular news briefing . " That is a determination by the State Department , " he added . <p> He had been asked about South Africa 's request on Tuesday that the World Court issue an urgent order declaring that Israel was in breach of its obligations under the 1948 Genocide Convention . <p> The court has scheduled public hearings for January 11 and 12 on SA 's request . Israel said it would defend itself from the charges . Israel 's crackdown has killed more than 22,000 Palestinians , laid waste to much of the enclave and engulfed its 2.3 million residents in a humanitarian disaster .
