<h> Massive protest in Cape Town in support of people of Gaza <h> This was part of a Global Day of Action for Palestine <p> A large crowd gathered at the Sea Point Promenade on Saturday afternoon in support of the people of Gaza . Photo : Ashraf Hendricks <p> A large crowd gathered on a hot and windy day at the Sea Point Promenade in Cape Town for a Global Day of Action for Palestine rally , calling for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza . According to Al Jazeera tens of thousands of people took part in similar protests across the world . <p> Roses were laid out on the railings of the promenade in memory of people killed in Gaza . This one says : " For Rahaf Raafat Abdel Kram Al-Astal 11 . The sight of dust , debris and chaos that destroyed your toys and our memories have been replaced with flowers and beauty . Play well , little ones . " Photo : Ashraf Hendricks <p> The rally , which was organised by the Palestine Solidarity Campaign ( PSC ) , started with a traditional ceremony from Save Our Sacred Lands member Tauriq Jenkins @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ were laid across the Promenade to commemorate the lives lost in Gaza . Watermelons , which have become a symbol of Palestinian resistance , were also handed out . People were seen holding banners that read " Zionism is Racism " and " Boycott Apartheid Israel " . Many people flew Palestinian flags . <p> Watermelon has become a symbol of resistance for Palestinians . Here a woman wears watermelon earrings . Watermelon was also handed out at the event . Photo : Ashraf Hendricks <p> The Global Solidarity Day followed two days of public hearings at the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) in The Hague , Netherlands , in which South Africa asked the court to make a prima facie ( on the face of it ) finding that Israel may be committing genocide . Israel disputed the charges and claimed South Africa was siding with Hamas . <p> A statement read out by the organisers said : " We are here today to be part of the global day of action that will see demonstrations planned in more than 66 cities and at least 36 countries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ front of global voices , calling unconditionally for an immediate and permanent ceasefire . " <p> Imtiaz Sooliman , founder of Gift of the Givers , was one of the speakers at the event . Photo : Ashraf Hendricks <p> Sooliman told the crowd that Gift of the Givers had teams in Gaza and the West Bank . It also had an aid team stationed at the Egyptian border , ready to go into Gaza as soon as there was a ceasefire . <p> " People like us and people all over the world will make sure that the ceasefire comes . The power of the people is more powerful than any government and any weapon in the world , " he told the crowd . <p> Referring to the court case at the ICJ , he said : " I feel great to be South African . When we sent our people to the ICJ we brought back the Mandela magic . " <p> Koni Benson of South African Jews for a Free Palestine addresses the crowd . We are living in the wake of the unresolved and ongoing history of imperialism . " <p> " SAJFP rejects the way that Jewish trauma has been weaponised to justify the occupation and oppression of Palestine . This framework where victimhood justifies all subsequent behaviour goes against the core ethics of Judaism , " she added . <p> There was also a protest in Johannesburg outside the United States consulate . <p> Hundreds of protesters gathered outside the US consulate in Johannesburg to protest against the ongoing bombing of Gaza .
