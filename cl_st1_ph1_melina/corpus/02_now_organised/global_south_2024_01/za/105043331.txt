<h> Statue of Tutu with special Palestinian-African scarf unveiled in Cape Town <p> Desmond and Leah Tutu Foundation CEO , Janet Jobson , said the erection of the statue was part of an ongoing protest against Israel 's war on Gaza . <p> An installation of Archbishop Desmond Tutu wearing a special Palestinian-African scarf has been unveiled at the Old Granary Building in Cape Town . The statue will be displayed daily until the war in Gaza stops .
