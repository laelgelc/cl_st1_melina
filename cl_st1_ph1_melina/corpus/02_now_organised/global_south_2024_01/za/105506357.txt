The court 's ruling was significant on three fronts . <p> The first is legal . South Africa argued that Israel is committing genocide against Palestinians in the Gaza Strip . This evidence paired the scale of civilian deaths -- estimated at more than 26 000 -- with the dehumanising language used by some of Israel 's most senior leaders . <p> It is almost incomprehensible that a nation founded by survivors of genocide stands accused of committing that gravest of crimes . But the world 's highest court has confirmed that those allegations are plausible . <p> The second front is geopolitical . In taking this case to The Hague , South Africa defied not only Israel but also its backers , including the US , Germany , France and the UK . <p> It was brave politics .
