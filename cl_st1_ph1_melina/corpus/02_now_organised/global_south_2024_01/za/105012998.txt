<p> About 100 people gathered outside the Western Cape High Court on Thursday to express support for South Africa 's legal team at the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) in The Hague , Netherlands . <p> The South African government submitted an application on 29 December to the ICJ . It describes the recent history of the Israeli-Palestinian conflict , the devastating effects of the ongoing war in Gaza and statements by Israel 's president , prime minister and cabinet ministers that appear to imply genocidal intent against the Palestinians . <p> The application asks the court to find that there is a prima facie ( on the face of it ) evidence that Israel is committing genocide in Gaza . It asks the court to order a ceasefire and take other measures to prevent genocide from being committed . The South Africans presented their case on Thursday and Israel will respond on Friday . <p> Thursday 's gathering outside the High Court was organised by the Palestine Solidarity Campaign ( PSC ) and included a number of civil society groups .
