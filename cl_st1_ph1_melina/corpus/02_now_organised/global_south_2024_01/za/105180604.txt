<h> TURKMEN TERZI No commercial or political gain from genocide case <h> SA leaders understand what it means to face decades of oppression <p> South Africa 's delegation at the International Court of Justice . Image : GCIS <p> SA 's genocide case against Israel at the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) goes beyond just solidarity with the Palestinian cause . <p> It is a long-term initiative that extends even beyond Pretoria 's calculations of domestic and foreign policy interests . The leaders of the governing ANC who fought against apartheid and succeeded in establishing a democratic SA continue in efforts to transform international institutions that favour Western powers . <p> The ANC is the oldest liberation movement in Africa and it celebrated its 112th birthday in Mpumalanga at the weekend . The party is not only popular on the African continent but remains widely respected among leftist organisations in many parts of the world , especially the Middle East and Latin America . <p> SA stands as the natural representative of the African continent in international organisations and continues raising its voice for historically disadvantaged Africans and other communities who suffered under Western colonial powers . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ its voice for Palestine is just one example among many in the fight for justice around the world . SA 's former presidents Thabo Mbeki and Jacob Zuma criticised the Western world over the killing of Libya 's leader Muammar Gaddafi and Iraq 's Saddam Hussein on many occasions . <p> President Cyril Ramaphosa also led a peace initiative of seven African nations and travelled to Ukraine and Russia in June in efforts to end the ongoing Ukraine-Russia conflict . <p> Israel accuses the SA government of being the " legal arm of Hamas " but SA has repeatedly condemned Hamas 's October 7 attack . The ANC has long and established ties with the Palestine Liberation Organisation ( PLO ) . While Nelson Mandela was in prison he supported the PLO and its leader Yasser Arafat . <p> The ANC is currently engaging with Hamas as the organisation ruling the Gaza Strip . Apart from this , the party has not necessarily developed any strategic relations with the Hamas leaders . <p> Many ANC leaders are well aware that proving Israel is committing acts of genocide is no easy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of SA , Israel is unlikely to stop harming civilians . <p> However , dragging Israel into the defendant dock at the ICJ carried great symbolic importance for the oppressed around the world . SA has gone to great lengths to maintain close ties with the US , especially after the Lady R incident wherein the US ambassador accused the country of providing weapons to Russia . <p> However , none of these countries are principal international trading partners . It is therefore clear that the ANC government is not prioritising commercial or political gain from the genocide case . SA 's genocide case against Israel has already made the ANC a global human rights champion . <p> ?
