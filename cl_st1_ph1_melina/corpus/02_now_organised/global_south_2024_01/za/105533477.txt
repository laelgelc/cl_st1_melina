Despite claims of victory by some , the court did not order an immediate ceasefire , only requiring Israel to prevent genocidal acts in accordance with the Genocide Convention . Misrepresentations by media figures are highlighted , with dissenting opinions pointing out the lack of evidence for genocidal intent .
