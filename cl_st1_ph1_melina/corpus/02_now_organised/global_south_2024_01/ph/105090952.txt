<h> Israel-Hamas war reaches 100-day mark <p> THE latest Israel-Hamas war reached its 100th day on Jan. 14 , 2024 . It is by far the longest , bloodiest , and most destructive conflict between the bitter enemies . <p> The fighting erupted on Oct. 7 when Hamas carried out a deadly attack in southern Israel . Since then , Israel has relentlessly pounded the Gaza Strip with airstrikes and a ground offensive that have wrought unprecedented destruction , flattening entire neighborhoods .
