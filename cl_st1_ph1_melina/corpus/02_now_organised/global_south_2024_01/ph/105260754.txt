<h> Gaza 's oldest mosque destroyed by Israeli airstrike <p> Courtyard , arcades and minaret of the Omari Mosque , also known as the Great Mosque of Gaza . The image , at the US Library of Congress , was taken between 1867 and 1899 by Maison Bonfils . ( Wikimedia Commons ) <p> The Omari Mosque in Gaza was largely destroyed by Israeli bombardment on December 8 , 2023 . It was one of the most ancient mosques in the region and a beloved Gazan landmark . <p> The mosque was first built in the early seventh century and named after Islam 's second caliph , Umar ibn al-Khattab , a successor to the Prophet Muhammad and leader of the early Islamic community . <p> It was a graceful white stone structure , with repeating vistas of pointed arches and a tall octagonal minaret encircled by a carved wooden balcony and crowned with a crescent . <p> The lower half of the minaret and a few exterior walls are reported to be the only parts of the mosque still standing .
