<h> Navigating thru rough waters : Israel 's economic resilience in times of war <p> OVER the past years , the Israeli economy has proven its resilience in the light of conflicts and geopolitical challenges . <p> Recently , I returned from a work trip to Israel following an annual conference of the Foreign Trade Administration -- the overall head unit of Israel 's economic and trade missions abroad . As expected , the five-day gathering was very much focused on the ongoing war , vis-a-vis its effects on the Israeli economy and tech industry . <p> As part of the program , I visited Kibbutz Kfar Aza , one of the peaceful communities next to the border with Gaza , which was severely hit by Hamas in the horrific terror attack of October 7 . Out of the 400 residents , 79 were brutally murdered and 18 were kidnapped -- among them women , children , and elderly .
