<h> US , Israel agree on UN ' assessment mission ' in northern Gaza <p> The US and Israel agreed Tuesday that the UN can carry out " an assessment mission " in northern Gaza to determine what needs to be done so that displaced Palestinians can return home . <p> " It will determine what needs to be done to allow displaced Palestinians to return safely to homes in the north , " Secretary of State Antony Blinken told reporters during a news conference in Tel Aviv after meeting Israeli officials , including Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu , Foreign Minister Israel Katz and the War Cabinet . <p> " This is not going to happen overnight . There are serious security , infrastructure and humanitarian challenges , but the mission will start a process that evaluates these obstacles , and how they can be overcome , " said Blinken . <p> He noted that the US continues to offer its best advice for how Israel can achieve its essential goal of ensuring that Oct.7 can never be repeated . <p> " We believe Israel has achieved significant progress toward this fundamental objective as Israel 's campaign moves to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the IDF ( Israel Defense Forces ) scales down its forces there , " he said . <p> Blinken held a telephone call with UN Humanitarian Coordinator for Gaza Sigrid Kaag late Monday and underscored facilitating assistance to the northern Gaza Strip to enable the return of displaced people . <p> Blinken is on an official visit to the region until Jan. 11 with stops that include T ? rkiye , Greece , Qatar , the United Arab Emirates , Saudi Arabia , Israel , the West Bank and Egypt . <p> His tour comes at a time when the Israeli army has waged a devastating war on Gaza since Oct. 7 that has resulted in the killing of at least 23,084 Palestinians and wounding 58,926 others . <p> Israeli authorities claim that attacks by Hamas in early October have killed around 1,200 Israelis . <p> The Israeli onslaught has left Gaza in ruins , with 60% of the enclave 's infrastructure damaged or destroyed and nearly 2 million residents displaced amid acute shortages of food , clean water and medicine
