<h> Oil jumps around 4pc as Middle East tensions escalate <p> Crude oil prices jumped around 4 percent for the fourth straight session on Friday after the US and UK struck Houthi targets in Yemen and Iran seized a tanker in the Gulf of Oman , stoking fears of an escalation in geopolitical tensions that could threaten trade and oil flows . <p> As of 1235 hours GMT , Brent , the international benchmark for two-thirds of the world 's oil , gained $2.76 ( +3.57 percent ) to reach $80.17 a barrel . Similarly , the West Texas Intermediate ( WTI ) , the main oil benchmark for North America , went up by $2.69 ( +3.74 percent ) to $74.71 a barrel . <p> Brent ended last week higher by 2.23 percent , while WTI closed the week up by 3.01 percent and both are on the way to register another weekly gain . Both benchmarks shed more than 10 percent in 2023 on a year-on-year basis . <p> On the other hand , the price of Russian Sokol increased by $1.48 ( +2.07 percent ) to $72.95 .
