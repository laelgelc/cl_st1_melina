<h> Unending brutality <p> By Web Desk <p> January 22 , 2024 <p> The conflict between Israel and Gaza has been going on for over a hundred days now . Many Palestinians have suffered , facing death , eviction , and hunger . Despite this crisis , Western governments have not strongly condemned Israel . The US , a major supporter of Israel , has been hesitant to call for a ceasefire . Israel 's attempt to eliminate Hamas seems unlikely and strategically unsuccessful . <p> The international community , including the UN and OIC , has failed to stop the violence . South Africa took the matter to the International Court of Justice , but enforcing any rulings may be tough due to US support for Israel . Without decisive action , the situation in Gaza could get worse , leading to more bloodshed and conflict in the Middle East .
