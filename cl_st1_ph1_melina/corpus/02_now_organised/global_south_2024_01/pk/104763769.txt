SCREENGRAB <p> While lauding the operational excellence of the Pakistan Air Force ( PAF ) , Chief of Army Staff ( COAS ) General Syed Asim Munir on Tuesday stated that the armed forces of Pakistan are fully prepared to thwart any aggression . <p> According to Inter-Services Public Relations ( ISPR ) , the army chief expressed these remarks during an induction and operationalisation ceremony at an operational base of PAF . He attended the distinguished ceremony as the chief guest <p> Upon his arrival at the base , General Munir was received by Chief of the Air Staff ( CAS ) Air Chief Marshal Zaheer Ahmed Baber Sidhu .
