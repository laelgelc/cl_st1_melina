<h> Gold price advances to Rs215,000 a tola <p> Gold price snapped a three-day losing streak in the local market on Friday and increased by Rs1,300 per tola , following an uptick in prices in the international markets . According to the data provided by Karachi Sarafa Association , the price for one tola of 24-karat gold increased to Rs215,000 from Rs213,700 . Similarly , the price for 10 grams of 24-karat gold increased to Rs184,328 from Rs183,213 , showing an uptick of Rs1,115 . Similarly , the price for 10 grams of 22-karat gold increased to Rs168,976 from Rs167,945 , surging by Rs1,031 . Despite this increase , the gold price is likely to go down this week on week-on-week . Earlier , gold price in the country declined last week by Rs550 per tola . It was the second consecutive week when the prices of the yellow metal decreased in the country . On the other hand , as of 1155 hours GMT , gold futures in the international market were available at $2,031 per ounce , showing an uptick of $8.60 .
