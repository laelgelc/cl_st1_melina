<h> Israeli minister lays out post-war Gaza plan as fighting rages <p> Jerusalem - Israel 's defence minister has publicly presented for the first time proposals for the post-war administration of Gaza , where the military on Friday reported deadly overnight strikes . <p> Defence Minister Yoav Gallant 's plan for the " day after " , shared with the media late Thursday but not yet adopted by Israel 's war cabinet , says that neither Israel nor Palestinians will govern Gaza and rejects future Jewish settlements there . <p> The minister 's broad outline was unveiled on the eve of US Secretary of State Antony Blinken 's fourth trip to the region October 7 attack that triggered the war . <p> Questions over the future of the besieged Palestinian territory have been front of mind for many as calls mount for a ceasefire after nearly three months of devastating fighting . Much of the Gaza Strip has been reduced to rubble , while civilian deaths have soared and the UN has warned of a humanitarian crisis that has left hundreds of thousands displaced , facing famine and disease . <p> Bombing continued through the night in the southern @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of central Gaza , according to AFP correspondents . <p> The Israeli army said its forces had " struck over 100 targets " across Gaza over the past 24 hours , including military positions , rocket launch sites and weapons depots . <p> A fighter jet hit the central area of Bureij overnight , after what the army described in a statement as an attempted attack on an Israeli tank . <p> According to Gallant 's proposed outline , the war will continue until Israel has dismantled " military and governing capabilities " of Palestinians and secured the return of hostages taken on October 7 . <p> After Israel achieves its objectives -- for which the proposal sets no timeline -- Palestinian " civil committees " will begin assuming the territory 's governance , it said . " Palestinian bodies will be in charge , with the condition that there will be no hostile actions or threats against the State of Israel . "
