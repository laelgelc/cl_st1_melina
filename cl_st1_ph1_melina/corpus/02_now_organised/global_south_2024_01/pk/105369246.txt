<h> Oil prices rise amid strong US demand , weak dollar <p> Oil prices rose on Wednesday , mainly influenced by demand increases in the world 's biggest oil consumer , the US , and a weak US dollar . <p> The international benchmark crude Brent traded at $79.70 per barrel at 09.49 a.m. local time ( 0649 GMT ) , a 0.19% rise from the closing price of $79.55 a barrel in the previous trading session on Tuesday . <p> The American benchmark , West Texas Intermediate ( WTI ) , traded at the same time at $74.48 per barrel , up 0.15% from Tuesday 's close of $74.37 per barrel . <p> The American Petroleum Institute 's ( API ) data showed a larger than expected drop in crude oil inventories in the US , easing market concerns over falling demand .
