<p> WASHINGTON : U.S. Central Intelligence Agency Director William Burns plans to meet with Israeli , Egyptian and Qatari officials in coming days for talks on a potential Gaza hostage deal , a source familiar with the matter told Reuters on Thursday . <p> The Washington Post , which first reported Burns ' trip , said Israel has proposed a two-month pause in fighting to allow for the phased release of the hostages still being held following Hamas ' Oct. 7 attack on Israel , citing officials familiar with the matter . <p> Burns " has been ... involved in helping us with the hostage deal that was in place and trying to help us pursue another one , " White House national security spokesman John Kirby told reporters traveling with U.S. President Joe Biden aboard Air Force One , referring specific questions to the agency .
