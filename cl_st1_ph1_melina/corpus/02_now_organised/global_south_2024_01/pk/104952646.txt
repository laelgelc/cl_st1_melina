<h> Oil rises $1 as Middle East crisis , inflation fight in focus <p> Oil prices rose by more than $1 on Tuesday after sliding in the previous session as markets weighed Middle East tensions against demand worries and rising OPEC supply . <p> Brent crude futures rose $1.33 , or 1.75% , to $77.45 a barrel at 1131 GMT , while U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude futures gained $1.29 , or 1.82% , to $72.06 a barrel . <p> Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and an ongoing supply outage in Libya offered support to prices on Tuesday , analysts said . <p> " On the supply side , there are some bullish factors from the closure of Libya 's largest oilfield , which has affected around 0.3 million barrels per day of oil production , " said Suvro Sarkar , energy sector team lead at DBS Bank . <p> Some major shipping firms are still avoiding the Red Sea .
