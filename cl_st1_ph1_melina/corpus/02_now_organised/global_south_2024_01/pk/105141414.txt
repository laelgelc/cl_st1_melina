" <p> Humanitarian aid <p> Guterres also outlined certain basics for an effective humanitarian operation in Gaza . <p> Guterres said the UN and its partners can not effectively deliver aid while Gaza is under such heavy and unrelenting bombardment by Israel . <p> He also noted that the aid operation faces " significant hurdles " at the Gaza border and major impediments to distribution within Gaza . <p> " This includes repeated denials of access to the north , where hundreds of thousands of people remain . Since the start of the year , just 7 of 29 missions to deliver aid to the north have been able to proceed , " he added .
