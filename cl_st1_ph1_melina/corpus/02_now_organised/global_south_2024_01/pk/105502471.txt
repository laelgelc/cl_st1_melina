<h> Israel urges more countries to cut financial support to UN agency <p> Israeli Foreign Minister Israel Katz urged more countries on Saturday to stop funding the UN Agency for Palestinian Refugees ( UNRWA ) following allegations of involvement by employees in the Oct. 7 attacks on Israel . <p> " UNRWA 's ties with Hamas , providing refuge for terrorists , and perpetuating its rule are undeniable , " Katz wrote on X. <p> He urged authorities to remove the leadership of UNRWA and said it should be subjected to a comprehensive investigation regarding their awareness of the actions . <p> " In Gaza 's rebuilding , UNRWA must be replaced with agencies dedicated to genuine peace and development , " he said . <p> The US , UK , Italy , Australia , Canada , Finland , and the Netherlands decided to suspend funding to UNRWA following claims by Israel on Friday . <p> UNRWA said it terminated contracts with several staffers for their alleged involvement in the attack . <p> Norway welcomed investigations of some staffers but said it would continue to support Palestinians via UNRWA . <p> Despite a provisional ruling Friday by the ICJ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Gaza , Israel continued its onslaught against the coastal enclave where at least 26,257 Palestinians have been killed , mostly women and children , and 64,797 injured since Oct. 7 , according to Palestinian health authorities . <p> The Israeli offensive has left 85% of Gaza 's population internally displaced amid acute shortages of food , clean water and medicine , while 60% of the enclave 's infrastructure was damaged or destroyed , according to the UN .
