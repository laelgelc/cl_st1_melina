<h> Pakistan sends 4th aid consignment for people in Gaza <p> Pakistan sends fourth consignment of relief goods to Gaza from Nur Khan Air base by a special flight of the Pakistan Air Force . <p> The Palestinian Ambassador to Pakistan , Ahmed Jawad Rabi'i , the NDMA , the Armed Forces , and Jalil Abbas Jilani , the acting Foreign Minister , were all present at the airbase . <p> Speaking on this occasion , Jalil Abbas Jilani expressed full support to the Palestinian people . <p> He condemned Israel 's use of disproportionate and indiscriminate force against civilians , especially women and children in Gaza . <p> The foreign minister also reiterated full support to South Africa 's genocide case against Israel at the International Court of Justice . He called for an immediate ceasefire as well as ensuring the immediate delivery of humanitarian aid to the people of Gaza . <p> Palestinian ambassador expressed gratitude to the Pakistani government for the aid supplies . <p> Essential supplies such as hygiene kits , dry food products , and surgical and medical supplies are included in the fourth relief shipment .
