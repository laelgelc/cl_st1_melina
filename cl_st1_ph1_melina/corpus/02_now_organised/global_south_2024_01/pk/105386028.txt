" <p> Israel has pounded the Gaza Strip since a cross-border incursion by Palestinian group Hamas last October , in which nearly 1,200 Israelis are believed to have been killed . <p> About 85% of Gazans have since been displaced , all of them are food insecure and the health system is collapsing .
