And in terms of Israel 's own security , the Arab piece of the equation , and the Palestinian piece , that 's the way to true , lasting security , " he added . <p> " You 're in a place right now where , again , Arab @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to do things in their relationship with Israel they were never prepared to do before , " Blinken said .
