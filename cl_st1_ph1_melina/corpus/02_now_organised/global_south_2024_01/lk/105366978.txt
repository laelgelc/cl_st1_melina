<h> Sri Lanka Navy says ready for Red Sea deployment <p> Colombo , Jan 24 ( Daily Mirror ) - Although a date has not been fixed , the Sri Lanka Navy said yesterday that it is now prepared to send a deployment to the Red Sea to assist in defending shipping lanes from Houthi activities , once the government greenlights and authorizes the deployment . <p> Navy spokesperson Capt . Gayan Wickramasinghe told the Daily Mirror that discussions are still underway at higher levels regarding the Red Sea deployment , but the Sri Lanka Navy is ready . " We are now on standby to send our deployment to the Red Sea at any time , " he said . <p> On January 3 , during an event , President Ranil Wickremesinghe said that the government would deploy a Navy vessel to the Red Sea to safeguard shipping lanes . He said that the disruption of these lanes would lead to increased freight charges and cargo costs , affecting import prices in the country .
