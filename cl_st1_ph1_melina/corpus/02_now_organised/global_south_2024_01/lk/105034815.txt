<p> UN court hears South Africa genocide case against IsraelPosted on January 11th , 2024 <h> Courtesy Adaderana <p> The UN 's International Court of Justice is hearing a case brought by South Africa accusing Israel of committing genocide against Palestinians in Gaza . <p> The submission also calls on the court to order Israel to stop military operations there . <p> The ICJ will deliver only an opinion on the genocide allegation as the case is not a criminal trial , although it is being closely watched . <p> Israel has vehemently rejected the accusation as baseless " . <p> There were angry scenes outside the ICJ 's Peace Palace on Thursday as Dutch police struggled to keep groups of Palestinian and Israeli supporters apart . <p> Hundreds of people waving Palestinian flags gathered outside the ICJ , calling for a ceasefire . Israeli supporters set up a screen showing images of some of the hostages still being held in Gaza . <p> South Africa will present its case on Thursday and Israel its defence on Friday . <p> In its submission , South Africa says Israel 's actions are intended to bring about the destruction of a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ group " . <p> It says Israel 's actions include killing Palestinians in Gaza , causing them serious bodily and mental harm , and inflicting on them conditions of life calculated to bring about their physical destruction " . <p> It calls for provisional measures " to be implemented by the court as a matter of urgency , including that Israel cease all military activities in Gaza . <p> The ICJ is the UN 's top court , based in the Hague in the Netherlands . Its rulings are theoretically legally binding on parties to the ICJ -- which include Israel and South Africa -- but are not enforceable . <p> In 2022 , the court ordered Russia to immediately suspend military operations " in Ukraine , an order that was ignored . <p> Under international law , genocide is defined as committing one or more acts with the intention to destroy , in whole or in part , a national , ethnic , racial or religious group . <p> Unlike the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) , the ICJ can not prosecute individuals for crimes such as genocide @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ other international institutions . <p> On Wednesday , South African President Cyril Ramaphosa said : Our opposition to the ongoing slaughter of the people of Gaza has driven us as a country to approach the ICJ . " <p> Israeli President Isaac Herzog called the accusations atrocious and preposterous " . <p> We will be in the International Court of Justice and we will present proudly our case of using self-defence ... under humanitarian law , " he said . <p> He added that the Israeli army was doing its utmost under extremely complicated circumstances on the ground to make sure that there will be no unintended consequences and no civilian casualties " . <p> The ICJ could rule quickly on South Africa 's request for Israel to suspend its military campaign -- but a final ruling on whether Israel is committing genocide could take years . <p> William Schabas , a professor of international law who chaired a UN committee investigating Israel 's conflict with Hamas in 2014 , said there is a low bar for the court to issue an injunction against Israel . <p> He told the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ elements that support its case " , for the court to issue an order , although this would not involve making a definite decision as to whether or not genocide is actually taking place " . <p> South Africa has been highly critical of Israel 's military operation in Gaza , and its governing African National Congress has a long history of solidarity with the Palestinian cause . <p> It sees parallels with its struggle against apartheid -- a policy of racial segregation and discrimination enforced by the white-minority government in South Africa against the country 's black majority , until the first democratic elections , in 1994 . <p> In Gaza , more than 23,350 people have been killed , according to the Hamas-run health ministry , since the war began in the aftermath of Hamas 's 7 October attacks on southern Israel .
