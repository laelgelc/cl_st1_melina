The President also said that cargo ships have ceased using the Red Sea route owing to persistent attacks by Yemen 's Houthi rebels . The re-routing of these vessels around South Africa is expected to indirectly contribute to an escalation in the prices of goods . <p> Consequently , President Wickremesinghe stated that a decision has been made for Sri Lanka to participate in the mission aimed at safeguarding against attacks by Houthi rebels .
