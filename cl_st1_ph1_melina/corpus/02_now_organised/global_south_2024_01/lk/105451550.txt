Not even the vaunted ' World Court ' has thought it urgent enough to demand a ceasefire in that tortured , ' Holy ' , land . <p> Next week , Pakistan goes to elections that are months late . In early April , India expects to vote but , given the early pre-poll campaign fervour of the ruling Bharatiya Janatha Party ( BJP ) , Indians may feel like they will be queuing up to vote much sooner . As far as media statistics indicate , the ICJ ruling at The Hague , Netherlands , was the most watched Court ruling ever . <p> Due to the false expectations of many across the world who are impassioned by the sick spectacle of ongoing genocide done so blatantly and with such impunity , the ICJ ruling was actually a damp squib . <p> The 17 judges of the ICJ 's full bench , ruled on many things but not exactly what litigant South Africa asked for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for , and certainly not what the suffering , starving , dying , shell-shocked Gazans so desperately need . The World Court , after all , is not a conventional , neutrally appointed , judicial institution . What the Court delivered was well within that political power framework . <p> international law <p> Its recognition of the legality of the South African plaint is no more than the assertion of the Court 's ' stamp ' at one of the more crucial junctures of international law .
