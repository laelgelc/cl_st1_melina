Israeli officials declined to comment . <p> If Israel is behind the attack it could mark a major escalation in the Middle East conflict . Hezbollah leader Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah has vowed to retaliate against any Israeli targeting of Palestinian officials in Lebanon . <p> Hamas official Bassem Naim confirmed to The Associated Press that Arouri was killed in the blast .
