Reuters <p> Is Israel guilty of genocide in Gaza ? This question is central to the case that South Africa has filed before the International Court of Justice in The Hague , a key judicial body of the United Nations . <p> Yet , the question of guilt is not Israel 's most urgent concern . The court may indeed find that the people of Gaza are victims of an attempted genocide by the Israeli army . But that will be dealt with later , in a procedure that could take years . <p> What Israel now fears above all is that the court will issue " provisional measures " , possibly as early as January , requiring Israel to stop its military operation in the Gaza Strip @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this will be " to protect the rights of the parties " while the case is before the court . <p> That fear became clear during the first preparatory session on January 11 and 12 at the Peace Palace in The Hague . On the first day , South Africa presented its plea . The next day it was the defendant 's turn . Of course , the Israeli counselors argued that the accusation of genocide is without merit . <p> However , the main purpose of this was to demonstrate that prima facie there was no question of genocide . In that case , the court has no jurisdiction and there would be no need to open a case at all . On the question of whether is it prima facie plausible that the Genocide Convention has been violated , the judges must rule at short notice , even before the substantive hearing of the case . <h> Provisional measures <p> The second matter on which the judges must quickly provide clarity is that of provisional measures . If Israel ignores the court order , it will confirm an accusation that it already faces , which is that it does not care about international law -- as the illegal settlements in the West Bank indicate . <p> The International Court of Justice is not a UN body with a tarnished reputation but a highly authoritative institution . It handles disputes between states and sometimes provides legal advice . More than anything else , its judgements are decisive for the state of international law . In fact , the Israeli army in January scaled down operations and withdrew some of its troops from the Gaza Strip .
