<p> US negotiators , spearheaded by CIA Chief Bill Burns , are actively engaged in talks to broker a potential deal between Israel and Hamas . Key intelligence and diplomatic figures , including leaders of Mossad , Shin Bet , Egyptian intelligence , and the Qatari Prime Minister , are convening in Paris for high-level discussions . The primary focus is on securing a two-month ceasefire between Israel and Hamas , featuring phased releases of over 100 hostages , initially prioritising women , elderly , and wounded captives , followed by the release of Israeli soldiers and civilian men . The evolving agreement also encompasses provisions for increased humanitarian aid into Gaza . While seen as a positive step , the proposed deal is not considered a conclusive resolution , but rather a foundation for future diplomatic efforts . US officials express optimism about its potential to pave the way for a more enduring solution to the conflict .
