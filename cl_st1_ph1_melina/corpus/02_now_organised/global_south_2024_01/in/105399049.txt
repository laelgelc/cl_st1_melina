<h> Rs 1.4 lakh/month for a carpenter : Why Indians are queuing up to work in war-torn Israel <p> As states like Haryana and UP begin the recruitment process , trade union activists have accused the Centre of bypassing the safety rules meant for workers going to conflict zones <p> For the past few months , the Narendra Modi-led government has been making a push for recruitment of Indians to work in Israel as part of a government-to-government agreement . New Delhi 's move is aimed at trying to help its West Asian ally overcome manpower shortage in key sectors , triggered by the ongoing war in Gaza.The Haryana and Uttar Pradesh state governments have recently come out with notifications calling interested candidates for interviews and job screenings . The Haryana notification claims it is aimed at filling 10,000 positions in Israel .
