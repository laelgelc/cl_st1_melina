At one level , this is just a coincidental alignment of electoral cycles . But the idea of close to two billion voters consciously shaping their future is an exhilarating one . There is also a bit of foreboding , about whether this marvellous act of political agency will measure up to the problems that confront the world . <p> The case for foreboding about these elections is strong .
