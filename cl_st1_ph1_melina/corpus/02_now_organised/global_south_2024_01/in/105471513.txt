<p> In a tragic development , three US Army troops lost their lives , and approximately two dozen soldiers sustained injuries during an overnight drone attack on a small US outpost in Jordan . This marks the first instance of US troops being killed by enemy fire in the Middle East since the commencement of the Gaza war . The assumed culprits behind this deadly attack are Iranian-backed militants , resulting in the deaths of three American service members and injuries to several others . The targeted outpost , Tower 22 , is located in Jordan , near the border with Syria , making this assault a significant escalation in the broader Middle East situation . The casualties have been officially confirmed by the US Central Command , adding gravity to the incident.Despite the severity of this attack , the US reported that over 158 attempted attacks on US and coalition forces in Iraq and Syria were unsuccessful , underscoring the resilience of the defenses . Curiously , the reasons for the failure of air defenses to intercept the drone remain unclear , marking the first known attack on Tower 22 since October 17 .
