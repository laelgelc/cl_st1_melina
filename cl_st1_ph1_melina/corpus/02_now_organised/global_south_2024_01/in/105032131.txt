<h> South Africa Sack U-19 Captain For Comments On Gaza War To Avoid Conflict And Violence At U-19 World Cup <p> Cricket South Africa removed U-19 captain David Teeger from his leadership duties a few days before the U-19 World Cup after he praised young Israeli soldiers at a Jewish event . CSA said that it was forced to make such a decision to avoid conflict and violence at the U-19 World Cup . <p> Cricket South Africa has sacked their captain David Teeger following his pro-Israel comments . The 19-year-old stirred a controversy after he praised young soldiers in Israel at a Jewish event . Following an inquiry after complaints from the Palestine Solidarity Alliance , CSA decided to remove the 19-year-old captain just a week before the U-19 World Cup . <p> CSA said that the decision was taken to avoid conflict and violence during the U-19 World Cup . <p> " We have been advised that protests related to the war in Gaza can be anticipated at the venues for the tournament , " the board said . " We have also been advised that they are likely to focus on the position of the SA @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and that there is a risk that they could result in conflict or even violence , including between rival groups of protestors . " <p> CSA added that the board has the responsibility for the safety of all the participating teams in the U-19 WC and the decision was best in the interests of all the players , the SA U19 team and David himself . <p> " CSA has a primary duty to safeguard the interests and safety of all those involved in the World Cup and must accordingly respect the expert advice of those responsible for the safety of participants and spectators . In all the circumstances , CSA has decided that David should be relieved of the captaincy for the tournament . This is in the best interests of all the players , the SA U19 team and David himself , " it added . <p> CSA further confirmed that David will remain an integral part of the South Africa squad . South Africa are set to host the U-19 World Cup from January 19 . <p> " David will remain an important and active member of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ success in the tournament .
