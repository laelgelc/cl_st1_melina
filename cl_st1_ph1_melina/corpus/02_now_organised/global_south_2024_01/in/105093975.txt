On Thursday , Israel faced down accusations under the United Nations ' Genocide Convention , drawn up after WWII following the atrocities committed against the Jewish people during the Holocaust . <p> Two weeks ago , South Africa filed the case against Israel at the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) , accusing it of crimes of genocide against Palestinians in Gaza .
