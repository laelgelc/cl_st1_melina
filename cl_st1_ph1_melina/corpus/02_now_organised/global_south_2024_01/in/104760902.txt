<p> Arouri was one of the founders of Hamas ' military wing . He headed the Palestine-based group 's presence in the West Bank . Israel 's prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu had threatened to kill him even before the Israel-Hamas war began on October 7 , Associated Press reported . <p> While Israel is yet to take responsibility of the strike on Beirut that killed Arouri , a US defense official told The Washington Post that Tel Aviv was behind the attack . Meanwhile , Hamas vowed that the killing will not " undermine the continued brave resistance " in Gaza . <p> Senior Hamas official Izzat al-Rishq said in a statement , " It proves once more the utter failure of the enemy to achieve any of its aggressive goals in the Gaza Strip . " Lebanon 's caretaker Prime Minister Najib Mikati condemned the " new Israeli crime " and warned that Jerusalem is aiming to drag Lebanon into a new phase of confrontations .
