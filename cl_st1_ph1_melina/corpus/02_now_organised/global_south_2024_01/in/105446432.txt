<p> The Israel-Hamas war broke out on October 7 , 2023 , after the Palestinian militant group unleashed a deadly terror attack in southern Israel , killing around 1,200 . Since then , Israel has waged a war on Hamas and has vowed to not stop its Gaza offensive until it " achieves complete victory " . Since October 7 , Israeli bombardment and strikes have killed nearly 26,000 Palestinians in Gaza . <p> Amid several claims that staff members were involved in the October 7 Hamas attack on Israel , several countries have stopped funding for the United Nations refugee agency for Palestinians ( UNRWA ) . Britain , Italy , the Netherlands and Finland became the most recent countries to pause funding for UNRWA . <p> Earlier , the United States , Australia and Canada had paused funding to the aid agency , which is a crucial source of support for people in Gaza , following the allegations put forth by Israel .
