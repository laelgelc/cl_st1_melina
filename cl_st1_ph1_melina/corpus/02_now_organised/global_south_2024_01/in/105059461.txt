<h> How America 's elite universities came under fire <p> The culture wars have arrived on campus . Moderation and nuance are the need of the hour <p> With the resignation of University of Pennsylvania President Liz Magill followed by that of Harvard President Claudine Gay , America 's culture wars caught up with its elite educational institutions . Heavily politicised controversies that led to the resignations touch upon weighty issues such as equality , merit and affirmative action . Given America 's powerful cultural influence across the world , including in India , those issues merit analysis.The resignations followed pressure from donors , alumni and politicians , after large-scale campus protests broke out against Israel 's bombardment of the Gaza strip . The protests have been tagged as " anti-semitic " and the presidents of Penn , Harvard and MIT -- among America 's most storied universities -- were hauled over the coals by a congressional committee .
