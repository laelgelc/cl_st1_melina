<p> Sanaa : The US Military on Tuesday carried out a strike in a Houthi-controlled area of Yemen . The attack targeted Houthi anti-ship ballistic missiles , US officials told Reuters . <p> As per the officials , the strike targeted four anti-ship missiles . The development was reported a day after Houthi rebels in Yemen fired a missile , at a US-owned and operated container ship M/V Gibraltar Eagle , officials said . There were no reports of injuries in the attack . The US-owned ship was reportedly hit by an " unidentified projectile " . The attack was carried out when it was sailing almost 160 km off the Gulf of Aden . <p> Notably , in recent times , Houthis have started targeting merchant vessels passing through the Red Sea . <p> Last week , the United States and the United Kingdom launched an initial wave of strikes to against Houthis . Notably , the Houthis control the most populous parts of Yemen . They have launched attacks on commercial ships in support of Palestinians in the war against Israel in Gaza .
