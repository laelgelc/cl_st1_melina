<p> Iran 's President Ebrahim Raisi recently met with Turkish President Tayyip Erdogan in Turkey . The talks in Ankara primarily focused on the Gaza conflict and potential collaboration in the energy sector . The leaders are slated to co-chair a Turkish-Iranian business council , opening the door for potential agreements . Despite Turkey supporting a two-state solution for the Israeli-Palestinian conflict , it has strongly criticized Israel 's actions in Gaza , calling for an immediate ceasefire and endorsing legal measures , even alleging genocide . Notably , Turkey 's maintenance of commercial ties with Israel , amid domestic and international criticism , sets it apart from Western allies and some Arab nations . Unlike them , Turkey , as a NATO member , does not designate the Palestinian group Hamas as a terrorist organization . The broader geopolitical landscape reveals Iran leading the Axis of Resistance , a coalition confronting Israel and Western allies , including Hamas and Shia Muslim groups . Turkish President Erdogan criticized recent US and British strikes targeting Iran-backed Houthi forces in response to Red Sea ship attacks , denouncing the actions as a disproportionate use of force .
