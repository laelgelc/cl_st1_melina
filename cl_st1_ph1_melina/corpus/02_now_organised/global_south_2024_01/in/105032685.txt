<p> On January 11 , South Africa accused Israel of genocide in Gaza before the International Court of Justice -- the UN 's top court . The country , which moved this court asked it to issue orders that will compel Israel to suspend military operations in the Palestinian region . <p> Adila Hassim , advocate of South Africa 's high court , presented the case for why Israel 's actions can be clearly construed as genocidal . <p> Below is her submission in full .
