<p> Israel plans military control in post-war Gaza , emphasizes operational freedom and strategic approach <p> 4 hours ago <h> Israel plans military control in post-war Gaza , emphasizes operational freedom and strategic approach <p> Jan 30 , 2024 , 11:01PM ISTSource : TOI.in <p> Defense Minister Yoav Gallant asserts that Israel will maintain military control over the Gaza Strip post-war , allowing operational freedom similar to the current situation in the West Bank . Gallant distinguishes between military and civilian control , citing the recent Jenin operation as an example of achieving military freedom without civilian dominance . He believes this approach is feasible in Gaza but acknowledges it will take time . The ongoing efforts to dismantle Hamas 's military infrastructure are described as finite but progressing amid challenges . Gallant emphasizes the need for national unity as a prerequisite for achieving Israel 's war goals , including the elimination of Hamas 's military capabilities and leadership , as well as the return of hostages .
