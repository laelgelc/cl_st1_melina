<h> Israel-Hamas war : Palestinian death toll soars past 25,000 <h> In the Israel-Hamas war , women and children are the main victims , according to the United Nations ( UN ) . <p> Nearly 85 per cent of the population of Gaza fled their homes . ( Photo : Reuters ) <p> In Gaza , the Palestinian death toll from the war between Israel and Hamas has soared past 25,000 , the Gaza Health Ministry said on Sunday . The war has been going on for three months . <p> In the last 24 hours , at least 178 bodies were brought to Gaza 's hospitals along with nearly 300 injured people , said Health Ministry spokesperson Ashraf al-Qidra . In the Israel-Hamas war , women and children are the main victims , according to the United Nations ( UN ) . <p> The war started after Hamas surprisingly attacked Israel on October 7 , 2023 . In the attack , Palestinian militants murdered some 1,200 people . Most of them were civilians . They also took around 250 hostages , including men , women and kids . <p> Israel immediately responded . For three weeks , it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ground invasion into northern Gaza took place . Now , ground operations are focused on the southern city of Khan Younis . <p> Nearly 85 per cent of the population of Gaza fled their homes . UN-run shelters and tent camps are packed with refugees . <p> Health Ministry of Gaza said a total of 25,105 Palestinians have been killed , and another 62,681 have been injured . Many casualties remain buried under the rubble from Israeli strikes , said Al-Qidra . <p> The army of Israel said it has killed around 9,000 militants , without providing evidence . Since the start of the Gaza offensive , the military says 195 of its soldiers have been killed .
