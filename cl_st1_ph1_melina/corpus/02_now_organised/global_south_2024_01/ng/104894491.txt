<h> Israeli troops destroy Hamas command in Gaza <p> The Israeli army says it has " completed the dismantling " of Hamas 's command structure in the northern Gaza Strip . <p> Army spokesman Daniel Hagari told reporters that Palestinian militants are now operating in the area only sporadically and " without commanders " . <p> He said Israel had killed around 8,000 militants in north Gaza . The BBC can not independently verify this number . <p> The Israel Defence Forces ( IDF ) are now focused on dismantling Hamas in south and central Gaza , he said . <p> Israel has killed more than 22,000 people since the war began , according to the Hamas-run health ministry . On Saturday it said it had recorded more than 120 deaths over the past 24 hours . <p> The territory has been devastated and most of the population of 2.3 million has been displaced . <p> Israel 's offensive started after Hamas gunmen launched a surprise attack on southern Israel on 7 October , killing 1,200 people , most of them civilians , and taking about 240 people hostage . <p> More than 120 remain , following agreed releases @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Israel would continue its campaign to " eliminate Hamas , return our hostages and ensure that Gaza will no longer be a threat to Israel " . <p> " We have to put everything aside ... until the complete victory is achieved , " Mr Netanyahu said in a statement . <p> Meanwhile , Hezbollah fired dozens of rockets at northern Israel on Saturday , in what it called a preliminary response to the killing of a top Hamas official in Beirut earlier this week . <p> Saleh al-Arouri was assassinated in a suspected Israeli attack in the Lebanese capital . <p> A statement by the Iranian-backed Lebanese movement says it hit an air traffic control base in Meron with 62 rockets .
