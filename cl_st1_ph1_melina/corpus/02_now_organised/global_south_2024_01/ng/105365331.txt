<h> IDoE : Nigeria 's education system gasping for breath <p> AS Nigeria joins the rest of the world to celebrate the International Day of Education today , the country must refocus its lenses on its education sector , which is gasping for breath . Initiated by UNESCO in 2018 , the 2024 theme , " Learning for Lasting Peace , " is apt for Nigeria , a country whose education is mired in poor funding , strikes , and shabby infrastructure . The Nigerian leadership should recognise that education is a right , not a privilege . <p> Presently , 250 million children and youth are out of school , and 763 million adults are illiterate , the UN body says . Nigeria alone contributes over 20 million children to that number , trailing India as the country with the second highest out-of-school children in the world . This is shameful . UNICEF in 2023 stated that over 75 per cent of children aged seven to 14 years in Nigeria could not read simple sentences or solve basic mathematics . <p> In Nigeria , terrorism , banditry and abductions have brought schools in the North to their knees @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ were shut in northern Nigeria from 2020 to 2022 due to violence , affecting 1.3 million children . It projected that these children would lose $3.4 billion in lifetime earnings . In this period , 1,436 pupils and 17 teachers suffered kidnapping ; 16 pupils died . <p> Unfortunately , too many children are growing up without books . UNESCO stated on Monday that only two per cent of children under age five in sub-Saharan Africa are growing up with three or more children 's books in their homes . Even in places where books are available , their high cost makes it unaffordable for families . Thousands learn in decrepit structures or under trees in the rural areas in Nigeria . <p> Therefore , it is inexplicable that most of the 36 states are guilty of spurning the support offered to them by the Federal Government through the Universal Basic Education scheme . The public tertiary institutions are groaning under strikes , inadequate hostel facilities and lecture halls . There is a shortage of lecturers as many are trooping overseas , exacerbated by the country 's depressing economy . <p> The Bola Tinubu administration , to be fair , has just released N683 billion to the tertiary sector and removed the universities from the Integrated Payroll and Personnel Information System as demanded by the Academic Staff Union of Universities . A student loan scheme is about to take off . <p> The challenge before the three tiers of government is to make education accessible to all irrespective of the social strata ; it should not be for the highest bidder . <p> Education plays a vital role in global peace discourses .
