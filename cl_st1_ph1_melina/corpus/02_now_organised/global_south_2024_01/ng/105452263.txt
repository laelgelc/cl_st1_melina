<h> Israeli Bombardment On Gaza Strip Results In martyrs , Casualties <p> Dozens of Palestinians , mostly children and women , were killed , and others were injured , as a result of the ongoing Israeli bombardment on the Gaza Strip , by land , sea , and air , on the 113th day of the aggression that began on October 7th last year . <p> The Palestinian news agency ( WAFA ) reported , citing medical sources , that martyrs and wounded arrived at Al-Aqsa Martyrs Hospital , following the Israeli shelling of several houses in Deir al-Balah in the central Gaza Strip , noting that 9 citizens were killed due to the Israeli shelling of homes in Khan Yunis . <p> The continuous shelling of Khan Yunis during the past 24 hours resulted in 28 martyrs and several injured . <p> The Palestinian Media Center stated that Israeli warplanes and artillery continued their violent raids and shelling on Saturday on various parts of the Gaza Strip , amid the concentration of the aggression on Khan Yunis , targeting homes , refugee camps , and streets , resulting in hundreds of martyrs and wounded . <p> Three martyrs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the Israeli shelling of a house belonging to the Al Siksik family in the Jenin neighborhood east of Rafah . <p> In a preliminary toll , the number of martyrs in the Strip since the start of the aggression has risen to more than 26,000 martyrs , with around 64,000 wounded , and thousands missing , most of whom are children and women , in addition to the massive destruction and unprecedented humanitarian catastrophe .
