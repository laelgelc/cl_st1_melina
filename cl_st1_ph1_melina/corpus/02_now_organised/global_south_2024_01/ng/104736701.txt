<h> Erdogan Calls On World To Put End To Killing Of Gaza People <p> " All countries ( and ) institutions need to take a united stand against the killing of innocent children and women in Gaza , " Erdogan said in his video message on New Year 's Eve , Anadolu Agency reports . " We hold hope for a better future for humanity . So-called democratic , freedom-loving countries must stop supporting bloody terrorists for this to happen , " the president said . Beginning with the war between Russia and Ukraine , he stressed , sincere attempts should be made to put an end to conflicts that cause suffering for people and waste national resources . " We need to see that wealth of societies , which has been exploited for centuries , their dignity trampled upon , is used for their future , prosperity , security , " he added . MNA/PR
