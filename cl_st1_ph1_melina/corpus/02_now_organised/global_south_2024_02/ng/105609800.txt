<h> About 81% of working Nigerians are not in a productive sector -- Taiwo Oyedele <p> The Chairman of the Presidential Committee on Fiscal Policy and Tax Reforms , Taiwo Oyedele , has said that about 81% of Nigerians in the employment pool are not in any productive sector of the economy . <p> Oyedele , who was speaking on Thursday at an Africa Trade and Investment Summit , said that while the unemployment rate in Nigeria is relatively low at 4.2% , most employed Nigerians do not add any meaningful value to the economy . <p> According to him , Nigeria has the same unemployment rate with the United Kingdom . Meanwhile , Nigeria still has over 113 million people living in multidimensional poverty . <p> He said , <p> " About 81% of people in employment in Nigeria are engaged in a non-productive sector of the economy .
