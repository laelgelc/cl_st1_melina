<h> Putin Says Russia Is Working To Free Hostages In Gaza Conflict <p> President Vladimir Putin told leaders of Russia 's Jewish community on Wednesday that Moscow had achieved " specific results " in diplomatic efforts to free hostages caught up in Israel 's conflict with Hamas , Russian news agencies reported . <p> The agencies said Putin made the comments at a meeting with Russia 's chief rabbi , Berl Lazar , and the head of the Federation of Jewish Communities , Alexander Boroda . Putin said Russia had made use of its contacts with the political wing of Hamas . <p> " You know , since the situation became more tense in the Middle East , Russia has been doing everything to help people who became hostages , " Putin was quoted as saying . <p> Russia , which has drawn closer to Israel 's arch-rival Iran since the outbreak of the nearly two-year-old Ukraine conflict , has restated its support for Palestinian statehood , and criticised Israeli military action in the Gaza Strip . <p> Moscow also has said the violence in the Middle East is a reflection of US policy failures in the region . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after Russia 's foreign ministry told the Israeli ambassador , Simona Halperin , of its " negative reaction " to her criticisms of Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov and Russian policy . <p> Authorities criticised her " unacceptable comments " in an interview with Russian newspaper Kommersant in which she said Lavrov had played down the importance of the Holocaust and that Russia was too friendly with Hamas.
