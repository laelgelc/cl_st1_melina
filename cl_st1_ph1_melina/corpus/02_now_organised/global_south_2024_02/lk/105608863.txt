<p> In war , people die for absurd reasons or often no reason at all . They die due to accidents of birth , the misfortune of being born in the wrong place -- Cambodia or Gaza , Afghanistan or Ukraine -- at the wrong time . They die due to happenstance , choosing to shelter indoors when they should have taken cover outside or because they ventured out into a hell-storm of destruction when they should have stayed put . They die in the most gruesome ways -- shot in the street , obliterated by artillery , eviscerated by air strikes . Their bodies are torn apart , burned , or vaporized by weapons designed to destroy people .
