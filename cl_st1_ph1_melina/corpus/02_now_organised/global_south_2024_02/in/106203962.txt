<h> India and Middle East : The Evolving Dynamics <p> Let multi-alignments , rather than a singular focus , be the basis of its policy recalibration . <p> India must remain nimble , constantly ready to respond to the escalating security concerns in the Middle East . However , this agility should not entail impulsive shifts in foreign policy or inconsistencies with its long-term objectives . The calibrated shift must consider the emergence of multiple regional powers , each supported by a cohort of allies from both within and outside the region . The emerging multipolar situation , with more than one global or regional power calling the shots and , at times , punching above its weight , will not , however , be without its own set of peculiarities and imponderables . <p> With Iran , Saudi Arabia , and Israel emerging as the new regional powers , the Western bloc , currently led by the US , will need to reassess its allies and adversaries . This re-evaluation will necessitate acknowledging both formal and less recognized entities , including armed local groups in the countries of West Asia .
