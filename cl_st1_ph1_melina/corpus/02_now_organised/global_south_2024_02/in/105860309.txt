<p> The Israeli Defense Forces ( IDF ) released footage of a successful hostage rescue operation in Rafah , Gaza . Special forces from the Shin Bet security agency and the police 's Yamam unit conducted the operation to extract hostages Fernando Simon Marman and Norberto Louis Har . At around 1 a.m. , the forces arrived at the location and breached the second-floor apartment where the hostages were held , swiftly eliminating three terrorists guarding them . The IDF also conducted airstrikes in the area to support the extraction . Following the rescue , the hostages were evacuated in armored vehicles and airlifted to Sheba Medical Center , where they were reported to be in good condition after examination . The operation showcased coordinated efforts between various branches of Israeli security forces and highlighted the IDF 's transparency in military operations .
