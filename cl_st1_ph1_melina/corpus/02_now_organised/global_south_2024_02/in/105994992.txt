( File photo ) <p> External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar , US Secretary of State Antony Blinken and German Foreign Affairs Minister Annalena Baerbock held a discussion on several issues during one of the panels as part of the ongoing security conference in the German capital . The discussion was held at the Munich Security Conference ( MSC ) which is being conducted under the chairmanship of the German Ambassador to the US , Christoph Heusgen . <p> At the discussion , the leaders discussed the current situation in Gaza as the war between Israel and the Hamas militants continues . EAM Jaishankar stressed the need for a " permanent resolution " and " two-state solution " to the Israel-Hamas war . " There has to be a long-term and a permanent solution . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " he reportedly said at the conference . <p> Further , Jaishankar termed the October 7 attack on Israel " an act of terrorism " , however , said that the response taken by Israel should have been cautious of civilian casualties . <p> While talking about India 's global standing , he said , " It 's important to distinguish between being non-West and anti-West . I would categorise India as a country that is non-West but has an extremely strong relationship with Western countries , getting better by the day . " <p> At the discussion , he reaffirmed India 's stand and commitment to buy Russian oil despite respective sanctions on Moscow amid the war with Ukraine . Jaishankar said that it is difficult to have a unidimensional relationship in the world today . <p> The 60th Munich Security Conference ( MSC ) is being held from February 16 to 18 this year at its traditional venue , the Hotel Bayerischer Hof in Munich .
