Despotic rulers , tyrannical monarchs , and repressive political leaders have all been targeted in pursuit of political interests . Similarly , military leaders have been neutralised to obtain favourable outcomes in wars . In ancient Greece , several city states authorised and rewarded such actions to defend democratic rule , while in 15th and 16th century Europe , targeted assassinations were widespread and morally acceptable . <p> While there have been calls to ban such operations , these have not had much success .
