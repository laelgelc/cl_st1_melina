It is also known as the United States-Iran proxy war that began on October 19 , 2023 , when the Houthis from Yemen launched a series of attacks targeting southern Israel and ships traversing the Red Sea that it claimed were related to Israel . <p> According to CRISIL Ratings , India depends significantly on the Red Sea route through the Suez Canal for its trade with the Middle East , Europe , North America and North Africa .
