Israel will bolster its combat capabilities in Gaza war with European-trained Canines <p> Feb 06 , 2024 , 10:40PM ISTSource : TOI.in <p> Israel is procuring combat-trained dogs from Europe for deployment in the Gaza war after the IDF 's ' Oketz ' unit dogs faced challenges against Hamas ' large dogs . The revelation that 17 IDF dogs were killed in Gaza prompted Israel to expect new trained dogs , primarily of the Malino breed , from the Netherlands and Germany . The Israeli Ministry of Defense is revamping its strategy due to the critical failure of the Oketz unit 's dogs . Additionally , Israel faced accusations of deploying attack dogs on wounded Palestinians in a Gaza hospital in December , raising serious concerns about the use of force . Thousands of Palestinian animals , including pets and zoo animals , were reportedly casualties , emphasizing the broader impact of conflict on innocent lives .
