<p> Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau expressed strong condemnation for the recent attack on a mosque in Mississauga , Ontario . This incident revives memories of the 2017 mosque attack in Quebec City , resulting in six fatalities . Investigated as a hate crime , the recent assault with rocks thrown through the mosque 's window on the eve of a significant anniversary has shocked the nation . The surge in Islamophobia has raised concerns about escalating hatred in Canada , prompting Trudeau to not only condemn the act but also call for collective efforts to address the rising instances of Islamophobia . The National Council of Canadian Muslims identified this attack as part of a broader trend in Islamophobic hate , emphasizing that such incidents are indicative of a more significant issue . This unsettling event aligns with a surge in antisemitic and anti-Muslim hate crimes in Toronto , coinciding with the start of the Gaza conflict . The global stage has witnessed a distressing rise in antisemitism and Islamophobia since the October 7th attack by Hamas on Israel , leading to a military assault on the Gaza Strip with significant casualties .
