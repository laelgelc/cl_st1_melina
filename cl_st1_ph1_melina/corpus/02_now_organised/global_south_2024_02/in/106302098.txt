If life is sacred , logically , suicide is abhorrent . Philosopher David Hume , though , points out why this is not the case . One , given that human beings , as a fundamental part of their existence , often end life -- animal , human , plant -- and alter nature in various ways , an act of will towards one 's own existence is not against the natural order .
