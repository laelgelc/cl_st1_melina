<p> In a heart-wrenching update on the Israeli hostages held captive by Hamas , a recent report discloses that 32 out of over 130 hostages still in the grip of the terror group are no longer alive . This shocking revelation intensifies the somber reality on the eve of the fourth month of the intense war between Israel and the Gaza terror group . Emotions are running high as the plight of these hostages paints a poignant picture of the unbearable ordeal they endure.Citing a confidential assessment conducted by Israeli intelligence officials , The New York Times brought to light this distressing report amid ongoing efforts by negotiating parties to secure the release of the remaining hostages . Until now , the Israeli Defense Forces ( IDF ) had only confirmed the deaths of 29 captives still in Gaza . Reports now suggest that , in addition to the 32 confirmed deaths , the IDF is assessing that at least 20 more hostages may have also met a tragic fate.Officials have informed the families of the deceased , adding another layer of sorrow to this already devastating situation .
