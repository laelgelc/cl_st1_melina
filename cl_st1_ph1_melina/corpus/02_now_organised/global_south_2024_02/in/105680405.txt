While it is very early to predict the outcome of this election , there is little doubt that it will be close . <p> Losing presidential candidates from the two major parties usually fade into historical footnotes . While there have been cases where such candidates try again , at least in recent American political history , no sitting president who has lost the election has been renominated . <p> But Trump could prove to be an exception . Republican Party candidate Nikki Haley , former US Ambassador to the United Nations and Governor of South Carolina , is his sole contender . This is a change because , like other Trump challengers , Haley had once been reluctant to target Trump directly . <h> Haley unlikely to beat Trump <p> Haley 's end game is unclear since she is highly unlikely to be able to defeat Trump .
