<h> United Nations likely to vote on Tuesday on Gaza ceasefire ; US signals veto <p> Discussions between the USA , Egypt , Israel , and Qatar are on to seek a pause in the war and the freedom of hostages held by Hamas . ( Bloomberg ) <p> On Tuesday , the United Nations Security Council is likely to vote on an Algerian push for the 15-member body to demand an immediate humanitarian ceasefire in the Israel-Hamas war , said diplomats , a move the USA signaled it would veto . <p> Discussions between the USA , Egypt , Israel , and Qatar are on to seek a pause in the war and the freedom of hostages held by Hamas . <p> The Gaza war started when fighters from the Hamas militant group that runs Gaza attacked Israel on Oct. 7 . This led to the killing of 1,200 people . 253 hostages were captured . In revenge , Israel launched a military attack on Gaza that health authorities say has killed more than 28,000 Palestinians with thousands more bodies feared lost amid the ruins .
