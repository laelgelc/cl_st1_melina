<p> Families of Israelis held hostage by Hamas were forcefully denied entry to Israeli PM Benjamin Netanyahu 's Likud party 's weekly meeting , despite their persistent efforts . This sharply contrasts with their regular presence in the Knesset , where they advocate for increased government efforts to secure their loved ones ' release . The families have been actively lobbying for government intervention , intensifying their awareness-raising endeavors regarding their relatives held captive by Hamas in Gaza . Likud 's refusal to let these families speak raises questions about the party 's stance , and skepticism surrounds the Israeli authorities ' handling of the hostage situation . The situation adds fuel to the ongoing debate . In a heated exchange , Economy Minister Nir Barkat accuses Netanyahu of obstructing efforts to bring in foreign workers , escalating tensions and revealing internal conflicts over labor policies within the Israeli government .
