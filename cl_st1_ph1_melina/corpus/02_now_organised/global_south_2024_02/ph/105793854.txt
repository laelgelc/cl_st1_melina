<h> Ireland shuns handshakes with Israel at basketball qualifier <p> RIGA , Latvia -- The Ireland women 's team shunned normal pre-match courtesies when it faced Israel in EuroBasket qualifying on Thursday after being outraged by accusations of antisemitism by an Israeli player . <p> The accusations by Israel player Dor Saar on the Israeli Basketball Association 's official channels prompted Basketball Ireland to report them to governing body FIBA Europe . Forfeiting the match was rejected because Ireland would have faced sanctions . <p> Instead , there were no handshakes or other pleasantries before the qualifier in Riga , Latvia , an alternative venue because of the war in Gaza . <p> " Basketball Ireland informed FIBA Europe yesterday that as a direct result of recent comments made by Israeli players and coaching staff -- including inflammatory and wholly inaccurate accusations of antisemitism , published on official Israeli federation channels -- that our players will not be partaking in traditional pre-match arrangements with our upcoming opponents , " a statement read on Thursday . <p> " This includes exchanging of gifts , formal handshakes before or after the game , while our players will line up for the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Basketball Ireland fully supports our players in their decision . " <p> While Ireland defied pressure to boycott the match -- the federation said it was not feasible -- several players opted not to travel to Riga . <p> " It 's known that they are quite antisemitic and it 's no secret , and maybe that 's why a strong game is expected , " Saar , a United States-based student , said in an interview published by the Israeli Basketball Association . <p> " We have to show that we 're better than them and win . We talk about it among ourselves , We know they do n't love us and we will leave everything on the field always and in this game especially . " <p> Israel won 87-57 . The teams are in a group with France and Latvia . Ireland is scheduled to host Israel on Nov. 10.
