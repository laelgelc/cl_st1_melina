<h> Over 100 killed as Israeli forces shell crowd waiting for aid in Gaza <p> At least 104 Palestinians were killed and 760 others injured when Israeli forces shelled a crowd waiting for humanitarian aid south of Gaza City on Thursday , the Health Ministry in the enclave said . <p> According to witnesses , hundreds of Palestinians were waiting to receive aid near Dowar al-Nablusi when they came under Israeli fire , eyewitnesses said . <p> The government media office accused the Israeli army of killing the victims " in cold blood " as Israel was aware of their presence in the area " to obtain food and aid .
