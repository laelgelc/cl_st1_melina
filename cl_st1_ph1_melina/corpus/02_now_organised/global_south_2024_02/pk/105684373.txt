<h> We do not need a Tonkin Gulf Resolution <p> August 7 , 1964 , was a dark day for the United States when Congress passed with only two dissenting votes the Tonkin Gulf Resolution . The resolution was predicated on a non-existent North Vietnamese PT boat attack on two American destroyers on patrol in the Gulf . And it led to the Vietnam War we lost . <p> Is America repeating a similar blunder in responding to Houthi attacks on Red Sea shipping and the latest Islamic military groups ' drone strike that killed three American service personnel and wounded dozens more in Jordan ? The critical difference was that these attacks , unlike in the Tonkin Gulf , took place . <p> Islamic groups and the Houthis have plagiarized Hamas 's October 7th attack on Israel diabolically designed to elicit an Israeli overreaction . Sadly , that attack succeeded perhaps far more than Hamas ' leaders envisaged . Now , Houthis and Islamic militants are deliberately provoking the US in the expectation that overreaction will lead to an escalation of the conflicts .
