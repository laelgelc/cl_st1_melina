<h> Pentagon confirms US drone downed near Yemen by Houthis <p> The Pentagon confirmed Tuesday that a military MQ-9 Reaper drone was downed Monday by the Houthis near Yemen . <p> The drone went down off the coast of Houthi-controlled areas in Yemen in the Red Sea , deputy spokeswoman Sabrina Singh told reporters . <p> " Initial indications are that it was shot down by a Houthi surface-to-air missile , " she said , adding that the drone has not been recovered . <p> The Yemeni group downed the same model drone in November off the coast of Yemen , according to CNN . The unmanned drone is used for intelligence collection . <p> The Houthis have been targeting cargo ships in the Red Sea owned or operated by Israeli companies or transporting goods to and from Israel in solidarity with Gaza , which has been under an Israeli onslaught since Oct. 7 . <p> With tensions escalating due to joint counterstrikes by the US and UK against Houthi targets in Yemen , the group declared that it considered all American and British ships legitimate military targets . <p> Asked if the US has been able to degrade @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ shipments headed toward Yemen , Singh said the US military had two successful interdictions in January and earlier this month by the US Coast Guard . <p> " So , we have been able to disrupt some delivery of capabilities to the Houthis . We 've also continued to conduct coalition strikes and dynamic strikes in Houthi-controlled areas in Yemen , " she said . <p> The US has accused Iran of supporting the Houthis . <p> " So , we know Iran finds ways to get weapons and get capabilities to the Houthis , " she said . <p> Singh also said as of Feb. 20 , the US has taken 32 self-defense strikes against the Houthis -- the most recent Monday .
