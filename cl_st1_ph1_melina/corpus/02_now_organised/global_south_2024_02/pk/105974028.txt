<p> GAZA STRIP : Hamas 's armed wing said on Friday that hostages in Gaza were " struggling to stay alive " as conditions across the war-battered Palestinian territory deteriorate due to relentless Israeli bombardments . <p> " The wounded and sick enemy prisoners are going through very difficult conditions and are struggling to stay alive , " Abu Obeida , spokesman for Ezzedine al-Qassam Brigades said in a televised statement . <p> " This is not surprising because everything that our people are suffering from , be it hunger , thirst and lack of medical help , is also what the enemy prisoners are suffering from . " <p> This week , mediators from the United States , Qatar and Egypt gathered in Cairo to try to broker a deal to halt the fighting and secure the release of the remaining hostages in exchange for Palestinian prisoners held by Israel .
