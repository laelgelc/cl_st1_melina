<p> WASHINGTON : The United States on Thursday imposed sanctions on a handful of extremist Jewish settlers over violence against Palestinian civilians in the West Bank , in a rare step against Israelis as war rages with Hamas , officials said . <p> President Joe Biden was also issuing an executive order laying out the groundwork for actions over attacks and " acts of terrorism " in the West Bank , where settlers have rampaged against Palestinians amid the separate military campaign in the Gaza Strip . <p> The United States will announce initial sanctions against four people . Any assets they hold in the United States will be blocked , with Americans forbidden from financial transactions with them . <p> The action marks the first financial sanctions against settlers although the Biden administration earlier announced that it would refuse visas for extremists involved in violence . <p> The move comes as Biden travels Thursday to Michigan , a crucial state in November presidential elections and home to a major Arab American community , in which anger has been growing over Biden 's support for Israel . The mayor of one Detroit suburb is snubbing the president over the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ new actions , said that Biden has consistently raised concern with Israel about violence by settlers . <p> " These actions pose a grave threat to peace , security , stability in the West Bank , Israel and the Middle East region , " the official said on customary condition of anonymity . <p> " They also obstruct the realization of , ultimately , an independent Palestinian state , existing side by side of the state of Israel and , by extension , the enduring peace and stability for Palestinians and Israelis alike , " he said . <p> Israeli settlers killed at least 10 Palestinians and torched dozens of homes in the occupied West Bank in 2023 , making it the " most violent " year on record for settler attacks , according to the human rights group Yesh Din . <p> About 490,000 settlers live among approximately three million Palestinians in the West Bank , in settlements that are considered illegal under international law .
