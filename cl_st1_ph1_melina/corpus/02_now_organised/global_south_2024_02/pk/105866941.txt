<p> WASHINGTON : The White House on Monday pressed Israel to work toward a pause in the Gaza conflict to win freedom for more hostages held by Hamas and rapidly increase the flow of humanitarian aid to Palestinian civilians . <p> John Kirby , the White House national security spokesperson , told reporters that some progress had been made in negotiations toward a humanitarian pause but that more work remained to be done . <p> He spoke a day after U.S. President Joe Biden spoke by phone to Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and told him a military operation in Rafah " should not proceed " without a plan to protect civilians there . <p> Kirby welcomed news that the Israeli military had freed two hostages during a raid by special forces in Gaza 's southern Rafah neighborhood . <p> He said there can be no end to the Gaza crisis until Hamas releases all hostages .
