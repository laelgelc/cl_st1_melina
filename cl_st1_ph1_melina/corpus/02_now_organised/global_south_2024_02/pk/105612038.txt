<h> 112 more Palestinians killed in fresh Israeli airstrikes on Gaza <h> Qatar claims Hamas has given ' initial ' support to a hostage-prisoner exchange deal <p> At least 112 Palestinians were killed and 148 injured in the Israeli bombing in Gaza in the last 24 hours , the health ministry in Gaza said in a statement on Friday . <p> Israeli airstrikes on Gaza have left 27,131 Palestinians dead and 66,287 wounded since October 7 , the ministry said . <p> As fighting in Gaza raged with scores reported killed overnight , mediator Qatar said Hamas had given its " initial " support to a hostage-prisoner exchange deal that would pause the conflict in the besieged enclave . <p> After a truce proposal agreed with Israeli negotiators was presented to Hamas a day earlier , Qatar 's foreign ministry spokesman Majed al-Ansari said there were hopes of " good news " about a fresh pause to the fighting " in the next couple of weeks " . <p> Ansari said a truce plan thrashed out with Israeli negotiators by Egyptian , Qatari and US mediators in Paris earlier this week had received a " positive " initial response @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by the Israeli side and now we have an initial positive confirmation from the Hamas side , " he said . <p> But a source close to Hamas said : " There is no agreement on the framework of the agreement yet -- the factions have important observations -- and the Qatari statement is rushed and not true . " <p> A Hamas source said the group had been presented with a three-stage plan which would start with an initial six-week halt to the fighting that would see more aid deliveries into Gaza . <p> The pause would also see the release of " women , children and sick men over 60 " among the Israeli hostages in exchange for Palestinian prisoners in Israel , the source said , requesting anonymity because of the sensitivity of the talks .
