<h> Severe food insecurity looming in Gaza : Red Cross chief <p> The head of the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies ( IFRC ) expressed concern Monday over food security in the Gaza Strip . <p> " A severe food insecurity is looming in Gaza . The risk of hunger is increasing daily , as about 80% of the population already faces emergency or catastrophic acute food insecurity conditions , " said Jagan Chapagain on X. <p> More than a million children and elderly people in shelter centers suffer from the risk of dehydration , digestive and respiratory diseases , skin diseases and anemia , according to Palestine Red Crescent reports , he added . <p> " This is the harsh reality that people in Gaza face every day .
