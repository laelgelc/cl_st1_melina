<p> ' Dahomey , ' French-Senegalese director Mati Diop 's film about how returning 26 treasures to Benin , art looted by western powers in the 19th century , sparked a reckoning with colonialism 's legacy , won the Berlin Film Festival 's Golden Bear prize for best film on Saturday . <p> The documentary , named after the West African kingdom where the artworks were created before they were looted by a French colonel during his conquest of Dahomey in 1892 , looks at the response to the return of some of them from Paris to Benin , of which Dahomey is now part . " It 's an opportunity to shine a light on a story that 's too little known , " the Paris-born filmmaker said of the prize . <p> " A reality that France is doing everything to cover up , to define as something to be gotten rid of .
