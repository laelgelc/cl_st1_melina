<p> GAZA STRIP : Palestinian group Hamas on Sunday condemned a wave of US and British strikes on Yemen , warning the attacks would bring " further turmoil " to the Middle East . <p> Hamas in a statement called the bombing of Houthi targets " a blatant assault on the sovereignty of a sister Arab country , and an escalation that will drag the region into further turmoil " . <p> The United States and Britain struck dozens of targets in Yemen late Saturday , their third joint operation in response to repeated attacks on Red Sea shipping by the Houthis that have disrupted global trade . <p> The air raids in Yemen came a day after a separate wave of unilateral American strikes against Iran-linked targets in Iraq and Syria in retaliation for the killing of three US soldiers in Jordan on January 28 . <p> Anger over Israel 's devastating campaign in Gaza -- triggered by an unprecedented Hamas attack on October 7 -- has grown across the Middle East , stoking violence involving Tehran-aligned groups in Lebanon , Iraq , Syria and Yemen .
