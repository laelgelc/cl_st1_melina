<h> Unceasing horror <p> By News Desk <p> February 22 , 2024 <p> The heart-breaking and horrifying death of Hind Rajab , a six-year-old Palestinian girl , who was killed along with her family members after they tried to flee the fighting in Gaza . The peace-loving people of the world are aghast and trembling in shock and horror by the accounts emanating from Gaza of innocent people being mercilessly slaughtered . <p> Death , destruction , disease , acute scarcity of water , food , and lifesaving drugs stalk the streets of the entire enclave of Gaza . Palestinians have been abandoned to suffer this atrocity . Surrounded by lethal tanks and with ceaseless aerial bombings , there is no safe space left for them to go to . Should the world just remain a spectator ? We have a duty to stop this genocide before it takes any more innocent lives .
