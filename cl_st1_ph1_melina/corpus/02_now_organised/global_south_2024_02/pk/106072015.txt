It was the third US veto of a draft resolution since the start of the current fighting on Oct. 7 . Washington has also used its veto to block an amendment to draft resolution in December . <p> " A vote in favor of this draft resolution is support to the Palestinians right to life . Instead , it could extend the fighting between Hamas and Israel , " Thomas-Greenfield told the council ahead of the vote . <p> The Algerian-drafted resolution vetoed by the US did not link a ceasefire to the release of hostages .
