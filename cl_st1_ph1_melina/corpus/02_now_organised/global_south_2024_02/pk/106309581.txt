<p> Israel has imposed a crippling blockade on the Gaza Strip , leaving its population on the verge of starvation , particularly residents of northern Gaza . <p> Israel has launched a deadly offensive on the Gaza Strip following a cross-border incursion by the Palestinian group Hamas on Oct. 7 . The ensuing Israeli bombardment has killed 29,954 people and injured over 70,000 with mass destruction and shortages of necessities . <p> The Israeli war has pushed 85% of Gaza 's population into internal displacement amid acute shortages of food , clean water and medicine , while 60% of the enclave 's infrastructure has been damaged or destroyed , according to the UN . <p> Israel stands accused of genocide at the International Court of Justice . An interim ruling in January ordered Tel Aviv to stop genocidal acts and take measures to guarantee that humanitarian assistance is provided to civilians in Gaza .
