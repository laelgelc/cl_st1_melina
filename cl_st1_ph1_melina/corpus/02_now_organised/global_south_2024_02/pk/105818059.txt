from the city is completed , and after an agreement between Tel Aviv and Cairo about the Israeli military activity in border areas between Gaza and Egypt , known as the Philadelphi Axis , according to Israeli public broadcaster KAN , <p> KAN claimed that Israel informed several countries in the region and the US about its preparedness for the operation in Rafah . <p> According to the Yedioth Ahronoth newspaper , the Israeli army has approved ground operation in Rafah in southern Gaza Strip . <p> Meanwhile , Israeli Channel 12 reported that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has informed US
