<h> Gaza death toll from Israeli attacks nears 27,500 <p> At least 27,478 Palestinians have been killed and 66,835 others injured in Israeli attacks in the Gaza Strip since Oct. 7 , the Health Ministry in the enclave said on Monday . <p> " Most of the victims were children and women , " the ministry said in a statement . <p> According to the statement , at least 113 people have been killed and 205 others injured in Israeli attacks in the last 24 hours alone . <p> " Many of the victims are still trapped under rubble and on the road and rescuers ca n't reach them , " it added . <p> Israel launched a deadly offensive on the Gaza Strip since an Oct. 7 Hamas attack in which Tel Aviv says nearly 1,200 were killed . <p> The Israeli offensive has left 85% of Gaza 's population internally displaced amid acute shortages of food , clean water , and medicine , while 60% of the enclave 's infrastructure has been damaged or destroyed , according to the UN .
