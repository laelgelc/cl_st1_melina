<h> Bangladesh-Turkey to work together to prevent global disinformation and misinformation : Info State Minister <p> State Minister for Information and Broadcasting Mohammad Ali Arafat said on Saturday that Bangladesh and Turkey will work together to prevent global disinformation and misinformation . <p> He said this after a bilateral meeting with the President of the Directorate of Communications of Turkey , Prof. Fahrettin Altun , before the special session of the Islamic Conference of Information Ministers of the member states of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) in Istanbul of Turkey on Saturday afternoon ( local time ) . <p> Arafat said the spread of false and misleading information is being noticed all over the world . Sometimes disinformation and misinformation spread with motives . <p> Like other countries in the world , Bangladesh is also a negative victim of this . Combating disinformation and misinformation is therefore now a global challenge , he said . <p> In this case , the scope for joint cooperation between Bangladesh and Turkey can be created . Both countries can work together on mutual cooperation including exchange of information and experience and technical assistance , the state minister said . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the continuous spread of disinformation in Gaza , Palestine . To combat this type of disinformation a collective effort is needed . <p> Prof. Fahrettin assured the Bangladesh state minister that both the countries will work together against disinformation and misinformation in the bilateral meeting .
