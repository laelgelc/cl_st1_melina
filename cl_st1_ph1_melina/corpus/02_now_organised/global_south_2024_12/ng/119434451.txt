<h> IDF soldier killed in Gaza <p> The IDF reported that the soldiers were targeted by anti-tank fire while inside a building in Beit Hanoun . <p> Among the eight wounded soldiers , three are in serious condition , while the remaining five are in moderate or good condition , according to the IDF . <p> The death of Sgt Peretz brings the toll of IDF fatalities in the ongoing ground operation in Gaza to 395 . <p> The military also reported three rockets fired from Gaza toward Israeli communities on Monday . Two were intercepted by air defences , and one struck an open area , causing no injuries or major damage . <p> The IDF has faced fierce resistance from Hamas operatives in northern Gaza , particularly in Jabalia , where fighting has intensified . <p> Overnight , the military said its 162nd Division conducted ambushes that killed " many dozens of terrorists " with gunfire and tank shelling . <p> Separately , the IDF released footage showing Hamas operatives planting explosives near the Indonesian Hospital in Jabalia . According to the military , the bombs were placed just 45 meters ( 148 feet ) from the hospital . <p> The IDF stated that it had operated in the hospital last week , killing several terrorists who attempted to flee and neutralising explosives planted in the area . The military condemned Hamas for using civilian institutions as shields , calling it a violation of international law . <p> DAILY POST had earlier reported that the IDF completed a major raid at the Kamal Adwan Hospital in Beit Lahiya , where it apprehended 240 suspected terror operatives and killed 19 others . The military described the hospital as a stronghold for Hamas fighters . <p> Efforts were made to evacuate 350 patients , caregivers , and medical personnel from the facility before the operation , in coordination with the Defense Ministry . <p> The intensified ground operations , coupled with an uptick in rocket fire , come as negotiations over a potential ceasefire and hostage releases continue .
