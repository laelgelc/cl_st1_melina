<h> Israel unleashes strikes on Yemeni Houthis <p> Israeli forces on Thursday launched a massive barrage of strikes on Houthi positions in Yemen in the latest exchange of attacks between Israel and the Iranian-backed rebel group . <p> The Israeli military said in a statement on the social platform X that fighter jets struck military targets on the western coast of Yemen . <p> Advertisements <p> The targets included military infrastructure at Sana'a International Airport along with the Hezyaz and Ras Kanatib power stations and three seaports on the western coast , according to the military . <p> " These military targets were used by the Houthi terrorist regime to smuggle Iranian weapons into the region and for the entry of senior Iranian officials , " the Israeli military said in the statement . " This is a further example of the Houthis ' exploitation of civilian infrastructure for military purposes . " <p> Houthi media also reported a series of Israeli strikes on the power stations , the airport and the Ras Isa oil port . <p> The attack comes just days after a Houthi rocket hit Tel Aviv , injuring at least 16 people . <p> Israel has struck the Houthis several times in Yemen , looking to cripple the Iranian-backed rebel group from launching attacks . <p> The Houthis , however , have proven resilient , and are continuing to attack not just Israel but also merchant shipping in the Red Sea , part of a campaign the rebel group claims is in solidarity with Palestinian militant group Hamas in Gaza , which has been fighting against Israeli forces since Oct. 7 , 2023 . <p> The U.S. has also positioned naval assets in the Red Sea and the Middle East region that has been fighting directly against the Houthis since January . The Houthis have been launching attacks in the Red Sea region since shortly after the outbreak of the Israel-Hamas war . <p> Israeli Air Force chief General Tomer Bar had earlier on Wednesday announced that tougher counter-attacks awaited the Houthi militia in response to rocket attack on the Tel Aviv metropolitan area . <p> At a ceremony at the Hatzerim air base in the Negev Desert in southern Israel on Wednesday , Bar stated that Israeli forces have already attacked the Houthis three times . <p> " We will continue to increase the frequency and intensity of the attacks as needed , " he said . <p> According to military reports from Wednesday , Israeli air defences intercepted another missile fired from Yemen overnight . In numerous areas in central Israel , warning sirens wailed for the second consecutive night . People had to leave their beds and take shelter in safe rooms . <p> Such nocturnal Houthi attacks have increased recently . According to media reports , a rocket fragment struck a house south of Tel Aviv . <p> Foreign Minister Israel Katz has already threatened that Israel would target and kill Houthi leaders . The militia then claimed to have uncovered espionage rings of Israel 's Mossad secret service and the US Central Intelligence Agency ( CIA ) , which had gathered information on Houthi leaders . <p> The Houthis , like Hamas in the Gaza Strip and the Lebanese Hezbollah militia , are aligned with Israel 's arch-enemy Iran . <p> This is the second night in a row that Israel has said it has intercepted a Houthi missile and shot it down outside Israel 's borders . <p> The Houthis said that ballistic missile was fired towards the coastal metropolis of Tel Aviv . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced on Sunday that , in response to continued attacks by the militia , there would be severe military strikes against the Islamists in Yemen . <p> Since the start of the Gaza war between Israel and the Palestinian Islamist Hamas movement in October 2023 , the Houthi militias , allied with Hamas , have been attacking targets in Israel and ships in the Red Sea with rockets and drones . <p> Israel , the United States and their allies have been repeatedly targeting Houthi positions in Yemen as a result .
