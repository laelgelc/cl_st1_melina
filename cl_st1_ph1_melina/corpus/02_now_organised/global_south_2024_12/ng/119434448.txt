<p> They also pointed out potential war crimes , such as indiscriminate attacks on civilians and civilian infrastructure , the use of starvation as a weapon of war and collective punishment . <p> " Civilians are protected under international law and are not legitimate military targets , " the experts stressed . " Actions aimed at their destruction in whole or in part are genocidal . " <p> The group urged immediate , independent investigations into these alleged violations . They warned that Israel 's continued immunity from accountability sends a " dangerous message " and demanded that Israeli leaders be held responsible for their actions . <p> The experts expressed particular concern over Israel 's operations in northern Gaza , describing the military offensive as potentially aimed at displacing the population permanently to pave the way for Gaza 's annexation . <p> The signatories included special rapporteurs on issues such as displacement , education , health , food security , and counter-terrorism . <p> Francesca Albanese , the special rapporteur on the rights of Palestinians , was among them . <p> Israel has previously criticised Albanese , accusing her of political bias .
