<p> Senate passed a unanimous resolution on Thursday condemning Israel 's ongoing military campaign in Gaza and Lebanon , urging the international community to take immediate action to protect Palestinian lives and hold Israel accountable for war crimes . <p> The resolution , which was introduced by Senator Sherry Rehman of the Pakistan Peoples Party ( PPP ) on behalf of all political parties , denounced Israel 's actions as a blatant violation of international law , particularly in its attacks on Palestine . <p> The Senate also condemned Israel 's exploitation of Syria 's political vacuum following the fall of Bashar al-Assad 's government earlier this week . <p> " The Senate strongly condemns Israel 's blatant disregard for international law , " the resolution stated , also calling for Israel to be held accountable for its actions . <p> The Senate condemned the killing of United Nations Relief and Works Agency ( UNRWA ) staff , with reports indicating that 249 UNRWA personnel had been killed by Israeli forces up to November 2023 . <p> The resolution also highlighted the killing of over 180 journalists in Gaza and the destruction of vital infrastructure , including hospitals , schools , and residential areas , as part of Israel 's military campaign . <p> " The Senate reaffirms Pakistan 's unwavering support for the Palestinian cause , recognizing it as a moral , political , and legal obligation , " the resolution read . Pakistan reiterated its commitment to the two-state solution for Palestine , based on internationally recognised borders and with Al-Quds Al-Sharif as its capital . <p> Pakistan has consistently raised the issue of Israel 's actions at the United Nations , the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) , and other multilateral platforms , urging international powers to stop the Israeli military actions in Gaza . <p> The resolution also called on the global community to uphold international humanitarian law and protect Palestinian lives . It further urged the international community to hold Israel accountable for its military actions , particularly those in Gaza and Lebanon . <p> Pakistan has been a vocal critic of Israel , which it does not recognise diplomatically , and has called for the creation of an independent Palestinian state based on the pre-1967 borders . <p> Since the beginning of Israel 's military operations in Gaza , Pakistan has dispatched 1,273 tons of humanitarian aid to the region , according to the National Disaster Management Authority .
