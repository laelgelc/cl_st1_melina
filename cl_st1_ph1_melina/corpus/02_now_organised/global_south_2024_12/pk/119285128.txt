<h> Islam 's dominance in all spheres of life inevitable : Ameer ul-Azeem <p> Secretary-General of Jamaat-e-Islami Pakistan Ameer ul-Azeem , emphasized the importance of Islam 's supremacy in all aspects of life during his speech at a family gathering in Orangi Town , Karachi . He stated that for Islamabad to thrive , the leadership must be honest , accountable , and responsible , and the country must be governed by leaders who adhere to Islamic values . <p> He pointed out that the future of Pakistan and Islamic movements globally lies with Jamaat-e-Islami , noting that the party 's vision of a just and prosperous society was the key to Pakistan 's development . " Jamaat-e-Islami should not be measured by the number of votes but by the strength of its ideology . The leadership and vision of Jamaat-e-Islami are the hope for the entire Muslim Ummah , " said Ameer ul-Azeem . <p> He further stressed the importance of a truthful leadership that would restore the Islamic values upon which Pakistan was founded , warning against the current divisions that have led to the downfall of many nations . Dr. Osama Rizvi , the Deputy Amir of Jamaat-e-Islami , also addressed the gathering , highlighting the ongoing genocide of the people of Gaza and Palestine . <p> He condemned the lack of action from Muslim rulers and announced a " Palestine Solidarity Million March " to be held in Islamabad on December 29 to show solidarity with the Palestinian people . During the event , Ameer ul-Azeem reminded the audience of the importance of unity and the necessity for a genuine Islamic governance system . <p> He urged the attendees to remain active in spreading the true message of Islam and to work for the implementation of Islamic laws in every aspect of life . The gathering also featured notable figures such as Maulana Abdul Haq Hashmi , Sheikh Usman Farooq , and other senior leaders of Jamaat-e-Islami . At the end of the event , several leaders were awarded shields in recognition of their services to the party .
