<h> PM Shehbaz lands in Egypt for D-8 summit <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Wednesday began a three-day official visit to Egypt to participate in the Eleventh Summit of the Developing Eight ( D-8 ) countries , held under the theme , ' Investing in Youth and Supporting SMEs : Shaping Tomorrow 's Economy ' . <p> Representing Pakistan , the prime minister will underscore the significance of investing in youth and small and medium enterprises ( SMEs ) as a pathway to economic resilience . <p> He will reiterate Pakistan 's commitment to the D-8 's foundational principles and advocate for enhanced cooperation in agriculture , food security , and tourism to drive mutual growth among member states . <p> PM will also highlight initiatives taken by Pakistan for youth empowerment and economic development . " Investing in our youth is not just an opportunity ; it is a necessity for a sustainable future , " he is expected to convey . <p> A special meeting will address the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Gaza and Lebanon following Israeli aggression . The prime minister will reaffirm Pakistan 's unwavering support for peace in the Middle East and its long-standing position on the Palestinian cause . <p> In addition to addressing the summit , PM Shehbaz will hold bilateral talks with leaders from participating nations . Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar , Information Minister Attaullah Tarar , and Special Assistant Tariq Fatemi are accompanying him on this visit .
