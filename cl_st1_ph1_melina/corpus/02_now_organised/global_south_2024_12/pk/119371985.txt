<h> One fifth of the world 's children affected by war : says UNICEF <p> Displaced children in front of makeshift tents in Rafah on March 14 , 2024 . PHOTO : AFP <p> The United Nations has issued a warning that nearly one in five children worldwide reside in regions impacted by war . UNICEF , the global body 's children 's agency , has reported that 473 million children are facing the most severe violence witnessed since World War II , a number that has nearly doubled since 1990 . <p> The UN has documented a record 32,990 grave violations against 22,557 children , marking the highest number on record . Shockingly , approximately 44 percent of the nearly 45,000 victims of the conflict in Gaza were children . Furthermore , there have been more child casualties in the war in Ukraine in the first nine months of 2024 than in the entire previous year . <p> A Palestinian child on the rubble left by an Israeli strike on Nuseirat refugee camp , on December 13 , 2024 . Reuters <p> UNICEF 's Executive Director , Catherine Russell , expressed deep concern , stating , " By almost every measure , 2024 has been one of the most devastating years for children in conflict in UNICEF 's history . Children living in conflict zones are more likely to face challenges such as lack of education , malnutrition , and displacement compared to those in peaceful areas . " <p> UNICEF also highlighted a significant rise in violence against young women and girls , particularly in Haiti where cases of abuse and assault surged by 1,000 percent in 2024 . Malnutrition is another major issue affecting children in conflict zones , with Sudan and Gaza being particularly affected . It was noted that half a million individuals in five conflict-affected countries are experiencing famine . <p> A Palestinian girl reacts in the aftermath of Israeli strikes , in Khan Younis in the southern Gaza Strip , October 14 . PHOTO : REUTERS <p> Gaza is facing a healthcare crisis , with a polio outbreak detected in July . The UN responded by launching a mass vaccination campaign , reaching 90 percent of children in the enclave despite challenging conditions . However , the UN emphasized that beyond Gaza , 40 percent of children in conflict zones globally are in urgent need of humanitarian assistance .
