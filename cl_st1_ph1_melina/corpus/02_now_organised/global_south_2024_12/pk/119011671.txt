<p> Prime Minister Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif has arrived in Cairo on a three-day visit to Egypt . <p> Minister for Public Business Sector of Egypt , Mohamed Shimi along with senior Egyptian and officials of the Pakistan Embassy in Cairo received the Prime Minister upon arrival at the airport . <p> The Prime Minister will lead the Pakistan delegation at the 11th Summit of the Developing Eight ( D-8 ) countries . <p> The theme of the 11th D-8 Summit is " Investing in Youth and Supporting SMEs : Shaping Tomorrow 's Economy . " <p> At the Summit , the Prime Minister will underline the importance of investing in youth and SMEs for building a strong and inclusive economy ; creating jobs ; advancing innovation ; and promoting local entrepreneurship . <p> The Prime Minister will also attend the Special Session of D-8 on the Humanitarian Crisis and Reconstruction Challenges in Gaza and Lebanon to deliberate on the situation resulting from Israeli aggression in the Middle East . <p> On the sidelines of the Summit , the Prime Minister is expected to hold bilateral meetings with the participating leaders .
