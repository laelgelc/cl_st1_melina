<h> Legal experts in Spain call for arms embargo on Israel <p> A group of legal experts in Spain called on the government Tuesday to impose an arms embargo on Israel over its war in the Gaza Strip . <p> Jurists for Palestine , a non-governmental organization ( NGO ) comprising Spanish judges , prosecutors and lawyers submitted a petition with more than 1,200 signatures to parliament calling for sanctions , including an arms embargo . <p> Speaking outside parliament , Pilar Barrado , a judge and spokesperson for the NGO , said Israel 's genocide in Gaza and violations of international law have reached an intolerable level . <p> " Human rights are universal . There ca n't be a world of masters and slaves who are allowed to be massacred , " Barrado said . <p> He noted that according to Article 93 of the Spanish Constitution , the government and parliament are responsible for guaranteeing compliance with international treaties and their resolutions . <p> " We demand that the government and all parliamentary groups comply with these resolutions . It is essential to adopt measures such as the ( International Criminal Court 's ) arrest warrants against Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant , " he said . <p> " We ( also ) demand that the Spanish parliament pass resolutions that implement UN resolutions , provide guarantees for Palestinian refugees , sever diplomatic , commercial and military relations with Israel , and guarantee UN supervision to restore living conditions in Gaza . " <p> Representatives of the Sumar alliance , the junior partner of the minority left coalition government in Spain , as well as the Podemos party and the Republican Left of Catalonia ( ERC ) also supported the initiative . <p> Separately , the El Diario newspaper reported that Spain allowed a US ship carrying weapons to Israel to stop at the port of Rota . <p> The report was denied by government spokesperson Pilar Alegria , however . <p> Israel has continued a genocidal war on the Gaza Strip that has killed more than 45,000 people , mostly women and children , since Oct. 7 , 2023 . <p> Last month , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza .
