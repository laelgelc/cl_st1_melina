<p> Fifty-five more Palestinians were martyred and one hundred seventy others injured during Israeli air-strikes in Gaza over past twenty-four hours . <p> According to Palestinian Health Ministry , confirmed martyrdom toll from Israeli onslaught since October 7 last year has risen to forty-four thousand nine hundred thirty . <p> The Ministry said Israel 's genocidal onslaught continues unabated despite calls from the UN Security Council for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza . <p> Meanwhile , Saudi Arabia has renewed call for international community to take serious and effective action to put an end to these flagrant and repeated violations . <p> In a statement , The Saudi Ministry said occupation forces ' persistence in their repeated violations of international law is an inevitable result of absence of activation of international accountability mechanisms . <p> Similarly , Jordan has also condemned targeting of a residential area in central Gaza Strip by Israeli occupation authorities .
