<h> Melissa Barrera says her views on Palestine almost closed Hollywood 's doors for her <p> Actor Melissa Barrera , who gained popularity after doing the role of Sam Carpenter , has shared her views on how she has been struggling to find work after her views on Palestine drew the ire of powerful quarters linked to Hollywood . <p> The 34-year-old Mexican actor said the previous year was the most difficult year of her life . She said it seemed , during that period , that her career was over . <p> Barrera shared her views on a social media post in October 2023 . She said in an Instagram post , " Gaza has been turned into a concentration camp . This is a clear genocide . " <p> She also blamed the Western media for playing a biased role . " The Western media shows a one-sided picture . We do not need more hate . No more Islamophobia and no more hostility , " she said . Barrera said after that controversy , she struggled to find opportunities " as she has been getting run-of-the-mill roles but not something she is hopeful for . " <p> She said no doubt if she continued to be silent on the Gaza issue , the Hollywood roles were lucrative but she could not compromise on her principles .
