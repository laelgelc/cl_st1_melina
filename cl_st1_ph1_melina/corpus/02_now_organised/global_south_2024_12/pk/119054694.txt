<h> PM in Cairo for D-8 Summit , Gaza talks <p> Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif arrived in Cairo on Wednesday on an official visit to Egypt to lead the Pakistan delegation at the 11th Summit of the Developing Eight ( D-8 ) countries being held in Cairo from 18-19 December . Minister for Public Business Sector of the Arab Republic of Egypt H.E . Mr. Mohamed Shimi along with senior Egyptian and officials of the Pakistan Embassy in Cairo received the Prime Minister upon arrival at the airport . The theme of the 11th D-8 Summit is " Investing in Youth and Supporting SMEs : Shaping Tomorrow 's Economy . " At the Summit , the Prime Minister will underline the importance of investing in youth and SMEs for building a strong and inclusive economy ; creating jobs ; advancing innovation ; and promoting local entrepreneurship . The Prime Minister will also attend the Special Session of D-8 on the Humanitarian Crisis and Reconstruction Challenges in Gaza and Lebanon to deliberate on the situation resulting from Israeli aggression in the Middle East . On the sidelines of the Summit , the Prime Minister is expected to hold bilateral meetings with the participating leaders . Pakistan delegation includes Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Muhammad Ishaq Dar and Federal Minister for Information &amp; Broadcasting Ataullah Tarar.
