<h> President Asif Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif have extended heartfelt greetings to the Christian community <p> Christian communities across Pakistan on Wednesday celebrate Xmas with traditional zeal and fervour . <p> In Karachi , the central celebration was held at St. Patrick 's Church , drawing a large gathering of worshippers . Comprehensive security measures were implemented at various churches throughout the city to ensure a safe and peaceful event . <p> In Lahore , the festivities included the cutting of a 100-pound cake at St. Luke 's Church in Shahdara . Similar celebrations were held in Bahawalpur , Quetta , Multan , Sargodha , and Lodhran , where churches organized prayer gatherings under stringent security arrangements . <p> In Multan , 1,300 police personnel were deployed to safeguard churches as the community commemorated the day with religious fervour . <p> Addressing a special Christmas ceremony at Prime Minister House , Islamabad this afternoon Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif emphasized the need for collective efforts towards peace , progress , and prosperity , stating , " In my opinion , we all should work together for peace , progress and prosperity " . <p> The Prime Minister noted that both Prophet Isa ( Jesus ) and his mother , Maryam ( Mary ) , are honoured in the Holy Quran , underscoring their significance to Muslims . He expressed concern over the violence in Bethlehem , the birthplace of Prophet Isa , where he remarked that " murder and brutality are rampant today , and thousands of Palestinians have been killed " . <p> He urged the international community to speak out against Israel 's actions and advocate for a ceasefire in Gaza . <p> PM Shehbaz also celebrated the contributions of the Christian community in Pakistan , highlighting their peaceful coexistence and achievements in various sectors such as education and national defence . He recognized notable figures like former Chief Justice Alvin Robert Cornelius and human rights activist Cecil Chaudhry . <p> In his address , he called for fostering brotherhood and urged citizens to practice patience and tolerance , advocating for the respectful treatment of religious minorities so they feel proud to be part of the nation . <p> Meanwhile , Chief of Army Staff General Asim Munir extended heartfelt greetings to the Christian community and expressed solidarity with them while attending Christmas festivities at St. Joseph Catholic Cathedral Church in Rawalpindi . <p> In his message , president Asif Ali Zardari said that the spirit of Christmas offered a universal lesson to humanity , encouraging people of all faiths to embrace love , generosity , mercy , unity , and hope . <p> Zardari said that Pakistan 's Constitution guaranteed the fundamental rights of all citizens , irrespective of their faith , and protected their freedom to worship and live following their beliefs . <p> I am a freelance journalist with a Master 's Degree in Mass Communication and an MS in Peace and Conflict Studies .
