<p> ISLAMABAD : Jamaat-e-Islami ( JI ) announced the Gaza million march in the federal capital on December 29 to express solidarity with Palestinians who are facing oppression by Israel . <p> JI has been granted permission to hold the million march at Jinnah Avenue in New Blue Area following the negotiations with the district administration . <p> Two rounds of talks were held between JI and district administrations as JI wanted to conduct the million march in the city 's centre while the administration had been pressing to hold the show at Sangjani , Tirnol . <p> The development came as today a Palestinian TV channel said five of its journalists were martyred Thursday in an Israeli strike on their vehicle in Gaza . <p> A missile hit the journalists ' broadcast truck as it was parked in the Nuseirat refugee camp in central Gaza , according to a statement from their employer , Al-Quds Today . <p> The Israeli military claimed in its own statement that it had conducted " a precise strike on a vehicle with an Islamic Jihad cell inside in the area of Nuseirat " . <p> According to witnesses in Nuseirat , a missile fired by an Israeli aircraft hit the broadcast vehicle , which was parked outside Al-Awda Hospital , setting the vehicle on fire and killing those inside . <p> The Committee to Protect Journalists ' Middle East arm said the organisation was " devastated by the reports that five journalists and media workers were killed inside their broadcasting vehicle by an Israeli strike " . <p> " Journalists are civilians and must always be protected , " it added in a statement on social media . <p> The Palestinian Journalists Syndicate said last week that more than 190 journalists had been martyred and at least 400 injured since the start of the war in Gaza . <p> Israel 's military campaign has killed at least 45,361 people in Gaza , a majority of them civilians , according to figures from the territory 's health ministry that the UN considers reliable . <h>
