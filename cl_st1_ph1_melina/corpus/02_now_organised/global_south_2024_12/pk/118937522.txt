<p> Prime Minister Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif will undertake an official visit to Egypt from 18 to 20 of this month to participate in the Eleventh Summit of the Developing Eight ( D-8 ) countries being held in Cairo . <p> According to Foreign Office , preceding the Summit , Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Mohammad Ishaq Dar will attend the 21st Session of the D-8 Council of Ministers on Wednesday . <p> The theme of the 11th D-8 Summit is " Investing in Youth and Supporting SMEs : Shaping Tomorrow 's Economy . " <p> The Prime Minister will express Pakistan 's strong commitment to the ideals of D-8 , underscore the importance of fostering partnerships for mutual benefit and prosperity , and promoting cooperation in agriculture , food security and tourism . <p> The Prime Minister will also underline Pakistan 's incentives for youth empowerment and financial development . <p> Shehbaz Sharif will also attend the Special Session of D-8 on the Humanitarian Crisis and Reconstruction Challenges in Gaza and Lebanon to deliberate on the situation resulting from Israeli aggression in the Middle East . <p> He will underline Pakistan 's principled position on the situation in Palestine and call for peace in the Middle East . <p> On the sidelines of the Summit , the Prime Minister is expected to hold bilateral meetings with participating leaders .
