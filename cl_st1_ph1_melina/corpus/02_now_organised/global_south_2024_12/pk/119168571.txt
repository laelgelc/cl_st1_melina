<h> Gaza death toll surpasses 45,250 amid relentless Israeli attacks <p> At least 32 more Palestinians were killed in Israeli attacks in the Gaza Strip , bringing the overall death toll since last year to 45,259 , the Health Ministry in the enclave said on Sunday . <p> A ministry statement added that some 107,627 others were injured in the ongoing assault . <p> " Israeli forces killed 32 people and injured 54 others in four massacres of families in the last 24 hours , " the ministry said . <p> " Many people are still trapped under the rubble and on the roads as rescuers are unable to reach them , " it added . <p> Israel has continued a genocidal war on the Gaza Strip since a Hamas attack on Oct. 7 , 2023 , despite a UN Security Council resolution calling for an immediate cease-fire . <p> The second year of genocide in Gaza has drawn growing international condemnation , with officials and institutions labeling the attacks and blocking of aid deliveries as a deliberate attempt to destroy a population . <p> Last month , the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza .
