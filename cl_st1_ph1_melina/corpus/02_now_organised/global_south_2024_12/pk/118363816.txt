<h> ICC collected more evidence in last 18 months than it has in past 20 years , says chief prosecutor <p> The International Criminal Court ( ICC ) has collected more evidence and information in the past 18 months than it has in the 20 years since it was founded , its chief prosecutor said on Monday . <p> Karim Khan emphasized the court 's recent intense efforts during his address at the 23rd session of the Assembly of States Parties ( ASP ) to the ICC 's Rome Statute . <p> The ICC has received the most evidence and documents in its history in the last year , with non-governmental organizations ( NGOs ) and victims submitting a large number of documents and evidence under Article 15 of the Rome Statute , he noted . <p> Of the 30 arrest warrants issued by the court , the chief prosecutor said 18 were issued within the last three years . <p> Stressing that the law must be applied equally to everyone , Khan said the protection that people on one side of the world enjoy is no different than that on the other . <p> He touched on the importance of working and acting together to better fulfill the court 's purpose and the tasks assigned to it under the Rome Statute . <p> On Nov. 21 , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza .
