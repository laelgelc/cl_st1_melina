<h> Nearly 1 million displaced Gazans face extreme winter cold : UN <p> Nearly one million displaced Palestinians in war-torn Gaza are at risk of extreme cold and rain this winter , the UN agency for Palestine refugees ( UNRWA ) warned on Sunday . <p> " Displaced people in Gaza need protection from the rain and cold . Only around 23% of this need has been met , leaving 945,000 people at risk of exposure this winter , " the UN agency said in a statement . <p> " Aid is urgently required to address the overwhelming needs as the crisis deepens . " <p> UNRWA said that Palestinian civilians in the central city of Deir al-Balah and across the enclave " search through the rubble of their destroyed homes , trying to salvage what little remains after an Israeli airstrike . " <p> Israel has launched a genocidal war on the Gaza Strip that has killed more than 44,700 people , mostly women and children , since Oct. 7 , 2023 . <p> Last month , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza . <p> Israel also faces a genocide case at the International Court of Justice for its war on Gaza .
