<p> Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi and representatives of multiple parties on Thursday criticised the PTI for what they said was the party 's " rhetoric centred on violence and conflict " , as the party skipped a multi-party conference in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa hosted by Kundi . <p> Last month , Kundi announced the conference in December to discuss various issues faced by the province , including terrorism . <p> The KP governor has repeatedly highlighted the province 's longstanding issues , including law and order , equitable distribution of resources , and financial rights . <p> Various matters , such as the peace and security of the province , its resources , and the province 's issues with the Centre , will be discussed , Kundi had said while speaking to reporters . <p> PTI had also skipped an MPC on October 7 which vowed all-out support for Gaza and demanded an immediate ceasefire a year after the catastrophic Israeli invasion . <p> At Thursday 's conference , Governor Kundi said that KP is not a prerogative of a certain political party . <p> " We extended an invitation to PTI but perhaps they are unable to talk about peace , " he said . " PTI has always talked about violence and bullets . " <p> He went on to say that the province 's chief minister Ali Amin Gandapur had said on the floor of the provincial assembly that he would buy ammunition and bullets . <p> The governor then lamented the loss of life in Kurram , and that the provincial government had not shown seriousness on the issue . The death toll from recent violence in Kurram has reached 130 while 186 people are injured following a November 21 ambush on civilian passenger convoys going from Parachinar to Peshawar . <p> On November 27 , warring sides agreed to extend a week-long tenuous ceasefire , ending on Nov 30 , for another 10 days despite sporadic incidents of violence . <p> " The federal and provincial governments have failed on the issue of law and order , " Kundi said at the conference , adding that more than 70 law enforcement officials have been killed . <p> Kundi announced that a political committee and technical committee will be established following the conference . <p> He noted that the province 's minerals are the right of the people , adding that all routes will be opened for Pak-Afghan trade . <p> " Residents of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa should not be harassed in Islamabad and other provinces , " Kundi said . <h> PTI snubs KP governor 's invite for Kurram moot <p> PTI announced its decision not to attend the conference on Wednesday , saying that the PPP -- to which Kundi belongs -- was working with the federal government to get PTI banned and even got a resolution about it passed by the Balochistan Assembly . <p> PTI insisted that , unlike the PPP , it had a mandate from residents to rule KP and was performing its duties well .
