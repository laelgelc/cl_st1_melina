<p> Minister for Information and Broadcasting Attaullah Tarar has proposed a regulatory framework at the international level to counter misinformation and to uphold ethical standards for the common good of humanity . <p> Speaking at the STARTCOM summit in Istanbul on Friday , he stressed the need to use the Artificial Intelligence in a positive manner in the health , education , social , agriculture and financial sectors to benefit people . <p> The Minister said the whole world is now grappling with the challenge of misinformation as it eventually leads to instability , which can cause destruction beyond imagination . <p> He highlighted that AI is being used negatively on the social media in the name of freedom of expression when it comes to the issues of Gaza , Kashmir , Lebanon and the plight of their people . He said one can not mention the name of Kashmiri freedom fighter Burhan Wani or upload his picture on the social media . <p> Attaullah Tarar resolved that Pakistan is fully committed to implementing AI policy with the best ethical standards in the world . He said the government has launched the Pakistan Digital Dialogue comprising AI and Large Language Model . It helps harmonizing public opinion and assessing the needs of the citizens . <p> The Minister said we are also heavily investing in the skill development of our youth . He said one million youth will be imparted training in the IT sector next year .
