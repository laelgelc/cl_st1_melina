<h> Mayor among 17 killed in Israeli airstrikes on Gaza <p> A Palestinian child on the rubble left by an Israeli strike on Nuseirat refugee camp , on December 13 , 2024 . Reuters <p> Israeli airstrikes in Gaza on Saturday killed 17 people , including Deiab al-Jaro , the mayor of Deir el-Balah , and seven others at a UN-run school sheltering displaced civilians . <p> Gaza 's civil defence agency spokesperson Mahmud Bassal confirmed that the strike on the Al-Majida Wasila school , west of Gaza City , killed seven people , including women and children , and wounded at least 10 others . <p> The school , operated by the UN agency for Palestinian refugees , had been turned into a makeshift shelter for those displaced by the ongoing war . <p> Bassal also reported that an Israeli strike on the town hall in Deir el-Balah killed Mayor Jaro and nine others . <p> The Israeli military stated that Jaro was targeted as an alleged Hamas operative in the military wing , though this claim was not independently verified . <p> The conflict , which erupted after Hamas 's attack on October 7 , 2023 , has caused significant casualties , with at least 44,930 killed in Gaza , the majority being civilians , according to the Hamas-run health ministry . <p> Israel 's ongoing strikes in Gaza have continued to target civilians , with at least 52 people killed over the past day . <p> Strikes hit two schools in Gaza City , and in northern Gaza , a tighter siege has led to the destruction of buildings and homes , particularly in Beit Lahiya , with Israeli forces also firing on Kamal Adwan Hospital .
