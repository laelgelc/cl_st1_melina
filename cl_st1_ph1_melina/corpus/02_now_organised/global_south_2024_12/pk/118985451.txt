<h> In a statement , Qatar-based Al Jazeera denounced the death of one of its journalists in an Israeli strike on Gaza yesterday , referring to the incident as a " targeted killing . " <p> Since Israel 's offensive in Gaza started more than 14 months ago , Ahmad Baker Al-Louh is the fifth journalist from Al Jazeera to be slain , according to AFP . <p> " The death of its cameraman , Ahmad Baker Al-Louh , 39 , at the hands of Israeli occupation forces is strongly condemned by Al Jazeera Media Network , " the broadcaster stated . <p> " A Civil Defense post in the market area of Al-Nuseirat Camp , central Gaza Strip , was the target of an airstrike that brutally killed him , " the statement continued . <p> Louh was a member of Islamic Jihad and " previously served as a platoon commander " for the organization , according to a statement from the Israeli military , which acknowledged that it had killed him . <p> Mahmud Bassal , the spokesman for Gaza 's civil defense agency , said that Louh had died in the attack on the Nuseirat camp , which also killed three people from Bassal 's rescue organization .
