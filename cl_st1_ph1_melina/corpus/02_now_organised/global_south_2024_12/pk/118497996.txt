<h> Gaza death toll nears 44,600 as Israel kills 48 more Palestinians <p> Israeli attacks killed 48 more Palestinians in the Gaza Strip , pushing up the overall death toll since last year to 44,580 , the Health Ministry in the enclave said on Thursday . <p> A ministry statement added that some 105,739 others have been injured in the ongoing assault . <p> " Israeli forces killed 48 people and injured 201 others in five massacres of families in the last 24 hours , " the ministry said . <p> " Many people are still trapped under the rubble and on the roads , with rescuers unable to reach them , " it added . <p> The Israeli army has continued a genocidal war on the Gaza Strip since a Hamas attack on Oct. 7 , 2023 , despite a UN Security Council resolution calling for an immediate cease-fire . <p> International recognition of the Gaza genocide has grown in the second year of the deadly Israeli war , as organizations and leaders have labeled the events a deliberate attempt to destroy the population . <p> Last month , the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza .
