<h> Pope kicks off Christmas under shadow of war <p> Pope Francis opens the Holy Door of St Peter 's Basilica , during a special ceremony to mark the launch of the Jubilee 2025 , a year of Catholic celebrations set to draw more than 30 million pilgrims to Rome , at the Vatican , on Dec 24 , 2024. -- AFP photo <p> ROME ( Dec 25 ) : Christmas revellers around the world donned red and white Santa hats , offered meals to the homeless and lit candles on Wednesday , as Pope Francis launched observation of the global holiday with a sombre mass in the Vatican . <p> At Saint Peter 's Basilica , Francis used his Christmas Eve mass to urge Christians to think " of the wars , of the machine-gunned children , of the bombs on schools or hospitals " as this year 's Christmas once again takes place under the shadow of Israel 's war on Hamas and Russia 's invasion of Ukraine . <p> His remarks come just days after he denounced the " cruelty " of Israeli strikes , which prompted objections from Israeli diplomats . <p> Pope Francis is surrounded by children at the end of the Christmas Eve mass at The St Peter 's Basilica in the Vatican on Dec 24 , 2024. -- AFP photo <p> Francis is due to deliver his traditional Christmas Day blessing , Urbi et Orbi ( to the city and the world ) , at midday on Wednesday , while in the biblical birthplace of Jesus , the Israeli-occupied West Bank city of Bethlehem , observations of the holiday have been muted . <p> For the second year in a row , Bethlehem has done away with its giant Christmas tree and the elaborate decorations that normally draw throngs of tourists , settling for just a few festive lights . <p> Latin Patriarch of Jerusalem Pierbattista Pizzaballa leads the yearly Christmas procession outside the Church of the Nativity in Bethlehem town in the Israel-occupied West Bank on Dec 24 , 2024. -- AFP photo <p> Prayers , including at the Church of the Nativity 's famed midnight mass , will still be held in the presence of the Catholic Church 's Latin patriarch , but the festivities will be of a more strictly religious nature . <p> The patriarch , Archbishop Pierbattista Pizzaballa , told a small crowd on Tuesday that he had just returned from Gaza , where he " saw everything destroyed , poverty , disaster " . <p> " But I also saw life -- they do n't give up . So you should not give up either . Never . " <p> At Manger Square , in the heart of the Palestinian city , a group of scouts held a parade that broke the silence . <p> " Our children want to play and laugh , " read a sign carried by one of them , as his friends whistled and cheered . <p> Jerusalem resident Hisham Makhoul said spending Christmas in the holy city offered an " escape " from the Israel-Hamas war , which has raged for more than 14 months in the Gaza Strip . <p> " What we 're going through is very difficult and we ca n't completely forget about it , " said Makhoul of the plight of Palestinians in the besieged territory . <p> -- Gaza and Syria -- <p> About 1,100 Christians live in Gaza , which is separated from the West Bank by Israeli territory . <p> Hundreds of Gazan Christians gathered at a church to pray for an end to the war . <p> " This Christmas carries the stench of death and destruction , " said George al-Sayegh , who for weeks has sought refuge in the 12th-century Greek Orthodox Church of Saint Porphyrius in Gaza City . <p> " There is no joy , no festive spirit . We do n't even know who will survive until the next holiday . " <p> In a message to Christians all over the world , Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu thanked them for supporting Israel 's fight against the " forces of evil " . <p> Elsewhere in the Middle East , hundreds of people took to the streets in Christian areas of Damascus to protest the burning of a Christmas tree in a Syrian town , just over two weeks after Islamist-led rebels ousted president Bashar al-Assad . <p> " If we 're not allowed to live our Christian faith in our country , as we used to , then we do n't belong here anymore , " said a demonstrator who gave his name as Georges . <p> -- Santa tracker -- <p> In Germany , Christmas was also a grim affair for many families after a deadly attack at a market , prompting President Frank-Walter Steinmeier to issue a message of healing . <p> " Hatred and violence must not have the final word , " he said . <p> A volunteer serves food to a group of people as they take part in a Christmas solidarity dinner called ' No Families Without Christmas ' in front of the National Congress in Buenos Aires on Dec 24 , 2024. -- AFP photo <p> In Buenos Aires , a Christmas solidarity dinner for the homeless fed around three thousand people at a time when more than half of Argentina 's population is affected by poverty . <p> " To say that it is a special year because there is more and more poverty is sad , but it is true , " Mariana Gonzalez , spokesperson for the Movement of Excluded Workers , one of the organisers , said . <p> Still , the atmosphere was joyful with floating balloons , music and clowns , as elsewhere on Christmas Eve families shared meals and gifts . <p> In the United States , where the annual tradition of " tracking " Santa Claus swung into action , a US Air Force general said there was no need to worry that recent mystery drone sightings might affect deliveries . <p> General Gregory Guillot 's reassurances came as the joint US-Canadian North American Aerospace Defense Command reported that Santa and his reindeer were making stops across Asia , including Japan and North Korea . <p> " Of course , we are concerned about drones and anything else in the air , " NORAD commander Guillot told Fox News . " But I do n't foresee any difficulty at all with drones for Santa this year . " <p> And in Paris , worshippers gathered at the Notre Dame cathedral for the first Christmas mass since its reopening following a devastating fire in 2019 . <p> " We got here early to attend 4pm mass , and to get a good spot . It 's a superb monument , " said Julien Violle , a 40-year-old engineer who travelled to Paris from Switzerland along with his two children . -- AFP <p> People take a selfie in front of Notre-Dame de Paris cathedral ahead the traditional Christmas mass in the cathedral in Paris on Dec 24 , 2024 , for the first time since the fire in 2019. -- AFP
