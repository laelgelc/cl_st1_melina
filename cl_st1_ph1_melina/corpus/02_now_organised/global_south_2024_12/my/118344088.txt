<p> GAZA 's civil defence agency said on Friday that at least 15 people were killed in Israeli strikes on Beit Lahia , in the north of the Hamas-run territory . <p> The area has been subjected to a major Israeli offensive for almost two months . <p> The homes of the Baba and Ahmed families were hit in the past 24 hours by Israeli strikes , agency spokesman Mahmud Bassal told AFP , adding that they " were able to retrieve only 10 bodies " . <p> He said dozens more were feared buried under the rubble of the two homes . <p> " A person went with a horse-drawn cart to retrieve the dead from the bombing of the Baba family home . It was targeted by an Israeli drone while it was on its way back to Kamal Adwan Hospital , which resulted in the deaths of five additional people . " <p> The north of the Gaza Strip has been the target of an Israeli offensive since October 6 , aimed at preventing Hamas from regrouping . <p> The Kamal Adwan hospital , on the edges of Beit Lahia and Jabalia , said that the head of its intensive care unit , Ahmed al-Kahlout , was killed in an Israeli strike . <p> Asked about the doctor 's death , the Israeli military said it was " unaware of a strike that occurred in this location or timeframe " . <p> The war was sparked by Palestinian group Hamas 's attack on Israel on October 7 , 2023 , that resulted in the deaths of 1,207 people , most of them civilians , according to an AFP tally of Israeli official figures . <p> Israel 's retaliatory military offensive has killed 44,363 people in Gaza , according to figures from the territory 's health ministry which the United Nations considers reliable .
