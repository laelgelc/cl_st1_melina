<p> Palestinian inspect the damage after an Israeli strike on the Nuseirat refugee Camp in the Gaza Strip ( AFP or licensors ) <p> By Linda BordoniPope Francis on Sunday launched a plea directly to political leaders and to the international community that ceasefires may be agreed upon in all countries torn by war before Christmas . <p> " I appeal to Governments and the International Community , that a ceasefire may be reached on all war fronts by the Christmas celebrations , " he said during the Angelus prayer . <p> " I appeal to Governments and the International Community , that a ceasefire may be reached on all war fronts by the Christmas celebrations . " <p> His words followed his request that all men and women of goodwill join in prayer for peace in war-torn countries worldwide . <p> " Let us continue to pray for peace , in tormented Ukraine , in the Middle East -- Palestine , Israel , Lebanon , and now Syria -- in Myanmar , in Sudan , and wherever people suffer from war and violence , " the Pope pleaded . <p> Countries at warHis reiterated appeal comes as violence continues to rage in Gaza , where over 40,300 people have been killed since the Hamas attack on Israel on 7 October 2023 and in neighbouring Lebanon where Israeli attacks have also escalated in the past months ; as Ukraine recently marked the tragic milestone of 1,000 days from a full-blown Russian invasion of the country ; in Myanmar where civil war has been ongoing g since a military coup overthrew the government in 2021 ; in Sudan where fighting between the army and paramilitary rebels has killed over 60,000 people and displaced millions since April 2023 . <p> The Pope also mentioned the volatile situation in Syria where a 14-year conflict appears to have come to a head in the past hours with rebels claiming to have captured the capital , Damascus. --Vatican News
