<p> Displaced Palestinians , who fled their houses due to Israeli strikes , warm themselves by a fire on a rainy cold day at a tent camp , amid the ongoing conflict between Israel and Hamas , in Rafah <p> By Nathan MorleyThe UN children 's agency Unicef says that in addition to Israeli attacks , children are now dying from the cold and lack of adequate shelter . <p> Edouard Beigbeder , UNICEF Regional Director for the Middle East and North Africa , said , ' These preventable deaths reveal the desperate and worsening situation in which families and children in Gaza are living . ' <p> Elsewhere , media in Israel is reporting that Israeli health authorities have completed a report to be submitted to the UN on the health of Israeli prisoners released by Hamas . <p> Reports suggest prisoners have suffered numerous problems , including losing 10-17 per cent of their body weight while in captivity due to malnutrition , being beaten and kept in isolation . Children were also beaten and women subjected to sexual violence . <p> In addition , the hostages did not have proper medical care . As a result , some elderly hostages , for example , developed thrombosis when forced to remain in place for long periods of time . <p> Military operation on hospitalMeanwhile , the Israeli army has ended a military operation at Kamal Adwan Hospital in northern Gaza . <p> The hospital was put out of service as a result of the raid . <p> Reports suggest the Israeli army arrested 240 people , whom it claims are members of the extremist groups Hamas and Islamic Jihad , in the operation . The army also says it has arrested the director of the hospital . <p> The World Health Organization ( WHO ) has repeatedly called for healthcare to be protected in Gaza. --Vatican News
