<p> ISTANBUL/GAZA CITY : Seven displaced civilians , including six children , have frozen to death in Gaza due to extreme winter cold after being forced to live in unhealthy conditions in tents amid the ongoing Israeli onslaught , a local official told Anadolu Agency ( AA ) . <p> " Six children and a nurse lost their lives due to the cold wave in the past days , " Ismail Thawabteh , who heads Gaza 's government media office , said on Monday . <p> He warned that the death toll may rise due to the current tragic conditions in the enclave amid relentless Israeli attacks . <p> " The lives of displaced Palestinians are in a real danger amid heavy rainfall and cold waves as their tents are worn out and do n't protect them , " Thawabteh said . <p> The Gaza Strip has been hit by a cold wave and torrential rains since Sunday , bringing more misery to the territory 's 2.3 million population . <p> Many displaced civilians are living in makeshift tents amid worsening living conditions as temperatures drop . Families lack basic necessities such as clothing , bedding , and blankets , leaving many infants vulnerable to the cold . <p> The United Nations ( UN ) says that nine out of every 10 people in Gaza have been displaced due to the ongoing Israeli onslaught . <p> Thawabteh held the US , Israel and their backers responsible for the deteriorating humanitarian conditions in the Palestinian territory . <p> He called on the international community and aid groups to urgently intervene to pressure Israel " to stop its genocidal war on Gaza and save the lives of civilians . " <p> Israel has killed more than 45,500 people in Gaza since Oct 7 , 2023 , despite a UN Security Council resolution calling for an immediate ceasefire . <p> Tel Aviv has imposed a stifling blockade on Gaza , leaving the territory 's entire population on the verge of famine . <p> In November , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former Defence Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza . <p> Israel also faces a genocide case at the International Court of Justice for its war on the enclave .
