Exclusively for Malay Mail readers : Use code VERSAMM10 <p> By Dhesegaan Bala Krishnan <p> Sunday , 29 Dec 2024 7:00 AM MYT <p> KUALA LUMPUR , Dec 29 -- On January 1 , Malaysia will assume the chairmanship of the Association of South-east Asian Nations ( Asean ) , a 10-member bloc with a combined GDP ranking it among the world 's top economic entities . <p> The chairmanship rotates annually in alphabetical order based on the English names of member states , with Laos currently in charge . <p> This marks Malaysia 's fifth time in chairing Asean , following its last tenure in 2015 . <p> Why Malaysia 's leadership matters <p> Malaysia takes the reins during a period of shifting geopolitical tensions , marked by strained ties with the United States and strengthened partnerships with Russia and China . <p> Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim recently disclosed that US officials had questioned Malaysia 's envoy to the United States over the nation 's refusal to designate Hamas as a terrorist organisation . <p> The US Embassy in Malaysia also issued two demarches urging action to prevent a suspected state -- widely believed to be Iran -- from escalating the Gaza conflict via proxies . <p> In October , Malaysia joined Brics as a partner nation , alongside Indonesia , Thailand , and Vietnam . <p> Brics is an acronym for a coalition of five major emerging economies -- Brazil , Russia , India , China , and South Africa -- focused on promoting economic cooperation , development , and reform of the global financial system . <p> The intergovernmental organisation is often portrayed as a counterweight to the Western-led world . <p> Anwar described the move as essential for enhancing Global South cooperation and reducing Malaysia 's reliance on the US dollar . <p> A former Portuguese colony and Indonesian territory until its independence in 2002 , Timor-Leste has been an Asean observer state since 2005 . In 2022 , Asean leaders agreed in principle to grant full membership . <p> 2 . The Myanmar crisis <p> In November , Anwar proposed temporarily " carving out " Myanmar from Asean to foster stronger consensus on addressing the military junta , which has ruled since a 2021 coup . <p> Malaysia has been an outspoken critic of the junta , with Anwar previously urging Myanmar 's suspension unless it restores democratic governance and releases civilian leaders . <p> Although Myanmar plans to hold elections in 2025 , Asean insists on an inclusive process involving all stakeholders . <p> 3 . South China Sea Code of Conduct ( CoC ) <p> Finalising the long-delayed CoC for the South China Sea will be a priority . <p> Negotiations , which began in 2002 , have progressed slowly , with the second reading completed only in 2023 . <p> The CoC aims to manage disputes in the resource-rich waters claimed by several Asean members -- Malaysia , Vietnam , Brunei , and the Philippines -- as well as China , which asserts near-total sovereignty through its " nine-dash line .
