" He further warned that those responsible would face the harshest consequences in US history and demanded the immediate release of the hostages . <p> Trump has strongly supported Israel and criticised Biden 's occasional stance while also expressing a desire to negotiate global deals . <h> Watch : Trump demands Hamas releases hostages <p> Hamas launched the deadliest attack on Israel on 7 October 2023 , killing 1 208 people , mostly civilians .
