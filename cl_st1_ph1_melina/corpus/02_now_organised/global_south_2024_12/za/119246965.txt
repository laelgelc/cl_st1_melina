<h> Pope Francis to launch Jubilee year <h> Warning : <p> Pope Francis will mark Christmas Eve on Tuesday with a special ceremony launching Jubilee 2025 , a year of Catholic celebrations set to draw more than 30 million pilgrims to Rome . <p> The 88-year-old , who has been suffering from a cold in recent days , will at 7:00 pm ( 1800 GMT ) open the " Holy Door " of St Peter 's Basilica at the Vatican , in front of 30,000 people and a live TV audience around the world . <p> Over the next 12 months , pilgrims will pass through the large and imposing bronze door , which is normally closed , by tradition benefiting from a " plenary indulgence " , a type of forgiveness for their sins . <p> The Argentine pontiff will then preside over the Christmas Eve mass in St Peter 's , before delivering his traditional Christmas Day blessing , Urbi et Orbi ( to the city and the world ) , at midday on Wednesday . <p> Ahead of a busy week , the pontiff missed his usual Angelus prayer in St Peter 's Square on Sunday because of a cold , instead delivering it online from his home in the Vatican . <p> On Christmas Day , Francis is expected to repeat his calls for peace in a world riven by conflict , particularly in the Middle East . <p> He drew an angry response from Israel at the weekend for condemning the " cruelty " of Israel 's strikes in Gaza that killed children . <p> Some 700 security officers are being deployed around the Vatican and Rome for the Jubilee celebrations , with measures further tightened following Friday 's car-ramming attack on a Christmas market in Germany . <h> - ' Little miracle ' - <p> Organised by the Church every 25 years , the Jubilee is intended as a period of reflection and penance for Catholics , and is marked by a long list of cultural and religious events , from masses to exhibitions , conferences and concerts . <p> Much of Rome has been given a facelift in preparation , with monuments such as the Trevi Fountain and the Ponte Sant'Angelo cleaned up and roads redesigned to improve the flow of traffic . <p> Many residents have questioned how the Eternal City -- where key sites are already overcrowded and public transport is unreliable -- will cope with millions more visitors next year . <p> Key Jubilee projects were only finished in the last few days after months of works that turned much of the city into a building site . <p> Inaugurating a new road tunnel at Piazza Pia next to the Vatican on Monday , Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said it had taken a " little civil miracle " to get the project finished in time . <p> Over the course of the next few days , Holy Doors will be opened in Rome 's three major basilicas and in Catholic churches around the world . <p> On Thursday , Pope Francis will open a Holy Door at Rebibbia prison in Rome and preside over a mass in a show of support for the inmates . <h> - LGBTQ faithful - <p> While primarily aimed at the world 's almost 1.4 billion Catholics , the Jubilee aims to also reach a wider audience . <p> And traditions have evolved since the first such event back in 1300 , launched by Pope Boniface VIII . <p> This year , the Vatican has provided pilgrims with online registration and multilingual phone apps to navigate events . <p> Jubilee 2025 also has a mascot named Luce ( meaning Light in Latin ) inspired by Japanese anime cartoons -- a choice that drew some criticism from traditionalists , but which is intended to target the younger generation . <p> With the motto " Pilgrims of Hope " , the event will see groups from around the world come to Rome throughout 2025 , from sports and business figures to migrants , artists and young people . <p> Among the groups registered on the official site is Italian LGBTQ group La Tenda di Gionata , reflecting the pope 's call for the Church to be open to all . <p> As well as the regular Jubilees every 25 years , the Church has organised extraordinary Jubilees , the most recent in 2016 . The next is in 2033 to commemorate the crucifixion of Jesus Christ .
