<p> CAPE TOWN - Newly appointed US ambassador , Ebrahim Rasool , said that Palestine 's neighbours could have done more to help end the war in Gaza . <p> While he acknowledged the role played by countries like Qatar , he said that South Africa had to take a moral stand . <p> Rasool was addressing Friday prayers at the Gatesville Mosque in Athlone , as he bid farewell to take up his ambassadorship . <p> " And I say that with enormous pride and with great respect from our brother from Qatar , I do n't believe that the neighbours of Palestine have done what they could to help the people of Palestine against genocide . " <p> He also described South Africa as a " moral superpower " after taking Israel to the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) . <p> " I believe that I go as a representative of a moral superpower in a world that has lost its moral anchors . "
