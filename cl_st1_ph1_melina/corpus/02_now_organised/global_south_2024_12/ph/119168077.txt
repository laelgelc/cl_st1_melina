-- Gaza 's civil defense agency said Israeli strikes overnight and early Sunday killed at least 28 Palestinians , including at one family 's home and at a school building the military said was used by Hamas . <p> More than 14 months into the Israel-Hamas war , there was no let-up in the violence in the Gaza Strip even as Palestinian groups involved in the fighting said a ceasefire deal was " closer than ever " . <p> Israel has faced growing criticism of its actions during the war , triggered by Hamas 's October 7 , 2023 attack , including from rights groups accusing it of " acts of genocide " which the Israeli government strongly denies . <p> Pope Francis on Saturday condemned deadly bombardment a day earlier that killed several children as " cruelty " , prompting a sharp response from Israel which accused the pontiff of double standards . <p> On the ground in Gaza , civil agency spokesman Mahmud Bassal said that at least 13 people were killed in an air strike on a house in central Gaza 's Deir el-Balah belonging to the Abu Samra family . <p> Hours after the strike , an AFP photographer saw residents searching through the debris for survivors , while others looked for belongings they could salvage . <p> In a nearby compound , bodies covered in blankets were laid on the floor . <p> There was no immediate comment from the Israeli military , which has confirmed a separate strike further north , on a school in Gaza City . <p> Bassal said that eight people including four children were killed in the attack on the school , which had been repurposed as a shelter for Palestinians displaced by the war . <p> The Israeli military said it had carried out a " precise strike " overnight targeting Hamas militants operating there . <h> Ceasefire talks <p> A military statement said that a Hamas " command and control centre ... was embedded inside " the school compound in the city 's east , adding that it was used " to plan and execute terrorist attacks " against Israeli forces . <p> AFP impages showed the damaged school building where mangled concrete slabs and iron beams lay strewn amid patches of blood . <p> Contacted by AFP , an Israeli military spokesperson said they were unable to comment on other reported strikes elsewhere in Gaza . <p> Bassal said in a statement that an overnight strike killed three people in Rafah , in the south . <p> And a drone strike early on Sunday hit a car in Gaza City , killing four people , the spokesman added . <p> Hamas and two other Palestinian armed groups said on Saturday in a rare joint statement that an agreement to end the bloodshed was " closer than ever " . <p> The groups , which include Islamic Jihad and the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine , said that a truce in Gaza and hostage release deal may be within reach , provided Israel does not impose new conditions in negotiations . <p> Negotiations have faced multiple challenges since a one-week truce in November 2023 , with the primary point of contention being the establishment of a lasting ceasefire . <p> Israeli leaders have repeatedly stated they oppose a full military withdrawal from Gaza . Another unresolved issue is the territory 's post-war governance . <p> Indirect talks between Israel and Hamas , mediated by Qatar , Egypt and the United States , were held last week in Doha , rekindling hope of a potential breakthrough after months of stalling . <h> ' Cruelty ' <p> On Wednesday , US Secretary of State Antony Blinken said he was " hopeful " for a deal , but avoided making any predictions as to when it would materialise . <p> The unprecedented Hamas attack last year that sparked the war resulted in the deaths of 1,208 people , most of them civilians , according to an AFP tally based on official Israeli figures . <p> Militants also took 251 hostages , of whom 96 remain in Gaza , including 34 the Israeli military says are dead . <p> Israel 's retaliatory offensive in Gaza has killed at least 45,227 people , a majority of them civilians , according to figures from the Hamas-run territory 's health ministry that the United Nations considers reliable . <p> The dead include seven children from one family , killed in a bombing on Friday that sparked a strong reaction from Pope Francis . <p> He said that " children were bombed . This is cruelty , this is not war .
