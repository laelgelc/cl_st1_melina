The 388-year-old institution and more than a dozen other schools have advised foreign students to return to their campuses before Donald Trump is expected to enforce a tougher immigration policy after his inauguration on Jan. 20. -- AP <p> CONCORD , NEW HAMPSHIRE -- A growing number of US colleges and universities are advising international students to return to campus before President-elect Donald Trump is inaugurated , amid concerns he might impose travel bans as he did during his first administration . <p> More than a dozen schools have issued advisories , even though Trump 's plans remain uncertain . At some schools , the spring semester begins before he takes office , so students may have to be back in class anyway . <p> But for anyone whose ability to stay in the United States depends on an academic visa , they say it 's best to reduce their risks and get back to campus before Jan. 20 . <p> Here 's a look at what Trump has said and done and how schools and students are preparing for his second term : <p> Article continues after this advertisement <p> Trump issued an executive order in January 2017 banning travel to the US by citizens of seven predominantly Muslim countries -- Iraq , Syria , Iran , Sudan , Libya , Somalia and Yemen . Travelers from those nations were either barred from getting on their flights or detained at US airports after they landed . They included students and faculty as well as business people , tourists and visitors to friends and family . <p> Article continues after this advertisement <p> Later he removed some countries but added others to the list -- 15 nations were affected at some point during his presidency . <p> Article continues after this advertisement <p> Because of the ban , more than 40,000 people were ultimately refused visas , according to the US State Department . President Joe Biden rescinded the orders when he took office in 2021 . <p> More than 1.1 million international students were enrolled in US colleges and universities during the 2023-2024 school year , according to Open Doors , a data project partially funded by the US State Department . Students from India and China accounted for more than half of all international students in the US , and about 43,800 come from the 15 countries affected by Trump 's travel restrictions . <p> Article continues after this advertisement <h> ' Ideological screening ' <p> Jacky Li , a third-year environmental studies major at University of California , Berkeley , will be traveling home to China on Dec. 21 and returning Jan. 16 , 2025 . Though he made his plans months before Berkeley officials sent the advisory , he said worry is growing among international students . <p> " There 's a fear that this kind of restriction will enlarge into a wider community , considering the geopolitical tensions nowadays around the world , so the fear is definitely there , " said Li , who urged Trump to support , rather than thwart , important academic research . <p> " If the US is really a champion of academic freedom , what you should do is not restrict this kind of communications between different countries of the world , " he said . <p> Trump 's transition team did not respond to questions on the topic . But in the past he said he would revive the travel ban and expand it , pledging new " ideological screening " for non-US citizens to bar " dangerous lunatics , haters , bigots and maniacs . " <p> " We are n't bringing in anyone from Gaza , Syria , Somalia , Yemen or Libya or anywhere else that threatens our security , " Trump said at an October 2023 campaign event in Iowa . <h> Possible delays <p> He also vowed to " revoke the student visas of radical anti-American and anti-Semitic foreigners at our colleges and universities " in response to campus protests . <p> School officials have also advised international students heading home for winter break to prepare for possible delays at immigration control . <p> The list includes Ivy League universities such as Harvard and Brown , Boston schools such as Northeastern University and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology , and other schools around the country , from Johns Hopkins University to the University of Southern California . Some offer classes that begin the day after Inauguration Day . <p> Cornell University told its students that a travel ban involving the countries Trump previously targeted " is likely to go into effect soon after inauguration , " and that new countries could be added to the list -- particularly China and India .
