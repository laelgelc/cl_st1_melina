<h> Terrorist Nidal Al-Najar , leader of Hamas ' Aerial and Aerial Defense Units in Gaza City , eliminated in a targeted joint operation by the IDF and ISA . <h> Advertisement <p> Gaza : Terrorist Nidal Al-Najar , the Head of Hamas ' Aerial Unit and Aerial Defense Unit in Gaza City , was eliminated in a precise joint operation by the IDF and ISA , IDF said on X. <p> Al-Najar , a key mastermind behind the October 7th aerial infiltration into Israel , played a leading role in attacks on Israel and IDF troops in central Gaza , using explosive drones and UAVs . <p> This follows Hamas 's statement a day earlier , claiming it had information about Israel 's plans to launch a hostage rescue operation similar to the one in Gaza 's Nuseirat camp in June . Hamas threatened to " neutralize " the captives if such an operation occurred , according to reports . <p> " Al-Najar was one of the masterminds of the aerial infiltration into Israel on October 7 . Moreover , throughout the war , Al-Najar was one of the leaders of the attacks against the State of Israel and IDF troops operating in central Gaza , including by means of explosive drone and UAV attacks toward IDF troops , " the IDF Spokesperson 's Unit said in a statement . <p> The IDF stated that the operation , conducted on Tuesday , December 3 , was a joint effort with the Israel Security Agency ( Shin Bet ) .
