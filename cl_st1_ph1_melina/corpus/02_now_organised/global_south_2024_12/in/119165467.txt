<h> The USS Gettysburg shot down a F/A-18 F Super Hornet as it was taking off from USS Harry S. Truman . Incident occurs as US forces strike Houthi 's storage facilities in Yemen . <p> New Delhi : A US Navy ship accidentally shot down a F/A-18 F Super Hornet in a case of friendly fire , forcing the two pilots to eject in the Red Sea late Saturday evening , a statement by the American Central Command said , adding that the pilots were safe . <p> " The guided-missile cruiser USS Gettysburg ( CG 64 ) , which is a part of the USS Harry S. Truman Carrier Strike Group , mistakenly fired on and hit the F/A-18 , which was flying off the USS Harry S. Truman , " the statement from the US Central Command ( CENTCOM ) said . <p> The two pilots were safely recovered , with one suffering minor injuries . An investigation is underway , it said . <p> On 15 December , the USS Harry S. Truman Carrier Strike Group came under Central Command 's area of responsibility , according to its statement . <p> The Central Command 's area of responsibility includes Yemen . Just before the statement of friendly fire , CENTCOM announced that it had carried out " precision airstrikes " against a missile storage facility in Sanaa , Yemen , which is under the control of the Houthi rebel group . <p> " CENTCOM forces conducted the deliberate strikes to disrupt and degrade Houthi operations , such as attacks against US Navy warships and merchant vessels in the Southern Red Sea , Bab al-Mandeb , and Gulf of Aden , " said the CENTCOM . <p> The statement added : " During the operation , CENTCOM forces also shot down multiple Houthi one way attack uncrewed aerial vehicles ( OWA UAV ) and an anti-ship cruise missile ( ASCM ) over the Red Sea . " <p> F/A-18 Super Hornets were a part of the operation along with other assets of both the US Air Force and Navy . The CENTCOM statement did not mention what other assets were used in the strike . <p> The Houthis , backed by Iran , have been disrupting shipping in the Red Sea in support of Palestinians in the Gaza Strip since Israel launched its retaliation to the 7 October 2023 attacks carried out by the Hamas group . <p> The Houthis have used uncrewed aerial vehicles to attack commercial ships linked to Israel , the US and the UK . Earlier this year , it had attacked a ship carrying goods to India . <p> The Indian Navy has been involved in patrolling the region since the Houthi strikes against merchant shipping began in November last year . In March , the Indian Navy rescued crew members of a commercial vessel that was attacked by Houthi missiles .
