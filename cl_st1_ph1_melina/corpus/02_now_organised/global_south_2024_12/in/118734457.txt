<h> Israeli strike on northern Gaza kills 19 , Palestinian medics say <h> Share <h> Israeli strike on northern Gaza kills 19 , Palestinian medics say <p> Palestinian medical officials say an Israeli strike on a home where displaced people were sheltering in the northern Gaza Strip has killed at least 19 people . <p> That 's according to the Kamal Adwan Hospital , which received the casualties on Wednesday after the overnight strike in the town of Beit Lahiya . <p> There was no immediate comment from the Israeli military . Israel has been waging a renewed offensive against Hamas militants in northern Gaza since early October . <p> Hospital records show that a family of eight were among those killed , including four children , their parents and two grandparents . <p> The war began when Hamas-led militants stormed into Israel on October 7 , 2023 , killing some 1,200 people , mostly civilians , and abducting around 250 people , including children and older adults . Around 100 hostages are still inside Gaza , at least a third of whom are believed to be dead . <p> Israel 's retaliatory offensive has killed over 44,000 Palestinians in Gaza , according to local health officials . They say women and children make up more than half the dead but do not distinguish between fighters and civilians in their count .
