<h> Yemen 's Houthis on Tuesday said that they have launched a " hypersonic " ballistic missile at a military target in Jaffa near Tel Aviv , Israel . <p> Sanaa : Yemen 's Houthis on Tuesday said that they have launched a " hypersonic " ballistic missile at a military target in Jaffa near Tel Aviv , Israel . <p> Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz said that " the heads of the Houthis " would face a similar fate to " Yahya Sinwar in Gaza , Ismail Haniyeh in Tehran , and Hassan Nasrallah in Beirut , " referencing the leaders of Hamas and Hezbollah . <p> Israel has struck both civilian and military infrastructure in Yemen without targeting specific individuals . During a visit to an Arrow air defense system battery , which intercepted the Houthi missile over Israel overnight , Defense Minister Israel Katz declared : " Just as we dealt with Sinwar in Gaza , Haniyeh in Tehran , and Nasrallah in Beirut , we will address the heads of the Houthis in Sanaa or anywhere in Yemen , " according to a report in Times of Israel . <p> " We will target both their infrastructure and their leadership to eliminate the threat , " Katz added , emphasizing that " those sponsoring Houthi terrorism in Hodeidah or Sanaa will also pay the full price . "
