It is the capital of a province of about 1 million people , bordering Jordan . <p> Daraa 's seizure followed the rebels ' claim late on Friday that they had advanced to the edge of the central city of Homs , a key crossroads between the capital and the Mediterranean coast . <p> Capturing Homs would cut off Damascus from the coastal stronghold of Assad 's minority Alawite sect , and from a naval base and air base of his Russian allies there . <p> " Our forces have liberated the last village on the outskirts of the city of Homs and are now on its walls , " the Syrian faction leading the sweeping assault said on the Telegram messaging app . <p> A coalition of rebel factions that include the Islamist group Hayat Tahrir al-Sham ( HTS ) made a last call on forces loyal to Assad 's government in Homs to defect . <p> Ahead of the rebel advance , thousands of people fled Homs towards the coastal regions of Latakia and Tartus , strongholds of the government , residents and witnesses said . <p> ASSAD REGIME UNDER THREAT <p> A U.S.-backed alliance led by Syrian Kurdish fighters captured Deir el-Zor , the government 's main foothold in the vast eastern desert , on Friday , three Syrian sources told Reuters . <p> The rebels seized Aleppo and Hama in the northwest and centre earlier in the lightning offensive that began on Nov. 27 . <p> In another ominous sign for Assad in the east , the Syrian Kurdish force said Islamic State -- a jihadist group that imposed martial law under its harsh rule before its defeat by a U.S.-led coalition in 2017 -- had taken control of some areas in eastern Syria . <p> Aron Lund , a fellow at think-tank Century Foundation , said Assad 's government was " fighting for their lives at this point " . <p> It was possible the government could hold Homs , " but given the speed at which things have moved so far , I would n't count on it " , he said on Friday . <p> A Syrian army source said Iran-backed Hezbollah forces were positioned to bolster government defences in and near Homs . <p> Syrian state media reported dozens of rebels were killed in the Homs countryside on Friday in an operation by Syrian and Russian air forces , artillery , missiles and armoured vehicles . <p> Capturing Homs would solidify a chain of powerful positions under the Islamic insurgents ' control from Aleppo on the Turkish border in the north to Daraa on the Jordanian border to the south . <p> Gaining Homs would also increase the rebels ' chances of isolating the seat of Assad 's regime in Damascus with the ability to block the route northwest from the capital to the sea . <p> REBELS REENERGISED <p> As the rebels pressed their offensive , Russia and Jordan on Friday urged their nationals to leave Syria . <p> After years locked behind frozen front lines , rebel forces have burst out of their northwestern Idlib bastion to achieve the swiftest battlefield advance by either side since a street uprising against Assad mushroomed into civil war 13 years ago . <p> Syria 's conflict killed more than 305,000 people between 2011 and 2021 , the United Nations Human Rights Office said in 2022 . <p> Assad regained control of most of Syria after key allies -- Russia , Iran and Lebanon 's Hezbollah -- came to his rescue .
