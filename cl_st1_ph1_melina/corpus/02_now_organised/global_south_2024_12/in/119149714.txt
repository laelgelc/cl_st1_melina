<h> PM Modi embarks on 2-day visit to Kuwait <p> New Delhi : As he embarked on a two-day visit to Kuwait , Prime Minister Narendra Modi Saturday said India and the Gulf nation have shared interest in peace , security and stability of the West Asian region . <p> Modi 's visit to Kuwait is taking place two weeks after the collapse of President Bashar al-Assad 's regime in Syria and the continuing Israeli offensive in Gaza . <h> Also Read <p> In a departure statement , Modi said his talks with top Kuwaiti leadership would be an opportunity to chalk out a roadmap for a futuristic partnership between India and Kuwait . <p> " We deeply value the historical connection with Kuwait that has been nurtured over generations . We are not just strong trade and energy partners but also have shared interest in peace , security , stability and prosperity in the West Asia region , " he said .
