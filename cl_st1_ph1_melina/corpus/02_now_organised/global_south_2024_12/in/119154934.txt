<h> Possibility to reach a deal ' closer than ever ' if Israel stops bringing new terms : Hamas <h> Story highlights <p> The delegation of leaders from Hamas , Palestinian Islamic Jihad , and the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine met on Friday in Cairo to discuss the ongoing negotiations for a ceasefire and hostage release deal . <p> Palestinian militant group Hamas on Saturday ( Dec 21 ) said that the possibility of reaching a ceasefire deal with Israel is " closer than ever " if the enemy stops setting new conditions . <p> The delegation of leaders from Hamas , Palestinian Islamic Jihad , and the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine met on Friday in Cairo to discuss the ongoing negotiations for a ceasefire and hostage release deal . <p> All these three groups have been holding hostages throughout Gaza . <p> The Hamas statement read that the factions " stressed everyone 's keenness to stop the aggression against our people " , apparently referring to its demand for a permanent ceasefire . <p> The key area of disagreement in the talks is the nature of the ceasefire deal , with Hamas demanding a permanent end to the fighting , while Israel is seeking a temporary pause during which some of the hostages would be released followed by a resumption of its fighting . <p> This has been demanded in order to finish demolishing the Palestinian group 's military and governing capabilities , an Arab diplomat told The Times of Israel . <p> Israel has sought an agreement that " ends the military operation " . Meanwhile , Hamas is insisting that the text states that the ceasefire will " end the war " . <p> The statement further read that all the sides appreciated the Egyptian effort to strike a reconciliation deal between the various Palestinian factions . <p> The delegation is scheduled to hold another meeting as soon as possible . <p> Earlier this week , Hamas said that talks in Qatar 's Doha aimed at a truce and hostage-prisoner exchange in Gaza were " serious and positive . " <p> Israel and Hamas have been fighting a war in the Gaza Strip since October 7 , 2023 .
