In his New Year message to employees , Chandrasekaran said the conglomerate plans to add another half-million jobs in the next five years . Tata Steel comes in a distant second with a headcount of 78,321 . <p> These jobs will be spread across key sectors such as semiconductors , electric vehicles , solar equipment , and other critical hardware industries , said Chandrasekaran .
