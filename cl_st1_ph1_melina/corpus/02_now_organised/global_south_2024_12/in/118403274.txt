<p> The Syrian civil war , a conflict that has lasted for 13 years and claimed the lives of an estimated half-million people , has once again captured global attention . A recent and unexpected offensive by rebel forces , spearheaded by the group Hayat Tahrir al-Sham ( HTS ) , has led to the capture of Aleppo , one of Syria 's most significant cities . This surprise development marks the first major attack on Aleppo since 2016 and signals a potential turning point in a war that has already left deep scars on the Middle East and beyond . <h> Aleppo : A Symbol of Syrian Conflict <p> Aleppo , a city steeped in history and once a vibrant hub of trade and culture , has been central to Syria 's civil war . Before the conflict , it was home to 2.3 million people . In 2012 , rebel forces captured the eastern part of the city , making it a stronghold of opposition against Syrian President Bashar Assad 's regime . However , in 2016 , government forces -- backed by intense Russian airstrikes -- recaptured Aleppo after a devastating siege . The fall of Aleppo symbolized a significant turning point in the war , solidifying Assad 's grip on power . <p> The current rebel assault on Aleppo , led by HTS , is the first since the city 's recapture and represents a significant challenge to Assad 's authority . The offensive extends into the surrounding countryside of Idlib and Hama provinces , with Syrian government forces scrambling to launch airstrikes and deploy reinforcements to halt the insurgents ' advance . <h> Why This Offensive Matters <p> The resurgence of fighting in Aleppo comes at a precarious time for the region . The war in Syria had seen little change in territorial control in recent years , but this attack has the potential to reshape the battlefield . Charles Lister , a Middle East analyst , suggests this offensive could have " game-changing " consequences if the Syrian military fails to defend its positions effectively . <p> The situation is further complicated by the involvement of multiple foreign powers . Russia , a key backer of Assad , is preoccupied with its ongoing war in Ukraine , potentially limiting its ability to provide immediate support . At the same time , Turkey maintains forces in Syria and holds sway over the opposition coalition that includes HTS . The possibility of heightened tensions between Russia and Turkey looms large , as both nations have strategic interests in Syria . <p> The implications extend beyond Syria 's borders . The conflict could become a destabilizing factor across the Middle East , especially as other regional tensions persist . The ongoing Israeli-Hamas conflict in Gaza and Hezbollah 's involvement in Lebanon create an already volatile backdrop . The resurgence of fighting in Syria risks adding another layer of complexity to an already fraught geopolitical landscape . <h> Who is Leading the Rebel Offensive ? <p> HTS , the group spearheading the attack , has a complex and controversial history . Originally an offshoot of al-Qaida , HTS has been designated as a terrorist organization by the U.S. and the United Nations . Its leader , Abu Mohammed al-Golani , first emerged during the early days of the Syrian conflict in 2011 . The group initially engaged in violent extremist activities , including bombings , religious policing , and property confiscation from minorities . <p> In recent years , HTS has attempted to distance itself from its extremist roots . The group officially severed ties with al-Qaida in 2016 and has worked to present itself as a more moderate force in opposition-controlled territories . It has focused on civilian governance and cracked down on extremist factions within its ranks . For example , last year , HTS permitted the first Christian Mass in Idlib in years . However , critics argue that HTS continues to harbor and cooperate with armed groups that operate against U.S. and allied forces . <h> The Role of Foreign Powers <p> The involvement of foreign powers has been a defining feature of Syria 's civil war . Russian airstrikes and Iranian-backed militias have been instrumental in helping Assad maintain control over 70% of the country . Meanwhile , the U.S. has about 900 troops stationed in northeast Syria , primarily to prevent the resurgence of the Islamic State ( IS ) . Turkey also plays a significant role , both militarily and diplomatically , in supporting opposition forces . <p> The renewed fighting in Aleppo raises questions about how these foreign powers will respond . Russia 's focus on Ukraine and Turkey 's support for Syrian rebels could lead to increased friction . Additionally , Israel 's recent strikes in Aleppo targeting Hezbollah and Syrian military assets add another layer of complexity . Such actions underscore the fragility of the situation and the potential for broader regional escalation . <h> Potential Consequences <p> The offensive has highlighted the ever-present threat of IS sleeper cells , which could exploit the chaos to regain a foothold in Syria . Although IS no longer controls territory , it remains a lethal force , capable of carrying out attacks in both Syria and Iraq . <p> Furthermore , the offensive could exacerbate Syria 's already dire humanitarian crisis . Over 6.8 million Syrians have fled the country , and millions more are internally displaced . A resurgence in fighting could displace even more people , adding to the strain on neighbouring countries and fuelling anti-immigrant sentiment in Europe , where Syrian refugees have been a contentious issue . <p> The unexpected rebel advances in Aleppo highlight the persistent instability of Syria 's civil war . This escalation not only poses a formidable threat to Assad 's regime but also threatens to drag the region into even greater chaos . Major global powers , including Russia , Turkey , and the U.S. , must navigate this crisis cautiously to prevent worsening the already fragile situation . <p> As the battle for Aleppo unfolds , its outcome will likely have far-reaching implications -- not just for Syria but for the broader Middle East . Whether this offensive marks a turning point in the conflict or a temporary surge in violence remains to be seen . As the fighting intensifies , it serves as a poignant reminder of the immense human suffering and geopolitical complexities that have defined Syria 's 13-year war .
