<h> ' I want to write my story ' : A Journalist 's Voice from Gaza 's Endless War <p> " They also serve who only stand and wait . " - John Milton 's Sonnet 19 Motasem Dalloul , a journalist in Gaza , in his voice notes to Outlook 's Editor Chinki Sinha revealed the reality of a Palestinian journalist , he has to cover the deaths of his loved ones and the destruction of his homeland . He sent a couple of lines too . He said he would send more later . We will wait . Waiting has no ending . He can send it a century later . Our hope is sharper than a machete , more piercing than a bullet . Dalloul lost his two young sons and wife in 2024 in the war in Gaza that has unleashed images of violence and loss and brutality and seems endless . He lost nephews and nieces and brothers and sisters and uncle and his home . We bear witness .
