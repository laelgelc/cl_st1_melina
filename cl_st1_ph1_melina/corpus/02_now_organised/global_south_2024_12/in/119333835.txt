<p> " The world is on the brink of a major , major disaster . The prospects are catastrophic . And , I 'm not exaggerating at all , " says Gilbert Achcar , a Lebanese professor at SOAS , University of London . He points out that the world is hearing more about the possible use of nuclear weapons than it did during the Cold War . In an interview with Outlook on the West Asian crisis over Israel 's uncontrolled aggression backed by the US and other Western powers , he says that the episode has completely destroyed all Western pretence of the Rule of Law based on international standards . " It 's Jungle Rule . We live in a world where might is right . The Western hypocrisy over the Russia-Ukraine war and Israel 's war on Gaza has proved it beyond doubt , " he says .
