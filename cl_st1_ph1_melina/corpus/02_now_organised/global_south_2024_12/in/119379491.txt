<p> Israel used the United States ' High Altitude Area Defense ( THAAD ) system to obstruct a missile , according to British news agency Reuters who quoted a source close to the matter as saying on Friday . <p> THAAD was used to intercept a missile fired from Yemen , the source told Reuters . However , there is no confirmation yet regarding the success of the interception . <p> This was carried out after Israel countered strikes by attacking various targets on December 26 , Thursday , including Sanna International Airport , associated with Yemen 's Iran-aligned Houthi movement . At least six people died due to these strikes from Israel , said Houthi media . <p> Israel used at least five ballistic missiles in the past 10 days . Launched from its central area to counter attacks from Yemen . It used one of the ballistic missiles on early morning Christmas , according to a report in Financial Times . <p> In two of these occasions , Israel failed to fully obstruct two incoming missiles as one of them attacked a school while another struck a playground in Tel Aviv which injured 16 people . <p> To strengthen Israel 's already capable defence system , US President Joe Biden facilitated deployment of THAAD , an air missile defence system capable of intercepting short , medium and intermediate-range ballistic missiles , in October . The President also deployed around 100 US troops in addition to the missile system . <p> It is the only air defence system by the US , manufactured by Lockheed Martin , that can obstruct air weaponry within and outside the earth 's atmosphere . The high altitude defence system has been improved to adapt to new threats . The US deployed the THAAD system in US allied nations like Isreal , South Korea , Romania and the United Aram Emirates . <p> In the midst of the continuous fighting , the Houthis have regularly fired missiles and drones toward Israel , claiming that their activities are in sympathy with the Palestinians in Gaza .
