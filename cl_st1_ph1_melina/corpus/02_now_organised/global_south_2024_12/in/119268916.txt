<h> Israel hits Baalbek region , violating ceasefire : Lebanon <h> Before dawn on Wednesday , an Israeli air strike targeted the Baalbek region in eastern Lebanon , alleged to attack Hezbollah warehouses , marking the first ceasefire violation in the area . The truce between Israel and Hezbollah began on November 27 , after over a year of hostilities , with both sides accusing each other of breaches . <p> APWeapons and other equipment seized by the Israeli military during its ground invasion of southern Lebanon are displayed . <p> Lebanon 's state media said an Israeli air strike targeted the Baalbek region in the east of the country before dawn on Wednesday , branding it a " violation " of the fragile ceasefire between Israel and Hezbollah . <p> The truce went into effect on November 27 after more than a year of hostilities that began with the outbreak of the war in the Gaza Strip . <p> Both sides have since accused the other of breaching the ceasefire . <p> Wednesday 's strike near the town of Tarya did not result in casualties , the state-run National News Agency said , calling the attack the " first violation of the ceasefire agreement " in the Baalbek area . <p> A Lebanese security source , speaking on condition of anonymity , said the strike targeted " warehouses believed to belong to Hezbollah " .
