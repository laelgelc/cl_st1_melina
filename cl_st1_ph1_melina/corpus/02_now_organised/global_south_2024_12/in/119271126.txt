<h> Silence arms , overcome divisions , Pope to nations <p> Vatican City : Pope Francis in his traditional Christmas message on Wednesday urged " all people of all nations " to find courage during this Holy Year " to silence the sounds of arms and overcome divisions " plaguing the world , from the Middle East to Ukraine , Africa to Asia . <p> The pontiff 's ' Urbi et Orbi ' -- " To the City and the World ' -- address serves as a summary of the woes facing the world this year . <p> As Christmas coincided with the start of the 2025 Holy Year celebration that he dedicated to hope , Francis called for broad reconciliation , " even ( with ) our enemies " . <p> " I invite every individual , and all people of all nations ... To become pilgrims of hope , to silence the sounds of arms and overcome divisions , " the Pope said from the loggia of St Peter 's Basilica to throngs of people below . <p> The Pope invoked the Holy Door of St Peter 's Basilica , which he opened on Christmas Eve to launch the 2025 Jubilee , as representing God 's mercy , which " unties every knot ; it tears down every wall of division ; it dispels hatred and the spirit of revenge " . <p> He called for arms to be silenced in war-torn Ukraine and in the Middle East , singling out Christian communities in Israel and the Palestinian territories , " particularly in Gaza where the humanitarian situation is extremely grave , " as well as Lebanon and Syria " at this most delicate time " . <p> Francis repeated his calls for the release of hostages taken from Israel by Hamas on October 7 , 2023 . <p> He cited a deadly outbreak of measles in the Democratic Republic of Congo , and the suffering of the people of Myanmar , forced to flee their homes by " the ongoing clash of arms " . The Pope likewise remembered children suffering from war and hunger , the elderly living in solitude , those fleeing their homelands , who have lost their jobs and are persecuted for their faith . <p> Pilgrims were lined up on Christmas Day to walk through the great Holy Door at the entrance of St Peter 's Basilica , as the Jubilee is expected to bring some 32 million Catholic faithful to Rome . <p> Traversing the Holy Door is one way that the faithful can obtain indulgences , or forgiveness for sins during a Jubilee , a **26;710;TOOLONG tradition that dates from 1300 . <p> Pilgrims submitted to security controls before entering the Holy Door , amid new security fears following a deadly Christmas market attack in Germany . Many paused to touch the door as they passed and made the sign of the cross upon entering the basilica dedicated to St Peter , the founder of the Roman Catholic Church . <p> " You feel so humble when you go through the door that once you go through is almost like a release , a release of emotions , " said Blanca Martin , a pilgrim from San Diego . <p> " ... It 's almost like a release of emotions , you feel like now you are able to let go and put everything in the hands of God . See I am getting emotional . It 's just a beautiful experience . "
