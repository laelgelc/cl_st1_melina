In Jordan , Blinken mentioned Israeli and Turkish military activity on Syrian soil in recent days . <p> United States ( US ) Secretary of State Antony Blinken on Thursday ( Dec 12 ) warned against triggering further conflicts in Syria , days after rebel factions ousted President Bashar al-Assad from the West Asian country . <p> Blinken is currently on visits to Jordan and Turkey to discuss the ongoing situation in Syria . In Jordan , Blinken mentioned Israeli and Turkish military activity on Syrian soil in recent days . <p> " When it comes to many actors who have real interests in Syria , it 's also really important at this time that we all try to make sure that we 're not sparking any additional conflicts , " he said . <p> Blinken added that Washington was working to bring home US citizen Travis Timmerman after Syria 's interim government said he had been " released . " <h> Blinken , Jordan 's king agree on need for a secure Syria <p> Earlier in the day , the US secretary of state met Jordan 's King Abdullah in the Red Sea town of Aqaba . Both called for a secure Syria , a report by the news agency AFP said . <p> During the meeting , Blinken promised US support for " the stability of Syria 's neighbours , including Jordan , during this period of transition " , State Department spokesperson Matthew Miller said . <p> Blinken called for " an inclusive transition that can lead to an accountable and representative Syrian government chosen by the Syrian people , " Miller said . <p> King Abdullah , meanwhile , " reaffirmed Jordan 's respect " for " the Syrian people 's choices , stressing the need to safeguard Syria 's security and the safety of its citizens " , a palace statement said . <h> This is Blinken 's 12 visit to West Asia since Gaza war began <p> This is Blinken 's 12th visit to West Asia since the Israel-Hamas war in the Gaza Strip began on October 7 last year .
