<p> Misr El Kheir Foundation ( MEK ) and Islamic Relief Worldwide have officially signed a 3-year Memorandum of Understanding ( MoU ) to collaborate on critical humanitarian and development projects aimed at serving vulnerable communities in Gaza and Egypt . <p> The signing ceremony in Cairo was attended by the British Ambassador to Egypt , HMA Gareth Bayley , and the Egyptian Assistant Minister of Social Solidarity for Civil Society , Dr Ayman Abdel Mojoud , underscoring the importance of this partnership in fostering international collaboration and addressing pressing humanitarian challenges . <p> This partnership reflects a shared commitment to alleviating suffering and empowering individuals in need . The projects will benefit Egyptians , as well as Palestinians residing in Egypt , and will focus on addressing the urgent and long-term needs of the targeted communities . <p> Key areas of collaboration <p> The MoU outlines a range of impactful initiatives designed to address pressing challenges and contribute to sustainable development , including : <p> Seasonal programmes : Providing vital support during key periods such as winterization , Ramadan , qurbani , and back-to-school campaigns . <p> Orphan sponsorship : Ensuring the well-being and development of orphaned children through sustained care and support . <p> Education : Enhancing access to quality education for underprivileged children and youth . <p> Waseem Ahmad , CEO of Islamic Relief Worldwide , added , " Collaborations like this one are critical in addressing the multifaceted challenges faced by vulnerable populations . We look forward to working hand-in-hand with Misr El Kheir to bring transformative change to communities in Gaza and Egypt . " <p> British Ambassador to Egypt , Gareth Bayley said : " This three-year partnership between Misr El Kheir Foundation and Islamic Relief Worldwide represents a powerful collaboration between Egypt and the UK to respond to the urgent needs of the vulnerable communities , including Egyptians and Palestinians residing in Egypt . The UK continues to work closely with Egypt and values its efforts in alleviating the humanitarian situation in Gaza . Together , we can make a significant impact on the lives of those in need , by addressing humanitarian challenges and fostering sustainable development . I am proud to witness this milestone in UK-Egypt partnership , through the collaboration between Misr El Kheir and Islamic Relief Worldwide , and commend both organisations for taking this crucial step towards alleviating suffering and empowering individuals in need . " <p> A shared vision for a better future <p> The MoU reflects the shared values and commitment of both organizations to uphold the principles of humanitarianism and development . By pooling resources , expertise , and networks , MEK and Islamic Relief aim to create sustainable solutions that bring lasting change to the lives of vulnerable people while contributing to global efforts to achieve the SDGs.
