<p> Air strikes are reported to have hit the Yemeni capital , Sanaa , and the port city of Hudaydah on the Red Sea . <p> AFP news agency reports that Houthi TV called the strikes " Israeli aggression " . Israel has not commented . <p> The scale of the strikes or any casualties are not clear from initial reports . In recent days Israel has warned it was preparing a significant attack on Houthi rebel targets in Yemen after the group fired missiles at it . <p> Houthis have been attacking Israel with drones and missiles since the first months of the Gaza war , which began in October 2023 . The war was triggered by an unprecedented attack on Israel by Hamas gunman from Gaza , which was praised by the Houthis . <p> Israel has carried out intermittent strikes against Houthis in retaliation since then . <p> Earlier this week , Israel 's defence minister said it was preparing to " strike hard " at the Houthis , warning it would " decapitate " the group 's leadership .
