<h> Fitzcarraldo acquires ' vital ' oral testimonies of Gazan survivors <p> Fitzcarraldo Editions is set to publish Your Presence Is a Danger to Your Life : Voices from Gaza , a work of oral history collating the testimonies of civilians injured in Gaza . <p> Associate publisher Tamara Sampey-Jawad acquired world English rights from Samar Hammam at Rocking Chair Books , on behalf of RAYA Agency . <p> Authored by Samar Yazbek and translated by Leri Price , the book is set to be published in May 2026 . <p> Throughout 2024 , Yazbek met with hundreds of survivors from Gaza , posing a singular question : What were you doing on 7th October , 2023 ? She has now selected 26 of these narratives to share with the world . <p> The book captures chilling accounts of these witnesses to history , their stories revealing a " nightmarish dystopia " , where each survivor has endured loss . The title , Your Presence Is a Danger to Your Life , is adapted from one of the flyers dropped on the residents of Gaza before a bombing . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ campaigner . In 2012 she was awarded the PEN Pinter " International writer of courage " award for her book In the Crossfire . In 2022 , Yazbek was chosen by the Royal Society of Literature as one of 12 international writers . <p> Sampey-Jawad said : " From parents forced to make unbearable choices to the experience of being buried alive beneath the remnants of your home , Your Presence Is a Danger to Your Life is a vital record of the incomprehensible suffering endured by the people of Gaza . Their unvarnished voices call us to bear witness , to their pain but also to their immense strength and resilience . I am immensely proud to be publishing it and to be welcoming Samar Yazbek , whose writing on Syria I have long admired , to Fitzcarraldo Editions . "
