<h> The Irish Independent 's View : A feast of weekend sport is distraction we need in a world full of suffering <p> Dan Sheehan celebrates after scoring a try during the Guinness Six Nations Rugby Championship against England at Aviva Stadium in Dublin in February . Photo : Sportsfile <p> Editorial <p> Today at 06:30 <p> There are times when switching off from the woes of an increasingly volatile world is not only desirable , it is vital for physical , mental and spiritual health . <p> Over the past fortnight many of us have been stricken by a series of international crises , as the agenda of horrors changed hourly , epitomised by the outrages in Gaza and Ukraine slipping down the world news agenda . <p> We all deserve an opportunity to switch off and get a little quality time . So , the upcoming weekend sporting banquet for Irish fans of all kinds brings us some ideal opportunities to simply enjoy the coming days . <p> The fun starts in earnest today with the opening game of the British and Irish Lions tour of Australia in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ all games kick off at 11am Irish time , allowing many of us a great chance to join in the excitement . <p> A big talking point is Lions ' team manager Andy Farrell 's surprise decision to give the captaincy to Dan Sheehan of Ireland as he makes his debut in the red jersey . This honour has fed speculation on still more such preferments that may be coming his way . <p> But let 's avoid putting pressure on such a gifted athlete . Let 's just enjoy the nine games of this tour as they build towards the first of three test matches against Australia in a fortnight , on July 19 , in Brisbane . <p> On the GAA fields , hurling is given a rest after recent thrills and upsets , and the focus is very much on Gaelic football , not forgetting Camogie and women 's football . <p> New football rules have improved things for spectators , and today and tomorrow the four quarter-finals of the All-Ireland go ahead at Croke Park . <p> First , this afternoon , Donegal play old rivals @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after six-day turnaround from their last outing . <p> Then comes Dublin versus Tyrone , which brings intrigue , as both sides have been something of an enigma this season . <p> Tomorrow , Meath play Galway and , with both sides coping with injury problems , this one could go either way . <p> Then Kerry face-off against champions Armagh in a potential game of the season . <p> Tomorrow , all roads for racing enthusiasts lead to " horse country central " , on the Curragh of Kildare , for the Dubai Duty Free Irish Derby . Organisers hope the weather gods will smile and this fixture will gather crowds worthy of its glories in the recent past . <p> All that is a mere flavour of what is on offer for the sports-mad Irish nation this weekend . These , and many other delights , offer a chance for us to forget the world 's woes and have fun .
