<h> Huge crowd support Gaza gig <p> There was a fine crowd at last Saturday 's ' Gig For Gaza ' held in The Yard Bar , which forms part of Walsh 's Bar in Mitchelstown . ( Pic : John Ahern ) <p> A capacity crowd attended last Saturday evening 's ' Gig For Gaza ' in Walsh 's Yard Bar , Mitchelstown . Organisers ( North Cork Palestine Solidarity Group ) were delighted with the level of backing the event received , stating it underscored Irish people 's support for the Palestinian cause . <p> All proceeds from the concert went to the Palestine Children 's Relief Fund . Admission was free , however , patrons gave generously to volunteer collectors . <p> There was an impressive line up with locals , Seamus Hennessy , Ciaran O'Gorman and Shauna Mansell , joining the likes of Martin Leahy , Niall Hanley and veteran , Freddie White on stage .
