<p> For some weeks now , we have seen disturbing images of malnourished skeletal infants , with visible ribs and skin barely covering their skulls . After three months of a cruel and heartless blockade of Gaza which left its population of two million people starving , homeless , desperate and humiliated , it is clear that the Israeli government is indifferent to the lives of those who live there . <p> Those they wo n't kill , they want to drive them out , to exile them to the arid deserts of Jordan and Egypt , in an act of ethnic cleansing . <p> More than 53,000 Gazans have been killed since the outbreak of the war , tens of thousands of them children . Schools , hospitals , homes , and infrastructure have been deliberately attacked . Benyamin Netanyahu and his government say they want to destroy Hamas but it is hard to come to any other conclusion that they equate Hamas with every man , woman and child who lives in Gaza .
