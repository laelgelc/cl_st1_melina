<p> THIS Sunday evening , Kilcullen Town Hall will host the launch of a compelling new work by Palestinian author and scholar Shahd Abusalama . The event , organised by the Kildare Palestine Solidarity Campaign ( KPSC ) will begin at 6pm and is open to the public with no booking required . <p> Shahd Abusalama is a Palestinian academic , author and artist , born and raised in Jabalia Refugee Camp , northern Gaza , currently based in Barcelona with her recently displaced family from Gaza .
