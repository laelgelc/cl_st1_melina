<h> Three Irish people detained in Cairo ahead of protest walk to Gaza border <p> At least three Irish people who have travelled to Egypt to take part in a protest walk to the Gaza border have been detained in Cairo . <p> Around 3,000 activists from across the world are due begin a march across the Sinai dessert on Friday to the Rafah border -- including dozens of Irish campaigners -- demanding the opening of all crossings , the entry of aid , and an end to the bombardment . <p> The Department of Foreign Affairs ( DFA ) has confirmed that it has been contacted by " a small number of citizens and is providing consular assistance " . <p> As well as Irish citizens , campaigners from the US , Australia , the Netherlands , France , Spain , Morocco and Algeria have been detained after arriving in Egypt . <p> " Over 200 participants were detained at Cairo airport or questioned at hotels across Cairo , " the march 's spokesperson Saif Abukeshek told the AFP . <p> Mr Abukeshek said plain-clothes police entered hotels in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ some cases , confiscated mobile phones and searched personal belongings . <p> " After interrogations , some were arrested and others were released , " he added . <p> DFA has urged all citizens to heed advice which strongly warns against travel to the Governorate of North Sinai , including the Taba-Suez Road , where the security situation is " extremely dangerous " . <p> " Travel to Gaza via the Rafah border crossing , the vicinity of the Rafah border crossing is particularly dangerous at the moment and the border crossing is currently closed , " DFA advice currently states . <p> In total around 40 Irish people , including TD Paul Murphy , are hoping to take part in the protest march which is expected to take a number of days to arrive at the border . <p> Since March this year , all of the crossings entering Gaza have been blocked by Israel -- no food , water , or medical supplies have been allowed to enter , which the group wants to highlight through its peaceful protest .
