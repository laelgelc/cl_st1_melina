<p> The track was written in direct response to the ongoing conflict in Gaza <p> Irish folk rock trio Scullion are set to release a new single ' The House Came Down ' , whose proceeds will go to a charity supporting children affected by war , including those in Palestine . <p> The track features singer Natacha Atlas , an artist of Palestinian and Jewish heritage who is known for her distinctive blend of Western and Middle Eastern musical influences . <p> Written by Scullion member Sonny Condell , ' The House Came Down ' was created in direct response to the ongoing conflict in Gaza . <p> " The sight of a little girl in tears , her blood-soaked bandages , her broken limbs -- the result of this cruel and meaningless conflict -- can not but stir gut feelings of disbelief and despair at the depravity of war , " Condell explained . <p> " What can a song do ?
