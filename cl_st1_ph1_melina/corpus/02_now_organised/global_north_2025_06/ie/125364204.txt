Photo : Getty <p> Kevin Cunningham <p> Today at 03:30 <p> The war in Gaza has shot up the political agenda with 15pc of people now saying it should be a priority . Last month this figure was just 3pc . So what was once a peripheral issue is now on a par with healthcare as a major public concern . <p> This is likely to have serious political implications . As we saw with the rise of other issues such as the cost of living in 2022 and immigration in 2024 , a surge in attention can reshape the political landscape and strengthen the link between parties ' positions and how voters respond .
