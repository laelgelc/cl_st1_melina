<p> The event will be introduced by the Palestinian Ambassador to Ireland Dr. Jilan Wahba Abdalmajid <p> Animated short films created by children in Gaza will be shown at The Stella Cinema in Rathmines , Dublin on Monday , June 16 . The screening , titled Gaza in Colour , is a collection of 17 short films and will take place at 6pm . <p> The films were created through workshops led by Haneen Koraz , an animation director and former teacher from Gaza . Working with children in refugee camps , Koraz has helped the young artists express themselves , process trauma and preserve their stories through a sense of childhood . <p> The filmmakers , some as young as five years old , explore themes of daily life , imagination and resilience . Some of the short films include Six Children and One Plate and My Grandfather 's Magical Cane , which were created by children from the Basmat Almat Camp in the city of Deir al-Balah in Gaza . <p> Organised by Shauna Cullen of JAM Media , with support from Animation Ireland and Avalon Films , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to Ireland , Dr. Jilan Wahba Abdalmajid . <h> Advertisement <p> " Like many , I 've been heartbroken and overwhelmed by the horrors unfolding in Palestine . Amid this devastation , I came across something extraordinary : children , living in tents , creating animated films under the guidance of Haneen Koraz , " explained Cullen . <p> " What these children have been able to produce is remarkable , " she added , " even when the full dimensions of reality become too much to bear , these children are able to find escape through art and creativity . " <p> A UNICEF finding from earlier this year reported that roughly 1 million children in the Gaza Strip need mental health and psychosocial support for depression , anxiety and suicidal thoughts . The report also stated that " no child will emerge from the horrors of the war without the imprint of trauma . "
