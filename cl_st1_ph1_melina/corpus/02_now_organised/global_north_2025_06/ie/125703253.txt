<h> Mainstream television coverage took the story more seriously than the mocking statements by the Netanyahu government <p> News of Israeli commandos taking control of the Madleen Freedom Flotilla Coalition vessel bound for Gaza made headline news in Israel on Monday morning , as it did in many places around the world . But by early afternoon , the story had already moved down Israelis ' newsfeeds . Dramatic images of the Hamas tunnels of Gaza ; the funerals of four Israeli soldiers ; the Iran nuclear deal ; and a potential constitutional crisis between the Binyamin Netanyahu government and the attorney general were a few of the stories competing for the attention of people here . It is a truism that there is never a slow news day in Israel , and certainly not in the past two years . <p> Climate activist Greta Thunberg and her crew had the misfortune of nearing Israeli-controlled waters in the week that prime minister Netanyahu , on trial for bribery for five years , was finally cross-examined in a Jerusalem court .
