<h> Dunamaise Arts Centre to stage ' At War with Mercy ' <p> IN a remarkable fusion of music and theatre , which revives the legacy of Canada 's renowned singer/songwriter , author and poet Leonard Cohen , playwrights John MacKenna and Angela Keogh are bringing their show At War with Mercy to the Dunamaise Arts Centre in Portlaoise early next month . <p> This unique production , co-written with the legendary Leonard Cohen , delves into themes of war , peace and mercy through the lens of Cohen 's timeless music and writings . <p> John MacKenna first met Leonard Cohen in 1984 and their friendship blossomed over the years through letters and later through emails . <p> The talented wordsmiths collaborated on the 2016 production titled Between Your Love and Mine , which toured 20 theatres nationally during 2017-2019 , including the National Concert Hall , Aras an Uachtar ? in and the Dunamaise Arts Centre . <p> At War with Mercy continues the Leonard Cohen/John MacKenna legacy by exploring the profound impact of war and the enduring need for mercy . <p> The show features a talented @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Shane Sullivan and percussionist Dave Day , with actors John MacKenna and Angela Keogh .
