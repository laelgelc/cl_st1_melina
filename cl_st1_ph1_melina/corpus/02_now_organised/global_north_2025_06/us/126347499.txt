( Photo by Mark Rightmire , Orange County Register/SCNG ) <p> The nation 's largest Muslim civil rights and advocacy organization has designated UC Irvine as a " hostile campus " for Palestinian , Arab and Muslim students and faculty for what it describes as a pattern of discriminatory treatment toward students and faculty . <p> UC Irvine joins 23 other colleges , including UCLA , Pomona College and Stanford in California , on the Council on American-Islamic Relations ' list of " hostile campuses . " <p> UCI officials did not immediately respond on Thursday , June 26 , to a request for comment on CAIR 's announcement . <p> CAIR officials said the designation comes in response to UCI 's crackdown on pro-Palestine @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ faculty , and a " long-standing pattern of suppressing pro-Palestinian advocacy and academic freedom . " <p> " From criminal charges to indefinite suspensions and violent police crackdowns , UC Irvine has become a national symbol of how universities punish students who dare to speak up for Palestine and against genocide , " said Maryam Hasan , CAIR 's Research and Advocacy Specialist . <p> Last spring , 47 people were arrested at UCI as police in riot gear from law enforcement agencies across Southern California responded to protests at a makeshift Gaza solidarity encampment that had stood for two weeks . The encampment was broken up during the response . <p> Several months after the protests , the OC District Attorney 's Office announced it was bringing misdemeanor charges against those arrested . <p> On the day of the arrests , UCI issued an emergency campuswide alert stating that a " violent protest " was in the area . However , the alert did not detail the nature of the violence , and no one was arrested on violent charges .
