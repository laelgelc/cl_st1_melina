" <h> Recommended Stories <p> The statement noted that the passengers would be expected to return to their home countries after the ship docks in Israel . It also said the " tiny " amount of aid the activists were attempting to deliver to Gaza would be transferred " through real humanitarian channels . " <p> " There are ways to deliver aid to the Gaza Strip -- they do not involve Instagram selfies , " the foreign ministry added .
