<p> A congressional delegation to the Middle East that will include U.S. Rep. Brad Schneider of Highland Park will not be deterred by Israel 's fresh military attack against Iran . <p> " I think this is an especially important time to be in the region , " Schneider , a Democrat representing the suburban 10th District , said in a telephone interview Friday . <p> Schneider is one of four lawmakers headed to the region . The others are fellow Democrat Jimmy Panetta of California and Republicans Zach Nunn of Iowa and Don Bacon of Nebraska . <p> The group intends to visit Israel , Saudi Arabia , the United Arab Emirates and Bahrain and meet with leaders of those nations , a Schneider spokesperson said . They 'll discuss issues including expansion of the Abraham Accords , the bilateral agreements concerning normalization between Israel and the United Arab Emirates and Bahrain . Inked in 2020 , the agreements were mediated by the U.S. <p> Schneider is co-founder and co-chair of the U.S. House 's bipartisan Abraham Accords Caucus .
