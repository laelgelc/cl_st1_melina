Ever since , Israel has dramatically stepped up plans to attack Iran . <p> In October 2024 , following an Iranian ballistic missile attack , Israel carried out an hours-long operation inside Iran , reportedly taking out many Iranian missile defence systems . The strike left Iran 's nuclear facilities vulnerable for future attacks . So if Israel 's mini regional war weakened Iran 's deterrence , the October 2024 attack turned Tehran 's domestic defence more vulnerable . Israelis saw this as a historic opportunity . They wanted to act before Iran rebuilds its domestic and regional capabilities .
