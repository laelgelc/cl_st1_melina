<p> Washington -- House Speaker Mike Johnson announced Wednesday he will travel to Israel later this month to address the Israel 's parliament , known as the Knesset , in Jerusalem . <p> " It will be one of the highest honors of my life to address the Israeli Knesset at this fateful moment , " Johnson said in a statement announcing the June 22 address . " Our ties run deeper than military partnerships and trade agreements . We 're bound by the same beliefs , the same psalms , and the same sacred pursuit of liberty . " <p> Johnson , a Louisiana Republican , has led House Republicans in their staunch support of Israel in the war in Gaza that began following Hamas ' Oct. 7 , 2023 , attack . Johnson 's speakership began weeks later , after the ouster of his predecessor , Kevin McCarthy . <p> McCarthy also addressed the Knesset during his speakership in May 2023 . And for Johnson , the overseas trip marks one of a small number he 's taken since becoming speaker .
