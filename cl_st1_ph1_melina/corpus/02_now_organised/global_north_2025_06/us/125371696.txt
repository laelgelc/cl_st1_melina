<h> Is America 's Jewish leadership failing American Jews ? <p> The murder of a young Israeli couple outside the Capital Jewish Museum in Washington , DC , last month traumatized Jews nationwide -- leaving many asking tough questions about the state of Jewish leadership in America . Their concern is understandable . <p> While the Hamas attack on Israel two Octobers ago thrust the Jewish nation into its longest war ever , it also ushered in unprecedented levels of antisemitism in the US . There were nearly 10,000 antisemitic incidents nationwide last year , according to the Anti-Defamation League , a 5% increase over the record-breaking numbers in 2023 sparked by the war in Gaza . <p> Amid this surge of hate , American Jews have begun questioning whether major Jewish groups like the ADL are doing enough -- and have done enough -- to keep Jews safe .
