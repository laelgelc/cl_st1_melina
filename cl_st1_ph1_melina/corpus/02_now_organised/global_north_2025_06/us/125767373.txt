<p> A delegation from the United States ' Federal Bureau of Investigation ( FBI ) recently arrived for a tour of the Gaza border communities to gather information and investigate crimes committed against US citizens during Hamas 's terrorist attack on Israel on October 7 , 2023 . <p> As part of the investigation , FBI representatives are working to obtain a full picture of the events from the crime scenes themselves . <p> An FBI logo is pictured on an agent 's shirt . ( credit : REUTERS/CARLO ALLEGRI ) <h> US ongoing involvement in investigating Oct. 7 <p> The agents ' visit to the communities is an additional step in the US involvement in investigating the events of that day , particularly regarding the harm to Americans . <p> US officials have previously stated that the American administration views this as " a matter of paramount importance " and is committed to holding those responsible accountable .
