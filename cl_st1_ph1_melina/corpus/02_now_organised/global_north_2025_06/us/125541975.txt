It was also not nonstop , on a route that is estimated to require 16 hours of flight time from Tel Aviv . <p> On May 19 , 1960 , a special Israeli delegation arrived in Buenos Aires on an El Al flight to attend the 150th anniversary of Argentine independence . Their visit had a secondary purpose : The following day , the Nazi war criminal Adolf Eichmann , whom Mossad agents had apprehended in Argentina , was disguised as a crew member and returned to Israel on the same plane .
