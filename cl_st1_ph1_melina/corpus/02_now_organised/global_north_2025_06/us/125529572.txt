<h> Israel recovers bodies of two hostages from Oct. 7 attacks <p> Two bodies of Israeli American hostages from the Hamas terrorist attack on Oct. 7 , 2023 , were recovered . <p> The remains of Judith Weinstein and Gad Haggai were returned to Israel on Thursday morning by the Israeli army , according to the Associated Press . Weinstein , 70 , and Haggai , 72 , have Israeli and U.S. citizenship . Weinstein also had Canadian citizenship . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced their bodies were recovered in a special mission conducted by the army and the Shin Bet internal security agency . <p> " Together with all the citizens of Israel , my wife and I extend our heartfelt condolences to the dear families , " Netanyahu said in a statement . " Our hearts ache for the most terrible loss . May their memory be blessed . " <p> The married couple were killed while taking a walk on the morning of Oct. 7 in the kibbutz Nir Oz . Both were shot during the attack , the Associated Press reported .
