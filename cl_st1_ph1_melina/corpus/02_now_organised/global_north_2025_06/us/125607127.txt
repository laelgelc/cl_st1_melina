<h> Rs 5 Parle-G Costs Rs 2,400 In Gaza : A Manufactured Famine ? <p> We bring you a story that starkly illustrates the devastating human cost of conflict and famine . For many of us , the sight of a familiar red and yellow packet of Parle-G biscuits evokes warm memories of childhood , a simple , affordable treat found in nearly every Indian home . In India , a small packet might cost you just a couple of rupees , a larger one perhaps Rs 25 . But now , imagine that this same packet , a basic sustenance , becomes a luxury item costing thousands of rupees . That 's the unimaginable reality unfolding in Gaza , where a single packet of Parle-G is reportedly being sold for an astronomical Rs 2,400 equivalent in the local currency . This is n't just about biscuits ; it 's about the erosion of dignity , the desperation of hunger , and the shocking reality of basic food items becoming utterly unattainable.
