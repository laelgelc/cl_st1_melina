<p> GAZA CITY , Gaza Strip ( AP ) -- An Associated Press photographer captured the moment an Israeli airstrike slammed into a building in Gaza City , showing a rare glimpse of the devastation Israeli air power has had on the Palestinian territory during the 19-month war with Hamas . <p> Jehad Alshrafi 's photos show a fireball , a massive cloud of smoke and people running away after Sunday 's strike . Israel had warned residents before hitting the building , and there were no reports of casualties . <p> The target of the strike was not known . <p> This is a photo gallery curated by AP photo editors . <p> Copyright 2025 The Associated Press .
