<p> The Russian writer Ivan Pavlov wrote about the tendency of humans and animals to respond automatically to external stimuli . This phenomenon , which became known as the " Pavlovian response , " aptly describes the Arab reaction to Israel 's attack on Iran 's nuclear facilities . <p> Almost all Arab states that have signed peace or normalization agreements with Israel -- or maintain behind-the-scenes ties with it -- issued statements of condemnation , nearly identical in wording , against the attack . <p> Once again , a familiar pattern has emerged , of conflict between their public positions and hidden interests . The Saudi foreign minister spoke with his Iranian counterpart and expressed " the Kingdom 's condemnation of the hideous Israeli aggression .
