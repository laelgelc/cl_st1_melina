Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met with top Egyptian officials , including President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi , Iranian state media said . This is important . <p> Egypt borders Gaza and historically has played an important role in Gaza . Iran likely wants to discuss this and other issues with Egypt . <p> Egypt has peace with Israel . However , any Iranian involvement in Egypt will be of concern to Israel .
