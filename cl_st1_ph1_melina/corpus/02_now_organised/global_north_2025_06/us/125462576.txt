<h> Have we passed peak Trump ? <p> Have we reached peak Trump ? Is it possible that we have arrived at a moment , a mere four months into his second term , when the president 's capacity to do harm is diminishing ? <p> That is undeniably a provocative question . Like any US president , Donald Trump remains immensely powerful . It is early days ; he can still cause plenty of damage -- and certainly will . <p> But after an initial flurry of activity when the opposition often seemed deer-in-the-headlights stunned , Trump 's power to daze and paralyze may now be on a downward trajectory . Recognizing that possible shift is important to embolden resistance to his dangerous , would-be autocratic rule . <p> Many of Trump 's actions followed the classic autocrat 's playbook as he deliberately attacked the checks and balances on his power . Plans proceeded to remove the tax-exempt status of some private foundations and civic groups . <p> Trump made a mark in his first few months in part because the brazenness and velocity of his actions encouraged a save-yourself mentality among many targets . Some law firms , universities and media outlets struck deals with him , hoping to protect themselves at the expense of the rule of law , academic freedom or freedom of the media . <p> Yet over time , the resistance regrouped .
