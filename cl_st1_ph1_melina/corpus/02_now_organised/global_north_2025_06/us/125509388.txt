<p> ( WASHINGTON ) -- The Department of Education sent a warning to the accreditor of Columbia University on Wednesday , saying the Ivy League institution violated federal anti-discrimination laws . <p> U.S. Secretary of Education Linda McMahon said in a press release that the decision came after the spate of protests on university grounds opposing the Israel-Hamas war in Gaza . <p> McMahon alleged Columbia leadership " acted with deliberate indifference towards the harassment of Jewish students on its campus . " <p> " This is not only immoral , but also unlawful , " McMahon said . <p> Columbia is accused of violating Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 , which prohibits recipients of federal funding from discriminating on the basis of race , color or national origin , according to the department . <p> The department said that by violating federal anti-discrimination laws , Columbia has failed to meet the standards of accreditation that have been set by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education . <p> The government claims Columbia leadershup failed to take several actions , including failing to establish effective reporting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to abide by its own policies and procedures when responding to Jewish students ' complaints as well as governing misconduct against Jewish students and not investigating or punishing vandalism in its classrooms . <p> Now , the university 's federal accreditor is required to establish a plan to come into compliance with the federal law or " take appropriate action against " Columbia , the department said . <p> ABC News has reached out to Columbia University for a comment . <p> This is President Donald Trump 's administration 's latest action taken against prominent universities , claiming they have fostered a breeding ground for harassment of Jewish students amid the ongoing Israel-Hamas war . <p> Last month , the Trump administration announced and then backtracked on a decision to bar Harvard University from allowing international students to enroll at the university . <p> Harvard is also fighting the Trump administration 's attempt to freeze more than $2.2 billion in grants and $60 million in contracts to the school .
