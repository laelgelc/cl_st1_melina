Sundar Pichai 's Stanford and Wharton degrees were stepping stones to becoming Google 's boss . Indra Nooyi earned her MBA from Yale before reshaping PepsiCo . Arvind Krishna , who heads IBM , trained at the University of Illinois , while Nobel laureate Amartya Sen 's ideas on justice and welfare economics were sharpened at Harvard .
