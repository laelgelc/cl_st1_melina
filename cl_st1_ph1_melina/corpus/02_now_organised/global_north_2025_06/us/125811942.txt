<p> In May of 1971 , Marvin Gaye released what many consider to be his masterwork , " What 's Going On ? " A song cycle told from the point of view of a Vietnam veteran returning to the United States after serving abroad , having witnessed all manner of suffering , violence , and injustice , it is not an entirely idyllic album . Yet the inquisitive nature of its title , and the question that foregrounds the work , suggests a faith in America held , if not by Gaye himself , then certainly by the speaker he invents to guide listeners through the forest of concerns the album conjures .
