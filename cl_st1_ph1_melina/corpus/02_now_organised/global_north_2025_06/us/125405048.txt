<p> Armed and masked gunmen in Gaza opened fire on civilians collecting aid at a distribution center , drone footage released by the Israel Defense Forces shows . <p> The footage , shared by the IDF on X on Sunday and purportedly taken in the nearby city of Khan Younis , shows the armed thugs throwing stones and shooting at civilians trying to collect basic supplies . <p> " Drone footage shows gunmen in Gaza shooting at civilians going to collect aid . Hamas is doing everything in its power to prevent the successful distribution of food in Gaza , " the IDF wrote in the post accompanying the footage . <p> The IDF footage was reportedly taken in Khan Yunis in the south of the Gaza Strip .
