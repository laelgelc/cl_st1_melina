<h> The future of the Palestinian movement <p> The Palestinian movement is at a crossroads . Fatah and its foremost rival for power , Hamas , are both weakened , the former by internal divisions and unpopularity and the latter by the Israeli military . Palestinian politics are entering a transitional phase , and Palestinian political institutions are dead or decaying .
