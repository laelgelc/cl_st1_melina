Her entire life has been sped up physically but not emotionally . She has been through menopause but has n't yet had her first crush on a boy . We first meet Kimberly 's father Buddy ( Jim Hogan ) when he shows up in the rink 's parking lot , awfully tipsy and two hours late to pick her up , which means she 's been waiting in the snowstorm for him .
