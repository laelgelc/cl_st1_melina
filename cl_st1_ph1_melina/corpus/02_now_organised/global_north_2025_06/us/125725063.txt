<p> Over 7,000 pro-Palestinian activists from Mauritania , Morocco , Algeria , Tunisia , and Libya have begun a protest convoy on Monday , with the aim of reaching the Rafah crossing by land . <p> The Maghreb Resilience Convoy 's 300-vehicle caravan departed from Tunis on Monday morning , before crossing the Libyan border on Tuesday . The ' Soumoud convoy ' aims to " bring food and humanitarian aid to Gaza and open a humanitarian corridor , " the organizers said . <p> According to participating groups , " doctors , students , lawyers , and everyday people " are attending the convoy . <p> The convoy forms part of the Global March to Gaza on June 15 , during which 2500 activists from over 50 countries plan to march from Al Arish in Egypt to Rafah , Gaza . <p> Palestinians seeking aid gather near an aid distribution site in Rafah , in the southern Gaza Strip , May 27 , 2025 ( credit : REUTERS/Hatem Khaled ) <h> March will take three days <p> The march is 48km and will take three days , according to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Palestine in Tunisia and Libyans For Palestine . <p> The Global Marchers claimed they are " planning a short stay in Rafah , from June 15- 19th , " but " this will depend on authorizations from the Egyptian government . " <p> " We aim to negotiate the opening of the Rafah terminal with the Egyptian authorities , in collaboration with NGOs , diplomats , and humanitarian institutions , " they said . " <p> Both the convoy and the March to Gaza have been hugely publicized among pro-Palestine groups around the world .
