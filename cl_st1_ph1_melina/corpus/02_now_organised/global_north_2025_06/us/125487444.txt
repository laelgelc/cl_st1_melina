<p> Ukraine 's surprise drone attack targeting dozens of Russian aircraft last week was a wake-up call for the Defense and Homeland Security departments to the emerging threats that unmanned aerial vehicles can pose domestically . <p> The mission , dubbed Operation Spider 's Web , involved the use of cheap drones to target much more expensive Russian aircraft deep behind enemy lines , which were used to carry out repeated bombings of Ukraine 's infrastructure . <p> In the daring operation , Ukraine smuggled more than 100 relatively cheap drones across the Russian border . They were then deployed with explosives , targeting Russia 's nuclear bomber fleet at multiple military bases .
