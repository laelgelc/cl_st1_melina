<p> Following the U.S. attack on Iran 's primary nuclear facilities at Fordo , Natanz and Isfahan , Tehran faces nothing but bad options . Militarily , Iran can escalate the conflict by attacking U.S. forces and allies in the region , as it did on Monday with missile attacks on U.S. bases in Qatar and Iraq . Iran could also close the Strait of Hormuz , withdraw from the nuclear nonproliferation treaty or even attempt a rapid " breakout " run to a bomb with its residual capabilities . Each of these options virtually assures an American military response that goes far beyond Iran 's nuclear program , possibly leading to a targeted campaign to topple the regime , the Islamic Republic 's greatest nightmare . <p> A more likely military response would therefore be for Iran to respond by continuing to attack Israel -- as it did just hours after the U.S. strike -- in an attempt to turn the conflict into a war of attrition that Israel can ill afford .
