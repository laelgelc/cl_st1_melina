<h> See photos before and after the Arb 's peony garden was vandalized <p> ANN ARBOR , MI -- The nation 's largest peony garden was vandalized over the weekend as protesters sheared the flowers from about one third of the blooming plants . <p> The vandalism was discovered around 6:14 a.m . Sunday , June 1 by staff at the Nichols Arboretum , University of Michigan Division of Public Safety and Security said in a statement . Roughly 250 peony plants had their flowers removed , and police say staff discovered flyers calling for more support of Palestine amid the ongoing war with Israel in Gaza . <p> As many as 100,000 visitors flock to the garden each year to witness about 10,000 flowers at peak bloom , which was expected at around the same time that the vandalism occurred . The damage was limited to the west beds of the garden , which are divided from the rest by a gravel walking path . <p> Plein air artist Janet Kohler , who has used pastels and oil paints to depict the flowers for more than 20 years , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to witness the aftermath . Some visitors arranged the fallen petals in a heart-shaped memorial . <p> " The mood was pretty somber , people were crying . " Kohler said . " It 's really sad that political group felt they had to do that . " <p> Her subjects for a pastel landscape painting have been cut down . Kohler was working on a piece featuring pink flowers in the foreground and the flowering azaleas of the centennial shrub garden behind . <p> Kohler said she was approached on Sunday by Tony Kolenic , Director of Matthaei Botanical Gardens and Nichols Arboretum , who spoke briefly about the vandalism in a Sunday social media post . <p> " He was in tears , " she said . " This is a healing place for all of Ann Arbor .
