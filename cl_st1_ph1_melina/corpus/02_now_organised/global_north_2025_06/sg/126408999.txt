<h> 16 tonnes of korban meat donated by Muslims in Singapore to be distributed in Gaza <p> SINGAPORE -- Some 16 tonnes of canned sheep meat donated by Muslims in Singapore will be delivered to recipients in Gaza later in 2025 . <p> Acting Minister-in-charge of Muslim Affairs Faishal Ibrahim said on June 28 that this contribution shows that Singapore 's Muslim community is " deeply mindful of our responsibility to support those facing humanitarian challenges abroad " . <p> Speaking on the sidelines of a korban meat packing and distribution event in Toa Payoh , he said the korban meat -- after being processed and canned -- is slated for distribution to Palestinians in Gaza in the second half of 2025 . <p> Distribution will be carried out by trusted partners on the ground , added Associate Professor Faishal , citing the Jordan Hashemite Charity Organisation as an example . <p> Korban refers to the Islamic ritual of slaughtering farm animals such as sheep and lambs , followed by the distribution of the meat to worshippers and the needy . <p> It takes place annually during Hari Raya Haji @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of SalamSG -- a platform for key mosque programmes in Singapore . <p> Locally , 39 tonnes of meat will be distributed by various mosques to about 1,000 beneficiaries . <p> Korban rituals are fulfilled mostly overseas , in Australia , where livestock is slaughtered , before their meat is chilled , packed and sent to Singapore . <p> In Singapore , six mosques have approvals to host korban rituals . <p> Prof Faishal said close to 5,700 orders for overseas korban livestock were placed in 2025 , the highest since 2022 and a 20 per cent increase on 2024 's orders . <p> On the donation of korban meat to Palestinians , Prof Faishal said : " We are closely monitoring the situation to ensure that the aid reaches its intended recipient .
