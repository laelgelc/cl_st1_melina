<p> ( JTA ) -- Multiple people were burned and injured in Boulder , Colorado , in what the local Jewish community said was a firebombing attack on people who had gathered to draw attention to the Israeli hostages being held by Hamas in Gaza . <p> An eyewitness account posted by a pro-Israel X user said that most of the victims were in their 70s . Video from the scene shared on social media showed smoke and people on the ground . Screaming was audible . Another video showed a shirtless man , holding spray bottles , yelling , " End Zionists . " <p> An adult male suspect was arrested but police declined to name him or speculate on his motive in the hours after the incident . Without offering details , FBI Director Kash Patel posted that his agency was investigating what he said was a " targeted terror attack . " <p> The incident took place shortly after 1 p.m. on the Pearl Street Mall in downtown Boulder , which hosts the city 's Run for Our Lives . The weekly walks are held in communities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ held by Hamas . <p> It comes 10 days after a pro-Palestinian activist shot and killed two attendees of an event held at the Capital Jewish Museum in Washington , D.C. <p> " We are saddened and heartbroken to learn that an incendiary device was thrown at walkers at the Run for Our Lives walk on Pearl Street , " local Jewish leaders , including rabbis , said in a statement distributed by the Boulder Jewish Community Center . They added , " Our hearts go out to those who witnessed this horrible attack , and prayers for a speedy recovery to those who were injured . " <p> Boulder Police Chief Stephen Redfearn said at a press conference that the initial emergency call had said people were being set on fire and that multiple people had been transported to hospitals with injuries " fairly consistent " with that description .
