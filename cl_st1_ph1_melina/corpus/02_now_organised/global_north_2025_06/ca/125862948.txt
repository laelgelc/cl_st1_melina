<p> The 55th Regular Session of the General Assembly of the Organization of American States ( OAS ) will be held in one of its smallest member states at a time of global political and economic uncertainty . That uncertainty is reflected across the Americas and within the OAS itself . <p> In this regard , the General Assembly in Antigua and Barbuda , from June 25 to 27 , takes on special significance : it must provide atmosphere and leadership to reinforce the OAS as a relevant instrument for advancing both individual and collective interests in the hemisphere . <p> Adding to the importance of this session is the first official appearance of the organisation 's new secretary-general , Albert Ramdin , who assumed office on May 26 . He is the first CARICOM national elected to this top post . For 40 years , CARICOM nationals held only the ssistant aecretary-general role while Latin American countries dominated the leadership . His unanimous election , following a contest with Paraguay 's foreign minister , reflects confidence in CARICOM 's contributions within the OAS . This assembly takes place amid overlapping political , economic , social , security , and environmental pressures , and against a backdrop of longstanding institutional strains within the OAS . The June assassination attempt on Miguel Uribe , a presidential candidate in Colombia , underscores risks to electoral integrity and citizen confidence . However , the most acute emergency grips Haiti , where armed groups now control the majority of Port-au-Prince , blocking basic state functions , precipitating a humanitarian catastrophe , and preventing the restoration of democratic institutions and the election of a representative government . At the General Assembly , it is incumbent upon the OAS and its member states , within the framework of the OAS Charter and their respective means , to continue demonstrating solidarity with the people of Haiti . <h> DISCUSSIONS IN THE MARGINS <p> As important as the formal agenda is , equally vital are the informal discussions among high representatives of member and Observer states that will take place outside public sessions .
