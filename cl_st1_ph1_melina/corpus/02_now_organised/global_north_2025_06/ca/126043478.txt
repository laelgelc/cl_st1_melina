<h> How an 85-year-old Canadian Holocaust survivor is riding out the Israel-Iran war in a Jerusalem hotel <p> Canadian Holocaust survivor and educator Eva Kuper in the lobby of her Israeli hotel during the Israel-Iran war on June 16 , 2025 . ( Submitted photo ) <p> Eva Kuper , a Holocaust survivor from Montreal , spent the first five years of her life hiding from the Nazis in German-occupied Poland . Now , the well-known Holocaust educator is stranded in Israel together with a group of university and CEGEP students on the " Journey of Hope " , a trip supported by Montreal 's Federation CJA . <p> After visiting Auschwitz with the students two weeks ago , and taking them to see the sites in Poland where Kuper grew up between 1940 and 1945 , the delegation landed in Israel a week ago -- just before Israel launched its surprise air raids on Iran on June 13 . Since then , Iran has been retaliating with nightly barrages of missiles and drones that have now killed 24 people and injured more than 600 others .
