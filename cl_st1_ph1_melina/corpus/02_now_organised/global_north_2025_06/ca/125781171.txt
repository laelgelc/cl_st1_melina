The August 2025 futures contract has been the focal point of intense buying activity , gaining $31.20 in yesterday 's session followed by an additional $31.50 today , representing a substantial 0.97% increase to settle at $3,408.60 as of 4:00 PM Eastern Time . <p> The rally marks a significant technical achievement for gold , which opened Monday 's trading session at $3,333.40 and has since surged more than $75 per ounce in just three trading days . This upward trajectory has been substantially aided by corresponding weakness in the U.S. dollar , which has declined 0.72% today alone , pulling the dollar index down to 97.93 . <p> The fundamental drivers behind this rally paint a complex picture of global uncertainty .
