<p> Dozens of students walked out of class at a west end Toronto District School Board ( TDSB ) high school on Monday after faculty distributed yearbooks with a sticker covering up a photo of two students wearing Palestinian keffiyehs . <p> When yearbooks were handed out at Ursula Franklin Academy ( UFA ) last Friday , students discovered a sticker on one page that when peeled back , revealed a photo of two students , smiling and wearing keffiyehs -- a traditional Arab head covering with a distinctive woven pattern . Though the scarf often symbolizes support for Palestinian freedom and nationhood , some claim the scarf is a symbol associated with violence or terror . <p> The sticker covered the whole photo and had the same colour as the background design of the page -- giving the initial appearance that no photo was there at all . <p> A student who spoke anonymously with TorontoToday alleged that when she brought her concerns to a teacher and the school principal Petal Farquharson last Friday afternoon , she was told the sticker was placed to cover an error . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ claimed the photo had been obscured due to a " misprint " by the yearbook company , according to a recording shared by a student with TorontoToday . <p> But days later , the TDSB told TorontoToday that the decision to cover the photo had been made by the school for political reasons -- and that on further review , it should have been permitted . <p> " The initial decision was made to cover the picture with a sticker , as it was viewed as one of a political nature , " the TDSB spokesperson said in an emailed statement . <p> " Upon further review , it has been determined that as students are permitted to wear cultural attire in school , there is no need for the picture to be covered .
