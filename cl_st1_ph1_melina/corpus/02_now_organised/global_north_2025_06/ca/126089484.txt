RGW supports refugee girls and women affected by displacement . MedGlobal delivers healthcare in places where medical systems have collapsed or are severely limited . Together , they can expand their reach and respond to a wider spectrum of needs across diverse regions . <p> Refugee Girls Worldwide ( RGW ) supports refugee girls through access to education , health care , and protection programs . The organization is dedicated to rebuilding lives and restoring dignity for women and girls affected by displacement . <p> MedGlobal is a humanitarian organization that provides medical aid in fragile and underserved areas . Their teams work with local partners to support clinics , train healthcare workers , deliver urgent services in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ healthcare solutions . <p> Expanding Impact Through Strategic Partnership <p> RGW has supported over 220,000 people across more than ten countries . In recent years , they 've opened schools , distributed food and hygiene kits , built recovery housing , and launched clean water projects in post-disaster areas . More than 50,000 patients have received care at RGW clinics . Thousands of girls have enrolled in scholarship programs or vocational training . <p> With MedGlobal , RGW will be able to expand these services and introduce new layers of support . This includes better-equipped clinics , additional recovery programs , and greater capacity to respond during times of acute need . <p> The partnership will also allow both organizations to bring increased impact in shared regions including Gaza , Syria , Ukraine and Sudan . It reflects a shared vision that deeper collaboration strengthens humanitarian work , especially in areas where systems are strained and needs remain high .
