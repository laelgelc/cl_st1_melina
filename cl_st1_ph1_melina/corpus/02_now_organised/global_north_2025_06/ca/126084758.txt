<h> Canada announces travel assistance for its citizens in Israel <p> Canadians in Israel seeking to get back home as the Israel-Iran war enters its seventh day , now have some options thanks to the Canadian government organizing commercial flights from neighbouring countries . <p> According to the Registration of Canadians Abroad service , there are more than 6,600 Canadians registered in Israel . It is not known if those individual registrations are up-to-date and if all registrants are planning or seeking assistance to leave . <p> " In the interest of Canadians in the region , we are planning commercial options for Canadians in Israel , the West Bank , and Iran to leave the region via certain neighbouring countries , " Foreign Affairs Minister Anita Anand announced Thursday morning , June 19 . " This is in addition to ongoing efforts to assist Canadians attempting to leave Gaza .
