<p> Jon Allen is a senior fellow at the Munk School of Global Affairs and a former Canadian ambassador to Israel and Spain . <p> Canada joined four other Western countries on Tuesday ( Britain , Australia , New Zealand and Norway , but not France ) in sanctioning two far-right senior ministers in Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's cabinet . The two ministers represent different parties in Mr. Netanyahu 's right-of-centre coalition government and occupy key positions . <p> Itamar Ben-Gvir , the Minister of National Security who is responsible for the police , was so extreme in his youth that he was barred from serving in the Israeli army and was subsequently convicted of both incitement to racism and supporting terrorism . Bezalel Smotrich , the Finance Minister who has special responsibilities for the West Bank , is a self-described homophobe and proponent of a greater Israel . He has called recently for the depopulation of Palestinians from the Gaza Strip and for the full annexation of the West Bank .
