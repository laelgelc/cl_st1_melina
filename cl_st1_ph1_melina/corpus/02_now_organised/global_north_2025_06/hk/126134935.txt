Hitler and Mussolini both loudly proclaimed that democracies were weak and decadent , yet it was they who ended up in history 's graveyard . <p> In fact , there 's pretty robust evidence that democracies -- at least , as we currently identify them -- tend to win wars more often than autocracies do .
