<p> The head of the world 's largest academic association on genocide has declared Israel 's actions in Gaza a genocide . <p> Dr Melanie O'Brien , president of the International Association of Genocide Scholars ( IAGS ) , outlined her legal opinion in an interview with Middle East Eye , ahead of an upcoming episode of the Expert Witness podcast featuring leading genocide experts . <p> " Applying the legal definitions of genocide as found in the Genocide Convention and the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court , I would determine that what is happening in Gaza constitutes genocide , " O'Brien said . <p> " The definition involves acts committed with intent to destroy , in whole or in part , a national , ethnic , racial , or religious group . These include killing or imposing conditions meant to bring about physical destruction , " she explained , referring to the definition of genocide under the Genocide Convention . <p> " What we are witnessing fits within that legal definition .
