The first is the way the groups chosen for infiltration were cherry-picked . The Special Demonstration Squad ( then called the Special Operations Squad ) was set up in 1968 to target Vietnam War protesters . In the following 50 years , it sent officers into , amongst others , the anti-apartheid movement , Camps for Climate Action , and the campaign to bring the killers of Stephen Lawrence to court . Right-wing groups were largely left alone . <p> Most of those left-wing groups are now regarded as having occupied the moral high ground .
