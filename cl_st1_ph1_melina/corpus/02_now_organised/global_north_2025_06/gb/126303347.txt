<h> Argentina begins evacuating nationals stranded in Israel <p> The Argentine government launched a repatriation operation to evacuate around 450 nationals of the South American country stranded in Israel amid the conflict with Iran , which has nevertheless been reported to have ended , at least for now . <p> The first evacuation flight , prioritizing families with infants , the elderly , and people with health issues , departed from Tel Aviv to Rome on Monday . A second flight followed Tuesday , with two more flights scheduled by Thursday . <p> Argentina chartered aircraft from the few Israeli airlines still operating : Israir , El Al , and Arkia . Due to security protocols , early flights carried just 50 passengers , though later flights are expected to carry up to 178 . In Rome , evacuees will board Aerol ? neas Argentinas flights to Buenos Aires . <p> Initially delayed due to escalating tensions , the operation resumed following a ceasefire announcement by US President Donald Trump and updated security clearances from Israel .
