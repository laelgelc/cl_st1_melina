<p> 75-year-old Lionel Richie may have poked fun at his advancing years last night during his show at Manchester 's Co-op Live , but his performance was as energetic as ever . " The coffee has kicked in , as have the vitamin pills , " said the legendary singer songwriter , as he got himself acquainted with the half-full arena . In his 43-year career , he has sold over 125 million albums , making him one of the most successful artists of all time . Last night was a celebration of this .
