<h> Watch : Moment Israeli forces board Gaza aid boat <p> The 12 activists , including Swedish campaigner Greta Thunberg , were detained and their boat , the Madleen , was towed to the Israeli port city of Ashdod . <p> The Madleen 's journey was organised by the Freedom Flotilla Coalition , a campaign group that has been trying to breach a blockade on Gaza that was imposed by Israel in 2007 when Hamas took control of the territory . <p> The activists who set sail on 1 June , said the goal of the mission , which carried a small amount of supplies including rice and baby formula , was to raise awareness of the current humanitarian crisis in Gaza caused by Israel 's restrictions on the delivery of aid . <p> Israel dismissed it as a " selfie yacht " , saying it would deport the activists to their home countries .
