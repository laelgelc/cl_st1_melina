Of course not ! I 'm the realist in the room , facing the world with honesty and without delusion ! <p> But I also have to check myself on my doomscrolling preferences -- how much gratification I 'm getting out of them . So what might seem to others as doomscrolling , obsessively informing myself about what will supersede humanity , I see as readying myself for a coming new era . <p> Again , I do n't feel lost in doom . It 's more that I 'm preparing myself for an oncoming future of greater difficulties -- ones that will compel profound transformations in what counts as a " normal lifestyle " , our consumptions , productions and values . <p> I 'm getting myself ready for things dropping out of , and into , my life .
