" <p> Independent MP Jeremy Corbyn told Mr Healey : " Surely we could be doing things in a way that brings about a more peaceful world , rather than just pouring more and more money into weapons . " <p> And left Labour MP Richard Burgon demanded to know how the expansion of the nuclear submarine fleet under the Aukus deal with Australia and the US could be squared with Britain 's non-proliferation obligations .
