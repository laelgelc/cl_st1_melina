<p> ISRAEL 'S illegal and unprovoked bombing of Iran , killing military leaders , scientists and numerous other civilians , puts us on the brink of catastrophic conflict . <p> An attack on this scale would be treated as an act of war by any country . The aggressor is Israel and its conduct puts us all at risk . Its attack on Iran must not just be unequivocally condemned : the already overwhelming case for a halt to military co-operation and arms supplies has become more urgent . <p> Its formal excuse for attacking Iran is illegitimate . Suspicion that Iran might be close to developing nuclear weapons is not grounds for attacking it .
