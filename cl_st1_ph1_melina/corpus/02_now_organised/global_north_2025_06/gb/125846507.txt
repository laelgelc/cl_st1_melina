But I know one : Pessy , who was a toddler back in 1940 , a Jewish refugee fleeing the Nazis with her parents . My book Shepherd of Another Flock , published in 2017 , featured a fair few folk with remarkable stories of their experience of the Second World War . But none was as remarkable as that of Pessy , who had been my wife 's neighbour when she was growing up in Sheffield , before Pessy and her family relocated to Jerusalem . <p> Pessy and her parents originally lived in Leipzig , but Nazi storm troopers started rampaging through the town on Kristallnacht in 1938 .
