More than 225 journalists have been killed in Gaza over the course of the war - the deadliest period for media workers in living memory . Yet the BBC saw fit to report on just six percent of those deaths . <p> These were not nameless casualties . They were mothers , fathers , sons and daughters , documenting their people 's suffering with extraordinary courage . Many died with their cameras still in hand . Their deaths should have been a central part of any newsroom 's self-reflection . Instead , they were treated as background noise . <p> Compare this with Ukraine , where 62 percent of journalist deaths were covered by the BBC . The disparity speaks volumes .
