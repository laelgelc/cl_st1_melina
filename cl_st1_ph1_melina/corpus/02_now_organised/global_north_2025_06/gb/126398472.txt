<p> Vali Nasr , an Iranian-American political scientist based in Washington , must be cursing Benjamin Netanyahu . As Israel 's ebullient prime minister seeks to reshape the Middle East , with the help of Donald Trump 's bunker-busting bombs , it may feel like Nasr 's new book is already yesterday 's news . That would be a shame , because Iran 's Grand Strategy is seriously good , profoundly insightful and full of fresh thinking . <p> A central pillar of Nasr 's argument is that the West 's understanding of Iran 's strategic calculations is " hopelessly inadequate and dangerously outdated " .
