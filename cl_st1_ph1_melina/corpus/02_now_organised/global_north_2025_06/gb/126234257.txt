<h> The UK justice system is mutating into a tool of ideological control <p> This article is brought to you by our exclusive subscriber partnership with our sister title USA Today , and has been written by our American colleagues . It does not necessarily reflect the view of The Herald . <p> THE Home Secretary Yvette Cooper 's decision to proscribe Palestine Action as a terrorist organisation is a deeply troubling development -- not because the group 's actions are beyond reproach , but because the framework used to criminalise them sets a dangerous precedent . It establishes a two-tier justice system , but unlike the fictional grievance pushed by those who claim anti-racist or inclusive causes are " above the law . " <p> Palestine Action 's tactics are unquestionably disruptive and often illegal , but so were those of the suffragettes in smashing windows , burning vacant buildings , and assaulting police officers by knocking their hats off . More recently , Trident Ploughshares have , like Palestinian Action , targeted military infrastructure in acts of non-violent civil disobedience , including extensive damage to a submarine at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ earlier campaigners were charged with criminal offences like trespass or damage -- not branded terrorists . Their illegality was dealt with proportionately . No special powers were needed . <p> What 's different now is the move from punishing actions to punishing affiliation . Under terrorism legislation , an individual can commit a disruptive , unlawful protest act and be charged accordingly , yet someone who does nothing illegal but is a member of a proscribed group is classed as a terrorist by association alone . This flips a foundational legal principle on its head . We no longer judge people by what they do , but by who they are linked to -- or perceived to be . <p> This is not the how any justice system should function . The UK system is mutating into a tool of ideological control , and it raises the question of why now , and why this group ? When far-right movements provoke hate and violence , they are often treated with caution . Not the imagined version fuelling bigotry and backlash , but a hard , legal fact .
