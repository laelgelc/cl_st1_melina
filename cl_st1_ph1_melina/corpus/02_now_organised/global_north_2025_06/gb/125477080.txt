<h> Three days in Tashkent : Negotiating a two-state Israel-Palestine resolution <p> SNP MP for Dundee Central Chris Law recently travelled to Uzbekistan to help negotiate a resolution on a two-state solution for Israel and Palestine . Israel has only partially lifted an 11-week blockade of all food and other humanitarian supplies into Gaza . Having returned to northern Gaza after a brief Israeli-Hamas ceasefire , Gazans now face the prospect of another Israeli ground offensive . None have been more urgent or emotionally resonant than this . <p> About BGIPU and the IPU <p> The British Group Inter-Parliamentary Union ( BGIPU ) is a fully funded arms-length body of the UK Parliament , representing the UK in the IPU , the world 's oldest multilateral political organisation . <p> Founded in 1889 , the IPU now brings together 181 national parliaments to promote peace , democracy , and international cooperation . It works closely with the United Nations and other multilateral bodies . <p> The 12+ is a 47-member ' European and Others ' geopolitical group of the IPU . It broadly resembles the Western European and Others Group at the United Nations , but also includes most Eastern European parliaments .
