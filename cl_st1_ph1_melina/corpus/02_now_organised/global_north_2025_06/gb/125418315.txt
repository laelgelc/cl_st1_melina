<p> ISRAEL is deporting international activists for seeking to expose the forced displacement of Palestinian communities in the West Bank , campaigners have warned . <p> Swedish citizen Susanne Bjork , 48 , and 70-year-old Irish citizen D Murphy , both based in Britain , were arrested by Israeli authorities on Saturday in Khalet al-Daba'a , a village in the Masafer Yatta area whose residents are under imminent threat of expulsion . <p> Ms Bjork was deported this morning , while Ms Murphy is contesting her deportation and faces imprisonment . <p> According to the International Solidarity Movement ( ISM ) , the Israeli military ordered the two women to leave the village . <p> As they complied , they were stopped and harassed by Israeli settlers in military uniform -- reservists called up since the start of the onslaught on Gaza in October 2023 . <p> Israeli police later arrested Ms Bjork and Ms Murphy , accusing them of being in a military zone . <p> The Irishwoman said : " When most governments all around the world are ignoring the genocide in Gaza and the ethnic cleansing of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Palestinians ' call to come and be a witness to these events being carried out by the zionist Israeli entity . <p> " It 's not about politics , it 's about justice and freedom for all people . " <p> Her son Dale Ryan said : " As far as I can see , her only crime was observing crimes against Palestinian people . " <p> Last year , Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben Gvir set up a task force to expel international activists from the West Bank , claiming it was a response to US sanctions targeting violent settlers . <p> Activists have faced arrest and deportation for reporting settler violence to their governments and communities . <p> The 120 residents of Khalet al-Daba'a have faced escalating violence . <p> Since February , Israeli forces have demolished homes , tents , and vital infrastructure , including electricity , water and internet systems . <p> On May 5 , 90 per cent of the village 's remaining structures were destroyed . <p> Settlers have since arrived daily to harass families who continue to resist eviction . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 22 new settlements in the West Bank -- the largest such scheme in decades -- defying an International Court of Justice ruling that the occupation and settlements are illegal . <p> The ISM said : " We ask the international community to act now , to stop the forced displacement , to bring justice for the Palestinians and to stop deporting activists struggling alongside families .
