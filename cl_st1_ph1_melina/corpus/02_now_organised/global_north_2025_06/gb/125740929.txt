<h> US 's worldwide popularity plummets thanks to ' arrogant ' Trump <p> Respondents have little confidence in Trump as a world leader and his ability to address global challenges , from wars in Ukraine and Gaza to climate change and U.S. immigration policies . Stan Gilliland/EPA-EFE <p> The United States has become much less popular abroad since President Donald Trump returned to office , according to a new survey . <p> Pew Research Center surveyed people in 24 countries , including 10 in Europe , from late February through early April . It found that the share of respondents who view the U.S. favorably has dropped in most of them compared to the spring of 2024 . <p> Respondents have little confidence in Trump as a world leader or in his ability to address global challenges , from wars in Ukraine and Gaza to climate change and U.S. immigration policies . <p> Advertisement <p> Advertisement <p> When Trump was first elected in 2016 , the U.S. 's popularity worldwide similarly dropped , but the country recovered to previous levels during the Joe Biden presidency , according to data from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ non-partisan . <p> In Europe , an outlier when it comes to Trump is Hungary , the only country where the majority of respondents are confident in the American president . <p> The majority of responders across the countries surveyed agreed that Trump is " dangerous " and " arrogant , " and few think he is honest . Most , however , also see him as a " strong leader . " <p> Along with Trump , the survey also measured global confidence in French President Emmanuel Macron , Russia 's Vladimir Putin , and China 's Xi Jinping . Of the four , Macron has a solid lead , with a median of 46 percent expressing confidence in the French leader , compared to 34 percent for Trump , 25 percent for Xi and only 16 percent for Putin . At home , however , Macron is less popular -- only 35 percent of French respondents expressed confidence in him .
