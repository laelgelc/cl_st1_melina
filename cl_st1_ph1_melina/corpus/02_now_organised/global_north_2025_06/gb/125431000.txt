The carbon footprint , it said , of the first 15 months of Israel 's war on Gaza will be greater than the annual planet-warming emissions of a hundred individual countries . <p> I could n't help doing the maths in my head and throwing those numbers into the soup of figures I now carry around regarding Scotland 's greenhouse gas emissions . The latest report had them at 40.6 MtCO2e released over the year of 2022 . It 's not less than the 31Mt estimated in the report , but it is three-quarters . <p> I toyed - or perhaps ' worried ' is more the word - with the figure some more . Why am I writing about climate when there are people out there involved in the mass destruction of lives , homes and natural environment ? <p> It 's why , I think , in a world where a man who declares " drill , baby , drill " is leader of one of the United States , and war continues in both Gaza and Ukraine , climate change , never really a top of the list priority for most , appears to be sliding down . <p> What Israel is doing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , incomprehensible and devastating at both a global and local level . There are other conflict emissions -- not least those ramped up by the war in Ukraine . <p> A report published in February this year found that emissions in Ukraine had reached a total of 230 MtCO2e since Russia 's invasion in 2022 . This was , it said , " the equivalent of the annual emissions of Austria , Hungary , Czech Republic and Slovakia combined " . <p> Shockingly , this was dramatically up from the 150 MtCO2e estimated by an assessment made at the war 's 18-month mark . The first is that climate change suddenly feels irrelevant . If we humans , particularly those in power , are capable of committing such terrible destruction even on our own species , why are we bothering to make the effort of climate mitigation , a planet-saving effort that is chiefly about saving ourselves ? <p> The other is that , in terms of emotion , the story of the impact of climate change does n't hit me with that same hammer blow that I feel with each news story from Gaza .
