<p> The Middle East is undergoing a realignment of power . With Israel 's attack on Iranian nuclear sites and the assassination of at least two of Iran 's senior security officials , Benjamin Netanyahu is showing his willingness to go it alone and ignore pressure from the Trump administration . <p> Though Donald Trump sought diplomatic solutions to the growing tensions between Israel and Iran , it appears that the US president , despite his previously strong relationship with the Israeli leader , was unable to restrain Netanyahu . <p> The timing of the strikes -- which Iran is now returning -- is important . The Trump administration probably knew that they could not prevent Israel from striking Iran , but they did think they could pressure Israel to hold off launching an attack until after the US had solidified a new nuclear deal with Iran , talks for which were scheduled for June 15 . <p> Trump , following months of groundwork laid by the Joe Biden administration , managed to secure a ceasefire deal with Israel in January .
