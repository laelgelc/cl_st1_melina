The brouhaha over Donald Trump 's decision to decamp early from the G7 meeting , denying Anthony Albanese his first face-to-face meeting with the US president , was redolent of an age that has been and gone . Commentators , while acknowledging that the prime minister was n't to blame , concluded it was an unfortunate look . <p> Meanwhile , the Australian public looked on , unperturbed . A large majority of Australians see Trump for what he is and know that a meeting with him is the ultimate crapshoot . Perhaps more than some of the inhabitants of Canberra 's storied institutions , most ordinary Australians understand how much Trump 's election win has changed their reality . <p> That was one of the reasons voters produced an historical anomaly at last month 's election , handing a first-term government an extra 17 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Trump dominated the news , before and during the campaign , Labor 's numbers went up . The Albanese government was , for many voters , a safe haven in a world being disturbed and disrupted by a re-elected Trump , now free of the guardrails that limited him in his previous term as president . <p> The one thing they do n't want is to see Albanese going to Trump with cap in hand or jumping to attention when Trump 's defence secretary decides to mandate the size of Australia 's defence spending . <p> For Albanese , Trump offers the prospect of being the political gift that will keep on giving . When the next Australian election comes around in 2028 , Trump will still be in the White House . Who knows how much mayhem he will have wreaked by then ? <p> Loading <p> His recent behaviour suggests the answer will be " a lot " . After all , Trump quickly lost interest in doing the hard work to bring about peace in Gaza and Ukraine . In both cases , he has recently said the combatants needed to continue to fight out their conflicts like unruly schoolchildren -- that is , keep on with the killing . That is statecraft Trump-style . <p> So Israel went ahead in Iran anyway . Once Trump saw how successful Israel 's initial actions were , he progressively sought to deal himself into them as though they were his idea . He has no doctrine , no real belief system , just ego and what former UK cabinet minister Michael Gove described as " a sort of feral intelligence " . <p> Advertisement <p> That 's why the sooner the institutional perception of not just Trump , but the United States , adjusts itself , the better off Australia will be . He won 31 states to Kamala Harris ' 19 . <p> This suggests Albanese needs to confront some big issues within the American relationship . How willing is he to spend some of his political capital to deal with them ? It 's four years since the AUKUS pact was announced jointly by Boris Johnson , Joe Biden and Scott Morrison -- none of whose careers came to a happy end -- as a way of getting the old Anglosphere gang back together to stave off the strategic rise of China . <p> What amounts to Labor 's original sin in opposition of endorsing AUKUS sight unseen is a mistake from which it will have to find a way to either extricate itself or initiate a renegotiation . AUKUS is crushingly expensive , with unreasonably long timelines , and is almost certainly undeliverable . And it did not countenance a reborn and rampant Trump . <p> Loading <p> There are signs that the government is at least trying to moderate its reliance on this ill-begotten agreement . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that is less dependent on the US . When Albanese talks about " an Australian way " of doing things and highlights his deeper engagement with Indonesia and Papua New Guinea , our two northern neighbours that stand between us and China , he 's signalling a slow movement out of the American orbit , or at least some hedging of bets . At the same time , the government has worked hard to normalise its relationship with China . <p> The Labor Party , before its embrace of AUKUS , had a long history of a more independent security stance , going back to the Fisher government establishing the Australian navy before World War I , and John Curtin bringing troops back from Europe to defend Australia after the fall of Singapore in World War II . Labor also controversially opposed our involvement in the wars in Vietnam and Iraq . <p> Whether Albanese has the will and intestinal fortitude to continue to create a new path remains to be seen . In other words , his main task will be to mostly play a dead bat and protect us from both of them . <p> Albanese is right to leave open the possibility of being able to catch up with Trump next week on the sidelines of the NATO summit in the Netherlands . There are so many uncertainties about an Australian leader getting time with America 's leader .
