<h> Bodies of two hostages recovered in Gaza <p> The bodies of two deceased hostages were recovered from southern Gaza in a military operation , the Israel Defence Forces ( IDF ) and Shin Bet security agency announced earrly this morning . <p> Yair Yaakov was killed during by Islamic Jihad militants during the Hamas-led terror attack on October 7 , 2023 . Yaakov , who was 59 years old at the time , was killed in Kibbutz Nir Oz and his body taken into Gaza . <p> His partner , Meirav Tal , and two of his children were taken hostage and subsequently released during a previous hostage agreement . <p> The body of Israeli hostage Yair Yaakov has been recovered in Gaza . ( Hostages and Missing Families Forum ) <p> The body of an additional hostage has also been recovered , according to the IDF and Shin Bet , but the second name has not yet been made public at the request of the family . <p> " Together with all the citizens of Israel , my wife and I extend our heartfelt condolences to the families @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Netanyahu in a statement . <p> " Alongside the pain and mourning , we feel a measure of relief knowing that the two will be laid to rest with dignity -- and that the unbearable suffering their loved ones have endured for 614 days may now be eased , if only slightly , " said the Hostages and Missing Families Forum in a statement . <p> " We repeat our demand to the decision-makers to reach a full and comprehensive agreement that will bring home all 53 remaining hostages -- even if that requires ending the fighting . " <p> The recovery of these two bodies comes less than a week after the Israeli military recovered the bodies of Judy Weinstein-Haggai , 70 , and Gadi Haggai , 72 , who were also killed during the attack on Nir Oz . <p> Last week , the Israeli military also recovered the body of Thai hostage Nattapong Pinta .
