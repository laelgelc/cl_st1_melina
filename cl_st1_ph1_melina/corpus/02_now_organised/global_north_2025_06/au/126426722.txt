<p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said his country 's recent war with Iran had created " opportunities " for freeing hostages held by Hamas militants in Gaza , where witnesses and rescuers reported more than 30 people killed Sunday . <p> His comments lifted hope for a new ceasefire in the devastating conflict in the Palestinian territory , after US President Donald Trump said he hoped a truce could be sealed within days . <p> Israel is bombarding Gaza in a bid to destroy the militant group Hamas after its deadly attack on Israel in October 2023 . <p> Netanyahu said that after his country 's recent " victory " over Iran in their 12-day war , " many opportunities have opened up ... first of all , to rescue the hostages . " <p> " Of course , we will also have to solve the Gaza issue , to defeat Hamas , but I estimate that we will achieve both goals , " he said .
