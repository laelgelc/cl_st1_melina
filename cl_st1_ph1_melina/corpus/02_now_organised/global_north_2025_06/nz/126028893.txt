<p> Ninety-five New Zealand lawyers -- including nine king 's counsel -- have signed a letter demanding Prime Minister Christopher Luxon , Foreign Minister Winston Peters and two other ministers urge the government to take a stronger stand against Israel 's " catastrophic " actions in Gaza . <p> The letter has been sent amid rising tensions in the region , following Israel 's surprise attacks on Iran last Friday , and Iran 's retaliatory attacks . <p> A statement by the Justice For Palestine advocacy group said the letter 's signatories represented all levels of seniority in the legal community , including senior barristers , law firm partners , legal academics , and in-house lawyers . <p> The letter cited the 26 July 2024 joint statement by the prime ministers of Canada , Australia and New Zealand which acknowledged : " The situation in Gaza is catastrophic . The human suffering is unacceptable . It can not continue . " <p> " But it has continued , " said the letter .
