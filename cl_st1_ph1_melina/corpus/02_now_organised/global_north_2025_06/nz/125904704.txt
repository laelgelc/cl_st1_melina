<h> Government Must Ensure Safety Of Its Citizens In The Global March To Gaza -- Palestine Forum of New Zealand <p> The Palestine Forum of New Zealand reminds the government of its duty to protect all New Zealand citizens , wherever they may be . <p> Among those currently participating in the Global March to Gaza are New Zealand citizens who travelled from across the country , alongside others who joined the mission from overseas . The government of NewZealand bears full responsibility for their safety and well-being . <p> In light of escalating risks in international waters and Israeli aggression against humanitarian convoys , we call on the New Zealand Government to immediately take all necessary diplomatic and consular actions to safeguard its citizens and demand their protection in accordance with international law .
