<p> ' Who knows what might happen next with an erratic President in Washington and a fanatical theocratic regime in Iran , both convinced God is on their side ? ' Photo : Getty Images <p> Comment : Prime Minister Christopher Luxon put a small but significant stake in the ground last week . Speaking before the sudden United States bombing raids on alleged nuclear sites in Iran , Luxon said " the right response here is negotiation and diplomacy . It can not be more military action . " <p> His assessment was correct , both from a New Zealand and a wider international perspective . Unilateral military incursions by any state into the territory of another can never be justified . Against that principle , the United States ' attacks on Iran are as unjustified as Russia 's invasion of Ukraine , or Israel 's assaults on Gaza , and deserve equal condemnation .
