There 's still a sense that larger truths ca n't be drawn from the small things in life : the psychology of mundane encounters , the day-to-day behaviour of the human animal . <p> " Feminine " writing may have been historically viewed as minor , but women are particularly well-equipped to know that the personal is political . Any mother with a personality sufficiently robust to withstand the introspection will realise the degree to which life is a cycle . <p> Everything we 've experienced , we will be hardwired to act out .
