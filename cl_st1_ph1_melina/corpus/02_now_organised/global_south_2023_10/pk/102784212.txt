<h> " Kill India " rallies of SFJ to show solidarity with Gaza 's tragic loss <p> ISLAMABAD : The Sikhs international representative organization , Sikh for Justice ( SFJ ) is organising " Kill India " rallies from Tomorrow ( Saturday October 21 ) from Canada to Palestine in solidarity with the lives lost at the Gaza hospital , denouncing India 's leadership under Prime Minister Modi for supporting and celebrating such tragic events . <p> According to Kashmir Media Service , General Counsel for SFJ , Gurpatwant Singh Pannun , in a video statement said the world community mourns the brutal bombings , but India under Modi celebrates , delivering a slap and a black spot on global democracy . <p> He expressed deep concern over the hospital bombings in Gaza . He highlighted that the Indian agents have been implicated in acts of terrorism within G7 nations , including the brutal murder of Sikh leader , Hardeep Singh Nijjar , in Canada . <p> Gurpatwant Singh Pannun has urged the Pro-Khalistan Sikhs and Palestinians to participate in ' Kill India ' rallies to be organized from Vancouver to Ramallah . He demanded the closure of Indian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and its support for the Gaza hospital bombings . <p> The SFJ General Counsel emphasized the shared solidarity between Pro-Khalistan Sikhs and the Palestinian people , who are facing genocide . This solidarity is based on the Sikh community 's own history of enduring genocide under successive Indian regimes since 1984 , he added .
