<h> Indian brand refuses to supply uniforms to Israeli police citing atrocities against Palestinians <p> Despite the fact that India has extended its full support to Israel in its ongoing war on Gaza , an Indian clothing brand has refused to bow to the pressure and taken a stand for the Palestinian victims . <p> Maryan Apparel Private Limited , based in the Kannur district of the southern state of Kerala , is an Indian apparel manufacturer which -- since 2015 -- supplied tens of thousands of uniforms every year to the Israeli police . <p> The brand decided not to take any more orders after Israel 's deadly onslaught on civilians in Gaza , and the nerve-wrecking Al-Ahli Al-Arabi Hospital incident in central Gaza was bombed , killing hundreds of people , mostly women , children and the elderly . <p> The brand this week decided to sever ties with the its loyal customer , saying " killing the innocent common people is the reason " . <p> " Killing the innocent common people is the reason , " Thomas Olickal , the company 's director , told Arab News . <p> " The attack on the hospital @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " Olickal added . " I am not able to see the disturbing pictures of children and ladies crying in pain and with no medicine and food , " he pointed out . <p> " All employees wholeheartedly supported me . We have to take a stand when common people are killed ... Financial difficulties are nothing compared with the suffering of innocent people , " Olickal stated .
