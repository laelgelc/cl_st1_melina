<p> Caretaker Prime Minister Anwaar-ul-Haq Kakar called on Monday for an immediate ceasefire and lifting of the blockade in Gaza , saying that Israel 's " deliberate , indiscriminate and disproportionate targeting of civilians in Gaza is against all norms of civility " . <p> In a statement on social media platform X ( formerly Twitter ) , Kakar said Pakistan is deeply concerned on the ongoing violence and loss of life in Gaza . <p> " We stand in solidarity with the oppressed people of Palestine and call for an immediate ceasefire and lifting of the blockade in Gaza . " <p> He said that the violence needed to be viewed in the context of years of forced and illegal occupation of Palestinian territory and repressive policies against its people . <p> " The UN and international community must immediately act to open safe and unrestricted humanitarian corridors for transportation of urgently needed relief supplies to the besieged Gaza , " the caretaker PM said . <p> He further said that Pakistan was closely coordinating with the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation ( OIC ) and its member states on the fast deteriorating situation in Gaza . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Committee on 18 October , and call for urgent action to alleviate the suffering of people of Gaza , " the interim premier said . <p> Israel has carried out relentless bombing , killing at least 2,670 people , the majority ordinary Palestinians , in the blockaded and impoverished Gaza Strip . <p> Authorities in Gaza said nearly 10,000 were wounded . Another 1,000 people were missing and believed to be under rubble . <p> Israel also imposed a " total siege " to stop food and fuel from reaching the enclave of 2.3 million people , many poor and dependent on aid . However , under US pressure , Israel on Sunday resumed the supply of water to the southern Gaza Strip after earlier vowing to keep out all supply of food , water and energy to the densely populated territory . <p> Meanwhile , hundreds of tonnes of aid from NGOs and several countries were waiting on trucks in the nearby Egyptian town of Al-Arish on Monday for permission to enter Gaza , two sources there and an eyewitness told Reuters .
