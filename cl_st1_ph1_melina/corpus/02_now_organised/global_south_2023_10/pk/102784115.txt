<h> Generally , African countries have had long-standing sympathies with the Palestinians <p> For the past few days , the Palestine issue has been covered intensively in the world media . The brutal use of air force on the vulnerable residents of Gaza has shocked and saddened everyone . <p> While the US used its veto power against the resolution presented by Russia in the UN Security Council for a ceasefire in Gaza , Israel has given an ultimatum to the Palestinians to vacate in order to attack with full force . Currently , US President Joe Biden is also making an official visit to Israel to show solidarity with the Israelis . <p> The rapidly worsening situation in the Middle East proves my prediction that I have been expressing for the last many years that the international community has finally divided into two blocs , due to which the world has reached the brink of a third world war . <p> Generally , African countries have had long-standing sympathies with the Palestinians . However , after the recent conflict , there are reports that African countries are also divided . Reportedly , Russia , China and Iran are forming a new alliance with like-minded countries to counter the US and the West over the Gaza issue .
