<h> Shae Gill , Natasha Noorani , and more join forces in ' Sounds of Solidarity " with Gaza <p> Pakistani artists are uniting for " Sounds of Solidarity with Gaza , " a fundraising initiative dedicated to supporting the people of Palestine . This event is scheduled for October 27th at 6 p.m. and will take place at the COLABS Campus in Gulberg , Lahore . <p> The roster of participating artists includes Shae Gill , Natasha Noorani , Maanu , Towers , Izzchughtai , Asteria , and Salor . Tickets are available for purchase on the online ticketing platform Ticketwala , with single passes priced at Rs6,000 and couple passes at Rs10,000 . <p> All proceeds generated from this event will be directed towards the Anera organization , an American non-governmental organization committed to delivering humanitarian and developmental assistance to regions including the West Bank , the Gaza Strip , Lebanon , and Jordan . <p> Recently , Israeli troops and tanks have carried out a brief yet relatively large overnight ground incursion into Gaza ahead of a widely expected full-scale ground invasion after nearly three weeks of devastating air raids on the besieged Palestinian enclave .
