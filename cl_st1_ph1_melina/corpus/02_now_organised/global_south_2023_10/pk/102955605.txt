<p> Palestinian Foreign Minister Riyad al-Maliki on Wednesday briefed the heads of the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) in the Hague on the Israeli " crimes and destruction " in Gaza . <p> According to the official Wafa news agency , al-Maliki met with ICC President Judge Piotr Hofmanski and ICC Registrar Osvaldo Zavala during which he informed them on " the extent of crime and destruction caused by Israel , the occupying power , without any accountability . " <p> The top Palestinian diplomat " stressed the need for ICC to complete its criminal investigation and bring the Israeli perpetrators of war crimes to justice . " <p> On Wednesday , the UN Security Council failed to draft a resolution on the ongoing Israel-Palestine conflict as two draft resolutions proposed by the US and Russia failed to pass . <p> The conflict in Gaza began when the Palestinian group Hamas initiated Operation Al-Aqsa Flood -- a multi-pronged surprise attack on Oct. 7 that included a barrage of rocket launches and infiltrations into Israel by land , sea , and air .
