<h> Israel-Gaza inflames tensions on US college campuses <p> NEW YORK-A debate over the war between Israel and Hamas is raging on US college campuses . In a social media age that demands people pick a side on divisive issues , is there still space for young Americans to talk to each other ? Hundreds of students gathered on the main quad at Columbia University in New York City last week to engage in duelling protests in support of Israel and the Palestinian people - and to condemn the loss of life.Pro-Israel protesters , most of whom were Jewish , draped themselves in white and blue Israeli flags . For much of the event , they remained silent , letting signs with photos of the victims of Hamas ' deadly attack speak for them . Across the square , students who supported the Palestinians waved signs declaring " Free Palestine " and " End the Occupation " . A female student from Gaza spoke of her mother , trapped by an Israeli blockade .
