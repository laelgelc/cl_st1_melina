This is not the case . <h> The right to resist <p> The Palestinians have the right to resist an Israeli occupation , especially one that forcibly denies them their right to self-determination . This right crystallised during the era of decolonisation and is confirmed in a number of UN General Assembly resolutions . <p> The UN 's General Assembly Resolution 37/43 entitles an occupied people to resist occupying forces by all available means , " including armed struggle " .
