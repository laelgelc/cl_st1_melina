press release issued here . <p> During the IPU conference , Senator Mushahid Hussain Syed was invited to address the General Assembly thrice , address the Islamic and Asia-Pacific regional group meetings , and invited for an address to the Socialist International group . <p> In his first point , Syed said that Palestinian resistance to illegal Israeli occupation was justified under
