The Palestinian people have resisted the brutal occupation by Israel for over 70 years , only to be even more brutally crushed . Yesterday , the famed Palestinian resistance may just have sprung one of the biggest surprises on the Israeli state , with Hamas launching a sudden multi-front attack into Israel early morning Saturday , leading to the deaths of nearly 100 Israelis with another 800 injured . That Israel was taken by surprise would be an understatement , given the kind of surveillance the occupying force has used over the years in the ghettos that Palestinians are shoved into .
