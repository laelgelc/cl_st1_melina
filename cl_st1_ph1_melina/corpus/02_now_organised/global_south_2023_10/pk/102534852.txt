<p> Palestinian-American model Gigi Hadid has called the violence in Palestine an " unjustifiable tragedy " and clarified that while she supports the Palestinian cause , she does not support violence . " While I have hopes and dreams for Palestinians , none of them include the harm of a Jewish person , " she wrote in a lengthy statement on Instagram . <p> Hadid , whose father is a Palestinian , said her thoughts were with everyone affected by the tragedy , especially children . " The terrorising of innocent people is not in alignment with and does not do any good for the ' Free Palestine ' movement . The idea that it does has fuelled a painful , decades-long cycle of back and forth retaliation ( which no innocent civilian , Palestinian or Israeli , deserves to be a casualty of ) , and helps perpetuate the false idea that being Pro-Palestine equals being antisemitic , " she wrote . <p> She expressed her condolences to anyone " hurting " .
