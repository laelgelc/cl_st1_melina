" He appealed to " all parties and those with influence over them " to immediately allow fuel supplies into Gaza and to ensure that it is strictly used to prevent humanitarian operations from collapsing . <p> Although he welcomed the entry of the first humanitarian convoy into Gaza on Saturday , Mr. Lazzarini said it was " far from enough " , stressing the need for sustained aid .
