<h> The death of humanity <p> By News Desk <p> October 29 , 2023 <p> People all over the world are silently watching the live coverage of a genocide . Gaza is bleeding ; and Israel is bent upon destroying every nook and corner of the besieged city . We should feel ashamed as we are more focused on watching cricket matches instead of demanding our government to repeat the calls for a ceasefire . Muslim-majority countries should also feel ashamed . Gaza will always be a reason for humanity to feel a twinge of guilt and shame . In the West , any support for Gaza is labelled as ' anti-Semitic ' , but there is a silver line at the end of the tunnel . On Friday ( Oct 27 ) , Jews in the US strongly protested against Israel 's aggression and called for an immediate ceasefire . This is a good sign .
