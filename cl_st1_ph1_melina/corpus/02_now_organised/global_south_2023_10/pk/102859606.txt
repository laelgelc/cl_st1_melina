And two , the US veto of a UN Security Council resolution seeking a " humanitarian pause " in the war , which followed its rejection of another resolution calling for a ceasefire . <p> Biden 's visit took place in the immediate aftermath of the deadly Israeli strike on Al Ahli hospital in Gaza that claimed the lives of almost 500 people . The massacre was condemned across the world and deemed a war crime by international legal experts . A group of independent UN experts said the strikes amounted to collective punishment prohibited by international humanitarian law .
