<h> Rupee declines by Rs 1.77 against dollar <p> During past week , US Dollar saw an overall increase of Rs1.77 . On Saturday , the current rate for US Dollar stands at Rs280.57 , as the interbank exchange rate saw a notable Rs1.77 rise during the past week . After a continuous decline in the value of the US dollar against the Pakistani currency for five weeks , there has been a trend of increasing over the past two weeks . According to the State Bank of Pakistan , on Friday , the dollar closed at 280.57 rupees in the interbank market . During the past week , the dollar saw an overall increase of Rs1.77 . The value of the US dollar had been on a downward trend against the Pakistani rupee for 28 consecutive business days , dipping below 277 rupees at one point . However , the decline in the value of the dollar stopped during the last week . Currency market analysts suggest that the dollar 's value had significantly dropped , and a recovery was expected . However , pressure on income payments in the interbank market emerged last Wednesday .
