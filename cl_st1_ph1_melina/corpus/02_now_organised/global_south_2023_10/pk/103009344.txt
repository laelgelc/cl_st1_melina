<h> Imran Khan urges muslim world to join hands and bring an end to Gaza genocide <p> ISLAMABAD : Pakistan Tehreek-i-Insaf ( PTI ) Chairman Imran Khan on Saturday urged that the Muslim world must not just raise their voice against Israel war crimes but should move to ensure an end to the Gaza Genocide . <p> In a message transmitted from Adiala Jail , PTI Chairman said : " There is a genocide of Palestinians going on and it is utterly condemnable to see how the powerful are allowing it to happen with impunity . " <p> Imran Khan stated : " Israel is being allowed to break all international laws as it commits war crimes and crimes against humanity in its massacre of the Palestinian people . " <p> PTI Chairman underlined the need that the Muslim world must not just raise their voice against this but should move to ensure an end to the Gaza genocide . <p> He warned : " Our people will not forgive us if we do not stand on the side of justice and humanity . "
