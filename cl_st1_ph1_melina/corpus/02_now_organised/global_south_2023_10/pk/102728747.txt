<p> Israeli forces arrested on Monday night Palestinian singer Dalal Abu Amneh after she posted in support of Gaza , claiming she engaged in " hate speech " . <p> The singer is an Israeli national and was arrested from her home in Nazareth , according to Middle East Eye . <p> The publication quoted her lawyers saying the Israeli police had interrogated her , but " no official decision has been taken to extend her detention " . <p> The singer , who was born in Nazareth , is also a neuroscientist and is known for her songs about Palestine . <p> According to the Jerusalem Post , there were several " social media posts and public complaints regarding the influencer 's contentious content " , specifically targeting the Israel and voicing support for Palestine . <p> According to officials , Amneh frequently published posts on social media and engaged in activities perceived as ' hostile ' to Israeli interests . <p> She was due to be presented in the Nazareth Magistrate 's Court on Tuesday where the authorities sought an extension of her detention .
