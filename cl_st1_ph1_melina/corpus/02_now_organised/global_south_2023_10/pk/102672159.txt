<h> Gaza aid stuck as Egypt says Israel not cooperating <p> Trucks carrying humanitarian aid , as they wait for the re-opening of the Rafah border crossing to enter Gaza , in the city of Al-Arish , Sinai peninsula , Egypt , October 16 , 2023 . PHOTO : REUTERS <p> ISMAILIA : <p> Egypt said on Monday that Israel was not cooperating with delivery of aid into Gaza and evacuations of foreign passport holders via the only entry it does not wholly control , leaving hundreds of tonnes of supplies stuck . <p> Cairo says the Rafah crossing , a potentially vital opening for desperately-needed supplies into the besieged Palestinian enclave , is not officially closed but is inoperable due to Israeli air strikes on the Gaza side . <p> " There is an urgent need to alleviate the suffering of Palestinian civilians in Gaza , " Egypt 's Foreign Minister Sameh Shoukry told reporters , adding that talks with Israel had not been fruitful . <p> " Until now the Israeli government has not taken a position on opening the Rafah crossing from the Gaza side to allow the entrance of assistance and exit of citizens of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have been under siege since Israel launched an intense bombardment and blockade in retaliation for an assault by the Hamas Fighters . <p> Two Egyptian security sources had told Reuters a ceasefire in southern Gaza to last several hours had been agreed for Monday morning to facilitate aid and evacuations at Rafah . <p> " There is currently no truce and humanitarian aid in Gaza in exchange for getting foreigners out , " a statement from Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's office said . <p> Hamas official Izzat El-Reshiq told Reuters the same . <p> On the ground at Rafah , one source said that there had been no bombardments on Monday and that the Egyptian side of the crossing was ready . Shoukry said Egypt aimed to allow normal flow through the crossing , including for Palestinians seeking medical treatment or normal travel . <p> Hundreds of tonnes of aid from NGOs and several countries were waiting on trucks in the nearby Egyptian town of Al-Arish for conditions to allow entry to Gaza , according to two sources there and a witness .
