<p> ISTANBUL : Eleven journalists have been killed in the Gaza Strip , with more than 20 injured and two missing since the start of the recent Israeli airstrikes , a Palestinian journalists group said in a new report . <p> According to the report released Sunday by the Freedom Committee , affiliated with the Palestinian Journalists Syndicate , 11 Palestinian journalists have been documented as killed by Israeli airstrikes since Oct. 7 . <p> A statement by the group provided " documentation of the targeting of journalists " from the start of the war on the Gaza Strip until the evening of Oct. 15 . It also decried " the violent escalation in the targeting of Palestinian journalists . " <p> Over 20 injuries were also observed , the most serious of which occurred in the Gaza Strip . Following the announcement of the complete or partial shelling of approximately 20 homes belonging to journalists , the Israeli army escalated by targeting the residences of several journalists directly , said the report . This led to injuries and the tragic loss of family members . <p> In addition , approximately 50 headquarters and media institutions were destroyed as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ma'an News Agency , Al-Quds newspaper , Baladna Radio , Zaman Radio , Al-Quran Radio , the Al-Jazeera Network office , Palestine TV , and the AFP offices , according to the report . <p> In the occupied West Bank , the report also documented several Palestinian journalists being injured and the assault , detention , blocking of coverage , firing on journalists , and confiscation and destruction of journalists ' equipment by Israel forces . <p> The report also decried interference with media broadcasts . The report said : " The Al-Aqsa channel ceased transmission via the Eutelsat satellite in response to the occupation 's pressure . " <p> The report also noted " direct threats to many journalists and campaigns of incitement by Israeli pages on social media platforms " and " many Israeli posts calling for the elimination of journalists and describing them as saboteurs and terrorists . "
