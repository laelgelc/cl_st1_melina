<h> Arab League hails UN resolution for Gaza humanitarian truce <p> The Arab League welcomed a UN General Assembly resolution early Saturday that demands a " humanitarian truce " in the Gaza Strip . <p> " The adoption of the resolution by a large majority reflects a clear trend in world public opinion that rejects the continuation of the aggression against the Gaza Strip due to its catastrophic humanitarian impact and the clear targeting of civilians , " Arab League Secretary-General Ahmed Aboul Gheit said in a statement . <p> " The resolution includes an emphasis on protecting civilians , opening humanitarian corridors , and the necessity of adhering to international humanitarian law , " he added . <p> The UN General Assembly approved Friday the resolution that called for an immediate " durable and sustained humanitarian truce " in Gaza . <p> The resolution , which was presented by nearly 50 countries , including T ? rkiye , Palestine , Egypt , Jordan , Saudi Arabia , and the United Arab Emirates ( UAE ) , was approved by a vote of 120-14 , with 45 nations abstaining . <p> Adopted at the 10th Emergency Special Session meeting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ resolution expresses " grave concern " over the " latest escalation of violence " since Hamas ' Oct. 7 attack on Israel . <p> The resolution condemned " all acts of violence against Palestinian and Israeli civilians , including all acts of terror and indiscriminate attacks , as well as all acts of provocation , incitement and destruction . " <p> It also demanded that " all parties immediately and fully comply with their obligations under international law .
