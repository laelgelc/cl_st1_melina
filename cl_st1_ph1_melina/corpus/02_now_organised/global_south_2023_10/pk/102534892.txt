<h> Israeli airstrikes displace over 260,000 Palestinians in Gaza : UN <p> Israeli airstrikes have displaced more than 260,000 Palestinians in the Gaza Strip , the UN humanitarian office said . <p> " Over 263,934 people in Gaza are believed to have fled their homes , " the UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs ( OCHA ) said in an update on Tuesday . <p> Fears are mounting that the number of displaced Palestinians in Gaza will increase amid continuing Israeli attacks and Hamas rocket fire . <p> The situation escalated in the Gaza Strip following an attack by Palestinian group Hamas on Israeli towns near the seaside territory . Israel retaliated with massive airstrikes in Gaza and placed the enclave under total blockade . <p> Some 2,100 people were killed in the violence , including at least 900 Palestinians and 1,200 Israelis. ? <p> Israel has also cut water and electricity supply to Gaza , inevitably worsening the already dire humanitarian situation . <p> Home to nearly 2.2 million people , the Gaza Strip has already been reeling under a crippling Israeli siege since 2007.
