<h> Atif Aslam donates Rs15m for war-hit Gaza <p> In a heartwarming gesture of generosity , celebrated and beloved Pakistani singer Atif Aslam has stepped forward to make a substantial contribution of PKR15 million towards essential medical and food aid for Gaza , Palestine . The singer 's benevolent donation comes at a time when the region is grappling with trying circumstances , and his act of kindness has been acknowledged with deep gratitude by the organisation to which he donated . <p> Taking to Instagram , the Alkhidmat Foundation Pakistan shared a post featuring the talented star , expressing their profound appreciation for his altruistic gesture . The post read , " Deep gratitude to the esteemed Atif Aslam for his generous contribution of 15 million PKR towards essential medical and food aid for Gaza , Palestine during these trying times . We humbly request your support for the Alkhidmat Gaza Fund . " <p> Atif 's contribution to the Gaza relief fund is not only a testament to his compassion but also a shining example of how influential figures can make a positive impact on the lives of those in need .
