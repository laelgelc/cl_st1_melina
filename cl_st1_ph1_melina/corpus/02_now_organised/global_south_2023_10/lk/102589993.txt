<h> Sri Lanka releases USD 30,000 for Lankans ' welfare in Israel <p> Sri Lanka has allocated a total of USD 30,000 -- USD 20,000 from the government and USD 10,000 from the Sri Lanka Foreign Employment Bureau - to be used for the welfare of Sri Lankans in Israel in case of any emergencies , an official said . <p> Sri Lanka 's ambassador in Israel , Nimal Bandara told the Daily Mirror that the funds have been repatriated to the embassy to be used as and when required . <p> He said two Sri Lankans are still missing and one injured after the tension flared up . The Israeli Foreign Ministry today released the list of foreign nationals affected by the war situation . <p> There are around 8,000 Sri Lankans working in Israel and 90 percent of them are caregivers in elders ' homes . Despite tension in the areas bordering the Gaza strip and Lebanon , the ambassador said normalcy remains in other parts of the country . ( Kelum Bandara ) <p> Good move .
