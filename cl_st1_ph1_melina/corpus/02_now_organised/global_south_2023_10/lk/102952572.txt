<h> Refrain from commenting on SL 's internal affairs <p> The Parliament 's Sectoral Oversight Committee on National Security has recommended informing US Ambassador Julie Chung , urging her to refrain from making statements regarding the internal affairs of Sri Lanka in the future . <p> This recommendation comes in response to the ambassador 's remarks following the incidents that occurred on May 9 , 2022 . <p> Two weeks ago , the Committee convened at the Parliament complex , with Committee members , the Defense Secretary , three forces commanders , the Police Chief , and other government officials in attendance , to discuss matters pertaining to national security . <p> In a letter addressed to the State Defense Minister last week , the Chairman of the Sectoral Oversight Committee , Retired Rear Admiral Sarath Weerasekera , who is also a Member of Parliament , expressed concern over the American ambassador 's statement concerning the aforementioned incident . <p> He emphasized that the ambassador had advocated for a transparent investigation into the shooting of unarmed protesters and had stressed the importance of safeguarding the people 's right to peaceful protest . <p> Rear Admiral Weerasekera questioned the ambassador @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ blocking highways and railway lines , destroying petrol stations , slashing tires of oil bowsers , and throwing stones at the police constitute a protest or peaceful acts .
