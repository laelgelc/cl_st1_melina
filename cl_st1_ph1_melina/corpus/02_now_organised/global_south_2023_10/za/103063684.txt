It was anchored on the Nakba , also known as the Palestinian catastrophe . <p> The Nakba describes the events of the 1948 ( and ongoing ) occupation that saw the mass displacement and dispossession of Palestinians . By 1949 , almost a million Palestinians were violently uprooted from their homes .
