<h> Beware the one-eyed Middle East critic <p> Palestinians mourn while sitting around a mock coffin as they hold a symbolic funeral of prisoner Nasser Abu Hamid during a protest in front of the International Committee of the Red Cross office in Gaza City . Nasser Abu Hamid died of lung cancer in Israel . Palestinians claim that his death was as a result of medical negligence thus their protest . Abu Hamid was a former leader of the Al Aqsa Martyrs ' Brigade , the armed wing of Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas 's Fatah , party . He had been serving seven life sentences after being convicted in 2002 for involvement in the deaths of seven Israelis during the second Palestinian intifada , or uprising , against Israel 's occupation in the early 2000s .
