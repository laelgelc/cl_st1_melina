<p> Chennai : A total of 21 Tamilians from Israel are part of the first batch of Indian nationals who arrived in the country under Operation Ajay launched by the Indian government . These returnees are among the 212 nationals who arrived from Israel today . Seven of them will land at Coimbatore Airport , while the remaining 14 are expected to reach Chennai Airport shortly after flying in from Delhi . Health Minister Ma Subramanian will personally welcome the group arriving in Chennai , signifying the importance of their safe return . <p> Union Minister Rajeev Chandrasekhar extended a warm reception to around 200 Indian nationals who arrived from Israel on Friday , as part of the " Operation Ajay " initiative . The move was in response to the recent attacks on Israeli towns by Hamas militants . <p> The minister greeted the returnees with folded hands and expressions of relief , saying , " Welcome home .
