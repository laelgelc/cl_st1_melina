Diplomatic and multilateral initiatives are being urged to achieve long-term peace in the region . <p> The leaders expressed deep concern about the ongoing crisis in Gaza and the suffering of the Palestinian people , and they strongly condemn Israel 's indiscriminate bombing , describing it as an attempt at genocide .
