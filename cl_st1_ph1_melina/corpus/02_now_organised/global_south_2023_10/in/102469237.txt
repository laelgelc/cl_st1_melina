<p> Jerusalem : After three days of heavy fighting and thousands of deaths , the militant group Hamas is open to a truce with Israel . Speaking to Al Jazeera , a senior Hamas official has stated that the group would be open to calling a truce with Israel and having discussions . <p> Moussa Abu Marzouk , a senior Hamas official told Al Jazeera and Reuters in a phone interview that the group is open to " something of that sort " and " all political dialogues " when asked whether the group is willing to discuss a possible ceasefire . <p> Hours before this possibility of a truce , Hamas threatened to execute all hostages and broadcast the massacre to the world if Israel did not stop its attacks on Gaza . Hamas ' threat came after Israel launched its largest ever air strike on Gaza . <p> This is a developing story . More information will be added as and when available .
