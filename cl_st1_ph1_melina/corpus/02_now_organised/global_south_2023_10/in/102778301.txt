<p> Amid the ongoing Israel-Hamas war , a doctor working in Gaza has revealed a horrifying incident he went through after a huge blast hit the Al-Ahli Arab hospital and netizens have been left disturbed . The ceiling of the operation room fell down while the procedure was going on . <p> While the missile struck the hospital , there were people with primary amputations . The doctor said most of them in the hospital were children . In a video shared by the BBC on Instagram , the British-Palestinian doctor Ghassan Abu-Sittah is heard saying , " I was in the operating room when the missile struck and the full ceiling of the operating cell fell on us . There were amputated parts . There were people with primary amputations . " <p> He added , " 40% of all the cases that I 've seen have been children . A lot of them have been like this , they have lost one or two parents . And unfortunately , these kids have a long , long recovery road in front of them .
