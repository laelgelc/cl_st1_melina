What General Moshe is saying is that the Palestinians will continue violently and legitimately to regain their homeland , while the Israelis will continue to fight for the same land that they believe is in their destiny . There are no good moral options on the table . According to Moshe Dayan , every option leads to a standstill between two sets of people who are determined to harm each other .
