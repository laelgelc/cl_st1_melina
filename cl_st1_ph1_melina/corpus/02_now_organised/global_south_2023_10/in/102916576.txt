<p> United Nations : United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres on Wednesday rejected accusations by Israel that , in a statement to the Security Council , he had justified attacks by the Palestinian militant group Hamas on Israel . <p> " I am shocked by the misrepresentations by some of my statement ... as if I was justifying acts of terror by Hamas . This is false . It was the opposite , " he told reporters , without specifically naming Israel . <p> Guterres on Tuesday pleaded for civilians to be protected in the war between Israel and Hamas , voicing concern about " clear violations of international humanitarian law " in the Gaza Strip . <p> He told the 15-member U.N . Security Council that it was vital to be clear that war has rules , starting with the fundamental principle of respecting and protecting civilians . <p> " It is important to also recognize the attacks by Hamas did not happen in a vacuum . The Palestinian people have been subjected to 56 years of suffocating occupation , " Guterres said . <p> " But the grievances of the Palestinian people can not justify the appalling attacks @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the collective punishment of the Palestinian people , " he said . <p> Israel 's U.N . Ambassador Gilad Erdan described Guterres ' speech as " shocking " and called on Guterres to resign immediately , while Israel 's visiting Foreign Minister Eli Cohen said he would not meet with Guterres on Tuesday as planned . <p> Guterres instead met with family representatives of the hostages held in Gaza . <p> Guterres noted on Wednesday that in his statement to the Security Council he " condemned unequivocally " the Oct. 7 Hamas attacks that killed 1,400 people , saying : " Nothing can justify the deliberate killing , injuring and kidnapping of civilians -- or the launching of rockets against civilian targets . " <p> Referring to his statement on Wednesday , he said : " I believe it is necessary to set the record straight -- especially out of respect for the victims and their families . "
