Add to this transformative mix a modern nation-state with a strong military , and you end up with a formidable ethno-state . <p> In the eyes of the Hindutva intelligentsia , the state of Israel is a striking manifestation of this complex arc that begins at cultural assertion and culminates in the creation of an ethnic ' homeland ' protected by military force and exclusivist laws .
