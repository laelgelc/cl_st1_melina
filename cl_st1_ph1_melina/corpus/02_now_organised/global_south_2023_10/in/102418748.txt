<p> At least 150 additional rockets were fired from Gaza Strip in the direction of Tel Aviv on Saturday evening as death toll from the Hamas attack rose to over 150 , Times of Israel reported . Sirens and loud explosions were heard across Tel Aviv , CNN reported . <p> At least nine people were injured in the cities of Rishon Lezion , Tel Aviv , Bat Yam and Yavne during rocket barrage on Saturday evening , Times of Israel reported , quoting medics . Two men aged 28 were treated for serious injuries in Rishon Lezion . Two individuals , aged 73 and 77 , were treated in Yavne . <p> In Bat Yam , a 24-year-old man suffered non-life-threatening injuries and at least four people were wounded in Tel Aviv . All victims were being treated at local hospitals . <p> Hundreds of Hamas militants infiltrated into Israel on Saturday morning and launched a land , air and sea attack inside the country . Several soldiers and civilians were taken hostage by the Palestinian militant group . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu declared war following the attack . <p> " We are at war -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a war , " he said in a televised address . " The enemy will pay an unprecedented price . ... We are at war and we will win it . " <p> The United Nations Security Council will be meeting on Sunday to discuss the situation . <p> " The Secretary-General is deeply concerned for the civilian population and urges maximum restraint .
