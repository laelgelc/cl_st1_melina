<h> Israel says it will not occupy Gaza Strip <p> Tel Aviv : Israel Defence Minister Yoav Gallant said that the main objective of the expected invasion of the Gaza Strip will be the destruction of the Hamas government and terror group . <p> Following the elimination of Hamas , Galant said there would be a new " security reality " in the Gaza Strip . <p> Gallant made his remarks while speaking before the Knesset Foreign Affairs and Defence Committee meeting in Tel Aviv on Friday . <p> Gallant also outlined the war 's objectives , which include destroying both the military and government operations of Hamas . <p> Gallant also said Israel will move quickly to remove any governmental responsibility Israel has over Gaza by creating a new " security regime " in the strip . <p> Speaking to committee members , Gallant outlined the three phases that will occur during the war . <p> " We are in the first phase , in which a military campaign is taking place with airstrikes and later with a ground manoeuvre , with the purpose of destroying operatives and damaging infrastructure in order to defeat and destroy Hamas , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the elimination of " pockets of resistance . " Overall , according to Gallant , there will also be a lower intensity of fighting during the second phase . <p> " The third step will be the creation of a new security regime in the Gaza Strip , the removal of Israel 's responsibility for day-to-day life in the Gaza Strip , and the creation of a new security reality for the citizens of Israel and the residents of the area surrounding Gaza , " he said . <p> The Israeli government has been under pressure to ensure that the army will not become bogged down in endless fighting in the Gaza Strip . <p> In response , it has set out its war aims to ensure there is no protracted fighting while guaranteeing the end of the Hamas presence in Gaza . <p> War was declared after Hamas invaded Israel on October 7 . Some 2,500 terrorists attacked border communities , killing some 1,400 people and seizing 200-250 hostages of all ages .
