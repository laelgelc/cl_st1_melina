He accused Iran of " actively facilitating " attacks on US bases in the Middle East <p> US accused Iran of " actively facilitating " attacks on US bases in the Middle East . ( File ) <p> Washington : <p> The White House accused Iran Monday of " actively facilitating " attacks on US bases in the Middle East , as it expressed concern over the potential for escalation of the war between Israel and Hamas militants in Gaza . <p> " Iran continues to support Hamas and Hezbollah , and we know that Iran is closely monitoring these events and in some cases , actively facilitating these attacks and spurring on others who may want to exploit the conflict for their own good or for that of Iran , " National Security Council spokesman John Kirby told reporters at the White House .
