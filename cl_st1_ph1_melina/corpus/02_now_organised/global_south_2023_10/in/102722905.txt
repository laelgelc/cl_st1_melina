The problem is that such weapons have the potential to cause civilian harm due to the severe burns . They are known to have a lingering long-term effect on survivors . <p> As per the available literature , white phosphorus is a chemical substance , dispersed in artillery shells , bombs , and rockets , that ignites when exposed to oxygen . The problem is that the impact is not restricted only towards the creation of thick smoke , but also imposes terrible injuries to the people .
