<p> Israel slammed United Nations on October 24 as it complained of fuel shortage in Gaza Strip . In sharp response , Israel suggested UN to ask Hamas for permission to use the fuel tanks present in Gaza . Replying to UN , Israel claimed that there were fuel tanks in Gaza having over 50,000 litres of fuel . This came after United Nations Relief and Works Agency ( UNRWA ) said it will be forced to halt relief operations due to fuel shortage . UNRWA warned that if they do not get fuel urgently , they would be forced to halt operations in Gaza Strip . Rejecting UNRWA 's ' SOS ' , Israel 's Chief Military Spokesperson said that country will not allow fuel to enter Israel . All hospitals in Gaza say they are running out of fuel to power their electricity generators , leaving them unable to treat the injured . More than 40 medical centres have halted operations , a Palestinian health ministry spokesman said . Meanwhile , the UN said 20 trucks that had been due to deliver aid to Gaza via the Rafah crossing from Egypt had not entered the enclave .
