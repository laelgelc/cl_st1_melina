<h> Watch : How Israel 's music festival near Gaza became a war zone <p> Oct 10 , 2023 , 07:08PM ISTSource : TOI.in <p> In a shocking turn of events , a music festival in the Gaza strip went from being a joyous celebration to a nightmarish ordeal for hundreds of young attendees , including Noa Argamani , a 25-year-old woman who was captured and seen crying for help while being abducted on a motorcycle by Hamas militants . The festival , which had moments earlier been filled with carefree dancing and music , quickly transformed into a scene of terror and chaos . As the attendees attempted to flee , they found themselves trapped with no escape route . Bullets rained down on them as they desperately sought shelter . Tragically , at least 260 young festivalgoers lost their lives , and others were taken as hostages by the heavily armed Hamas militants who showed no mercy . The aftermath of the event was marked by a harrowing scene of scorched cars strewn across the road , a stark contrast to the joyful atmosphere that had prevailed just hours earlier .
