<h> First electric traction train to run in Marathwada today <p> Sunday 15th October , 2023 <p> Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar : The Jalna-CST Mumbai Jan Shatabdi Express will be flagged off from Jalna on Sunday morning , making it the first train in Marathwada to run on electric traction . The train will have an environmentally friendly , pollution-free and energy-efficient alternative to diesel locomotive . Public relations officer for the Nanded division of the South Central Railway ( SCR ) , Rajesh Shinde said that the electric traction for Jalna-CST Mumbai Jan Shatabdi Express was facilitated by the completion of track electrification on the Manmad-Jalna-Nanded route . Track electrification has completed only till Jalna . The remaining electrification work on the Manmad-Jalna-Nanded route is in progress and expected to be over by January next year , he said . The electrification work on routes covering Parli and Akola stations is also in progress and has a similar deadline . These works will pave the way for electric traction for more trains , he later added .
