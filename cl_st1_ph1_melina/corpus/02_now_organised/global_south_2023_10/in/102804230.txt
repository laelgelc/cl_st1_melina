" said spokesman Admiral Daniel Hagari at a conference <p> Israel is to immediately step up its strikes in Gaza to increase pressure on Hamas , a military spokesman told a press conference on Saturday . <p> " We have to enter the next phase of the war in the best conditions , not according to what anyone tells us .
