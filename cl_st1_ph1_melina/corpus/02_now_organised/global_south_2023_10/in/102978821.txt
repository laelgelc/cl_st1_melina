That is a political goal no military can deliver . <p> The easiest way to lose a war is to set impossible or contradictory goals . <p> For example , remaking the Middle East by invading and remaking Iraq into a democratic state aligned with the United States was a delusional goal -- one whose failure can be seen in massive protests in front of the US embassy in Baghdad , and the targeting of US personnel there . As Von Clausewitz noted so long ago , " War is the continuation of policy with other means . " If the policy goals are not achieved , all military victory is hollow .
