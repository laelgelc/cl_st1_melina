<h> US govt 's assessment shows Israel not responsible for Gaza hospital blast : White House <h> Share <h> US govt 's assessment shows Israel not responsible for Gaza hospital blast : White House <p> Thursday , 19 October 2023 PTI Washington <p> A US analysis indicates that Israel was not responsible for the deadly blast at a Gaza hospital that killed hundreds of civilians on Tuesday , the White House has said , basing its assessment on intelligence overhead imagery , intercepts and open source information . <p> The assessment by the US came on Wednesday the same day when President Joe Biden made a show of solidarity with Israel during his visit there . <p> The massive explosion at the Al-Ahli Baptist Hospital late on Tuesday night killed 471 people and injured 314 others , the Palestinian Health Ministry has said . " The US government assesses that Israel was not responsible for an explosion that killed hundreds of civilians yesterday at the Al Ahli Hospital in the Gaza Strip , " White House National Security Council Spokesperson Adrienne Watson said in a statement .
