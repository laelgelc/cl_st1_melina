<h> Why stocks are n't in meltdown <p> Threats of long-drawn conflicts , war , are ever-present but rarely materialise full blown . The collective mind of markets knows this . Thus , their relative calm in the geopolitical storm <p> The attacks this month on Israel have raised fears of a wider Middle East conflict , even of a third world war . Serious voices are calling this the most dangerous time in living memory , with threats looming from Russia , China , North Korea and Iran.But financial markets have mostly been subdued in reaction to the conflagration brewing in Gaza . The benchmark US S&amp;P 500 index has barely budged since the Hamas attack on October 7 . Even stock markets that are close to the battle zone , from Saudi Arabia to Egypt and the Gulf states have experienced moderate pullbacks . There has been no rush to safety in the bond markets , where prices have been falling , and little drama in oil prices either .
