<h> IPO pipeline may continue to be robust <h> Geopolitical tensions notwithstanding <p> Sanjiv Bhasin , director , IIFL Securities said , " If the IPOs have enough room for giving appreciation , then definitely there is going to be appetite for that , " he added . <p> The stock market 's quick recovery from the Israel-Gaza conflict is giving hope to market players that the initial public offering ( IPO ) market will continue to boom during the rest of this financial year . <p> Sanjiv Bhasin , director , IIFL Securities said , " If the IPOs have enough room for giving appreciation , then definitely there is going to be appetite for that , " he added . <p> Agreed Mahavir Lunawat , MD , Pantomath Capital Advisors , " We believe that this will not impact investors ' sentiments for IPOs scheduled in the second half of FY24 as Indian economy fundamentals are extremely strong with robust GDP growth projections , " , said . He added that companies with good fundamentals and business model will continue to perform well irrespective of geopolitical scenarios . <p> In the first half of the financial year @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Rs 40,000 crore of public offers are expected in the second half of the year . These include big names like Oyo and Tata Technologies . <p> Currently , around 28 entities have received approval from Securities and Exchange Board of India ( Sebi ) for IPOs totaling Rs 40,740 crore , while 41 entities with an IPO size totaling Rs 43,659 crore are yet to receive the capital markets regulator 's nod , according to PRIME database . <p> The only worry that some market experts feel is that there could be some dampening of spirits if some of the IPOs do not do well . <p> Another important thing to note is that there could be some selling pressure on companies when anchor investors who complete one year may choose to exit . <p> Bhasin , however , disagreed and said that this type of selling will not happen in the market .
