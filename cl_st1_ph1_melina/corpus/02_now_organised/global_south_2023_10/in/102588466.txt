<h> IDF Explains The Strategy Of Hamas In Gaza <p> BusinessToday.In <p> New Delhi , <p> Oct 13 , 2023 , <p> Updated Oct 13 , 2023 , 7:48 PM IST <p> IDF spokesperson Maj ( Res ) Libby Weiss on Wednesday ( October 11 ) stated , ' It 's time to set the record straight on Israel 's border with Gaza ' . She further said the Hamas since gaining control over the Gaza region in 2007 has spent millions of dollars on enhancing terrorist activities and not establishing facilities for the civilians including on infrastructure . She also defended the Israeli stance of not supporting any supplies to Hamas in the Gaza Strip , adding that Israel has no obligation to provide facilities to the country 's enemy . Libby Weiss also spoke about the clean track record of Israel maintaining two crossings on Israel 's border with Gaza .
