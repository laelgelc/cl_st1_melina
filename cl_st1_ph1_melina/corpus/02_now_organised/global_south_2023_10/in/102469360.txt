The first information report has been lodged against the students for carrying out a demonstration without prior permission . <p> Aligarh : An FIR has been filed against Students of Aligarh Muslim University for carrying out a pro-Palestine protest on campus earlier today . The first information report has been lodged against the students for carrying out a demonstration without prior permission . <p> Out of all those who participated in the protest , four of the students have been named - Khalid , Kamran , Naved Choudhary , and Aatif . For the rest , an FIR has been filed against several unidentified people . <p> As per a report by India Today , the pro-Palestine march was carried out without permission . Aligarh SP received a tip-off about the students ' protest inside the campus and then took action accordingly . <p> The report adds that the FIR has been filed under Sections 155A , 188 and 505 of the Indian Penal Code @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the Aligarh Muslim University adminsitration has stated that they would be taking strict action against the students who organised the protest and those who participated in it . <p> On October 7 , 2023 , the Hamas militant group based out of Gaza , carried out a 5,000 rocket strike on Israel and proceeed to infiltrate Southern Israel .
