<p> Hamas Terrorists Group continues to attack various locations in the Kibbutz near the Israeli borders . Beeri , a Kibbutz , is among the worst-hit of the 14 Israeli border communities overrun by Hamas gunmen . Two women had a narrow escape when they were caught in a crossfire between Hamas militants and Israeli forces . In an attempt to save their lives , the women hid behind a car and the Israeli security personnel came to rescue them . Israeli media reported that over 100 people were killed in the Beeri Kibbutz by the Hamas terrorists . The conflict escalated when Hamas terrorists attacked several towns and Kibbutz in Israel . Israel 's PM immediately announced a state of war and the forces retaliated with attacks in the Gaza Strip . The Israel-Gaza war has entered day 06 and till now , over 2,400 people have lost their lives .
