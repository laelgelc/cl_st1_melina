<h> While the gaze in on Gaza <p> The last two weeks since Hamas 's brutal attack have shown that it is upon Israel to recognise that only in Palestinian well-being can it find real long-lasting security <p> No matter how upset or angry you are , no matter how desperate you are to be heard -- nothing can justify the killing of children . There is no debate that what Hamas did to Israel was condemnable and sank humanity to a new low . Indiscriminate killing of civilians , taking them hostage , and opening fire at a music concert have shaken , scarred and scared Israel . If this is not terrorism , what is ? The purpose of this article is not to diminish the severity of Hamas 's terrible actions or the pain it caused Israel . Support for Israel is unequivocal and it has the right to defend itself . However , support for Israel does n't have to mean a carte blanche that it can do anything . The sentiment can not be , " Israel has suffered so much that from now on , it can do no wrong .
