<h> Back from conflict zone <p> Bollywood actress Nushrratt Bharuccha , who was earlier stranded in Israel amidst the escalating tension between Israel and Gaza , has landed in Mumbai safely . <p> However , the actress looked distressed as she walked out of the Mumbai airport . She was seen wearing crepe coloured track pants and a sweatshirt . As she walked from the arrivals to her car , she was surrounded by the media , the actress visibly distraught , could barely speak given the commotion . <p> She was almost in tears and repeatedly requested the camera persons to move behind so she could get to her car and go home having been through a testing period of more than 24 hours . <p> Nushrratt was in Israel to participate in Haifi Film Festival . With the help of the Indian Embassy , she was brought back home . She took a connecting flight .
