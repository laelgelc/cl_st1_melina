She returned to Palestine in 1984 and joined Dar al-Tifel al-Arabi ( School for the Arab Child ) in Jerusalem . She was arrested on June 15 , 1991 , at Bab el Amoud Gate in occupied Jerusalem . She was sentenced for life plus 12 years . During her years in prison , al Ghosain drew on the walls of every cell she occupied as well as on the walls of the cells she visited . With a diploma in art , her favourite hobby is painting about life in prison .
