<p> Al Jazeera 's chief correspondent in Gaza Wael Dahdouh lost four members of his family in an Israeli airstrike on October 25 . <p> The victims included Dahdouh 's wife , son , and daughter , with his grandson declared dead two hours later . The devastating news reached Dahdouh while he was broadcasting live images of the besieged territory 's night sky . <p> Al Jazeera released a heart-wrenching video showing Dahdouh entering al-Aqsa Hospital in Gaza , overcome with grief as he looked upon his deceased son . The video showcased the grim reality of the ongoing conflict . <p> Dahdouh , a revered figure in his native Gaza , is known for his relentless efforts to bring the stories of suffering and hardship faced by Palestinians to the global stage . <p> The Israeli airstrike that claimed the lives of Dahdouh 's family members hit Nuseirat Refugee Camp , an area where residents had been advised to seek safety .
