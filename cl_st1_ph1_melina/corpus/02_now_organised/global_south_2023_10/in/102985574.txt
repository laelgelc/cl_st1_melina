Israel 's land invasion of Gaza , which seemed imminent at one time , has been delayed . The Palestinian cause , which had already lost its centrality in Arab politics , would have been further marginalised with these accords . If Israel succeeds , the issue of who will govern Gaza will arise . <p> The Palestinian Authority in the West Bank , already in disrepute as being ineffective , may not want to enter Gaza on the back of an Israeli victory coming at a great human cost for Gaza 's population .
