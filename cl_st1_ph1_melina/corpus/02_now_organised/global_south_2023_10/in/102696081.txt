<p> Iran 's supreme leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei issued a warning on Tuesday , stating that forces opposed to Israel would be unstoppable if Israel continued its bombardment of the Gaza Strip in response to the surprise attack by Hamas . Khamenei made the comment at a meeting in Tehran , reported news agency AFP . The Islamic republic has maintained close contact with regional allies since Hamas militants attacked the Gaza border , resulting in over 1,400 casualties , primarily civilians . <p> In response , Israel carried out a devastating air and artillery bombardment of the Hamas-controlled Gaza Strip , resulting in at least 2,750 Palestinian casualties , predominantly civilians .
