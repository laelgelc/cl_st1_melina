<h> New consumer grievances president for Nashik city <p> Tuesday 17th October , 2023 <p> Nashik : The citys consumer grievances and redressal commission got a new president after seven months . The state government appointed Mandakini Bhosale as the president of the three-member bench of the Nashik consumer commission . The bench turned dysfunctional since the term of the previous president ended in February 2023 . Without a president , they did not have the power to conduct hearings . Now there are more than 4,300 grievances registered with the commission , said Konika Jadhav , a coordinator for the legal aid department of the commission . When the bench is complete , the commission has the power to issue notices to concerned people after conducting hearings and giving judgments . Though a new president was appointed , one of the two other members was transferred to Dhule . This will not affect the business of the commission as a two-member bench with the president in the chair can conduct hearings , said Siddharth Varma , a lawyer from Nashik .
