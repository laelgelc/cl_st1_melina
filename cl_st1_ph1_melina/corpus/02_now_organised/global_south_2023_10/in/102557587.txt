<p> New Delhi : The recent eruption of conflict in the Israel-Gaza region has once again highlighted the fragile nature of peace in this part of the world . The audacious attack led by Hamas , which took a tragic toll on Israeli lives , including the heart-wrenching strike on a hospital in Ashkelon , is both deplorable and deeply concerning . But as world leaders grapple with the immediate ramifications of this violence , it is imperative that the broader , transformative potential of the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor ( IMEC ) is n't lost amidst the smoke of these skirmishes . <p> The IMEC , spanning 428 kilometers from Eilat to the Port of Haifa , represents more than just infrastructure ; it 's a beacon of regional integration and shared economic prosperity . Designed to enhance connectivity between India , the Arabian Gulf , and Europe , its visionary objective is to provide an alternative to China 's Belt and Road Initiative ( BRI ) .
