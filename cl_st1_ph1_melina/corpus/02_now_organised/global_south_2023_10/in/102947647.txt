<p> IDF Airstrike Eliminates Hamas Deputy Head of Intelligence Responsible for October 7 Attacks ( Screengrab from the footage shared by IDF ) <p> The Israel Defence Forces ( IDF ) on Thursday announced the elimination of Hamas 's Deputy Head of Intelligence , Shadi Barud , in a Gaza Strip airstrike , Times of Israel reported . Barud is accused of planning the October 7 attacks in southern Israel in collaboration with Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar . Barud had previously served as a battalion commander in the Khan Younis region and held various roles within Hamas ' intelligence directorate . <p> According to the IDF , he was responsible for planning multiple terrorist attacks targeting Israeli civilians . The IDF has also released a video depicting the airstrike that resulted in his death . <p> " Based on precise IDF and ISA intelligence , IDF fighter jets struck the Deputy Head of the Hamas ' Intelligence Directorate , Shadi Barud , " the Israeli forces said in a joint statement . <p> Meanwhile , Hamas 's military wing claimed on Thursday that around 50 hostages held by their militants in Gaza have been killed in Israeli airstrikes since @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ today , the Israel Defense Forces announced that they conducted a " targeted raid " in northern Gaza using tanks and infantry troops . While not a full-scale ground invasion , the IDF stated that the raid was part of their preparations for the next phases of the conflict .
