<h> Hamas Naval Chief Abu Sahiban killed , says Israel Defense Forces <p> Tel Aviv : The Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) , which has commenced ground assault in Gaza , on Saturday said that it has killed Hamas Naval commander Abu Sahiban in an overnight attack . <p> The IDF said that the slain commander had planned and executed Hamas infiltration by sea which was foiled by the military . <p> With the killing of aerial Chief Abu Rakbeh and Naval Commander Abu Sahiban , the IDF seems to be focusing on its targets . IDF spokesperson Rear Admiral Daniel Halagary had told media persons that the army has advanced into the Gaza Strip and may step up its operations . <p> The IDF has already claimed that it had destroyed 140 underground structures of Hamas on Friday and Saturday .
