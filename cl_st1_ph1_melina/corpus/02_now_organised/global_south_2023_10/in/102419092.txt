<p> Palestinian armed group launches largest attack on Israel in years , PM Netanyahu says ' we are at war ' -- Images Inside <p> The Palestinian armed group Hamas has initiated its most substantial attack on Israel in years . This attack included infiltrations into southern regions of Israel , following a barrage of over 2,000 rockets launched from the Gaza Strip . Local media reported that these rockets resulted in the deaths of at least 22 Israelis and left hundreds wounded . <p> " Citizens of Israel , we are at war , " Israeli PM Benjamin Netanyahu said in a video message . Israel 's Defence Minister , Yoav Gallant , issued a stern warning to Hamas , emphasising that the group had committed a " grave mistake . " The attack , which commenced at 03:30 GMT on Saturday , featured an onslaught of rocket fire from various locations within Gaza , along with fighters infiltrating the Israeli border by land , sea , and air .
