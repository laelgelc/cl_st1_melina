<h> Video : German woman was at music festival moments before Hamas caught her <p> A 30-year-old German-Israeli , Shani Louk attended a music festival in southern Israel near the Gaza border . She worked as a tattoo artist . <p> The festival , which was supposed to be a joyous celebration , turned into a nightmare when Hamas militants launched an attack on October 7 . <p> A video that has since gone viral captured the terrifying moments of Louk 's abduction . In the video , Louk is seen motionless , surrounded by armed militants . Her dreadlocks are held by one militant while another drapes his leg over her waist . The chilling scene ends with the militants cheering " Allahu Akbar " . <p> This video , along with others from the festival , has been widely circulated , revealing the horrifying reality of the attack . The aftermath left at least 260 dead and several , including Louk , taken hostage .
