The Western Powers are only adding fuel to the fire with their unequivocal support , and total disregard , for Israel 's barbaric and utterly disproportionate response to the Hamas attack of 7th October , which clearly borders on genocide and ethnic cleansing . The move of the US Carrier Groups into the theatre and its missile attacks against Pro-Iranian Syrian militias is a clear indication of this . <p> The hypocrisy of Western Governments is mind-boggling .
