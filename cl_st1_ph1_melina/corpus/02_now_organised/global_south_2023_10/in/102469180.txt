<h> Israel releases footage of airstrikes on Gaza Aerial view <p> Israel on Monday released footage of its airstrikes on the Gaza Strip which it carried out in retaliation for the Hamas ' attacks . The Israeli Defense Forces said over 1,200 targets were hit by Israeli aircraft across the Gaza Strip between Saturday and Monday morning . <p> " Today we doubled that number " , it added . <p> The Israeli Air Force said it carried out extensive attacks along the length and breadth of the Gaza Strip , " wreaking havoc on Hamas terrorists " . It further said 130 targets in Hamas were attacked in just three hours . The focus of the attacks were Beit Hanon , Sajaya , Al Furkan and Rimal . <p> Hamas fired thousands of missiles from the Gaza Strip towards Israel .
