<h> French Football Investigates ' Hate Speech ' By Nice Player <h> The French Football Federation ( FFF ) said on Sunday its ethics committee would investigate Nice player Youcef Atal for social media posts containing " calls for violence " , linked to the Hamas-Israel conflict <p> The French Football Federation ( FFF ) said on Sunday its ethics committee would investigate Nice player Youcef Atal for social media posts containing " calls for violence " , linked to the Hamas-Israel conflict . The Algerian international defender posted a video on Instagram account of a speaker making , according to people who saw it , anti-Semitic remarks calling for violence . The post sparked outrage from French politicians . It has since been deleted and AFP has been unable to independently verify its content . <p> " They are contrary to the ethics of our sport and the values that soccer relentlessly defends " , he said . <p> " The FFF condemns them in the strongest possible terms . Hate speech can not be tolerated , " he declared , announcing that the FFF 's national ethics council had been informed . <p> Atal apologised on Instagram @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that my publication shocked people , which was not my intention , and I apologise for that , " he wrote , adding that he wanted to " clarify ( his ) point of view without any ambiguity : I strongly condemn all forms of violence , anywhere in the world , and I support all victims " . <p> " I will never support a message of hatred . Peace is an ideal in which I firmly believe " , he said . <p> Contacted by AFP , the Nice public prosecutor 's office said it was not yet investigating . <p> Israel declared war on Hamas last Sunday , a day after waves of fighters broke through the heavily fortified border with Gaza and killed more than 1,400 people , most of them civilians .
