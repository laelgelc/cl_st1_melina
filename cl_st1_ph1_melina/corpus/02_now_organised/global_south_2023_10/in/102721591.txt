<p> Hamas authorities in Gaza said that the explosion at the Ahli Arab Hospital was the result of the latest Israeli airstrikes . However , the Israeli Defense Forces pointed fingers at Palestinian militants , contending that an outgoing Islamic Jihad rocket had misfired.Islamic Jihad , established in 1981 , is a Palestinian militant organization with a steadfast opposition to peace with Israel . It ardently strives to establish an Islamic state within Palestine and operates predominantly in the Gaza Strip . This group is known for its radical ideology and frequent engagement in violent tactics , setting it apart from other Palestinian factions like Hamas.Islamic Jihad maintains strong alliances with Iran and Syria , receiving significant support from these nations , both financially and militarily . These connections have enabled the group to sustain its activities and bolster its position in the region .
