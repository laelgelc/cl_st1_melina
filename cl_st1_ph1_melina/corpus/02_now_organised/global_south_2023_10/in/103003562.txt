BJP Kerala unit president K Surendran has criticized the participation of Khaled Mashal in the event and has questioned the Kerala Police 's inaction in this regard . <p> New Delhi : A virtual rally led by a former Hamas chief on 27 October has returned to bite the Kerala government . Palestinian leader and former Hamas chief Khaled Mashal addressed online a rally taken out by the Solidarity Youth Movement expressing support for the Palestinian people in Malappuram on October 27 , according to a report in The Hindu . <p> Solidarity Youth Movement is the youth organization of Jamaat-e-Islami . Its members raised the slogan of ' Unite against Zionist-Hindu Racism . ' A video of Hamas leader Khaled Mashal participating in the event was released online by the organizers of the rally , according to The Statesman . <p> " Al-Aqsa is our pride , our noble place , the place where our beloved Prophet Muhammad ( PBUH ) started his Mi'raj journey to the celestial world . Your beloved brothers in Gaza @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ three weeks of military defeat , today Israel is taking revenge on our people in Gaza . Houses are being destroyed , " said Khaled Mashal in his speech in Arabic ( as per the translation released by the organizers ) . <p> The Bharatiya Janata Party ( BJP ) Kerala state president K Surendran has slammed the police for inaction and has sought legal action against the organisers . The BJP leader at a press conference in Thiruvananthapuram alleged that Congress leader Shashi Tharoor 's participation in the rally , given his previous role as a UN diplomat , goes against the country 's established stance on the issue . <p> Surendran also said the Indian Union Muslim League ( IUML ) rally held in Kozhikode on Thursday was " pro-Hamas , " and anti-national slogans had been raised throughout the programme . <p> BJP leader Shehzad Poonawalla says , " This is very unfortunate ... The Kerala govt is giving a platform to such organizations and their leaders who have a terrorist mindset and killed more than 700 innocent people ... Will the Congress party which is part @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ INDI alliance supporting Hamas who killed more than 700 people ... Terrorists are being given platforms . In the name of vote bank politics , terrorists are being given platforms . Using Palestine as an excuse , attempts are made to protect Hamas . " <p> It is not merely the pro-Hamas tilt of the gathered youths that bothers the Centre but the fact that the Solidarity Youth Movement raised the slogan " Uproot bulldozer Hindutva and apartheid Zionism " during its rally and attempted to strike a close relationship between the ideologies of Hindutva and Zionism.
