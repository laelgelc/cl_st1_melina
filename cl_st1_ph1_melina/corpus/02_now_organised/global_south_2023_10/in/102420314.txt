<h> Watch : Hamas infiltrator uses motorised hang glider to enter Israel <p> A video showed an infiltrator of Hamas , a Palestinian outfit , using a motorised hang glider to enter Israel . The clip , the authenticity of which can not be verified , surfaced on social media as Israel declared a ' state of war ' . <p> In what is said to be the biggest attack on Israel in years , the Islamist movement Hamas launched 5,000 rockets towards Israel from the Gaza Strip . <p> " This is the day of the greatest battle to end the last occupation on earth , " said Hamas military commander Mohammad Deif , as he announced the start of the operation in a broadcast on Hamas media , calling on Palestinians everywhere to fight . <p> The ITBP personnel on Friday rescued 68 people stranded in north Sikkim following flash floods . In a video , the ' Himveers ' of the ITBP rescue team could be seen evacuating those stranded in the region for over three days .
