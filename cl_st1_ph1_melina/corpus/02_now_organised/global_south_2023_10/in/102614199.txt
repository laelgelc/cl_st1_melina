<p> New Delhi : Even as it is engaged in a war against Hamas in Gaza , Israel has formally assured India that defence supplies will not take a hit , ThePrint has learnt . <p> India is Israel 's largest weapons market and procures an array of sophisticated military hardware such as missiles , sensors , radars , drones and night-vision devices . <p> Sources in the defence establishment told ThePrint that Israel has formally informed that despite the conflict , there would be no " upset " of weapons deliveries to India . <p> The sources also said that Israel sees a big market for arms imports in India and the war has opened up opportunities for both countries to collaborate . <p> " Given the ongoing violence between Israel and Gaza , this could be an opportunity for India to set up manufacturing of equipment in the country with Israeli know-how . This will prove to be a cost-effective solution for both the countries if they decide to cooperate on those lines , " a source said . <p> India has placed orders for Spike-LR ( Long Range ) Anti-Tank Guided Missiles ( ATGM ) from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ other equipment . India also operates radars such as MF-Star. which are fitted on board several navy ships . <p> India is also one of the largest users of Israeli drones including the Herons , Harpy and Searcher Mk-II . Israeli Spice 2000 bombs were used by the Indian fighter jets during the Balakot airstrike in 2019 , besides a host of other weapons and radars . <p> The defence cooperation took off during the Kargil war in 1999 , when Israel provided India with laser-guided missiles for its fighter jets , apart from mortar ammunition . Between 2018 and 2022 , India was Israel 's largest arms market , accounting for 37 percent of Israel 's arms exports , as per the latest SIPRI data published in March 2023 . <p> Israel has been a close defence and security partner for India . Several Israeli components and technical know-how is used in some of the weapon systems being manufactured by India .
