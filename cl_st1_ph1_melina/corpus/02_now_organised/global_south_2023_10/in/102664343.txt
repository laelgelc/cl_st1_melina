<p> The head of Israel 's Shin Bet , the country 's internal security service , Ronen Bar said on Monday that his organisation " failed to provide a sufficient warning " to thwart the Hamas attack . He also took " responsibility " for the same , The Times of Israel reported . <p> Bar said that despite carrying out a series of actions , his organisation was unable to " generate a sufficient warning " to prevent the deadly Hamas attack .
