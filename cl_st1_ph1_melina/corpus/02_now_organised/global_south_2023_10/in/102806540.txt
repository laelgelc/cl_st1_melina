" Today , when we are told almost every day that the situation is far improved . It is as close to normal ... I believe , unfortunately , Jammu and Kashmir is that place where democracy in India goes to be buried , " he said . Addressing a talk on " Kashmir Today " at the Foreign Correspondents ' Club ( FCC ) of South Asia here , Abdullah said the " people are suffering " in Jammu and Kashmir and public anger growing .
