<p> Jerusalem/Tel Aviv : The Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) continued heavy bombing against the Gaza Strip on Sunday , along with artillery strikes with air power and tanks , the media reported . <p> It hit more than 450 targets over the past day , including command centers , observation posts , and missile launch sites , CNN reported quoting IDF . The IDF said an officer was severely injured by a mortar shell in the northern Gaza Strip and was evacuated for medical treatment . <p> In a facebook post , Palestine Telecommunications ( Paltel ) said connectivity is being partially restored in Gaza after being disrupted since Friday night . In a televised address on Saturday Israeli PM Benjamin Netanyahu said it will be a " long and difficult " war with Hamas . <p> Since the October 7 Hamas attacks in which 1,400 people were killed and 229 were held hostages , Israel has been bombing Gaza .
