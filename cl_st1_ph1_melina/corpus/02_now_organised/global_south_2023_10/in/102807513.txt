<p> SRINAGAR , Oct 21 : While the situation in Jammu and Kashmir is " normal " , the continuing delay in holding assembly elections is a matter of " grave concern " , former chief minister Ghulam Nabi Azad said here on Saturday . " When the situation was bad in J-K , and there was bloodshed and leaders left the Valley , Kashmiri Pandits left the Valley , that time also elections took place after five to six years , " Azad said .
