<h> Bishop Santos urges faithful to pray for peace amid Gaza bombardment <p> It is urgent to pray and hope for peace amid the Israel-Hamas war , given that peace is " what the world needs , " said Bishop Ruperto Santos of Antipolo on Tuesday . <p> " We turn to God for the change of heart , for conversion that they renounce violence and give peace a chance , " said Santos . <p> On Saturday , a surprise attack took place in Israel as Hamas , a Palestinian militant group , launched 5000 rockets in an initial barrage . In response to the attack , Israel had its jets bomb the Gaza Strip . <p> Santos expressed his objection against the raging violence in the ongoing war between Israel and Palestine . <p> " Conflicts are resolved through dialogue , " the prelate said . " Violence only begets violence . "
