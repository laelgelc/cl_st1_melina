<p> SEVEN Filipinos in Gaza Strip have remained unaccounted for amid the ongoing conflict in Israel due to attacks by the Hamas terrorist group . <p> The Department of Foreign Affairs ( DFA ) said Monday evening , October 9 , 2023 , that according to the Philippine Embassy in Tel Aviv , 29 Filipinos were initially reported to have been missing but it was verified that 22 of them have already been rescued by Israeli security forces . <p> The rescued Filipinos were brought to a safer area . <p> Two of them were injured but have already received necessary treatment . <p> " Seven remain unaccounted for , who can not be contacted via their mobile number and social media accounts , " the DFA said . <p> It said they are in close coordination with the Israel government to identify their whereabouts . <p> He said four others , Loreta de Costa , Buena Besol , Marilyn Magana , and Joey Fasulingan , were rescued and are now in a safer place . <p> It is not confirmed yet if the DFA and Owwa are referring to the same individuals . <p> In a television interview @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to the Philippines Ilan Fluss said the Israeli government is committed for the well-being of Filipinos and other nationals living in their country , the same way they are committed for the protection of their fellow nationals . <p> He said around 100 foreign nationals were reported to have been abducted by the Hamas group whose primary demand is the release of some of their members arrested by the Israel government . <p> Fluss said the Israel government is " slowly regaining control " of the affected areas particularly in the southern area . <p> " We are now hopefully in the final stages of stabilizing the situation and we still have attempts to enter Israel through the border , by air , by sea and by land but we still have some pockets where there are still some terrorists inside .
