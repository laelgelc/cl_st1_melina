<h> Volcanic smog hits Batangas towns anew <p> MANILA , Philippines -- Volcanic smog or vog blanketed several cities and municipalities near Taal Volcano in Batangas yesterday , disrupting classes in some localities including nearby provinces . <p> The Office of Civil Defense ( OCD ) urged residents to take precautionary measures against the smog such as staying indoors , shutting doors and windows , and drinking water to prevent throat irritation , and to seek medical attention in case of emergency . <p> " The OCD is monitoring the activity of the volcano and the situation in affected areas , " OCD administrator Ariel Nepomuceno said . <p> " Our regional counterpart in Calabarzon has been on alert to ensure the safety of the communities . The government is ready to send assistance if necessary , " he said , referring to Cavite , Laguna , Batangas , Rizal and Quezon . <p> As of 11 a.m. yesterday , vog was monitored in the towns of Agoncillo , Alitagtag , Balayan , Balete , Calatagan , Laurel , Lemery , Lian , Malvar , Mataasnakahoy , Nasugbu , Rosario , San Nicolas , San Pascual , San Jose @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as in the cities of Calaca and Tanauan in Batangas . <p> The continuous smog emission from Taal compelled school authorities to suspend classes in several areas of Batangas as well as in Cavite , Laguna and Rizal . <p> To protect communities from health hazards , the OCD and the Department of Health ( DOH ) sent 96,800 face masks to affected areas in Batangas , Cavite and Laguna . <p> The DOH said it recorded 714 cases of respiratory complaints including 638 in Batangas , 49 in Cavite , 16 in Laguna and 11 in Rizal due to volcanic smog . <p> Affected residents complained of sore throat , difficulty in breathing , cough and other symptoms . <p> The Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology ( Phivolcs ) described the vog as fine droplets containing volcanic gas like sulfur dioxide , which is acidic and can irritate the eyes , throat and respiratory tract . <p> Severity of the cases depend on the gas concentration and duration of exposure . <p> People vulnerable to vog are those with health conditions such as asthma , lung and heart @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ children . <p> Phivolcs chief Teresito Bacolcol said that strong winds could immediately disperse the vog . <p> As of yesterday , the main crater of Taal Volcano was visible , Bacolcol said . <p> " The steam or plume that we see is easily dispersed .
