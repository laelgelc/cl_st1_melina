<h> Rattled by war , investors sought high yields for T-bills <p> SEEN rattled by the armed conflict in the Middle East , investors sought higher yields for the promissory notes auctioned off by the Philippine government last Monday . <p> The Treasury suffered another week of failing to fully award its Treasury bills ( T-bills ) tender , marking the third straight week of settling with mixed or partial awards . It was also the fourth consecutive week in the Bureau of the Treasury 's ( BTr ) auction of short-term debt papers that investors stood their ground , hedging against the economic implications of the escalating Israel-Hamas war . <p> The Treasury raised only P11.947 billion out of its P15-billion programmed amount during Monday 's auction that saw average yields increasing across all three tenors . <p> The only tenor that the Treasury was able to make a full award ( P5 billion ) was on 365-day T-bills . <p> Both the 91-day and 182-day government securities were partially awarded raising P3.637 billion and P3.31 billion , respectively , for the national government . <p> The average yield for the 91-day T-bills was 5.99 percent , higher @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 5.871 percent secondary market benchmark level . Investors ' asking rates for the 91-day T-bills ranged from 5.85 percent to 6.1 percent . <p> The 182-day debt instrument fetched an average yield of 6.207 percent , up from last week 's 6.115 percent . It was also higher than the 6.146 percent secondary market benchmark level . <p> Meanwhile , the average yield of the 364-day government security settled at 6.388 percent up from the 6.305 percent recorded in the previous week 's auction . <p> " The US and Iran recently warned of a wider Israel-Hamas war . The markets are on wait-and-see stance as Israel plans soon a major ground offensive/war vs . Hamas in Gaza , " Rizal Commercial Banking Corp . ( RCBC ) Chief Economist Michael L. Ricafort said in an interview . <p> Ricafort noted that global oil prices have increased since the Israel-Hamas war broke out last October 7 . The RCBC Chief Economist also pointed out that T-bills auction yields " are already unusually higher " than their secondary market counterpart in PHP BVAL yields as of October 13 . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for this week at PHP19.371 billion vs. the previous week 's P22.564 billion ; higher than the P15 billion , but again partially awarded at P11.947 billion due to some higher bid yields rejected , " Ricafort added . <p> Ricafort said investors have also factored in their bids the possibility that the Monetary Board of the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas would hike policy rates in its November meeting . <p> For October , the Treasury plans to raise P150 billion from the domestic market through the auction of P60 billion T-bills and P90 billion Treasury bonds .
