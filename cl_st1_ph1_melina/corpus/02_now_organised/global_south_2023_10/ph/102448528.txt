<p> THE Department of Foreign Affairs ( DFA ) said on Sunday , October 8 , 2023 , that there were no reported Filipino casualties in Israel following the attack of Palestine militants . <p> In a radio interview , DFA Undersecretary for Migrant Affairs Eduardo Jose de Vega said the Philippine embassy in Israel is in close coordination with the Filipino community there . <p> He said there are around 30,000 Filipinos in Israel , most of them were domestic workers and caregivers situated in Tel Aviv , Haifa , which is about 70 kilometers away from the Gaza Strip , the center of the attack . <p> ( There are Filipinos who are worried about what is happening . However , all of them are actually following the policies of the Israeli government and the Philippines to stay at home and should be close to a bomb shelter . Because all buildings in Israel have one , you see . ) <p> He said they have yet to receive reports about the alleged abduction of several Filipinos , noting that all the 300 agricultural workers near the Gaza Strip were accounted for . <p> In a separate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Arnell Ignacio said they are closely monitoring around 200 OFWs in the Gaza Strip . <p> " Meron five caregivers sa Gaza na all accounted for na meron pa tayong hinihintay na update from anim pa na mga OFWs ( five caregivers in Gaza are all accounted for , and we are still waiting for updates from six other Filipino overseas workers ) , " he said . <p> Ignacio said they have yet to receive a request for repatriation , noting that Filipino workers are already used to such incidents . <p> The Palestinian Islamist group Hamas launched the attack on Saturday , October 7 , which was tagged as the " biggest " offensive in years , prompting the Israel Home Front Command to issue a state of war alert . <p> The death toll crawled up to 250 as of Sunday morning .
