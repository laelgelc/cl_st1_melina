<h> XBB subvariants , sublineages maintain dominance in PH <p> THE Omicron XBB subvariant and its sublineages continue to be dominant in the country based on the coronavirus disease ( Covid-19 ) samples subjected by the Department of Health ( DOH ) to genome sequencing . <p> Based on the latest Covid-19 Biosurveillance Report , the DOH said the trend continues on which Covid-19 subvariants and sublineages are most commonly detected in the country . <p> " From August 2023 onwards , XBB and its sublineages were the most detected variants , " said the DOH . <p> According to the results of the October 10 to 17 genome sequencing , 50 out of the 65 samples were classified as XBB . <p> These include three XBB.1.16 cases , three XBB.1.9.1 cases and 42 XBB.2.3 cases . <p> The DOH said all detected XBB subvariant cases were local cases from Northern Mindanao , Davao Region , Soccsksargen , and Cordillera Administrative Region . <p> The DOH said there were also 12 samples that were classified under " other Omicron sublineages.covi " <p> The remaining three samples , meanwhile , had no assigned Covid-19 lineages. &lt;b&gt; ( HDT/SunStar Philippines ) @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for OFWs in Lebanon opened <p> LEBANON .
