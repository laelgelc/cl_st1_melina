<h> Mikoko Pamoja awarded UN in Kenya Person of the Year 2023 <p> Mikoko Pamoja was developed by scientists of the KMFRI together with their partners in the United Kingdom . According to Dr James Kairo , Project Developer and Chief Scientist at KMFRI , " Mikoko Pamoja is an excellent example of ' triple win ' project -- a win for climate , community , and biodiversity . " <p> The Mikoko Pamoja , an environmental initiative in the Gazi Community has gained global recognition from the United Nations in their bid to offer sustainable community development through mangrove conservation . <p> The initiative which translates to ' Mangroves Together ' is a transformative community empowerment movement aimed to cultivate revenue streams and channel funds from sales of carbon credit directly into the community 's development . <p> Project Coordinator of the Initiative , Kassim Juma says the restoration of mangroves helps to protect the coastline and empower the future of people in Gazi . <p> " The essence of Mikoko Pamoja lies in its ethos of interweaving environmental stewardship with community empowerment . our story is one of transformation , " he said . <p> According to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Fisheries Research Institute ) , James Kairo , " Mikoko Pamoja " is an excellent example of " triple win " project t -- a win for climate , community and biodiversity . <p> " Success of Mikoko Pamoja has been replicated in Vanga Blue Forest ( VBF ) , and plans are underway to expand the programme to Lamu and other areas along the coast , " he said . <p> The United Nations has recognized the multifaceted approach and placed the Gaza Community at the forefront of community -- led environmental action .
