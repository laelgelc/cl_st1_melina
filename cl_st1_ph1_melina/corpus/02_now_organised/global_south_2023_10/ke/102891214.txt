<p> King Mohammed VI of Morocco , who is also the President of the Al-Quds Committee , has dispatched emergency humanitarian aid to the Palestinian populations . <p> According to the statement from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Moroccan Expatriates on Monday , the aid includes significant quantities of foodstuffs , medical supplies , and water , as specified in a statement released by the ministry . <p> The modalities of sending the emergency aid will be determined in coordination with the Egyptian and Palestinian authorities . <p> King Mohammed VI 's decision is part of the constant commitment of the Royal Kingdom to the Palestinian cause . <p> Morocco is the latest country to give humanitarian aid to over 20,000 displaced Palestian families in the Gaza Strip as Israel attacks intensified . <p> The country has also expressed its willingness to mediate on the ongoing standoff in the Gaza Strip to stop bloodshed and restore peace .
