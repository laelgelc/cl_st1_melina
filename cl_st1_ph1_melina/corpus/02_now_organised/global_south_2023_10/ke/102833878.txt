<h> MTV Europe Music Awards 2023 cancelled due to war <p> The MTV Europe Music Awards ( EMAs ) , scheduled to take place in Paris in November , have been canceled due to the " volatility of world events . " The decision to call off the awards show comes in the wake of the escalating violence between Israel and the militant group Hamas . <p> The awards show , which was expected to feature nominees such as Taylor Swift , who had received six nominations , will not proceed as planned . " <p> The decision to cancel the awards show comes as the conflict between Israel and Hamas has escalated , with violence breaking out on October 7 when Hamas militants launched an attack on Israel , resulting in the deaths of 1,400 people and more than 200 hostages . Palestinian officials have reported over 3,700 deaths in Gaza due to Israeli air strikes . <p> France has also been on high alert following a fatal attack in Arras , where a school teacher was stabbed to death on October 13 .
