<h> Morocco 's King call for urgent Arab League States meeting to seek solution for Gaza attacks <p> King Mohammed VI of Morocco , who chairs the current session of the Council of the League of Arab States at the ministerial level , has called for an emergency meeting to deliberate on ongoing Gaza Strip attacks by the Israeli military as a retaliation for the Hamas invasion . <p> King Mohammed VI has called for an urgent meeting for consultation and coordination on the deteriorating situation in the Gaza strip and the outbreak of military actions targeting civilians , as well as for the search for ways to halt this dangerous escalation , said the Ministry of Foreign Affairs , African Cooperation , and Moroccan Expatriates . <p> According to the statement from the Royal Kingdom of Morocco , intensive consultations are underway for the meeting to be held this week at the headquarters of the League of Arab States in Cairo , the ministry noted in a statement . <p> Over 300 people have been killed following Israeli military attacks on Palestinians near the Gaza Strip .
