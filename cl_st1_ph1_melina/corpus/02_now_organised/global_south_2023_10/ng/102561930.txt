<h> US committee report say Egypt warned Israel days before Hamas struck <p> A report by the US House of Representatives Foreign Affairs Committee has revealed that Egypt had warned Israel of potential violence three days before Hamas militants struck in deadly cross-border raid which has turned into a war with thousands killed . <p> Head of the Committee , head Michael McCaul , told reporters on Wednesday that US intelligence can confirm the warning which was not adhered to by Israel . <p> " We know that Egypt has warned the Israelis three days prior that an event like this could happen , " McCaul told reporters following a closed-door intelligence briefing for lawmakers about the Middle East crisis . <p> " I do n't want to get too much into classified , but a warning was given . I think the question was at what level , " he stated . <p> He noted that an Egyptian intelligence official had told the Associated Press news agency that Cairo had repeatedly warned the Israelis " something big " was being planned from Gaza . <p> " We have warned them an explosion of the situation is coming , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ But they underestimated such warnings , " the official was quoted on the condition of anonymity . <p> The official further said Israeli officials played down the threat from Gaza , instead focusing on the West Bank .
