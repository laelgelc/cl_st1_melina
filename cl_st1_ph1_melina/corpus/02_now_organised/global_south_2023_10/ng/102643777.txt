<h> Israel-Palestine War : EU convenes special leaders ' meeting <h> " We stand in full solidarity with the people of Israel and the victims of the terrorist attacks . " <p> European Union ( EU ) leaders are set to convene an extraordinary meeting via video link on Tuesday to discuss the situation in Israel and the Gaza Strip , European Council President Charles Michel said . <p> " We stand in full solidarity with the people of Israel and the victims of the terrorist attacks , " Mr Michel said in an invitation issued on Saturday evening . <p> " Israel has the right to defend itself in full compliance with international law , in particular international humanitarian law , " he added . <p> Palestinian extremist organisation Hamas , which rules Gaza , launched thousands of rockets toward Israel while hundreds of its fighters breached border defences in an unprecedented surprise attack last weekend , rampaging across communities of southern Israel as well as a music festival in a massacre that left more than 1,300 dead .
