<h> Israel-Palestine war : Nigeria insists on two-state solution <p> The Federal Government has called for a two-state solution that will end the Israeli-Palestinian age-long conflict . <p> Nigeria 's Minister of Foreign Affairs , Ambassador Yusuf Tuggar , made this known to journalists after a meeting with the Palestinian Ambassador to Nigeria , Abdallah Shawesh , on Tuesday . <p> Israel has heavily bombarded Gaza since Hamas gunmen stormed across the border on October 7 , killing more 1,400 people , mostly civilians , and kidnapping at least 240 others , including children , according to Israeli officials . <p> The health ministry in Hamas-run Gaza said the strikes have killed 8,525 people , mainly civilians and including more than 3,500 children . <p> Tuggar lamented that innocent civilians were being killed in the conflict , calling on both parties to stop hostilities . <p> He stated " I reiterated to him Palestinian Ambassador to Nigeria Nigeria 's position which is for a two-state solution and we are totally against wanton and indiscriminate violence against civilians . <p> " It does n't matter who is carrying the violence out , Nigeria stands against that and with regards to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of a cessation of hostilities in Gaza .
