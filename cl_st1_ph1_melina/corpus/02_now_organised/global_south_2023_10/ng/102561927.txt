<h> US Sec of State , Blinken , in Israel to show support after Hamas ' surprise attack <p> The US Secretary of State , Antony Blinken , on Thursday , arrived in Israel on a trip that 's meant to show support following weekend 's unprecedented surprise attacks by Hamas . <p> Among other things , Blinken will seek the release of hostages including Americans kidnapped by Hamas fighters . <p> Blinken is also expected to urge restraint and seek safe passage for Gaza 's civilians ahead of a possible Israeli ground offensive . <p> Latest reports say at least 1,200 Israelis died in the attack , and over 1,100 Palestinians have died in air strikes on Gaza .
