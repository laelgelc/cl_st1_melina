<h> Over 263,934 people displaced in Gaza , UN reveals <p> The United Nations ( UN ) , on Tuesday disclosed that over 263,934 people have been forced to run from their homes in Gaza Strip , following the continuous heavy Israeli strikes from the land , air and sea on the Palestinian territory . <p> It noted that the intense figting has left thousands dead on both sides since Hamas launched an unexpected assault on Israel last Saturday , which led Israel to bombing reprisals . <p> " Over 263,934 people in Gaza are believed to have fled their homes , " said UN humanitarian agency OCHA , warning that " this number is expected to rise further , " UN revealed . <p> It also revealed that around 3,000 people had since been displaced due to previous escalations . <p> " More than 1,000 people have been killed in Israel in the worst attack in the country 's 75-year history , while Gaza officials have reported 900 people killed since the air strikes began . <p> " Among the displaced , nearly 175,500 people sought shelter in 88 schools run by the UN agency supporting Palestinian refugees @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fled to 12 government schools , while close to 74,000 were estimated to be staying with relatives and neighbours or seeking shelters in churches and other facilities , " UN added . <p> It further specified that the number of displaced people inside of Gaza " represents the highest number of people displaced since the 50-day escalation of hostilities in 2014 , " it said . <p> " Meeting basic needs is becoming increasingly challenging for those who have not been displaced , " UN warned . <p> Israel had since imposed what it termed a " complete siege " on the already blockaded Gaza Strip . <p> Hence , it had cut off food , water , fuel and electricity which the UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres cautioned that it would worsen the already humanitarian situation .
