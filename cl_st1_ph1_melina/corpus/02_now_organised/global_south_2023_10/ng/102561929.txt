<h> Germany offers Israel support against Hamas <p> German Defense Minister Boris Pistorius , says his country is offering Israel further support against Hamas militants in the wake of attacks carried out in the Gaza strip which has led to casualty figure rising steadily . <p> Pistorius told his Israeli counterpart Yoav Galant on Wednesday after a meeting of the Parliament 's Defense Committee that the German government decided on the support following the unprecedented killing of Israeli civilians by the Islamist militant group Hamas . <p> Pistorius said Germany gave the green light to Israel to use two of its Heron TP combat drones in its attacks on the Gaza Strip , and also approved an Israeli request to use the unmanned aerial vehicles in the fight against the Islamist group . <p> He noted that Germany 's armed forces , the Bundeswehr , currently leases five of the drones from the Israeli arms manufacturer which builds them while two of the aircraft are in Israel , where German soldiers are trained to use them . <p> " Germany is ready to help , including delivering humanitarian aid if Israel requested it , " he said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ need military or technical support .
