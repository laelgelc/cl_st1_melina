<h> Nigeria calls for humanitarian ceasefire in Israel-Hamas war <p> The Federal Government of Nigeria reiterated on Saturday its call for an immediate and sustained humanitarian truce between Israeli forces and Hamas in Gaza . <p> The October 7 attack on Israel by Hamas fighters has killed at least 1,400 people in Israel and prompted retaliation that in turn killed more than 7,000 people in Gaza . <p> The FG , in a statement signed by the Minister of Foreign Affairs , Amb . Yusuf Tuggar urged parties in the conflict to uphold the fundamental values of human rights and international humanitarian law , which place a premium on ensuring civilians ' safety and well-being even in times of conflict . <p> According to the statement , Nigeria also called on the Israeli government " to grant humanitarian access to the millions of people that have been displaced since the crisis broke . <p> The minister called on all parties involved to immediately agree to a ceasefire and engage in diplomatic negotiations to resolve the conflict . <p> " Both parties are urged to exercise utmost restraint and prioritize the safety and well-being of civilians .
