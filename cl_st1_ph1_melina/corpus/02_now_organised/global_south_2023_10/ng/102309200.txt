<h> Reps propose 65 years as retirement age for Police officers <p> The House of Representatives is expected to commence debate on a bill which seeks to increase the age of retirement of Police Officers from 60 years to 65 years . <p> The private member bill sponsored by Hon. Gaza Gbefwi seeks to amend the Police Act , No.2 of 2020 , to provide for the engagement of compulsorily retired Senior Police Officers as Consultants to provide training and guidance to serving officers and for related matters . <p> The affected Senior Police Officers are those compulsorily retired by reason of the appointment by the President of an Inspector General of Police . <h> Related Posts <p> According to the bill seen by Nigerian Tribune , Hon.
