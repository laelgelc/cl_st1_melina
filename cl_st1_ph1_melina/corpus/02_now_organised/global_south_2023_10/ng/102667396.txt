<p> The plan for a brief ceasefire on Monday , October 16 , in southern Gaza , to allow foreigners leave the Palestinian enclave and aid be brought in has failed as the bombardments from Israel intensifies . <p> An effort to get aid into the enclave has failed after relentless bombing since the attack on Israel by Hamas militants on October 7 , that left 1,300 people dead , making that day the worst in the state 's 75-year history . <p> The authorities in Gaza has confirmed that the Israeli airstrikes have resulted in at least 2,750 fatalities , with nearly 10,000 injuries , and 25 percent of deaths being children . Additionally , 1,000 persons were reported missing and thought to be buried under debris . <p> The Egyptian security has said that an agreement had been reached to open the crossing to allow aid into the enclave . <p> However , Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's office said in a statement " There is currently no truce and humanitarian aid in Gaza in exchange for getting foreigners out . " <p> Egypt added that the crossing remained open from the its side in recent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bombardments on the Palestinian side . <p> Meanwhile , the United States has told its citizens in Gaza to get close to the crossing so they could be brought out . The estimated number of dual-citizens , Palestinian-Americans in Gaza are about 500 to 600 . <p> However , President Joe Biden , had emphasized on the urgent need to get aid to Palestinian civilians , on Saturday , October 15 , in the Whitehouse . <p> He urged Israel to follow the rules of war in its response to the Hamas attacks . <p> " The overwhelming majority of Palestinians had nothing to do with Hamas ' appalling attacks and are suffering as a result of them , " he said .
