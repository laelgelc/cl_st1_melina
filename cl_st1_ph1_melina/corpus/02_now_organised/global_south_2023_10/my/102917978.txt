<p> It 's Wednesday once more , which means it is time for the weekly fuel price update in Malaysia , and the ministry of finance has announced the retail prices of fuels for the coming week of October 26 to November 1 . <p> There is once again no change to the price of RON 97 petrol for the coming week , and so pricing for the premium grade of petrol remains unchanged from the RM3.47 per litre rate where it was last week . As before , RON 95 petrol remains at its ceiling price of RM2.05 per litre as set by the Malaysian government in February 2021 . <p> Retail prices for diesel fuels also remain unchanged , with the Euro 5 B10 and B20 blends capped at RM2.15 per litre , and the Euro 5 B7 blend is priced at RM2.35 per litre . <p> These prices will take effect from midnight tonight until Wednesday , November 1 , 2023 , when the next set of fuel price updates will be announced .
