<h> " Pray for them " -- Khairul Aming Donates RM100K from His Sambal Nyet Sales to Victims of War in Palestine <p> Palestine and Israel are at war , again . The loss of lives and the anxiety of living through another day while wondering if you 're going to make it is no doubt torturous . Amidst the war , people from around the world help in any way they can , be it through prayers or donations . <p> As for our very own celebrity chef , Khairul Aming , he decided to donate a chunk of his sales from Sambal Nyet to the Cinta Gaza Malaysia ( CGM ) donation box in an effort to help Palestinians who are affected by the ongoing war . The donation was revealed via his TikTok @khairulaming last night ( October 15 ) , where the humble chef said that he has donated a whopping RM100,000 to help the war victims . <p> In the clip , Khairul also invited all Malaysians to contribute financially to the donation box , which is targeting a collection of RM10 million for the victims in Palestine . He also thanked his loyal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a huge amount to the victims . <p> " This contribution came from all your support , " Khairul told his followers . <p> What if you ca n't contribute financially ? <p> Fret not ! It is , of course , your discretion whether to donate or not but if you are unable to do so , Khairul said simple prayers about the well-being of the victims of war would be a great help too . <p> " At least know about what 's going on and and spread the awareness as much as you can . Hopefully , our brothers and sisters there can fight to live another day . " <p> As of now , the CGM donation box has collected about RM6 million in donations , which will be used to obtain medicines , food , equipment for rescue work , and others . CGM is set up by a non-governmental organisation . Khairul also said that he wished to help CGM in achieving the RM10 million milestone.
