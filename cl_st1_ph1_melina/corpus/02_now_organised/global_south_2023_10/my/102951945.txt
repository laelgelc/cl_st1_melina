<h> Hundreds congregate at Masjid Jamek Sarawak to pray for Palestine <p> Members of the congregation listening to a brief ' tazkirah ' ( religious speech ) on the importance of showing solidarity to Palestine who have suffered by the regime during the special ' Solat Hajat ' prayers after the Isha prayer at Masjid Jamek tonight . -- Photo by Nur Shazreena Ali <p> KUCHING ( Oct 26 ) : Hundreds of Muslims tonight performed congregational prayers at the Masjid Jamek Sarawak , praying for the safety and well-being of the Palestinian people facing aggression by Israel . <p> The members of congregants gathered at the mosque as early as 5.30pm . Deputy Minister of Utilities and Telecommunications Sarawak Datuk Dr Abdul Rahman Junaidi and wife were among the early at the location as early as 6pm with his wife . <p> Led by the Grand Imam of Sarawak Datuk Mustapha Kamal Ahmad Fauzi , the special " Solat Hajat " prayers were held after the Isha prayer . <p> The prayer for peace had touched the hearts of the congregation members . Many were donning the Palestinian flag . <p> Chants of " Allahu Akbar " ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ were also heard throughout the gathering . <p> Those present also listened to a brief " tazkirah " ( religious speech ) on the importance of showing solidarity to Palestine who have suffered by the regime . <p> Sarawak Islamic Council president Datuk Misnu Taha and Sarawak Mosque Welfare Trust Board ( LAKMNS ) chief executive officer Datuk Abang Abdul Wahap Abang Julai were the members of the congregation who joined in the prayers . <p> The special prayers were held in response to the de-escalation of the war after Israel launched airstrikes and other military operations targeting Gaza that have killed more than 1,500 Palestinian civilians , following last weekend 's raids and rocket attacks by Hamas militants into Israel that killed more than 1,300 Israelis .
