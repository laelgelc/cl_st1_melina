<p> KUALA LUMPUR ( Oct 24 ) : Members of the public have begun flocking to the Axiata Arena in Bukit Jalil here since 5pm to attend the Malaysia Stands with Palestine Rally tonight . <p> Like a " white sea " , participants came wearing white clothing to show their solidarity with the Palestinians who suffered from the brutal attacks by the Israeli regime . <p> The rally , expected to be attended by 20,000 people , is also marked by the waving of Jalur Gemilang and the Palestine flag , besides the continuous chanting of " Free Palestine " . <p> The rally officially began with mass Maghrib prayers and solat hajat ( prayer of needs ) to pray for the wellbeing of the people of Palestine , followed by a series of speeches on the history and developments of the Palestinian-Israeli conflict . <p> Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim is also scheduled to deliver a special message at the rally . <p> The recent clashes in Palestine involving Hamas , the ruling authority in Gaza , and the Israeli regime have resulted in the loss of thousands of lives on both sides since October 7. @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @
