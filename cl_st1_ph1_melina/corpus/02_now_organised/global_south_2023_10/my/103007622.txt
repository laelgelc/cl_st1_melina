<h> Wrong to poison innocent minds : SUPP <p> MIRI : Gabungan Parti Sarawak component , SUPP , has voiced displeasure over the directive instructing all schools to observe a solidarity week in support of the Palestinian cause in the wake of the Israel-Gaza war . <p> It called for the rescinding of the directive by the Education Ministry in light of the objections raised by many quarters across the state and country . <p> " We firmly assert that the federal government must refrain from entangling international politics and conflicts within the confines of our educational institutions , " asserted SUPP secretary-general Datuk Sebastian Ting Chiew Yew in a statement . <p> He added that it is imperative for schools to maintain their neutrality , for it is within this neutrality that they can continue to provide an inclusive and harmonious environment for all students to acquire knowledge and foster virtuous values . <p> In Sarawak , the schools teach and inculcate good values to students , he said , adding , the teachers constantly remind them of the state 's multi racial and multi religious composition . <p> " We have indeed 34 ethnic groups , living @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ other and total acceptance for one and other . Race and religion are never an issue in Sarawak . <p> " This is what the school should be , teaching and guiding our students to be positive and peace loving at all times , " he pointed out . <p> In this respect Ting said it is therefore wrong to poison the innocent minds of students , pointing to parents voicing their objections to the directive from the ministry . <p> They vehemently oppose the exposure of their young children to elements of racism , hatred , violence , death , and war , he said . <p> " We call upon the Federal Minister and Deputy Minister of Education to give serious consideration to this widespread rejection by the people and promptly instruct the ministry to rescind its directive . <p> " In the ongoing conflict , there is no absolute right or wrong , and both sides have suffered immeasurable losses , with countless loved ones falling victim to the conflict . <p> " As part of the international community , we have a moral obligation to demand @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to facilitate the delivery of essential provisions such as food , water , and humanitarian aid to the thousands of displaced victims in the region , " he said . <p> In a wider context Ting called for education autonomy to be given to Sarawak , asserting that it will give its best to students . <p> " SUPP would like to reiterate that the sooner the education autonomy is returned or given back to Sarawak the better , " he added .
