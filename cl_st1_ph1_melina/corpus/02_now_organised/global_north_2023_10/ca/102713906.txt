<h> Tiferet Lapidot , 22 , an Israeli volunteer , now considered the sixth Canadian killed by Hamas <p> Tiferet Lapidot had been teaching English to children in Africa before she returned to Israel this fall to be with her family for the High Holidays . She was planning to go to Australia after holidays ended , around the time she was going to turn 23 . But first , she decided to go to the Supernova music festival near the border with Gaza . <p> That 's where she found herself frantically running for her life on Oct. 7 , trying to escape from Hamas terrorists , who committed a murderous massacre at the now-notorious festival . Lapidot managed to make one last phone call home to her mother . But after that , the family heard nothing . They feared she was among the nearly 200 kidnapped Israelis and foreign tourists taken hostage in Gaza . <p> But on Oct. 18 , after an agonizing wait that took over a week , the family learned their daughter was among the 260 partygoers who died that day . <p> On The CJN Daily , host Ellin Bessner speaks @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and with her uncle , Harel Lapidot , both of whom are Canadians living in Israel .
