<h> The Sunday Magazine with Piya Chattopadhyay <p> Understanding the Hamas attack on Israel -- and what may come next <p> Play Segment39:58 <p> This weekend , Hamas launched unprecedented attacks in Israel , followed by Israel declaring war and launching airstrikes in Gaza . The crisis has left hundreds of Israelis and Palestinians dead and many more injured . Piya Chattopadhyay speaks with The Economist Middle East correspondent Gregg Carlstrom about the latest news on this story and the historical context of how we arrived at this situation . Then , Thomas Juneau , an associate professor of Public and International Affairs at the University of Ottawa unpacks the broader geopolitical implications . And Dan Arbell , scholar-in-residence at the Center for Israeli Studies at American University in Washington , D.C. and a former long-time member of the Israeli Foreign Service , talks about the security breach and potential for wider regional conflict .
