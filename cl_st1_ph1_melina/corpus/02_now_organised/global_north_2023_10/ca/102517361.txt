He was 22 . <p> " Ben was one of those kids with a big smile and a wonderful heart . He was a centerpiece that made everyone around him smile , " said Russ Klein , head of school at Vancouver 's King David High School , where Mizrachi graduated in 2018 . <p> Mizrachi had been at the Supernova outdoor music festival in southern Israel , near the border with Gaza , early Saturday morning when Hamas attacked . Before his death , Mizrachi had been working for his uncle 's construction company and had plans to became a real estate developer . <p> Mizrachi had recently moved into an apartment with both of his sisters who were living in Israel as well . <p> " He felt it was his duty to give them a home and a sense of family in Israel .
