<h> Leroy Robert Barkman <p> Mr. Leroy Robert Barkman , age 53 years and member of Sachigo Lake First Nation , passed away peacefully in Thunder Bay Regional Health Sciences Centre on Wednesday , October 4 , 2023 . <p> Robert was born in Red Lake , Ontario on December 8 , 1969 and worked various jobs throughout his life , primarily in the School system and most recently as a Health Director . Robert had a passion for the outdoors and always enjoyed camping , fishing , hunting and trapping with family and friends . He was very proud of the Native traditional and cultural values passed down to him . Robert loved music and was very talented , playing numerous instruments and singing . He also loved spending time with family . Robert 's kindness , smile and big heart will be missed , but the memories shared with him will be cherished forever . <p> He was predeceased by mother Delia ( nee Harper ) Barkman ; grandson Jovaun McKay ; baby McKay , baby Barkman and babies Brown ; nephew Karl Barkman as well as several other relatives and extended family . <p> A Wake @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Friday , October 6 , 2023 from 1:00pm until 3:00pm in the WESTFORT CHAPEL , 420 West Gore Street at James , officiated by Evangelist Tamar Plummer , assisted by Agrippa Barkman . <p> Robert will then be transported home to Sachigo Lake First Nation where a Wake will be held beginning Saturday evening , in the Sachigo Lake Community Centre .
