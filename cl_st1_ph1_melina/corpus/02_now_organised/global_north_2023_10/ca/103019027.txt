<h> Rebel reporters cover Israel-Hamas conflict , Canadian reaction <p> As the ongoing war between Israeli forces and Hamas militants heads into a third week , Australian Rebel reporter Avi Yemini continues to file regular reports from the front-lines along the Israel-Gaza border , including a compilation of interviews with Israel Defence Force soldiers " on the precipice of a ground offensive against Hamas terrorists , " which was posted on Wednesday . <p> His goal , according to the overview that accompanied the video : To " ( uncover ) their humanity in the face of adversity , " he explains . <p> " For these men , the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) is n't a profession -- it 's a collective vow to protect their homeland . They are teachers , doctors , and neighbours who , momentarily , put aside their everyday lives to defend their communities , " who " reveal a shared sentiment : a longing for a peaceful Israel where families can play without fear , a reality that 's been a distant dream for too long , " he notes .
