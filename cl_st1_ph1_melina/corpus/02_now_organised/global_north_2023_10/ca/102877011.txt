<p> Police in Israel on Tuesday arrested actress and social activist Maisa Abd Elhadi for seemingly endorsing the Hamas militant group in a series of social media posts . <p> According to Israel authorities , she has made several statements that could be considered as endorsing the raging violence and mayhem unleashed by the Hamas on October 7 against citizens of the Jewish nation . <p> Available information suggests that Elhadi was arrested from Nazareth on Tuesday morning . <p> The police said that one of her post was an image of the broken border fence between Israel and Gaza with the caption . " Let 's Go Berlin Style " . <p> This was a clear message , a call for barrier breaking conflict and depicting the image to be like fallen Berlin Wall , the police added . <p> Irael is on an all out mission to destroy Hamas after the October 7 incidents of violence leading to the death of around 1400 people .
