Credit : Democracy Now ! <p> The death toll in Gaza continues to climb as Israel inflicts collective punishment on the 2.4 million Palestinians trapped inside the occupied enclave , the world 's largest open-air prison . Palestinians in Gaza have lived under a complete Israeli military blockade since 2007 , following Hamas ' victory in Palestinian legislative elections . Since then , Israel has decided how much food , fuel and water gets into Gaza , and completely controls the movement of Palestinians in or out of Gaza in a system of control that Human Rights Watch and numerous other organizations have called apartheid . <p> Since the brutal Hamas surprise attack on southern Israel on October 7 that killed more than 1,300 Israelis , mostly civilians and including many children , Israel has intensified its siege , unleashing a relentless bombardment on the narrow Gaza Strip .
