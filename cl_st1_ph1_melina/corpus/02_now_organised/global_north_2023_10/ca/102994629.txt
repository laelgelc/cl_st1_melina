<p> MANILA -- The International Labor Organization ( ILO ) has pledged to provide assistance to communities affected by the ongoing conflict in the Middle East . <p> ILO Director-General Gilbert Houngbo said Saturday the group is working with other international organizations to help people affected by the escalation of hostilities between Israel and Hamas in Gaza . <p> " The ILO stands ready to continue working with other UN agencies , the international community , and our partners to assist and support the impacted communities , within the terms of our mandate , " he said in a press release . <p> The ILO is currently providing emergency assistance to Palestinian workers who are facing dire living conditions and is working to put in place a program focusing on social protection , skills , jobs , and small business recovery . <p> Houngbo said they are particularly concerned about the situation of at least 20,000 Gazan daily workers in Israel . <p> At the same time , he expressed strong disapproval of the unprecedented attacks by Hamas against Israel on Oct. 7 , which resulted in more than 1,400 deaths and at least 200 abductions of Israeli and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ thousands of civilians in Gaza , including UN staff , as a consequence of Israeli military action in response to these attacks . <p> " The hostilities have resulted in -- and continue to cause -- not only tragic loss of human life , but also an unprecedented loss of livelihoods , jobs , income , businesses , and civilian infrastructure , with a devastating impact on labor markets in Gaza , the West Bank , and Israel , " the ILO official added . <p> More than 7,000 people have been killed , including children , in the armed conflict between the Palestinian militant group Hamas and Israel military forces since Oct. 7.
