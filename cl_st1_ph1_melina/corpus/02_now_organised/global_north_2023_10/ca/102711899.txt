<p> In conflicts around the world , evacuations have long been used to rescue people from serious harm . During the second world war , for instance , thousands of children across Europe were sent to rural areas or abroad under evacuation schemes initiated by governments and child welfare agencies . <p> The contrast in Gaza today is stark . We are witnessing an urgent , chaotic evacuation ordered by a belligerent party to the conflict , which is fast becoming a humanitarian catastrophe .
