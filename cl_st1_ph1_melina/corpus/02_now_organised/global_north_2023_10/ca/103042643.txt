<p> Since October 7 , after attacks on Israel by the militant group Hamas and retaliatory strikes on Gaza by the Israeli military , U of T students and faculty have organized rallies , vigils , and protests across campus in support of Palestine and Israel . <p> Some protesters organized in support of Israel and spoke about antisemitism and anti-Zionism on campus . Some called on U of T to publicly acknowledge the historical harms and violence perpetrated by the Israeli government against Palestinians . Others called on the university to sever ties with the Royal Bank of Canada ( RBC ) given its financial holdings in technology used by the Israeli military to monitor Palestinians . <p> Current state of the conflict <p> As of October 29 , more than 8,000 Palestinians in Gaza and 1,400 people in Israel have died in the ongoing violence between Hamas -- a militant group which currently controls the Gaza Strip -- and the Israeli military and the ensuing humanitarian crisis in Gaza . <p> On October 7 , Hamas launched surprise attacks against Israel and took more than 200 Israeli civilians and soldiers hostage .
