<p> Games For Gaza , an itch.io fundraiser for Medical Aid For Palestinians ( MAP ) , has raised more than US$230,000 five days into its launch . Organised by Esther Wallace of Oak Grove Games , the bundle includes a generous 256 games , physical games , assets , soundtracks , and books from 140 designers for US$10 . The initiative is due to run till 9 November , with all proceeds going to the UK-based charity . <p> This effort from Wallace and her collaborators comes after a colossal escalation in Israel 's siege on the Gaza Strip . On 7 October , Hamas , the militant group controlling the Gaza Strip , executed a surprise raid across the border and killed over 1,400 people .
