<h> Destruction in Gaza <p> Today is the 19th day of Israeli air strikes across the Gaza Strip , following the surprise attacks by Hamas on October 7 that left 1,400 people dead in Israel and more than 200 taken hostage . Thousands more civilians have been killed and injured in the subsequent hostilities , as a humanitarian crisis grows in Gaza and Israel prepares for a ground invasion .
