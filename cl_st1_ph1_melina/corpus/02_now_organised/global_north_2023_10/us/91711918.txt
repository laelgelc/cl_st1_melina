<p> If you ask most people , including most journalists , what the job of the press is , they will reasonably respond with a number of high-minded cliches : " report the news , " " hold the powerful accountable , " " inform the public , " etc . <p> That 's all well and good . But you almost never hear anyone say the job of the media is to edit the news . Indeed , to the extent you ever hear discussion of the press ' editorial function , it 's almost always negative . Media critics , both amateur and professional , spend much -- perhaps most -- of their time complaining about the way " the media " is more concerned with shaping or pushing narratives , framing debates or keeping ideologically inconvenient facts out of the news . <p> There 's a lot of merit to such criticisms .
