Founders , investors and others from the technology community in Israel have gone ballistic over comments made by the founder and figurehead of WebSummit , Paddy Cosgrave , related to the fighting underway across Israel and Gaza , specifically his criticism of Israel 's retaliatory actions . Reservations for the Kia EV9 are $750 and can be applied to the purchase price , according to the company .
