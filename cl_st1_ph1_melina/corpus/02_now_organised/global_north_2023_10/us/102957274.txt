<p> TEL AVIV -- For most world leaders , the simultaneous crises encircling Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu would feel like the walls closing in . Meanwhile , he has been condemned by some families of the roughly 220 hostages , and even close ally the United States has called for what it calls a pause in the bombing of Gaza amid a mounting civilian death toll . Finally , polling suggests most Israelis think he should resign . <p> " Netanyahu was n't particularly popular before Oct. 7 , and is now widely unpopular , including among his own supporters , " said Michael A.
