<p> BALTIMORE -- The Internal Revenue Service is providing tax relief to people and businesses affected by the terror attacks in Israel . <p> Business owners or people who can not meet their tax-filing or tax-payment obligations due to the attacks will be allowed to postpone the due dates associated with the filings and payments , according to the IRS . <p> Taxpayers impacted by the attacks will be given until Oct. 7 , 2024 , to file tax returns , make tax payments , and perform other time-sensitive acts . <p> " Any taxpayer acts that are due to be performed on or after October 7 , 2023 , and before October 7 , 2024 , are postponed until October 7 , 2024 , " the IRS said in a notice posted on its website . <p> The following types of taxpayers are eligible for the relief provided by the IRS notice : <p> Any individual whose principal residence , and any business entity or sole proprietor whose principal place of business , is located in the State of Israel , the West Bank or Gaza ( covered area ) ; <p> Any individual affiliated with a recognized @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ covered area , such as a relief worker ; <p> Any individual , business entity or sole proprietor , or estate or trust whose tax return preparer or records necessary to meet a deadline for postponed acts are located in the covered area ; <p> Any spouse of an affected taxpayer , solely with regard to a joint return of two married individuals ; and <p> Any individual visiting the covered area who was killed , injured , or taken hostage as a result of the October 7 , 2023 , terrorist attacks
