<p> Dramatic video captured the moment an Israeli officer rode a motorcycle one-handed while using the other hand to shoot at a vehicle being driven by Hamas thugs in a James Bond-esque chase near Gaza . <p> The newly released footage shows the cycle-riding officer and an Israeli cop car chasing the van carrying at least two armed Hamas members outside Netivot on Saturday , not long after the terror group launched the worst attack on Israel in 50 years . <p> As the motorcycle cop shoots at the vehicle , he and the cop car continue to follow the van , which appears to have its trunk open before it suddenly stops . <p> The officer on the motorcycle then revs his engine , zooming up closer to the van and shooting several times through the open trunk door . <p> The Israeli officers also shout commands while closing in on the vehicle , which has all of its windows shattered . <p> Israel Police said the two Hamas gunmen were " neutralized , " without elaborating . The inside of the vehicle is not visible in the footage.
