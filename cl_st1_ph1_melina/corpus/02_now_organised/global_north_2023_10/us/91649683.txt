<h> County to award $3M contract for new jail feasibility study <p> The Niagara County Sheriff 's Office is looking to hire LaBella PC to do a feasibility study on replacing the older half of the county jail . The study cost is $3 million . The county Legislature is set to approve a contract during its monthly business meeting today . <p> Such a study would look at replacing the portion of the jail built in 1961 , which has a design of linear blocks for cells and a 209-inmate capacity . The replacement would better match the other half of the jail , which was opened in 1996 and utilizes a pod-style system with a capacity of 236 . <p> Sheriff Michael Filicetti said the older jail 's design is manpower-intensive and been in use 24/7 since its completion in 1961 .
