" Civilians were shot and most were massacred in cold blood walking on the streets . This is something that , I mean , is truly unprecedented , " he told Fox News ' Eric Shawn . <p> Israeli officials estimate that at least 250 people have died in the conflict so far . <p> " This is our 9/11 , " he added .
