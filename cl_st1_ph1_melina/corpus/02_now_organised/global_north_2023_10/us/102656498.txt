<p> Investors flee to safe-haven assets during times of geopolitical uncertainty . Gold is a rare bright spot in such turbulent periods . <p> Gold XAUUSD added a solid 3.5% on Friday but slipped from its peaks in early-Monday deals . The Israel-Hamas conflict heated last week as Israeli troops stormed certain areas in Gaza . The escalating offensive on the city prompted a market rotation from risk assets to haven trades . <p> Against that backdrop , gold turned up as a rare bright spot , moving to a Friday close of $1,933 , up from a session low of $1,868 per ounce . Earlier today , the precious metal tumbled down to $1,910 as market participants kept a close eye on the ongoing clashes in the Middle East . <p> Geopolitical fluctuations are a strong reason for investors to seek the safe-haven allure of gold . Despite its zero yields , the yellow metal is a preferred asset for money managers who want to wait out scary periods in global relations . Gold 's closest peer , silver , also got a boost on Friday , moving 4.5% to $22.80 per ounce .
