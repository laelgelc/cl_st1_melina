<h> The Utensil Mistake You Must Avoid When Grilling Burgers <p> When it comes to making the best burgers , there are a few tricks of the trade . Ground beef can be temperamental ; its fat content and the cooking style , among other things , can impact its flavor . But whether you 're cooking burgers over a charcoal grill or right on the stove top , there 's one cardinal rule : Avoid letting the juices run . That means you should never poke those burgers with a fork , even if you think it will cook them better -- especially if you 're cooking them right on the grill grates . <p> When the juices run from a burger , it allows the burger to dry up . And once they fall through the grill grates , they 're gone forever . Your best bet is to avoid poking them at all and just cook them on lower heat so the inside has time to cook before the outside burns . This method results in a perfectly-cooked patty without the loss of any juices . <p> Once the patties are done , make sure to let the meat rest . The juices run to the center as burgers cook . Giving the meat that extra time to rest lets those juices redistribute throughout the burger , ensuring a mouthwatering bite from start to finish .
