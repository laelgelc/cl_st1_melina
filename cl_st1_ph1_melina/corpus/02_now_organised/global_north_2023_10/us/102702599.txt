<p> JERUSALEM -- The head of Israel 's Shin Bet security service has taken responsibility for the bloody Oct. 7 Hamas attack that killed over 1,400 Israelis . <p> In a message sent to Shin Bet workers and their families over the weekend , Ronen Bar wrote that " despite a number of actions we took , unfortunately , on Saturday we were unable to create enough early warning to prevent the attack . " <p> The letter was obtained by The Associated Press on Monday . <p> The Shin Bet leads Israel 's efforts to track and monitor Palestinian militants . The Israeli news site Ynet has reported that on the eve of the attack , Bar was summoned to the office because of abnormal activity detected in Gaza . But officials believed that only a limited attack would take place , according to the report .
