<h> For Gazans , There Are No Safe Havens <p> Since the Israeli military issued an order last week for the 1.1 million Palestinians living in northern Gaza to flee south , in an apparent prelude to its anticipated ground invasion , a mass exodus has ensued . While those with enough fuel have made the journey by car or truck , others have hitched rides on trailers and donkey carts . Some have even resorted to making the perilous journey on foot . <p> " We have seen a huge shift of people going south , which we 're very happy with , " Israel Defense Forces spokesperson Peter Lerner tells TIME , noting that hundreds of thousands of Palestinians living in the north have heeded their warnings despite calls by Hamas for residents to " remain steadfast in your homes . " Lerner also says that the IDF has seen evidence of Hamas " establishing checkpoints to physically prevent people from leaving .
