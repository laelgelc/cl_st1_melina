Both steaks are cut from the short loin of cattle and have a T-shaped bone . Two cuts of meat flank the bone : a strip steak cut and a tenderloin filet . <p> Porterhouse steaks have larger filet sections than T-bone steaks and are therefore pricier . Porterhouse steaks are also cut thicker , at least 1 1/4 inches or more . T-bones are slightly less expensive and usually top out at 1 inch thick , but they are no less indulgent . <p> Whichever steak you choose , you are guaranteed a meaty feast , and there 's no better way to cook them than on a hot grill . <p> In this recipe , a simple balsamic and red wine reduction creates a light sauce .
