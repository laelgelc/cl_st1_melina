<p> House Republicans are moving to introduce legislation to block the United States from accepting any new Palestinian refugees who may emerge from the current crisis in Gaza . <p> Experts told The Post that more than a million such refugees could be created as Israel continues its bombardment of the Hamas-controlled enclave , one of those most densely populated regions in the world . <p> Reps . Tom Tiffany ( R-WI ) and Andy Ogles ( R-TN ) told Breitbart News they planned to imminently introduce the " Guaranteeing Aggressors Zero Admission Act " or the GAZA Act in order to prevent the Biden administration from granting visas to any holder of a Palestinian passport . <p> The bill would also prevent the Department of Homeland Security from allowing Palestinians into the United States through the agency 's parole program . <p> " We ca n't let President Biden abuse our parole and visa rules to bring unvetted Palestinians into American communities the way he did with thousands of unvetted Afghans , " Tiffany said in a posting to X Saturday .
