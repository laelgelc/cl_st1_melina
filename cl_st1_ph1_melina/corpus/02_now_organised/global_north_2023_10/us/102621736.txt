<h> Newsletters <p> An Associated Press reporter in Gaza was removed from his duties after a series of inflammatory social media posts regarding Israel came to light . <p> Issam Adwan has posted several wires from Gaza since the war began last Saturday , focusing on the conditions of Palestinians under bombardment from the Israeli Air Force .
