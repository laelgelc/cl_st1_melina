<p> On April 22 , 1979 , four Palestinian terrorists set out from southern Lebanon on a rubber dinghy and landed on the Israeli coast , near the northern town of Nahariya . Meanwhile Danny 's wife , Smadar , hid in the attic with her 2-year-old daughter , Yael . <p> The terrorists took their two hostages to the beach , where they shot Danny and smashed Einat 's skull against a rock .
