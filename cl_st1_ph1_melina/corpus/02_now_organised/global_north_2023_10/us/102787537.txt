<h> How big are Israel and Gaza ? Smaller than you might think <p> If you have n't visited Israel or the Gaza Strip or spent much time thinking about their geography , it can be hard to understand how compact they are and how violence in one spot can feel immediate and personal throughout the area . <p> Maps of the region often look like this : Israel fills the frame . To people who are used to looking at maps of the United States , there seems to be plenty of space between major cities . <p> But America is vast . A road trip across the continental U.S. takes days . For a solo driver who needs to rest , it can last a week . Driving across the broadest part of Israel , meanwhile , from its northernmost point to its southernmost tip , takes less than six hours .
