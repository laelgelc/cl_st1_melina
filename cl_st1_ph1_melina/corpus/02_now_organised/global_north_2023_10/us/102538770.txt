The goal is to have a shrine dedicated to basketball -- at all levels -- in Illinois . <p> Those visiting the Wintrust Sports Complex will already find exhibits featuring Hebron 's 1952 state championship team , the impact of Title IX , legendary women 's coach Dorothy Gaters and Loyola 's historic Game of Change that knocked down racial barriers during the 1962-63 season .
