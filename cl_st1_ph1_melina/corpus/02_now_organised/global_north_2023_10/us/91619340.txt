<p> " Forgive me , a slightly un-elegant position , " Ward said as she reported live from the side of the ditch . " We have just had a massive barrage of rockets coming in here not too far from us so we have had to take shelter here by the roadside . " <p> " They went down this strip just spraying lead wherever they went , " Ward explained to viewers . The team then stood up from the ditch and showed the entire scene , which included a destroyed intersection and smoke coming from Gaza . <p> Ward is no stranger to war zones . In February 2022 , she reported live from Ukraine as Russia invaded , first in Kharkiv and later in Kyiv . She has also reported from Taliban-controlled areas of Afghanistan . <p> About 1,600 people have been killed in the latest round of conflict , health officials on both sides said Monday . Israel reported 900 deaths , while Palestinian authorities said 680 people had been killed in Gaza . Thousands more were injured in both Israel and Gaza .
