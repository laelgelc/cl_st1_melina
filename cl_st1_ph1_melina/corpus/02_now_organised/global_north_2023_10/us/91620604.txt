It was below 4.3% toward the end of last week . <p> Oil prices have retreated slightly after supply concerns caused a sharp jump Monday . The fighting in Israel and Palestine sparked concern of possible tougher sanctions against Iran , which could reduce supply . <p> Natural gas prices are continuing to move higher though , adding to strong price gains seen Monday over supply worries .
