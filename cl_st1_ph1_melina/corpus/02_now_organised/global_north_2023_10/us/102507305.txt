<p> " Critics Welcome " is the new tagline for the 59th Chicago International Film Festival . Everybody 's a critic , goes the saying , so that means everyone is welcome to appreciate and critique 104 features and 57 shorts over the 12-day run of the cinema extravaganza . <p> Also new is festival venue Newcity 14 , formerly the ArcLight Cinemas , in Lincoln Park . And for the first time , the Gene Siskel Film Center is devoting its two screens exclusively to festival screenings . The Music Box Theatre once again hosts much fest fare , including opener " We Grown Now " at 7 p.m . Wednesday . <p> Reprising last year 's free street fair celebration , the festival takes over Southport Avenue between Grace and Waveland on opening night from 5 to 10 p.m . <p> The non-profit Cinema/Chicago , founded by Michael Kutza , presents the festival , continuing through Oct. 22 . <p> Here are 10 recommended films picked from what I could preview by press time . <h> Dramas <p> " Goodbye Julia " ( Sudan ) Mohamed Kordofani portrays a Christian maid and her Muslim employer in Khartoum @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with empathy .
