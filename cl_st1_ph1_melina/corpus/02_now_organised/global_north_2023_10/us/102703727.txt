<h> Visualizing the size of Gaza City compared with U.S. cities <p> The Gaza Strip has almost exactly the same land area as Las Vegas but more than three times the population . Its largest city , Gaza City , is more tightly packed than New York City , with more than 650,000 people living within its 18 square miles . <p> Israel began a barrage of airstrikes after an Oct. 7 surprise attack by Hamas , the militant group that controls the Gaza Strip . About 1,300 Israelis have been killed and more than 4,100 injured , according to official Israeli sources cited in the U.N . update . A Hamas official claimed the group also took more than 200 hostages . The same update cites more than 2,800 Palestinian deaths and 12,000 injuries in Gaza and the West Bank , according to the Health Ministry in Gaza .
