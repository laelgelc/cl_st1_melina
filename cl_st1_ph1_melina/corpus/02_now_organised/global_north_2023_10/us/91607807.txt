<p> On the morning of Saturday , October 7 , the Palestinian group Hamas carried out a surprise attack on Israel on an unprecedented scale : firing thousands of rockets , infiltrating militants into Israeli territory , and taking an unknown number of hostages . At least 100 Israelis have died , and at least 1,400 have been wounded ; Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu declared that his country was " at war . " As Israeli forces responded , around 200 Palestinians were killed and around 1,600 wounded . <p> For insight into what this means for Israel , the Palestinians , and the region , Foreign Affairs turned to Martin Indyk , the Lowy Distinguished Fellow in U.S.-Middle East Diplomacy at the Council on Foreign Relations .
