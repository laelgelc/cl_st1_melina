<h> Videos show captives in Gaza and Israel <p> The Washington Post has independently verified three videos appearing to show captives held in Gaza and surrounding areas in Israel . Both Hamas and the Israeli military have confirmed that Hamas militants are holding Israeli military personnel and civilians captive , but the total number is unknown . <p> The Post verified videos from three separate locations , spanning several miles -- from Shejaiyia in Gaza to Be'eri , a Kibbutz in southern Israel . <p> Two of the videos alleging to show captured Israeli citizens appear to have been filmed 8 a.m. local time shortly after the attack , which began around 6:30 a.m . Saturday . They show graphic content . <p> In one video , from Be'eri , first geolocated on the platform X , armed men in military garb are seen dragging at least five people away from a residential area near the border with Gaza . One of the militants films the scene , and his shadow shows he is holding a walkie-talkie . Two of the captured appear older and thinly dressed . It is unclear whether the men are alive ; they lie in the bed of the trucks , shirtless and immobile . Bystanders film , cheer and run alongside the vehicles .
