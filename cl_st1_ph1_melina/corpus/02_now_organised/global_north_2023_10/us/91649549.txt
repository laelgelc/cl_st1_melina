People with Tetralogy of Fallot have four specific defects , including a boot-shaped heart and an overriding aorta . The condition is usually diagnosed soon after birth . Babies may have tet spells , also known as tetralogy spells , which are episodes of bluish skin . Most babies with tetralogy of Fallot need tetralogy of Fallot surgery to repair the heart before they 're about six months old .
