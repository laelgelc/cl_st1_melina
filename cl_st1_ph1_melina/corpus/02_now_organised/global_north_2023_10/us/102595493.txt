<p> The terror attack on Israel by Hamas has been a divisive -- if clarifying -- moment for the left . The test that it presented was simple : Can you condemn the slaughter of civilians , in massacres that now appear to have been calculatedly sadistic and outrageous , without equivocation or whataboutism ? Can you lay down , for a moment , your legitimate criticisms of Benjamin Netanyahu 's government , West Bank settlements , and the conditions in Gaza , and express horror at the mass murder of civilians ? <p> In corners of academia and social-justice activism where the identity of the oppressor and the oppressed are never in doubt , many people failed that test .
