From the very beginning of the Jewish-Arab conflict , the only viable long-term solution has been to divide the land between the Jordan River and the Mediterranean Sea by creating two countries for two people . <p> Both the Arabs and Jews have had strong self-conceptions of nationhood tied to the same land -- self-conceptions that did not reckon for the other . But for much of this 100-year war , Jews accepted the inevitability of partition while the Arabs rejected it . For the last few decades , however , the situation seems to have been reversed .
