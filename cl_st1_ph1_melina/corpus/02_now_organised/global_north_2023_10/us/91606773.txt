Israel was in shock Saturday , with the unprecedented scenes of violence and chaos unfolding across the country 's south seared into people 's minds . Even the steely-nerved residents of communities near the Gaza Strip who have grown used to the wail of air-raid sirens described Saturday 's ground assault -- with fighters entering their communities in pick-up trucks , on boats and by hang-gliders -- as a nightmare come true .
