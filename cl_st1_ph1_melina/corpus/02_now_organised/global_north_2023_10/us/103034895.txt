Israel also has a responsibility to distinguish between terrorists and ordinary civilians , " he told ABC 's Martha Raddatz . <p> He added , " The United States has a responsibility to do everything we can to make sure that life-saving humanitarian assistance gets to those people who have been affected by this conflict . And we are working with that every day .
