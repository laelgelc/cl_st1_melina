<p> The American military thwarted two separate drone strikes in Syria , hours after U.S. forces shot down three unmanned aerial systems targeting troops in Iraq early Wednesday , according to a U.S. official and another person familiar with the incidents . <p> U.S. forces intercepted a number of UAVs headed toward their positions at two separate bases : Al Tanf in the south and Conoco in the northern Deir al-Zor region , according to the U.S. official , who like others interviewed for this story was granted anonymity to speak ahead of a public announcement . The official declined to say how many drones were involved . <p> The attempted attack was part of a spate of incidents over the last 48 hours in which drones have targeted U.S. forces in Iraq and Syria .
