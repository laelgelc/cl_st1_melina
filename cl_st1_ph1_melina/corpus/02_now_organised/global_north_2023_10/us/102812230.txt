<h> In photos : Humanitarian aid arrives in Gaza as Rafah crossing reopens <p> By Washington Post Staff Oct 21 , 2023 <p> For the first time since the start of the war between Israel and Hamas , a convoy carrying humanitarian aid entered the Gaza Strip through the Rafah crossing between Gaza and Egypt after a days-long wait at the border . <p> Loay Ayyoub for The Washington Post <p> The crossing had been closed since Oct. 10 due to deadly Israeli strikes nearby , cutting off the sole path for vital supplies to reach the strip as Palestinians started fleeing northern Gaza , threatened by a possible Israeli ground incursion . Up to 20 trucks carrying food and medical supplies were allowed to enter Gaza under a deal negotiated by President Biden . <p> Loay Ayyoub for The Washington Post <p> Humanitarian aid arrives in the Gaza Strip through the Rafah land crossing .
