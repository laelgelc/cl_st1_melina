<p> Russian President Vladimir Putin attended a Friday summit of a Moscow-dominated alliance of several ex-Soviet nations and commended the growing cooperation between them despite Western sanctions against Russia . <p> Putin met in Kyrgyzstan 's capital , Bishkek , with the leaders of most fellow members of the Commonwealth of Independent States . He told reporters that economic exchanges between Russia and the alliance 's other nations have increased despite Western threats to punish them for maintaining ties with Moscow . <p> He specifically hailed a new deal on joint air defenses with Kyrgyzstan , saying it would help further bolster military ties . Kyrgyzstan , a member of a Moscow-led security pact , already hosts a Russian air base . <p> At the same time , Putin sought to downplay Armenian Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan 's failure to attend the summit in Bishkek .
