Garnish with pumpkin seeds for added texture and flavor . <p> stock.adobe.com <h> Pumpkin curry soup <p> Makes 6 servings <p> Preparation time : 10 minutes <p> Cooking time : about 15 minutes <p> 2 tablespoons pumpkin seeds <p> 2 tablespoons butter <p> 3 tablespoons flour <p> 2 tablespoons curry powder <p> 4 cups unsalted vegetable broth <p> 1 ( 15-ounce ) can pumpkin ( not pumpkin pie filling ) <p> 1 1/2 cups half-and-half <p> 2 tablespoons soy sauce <p> 1 tablespoon sugar <p> Coarse salt and pepper to taste <p> Heat oven to 375 degrees . Arrange pumpkin seeds in a single layer on rimmed baking pan . Toast in oven 10 minutes or until seeds begin to brown . Melt butter in a large pot on medium . Stir in flour and curry powder until smooth and mixture begins to boil . Gradually whisk in broth ; cook until thickened . Stir in pumpkin and half-and-half . Season with soy sauce , sugar , salt and pepper to taste . Bring just to a boil , then keep warm on low .
