<p> For the first time since 2006 , the Lebanese are again facing the prospect of a devastating war with Israel , on the back of the current conflict in Gaza . Much of the population does not want , and knows it can not afford , such a war . Lebanon is still in the throes of an economic collapse that began in 2019 . Yet Hezbollah , which dominates Lebanon 's political scene , seems moved less by what its countrymen want than by the strategic priorities of its sponsor , Iran . <p> The Iranians have worked painstakingly in the past decade to build up a redoubtable deterrence capability on Israel 's borders with Lebanon , Syria , and Gaza .
