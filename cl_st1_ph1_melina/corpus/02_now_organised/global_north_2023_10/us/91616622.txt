Kay Ivey has ordered state agencies to lower flags to half-staff this week to remember the people killed in Israel in the attacks by Hamas . <p> " The repeated acts of terrorism and violation of Israel 's sovereignty must be stopped at all costs , " Ivey said in the directive . " Those responsible for the deaths of hundreds of Israeli citizens are not only the enemies of our Jewish brothers and sisters , but the United States and her people , too . For more than 80 years , Alabama has proudly stood in solidarity with the State of Israel , and we will continue to do so through these challenging times . That includes 11 U.S. citizens . <p> " Alabama and Israel share a commitment to deeply-held common values -- one of which is the belief in the power of prayer , " Ivey said .
