<p> We remember the Holocaust and the need for the survivors to have a country of their own , a place where they could hold their heads up and walk down the streets in safety . What decent person could want to withhold that from them ? But then there were the Palestinians living in the spaces that Israel would require to form a modern state . They might reasonably have asked what wrongs they had committed against the Jews to be driven off their own property , to be made refugees ? <p> But these areas are under double occupation because Hamas and other terrorist organizations live there too . Historically , these groups have served as proxies for Syria and particularly for Iran . At this writing , a direct link to Iran has not been established for the latest series of attacks , kidnappings , and outright murders of civilians in Israel . All we can do at this moment is look at the horrific scenes of brutality and ask ourselves who might be benefiting from all of this . <p> First of all , it might be useful to understand the Middle East as a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Iran . Both are powerful oil-rich states ; but for reasons of religion and history , they are mortal enemies . <p> Since Russia invaded Ukraine , Putin and Iran have been cozying up . Iran is supplying Russia with weapons , particularly drones . It seems reasonable to suggest that with the U.S. Congress in historic disarray , with American aid to Ukraine on pause in the House , the timing of the attack on Israel certainly benefits the Russians . Suddenly the United States has its attention turned away from the plight of the Ukrainians . American munitions , already in reduced supply , may be diverted to Israel while Russia continues to pound the Ukrainians day and night . Score more points for Iran and for Russia . <p> Mind you , we must remember that just because someone benefits from an event is not sufficient proof that they caused the event to happen . But it does smell bad enough to make us pay attention . <p> Israel , like Ukraine , has become the victim of deliberately cruel aggression . Israel 's ultimate military victory will be scant comfort for thousands of Israelis who have lost people they loved -- knowing they died in terror and agony . <p> Years ago , I got to know a fellow who had survived Auschwitz as a child . No one from his extended family was left . Here he was , swept along with a tide of emaciated refugees . He ended up in Israel at the time of its formation , growing up on a collective farm , a kibbutz . <p> There were thousands of parents without children , children without parents , traumatized by what had happened to all of them . There were no unwanted pregnancies . " <p> Having noticed that he was not living in Israel still , I asked him what he thought of the place today . <p> " In the beginning , " he said , " we were an idea . Now we are just another nation in the world , doing all the things the other nations do . It broke my heart and I had to leave there .
