<p> Nearly all Americans surveyed in a new CNN poll feel some level of sympathy for the Israeli people after the Oct. 7 surprise attack by Hamas , and a majority say Israel 's military response is justified . <p> Ahead of Israel 's expected ground invasion of Gaza , most respondents said the Israeli government 's military response to the Hamas attacks is fully justified ( 50% ) or partially justified ( 20% ) . A total of 21% said they were n't sure , while 8% said it is not justified at all . <p> Of those who responded to the poll , which was conducted Oct. 12-13 by text message , 71% said they feel a lot of sympathy for the Israeli people in their current situation , and 25% said they feel some sympathy . <p> The majority of people who responded also said they feel a lot or some sympathy for the Palestinian people , but the depth of sympathy was less than it was for the Israelis . A total of 41% said they felt a lot of sympathy , with an additional 46% saying they feel some sympathy for Palestinians . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and only surveyed adults with mobile phones .
