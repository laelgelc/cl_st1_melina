<p> Defense Secretary Lloyd Austin is bolstering U.S. forces in the Middle East in an effort to deter others from escalating the fighting between Israel and Hamas . <p> On Saturday , Austin ordered an aircraft carrier to move to the Central Command area of responsibility in the Middle East in an effort to " bolster regional deterrence efforts , " according to a press release . <p> The USS Dwight D. Eisenhower , along with its associated warships and fighter jets , was originally set to join the USS Gerald R. Ford , which is stationed in the eastern Mediterranean Sea . Eisenhower has now been redirected to Central Command , which covers the Persian Gulf , the Red Sea and other Middle Eastern waters . The Pentagon is also fortifying its missile defense systems in the region and putting troops on standby . <p> " We 've seen rocket and UAV attacks against bases housing our troops in Iraq and Syria .
