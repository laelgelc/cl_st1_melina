This transition occurred during the second intifada , which raged from 2000 to 2005 and then slowly petered out . Arafat saw Hamas as a weapon to wield against Israel , giving the organization space to operate when he wanted to pressure the country but reining it in when he wanted to calm the situation . He believed he could crush it at will . <p> But Hamas soon became too strong to control . The militant group gained considerable credibility for its repeated attacks on Israel during the second intifada , as the PA succumbed to corruption and infighting . Abbas lacked Arafat 's charisma and revolutionary legitimacy , and his rise further weakened the PA 's popularity . Because of the bitterness , when Israel withdrew from Gaza in 2005 , it did so without consulting Abbas or the other Palestinian leaders willing to make peace , a deliberate gesture of disrespect . As a result , Hamas was able to claim credit , pointing out that the threat of unending violence , not negotiations , was what led Israel to remove its troops .
