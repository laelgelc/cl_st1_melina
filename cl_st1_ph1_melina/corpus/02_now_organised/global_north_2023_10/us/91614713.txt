For us as American Jews , the mislabeling and omission of information about the war between Israel and Gaza has been startling . <p> We do not represent multiple perspectives , but we do feel that it is important to provide more context about the historic events taking place in the Middle East . Here are seven facts that will help people understand the conflict with greater nuance , from an Israeli standpoint : <p> First , this is a war . Multiple news sources are using a narrative of Hamas " militants , " or even as " resistance fighters , " attacking Israelis . We suggest calling it the " Simchat Torah War , " for the celebratory holiday on which it was launched Saturday . <h> Timing of Hamas attack on Israel was heinous <p> Second , the timing . There is something uniquely heinous about attacking Israelis on Shabbat , at the end of Sukkot , the weeklong fall harvest festival for Jews , and immediately preceding Simchat Torah . It is intended to undermine and even desecrate Jewish practices and make them untenable for the 80% of Israelis who identify as Jewish . <p> Third , the anniversary . The war against Israeli civilians took place almost 50 years to the day from when Egypt and Syria launched a surprise attack against Israel on Yom Kippur -- and nearly routed the Israeli army . While Israel ultimately turned the war around , it did so only after extensive casualties , and memories of its vulnerability run deep . <p> Fourth , rocket barrages against civilians are horrific , but kidnappings are unendurable . Jews have long been subject to kidnappings -- notably during the Crusades and pogroms . We pray daily for the freeing of captives and are supposed to stop at nothing to liberate our kin . Kidnapping Israeli civilians -- including children , parents and the elderly -- is a uniquely brutal war crime . <p> Fifth , Israel unilaterally withdrew from Gaza in 2005 . That Israel has been subject to rocket attacks and other acts of war from Gaza and Hamas since 2007 is deeply ingrained in the memories of Israelis . The lesson that many Israelis have gleaned is that when Israel withdraws from territory , it faces war , not peace . This is an unfortunate lesson , particularly given our hopes of a two-state solution that would have included Israeli withdrawal from the West Bank . <p> Sixth , Hamas sought out this war because it had been sidelined .
