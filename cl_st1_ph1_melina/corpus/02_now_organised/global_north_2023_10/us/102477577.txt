My mind writhed thinking of the reports of unbearable Israeli casualties , the images of the captured and the dead , and the prospect of wider war . <p> Alongside those waking nightmares was an agonizing irony . None , however , could compare to the scope , the destructiveness , and the agony of the Yom Kippur War .
