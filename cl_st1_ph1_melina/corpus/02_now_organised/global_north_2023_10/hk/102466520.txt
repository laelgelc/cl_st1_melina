<h> Several airlines suspend flights after attack on Israel <p> Major international air carriers have suspended or reined in flight services to or from Tel Aviv after a surprise attack by Hamas militants on Israel over the weekend , saying they were waiting for safety conditions to improve . <p> Israel 's national carrier El Al was the exception , adding more flights to bring reservists back from around the world to assist in the country 's biggest mobilization in history . <p> Fighters from the Islamist group killed 700 Israelis and abducted dozens in Saturday 's attacks , the deadliest such incursion in decades , prompting Israel to retaliate by pounding the Palestinian enclave of Gaza . <p> Israel 's tourism sector is set to take a major hit as flight cancellations pile up .
