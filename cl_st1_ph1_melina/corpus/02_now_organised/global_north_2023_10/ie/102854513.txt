<p> A SOURCE inside the chronically overcrowded University Hospital Limerick today ( Monday ) summed up the chaotic scenes that have become commonplace inside Limerick 's embattled hospital : " There is no dignity here , that was lost a long time ago . " <p> Videos shared with this journalist showed patients on trolleys crammed up against one another on corridors outside entrance zones to the ED and inside the ED at UHL today . <p> The footage also shows overworked and exhausted doctors , nurses , and relatives attempting to keep patients calm in the midst of the chaotic scenes around them . <p> This comes as the overstretched Dooradoyle hospital had 130 patients waiting on trolleys this morning , according to the Irish Nurses and Midwives Organisation , a new record at UHL . <p> " It looks like a war zone at the moment , you would be forgiven for thinking that the video is of a hospital in Gaza , there are so many patients lying on trolleys in the main corridor , " said a hospital source . <p> " There 's no place for the patients to go , no more rooms @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ up on top of one another in the corridors . <p> " This is the worst it has been , ever , and we keep saying that , year on year , but it keeps getting worse , " the source added . <p> " There a lot of problems with trolleys in corridors , it slows down the system , patients are uncomfortable , and staff bringing in new patients have to move trolleys out of the way and squeeze their way through to get to the ED . <p> " There is no dignity here , that was lost a long time ago , " they said . <p> Sinn Fein TD Maurice Quinlivan said the record-breaking trolley numbers in Limerick called for the Minister for Health , Stephen Donnelly , to resign over the ongoing trolley crisis . <p> Deputy Quinlivan , who has been a consistently loud voice on patient overcrowding at UHL , said it was " a damning indictment of the Government 's health policies " . <p> " For perspective , that 's almost the same number of people packed into UHL on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Ennis hospitals combined .
