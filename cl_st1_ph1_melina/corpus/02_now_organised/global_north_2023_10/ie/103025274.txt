<h> The Indo Daily : Wix , Web Summit and why work and politics do n't mix <p> When a social media post ends up being discussed at the highest levels of government - it 's a reminder that nothing online is private . That 's what happened to Dublin woman Courtney Carey , who made national news this week when it was reported that she had lost her job at Wix , an Israeli tech company , after criticising Israel on social media . <p> Her story echoed the recent experience of Paddy Cosgrave who resigned from his role as CEO of the Web Summit following backlash about comments he had made regarding the conflict in Gaza . <p> Ellen Coyne is joined by Adrian Weckler , Technology Editor with the Irish Independent , and Julie Galbraith , Employment Law partner at Eversheds Sutherland to ask ; where is the line between the private and professional on social media ? How can employers and employees protect themselves ? And can expressing political sympathies cost you your job ?
