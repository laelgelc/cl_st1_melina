<h> Senan Molony : As Middle East conflict deepens , it could unleash horrors across the world <p> People mourn the children killed in Gaza during vigil in central London <p> Senan Molony <p> Yesterday at 04:30 <p> The wisps of war are everywhere as the world faces into the most perilous period since the beginning of the 21st century . The Israel-Gaza conflict is about to deepen into a trough of horrors that will destabilise the entire region and make more likely a spate of rage killings on the streets of Europe . <p> EU leaders met this week in Brussels , where two Swedish football fans were randomly murdered earlier this month and a third left critically injured . None of those fans will have imagined that festering resentment over the burning of the Koran in Stockholm could have led to their deaths .
