<h> Eilis O'Hanlon : Facts matter more than ever in the fog of war <p> Paddy Cosgrave apologised for comments he made about the Israel-Hamas conflict . Photo : Getty <p> Eilis O'Hanlon <p> Sun 22 Oct 2023 at 03:30 <p> On being told that millionaire Irish tech guru Paddy Cosgrave has condemned Israel for committing war crimes in Gaza , people could be forgiven for wondering : Paddy who ? Many far-better known public figures have , after all , similarly decried the Israel Defence Forces ( IDF ) for arguably breaking international law in their response to the deadly assault on the country 's citizens two weeks ago by the Palestinian militant group Hamas -- our own President included . <p> A few have gone much further . At least Cosgrave " unequivocally " accepts Israel 's right to exist . That 's more than some elected representatives have done .
