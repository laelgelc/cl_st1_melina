Those on the right of the party tend to be members of Labour Friends of Israel , those on the left are more likely to be supportive of the Palestinian Solidarity Campaign . <p> There is little common ground between these two groupings at the best of times but there is even less consensus when the debate is taking place against the horrors currently being witness in Israel and Gaza . <p> The Labour leadership is increasingly concerned that the show of party unity on display in Liverpool is starting to fray as the arguments over the Israeli-Hamas conflict become more intense .
