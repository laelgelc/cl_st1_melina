<h> University professor speaks on Israel-Palestine conflict <h> Bradford University professor on Israel-Palestine conflict <p> University of Bradford Professor PB Anand has expressed his shock about the escalation of the conflict between Israel and Palestine . ( Image : UGC ) <p> A BRADFORD university professor has spoken about the importance of peace as tensions rise over the violence in Israel and Gaza . <p> Speaking on behalf of the University of Bradford , Professor PB Anand , head of the Department of Peace Studies and International Development , said that staff , students and the wider University of Bradford community were in " deep shock and pain " over the escalation of violence . <p> " We know that this is a complex and protracted conflict but at this moment our thoughts are with all those who have lost their loved ones , " he said . <p> " The university founded the first Peace Studies Department in the UK in 1973 - that was the very year that Israel faced the Yom Kippur War . <p> " The quest for long-lasting peace remains as valid today as it was in 1973 .
