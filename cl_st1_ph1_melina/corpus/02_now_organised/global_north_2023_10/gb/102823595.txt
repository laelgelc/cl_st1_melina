The middle , including both the Democratic and Republican establishment , is putting it among the great tragedies in Israel 's history and is all in for avenging it . And then there is Donald Trump . <p> On the most emotional issue of the day , an issue which imperils world stability , and which defines , perhaps like no other , political sense and character , Trump is out there on his own -- and , as always , happily so . <p> His cranky position first and foremost involves a grudge he 's held against Benjamin " Bibi " Netanyahu for several years .
