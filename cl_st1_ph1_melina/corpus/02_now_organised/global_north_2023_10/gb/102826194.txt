<p> Speaking to KUNA , Minister Al-Sabah stated that this initiative is in response to call of the Palestinian people and as per directions of Kuwait Amir Sheikh Nawaf Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah , Crown Prince Sheikh Mishal Al-Ahmad Al-Jaber Al-Sabah and the Prime Minister Sheikh Ahmad Nawaf Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah . <p> This is an extension of Kuwait 's unwavering position by the Palestinian cause , and an attempt to alleviate suffering brought about by the Israeli occupation . <p> The Kuwaiti Air Bridge launching on Monday and continuing on daily basis this current week , consists of humanitarian relief planeloads carrying of urgent supplies and medical necessities to Gaza Strip through Al-Arish area in the Sinai Peninsula in Egypt .
