<p> BEIRUT - The Lebanese authorities on Saturday issued precautionary guidance for evacuating Beirut International Airport and its surrounding facilities in case of emergency , as tensions rise on the Lebanese-Israeli border . <p> The guidance for Beirut Rafic Hariri International Airport , which lies on the southern fringe of the capital , did not indicate any immediate escalation on the border . <p> There have been daily but relatively contained exchanges of fire there recently between Israel and the heavily armed Lebanese group Hezbollah . <p> The message comes after Israel 's military widened its air and ground attacks on the Gaza Strip overnight . <p> In 2006 , the airport 's runways were hit by Israeli airstrikes , forcing it to close , as part of reprisals following Hezbollah 's capture of two Israeli soldiers . <p> ( Writing by Riham Alkousaa ; editing by Edmund Blair and Jason Neely ) <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
