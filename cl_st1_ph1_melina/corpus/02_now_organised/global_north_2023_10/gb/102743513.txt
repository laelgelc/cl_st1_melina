<p> The MTV EMAs will no longer go ahead as planned in Paris after the decision was made to cancel the prestigious event due to the ongoing Israel-Hamas conflict . <p> The awards were due to be held in Paris at the Paris Nord Villepinte on November 5 . However , MTV has axed the ceremony and the 30th annual music event will no longer take place . <p> MTV told The Mirror : " Given the volatility of world events , we have decided not to move forward with the 2023 MTV EMAs out of an abundance of caution for the thousands of employees , crew members , artists , fans , and partners who travel from all corners of the world to bring the show to life . <p> " The MTV EMAs are an annual celebration of global music . As we watch the devastating events in Israel and Gaza continue to unfold , this does not feel like a moment for a global celebration . With thousands of lives already lost , it is a moment of mourning . We look forward to hosting the MTV EMAs again in November of 2024 . The annual awards ceremony features stars from across the music industry and is broadcast live on MTV in over 150 countries . <p> The last time the French capital hosted the awards , designer Jean-Paul Gaultier presented the ceremony while French president Emmanuel Macron was only 17-years-old . Since the show began in 1994 in Berlin , the MTV EMAs have been hosted in 23 European cities , with London the only one to have hosted it three times . <p> Last year 's ceremony was hosted by Rita Ora and her film director husband Taika Waititi from PSD Bank Dome in Dusseldorf , Germany . Taylor Swift was the big winner on the night , taking home four awards from six nominations . <p> The 32-year-old singer took home the award for Best Video and Best Longform Video for her 110-minute version of All Too Well ( Taylor 's Version ) .
