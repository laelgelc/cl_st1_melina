<p> Good Morning Britain bosses have issued a statement after remarks made by Richard Madeley prompted calls for the presenter to be sacked . <p> Richard , 67 , was at the helm of the ITV daytime programme on Tuesday ( October 17 ) when he asked a senior Liberal Democrat MP if she knew Hamas was planning to kill innocent Israelis . His comments have been slammed as " appalling " after he asked Layla Moran -- who is of Palestinian descent -- if she was aware in advance of the terrorist attack which saw hundreds of people murdered . <p> MP Moran was left visibly stunned and chose not to answer his question . <p> Thousands of people took to social media to slam Richard for what he said , including Linda who asked : " How is this allowed ? " Mary said : " Sack him now " and Victoria said : " This is ghastly " . <p> The outcry prompted GMB bosses to put out a statement on Tuesday afternoon . A spokesperson for the programme told the ECHO : " Richard is sorry that he has upset viewers with his question to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the mood and atmosphere amongst the civilian population of Gaza immediately before the attacks . He asked Layla about her family in Gaza City because she had discussed speaking to them earlier in the interview . He did not mean to imply that she or her family might have had any prior knowledge of the attacks . " <p> The ECHO understands MP Moran appreciated the interview and would like to return to GMB in the future .
