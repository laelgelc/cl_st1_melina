<h> UAE asks UN Security Council to meet on Gaza <p> The United Arab Emirates asked the United Nations Security Council on Saturday to meet " as soon as possible " following Israel 's expanded ground operations in Gaza and the disconnection of telecommunications networks , diplomats said . <p> The 15-member council could meet as early as Sunday , diplomats said , and the UAE has asked for U.N . aid chief Martin Griffiths and Philippe Lazzarini , the head of the U.N . agency providing aid to Palestinians ( UNRWA ) , to brief . <p> ( Reporting by Michelle Nichols ; Editing by Ismail Shakil ) <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
