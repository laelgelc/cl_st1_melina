demonstrations across the UK amid growing international concern over the situation in Israel and Gaza . <p> Marchers filled the streets of central London on Saturday as thousands also gathered in Manchester , Liverpool , Edinburgh and other parts of the UK . <p> Demonstrators carried flags and flares and chanted pro-Palestinian slogans as marchers heard calls for world leaders to condemn Israel 's actions . <p> It comes as thousands of Palestinians attempted a mass exodus from northern Gaza after Israel 's military told them to evacuate before an expected ground invasion . <p> The UN
