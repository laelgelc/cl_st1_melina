<p> An Israeli festivalgoer has described the horrifying moment when gunmen opened fire in the early hours of Saturday morning . <p> The outdoor Nova Festival event , in a rural farmland area near the Gaza-Israel border , was supposed to be a time of carefree happiness . Revellers were looking forward to an all-night dance party celebrating the Jewish holiday of Sukkot . But as dawn broke people began hearing rockets whoosing overhead . Gili Yoskovich described to the BBC how she hid under a tree in a field as gunmen roamed about shooting anybody they found . The terrifying attack was just one of multiple locations hit on Saturday morning by one the biggest and most unprecedented assaults inside Israel carried out by the Hamas group . <p> A video circulating on social media showed hundreds of attendees running across an empty field as gunshots were ringing out all around . The gunmen left multiple people dead and hundreds more are missing with many feared dead .
