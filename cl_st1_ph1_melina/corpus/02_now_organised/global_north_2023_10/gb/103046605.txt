The Hamas army , with its rockets , armaments , supplies , barracks , fighters and command centre has embedded itself within and beneath its civilian population in Gaza City . Hamas has no interest in peace . Israel has encouraged all non-combatants to temporarily move out of harm 's way and they have had weeks to do so . Any ceasefire , as urged by the UN , while bringing relief to civilians , would enable Hamas to bring in everything they need to re-arm and continue the war against Israel at will .
