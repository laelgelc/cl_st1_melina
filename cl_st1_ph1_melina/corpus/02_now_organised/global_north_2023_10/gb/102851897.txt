<p> GENEVA - Almost 20,000 people have been internally displaced in south Lebanon and elsewhere since early October , a U.N . agency said on Monday , as violence escalates on the Lebanese-Israeli border following the eruption of the Gaza war . <p> The International Organization for Migration said 19,646 people had been displaced inside Lebanon since it began tracking movements on Oct. 8 , the day after an assault on Israel by Hamas militants and an Israeli counteroffensive on Gaza . <p> It said the movements were mostly by those fleeing the south although some departures were also reported elsewhere . <p> The Israeli authorities have also been evacuating dozens of towns and communities from the north of Israel . <p> Lebanon 's heavily armed Hezbollah group and Israel have been exchanging fire on an increasingly frequent basis all along the border , the worst escalation since the two sides fought a war in 2006 . <p> Hezbollah says 27 of its fighters have been killed in the clashes since Oct. 7 , while Lebanese security sources say 11 fighters from Palestinian groups in Lebanon , which are allied to Hezbollah , have also died . <p> Israel @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ frontier area . <p> ( Reporting by Emma Farge ; Editing by Edmund Blair ) <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
