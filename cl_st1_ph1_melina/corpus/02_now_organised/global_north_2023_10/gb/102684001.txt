<h> Oil markets await fresh signals ahead of Biden 's visit to Israel <p> President Joe Biden is set to visit Israel , with investors awaiting fresh signals amid geopolitical volatility <p> Oil prices have risen slightly on both major benchmarks with investors awaiting fresh signals amid sustained geopolitical uncertainty , centred on Israel and Gaza . <p> Brent Crude has ticked up 0.44 per cent to $90.04 per barrel in this morning 's session , with WTI Crude has climbed 0.32 per cent to $86.94 per barrel over the same period of trading . <p> This consolidates last week 's gains , when prices surged on fears the conflict could escalate , with Brent Crude posting a 7.5 per cent gain last week -- the benchmark 's fastest five-day rally since February . <p> This comes ahead of US President Joe Biden 's trip to the Middle East this week , with the politician hoping to strike a balancing act between support for Israel in its war with Hamas , and containing a conflict that threatens to involve Iran and Lebanon . <p> His visit to Israel comes as the country prepares to escalate military operations in Gaza @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ crisis . <p> Meanwhile , Iran has pledged " preemptive action " from the " resistance front " of its allies which include the Hezbollah movement in Lebanon . <p> Ricardo Evangelista , senior analyst at Activtrades said : " Uncertainty still lingers over a potential spillover of the conflict in Israel in a way that could involve major oil producers in the region , in a dynamic that continues to create upside risk for prices . " <p> The US decision to impose sanctions on two oil tankers for allegedly violating the G7 price cap on Russian oil has also raised uncertainty , with investors pricing in a robust Western response to future breaches . <p> Carsten Fritstch , commodities analyst for Commerzbank , said : " This could deter potential customers such as India from buying Russian oil given that Urals -- at just shy of $80 -- is still trading well above the $60 price cap . If the US were then also to enforce the oil sanctions against Iran more strictly again , the oil market would tighten noticeably . But such geopolitical risk premiums are usually short-lived unless actual supply disruptions occur , " he said . <p> There are also hopes that supply pressure could be eased with hopes the US will lift sanctions on US imports -- with the country 's government and opposition set to resume long-suspended talks today . <p> This could lead to Washington easing sanctions , multiple sources told news agency Reuters .
