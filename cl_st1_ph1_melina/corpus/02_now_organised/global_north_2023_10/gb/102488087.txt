<p> WARNING , DISTRESSING IMAGES : Hundreds of people have been killed and thousands have been injured on both sides of the conflict , with a UN commission saying there is ' clear evidence ' of war crimes being committed <p> Israel has hit the Gaza Strip for a fourth day in a row after announcing a blockade of the besieged area - with entire buildings blown apart , hundreds of people killed and thousands injured . <p> On Saturday morning , at about 6.30am Hamas fired rockets into southern Israel , with sirens heard as far away as Tel Aviv and Beersheba . About one hour later , fighters crossed into Israel in a surprise attack , using breaches in security barriers separating Gaza and Israel . <p> The attack came after Israeli settlers stormed the Al-Aqsa Mosque compound in recent days and killed a record number of Palestinians in recent months . At 9.45am , blasts were heard in Gaza and at 10am , Israel 's military spokesperson said the air force was attacking the area .
