The loss of Yahav has left an indelible void in our hearts , and we extend our deepest condolences to Shaylee Atary , his wife , his family , friends , and all who were touched by his artistry and spirit . Atary 's family and friends are currently gathering donations for her and Shaya , the daughter , who were left with nothing after the attack . <p> " In the wake of this tragedy , we find solace @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Yahav 's short film , The Boy , unfolds a narrative that sheds light on life in Kfar Aza , the kibbutz of his upbringing , and numerous other Israeli villages along the border with Gaza . These communities have borne the heavy weight of losses during the ongoing attacks . The film won the Best Cinematography Award at the Tel Aviv International Students Film Festival and will be screened as part of the international competition at Filmschoolfest Munich . To honour his memory and bravery , we are committed to screening The Boy in as many places abroad as possible .
