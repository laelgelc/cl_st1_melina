<p> Saudi Arabia 's crude oil exports in August hit their lowest level in 28 months as they fell for a fifth straight month , data from the Joint Organizations Data Initiative ( JODI ) showed on Monday . <p> Crude exports from the world 's largest oil exporter fell to 5.58 million barrels per day ( bpd ) , down 7.1% from 6.01 million bpd in July and the lowest since April 2021 . <p> Saudi Arabia and Russia have agreed to combined voluntary oil supply cuts of 1.3 million bpd , or more than 1% of global demand , until the end of the year . <p> Russian President Vladimir Putin this month indicated that OPEC+ supply reductions would last well into 2024 and likely beyond , a measure that is nearly guaranteed to support oil prices . <p> Saudi crude output fell to 8.92 million bpd in August , down 95,000 bpd from July , while inventories rose by 4.16 million barrels to 150.89 million . <p> Domestic refineries processed 2,900 bpd less crude at 2.53 million bpd in August , while direct crude burn rose by 134,000 bpd to 726,000 bpd .
