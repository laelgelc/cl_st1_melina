<p> A female soldier has been freed from Hamas capitivity during Israel 's ground operation in Gaza , according to authorities . <p> Private Ori Megidish was abducted during the attacks from the militant group on the morning of October 7 . The Israeli military said she had been given medical checks and is " doing well " after being reunited with her family . <p> She is the first member of Israeli forces taken hostage to have been confirmed rescued since the incursion just over three weeks ago . <p> A joint statement from the Israel Defence Forces and the Israel Securities Authority read : " Overnight , the soldier PVT Ori Megidish was released during IDF ground operations . PVT Megidish was kidnapped by the Hamas terrorist organization on October 7th . The soldier was medically checked , is doing well , and has met with her family . The IDF and ISA will continue to do everything it takes in order to release the hostages .
