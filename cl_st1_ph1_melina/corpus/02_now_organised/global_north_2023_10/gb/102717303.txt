<p> RAMALLAH , West Bank - Israeli forces shot dead two Palestinian teenagers near Ramallah in the West Bank on Wednesday after protests against Israel 's bombing of the Gaza Strip , Palestinian officials said . <p> The deaths brought the toll of Palestinians killed in the latest flare-up of Israeli-Palestinian violence to at least 64 in the West Bank , a sharp uptick in fatal clashes with the army and settlers . <p> Israel is preparing a ground assault in the Gaza Strip in response to a deadly attack by Palestinian militant group Hamas that killed 1,400 Israelis , mostly civilians , on Oct. 7 . <p> Israeli forces have carried out their fiercest bombardment of Gaza in response , killing more than 3,000 Palestinians and imposing a total siege on the blockaded enclave that Hamas controls . <p> A statement from the Palestinian Authority 's health ministry said Israeli forces shot a 15-year-old and a 17-year-old in the village of Shuqba west of Ramallah .
