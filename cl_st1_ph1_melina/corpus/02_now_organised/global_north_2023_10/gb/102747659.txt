<h> Thank you for subscribing ! <p> A vigil was held at Coventry Cathedral yesterday ( 18th October ) to honour those affected on both sides of the conflict raging in Israel and Gaza . People gathered to call for peace after thousands of deaths of Israelis and Palestinians in the worst violence in the region for years . <p> More than 200 people attended . Pieces of music were sung by the Cathedral Choir interspersed with prayers offered by members of different faith groups . <p> The Very Reverend John Witcombe , from Coventry Cathedral , spoke to CoventryLive about the service and why it was important for Coventry as a city to hold this event . He told us : ' ' Coventry has this experience of having been destroyed through violent aggression but the best way to respond to this is seeking to re-build relationships . ' ' <p> He added : " We have stood for this for over 80 years and many people have been inspired by our story from across the world , so we have a particular role , internationally and nationally , to speak from our Coventry experience .
