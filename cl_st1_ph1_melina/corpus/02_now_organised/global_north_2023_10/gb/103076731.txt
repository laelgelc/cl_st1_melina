<p> JERUSALEM - Israeli National Security Adviser Tzachi Hanegbi said in a televised briefing on Tuesday there was " no deal in sight " for the release of hostages held in Gaza as Israel continues its ground invasion of the enclave . <p> Hanegbi said attacks against Israel by the Houthi militant group in Yemen were " intolerable " but declined to elaborate when asked what an Israeli response might be . <p> He said Egyptian hospitals should serve as an alternative for treating Gaza casualties , with hospitals in the enclave under pressure and suffering power cuts , but said Israel understood Egypt 's concern about an influx of Palestinian refugees . <p> Hanegbi said the Israeli military 's focus was on fighting Hamas militants in northern Gaza , but that it would shift to the southern part of the strip at a later stage . <p> ( Reporting by Henriette Chacar ; Editing by John Davison and Tomasz Janowski ) <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
