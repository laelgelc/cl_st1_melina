<p> JERUSALEM - Israel 's military said it deployed troops against a suspected infiltration from Lebanon on Monday , which a local radio station said appeared to have taken place in the central part of the countries ' border . <p> Tensions in Israel 's north have surged as it battles Palestinian Hamas gunmen who staged a mass-infiltration from the southern Gaza Strip on Saturday . <p> " A report was received regarding the infiltration of a number of suspects into Israeli territory from Lebanese territory . IDF soldiers are deployed in the area , " the military said in a statement . <p> Israel 's Army Radio gave the location as near Adamit , across from the Lebanese border towns of Aalma El Chaeb and Zahajra . <p> ( Reporting by Henriette Chacar ; Editing by Hugh Lawson ) <p> Disclaimer : The content of this article is syndicated or provided to this website from an external third party provider .
