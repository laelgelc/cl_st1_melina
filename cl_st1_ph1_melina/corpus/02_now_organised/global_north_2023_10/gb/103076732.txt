<p> JERUSALEM - Israel is assuming a defensive posture on the Lebanese front in order to avoid overstretching its forces as it focuses on waging the war against Hamas in the Gaza Strip , Israeli National Security Adviser Tzachi Hanegbi said in a briefing on Tuesday . <p> Hanegbi added that , " the day after Hamas " , Israel would apply " the lessons learned " against Lebanon 's Hezbollah militants .
