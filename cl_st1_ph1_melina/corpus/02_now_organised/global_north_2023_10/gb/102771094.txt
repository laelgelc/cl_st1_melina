<p> Mohammed Ghalayini works as an atmospheric scientist on air quality policy in the UK , where he lives in Manchester . Earlier this month the 44-year-old took some time off work to travel back to Gaza , which he 'd last visited in 2004 . The trip was to " reconnect " with his family , including meeting half-brothers for the first time . On the morning of October 7 , the former UK civil servant got up early to help his uncle with the olive harvest , but it soon became clear that " something big was happening " . <p> Sadly this was not new for the family , and they knew to pre-emptively move ahead of any possible bombing from Israel . What they were n't initially aware of was that militant group Hamas had launched a bloody attack across the border into Israel , claiming over a thousand lives , and in response Israel effectively placed Gaza under siege and launched a massive bombardment . <p> In the days since , thousands of Palestinian lives have been claimed and even more have been forced to leave their homes .
