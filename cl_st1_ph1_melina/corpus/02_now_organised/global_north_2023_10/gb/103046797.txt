<h> As the West divides itself over how to respond to Hamas 's horrors , the urge to call for peace is strong -- but not a long-term solution <p> Which is the moral high ground ? To call for a ceasefire when you know both sides will ignore it ? Or to argue for something less than that -- a " humanitarian pause " to allow for aid to get through to Gaza ? For some the pause must sound like the worst kind of cop-out -- the watering down of a clearly stated moral principle . It divides the Labour Party ( and even the Tories ) down the middle , and it is damaging the reputation of its leader , Sir Keir Starmer . <p> Yet the over-arching aim must surely be to get aid to the Palestinians trapped in Gaza . Pressing for something that just might work , as against something you know will not , makes the stronger case .
