Bernard Stever , Richmond <h> ADHD information <p> It 's crucial for the ADHD community that published information on ADHD is accurate and informative ( Letters , October 18 ) . This community suffers enough from denial of its existence and the myths that are spread . ADHD is a neurodevelopmental condition that occurs in about 8 per cent of the population and is usually accompanied by other conditions ( called comorbidity ) . It is a learning disability which our education system is currently ill-equipped to manage . It can only be diagnosed by a paediatrician ( for children ) or psychiatrist ( for adults ) and medication can help . It is not addictive but also not for everyone . Medication made my ADHD son nauseous , and he lost his appetite , so I used behavioural management techniques and worked closely with his school to ensure he was n't over disciplined . He is now finishing his degree full-time while working and teaching in this field . He is a brilliant high achiever , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as the school journey was so hard . If we ignore ADHD 's existence , we blame the child for something outside their control . When a child struggles every day to do what is required but still fails , and is punished , they give up and drop out of learning , and life . ADHD kids are expelled from school up to seven times the rate of other kids . It is the undiagnosed and untreated child that will grow up to self-medicate with alcohol , cigarettes , and illicit drugs . Our prisons are brimming with ADHD adults who were undiagnosed and over-disciplined as kids . Conversely , if we choose to foster their strengths , society benefits from their creativity and energy because nurtured ADHD kids become high achievers and entrepreneurs . That 's the choice .
