<h> New rally planned as thousands gather for Palestinians <p> Rallies in Sydney and Melbourne on Sunday follow similar demonstrations in other capital cities . ( James Ross/AAP PHOTOS ) <p> Young families were among thousands of people who attended rallies calling for a free Palestine and an end to deadly Israeli strikes on Gaza . <p> Despite fears of violence and police warnings to stay away , more than 6000 people gathered at demonstrations in Sydney , Melbourne and Adelaide on Sunday , following rallies on Friday in Brisbane and Perth . <p> The strikes are retaliation to the massacre of more than 1000 people by Hamas militants in co-ordinated attacks last weekend . <p> Rally organiser Palestine Action Group Sydney on Sunday announced plans for a march next weekend at Town Hall . <p> NSW Police Assistant Commissioner Tony Cooke said the group 's new application would be considered on its merits but no decision had been made . <p> It comes after original plans were rejected on technical grounds for a pro-Palestine rally at Town Hall in Sydney 's CBD on Sunday . <p> That decision followed anti-Semitic chants and flares being set off at a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ when the landmark was lit in Israeli colours in a show of solidarity . <p> A denied application to hold a protest or rally could effectively be appealed in the Supreme Court , a step that organisers were conscious of when warning Sunday 's crowd to remain peaceful . <p> " What happens today will help determine how the courts and the government look upon that ( application ) , " attendees were told . <p> " Let 's be clear , we will not stop but we do not want to give them a free kick . " <p> Mr Cooke said the largely peaceful gathering and lack of arrests on Sunday was " certainly encouraging " for future applications .
