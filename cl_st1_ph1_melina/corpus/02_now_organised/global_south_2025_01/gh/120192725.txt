<p> Israel 's government approved a Gaza ceasefire and hostage deal after deliberating for more than seven hours early into Saturday . <p> The vote clears the way for the first pause in fighting in months , and the phased exchange of hostages held by Hamas and its allies for Palestinian prisoners . <p> Israel says the agreement will go into effect at 8:30 a.m. local time ( 1:30 a.m . ET ) .
