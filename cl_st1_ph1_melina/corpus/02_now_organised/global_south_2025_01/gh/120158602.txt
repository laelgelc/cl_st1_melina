<p> The office of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu says a " deal to release the hostages " has been agreed . <p> Netanyahu had delayed a cabinet vote to approve the Gaza ceasefire deal , due on Thursday , accusing Hamas of seeking last-minute changes to the agreement . <p> On Friday morning his office said Netanyahu had been informed by the negotiating team that agreements on the deal had been reached . <p> He has ordered the political-security cabinet to convene later on Friday and the government " will then convene to approve the deal " , Netanyahu 's office said . Families of the hostages have been informed , it added . <p> Representatives of Israel , Hamas , the United States , and Qatar have officially signed the deal in Doha , Israeli media reports . <p> The ceasefire deal was first announced on Wednesday by mediators the US and Qatar .
