<h> Gaza ceasefire : $10bn needed to restore healthcare system -- WHO <p> The World Health Organization ( WHO ) has emphasized that over $10 billion is required to restore Gaza 's devastated healthcare system . <p> The announcement comes amid reports that Israel 's security cabinet has approved a ceasefire deal with Hamas , which could facilitate a major increase in aid deliveries . <p> WHO 's representative in the Occupied Palestinian Territories , Dr. Rik Peeperkorn , expressed cautious optimism , highlighting that the ceasefire could enable the entry of 500 to 600 aid trucks daily in the coming weeks , a significant rise from the 40-50 trucks that have been reaching Gaza in recent months . <p> Dr. Peeperkorn referred to the ceasefire as " a sign of hope , " although he cautioned that the challenges ahead are immense .
