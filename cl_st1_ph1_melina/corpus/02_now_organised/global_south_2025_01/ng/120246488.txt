<h> Nigeria applauds implementation of Gaza ceasefire deal <p> ABUJA- The Federal Government has expressed support for the ceasefire deal over Gaza between Israel and Hamas . <p> The ceasefire agreement was recently implemented to bring an end to the violence in Gaza and ensure sustainability , a peaceful resolution to the crisis . <p> The ministry 's acting Spokesperson , Kimiebi Ebienfa disclosed this in a statement on Monday in Abuja . <p> The statement said that Nigeria would continue to support the cause for a two-state solution to ensure lasting peace in the Middle East . <p> " The Federal Government of Nigeria welcomes the agreement of a ceasefire in Gaza between Israel and Hamas . <p> " The agreement offers the prospect of an end to the appalling loss of civilian life and human tragedy witnessed across Gaza over the last 15 months . <p> " As well as relief to the families of Israeli hostages held in Gaza and some of the Palestinians detained in Israeli prisons . <p> " Nigeria also welcomes the supply of much-needed aid and relief materials that the peace deal facilitates @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ceasefire agreement was commendable because it presented the people in the region with hope for a future and compassion . <p> " The government praises the Arabic Republic of Egypt , Qatar , and the U.S. for the painstaking work during the negotiations that delivered the ceasefire agreement , and for which they will now act as international guarantors . <p> " Nigeria urges all stakeholders to build on the new momentum and work towards the successful implementation of the second and third phases of the agreement . <p> " Also , Nigeria re-affirms support for a two-state solution as the blueprint for a just and lasting peace for Palestine , Israel , and the entire region , " it said . ( NAN )
