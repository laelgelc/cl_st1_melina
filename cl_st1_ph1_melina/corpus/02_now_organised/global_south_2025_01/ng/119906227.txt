<h> National Assembly rejects N9bn mining sector capital budget in 2025 <p> The Joint National Assembly Committee on Solid Minerals has rejected the proposed N9 billion capital budget for the mining sector in the 2025 budget . <p> It said that the allocation falls drastically short of the investment required to position solid minerals as the cornerstone of economic diversification a statement <p> Leading the debate during the budget defense session on Friday , Ekong Sampson , chairman of the Joint Senate and House of Representatives Committee on Solid Minerals , expressed the committee 's displeasure with the proposed allocation , noting that without substantial investments in exploration and other critical areas , the potential of solid minerals to drive the transition to green energy would remains in doubt . <p> In the same vein , Gaza Gbefwi , co-chairman and House Committee chairman on Solid Minerals , condemned the reduction of the proposed N531 billion 2025 capital expenditure proposal to N9 billion , describing it as a disservice to the sector , which he argued is crucial for generating revenue beyond oil . <p> " As a House , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ compromised because of misplaced priorities . We , as elected representatives , understand the urgent need to diversify our economy , and no sector holds more promise in this regard than solid minerals . If we miss this opportunity to invest today , future generations will not forgive us , " Gbefwi asserted . <p> Gbefwi pointed to countries like Botswana , South Africa , and Ghana , which are investing heavily in exploration , and stressed that with Nigeria 's vast reserves of critical minerals , underfunding the mining sector would prevent it from becoming a key revenue driver for the nation . <p> In his remarks , Dele Alake minister of solid minerals development , highlighted the positive outcomes of the reforms over the past year , which has created 45,000 new jobs in the sector in 2023 , up from 30,000 the previous year . He also noted that the 2024 revenue projection of N11 billion has been exceeded , with N38 billion generated in the year under review . <p> " We have not only succeeded in attracting global attention to Nigeria 's mining sector , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ despite receiving only 18 percent of our 2024 capital budget . <p> " In our budget proposals for 2025 , we estimated over N531 billion for capital expenditure but was allocated a mere N9 billion . This is unacceptable , as it will hinder any significant investment in exploration , which is crucial for generating the geo-data that will attract major players . We seek the support of the National Assembly for a radical upward review of the budget , " Alake added . <p> Joining the debate , Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan , a senator from Kogi State , emphasised that if Nigeria is serious about developing the mining sector , the 2025 budget proposal must be drastically reviewed upwards . She added her voice to the call for the rejection of the proposed budgetary estimates and a suspension of the screening process until the review is implemented . <p> In a unanimous voice vote , Ekong Sampson , chairman of the Joint Committee , announced the committee 's resolution to reject the proposed 2025 budgetary estimates , suspend further screening of the ministry 's budget , and invite the ministers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , to make a case for an upward review of the mining sector 's budget allocation to ensure the nation fully maximises the potential of its mineral resources . <p> Business Day , established in 2001 , is a daily business newspaper based in Lagos .
