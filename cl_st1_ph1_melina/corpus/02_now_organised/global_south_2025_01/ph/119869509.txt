<p> JAKARTA -- Indonesia will expand its existing defence partnerships and step up its handling of strategic issues impacting its sovereignty , including maritime security and the safety of sea passage and fisheries , its foreign minister said on Friday . <p> Sugiono , who uses only one name , said Indonesia would continue to advocate for the completion of a code of conduct between the Southeast Asian bloc ASEAN and China on the South China Sea and prioritise ASEAN 's centrality . <p> Indonesia considers itself not a party in disputes over the sea , a waterway crucial to global trade , but has recently been tested by forays by China 's coast guard into its exclusive economic zone . <p> Beijing claims sovereignty over almost the entire South China Sea , putting it at odds with Malaysia , Vietnam and the Philippines , with disputes frequent over the conduct in their EEZs of China 's massive fleet of coast guard . China insists it is operating lawfully in its territory . <p> " In the geostrategic sense , Indonesia is close to a source of regional conflict , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ conflict resolution that is peaceful , " Sugiono said , adding Indonesia would keep pushing for constructive dialogue on a code of conduct . <p> Regional commitments to draft a code were first made in 2002 but talks towards its creation only started in 2017 and progress has been limited , with years spent discussing the framework for negotiations and numerous agreements signed to expedite the process . <p> Thorny issues include whether the code will be legally binding , enforceable and based on international maritime law , under which a 2016 international arbitration panel ruled Beijing 's expansive territorial claims had no legal basis . <p> China does not recognise the ruling . <p> In a wide-ranging speech setting out Indonesia 's foreign policy that was attended by the diplomatic community , Sugiono also said Indonesia would prioritise completion of talks on free trade agreements and expand its international trade , including with non-traditional partners in Africa and the Pacific . <p> He said Indonesia 's joining of the BRICS grouping -- which includes Russia , China , Brazil , India , Iran , Egypt and South Africa -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ but an underlining of its free and active foreign policy . <p> He also said Indonesia would never abandon its support for the Palestinian cause , calling for a ceasefire and accountability for Israel over its role in the Gaza conflict . <p> Sugiono was appointed in October when new President Prabowo Subianto took office . -- Reuters
