<h> EU welcomes ceasefire deal in Gaza <p> Brussels : High-ranking European Union ( EU ) officials welcomed a ceasefire agreement in Gaza and called on both parties to fully implement the deal . <p> " This brings hope to the entire region , where people have endured immense suffering for far too long , " European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said on her X account , Xinhua news agency reported . <p> " Both parties must fully implement this agreement , as a stepping stone toward lasting stability in the region and a diplomatic resolution of the conflict , " she added . <p> " This is a major , positive breakthrough towards ending the violence , " noted Kaja Kallas , the EU High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy , also on X. <p> Qatar announced Wednesday evening that Israel and Hamas have agreed to a ceasefire-for-hostages deal in Gaza . According to the agreement , Hamas will release 33 hostages in the first phase , spanning six weeks , in exchange for the release of Palestinian prisoners .
