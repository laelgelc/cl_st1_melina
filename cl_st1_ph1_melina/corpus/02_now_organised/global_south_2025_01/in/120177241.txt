<h> Never Ending Wars In The Age Of Democracy Outlook 's Twin Editions <p> Outlook 's twin editions , published at the beginning of 2025 , focus on never-ending wars across the globe . The first issue tells the stories of wartime , while the other looks at the troubled relationship between democracy and peace . <p> The wars of the present show no signs of ending . Images of people dying , starving , fleeing their homes , and losing hope from Gaza and Ukraine to lesser-known yet deadly conflicts in Sudan , Myanmar , and other countries show a world trapped in an endless cycle of war .
