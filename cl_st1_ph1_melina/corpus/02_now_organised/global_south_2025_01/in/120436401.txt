<h> What is the World Health Organisation and why Does it Matter ? <p> The WHO , born from the 1851 International Sanitary Conference , has advanced global health since 1948 . It combats diseases , strengthens healthcare , and responds to crises like polio resurgence , climate health threats , and pandemics . With AI and public health milestones , WHO aims for universal health coverage , emergency protection , and global well-being by 2025 . <p> World Health Organisation ( WHO ) <p> When the plague , cholera and yellow fever rippled deadly waves across a newly industrialised and interconnected world in the mid-19th century , taking a global approach to health became imperative . Doctors , scientists , presidents and prime ministers urgently convened the International Sanitary Conference in Paris in 1851 , a precursor to what is now the largest of its kind : the World Health Organization , known as WHO . <p> From laboratories to battlefields , the United Nations specialised health agency has been dedicated to the well-being of all people since 1948 .
