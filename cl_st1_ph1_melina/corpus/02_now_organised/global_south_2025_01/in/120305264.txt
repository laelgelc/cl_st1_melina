<h> President Trump Announces United States ' Withdrawal From WHO <p> SRINAGAR : President Donald Trump signed an executive order on his first day back in office to withdraw the United States from the World Health Organization ( WHO ) . Citing dissatisfaction with the organisation 's performance and governance , the executive order marks a return to Trump 's prior stance on the WHO during his first term , reports in the global media suggest . <p> The order criticises the WHO for handling the COVID-19 pandemic , alleged political influences , and what it describes as an inequitable funding structure . As an example of the organisation 's flaws , Trump pointed to China 's disproportionately low financial contributions despite its population size . The United States had previously issued a withdrawal notice in 2020 , later reversed under the Biden administration in 2021 . <p> The directive mandates the immediate cessation of US funding to the WHO , the reassignment of American personnel working with the organisation , and a review of global health partnerships to identify credible alternatives .
