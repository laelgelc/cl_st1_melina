I believed that a person could start anew from zero each time they reached a point of loss . I thought that if someone lost their home , they could build another as if the first never existed . If they lost a part of their family , they could find solace in the ones left behind . And if they lost a dream they had been striving for , they could change the course of their aspirations and find themselves in a new life that suited them . <p> Here in Gaza , the scales of everything shift with each thought and principle . Here , dreams were lost at the threshold of the beginning of the genocide , and the entire compass of hope was lost . <p> Gaza , besieged throughout its life , how can its people find solace anywhere else ?
