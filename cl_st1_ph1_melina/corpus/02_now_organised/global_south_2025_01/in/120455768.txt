<h> Hamas hands four Israeli hostages over to ICRC in Gaza <h> Hamas released four Israeli soldier hostages to the International Committee of the Red Cross ( ICRC ) . The handover occurred on a podium in Gaza City . A crowd of Palestinians and armed Hamas members were present . The hostages waved and smiled . They then entered ICRC vehicles . The vehicles will transport them to Israeli forces . <p> APRepresentational <p> Hamas handed four female Israeli soldier hostages over to the International Committee of the Red Cross on Saturday , live TV showed . <p> The four were led onto a podium in Gaza City amid a large crowd of Palestinians and surrounded by dozens of armed Hamas men . They waved and smiled before being led off and entering ICRC vehicles that will transport them to Israeli forces .
