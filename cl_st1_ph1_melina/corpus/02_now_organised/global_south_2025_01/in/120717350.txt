<p> Jerusalem : The Palestinian militant organization Hamas released 8 more hostages held captive in the Gaza Strip for 482 days on Thursday , January 30 . However , Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu was furious about Hamas 's methods of releasing the hostages . <p> This anger can be understood from the fact that Israel announced a halt to the release of 110 Palestinian prisoners . The Israeli Prime Minister made it clear that the scene that unfolded during the handover of the hostages to the Red Cross was unacceptable . <h> What happened in the Gaza Strip ? <p> Hamas handed over Israeli female soldier Agam Berger ( 20 ) to the Red Cross on Thursday morning . However , before the handover , Hamas paraded Berger in front of a crowd at the Jabalia refugee camp in northern Gaza . After Berger , Hamas handed over seven other hostages to the Red Cross on Thursday afternoon , including two Israelis and five Thai nationals . The Israeli army later confirmed that the Red Cross had handed over all seven hostages to its forces in the Gaza Strip @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Hamas chose the devastated house of Hamas leader Yahya Sinwar , who was killed in Khan Younis city in southern Gaza , to hand over these seven hostages to the Red Cross . <p> During the handover of the hostages in front of Sinwar 's house , a highly chaotic scene was witnessed . Thousands of Palestinians gathered at the scene surrounded the hostages and shouted offensive slogans against them . Armed Hamas militants were also seen pushing back the crowd as they led the hostages towards the Red Cross vehicles . <h> Release of Palestinian prisoners halted <p> Due to the chaos , the process of handing over the hostages to the Red Cross and their evacuation from Gaza was delayed . Later , the Red Cross handed over the hostages to Israeli forces in the Gaza Strip . The scene in the Gaza Strip angered Tel Aviv . Netanyahu criticized the ' shocking ' scenes witnessed during the release of the hostages and called on international mediators to ensure that such incidents do not happen again . <p> Immediately after this , an order was given to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ freed in exchange for hostages . Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu made it clear that Palestinian prisoners would not be released until Hamas provides assurances for the ' safe release ' of the hostages . <p> After being persuaded by mediators , Netanyahu later stated that they had assured him that the ' safe evacuation ' of hostages from the Gaza Strip would be ensured . Subsequently , on Thursday night , a convoy of buses carrying Palestinian prisoners left the Ofer prison in the West Bank . <p> During this time , some Palestinian youths threw stones outside the prison premises , prompting Israeli forces to use tear gas canisters to disperse them . According to the Palestinian Red Crescent , three Palestinians were injured during the clashes outside Ofer prison . <p> It reported that Israeli forces also resorted to gunfire and stun grenades to disperse the crowd . Nevertheless , the buses headed towards Beitunia near the city of Ramallah in the Israeli-occupied West Bank , where the relatives of the Palestinian prisoners awaited .
