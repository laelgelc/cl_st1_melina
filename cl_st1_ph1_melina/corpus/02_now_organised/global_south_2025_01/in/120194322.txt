Syria , too , entered a new post-Assad era -- one fraught with hope and uncertainty . Against this backdrop , the shifting international landscape , especially with the incoming Trump administration , will further influence regional and global dynamics . While reconstruction efforts in Gaza , Lebanon , and Syria demand global attention , the broader process of regional rapprochement should be a central focus in 2025 .
