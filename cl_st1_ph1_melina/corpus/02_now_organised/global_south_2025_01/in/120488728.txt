<p> Italian Prime Minister , Giorgia Meloni , met on Sunday ( January 26 ) with the Crown Prince of Saudi Arabia , Mohamed bin Salman , at the UNESCO World Heritage archaeological site of Al-Ula . The bilateral was part of a three-day mission of the Italian prime minister to the Persian Gulf countries , which began on Saturday ( January 25 ) in Jeddah , Saudi Arabia , and will end on Monday ( January 27 ) in Bahrain . The topics on the agenda included the reconstruction of Syria after the civil war and Italy 's commitment to support the ceasefire in Lebanon . Meloni and Bin Salman also talked about Iran , Gaza and Yemen , as well as the Red Sea , where the Italian Navy is engaged in the EU mission Aspides in defence of freedom of navigation .
