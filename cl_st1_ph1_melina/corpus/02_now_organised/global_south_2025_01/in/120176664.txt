Some businesspeople had long harboured private doubts about Trump 's anti-globalisation campaign . But overall confidence began to fall when they came face to face with the possible consequences of cutting the US economy off from international trade and migration : sharply higher prices of imported products and labour shortages in some sectors . <p> Public anxieties multiplied as mysterious cyber-attacks caused a series of failures affecting US power infrastructure , and water and sanitation systems .
