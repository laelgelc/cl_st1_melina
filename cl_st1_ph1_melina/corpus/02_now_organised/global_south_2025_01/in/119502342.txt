<h> Just In <h> Yemen 's Houthis claim downing US drone <h> Yemen 's Houthi group said on Wednesday that it has shot down a US-made MQ-9 drone over the central Yemeni province of Marib . <p> Sanaa : Yemen 's Houthi group said on Wednesday that it has shot down a US-made MQ-9 drone over the central Yemeni province of Marib . <p> The drone was shot down by a " locally-made surface-to-air missile " while " carrying out hostile activities , " Houthi military spokesperson Yahya Sarea said in a statement aired by the Houthi-run al-Masirah TV . <p> It is the second MQ-9 drone shot down by the group within 72 hours , also the 14th of its type downed since November 2023 , according to Sarea . <p> The Houthi television said footage of the downed drone would be broadcast in the next hours , Xinhua news agency reported . <p> The US army has not yet commented on the Houthi claim . <p> The drone downing came one day after the US Central Command claimed multiple precision strikes since Monday against Houthi targets in Yemen @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and control facility , weapons production and storage facilities , and a coastal radar site . <p> The Houthi group , which controls much of northern Yemen , has been launching rocket and drone attacks against Israel and disrupting " Israeli-linked " shipping in the Red Sea since November 2023 , to show solidarity with Palestinians in the Gaza Strip amid their ongoing conflict with Israelis . <p> In response , the US-led navy coalition stationed in the area has regularly conducted air raids and strikes against Houthi targets to deter the group , prompting the Houthis to expand attacks to include US targets .
