<p> Ahead of the implementation of the ceasefire deal with Hamas , Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has stated that Israel can not proceed with the agreed framework until Hamas provides the list of hostages to be released . <p> He further emphasised that any violations of the agreement will not be tolerated . Quoting Netanyahu , the Office of the Prime Minister of Israel on X wrote , " We will be unable to move forward with the framework until we receive the list of the hostages who will be released , as was agreed . Israel will not tolerate violations of the agreement . Hamas is solely responsible . " <p> On Saturday , the Israeli government approved the ceasefire and hostage release deal with Hamas by a vote of 24-8 , according to a report by Times of Israel .
