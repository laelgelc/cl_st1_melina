It was only a mushroom cloud , and the mushroom cloud meant the war was over . <p> Seventeen years later , Alice was a young mom who had moved to the suburbs of New York City . Alice did n't become an anti-nuke activist after the missile crisis , and neither Carol nor Kathy mentioned it as a moment they remembered in the awakening of their conscience .
