<p> As Donald Trump assumes office again as US president , the world is caught amidst seminal change . Since World War II even the most powerful countries were willing to limit national sovereignty , in that they agreed for the most part to abide by global rules and cooperative action . Such rules were framed on a wide range of subjects . Not just trade and tariffs , but also nuclear arms , the law of the sea , and the sanctity of national borders . <p> This has changed , for two reasons . The first is the rise of China , and the accompanying shift of global power . China is not a status quo power , so it wants to shake things up and challenge the US . The second is the rise of strongmen as rulers in the great-power countries : Vladimir Putin , Xi Jinping and now Donald Trump . <p> These two developments have revived something that had more or less died out after World War II , namely wars of choice by the great powers , aimed at territorial conquest .
