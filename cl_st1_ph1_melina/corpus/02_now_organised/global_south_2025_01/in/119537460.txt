<p> An Israeli airstrike early Thursday killed 10 people in Gaza , including three children and two top-ranking officers from the Hamas-run police force . The strike targeted a tent in Muwasi , an Israeli-declared humanitarian zone for displaced people . Among the victims were Major General Mahmoud Salah , the general director of Gaza police , and his deputy , Brig Gen Hossam Shahwan , hospital records confirmed . The Nasser Hospital reported that the dead included three women , three children , and four men . There was no immediate comment from the Israeli military regarding the strike . <p> The Hamas-run police have largely disappeared from the streets after Israel began its offensive against Hamas . This has contributed to a breakdown in law and order , affecting the delivery of humanitarian aid .
