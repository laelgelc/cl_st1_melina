<h> China and Saudi Arabia : A Deepening Strategic Partnership <h> China and Saudi Arabia : A Deepening Strategic Partnership <p> Iran and Saudi Arabia resumed bilateral relations in March 2023 , following a deal brokered by China and ending a hiatus of seven years . The diplomatic breakthrough underlined Beijing 's political inroads into the Middle East .
