<p> A video showing a confrontation between a Jewish passenger and a flight attendant on an American Airlines flight has gone viral , sparking heated debate online . The incident occurred on a Miami-bound flight when the passenger accused the attendant of antisemitism for wearing a watermelon pin , which he claimed symbolised support for Palestine . <p> In the video , the passenger can be heard shouting , " You support terrorism , you 're antisemitic , " and accusing the flight crew of treating him unfairly due to his Jewish background . The flight attendant , alongside a colleague , responded by stating that recording them was against US aviation regulations . <p> The passenger was reportedly accused of touching the flight attendants , which he denied . American Airlines staff told him he could not leave the plane until he deleted the video . Police were called , but the passenger claims he contacted authorities himself . According to him , the police determined he had not broken any laws . He was eventually escorted off the plane . <p> American Airlines has launched an investigation into @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ wearing unauthorised pins that are not part of the official uniform . Exceptions include pins recognising company milestones , military service , and those representing religion , ethnicity , or LGBTQ pride . <p> The confrontation comes amid heightened global tensions following the October 7 Hamas attacks on Israel and ongoing violence in Gaza . According to Gaza 's health ministry , over 45,000 Palestinians have been killed in the conflict .
