<p> In a significant development under the fragile Gaza ceasefire , four Israeli women soldiers , including Naama Levy , were released by Hamas today . Naama Levy , who became the face of the October 7 hostage crisis after being seen bloodied , limping , and dragged by her hair in widely circulated images , was among those freed . The soldiers , dressed in military uniform , were presented on a podium and made to wave before being handed over to Red Cross representatives . The vehicle carrying the hostages has now departed Gaza . <p> This is the second exchange since the ceasefire agreement brokered by Qatar , Egypt , and the United States .
