The country now aims to achieve its Intended Nationally Determined Contributions ( INDCs ) for carbon emissions by 2050 , a decade earlier than the previously declared 2060 target . To align with the Paris Agreement , Indonesia plans to phase out all coal plants by 2040 . For a major coal-producing country , this is quite ambitious . <p> Indonesia is also striving towards energy self-sufficiency in the green sector .
