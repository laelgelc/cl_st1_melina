<p> Dr. Abed Elrazeg Abu Jazer is the Charge d'Affaires at the Embassy of the State of Palestine in New Delhi . With a PhD in Political Science from Delhi 's Jamia Millia Islamia , he has a distinguished career spanning over three decades as a journalist . He has worked with various media organisations . His experience in journalism and academic expertise have contributed to his current diplomatic role , where he represents Palestinian interests in India . He shares with Outlook about what is happening in Gaza . He said , there is a severe food crisis in Gaza as no consignment of wheat is allowed to enter Gaza , and bakeries are unable to operate . International aid convoys , including those from the United Nations , have been targeted . Such attacks have made it increasingly difficult to provide food for the people . This is a form of political pressure . Despite entering the 21st century , Palestinians are still suffering from the same types of aggression we have seen for decades .
