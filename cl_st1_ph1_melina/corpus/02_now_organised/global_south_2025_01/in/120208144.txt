<p> Three hostages returned to Israel as the Gaza ceasefire took effect following delays over the release list . <p> Updated Jan 19 , 2025 , 22:01 IST <p> The three Israeli women hostages released Sunday <p> Nearly 15 months after the Israel-Hamas war started , three of the remaining hundred Israeli hostages are back in the country , the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) confirmed Sunday . Posting a short message on X along with the pictures of the three women who were freed , IDF wrote : " They are home . " <p> The ceasefire in Gaza took effect on Sunday after a nearly three-hour long delay over the hostages list . Israel PM Benjamin Netanyahu gave last-minute instructions to delay the ceasefire unless Hamas shared the names of the hostages to be released . His office stated that the ceasefire would not commence " until Israel has received the list " of hostages . The ceasefire started at 11:15 am local time ( 09:15 GMT , 2:45 pm IST ) after Hamas released the names of the first three hostages that would be freed . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ agency reported continued Israeli airstrikes during the delay , with Palestinian medics confirming that 13 Palestinians were killed between the original ceasefire start time of 8:30 am and the eventual implementation . The Israeli military acknowledged " continuing to strike within the Gaza area " as per Netanyahu 's directive . <p> Hamas attributed the delay to " technical reasons , " citing " complexities of the field situation and the continued bombing . " Mediators reportedly requested 48 hours of calm before the ceasefire , but ongoing strikes complicated the process of sharing the list . A Palestinian official explained that the situation on the ground made delivering the names challenging .
