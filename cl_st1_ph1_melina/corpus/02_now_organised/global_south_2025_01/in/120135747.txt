( Reuters ) <p> Israel 's security cabinet has approved a ceasefire agreement involving the exchange of hostages held by Hamas for Palestinian prisoners in Israeli jails , marking a significant pause in the 15-month-long conflict for an initial six weeks . <p> The deal will now go to the full cabinet for the final signoff in meeting expected later on Friday . <p> " After reviewing all the political , security and humanitarian aspects , and understanding that the proposed deal supports achieving the objectives of the war , ( the security cabinet ) has recommended that the government approve the proposed framework , " a statement from Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu 's office said . <p> Advertisement <p> The approvals come after @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ agreement due to disagreements between Israel and Hamas , as well as opposition from far-right members of Netanyahu 's coalition government . With the cabinet 's ratification , the deal is set to be implemented on Sunday , pending approval by the Israeli high court , which is unlikely to intervene . <h> The Agreement <p> Hamas will release 33 hostages over the first 42 days , including children , women ( such as female soldiers ) , and individuals aged over 50 , while Israel will free 50 Palestinian prisoners for each female Israeli soldier released and 30 prisoners for each non-soldier female hostage . <p> The hostages ' names will be disclosed only after their handover to the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) , while lists of prisoners to be released have already circulated in Israeli media .
