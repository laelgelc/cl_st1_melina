<p> Tel Aviv : A key Hamas commander who led the attack Kibbutz Nir Oz during the October 27 , 2003 massacre in Israel has been killed , confirmed the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) on Tuesday . The statement said that Hamas ' Nukhba Platoon commander , Abd al-Hadi Sabah was eliminated in a recent drone strike in the Khan Yunis area of southern Gaza <p> " Abd al-Hadi Sabah , a Nukhba Platoon Commander in the Western Khan Yunis Battalion was eliminated in an intelligence-based IDF and ISA strike , " the IDF said in a post on X. <p> " Abd al-Hadi Sabah -- who operated from a shelter in the Humanitarian Area in Khan Yunis -- was one of the leaders of the infiltration into Kibbutz Nir Oz during the murderous October 7 Massacre . Sabah also led and advanced numerous terrorist attacks against IDF troops throughout the current war , " it added . <p> The Israeli forces further said , " The IDF and ISA will continue to operate against all of the terrorists who took part in the murderous October 7 Massacre @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ launched a massive terror attack on Israel , killing over 1,200 people and taking more than 250 hostages . Around 100 hostages remain in captivity , with many feared dead . Israel retaliated with a multi-directional attack on Hamas across the Gaza Strip leading to an all-out war with the outfit . <p> Earlier , the IDF reported that its units , working with the Shin Bet ( Israel 's General Security Service ) , had killed 14 Hamas terrorists , six of whom had participated in the October 7 massacre . These operations were carried out as part of the IDF 's 162nd " Steel " Division 's ongoing activity in the Gaza Strip . <p> As per a report , the 162nd Division has been operating in the areas of Jabalia and Beit Lahia , as a joint effort of the IDF and Shin Bet to locate and eliminate terrorists involved in the October 7 attack .
