Here are the details about the hostages . <h> Romi Gonen <p> Romi , from Kfar Veradim in northern Israel , had traveled to the Nova festival in the Negev Desert in southern Israel when the attack occurred . Over 360 people were killed at the festival when Hamas militants crossed the border . Romi called her family during the attack , and her mother , Meirav , recalls hearing gunshots and shouting in Arabic during their final conversation .
