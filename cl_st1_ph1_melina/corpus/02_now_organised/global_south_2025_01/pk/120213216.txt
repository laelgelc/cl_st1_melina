<h> Israel pulls back forces in Rafah as Gaza ceasefire starts today after 460 days of bloodshed <p> JERUSALEM -- Israeli armed forces have started gradual withdrawal of troops from Gaza after reaching a cease-fire deal with the resistance group Hamas , in what is said to be the end of bloody war that killed nearly 47,000 victims and left the region in ruins . <p> Reports in international media said IDF started their withdrawal from Gaza 's Rafah area , moving towards Philadelphi corridor along the Egypt-Gaza border , as ceasefire agreement is set to take effect today . <p> The multi-phase ceasefire will start on Sunday , with the first phase lasting six weeks . As part of the deal , nearly three dozen hostages will be released in exchange for nearly 2,000 Palestinian prisoners , including those convicted of attacks that killed dozens of Israelis . <p> Hostage releases will done every 7 days , with four more female hostages set to be freed after a week .
