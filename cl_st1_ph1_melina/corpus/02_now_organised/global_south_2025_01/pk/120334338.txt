I do n't know who sold the homeland , but I know who paid the price . " These lines resonate with the current reality of Palestinians . <p> Though the ceasefire has ended , families in Gaza dream of returning from refugee camps to their destroyed homes , hoping their lives might somehow resume . Tragically , this hope often clashes with the grim reality of relentless bombing . Darwish 's words reflect a recurring history -- Palestinians displaced from their homeland , only to return to find devastation , as was the case during his lifetime .
