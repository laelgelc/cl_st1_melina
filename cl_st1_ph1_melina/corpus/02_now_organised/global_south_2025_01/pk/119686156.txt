<p> Germany has called on Israel to grant the International Red Cross access to the director of Gaza 's Kamal Adwan Hospital , Dr. Hussam Abu Safiya , who has been detained since December 27 . <p> The German government insisted that he be treated in accordance with international standards , granted access to legal support , and allowed contact with the Red Cross . However , Germany did not demand his release . <p> Dr Abu Safiya 's detention followed a raid by Israeli forces on the hospital , which remains non-operational . The World Health Organization ( WHO ) also echoed calls for his release , with WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus stressing the urgent need for hospitals and healthcare professionals to be protected in Gaza .
