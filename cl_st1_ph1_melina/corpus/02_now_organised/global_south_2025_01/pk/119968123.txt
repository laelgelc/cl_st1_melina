<h> Warplanes Must Be Silent <p> Palestinian writer Marwan Makhoul once wrote , " In order for me to write poetry that is n't political , I must listen to the birds , and in order to hear the birds , the warplanes must be silent . " <p> Makhoul 's words poignantly capture the tragic reality of Gaza , where nowhere is safe from the bombing of warplanes . Even the birds are silenced , unable to sing their morning songs amidst the destruction of entire neighbourhoods . The once-familiar sounds of nature have been replaced by the deafening roar of bombs . Daily life in Gaza has been irreparably altered , with people struggling to survive and birds left without trees to perch on . <p> Makhoul 's lines may seem poetic , but they are a call for justice and peace . The global community must intervene to silence the warplanes , allowing children to sleep , writers to create , and birds to sing once more .
