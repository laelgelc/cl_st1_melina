<p> The act of boycotting brands is referred to as " political consumerism . " According to the political scientists Dietlind Stolle and Michele Micheletti , political consumerism refers to the use of the market as an arena for politics , in order to change institutional or market practices found to be ethically , environmentally or politically objectionable . <p> Political consumerism can also be about people deliberately purchasing brands to reward them . This is referred to as " buycotting " . For example , in 2023 , millennials and Gen Z , the core consumers of the American ice cream brand Ben &amp; Jerry 's , increased their consumption of the brand due to its overt support for ' progressive causes . ' Ben &amp; Jerry 's ' reward ' in this regard was that it became 2023 's leading ice cream brand in the US . <p> Boycotting or buycotting brands are both political acts .
