Email : amoonis@hotmail.com <p> A narrow strip , 350 km long and 34 km wide , located at very end of Afghanistan 's Badakhshan province , Wakhan corridor is a legacy of 1893 Durand Line agreement drawn during the British era in order to create a buffer between Russian and British empires . With Tajikistan located in the north , Pakistan in the south and China in extreme east , Wakhan corridor has recently emerged as a regional flashpoint . <p> Having a population of hardly 12,000 people but with enormous significance , Wakhan corridor is the only geographical impediment to establishing a direct link between Pakistan and Central Asia . Without their consent and support , Pakistan 's ostensible ambition can not translate into reality .
