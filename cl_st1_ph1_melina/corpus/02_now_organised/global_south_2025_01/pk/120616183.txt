<p> CAIRO : Two Hamas officials Wednesday accused Israel of delaying the delivery of vital humanitarian aid to Gaza , as agreed in the ceasefire deal , and warned that it could impact the release of hostages . <p> " We warn that continued delays and failure to address these points ( delivery of key aid ) will affect the natural progression of the agreement , including the prisoner exchange , " a senior Hamas official told AFP , speaking on condition of anonymity . <p> Another official , also speaking on condition of anonymity , said the group had asked mediators to intervene in the issue . <p> Hamas officials said Israel was failing to send key aid items - such as fuel , tents , heavy machinery and other equipment - into the Gaza Strip , as agreed for the first stage of the ceasefire that took effect on January 19 . <p> " According to the agreement , these materials were supposed to enter during the first week of the ceasefire , " the senior Hamas official said . <p> " There is dissatisfaction among the resistance @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ implement the terms of the ceasefire , particularly regarding the humanitarian aspects . " <p> The two officials said the group raised the issue during an ongoing meeting with Egyptian mediators in Cairo on Wednesday . <p> " We hope and call on the mediators and guarantors to do everything possible to ensure that the occupation implements the terms of the agreement and allows the entry of these materials , " the senior official said . <p> The latest warning by Hamas comes as the group is expected to release three hostages on Thursday , including two women . <p> A further three hostages are set to be released on Saturday . <p> Israel and Hamas are currently implementing the first 42-day phase of a ceasefire that aims to end the war in Gaza . <p> Under the deal , seven Israeli hostages have already been released in exchange for 290 prisoners -- almost all of them Palestinian , except for one Jordanian .
