<p> As part of the ceasefire agreement , Hamas handed over eight Israeli hostages to the Red Cross today , paving the way for the release of 110 Palestinian prisoners from Israeli jails . <p> According to Israeli media , Hamas freed an Israeli female soldier , five Thai citizens , and two more Israeli women from Gaza . The 20-year-old soldier , Agam Berger , was released from the Jabalia refugee camp , followed by 29-year-old Erbel Yehud and 80-year-old Gadi Moses . <p> In a surprising move , Hamas chose the destroyed home of its slain leader , Yahya Sinwar , as the location for the handover . A large crowd of Palestinians gathered there , mourning their fallen leader . <p> Despite the destruction , Hamas fulfilled its commitment with patience and discipline , ensuring the safe transfer of hostages to the Red Cross . <p> Last week , Hamas freed four Israeli female soldiers in exchange for 200 Palestinian prisoners . This hostage-prisoner exchange marks the first phase of the Gaza ceasefire agreement brokered in Qatar .
