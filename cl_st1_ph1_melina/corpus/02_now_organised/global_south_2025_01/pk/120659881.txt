<p> Hamas has confirmed the death of its top military commander , Mohammed Deif , months after Israel claimed to have killed him in an airstrike . The announcement was made by Abu Obeida , the spokesman for the Qassam Brigades , the armed wing of Hamas , on Thursday . <p> Deif , a key figure in Hamas for over three decades , was reported to have orchestrated multiple attacks against Israel and played a crucial role in the development of the group 's tunnel network and bomb-making operations . <p> He had led the Qassam Brigades for more than 20 years and was one of its original founders in the 1990s . <p> In July , the Israeli military stated it had killed Deif in an air attack , but Hamas had not confirmed his death until now . Abu Obeida described his passing as " befitting , " saying , " How , by God , could Mohammed Deif be mentioned in history without the title ' martyr ' and without the medal of martyrdom ? " <p> On October 7 , 2023 , Deif issued a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , linking the Hamas-led attacks on southern Israel to Israeli raids at Al-Aqsa Mosque in Jerusalem . <p> In November , the International Criminal Court ( ICC ) issued an arrest warrant for Deif for alleged crimes against humanity and war crimes . The ICC also issued warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant over Israel 's military actions in Gaza . <p> Hamas also confirmed the deaths of deputy military commander Marwan Issa , along with several other senior figures , including Ghazi Abu Tamaa , commander of weapons and combat services ; Raed Thabet , commander of manpower and head of the supplies unit ; and Rafei Salama , commander of the Khan Younis Brigade . <p> Israel claimed to have killed Issa in March , though Hamas had previously denied confirming his death .
