<h> Hamas releases 4 female Israeli soldiers under Gaza ceasefire deal <p> Palestinian resistance group Hamas handed over four female Israeli soldiers on Saturday under a Gaza ceasefire and prisoner exchange agreement with Israel . <p> The four freed soldiers wearing the Israeli military uniform smiled and waved on a stage at Palestine Square in Gaza City as they were transferred to representatives of the International Committee of the Red Cross ( ICRC ) . <p> The Israeli army confirmed that the four freed soldiers were handed over to its forces in Gaza before being transferred to Israel . <p> During the handover process , ICRC representatives were taken on stage to sign a document ahead of the release of the four soldiers . <p> Thousands of Palestinians gathered at the Palestine Square to witness the handover ceremony of the four Israeli soldiers , including dozens of fighters from Hamas ' armed wing , the Qassam Brigades . <p> They are members of the Israeli army 's surveillance unit at the Nahal Oz base . <p> Some 200 Palestinian prisoners are set to be freed in exchange for the four @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Gaza ceasefire agreement took effect on Jan. 19 , suspending Israel 's genocidal war that has killed nearly 47,300 Palestinians , mostly women and children , and injured more than 111,400 since Oct. 7 , 2023 . <p> The three-phase ceasefire agreement includes a prisoner exchange and sustained calm , aiming for a permanent truce and the withdrawal of Israeli forces from Gaza . <p> The Israeli onslaught has left more than 11,000 people missing , with widespread destruction and a humanitarian crisis that has claimed the lives of many elderly people and children in one of the worst global humanitarian disasters ever . <p> The International Criminal Court ( ICC ) issued arrest warrants in November last year for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former Defense Minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza .
