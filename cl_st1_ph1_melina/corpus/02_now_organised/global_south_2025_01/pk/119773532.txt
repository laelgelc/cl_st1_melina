<h> Trudeau 's Legacy <p> One of Canada 's longest-serving prime ministers and its most popular leader in recent history , Justin Trudeau , announced on Monday that he will step down as the leader of the ruling Liberal Party after nine years in office . However , he will remain in his post until the party selects a replacement . Trudeau , who came to power in 2015 , was often seen alongside figures like President Barack Obama , embodying the liberal face of North America as modern , articulate , and charismatic democratic leaders . Yet , as time has passed , Trudeau 's legacy has grown increasingly contentious . <p> Canada , once celebrated as a tranquil , snow-covered nation known for its kindness and hospitality , now faces significant turmoil .
