<h> Temporary pause <p> By News Desk <p> January 21 , 2025 <p> After 15 months of brutal conflict that resulted in the deaths of almost 50,000 Palestinians , a ceasefire has finally been reached in Gaza . This devastating war caused significant casualties on both sides , along with the complete destruction of Gaza 's infrastructure . From the outset , Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu had vowed to eliminate Hamas . However , neutralising a non-conventional military force that operates using guerrilla warfare tactics proved to be a formidable challenge . Hamas remained resilient throughout the conflict . <p> For the Palestinian people , who have endured extreme suffering , the ceasefire brings a sense of relief and hope . But while the truce offers a temporary and fragile peace , the broader challenges of achieving a lasting resolution to the conflict and Israel 's illegal occupation and genocide of Palestine remain uncertain .
