<p> LONDON : Malaysia and the United Kingdom are quite optimistic about securing a settlement on the issue of Palestine and Gaza as soon as possible , says Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim . <p> The Prime Minister said the matter was discussed during his meeting with his UK counterpart , Keir Starmer , at No. 10 Downing Street here Wednesday ( Jan 15 ) . <p> " We discussed the resolution of the Palestine and Gaza issue , with the final ( resolution ) of them achieving the two-state solution , " he said . <p> " But prior to that , an immediate ceasefire . We are quite optimistic about securing a settlement as soon as possible , " he told the media after the meeting . <p> Anwar 's criticism of Western countries ' lack of action against Israel 's atrocities in Gaza has garnered support from prominent figures across various fields globally . <p> The government led by Starmer , which came to power six months ago , has meanwhile suspended the sale of certain weapons to Israel and has called for accelerated humanitarian aid to Gaza @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , as of Jan 8 , 2025 , over 47,000 people have been reported killed since the war began in Gaza in October 2023. - Bernama
