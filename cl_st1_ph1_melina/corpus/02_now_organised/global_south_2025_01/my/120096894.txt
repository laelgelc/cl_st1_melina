<h> Gaza ceasefire to begin Sunday : Qatar PM <p> ISTANBUL : Qatari Prime Minister and Minister for Foreign Affairs Mohammed bin Abdulrahman announced Wednesday evening the success of mediators in reaching a ceasefire agreement in the Gaza Strip , noting that its implementation will begin this Sunday , Anadolu Agency ( AA ) reported . <p> During a press conference held in Doha , Mohammed said that the first phase of the agreement will last 42 days and include the release of 33 Israeli detainees in exchange for an unspecified number of Palestinian prisoners . <p> Mohammed noted that Qatar , Egypt , and the United States ( US ) " will work to ensure the implementation of the agreement , " with mechanisms in place to monitor its execution and address any potential violations . <p> The Qatari minister expressed his hope that " no military operations would be conducted during the period leading up to the agreement 's implementation . " -- BERNAMA-AA
