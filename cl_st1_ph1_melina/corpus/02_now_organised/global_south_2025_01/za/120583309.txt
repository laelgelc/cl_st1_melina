<h> Houthis release Galaxy Leader crew after over a year in captivity <p> Yemen 's Houthis have released the 25 crew of the Bahamas flagged car carrier Galaxy Leader , 14 months after it was seized off Yemen 's Red Sea coast . <p> The crew are from Bulgaria , Ukraine , the Philippines , Mexico , and Romania . They were held captive from 19 November 2023 when Houthi forces boarded the vessel at sea via helicopter and escorted it into the port of Hodeidah . They targeted the vessel due to its apparent links to Israeli billionaire Abraham ' Rami ' Ungar ( the vessel is owned by Galaxy Maritime Ltd , which is owned by Ray Car Carriers , which is co-owned by Ungar ) . <p> International Maritime Organisation ( IMO ) Secretary-General Arsenio Dominguez welcomed the release of the vessel 's crew .
