<h> 48 hours in pictures , 5 January 2025 <h> Through the lens : The Citizen 's Picture Editors select the best news photographs from South Africa and around the world . <p> Members of the Cape Minstrel bands attend the annual ' Tweede Nuwe Yaar ' ( second new year ) carnival through the streets of Cape Town , South Africa , 04 January 2025 . This yearly parade dates back to the mid-nineteenth century when the slaves in Cape Town were granted by their colonial masters one day off in the year . To celebrate , groups would dress up as minstrels , waving parasols , strumming banjos , making music , dancing , and parading from the District Six area through to the city center . Many of the songs still sung today date back to the 1800s .
