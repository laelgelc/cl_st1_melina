<p> CDEDI is pressing the Malawi government to provide clarity on its stance regarding the Lake Malawi boundary dispute with Tanzania , following the receipt of a contentious map from the Tanzanian government . <p> This is in light of the Tanzanian government 's new map outlining new boundaries .
