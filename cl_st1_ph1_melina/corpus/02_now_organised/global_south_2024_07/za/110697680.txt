<h> President welcomes UNSC resolution on Gaza ceasefire <p> Tuesday , March 26 , 2024 <p> President Cyril Ramaphosa is the latest world leader to back the United Nations Security Council ( UNSC ) decision which demands an " immediate ceasefire " between Israel and the Palestinian group Hamas in Gaza . <p> This follows the UNSC 's adoption of Resolution 2728 ( 2024 ) on Monday , which demands an immediate ceasefire in the Gaza Strip for the duration of the Muslim holy month of Ramadan , ending in two weeks . <p> The United States abstained from the vote , with the 14 other council members all voting in favour to none against the Security Council ceasefire resolution . <p> " We must seize the opportunity presented by this Security Council resolution to create a firm foundation for a permanent ceasefire and the resumption of negotiations . We need to stop the carnage and begin walking a path to peace , " said President Ramaphosa in a statement .
