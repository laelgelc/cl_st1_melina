<h> SA stands by decision to file lawsuit against Israel <p> Wednesday , March 13 , 2024 <p> South Africa has no regrets about becoming the first country to file a lawsuit against Israel at the International Court of Justice ( ICJ ) to end its " deadly and relentless genocidal bombardment " of Gaza . <p> This is according to Deputy President Paul Mashatile who attended a question and answer session in the National Council of Provinces on Tuesday . <p> Speaking in Parliament , Deputy President Mashatile said the United Nations estimates that more than 30 000 Palestinians , including over 10 000 children , have been killed . <p> " Over 70 000 have been injured while 10 000 are missing under rubble . This brings the number of civilian casualties to about 110 000 , amounting to roughly 5% of Gaza 's 2.3 million population . We will not condone genocide , " he told Members of Parliament . <p> Last year , South Africa led a case against Israel at the ICJ in The Hague , under the Genocide Convention , for acts committed by Israel in the context of its attacks on Gaza @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the Hamas military and political organisation massacred at least 1 200 people and abducted 240 others during surprise attacks on its territory . <p> In January 2024 , the ICJ ordered Israel to , among others , take all measures within its power " to prevent and punish the direct and public incitement to commit genocide " and to immediately ensure that Palestinians have access to basic services and humanitarian assistance . <p> While South Africa had called for the suspension of Israeli military operations in Gaza , the court did not grant this provisional measure . <p> " Similarly , I must reiterate South Africa 's condemnation of Hamas for the indiscriminate and wanton violence meted against Israeli civilians , " the country 's second-in-command added . <p> " We are mindful that not every citizen supports our government 's decision to refer the Gaza bombardment to the ICJ . <p> " We are also aware that some , here at home and abroad , started by opposing our position .
