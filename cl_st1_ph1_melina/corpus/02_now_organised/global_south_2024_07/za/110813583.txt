<p> The canal is a crucial source of foreign currency for Egypt , which is currently grappling with a severe financial crisis . <p> Since November , the Huthis have launched numerous attacks on ships in the Gulf of Aden and Red Sea . <p> The Iran-backed group says the attacks are aimed at vessels with links to Israel in solidarity with Palestinians in the war-torn Gaza Strip . <p> The attacks have forced many major shipping firms to suspend passage through the Red Sea , which usually carries about 10 percent of global trade , and divert their vessels thousands of miles around Africa . <p> The canal recorded " during the financial year 2023/24 the passage of 20,148 ships , generating revenues of $7.2 billion , compared with 25,911 ships during the financial year 2022/23 , with revenues of $9.4 billion , " the Suez Canal Authority said in a statement . <p> The financial year in Egypt runs from the beginning of July to the end of June of the following year . <p> Oussama Rabie , the president of the public body that administers the canal , attributed the decline to " the severe impact @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , because security problems encourage many ship owners and operators to take alternative routes " . <p> The use of these alternative routes has " resulted in an increase in travel times and costs " , he was quoted as saying in the statement .
