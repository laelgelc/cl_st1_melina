French President Emmanuel Macron dissolved parliament in June calling for snap elections . <p> The recent run-off elections held on July 7 created the conditions for the emergence of the Nouveau Front Populaire ( New Popular Front ) composed of an alliance of leftist parties which received the largest bloc of parliamentary seats .
