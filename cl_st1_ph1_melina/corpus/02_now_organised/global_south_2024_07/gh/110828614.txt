" <p> In Uganda , people 's livelihoods depend very much on forests , woodlands , and wetlands , as does the country 's tourism sector . Uganda has one of the highest rates of forest loss in the world , and the overall cost of soil erosion and land degradation is estimated at about 17% of the GDP , despite the country 's high percentage of arable land . Forest cover has declined by 1.61% every year , on average , between 2010-2020 , with the depletion of forests on private land due to wood extraction and small-scale agricultural expansion being main drivers of deforestation .
