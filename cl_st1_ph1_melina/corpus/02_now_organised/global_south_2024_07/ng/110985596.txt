<h> Macron says France is ready to host Paris Olympics <p> President Emmanuel Macron said on Monday that France was ready to host the Paris Olympics as he visited the Athletes ' Village four days before the Games begin . <p> " We are ready and we will be ready throughout the Games , " Macron said . <p> " We have been working on these Games for years now and we are at the start of a decisive week which on Friday will see the opening ceremony and then the Olympiad which will be held in Paris , 100 years since the last one . " <p> He added , " This is the fruit of an immense amount of work which has profoundly changed the country , in particular the area " of Seine-Saint-Denis , where the Athletes ' Village is situated .
