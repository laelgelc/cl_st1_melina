<h> Paris 2024 : Pro-Palestinian protests mar Israel 's Olympic match <p> Football fans unfurled Palestinian flags to protest Israel during a match at the Paris 2024 Olympic Games on Wednesday . Israel drew 1-1 with Mali in an Olympic football match at Paris ' Parc des Princes Stadium , where many fans in the stands waved Palestinian flags in protest . <p> Before the kick-off , some sections of the crowd booed the Israeli national anthem . Despite the protests , the match was held without major security incidents . <p> Israel has faced international condemnation amid its continued offensive on Gaza following the October 7 , 2023 , attack by Hamas . According to local health authorities , over 39,000 Palestinians have been killed , mostly women and children , and nearly 90,000 injured . <p> Earlier this week , the Palestinian Olympic body called on the International Olympic Committee to exclude Israel from the Paris Olympics .
