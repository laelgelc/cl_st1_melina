<h> Aliko Dangote not above parliament -- Reps panel <p> The House of Representatives Committee investigating the arbitrary increase in cement prices has stated that Alhaji Aliko Dangote is not above the parliament . <p> The joint Committees on Commerce , Industry , Special Duties , and Solid Minerals had summoned the management of Dangote Cement for questioning over alleged arbitrary increase in the prices of cement in Nigeria . <p> The committee , therefore , threatened to arrest the management of Dangote Cement if it failed to appear at its next hearing . <p> This followed the absence of officials of the cement company during an investigative hearing to address the problem of cement increase in the country . <p> The special panel , led by Rep. Jonathan Gaza , while advocating for the downward review of cement prices , expressed worry over the increase in cement prices . <p> According to the committee : " No single individual is bigger than the parliament of his country .
