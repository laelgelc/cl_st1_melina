<p> The House of Representatives has urged the Revenue Mobilisation , Allocation and Fiscal Commission ( RMAFC ) to withhold allocations from the Federation Account to local government areas being run without elected officials but caretaker committees . <p> The call followed the adoption of a motion of Urgent Public Importance sponsored by Hon. Ademorin Kuye ( APC , Lagos ) and Jonathan Gaza Gbewfi ( SDP , Nasarawa ) at plenary on Tuesday . <p> Moving the motion , Kuye expressed concern that the dissolution of democratically elected council officials directly contravenes Section 7 of the Nigerian Constitution , the Supreme Court pronouncement on such matters and a deliberate affront on democracy . <p> " Worried is the increasing number of states acting with impunity and constitutional disregard as about 21 states governors are currently running councils with caretaker committees <p> " Dismayed that this impunity and constitution disregard is a deliberate effort to upstage democracy , shrink the development potential of local councils , enshrine lack of accountability and limit transparency in local governments and the state as a whole , " he added .
