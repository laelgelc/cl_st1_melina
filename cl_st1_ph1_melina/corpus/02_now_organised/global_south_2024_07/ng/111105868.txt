<h> Nasarawa to Vaccinate .9m Children in 2024 <h> Nasarawa to Vaccinate .9m Children in 2024 <p> Wednesday , July 24 , 2024 11:24 am <p> Dr. Gaza Gwamna.jpg <p> NKRUMAH BANKONG-OBI/Lafia <p> Nasarawa State government says plans have been concluded to commence the seasonal malaria chemoprevention , SMC , for the fourth year in a row . During the period , nine hundred and eighty-five thousand seven hundred and eighty-eight children of age three to fifty-nine months will be vaccinated against one of the six killer diseases in Africa . The prevention strategy is to be implemented through the process of chemoprevention . The state commissioner of Health , Gaza Gwamna gave this overview during a press briefing , on Monday , in Lafia , the State capital to mark the commencement of the exercise . <p> The chemoprevention which is endorsed by the World Health Organisation and supported by Malaria Consortium , Dr Gwamna said , has already reduced the malaria disease burden in the State by 15 per cent after the three previous administrations . <p> Technically , the drug combination is found to be safe as it is not new to healthcare providers . " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ us and has been in use to prevent malaria amongst pregnant women during pregnancy , the Sulfadoxine -- Pyrimethamine , popularly known as Fansidar , or Amalar , Maldox and Amodiaquine . We are conversant with these drugs and they are technically known to be safe , effective as well as tolerated by children in the respective concentrations used in the formulation , " Dr. Gwamna noted . <p> By design , the medication will be distributed to healthy kids within the specified age range across the thirteen Local Government Areas of the State . Requisite personnel , Dr. Gwamna said , have been trained for that purpose , including eleven thousand , seven hundred and fifty-five ad hoc staff drawn from the communities , trained and equipped for the interventionist exercise . The plan which follows a categorisation of the personnel , Gwamna said , will see some of them , the Community Drug Distributors , go house-to-house to deliver the drugs . There are also Town Announcers , The Lead Mothers , The Supervisors and the Health Facility Workers .
