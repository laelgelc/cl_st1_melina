<h> World <h> US-British coalition launches airstrike on Yemen 's Kamaran Island <p> SANAA -- The US-British naval coalition launched an airstrike on Kamaran Island , the largest Yemeni island in the Red Sea , the Houthi-run al-Masirah TV reported . <p> The television did not provide further details , but the island 's residents said on social media that the strike hit a Houthi explosive drone boat on Saturday , reports Xinhua news agency . <p> The US-British coalition has not yet commented on the alleged strike . <p> Kamaran Island is located off the Yemeni port city of Hodeidah . The island and the port city are currently under Houthi control . <p> The Houthi group , which controls much of northern Yemen , began in November 2023 to launch anti-ship ballistic missiles and drones targeting what it said were Israeli-linked ships transiting the Red Sea , to show solidarity with the Palestinians under attack by Israel in the Gaza Strip . <p> In response , the US-British naval coalition stationed in the waters has since January conducted air raids and missile strikes against Houthi targets to deter the group .
