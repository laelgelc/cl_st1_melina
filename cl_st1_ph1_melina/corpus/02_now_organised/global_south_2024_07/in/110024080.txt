This ends 14 years of Conservative rule scarred by chaos and controversy . The scale of the victory that Labour has been handed -- sweeping to power with 412 seats , well past the majority mark -- makes this a historic election for the party . Coming five years after its worst poll defeat in a century , it is also a reminder that a volatile electorate that seems to have less enthusiasm for Labour than fury for the Tories could very well swing away from it again in 2029 .
