However it should be pointed out that any such awkwardness is very far from the real issues on the basis of which the performance of any country 's leadership should be examined , particularly in the context of the most powerful and influential countries whose top leadership can be said to constitute the world leadership for all practical purposes . <p> The USA is certainly the most powerful and influential country in the world . Despite being very powerful and very rich , its important human and social development indicators are low in comparison to other comparably rich countries and have suffered recent declines . This is true of such leading indicators as maternal mortality rate , infant mortality rate and life expectancy .
