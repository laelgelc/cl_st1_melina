<p> New Delhi : In a pointed message to China amidst its maritime disputes with Southeast Asian countries , external affairs minister S. Jaishankar emphasised the need for an " effective " code of conduct in the South China Sea , aligned with international law to secure sea lines of communication ( SLOC ) in the South China Sea which he said are critical for peace and stability of the Indo-Pacific region , amidst growing concerns about China 's maritime coercion . <p> SLOC is a term describing the primary maritime routes between ports , used for trade , logistics and naval forces . <p> Speaking at the 14th East Asia Summit ( EAS ) foreign ministers meeting in Vientiane , Laos , Jaishankar highlighted the critical importance of the SLOC . The " Code of Conduct should be substantive and effective , consistent with international law and should not prejudice legitimate rights and interest of nations not party to discussions , " he said . <p> Jaishankar 's comments hold significance as his Chinese counterpart Wang Yi is also in Vientiane to attend the summit .
