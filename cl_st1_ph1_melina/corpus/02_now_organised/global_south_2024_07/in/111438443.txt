<h> Starbucks sales drop larger than expected on China weakness ; profit in line <p> Starbucks sales drop larger than expected on China weakness ; profit in line <p> July 30 - Starbucks reported a bigger-than-expected drop in third-quarter comparable sales on Tuesday , dragged by stubbornly weak demand in the U.S. and a 14% drop in its second-biggest market , China . <p> The coffee chain 's global comparable sales fell 3% in the quarter , compared with a 2.35% decline expected on average by analysts , according to LSEG data . <p> However , Starbucks reported a profit that was in line with estimates . The company 's shares , which have fallen 22% so far this year were last up 1% in choppy trading after the bell . <p> Starbucks , like other U.S. fast-food chains , has rolled out promotions to bring back to stores consumers looking to cook at home due to sticky inflation , while it has offered cheaper options in a weak macroeconomic environment in China . <p> International same-store sales fell 7% in the third quarter , compared with expectations of a 4.3% drop .
