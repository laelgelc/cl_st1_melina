<p> The Israeli army took journalists on a visit to Gaza on Wednesday ( July 3 ) , driving along the southern border area with Egypt and showing a tunnel shaft uncovered in Rafah . Israeli military chief spokesperson , Rear Admiral Daniel Hagari , who accompanied the journalists , stood next to a tunnel shaft which he said was part of a network of tunnels built by Hamas in civilian areas and proximity to the Egyptian border . Hagari said the tunnels were used by Hamas to smuggle weapons into Gaza and store them , to fire mortars and rockets at Israel , and to allow cover for militants in hostile activities against Israel . He said the army would destroy those elements . Rafah , the southernmost city in Gaza , has been the focus of an Israeli assault since May . The operation has displaced some 800,000 Palestinians from Rafah , as estimated by the UN .
