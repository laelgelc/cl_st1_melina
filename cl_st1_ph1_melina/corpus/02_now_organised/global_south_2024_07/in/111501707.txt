A Sunni Muslim , Ismail Haniyeh had a major hand building up Hamas 's fighting capacity , partly by fostering ties with Shi'ite Muslim Iran . Three of his sons - Hazem , Amir and Mohammad - were killed on April 10 when an Israeli air strike struck the car they were driving .
