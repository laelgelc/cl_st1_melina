<h> ' Israel has made largest West Bank land seizure in three decades ' <p> An anti-settlement watchdog says Israel has approved the largest seizure of land in the occupied West Bank in over three decades . <p> The Israeli group Peace Now said Wednesday that authorities recently approved the appropriation of 12.7 sq km of land in the Jordan valley . The group 's data indicate it was the largest single appropriation approved since the 1993 Oslo accords at the start of the peace process . <p> The land appropriation was likely to worsen already soaring tensions linked to the ongoing Israel-Hamas war in Gaza .
