Although there has been fire exchange between Israel and Hezbollah for over 8 months , its intensity has increased very recently , giving rise this to this question being asked more frequently . <p> Of course what the Middle-East region needs the most is the immediate resolving of the Gaza conflict , based on immediate and permanent ceasefire plus release of hostages and prisoners , and not any escalation or widening of the conflict .
