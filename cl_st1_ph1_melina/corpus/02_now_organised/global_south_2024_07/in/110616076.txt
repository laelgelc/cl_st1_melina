<h> Israel Intensifies Strikes on Gaza Amid Renewed Ceasefire Talks <h> Israel has ramped up strikes on the Gaza Strip , targeting Hamas leaders and causing significant civilian casualties . The offensive follows heightened military actions , including attacks on densely populated areas and camps . Despite the violence , talks for a ceasefire continue , with both sides appearing committed to negotiations . <p> Israel intensified its strikes on the Gaza Strip on Monday , aiming to exert more pressure on Hamas following a deadly weekend attack that targeted the militant group 's leadership . The strike killed scores of Palestinians seeking shelter in a makeshift camp . <p> Two days after the assault turned a crowded area of Mawasi into a devastated landscape , displaced survivors struggled to find refuge . ' Where to go is what everybody asks , and no one has the answer , ' said Aya Mohammad , 30 , who survived the attack . <p> Despite the violence , ceasefire talks between Israel and Hamas have not been abandoned , with U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken urging both sides to reduce civilian casualties while continuing negotiations .
