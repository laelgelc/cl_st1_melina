<p> Israeli government official says delegation sent to negotiate hostage deal with Hamas <h> Israeli government official says delegation sent to negotiate hostage deal with Hamas <h> Under a deal brokered in late November by the United States , Qatar and Egypt , more than 100 of the estimated 240 hostages taken to Gaza during an attack by Hamas-led militants on Oct. 7 were freed in exchange for the release of 240 Palestinians held in Israeli prisons . <p> An Israeli government official said on Thursday that a delegation has been sent to negotiate a hostage release deal with Hamas . <p> Under a deal brokered in late November by the United States , Qatar and Egypt , more than 100 of the estimated 240 hostages taken to Gaza during an attack by Hamas-led militants on Oct. 7 were freed in exchange for the release of 240 Palestinians held in Israeli prisons . <p> Since then , Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has faced mounting pressure to secure the release the 136 hostages who remain in captivity .
