The operation aims to evacuate 148 individuals , including 85 sick and severely injured Palestinians , along with 63 accompanying family members , to Abu Dhabi . This effort , which includes cancer patients needing extensive treatment , underscores the UAE 's humanitarian commitment to the Palestinian people amidst the ongoing crisis . <p> The evacuation will proceed through a coordinated route starting from the Ramon airport in Israel and passing through the Karam Abu Salem crossing .
