<h> Early End of Conflicts Essential but Elusive <h> Share : <p> The most worrying aspect of some extremely costly and threatening conflicts in recent times has been that despite their high costs , these seem to be getting more and more prolonged . The added risk is that the more prolonged there are , there are increased chances of escalation and a wider conflict which would be even more threatening for world peace . <p> In the case of the Gaza conflict at least several efforts for achieving ceasefire and peace have been made ( all such efforts have also included the release of Israeli hostages ) and for a considerable stretch of time there were reports on almost daily basis regarding the progress ( or not ) of these efforts . Qatar and Egypt in particular have been involved more in these efforts but they have been seeking the cooperation of others too , particularly the USA , in their efforts . The USA also claimed to have taken the initiative for peace at times . Then , even more significantly , there have been UN resolutions calling for ceasefire . In addition there have been several reports regarding the increasing violence and aggression in the West Bank region also . <p> While in Gaza at least peace-making efforts have been seen somewhat frequently even though without raising much hope , in the Russia-Ukraine conflict such visibility of any serious efforts is even less . Of course a very significant peace effort was made at a very early stage of the war which had reached a very advanced stage and raised hope of ending this war with all its dangers just within two months or so , but these peace efforts were sabotaged by very powerful forces .
