<h> World <p> JERUSALEM -- Israel 's army said on Sunday it has intercepted a surface-to-surface missile launched by Houthi forces in the Red Sea targeting the resort city Eilat , amid an escalation between the Yemenite forces and Israel . <p> The attack came a day after Israeli airstrikes killed and injured several people in the Houthi-held Red Sea port city of Hodeidah , in retaliation for a drone attack that killed a man and injured about 10 people in Tel Aviv . <p> The Houthis have claimed responsibility for the attack . Yahya Saree , the group 's spokesman , said in a statement on social network platform X that " a number of ballistic missiles " targeted a military site in Eilat . <p> He added that ballistic missiles and drones also targeted the American ship " Pumba " in the Red Sea . The US military confirmed intercepting a drone fired by the Houthis over the Red Sea . <p> Saree vowed that the attacks " will not stop until the aggression ceases and the siege on the Palestinian people in the Gaza Strip is lifted . "
