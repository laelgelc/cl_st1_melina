<p> Gaza : At least 25 Palestinians were killed and some others wounded in an Israeli airstrike targeting a school in the city of Khan Younis in the southern Gaza Strip , Palestinian sources said . <p> Security sources told Xinhua news agency on Tuesday that Israeli aircraft targeted the gate of the Al-Awda School , which houses hundreds of displaced people in the town of Abasan al-Kabira , east of Khan Younis , using at least one missile . <p> The videos shared by Palestinian activists on the social media platform Facebook showed dozens of bodies lying on the ground , covered in blood , Xinhua news agency reported . <p> Medical sources said the Israeli airstrike killed at least 25 people , including women and children , and wounded dozens in varying degrees . <p> The sources told Xinhua that the number of victims is expected to rise due to the overcrowding of displaced people in the area . <p> There was no immediate comment from the Israeli military about the incident .
