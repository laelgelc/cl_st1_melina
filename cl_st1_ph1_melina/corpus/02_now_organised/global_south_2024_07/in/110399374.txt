<h> NSA Ajit Doval and his US counterpart Jake Sullivan discussed bilateral , regional , and international issues , including upcoming Quad engagements . <p> National Security Adviser Ajit Doval on Friday held a telephone conversation with his US counterpart Jake Sullivan .
