<h> Golan Heights attack : Funerals of 11 children held amid fear of Israel-Hezbollah war escalation <p> Funerals for 11 children who died in the Golan Heights attack were held . Photo Courtesy : IDF X page <p> The funerals of eleven children , who died during a rocket attack on a football field in the Israeli-occupied Golan Heights , were held on Sunday . <p> Israel has blamed the Saturday 's attack on the Druze town of Majdal Shams on the Lebanese militant group Hezbollah , though Hezbollah has strongly denied any involvement , BBC reported . <p> Following the attack , Israeli Defense Forces chief Herzi Halevi was quoted as saying by IDF X page : " We know exactly where the rocket was launched from today ... This is a Hezbollah rocket , and whoever launches such a rocket into a built-up area wants to kill civilians , wants to kill children . " <h> Israel 's Defence Minister says Hezbollah will pay the price <p> Israel Defence Minister Yoav Gallant vowed revenge and wrote on X : " We mourn the innocent boys and girls killed in Majdal Shams.There are 150,000 Druze in Israel , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We live side by side and all suffer from Hezbollah 's terror.We will ensure Hezbollah , the proxy of Iran , pays a price for this loss . " <p> The escalating tensions risk sparking a full-scale war between Israel and Hezbollah , which have frequently exchanged fire since the onset of the Israel-Gaza conflict in October . <p> Saturday 's incident at the football pitch is the deadliest attack on Israel 's northern border since the conflict began on October 7 , according to the report . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu quickly pledged retaliation against Hezbollah , stating that the group would " pay a heavy price . " <p> Though Israel and Hezbollah have frequently exchanged fire and both sides have incurred casualties since October , they have so far avoided actions that could escalate into a broader war in southern Lebanon .
