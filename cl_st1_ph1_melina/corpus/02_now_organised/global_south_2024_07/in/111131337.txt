<p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu , during his address at the US Congress , emphasized the critical role of U.S. support as Israel continues to navigate complex security challenges . In recent statements , Netanyahu highlighted the longstanding and " generous " military assistance provided by the United States , which he says has been pivotal in Israel 's defense efforts . <p> For decades , Netanyahu noted , this robust support has enabled Israel to offer significant intelligence contributions , which have been instrumental in safeguarding lives . He underscored the collaborative efforts between the U.S. and Israel in developing some of the world 's most advanced weaponry , enhancing the defense capabilities of both nations . <p> Netanyahu expressed his gratitude for American support , yet stressed that the current situation is " exceptional . " He argued that an acceleration of military aid could be crucial in hastening the resolution of the ongoing conflict in Gaza and averting a larger regional war .
