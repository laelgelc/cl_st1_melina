<p> The UNRWA , which has carried out direct relief and works programmes for registered Palestinian refugees since 1950 , is making efforts to keep up its functioning in the middle of the Israel-Hamas war in Gaza . <p> The Indian government has released the first tranche of USD 2.5 million to the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestinian Refugees in the Near East ( UNRWA ) as part of its annual contribution of USD 5 million for the year 2024-25 , the Representative Office of India here said on Monday . <p> The UNRWA , which has carried out direct relief and works programmes for registered Palestinian refugees since 1950 , is making efforts to keep up its functioning in the middle of the Israel-Hamas war in Gaza . <p> The Representative Office of India in Ramallah in its statement on X said : " The Government of India released the first tranche of USD 2.5 million to the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestinian Refugees in the Near East ( UNRWA ) , as part of its annual contribution of USD 5 million for the year 2024-25 .
