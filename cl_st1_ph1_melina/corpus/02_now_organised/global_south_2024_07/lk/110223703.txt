<h> After the war is over <p> People with disabilities are often at greater risk of death and injury in the theatre of war . During the past months we have witnessed many conflicts -- with the wars in Gaza and Ukraine gaining global media attention and also the growing conflict in Sudan . Within these 3 wars are thousands of innocent civilians -- on each of the fighting sides . Disabled people who rely on assistive devices such as wheel-chairs are not able to move to safety without support , and can not make use of vehicles for evacuation , which are often not accessible to them . Blind civilians and those with vision impairment are heavily reliant on sighted people to be able to navigate their evacuation and escape to safety . <p> Challenges such as lack of accessible food distribution points , or consuming lesser portions of food , expose people with disabilities to risk of starvation , dehydration , worsening health conditions , and imminent death .
