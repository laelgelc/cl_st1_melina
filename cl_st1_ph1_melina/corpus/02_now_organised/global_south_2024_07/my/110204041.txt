intergovernmental organisation , BRICS ( Brazil , Russia , India , China and South Africa ) is not to interfere in the domestic and political affairs of the countries involved . <p> On the other hand , he said that Malaysia emphasises the importance of trade , economy and the expansion of networks that can provide benefits to the country and the people , especially traders and investors . <p>
