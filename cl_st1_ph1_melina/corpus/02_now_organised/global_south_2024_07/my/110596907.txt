<p> CAIRO , July 14 -- At least 17 Palestinians were killed and 50 were wounded in Israeli strikes on Gaza City in the early hours this morning , civil emergency and health officials said . <p> The fatalities resulted from at least four separate Israeli airstrikes on four houses in different areas of the city . Residents and Palestinian health officials said the Israeli military had stepped up aerial and ground shelling . <p> Yesterday an Israeli airstrike killed at least 90 Palestinians in a designated humanitarian zone in Gaza , the enclave 's health ministry said . The attack was the deadliest in Gaza for weeks . <p> Israel said the attack targeted Hamas military chief Mohammed Deif but it was uncertain whether he had been killed . Hamas said that Israeli claims it had targeted leaders of the group were false and were aimed at justifying the attack . -- Reuters
