These settlements , in fact , make it much harder to arrive at a negotiated two-state solution , " he said . <p> Balakrishnan said Singapore had consistently voted in support of United Nations ( UN ) General Assembly resolutions that reject @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of all UN General Assembly resolutions calling upon Israel to rescind all unilateral measures to change the status of Jerusalem . <p> Meanwhile , Balakrishnan said Singapore is sending 1,000 tonnes of rice and 300 tonnes of canned sardines to Gaza under its fourth tranche of humanitarian assistance . <p> These essential supplies will be transported via commercial sea freight from Singapore to Larnaca , Cyprus , for onward conveyance to Gaza , in cooperation with the United Arab Emirates ( UAE ) and Cyprus . <p> With this latest tranche of aid , Singapore has to-date contributed more than S$17 million ( RM60 million ) worth of donations for Gaza . <p> The ceremony was also attended by Hawazi , Charg ? d'Affaires at the UAE Embassy in Singapore Dhabia Al Mehairbi , and Honorary Consul of Cyprus in Singapore Raja Bose . -- Bernama
