<h> 9,241 Palestinian students killed in Israeli aggression since Oct 7 <p> GAZA : At least 9,241 students were killed and 15,182 were injured in the Israeli aggression on the Gaza Strip and the West Bank since Oct 7 last year . <p> Palestinian news agency WAFA reported that the Education and High Education Ministry said 9,138 students were killed and 14,671 were injured in the Gaza Strip , while in the West Bank , 103 students were killed and 505 were injured . <p> The ministry said 357 students have been detained in the West Bank . <p> It indicated that 497 teachers and administrators were killed and 3,426 were injured in the Gaza Strip and the West Bank . <p> WAFA reported 353 government schools , universities , university buildings , and 65 buildings belonging to the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Refugees ( UNRWA ) were bombed and vandalised in the Gaza Strip , which led to 139 of them being severely damaged , and 93 completely destroyed . <p> In the West Bank , 69 schools and five universities were stormed and vandalised . <p> It added that 133 government schools were used @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ affirmed that 620,000 students in the Gaza Strip are deprived of enrolling in their schools since the beginning of the aggression , and 88,000 students are deprived of enrolling in their universities . <p> The occupation also deprived 39,000 students from the Gaza Strip of taking their high school examinations . -- BERNAMA-WAFA
