<p> KUALA LUMPUR , July 11 -- Rap is a music genre marked by spoken word over beats , that thrives on rhyme , wordplay , and poetic techniques like metaphors and similes . <p> Its socially-driven lyrics trace back to the Bronx neighbourhood in New York City in the 1970s , forming a core part of hip-hop culture alongside DJing , graffiti , breakdancing , and fashion . <p> Closer to home , in Malaysia , rap burst out of the underground scene in Kuala Lumpur during the early 90s . <p> Krash Kozz , then a four-member group , introduced the Malaysian masses to hip-hop with their well-received mixtape , Pump it Up , in 1989 which was certified by Billboard as the first hip-hop album to be recorded by a Malaysian artiste . <p> Advertisement <p> After trimming down to a trio and releasing a
