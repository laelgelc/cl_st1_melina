<h> Israel strikes southern Gaza after ordering evacuations <p> GAZA STRIP : Israel carried out fresh strikes in southern Gaza on Tuesday , forcing hundreds of Palestinians to flee after the army once again ordered the evacuation of certain densely populated areas . <p> Witnesses reported multiple strikes in and around the city of Khan Yunis , where eight people were killed and more than 30 were wounded , according to a medical source and the Palestinian Red Crescent . <p> The bombardment came after a rare rocket barrage claimed by the militant group Islamic Jihad , which has fought alongside Hamas . <p> The rockets were aimed at Israeli communities near the Gaza border and were fired in retaliation for Israeli " crimes ... against our Palestinian people " , said the Al-Quds Brigades , the armed wing of Islamic Jihad . <p> The Israeli military said about " 20 projectiles were identified crossing from the area of Khan Yunis " , most of which were intercepted .
