<h> Cola Next in turmoil as top executives go missing after abduction of CEO <p> KARACHI -- Senior management of Pakistani local beverage brand Cola Next went missing from Lahore , days after company 's CEO Zulfiqar Ahmed was kidnapped . <p> As police lodged a kidnapping case after judicial intervention to find prominent businessman , the company 's Deputy Manager of Finance , Hassan Nawaz , and General Manager , Daniyal Khan , were also kidnapped . <p> Hassan Nawaz , the Deputy Manager of Finance at Cola Next , was abducted from Lahore , while General Manager Daniyal Khan was kidnapped by armed men in white double-cabin vehicles from Kasur , per reports . <p> The company 's lawyer told media that two senior employees were abducted from Punjab . <p> The abduction follows company 's rise in South Asian nation amid boycott of Western products supporting Israel 's war on Gaza . <p> Company 's CEO , who also manages Paracha Textile Mills and Mezan Group , was taken by armed men who intercepted his vehicle .
