<h> TLP calls off Faizabad sit-in after successful negotiations with govt <p> Tehreek-i-Labbaik Pakistan ( TLP ) on Friday called off Faizabad sit-in as negotiations between the government and TLP remained successful . <p> Addressing a press conference along with TLP leaders , Information Minister Atta Tarar and PM 's advisor Rana Sanaullah said that the government would make every possible effort to help the Palestinian brothers . <p> " We 'll dispatch 1,000 tons aid to Gaza by July 31 , " said the senior cabinet members . <p> Rana Sanaullah said that Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu has committed war crimes in Palestine . We 'll raise our voice at every forum for the Palestinian brothers , he added . <p> He also urged the international community to play its role in stopping the Israeli atrocities in Palestine . <p> The PM 's aide said that the release of Dr Afia Siddiqui will also be ensured as she is facing US ' brutality for years . <p> He also thanked the TLP delegation for calling off the sit-in in Islamabad .
