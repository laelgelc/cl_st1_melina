<h> SBP cuts key policy rate by 100bps to 19.5pc <p> KARACHI -- The State Bank of Pakistan ( SBP ) has cut key policy rate by 100bps to 19.5 per cent , effective from July 30 , 2024 . <p> SBP Governor Jameel Ahmed announced the new policy rate after meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee ( MPC ) on Monday afternoon . He said the MPC reviewed economic developments , and noted a drop in inflation from 38% in May to 12.6% last month . <p> MPC observed that June 's inflation was slightly better than expected and that the FY 2025 budgetary measures ' inflationary impact was as anticipated , he said , and adds that external account has improved , with central bank 's foreign exchange reserves increasing despite substantial debt repayments . <p> The committee sees room for a calibrated reduction in the policy rate to support economic activity while controlling inflation . Market participants had widely expected a rate cut given the lower inflation rate compared to the interest rate . <p> The Committee noted the following key developments since its last meeting .
