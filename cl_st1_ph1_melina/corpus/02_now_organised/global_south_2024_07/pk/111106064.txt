<p> NEW YORK : Oil prices fell about two per cent to a six-week low on Tuesday on rising expectations of a ceasefire in Gaza and growing concerns about demand in China . <p> Brent futures fell $1.39 , or 1.7pc , to settle at $81.01 a barrel , while US West Texas Intermediate crude ( WTI ) closed $1.44 , or 1.8pc , lower at $76.96 . <p> That was the lowest close for Brent and WTI since June 7 and pushed both benchmarks into technically oversold territory for the first time since early June . <p> US diesel futures also settled at their lowest since June 7 , while gasoline futures closed at their lowest since June 14 . <p> In the Middle East , efforts to reach a ceasefire deal between Israel and Hamas under a plan outlined by US President Joe Biden in May and mediated by Egypt and Qatar , have gained momentum over the past month . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu told families of hostages held in Gaza that a deal to secure their release could be near even as fighting raged in the Palestinian enclave .
