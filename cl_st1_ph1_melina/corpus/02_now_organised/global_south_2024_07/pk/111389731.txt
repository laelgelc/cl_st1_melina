<p> In Shakespeare 's Julius Caesar , Cassius complains , " The fault , dear Brutus , is not in our stars , but in ourselves that we are underlings " . <p> The stars have outlived their utility even for the soothsayers , but to be an underling is one of the lethal afflictions that continue to drag human beings to their nemesis . In a crumbling republic , where people are pariahs , one need not be a wizard to know that expatriates are the ones who become the proverbial underlings . <p> On behalf of the Pakistani diaspora , someone had to take up the Sisyphean task of revealing the complete alienation and unfortunate indifference of Pakistani institutions , especially the police , to our plights . <p> Being an academic , a physician , a writer , and an Op-Ed columnist for a few English Dailies , I decided to take this Herculean but unproductive task upon my shoulders , since " Those who are homeless " , Theodor Adorno says , " writing becomes their home " . <p> " Dwelling in the proper sense , " Adorno continues , " is now impossible @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ at home in one 's home " . It is a painful odyssey which may or may not end in pleasure but contains a story worth telling . <p> It is not a story of Helen whose beauty led to the Trojan War nor of the wisdom of Athena who surged into the world by splitting the scalp of Zeus , her father , and the Greek god of the universe .
