<h> Israeli strike on UN shelter school kills 16 Palestinians , mostly women and children <p> RAFAH -- At least sixteen Palestinians , mostly children and women , were killed in an Israeli bombing of a school sheltering displaced people in the Nuseirat area of central Gaza Strip . <p> Details shared by international news agencies said Al-Jaouni school comes under attack for the third time since start of Israeli assault . <p> Israeli defence forces said it was investigating the incident . Gaza government said the UN school was sheltering around 7,000 displaced individuals . Israeli forces have so far attacked over 17 schools and displacement centers in the Nuseirat refugee camp . <p> The air raid targetted upper floors of the school , which is reportedly being used as a shelter by as many as hundreds of people . <p> So far , the number of internally displaced people in Gaza soared to 19lac , approximately 90pc of the population . <p> Amid the unabated attacks , Palestinian death toll reached 38,098 , with 87,705 injuries since October last year .
