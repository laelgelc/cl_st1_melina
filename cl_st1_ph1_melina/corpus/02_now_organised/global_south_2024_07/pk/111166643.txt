<h> Pakistan welcomes Declaration of Unity by Palestinian political factions in Beijing <p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : Pakistan has welcomed the ' Declaration of Unity ' by the Palestinian political factions in Beijing . <p> " We recognize and commend China 's role in bringing the factions together for meaningful negotiations , " the Spokespersons of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Mumtaz Zahra Baloch said on Thursday . <p> The Spokesperson said that unity amongst the Palestinian people is of paramount significance in the wake of the ongoing war crimes against the Palestinian people , particularly in Gaza , and the blatant disregard for international norms by the Israeli occupation forces . <p> Ambassador Mumtaz Zahra Baloch said that Pakistan once again calls upon the international community to intervene immediately to stop the ongoing illegal and incessant aggression in Gaza . <p> " We also reiterate support for the realization of the inalienable right to self-determination of the Palestinian people , their right of return to Palestine and establishment of an independent homeland for the Palestinians , with Al-Quds Al-Sharif as its Capital , " she added .
