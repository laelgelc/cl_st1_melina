<h> Israeli airstrikes claim 64 Palestinian lives , 105 injured in Gaza <p> 07:12 PM 21 Jul , 2024 <p> GAZA - In a devastating escalation of violence , Israeli airstrikes over the past 24 hours have led to the deaths of 64 Palestinians and left 105 others injured in Gaza . The relentless bombardment has reduced several residential buildings to rubble , trapping numerous individuals beneath the debris . <p> According to international news agencies , the Israeli military targeted various residential areas in Gaza with heavy airstrikes . The ongoing recovery operations are uncovering more bodies from the wreckage , with the Palestinian Authority 's Ministry of Health reporting that the majority of the casualties are women and children . <p> The scale of destruction has forced many residents to spend the night along roadsides , as their homes lie in ruins . These displaced individuals are expected to be relocated to refugee camps as soon as possible . <p> This surge in violence follows a recent ruling by the International Court of Justice condemning Israeli aggression and occupation .
