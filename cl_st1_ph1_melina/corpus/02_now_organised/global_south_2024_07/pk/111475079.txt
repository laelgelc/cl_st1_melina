<p> WASHINGTON -- U.S. Vice President and Democratic presidential candidate Kamala Harris expressed deep concern over the deaths in Gaza during a meeting with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu . <p> According to a foreign news agency , Harris emphasized the urgency of a ceasefire in Gaza , stating that the time has come to end the violence . She assured that she would not remain silent on the Gaza issue . <p> During the meeting , the U.S. Vice President urged Prime Minister Netanyahu to take steps towards a ceasefire agreement . Harris highlighted that such actions would alleviate the suffering of Palestinian civilians . <p> Following the meeting , Harris spoke to journalists , saying that she had discussed the humanitarian crisis in Gaza with Netanyahu . <p> She acknowledged Israel 's right to self-defense but stressed the importance of how it exercises that right . <p> The observers said that Harris adopted a stern tone when discussing the Gaza issue . By this , they said , she suggested that if elected , she might take a more aggressive stance towards Netanyahu in the future .
