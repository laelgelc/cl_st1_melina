<h> Interference for me but not thee <p> By News Desk <p> July 02 , 2024 <p> The PTI is celebrating the passing of Resolution 901 by the US Congress . This is the same party whose leader boasted about saying ' absolutely not ' to the US . No doubt , America is a global policeman , with a self-proclaimed right to interfere in other countries ' sovereign affairs . Unfortunately , certain factions in our country welcome such interference if it is in their favour but , sooner or later , when it is used against them , they half-heartedly call out ' absolutely not ' . <p> It is an open secret that lots of money is doled out to get such favours from the senators and the lobbyists in Washington DC . Now , Pakistan is planning to pass a counter resolution to condemn the interference but that would be not enough . Why not ask the why they have not passed any resolution condemning the genocide by Israel in Gaza .
