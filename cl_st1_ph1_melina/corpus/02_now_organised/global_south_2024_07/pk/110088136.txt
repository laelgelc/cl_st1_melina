<h> Gaza peace talks <p> The breakthrough in talks between Israel and Hamas to secure a new peace deal in Gaza has come as a vital reprieve . According to reports , Hamas has agreed to release Israeli hostages 16 days after the first phase of an agreement aimed at ending the war in Gaza . The first phase will be three-week long . The news is a cause for quiet satisfaction ( as obstacles still remain ) for the brutally war-torn Palestinians of Gaza as well as the rest of the world , watching in horror Israel 's genocidal retaliation to Hamas ' October 7 , 2023 , cross-border raid on Israeli settlements . The raid claimed 1,200 lives and 250 settlers and Israeli army personnel were taken captive . In response , Israel has by now killed over 38,000 Palestinians in just Gaza , with thousands more missing under the rubble of buildings relentlessly bombarded . There are no tallies for the number of Palestinians injured in the war . Israeli military excesses have also been reported from the occupied Palestinian territory of West Bank .
