opportunities for Palestinian factions to engage in dialogue for reconciliation . <p> Lin Jian , spokesperson for the ministry , reiterated that China has always supported reconciliation and unity among Palestinian factions through dialogue and consultations . <p> Lin made the comments in response to reports that senior officials of the Palestinian National Liberation Movement ( Fatah ) and the
