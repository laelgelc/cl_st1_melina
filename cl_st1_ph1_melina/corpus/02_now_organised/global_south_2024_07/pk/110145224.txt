<h> A shrinking Palestine <p> Palestine continues to shrink . Israel 's recent approval of its largest land seizure in the occupied West Bank in over 30 years has laid bare the Israeli government 's stark hypocrisy . This audacious move , announced in June , snatches up 12.7 square kilometers of land in the Jordan Valley , marking the biggest land grab since the 1993 Oslo Accords . <p> Regrettably , the history of the region is replete with such betrayals . Since seizing the West Bank , East Jerusalem , and Gaza Strip in the 1967 Arab-Israeli war , Israel has dotted the West Bank with settlements , now home to over 490,000 Israelis . These settlements , illegal under international law , continue to mushroom despite global disapproval , turning the dream of a two-state solution into a distant mirage . This year alone , Israel has grabbed a staggering 23.7 square kilometers in the West Bank . This land grab spree exacerbates an already volatile situation , with both Israeli military and settler violence reaching new heights , especially since the start of the genocide in Gaza .
