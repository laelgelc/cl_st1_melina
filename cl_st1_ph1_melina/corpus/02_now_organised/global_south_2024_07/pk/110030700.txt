<h> Iran holds presidential election runoff <p> TEHRAN - Iran holds a presidential election runoff Friday between Masoud Pezeshkian and Saeed Jalili following a record-low turnout in the first round . <p> The vote comes amid heightened regional tensions over the Gaza war , a dispute with the West over Iran 's nuclear programme and popular discontent over the state of Iran 's sanctions-hit economy . In last week 's first round , Pezeshkian , who was the only reformist permitted to stand , won the largest number of ballots , around 42 percent , while the former nuclear negotiator Jalili came in second place with 39 percent , according to figures from Iran 's elections authority . <p> Only 40 percent of Iran 's 61 million eligible voters turned up at the polls -- the lowest turnout in any presidential election since the 1979 Islamic Revolution . On Wednesday , Iran 's supreme leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei , who wields ultimate authority in the country , called for a higher turnout in the runoff .
