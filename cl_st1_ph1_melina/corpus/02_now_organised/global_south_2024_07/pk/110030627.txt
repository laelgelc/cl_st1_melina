<p> DUBAI/CAIRO : Hamas has accepted a US proposal to begin talks on releasing Israeli hostages , including soldiers and men , 16 days after the first phase of an agreement aimed at ending the Gaza war , a senior Hamas source said on Saturday . <p> Hamas has dropped a demand that Israel first commit to a permanent ceasefire before signing the agreement , and would allow negotiations to achieve that throughout a first six-week phase , the source told Reuters on condition of anonymity because the talks are private . <p> A Palestinian official close to the internationally mediated peace efforts had said the proposal could lead to a framework agreement if embraced by Israel and would end the nine-month-old war between Israel and Hamas in Gaza . <p> The conflict has claimed the lives of more than 38,000 Palestinians , according to Gaza health officials . <p> The Hamas source said the proposal ensures that mediators would guarantee a temporary ceasefire , aid delivery , and withdrawal of Israeli troops as long as indirect talks continue to implement the second phase of the agreement .
