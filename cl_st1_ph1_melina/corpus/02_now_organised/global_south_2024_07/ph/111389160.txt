<h> Fuel prices cut today <p> In separate advisories , Shell , Caltex and Seaoil announced that the per-liter prices of gasoline will go down by P0.75 , diesel by P0.85 and kerosene by P0.80 . <p> The STAR / Walter Bollozos <p> MANILA , Philippines -- Following mixed adjustments last week , oil companies are set to lower pump prices of petroleum products by as much as P0.85 per liter today . <p> In separate advisories , Shell , Caltex and Seaoil announced that the per-liter prices of gasoline will go down by P0.75 , diesel by P0.85 and kerosene by P0.80 . <p> Petro Gazz , Cleanfuel , PTT Philippines and Jetti will apply the same price movements , except for kerosene , which they do not offer . <p> The latest adjustments would bring the year-to-date net increases for gasoline and diesel to P9.60 and P6.90 per liter , respectively . <p> On the other hand , kerosene would have a year-to-date net decrease of P0.30 per liter . <p> According to the Department of Energy ( DOE ) , the movements in domestic prices were due to bearish sentiment in the global oil market , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Likewise , the Gaza ceasefire negotiation threatens to erode the geopolitical risk premium of crude and petroleum products , " the DOE said . <p> The adjustments were also said to have been brought about by the implementation of a 15-day price freeze for kerosene and liquified petroleum gas , after Metro Manila was placed under a state of calamity . <p> Last week , the pump price of gasoline inched up by P0.10 per liter , while that of diesel and kerosene went down by P0.40 and P0.70 per liter , respectively .
