<p> OTTAWA -- The Prime Minister 's Office says the Emir of Qatar will arrive in Ottawa for a two-day visit this week . Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani is set to meet with Prime Minister Justin Trudeau on what will be his first official trip to Canada . The PMO says the two leaders will discuss the ongoing situation in Gaza , as well as Afghanistan and Ukraine . The United States , Qatar and Egypt have spent months trying to mediate a ceasefire in Gaza between Israel and Hamas . The PMO says Al Thani 's visit is also a chance to work on other priorities , including increasing trade and investment . Trudeau says in a statement the two countries are " important partners . " This report by The Canadian Press was first published Sept. 15 , 2024.
