" <p> The resolution is based on a July ruling by the International Court of Justice , which condemned Israel 's rule over lands it captured in 1967 by force , saying this violates international laws . <p> Last December , Canada stopped its decade-long policy of backing Israel in almost any United Nations vote over concerns about the lack of progress toward a humanitarian ceasefire in the war between Israel and Hamas.
