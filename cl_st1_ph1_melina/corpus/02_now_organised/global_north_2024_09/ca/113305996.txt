A Syrian member of parliament , Mohammed Habash , told the pan-Arab daily al-Sharq al-Awsat last Thursday that the listening stations in Syria were no secret . " Syria is doing all it could to defend its territory and is turning to military experts for help , " said Habash , who heads parliament 's Syrian-Iranian relations committee .
