THE CANADIAN PRESS/Adrian Wyld <p> NEW YORK -- Prime Minister Justin Trudeau is laying out a choice for world leaders in a time of increasing instability : bury your heads in the sand or put differences aside for the sake of future generations . In a speech to the Summit of the Future in New York , Trudeau says leaders have a collective responsibility to confront serious global challenges . The summit is taking place today and Monday ahead of the 78th meeting of the United Nations General Assembly . Its goal is to reform the UN , reinvigorate multilateralism and agree on solutions to new challenges at a time when the global institution has faced criticism for its handling of Russia 's invasion of Ukraine and the war in Gaza .
