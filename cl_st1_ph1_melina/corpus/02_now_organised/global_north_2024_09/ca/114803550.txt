Rocamora , Philippine News Agency <p> MANILA -- Filipinos in Lebanon are advised to take necessary precautions amid Israel 's intensified campaign against the Hezbollah in Lebanon which has wounded scores and killed over 400 . <p> " Filipino nationals residing in Lebanon are advised to remain vigilant and stay informed , following recent hostilities in South Lebanon . Your safety is our primary concern , " the Philippine Embassy in Beirut said in a statement dated Sept. 23 . <p> " It is essential to take these communications seriously while exercising caution and judgment , " it added . <p> Local reports citing the Lebanese Ministry of Health said the casualties rose to 492 , with at least 1,645 others injured as Israel launched retaliatory strikes on Hezbollah targets in south Lebanon . <p> On Monday , the United Nations said residents in the south have @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Israeli military telling them to keep away from any building or village linked to the militant group . <p> The development comes as Hezbollah pounded northern Israel with over a hundred projectiles last week , the latest in the two parties ' exchange of fire since the war in Gaza erupted and the " most intense " so far , sparking fears over a full-scale war . <p> The embassy said Filipinos in south Lebanon should stay put and remain indoors until the bombing subsides and wait for the situation to stabilize . <p> The advisory came days after the embassy made an urgent call to Filipinos to evacuate the country while commercial flights were still available .
