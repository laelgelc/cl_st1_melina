<h> Israeli medics say 3 people were shot and killed at the West Bank-Jordan border crossing <p> JERUSALEM ( AP ) -- Three people have been shot and killed near the border crossing between the West Bank and Jordan , Israeli first responders said Sunday . <p> Israeli police said the shooter was killed , without providing further details . The border crossing is used by Palestinians , Israelis and international tourists . Israel 's Magen David Adom rescue service was at the scene and confirmed the toll . <p> The Israeli-occupied West Bank has seen a surge of violence since Hamas ' Oct. 7 attack out of Gaza triggered the war there . Israel has launched near-daily military arrest raids into dense Palestinian residential areas , and there has also been a rise in settler violence and Palestinian attacks on Israelis .
