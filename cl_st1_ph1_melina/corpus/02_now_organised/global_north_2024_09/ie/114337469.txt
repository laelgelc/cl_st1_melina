<h> Proposal for Gaza vote of sympathy <p> September 17th , 2024 6:30 PM <p> The Bantry Group for Palestine took part in an international event , Swim with Gaza , last Sunday where people from all over the world took to the water to express solidarity with Palestinians suffering in Gaza . The Bantry event was to support a cause helping to provide a desalination plant in the Nuseirat Refugee Camp in Gaza . The Bantry Group for Palestine is a growing and active community group which has an information stall on the square in Bantry every Friday . <p> Share this article <p> A WEST Cork councillor called for a vote of sympathy for all of those affected by the deaths in Gaza at a recent meeting of the local authority . <p> Cllr Isobel Towse ( SD ) said that the chamber needs to acknowledge that 1,966 people have been horrifically killed in Gaza since the Council last met on July 22nd . <p> ' These deaths include 786 children , bringing the total deaths since last October to over 40,000 , by conservative estimates , ' said Cllr Towse . <p> ' Many constituents across @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Many of us are attending weekly vigils and marches across county Cork to acknowledge the lives lost . May they rest in peace . ' <p> Her colleague , Cllr Liam Quaide seconded her acknowledgement about the death toll , which he said is genocide . It was suggested afterwards the vote of sympathy could be sent to the Palestinian Embassy in Dublin .
