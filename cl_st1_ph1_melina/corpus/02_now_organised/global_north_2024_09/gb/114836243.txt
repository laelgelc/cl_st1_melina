<h> Churches in southern Lebanon open doors to those displaced by Israeli airstrikes <p> Christian churches in Lebanon have opened their doors to those seeking refuge from the devastating impact of ongoing military action as the standoff between Hezbollah and Israel escalates . <p> Israeli airstrikes targeting the Islamist militia Hezbollah , designated a terrorist group by the UK government and which is based in Lebanon , are " affecting everyone " , says Marielle Boutros , project coordinator at Catholic charity Aid to the Church in Need ( ACN ) in Lebanon . <p> " All south Lebanon is being targeted , " Boutros says , adding that this is resulting in many residents seeking shelter in churches . " These are not purely Shia or Hezbollah areas , and we have a lot of Christian families living there . " <p> Historically the southern area of Lebanon has been populated by a large Christian population . <p> ACN is working with local Church partners to look at ways of helping the thousands who have become displaced since the bombardment began on 23 September . <p> In addition to swathes of the local population fleeing the impacted area @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ September airstrikes , including 50 children , according to Firass Abiad , Lebanon 's Health Minister . <p> Boutros explains : " Some have lost their houses , and they are moving now from the south to other places in Beirut , Mount Lebanon and the north , to find safety . " <p> The attacks have also taken place in parts of Beirut , where Hezbollah has most of its support , Boutros says . <p> " Beirut is not a big city , so if a part of Beirut is targeted , all Beirut will feel it , and all day people hear the sound of military aircraft or drones . " <p> She highlights that the escalating conflict may lead to yet another wave of Christians leaving Lebanon -- adding to an exodus which has been ongoing for decades -- further diminishing the Christian presence and influence in the region . <p> Boutros says ACN has supported more than 300 projects in the country , including providing emergency aid since last October and the attack by Hamas against Israel , which also impacted southern Lebanon as the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ shares with Israel . <p> Since last October , the situation at the shared border has been described as " low-level " conflict but now it has escalated enormously with fears of a full-scale war breaking out between Israel and Hezbollah . <p> Boutros notes local schools that the charity has also been supporting " are closed , but this is temporary , and they are now moving to online teaching " . <p> She added : " People are now living in church halls , so they will need food , sanitary products , mattresses , blankets .
