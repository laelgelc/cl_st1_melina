<p> The killings - taking the Gaza death toll since October 7 to 41,020 - were among the deadliest strikes yet on the tent sprawl of al-Muwasi , along the Gaza coast . The area was designated as a humanitarian zone for hundreds of thousands of civilians to seek shelter from the Israel-Hamas war . Israeli evacuation orders have pushed hundreds of thousands into Muwasi . <p> Gaza 's Civil Defence said its first responders recovered 40 bodies after the strike and were still looking for people . It said entire families were killed in their tents .
