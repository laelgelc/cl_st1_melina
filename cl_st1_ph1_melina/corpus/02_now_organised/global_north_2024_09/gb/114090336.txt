<p> Labour has won the Romsey by-election at Cambridge City Council , but only 1,394 people turned up to cast their vote . Beth Gardiner-Smith was elected as a Labour Party councillor after receiving 596 votes , with the Green Party candidate , Zak Karimjee , coming in second place with 409 votes . <p> The by-election saw a turnout of just 20.2 per cent , lower than the 41 per cent turnout in the ward at the city council election in May this year . The newly elected Cllr Gardiner-Smith will join her fellow Labour councillors , Councillor Dave Baigent and Councillor Dinah Pounds , in representing the Romsey area at the city council . <p> Cllr Gardiner-Smith has previously said that she grew up in Romsey and therefore knows the area well .
