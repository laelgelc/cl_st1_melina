<p> The activists behind eye-catching billboards and projections that have held the country 's political leaders to account in recent years are holding an exhibition for the first time ever . Led by Donkeys , which once projected a mug shot of former Prime Minister Boris Johnson with the word ' liar ' onto the Houses of Parliament , is currently displaying a selection of their work in a gallery space in Old Market . <p> The free exhibition , which is located at 17 Midland Road , is available to view from 11am until 4pm from now until Sunday , September 15 . The four floor exhibition includes displays from previous billboard and banner campaigns on topics from the Black Lives Matters movement to the Government 's handling of the Covid-19 pandemic . <p> More recent displays include the Liz Truss lettuce banner which led to one of the Led By Donkeys Team being briefly arrested .
