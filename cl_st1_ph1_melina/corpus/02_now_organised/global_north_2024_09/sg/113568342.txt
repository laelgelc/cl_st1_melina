" <p> The ICC is an international institution that tries individuals charged with war crimes . Khan leads its Office of the Prosecutors , which requested arrest warrants in May against Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and another Israeli official for allegedly causing the humanitarian crisis in Gaza . <p> The United States , which supports Israel , is not a member of the ICC .
