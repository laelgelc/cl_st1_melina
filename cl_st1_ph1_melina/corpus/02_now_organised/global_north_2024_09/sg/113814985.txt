<h> Disappearing human rights <h> The Kathmandu Post <p> KATHMANDU -- For most of human history , human rights did not exist . The struggle to secure them arguably began in 1215 with the Magna Carta in England , which promised protection from illegal imprisonment as a right . But it took another 733 years and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights for those rights to be enshrined for all . <p> Now , just 76 years later , they 're at risk of being lost . <p> Amid an ongoing and very public massacre in Gaza , to which the countries of the West remain mute spectators or enablers , the moral distinction between the liberal democracies of the West and the illiberal democracies and dictatorships among the rest is disappearing . <p> An estimated 40,000 Palestinians , many of them babies , children and women , have been killed in the Israeli massacre in Gaza , which began after a terror attack by Hamas that killed 1,478 Israelis , most of them civilians . The targeting of human rights organisations and activists under anti-terror laws began soon after and became a worldwide phenomenon . <p> By 2012 , India had made " dissent " including causing or intending to cause disaffection against India , a terror offence under its anti-terror law , the Unlawful Activities ( Prevention ) Act .
