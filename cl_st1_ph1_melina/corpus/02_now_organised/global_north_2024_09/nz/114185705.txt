<h> Moko Kauae Dissertation Earns First Class Honours <p> Mia-Mae Taitimu-Stevens earned a Master 's degree in Indigenous Studies with First Class Honours for her moko kauae dissertation . <p> Waipapa Taumata Rau , University of Auckland Masters graduate Mia-Mae Taitimu Stevens earned first class honours for a Masters degree in Indigenous Studies , including her dissertation on moko kauae ( traditional tattoo that adorns the chin of Maori women . ) <p> Taitimu-Stevens ( Pawarenga , Te Rarawa , Togafuafua , Tokelau ) crossed the stage on 5 September , receiving her Masters degree . She wrote her dissertation on Moko Kauae Online : Social Media as a tool of resurgence for young Maori wahine receiving moko kauae , a topic of huge significance to her . <p> " I wrote my dissertation on the perceptions of moko kauae eligibility for wahine Maori on TikTok , in reaction to the ever growing presence of moko kauae on social media , on the news , and out in te ao . <p> " This topic has always been one I 've wanted to explore , and the discussion around expectations as a wahine Maori , speaks to my own @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ me in interrogating that . <p> " The extent of historical and generational unlearning done by wahine Maori today significantly contributes to the growing number of wahine receiving their kauae . " <p> " If you whakapapa Maori and meet certain expectations , such as speaking fluent te reo Maori or contribute to your community in specific ways , it 's your birthright . " <p> " Kauae is preserved by wahine Maori ; it is the only body adornment that continued unbroken despite prejudice and criminality . It is a symbol of survival and resistance in our contemporary colonial context . <p> " Kauae to me says - we are here , we are proud and we are not going anywhere ! " <p> Raised in a small rural community about 30 minutes out of Wellsford with her family , Taitimu-Stevens enjoyed an idyllic upbringing on a lifestyle block with animals , living close to the beach , and catching fresh kaimoana . <p> " It was a great place to grow up and my parents sacrificed a lot for us to live this way .
