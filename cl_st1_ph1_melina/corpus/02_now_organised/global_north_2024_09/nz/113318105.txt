<p> There 's a famous -- probably untrue -- story from classical history about the tiny island of Melos , which boldly demanded fair treatment from the mighty Athenian empire . The Athenians replied that they did n't have tobe fair because Melos was weak and Athens was powerful , and the way of the world is that " the strong do what they can and the weak suffer what they must " . <p> This was the essence of PM Christopher Luxon 's dramatic speech to Local Government New Zealand . Councillors and mayors may see themselves as democratically elected representatives , empowered by their constituents , but Luxon cheerfully explained that his government believes local government is empowered to pick up rubbish , fix pipes , fill in potholes , maintain local assets like pools , playgrounds and libraries , and not much else . Because central government can rewrite the Local Government Act any time it likes , local government is doomed to suffer what it must . <p> It is unlikely to suffer in silence , though .
