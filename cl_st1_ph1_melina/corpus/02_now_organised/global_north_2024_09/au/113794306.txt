<p> As former US president Donald Trump prepares to take the stage in the upcoming presidential debate , the real question is not if his microphone will be muted when Kamala Harris speaks , but whether his words will once again undermine democracy . <p> This is why it is essential to listen to what politicians say . It is not just about disagreeing with a policy position . It is about recognising the trajectory that this language sets us on . <p> Donald Trump 's illiberal rhetoric was not without consequence and cultivated an environment where democratic decay could take root . <p> The calamitous January 6 events on Capitol Hill are the most glaring manifestation of what happens when illiberal language goes unchecked . It is a stark reminder that when politicians speak , they are not just engaging in idle talk but shaping political realities . <p> By the time the world recognised what was happening , it was too late . Once these ideas become part of our public discourse , the harmful policies are not far behind . <p> When leaders speak in terms that dehumanise , polarise or glorify authoritarianism , they are testing the waters . They are gauging how much they can get away with and , crucially , preparing their base for the political change that will follow . <p> For those who care about democracy , this is a call to action . <p> We must not normalise the language of illiberalism .
