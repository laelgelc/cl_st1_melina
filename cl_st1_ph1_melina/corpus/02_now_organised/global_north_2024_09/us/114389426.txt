Jews from every part of the Western hemisphere showed up Sunday night to see Israeli musical sensation Ishay Ribo 's sold-out show at Madison Square Garden . <p> When Ribo performed in New York in September 2023 for his first sold-out MSG show , his " Elul " concert was meant to bring the audience a sense of joy and spirit as they ceremoniously prayed to be inscribed in the book of life . This time , following a heavy year of war and loss , the show looked to heal and uplift . <p> Yael Schlenger of New Rochelle said she attended last year 's show , a short while before the October 7 massacre . " Ishay Ribo was like my therapy this whole year , so I am excited .
