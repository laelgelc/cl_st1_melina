<p> Harvard University is the nation 's worst higher education institution when it comes to valuing and promoting free speech , at least according to a recently released report that draws on responses from more than 58,000 students across 251 colleges and universities . <p> While the Israel-Hamas war and the subsequent student protest movement heightened tensions and impacted the expression of free speech on campuses across America , Harvard was particularly affected by high-profile controversies and clashes . <p> The climate of free speech at Harvard was slapped with an " abysmal " rating by FIRE . Of all the schools examined , only Harvard , Columbia University , and New York University received " abysmal " ratings . Harvard received a score of -21.58 , which was rounded up to zero . For context , the top-scoring institution was the University of Virginia , which received an overall score of 73.41 . <p> The rankings are determined by a composite score that combines 14 different components .
