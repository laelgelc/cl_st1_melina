<p> Chinese state media reports that the Chinese military has achieved a paltry 40% hit rate when training to shoot down drone swarms . As CCTV reportedlast week , this surprising result was discovered during the People 's Liberation Army 's ( PLA ) latest testing of its anti-drone swarm capabilities . <p> During the tests , the PLA deployed its latest anti-drone artillery systems to see how it would perform in battle . As the 40% hit rate would indicate , it was not as effective as the PLA may have hoped . <p> " Shooting at drone swarms was still quite challenging due to their speed and small size , as well as their ability to change flight trajectories -- making it easy for gunners to lose their targets , " Duan Xiaolong , from an unnamed regiment with the PLA Ground Force 's 77th Group Army explained to CCTV . <p> According to Major Cui Yang of the PLA regiment behind the testing , the PLA 's most recent air defense training exercise was conducted to evaluate drone defense capabilities at various distances and pinpoint issues for future drills , as reported by CCTV . <h> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ China Morning Post ( SCMP ) reports that an unnamed military commentator who posted on WeChat , China 's X-like social media platform , that the artillery used in the exercise may have been older models from the 1990s or 2000s , which could have impacted the damage and success rate . <p> The commentary mentioned that using electronic jamming systems , surface-to-air missiles , and automatic rifles would improve the hit rate .
