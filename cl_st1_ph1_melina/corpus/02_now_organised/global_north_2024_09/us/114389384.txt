<p> The United Nations General Assembly on Wednesday voted 124-14 to strip Israel of the right to self-defense against Palestinian terrorism in the West Bank , Gaza Strip , and east Jerusalem . <p> The text of the resolution was based on the International Court of Justice 's advisory opinion in July that Israel 's occupation of Palestinian territory was illegal . <p> Prior to the vote , UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres said he supported the ICJ option and would abide by the vote , which calls on the IDF to withdraw to the pre-1967 lines within 12 months . <p> The resolution also calls on member states not to sell arms or military equipment to Israel that would be used in Gaza , the West Bank , and east Jerusalem .
