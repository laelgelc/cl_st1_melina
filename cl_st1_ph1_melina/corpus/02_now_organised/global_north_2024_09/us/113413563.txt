<h> Community members gather in Newton to mourn Israeli hostages found dead in Gaza <p> NEWTON , MASS . ( WHDH ) - Sadness and anger filled the streets in Newton Centre on Sunday , where a weekly gathering to honor those taken hostage by Hamas became a much more somber affair after news that six of them had been found dead . <p> Emily Brophy , who organized the event , said it was " devastating " to learn that six hostages were recovered from a tunnel under Gaza , including Israeli-American Hersh Goldberg-Polin , who was among those kidnapped during a massacre at a music festival on Oct. 7 . <p> IDF Spokesman Rear Adm . Daniel Hagar said the hostages were " brutally murdered by Hamas terrorists a short while before we reached them . " <p> The 23-year-old became one of the most recognized faces of the hostage crisis and his parents were among the most vocal of families demanding a deal to bring them home . <p> Hersh 's death hit hard in Newton , where the organizers say some of his relatives live . <p> " You ca n't put into words the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Brophy said . <p> The rallies in Newton will continue until every last hostage is home . <p> Three of the hostages who were found dead were reportedly supposed to be released during the first phase of a hypothetical ceasefire agreement .
