<p> For decades , the party has consistently cast itself as the party of business and economic growth while arguing that labor unions have stymied the economy , driven up costs , and engaged in partisan political activism that has little , if anything , to do with representing its workers . <p> As a consequence , labor unions have been a key constituency of the Democratic Party for nearly a century . In turn , the party 's presidents have championed numerous policies aimed at expanding union power , and they have used the power of the federal government 's labor oversight agencies to boost union creation . <p> As a matter of policy and law , there are two distinct categories of organized labor : public sector and private sector .
