( credit : YONATAN SINDEL/FLASH90 ) <h> IDF investigates <p> Over the last week , the IDF and other authorities have discussed the leak to prevent further classified information from leaking out , N12 reported . The authorities attempted to ascertain how details of such a sensitive operation were revealed , causing significant distress to the family members of the hostages . <p> The IDF spokesperson said , " As soon as the serious leak occurred , the IDF Information Security Department opened an investigation . When completed , appropriate measures will be taken . The IDF takes leaks of any kind very seriously , especially regarding the hostages . "
