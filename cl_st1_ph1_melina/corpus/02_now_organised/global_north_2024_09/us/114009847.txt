<p> Gaza Strip -- Israel bombed a school housing displaced Palestinians in Gaza on Wednesday , killing 18 people including two staffers with the United Nations agency for Palestinian refugees , UNRWA , according to rescuers . The militant group ruled over Gaza for almost two decades before it sparked the ongoing war with its unprecedented Oct. 7 terrorist attack on Israel .
