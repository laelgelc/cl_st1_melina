<h> Israel denounces Al Jazeera as ' mouthpiece of Hamas , ' raids offices in West Bank <p> Israeli soldiers raided Al Jazeera 's offices in the West Bank , accusing the outlet of " incitement to and support of terrorism . " <p> The raid occurred at around 3 a.m. local time on live TV . Those working overnight were ordered to leave within ten minutes , with the soldiers saying employees could only take their personal belongings . Israel 's Minister of Communications Shlomo Karhi shared Al Jazeera 's footage of the raid , adding that the outlet was " the mouthpiece of Hamas and Hezbollah . " <p> " After much pressure and as we promised , this morning the security forces raided Al Jazeera 's main offices in Yosh -- the mouthpiece of Hamas and Hezbollah , in order to comply with the order and close the station , " he wrote .
