<h> To mark 24 years since the October 2000 riots , the Arab Higher Monitoring Committee has called for a strike affecting businesses , schools , and public places . The strike also protests the ongoing war . <p> The Arab Higher Monitoring Committee has announced a general strike on October 1 to mark 24 years since the October 2000 riots , during which 13 Arab rioters were killed . <p> The strike , planned for Tuesday , will impact businesses , schools , and public places throughout the Arab community , Israeli media reported on Thursday . <p> According to the committee , the strike aims to express a collective stance against the ongoing destruction and violence faced by Arabs in Israel , including the demolition of Arab homes and rampant crime within the community . <p> The committee also cited solidarity with Gaza residents and opposition to the current war as key motivations for the strike . <p> Committee chairman Mohamed Barakeh emphasized the continuing nature of the conflict , stating that the region appears to be facing ongoing destruction , particularly in the West Bank .
