<p> NEAR ALLENBY BRIDGE CROSSING , West Bank -- A gunman crossing from Jordan killed three Israeli civilians at the Allenby Bridge border in the occupied West Bank before security forces shot him dead on Sunday , Israeli authorities said . <p> It was the first attack of its kind along the border with Jordan since Palestinian Islamist group Hamas carried out an assault on southern Israel on Oct. 7 , sparking the war in Gaza that has ratcheted up tensions across the region . <p> The attack took place in a commercial cargo area under Israeli control where Jordanian trucks offload cargo entering the West Bank , officials said . The crossing , also known as the King Hussein Bridge , is about midway between Amman and Jerusalem just north of the Dead Sea . <p> The assailant was a 39-year-old truck driver who came from the influential Huwaitat tribe in southern Jordan , according to family members . <p> " A terrorist approached the area of the Allenby Bridge from Jordan in a truck , exited the truck and opened fire at the Israeli security forces operating at the bridge , " the Israeli military said . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , three Israeli civilians were pronounced dead as a result of the attack , " it added . <p> Jordan was investigating the shooting and a Jordanian official said the crossing has been closed . <p> The Israeli manager of the crossing said three workers were shot dead at close range by the driver crossing from Jordan . <p> Israel and Jordan signed a peace treaty in 1994 and have close security ties . Dozens of trailers cross daily from Jordan , with goods from Jordan and the Gulf that supply both the West Bank and Israeli markets . <p> Israeli President Isaac Herzog , whose role is largely ceremonial , urged all parties to investigate the incident to prevent repeats .
