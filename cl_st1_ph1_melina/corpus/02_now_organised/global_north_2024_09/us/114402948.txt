<h> Some organizations call for boycott of Newton businesses after man charged in connection with shooting at pro-Israel protest <p> NEWTON , MASS . ( WHDH ) - Some people are taking action following a shooting at a pro-Israel protest in Newton last week , calling for a boycott of local businesses after prosecutors charged the man who allegedly opened fire . <p> The shooting happened at the intersection of Washington Street and Harvard Street on Thursday and left one person with serious injuries . Police arrested 47-year-old Scott Hayes and he was soon arraigned on a charge of assault and battery with a dangerous weapon . <p> Where some are pushing for a boycott in support of Hayes , who they say acted in self defense , others have opposed the measure . <p> " Across our country including here in our City of Newton , people are on edge and too often feeling unsafe , " said Newton Mayor Ruthanne Fuller in a statement .
