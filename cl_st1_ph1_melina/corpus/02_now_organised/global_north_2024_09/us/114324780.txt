( AP Photo/Ohad Zwigenberg ) <p> By now , most of us who try to make sense of the kaleidoscope of horror that is Palestine-Israel understand that strong words and finger-wagging by the Biden administration aimed at Israel have no impact at all on their violent policies . News we get from the Courant and elsewhere lets us know that stopping the flow of arms to Israel is a language that Netanyahu understands . Our government attempted that once , briefly . <p> There is also another tool to persuade Israel to negotiate a hostage exchange and a ceasefire ... diplomacy . Up until now , the US has afforded Israel a second , a political " Iron Dome , " at the UN . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and sanctions would move Israel to make peace . The Israeli people have shown , in their recent huge demonstrations and strikes , that they are desperate for an end to the killing . A huge Labor Day march in New York City also demonstrates that American youth have not abandoned Gaza . <p> It is in our hands . The other two western powers on the Security Council have been following the US lead . We have vetoed firm action against Israel 's suppression of the Palestinians 43 times . The new government of Britain is inching in the direction of peace by stopping some of the flow of weapons to Israel . <p> Is it reasonable to expect the Palestinians to accept a worse occupation than before , after 40,000 killed ? It 's time for the US to stop arming the Israeli offensive and let the UN move into the area to enforce peace . I believe neither Gaza nor the West Bank are Israeli territory to rule at will .
