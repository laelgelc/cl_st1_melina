The hostages and their families deserve nothing less . <p> What 's more , by killing thousands of innocent Palestinian and Lebanese civilians in pursuit of Hamas and Hezbollah terrorists , Israel is giving those groups what they want : a weakening of Israel 's standing in the world , and a new wave of radicalized fighters .
