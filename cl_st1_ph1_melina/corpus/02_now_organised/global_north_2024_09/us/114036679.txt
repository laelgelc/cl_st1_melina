<h> 9/11 documentary brings together daughters of terrorism victims from US and Israel <h> A new mini-documentary , " Our 9/11 Story , " connects Kristin Marino , daughter of a 9/11 firefighter , and Maya Peretz , whose father died saving her in Israel , highlighting their shared grief . <p> By CLINT VAN WINKLE/THE MEDIA LINE SEPTEMBER 12 , 2024 16:46 <p> Nova Heaven exhibit at Burning Man 2024 ( photo credit : NOVA HEAVEN ) <p> Wednesday marks 23 years since 19 Islamic terrorists hijacked four airliners and attacked the United States of America . The 9/11 attacks brought down buildings in New York City and damaged the Pentagon . Passengers on United Airlines Flight 93 perished after they attacked the hijackers . Their plane crashed in a Pennsylvania field . Nearly 3,000 people from over 100 countries were murdered . <p> Islamic terrorists had been targeting and killing Americans for decades and had already suffered greatly at the hands of Islamic terrorists . The US Marine Barracks Bombing that killed over 240 people in 1983 , the first World Trade Center bombing in 1993 that injured over 1000 people , and the 1998 US Embassy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ people were just a handful of attacks that rattled Americans leading up to 9/11 . <p> Israeli Prime Minister Ariel Sharon and Yasser Arafat condemned the attacks , too . Arafat donated blood for Americans at a Gaza hospital ; Sharon put an elite unit of emergency responders on standby and offered assistance to the US.People visit the 9/11 Memorial , in New York City , U.S. , March 21 , 2023. ( credit : REUTERS/AMANDA PEROBELLI ) <h> Survivors connect <p> Israelis would also create the 9/11 Living Memorial Plaza in Ramot , Jerusalem . The memorial , which is the 9/11 memorial outside of the United States , includes a plaque of all the victims ' names . Fourty-year-old New York Firefighter Kenneth J. Marino is one of those names . Thanks to his daughter Kristin , it would not be Kenneth 's only connection to Israel . <p> Kenneth 's daughter eventually connected with Israeli Maya Peretz , whose dad died trying to save her from the Nova Music Festival massacre . The meeting of these two daughters at Ground Zero is the basis of a new @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ week . <p> " I am grateful to be able to create a space with Kristin for children who have lost their parents to terrorism and show them that they are not alone in their journey . It was an honor to share my story and my bond with Kristin and the rest of the world . " Peretz continued . <p> The film was produced by ROVA Media and directed by Dan Luxenberg . ROVA Media focuses on telling important stories about the Middle East and Israel . <p> The film 's tagline is : Two women from different countries---who speak different languages , believe in different faiths , and would have never crossed paths---have found solace in each other . <p> " Bringing these stories to the world has been deeply moving , " Luxenberg told The Media Line . " Kristin and Maya 's stories are not just about the tragedies they endured but about the strength and connection they have found in each other , " he continued . <p> Like Kenneth Marino , Israeli businessman Mark Peretz , from Rishon Lezion , was tough . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ under attack by terrorists , he knew he had to act . His daughter Maya was at the festival . So , the 51-year-old father of three jumped in his car and headed into the fray . Mark was murdered . Maya lived . <p> " Being part of this project has allowed me to honor my father 's memory . It has shown me that even in the darkest of times , there is strength in connection , " Peretz told The Media Line . <p> Marino , who was only three years old when her father died , is now a well-known Christian social media influencer with a large audience . She called the project a " healing experience " and said she was thankful for being able to connect with Peretz . She hopes her mini-documentary helps others . <p> " One thing I have learned in the last 23 years is that grief is like a rock in your pocket . At first , it 's heavy and overwhelming , but as time goes on and you grow stronger , it becomes easier to carry . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ learn to live with it , " Marino says in the movie , which she said was " a testament to faith and resilience " as well as " a tribute to all of those who have experienced loss at the hands of terrorists .
