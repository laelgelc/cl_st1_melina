<p> While overall crime in San Diego County declined last year , hate crimes reported in the region increased by 39 percent over that period , according to an analysis of crime data released last week . <p> There were 133 hate crime incidents with 151 victims reported to local law enforcement in 2023 , compared to 96 incidents in 2022 and 81 incidents in 2021 , the San Diego Association of Governments , or SANDAG , said in its annual crime report . That increase mirrors a similar rise in hate crimes reported nationally and reflects a continuing upward trend that began in 2021 , officials said . <p> In San Diego County , the most commonly reported hate crime in 2023 related to the victim 's race , ethnicity or ancestry , making up 44 percent of the reports . Of those , 52 percent of the victims were Black , while victims identified as " other " made up 17 percent and Hispanic victims made up about 12 percent of the total . Officials said this is the first annual report that groups crime statistics under new , broad categories -- crimes against persons , crimes against property and crimes against society . <p> SANDAG 's bulletin is based on available data from the California Incident Based Reporting System , known as CIBRS , and the National Incident-Based Reporting System , which is known as NIBRS . The system , run by the FBI , offers a more detailed breakdown of offenses when compared to the Uniform Crime Reporting Program 's Summary Reporting System , which was previously used and which offered a snapshot of only seven serious crimes . Both the FBI and law enforcement agencies began transitioning away from that system in 2021 . <p> The new system provides statistics broken down into the three categories .
