<p> Eleven months into the war that triggered his steadily approaching downfall , Bibi Netanyahu was seen this week trapped in its most proverbial feature : the tunnel . <p> No , this one does not snake under Gaza , its lightlessness is not optical , and its material is not cement . Rather , as the shiva call he and his wife just made to the Danino family has made plain , this bunker is an emotional capsule in which our royal couple has become socially detached and morally bankrupt while marching in their tunnel 's darkness to political demise . <p> Ori Danino , who was executed underground by Hamas with five other hostages , hailed from the social milieu of Shas .
