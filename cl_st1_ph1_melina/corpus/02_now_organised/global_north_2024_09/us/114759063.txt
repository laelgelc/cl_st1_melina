He asserted that " the work of change has begun . " And he spoke of people 's need for " joy " in their lives -- seeming to borrow from Kamala Harris 's presidential campaign theme . <p> The speech was punctuated with standing ovations and rapturous applause , including when he announced that Labour would nationalize railways . <p> But Starmer did n't encourage much jubilation .
