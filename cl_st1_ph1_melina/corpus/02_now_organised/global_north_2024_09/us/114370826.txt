All are due to appear in court on Oct. 16 to be arraigned on misdemeanor charges , the office said . <p> " The right to peaceful assembly is a constitutional right and we encourage protestors to exercise their right to peaceful assembly on any issue , " Orange County District Attorney Todd Spitzer said in a statement .
