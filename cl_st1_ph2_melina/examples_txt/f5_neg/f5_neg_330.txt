We will continue to pray for lasting peace , not only here , but in Ukraine , Sudan and all other countries suffering through war and our brothers and sisters around the world who live in conflict zones . <p> " We also continue to advocate and pray for the release of all prisoners and captives , the return of the homeless and displaced , the treatment of the sick and wounded , the relief of those who hunger and thirst , the restoration of unjustly seized or threatened properties , and the rebuilding of all public and private civilian structures that have been damaged or destroyed , and provide what practical support we can to humanitarian activities . <p> " As we approach Christmas , we call upon all Christians and those of goodwill around the world to join us in praying and working towards these noble goals and make peace in our world our universal priority . <p> " The light shines in the darkness , and the darkness has not overcome it .
