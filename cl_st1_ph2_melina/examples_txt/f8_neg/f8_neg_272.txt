<p> The Australian government has been briefed on Israel 's " independent investigation " into an air strike that killed seven foreign aid workers , including an Australian , in Gaza . <p> Israel Defense Forces Lieutenant Colonel Peter Lerner said the independent investigation into the strikes had been presented to the ambassadors of the relevant nations of those who were killed . <p> He said the IDF would be presenting it to World Central Kitchen on Friday morning local time , and then it would be publicly released . <p> The ABC understands the Australian government has been briefed on Israel 's investigation into the killings . However , the Foreign Affairs Minister 's office was still awaiting a full and transparent report , as multiple members of the Australian government had called for , including Prime Minister Anthony Albanese . <p> The Australian government will now talk to other affected countries to ensure it can have confidence in the investigative process by Israel and its outcomes . <p> When asked how independent the investigation was , Lieutenant Colonel Lerner said it was independent of the " chain of command " , which he said means " the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ so people that are privy and understand and have extremely vast military experience , either pilots or infantry or artillery , intelligence , former senior officers of the IDF " . <p> " But they are not under the control of the IDF ... they 're not dependent on promotion or anything like that . They are completely independent of the system , " Lieutenant Colonel Lerner said . <p> Lieutenant Colonel Lerner repeated the position of the Israeli government and said the deaths were " the nature of warfare " which is " always full of tragedies " . <p> He said the strikes were the result of a " misidentification of a complex situation at night " . <p> " Since the dawn of war , civilians have been caught up in warfare , " he said . <p> " We have to be better than that , we have to do better .
