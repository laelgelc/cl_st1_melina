<p> Following is the full speech delivered by President Ranil Wickremesinghe ; Let me begin by congratulating you President Yoweri Museveni of Uganda for taking over the helm of the Non Aligned Movement at this 19th Summit . Your leadership is even more timely as Uganda assumes this role at a critical moment of collective awareness among countries of the global South . This is the first NAM Summit following the onset of the pandemic , the debt crisis , climate catastrophe , new global competition , and the ensuing multiple implications for the world , in particular , for the Global South . <p> As we meet today , a humanitarian crisis is unfolding in the Gaza Strip and beyond . For over 3 months immense suffering and losses have been endured by the Palestinian civilian population , endangering regional security and stability . Until now , the NAM was largely silent . How can we remain silent when the Gaza is destroyed ? People denied humanitarian aid and a vast majority of the dead are innocent civilians . Silence implies consent .
