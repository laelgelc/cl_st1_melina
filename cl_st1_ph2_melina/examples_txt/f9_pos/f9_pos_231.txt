<p> Amos Khaemba , a journalist at TUKO.co.ke , brings over three years of experience covering politics and current affairs in Kenya . <p> Nairobi -- Daystar University has announced the revocation of its deal with Israel , which sought to establish a green space at the university dubbed Zion Forest . <p> Signing of MoU between Daystar University and Israel ( left ) and aftermath of Israel-Hamas war ( right ) . Photo : Israel in Kenya/Getty Images.Source : UGC <p> The decision by the university on Wednesday , December 18 , follows public backlash over the war in Gaza . <h> Why Daystar University cancelled deal with Israel <p> A section of Kenyans took social media to criticise the recent Memorandum of Understanding ( MOU ) between Daystar University and the Israeli embassy in Kenya , arguing that it goes against the principles of the institution . <p> Those leading the campaign against the deal stated that it indirectly endorses the ongoing conflict and human rights violations inflicted upon the Palestinian people by the Israeli state . <p> Some had questioned the appropriateness of the deal given the ongoing war between Israel and Hamas over the last 14 days , which has seen more than 45,000 Palestinians killed . <p> While revoking the deal , Daystar University Vice Chancellor Laban Ayiro said the institution does not support the humanitarian conflicts in the Middle East . <p> " We are not in any way supporting the ongoing humanitarian conflict in the Middle East . Daystar University esteems the sanctity of life and advocates for the peaceful co-existence of humanity , " Ayiro said . <h> What was the deal between Israel and Daystar University ? <p> On Monday , December 16 , Israel Ambassador to Kenya Michael Lotem signed a Memorandum of Understanding ( MoU ) with Ayiro , which would see the planting of 5,000 indigenous trees on the Daystar University campus . <p> Yahel Margovsky-Lotem , an Israel national and First Lady Mama Rachel Ruto , initiated the project . <p> " This project , initiated by Yahel Margovsky-Lotem and the First Lady Mama RachelRuto , will see the planting of over 5,000 indigenous trees on the campus , creating a vibrant green space that will enhance the learning environment for students and enrich the lives of the local community . The Zion Forest will stand as a monument of friendship between the nations of Israel and Kenya , and in defiance of the global threat of terrorism , " Israel embassy said in a tweet.
