<p> A Christmas Day protest at a Staffordshire factory has led to five people being arrested . The protest , the latest in a series at the UAV Engines factory in Lynn Lane in Shenstone , saw protestors from Palestine Action smashing through a wall on two cherry picker platforms above ground level . <p> The group claims parts are made in the factory for drones which are ' used in Gaza ' by Israeli forces , something UAV Engines has always denied . A protestor said he was planning to take down the factory ' brick by brick ' , with a gaping hole seen in a wall . <p> But Staffordshire Police intervened and made five arrests , after being called to the site just before 1pm . Three men and two women were detained on suspicion of aggravated trespass and conspiring to destroy or damage property . <p> Four have been released on bail , while the fifth , was remanded in custody . A spokeswoman for Staffordshire Police said : " Three men and two women were arrested after a protest at a business premises near Lichfield on Christmas Day . We were called to reports of protestors at the site at 12.43pm on Wednesday , December 25 . <p> " Officers attended and arrested five people , who have been released on bail as we continue to investigate .
