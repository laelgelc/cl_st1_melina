This comes after Iran attacked Israel with missiles and drones that were largely repelled by Israel and a multinational coalition that included the U.S. , and a retaliatory strike by Israel . <p> " The downgrade follows what we view as a further increase in already high geopolitical risks that Israel faces in the aftermath of the first direct attack by Iran in mid-April 2024 , " the ratings agency wrote .
