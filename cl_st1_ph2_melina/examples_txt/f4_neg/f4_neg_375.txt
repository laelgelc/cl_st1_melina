<p> Activists and students who had gathered in Frazer Town to raise awareness regarding the state of Gaza alleged that they were manhandled by the police and forcefully detained . <p> Acquilin , a student who was part of the campaign and witnessed the alleged police violence , told Bangalore Mirror that those who were gathered to conduct an awareness campaign had not even begun to speak to people when the police began to approach them . " We had just gathered at a tea stall to drink tea , when the police both in uniform and civilian clothes , approached us and began to videotape us . When we questioned their actions , they began to detain us . Initially , there were only male policemen present in the vicinity and they alone carried out the detaining while female police only joined later , " she explained . " They violently put people into the van and brutalised them while being transported , " she added . <p> She further noted that activists gathered at another location to raise awareness were also detained .
