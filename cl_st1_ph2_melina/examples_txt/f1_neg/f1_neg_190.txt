<h> Montana Tucker keeps Israel hostages in spotlight : ' Being silent is not an option ' <p> The granddaughter of Holocaust survivors talks to Align about antisemitism , celebrity indifference , and why every voice counts . <p> Digital creator Montana Tucker has a beef with her fellow " influencers . " <p> " What drives me crazy , " Tucker says , " Is someone who has influence but is n't using it for good . What 's the point ? " <p> ' Major actors , major musicians came up to me or messaged me , " Thank you for what you 're doing . " Yet they do n't post or speak out publicly . ' <p> It 's why she wanted to do something to share with her 14 million-strong social media flock following Hamas ' Oct. 7 assault on Israel . <p> " If I do n't use it for what I believe in ... what is the point of having the influence ? " she asked herself at the time . <p> But how ? <h> Getting creative <p> She worried her background in dance , music , and acting did n't align with the hostages ' plight . Then she got creative . She created a series of videos to highlight the hostages ' plight , drawing international attention along the way . <p> One video invited survivors of the Nova music festival attack to dance with her , highlighting their perseverance in the face of the ghastly attack . The clip reportedly generated more than 10 million views . <p> " When we did that Nova video , it had a big impact , " she says . " Art is something you can really portray a message with and reach a different audience . " <p> Tucker even walked the Grammy 's red carpet in February wearing a yellow ribbon-themed dress to remind people about the remaining hostages . The evening 's presenters missed the cue and did n't bring the subject up on stage . Neither did the A-listers who attended other awards galas like the Emmys and the Oscars . <p> That hardly stopped Tucker , who has teamed with the Combat Antisemitism Movement , to flex her social media might for the cause . <h> ' I 'm a Survivor ' <p> Her latest CAM video , " I 'm a Survivor , " pairs her with 35 members of the Sol Dance Academy along with are three released hostages -- Moran Stela Yanai , Raz Ben Ami , and Hila Rotem Shoshani . The participants danced within a tunnel , representing the innocents still held hostage by the terrorist group . <p> " I 've built such strong personal relationships with many of the released hostages and the hostage families , " Tucker tells Align . " I feel like they 're my family now . " <p> The clip also features family members of Ohad Ben Ami , Shlomo Manzur , and Yossi and Eli Sharabi , some of nearly a hundred remaining Gaza hostages . New reports suggest significant progress is being made for a possible ceasefire and hostage release . <p> Some activists on both sides of the Israeli/Palestinian divide throw razor sharp-elbows to make their points . That can inflame the debate and drive people farther apart . Tucker tries to bring people together on her social media channels . <p> " I 've never criticized any group , beside Hamas , " she says with a gentle laugh . <h> Shedding followers <p> Her activism has come at a price . She realized some stopped reaching out to her for professional deals . A few other brands stood up when others shrank away . <p> " We love your activism , " they told her . <p> A few in the entertainment field shared their support for her work , but they did so quietly . <p> " Major actors , major musicians came up to me or messaged me , ' Thank you for what you 're doing . ' Yet they do n't post or speak out publicly , " she notes , fearing blowback or even death threats . <p> President-elect Donald Trump has repeatedly warned Hamas to release the hostages before he returns to the White House on Jan. 20 . Tucker says she hopes and prays that will change the dynamic , but she steers clear of overtly political messaging . <h> Anyone can help <p> Tucker , whose grandparents survived the Nazi Holocaust , is uniquely positioned to raise awareness in our digital age .
