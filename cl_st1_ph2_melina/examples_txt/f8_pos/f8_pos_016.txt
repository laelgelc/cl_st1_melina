<p> For forty years , the Educational Bookshop , owned by the Muna family , has been a safe haven amid the intimidation and ethnic cleansing elsewhere in occupied East Jerusalem and the West Bank . <p> Every Palestinian in the city knows its shops . They use the cafe above the shop in Salahuddin street , close to Jerusalem 's Old City , as a meeting place . <p> The shops are a Palestinian national institution and an enduring symbol of Palestine 's literary and political establishment . <p> On Sunday , Israeli plain-clothed agents violently raided two of its three branches . After five minutes they suddenly produced a search warrant and ordered customers to leave . <p> " They closed the door and started to move the books to the floor , " one witness told MEE , preferring to remain anonymous .
