<h> Bringing Gaza Home <h> Share : <p> Palestinians mourn their relatives killed in the Israeli bombardment of the Gaza Strip at a hospital morgue in Deir al-Balah , Friday , November 1 , 2024 . AP Photo/Abdel Kareem Hana <p> In Ft . Wayne , Indiana this September , I was arrested with a long time activist friend , Cliff Kindy , for blocking the entrance to a Raytheon Corp . facility . We both requested jury trials and the dates were set for mid-December and early January . Prosecutors dropped the charges in each case and the trials did not happen . <p> For my defense , I planned to bring Gaza home to jurors from Allen County , home to Ft . Wayne , with wire service photos and by extrapolating the effects of the U.S.-Israeli genocide in Palestine to their own county . <p> That same approach can be used for any city or county in the U.S. Simply find your population and area , then do the math based on Gaza 's population and area . The genocide statistics were published by Al Jazeera for its summary report on one year of Israel 's U.S.-funded genocide . <p> U.S. census figures show that Allen County 's population is 395,000 or 18% of Gaza 's 2.2 million people and its area is 660 sq . miles , roughly 4.5 times that of Gaza 's 144 sq . miles . <p> Here , then , is how to bring Gaza home to your city or county . <p> ? Tons of bombs dropped <p> As of October 2024 , Israel 's military has dropped over 85,000 tons of bombs -- 591 T/sq. mi. on Gaza , far exceeding that dropped on Dresden , Hamburg and London combined in World War II . <p> For Allen County the comparable number is 390,000 tons of bombs at 591 T/sq. mi . <p> ? Casualties <p> More than 43,000 Palestinian bodies , mostly of women and children , were recovered last year . Many thousands more remain buried under the rubble -- reliable reports say up to 200,000. 97,000 have been wounded . Anesthesia is rarely available . <p> For Allen County the comparable numbers ( 18% of the above ) are : <p> 7,740 bodies recovered <p> 17,460 wounded <p> Up to 36,000 buried under the rubble . <p> Over half are women and children . <p> ? Israeli attacks on hospitals and the continual bombardment of Gaza have killed at least 986 medical workers including 165 doctors , 260 nurses , 184 health associates , 76 pharmacists and 300 management and support staff . <p> For Allen County the comparable numbers are <p> 20 hospitals and health centers destroyed <p> 30 doctors , 47 nurses , 14 pharmacists , 54 support staff killed . <p> 24 ambulances damaged . <p> ? Disease <p> In the past year , 75% of Gaza 's population have been infected with contagious diseases from lack of sanitation , open sewage and inadequate hygiene .
