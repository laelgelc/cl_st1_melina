<p> Famine is looming by May in the Gaza Strip , a U.N.-backed report forecast on Monday , without an end to fighting that has decimated the Palestinian territory and cut off supplies . <p> WHAT IS FAMINE AND WHO DECLARES ONE ? <p> Famine is assessed by the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification ( IPC ) , an initiative of more than a dozen U.N . agencies , regional bodies and aid groups . <p> On Monday the IPC said that across the whole of the Gaza Strip the number of people facing " catastrophic hunger " has risen to 1.1 million , about half the population of the besieged coastal enclave . <p> That is nearly double the figure deemed at risk of catastrophic hunger in the previous IPC report in December , when there was already record hunger . <p> " Famine is now projected and imminent in the North Gaza and Gaza Governorates and is expected to become manifest during the projection period from mid-March 2024 to May 2024 , " it said . <p> For famine to be declared , at least 20% of the population must be suffering extreme food shortages , with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of every 10,000 dying daily from starvation or from malnutrition and disease . <p> Famine has been declared twice in the past 13 years : in Somalia in 2011 and in parts of South Sudan in 2017 . <p> WHAT IS THE CURRENT ASSESSMENT IN GAZA ? <p> On Monday the IPC said that from mid-March to mid-July , in the most likely scenario and assuming a worsening of the war including a ground offensive in Rafah city , half of Gaza 's population or 1.11 million people were expected to face catastrophic conditions . <p> In late December , the IPC said the situation in Gaza had already exceeded the 20% threshold . <p> It said the remaining two thresholds - the number of children acutely malnourished and the number of people dying daily from starvation or from malnutrition and disease - " may also be breached at some point " in the coming months . <p> WHAT DOES IT MEAN TO DECLARE FAMINE ?
