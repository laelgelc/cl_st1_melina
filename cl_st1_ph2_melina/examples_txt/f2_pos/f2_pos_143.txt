<h> WHO completes second Gaza hospital evacuation amid fighting <p> GENEVA/CAIRO : The World Health Organization said on Tuesday it had completed a second evacuation mission from Gaza 's Nasser Hospital but voiced concern for nearly 150 patients and medics who remain at the site amid continuing fighting . <p> The Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis , Gaza 's second-largest , stopped working last week after a week-long Israeli siege followed by a raid , the U.N . agency said .
