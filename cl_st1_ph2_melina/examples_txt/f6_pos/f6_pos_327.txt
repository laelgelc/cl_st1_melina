<h> UN chief says situation in Gaza ' appalling and apocalyptic ' <h> UN Secretary-General calls for increased humanitarian aid to Gaza , condemning aid blockades as a failure of international humanitarian law . <p> Participants attend a humanitarian conference for Gaza in Cairo on December 2 , 2024 , amid the continuing war between Israel and Hamas. -- The international ministerial conference to Strengthen the Humanitarian Response in Gaza " is organized by Egypt ? s Foreign Ministry , with the participation of UN representatives and a number of European ministers . ( Photo by Khaled DESOUKI / AFP ) <p> The United Nations chief said Monday the situation in war-torn Gaza was " appalling and apocalyptic " , warning conditions faced by Palestinians in the territory may amount to the " gravest international crimes " . <p> In remarks read out on his behalf at a Cairo conference aimed at increasing humanitarian aid , UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres urged the international community to " build a foundation for sustainable peace in Gaza and across the Middle East " . <p> The war in Gaza broke out when Palestinian militant group Hamas attacked southern Israel on October 7 , 2023 , resulting in 1,208 deaths , mostly civilians , according to an AFP tally of Israeli official figures . <p> Israel 's retaliatory campaign has killed 44,429 people in Gaza , according to figures from the territory 's health ministry that the UN considers reliable . <p> Guterres highlighted the devastating toll of the conflict and the urgent need for international action . <p> The UN chief added that Gaza now has " the highest number of children amputees per capita anywhere in the world " , with " many losing limbs and undergoing surgeries without even anesthesia " . <p> The secretary-general also criticised the severe restrictions on aid delivery , calling the current levels " grossly insufficient " . <p> According to the UN Palestinian refugee agency ( UNRWA ) count , only 65 aid trucks per day had been able to enter Gaza this past month , compared to a pre-war average of 500 . <p> International aid organisations have repeatedly raised alarm over the deteriorating conditions in Gaza , warning that civilians are on the brink of famine . <p> They have said aid shipments reaching the enclave are now at their lowest since the start of the war . <p> Israel , which early in the conflict imposed a complete siege for a period on the Hamas-ruled territory , has blamed aid issues on what it says is the inability of relief organisations to handle and distribute large quantities of aid . <p> UN 's Guterres said on Monday that the blockade of aid to Gaza " is not a crisis of logistics " but rather " a crisis of political will and of respect for fundamental principles of international humanitarian law " . <p> UNRWA said all the attempts it has made to deliver aid into northern Gaza have either been " denied " or " impeded " between October 6 , 2024 and November 25 , amid fierce fighting in the area . <p> Guterres said " if UNRWA is forced to close , the responsibility of replacing its vital services ... would rest with Israel " . <p> In his speech at the conference , UNRWA chief Philippe Lazzarini said that the agency " remains the backbone of humanitarian response " in Gaza . <p> He also called for the use of " a robust international legal and political framework " to ensure the continuation of humanitarian aid to Gaza .
