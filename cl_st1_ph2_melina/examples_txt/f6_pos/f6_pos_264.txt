<h> ' I 'm under no illusions about what I 'm going into ' , says aid worker on Gaza mission <h> Burley-in-Wharfedale aid worker a tells of situation in Gaza <p> AN AID worker from the Bradford district has been deployed to war-torn Gaza on a mission to avert famine . <p> Kate Newton is the World Food Programme 's ( WFP ) Deputy Country Director for Palestine and travelled to Gaza this week for her first stint in the conflict zone since the crisis erupted last October . <p> Kate Newton in the Gaza Strip in March 2023 ( Image : UGC ) <p> Kate is helping spearhead the United Nations ' organisation 's life-saving support for civilians , which has been backed by ? 14.25 million support from the UK Government since October 2023 . <p> Kate , from Burley-in-Wharfedale , told of the emotional toll the crisis has been having on aid workers carrying out life-saving work on the ground . <p> Kate Newton in Upper Egypt in October 2020 ( Image : UGC ) <p> Kate , 50 , said : " I 'm under absolutely no illusions about what I 'm going into having supported our teams on the ground in Gaza since October 7 . <p> " Our offices in Gaza have been destroyed and our staff have been moved multiple times with everyone else up and down the Strip . <p> " We had a guest house which was bombed at the end of August with staff inside but thankfully we have not lost any colleagues yet . We 've suffered a lot of attacks on our property , our warehouses and our vehicles . <p> Kate Newton in Beirut , Lebanon in October 2020 ( Image : UGC ) <p> " Many of our local staff have left . They took a decision it was too dangerous for them and their families to stay . <p> " We 've been trying to bring more international staff into Gaza , which has its challenges . The staff I liaise within Gaza have all gradually become really , really traumatised by this crisis . <p> " They do four-week stints . They go in thinking they 'll be able to cope and then you can really see the transformation . <p> " There are drones overhead all the time 24/7 . Even in the safer areas , where they are staying , they can hear bombing and shooting all day . No-one really sleeps while they are there . " <p> WFP has provided emergency food and voucher assistance to over one million people in Gaza . <p> Support includes food parcels and wheat flour to families , hot meals kitchens and nutrition support to pregnant and breastfeeding mothers and children . <p> Kate Newton in Kharkiv , Ukraine in May 2022 ( Image : UGC ) <p> UK Government funding through the WFP for the 2023/24 financial year supported over 651,000 people , and the UK has pledged to help the WFP procure a further 4,465 metric tons of fortified wheat flour to help 451,000 people across Gaza for the financial year 24/25 . <p> Kate said : " The UK Government has contributed much to the crisis and I feel it 's important for British people to know it is making a big difference .
