Their motion proposed that UBC end collaboration with Israeli academic institutions because Israel 's actions in Gaza " constitute a plausible risk to the rights of Palestinians to be protected from genocide , " a Palestinian professor at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem was suspended , and some universities in Israel have " direct ties to the Israeli military .
