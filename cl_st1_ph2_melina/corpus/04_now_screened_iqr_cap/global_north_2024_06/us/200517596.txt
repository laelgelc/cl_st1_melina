<p> A disgruntled George Clooney , a longtime supporter of Democratic candidates , rang up the White House last month after President Joe Biden denounced the International Criminal Court 's request to issue arrest warrants against Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his Minister of Defense , Yoav Gallant .
