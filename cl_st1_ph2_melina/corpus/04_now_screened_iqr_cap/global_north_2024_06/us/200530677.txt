<p> CLEVELAND , Ohio -- Cuyahoga County Council will no longer be considering formal action to force divestment in Israel bonds amid the war in Gaza , but the sponsors still hope that Executive Chris Ronayne and the county treasurer make that decision on their own . <p> The item will be removed from the agenda . But the sponsors said they still expect action . They continued to " urge " Treasurer Brad Cromes , who manages the county 's investment portfolio , to suspend investments in sovereign debt issued by any foreign country -- not just Israel -- and set policy preventing any such future investments , at least " until a thorough review of the policy can be conducted , " a copy of a letter addressed to Ronayne and Cromes said . <p> " This will allow us to make deliberate , informed decisions regarding the appropriate allocation of taxpayer funds moving forward , " the letter continued . " We believe in the strength of the American economy and therefore wish to invest exclusively in American workers and neighborhoods .
