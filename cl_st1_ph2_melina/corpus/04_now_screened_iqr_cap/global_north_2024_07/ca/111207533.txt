<p> LONDON ( AP ) -- British Prime Minister Keir Starmer 's office said Friday that the U.K. will not interfere with the International Criminal Court 's request for an arrest warrant against Israel 's Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu . <p> The announcement is reversal of plans announced by former Prime Minister Rishi Sunak , who was ousted earlier this month when Starmer 's Labour Party swept Conservatives from office in a landslide . <p> " This was a proposal by the previous government which was not submitted before the election , and which I can confirm the government will not be pursuing in line with our long-standing position that this is a matter for the court to decide , " a Starmer spokesperson said . <p> Netanyahu and other Israeli leaders condemned the move as disgraceful and antisemitic . U.S. President Joe Biden also criticized the prosecutor and supported Israel 's right to defend itself against Hamas -- as did Sunak . <p> ICC Prosecutor Karim Khan sought warrants for Netanyahu and Defense Minister Yoav Gallant in May over Israel 's war in Gaza , a symbolic blow that deepened Israel 's isolation over the war in Gaza @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
