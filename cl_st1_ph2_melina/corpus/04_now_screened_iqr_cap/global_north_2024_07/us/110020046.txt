<p> The New York primary may come to be seen as a key tipping point for the Democratic Party , said Li Zhou on Vox . Last week , Jamaal Bowman , a member of the ultra-progressive faction in Congress known as " the Squad " , and a fierce critic of Israel , lost the race to George Latimer -- a pro-Israel moderate . <p> It was a contest that highlighted stark divisions among Democrats over the war in Gaza . Bowman had received strong support from fellow squad member Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and from the veteran senator Bernie Sanders , who called the primary " one of the most important elections in the modern history of this country " . While some might challenge Sanders over that claim , what is certainly true is that it was the most expensive congressional primary in history .
