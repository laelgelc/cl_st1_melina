<p> CAIRO - Egypt opens a summit on the Gaza crisis on Saturday to try to head off a wider regional war but assembled Middle Eastern and European leaders are expected to struggle to agree a common position on the conflict between Israel and Hamas militants . <p> Two diplomats said it was unlikely there would be a joint statement from the gathering because of sensitivities around any calls for a ceasefire , and whether to include mention of Hamas 's Oct. 7 attack on Israel and Israel 's right to defend itself . <p> The absence of a top official from Israel 's main ally the United States and some other major Western leaders has cooled expectations for what the hastily-convened event can achieve . <p> The U.S. , which has no ambassador currently assigned to Egypt , is represented by its embassy Charge d'Affaires . <p> The summit meets as Israel prepares a ground assault on Gaza following Hamas ' attack that killed 1,400 people .
