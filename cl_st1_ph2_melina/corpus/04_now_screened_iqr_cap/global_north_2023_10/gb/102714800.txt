<p> Pro-Palestine protesters have stormed a drone factory in Leicester , drenching it in red paint , setting off flares and ramming a van through its gates . <p> Police raced to the scene this morning , where activists were seen waving flags and banners reading ' Shut Elbit Down ' while stood atop a vehicle . The UAV Tactical Systems factory , which is owned by Elbit Systems UK , has long been targeted by protests calling for its closure . <p> There was an attempted siege of the factory back in May , with demonstrators vowing to " stay put for weeks on end , through day and night , refusing to leave until Elbit does " . Palestine Action has claimed responsibility for the latest protest , which comes a day after hundreds of people were killed in an explosion at a Gaza Strip hospital . <p> Sharing footage of the demo to Twitter , the group wrote : " Within 12 hours of Israel massacring patients and doctors at Al Ahli hospital in Gaza , we shut down Israel 's military drone factory .
