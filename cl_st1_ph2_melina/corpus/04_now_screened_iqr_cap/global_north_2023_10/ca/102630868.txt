<p> Stories of strength and tragedy reverberated on the steps outside Kamloops City Hall on Wednesday night ( Oct. 11 ) as some 30 people gathered in support of the Jewish community amidst war in Israel . <p> The fighting began on Oct. 7 as Hamas militants launched a surprise attack by air , land and sea on civilians . Israel has retaliated with a counter-offensive on Gaza . <p> Thousands of civilians have thus far been killed in the fighting . <p> Wednesday 's vigil in Kamloops featured a non-denominational prayer , Israeli songs and many people sharing their feelings and stories of loved ones caught in the attacks . <p> Yoav Shimoni , 24 , lit a candle for his grandmother , Braha Levinson , who he said was killed in the attack by Hamas . He said his family learned of her death from a video posted to social media . <p> " My sister flagged the family group chat that my grandma posted something on Facebook and , when we went to see what was posted , it was a video of her dying on her kitchen floor , dead , with blood all around @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Shimoni said . <p> He said he did not want to believe what had happened , but soon heard of other people in her community who were kidnapped or killed . <p> He said his grandmother lived about 600 metres from the Gaza Strip , noting her kibbutz would have been one of the first infiltrated by Hamas after the terrorist group that controls Gaza fired rockets into Israel . <p> Shimoni lives in Toronto , but is in Kamloops visiting his girlfriend for the next few months .
