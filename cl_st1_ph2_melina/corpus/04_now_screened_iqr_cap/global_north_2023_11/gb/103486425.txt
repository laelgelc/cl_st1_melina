Nearly 70 Labour MPs had defied their leader to call for a ceasefire now by the time of the vote on Wednesday evening , but just 56 voted this way in the Commons . <p> Labour , like the Conservative government , the United States and the European Union , have been calling for " humanitarian pauses " to help aid reach Gaza . Compared with a formal ceasefire , these pauses tend to last for short periods of time , sometimes just a few hours - but many MPs have said the pauses do not go far enough . <p> The pauses are implemented with the aim of providing humanitarian support only , as opposed to achieving long-term political solutions .
