Currently just 40 per cent of drinking water production facilities remain functional in Gaza ( 87 out of 217 ) . Without fuel , every one of these will stop operating within weeks . <p> " Since all the electricity to Gaza was cut after the horrific attacks of 7 Oct 2023 , fuel became essential to produce , treat and distribute water to more than two million Palestinians . <p> " If the current more than 100-day blockade on fuel coming into Gaza does not end , children will begin to die of thirst . Diseases are already advancing , and chaos is tightening its grip . <p> " Whilst alarm bells rightly ring on the nutrition situation in Gaza -- just yesterday @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 6months to 5yrs ) admitted for treatment of acute malnutrition from April to May -- water can not be sidelined . <p> " And so in the most relatable terms : Gaza is facing what would amount to a man-made drought . Water systems are collapsing . <p> " However , because this is man-made , it can be stopped . None of these problems are logistical or technical . They are political . Denial has become policy . If there is political will , the water crisis will be eased overnight -- fuel would mean that water flows from hundreds of groundwater wells and restores supply within a day . But time is running out . <p> " To help paint the picture : without fuel , desalination plants that already operate on reduced capacity will cease completely , and critical membranes in the machinery will close , doing immense damage . Without fuel , trucking the millions of litres of water to people will stop . At major production points , large numbers of donkeys are starting to replace trucks .
