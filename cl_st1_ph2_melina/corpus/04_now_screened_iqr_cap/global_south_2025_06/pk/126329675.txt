Meanwhile , Iran framed the truce as one it had " imposed on the enemy " . <p> Notwithstanding the claims of victory , the reality on the ground suggests a far more inconclusive outcome , with both Iran and Israel suffering substantial costs and achieving only limited gains . <p> It is now of the utmost importance that the cessation of hostilities lasts and is not derailed through any miscalculation or reckless adventurism , particularly by Israel , which has a long history of scuttling peace processes and ceasefires on the most spurious of pretexts .
