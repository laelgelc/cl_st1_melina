<h> ' The crisis in Gaza is a crisis for the UN ' <p> The World Health Organization ( WHO ) aired its displeasure on Monday after Israel repeatedly bombarded the Indonesian Hospital in North Gaza , which reportedly resulted in the killing of at least 12 persons including patients and their companions residing at the hospital . <p> " WHO is appalled by the attack today on the Indonesian Hospital in North Gaza . Health workers and civilians should never have to be exposed to such horror , and especially while inside a hospital , " it said in a press statement . <p> According to the latest reports , the Indonesian Hospital continues to be besieged . No one has been allowed to enter or leave the hospital : there have been reports of shooting towards those attempting to leave but no injuries or fatalities thus far . The hospital , like others in northern Gaza and Gaza City , has faced power outages since the main and secondary generators stopped functioning several weeks ago due to lack of fuel . It also faces severe shortages of water , essential medicines and supplies .
