<p> An open letter signed by more than 500 South African Jews calls for an immediate ceasefire between Israel and Palestine . <p> ' We are a diverse group of South African Jews who are dismayed by the situation that is unfolding in Israel and Palestine . Through it , the world has witnessed a catastrophic loss of life and , indeed , a loss of humanity . We mourn every life lost : Palestinian and Israeli , ' the signatories write . <p> The letter also called for an end to the Israeli occupation of the Palestinian territories and for ' the release of all hostages and detainees unjustly held both in Gaza and in Israeli prisons ' . <p> Anthony Hodgson , who helped draft the letter , said that the process had been lengthy .
