<h> White House Left Defaced by Pro-Palestine Protestors <p> Saturday afternoon saw thousands of protestors march up to the White House in support of Palestine . Asking for a ceasefire and hurling expletives at President Joe Biden , the protestors defaced multiple statues as well as smeared red handprints across walls , symbolising the bloodshed in Gaza . The police have only made a single arrest over the weekend . <p> Washington D.C. witnessed the descent of thousands of pro-Palestinian protesters marching up to the White House over the weekend . The protestors defaced multiple statues and wrapped Palestinian flags around them , among which were statues of Benjamin Franklin and General Marquis de Lafayette . <p> Later , some protestors were also seen attempting to climb up the wrought-iron gates of the White House . <p> Come Monday morning , photos on social media highlight the aftermath of the pro-Palestine demonstration in Washington D.C. on Saturday - red handprints and stickers smeared across the wall , interspersed with messages advocating to " free Palestine " <p> The demonstrations were a response to US President Joe Biden 's continued show of support for and approval of Israel 's actions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Some protestors were even heard chanting " F -- Joe Biden " outside the White House . <p> Others chants to be heard were those in favour of a cease-fire .
