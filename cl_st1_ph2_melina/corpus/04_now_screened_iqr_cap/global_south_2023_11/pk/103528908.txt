<h> UN Security Council calls for pauses in Gaza fighting for aid <p> UNITED NATIONS : The United Nations Security Council on Wednesday called for urgent and extended humanitarian pauses in fighting between Israel and Palestinian Hamas fighters in the Gaza Strip for a " sufficient number of days " to allow aid access . <p> The 15-member council overcame an impasse , which saw four unsuccessful attempts to take action last month , to adopt a resolution that also calls for the immediate and unconditional release of all hostages held by Hamas . <p> The United States , Russia and Britain , who are council veto-powers , abstained from Wednesday 's vote on the resolution drafted by Malta . The remaining 12 members voted in favour . <p> The council stalemate has largely been centred on whether to call for a humanitarian pause or a ceasefire . A pause is generally considered less formal and shorter than a ceasefire , which has to be agreed by the warring parties .
