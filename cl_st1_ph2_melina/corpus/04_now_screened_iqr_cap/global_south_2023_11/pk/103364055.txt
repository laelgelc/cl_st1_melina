<h> Ceasefires break down <p> The slaughter in Gaza seems to have caused a breakdown of the much-vaunted Zionist propaganda machine . The powerful pro-Israeli press can simply not put a positive spin on the killing of Palestinians , who have death rained on them from the skies . With the number now having crossed 11,000 there are too many dead to hide . It seems that the initial Hamas attacks , which killed as many as 1100 Israelis , have now been exceeded by the number of Palestinian children killed , about 4500 , which is more than four times the number of Israelis killed . Though Israeli forces are moving into the Gaza Strip , they have only suffered 32 fatalities so far , which makes the initial hesitation about moving in seem a little exaggerated . At the same time , those forces have violated the very humanitarian pauses that it itself agreed to . These pauses are meant to allow people to leave northern Gaza , where fighting is going on , and have been conceded only after US pressure .
