( Pic by Pradeep Pathirana ) <p> " From the river to the sea , Palestine will be free " chanted people as they participated in a protest led by Dr. Sudath Dewapura in front of the World Buddhist Federation -- Sri Lanka on October 31 ( Tuesday ) .
