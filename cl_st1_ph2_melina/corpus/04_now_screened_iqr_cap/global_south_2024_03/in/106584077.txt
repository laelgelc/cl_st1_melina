<p> Approximately a thousand protesters gathered in Hollywood on Sunday , just hours before the highly anticipated 2024 Academy Awards ceremony , to demand an immediate cease-fire in the ongoing Israel-Hamas conflict . Their presence not only disrupted traffic but also irked Oscars organizers , causing frustration among attendees . <p> According to an article in Los Angeles Times , as the ceremony 's commencement drew near , chaos ensued on Highland Avenue when dozens of black-tinted vans carrying ceremony guests found themselves at a standstill . Frantically , organizers attempted to navigate the gridlock , urging vehicles to move through intersections near the Dolby Theatre . Hours prior , demonstrators began congregating by the hundreds at the intersection of Sunset Boulevard and Ivar Avenue , just east of the theater on Hollywood Boulevard . With Palestinian flags in hand , they spilled onto Sunset Boulevard , effectively occupying the eastbound side of the street .
