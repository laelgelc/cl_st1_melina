We eliminated more than 20 terrorists inside the hospital compound , " said spokesperson Rear Admiral Daniel Hagari . <p> One Israeli soldier was killed in the fighting , he said . <p> " Hamas tried to reestablish its base in the hospital and use it as a shelter for terrorists on the run .
