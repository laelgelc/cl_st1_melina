<p> New York -- The UN Security Council on Monday backed a proposal outlined by President Joe Biden for a ceasefire between Israel and Hamas in the Gaza Strip and urged the Palestinian militants to accept the deal aimed at ending the eight-month-long war . <p> Hamas welcomed the adoption of the US-drafted resolution and said in a statement that it is ready to co-operate with mediators over implementing the principles of the plan . <p> Russia abstained from the UN vote , while the remaining 14 Security Council members voted in favour of the resolution supporting a three-phase ceasefire plan laid out by Biden on May 31 that he described as an Israeli initiative . <p> " Today we voted for peace , " US ambassador to the UN Linda Thomas-Greenfield told the council after the vote . <p> The resolution welcomes the new ceasefire proposal , states that Israel has accepted it , calls on Hamas to agree to it and " urges both parties to fully implement its terms without delay and without condition .
