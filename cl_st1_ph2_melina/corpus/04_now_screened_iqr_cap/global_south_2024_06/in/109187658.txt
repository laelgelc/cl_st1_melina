( File Image ) <p> Tel Aviv:Israel 's military announced on Sunday that it would temporarily halt fighting during daytime hours along a route in southern Gaza to allow the delivery of humanitarian aid to Palestinians facing a crisis amid the war , which has been ongoing for nine months . <p> The " tactical pause " announced by the military , covering roughly 12 kilometres of the road in the Rafah region , is not a complete cease-fire , which has been requested by the international community , including Israel 's close ally , the United States . However , this limited pause in fighting could help address some of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ recent weeks due to Israel 's invasion of Rafah . <p> The pause in the fighting is intended to allow aid trucks to reach the nearby Israel-controlled Kerem Shalom crossing , the primary entry point for incoming aid , and travel safely to the Salah a-Din highway , a main north-south route . The crossing has experienced blockages since Israeli ground troops entered Rafah in early May . <p> COGAT , the Israeli military body responsible for overseeing aid distribution in Gaza , stated that the route would boost the flow of aid to various parts of Gaza , including Khan Younis , Muwasi , and central Gaza . The heavily affected northern Gaza , which was one of the initial targets in the war , is receiving supplies through a northern crossing . <p> As per the Israeli military , the pause was implemented following discussions with the United Nations and international aid agencies . <p> The pause along the southern route coincides with Israel and Hamas considering the latest cease-fire proposal , a plan detailed by US President Joe Biden as part of his administration 's most intense @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ensure the release of hostages taken by the militant group . Although Biden said Israel developed the plan , the country has not fully accepted it , and Hamas has requested changes that Jerusalem finds unacceptable . <p> Israel 's eight-month military offensive against the Hamas militant group , which began after Hamas ' attack on October 7 , has caused a severe humanitarian crisis in Gaza . The UN has reported widespread hunger , with hundreds of thousands on the brink of famine . The international community urged Israel to improve the situation , stating that the ongoing fighting , including in Rafah , has restricted aid deliveries throughout the conflict . <p> More than 37,000 people have been killed while over 85,000 injured in Israel 's war on Gaza since October 7 , as per the Hamas-run health ministry . Meanwhile , the death toll in Israel from the Hamas-led attacks stands at 1,139 , with several people still held captive in Gaza .
