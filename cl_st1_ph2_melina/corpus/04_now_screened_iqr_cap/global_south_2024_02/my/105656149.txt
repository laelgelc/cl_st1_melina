Israel says 132 remain in Gaza , including at least 27 captives believed to have been killed . <p> In response , Israel has launched a withering air , land and sea offensive that has killed at least 27,238 people in Gaza , mostly women and children , according to the health ministry in the Hamas-run territory . <p> A Hamas source said the current three-stage truce proposal included an initial six-week pause in fighting that would see some hostages released for Palestinian prisoners , with potential extensions . <p> Hamdan , whose movement demanded a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Israeli disinformation campaign " aimed at " distorting " Hamas 's position . <p> Israel has " rejected all initiatives made so far ... in order to continue the aggression " , he said . <p> Hamdan also criticised the decision of several countries to suspend financing for the UN agency for Palestinian refugees ( UNRWA ) because of the presumed involvement of a handful of its employees in the October 7 attacks . <p> He called it an " irresponsible measure based on Zionist lies and collective punishment . " <p> US Secretary of State Antony Blinken will travel to the Middle East yet again in the coming days to press for an agreement , the State Department said . -- AFP
