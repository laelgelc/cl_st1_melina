Those who were seriously injured mainly were performing their prayers outside the mosque , including children , women , and older people . <p> This is not the first time Israel killed civilians who were performing prayers . In July , Israel also bombed a mosque in the Shati refugee camp in Central Gaza , killing 22 Palestinians and injuring many others .
