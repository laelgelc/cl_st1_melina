It also provides an opening for Israel and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to press their advantage and secure the release of the estimated 101 hostages remaining in Gaza , including the seven Americans still being held . <p> Sinwar was Hamas ' political leader and head of Gaza operations . He was the mastermind of the Oct. 7 terror attacks that killed around 1,200 people and led to hundreds more being taken hostage . For the past year , Israel has been working to destroy Hamas through a massive military campaign in Gaza .
