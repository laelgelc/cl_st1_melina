France itself , Macron said , was not delivering any weapons . <p> Macron 's call comes amid mounting public scrutiny of thehigh death toll in Gaza and Israel 's widening conflict with Hezbollah in Lebanon . <p> Macron said Lebanon should not be allowed to " become a new Gaza , " referring to Israel 's ground and air offensive in the country .
