<p> The Republican and Democratic parties form the cornerstone of the American two-party system . However , voters not satisfied with the candidates from either of those organizations do have alternatives including the Libertarian and Green parties . Despite their differences , these parties share commonalities in some respects and play a role in contesting the established political order . <p> The Green Party of the United States , established in 2001 , emerged from the social justice and environmental movements of the 1970s and 1980s , an era characterized by Watergate and the rise of neo-conservatism . The party emphasizes environmental sustainability , grassroots democracy and social justice . <p> The Green Party is an active participant in elections across various levels of government .
