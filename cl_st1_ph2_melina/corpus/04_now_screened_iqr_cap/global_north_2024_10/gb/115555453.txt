" <p> Mandy Damari 's plea for her daughter Emily , 28 , and the other hostages , was made in a recorded message played on Monday at an evening of commemoration and reflection of the October 7 attacks on Israel . <p> To an audience which included Chief Rabbi Sir Ephraim Mirvis and former prime minister Rishi Sunak , she said that the recent news of six hostages being murdered in the tunnels beneath Gaza is a " horror beyond imaginable " . <p> She added : " We have clear photographic evidence of the conditions which Emily and the other hostages are likely living in . <p> " It is a desperate and living hell . <p> " I am @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ being to profess that I need help with the child that I know best , the child that I brought into this world , but I do so desperately need help and I ask you for it now . <p> " I have always believed in the power of prayer but more importantly , the power of collective prayer . " <p> Chief Rabbi Sir Ephraim Mirvis ( Jonathan Brady/PA ) <p> Ms Damari recalled that Emily was in an apartment on Kfar Aza , a peaceful kibbutz , when Hamas brought terror to her home . <p> Ms Damari has previously said that 64 neighbours -- men , women , children and elderly -- were sadistically murdered , 19 were kidnapped , 12 women were eventually released , two hostages were killed in friendly fire while trying to escape and five are still held in Gaza , including Emily . <p> Emily loved her dog Choocha , who was with her and was killed with a gunshot to the neck . <p> Ms Damari also spoke of her charismatic British-Israeli daughter as an Ed Sheeran and an Adele fan @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a mixed sense of classic British humour and Israeli " chutzpah " . <p> In her message , she said : " It 's not widely known or acknowledged that a British national has been held hostage in Gaza for one year now . <p> " As British Jews , it is our duty and responsibility .
