<h> TEHRAN BLOG : Pezeshkian 's dilemma over Haniyeh 's assassination <p> / bne IntelliNews <p> By bne Tehran bureauJuly 31 , 2024 <p> The assassination of Ismail Haniyeh , a senior Hamas leader , in Tehran on July 31 , together with the killing of Hezbollah leader Fouad Chaker in Beirut , poses a severe test for new Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian just one day into his tenure . <p> In the presidential election campaign , Pezeshkian positioned himself as a " moderate " who would try to rebuild links with the West . However , he will now be under severe pressure from Iran 's security apparatus to retaliate against Israel -- widely assumed to be behind the assassinations -- which would make any rapprochement stillborn . If he resists retaliation , however , it would undermine his rule before he has had a chance to establish his authority . <p> The pressure to respond decisively is immense , particularly from the hardline Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps ( IRGC ) . They issued a swift and strongly worded statement promising a " harsh and painful response " against Benjamin Netenyahu 's government in Tel Aviv . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ considerations . Allowing Israel to attack its proxies with impunity might also hasten the collapse of Iran 's so-called " resistance axis " across the region . <p> The assassination has exposed vulnerabilities in Iran 's security apparatus , and represents a humiliating blow to the nation 's image as a regional power . The inability to protect a high-profile guest like Haniyeh not only raises questions about Iran 's intelligence capabilities but also sends a troubling message to other allies and proxies who rely on Iran 's support and protection .
