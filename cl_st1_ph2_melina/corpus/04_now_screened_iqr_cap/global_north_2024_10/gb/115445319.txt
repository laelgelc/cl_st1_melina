<p> Shameful scenes have marred today 's pro-Palestinian protests ahead of the anniversary of the October 7 attacks in Israel as ex-Scottish First Minister Humza Yousaf was spotted in the crowd . <p> Officers have so far made 17 arrests , including some when protesters tried to get past officers who had formed a cordon to stop groups breaking away from the main protest . Three people have been arrested on suspicion of assaulting an emergency worker , the Metropolitan Police said . <p> Two people were arrested for supporting a proscribed organisation , three people have been arrested on suspicion of assault and eight people have been arrested for public order offences , including four believed to be racially aggravated . <p> One person has also been arrested after being accused of breaching Public Order Act conditions .
