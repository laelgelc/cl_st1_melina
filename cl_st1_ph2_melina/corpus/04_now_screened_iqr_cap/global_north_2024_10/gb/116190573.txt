<p> Gazing adoringly at her newborn , Zenab 's face paints a picture of motherly love . <p> But tender moments like these are rare in war-torn Gaza where death casts a shadow over life . Little Laila arrived by C-section in June after her mother braved a two-hour journey passing flattened buildings and ghost towns in the stricken enclave . <p> By contrast , Al-Awda Hospital in Nuseirat Camp -- where up to 55 women are said to give birth daily -- was abuzz with activity . Then aged 33 , Zenab was discharged just two hours after the procedure . " I am so happy to have the most beautiful girls in the universe , " she told Yousef Ruzzi , a photographer working for the charity , CARE International , who accompanied her to hospital . " I hope I can raise them in the best possible way and educate them in the best schools and universities in the world .
