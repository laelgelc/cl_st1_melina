As Israel has expanded its military aims from full-scale offensive military operations to degrade Hamas in Gaza , through defensive and then offensive air and ground operations in Lebanon , we have now arrived at what was previously considered the worst-case scenario -- acknowledged direct attacks between Israel and Iran on each other 's territory . <p> This was previously the red line that neither side would cross because of fears it would lead to a broader regional conflict . But the regional security landscape is much different to that of a year ago . For all its public rhetoric , Tehran understands the risks involved in a direct confrontation with Israel , which enjoys air supremacy and , through its Western support network , technical and intelligence superiority . After Israel 's targeting of the Iranian diplomatic compound in Damascus in April , Tehran had little choice but to directly respond . <p> In doing so , however , it favoured scale over effectiveness .
