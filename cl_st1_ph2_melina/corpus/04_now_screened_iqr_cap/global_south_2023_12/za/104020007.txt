Palestinians , an event known as the Nakba , or " catastrophe " . <p> Israel declared war on Hamas , which rules Gaza , after its brutal 7 October attacks on Israel killed 1,200 people , mostly civilians , according to Israeli authorities . <p> With the health ministry in the Palestinian territory reporting a death toll of more than 15,500 people , the impact of Israel 's relentless bombardment and ground offensive has sparked growing international concern . <p> Since the collapse last week of a truce that allowed for the release of dozens of Israeli and other hostages in
