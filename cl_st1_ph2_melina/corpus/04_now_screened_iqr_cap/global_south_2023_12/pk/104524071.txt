<p> The UN Security Council on Friday passed a key resolution , emphasizing the immediate acceleration of aid deliveries to the distressed civilians in Gaza , but without the original call for an " urgent suspension of hostilities " between Israel and Hamas . <p> Resolution 2720 demands parties to the conflict " allow , facilitate and enable the immediate , safe and unhindered delivery of humanitarian assistance at scale directly to the Palestinian civilian population throughout the Gaza Strip , and in this regard calls for urgent steps to immediately allow safe , unhindered , and expanded humanitarian access and to create the conditions for a sustainable cessation of hostilities . " <p> The resolution demands that the parties to the conflict " allow and facilitate the use of all available routes to and throughout the entire Gaza Strip , " and requests the UN secretary-general to " appoint a senior humanitarian and reconstruction coordinator " with responsibility for facilitating , coordinating , monitoring , and verifying in Gaza , as appropriate , the humanitarian nature of all humanitarian relief consignments to Gaza provided through states which are not party to the conflict . <p> The text demands @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " as well as ensuring humanitarian access to address medical needs of all hostages , and demands the provision of fuel to Gaza at levels that will meet requisite humanitarian needs . <p> The resolution reiterates its " unwavering commitment " to the vision of the " two-state solution " where two democratic states , Israel and Palestine , live side by side in peace within secure and recognized borders , consistent with international law and relevant UN resolutions , and in this regard stresses the importance of " unifying the Gaza Strip with the West Bank under the Palestinian Authority . " <p> The proposed resolution , presented by the United Arab Emirates , garnered support from 13 council members , while the United States and Russia opted to abstain from the vote within the 15-member council . <p> The vote followed a U.S. veto of a Russian amendment that would have restored the call for a " suspension of hostilities . " <p> " We expect action to expand humanitarian assistance in Gaza , including by the full use of Karem Shalom and the opening of other crossing points , " the ambassador said , while urging Israel to stop its collective punishment of the population of Gaza and insisting that realization of a ceasefire remains the overriding goal .
