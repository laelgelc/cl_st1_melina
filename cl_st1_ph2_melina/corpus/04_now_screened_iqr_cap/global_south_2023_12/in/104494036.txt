<h> India supported another resolution at the UN General Assembly this week , endorsing the right of the Palestinian people to self-determination . The resolution received support from 172 members , with the US and Israel being the only countries voting against it . <p> While 172 members supported the resolution , the US and Israel were the only countries that voted against it . <p> Earlier last week , India , two months after it abstained , voted in favour of a draft resolution in the General Assembly that demanded an immediate humanitarian ceasefire in the Israel-Hamas conflict as well as the unconditional release of all hostages . The US and Israel , besides eight others , voted against the resolution . <p> India 's voting pattern at the UN since last week has been able to soothe certain ruffled feathers in the Arab world , which felt that the country has titled more towards Israel , ET has learnt . New Delhi has been maintaining that no durable peace is possible in the region without a two-state solution . <p> The 193-member UN General Assembly adopted the draft resolution , introduced by Egypt , at an emergency @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ resolution was adopted with 153 votes in favour , while 23 nations abstained and 10 voted against the text . <p> The resolution , sponsored among others by Algeria , Bahrain , Iraq , Kuwait , Oman , Qatar , Saudi Arabia , the UAE and Palestine , demanded an immediate humanitarian ceasefire in Gaza and reiterated its demand that all parties comply with their obligations under international law , " notably with regard to the protection of civilians " . <p> It also demanded the " immediate and unconditional release of all hostages , as well as ensuring humanitarian access " . <p> Speaking on that occasion , India 's permanent representative to the UN , Ruchira Kamboj , had noted that there is an enormous humanitarian crisis and a large-scale loss of civilian lives , especially of women and children . <p> " There is the issue of observing international humanitarian law in all circumstances . That resolution , drafted by Jordan , had also demanded the immediate , continuous , sufficient and unhindered provision of essential goods and services to civilians throughout the Gaza Strip .
