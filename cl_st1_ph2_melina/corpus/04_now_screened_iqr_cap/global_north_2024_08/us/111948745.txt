Both Bell and the United Democracy Project , which spent over $8 million on his behalf , took issue with Bush 's uninhibited criticism of Israel 's war in Gaza and her ambivalence about condemning Hamas ( though she eventually came around to agreeing it 's a terrorist group ) .
