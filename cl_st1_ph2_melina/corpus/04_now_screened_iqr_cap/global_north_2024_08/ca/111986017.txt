<h> Canada pulls diplomats ' kids out of Israel as fear of broader war builds <p> The flags of Canada and Israel fly at half mast at a community building on Wednesday , Oct. 11 , 2023 in Ottawa . THE CANADIAN PRESS/Adrian Wyld <p> By Laura Osman , The Canadian Press <p> Posted Aug 7 , 2024 10:17:32 PM . <p> Last Updated Aug 7 , 2024 10:45:14 PM . <p> OTTAWA -- The Canadian government says it decided to pull its diplomats ' children and their guardians out of Israel , amid fears over an expanded Mideast war . <p> Global Affairs Canada says it has approved the temporary relocation of the children and their guardians to a safe third country . <p> Embassy staff are expected to remain in Israel . <p> " The Embassy of Canada to Israel in Tel Aviv , the Embassy of Canada to Lebanon in Beirut , and the Representative Office of Canada to the Palestinian Authority , all remain fully operational and continue to provide essential services to Canadians , including consular services , " Global Affairs Canada said in a statement Wednesday evening . <p> Diplomats stationed in nearby @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , do not have dependents living with them . <p> " Staff at our missions in Lebanon and Ramallah remain in place and are being regularly updated on the ongoing situation and the measures Global Affairs Canada is taking , " the department said . <p> Tensions in the Middle East have sparked fears about an all-out war after the killing last week of a senior Hezbollah commander in Lebanon and Hamas 's top political leader in Iran . <p> On Saturday , the government warned Canadians to avoid all travel to Israel due to the " ongoing regional armed conflict and the unpredictable security situation . " <p> The government also advises against travelling to the West Bank , Gaza Strip , Jerusalem and Lebanon . <p> The current conflict broke out on Oct 7 , after Hamas launched a deadly surprise attack on Israel that killed an estimated 1,200 people . The Hamas-run Health Ministry in Gaza says more than 39,100 Palestinians have since been killed in the war . <p> The Gaza Strip has been ravaged by the conflict , which has also seen a previously unprecedented @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Israeli attacks on Iran and Lebanon , including the capital city of Beirut . <p> Prime Minister Justin Trudeau spoke with Jordan 's King Abdullah II earlier this week to express his " deep concern " over the risk of expanded conflict between Israel and Iran , as well as Hezbollah and other Iran-aligned groups . <p> " The leaders underscored the urgent need to avoid further escalation , which puts the lives of civilians in the region at risk , " the Prime Minister 's Office said in a summary of the Tuesday call .
