<p> The United Nations World Food Programme ( WFP ) has announced a suspension of its operations in Gaza " until further notice " following a serious security incident in which one of its vehicles was struck by gunfire at an Israeli military checkpoint . <p> The incident occurred on Tuesday as the WFP vehicle , clearly marked with UN branding , approached the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) checkpoint at the Wadi Gaza Bridge after completing a mission in southern Gaza . <p> Cindy McCain , head of the WFP , condemned the attack , stating : " This is totally unacceptable and the latest in a series of unnecessary security incidents that have endangered the lives of WFP 's team in Gaza . As last night 's events show , the current deconfliction system is failing and this can not go on any longer . " <p> The vehicle , which was hit at least 10 times , had secured the necessary clearances prior to approaching the checkpoint . Despite the severity of the attack , no injuries were reported among the WFP staff .
