<h> Gaza medical students allowed to continue studies in Pakistan <p> ISLAMABAD -- In a landmark decision , the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council ( PM&amp;DC ) has permitted medical/dental students from Gaza to complete their studies in Pakistan . <p> The council 's decision enables Gaza students to continue their medical education in Pakistan on humanitarian grounds to ensure that their studies are not interrupted due to the challenging circumstances in their home country . <p> The PM&amp;DC Council in its recent meeting made this decision on the request of the Pakistani High Commissioner in London to adjust the students of Gaza in Pakistan keeping in mind the current scenario so that opportunity could be provided to form a stable and resourceful environment for Gaza students to pursue their medical degrees and contribute to the healthcare sector in Palestine . <p> However , under the PM&amp;DC law/regulations , the council does n't have any specific provision of clinical rotation of foreign students in Pakistan . According to the PM&amp;DC spokesperson , however , on the request of the high commission the matter was placed before the council .
