<h> Israeli air strike on Gaza school kills at least 16 <p> At least 16 people have been killed in an Israeli air strike on a school in the Gaza Strip , Palestinian officials have said . Dozens more have been injured . <p> The building was sheltering thousands of displaced people at Nuseirat refugee camp in central Gaza , according to the Hamas-run health ministry . <p> The Israel Defence Force ( IDF ) said it struck several " terrorists operating in structures located in the area of Al-Jaouni School " . <p> Meanwhile , there have been reports that 10 people were killed in a separate airstrike on a house at the camp . <p> Video from the scene of the Nuseirat school strike shows adults and children screaming in a smoke-filled street covered in dust and rubble , as they run to help the wounded . <p> Eyewitnesses told the BBC that the attack targeted the upper floors of the school , which is located near a busy market . <p> The BBC understands that up to 7,000 people were using the building as shelter . <p> One woman told the AFP news agency how some @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the building was hit . <p> " This is the fourth time they have targeted the school without warning , " she said . <p> A local source said the target was a room allegedly used by Hamas police . The BBC is unable to verify this claim . <p> Hamas said five local journalists were among those killed in Israeli attacks on Saturday . Members of their family were also reportedly targeted . <p> More than 100 journalists have lost their lives in Gaza since the 7 October attacks , according to Reporters Without Borders . <p> Hamas said the five latest fatalities brings the number to 158 . <p> In a statement posted to X -- formerly known as Twitter -- the IDF confirmed it had hit the school buildings , saying it had taken " numerous steps " to " mitigate the risk of harming civilians , including the use of precise aerial surveillance and additional intelligence " . <p> Hamas militants were using the location as a " hideout " to carry out attacks against IDF troops , it said . <p> " Hamas continues to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ civilian population as human shields for its terrorist attacks against the State of Israel , " it added . <p> Hamas called the attack a " massacre " on " defenceless displaced civilians " . <p> Many of the dead and wounded were women , children and the elderly , the group claimed via its English language Telegram channel .
