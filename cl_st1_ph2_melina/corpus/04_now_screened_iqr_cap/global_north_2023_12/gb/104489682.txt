aid to the Gaza Strip . <p> He added that the Kingdom has successfully established a new path for transporting humanitarian aid directly into Gaza via the King Hussein Bridge , the Jordan News Agency , Petra , reported . <p> Mubaidin noted that the Jordanian Hashemite Charity Organisation ( JHCO ) and the World Food Programme dispatched on Wednesday a 46-truck convoy loaded with 750 tonnes of basic food aid to Gaza . <p> The minister stressed that this initiative is a " significant triumph " for the Jordanian diplomacy in ensuring sustainable support for Gaza , and noted that this new path
