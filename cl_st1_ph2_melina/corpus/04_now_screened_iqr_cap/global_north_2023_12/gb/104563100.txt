<h> Pope blasts weapons industry as he makes Christmas appeal for world peace <h> Pope blasts weapons industry as he makes Christmas appeal for world peace <p> Pope Francis blasted the weapons industry and its " instruments of death " that fuel wars as he made a Christmas Day appeal for peace in the world and in particular between Israel and the Palestinians . <p> Speaking from the loggia of St Peter 's Basilica to the throngs of people below , Francis said he grieved the " abominable attack " of Hamas against southern Israel on October 7 and called for the release of hostages . <p> He also called for an end to Israel 's military campaign in Gaza and the " appalling harvest of innocent civilians " and urged that humanitarian aid reach those in need . <p> Francis devoted his Christmas Day blessing to a call for peace in the world , noting that the biblical story of the birth of Christ in Bethlehem sent a message of peace . <p> But he said that Bethlehem " is a place of
