protest against the country 's support of Israel . <p> More than 100 protesters , some banging drums and waving Palestinian flags , staged a protest on the roadway leading to the residence inside Phoenix Park . <p> A large police deployment , including a mounted unit , was present at the scene and after some protesters blocked the roadway in a sit-down protest , barriers were erected to behind the garda barriers . <p> Some demonstrators sat on the roadway as others chanted " from the river to the sea , Palestine will be free " , " United States you ca n't hide , you 're committing
