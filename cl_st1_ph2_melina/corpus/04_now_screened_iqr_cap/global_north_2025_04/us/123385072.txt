<h> MK Ohad Tal urged the Knesset to probe the Shin Bet 's Jewish Department over alleged illegal detentions , bias , and abuse of suspects in the West Bank . <p> Religious Zionist Party MK Ohad Tal demanded in a letter to Knesset Speaker Amir Ohana earlier this week that the Knesset establish a parliamentary commission of inquiry into the Shin Bet ( Israel Security Agency ) department that deals with acts of Jewish terrorism . <p> The demand came after the department head , as reported by Channel 11 , was recorded calling Jewish suspects in the West Bank " shmucks " and saying that his department arrests individuals " even without evidence " for a few days . <p> In the recording , the Shin Bet officer directed Israel Police Supt. Avishai Moalem , then head of the Judea and Samaria District Police , to " put them in detention cells with rats . " <p> Moalem has since been suspended over a criminal investigation into the alleged leaking of sensitive information from police investigations to National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir in exchange for promotion . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Jewish Department head said he would take a leave of absence while the agency conducts an inquiry into the matter .
