Civilian and military officials have been removed from their positions with the justification that they were " diversity hires " . Federal funding for schools has been conditioned on principals signing anti-DEI certificates . <p> The private sector has followed suit . Company after company has announced they are putting an end to DEI targets and abandoning DEI programmes .
