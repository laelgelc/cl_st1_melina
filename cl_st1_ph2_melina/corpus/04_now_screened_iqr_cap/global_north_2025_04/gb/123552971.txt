<p> Pro-Palestine activists have blocked access to a Leicestershire drone factory by dumping a truckload of manure at its gates . The protest at UAV Tactical Systems ( U-TacS ) is part of co-ordinated actions targeting Israeli-owned Elbit Systems ' operations in Britain . <p> Two activists locked themselves inside the vehicle to maintain the blockade at the Meridian Business Park site in Braunstone , according to Palestine Action . The group said the action today ( Monday , April 14 ) was intended to " halt operations " at the facility , which it claimed exports military drones to Israel . <p> The campaign group has repeatedly targeted the site over several years .
