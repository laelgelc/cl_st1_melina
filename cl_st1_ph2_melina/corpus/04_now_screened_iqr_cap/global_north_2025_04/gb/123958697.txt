It does not necessarily reflect the view of The Herald . <p> Protesters gathered outside an MP 's office today , April 25 , to protest against the government 's sale of weapons to Israel . <p> The protest was organised by campaign group Bolton For Palestine . <p> It took place at the office of Bolton North East MP Kirith Entwistle on Blackburn Road , Astley Bridge . <p> A large group of people stood outside Ms Entwistle 's office , holding placards which said " Free Palestine " and " Stop arming Israel " . <p> Horwich town councillor and former Bolton councillor , Kevin McKeon , said : " In Bolton there are thousands of people who are committed to the Palestinian cause . <p> " We decided to have small demonstrations outside MPs offices .
