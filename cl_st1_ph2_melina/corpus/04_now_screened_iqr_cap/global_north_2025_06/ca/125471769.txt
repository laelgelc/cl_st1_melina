Local authorities said on Monday that the number of people injured had risen to 12 , with two people remaining hospitalized . <p> Soliman , an Egyptian national who entered the United States on a tourist visa in 2022 , yelled " Free Palestine " during the attack , said he wanted to " kill all Zionist people " and did not regret his actions , according to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Soliman told detectives that he had learned about the event online , which is also where he learned to construct the homemade incendiary devices after being denied a gun permit because of his immigration status . <p> He said he had purchased supplies at Target and Home Depot and had dressed as a gardener carrying flowers , with a backpack containing sprayable gasoline , to gain proximity to the demonstrators .
