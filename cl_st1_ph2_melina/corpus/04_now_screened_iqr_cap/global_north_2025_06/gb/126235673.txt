Their aim was to put pressure on the Israeli government to allow much-needed aid into Gaza . <p> Karen said that , on Friday June 13 , she and four other protestors were in a taxi from Cairo on their way to the city of Al Arish , where the march was due to begin , when they were stopped at an Egyptian military checkpoint near Ismailia .
