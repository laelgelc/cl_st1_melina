The YPA said the force sent three unmarked police cars . <p> A Labour party spokesperson said that , on Gaza , Mr Charters " believes the situation there is currently intolerable " . <p> The YPA described their protest as an " important action " , as they feel that Mr Charters has not represented his constituents in parliament over their concerns on the ongoing war @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " Luke has blocked constituents on social media who voice views supportive of Palestine and refused to meet with them , " a representative for the YPA said . <p> They added : " Many constituents of York Outer were at the protest to ask Luke to follow the example of Rachael Maskell , Labour MP for York Central , in holding their government to account . <p> " Only last week Rachael discovered that the UK continues to train members of the IDF on our soil . She is a very active campaigner and willing to listen to all sides of the argument . " <p> Some 20 people stood outside St Oswald 's Church , adorned with flags and placards directed at Mr Charters and their complaints against him .
