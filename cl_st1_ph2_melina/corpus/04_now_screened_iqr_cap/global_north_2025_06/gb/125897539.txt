It does not necessarily reflect the view of The Herald . <p> AFTER the Second World War , when six million Jews were murdered in the Holocaust , the world came together and said : " Never again . " <p> It was a promise to stop genocide and mass killings - anywhere , anytime , to anyone . <p> But it happened again in Srebrenica in 1995 , when more than 8,000 Bosnian Muslim men and boys were killed in just a few days . <p> The world once more said : " Never again . " <p> Yet today , we are watching another human tragedy unfold in Gaza - and once again , the world seems @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ people of Gaza - over two million of them , including children and the elderly - have been living through horror . <p> Homes , hospitals , schools , and even places of worship have been bombed . Whole families have been killed . Children are dying not just from attacks , but from hunger , dehydration , and lack of medical care . The basic right to live in safety has been stripped away . <p> The pain is not just in the numbers - it 's in the silence . <p> Yes , the killing of innocent people in Israel on October 7 was horrific and wrong . That tragedy should never be ignored or justified . But what is happening in Gaza now is not justice - it is collective punishment on an entire population . International law is clear : targeting civilians , especially children , is never acceptable . <p> When genocide happens , it often begins with dehumanising a people - treating them as less worthy , less human . <p> That is what we saw in the Holocaust . That 's what we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we are seeing in Gaza today . <p> Why is it that when civilians are killed in Europe , it 's called a crime - but when it happens in Gaza , it 's called " self-defence " ? <p> Why are we so quick to act in some places , and so slow in others ? This double standard is not just unfair - it 's dangerous . <p> It tells some people that their lives do n't matter as much . <p> We must not let the words " never again " become meaningless . They should apply to all people , regardless of their race , religion , or nationality . <p> This is not just a Muslim issue . It is not just a Palestinian issue .
