They also announced , because of Diamond 's death , they would be filing additional charges against the suspect , Mohamed Sabry Soliman , including first-degree murder . <p> " This horrific attack has now claimed the life of an innocent person who was beloved by her family and friends , " said DA Michael Dougherty .
