( Angela Dennis/Tennessee Lookout ) <p> A grand jury in Knox County has indicted University of Tennessee protesters who participated in on-campus demonstrations more than a year ago , protesting Israeli actions in Gaza . <p> At a hearing in June 2024 , Judge Judson Kyle Davis acknowledged the courage of the demonstrators but stated that his role was not to make a decision driven by emotions . <p> " All I have to consider is the statute of criminal trespassing . There have been some good arguments that were obviously very well studied , and some of those arguments are hard for me to disagree with , but it 's not something for me to consider , " Davis said . " I think it 's more of a jury question . " <p> The Knox County District Attorney 's Office sent the case to a grand @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ June 18 , according to attorney Dillon Zinser , who is representing one of the plaintiffs . <p> According to records from the Knox County Sheriff 's Office , seven of the 12 protesters indicted are scheduled for arraignment July 16 and July 22 in Knox County Criminal Court . They are charged with criminal trespassing , a class C misdemeanor punishable by up to 30 days in jail and a $50 fine . The others are still awaiting court dates , according to Zinser . <p> Zinser said District Attorney Charme Allen 's office showed a significant delay in waiting a year before bringing the cases before a grand jury and could have chosen to dismiss each of the cases . <p> " We believed and hoped that perhaps they would do the right thing , " Zinser said . " But now that they 've chosen to indict , that 's a signal to all of us that they are deciding to prosecute this case to the fullest extent of the law . " <p> He also said that they plan to fight the charges . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ do n't believe they broke the law , and we do n't believe they broke the law , " Zinser said . " <p> Attorney Mike Whalen , who is representing five of the protesters indicted , said he was stunned by the decision to move forward with charges nearly a year after the arrests . <p> " The idea that they would just let these things sit in the grand jury for a year before they bring it out , it 's ridiculous . It just makes no sense .
