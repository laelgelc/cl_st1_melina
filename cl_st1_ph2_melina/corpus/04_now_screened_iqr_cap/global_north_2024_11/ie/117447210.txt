This time round it has become the hidden game . <p> When Ireland played Israel in Riga back in February in a FIBA women 's Eurobasket qualifier , it commanded not just the national media spotlight but the world 's . <p> It went well beyond Michael Darragh Macauley on behalf of Irish Sport for Palestine protesting outside Leinster House and appearing on Prime Time appealing for Ireland to boycott the fixture . <p> Everyone from CNN to BBC and Sky News Australia to Al Jazeera showed images of the Irish team standing by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ national anthems in protest of Israel 's Dor Saar accusing Ireland of being " quite anti-Semitic " in an interview with the Israeli federation 's own website . <p> People here were up in arms -- not least because the Israelis openly displayed arms , posing for photographs with the IDF and having assault weapons pictured courtside while they trained . <p> And caught in the maelstrom was an Irish team and federation that could please no one . <p> By declining to host the game in Dublin , they offended the Israeli federation who accused them for " not standing by us and not responding in the spirit of sports " . <p> But by playing it at all they were criticised at home here for failing to take the opportunity to show solidarity to a besieged people and instead inadvertently legitimising a terrorist state . <p> When the team refused to engage in the normal pre and post-match pleasantries , unimpressed commentators felt it was far too little too late : that it took Saar 's comments for them to take exception , not the actual bombardment on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Europe felt the Irish team went too far , hitting them with an official warning for such " unsportsmanlike conduct " . <p> The Israeli media were naturally even more scathing , and just as triumphant about the eventual result ( 87-57 ) as the Israeli team was on the evening . <p> This Sunday , at 12.15pm Irish time , the two sides meet again in Riga . Which is probably news to you . There 's been hardly a word about it . <p> Most competitive games featuring either the men 's or women 's senior national team are now televised by TG4 or its online platform , including last Thursday 's game against Latvia , the first of two games the women have in this international window .
