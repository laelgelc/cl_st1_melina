<p> The chair of the Holocaust Memorial Day Trust ( HMDT ) has issued an " unreserved " apology for mentioning ' devastating violence against Palestinians ' in the text of the official invitation to next year 's Holocaust Memorial Day ceremony . <p> Some Holocaust survivors and their descendants who spoke to the JC had accused HMDT of trivialising the memory of the Holocaust . <p> The official invitation for the ceremony referred to the " devastating violence against Palestinian civilians in Gaza " as a result of Israel 's war against terrorist group Hamas following its atrocities on October 7 . <p> One Holocaust survivor who spoke to the JC on the condition of anonymity said they were " uncomfortable as a survivor in the event being politicised , even indirectly " , adding that there were " legitimately differing views among survivors " . <p> The text of the invitation issued by the HMDT for the event on January 27 next year , which was seen by the JC , said : " 80 years on from the liberation of Auschwitz-Birkenau , our mission is more vital @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in Israel on 7 October 2023 , including the ongoing plight of those taken as hostages , and the devastating violence against Palestinian civilians in Gaza . " <p> Lord Carlile KC , the former independent reviewer of terrorism legislation and son of a Holocaust survivor , said HMDT " should have been much more careful in the words they used " . <p> He continued : " The Shoah is a uniquely horrible event that is not being replicated in Gaza . Whatever criticism one might make of Israel 's military tactics , to equate it with the Holocaust is factually and legally inaccurate . " <p> Baronness Deech said : " In many countries , sadly now including the UK , the Holocaust is being used to tell a nationalist or politically convenient story . It was shocking and insensitive to see how far HMDT has gone along that road . It is time for the Jewish community to reclaim the memory of our unique tragedy and explain its antisemitic roots our way . " <p> He added : " No wonder Israel is in the dock when even the HMDT can put this kind of stuff out . " <p> The child of one survivor , who agreed to speak to the JC anonymously , said that they had : " always been upset by the way the national memorial event compares the Holocaust to other genocides , but to see them criticise Israel 's campaign to defend itself against terrorism in this context is utterly disgusting " . <p> They went on : " The government should not be funding something like this and it 's high time ministers sorted it out .
