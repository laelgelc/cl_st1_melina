College students , as a population , are increasingly diverse and represent a variety of backgrounds and lived experiences . To elected officials , they continue to be filtered into two categories : college versus no college or the 18- to 25-year-old population , Monroe says . <p> " What I tell my students all the time , if the 18- to 25-year-old population voted in ways that are expected of those 65 and older , the government would be much more responsive to their desires , " Monroe says , referring to policy priorities among elected officials . <p> The most important thing is for students to be well informed and do their own research to truly know why they 're voting and whom they 're voting for , Monroe adds .
