<h> First , what 's the Palestinian Authority ? <p> The PA was established as an interim Palestinian government under the 1993 Oslo Accords , which then-Israeli Prime Minister Yitzhak Rabin signed with Arafat . <p> The PA was originally conceived to administer basic provisions -- such as education , security , water and electricity -- for Palestinians living under Israeli occupation in Gaza and parts of the West Bank , including occupied East Jerusalem . <p> Oslo divided the West Bank into Areas A , B and C , giving the PA security and administrative control over Area A and administrative control over Area B. <p> But Israel routinely conducts violent raids all over the occupied West Bank . <p> Critics say the PA effectively acts as a security apparatus on behalf of the Israeli occupation . <h> Why is the PA still around ?
