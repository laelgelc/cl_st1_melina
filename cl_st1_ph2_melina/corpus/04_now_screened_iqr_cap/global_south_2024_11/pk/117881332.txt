Trump 's campaign promise to end ongoing wars , particularly in Ukraine and the Middle East , captured widespread attention , but what the world failed to grasp was his lack of emphasis on achieving peaceful settlements to these long-standing conflicts . His rhetoric centered on withdrawing from prolonged engagements , not necessarily addressing their root causes or creating frameworks for durable peace .
