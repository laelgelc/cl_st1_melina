<p> CAIRO -- Saudi Arabia and Egypt roundly rejected the collective punishment of the Palestinian people and urged Israel to stop the aggression on unarmed civilians in the Gaza Strip . <p> In a joint press conference after the Egyptian-Saudi consultations in Cairo on Sunday , Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan and his Egyptian counterpart Sameh Shoukry emphasized that the priority of both the countries is to stop the fighting in Gaza and bringing in aid there . <p> The Egyptian-Saudi consultations at the level of the foreign ministers of the two countries focused on the situation in the region that poses a threat to the countries of the region . The foreign ministers stressed the importance of coordination between the two countries on regional issues , especially the Palestinian issue . <p> In his speech , Prince Faisal bin Farhan underlined the need for a resolution from the part of the international community that binds everyone regarding the Palestinians , stressing that the collective punishment of the Palestinian people is a gross violation of international law . <p> He stressed the need to continue pressure , along with other Arab countries , to protect the Palestinian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ necessity of an immediate ceasefire in Gaza .
