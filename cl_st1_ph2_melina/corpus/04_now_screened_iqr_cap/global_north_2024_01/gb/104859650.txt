<p> A British-Palestinian scientist who has returned to Manchester after 65 days living in war-torn Gaza has recounted the horrors he witnessed . There they slept in the basement along with around 500 others seeking shelter from the bombardment , including journalists and aid workers . <p> Recalling the horror of life in the basement , Mohammed said : " All the while there was bombing around us .
