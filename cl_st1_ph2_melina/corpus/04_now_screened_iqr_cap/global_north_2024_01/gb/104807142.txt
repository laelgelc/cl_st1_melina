<p> A Liverpool surgeon said he wakes up each morning and the first thing he does is check if his friends have " survived " the night . <p> Dr Abdel Hammad , a surgeon at the Royal Liverpool University Hospital , travelled to war-torn Gaza at the start of October to perform life-saving transplants . The surgeon went to the Palestinian city as a charity volunteer as part of a hospital project , Liverpool International Transplant Initiative ( LITI ) , that sends medics to the conflict-hit region . <p> The 67-year-old , who lives inWest Kirby but is originally from Iraq , arrived in Gaza on Friday , October 6 , and was scheduled to leave the following Monday after completing four transplants - two on children and two on adults .
