Phoebe Maltz Bovy on why some progressives are mad at the young Israeli refusing to serve in the Israel Defence Forces <p> January 3 , 2024 <p> By Phoebe Maltz Bovy <p> Tal Mitnick , an 18-year-old Israeli , has gone to jail for refusing to serve in the Israel Defence Forces due to his opposition to Israel 's war in Gaza . You might imagine that some hear this and wish he 'd serve , while others -- in agreement with his stance or not -- admire his commitment to his principles . <p> What you might not have seen coming -- what I , for one , did not expect -- was how furious people are at Mitnick ... from the pro-Palestinian left . At first it seemed like what I was noticing was the hard-to-explain online phenomenon of a lot of people complaining about a weird take they 'd seen , albeit one that 's not representative of anything and easily dismissed . I dug a bit and here it seems like yeah people really are arguing this . Below , a smattering of the posts in question . Social media favours extreme stances . Whether it 's clout or money you 're after , the more unhinged your take , the better it 's likely to fare . All publicity is good publicity for the hate-quote-tweeted . And yes , asking Israelis to go " home " to " Brooklyn " is unhinged . There is content similarly removed from its hinges on the pro-Israel side . If you want the clout-clicks , be sure to say you want all Palestinians moved to Sardinia .
