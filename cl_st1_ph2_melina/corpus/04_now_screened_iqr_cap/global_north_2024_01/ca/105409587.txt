<p> United Nations , Jan 26 ( IANS ) The World Court on Friday ordered Israel to ensure that its military does not commit any actions that could be genocide in its campaign in Gaza against Hamas , but it did not dictate a ceasefire . <p> The court , formally known as the International Court of Justice , also ordered Israel to immediately allow " urgently-needed basic services and humanitarian assistance " to the Palestinians of Gaza . <p> Citing inflammatory rhetoric from some Israeli leaders , the court also ordered Israel to punish and prevent incitement to genocide . <p> The court , which is based in The Hague , has no way of enforcing its judgments and countries can -- and do -- flout them . <p> It also turned down the request of South Africa to order a ceasefire , while considering whether a genocide had been committed . <p> The interim judgment delivered by the court 's President Joan Donoghue spoke at length about the sufferings of the people of Gaza and the long-term effects of Israeli action . <p> " The court is acutely aware of the extent of the human tragedy that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the continuing loss of life and human suffering , " it said . <p> The
