<p> Two Palestinian-Irish sisters living in Waterford are walking 320km throughout January to raise funds for relatives in Gaza . <p> Sara and Emma Abu Selmia set up the GoFundMe to raise money for their family currently suffering as a result of the devastating ongoing war in Gaza . Sara and Emma 's father left Gaza in 1984 to finish his studies , where he met their mother . <p> Their father has six brothers and two sisters in Gaza , who alongside their husbands , wives and children have been displaced from their homes , with some now living in tents and struggling to find food and clean water . <p> Sara and Emma 's family members left their homes in Northern Gaza with a single backpack , as the area they lived in had become unsafe to stay .
