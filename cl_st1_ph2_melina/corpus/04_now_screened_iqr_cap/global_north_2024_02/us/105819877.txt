<h> Why ' transfer ' from Gaza is the Hamas war 's biggest taboo <p> Hundreds of thousands of Gazans are facing brutal Hamas rule , endless Israeli airstrikes and devastating destruction . So why is the West so opposed to letting them emigrate if they desire ? And Israel 's military operations have displaced hundreds of thousands more . <p> The result : The Hamas assailants are increasingly portrayed as victims in a manipulative twisting of the truth . <p> In its 1988 Covenant , Hamas made its goals clear : " To raise the banner of Allah over every inch of Palestine .
