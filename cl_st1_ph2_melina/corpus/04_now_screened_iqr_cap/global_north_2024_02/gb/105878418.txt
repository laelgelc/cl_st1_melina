<p> This week , two hostages were rescued from Hamas by the Israeli Defense Forces ( IDF ) in a daring night-time raid in Rafah . Prime Minister Netanyahu hailed the triumph , proclaiming the war would continue until all the hostages were freed . <p> While this is the stuff movies are made of , the unvarnished fact is that 73 Israelis out of a total 112 hostages released in total by Hamas have been freed through negotiation and pauses in hostilities , with only two rescued by military force . Three others were accidently chased and shot dead by the IDF during a combat mission . More are believed killed in the military operation , which has also caused the deaths of more than 20,000 Palestinians in Gaza . <p> As 72-year-old Adina Moshe , the very first hostage freed back in November as part of a temporary ceasefire deal brokered by Qatar said at a press conference last week : " I 'm very afraid and very concerned that if you continue with this line of destroying Hamas , there wo n't be any hostages left to release .
