" <p> He explained that Hamas has clear demands for a ceasefire , which include ending the Israeli aggression , allowing the return of displaced persons to Gaza City and the northern sector without restrictions , rebuilding the region , and ending the siege . <p> The Hamas official stressed that no agreement will be reached until the demands are met . <p> Palestinian sources @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the latest round of talks for a ceasefire in three stages . <p> According to Palestinian sources , the proposal includes , in the first stage , the return of displaced civilians in Gaza to the northern sector without specifying their numbers . <p> Furthermore , the proposal entails Israel 's agreement to open the Rashid and Salah al-Din streets , move its forces 500 meters away from them , permit the daily entry of 500 aid trucks to all areas of the Gaza Strip , and release 900 prisoners , including 100 serving life sentences . <p> The second stage involves the release of all Israeli prisoners and the finalization of negotiations for a lasting peace . <p> The Israeli newspaper Yedioth Ahronoth reported that Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu did not agree with this proposal , while most members of the Israeli war cabinet supported it . <p> Israeli soldiers prepare their tanks in a army camp near Israel 's border with the Gaza Strip , April 8 , 2024. /CFP <p> Date for ground attack on Rafah set <p> Netanyahu said on Monday that Israel has set @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> He did not say when the offensive would take place , but said that " the victory requires entry into Rafah and the elimination of the terrorist battalions there .
