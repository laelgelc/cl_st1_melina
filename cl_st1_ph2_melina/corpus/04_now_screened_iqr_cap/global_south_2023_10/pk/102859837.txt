Authorities said Deir el-Balah in central Gaza , had suffered most from one of the most intense nights of shelling since Israel started its bombardment in response to Hamas ' cross-border attacks on October 7 . <p> More than 260 people died in 24 hours , according to the health ministry toll , which said 1,873 of those killed in the past fortnight were children .
