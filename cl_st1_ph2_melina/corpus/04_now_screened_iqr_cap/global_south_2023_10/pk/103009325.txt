This is why we 're here : we 're calling for a ceasefire , calling for Palestinian rights , the right to exist , to live , human rights , all our rights , " said protester Camille Revuelta . <p> " This is not about Hamas . This is about protecting Palestinian lives , " she added . <p> Echoing Washington 's stance , Sunak 's government has stopped short @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pauses to allow aid to reach people in Gaza . <p> Britain has supported Israel 's right to defend itself after the Oct. 7 attack by militant group Hamas that Israel said killed 1,400 people , mostly civilians . <p> The death toll in Gaza has climbed to 7,650 dead , also mostly civilians , since Israel 's bombardment began three weeks ago , according to a daily report released on Saturday from the Palestinian health ministry . <p> There has been strong support and sympathy for Israel from Western governments and many citizens over the Hamas attacks , but the Israeli response has also prompted anger , particularly in Arab and Muslim countries . <p> In Malaysia , a large crowd of demonstrators chanted slogans outside the US embassy in Kuala Lumpur . <p> Addressing hundreds of thousands of supporters at a huge rally in Istanbul , Turkish President Tayyip Erdogan said Israel was an occupier , and repeated his stance about Hamas not being a terrorist organisation . <p> Erdogan drew a sharp rebuke from Israel this week for calling the Palestinian group " freedom fighters " . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the Israeli-occupied West Bank , Palestinian protesters in Hebron called on Saturday for a global boycott of Israeli products . <p> " Do n't contribute to the killing of the children of Palestine , " they chanted . <p> Elsewhere in Europe , people took to the streets of Copenhagen , Rome and Stockholm . <p> Some cities in France have banned rallies since the war began , fearing they could fuel social tensions , but despite a ban in Paris , a small rally took place on Saturday .
