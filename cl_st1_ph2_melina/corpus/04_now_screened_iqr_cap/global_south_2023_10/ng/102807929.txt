<h> The Catholic cleric said international mediators were important in revisiting and reviving the two-state agreement reached in Oslo . <p> A Nigerian Catholic cleric , Hassan Kukah , has proposed a group of international mediators for the ongoing war between Israel and Hamas . The mediators should include ex-Nigerian President Olusegun Obasanjo , Mr Kukah said . <p> Speaking to PREMIUM TIMES , the cleric also proposed former US President Barack Obama , King Abdullah of Jordan , former UK Prime Minister Gordon Brown , and former Ireland President Mary Robinson to serve as international mediators . <p> Mr Kukah said international mediators were important in revisiting and reviving the two-state agreement reached in Oslo , Norway , where a decision was reached for both Palestine and Israel to each have a homeland . <p> The Oslo Agreement in 1993 was meant to resolve the Israel-Palestine crisis by ensuring a two-state solution .
