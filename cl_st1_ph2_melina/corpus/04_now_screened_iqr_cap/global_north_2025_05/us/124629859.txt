It presents a stark picture of public opinion over a year and a half after the October 7 Hamas-led massacre , which triggered the war . <p> The poll was conducted ahead of The Jerusalem Post Annual New York Conference next week . <p> The results showed that 66% @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ state along the 1967 lines , a figure that has remained stable since previous polls . Among Jewish respondents , opposition to a Palestinian state rose to 78% , while only 1% expressed unconditional support for its establishment . <p> One of the poll 's most telling findings was that 70% of all respondents -- and 81% of Jewish respondents -- expressed fear of a repeat of the October 7 attack originating from the West Bank . In contrast , 53% of Arab respondents said they were not concerned about such a scenario .
