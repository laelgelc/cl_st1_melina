Apart from pulling back its armed forces , it had to undertake the dismantlement of 21 illegal settlements housing 8,000 Jewish settlers . <p> Israeli troops were deployed to begin the process , which pulled at the heartstrings of international media outlets like The New York Times . The paper reported on the sobbing settlers affected by Israel 's " historic pullout from the Gaza Strip " , some of whom had to be carried " screaming from their homes in scenes that moved a number of the soldiers to tears " . <p> To be sure , there is nothing quite so tragic as illegal colonisers being uprooted from one section of land that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of land that does not belong to them .
