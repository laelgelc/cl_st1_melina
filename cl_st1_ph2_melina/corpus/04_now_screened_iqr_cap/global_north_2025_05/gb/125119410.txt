<p> Former hostage Mia Schem has reported that staff at the Cannes film festival confiscated the yellow ribbon she was wearing to show solidarity with the hostages still held in Gaza . <p> The 22-year-old , who has dual Israeli French citizenship , said that security personnel took it from her as soon as she arrived on the red carpet at the 78th annual film festival . <p> " I came to support the struggle to bring back the hostages , " Schem told Israel 's Channel 12 News . " Unfortunately , at the entrance to the red carpet , the festival organisers confiscated the ribbon I was supposed to wear . " <p> She said that she " refused to concede " and instead wore one of the hostage ribbons belonging to her delegation on her dress . <p> Fifty-eight hostages are believed to still be held captive in Gaza , with around 20 confirmed to be alive . <p> Schem had been invited to Cannes by the local Jewish community to raise awareness of the hostages ' plight .
