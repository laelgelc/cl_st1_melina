<h> East Lancashire protesters to attend London Gaza march <p> This article is brought to you by our exclusive subscriber partnership with our sister title USA Today , and has been written by our American colleagues . It does not necessarily reflect the view of The Herald . <p> Hundreds from Lancashire are expected to travel to London this weekend to take part in what is anticipated to be one of the UK 's largest protests for Palestine . <p> The march , on Saturday , May 17 , marks 77 years since the Nakba , which refers to the mass displacement and dispossession of Palestinians during the 1948 Arab-Israeli war . <p> Two coaches will depart from Blackburn and Accrington in the early hours of the morning , heading to the National Demo for Palestine at midday outside Embankment . <p> Green Party Hyndburn Councillor Shabir Fazal , who is helping organise the trip from East Lancashire , said the conflict in Gaza is " one of the worst genocides in the last 50 years " . <p> He said : " The reported numbers of deaths exceed 50,000 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the real figures as around 186,000 -- that was months ago . <p> " Gaza has the highest concentration of orphans in the world . Two million innocent people are starving to death . " <p> Cllr Fazal was also critical of the UK Government 's stance on the fighting between Israel and Hamas . <p> He added : " Ordinary people feel angry , hopeless , and helpless . Decent people are heartbroken . There is a desperate desire to do something to help . <p> " We will be attending the National Palestine protest in London marking the 77th anniversary of the Nakba . <p> " More than a million people are expected to attend . We are taking four coaches -- usually it 's only one . <p> " We protest to show solidarity to our Palestinian brothers and sisters , to remind them that they are not alone , that we will never forget them , never abandon their fight for justice , freedom , and self-determination . <p> " On Saturday , Muslims , Christians , Jews , people of all faiths and none will @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " The Palestinian people have said in interviews that they take strength and reassurance from these marches . This march will show that our love and kindness have no boundaries . <p> " We will not be silenced by the government 's repressive crackdown on freedom of speech and peaceful protest . " <p> To book a seat on the coaches , people should contact Cllr Fazal . <p> The Blackburn coach leaves from Masjid-e-Sajideen in Plane Tree Road at 5.30am , while the Accrington bus leaves at 6am from Richmond Hill Street . <h> follow us <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
