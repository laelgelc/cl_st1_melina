<h> Late night tears and hugs for released Palestinian prisoners <p> Two buses carrying Palestinian prisoners released in the Gaza ceasefire deal had to inch through a thick crowd when they at last arrived in the West Bank at 2 am Monday . <p> After the doors opened , women hugged their relatives and cried tears of joy while throngs of people chanted , waved flags and climbed atop the vehicles . Others lit fireworks in the normally quiet suburb of Beitunia . <p> Bushra al-Tawil , a Palestinian journalist jailed in Israel in March 2024 , was among the first batch of prisoners to be released in the truce . Over the next 42 days , around 1,900 Palestinians are due to be freed in exchange for 33 Israeli hostages held by Hamas in Gaza . <p> Tawil began her journey at 3 am the day before , when she was taken from her prison to another nearer the separation wall .
