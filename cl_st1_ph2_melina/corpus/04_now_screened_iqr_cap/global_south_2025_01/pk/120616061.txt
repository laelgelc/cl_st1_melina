El-Sisi called the displacement of the Palestinian people " an injustice " and reiterated Egypt 's refusal to participate in any plan that could threaten national security . <p> Speaking at a press conference on Wednesday , el-Sisi addressed the proposal put forward by Trump , which suggests that Palestinians be moved to neighbouring countries like Egypt and Jordan as part of efforts to alleviate the ongoing humanitarian crisis in Gaza . He emphasised that such a measure would not be tolerated , stating that it would have a significant impact on Egypt 's security . <p> " The deportation or displacement of the Palestinian people is an injustice in which we can not participate , " el-Sisi said . <p> The Gaza Strip , home to 2.2 million people , has experienced widespread displacement since Israel launched its military offensive against the region in October 2023 , following a Hamas-led attack on Israel .
