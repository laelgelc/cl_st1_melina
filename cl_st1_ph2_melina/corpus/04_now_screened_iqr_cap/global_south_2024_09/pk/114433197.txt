<h> Lebanon 's Hezbollah in disarray after second wave of deadly blasts <p> Hezbollah was in disarray on Thursday after a second wave of deadly explosions swept through its strongholds across Lebanon , putting pressure on its leader to exact revenge for the operation it blames on Israel . <p> The attack killed 32 people in two days , including two children , and wounded more than 3,000 others , according to Lebanese health ministry figures . <p> Israel has not commented on the unprecedented operation that saw Hezbollah operatives ' walkie-talkies and pagers exploding in supermarkets , at funerals and on streets . But its defence minister , Yoav Gallant , said on Wednesday , in reference to Israel 's border with Lebanon : " The centre of gravity is moving northward . " <p> " We are at the start of a new phase in the war " , he said . Hezbollah is an ally of Palestinian militant group Hamas , which has been fighting a war in Gaza since its October 7 attack on Israel .
