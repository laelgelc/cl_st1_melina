<p> A New Zealand advocacy group for Palestine has condemned the government for refusing to provide humanitarian visas for Palestinians with family in the country while welcoming a growing number of Israeli " visitors " . <p> The Palestine Solidarity Network Aotearoa ( PSNA ) claims the visitors are likely to be " complicit " in Israel 's genocidal war crimes in the 14-month war on Gaza . <p> The protest group said in a statement that " many if not most of these ' tourists ' are actively serving in the Israeli Defence Force 's genocidal attacks on Gaza and Lebanon " . <p> " The United Nations , the International Court of Justice , and most recently Amnesty International , have variously described the Israeli attack on Gaza as genocide , " said PSNA national chair John Minto . <p> The New Zealand government had no idea how many Israeli " tourists " were doing military service , because they were not required to provide that information as they arrived , he added . <p> " Genocide duty in the Israeli armed forces is compulsory for nearly all Israelis , so there will be a high proportion of active or reservist soldiers coming to Aotearoa with blood on their hands . " <p> Urgent to deny entry Service in the IDF for 32 months is compulsory for nearly all Israeli men when they reach 18 and women are required to serve 24 months . <p> Members of Israel 's ultra-orthodox community were included in the conscription from June after previously being exempt . <p> After the initial period , Israelis must be available as reservists until age 40 . <p> PSNA 's John Minto said the New Zealand government must urgently deny entry to any Israelis who were serving or had served in the IDF . <p> " Combat reservists are now on average serving four months in the IDF . So it 's not just a narrow younger age group . " <p> According to Minto , the New Zealand government had a list of extremist Israeli settlers who it banned from entering Aotearoa -- but he viewed this list as " hopelessly inadequate " . <p> Obligation to prevent genocide " The International Court of Justice has obligated countries to prevent Israeli genocide and work to end Israeli occupation of Palestinian Territory , " Minto said . <p> " When our border immigration officials are not required to even ask if an Israeli is serving in the military , or is an illegal settler , then our government is ignoring both of its obligations . <p> " The soldiers perpetrating this genocide might pretend to be innocent thrill-seeking tourists when they visit here , but they are directly responsible for operating occupation , apartheid , genocide and ethnic cleansing . "
