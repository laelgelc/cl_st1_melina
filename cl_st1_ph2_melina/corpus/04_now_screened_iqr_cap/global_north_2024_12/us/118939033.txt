<p> Yesha Council leaders on Sunday issued a letter to Israel 's Security Cabinet before its weekly meeting , calling on the government to take preventative military measures against terrorist activities . IDF <p> The letter implored the cabinet to change the situation on the ground , proposing three main steps be taken : <p> First , the council suggested temporarily relocating Palestinians residing in terror-prone areas , especially in the so-called " refugee camps , " so that Israeli forces could target terror infrastructure , similar to the Israeli military 's approach in Gaza . <p> " It 's time for a mindset transformation , from a defensive mode to a lethal and effective offensive mode in Judea and Samaria , " the letter concluded . <p> According to Israel 's Channel 14 broadcaster , cabinet members convened at the IDF 's Central Command in Jerusalem on Sunday to discuss the latest security developments in Judea and Samaria . <p> At the meeting , Israeli ministers received a summary of the military 's activities in Judea and Samaria , with National Missions Minister Orit Strook of the Religious Zionism Party urging the military to " drain the swamp " of terrorism rather than " eliminate one mosquito at a time , " according to the report . <p> Graffiti left on a wall of a Palestinian house in Hebrew reading ' War on Judea and Samaria " at the site where Israeli settlers attacked Al-Bireh near the West Bank city of Ramallah , 04 November 2024 .
