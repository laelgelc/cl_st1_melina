<p> The sound of tanks rumbling through the streets outside of Kamal Adwan Hospital woke everyone up , they were already on edge after enduring months of direct Israeli attacks . <p> Then came the loudspeakers ordering everyone to evacuate -- the sick , the wounded , medical staff , and displaced people seeking shelter -- early on Friday morning . <p> It was clear that the medical complex in northern Gaza 's Beit Lahiya was about to face an Israeli raid , like so many had before it as Israel seemed to systematically destroy all healthcare in Gaza . <p> It did n't matter that , according to the World Health Organization , the hospital was the last major health facility operational in northern Gaza , an area that has been suffocatingly besieged and decimated by Israel in its ongoing war . <p> Nor that it was a refuge for hundreds of Palestinians whose homes had been destroyed by Israel and had nowhere else to go . <h> Numbers written on their chests <p> Dr Abu Safia came back and told people in the hospital they had been ordered to evacuate .
