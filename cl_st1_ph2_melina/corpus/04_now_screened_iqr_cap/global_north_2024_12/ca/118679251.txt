<p> The Crown has withdrawn all charges against a pro-Palestinian protester who was accused of assaulting a Toronto police officer during a downtown rally last year . <p> The protestor , 33-year-old Adam Melanson , was arrested and accused of shoving an officer during a Palestinian Youth Movement rally outside the U.S. Consulate on University Avenue in December 2023 . <p> Melanson was filmed by bystanders as he appeared to be restrained by an officer who held a knee to Melanson 's neck and hit him repeatedly . <p> As video of Melanson 's arrest spread on social media , the Toronto Police Service ( TPS ) denied any wrongdoing and said an officer did not put his knee on a protester 's neck . TPS said Melanson pushed an officer who was on a bicycle and that the officer suffered injuries as a result of the fall . <p> Melanson was charged with assault and obstruction of an officer . He was facing up to seven years in prison . <p> The charges against Melanson were withdrawn on Tuesday . <p> In court , Crown attorney Andrew Weafer said there was a " reasonable prospect of conviction " in Melanson 's case . <p> However , he said the charges were withdrawn because Melanson showed he " is prepared to be held accountable for his actions . " The attorney said Melanson did so by completing community service hours and a course in non-violent communication , as well as making a donation to charity , CityNews reported . <p> " This resolution reflects accountability for the actions Mr. Melanson took during the December 2023 incident and highlights the serious consequences of violence against those who serve and protect our communities , " TPS told the outlet . <p> Melanson has denied any criminal liability in the incident . <p> At a press conference on Tuesday , Melanson claimed he approached the officer at the December 2023 rally because his wife was knocked to the ground by police . <p> " When I attempted to intervene in what I perceived to be an assault on her , I was arrested , " he said of his wife and the police . " Although I did not resist arrest in any way , multiple officers used force against me . " <p> Melanson said a TPS officer " slammed his knee on my head , " hit him in the head and knocked off his glasses . He said he was " screaming in pain " while a second officer " punched " his ribs . <p> He said he was left battered , concussed and traumatized by his arrest . <p> In a statement , Melanson said , " This is not just about me . " <p> " It 's about a police force that seems to operate with impunity while undermining the Charter rights of those who assemble and express their demands for an end to the genocide in Gaza , " he said . <p> " They tried to criminalize me , but our community stood together and revealed the truth . This victory belongs to everyone who documented what happened and reached out to provide video . "
