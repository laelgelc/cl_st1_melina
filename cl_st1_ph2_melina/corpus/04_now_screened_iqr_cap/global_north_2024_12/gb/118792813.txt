<h> Israeli strikes kill 12 guarding Gaza aid lorries , medics say <p> Gaza 's Civil Defence says seven guards were killed in a strike in Rafah , while another attack left five guards dead in Khan Younis <p> At least 35 Palestinians have been killed in Israeli strikes in Gaza , including 12 guarding incoming aid lorries , local medics and the Hamas-run Civil Defence authority say . <p> Seven guards were killed in a strike in Rafah while protecting aid lorries from violent armed theft , which UN workers say is the main obstacle to getting supplies into southern Gaza . Another attack left five guards dead in Khan Younis . <p> The Israeli military said it " conducted precise strikes on armed Hamas terrorists " who had planned to hijack the lorries . <p> In a separate Israeli attack , 15 people were killed near Nuseirat refugee camp , the Civil Defence said . <p> " The occupation once again targeted those securing the aid trucks , " Civil Defence spokesman Mahmoud Basal told the AFP news agency . <p> He added that around 30 people , most of them children , were also wounded in the two strikes . <p> The lorries were carrying flour to warehouses belonging to the UN agency for Palestinian refugees ( Unrwa ) , Mr Basal said . <p> Recently - amid severe food shortages - UN workers say violent armed thefts have been the main obstacle to getting aid into the southern part of Gaza . Civilians , as well as remnants of Hamas police , have mobilised to try to counter the gangs . <p> Hamas says Israeli military strikes have killed at least 700 police tasked with securing aid lorries in Gaza since the latest war began on 7 October 2023 . <p> In a statement , the Israel Defense Forces ( IDF ) said : " Overnight , following intelligence information indicating the presence of Hamas terrorists , the IDF conducted precise strikes on armed Hamas terrorists gathered at two different meeting points in southern Gaza . " <p> It added that " all of the terrorists that were eliminated were members of Hamas and planned to violently hijack humanitarian aid trucks and transfer them to Hamas " . <p> Separately , Israeli air strikes on two homes near Nuseirat refugee camp , in central Gaza , and Gaza City , in the north , killed 21 more people , the Civil Defence said . <p> At least six children were among the 15 people killed in Nuseirat , while the bodies of six other people were found after a strike on an apartment in Gaza City , Mr Basal said . <p> Israel launched a campaign to destroy Hamas in response to the Palestinian group 's unprecedented attack on southern Israel on 7 October 2023 , in which about 1,200 people were killed and 251 others were taken hostage . <p> More than 44,800 people have been killed in Gaza since then , according to the territory 's Hamas-run health ministry . <p> After months of failed international efforts to end the war , Israel 's defence minister has told his US counterpart there is a chance for a new deal that would allow the return of all of the remaining hostages , including American citizens . <p> Other reports have suggested a limited deal with Hamas is being discussed .
