<p> Over 50 British MPs and peers have signed a letter urging Prime Minister Keir Starmer to implement a medical evacuation programme for injured Palestinian children in Gaza . <p> In May , Middle East Eye reported that the UK had denied medical treatment to critically injured children from Gaza by not issuing them visas - unlike several other wealthy countries . <p> The letter , organised by Labour MP Kim Johnson and sent to the prime minister on Tuesday , was supported by MPs and peers across several parties . <p> Johnson told MEE : " It is a moral outrage that the UK has failed to provide any support to a single Gazan child in need of medical attention , when so many other caring countries have provided this assistance . " <h> ' Highest number of child amputees per capita ' <p> The letter called on the Labour government to work alongside the Palestinian Authority and the Israeli government , as well as " relevant NGOs " , to create a " short-term medical evacuation programme from Gaza to the UK " . <p> Johnson told MEE : " Gaza now has the highest number of child amputees per capita of any country in the world . <p> " Those suffering from cancer or other chronic and debilitating sicknesses are not able to get the care they need , even if they manage to make it to neighbouring countries . " <p> She added : " The UK is a wealthy nation with extensive medical expertise to save children 's lives , we just need the political will to make this happen , and to be on the right side of history .
