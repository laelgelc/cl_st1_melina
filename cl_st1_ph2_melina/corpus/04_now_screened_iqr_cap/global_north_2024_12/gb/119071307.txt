<p> Hundreds of Palestinians , including children , have been indiscriminately shot dead by Israeli soldiers in a " kill zone " established in central Gaza , according to a new investigation . <p> Soldiers told Haaretz that arbitrarily defined boundaries around the Netzarim Corridor - a military occupation zone established by Israel in the middle of Gaza - were being used to kill Palestinians seemingly at random and without authorisation needed . <p> Those killed are posthumously branded " terrorists " even if they are children . A senior officer in Division 252 said it was more than a simple exclusion zone . <p> " The division commander designated this area as a ' kill zone ' , " he explained . " Anyone who enters is shot . The commander said : ' Anyone crossing the line is a terrorist , no exceptions , no civilians . Everyone 's a terrorist . ' " <p> Another soldier made reference to a military spokesperson announcing that their division had killed more than 200 " militants " in Gaza . <p> " Standard procedure requires photographing bodies and collecting details when possible , then sending evidence to intelligence to verify militant status or at least confirm they were killed by the IDF , " they said . <p> " Of those 200 casualties , only 10 were confirmed as known Hamas operatives . Yet no one questioned the public announcement about killing hundreds of militants .
