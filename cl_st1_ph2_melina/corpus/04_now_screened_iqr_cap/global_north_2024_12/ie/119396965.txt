<h> Israel orders any remaining residents of Gaza 's Beit Hanoun to leave <h> Palestinian and UN officials say no place is safe in Gaza and that evacuations worsen humanitarian conditions <p> Smoke billowing after an Israeli strike in the north of the Gaza Strip on December 29th , 2024 . Photograph : Omar Al-Qattaa/AFP/Getty <p> Sun Dec 29 2024 - 18:12 <p> Israeli forces carrying out a weeks-long offensive in northern Gaza ordered any residents remaining in Beit Hanoun to leave the town on Sunday , citing Palestinian militant rocket fire from the area , residents said . <p> The instruction to leave has caused a new wave of displacement , although it was not immediately clear how many people were affected , the residents said . <p> Israel says its almost three-month-old campaign in northern Gaza aimed at Hamas militants is preventing them from regrouping . Its instructions to civilians to evacuate are meant to keep them out of harm 's way , the military says . <p> Palestinian and United Nations officials say no place is safe in Gaza and that evacuations worsen the humanitarian conditions of the population . <p> Much of the area around the northern towns of Beit Hanoun , Jabalia and Beit Lahiya has been cleared of people and razed , fuelling speculation that Israel intends to keep the area as a closed buffer zone after the fighting in Gaza ends . <p> The Israeli military announced its new push into the Beit Hanoun area on Saturday . It said rocket fire into Israel continued throughout Sunday , despite the intense operation there . <p> The Palestinian Civil Emergency Service said it had lost communication with people still trapped in the town , and it was unable to send teams into the area because of the raid . <p> Palestinian health officials said Israeli military strikes across the enclave killed at least 23 people on Sunday . One of those strikes killed seven people and wounded others at al-Wafa Hospital in Gaza City , the Palestinian civil emergency service said in a statement . <p> Later on Sunday , an Israeli air strike killed seven additional people in a house in Beit Hanoun , medics said . There was no immediate Israeli comment . <p> Health officials said an Israeli tank shell on Sunday had hit the upper floor of the Al-Ahly Arab Baptist Hospital in Gaza City , near the X-ray division . <p> The Israeli military said the strike targeted members of the Hamas " Aerial Defence Unit " operating from the compound , saying the place no longer served as a hospital . It said the militants was using the compound to plan and execute attacks against Israeli troops in the immediate future . <p> The military said the hospital had been used as a command centre for Hamas , adding on Sunday that 15 of those detained had participated in the October 7th , 2023 attack on Israel , and that its operation there had killed about 20 militants . <p> Hamas denies Israel 's claim that its fighters operate from hospitals , and called for UN observers to be sent to Gaza 's medical facilities . <p> The raid on Kamal Adwan , one of three medical facilities on the northern edge of Gaza , put the last major health facility in the area out of service , the World Health Organization ( WHO ) said in a post on X. <p> Israel 's campaign against Hamas in Gaza has killed more than 45,300 Palestinians , according to health officials in the Hamas-run enclave .
