<h> Seventeen killed in bombardments on Monday , say Palestinian officials <p> Relatives of Palestinians killed by Israel in Khan Younis gathered around their white-shrouded bodies on Monday before carrying them to their graves . <p> Palestinian health officials said on Sunday at least 20 people , including children , were killed in the strike at the school sheltering displaced families in the city in the southern Gaza Strip . <p> The Israeli military said on Monday it had struck Hamas militants operating from a compound that previously served as a UN-run school . It said the compound served also as a training camp to prepare and plan attacks against Israeli forces . <p> Women wept as the bodies of the family members were carried away on medical stretchers by men who laid them on the ground to perform funeral prayers . <p> " People were safe , staying in their homes shelters after they prayed the dinner prayer . They were sitting , sleeping , and staying put in their places , " said Manal Tafesh , whose brother and his children were among those killed . <p> " Our children are gone , our children are gone . Our youth are gone . Our children are gone , and our lineage ended . When will this darkness end ? " she told Reuters outside the morgue . <p> The military accuses Hamas of using civilian populations such as hospitals , schools , and mosques for military purposes . Hamas denies the allegations as an Israeli pretext to " justify indiscriminate killing of civilians " . <p> Israeli bombardment continued on Monday . Palestinian health officials said strikes across the enclave had killed at least 17 people . <p> Medics said four people were killed in an air strike in Beit Lahiya town in the northern edge of the Gaza Strip , where the army has operated since October , while three were killed in Israeli tank shelling that hit near the cemetery of Nuseirat camp in central areas and three others in Rafah in the south . <p> Later on Monday , seven Palestinians , including two women , were killed in two separate Israeli air strikes on Gaza City , medics said . <p> The war began when the Palestinian militant group Hamas stormed into Israel on October 7th , 2023 , killing 1,200 people , mostly civilians , and taking more than 250 hostages back to Gaza , according to Israeli authorities . <p> Israel then launched an air and land offensive that has killed almost 45,000 people , mostly civilians , according to authorities in the Hamas-run Gaza Strip . The campaign has displaced nearly the entire population and left much of the enclave in ruins . <p> A bid by Egypt , Qatar , and the United States to reach a truce , which would also include a hostage deal , has gained momentum in recent weeks , yet there has been no news of a breakthrough . <p> Israeli prime minister Binyamin Netanyahu said he had spoken with US president-elect Donald Trump , who returns to the White House on January 20th , about efforts to secure a hostage release . <p> " We discussed the need to complete Israel 's victory and we spoke at length about the efforts we are making to free our hostages , " Mr Netanyahu said in a statement on Sunday . -- Reuters
