<h> Palestinian baby girl freezes to death in Gaza amid Israel 's carnage <p> The 3-week-old girl , Sila , was the third to die from the cold in Gaza as Israel 's onslaught continues . <p> AA <p> Ahmed al-Farra , director of the children 's ward at Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis , confirmed that the baby died of hypothermia. / Photo : AA <p> A baby girl has frozen to death overnight in besieged Gaza amid Israel 's ongoing carnage in the blockaded enclave . <p> The 3-week-old baby was the third to die from the cold in Gaza 's tent camps in recent days , doctors said , deaths that underscore the squalid conditions , with hundreds of thousands of Palestinians crammed into often ramshackle tents after fleeing Israeli onslaught . <p> The father of 3-week-old Sila , Mahmoud al-Faseeh , wrapped her in a blanket to try and keep her warm in their tent in the Muwasi area outside the town of Khan Younis , but it was n't enough , he told The Associated Press . <p> He said the tent was not sealed from the wind , and the ground was cold , as temperatures on Tuesday night dropped to 9 degrees Celsius . <p> " It was very cold overnight , and as adults , we could n't even take it . We could n't stay warm , " he said . Sila woke up crying three times overnight , and in the morning , they found her unresponsive , her body stiff . <p> " She was like wood , " said al-Faseeh . They rushed her to a field hospital where doctors tried to revive her , but her lungs had already deteriorated . <p> The images showed the little girl with purple lips , her pale skin blotchy . <p> Ahmed al-Farra , director of the children 's ward at Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis , confirmed that the baby died of hypothermia . He said two other babies -- one 3 days old , the other a month old -- had been brought to the hospital over the past 48 hours after dying of hypothermia . <p> Israel has killed over 45,360 Palestinians , mostly women and children , so far in the blockaded enclave . <p> It has caused a massive shortage of basic necessities , including food , water , medicine and electricity , while displacing almost the entire population of the enclave . <p> Last month , the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former defence minister Yoav Gallant for war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza . <p> Israel also faces a genocide case at the International Court of Justice for its war on Gaza .
