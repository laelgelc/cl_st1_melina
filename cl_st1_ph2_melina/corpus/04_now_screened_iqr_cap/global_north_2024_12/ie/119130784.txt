<p> Critising Germany 's broadcaster for its biased viewpoint , a senior figure says " grand concepts like freedom of the press , freedom of expression , and freedom of conscience are merely used as a mouthwash . " <p> Reuters <p> They claim to have overheard colleagues making Islamophobic and dehumanizing remarks about Palestinians . / Photo : Reuters <p> Senior figures in the newsroom at Germany 's international broadcaster , Deutsche Welle , are fostering a climate of fear for journalists covering Israel 's war on Gaza , according to a report . <p> Al Jazeera said 13 staffers , freelancers and a former long-term correspondent have exposed the network . <p> They accuse Deutsche Welle of having a pro-Israel and anti-Palestinian bias . <p> They claim to have overheard colleagues making Islamophobic and dehumanising remarks about Palestinians and protesters in the Berlin office , without facing consequences . <p> Additionally , they disclosed several internal documents to Al Jazeera . <p> One of the documents includes a list of " possible comebacks " for anchors to use during live interviews with " pro-Palestinian voices " who make " controversial statements , " such as accusing Israel of war crimes . <p> Other documents include a separate style guide specifically for covering Israel and Palestinian territories . <p> The guide instructs staff to use " West Bank or Gaza , or the Palestinian territories " instead of Palestine , as it has not yet achieved " statehood " . <p> A handout from an antisemitism awareness training event indicates that " hatred towards Jews is often expressed through terms like ' Zionists ' or ' Israelis , ' therefore , criticising Israel can also be considered a form of anti-Semitism related to Israel . " <p> Loading ... <p> Atmosphere of fear and scrutiny <p> Marting Gak spent 10 years at Deutsche Welle as a religious affairs correspondent and senior producer for Conflict Zone , a political interview show that often addresses Israel 's war on Gaza . <p> " The sense of pressure was constant , " Gak , who has since left the network , told Al Jazeera . <p> " There is a constant sense of fear with ( senior staff ) looking at the things you were writing with enormous care , almost paranoia , " he added . <p> " From a journalistic point of view , Deutsche Welle fills its mouth with grand concepts like freedom of the press , freedom of expression , freedom of conscience . And it 's clear that this is just being used as a mouthwash , " said Gak . <p> Another employee said he was preparing a report and in the footage , a 10-year-old child was crying and saying : " The bombs fell and my father and I had to carry my uncle 's body and half his head was exploded . " <p> The employee wanted to discuss the ethics of using traumatic footage of children . <p> " It did create debate in the newsroom that we had in the end . I kept it in , but this particular ( executive ) produced his argument : <p> ' How do we know if this kid is acting ? ' " <p> For most people in the Berlin newsroom , however , it was clear that the child was not acting , the employee said .
