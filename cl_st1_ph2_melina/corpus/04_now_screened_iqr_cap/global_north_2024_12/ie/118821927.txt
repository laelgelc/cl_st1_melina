<h> Palestinian children share verse and drawings <p> " Wipe out that sadness and weave the Dress of impossible from the tears " . <p> These words are taken from a poem written by 13-year-old Roaa Al-Masri from Gaza , whose work is included in a collection of verse , written and illustrated by children from Palestine , that will go on display in a unique and poignant exhibition next week . <p> The show , named Moon Tell Me Truth , will be launched in the Courthouse , Kinvara , next Wednesday , December 18 , at 7.30pm . Further poems will also be on display in a Poetry Trail in Kinvara 's shop windows . <p> The pieces in this exhibition are being made available to the Kinvara Palestine Solidarity Group by UK Charity , The Hands Up Project . <p> Established by Nick Bilbrough in 2016 , this is a charity trust which has a network of volunteers who connect children around the world with young people in Palestine . Through online interaction , drama and storytelling activities , the organisation allows young people to use creativity and self-expression to promote mutual understanding , personal growth and to help with the development of English language skills . <p> In 2023 The Hands Up Project ran a poetry competition which resulted in a book Moon Tell Me Truth , featuring paintings and drawings from the Palestinian children , who were aged between aged nine and 15 at the time . <p> The book presents the the poems and drawings as they were received , and there is a foreword by Professor of Poetry at Oxford Alice Oswald . <p> Nick Bilbrough of Hands Up will attend next week 's launch at the Courthouse . <p> " I 'm delighted to see the work of these remarkable children reaching wider audiences and am especially pleased to be able to share their poems with the people of Kinvara who , I know , have for many years shown their solidarity with the people of Palestine , " he says . <p> In this season of peace and goodwill , the members of the Kinvara Palestine Solidarity Group are inviting people to visit the exhibition , to hear these children and become aware of their resilience and strength , and the beauty of their writing , as shown in the words below from the late Fatema Saida ( 2014-2023 ) . <p> Eyes are for looking and seeing sun <p> Tongues are for greeting and saying fun <p> Legs are for walking slowing and also run <p> Hands are for shaking with friends not for <p> Shooting gun <p> Moon Tell Me Truth will be open from 11am to 5pm each day until Sunday January 5 , 2025 and everyone is welcome . <p> Pictured : One of the images that will be shown in Kinvara from December 18 to January 5.
