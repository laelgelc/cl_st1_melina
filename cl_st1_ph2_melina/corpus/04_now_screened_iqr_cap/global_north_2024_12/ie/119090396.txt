<h> First Gazan children arrive in Ireland for medical treatment <p> More than 14,500 children have been reportedly killed in Gaza since the start of the war between Israel and Hamas , according to UNICEF ( file image ) <p> The first group of Palestinian children to be medically evacuated to Ireland for healthcare treatment has arrived , Minister for Health Stephen Donnelly has said . <p> The eight children , accompanied by eight carers and 11 siblings , travelled to Ireland late yesterday on a plane provided by the Slovakian government for the medical evacuation . <p> In April , the World Health Organisation ( WHO ) requested support from EU member states to medically evacuate Palestinian patients to Europe . <p> More than 14,500 children have been reportedly killed in Gaza since the start of the war between Israel and Hamas , according to UNICEF . <p> Ten states , including Ireland , offered to support the WHO request . <p> The group were met at Dublin Airport by a officials including ( l-r ) Minister of State for Foreign Affairs , Se ? n Fleming , Ambassador of the State of Palestine , Dr Jilan Wahba Abdalmajid and Ambassador to the Slovak Republic Andrej Droba <p> Before the arrival of the patients in Ireland , 65 patients and 163 relatives were already been evacuated to Spain , Italy , Germany , Romania , Belgium and Norway . <p> It is estimated that Ireland will evacuate and treat up to 30 children in total . <p> Minister of State for International Development and Diaspora Sean Fleming said that the children travelled from Egypt but are originally from Gaza . <p> He said that the children suffer from illnesses like cancer , haemophilia , have blood disorders while others require surgeries for injuries and had been receiving treatment in Egypt . <p> The Fianna F ? il TD explained that it was the WHO who first identified these children and then in consultation with Egypt , submitted to Ireland the list of patients asking if the country is in a position to take them . <p> Mr Fleming said that in total there are about 2,500 sick children needing treatment and that Ireland along with other countries have been stepping up to the plate to help . <p> " We will check with the relevant hospitals here and ask can they add an extra number of people and when we get the go ahead from our hospitals here and consultants , we will move at that point . " <p> Mr Fleming added that the process has many parts " because there 's no point in bringing somebody here if the specific treatment required is not ready " . <p> Orthopaedic conditions are excluded to ensure there is no impact on HSE efforts to increase the number of procedures being carried out , the Department of Health said in a statement . <p> Ireland will opt-in on medical evacuations on a case-by-case basis depending on the capacity available at the time , the statement added . <p> " I 'm pleased that Ireland is playing a part in treating children whose lives have been devastated by this needless war and I wish these children well in their time ahead here in Ireland , " said Mr Donnelly . <p> " These children are suffering from a range of conditions that without treatment can be very serious . We have been working with our specialist paediatric doctors and nurses in Children 's Health Ireland to plan their treatment , " said HSE National Medical Evacuation Coordinator , Ciaran Browne . <p> Head of International and Migration with the Irish Red Cross Niall O'Keeffe said it was very touching to meet the Gazan families when they arrived last night , as they took their first steps into Ireland . <p> The Irish Red Cross are dedicating a team of case workers and social workers to allow the group navigate and access any additional supports they may need . <p> Speaking to RT ? ' s Drive Time , Mr O'Keefe explained that after settling in last night , the children have been taken over to the hospital for their initial medical assessment , adding that this would be paramount in terms of determining their plan for the next number of days and weeks ahead <p> Mr O'Keefe added that lots of items including clothes and food have been provided for the families today and it is anticipating more generosity to come in the coming days . <p> " We 've received a massive amount of very generous donations from people in terms of those and food and other items and that 's been fantastic , " Mr O'Keefe said . <p> The health needs of those being evacuated are categorised under cancer , haemodialysis and other severe medical conditions . <p> The WHO has so far coordinated the evacuation of 2,300 patients from Gaza to Egypt since the start of the war . <p> An initial list of 1,400 patients put forward for medical evacuation included 1,100 already in Egypt and 300 still in Gaza . An additional 685 are expected to be added to the Egypt list . <p> It is estimated that around 85% of medical evacuation requests in Gaza are denied , with young men in particular being denied access to healthcare . <p> T ? naiste Miche ? l Martin said there needed to be an increase in the number of Palestinians allowed to access medical supports . <p> " I am conscious that many other critically ill patients remain in Gaza , and their requests for medical evacuation are being delayed or denied by Israeli authorities .
