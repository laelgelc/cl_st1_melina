<h> Gaza has the highest number of children amputees in the world , says UN Chief <p> UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres stated that Gaza currently has " the highest per capita number of child amputees in the world , " with " many children losing limbs and undergoing surgical procedures without anesthesia , " reports RTE . <p> In comments delivered on his behalf at a Cairo conference aimed at boosting humanitarian assistance , Mr. Guterres called on the global community to " establish a foundation for lasting peace in Gaza and throughout the Middle East . " <p> The UN Chief described the conditions in war-torn Gaza as " horrific and catastrophic , " cautioning that the hardships faced by Palestinians in the region could constitute the " most serious international crimes . " <p> The conflict in Gaza erupted when the Palestinian militant group Hamas launched an attack on southern Israel on October 7 , 2023 , leading to 1,208 casualties , primarily among civilians , as reported by an AFP count of Israeli official data , reports RTE . <p> According to its health ministry , Israel 's counteroffensive has resulted in the deaths of 44,446 individuals in Gaza , including 37 fatalities in the last 24 hours . <p> Mr. Guterres underscored the devastating impact of the conflict and the pressing need for a global response . " Widespread malnutrition is present ... Famine is approaching . Simultaneously , the healthcare system has collapsed , " he remarked , reports RTE . <p> The secretary-general also condemned the strict limitations on aid distribution , deeming the current levels " grossly inadequate . " <p> As per UNRWA 's assessment , only 65 aid trucks per day have been permitted into Gaza over the past month , compared to a pre-war average of 500 trucks . <p> International relief organizations have consistently voiced concerns over the worsening situation in Gaza , indicating that civilians are on the edge of famine , reports RTE . <p> They have noted that aid deliveries to Gaza are now at their lowest level since the conflict began . <p> Israel , which implemented a total siege on the Hamas-controlled territory early in the conflict , has attributed aid challenges to what it describes as the inability of relief organizations to manage and distribute substantial amounts of aid , reports RTE . <p> Mr. Guterres asserted that the blockade of aid to Gaza " is not merely a logistical problem " but instead reflects " a lack of political will and a disregard for fundamental tenets of international humanitarian law . " <p> UNRWA reported that all efforts to deliver aid to northern Gaza were either " denied " or " obstructed " between October 6 and November 25 amid intense fighting in the region , reports RTE . <p> Mr. Guterres emphasized that UNRWA serves as an " essential lifeline for millions of Palestinians , " adding that " should UNRWA be compelled to shut down , the burden of providing its crucial services ... would fall on Israel .
