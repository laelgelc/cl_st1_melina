<h> Home Affairs Minister issues 800 Palestinians humanitarian visas after over a year in limbo <h> Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke said he is starting to ' speed up ' processing and issuing more humanitarian visas for Palestinians . <p> The rapid processing of visas represents a sharp gear-change from May , when the government had issued just six bridging visas to Palestinians . Source : AAP / Mick Tsikas <p> More than 800 Palestinians who came to Australia on tourist visas have been granted work , Medicare and study rights as the government speeds up processing of humanitarian visas , ending more than a year of limbo . <p> In an interview with SBS Arabic , Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke confirmed he had personally transferred " a bit less than half " of the cohort in Australia on 12-month visitor visas to the 786 Temporary ( Humanitarian Concern ) visa -- which is valid for three years . <p> " And can I say they have been some of the happiest meetings I 've ever had , " Burke said . <p> " People are constantly worried about their 12 month visas , what happens when it runs out . " <h> READ MORE <p> " Eventually I 'm moving everyone from the protection pathway to the humanitarian pathway , subject to the normal security checks , " Burke said . <p> The process requires tourist-visa holders to first apply for a subclass 449 protection visa -- without Medicare access -- before they can apply for the longer-term visa . <p> " We have got through a bit less than half the caseload at the moment and we are now starting to speed that up . " <p> The rapid processing of visas represents a sharp gear-change from the government , which had in October granted humanitarian visas to just twelve families . <p> LISTEN TO <h> First humanitarian visas issued to Palestinian families <h> SBS News <p> 01/10/202404:09 <p> In contrast , more than 5,000 humanitarian visas were promptly issued to Afghans fleeing the Taliban , and 4,877 for Ukrainians fleeing Russia 's invasion . <p> " Effectively this is the government saying : ' You 've been through enough ' , " Burke said . <p> " ' We want to give you some security , we want to put you on the same visa the Ukrainians were on , with an understanding that during the life of that visa , we 'll make a decision about resolving your status ' , " Burke said . <p> Since Hamas ' October 7 attack on Israel and Israel 's subsequent offensive , about 3,000 tourist visas have been granted to Palestinians fleeing Gaza and 1,732 have arrived in Australia .
