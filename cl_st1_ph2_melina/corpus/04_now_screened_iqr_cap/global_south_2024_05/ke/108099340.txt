<h> UN general assembly calls on Security Council to admit Palestine as member <p> The United Nations General Assembly has enhanced Palestine 's rights within the organisation and called for it to be accepted as a member . <p> Palestine has had non-member observer state status since 2012 , which allows some rights short of a full member . <p> Membership can only be decided upon by the UN Security Council . <p> The US recently vetoed a bid for full membership , but Friday 's vote can be seen as a gesture of support for the Palestinians . <p> Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas welcomed the resolution , saying it supported Palestinian efforts for another vote on the issue by the Security Council . <p> " Palestine will continue its endeavour to obtain full membership in the UN , " he said in a statement . <p> Israel 's ambassador to the UN , Gilad Erdan , said the body had welcomed a " terror state " into its ranks . <p> Addressing the assembly , he shredded a copy of the UN Charter -- accusing members of having metaphorically done just that by passing the resolution . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ recognise a Palestinian state . <p> EU foreign policy chief Josep Borrell told Spanish broadcast RTVE on Thursday that Spain would do so on 21 May . He has previously said Ireland , Slovenia and Malta would also take the step , without confirming the date . <p> Friday 's UN resolution confers additional rights on Palestine at the world body , allowing it to take part fully in debates , propose agenda items and have its representatives elected to committees . <p> It will still not , however , have the right to cast a vote -- something the General Assembly does not have the power to grant and would have to be backed by the Security Council . <p> The issue of Palestinian statehood has vexed the international community for decades . <p> In 1988 , the Palestine Liberation Organisation ( PLO ) , the main representative of the Palestinians , first declared the establishment of the State of Palestine . <p> According to the Reuters news agency , Palestinian statehood has been recognised by 139 out of 193 UN member states -- although this is largely seen @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ limited self-government through the Palestinian Authority ( PA ) in parts of the Israeli-occupied West Bank . The PA lost control of the Gaza Strip to Hamas in 2007 . The UN considers both territories as occupied by Israel and comprising a single political entity . <p> Israel does not recognise Palestinian statehood and the current Israeli government opposes the creation of a Palestinian state in the West Bank and Gaza . It argues such a state would be a threat to Israel 's existence . <p> The US endorses the creation of an independent Palestinian state alongside Israel -- the so-called two state solution to the Israel-Palestinian conflict -- but says such a state should only come through direct negotiations between the two sides . <p> Last month , the US used its veto as one of the five permanent members of the Security Council to block a widely backed Algerian resolution seeking Palestine 's admittance as a state , calling it " premature " . <p> Security Council resolutions are legally binding , whereas General Assembly resolutions are not .
