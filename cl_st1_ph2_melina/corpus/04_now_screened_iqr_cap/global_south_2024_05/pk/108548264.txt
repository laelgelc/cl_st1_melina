Israel 's Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has said Sunday 's deadly Israeli airstrike on a Rafah camp had gone tragically wrong . " Despite our best effort , not to harm those not involved , unfortunately a tragic mistake happened last night . We are investigating the case , " Netanyahu said about the strike in a speech at the Israeli Knesset on Monday . <p> At least 45 people were killed and 200 wounded after the Israeli strike hit a camp for displaced people , according to the government media office in Gaza .
