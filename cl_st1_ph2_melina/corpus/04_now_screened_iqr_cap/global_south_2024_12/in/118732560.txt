<h> How Hamas ' Oct 7 attack smashed Iran 's ' Axis of Resistance ' <p> The October 7 , 2023 , Hamas-led attack on Israel marked a turning point for Iran and its network of proxies , leading to their strategic decline . While the attack demonstrated Hamas 's military capability , it overextended Iran 's resources and triggered widespread retaliatory campaigns by Israel . Key proxies , including Hezbollah and Hamas , faced severe setbacks due to depleted resources and loss of territorial control . The Assad regime in Syria collapsed under diminished support . Regionally , Gulf states and Egypt aligned with Israel , and the US intensified sanctions against Iran . This chain reaction weakened Iran 's influence , reshaping Middle Eastern geopolitics and highlighting the consequences of overreach . <p> Just as Hitler 's failed attempt to capture Stalingrad in World War II marked a decisive turning point against Nazi Germany , the events of October 7 , 2023 , signaled the beginning of a strategic collapse for Iran and its network of proxies . The Hamas-led assault on Israel , though bold and devastating , ultimately overextended Iran 's resources and provoked a massive retaliatory response that reshaped the geopolitical dynamics of the Middle East . What began as an ambitious display of proxy warfare became a moment of overreach , unraveling Tehran 's influence and culminating in the collapse of the Assad regime in Syria by December 2024 . <h> Hamas Attack on Oct 7 <p> The Hamas assault on Israel was among the most coordinated and deadly in recent history . It showcased the group 's capabilities but also provoked a swift and overwhelming response from Israel . This escalation set the stage for broader conflicts involving Iran-backed militias throughout the region . Iran 's material and logistical support for Hamas underscored its ambition to project power through its proxies . However , the attack revealed vulnerabilities in Tehran 's strategy , including overstretched resources and limited capacity to manage multiple fronts . The international backlash against Iran 's role in the attack led to intensified sanctions and increased scrutiny of its regional activities . <h> Israel Retaliates <p> Israel 's retaliatory military campaign extended beyond Gaza , targeting other Iran-linked groups such as Hezbollah in Lebanon and Iranian positions in Syria . These actions further strained Tehran 's resources and weakened its proxies . Following the October 7 attack , Iran faced mounting challenges . Its support for groups like Hamas , Hezbollah , and other militias heavily drained its limited resources , already strained by domestic economic struggles and sanctions . The regional escalation coincided with renewed anti-government protests in Iran , further undermining its stability . <p> Mourners carry bodies of Hezbollah fighters , who were killed during hostilities with Israeli forces , after a ceasefire agreement between the two parties took effect , in Al-Sawana , southern Lebanon December 3 , 2024 . REUTERS/Ali Hankir <p> Hezbollah , one of Iran 's most significant proxies , became increasingly vulnerable . Israel 's aggressive military campaign in southern Lebanon diverted Hezbollah 's focus from supporting Assad in Syria , depleting its resources and operational capacity . This weakened its ability to act as a stabilizing force for the Assad regime . Meanwhile , Iran and Hezbollah 's preoccupation with the fallout from October 7 left the Assad regime exposed . Syrian rebel groups , notably Hayat Tahrir al-Sham ( HTS ) , capitalized on the reduced support from Assad 's allies to launch a coordinated offensive . By December 2024 , Damascus fell , marking the end of the Assad regime and diminishing Iran 's strategic depth in the region.Hamas faced severe consequences as well . While the attack on Israel elevated its status among certain factions , it provoked devastating Israeli retaliation that crippled its infrastructure in Gaza . The group also faced international condemnation , leading to diplomatic and economic isolation . Similarly , the Houthis in Yemen , emboldened by Iran 's initial support , escalated their aggression against Saudi Arabia and the UAE . However , increased Saudi-led coalition efforts in Yemen stretched Iran 's ability to support the Houthis , weakening its influence in other theaters like Syria and Lebanon . <h> Deep Divisons <p> The October 7 attack deepened divisions within the Middle East . Gulf states and Egypt strengthened their alignments with Israel against Iran-backed groups , while Turkey and other regional players recalibrated their positions to counterbalance Iran 's waning influence . US military support for Israel post-October 7 included sanctions targeting Iran 's financial networks and enhanced coordination against Iranian proxies . Israel 's targeted strikes in Syria and Lebanon disrupted Iranian supply chains , limiting its operational freedom in the region . <p> The events of October 7 , 2023 , set in motion a chain reaction that reshaped the Middle East . Iran , overstretched and weakened , lost critical ground in its regional ambitions . Key proxies like Hezbollah and Hamas faced unprecedented challenges , from resource depletion to loss of territorial control . The Assad regime , long reliant on Iran and Hezbollah , collapsed under diminished support and a renewed rebel offensive . For Iran and its proxies , October 7 marked the beginning of a prolonged period of strategic setbacks and recalibrations .
