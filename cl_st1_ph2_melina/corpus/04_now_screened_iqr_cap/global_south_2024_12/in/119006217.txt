<h> Israeli Demolitions Rip through Palestinian Area of Jerusalem <p> Palestinians from the Abu Diab family inspect the rubble of their house after it was demolished by Israeli forces in the al-Bustan neighbourhood of the Arab town of Silwan in Israel-annexed east Jerusalem ( Photo : AFP ) <p> Jerusalem : Tired and sad , Palestinian activist Fakhri Abu Diab stood amid the rubble of his home in Israeli-annexed east Jerusalem , just a narrow valley away from the famed domes of the Old City . <p> In early November , bulldozers from the Israeli-controlled Jerusalem municipality tore down his house in the Silwan neighbourhood for a second time , citing unauthorised construction . <p> " They want to expel us from the area , " said the 62-year-old , who has organised protests against the demolitions in Silwan 's Al-Bustan area . <p> The destruction of homes built without permits -- which campaigners say are nearly impossible for Palestinians to obtain due to Israel 's restrictive planning policy -- has roiled East Jerusalem and the rest of the occupied West Bank for years . <p> Abu Diab 's house was among around 115 Palestinian residential properties marked for demolition by the Jerusalem municipality , which controls both the city 's Jewish-majority western part and its Palestinian-majority east , occupied by Israel since 1967 . <p> The municipality says it aims to address " illegal construction , allow the construction of proper infrastructure and new public buildings for the neighbourhood 's residents " , as well as to create green space . <p> But Israeli rights group Ir Amim said Israeli authorities often abuse the designation of areas in east Jerusalem as national parks or open spaces . <p> The group , fighting against demolitions , said the practice is " designed to suppress " Palestinian development " while enabling the seizure of their lands for Israeli interests " . <p> The status of Jerusalem remains one of the most contentious issues in the Israel-Palestinian conflict . <p> Israel conquered east Jerusalem , including the Old City , in the 1967 Arab-Israeli war , and swiftly annexed the area . <p> Silwan begins at the foot of the Old City walls where the Bible says the City of David was located after the Israelite king conquered Jerusalem from the Jebusites . <p> Today , hundreds of Israeli settlers live among nearly 50,000 Palestinians in Silwan . <p> The settlers ' homes are distinguished by Israeli flags flying from rooftops and windows as well as ubiquitous security cameras . <p> Meanwhile , Palestinians in east Jerusalem face a housing crisis , unable to build without permits amid a rapidly growing population . <p> Abu Diab 's house was first demolished in February . He rebuilt it , but it was destroyed again in November . <p> " This time , they wore me out , " he said , visibly exhausted . <p> " The original house was built in the 1950s . I was born , raised , married and raised my children here . " <p> But now , Abu Diab said that " even my children had to rent outside Silwan . " <p> Now , next to his flattened home , Abu Diab lives in a caravan , which is also under threat of demolition . <p> He and some of his neighbours rejected an offer from the municipality to relocate to another Palestinian neighbourhood in northern Jerusalem . <p> Near the ruins of Abu Diab 's home , 42-year-old day labourer Omar al-Ruwaidi sat by a fire with his son , surrounded by the rubble of his own demolished home and those of four of his brothers . <p> " About 30 people , including 12 children , are now homeless , " he said , his voice heavy with exhaustion . <p> " We 've been battling this in court since 2004 and have spent tens of thousands ( of Israeli shekels ) , but to no avail , " said Ruwaidi . <p> Several families who received demolition orders declined to speak to AFP , citing a fear of retribution . <p> According to Ir Amim , demolitions in east Jerusalem have surged to unprecedented levels since the start of the Gaza war , which was sparked by a surprise Hamas attack on Israel on October 7 , 2023 . <p> Between January and November 2024 , 154 homes were demolished across the area , the group said . <p> On November 13 , bulldozers destroyed the Al-Bustan Association community centre , whose director said it served 1,500 Palestinian residents , mostly teenagers . <p> " The association provided various services to its members , including skill-building , capacity enhancement as well as sports and cultural training , " said director Qutaiba Ouda . <p> " It was a safe haven and a cultural lifeline in a neighbourhood with no community centres . " <p> Ouda lamented the loss , saying that the Israeli authorities did not just destroy a building , but " our memories , dreams and hard work " . <p> Following the demolition , France , which had supported activities at the association , demanded an explanation from Israel . <p> Kinda Baraka , 15 , was among those who frequented the association . <p> " It was our safe space , " she said . <p> " When it was destroyed , I cried a lot . It felt like they could come and demolish my home next . " <p> Baraka said she believed the demolitions aimed to push out Palestinians in favour of settlers . <p> Ruwaidi echoed those fears but remained defiant . <p> " We will not leave Silwan . Outside Silwan , we can not breathe , " he said .
