moderate to severe condition were forced to evacuate to the destroyed , non-functioning Indonesian Hospital , the UN health agency said , adding it was " deeply concerned for their safety " . <p> Ammar al-Barsh , a resident of Jabalia where the military has focused its assault in recent weeks , said the raid on Kamal Adwan and its environs had left dozens of homes in the area in ruins . <p> " The situation is catastrophic , there is no medical service , no ambulances and no civil defence in the north , " Barsh , 50 , told AFP . <p> The army " continues to raid the Kamal Adwan Hospital and the surrounding houses , and we hear gunfire from Israeli drones and artillery shelling " , he added . <p> ' Heinous crime ' <p> In the days leading up to the raid , Abu Safiyeh had repeatedly warned about the hospital 's precarious situation , accusing Israeli forces of targeting the facility . <p> On Monday , he issued a statement accusing Israel of targeting the hospital " with the intent to kill and forcibly displace the people inside " . <p> On Thursday , Abu Safiyeh said five staff members of the hospital had been killed in an Israeli strike near the facility . <p> Since October 6 , Israel has intensified its land and air offensive in northern Gaza , saying its goal is to prevent Hamas militants from regrouping . <p> The military said Friday that it was acting on intelligence regarding " terrorist infrastructure and operatives " in the hospital 's vicinity . <p> Before initiating the latest operation near the hospital , the military said its troops had " facilitated the
