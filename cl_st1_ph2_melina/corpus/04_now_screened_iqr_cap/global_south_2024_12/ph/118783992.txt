<h> Gaza rescuers say Israeli strikes kill 58 , hit flour trucks <p> Gaza 's civil defence agency said a series of Israeli air strikes on Thursday killed at least 58 people , including 12 guards securing aid trucks , while the military said it targeted militants planning to hijack the vehicles . <p> The latest bloodshed came despite growing optimism that negotiations for a ceasefire and hostage release deal might finally succeed , with US National Security Adviser Jake Sullivan saying on Thursday that the regional " context " had changed in favour of an agreement . <p> Seven guards were killed in a strike in Rafah , in southern Gaza , while another attack left five guards dead in nearby Khan Yunis , agency spokesman Mahmud Basal said . <p> " The ( Israeli ) occupation once again targeted those securing the aid trucks , " Basal told AFP , though the military said it " does not strike humanitarian aid trucks " . <p> Basal added that around 30 people , most of them children , were wounded in the two strikes . <p> " The trucks carrying flour were on their way to UNRWA warehouses , " Basal noted , referring to the United Nations agency for Palestinian refugees . <p> Witnesses later told AFP that residents looted flour from the trucks after the strikes . <p> The military said its forces " conducted precise strikes " overnight on armed Hamas militants present in an Israeli-designated humanitarian zone in southern Gaza . <p> " All of the terrorists that were eliminated were members of Hamas and planned to violently hijack humanitarian aid trucks and transfer them to Hamas in support of continuing terrorist activity , " a military statement said . <p> - ' Apocalyptic ' - <p> The United Nations and aid agencies have repeatedly warned about the acute humanitarian crisis in the besieged Gaza Strip , exacerbated by the war that has persisted for more than 14 months . <p> " Conditions for people across the Gaza Strip are appalling and apocalyptic , " UNRWA spokeswoman Louise Wateridge told journalists during a visit to Nuseirat in central Gaza . <p> She added that life-saving aid to " besieged areas in north Gaza governorate has been largely blocked " since the Israeli military launched a sweeping assault there in early October . <p> In southern Gaza , UNRWA said earlier this week it had successfully delivered enough food aid for 200,000 people . <p> But on Thursday it said " a serious incident " meant that only one truck out of a convoy of 70 travelling along Gaza 's southern border reached its destination . <p> The agency did not provide any details on the incident , but called on " all parties to ensure safe , unimpeded and uninterrupted " aid deliveries . <p> As diplomacy aimed at ending the war appeared to be gaining pace again , the violence continued . <p> The civil defence agency said Israeli air strikes on two homes , near Nuseirat refugee camp -- which was again hit later in the evening -- and Gaza City killed 21 people . <p> Fifteen people , at least six of them children , died " as a result of an Israeli bombing " of a building sheltering displaced people near Nuseirat , Bassal said . <p> Bassam al-Habash , a relative of the dead in Nuseirat said : " These people are innocent , they are not wanted . They have nothing to do with the war . " <p> " They are civilians , and this is not a war between two armies , but a war armed with weapons , planes and Western support against a defenceless people who own nothing . " <p> Another strike late on Thursday killed at least 25 people and wounded 50 others in the Nuseirat refugee camp , the civil defence said . <p> - Diplomatic push - <p> In the latest diplomatic effort to secure an end to the violence , the UN General Assembly adopted a resolution on Wednesday calling for an immediate and unconditional ceasefire . <p> The non-binding resolution was rejected by the United States , Israel 's main military backer . <p> However , in recent days , there have been indications that previously stalled ceasefire negotiations could be revived . <p> Families of the 96 hostages still in Gaza since the Hamas attack that triggered the war , including 34 the Israeli military says are dead , are pressing for their release . <p> US National Security Advisor Jake Sullivan , who visited Israel on Thursday and met with Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu , said he " got the sense " that the Israeli leader was " ready to do a deal " . <p> He also said that the Hamas approach to negotiations had changed , attributing it to the overthrow of their ally Bashar al-Assad in Syria and the ceasefire that went into effect in the war between Israel and another ally , Lebanese group Hezbollah . <p> " We are now faced with a dramatically reshaped Middle East in which Israel is stronger , Iran is weaker , " he said . <p> Hamas , Hezbollah and Assad have all been backed by Iran . <p> Militants abducted 251 hostages during the October 7 , 2023 attack on Israel , which killed 1,208 people , mostly civilians , according to an AFP tally based on official figures . <p> This count includes hostages who died or were killed while held in Gaza . <p> Israel 's retaliatory offensive has killed at least 44,805 people in Gaza , a majority of them civilians , according to figures from the Hamas-run territory 's health ministry that the United Nations considers reliable .
