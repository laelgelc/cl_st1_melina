<p> Some documentarians settle for breaking your heart . Others struggle to clarify a long-view geopolitical panorama , with a specific subject or conflict as the viewfinder . <p> " No Other Land " achieves both , and more . It does so with deceptive ease under extraordinary duress . And foremost , it 's a riveting and humane experience pulled from the rubble of a never-ending war . <p> Premiering last year , it ended 2024 as the year 's most-awarded nonfiction film , a likely upcoming Oscar winner in the documentary feature category . " No Other Land " is also still without a U.S. distributor , so its producers are self-distributing . The Chicago premiere of this remarkable document , the work of four directors , opens Friday at the Music Box Theatre . <p> All four risked a great deal making it . Two of the directors are Palestinian : Basel Adra and Hamdan Ballal , residents of the Masafer Yatta region in the occupied West Bank .
