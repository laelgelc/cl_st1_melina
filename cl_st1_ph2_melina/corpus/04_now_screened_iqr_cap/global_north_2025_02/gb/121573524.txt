In Govanhill , Glasgow , this has inspired collective action across many campaigns and organisations . <p> The Young Communist League ( YCL ) is one of those organisations working in Govanhill to build support for and provide aid in solidarity with the people of Gaza . In particular , the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine ( DFLP ) . <p> The DFLP is a Marxist-Leninist organisation and the fourth-largest political party in Palestine . It is active at every level of the struggle , fighting on the ground and at the UN against its people 's ongoing displacement . It advocates a two-state solution , with Jerusalem as its capital and the right of return for all displaced Palestinians .
