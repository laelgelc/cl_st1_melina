It would also start a healing process for wider Israeli society . Several hostages released over a year ago in the November 2023 deal have spoken of their inability to fully recuperate while other hostages they know remain in captivity . For keen observers , the contours of the deal appear to closely resemble previous efforts that have been on the table since May last year , which begs the question : why now ? <p> ? <p> THE TRUMP FACTOR <p> Donald Trump has been clear since his re-election that he wanted the hostage deal off the agenda by the time he took office .
