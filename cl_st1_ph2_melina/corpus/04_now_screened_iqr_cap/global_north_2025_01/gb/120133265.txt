<h> What happened <p> Representatives from Israel , Hamas , Egypt , Qatar and the U.S. signed a Gaza ceasefire agreement in Doha Thursday , and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said early Friday morning his security cabinet would convene to approve it that day . Final approval by Netanyahu 's full cabinet is expected Saturday evening , a delay likely to push off the implementation of the ceasefire from Sunday to Monday , according to Axios . <h> Who said what <p> Netanyahu 's security cabinet had been expected to vote on the phased 42-day ceasefire and hostage deal Thursday , but the vote was " delayed amid last-minute disputes with Hamas and rifts over the agreement " inside Netanyahu 's governing coalition , The New York Times said . Two ultranationalist cabinet ministers oppose a ceasefire and one , Itamar Ben-Gvir , threatened to quit the government if it took effect . A Hamas spokesperson said Thursday the militant group was " committed to the ceasefire agreement that was announced by the mediators .
