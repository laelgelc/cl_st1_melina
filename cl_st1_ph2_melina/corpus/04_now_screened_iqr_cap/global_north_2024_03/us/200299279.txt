The U.S. sanctions block the men and their outposts from using the U.S. financial system and bar American citizens from dealing with them . <p> Rights groups said that among dozens of violent settlers in the volatile territory , the three named in the sanctions list Thursday were among the most dangerous . <p> " All three are hardcore leaders of settler violence , " said Dror Etkes , a veteran anti-occupation researcher . <p> Included in the Thursday sanctions are settler Zvi Bar Yosef and his outpost , known as the Zvis Farm .
