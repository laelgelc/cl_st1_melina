<h> Jonathan Glazer 's Warning at the Oscars <p> The Oscars are not built for somber appeals about current events , though the show has tried in the past to balance celebration with seriousness . Sometimes that effort has worked : In 2002 , after 9/11 , Tom Cruise opened the evening with a vague but elegant speech about needing movie magic " more than ever , " which eased the apparent anxiety in the room . Other times , it could n't completely control the proceedings : In 2003 , shortly after the Iraq War began , the show tried to dissuade flashy displays of emotion and even scrapped the red carpet . But the notoriously vocal director Michael Moore had other ideas , using his Best Documentary acceptance speech to criticize President George W. Bush until he was booed offstage . <p> This year seemed poised for another festive but dry evening , devoid of any real reminders of life outside Hollywood .
