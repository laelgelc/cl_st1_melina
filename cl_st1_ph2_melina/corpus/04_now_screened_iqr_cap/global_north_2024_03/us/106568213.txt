Earlier , the king and the Israeli president visited Amsterdam 's famous Portuguese Synagogue . <p> Herzog hailed the Netherlands 's initiative to create a new Holocaust museum amid what he said was raising antisemitism around the world . <p> " At this pivotal moment in time , this institution sends a clear powerful statement , " Herzog said . " Remember ! Remember the horrors born of hatred , antisemitism and racism , and never again allow them to flourish .
