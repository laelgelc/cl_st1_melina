<h> The derelict hotel sheltering Lebanese displaced from border <p> The Hotel Montana in Marwaniyeh has become home to displaced residents of Lebanese border towns <p> On an elevated position overlooking the town of Marwaniyeh in southern Lebanon , with its pastures and green lands , stands the imposing , three-storey structure of the Hotel Montana . <p> Despite its derelict state , there are reminders of its better days - a marble-floored reception hall , a large pool and wrought iron railings . After being sold at auction years ago , it was completely abandoned . <p> Four years ago , authorities agreed with its owner to use it as a quarantine facility during the Covid-19 pandemic . <p> Today , its owner has once more made it available for use , this time as a displacement centre . <p> A few months ago , the hotel opened its rooms again to shelter around 50 families from border towns following the start of the ongoing round of hostilities between Hezbollah and Israel on 8 October , the day after Hamas 's attacks on southern Israel triggered the war in the Gaza Strip . <p> Hezbollah - an Iran-backed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ UK , US and others - is the largest military force in Lebanon . It says it is attacking Israel in support of Palestinians in Gaza . <p> The Israeli military has carried out air and artillery strikes in response , fuelling fears of a major confrontation . <p> The escalation has prompted tens of thousands of residents from both sides of the border to flee their homes . <p> In Lebanon , around 90,000 people have been displaced . The overwhelming majority are staying with relatives or in rented houses . The rest are in centres like the Hotel Montana . <p> As the sun spread its warmth on a cold day , children here gathered to play football , racket games or take a walk . <p> Many of them are not attending any classes after their schools were shut because of the war .
