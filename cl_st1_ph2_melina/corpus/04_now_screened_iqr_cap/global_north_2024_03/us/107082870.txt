<h> How the U.S. and Israel can get back on the same page <p> As the war in Gaza grinds on into its sixth month , cracks are showing in the once unequivocally united front between Israel and the United States . Meantime , hunger threatens Gaza 's civilians , who , through displacement , disease and death , have already paid a horrible price . <p> If Israel and the United States do not resolve their differences and agree on a viable approach to Gaza 's next phase , including on how to alleviate Gazans ' suffering and to sideline Hamas , only Hamas itself might emerge as anything like a victor , after starting this war by massacring Israelis on Oct. 7 .
