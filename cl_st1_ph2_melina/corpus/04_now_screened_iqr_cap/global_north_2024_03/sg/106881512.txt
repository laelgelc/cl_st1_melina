<p> WASHINGTON - The United States will on March 22 ask the United Nations Security Council to back a resolution calling for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza and an Israel-Hamas hostage deal , increasing pressure on its ally Israel to allow more humanitarian aid and better protect civilians . <p> Mr Nate Evans , spokesman for the US mission to the UN , said on March 21 that the resolution resulted from " many rounds of consultations " with members of the 15-seat Security Council . <p> The resolution marks a further toughening of Washington 's stance towards Israel .
