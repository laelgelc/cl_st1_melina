<p> The Road Raptors are back once again running every day during Ramadan for a special cause . Whilst most Muslims power down as they observe fasting , a group of Muslim lads pound out the miles to inspire their communities to get active . <p> The Raptors , a Birmingham running club consisting of friends and cousins , blaze through every marathon across the UK . They aim to raise awareness of the benefits of running and collect money for charity . <p> This year they are raising money for Gaza amid the Israel-Hamas war by running 1,000 kilometres this Ramadan .
