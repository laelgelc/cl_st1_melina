<p> Around 1,000 campaigners marched through Birmingham 's streets this afternoon , Saturday , March 2 , to demand a ceasefire in Palestine . Two marches , from Sparkhill and Alum Rock , became one as they merged in the city centre to call for an immediate end to hostilities . <p> The peaceful demonstration ended in Centenary Square as the crowd listened to speakers and activists . The Alum Rock march began at 12.45pm as protestors gathered on the corner of Highfield Road to process down Alum Rock Road . <p> As those walking crossed major city roads and dual carriageways , including Saltley Road and Nechells Parkway , chants rang out from the tightly-packed group of around 200 protestors . <h> ' We did n't expect this many people ' <p> Many of those out today had attended marches in Birmingham since last year , while there have also been rush-hour sit-ins at Birmingham New Street station and demonstrations in the Bullring .
