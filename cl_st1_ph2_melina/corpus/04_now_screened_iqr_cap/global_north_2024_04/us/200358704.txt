<p> DEIR AL-BALAH , Gaza Strip ( AP ) -- An apparent Israeli airstrike killed four international aid workers with the World Central Kitchen charity and their Palestinian driver late Monday after they helped deliver food and other supplies to northern Gaza that had arrived hours earlier by ship , health officials in Gaza said . <p> Footage showed the bodies of the five dead at Al-Aqsa Martyrs Hospital in the central Gaza town of Deir al-Balah . Several of them wore protective gear with the charity 's logo . Staff showed the passports of three of the dead -- British , Australian and Polish . The nationality of the fourth aid worker was not immediately known . <p> The workers ' car was hit by an Israeli strike just after crossing from northern Gaza after helping deliver aid that had arrived hours earlier on a ship from Cyprus , Mahmoud Thabet , a paramedic from the Palestinian Red Crescent who was on the team that brought the bodies to the hospital , told The Associated Press .
