I intend to act immediately in accordance with the new law to stop the channel 's activity , " Netanyahu posted in Hebrew on X , the platform formerly known as Twitter . <p> The law passed Israel 's Knesset 71-10 in its second and third reading in the Knesset plenum , the Times of Israel reported .
