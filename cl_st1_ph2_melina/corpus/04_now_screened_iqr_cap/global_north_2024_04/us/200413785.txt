<h> U.S. House passes massive $95B aid package for Ukraine , Israel and Taiwan <p> The U.S. House of Representatives on Saturday passed a $95B legislative package that , if ultimately approved , would provide security aid to allies Ukraine , Israel and Taiwan , and possibly enforce a ban of popular short-form video platform TikTok . <p> The legislation is unusual in that it consists of four bills , three of which deal with aid and the other with national security . The passing of the security aid measures saw broad bipartisan support , despite hardline Republicans having voiced vehement objections . <p> The package will now head to the U.S. Senate , which is expected to pass the measure next week . President Joe Biden urged the Senate to " quickly " send the package to his desk so that it could be signed into law . <p> " Today , members of both parties in the House voted to advance our national security interests and send a clear message about the power of American leadership on the world stage , " Biden said in a statement . <p> " This package will deliver critical support to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Gaza , Sudan , Haiti , and other locations impacted by conflicts and natural disasters around the world ; and bolster security and stability in the Indo-Pacific , " Biden added . <p> The three bills outlining the security aid are titled the " Israel Security Supplemental Appropriations Act " - which passed by a vote of 366 to 58 ; the " Indo-Pacific Security Supplemental Appropriations Act " - cleared by a vote of 385 to 34 ; and the " Ukraine Security Supplemental Appropriations Act " - approved by a vote of 311 to 112 . <p> The Ukraine bill forms the largest part of the $95B aid package , allocating $60.83B in funds in 2024 to aid the country in its two-year old battle against Russia 's invasion . Of that total , about $23B would be used to replenish U.S. weapons and facilities in the region , while more than $11B would fund current military operations . Nearly $14B would help Ukraine purchase advanced weapons systems and other defense equipment . <p> The Israel aid package allocates $26.38B to the country in 2024 .
