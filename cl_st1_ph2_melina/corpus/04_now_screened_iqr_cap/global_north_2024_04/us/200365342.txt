<p> WASHINGTON -- A virtual meeting on Monday between top U.S. and Israeli officials to discuss Israel 's plans for a ground invasion of Rafah in Gaza grew contentious after the Americans pushed back on Israel 's proposal to evacuate Palestinian civilians sheltering there , said two U.S. officials and one former U.S. official familiar with the meeting . <p> Ron Dermer , Israel 's minister of strategic affairs , began yelling and waving his arms around as he defended the plan , the officials said . American officials in the meeting , including national security adviser Jake Sullivan and Secretary of State Antony Blinken , kept calm and did not respond in kind , the officials said . <p> Israel presented a plan to move 1.4 million civilians over several weeks from Rafah to tents that would be set up north of the city , the officials said . But the Israeli proposal did not include plans for addressing sanitation needs or an assessment of how much food or water would be required or where it would come from , the officials said .
