It will run from today ( April 2 ) until midnight on Friday , April 12 . <p> UK filmmaker Glazer has donated seven signed posters from his Oscar best international film winner The Zone Of Interest , plus a selection of posters from his previous film Under The Skin . <p> At the time of writing , the auction had raised over ? 16,500 in its first 90 minutes , with 84 live lots .
