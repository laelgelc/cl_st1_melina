<p> A Manchester arts venue has announced that an event showcasing work by Palestinian artists will now go ahead , following protests over its cancellation last week . HOME has apologised for the ' distress ' and ' upset ' caused by its initial decision to cancel the event . <p> The U-turn comes after artists removed their work from an exhibition at the city centre venue last night ( April 3 ) in protest of the decision to cancel the Voices of Resilience event . Around 70 pieces of art were removed from the Manchester Open exhibition . <p> The show which was due to take place on April 22 was billed as an evening of poetry and writing by Palestinians in Manchester and Gaza . It was cancelled after the Jewish Representative Council of Greater Manchester wrote a letter raising concerns about the event . <p> In a statement last week , HOME said it is a ' politically neutral space ' and that the safety of staff , audiences and artists was ' paramount ' . But this afternoon ( April 4 ) , the venue announced that the event will be going ahead .
