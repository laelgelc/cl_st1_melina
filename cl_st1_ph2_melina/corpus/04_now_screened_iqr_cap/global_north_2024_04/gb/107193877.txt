<p> Artists have removed their work from an exhibition at a Manchester venue after it cancelled a Palestinian event last week . Around 70 pieces of art were removed from the Manchester Open exhibition at HOME in protest of the arts venue ' silencing Palestinian voices ' . <p> The Voices of Resilience show , which was billed as an evening of poetry and writing by Palestinians in Manchester and Gaza , was due to take place at the city centre venue on April 22 . But the show was cancelled by organisers due to ' recent publicity around ' the event . <p> In a statement last week , HOME said it is a ' politically neutral space ' and that the safety of staff , audiences and artists was ' paramount ' The decision followed an open letter from the Jewish Representative Council of Greater Manchester raising concerns . <p> Last night ( April 3 ) , around 100 artists and their supporters took down around 70 of the 500 artworks with more set to be removed in the coming days .
