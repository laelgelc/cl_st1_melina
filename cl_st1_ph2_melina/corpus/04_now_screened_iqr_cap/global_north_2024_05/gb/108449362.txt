<p> Clothes have been displayed in Gloucester city centre today in memorial to the thousands of children killed in the Israel-Gaza conflict . Volunteer organisers called on the public as they passed by to stop for a while and take in the impact of children 's clothes , the title of the memorial titled Children 's Clothes Memorial , which is making a tour of the UK . <p> Throughout the day at Kings Square 2,000 sets of clothing have been laid each representing seven children killed as a result of the ongoing conflict . Figures by Save the Children UK say that since the Hamas attack on October 7 and subsequent response by the government of Israel , 33 children have been killed in Israel , more than 13,800 children in Gaza and 113 in the West Bank . <p> The organisers behind the clothes are from women of different religions , no religion , race and backgrounds . They said they decided to come together to extend solidarity and love to mothers who have lost their children during the conflict .
