<h> Palestinian filmmakers aim to challenge perceptions at Cannes <p> A delegation of Palestinian filmmakers is in Cannes , seeking both international support for projects and the opportunity to alter the discourse on how Palestinians are perceived internationally . <p> " A lot of the time , in western media , Palestinians are just a bunch of angry Arabs , " suggested Palestine-Denmark filmmaker Mahdi Fleifel , whose debut To A Land Unknown is playing in Directors ' Fortnight . He hopes his film " can contribute a level of humanity to the discourse " on Palestinians . <p> " People do n't see us as people with dreams , and hopes and fears . I hope that 's what will resonate with audiences . " <p> To A Land Unknown is about the desperate attempts of two Palestinian men stranded in Athens , trying to reach Germany . It is a co-production between eight countries . " To make a Palestinian film , you have to really create your own pathway , " noted Fleifel . " No film is made the same way again . Every Palestinian film that gets made is a miracle . " <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ October escalation of the Israel-Gaza war . " When we started filming , things had just kicked off , " said Fleifel .
