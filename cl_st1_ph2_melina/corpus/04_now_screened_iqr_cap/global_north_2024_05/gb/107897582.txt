<h> Tragic boy , 8 , killed in ' war crime ' as Israeli troops accused of shooting him in the head <p> Israeli soldiers have been accused of a possible war crime after an eight-year-old boy was shot dead as he tried to flee . The child , known as Adam , was hit in the back of the head . His friend Basil , 15 , was also shot and died from chest wounds . <p> BBC analysis of phone and CCTV footage prompted UN special rapporteur on human rights Ben Saul to say that Adam 's death appears to be a war crime . Israel said the deaths were under review but " live fire was used only to remove immediate threats after exhausting other options " . It claims the boys had been about to hurl explosives at their forces , putting them in immediate danger . <p> Video shows Basil next to a shuttered hardware store in Jenin in the occupied West Bank -- a Palestinian territory not run by Hamas .
