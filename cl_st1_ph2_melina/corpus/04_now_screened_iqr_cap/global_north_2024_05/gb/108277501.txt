<h> Israeli industry figures in Cannes call for dialogue with international industry <p> Israeli film professionals are " open to dialogue " at Cannes despite a reported reluctance to engage with them by some in the the international industry due to the ongoing war in Gaza , according to Osnat Bukofzer , director of the Israeli Pavilion at the Cannes Film Festival . <p> The Pavilion has programmed just two panel events on its seven-day market programme , down from 14 events with participants from 15 countries last year . <p> Security is at the same level as previous years with two security guards . <p> Contrary to some reports , there are no events addressing October 7 nor are family members of Israeli hostages in Gaza planning to attend . <p> There are some 60 Israeli delegates at this year 's market , roughly on a par with 2023 , but comprised of more buyers of international content than usual .
