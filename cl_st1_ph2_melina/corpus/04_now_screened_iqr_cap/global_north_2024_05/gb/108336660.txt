<h> Pictures as hundreds march through Cardiff in support of Gaza <p> The Gaza protest set off from the Aneurin Bevan statue on Queen Street ( Image : WalesOnline ) <p> Hundreds of people from across Wales gathered in Cardiff on Saturday in support for the Palestinian people . Protesters called for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza and an end to British arms sales to Israel . <p> The march comes shortly after the bodies of three more Israeli hostages were recovered this week . The three were among the 1,200 people killed and 250 abducted when Hamas led militants attacked Israel on October 7 last year . <p> The protest in Cardiff coincides with day 224 of Israel 's attack on Gaza in response to the Hamas attack . Organisers said it was held also to mark the 76th anniversary of the Nakba ( the " catastrophe " ) , the word Palestinians use to describe losing their homeland to refugees from the World War Two Holocaust . <p> The march was supported by an alliance of groups including Cardiff Stop the War Coalition , Palestine Solidarity Cymru , Muslim Council of Wales , Muslim Association of Britain @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Syrian Welsh Society , Cardiff for Sudan and PCS Wales trade union . Palestine Solidarity Campaign said they had booked coaches from across Wales , including from Aberystwyth and Abergavenny . <p> Students from Cardiff , Bangor and Swansea universities who have set up Gaza solidarity protest camps also joined the march which started ay the Aneurin Bevan statue on Queen Street . <p> Speakers included members of the Welsh Palestinian community , Muslim and Jewish activists , former Senedd Member Bethan Sayed , health workers , and representatives of local pro-Palestinian and peace movement organisations and students involved in protest encampments at Welsh universities . <p> Hussein Said , from Stand Up for Palestine said : " If we do not fight for a free Palestine now , then the cycle will only continue . We mark this day , 76 years on , to acknowledge not the victimhood of Palestinians , but their eternal ' sumud ' - steadfast perseverance - to exist , resist , and one day return .
