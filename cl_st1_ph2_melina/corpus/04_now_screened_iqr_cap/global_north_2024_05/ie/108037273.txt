<h> ' I survived brutal genocide ' : Irish-Palestinian man reunited with his family in Dublin <p> Zak Hania ( second left ) and his wife Batoul and their four children at home in Dublin <p> An Irish-Palestinian man reunited with his family having finally managed to flee Gaza has expressed his relief at surviving the war . <p> Zak Hania arrived in Ireland on a flight from Cairo on Saturday , having made it out of Gaza via the Rafah crossing into Egypt . <p> He has since been adjusting to life back in Dublin with his family . His wife , Batoul , and their four sons , were able to leave Gaza via the Rafah border last November but his exit was blocked and he was left behind in the besieged territory . <p> Mr Hania described the last seven months in Gaza as a form of dream . " I 'm delighted and happy to see my family and my lovely and kind friends , " he told the PA news agency . <p> " I 'm glad that I survived this brutal genocide .
