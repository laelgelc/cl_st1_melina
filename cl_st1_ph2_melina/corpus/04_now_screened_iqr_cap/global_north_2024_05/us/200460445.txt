For months , the American president voiced his opposition to an Israeli invasion of the densely-populated city absent a clear and credible plan to safeguard the more than 1 million people sheltering there . But he never articulated exactly what price the Israeli government would pay if it went ahead with the offensive anyway , as it repeatedly said it would . <p> That is , until this week .
