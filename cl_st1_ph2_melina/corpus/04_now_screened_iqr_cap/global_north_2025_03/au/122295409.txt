" <p> Since the conflict in Gaza erupted in October 2023 , literary festivals and arts organisations have been caught in the cross-hairs of rising antisemitism and counter claims of censorship of Palestinian voices . <p> Last month , this masthead revealed that the festival 's chair Kathy Shand had resigned after 12 years over her concerns the speaking program could damage the reputation of the festival in failing to present a range of opinions on issues such as the current conflict in the Middle East . Her name does not appear as a donor in the 2025 program . <p> Across five days , the festival will stage 220 events featuring more than 40 international guests and 100 Australian authors , with an expected attendance of more than 100,000 people .
