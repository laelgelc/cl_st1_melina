<p> October 7 is etched deep in the memory of some Telugus working in Israel . Hours before Hamas militants from the Gaza Strip launched a deadly wave of attacks on Israeli soil , executing and abducting hundreds of people , migrant workers from Telangana had gathered in Ramat Gan , a town near Tel Aviv . On that day in 2023 , elections were being held to choose office bearers for the Israel Telangana Association . They were voting for a new beginning for their small community , unaware that the day would mark a new beginning for the entire country . Members of the association recall how they were shocked by the unfolding events . <p> It was particularly challenging for R. Bhanu , a native of Nizamabad district in Telangana , who has been working as a caregiver in Israel for the past five years . He resides in Ashkelon , a town close to the Gaza border . He spoke about his experience in the days that followed . <p> " I was very scared and thought I should go back to India . He still remembers the day in 2020 when he saw the sky full of rockets fired from Gaza into Israel . " It was unbelievable , but my seniors assured me that it 's a common sight and that the Iron Dome defence system would protect us all . By now , Bhanu has gotten used to the drills . When he hears a siren or receives an alert on his phone , he says , he can reach the bunker below his house within a minute .
