<h> From McDonald 's to Coca-Cola : Indians Lead Boycott of US-Israeli Brands in Solidarity with War-torn Gaza <p> June 10 , 2024 <p> Sumaiya Ali , TwoCircles.net <p> New Delhi : It has been more than seven months since the Israel-Gaza war began , exposing the world to unimaginable horrors coming from the war-torn strip . As the situation escalated , thousands of Muslims in India shared pictures of wounded and deceased children , highlighting the atrocities occurring in the disputed territory . <p> Support TwoCircles <p> In a show of solidarity with Palestinians , people in India joined the global boycott movement against Israeli and US-based products perceived to have links to Israel . This movement is known as BDS . <p> What is BDS ? <p> BDS stands for Boycott , Divestment and Sanctions against Israel .
