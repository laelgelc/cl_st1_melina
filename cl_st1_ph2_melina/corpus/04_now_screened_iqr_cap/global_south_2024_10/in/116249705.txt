<h> Israel confirms death of IDF commander in battle in northern Gaza <p> Israeli brigade commander Colonel Ahsan Daksa was killed in a blast in Jabalia , northern Gaza , while fighting Hamas . Daksa , a Druze community member , was appointed four months ago and was leading the offensive .
