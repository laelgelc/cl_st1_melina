<p> The UK government has partially suspended arms export licences to Israel . <p> Foreign Secretary David Lammy said 30 out of 350 licences to Israel have been suspended . The suspension applies to " items that might be used in the current conflict " in Gaza . A review has found a " clear risk " UK arms may be used in " a serious violation of international humanitarian law " in relation to the war in Gaza . <p> Addressing the Commons , Mr Lammy said the Government was not an international court and could not itself determine whether Israel had broken international law . But he said it was the Government 's duty to review export licences .
