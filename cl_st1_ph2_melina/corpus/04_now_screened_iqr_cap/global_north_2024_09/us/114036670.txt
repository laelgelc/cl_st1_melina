<p> Since the beginning of the Israel-Hamas War , much criticism has been directed at Military Intelligence Chief Aharon Haliva , as well as other senior intelligence IDF commanders . Sariel is one such commander , sworn in in February 2021 . <p> The key criticism against these commanders is their failure to provide a warning about Hamas 's invasion on October 7 , when the terror organization took 253 people hostage and massacred approximately 1,200 other . Haliva was among the first senior IDF intelligence commanders to take responsibility for the failure ; in April , he resigned . <p> On Thursday , Sariel announced his resignation in the coming weeks .
