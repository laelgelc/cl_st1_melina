<p> Kate Thompson is an award-winning author of 20 books for children and adults who lives in Kinvara . The south Galway village became the first town in Ireland to boycott Israeli goods , back in 2014 , after Israel had launched another largescale attack on Gaza . <p> The Palestinian-Kinvara Solidarity group played a major role in local businesses coming together for that boycott and , 10 years on , it 's still working tirelessly to highlight what Palestinians are enduring in an ever-worsening situation . <p> The group has a core of about 10-15 members with others also involved and , Kate , who is central to the organisation , says , " when we decide to get things done , we do them " . <p> Their latest project is a short , invaluable primer , Palestine A-Z , which offers people key information on the origins and evolution of the ongoing situation in this troubled region . <p> The 44-page primer offers a short definition of just about everything relating to Palestine , from the Balfour Declaration to Zionism , Hamas and the Palestinian Authority , as it explains the terms behind the headlines . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the gauze we use for surgical dressings is named for that once-thriving port and city which has now been destroyed . <p> Kate is its main author , with contributions too from her colleagues Vicky Donnelly , Dearbhla Minogue and Martin Roper . <p> She decided to produce Palestine A-Z because , last October she realised there was nothing available for people like her , who wanted a greater understanding of this ongoing tragedy that has affected generations of Palestinian people . <p> " The information is n't out there , " she says simply .
