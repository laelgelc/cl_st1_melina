@@109864641 <h> Lavrov to chair ministerial debates at UNSC on July 16-17 <p> Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov will chair ministerial debates at the United Nations Security Council on July 16 and 17 , the Russian Foreign Ministry said . <p> " Three major events are planned during Russia 's presidency ( of the UN Security Council - TASS ) .
