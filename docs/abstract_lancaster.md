ABSTRACT

Detecting Global North and Global South Discourses after the Covid-19

Discourses of the Global South and the Global North varies drastically.
Since 1950 decolonization has been part of the academic research in many
areas, especially in Linguistics. In all areas, specially in Social
Sciences, great authors, deacons of knowledge have analyzed the many
aspects of the colonization, the outcomes and the effects for over 100
years. But it is now, with the eminence of a greater war; after Ukraine
and Gaza; after acknowledging that there are 23 wars being held in the
globe, that it's mandatory that we take a step back and analyze the
hegemonic media discourses of the Global North and South.

The idea of "creating consent" -- (Herman and Chomsky, 1988) is that the
hegemonic media present many articles in order to create a mass of
consonant opinions, responsible for misleading the perception of the
greater public around themes that are as crucial in today's political
scenario. This concept is far beyond doubt, accepted by the academia. By
creating a universe of undisputable truths, the media designed
ideologies. For over two years the question, for linguistics is: can we
use the geopolitical arena to understand the way the Global North
hegemonic media outlets, push a perception, hence the imperialist forces
perceive colonization, see themselves and the world? Understanding at
same time the way the Global South sees the world, themselves and the
many processes of decolonization? Our methodology involves Lexical
Multidimensional Analysis (Berber Sardinha, 2019, 2020; Berber Sardinha
& Fitzsimmons-Doolan, in prep.; Fitzsimmons-Doolan, 2019, forthcoming;
Clarke et al. 2022), stemming from Multidimensional Analysis (Biber,
1988).

State of art

Many researches have studied the aspects of decolonization, promoting
local voices, 'disinvention" of colonized languages, languages barriers,
mobility and identity and epistemological racism and Eurocentric
dominance, all in the linguistic fields up to know, with great
assertiveness and forming a construct of concepts that will be involved
in this study, and yet none have studied the discourses that surround
the wars that followed the COVID-19 and their aspects in the hegemonic
media that were found online and portrayed the extended use of language
in order to create consent that presented many divergences when looking
upon one or another event. As the Global North hegemonic media still
forces an imperialist predominance int their discourses, the Global
South, aware of its fragile economic and political governance towards
the North, when expressing their point of view, manage to talk more
openly and closer to the truth of the events portraited. Hence, the
difference in language, linguistic models appears as a symptom of
mannerisms in order to say and more effectively to hide the ideology
behind the article.

Lacuna

-   Due to Linguistic hegemony and epistemic and theoretical dominance,
    the newspapers found in English in other countries that are not
    English Native speakers are mainly translated. A glaring lack of
    geographic and linguistic diversity exists in the empirical base of
    published research, with Western, educated, industrialized, rich,
    and democratic (WEIRD) contexts being overrepresented in linguistics
    studies. This means that the vast majority of the world\'s languages
    and multilingual contexts in the Global South remain
    under-researched or studied through a \"deficit lens\". 

The overall lacuna highlights a need for more socially just, inclusive,
and decolonial approaches that promote equitable partnerships and
validate diverse voices and knowledge systems in the field of
linguistics. 

Objectives

Show the diversity of discourses that are presented by both the Global
North and South. As the mass medias in the Global North's hypocritical
and consent creator may show the biases programed governmental
engineered mass information designed to create and profit through war,
the Global South struggles to untie the long chains of servitude through
setting a more reliable discourse around facts and unveiling the truth,
the context behind what is being said. In the case of Russian media,
that outlets also a powerful engineered partial campaign of sovereignty
against Ukraine, analyze the contrasts will show the power grip behind
the hegemonic media. Ideology is a key part of what is being said or
concealed and how it plays a part in the diversity of the universe
around us.

Method

Through factorial LMDA (lexical Multidimensional Analysis) we will
analyze the collected corpus and investigate the variations of lexical
patterns, their co-occurrence and how often they appear though
statistical analyzes that constitutes dimensions. Those dimensions shows
the grouping of lexical choices that tend to exemplify an universe of
thoughts, hence, ideology, shows the latent discourses that represent
what the media outlets are engaged to defend and co-create with major
hidden forces.
